Sri Lanka’s 45 all out sends India into gold medal match

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daily Mirror) - Defending champions India secured their place in the men’s T20 cricket gold medal match at the 20th Asian Games after thrashing Sri Lanka by 124 runs in the semifinal on Thursday.

Sri Lanka won the toss and elected to field, with their spin-heavy attack making early inroads on a turning pitch. India lost Abhishek Sharma for seven runs before captain Shreyas Iyer and Axar Patel also departed during the middle overs.

However, 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi provided India with a crucial boost, scoring 38 off 21 deliveries to keep the innings on track.

Ishan Kishan then took control of the innings, producing an unbeaten 80 off 46 balls, including five sixes and four boundaries. His aggressive batting, combined with a late contribution from Washington Sundar, helped India collect 18 runs from the final over and post 169 for seven in their 20 overs.

Sri Lanka’s chase got off to a disastrous start when Jasprit Bumrah struck twice in the opening over. Opener Sineth Jayawardene was dismissed for a duck, leaving Sri Lanka under immediate pressure.

India’s spinners then dismantled the Sri Lankan batting order, with Axar Patel and Abhishek Sharma exploiting the turn on offer. Sri Lanka struggled to build partnerships and were eventually bowled out for just 45 runs in 9.5 overs.

The 124-run victory sent India through to the gold medal match, where they will face Pakistan on Saturday after Pakistan defeated Bangladesh in the other semifinal.

Sri Lanka will face Bangladesh in the bronze medal playoff, with a chance to finish the tournament with a medal.
