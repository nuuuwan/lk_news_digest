The Offices at Cinnamon Life receives SL’s 1st GreenRE Gold Certification

The Offices at Cinnamon Life by John Keells Properties has been awarded the Gold GreenRE Certification for Sustainable Real Estate Excellence in the Existing Non-Residential Category, marking a significant milestone for Sri Lanka’s commercial real estate sector.

This is the first time GreenRE has awarded a green building rating to a development in Sri Lanka, further reinforcing Cinnamon Life’s position as a benchmark for future-ready urban developments.

The Gold certification recognises the development’s high standards in sustainability performance, energy efficiency and environmentally conscious design. It reflects a strong commitment towards creating workspaces that not only support modern business requirements but also prioritise long-term environmental responsibility and operational efficiency.

Established in 2013 by Malaysia’s Real Estate and Housing Developers Association, GreenRE promotes sustainability within the property sector through green building certification and rating systems, training and awareness programmes as well as research and development initiatives. The certification framework is recognised for encouraging responsible development practices that contribute towards more sustainable built environments across the region.

Strategically located in the heart of Colombo as part of the larger City of Dreams integrated development, The Offices at Cinnamon Life offers a premium commercial environment that combines modern infrastructure with sustainability-focused design principles. The development integrates efficient building systems, optimised energy performance and environmentally conscious operational practices to create a healthier and more efficient workplace ecosystem.

John Keells Group Vice President and Property Group Head of Asset Management Rusiru Abeyasinghe stated, “Receiving the Gold GreenRE Certification is a significant achievement for The Offices at Cinnamon Life and an important milestone for Sri Lanka’s commercial real estate sector. This recognition reflects our continued commitment to developing and operating spaces that align with internationally recognised sustainability standards, while delivering long-term value for businesses, occupants and the wider urban environment.”

The Offices at Cinnamon Life forms part of the landmark City of Dreams integrated development, which brings together premium office spaces, hospitality, residences, retail, dining and entertainment within a single destination. As businesses increasingly prioritise sustainable and future-ready workplaces, certifications such as GreenRE further strengthen the appeal of developments that combine operational efficiency with global environmental standards.

The recognition also reflects the growing importance of sustainability within Colombo’s evolving commercial real estate landscape, where businesses are placing greater emphasis on environmentally responsible workspaces that support employee well-being, efficiency and long-term resilience.
