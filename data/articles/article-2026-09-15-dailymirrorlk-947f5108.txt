2027 Appropriation Bill to be presented to Parliament

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Cabinet approval has been granted to publish the Appropriation Bill for the financial year 2027 in the Government Gazette and subsequently present it to Parliament for approval.

The Cabinet had earlier approved the preparation of the 2027 Appropriation Bill at its meeting held on June 30, 2026.

Following its preparation by the Legal Draftsman, the Bill has now received clearance from the Attorney General.

The proposal to publish the Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter submit it to Parliament was presented by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

The Cabinet approved the proposal, paving the way for the 2027 Appropriation Bill to be formally presented to Parliament.
