‘Sri Lanka AI Week 2026’ set to amplify country’s AI potential

Pioneered by the Ministry of Digital Economy and SLT-Mobitel, Sri Lanka AI Week 2026 will take place from 25th to 30th September, bringing Colombo to the centre of AI innovation.

As the biggest AI event of its kind in Sri Lanka to date, the landmark week will bring together global AI leaders, technology experts, innovators, policymakers and business leaders across five major events: the AI Hackathon, AI Leaders Night, AI Conference, AI Exhibition and National AI Awards.

The AI Conference, taking place on 29th and 30th September at Monarch Imperial Colombo, will feature over 60 speakers from 16 countries, including global brands such as Huawei, ZTE, Deloitte, Google and Alibaba. Secure your seat at www.aiexpo.lk or purchase tickets via PickMe or OneTicket to hear global perspectives, explore the latest AI developments and discover what they could mean for Sri Lankan businesses and the wider economy.

Running alongside the Conference, the AI Exhibition will take place from 8.00 a.m. to 8.00 p.m. and is completely free and open to everyone, featuring over 35 exhibitors showcasing cutting-edge AI technologies and bringing the latest innovations closer to the people of Sri Lanka.
