Sri Lanka imports over 800,000 vehicles worth USD 3.8 Billion

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has imported more than 800,000 vehicles worth around USD 3.8 billion since vehicle import restrictions were lifted in 2024, Deputy Minister of Economic Development Nishantha Jayaweera said in Parliament today.

Jayaweera said Letters of Credit (LCs) amounting to USD 1.9 billion were opened during 2024 and 2025, while LCs worth USD 1.69 billion had been opened in 2026.

According to the Deputy Minister, more than 79,000 cars, 12,000 commercial vehicles, 3,148 buses and vans, 317,000 motorcycles and 40,000 three-wheelers have been imported so far this year.

In 2025, Sri Lanka imported more than 90,000 cars, 11,000 commercial vehicles, 2,185 buses and vans, 225,000 motorcycles and 22,000 three-wheelers, he said.

Responding to opposition claims that the country's foreign reserves had declined due to increased vehicle imports, Jayaweera said the government currently maintains foreign reserves of around USD 6.9 billion despite the expenditure on vehicle imports.

He added that the government expects to increase the country's foreign reserves to USD 8 billion by the end of this year.
