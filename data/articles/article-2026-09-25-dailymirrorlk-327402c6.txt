Former Buddhasasana Ministry Secretary arrested over Rs. 4.7 Mn corruption case

Colombo, Sep. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) today arrested former Secretary to the Ministry of Buddhasasana, Muthubandage Kamal Bandara Dissanayake, over an alleged corruption case involving government book-printing contracts.

According to CIABOC, the suspect allegedly bypassed established government procurement procedures on three occasions to award contracts for the printing of a book titled “Dahami Niwahanai Apekama Radhi Heta Dinayai” to a preferred entity.

The total value of the three contracts was Rs. 4.79 million. CIABOC alleges that payments covering the full contract value were authorized without ensuring that the printed books had been delivered to or properly accounted for at the ministry warehouse.

The anti-corruption commission alleges that the actions resulted in a financial loss to the State while providing an unlawful benefit to the entity involved in the contracts.

The suspect is scheduled to be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate's Court for further legal proceedings.
