LB Al-Salamah sweeps boards at SLIBFI Awards

LB Al-Salamah, the alternate financial services arm of LB Finance PLC, has been recognised at the 15th SLIBFI Awards 2026, securing the highest number of awards won by a single entity across diversified award categories, highlighting its position as one of Sri Lanka’s leading providers of Shari’ah-compliant financial solutions.

Among its achievements, LB Al-Salamah received multiple Gold awards, including Breakthrough Performance of the Year, Social Upliftment Project of the Year, Outstanding Shari’ah Supervisory Board, Best IBF Branch of the Year (Maradana Branch), Islamic Banker/Employee of the Year and Best In-house Shari’ah Scholar of the Year.

The institution also earned recognition through the Transformative Leadership Award, ESG Award of the Year, Silver awards for Best Entity, Best NBFI, Best Window Unit and Product of the Year for its Musharakah vehicle financing solution, together with a Bronze award for Best Branch Executive of the Year and a Merit award for Best Fintech Offering of the Year.

The awards also underscore the strength and diversity of LB Al-Salamah’s portfolio of Shari’ah-compliant financial solutions.

The recognition received at the 15th SLIBFI Awards reflects LB Al-Salamah’s commitment to maintaining high standards across governance, innovation, sustainability, digital transformation, product development, customer service, operational excellence and leadership, while reinforcing its continued efforts to advance ethical finance and contribute to the growth of Sri Lanka’s Islamic finance sector through responsible, customer-focused financial solutions.
