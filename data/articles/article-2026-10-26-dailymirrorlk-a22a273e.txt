Softlogic extends warrant conversion window to November

Softlogic Holdings PLC has extended the payment deadline for converting its warrants into shares to 19 November, giving remaining warrant holders additional time to take part in the conglomerate’s equity raising as it continues to strengthen its capital base and pare down debt, a stock filing showed.

The last date for payment, previously set for 7 October, was moved following a request to the Colombo Stock Exchange on 19 October, with the company noting that the change would not affect warrant holders who have already paid. The new shares will be deposited on or before 4 December and begin trading on 8 December.

The warrants were issued at zero cost alongside Softlogic’s 2024 rights issue, which raised Rs.2.02 billion at Rs.10 a share, with subscribers receiving three warrants for every five rights shares. Each of the roughly 121.6 million warrants is convertible into one share at Rs.10.50.

As per its annual report, the company had received Rs.498 million from the conversion, with major shareholders Chairman and Managing Director Ashok Pathirage and Samena Ceylon Holdings having exercised their warrants. A further Rs.777 million is expected, lifting total proceeds to Rs.1.27 billion, earmarked for settling debt and meeting working capital needs.

The fresh equity complements a debt restructuring drive under which Softlogic has signed offer letters for close to Rs.60 billion of loans, securing grace periods of six to 18 months on capital repayments, while requests covering a further Rs.15.7 billion await lender approval. Pathirage told Mirror Business last month that only a small fraction of the group’s debt remained to be restructured, with negotiations with lenders underway.

Group revenue rose 24 percent to Rs.129 billion in 2025/26, while EBITDA grew 43 percent to Rs.17.8 billion and operating cash flow climbed 60 percent to Rs.14 billion. Finance costs eased 15 percent to Rs.13.7 billion, helping narrow the loss after tax by 68 percent to Rs.3.59 billion.
