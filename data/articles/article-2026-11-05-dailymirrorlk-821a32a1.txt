Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Commercial bank PLC, Dialog Axiata PLC and Hatton National Bank PLC with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.9 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Hatton National Bank, Lanka

IOC and Tokyo Cement Company. Mixed interest was observed in Singer Sri Lanka, LOLC Holdings and Tokyo

Cement Company nonvoting whilst retail interest was noted in Softlogic Finance, Citrus Leisure and Muller &

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank) whilst the sector index gained 1.94%. The share price of Hatton National Bank increased by Rs 7.25 (1.92%) to close at Rs 385.00.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting) whilst the sector index increased by 2.27%. The share price of Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting recorded a gain of Rs 0.70 (1.22%) to close at Rs 58.20.

Singer Sri Lanka, Lanka IOC and Lolc Holdings were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Singer Sri Lanka closed flat at Rs 70.10. The share price of Lanka IOC moved down by Rs 0.50 (0.41%) to close at Rs 122.00. The share price of LOLC Holdings edged up Rs 0.25 (0.07%) to close at Rs 375.25.
