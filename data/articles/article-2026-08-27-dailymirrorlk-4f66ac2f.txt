Nuwara Eliya lowest, Jaffna, Mannar, Polonnaruwa highest temperature today

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror) - Nuwara Eliya recorded Sri Lanka’s lowest minimum temperature of 14.9°C, while the highest minimum temperature of 27°C was recorded in Jaffna, Mannar and Polonnaruwa, the Department of Meteorology said.

The minimum temperature readings were recorded on August 27, 2026, with significant differences observed between the central highlands and several lowland areas.

Bandarawela recorded 19.5°C, while Badulla and Katugasthota recorded minimum temperatures of 21.6°C and 22.0°C, respectively.

Meanwhile, Mullaitivu recorded 26.5°C, Trincomalee 26.8°C, Pothuvil 26.9°C, and Puttalam 26.7°C.

In the Western Province, Colombo and Katunayake recorded 25.0°C, while Ratmalana recorded 25.3°C.

The temperature data highlights the notably cooler conditions prevailing in Sri Lanka’s central highlands compared with the warmer conditions experienced in the northern and lowland areas.
