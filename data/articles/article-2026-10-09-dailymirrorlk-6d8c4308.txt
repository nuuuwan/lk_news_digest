Five-year project to upgrade ICT facilities in schools

Colombo, Oct. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has proposed a five-year project to upgrade computer facilities at secondary schools and Information Technology and Distance Learning Hubs (ITDLHs) across the country, Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya told Parliament.

Responding to a question on information technology education yesterday (8), the Prime Minister said an audit conducted in 2026 had found that many centres had not received new computer equipment since 2014.

She said most of the existing equipment was beyond repair and that the Government planned to provide new equipment under the proposed programme, with priority given to secondary schools.

There are 109 ITDLHs operating islandwide under the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education. Formerly known as Computer Resource Centres (CRCs), these centres provide ICT courses for students preparing for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level and Advanced Level examinations, NVQ courses for school leavers, and training for teachers.

They also conduct information technology training programmes for principals, public sector employees and the general public, while facilitating access to online examinations and university admission applications.

However, several areas remain without ITDLHs, while some previously operating centres have been closed due to teacher shortages, a lack of equipment and insufficient student applications.

The Prime Minister said proposals to establish new centres had not received approval from the National Planning Department, as no funds had been allocated for constructing new buildings in 2024, 2025 and 2026. Discussions are now underway with the department to resubmit proposals for establishing centres in the areas concerned.

Although annual allocations are made for maintenance and repairs, the proposed five-year programme is intended to provide a long-term solution to the existing infrastructure and equipment shortages.

Subject to the necessary approvals, the Government aims to address at least 50 per cent of the identified infrastructure and facility requirements at ITDLHs and secondary schools by 2027.
