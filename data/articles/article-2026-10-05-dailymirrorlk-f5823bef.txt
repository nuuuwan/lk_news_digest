Janashakthi Life among SL’s Top 20 Women-Friendly Workplaces 2026

Janashakthi Life has been recognised among Sri Lanka’s Top 20 Women-Friendly Workplaces 2026, at the Satynmag Women Friendly Workplace Awards.

The recognition reflects the company’s continued focus on creating an inclusive workplace where women are empowered to build meaningful careers and progress into leadership roles across all areas of the business.

Women represent a significant part of Janashakthi Life’s workforce and continue to take on leadership responsibilities across all disciplines of the company. This includes growing representation within life insurance sales management, an area traditionally dominated by men, alongside senior leadership roles across business, operations and corporate functions.

The company continues to focus on creating opportunities for women to develop their capabilities, broaden their experience and progress into leadership. Through leadership development, mentoring, cross-functional exposure and career development opportunities, Janashakthi Life aims to create an environment where women can build sustainable and rewarding careers.

“At Janashakthi Life, our endeavour is to provide opportunities for our female workforce to build professional careers, creating an inclusive environment and inspiring them to make meaningful contributions while paving the way to grow and accomplish both career and individual milestones. This recognition reflects the culture we are building and more importantly, celebrates their achievements as they reach their goals,” said Janashakthi Insurance Director and CEO Ravi Liyanage.

Janashakthi Insurance Head of Human Resources Danushka Samarasinghe commented, “Creating a women-friendly workplace is about supporting women through different stages of their lives while continuing to give them opportunities to learn, contribute and progress. We are focused on making this a part of the everyday employee experience.”
