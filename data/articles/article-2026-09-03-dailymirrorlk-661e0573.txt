Ex-ambassador praises efficient passport service

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Former Sri Lankan Ambassador to the United States, Mexico and Trinidad and Tobago Mahinda Samarasinghe has praised the staff at Sri Lanka’s Passport Office for their helpful and efficient service.

In a social media post, Samarasinghe said he visited the Passport Office today to obtain an ordinary passport and thanked the officials for assisting him.

“Everyone there was very helpful and efficient. Thank you very much,” he said.
