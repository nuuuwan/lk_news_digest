Customs tops Rs.2.2tn annual revenue target ahead of schedule

Sri Lanka Customs has surpassed its 2026 revenue target with more than two months remaining in the year, collecting Rs.2,208 billion by October 13, against a full-year goal of Rs.2,207 billion.

The milestone came nearly a month earlier than in 2025, when Customs reached its initial annual target of Rs.2,115 billion on November 11, despite this year’s target being Rs.92 billion higher.

Customs attributed the performance to more efficient revenue collection and efforts to minimise fraud and corruption.

The 2026 target is, however, below Rs.2,557.5 billion Customs collected for the whole of 2025. Last year’s initial target was revised upwards to Rs.2,231 billion after it was achieved, with final collections exceeding the revised goal by Rs.326.5 billion.

The latest revenue milestone comes as Customs moves towards the mandatory use of digital signatures for customs declarations.

According to the announcement by the Finance Ministry, the use of digital signatures began on October 01 and will become compulsory from October 19, when the documents submitted under the previous method will no longer be accepted.

Customs said the digitisation of the declaration process and other reforms were expected to further improve the efficiency of its operations.
