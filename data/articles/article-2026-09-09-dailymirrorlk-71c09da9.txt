Over 2,400 Cyclone Ditwah-displaced people to be resettled soon

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Around 2,422 people displaced by Cyclone Ditwah last year are expected to be resettled soon, a government MP told Parliament today.

MP Manoj Rajapaksa said land is currently being cleared to facilitate the resettlement, adding that 52 plots have already been allocated to some families, while houses are to be constructed on 10 plots.

He said the resettlement is being carried out on lands approved by the National Building Research Organisation (NBRO).

The MP also revealed that 642 displaced persons had opted to resettle outside the Kegalle District.

He said resettlement activities in areas including Bulathkohupitiya and Yatiyantota have already been completed.

Meanwhile, MP Sujith Sanjay Perera said the number of people displaced by the cyclone in Kegalle is as high as 3,064.
