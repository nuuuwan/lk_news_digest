Police open fire at three-wheeler in Borella

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - A three-wheeler came under police fire in the Veluwana area of Borella after its occupants allegedly ignored orders to stop, police said.

According to police, the vehicle had been driven despite instructions issued by officers from the Kompannavidiya Police Division.

Five people were inside the three-wheeler when police opened fire.

Three swords were subsequently discovered inside the vehicle and taken into police custody.
