Dreamron College of Art & Beauty celebrates 20 years in business

Chairman Dr. Priyanka Perera and Directress Nisha Perera handing over a scholarship

Dreamron College of Art & Beauty recently celebrated its 20th anniversary, marking two decades of empowering youth, transforming the beauty industry, and contributing to the nation’s economic development.

Parallel to the celebration, 20 students representing all regions of the country were awarded full scholarships.

Furthermore, Dreamron has announced a 25 percent discount on all courses throughout the month of July, providing aspiring students with an excellent opportunity to pursue professional education at a reduced cost.

Founded in 2006 as a nonprofit institution, under the leadership and concept of Dr. Priyanka Perera and the Dreamron Group, the institution was established with the mission of elevating Sri Lanka’s beauty and wellness industry through internationally recognized education and professional training.

Over the past two decades, Dreamron College of Art & Beauty has successfully trained more than 20,000 students, producing highly skilled beauty professionals who now serve both local

and international markets. Many graduates have also emerged as successful entrepreneurs, salon owners, and industry leaders.

The institution offers a wide range of professional programs, including Hair Artistry, Beauty Therapy, Nail Care Technology, and Bridal Styling, along with diploma courses, vocational training programs, and government-recognized NVQ Level 4 and Level 5 qualifications. These programs are conducted under the guidance and approval of relevant national vocational and educational authorities.

With a strong commitment to quality education, Dreamron College has continuously invested in modern training facilities, advanced equipment, air-conditioned classrooms, and innovative blended learning methods supported by a Learning Management System (LMS). Students are also provided with practical training opportunities through registered salons and hands-on industry exposure.

Today, Dreamron College of Art & Beauty operates through 10 branches across Sri Lanka, making professional beauty education accessible to thousands of young people nationwide. The institution has also expanded its reach internationally, attracting students and professionals from countries such as Bangladesh and the Maldives.

Beyond education, Dreamron College continues to play a vital role in social and economic empowerment by creating employment opportunities, supporting entrepreneurship, and contributing to foreign exchange generation through internationally employable graduates.
