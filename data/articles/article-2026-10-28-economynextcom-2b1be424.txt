Sri Lanka’s Alliance Finance to raise up to Rs1bn in orange bond issue

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Alliance Finance Company PLC plans to raise up to 1 billion rupees through an issuance of senior, gender-responsive, orange-labeled bonds, the company said in a stock exchange filing.

The finance company will issue up to 10 million listed, rated, unsecured, redeemable bonds at a par value of 100 rupees each.

“Proceeds will be allocated across eligible social project categories, including MSME Value Chain Financing, MSME Climate Financing and Micro Finance with ring-fenced targets directed towards women-led enterprises and female beneficiaries,” the company said in a regulatory filing.

The bonds will have a tenor of three years, with capital repayment structured as a bullet payment at maturity.

The company noted that the planned debt issue remains conditional on approvals from financial and market authorities:

“The proposed, Orange-Labeled Bond Issuance is subject to obtaining all necessary regulatory approvals, including clearance from the Rating agency, the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) and the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) and provided other conditions are acceptable to the Company.”

The issuance will move forward once regulatory clearances are finalized.(Colombo/Oct28/2026)
