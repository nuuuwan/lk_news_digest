Sri Lanka, India exchange agreements for USD 350 mn Credit Line

Colombo, August 5 (Daily Mirror)- Sri Lanka and India exchanged agreements today on INR-denominated Lines of Credit for USD 350 mn, which form part of the USD 450 mn reconstruction package extended by India in the wake of Cyclone Ditwah.

The agreements were signed following a meeting with Indian Foreign Secretary Shri. Vikram Misri and President Anura Kumara Dissnayake in Colombo.

"Discussions focused on issues of mutual interest and review of key bilateral projects, including India assisted development projects in Sri Lanka," the Indian High Commission in Sri Lanka said.

"The LoCs will support reconstruction, infrastructure development and procurement requirements that arose in the aftermath of the cyclone," the High Commission added.
