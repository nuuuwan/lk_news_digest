Union Assurance-SLIM launch Agency Management Development Programme

Union Assurance PLC has partnered with the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM) to launch the Union Assurance Agency Management Development Programme, a structured leadership development initiative designed to strengthen leadership capabilities across its agency distribution channel.

The SLIM advances marketing excellence through quality education, professional qualifications, leadership development and research, empowering professionals and organisations to drive business growth and contribute to national economic prosperity.

The partnership was formalised through the signing of a memorandum of understanding recently, marking another significant milestone in Union Assurance’s ongoing commitment to nurturing future-ready leaders and developing a high-performing sales force.

Developed specifically for the agency management team, the programme is designed to equip the participants with critical leadership competencies required to navigate an evolving business landscape.

The curriculum will focus on key areas including leadership and culture building, situational leadership, building high-performance teams, decision making and problem solving and business finance for leaders.

Through this collaboration, the participants will benefit from industry-recognised learning experiences delivered in partnership with SLIM, enabling them to strengthen their leadership effectiveness while driving sustainable business growth and performance across their respective teams.

Union Assurance Chief Agency Officer Imtiyaz Aniff stated, “Through this partnership with SLIM, we are empowering our agency leaders to strengthen their capabilities, broaden their perspectives and develop the skills needed to lead high-performing teams, drive sustainable growth and deliver on our promise of protecting the progress of all our stakeholders in an increasingly dynamic environment.”

SLIM CEO Chamil Wickremasinghe stated, “Our partnership with Union Assurance reflects a shared dedication to continuous learning, leadership excellence and professional growth. Through initiatives such as this, we aim to equip professionals with the knowledge, skills and strategic thinking needed to embrace change, drive innovation, enhance organisational performance and contribute to sustainable business growth and national economic prosperity.”
