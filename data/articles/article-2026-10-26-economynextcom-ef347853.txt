Sri Lanka stocks flat, ASPI moves marginal 0.02-pct

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Colombo Stock Exchange was flat within the first hour of trading on Monday, CSE data showed, with the benchmark All Share Price Index moving up a marginal 0.02 percent.

The ASPI was up 3.26 points at 19,688.17, while the more liquid S&P SL20 was up 0.06 percent, or 3.17 points, at 5,616.80.

Positive contributors to the ASPI were Commercial Bank of Ceylon (up 0.38 percent at 196.75 rupees), Dialog Axiata (up 0.48 percent at 42.30 rupees), Hatton National Bank (up 0.47 percent at 371.00 rupees), and Sampath Bank (up 0.36 percent at 139.00 rupees).

Hayleys (down 0.69 percent at 215.50 rupees), Odel (down 4.72 percent at 10.10 rupees), and Browns Investments (down 2.22 percent at 4.40 rupees) were top negative contributors.

Market turnover was 118.78 million rupees. Capital goods led turnover with 34.13 million rupees.

In corporate news, Sri Lanka’s Nations Trust Bank PLC announced plans to raise 13.87 billion rupees through a rights issue to strengthen its Tier 1 capital base and support balance sheet expansion. The bank will issue 45,467,286 Ordinary Non-Voting Convertible Shares at 305 rupees each, offering existing shareholders 3 new shares for every 22 shares held as of November 18. (Colombo/Oct26/2026)
