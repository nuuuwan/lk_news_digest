Vijitha Herath leaves for New Delhi to attend 25th IORA meeting

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment, and Tourism Vijitha Herath is scheduled to depart for New Delhi, India, today to attend the 25th Council of Ministers (COM) Meeting of the Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA), which takes place tomorrow.

In an official statement, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs noted that as a member of the IORA Troika and the Immediate Past Chair (2023–2025), Sri Lanka remains a committed partner in advancing regional economic cooperation, maritime safety, and sustainable development across the Indian Ocean region.

Ahead of tomorrow's ministerial gathering, the IORA Committee of Senior Officials (CSO) is currently convening in New Delhi from November 25 to 26.
