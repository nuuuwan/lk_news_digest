Rosamund Pike visits Galle Face Hotel during humanitarian mission to SL

Galle Face Hotel recently welcomed British actress and humanitarian advocate Rosamund Pike during her visit to Sri Lanka.

Pike’s visit also reflected her longstanding commitment to humanitarian advocacy.

Since 2021, Pike has served as the first official Ambassador for MAG (Mines Advisory Group), supporting its work to clear landmines and unexploded ordnance and helping raise awareness of their lasting impact on communities. Her visit carried particular relevance in Sri Lanka, where MAG has worked since 2002 to support clearance efforts and help communities safely reclaim land for homes, agriculture and livelihoods.

During her time in Colombo, Galle Face Hotel was honoured to host Pike, continuing a tradition of welcoming some of the world’s most distinguished personalities that stretches back more than 160 years.

As part of her visit, Pike signed the Galle Face Hotel VIP Guest Book, adding her name to a treasured record of eminent personalities, dignitaries, artists and global figures who have crossed the hotel’s threshold through generations.

The tradition reflects Galle Face Hotel’s longstanding commitment to recognising its distinguished visitors and preserving their place within the property’s remarkable living history.

To further commemorate the occasion, a dedicated museum frame honouring Rosamund Pike’s visit was placed within the hotel’s historic museum collection, creating a lasting record of her stay and her meaningful journey to Sri Lanka.

Rosamund Pike’s signature and museum frame now form part of this continuing legacy, marking another distinguished visit within the living history of Galle Face Hotel.

Her stay carried added significance, reflecting her commitment to a humanitarian cause with particular relevance to Sri Lanka.
