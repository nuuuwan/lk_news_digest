Maliban Group celebrates eight wins at SLIM Awards

Maliban Group has secured eight awards at the SLIM National Sales Awards 2026.

The recognition highlights the contribution of the Group’s people in strengthening the Maliban brand and supporting its continued growth.

The SLIM National Sales Awards (NASCO), organised by the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM), recognise outstanding sales professionals and teams for their achievements, capabilities, and contribution to organisational performance. The awards provide a platform to celebrate individuals and teams who demonstrate excellence in sales, leadership, customer engagement, and business performance.
