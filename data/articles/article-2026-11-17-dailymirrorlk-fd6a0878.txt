Sri Lanka make it four wins in a row at Emerging Nations Trophy

NOV.17 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka strengthened their position at the top of Group A with another convincing victory, while the Netherlands continued their winning streak in Group B on Day 4 of the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy 2026 in Ajman.

Vishmi Gunaratne starred with an unbeaten 63 off 45 balls as Sri Lanka chased down the UAE's total of 106 for eight, reaching 107 for five in just 15 overs to seal a five-wicket victory with 30 balls remaining.

Gunaratne received valuable support from Kaushini Nuthyangana, who scored 25 in a 67-run partnership for the third wicket. Kavisha Dilhari also played a key role with the ball, claiming three wickets for 22 runs, while Nimasha Meepage took two for 20.

The result gave Sri Lanka their fourth consecutive victory, taking them to eight points from four matches and keeping them at the top of Group A.

Meanwhile, the Netherlands maintained their unbeaten campaign with a five-wicket victory over Bangladesh. Sterre Kalis led the chase with an unbeaten 68 off 50 deliveries as the Dutch side reached their target of 130 with two balls to spare.

Pakistan also returned to winning ways, defeating Zimbabwe by 51 runs, with captain Muneeba Ali scoring 51 and Nashra Sandhu claiming three wickets for just eight runs.

In the other match, Ireland registered a 45-run victory over Papua New Guinea, powered by captain Gaby Lewis's 60 and Cara Murray's three-wicket haul.

With four matches completed, Sri Lanka lead Group A with eight points. In Group B, Pakistan and the Netherlands are both on six points, with Pakistan holding the advantage on net run rate.
