Rupee depreciates over 6% against USD by end of October

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan rupee depreciated 6.8% against the US dollar during the first ten months of 2026, continuing its downward trend in October, according to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL).

The rupee closed October at Rs. 332.61 against the US dollar, compared with Rs. 330.83 at the end of September and Rs. 309.99 at the end of 2025.

The year-to-date depreciation had stood at 6.3% at the end of September, indicating a further weakening of the local currency during October, according to the CBSL's External Sector Performance report for September.

Meanwhile, Sri Lanka's gross official reserves, including the swap facility with the People's Bank of China, stood at US$6.9 billion at the end of September 2026, the Central Bank reported.
