FBI team visits Sri Lanka for scam probe

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and the US Department of Justice (DOJ) have conducted a weeklong visit to Sri Lanka to strengthen cooperation with local law enforcement authorities in investigating scam centres targeting Americans.

The visit, held from November 16 to 22, focused on advancing investigations into transnational scam networks and strengthening collaboration between US and Sri Lankan authorities.

During the visit, FBI and DOJ personnel held discussions with the Attorney General’s Department and the Sri Lanka Police, including the Computer Crimes Investigation Unit (CCID), Criminal Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) and Technical Aid Unit (TAU).

The discussions focused on investigative strategies, digital evidence training, and the extraction of evidence recovered during Sri Lanka Police raids, while also strengthening coordination against transnational scam networks.

According to the US Embassy in Sri Lanka, two active scam centre investigations were reviewed during the engagement, while more than 80 electronic devices were examined for digital evidence.

The Embassy said Sri Lankan law enforcement authorities and their US counterparts would continue sharing information, pursuing investigative leads and working together to disrupt transnational criminal networks.
