New move to speed up Motor Traffic services

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) has launched a programme aimed at reducing the time taken by the public to obtain services, Commissioner General of Motor Traffic Retired Air Vice Marshal Thushara Fernando said.

He said discussions had been held with all Commissioners and necessary arrangements were being made to ensure that members of the public receive the services they require within the shortest possible time.

According to the Commissioner General, people who arrive at the Department as early as 7.00 or 8.00 a.m. have sometimes had to remain there for several hours, with some leaving only around 2.00 p.m.

He said the new measures were aimed at improving customer service and reducing waiting times, with the Department expecting to provide the required services within a maximum of two and a half hours.

Fernando said the initiative was being implemented to make the process more efficient and convenient for the general public.
