LAUGFS cuts gas prices

LAUGFS Gas PLC yesterday said it has cut the price of its 12.5 kg domestic LPG cylinder by Rs. 745 to Rs. 5,995. The cut took effect from midnight yesterday, four days after the company raised its prices.

The price of the 5 kg domestic cylinder came down by Rs. 322 to Rs. 2,398. The company said the reduction also took account of the difficulties consumers face.

The cut still leaves prices above where they were before the increase, which took effect from midnight on 13 November, 2026. At the new prices, the 12.5 kg cylinder costs Rs. 1,030 more than before the hike and the 5 kg cylinder Rs. 410 more.

“Although the company will continue to incur losses at the revised price, the management of LAUGFS Gas PLC has decided to absorb these losses, considering the request made by [portfolio] Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe on behalf of consumers, while prioritising consumer relief despite prevailing financial challenges,” the company said.

The company had held its prices steady for a quarter before the increase, even as its input costs rose. Saudi Aramco’s monthly contract prices are the benchmark for most LPG imported into Asia.

“Despite rising import costs, including Saudi Aramco contract prices and freight charges due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, LAUGFS Gas PLC absorbed these additional costs without revising prices for three consecutive months,” the company said.

“However, sustained financial losses subsequently compelled the company to increase the price of a 12.5 kg LPG cylinder by Rs. 1,775,” it added.

Consumers can get district-wise prices and check product availability through the LAUGFS Gas hotline on 1345.
