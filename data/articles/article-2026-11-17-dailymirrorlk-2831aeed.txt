Indices close in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Dialog Axiata and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 10.5 Bn.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Cargills, Convenience Foods and Dialog Axiata. Mixed interest was observed in Aitken Spence, Dipped Products and Commercial Bank whilst retail interest was noted in UB Finance Company, Browns Investments and Softlogic Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Food & Staples Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Cargills) whilst the sector index lost 1.06%. The share price of Cargills decreased by Rs 8.75 (1.27%) to close at Rs 679.00.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Convenience Foods) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.20%. The share price of Convenience Foods gained Rs 93.50 (4.62%) to close at Rs 2,119.25.

Dialog Axiata, Aitken Spence and Dipped Products were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata moved down by Rs 0.60 (1.20%) to close at Rs 49.60. The share price of Aitken

Spence recorded a loss of Rs 1.00 (0.66%) to close at Rs 150.00. The share price of Dipped Products declined by Rs 1.90 (2.57%) to close at Rs 72.00.
