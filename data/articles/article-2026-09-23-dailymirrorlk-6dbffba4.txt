Three new High Court Judges appointed

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake today appointed three new High Court Judges, with their letters of appointment formally handed over at the Presidential Secretariat.

According to the President’s Media Division (PMD), the new appointments were made from among Special Grade officers of the Judicial Service.

The appointees are Perumal Sivakumar, currently serving as the District Judge of Jaffna; Anandi Kanagaratnam, Senior Assistant Secretary of the Judicial Service Commission; and Gnanesha Lalith Kannangara, currently serving as the District Judge of Colombo.

President Dissanayake presented the letters of appointment to the three judges at a ceremony held at the Presidential Secretariat today (23), marking their appointment to the High Court bench.

The appointments come as the three senior Judicial Service officers take up their new roles as High Court Judges.
