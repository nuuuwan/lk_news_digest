RDB launches rural entrepreneurship drive with finance and market support

The Regional Development Bank (RDB) yesterday launched an islandwide programme to establish at least one entrepreneur in each rural service area, combining access to finance with business development support and market opportunities.

The ‘Entrepreneurs 14014 Operation’, launched on October 13, targets small and medium-scale enterprises through six initiatives covering fisheries, agriculture, livestock, women and youth entrepreneurs, and the Northern Province.

Labour Minister and Deputy Minister of Finance and Planning Dr. Anil Jayantha, who presided over the launch, said the programme would direct financial resources towards targeted business sectors and support new entrepreneurs as the economy moves towards growth.

He said RDB’s role would extend beyond providing loans to helping entrepreneurs develop their businesses, acquire knowledge and gain access to markets.

The sector-specific initiatives include ‘Dheewara Diriya’ for fisheries, ‘Krushi Navodaya’ for agriculture and ‘Kshira Viplavaya’ for livestock development.

The programme also includes ‘Liyasaviya’ for women entrepreneurs, ‘Heta Unikupa’ for youth entrepreneurship and ‘Prabathaya’ for entrepreneurship development in the Northern Province.

Jayantha said the era of bribery in government business activities and political interference in the provision of facilities and bank financing to entrepreneurs was over.

He said maintaining financial discipline remained a key government responsibility, adding that international institutions had recognised Sri Lanka’s progress from economic bankruptcy towards stability.

The Minister symbolically handed over loan cheques to a group of small and medium-scale entrepreneurs at the launch.

The programme aims to help rural entrepreneurs establish and expand businesses by pairing financing with practical business support and market links.
