HDFC Bank reports resilient 1H performance

HDFC Bank of Sri Lanka has demonstrated a resilient financial performance for the first half of 2026, reflecting steady progress despite a challenging operating environment.

According to the bank’s interim financial statements for the period ended June 30, 2026, the net interest income reached Rs.1,365 million, supported by an interest income of Rs.3,569 million.

The total operating income stood at Rs.1,499 million, reflecting stability in the core banking operations.

The bank recorded a profit after tax of Rs.105 million for the six-month period, indicating improved performance compared to the previous year, supported by disciplined cost management.

The year-on-year (YoY) profit before tax (PBT) growth is 85 percent. HDFC Bank’s financial position remains strong, with the total assets of Rs.63.2 billion and a total capital ratio of 32.05 percent, well above the regulatory requirements.

The liquidity levels also remained robust, with a liquidity coverage ratio of 203 percent.

With a net interest margin of 4.30 percent and return on equity improving to 2.75 percent, the bank is well-positioned for sustainable growth and continued value creation.

The bank operates with a strong ownership structure, with 51 percent shareholding held by the National Housing Development Authority and other government entities, while the remaining 49 percent is held by corporate and individual investors.

As a premier development bank, HDFC Bank has been instrumental in supporting low and middle-income earners to achieve home ownership. At the same time, the bank has evolved into a diversified financial institution, serving a wide spectrum of customers ranging from low-income segments to high-net-worth individuals, through an expanding product portfolio and a branch network covering all districts of Sri Lanka.
