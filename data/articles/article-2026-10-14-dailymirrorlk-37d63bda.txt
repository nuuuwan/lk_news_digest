Yamamoto Motor Corporation introduces Voge Experience Centre

Yamamoto Motor Corporation, the authorised agent for Voge in Sri Lanka, has officially opened the new Voge Experience Centre in Bambalapitiya, marking a significant milestone in the brand’s expansion into Sri Lanka’s premium motorcycle segment.

Going beyond the traditional showroom concept, the Voge Experience Centre offers motorcycle enthusiasts a dedicated space to explore the brand’s range of motorcycles, discover its engineering innovations and advanced technologies and experience Voge first-hand.

The facility also brings together professional customer assistance and comprehensive after-sales support, reflecting the brand’s commitment to delivering a premium ownership experience.

Since its international launch in 2018, Voge has established a growing presence in more than 60 countries across Europe, Asia, Latin America and other international markets. Backed by extensive motorcycle manufacturing expertise and technical development capabilities, the brand draws on engineering knowledge strengthened through its long-standing cooperation with BMW.

Voge’s growing international presence is further reflected in its strong sales performance. The brand recorded global sales of 106,389 motorcycles in 2025, while sales reached 92,237 units by end-July 2026, reflecting a 43.2 percent year-on-year increase.

Among the key highlights at the new Experience Centre is Voge R 125 S, a motorcycle that brings together sporty design, advanced technology and everyday practicality.

Equipped with a 124.8cc single-cylinder, four-valve, liquid-cooled engine, a six-speed gearbox and electronic fuel injection, R 125 S delivers a combination of performance and riding convenience.

Its features include a seven-inch colour LED instrument display, full LED lighting, a front camera, disc brakes with dual-channel ABS, upside-down front suspension and a rear single-shock system.

Yamamoto Motor Corporation CEO Rajeev Chandrakumar stated, “The launch of the Voge Experience Centre marks an important milestone in our commitment to bringing internationally recognised motorcycle brands and a truly premium ownership experience to Sri Lankan riders. Voge stands out for its contemporary design, engineering excellence and advanced technology. Through this dedicated centre, we aim to give the customers the opportunity to experience these qualities first-hand, supported by the professional service, reliability and confidence they expect from a premium motorcycle brand.”

The opening of the Voge Experience Centre introduces a dedicated destination for motorcycle enthusiasts in Sri Lanka, offering an opportunity to engage more closely with the brand and its products. The investment further reinforces Yamamoto Motor Corporation’s commitment to introducing high-quality international motorcycles to the local market, complemented by dependable after-sales service and long-term customer support.
