Jetour Owners’ Club  Sri Lanka officially launched with drive fest

A new chapter in Sri Lanka’s automotive community was marked with the official launch of the Jetour Owners’ Club Sri Lanka, as more than 70 Jetour owners came together for the inaugural Drive Fest 2026, celebrating their passion for adventure, driving, and the growing Jetour family.

The landmark event commenced at the iconic Lotus Tower, Colombo, where participants gathered with their vehicles before embarking on a scenic convoy drive to Water’s Edge, Battaramulla across several landmarks in Colombo such as the Galle Face Green, Green Path and Borella.

The event brought together Jetour enthusiasts from across the country, creating an unforgettable experience filled with camaraderie, excitement, and shared appreciation for the prestigious automobile brand.

Organized under the theme “Drive Together. Explore Together. Create Memories Together.”, the Drive Fest served as a platform to officially launch the Jetour Owners’ Club in Sri Lanka while strengthening the bond between owners and the brand.

The event was proudly sponsored by Euro Motors, the official sole agent for Jetour vehicles in Sri Lanka, whose continued commitment to enhancing the ownership experience has contributed significantly to the rapid growth of the Jetour community in the country.

Participants enjoyed a memorable day of driving, networking, and recreational activities, highlighting the spirit of exploration and adventure that defines the Jetour brand. The convoy journey from Colombo to Battaramulla turned heads along the route, showcasing an impressive lineup of Jetour vehicles and reflecting the growing popularity of the brand among Sri Lankan motorists.

Speaking at the event, organizers emphasized that the formation of the Jetour Owners’ Club is aimed at creating a vibrant platform where owners can connect, exchange experiences, participate in exclusive events, and celebrate their shared passion for driving.

The successful launch event was expertly managed and coordinated by ThePapare Events, ensuring a seamless experience for all participants from start to finish.

With the establishment of the Jetour Owners’ Club Sri Lanka, members can look forward to a host of exciting initiatives, road trips, community engagements, and exclusive experiences in the years ahead, further strengthening the bond among Jetour owners while fostering a thriving automotive community in Sri Lanka.
