Mixed investor sentiments amid possibility of positive corporate results

CSE trading closed on a downward trajectory yesterday amid mixed investor sentiments and an anticipation by investors of positive quarterly corporate results, market analysts said.

The ASPI was down 47.45 points at 21,160.65, while the more liquid S&P SL20 was up 0.18 percent, or 10.97 points, at 5,954.19.

Positive contributors to the ASPI were Dialog Axiata (up 1.62 percent at 43.80 rupees), JKSB (up 0.51 percent at Rs Rs 19.80), Aitken Spence (up 1.05 percent at Rs 145.00), and Lanka Ashok Leyland (up 6.53 percent at Rs 2,675.75).

Ceylinco Insurance (down 4.68 percent at 2,850.00 rupees), Harischandra Mills (down 10.98 percent at Rs 4,734.50 ), and HNB (down 0.52 percent at Rs Rs 384.00 ) were top negative contributors.

Market turnover was Rs 3.28 billion. Food & staples retailing led turnover with Rs 2.3 billion.

“Not seeing a lot of turnover; bit of high net worth activity is present on the side, large capital stocks are where the hunt is on, an analyst said.

“You have banking shares changing hands and some construction sector activity is taking place, he added.

Crossings accounted for a significant portion of the day’s turnover, dominated by Cargills (Ceylon) which saw 3.6 million shares being sold representing 68 percent of total market turnover. Additional crossings were recorded in Melstacorp (1 million shares at Rs 180 ) and Nations Trust Bank (105,000 shares at Rs 306 ).

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 336.35/40 to the US dollar in the spot market, from Rs 336.25/35 the previous day, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was 331.80 buying, 340.80 selling; the euro was 376.2404 buying, 390.1574 selling; and the pound was 444.8948 buying, 458.9404 selling.
