Rain sends Jaffna into Playoffs, Dambulla knocked out

AUG.03 (Mirror Sports) - Jaffna Kings booked their place in the Lanka Premier League 2026 playoffs after persistent rain washed out their league-stage clash against Dambulla Sixers at the Pallekele International Cricket Stadium.

The abandoned match saw both teams share a point, ending Dambulla Sixers' playoff hopes while confirming the tournament's final four. Galle Gallants, Jaffna Kings, Colombo Kaps and Kandy Royals will now battle for the LPL 2026 title.

Earlier, Dambulla Sixers posted an imposing 203 for 8 after being asked to bat first. Pavan Rathnayake top-scored with a rapid 47 off 28 balls, while Dinesh Chandimal hammered 44 off 24 deliveries to provide early momentum. Dilshan Madushanka starred with the ball for Jaffna Kings, claiming 3 for 32, while Dunith Wellalage and Lizaad Williams picked up two wickets apiece.

Jaffna's chase never got underway as heavy rain arrived before the start of the second innings, forcing the match to be abandoned. The shared point officially sealed the LPL 2026 playoff line-up, with Dambulla Sixers becoming the only team to miss out on the knockout stage.

Dambulla Sixers 203/8 (20 overs) – Pavan Rathnayake 47 (28), Dinesh Chandimal 44 (24); Dilshan Madushanka 3/32, Dunith Wellalage 2/24, Lizaad Williams 2/39.
