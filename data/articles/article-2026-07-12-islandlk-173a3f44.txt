Bank of Ceylon pioneers contactless payments with launch of ‘BOC Flex – Tap & Pay’

The Bank of Ceylon (BOC) has officially launched “BOC Flex – Tap & Pay,” marking Sri Lanka’s first mobile banking wallet-based contactless payment solution.

Introduced on July 9, 2026, this landmark digital milestone builds upon the bank’s next-generation “BOC Flex” internet and mobile banking platform, which originally debuted in March 2025.

The launch event was attended by key state officials, including the Minister of Labour, the Deputy Minister of Finance, and the Minister of Digital Economy, alongside representatives from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka and Mastercard, highlighting the national significance of the rollout.

Integrated directly within the existing BOC Flex mobile app, the Tap & Pay solution combines a secure digital wallet with advanced virtual card technology. This allows users to conduct fast, secure, and seamless cardless transactions at any Near-Field Communication (NFC)-enabled Point-of-Sale (POS) terminal, as well as across online merchant networks.

Flexible Wallet Management: Users can transfer funds from their main BOC accounts to the wallet to pay utility bills, transfer money, or settle credit card balances. An automated “Auto Top-Up” feature instantly replenishes the wallet from a linked account if balances run low.

Virtual Card Controls: Customers can generate up to three virtual cards simultaneously. The system includes robust self-service features allowing users to instantly suspend, reactivate, or change the primary funding source of their cards on the go.

National Alignment: The initiative directly supports the government’s Digital Economy agenda, aiming to drive financial inclusion and accelerate the domestic transition toward a modern, cash-lite society.

BOC’s digital push continues to gain widespread acclaim. The BOC Flex platform recently made history at the Digital CX Awards 2026 in Singapore, becoming the first Sri Lankan banking application to secure three global awards for customer experience and digital banking excellence. This global recognition follows a sweep of domestic honors at the National ICT Awards and the LankaPay Technovation Awards, cementing BOC’s position as the pioneer of Sri Lanka’s digital financial evolution.
