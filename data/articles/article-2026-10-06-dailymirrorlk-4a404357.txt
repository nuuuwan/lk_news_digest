Anunayake Thera asks Prisons not to remove Gnanasara Thera’s robes

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Retired Senior Professor and Anunayake Maha Thera Ven. Kotapitiye Rahula Thera has requested the Prisons authorities to allow Venerable Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera to remain in his robes and to appear in them whenever he is brought outside his place of detention.

In a letter addressed to the Commissioner General of Prisons, Ven. Kotapitiye Rahula Thera said the authority to change or remove the robes of an ordained monk rested with the monk concerned and the Maha Sangha to which he belonged.

Ven. Kotapitiye Rahula Thera, an Anunayake Maha Thera of the Sri Jayawardenapura Kotte Sri Kalyani Samagridharma Maha Sangha Sabha, said Gnanasara Thera belonged to the same Sangha Sabha of the Maha Vihara lineage.

He said the Sangha Sabha had no objection to the court decision concerning Gnanasara Thera and fully accepted the supremacy of the judiciary.

However, he said the Sabha had taken note of media reports concerning the removal of the Thera’s robe while in prison.

According to Ven. Kotapitiye Rahula Thera, under established religious practices and traditions, the removal of a monk’s robe should be carried out with the approval of the Mahanayake Thera or the relevant Maha Sangha.

He said it was also an established religious and traditional practice to preserve a monk’s robes and allow the monk to appear in proper robes whenever he was brought outside the place of detention.

The Anunayake Thera said Gnanasara Thera had not been reported to the Sangha Sabha as having committed a Parajika offence under the Vinaya Pitaka or any offence considered serious enough to warrant the removal of his robes.

He said it was the responsibility of the relevant Sangha Sabha to conduct a disciplinary inquiry into the matter and determine whether the Thera should retain or be deprived of his robes.

Therefore, Ven. Kotapitiye Rahula Thera requested the Commissioner General of Prisons not to remove Gnanasara Thera’s robe and to allow him to appear in his robes.

Meanwhile, Bodu Bala Sena General Secretary Ven. Galagodaathe Gnanasara Thera, who is serving a prison sentence following his conviction for contempt of court, has been dressed in standard prisoner attire at Welikada Prison, prison authorities said.

Prison media spokesperson and Commissioner of Prisons (Operations) Senaka Palllegetenna said the Thera’s robes had been removed and that he had been issued a standard prisoner registration number as an ordinary inmate.

The development follows an order issued by the Court of Appeal today directing the Commissioner General of Prisons to take necessary steps to implement the remaining period of the prison sentence imposed on Gnanasara Thera following his conviction for contempt of court.

Gnanasara Thera was arrested on Sunday on an open warrant and produced before the court.

He was taken to Welikada Prison by prison officials yesterday after the Court of Appeal ordered that the six-year prison sentence imposed on the Thera take effect from Tuesday (05), following the Supreme Court judgment that annulled the presidential pardon granted to him by former President Maithripala Sirisena.

The dispute over Gnanasara Thera’s prison attire has therefore emerged alongside the implementation of his prison sentence, with the Anunayake Thera maintaining that any decision to remove his robes should be left to the relevant Maha Sangha, while prison authorities say he has been registered and treated as an ordinary inmate in accordance with prison procedures.
