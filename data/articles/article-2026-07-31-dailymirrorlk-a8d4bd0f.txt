Market ends steady as buying interest persists

The market extended its positive momentum yesterday, closing with sustained buying interest despite the moderate trading volumes.

Activity remained steady across the curve, while the overall yield curve was largely unchanged.

In the short-tenor segment, 15.03.2028 and 10.10.2028 traded at 10.50 percent. In the 2029 maturities, 15.09.2029 and 15.12.2029 traded between 11.00–11.05 percent. The 2030 segment attracted notable investor interest, with 15.05.2030, 01.07.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 trading between 11.60 percent -11.30 percent. Further along the curve, 01.02.2031 traded at 11.70 percent - 11.60 percent. Meanwhile, 15.01.2033 traded at 12.05 percent - 12.00 percent, while 15.10.2034 traded between 12.25 percent to 12.18 percent. Finally, 15.08.2036 and 01.07.2037 traded between 12.75 percent to 12.60 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar standing at Rs.336.05/US dollar, compared to Rs.336.18/US dollar seen earlier. Overnight liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.183.34 billion, from Rs.195.00 billion recorded previously.
