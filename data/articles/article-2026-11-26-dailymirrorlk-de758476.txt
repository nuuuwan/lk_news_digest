LOLC Finance expands branch network across Sri Lanka

LOLC Finance PLC continues to strengthen its islandwide presence with the opening of two new branches in Pallepola and Katana and the relocation of its Horowpothana and Melsiripura branches to new premises, bringing its financial services closer to customers and communities across these regions.

The new Pallepola Branch, located at Madipola Road, Pallepola, and the Katana Branch situated at, Mahagedarawatta, Katana, East Katana, were ceremonially opened in the presence of  Montini Warnakula, Chief Operating Officer of LOLC Finance PLC, who attended as Chief Guest at the Katana opening alongside Nishantha Jayasekara, Head of Gold Loan, who was the Chief Guest at the Pallepola opening.

Meanwhile, the Horowpothana Branch has relocated to new premises at Rest House Road, Horowpothana, with Nishantha Jayasekara, Head of Gold Loan, attending the relocation ceremony as Chief Guest. The Melsiripura Branch has also moved to Dambulla Road, Melsiripura, with Prasanna Karandagolla, Head of Channels at LOLC Finance PLC, attending as Chief Guest.

Commenting on these developments, Montini Warnakula, Chief Operating Officer of LOLC Finance PLC said, “Our continued expansion and relocation initiatives reflect our commitment to making financial services more accessible and convenient for the communities we serve. While opening new branches enables us to extend our reach to more customers, relocating existing branches to improved premises allows us to enhance the overall customer experience and service delivery. We remain focused on strengthening our regional presence, building lasting customer relationships and providing accessible, reliable and customer-focused financial solutions that support the financial aspirations of individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses across the country.”
