25 Indonesian fishermen rescued

The Sri Lanka Navy safely brought ashore 25 Indonesian fishermen at the Colombo Port on September 24, 2026, following a multi-agency rescue operation after their fishing vessel capsized south of Sri Lanka.

The fishermen were aboard the Indonesian fishing trawler KM Garuda Jaya VI, which capsized on September 22 while in the Australian Search and Rescue Region. The crew members were left distressed in the sea following the incident.

The merchant vessel MV Moonstone responded to the emergency and rescued all 25 fishermen from the water. The vessel then alerted the Maritime Rescue Coordination Centre (MRCC) in Colombo about the incident and the rescued crew.

Following the distress alert, the Sri Lanka Navy coordinated with MRCC Colombo and dispatched two naval craft to bring the fishermen safely to shore. The operation was carried out with the assistance of the relevant authorities.

The 25 survivors were safely transferred to the Colombo Port, where the Navy provided them with humanitarian assistance. They were subsequently handed over to the Indonesian Embassy in Sri Lanka for further arrangements and onward action.
