Showers expected in several areas after 2 p.m.

Colombo, August 30 (Daily Mirror) - Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in the Uva Province and in the Ampara, Batticaloa and Hambantota districts after 2 p.m. today, while several spells of showers are expected in the Western and North-western provinces.

Showers will occur at times in the Sabaragamuwa Province and in the Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya districts.

Mainly dry weather is expected to prevail over the other areas of the island.

Strong winds of around 40–50 kmph may occur at times over the western slopes of the Central Hills, the Northern, North-central and North-western provinces, and in the Trincomalee, Monaragala, Hambantota and Matale districts.

The public is advised to take adequate precautions to minimize possible damage from temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
