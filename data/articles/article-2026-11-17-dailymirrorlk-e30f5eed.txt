Sri Lanka to establish permanent Nuclear Security watchdog

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the establishment of a National Nuclear Security Coordination Committee under the Ministry of Science and Technology to strengthen Sri Lanka's national nuclear security framework.

As a member state of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), Sri Lanka is committed to maintaining an effective nuclear security regime to prevent, detect and respond to unauthorised or criminal activities involving nuclear and other radioactive materials, related facilities and activities.

The Government has identified the need for a permanent, centralised coordination mechanism involving representatives from the defence, legal and other relevant sectors to harmonise nuclear security policies, conduct threat assessments and coordinate operational responses.

Accordingly, the proposed committee will be established under the chairmanship of the Secretary to the Ministry of Science and Technology, while the Director General of the Sri Lanka Atomic Energy Regulatory Council will serve as its Secretary.

The proposal to establish the committee was submitted by the Minister of Science and Technology and received Cabinet approval.
