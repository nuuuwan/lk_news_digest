BOC strengthens entrepreneurs across Sri Lanka through special development programme

The Bank of Ceylon (BOC) continues its commitment to empowering entrepreneurs and strengthening the country’s business sector through its Entrepreneur Development Programme (EDP) 2026.

The initiative aims to encourage entrepreneurship, enhance business capabilities and equip aspiring entrepreneurs with the knowledge and guidance required to establish sustainable businesses.

The programme spanned five consecutive Sundays, commencing on 18 October and concluding on 15 November 2026. Approximately 100 participants were selected through an open public application process, welcoming applications from Sri Lankan citizens. Both BOC customers and non-customers were eligible, ensuring equal opportunities for aspiring entrepreneurs across the country, including individuals with business ideas who had yet to establish their own ventures.

The five-day programme was successfully conducted with the valuable contributions of experts from the Bank of Ceylon and experienced resource persons from various professional fields. Their knowledge and practical insights provided participants with guidance on entrepreneurship, financial management and business development, helping them transform business ideas into sustainable ventures.

A special discussion forum featuring representatives of the Bank of Ceylon’s senior management was a key highlight of the programme. Discussions focused on banking services, financial assistance, business development and strategic management, providing participants with a valuable opportunity to gain insights, expert guidance and practical advice from industry professionals. The session also reflected the Bank’s strong commitment to supporting entrepreneurs by sharing its expertise and providing guidance to help them develop and strengthen their businesses

Through EDP 2026, the Bank of Ceylon further strengthens its contribution to national economic development by nurturing entrepreneurial talent, promoting sustainable business growth and creating opportunities for a stronger entrepreneurial community across Sri Lanka.
