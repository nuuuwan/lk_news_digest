Air Chief Marshal Kolitha Gunathilake appointed KDU Chancellor

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has appointed former Commander of the Sri Lanka Air Force and former Chief of Defence Staff, Air Chief Marshal Kolitha A. Gunathilake (Retd.), as the new Chancellor of General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU).

The letter of appointment was handed over to Air Chief Marshal Gunathilake by Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake at the Presidential Secretariat yesterday (5).

Air Chief Marshal Gunathilake previously served as Commander of the Sri Lanka Air Force and later as Chief of Defence Staff.
