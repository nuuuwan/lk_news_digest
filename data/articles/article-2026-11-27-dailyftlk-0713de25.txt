India-Sri Lanka discuss bilateral ties and regional developments

Indian External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar met Sri Lankan Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath yesterday for talks on strengthening bilateral relations and developments in the region, reaffirming New Delhi’s focus on its neighbourhood engagement.

In a post on X following the meeting, Jaishankar described the discussions as positive and said he looked forward to continuing the engagement at the Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA).

He also underscored India’s ‘Neighbourhood First’ policy and ‘Vision MAHASAGAR’ initiative, reflecting the importance New Delhi places on regional cooperation and maritime engagement.
