Toyota Lanka unveils all-new Toyota Hilux and Toyota RAV4 2026

From left: Naotaka Takeda (Head – Value Chain), Masaaki Kawabata (Chairman), Manohara Atukorala (Chief Operating Officer), and Mathisha Samaranayake (Chief Operating Officer) of Toyota Lanka.

Toyota Lanka, the sole authorized distributor of Toyota Brand New Vehicles in Sri Lanka, officially unveiled the All-New Toyota Hilux and All-New Toyota RAV4 2026 at its exclusive launch event, ‘Unlocked’ held at Toyota Plaza, Wattala.

The grand unveiling welcomed over 250 business partners, industry stakeholders, customers, and media representatives, celebrating another significant milestone in Toyota Lanka’s journey of delivering world-class mobility solutions to Sri Lankan customers.

The launch of the Toyota Hilux and Toyota RAV4 further strengthens Toyota Lanka’s product portfolio with two iconic and innovative models that have earned global recognition for their performance and dependability.

This milestone event, celebrating the launch of the Toyota Hilux and Toyota RAV4, provided an excellent opportunity for stakeholders to experience Toyota’s renowned reliability and the latest Toyota innovations designed to meet the evolving needs of Sri Lankan customers. Both models have been tropicalized to suit the country’s diverse geographical and driving conditions, offering exceptional performance, safety, and versatility.

The RAV4, the best-selling SUV in the United States, continues to embody modern SUV comfort, advanced innovation, and everyday practicality, while the Hilux builds on Toyota’s legendary reputation for strength, durability, and outstanding performance, making it the trusted choice for commercial mobility needs.
