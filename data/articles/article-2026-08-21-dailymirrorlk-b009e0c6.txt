SLPP files petition against 22nd amendment

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror)- General Secretary of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) Attorney-at-Law Sagara Kariyawasam today filed a Special Determination petition before the Supreme Court challenging the constitutionality of the Bill titled “Twenty-Second Amendment to the Constitution,” which proposes to increase the retirement age of Supreme Court judges to 67 and Court of Appeal judges to 65.

Kariyawasam is seeking a declaration that the Bill requires approval by the people at a referendum and a two-thirds majority in Parliament.

The Petitioner stated that the Bill would change the terms of office of serving superior-court judges in a way that could affect the independence of the judiciary. Therefore, according to the Petitioner, the Bill falls within a principle already recognised by the Supreme Court and must be approved by the people at a Referendum.

The Bill was published as a Gazette Supplement dated August 7, 2026 and was tabled in Parliament on August 18 by the Minister of Justice and National Integration.
