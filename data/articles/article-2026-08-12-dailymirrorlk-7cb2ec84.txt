Passing through Boralesgamuwa? Check the traffic plan

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Temporary traffic restrictions will be imposed in the Boralesgamuwa Police Division on August 13, 14 and 15 in connection with the Bellanwila Esala Maha Perahera, police said.

Police urged motorists and the public to use designated alternative routes during the three procession nights.

The first restriction will be imposed tomorrow (13) from 7.00 a.m. to around 10.00 a.m. for the Pawada Perahera.

During this period, traffic along the 119 Maharagama-Dehiwala bus route between Boralesgamuwa Junction and Bellanthara Junction will be restricted.

Vehicles travelling from Maharagama or Piliyandala towards Dehiwala can proceed via Papiliyana Junction, turn left towards Bellanthara through the Spar Roundabout, and continue via Nedimala.

Motorists travelling from Werahera Junction towards Boralesgamuwa and Galle Road can use Gangaramaya Road and proceed via Golumadama Junction.

The Ranseevali Perahera will be held on August 14 from 8.00 p.m. until approximately 2.00 a.m. on August 15.

Traffic restrictions will again be imposed along the 119 route between Boralesgamuwa and Bellanthara Junctions as the procession travels towards Bellanthara Junction before returning via Somaratne Mawatha and Vihara Mawatha.

Vehicles travelling from Dehiwala towards Maharagama can turn left at Bellanthara Junction and proceed to Papiliyana Junction via Route 117 before turning towards Boralesgamuwa Junction.

Heavy vehicles travelling from Maharagama towards Dehiwala can use Papiliyana Junction and Route 117, or travel towards Piliyandala and access Katubedda Road. Light vehicles can use Malani Bulathsinhala Road, Pitawella Road and Kahawita Mawatha to reach Route 117.

The main Randoli Maha Perahera will take place from 8.00 p.m. on August 15 until approximately 4.00 a.m. on August 16, with wider traffic restrictions in and around Boralesgamuwa, Bellanthara and Nedimala.

Restrictions will also be imposed along Route 117 (Ratmalana-Nugegoda Road) between the Attidiya Police Training College Junction and Papiliyana Junction.

During the final procession, vehicles travelling from Maharagama and Piliyandala towards Dehiwala can use Kohuwala Junction and the Kohuwala-Kalubowila Road.

Vehicles travelling from Mount Lavinia and Ratmalana towards Maharagama can use Attidiya Bakery Junction, Galle Road, Kohuwala and William Grinding Mills.

Vehicles travelling from Dehiwala towards Papiliyana can use Andrews Road from Nedimala Junction and proceed towards Kohuwala.

Heavy vehicles travelling from Horana, Piliyandala, Kesbewa and Maharagama towards Galle Road have been advised to use Kohuwala Junction and the Kohuwala-Kalubowila Road.

Meanwhile, heavy vehicles travelling from Colombo towards Galle Road via Papiliyana or Boralesgamuwa Junctions should proceed to Piliyandala Junction, turn right onto Katubedda Road, and access Galle Road via Katubedda Junction.
