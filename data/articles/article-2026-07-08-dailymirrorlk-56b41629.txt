Stipulated fares for buses, three-wheelers, office and school vans: Minister

Colombo, July 8 (Daily Mirror) - A new system of stipulated fares for buses, three wheelers, school vans and office vans will be introduced soon, Minister of Transport Bimal Ratnayake told Parliament today.

The Minister said the Transportation Act makes provisions for such a system.

Ultimate objective should be to make public transport free of charge: Minister
