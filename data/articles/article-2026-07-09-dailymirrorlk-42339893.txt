KleenPark marks 38 years with the opening of ‘Lakeside Detail’

KleenPark marked two significant milestones on 22 June — its 38th anniversary and the opening of its newest detailing facility, “Lakeside Detail by KleenPark”, at Cinnamon Lakeside Colombo.

The occasion was especially significant as KleenPark stands today as Sri Lanka’s first fully employee-owned company in the automotive detailing industry.

The opening of this new outlet marks another important chapter in KleenPark’s journey of innovation, service excellence, customer care, and continuous growth.

Strategically located within a five-star hospitality setting, Lakeside Detail is designed to deliver world-class automotive detailing services, offering hotel guests, corporate clients, and vehicle owners the convenience of premium vehicle care while maintaining the exceptional standards of quality for which KleenPark has been renowned for nearly four decades.

The ceremonial opening was attended by the staff of KleenPark (Pvt) Ltd, the management of Cinnamon Lakeside Colombo, distinguished guests, valued customersand members of the KleenPark family. Addressing the gathering, KleenPark’s management reflected on the company’s remarkable journey, which began in 1988 with the vision of its Founder, Ranjan Ratnasena, who introduced professional automotive detailing to Sri Lanka.

The opening of Lakeside Detail represents more than just the launch of a new outlet; it symbolizes KleenPark’s continued investment in elevating the standards of automotive care in Sri Lanka while creating greater convenience and value for customers. Operated by highly trained and internationally certified detailing professionals using modern detailing tools and equipment, the facility offers a comprehensive range of premium services, including express detailing, interior and exterior detailing, ceramic coating, paint protection film (PPF), nano-ceramic window films, customised floor mats, and other specialised vehicle care treatments.

As KleenPark celebrates 38 years of detailing excellence, the company extends its heartfelt appreciation to its loyal customers, business partners, suppliers, and dedicated employees whose unwavering trust and support have made this remarkable journey possible.

With a proud heritage, a unique employee-owned culture, and an unwavering commitment to quality, Lakeside Detail by KleenPark represents the next step in the company’s vision to deliver world-class automotive detailing experiences to vehicle enthusiasts across Sri Lanka, while continuing to raise the standard of vehicle care for generations to come.
