Nimal Perera must face legal action over bond scam: Lawyer

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - Nimal Perera, who is alleged to have given money to Namal Rajapaksa in connection with the Airbus deal, must face legal action over the Central Bank bond scam and alleged insider trading in the share market, Convenor of the organisation called "Purawesi Paliha Collective" Attorney-at-Law Sanjeewa Weerawickrama said.

The lawyer said the Securities and Exchange Commission’s (SEC) 2022 annual report clearly states that Perera, as an investor and stock market trader, had engaged in insider dealing and violated Rule 12. He said Perera was fined Rs. 6.6 million in connection with the incident.

He further said the three-member Presidential Commission of Inquiry appointed by then-President Maithripala Sirisena to investigate the Central Bank bond scam had recommended a detailed investigation into Treasury Bond transactions which Nimal Perera and his companmy had with the Employees’ Provident Fund (EPF).

The lawyer said these matters indicate that Perera should also face legal action in Sri Lanka.
