Musk takes sarcastic swipe at India over Starlink access

Elon Musk has taken a sarcastic swipe at India over access for his satellite internet service Starlink, urging the country to allow it to compete in the market.

In a post on X dated October 9, Musk addressed Prime Minister Narendra Modi as “Prime Minister Ambani”, an apparent reference to Indian billionaire Mukesh Ambani, whose Reliance Industries has interests in the country's telecommunications sector.

“Please accept my humble apologies for not realizing that you are the real boss of India,” Musk wrote, sarcastically criticising what he described as monopolistic exploitation of the Indian public.

He urged the Indian leadership to consider allowing Starlink to compete, arguing that the service could help bridge the digital divide in areas without internet access.

Musk said Starlink could improve educational opportunities for children and help small businesses reach global markets.

He also highlighted the service's role in providing communications during natural disasters, when conventional communication systems fail.

“Starlink has proven to be essential for saving lives during natural disasters throughout the world,” he said, adding that its availability could help save lives in India.

The post drew widespread attention on X, reportedly receiving more than 109 million views.

Starlink, operated by Musk's SpaceX, provides satellite-based internet services and is seeking to expand its presence in the Indian market.
