US reminds non-immigrant visa applicants to prove financial readiness

Colombo, Oct. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The U.S. Embassy in Sri Lanka has reminded non-immigrant visa applicants to ensure they have sufficient financial means to support themselves during their stay in the United States, including in the event of travel disruptions or medical emergencies.

In a reminder shared by the Embassy, applicants were advised to be prepared for their visa interviews with evidence demonstrating their ability to meet their expenses without becoming a public charge after entering the United States.

The Embassy stressed the importance of having adequate financial resources to cover travel-related costs and unexpected emergencies during their stay.

Applicants were also encouraged to bring relevant supporting documents to their visa interviews to demonstrate their financial readiness.
