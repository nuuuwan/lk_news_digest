High COL has prompted Sri Lankans to produce less children: FLSP

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - High cost of living has prompted Sri Lankans to produce less children and also it is the reason why the child population in the country has been reduced by 100,000, Frontline Socialist Party Executive Member Duminda Nagamuwa said.

"Sri Lankans have become reluctant to produce children and they cannot afford to spend for children. Those who shouted about forced sterilization of women are silent about it" Nagamuwa said.

"There are other social issues which would arise as a result of high cost of living. There will be unrest in families as a result of financial constraints while some may be compelled to commit crimes, " he added.

Furthermore, he said a heavy burden has been inflicted on people because of taxes. "People have to pay a tax worth Rs 11 per egg while sanitary pads are subjected to 20 percent tax. A new law is being introduced under which dogs will have to be registered. What the government is trying to fulfil out of this law is to charge a tax from dogs," Nagamuwa alleged.

"This government is engaged in continuing the economic policy which was introduced by former President Ranil Wickremesinghe together with IMF," he also said.

Referring to the arrest of six activists of FLSP affiliated Aragala Sandanaya protest, Nagamuwa said the government is out to ban protests by defining such exercises as acts of terrorism under the new Prevention of Terrorism Bill.
