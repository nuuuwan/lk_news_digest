Buying interest strengthens as market activity picks up

The week began on a firm note, with renewed buying interest driving a marginal decline in the yields across the curve.

Investor activity picked up alongside robust trading volumes, signalling strong market participation.

At the short end of the curve, 15.12.2029 and 01.03.2030 both traded between 10.90 percent to 11.00 percent. In the 2030 segment, 01.07.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 were seen trading within the 11.25 percent-11.15 percent range.

Further along the yield curve, 01.02.2031 and 15.03.2031 changed hands between 11.40 percent and 11.25 percent while 01.07.2032 traded higher at 11.85 percent.

Activity was evident across the mid tenures as well, with 15.01.2033 trading at 11.90 percent while 15.10.2034 and 15.03.2035 traded between 12.10 percent and 11.97 percent and 12.25 percent and 12.05 percent, respectively.

Finally, at the long end of the curve, 15.08.2036 and 01.07.2037 were seen trading within the 12.60 percent-12.50 percent range.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee marginally depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.335.45/US dollar, compared to Rs.335.43/US dollar seen earlier.

Overnight liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.256.4 billion, from Rs.246.8 billion recorded previously.
