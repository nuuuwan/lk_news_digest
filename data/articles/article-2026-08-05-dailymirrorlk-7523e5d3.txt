Siyapatha Finance opens 65th branch in Chunnakam

Siyapatha Finance PLC unveiled its 65th branch in Chunnakam, Jaffna recently, further strengthening the company’s presence in the Northern region.

The branch was declared open by Siyapatha Finance PLC Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Mathisha Hewavitharana, in the presence of Chief Operating Officer (COO) Rajeev De Silva, P. Shanmugapriya, student of Alaveddy Arunodaya College who won the Under-18 Girls' Pole Vault event at the All Island School Championship.

Speaking at the ceremony, CEO Hewavitharana noted, “Chunnakam first captured our attention as a steadily developing town in the heart of the Valikamam division in the Jaffna Peninsula and a high-potential economic landscape significantly driven by foreign remittances. The region has rapidly gained traction as a central commercial hub in the district over the past few years. As always, our expansion strategy is rooted in the impact we make on each community’s standard of living and how far we commit to make that a reality.”

Offering a product portfolio catering to the ever-evolving financial needs of entrepreneurs, small and medium-scale enterprises (SMEs), and ambitious individuals, the Chunnakam branch offers a range of services including leasing, fixed deposits, gold financing, business loans, personal loans, fast draft, factoring, and Smart Pay facility, flexible financial solutions.

Siyapatha Finance PLC, is a fully owned subsidiary of the Sampath Bank Group.
