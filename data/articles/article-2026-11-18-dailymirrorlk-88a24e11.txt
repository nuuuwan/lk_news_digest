Pakistan, Sri Lanka handed big WTC27 points deduction

ICC - Sri Lanka have suffered a massive setback in World Test Championship standings after being docked 15 points for maintaining a slow over rate in the first Test against Pakistan.

Pakistan have also been penalised, losing five points for falling five overs short of the required rate.

As a result, Sri Lanka have slipped from 7th to 8th, with their point percentage taking a massive hit from 33.33% to 15.47%. Pakistan are at the bottom, in 9th with 3.33%.

As per the ICC World Test Championship Playing Conditions, teams are deducted one point for every over they fall short of the required rate. Sri Lanka were found to be 15 overs behind the target, while Pakistan fell five overs short.

Both teams have also been fined in accordance with Article 2.22 of the ICC Code of Conduct for Players and Player Support Personnel, as per which players are fined five per cent of their match fee for every over their side fails to bowl in the allotted time, subject to a maximum cap of 50%. Pakistan have been fined 25%, while Sri Lanka were fined 50% of their match fees.

Dean Cosker of the Emirates ICC International Panel of Match Referees imposed the sanctions after charges were levelled by on-field umpires Adrian Holdstock and Rod Tucker, third umpire Allahuddien Paleker and fourth umpire Faisal Afridi.

Captains Babar Azam and Kusal Mendis pleaded guilty to the offences and accepted the proposed sanctions, so no formal hearing was needed.

The opening Test ended in a hard-fought draw following five days of gripping cricket in Rawalpindi.

Pakistan declared their first innings after posting a strong total of 434/9, with five batters reaching half-centuries. In reply, Sri Lanka edged ahead with a slender 12-run lead, thanks to centuries from Dhananjaya de Silva and captain Kusal Mendis. The visitors were on course for a massive lead before debutant Saqib Khan’s seven-for derailed their innings.

Pakistan batted out the rest of the match for 284/8, the Test thus ending in a stalemate.

The two teams are currently contesting the second and final Test, where the hosts made a strong start to the match by bowling out Sri Lanka for 98 in the first innings.
