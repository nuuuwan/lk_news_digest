Police Sergeant interdicted over demanding chicken bribe for bail

Colombo, Oct. 3 (Daily Mirror) - A police sergeant attached to the Malwatuhiripitya Police Station has been interdicted following allegations that he demanded one kilogram of chicken as a bribe to grant police bail to an arrested suspect.

The action follows video footage circulated on social media, which allegedly shows the officer demanding the chicken from a friend of the suspect who had visited the Police Station to arrange bail. The suspect had been arrested on the night of September 26 while travelling in a lorry transporting chickens.

Following the circulation of the footage, an inquiry was launched under the direction of the Assistant Superintendent of Police in charge of Gampaha. Based on the preliminary findings, police interdicted the accused sergeant from service with immediate effect.

Further investigations into the incident are being conducted under the direct supervision of the Senior Superintendent of Police in charge of the Gampaha Police Division.
