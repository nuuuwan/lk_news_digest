Veteran artist and animation pioneer Givantha Arthasad dies at 73

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Veteran artist and film technician Givantha Arthasad, who created Sri Lanka’s first animated cartoon film, has passed away at the age of 73.

Arthasad died while receiving treatment at the Panadura Base Hospital.

The brother of veteran film director Dinesh Priyasad, Arthasad is regarded as a pioneer in animation and special effects in Sri Lankan cinema and television.

He created ‘Dutugemunu’, considered Sri Lanka’s first animated cartoon film, which was released in 1979.

Throughout his career, Arthasad demonstrated his talents in several areas of filmmaking, including painting, cinematography, make-up, art direction and film editing.

He also authored Cinema - Maya, an important work on cinema technology and language.

Arthasad briefly served as the Director General of the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation.

His contribution to animation, special effects and film technology is considered an important chapter in the development of Sri Lankan cinema and television.
