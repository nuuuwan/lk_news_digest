DIMO Mega Fiesta Kurunegala brings together over 400 Tata commercial vehicles

DIMO Mega Fiesta Kurunegala 2026, a large-scale regional customer engagement programme, was successfully completed recently on the DIMO Kurunegala branch premises.

Designed to support the long-term performance of commercial vehicles, the five-day initiative brought together over 400 Tata commercial vehicle owners and operators from Kurunegala and surrounding regions.

Kurunegala, one of Sri Lanka’s key commercial and agricultural hubs, was selected as the venue due to its strong concentration of Tata commercial vehicle customers engaged in transportation, logistics, construction and agriculture. The campaign provided the customers with convenient access to DIMO’s after-sales expertise while strengthening relationships with the regional Tata commercial vehicle community.

DIMO Chief Operating Officer Automotive Engineering Solutions Mahesh Karunaratne said, “For many commercial vehicle owners, their vehicle is more than just a mode of transport; it is their livelihood. DIMO Mega Fiesta was designed to offer a complete support platform where the customers can access expert technical advice, genuine parts, financial solutions and well-being initiatives, all in one place. This initiative reflects our commitment to being the perfect partner for our customers throughout their ownership journey.”

The campaign offered free bumper-to-bumper vehicle inspections and detailed technical reports conducted by Tata Motors-trained technicians, enabling the customers to identify potential concerns and receive professional recommendations on maintenance and repairs.

The customers also benefited from free engine oil, brake oil and coolant top-ups, along with special discounts of up to 50 percent on Tata Genuine Parts, helping them maintain vehicle performance and minimise unexpected repair costs.

During the inspections, engine-related concerns, diesel pump-related repair needs and routine maintenance requirements emerged as some of the most common areas requiring attention. Through one-to-one technical consultations, the customers received guidance on improving vehicle reliability, the importance of timely repairs and the benefits of using Tata Genuine Parts to enhance productivity and service life.

Beyond after-sales support, DIMO Mega Fiesta Kurunegala 2026 featured displays from DIMO Retail and Tata passenger and commercial vehicles, alongside banking, leasing and insurance partners offering tailored promotions and consultations.
