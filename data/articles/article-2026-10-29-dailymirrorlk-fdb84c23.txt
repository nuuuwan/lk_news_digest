Rupee weakens further against US Dollar

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan rupee weakened further against the US dollar today (29), with the dollar’s selling rate increasing to Rs. 337.13, according to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The dollar’s buying rate also edged up to Rs. 328.02 from Rs. 327.99 recorded on Wednesday.

Meanwhile, the selling rate increased from Rs. 337.00 to Rs. 337.13.

The rupee also recorded slight movements against several other currencies, including Gulf currencies.
