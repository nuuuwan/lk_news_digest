Landslide blocks Hatton-Bagawantalawa Road

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A large earth mound has collapsed onto the Hatton-Bagawantalawa-Maskeliya-Balangoda road at Wanraja Estate in Dikoya, completely disrupting traffic along the route amid heavy rainfall in the Hatton area.

The landslide occurred at around 11.00 a.m. today (20), and Hatton Police, together with residents, have commenced clearing the earth from the road.

Police said the Road Development Authority has also been informed to deploy machinery and expedite the removal of the earth.

As there is a further risk of landslides at the location, Hatton Police have urged motorists travelling along the affected section of road to exercise extreme caution.
