Guidance of Holy Prophet Muhammad as an exemplar of peace vital for  coexistence in a multi-ethnic society: Sajith

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa said today that the guidance of Holy Prophet Muhammed is important for coexistence in a multi-ethnic society.

He said this in a message to mark the birthday of the Holy Prophet.

On this sacred Milad-un-Nabi day, as Muslims around the world celebrate the birth of the Holy Prophet Muhammad, I extend my heartfelt best wishes to all Sri Lankan Muslims.

Looking at the life and teachings of the Holy Prophet Muhammad, what stands out most prominently is the respect he showed for social peace, justice and diversity.

In the multi-ethnic society of Madinah, comprising people of various religions, races and cultures, he demonstrated through his own actions how people can live together in peace and harmony. In particular, through the Constitution of Madinah, he worked to ensure unity and security among diverse tribes and religious communities.

He taught that peace is not merely the absence of conflict, but also the presence of mutual respect and justice. Our motherland, Sri Lanka, is likewise a multi-ethnic, multi-religious and multi-cultural nation. Maintaining mutual understanding, coexistence and brotherhood in such a society is essential for sustainable development and lasting peace.

Following the noble path of peace and harmony shown by the Holy Prophet, let us all work together to build a just Sri Lanka where everyone respects one another, regardless of race or religion.  I pray that this Milad-un-Nabi day will strengthen mutual respect, foster greater unity and help bring about a peaceful new tomorrow for our country.
