Over 187 acres of land released in Jaffna for civilian use

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - More than 187 acres of State-owned land in Kankesanthurai, Jaffna, have been formally released by the Security Forces for future civilian use, paving the way for potential investment and development activities in the area.

A total of 187.56 acres belonging to the State Cement Corporation, located in Grama Niladhari Division J/233, Kankesanthurai West, within the Valikamam North (Tellippalai) Divisional Secretariat Division, had been utilised by the Sri Lanka Army since 1997.

The documents relating to the release of the land were formally handed over to Jaffna District Secretary M. Piratheepan by Colonel W. M. A. M. Nissanka, Officer in Charge of Civil Affairs at the Security Forces Headquarters – Jaffna, Palaly.

The handover took place at the Jaffna District Secretariat on Wednesday (September 30).

The release is part of an initiative to make State-owned land available for the proposed investment zone in Kankesanthurai, with the aim of supporting future investment, economic activity and development in the region.

The move also marks another step towards the civilian and productive utilisation of State land in the Northern Province that had previously been occupied or utilised by security forces.
