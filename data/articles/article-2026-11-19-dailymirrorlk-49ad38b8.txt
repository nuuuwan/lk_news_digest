Govt. firm against releasing substandard chilli containers

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Amid concerns over approximately 120 containers of dried chillies currently held at the port, the government has reiterated that any stocks found to be below quality standards will not be released into the market, regardless of potential impacts on domestic prices.

Responding to a question during Tuesday's weekly Cabinet press briefing, Cabinet Spokesman Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa emphasized that neither the Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI) nor the Ministry of Health will authorize the release of imported containers—particularly food items—that fail to meet required safety benchmarks.

The Minister noted that a significant number of the detained containers were found to hold substandard chilli stocks and stressed that they cannot be cleared under any circumstances.

"Holding back substandard food containers at Customs is not a new practice; it is what must be done. We are obligated to enforce these laws to ensure public health and food safety," Dr. Jayatissa said.

He firmly added that authorities cannot compromise on food quality or release unfit consumables simply to curb rising market prices.
