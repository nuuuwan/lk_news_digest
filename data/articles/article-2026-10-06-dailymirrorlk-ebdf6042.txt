Lady Ridgeway turns to donors for essential medicines, surgical gear

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children in Borella has sought donations for dozens of essential medicines and surgical items, raising concerns over the availability of critical supplies at the country’s premier children’s hospital, Medical and Civil Rights Doctors' Trade Union Alliance Chairman and Consultant Physician Dr Chamal Sanjeewa said.

He said a donation request published on the hospital’s official website listed around 40 categories of medicines and nearly 60 types of surgical instruments and equipment.

The medicines listed include Cyclosporine, Melatonin, Flucloxacillin, Biotin, Clonidine, Calcium, Vitamin D, Oxybutynin, Cephalexin, Atomoxetine, Cholecalciferol, Sodium Valproate and Clobazam, among others.

The surgical items requested include JJ stents, thoracic drainage sets, feeding tubes, urinary catheters, laryngeal masks, diathermy pads, femoral artery catheters, various types of sutures and Plaster of Paris.

The hospital has also requested around 25 laboratory items and raw materials required for testing.

Dr Sanjeewa said the request warranted the immediate attention of the Health Ministry, questioning why a major government children's hospital had to seek donations for such a large number of medicines and medical supplies.

He said medicines, surgical instruments and laboratory reagents required by government hospitals are normally expected to be procured and supplied through the Medical Supplies Division.

Dr Sanjeewa questioned whether the extensive list indicated a wider shortage of essential supplies at the hospital and urged the Health Ministry to clarify the situation.

He also said authorities should explain whether the items listed were temporarily unavailable or whether the hospital was experiencing continuing difficulties in obtaining essential medical supplies.

The Lady Ridgeway Hospital provides specialised treatment to children from across the country and is one of the country's key public healthcare institutions.

The hospital has invited philanthropists willing to assist to obtain further information on the required medicines, surgical equipment and laboratory supplies through its official website.
