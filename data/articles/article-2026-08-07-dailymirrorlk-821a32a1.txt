Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Central Finance Company, Commercial Bank and Cargills with the turnover crossing Rs. 12.2bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Commercial Credit & Finance, Lanka Milk Foods and Dialog Axiata.

Mixed interest was observed in Sampath Bank, John Keells Holdings and Sierra Cables whilst retail interest was noted in SMB Leasing, Waskaduwa Beach Resort and HNB Finance.

Furthermore, foreigners remained active closing as net sellers. Diversified Financials sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Credit & Finance and Mercantile Investments & Finance) whilst the sector index gained 1.39%. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance increased by Rs. 1.75 (1.65%) to close at Rs. 107.50. The share price of Mercantile Investments & Finance moved down by Rs. 1.50 (6.38%) to close at Rs. 22.00.

Retailing sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to United Motors Lanka) whilst the sector index increased by 1.32%. The share price of United Motors Lanka closed flat at Rs. 26.50. Sampath Bank and John Keells Holdings were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Sampath Bank recorded a loss of Rs. 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs. 136.25. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs. 20.00.
