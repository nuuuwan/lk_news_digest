FCID to seek suspension of use of property linked to Basil Rajapaksa

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID) has informed the Matara Magistrate’s Court that steps will be taken to suspend the use of a property in Browns Hill, Matara, allegedly belonging to former Minister Basil Rajapaksa.

The matter relates to an ongoing case filed by the FCID over the acquisition of an approximately one-and-a-half-acre property located along Eliyakanda Road, Browns Hill, Matara.

According to information presented to court, the property, reportedly used for coconut cultivation, had previously been owned by a German national identified as Herbert Keller.

The FCID alleges that the property was subsequently purchased by Rajapaksa for approximately Rs. 60 million.

The investigation is focused on the circumstances surrounding the acquisition of the property, with the FCID informing court that further steps would be taken regarding its use.

The court is expected to consider the relevant submissions in due course.
