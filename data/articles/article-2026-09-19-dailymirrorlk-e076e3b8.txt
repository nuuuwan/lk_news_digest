Rs. 1.5bn allocated to make dangerous roads safer

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has allocated Rs. 1.5 billion to identify and make safe unsafe and dangerous locations on roads outside the national road network, Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Ratnayake said.

Under the programme, Rs. 60 million will be allocated to each district, with local government institutions responsible for identifying dangerous locations and carrying out necessary safety improvements.

Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) were signed with chairmen and mayors of relevant local government institutions on September 18 to implement the programme.

The Minister said the Cabinet had approved the programme in June following a Cabinet memorandum submitted on May 20.

The National Council on Road Safety has been assigned responsibility for providing technical guidance and monitoring progress in collaboration with local government institutions.

Speaking at the event, Minister Ratnayake said road safety could not be improved through laws and public awareness campaigns alone.

“Road accidents cannot be reduced by enacting laws or educating the public alone. The physical unsafe conditions on the roads that cause accidents must also be changed,” he said.

He said road accidents caused not only loss of life but also significant economic and social costs, making road safety a national priority.

The MoUs will remain in effect until December 31, 2026, subject to their terms and conditions.
