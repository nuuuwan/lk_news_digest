GEW Sri Lanka 2026 to connect entrepreneurs and open new opportunities for biz growth

Global Entrepreneurship Week (GEW) Sri Lanka 2026 brings together aspiring entrepreneurs, startups, small and medium enterprises (SMEs), business leaders and young innovators to strengthen the country’s entrepreneurial ecosystem and create opportunities for business growth.

Organised by the Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN Global), in close collaboration with the Ministry of Digital Economy, the Ministry of Industries and Entrepreneurship Development, and the Ministry of Science and Technology, GEW Sri Lanka 2026 connects entrepreneurs, innovators, businesses and ecosystem stakeholders across all 25 districts.

The GEW Sri Lanka 2026 Launching Ceremony was held on the second day of Science Week, in collaboration with the Ministry of Science and Technology. The national campaign commences during Global Entrepreneurship Week, from 16 to 22 November 2026, with activities continuing throughout the year to expand and scale entrepreneurship initiatives across the country.

The campaign features panel discussions, workshops, networking sessions and business exhibitions, providing entrepreneurs with opportunities to exchange knowledge, showcase their businesses and connect with industry stakeholders.

The launching ceremony was attended by Deputy Minister of Digital Economy, Eranga Weeraratne; Deputy Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development, Chathuranga Abeysinghe; Secretary to the Ministry of Science and Technology, Roshanie Dissanayake; Mastercard Country Manager for Sri Lanka, Mahesha Amarasuriya; GEN Global National Ambassador for Sri Lanka, Suresh de Mel; Managing Director of NGage Strategic Alliance, Sheron Jayasundara; Founder and Chairman of Kapruka Holdings PLC, Dulith Herath; and LMS, AI and SaaS Consultant, CEO of eLearning.lk and Co-Founder of MayaHive, Sanjaya Elvitigala, who served as the moderator.

A key highlight of this year’s initiative will be the Entrepreneurship Marathon. It will bring together government, private sector, academia, ecosystem enablers and bilateral and multilateral organisations to help local entrepreneurs develop and grow their businesses.

Minister Weeraratne said, “Global Entrepreneurship Week commenced in Sri Lanka, with a three-day programme taking place primarily at the BMICH in Colombo, alongside activities across all 25 districts. Organised in collaboration with the Global Entrepreneurship Network, the initiative is being observed in nearly 200 countries, with Sri Lanka securing fourth place for its successful implementation. Entrepreneurship is vital to Sri Lanka’s economic development, and we need entrepreneurs who can transform innovative ideas into products and services, access global markets and generate foreign exchange. This initiative provides an important platform for people from all walks of life, including students and rural communities, to gain entrepreneurial knowledge, access guidance and connect with local and international business networks.” Commenting on the initiative, Charith Kandamulla, Program Director, GEW Sri Lanka 2026 said, “Building on last year’s progress, our focus is to expand opportunities and provide support to entrepreneurs across the country. In 2025, we conducted 1,864 events nationwide, and GEN Global ranked Sri Lanka fourth among more than 200 countries for its active engagement in entrepreneurship. GEW also supported policy round-table discussions that led to the launch of the Digital Nomad Visa, while Startup Genome ranked Sri Lanka as the world’s fastest-growing startup ecosystem. This year, we aim to build on these achievements by delivering 2,500 events, engaging 100,000 participants and supporting 5,000 entrepreneurs. Through the Entrepreneurship Marathon and collaboration across the ecosystem, we want to help local entrepreneurs access the connections and support they need to thrive.”

Through these efforts, GEW Sri Lanka 2026 aims to create a more connected and supportive environment where entrepreneurs can access opportunities, build networks and grow their businesses.
