’’Kedalla Colombo Trade Fair 2026’’

The premier housing and construction exhibition, "Kedalla Colombo Trade Fair 2026", will be held at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) in Colombo from September 11 to 13. With over 200 stalls, the event will feature a wide range of services related to housing and construction. Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
