Quazi Judge, woman arrested over Rs. 65,000 bribe

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) has arrested a Quazi Judge of the Colombo North Quazi Court and a woman for allegedly soliciting and accepting a bribe of Rs. 65,000 in connection with a divorce case.

The suspects were identified as Quazi Judge Mohamed Sawahir Mohamed Ramees and Fathima Nasreena.

The Quazi Judge was arrested inside the office of the Colombo North Quazi Judge.

The bribe had allegedly been solicited and accepted in exchange for delivering a favourable ruling for the complainant in a divorce case (Mubara) and a compensation case filed by his wife under the Muslim Marriage and Divorce Act.

The two suspects were produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court and remanded until December 1, 2026.
