Sri Lanka stocks trend up; Alliance Finance to raise up to Rs1bn in bond issue

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Colombo Stock Exchange was trading up on Wednesday, CSE data showed, with the benchmark All Share Price Index (ASPI) moving up 0.48 percent.

The ASPI was up 94.84 points at 19,787.51, while the more liquid S&P SL20 was up 0.55 percent, or 30.97 points, at 5,643.28.

Positive contributors to the ASPI were Dialog Axiata (up 1.42 percent at 42.80 rupees), Hatton National Bank (up 1.97 percent at 374.75 rupees), Vallibel One (up 3.01 percent at 85.50 rupees), LOLC Holdings (up 0.26 percent at 391.00 rupees), and Renuka Foods (up 9.38 percent at 21.00 rupees).

Capital Alliance (down 5.00 percent at 39.90 rupees), Teejay Lanka (down 2.11 percent at 27.90 rupees), Sri Lanka Telecom (down 1.14 percent at 78.00 rupees), and Haycarb (down 0.63 percent at 198.50 rupees) were among the negative contributors.

Market turnover was 964.6 million rupees. The S&P/CSE Telecommunication Services Industry Group led turnover with 832.13 million rupees.

Meanwhile, Alliance Finance Company PLC announced plans to raise up to 1 billion rupees through an issuance of senior, gender-responsive, orange-labeled bonds. The company’s board resolved on October 27, 2026, to issue up to 10 million listed, rated, unsecured, redeemable three-year bonds at a par value of 100 rupees each. Proceeds are intended for eligible social project categories, including MSME Value Chain Financing, MSME Climate Financing, and Micro Finance directed toward women-led enterprises and female beneficiaries, subject to regulatory clearances. (Colombo/Oct28/2026)
