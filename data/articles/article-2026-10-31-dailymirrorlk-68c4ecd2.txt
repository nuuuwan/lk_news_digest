Man fined Rs. 310,000 for setting up trap gun to harm elephant

Oct 31 (Daily Mirror) - A man has been fined Rs. 310,000 by the Kahatagasdigiliya Magistrate’s Court for allegedly setting up a trap gun intended to injure a wild elephant and possessing an unlicensed firearm, the Department of Wildlife Conservation said.

The suspect was arrested on October 24 by a team of wildlife officers during a patrol along the boundary of the Kokebe Forest Reserve in the Kahatagasdigiliya Divisional Secretariat Division.

The operation was led by M.A.K.S. Dissanayake, Site Assistant of the Kahatagasdigiliya Wildlife Beat Office.

According to the Department of Wildlife Conservation, the trap gun had been set up along the boundary of private land and was allegedly intended to harm a wild elephant.

The wildlife officers recovered the firearm and arrested the suspect during the operation.

The suspect, along with the seized firearm, was produced before the Kahatagasdigiliya Magistrate’s Court on October 25.

Following the proceedings, the court imposed a fine of Rs. 310,000 on the suspect.
