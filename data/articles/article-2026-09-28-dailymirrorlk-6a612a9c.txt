CSE slips below 21,000 as turnover hits YTD low

The Colombo Stock Exchange opened the week on a weak note on 28 September 2026, with both benchmark indices declining amid broad-based selling and exceptionally subdued investor participation.

The ASPI fell 93.56 points, or 0.44%, to close at 20,943.79, while the S&P SL20 declined 20.01 points, or 0.34%, to 5,920.72. The ASPI remained under pressure through most of the session and closed near the day’s low.

The standout feature of the session was the exceptionally low market activity. Turnover fell to just LKR 595.97 million, the lowest daily turnover recorded YTD, with only 32.12 million shares traded. The Diversified Financials sector led activity with LKR 128.71 million in turnover, while John Keells Holdings recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 74.69 million.

Crossings accounted for around 15% of total turnover, led by JKH.N with LKR 51.98 million and CDB.N with LKR 39.55 million.

Market breadth remained firmly negative, with only 55 gainers against 143 decliners, producing an advance-decline ratio of 0.38. Foreign investors were marginal net buyers with an inflow of LKR 7.89 million, although overall foreign participation remained limited.

Carson Cumberbatch was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Dialog Axiata, Commercial Bank, Ceylinco Insurance and SMB Finance. Distilleries provided the strongest positive contribution.

The combination of YTD-low turnover, weak market breadth and both indices closing near their intraday lows points to a clear lack of buying conviction. With the ASPI now below 21,000, the next few sessions will be important in assessing whether investor participation improves or whether subdued sentiment continues.
