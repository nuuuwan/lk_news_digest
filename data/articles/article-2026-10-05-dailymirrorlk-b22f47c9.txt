Uber Eats expands to Badulla, Bandarawela, Ella

Continued expansion brings Uber Eats to more communities across Sri Lanka

Uber Eats is now live across Badulla, Bandarawela and Ella, bringing the convenience of delivery to a region known for its Ceylon Tea and landmarks such as Ella’s iconic Nine Arch Bridge.

The locals and tourists can now enjoy their favourite meals – from a comforting rice and curry meal, to short eats or their morning coffee – delivered right to their doorstep.

The expansion marks Uber Eats’ third district launch this year, following Nuwara Eliya and Ratnapura.

With Uber Eats now available, the consumers can discover and order from a growing range of local restaurants and neighbourhood favourites across the district. In Ella, one of Sri Lanka’s most popular tourist destinations, the service will also give the domestic and international visitors greater choice and convenience during their stay, while helping the restaurants cater to a wider base of customers.

The launch supports the local community by enabling the merchants to reach more customers through the Uber Eats platform, while creating flexible earning opportunities for the delivery partners.

For the businesses, particularly those serving Badulla’s growing tourism economy, the platform provides another avenue to grow their business and benefit from the increasing demand for online delivery.

Uber Sri Lanka Country Manager Delivery Varun Wijewardane said, “Our expansion across Sri Lanka is about ensuring that the convenience and opportunities enabled by technology are not limited to the largest cities. As we enter Badulla, we look forward to partnering with the district’s restaurants, merchants and delivery partners and becoming a part of the everyday lives of the communities we serve.”

Uber Eats remains committed to expanding its footprint across Sri Lanka, touching more local communities and supporting merchants and delivery partners as its business grows across the country.
