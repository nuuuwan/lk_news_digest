Seylan Bank-Toyota Lanka offer enhanced leasing solutions

From left: Toyota Value Chain Department Head Naotaka Takeda, New Vehicle Sales General Manager Ravi Opatha, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Manohara Atukorala, Chairman Masaaki Kawabata, Seylan Bank Deputy General Manager Retail Banking Eugene Seneviratne, Assistant General Manager Marketing and Sales Asiri Abhayaratne, Head of Leasing Nalin Samaratunge, Chief Manager Marketing Nalin Karunaratne, Senior Manager Leasing Thakshila Dissanayake and Manager Leasing Nilakshi Pathirana

Seylan Bank PLC has entered a strategic partnership with Toyota Lanka (Private) Limited to offer enhanced leasing facilities for customers purchasing passenger and commercial vehicles, combining competitive financial solutions with greater convenience and a streamlined approval process.

The memorandum of understanding effectively introduces a dedicated leasing facility, bringing together Seylan Bank’s expertise in financial services and Toyota Lanka’s long-standing presence in Sri Lanka’s automotive sector, creating a comprehensive and customer-centric financing solution for individuals and businesses seeking access to a range of Toyota vehicles.

Under the partnership, the eligible customers will be able to obtain Seylan Leasing facilities with flexible repayment periods of up to seven years, together with competitive interest rates and a fast-track approval process that can facilitate approvals within 24 hours.

The initiative is designed to make vehicle ownership more accessible while providing the customers with a smoother and more efficient leasing experience, ensuring quicker access to the Toyota range.

The partnership comes at a time when the need for reliable passenger and commercial transportation continues to grow alongside Sri Lanka’s economic activity. Efficient and safe transportation is increasingly essential for the businesses operating in a competitive environment, while individuals and families also seek dependable mobility solutions. Through this collaboration, Seylan Bank aims to provide flexible financing options that support these evolving requirements.

Seylan Bank Deputy General Manager Retail Banking Eugene Seneviratne said, “Our partnership with Toyota Lanka represents another step in Seylan Bank’s ongoing commitment to making financial solutions more accessible and relevant to the needs of our customers. By combining attractive leasing facilities, flexible repayment periods and a fast-track approval process with the strength of Toyota Lanka’s vehicle offering, we are creating a convenient pathway for the customers to access both passenger and commercial vehicles with greater confidence and ease.”

The Seylan leasing offering will also be supported by dedicated leasing specialists who provide personalised guidance, including doorstep assistance and free consultancy throughout the lease application process.

Backed by Seylan Bank’s islandwide branch network, the customers will receive support with documentation, application procedures and post-approval services, ensuring a seamless experience from initial inquiry through to completion.

Toyota Lanka Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Manohara Atukorala said, “We are pleased to partner with Seylan Bank on an initiative that supports Toyota Lanka’s vision of ‘Mobility for All’ by helping make mobility more accessible to people across Sri Lanka. Through greater access to flexible and convenient financing solutions for Toyota passenger and commercial vehicles, we hope to empower individuals, families and businesses with the mobility they need to live, work and grow. This collaboration reflects our shared commitment to enhancing customer convenience and extending the benefits of mobility to communities across the country.”
