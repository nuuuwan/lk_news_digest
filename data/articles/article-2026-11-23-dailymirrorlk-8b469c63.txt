Colombo–Kandy main road to close for seven hours tonight

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo–Kandy main road will be temporarily closed at Lower Kadugannawa from 11.00 p.m. today (23) until 6.00 a.m. tomorrow (24), the Disaster Management Centre (DMC) announced.

The seven-hour closure is necessary to remove hazardous rocks at risk of falling onto the road and carry out related maintenance work in the Lower Kadugannawa area, the DMC said.

The District Disaster Management Unit has informed the District Secretary and other relevant officials about the planned closure.

The DMC’s Emergency Operations Centre has also notified all relevant parties to ensure the necessary arrangements are made ahead of the closure.
