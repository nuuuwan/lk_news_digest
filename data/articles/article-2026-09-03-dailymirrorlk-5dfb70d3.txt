AI Academy and Tomorrow University bring German AI Degrees to Colombo

From left: Chaturanga Abeysinghe, Deputy Minister Industry and Entrepreneurship Development, Lin Gong-Deutschmann, Advisor AI Academy & Tomorrow University, AI & Tech Angel Investor, Dr. Linda Speldewinde, Founder & Chairperson, AI Academy & AOD South Asia group of companies, Christian Rebernik, Founder & CEO, Tomorrow University of Applied Sciences, Tech Entrepreneur ,Jeevan Gnanam, Serial Entrepreneur, Angel Investor, Data Scientist

Sri Lankan students and working professionals will be able to earn degrees from Germany’s state-recognised Tomorrow University of Applied Sciences while building real-world work experience in Colombo, following a new partnership between AI Academy and the Berlin-based university.

Two Tomorrow University-awarded qualifications, a BSc in Applied AI and an MBA in AI & Technological Transformation, will be offered through AI Academy’s local incubator model as part of the partnership, which will be announced at a press conference at Hatch on August 24. The model is designed around a simple proposition: a job and a degree at the same time, from day one.

Through the partnership, Sri Lankan students can obtain a German higher education degree without having to move abroad. Under the State of Hesse, Tomorrow University is a private institution in Germany with state recognition. Its MBA programmes are accredited by the German Accreditation Council via ACQUIN, while its Bachelor’sprogrammes are accredited by FIBAA and recognized internationally.

Linda Speldewinde, Founder, AI Academy said, “At AOD, we didn’t just teach design — we sent Sri Lankan designers out into the world and they are now building names for themselves internationally, going head-to-head with graduates from the best design schools globally. That happened because we approached design education differently: real work, real clients, real stakes, rooted in impact, from day one. AI Academy is built on that exact same conviction, now applied to a field that’s transforming every industry. It shouldn’t be required for students to choose between earning, learning, and creating something of their own. From Colombo, they should be able to accomplish all three.”

Chathuranga Abeysinghe, Deputy Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development, who will deliver the keynote at the event said, “The next stage of Sri Lanka’s industrial and economic development will depend not only on the number of qualified individuals but also on what they can accomplish with their qualifications. People that can work with technology, understand problems, and turn knowledge into value are needed in the industry. Therefore, the country’s skills and entrepreneurship agenda depends on educational models that bring learning closer to real enterprise.”

A shared approach to education is another foundation of the partnership. Both AI Academy and Tomorrow University independently came to the same conclusion: when education is challenge-based, work-integrated, and supported by an environment where ideas can be tested and developed, students are better prepared for an AI-native economy.

Christian Rebernik, Founder, Tomorrow University of Applied Sciences said, “The future of employment and education are inextricably connected. Since AI is already altering how organisations function, students need to be able to apply technology to complex and changing situations in addition to having a basic understanding of it. That belief is reflected in our partnership with AI Academy. While the work surrounding the degree allows students a place to test, question, and build, the degree itself provides the academic foundation.”

Lin Gong Deutschmann, Connector, ToU-AOD, helped the connection between the two institutions by recognising that, despite their independent development, AI Academy and Tomorrow University were pursuing closely related educational models.
