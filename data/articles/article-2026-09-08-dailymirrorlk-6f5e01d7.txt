SDIG Ajith Rohana retires after 38 years of service

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Senior Deputy Inspector General of Police (SDIG) Attorney-at-Law Dr. Ajith Rohana retired today after completing an illustrious 38-year career in Sri Lanka Police. A ceremonial police guard of honour was held at the Welagedara Stadium in Kurunegala to mark his retirement and honour his longstanding service to the country.

Senior DIG Rohana holds a unique place in the history of the Sri Lanka Police as the youngest officer to be promoted to the rank of Deputy Inspector General of Police (DIG). A qualified attorney-at-law with a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree, he earned widespread public respect throughout his career for his professionalism, leadership and communication skills.

He became a household name across Sri Lanka during the COVID-19 pandemic while serving as the official Police Media Spokesman. His regular public briefings and clear communication during the pandemic earned him considerable recognition, with the public particularly appreciating his professional yet approachable manner.

Born in Kosgama, Avissawella, Senior DIG Rohana is an alumnus of Ananda College, Colombo, where he was recognised as an accomplished speaker during his school years. He later obtained his LL.B. degree from the Open University of Sri Lanka and went on to practise as an Advocate of the Supreme Court. In recognition of his distinguished contribution to public service, he was awarded an honorary doctorate in 2020.

During his 38 years in the police service, Rohana held several key positions, including Police Media Spokesman, a member of the Board of the National Child Protection Authority, and Senior Deputy Inspector General overseeing the Southern, North Western and Eastern Provinces.

Although his official retirement date is September 11, a ceremonial guard of honour and farewell event were held today in Kurunegala in recognition of his long and distinguished service.
