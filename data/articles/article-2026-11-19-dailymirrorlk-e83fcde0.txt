Janashakthi Life reports 21% GWP growth as of 3Q 2026

From left: Janashakthi Insurance Chairperson Annika Senanayake and Director and CEO Ravi Liyanage

Janashakthi Life reported its financial results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, outperforming the industry with continued growth in its core business, amid a challenging investment environment.

The gross written premiums (GWP) reached Rs.7,424 million, reflecting a 21 percent growth from Rs.6,142 million in the corresponding nine-month period of 2025.

The new business premiums also recorded a double-digit growth of 14 percent, highlighting continued momentum in the company’s core operations.

Total assets grew marginally to Rs.40.8 billion, from Rs.40.3 billion at end-December 2025. The claims and benefits paid increased to Rs.3,587 million, compared to Rs.2,603 million from the corresponding period of last year, reflecting the company’s continued commitment to fulfilling its obligations to the policyholders.

However, the profitability was impacted by lower investment income arising from the changes in the prevailing policy rates and resulting mark-to-market losses on investments. A profit before tax of Rs.391 million was recorded before the surplus transfer.

Janashakthi Insurance Chairperson Annika Senanayake said, “Our nine-month performance demonstrates the continued growth of our core business, reflected in the 21 percent expansion in GWP and double-digit growth in new business. While the profitability was impacted by the fluctuations in the investment income, arising from the prevailing interest rate environment, our underlying business fundamentals stand strong. We remain focused on sustaining this growth momentum while continuing to deliver value to our policyholders and stakeholders.”

Janashakthi Insurance Director and CEO Ravi Liyanage added, “Despite the external challenges impacting the operating environment, the company has outperformed the industry, while our priority remains firmly on our customers and delivering a consistent, high-quality experience. We will continue to strengthen our sales channels, enhance customer care and streamline processes through technology to improve accessibility, service delivery and cost efficiency. Our business is well-positioned, with the operational strength and stability to navigate the external market conditions while continuing to meet our commitments to the policyholders. We will continue building on this foundation by expanding our distribution network, introducing innovative solutions and accelerating digitisation to serve a broader customer base.”
