Union Bank takes to the road with cycle parade

Union Bank strengthened its commitment to team spirit and employee wellbeing by organising cycle parade that brought together staff and management in a vibrant display of unity. The parade commenced at the Union Bank Head Office, proceeded to the Wellawatte Branch, and returned to Head Office via the Marine Drive, creating an engaging and energetic experience for all participants. Organised by the Union Bank Sports Club, the initiative highlighted the Bank’s focus on fostering a healthy lifestyle, enhancing employee wellness, and nurturing a positive and collaborative workplace.
