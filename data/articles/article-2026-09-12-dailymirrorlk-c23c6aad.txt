PC polls - Opposition members of Select Committee looking into electoral system resign

Colombo, Sep. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition members of the select committee appointed to look into the drawing up of an electoral system for Provincial Councils announced their resignation in protest against the extension of the timeframe given to it until December 17, 2026.

Opposition members, MPs Ranjith Madduma Bandara, Mano Gansean and Shanakiyan Rasamanikam, have resigned in protest against the move to extend the timeframe.

MP Rasamanikam said he walked out of the Select Committee meeting in protest against the move to extend the timeframe.

MP Madumma Bandara said the move by the government to extend the timeframe given to the Select Committee to come up with an electoral system is aimed at avoiding the holding of the Provincial Council election this year as well.

Chief Government Whip Nalinda Jayatissa, who responded, said there is no move to avoid the election.

“We are also keen on holding the Provincial Council election,” he said.

An uproar was witnessed when the resolution was presented for the approval of the House, as the Opposition called for a division and the ruling party opposed it. The argument continued for some time, creating a tense situation. However, Chairperson of Committees Hemali Weerasekera, who was at the Chair, suspended the sittings as the lunch interval drew near.

The arguments continued when the sittings resumed after lunch, with the Opposition insisting on a vote. However, Dr. Jayatissa argued that the motion had already been approved by the House at the time when the Opposition called for a division.

A while later, Deputy Speaker Rizvi Salih said officials of the Parliamentary General Secretary’s Office had informed him that the motion had been duly approved by the House. He said the objections raised by the Opposition would be noted.
