Joint Opposition seeks full 12-judge SC bench on 22A petitions

Colombo, Aug. 10 (Daily Mirror) - While highlighting that they will file petitions against the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution which seeks to extend the retirement age of judges, the Joint Opposition said today it would call for a bench comprising all 12 judges in the Supreme Court to look into the petition.

“There are 17 judges in the Supreme Courts, but four have retired. Another is expected to retire early next month. Therefore, we will call for a bench comprising all 12 Supreme Court judges to look into petitions against the 22nd Amendment,” Convenor of the Joint Opposition, Professor G.L. Peiris told a media briefing.
