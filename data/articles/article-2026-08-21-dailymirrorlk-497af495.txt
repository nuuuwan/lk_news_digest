Gazette notification on deploying elephants for Peraheras soon

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror) - The Environment Ministry is preparing to issue a Gazette notification establishing a mechanism to regulate and deploy captive elephants for Peraheras and other cultural and religious events, following Cabinet approval for the initiative.

The move is aimed at strengthening governance, accountability and transparency in the management of captive elephants while ensuring compliance with Sri Lanka’s national laws and international obligations, the Ministry said.

The initiative follows a comprehensive review of the legal and administrative framework governing the registration and management of captive elephants, changes in conservation priorities, law enforcement challenges, requirements for deploying elephants at cultural and religious events and animal welfare concerns.

A special committee has been appointed by the Secretary to the Ministry of Environment to conduct a comprehensive study of these matters, obtain views from relevant stakeholders and review the draft “Registration and Licensing of Elephants Regulations 2026” prepared by the Department of Wildlife Conservation.

The committee has also been tasked with submitting recommendations on a mechanism for deploying captive elephants for cultural and religious purposes, with particular attention to their welfare.

The committee is chaired by H.M.B.P. Herath, Additional Secretary (Policy, Legal Reforms and Parliamentary Affairs) of the Ministry of Environment, while D.P.C. Udaya Kumara, Additional Secretary (Development) of the Ministry, serves as its Convener and Secretary.

The committee is expected to submit its recommendations on the allocation of elephants for Peraheras in the near future.

The Ministry said the proposed regulatory framework is intended to ensure that the use of captive elephants for cultural and religious events is carried out under a properly regulated system while safeguarding animal welfare and strengthening oversight of the sector.
