Warrants issued on Aravinda de Silva, his wife recalled

Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena today ordered the recall of warrants issued yesterday on former Sri Lankan cricketer Aravinda de Silva and his wife, who had stood as sureties for the wife of Kapila Chandrasena, a suspect in the alleged payment of a USD 2 million bribe in connection with the purchase of Airbus aircraft by SriLankan Airlines.

However, appearing before court today by way of a motion, counsel representing Aravinda de Silva and his wife submitted that they had been unable to appear before court yesterday due to the funeral arrangements of Aravinda's mother.

Considering the submissions, the Magistrate ordered the warrants issued on Aravinda de Silva and his wife to be recalled.
