Sri Lanka Tourism showcased in Johannesburg

The High Commission of Sri Lanka in Pretoria held a tourism promotion event at the Rosebank Sunday Market in Johannesburg, showcasing Sri Lanka’s diverse attractions and travel experiences to an international audience.

The event provided an opportunity to promote Sri Lanka’s rich cultural heritage, natural beauty, wildlife, cuisine and range of tourism experiences to visitors from South Africa and other countries.

Tourism promotional materials were distributed among visitors, many of whom expressed interest in travelling to Sri Lanka and experiencing the country in the near future.

Members of the diplomatic and business communities in South Africa, along with members of the Sri Lankan community, also attended the event, creating opportunities to further promote Sri Lanka as a tourism destination and strengthen people-to-people links.

The High Commission said the strong response from visitors and the high level of engagement contributed to the success of the programme.

The event forms part of the High Commission’s ongoing efforts to increase awareness of Sri Lanka’s tourism offerings among the South African public and the wider international community, while encouraging more visitors to explore the island nation.
