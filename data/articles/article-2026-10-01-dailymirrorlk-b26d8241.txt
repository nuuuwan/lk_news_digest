Court of Appeal prevents police from arresting Tiran Alles and others

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal yesterday (30) issued a writ of prohibition preventing the police from arresting former Public Security Minister Tiran Alles and directors of Ceylon Newspapers (Private) Limited in connection with an incident involving the issuance of cheques.

A two-judge bench of the Court of Appeal, comprising Justices Sasi Mahendran and Pradeep Hettiarachchi, delivered the judgment pursuant to a writ petition filed by Ceylon Newspapers (Private) Limited, seeking an order preventing the police from arresting its Chairman, former Minister Tiran Alles, as well as its directors and employees, over an ongoing investigation into allegedly dishonoured cheques issued to a newsprint supplier.

The court held that the transaction involved in the writ application was a commercial transaction and that there was insufficient evidence to incriminate the petitioner, its directors or employees in respect of the alleged offences.

However, the court noted that the order should not prevent the police from investigating the matter further and, if any new material is discovered in relation to the disputed transaction, from taking any further action necessary in accordance with the law.

Dr. Romesh de Silva, PC, appeared for the petitioner, while Viran Corea, PC, appeared for the fifth respondent.
