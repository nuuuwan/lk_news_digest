Nanaska Edge receives national recognition

Nanaska Edge, the AI-powered learning and assessment platform developed by Nanaska, has been recognised in the Best AI Solution in Education Category at National AI Awards 2026, pioneered by the Digital Economy Ministry and SLT-Mobitel.

Nanaska is the only professional education institute to receive recognition at National AI Awards 2026 under this category, with Nanaska Edge being recognised for its innovative application of artificial intelligence (AI) in professional education.

Nanaska Edge was introduced in January 2026, marking a significant step in Nanaska’s use of AI to transform professional education.

Nanaska is a leading professional education institute specialising in CIMA and a registered CIMA tuition provider preparing students for the CIMA qualification.

Nanaska Edge is designed as a complete AI-powered learning ecosystem for students; they can complete the CIMA Case Study mocks and receive instant marks, strengths, weaknesses and personalised improvement plans.

Edge also acts as an intelligent study partner throughout their preparation. It provides personalised timetables and study schedules, adapts to each student’s learning journey and continuously identifies areas requiring improvement based on their activity and performance. This enables Edge to provide ongoing, personalised guidance beyond individual mocks, helping students understand what they need to improve and what they should focus on each week.

With only around six weeks to prepare for the Case Study, regular practice is critical. By eliminating the wait for manual marking, Edge has enabled students to complete more than twice the number of mock papers compared to before, while allowing tutors to dedicate more time to individual guidance and one-to-one support.

The improvement in student performance through Nanaska Edge is increasingly evident, with more students achieving exceptional marks in their CIMA Case Study examinations. With the pass mark set at 80, over 50 students have scored over 100+ marks across the three examination windows in 2026, a significant achievement that reflects the growing level of performance among the Nanaska students.

Having already marked over 2000+ mock papers, Nanaska Edge has enabled students to practise more, receive immediate feedback and continuously identify and address areas for improvement.
