Prime Lands receives YouTube Silver Play Button

Makes history as SL’s 1st real estate developer to receive it

Prime Lands has made history in Sri Lanka’s real estate sector by becoming the country’s first real estate developer to receive the prestigious YouTube Silver Play Button, after surpassing 100,000 subscribers.

The milestone comes as Prime Group celebrates its 30th anniversary, adding a significant digital achievement to three decades of growth and innovation.

As part of YouTube’s Creator Awards programme, the Silver Play Button recognises the channels that reach significant subscriber milestones.

For Prime Lands, reaching this milestone marked more than a growth in audience; it reflected the developer’s transformation from traditional marketing to a digital-first approach and its commitment to using content, technology and storytelling to redefine how Sri Lankans discover and engage with real estate.

Today, with a YouTube community of more than 600,000 subscribers, Prime Lands continues to expand its digital reach and deepen its connection with property seekers across Sri Lanka.

Prime Lands CEO and Executive Director Ruminda Randeniya said, “Through the YouTube journey, we have transformed the way people make real estate decisions, guiding smarter property choices to immersive, insightful content, helping them visualise opportunities, understand market dynamics and make informed choices.”
