How global diesel price should be reflected? Decision today

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - In the wake of rising diesel prices in the global market, the Finance Ministry and the Energy Ministry will decide today how the increase should be reflected in next month’s fuel-price revision, an official said.

The revision will be carried out based on inputs from the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC).

A CPC official said the increase in diesel prices in the global market is currently the main concern.

“We will try our best to minimise the impact of the increase on local prices. However, everything will ultimately be decided by the two ministries,” he said.

The average price of diesel worldwide is US$1.59 per litre. However, there are substantial differences in diesel prices among countries. As a general rule, richer countries tend to have higher fuel prices, while poorer countries and oil-producing and exporting countries have significantly lower prices.
