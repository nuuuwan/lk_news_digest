Indices close in green

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as ACL Cables, Sri Lanka Telecom and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.1 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Amana Takaful, Hatton National Bank and Hatton Plantations. Mixed interest was observed in Overseas Realty, Access Engineering and Commercial Bank whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, ACME Printing & Packaging and Janashakthi Limited. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank) whilst the sector index gained 0.31%. The share price of Hatton National Bank gained Rs 1.50 (0.39%) to close at Rs 381.50.

Insurance sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Amana Takaful) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.02%. The share price of Amana Takaful increased by Rs 1.10 (4.80%) to close at Rs 24.00.

Hatton Plantations, Overseas Realty and Access Engineering were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Hatton Plantations moved down by Rs 0.10 (0.41%) to close at Rs 24.40. The share price of Overseas Realty recorded a gain of Rs 0.10 (0.19%) to close at Rs 53.20. The share price of Access Engineering appreciated by Rs 0.80 (1.01%) to close at Rs 79.90.
