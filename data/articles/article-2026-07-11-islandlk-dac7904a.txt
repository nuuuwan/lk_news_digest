IS THERE AN ALTERNATIVE TO OIL?

When I say oil I mean petroleum. The question seems to me foolish and primitive. If oil is a source of energy only we have other sources of energy already, ie the sun, wind and water which are extensively tapped already. Yet Oil has become most essential item for the survival of the living beings.

Since oil was discovered by the human beings a few centuries ago it has become the power, running the world. Oil has caused conflicts within and between nations. Oil producing countries have become the richest countries in the world .Nowadays oil is referred to as Black Gold. Without oil we cannot survive. The impact of the recent conflicts in the world has proved it beyond any doubt. In other words oil is the most essential energy that drives the world.

One has to think deeply to find how the human societies prospered and developed civilizations all over the world, East, West and Middle East before the discovery of oil. Those societies were far more developed and advanced than we are today. Egypt, Mesopotamia, Mohenjo-Daro and China are some examples. In our own country, highly developed Irrigation systems are an example.

Oil has developed technology, and in turn has helped to extend the life span of living beings by developing various medicines, improved health, improved mobility and production of food.

Only aspect sad to note is the deterioration of the human ethics and values. Philosophers and wise men had seen this aspect of deterioration before and even after the discovery of Black Gold.

So what is the alternative to Black Gold? Can AI give an answer?
