Ibrahim Zadran’s 95 powers Jaffna Kings to win over Colombo Kaps

July.29 (Mirror Sports) - Jaffna Kings boosted their Lanka Premier League 2026 playoff hopes with a convincing 19-run victory over Colombo Kaps at the Pallekele International Cricket Stadium, powered by Ibrahim Zadran’s brilliant half-century and Dilshan Madushanka’s match-winning bowling spell.

Zadran produced his best innings of the tournament, anchoring the Jaffna innings with a classy 95 from 65 deliveries. The Afghanistan opener combined timing and power, smashing 12 boundaries and three sixes before narrowly missing out on a well-deserved century.

Kamil Mishara provided valuable early momentum with 44, while Towhid Hridoy’s unbeaten 31 helped Jaffna finish strongly as they posted an imposing 202 for 5 from their 20 overs.

Colombo needed an aggressive start in their chase but struggled to build momentum throughout the innings. Sadeera Samarawickrama top-scored with 44, while Kamindu Mendis and Milan Rathnayake contributed 28 and 26 respectively. However, regular wickets prevented Colombo from building the partnerships required.

Madushanka turned the game decisively in Jaffna’s favour with an outstanding left-arm pace spell, claiming 3 for 30 — his best bowling figures of the tournament. He received strong support from the rest of the attack as Colombo’s required run rate continued to rise.

Colombo eventually finished on 183 for 9, handing Jaffna Kings another valuable victory as the race for the LPL 2026 playoffs intensifies.

Jaffna Kings 202/5 (20 overs): Ibrahim Zadran 95 (65), Kamil Mishara 44, Towhid Hridoy 31*; James Neesham 2/30.

Colombo Kaps 183/9 (20 overs): Sadeera Samarawickrama 44 (36), Kamindu Mendis 28, Milan Rathnayake 26; Dilshan Madushanka 3/30.
