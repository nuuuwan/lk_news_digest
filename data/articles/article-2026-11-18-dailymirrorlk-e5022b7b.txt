Licence renewal on hold? here’s your penalty-free window

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The issuance of vehicle revenue licences across the Western Province will be temporarily suspended for five days from midnight today (Nov. 18) due to a technical upgrade of the electronic system, the Western Provincial Department of Motor Traffic said.

The suspension will remain in effect until midnight on November 23. Online revenue licence services through the department's official website (www.motortraffic.wp.gov.lk) will also be unavailable during this period.

However, vehicle owners whose revenue licences are due for renewal during the suspension period will be given a grace period from November 25 to 27 to obtain their licences without incurring late payment penalties, the department said.
