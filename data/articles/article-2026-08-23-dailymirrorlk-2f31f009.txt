Two arrested with over 1,044kg of cannabis

Colombo, Aug. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Two suspects have been arrested with more than 1,044 kilograms of cannabis during two separate raids in Gomagala.

Acting on a tip-off, officers from the Walana Central Anti-Corruption Strike Force conducted the raids.

Police seized 1,044 kilograms and 180 grams of cannabis along with a cannabis plantation containing 54,096 plants.

The suspects and the seized cannabis have been handed over to the Hambegamuwa Police Station, which is conducting further investigations.
