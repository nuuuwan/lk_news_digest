Wife of Kalutara restaurant owner dies following shooting

Kalutara, Oct 11 (Daily Mirror) - The wife of a restaurant owner who was shot in front of their establishment in Nagoda, Kalutara, on October 9 has died from her injuries, police said.

The shooting occurred at around 7.40 a.m. when two unidentified gunmen arriving on a motorcycle opened fire on the restaurant owner and his wife before fleeing the scene.

The restaurant owner died at the scene, while his critically injured wife was admitted to the Intensive Care Unit of Nagoda Teaching Hospital.

The 52-year-old woman, a resident of Dodangoda, succumbed to her injuries early this morning (11), police said.

Kalutara South Police are conducting further investigations into the shooting.
