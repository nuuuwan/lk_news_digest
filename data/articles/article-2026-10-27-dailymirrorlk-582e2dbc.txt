BOC plans debenture issue of up to Rs.30 billion

Bank of Ceylon (BOC) plans to raise up to Rs.30 billion through a listed debenture issue, offering five and seven-year instruments, with fixed or floating interest rates.

The bank’s board has approved an initial issue of Rs.10 billion, with two options to raise a further Rs.10 billion each in the event of oversubscription, according to a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) dated October 26. Both increases will be at the bank’s discretion.

The Basel III-compliant Tier 2 debentures will be issued at Rs.100 each. The CSE has granted an in-principle approval for the proposed issue.

The five-year fixed-rate debentures will pay 12.75 percent annually, while the seven-year fixed-rate instruments will carry an annual interest rate of 13 percent.

The floating-rate investors will receive the 12-month treasury bill rate plus 1.50 percentage points for the five-year instruments and plus 1.75 percentage points for the seven-year instruments. The interest on all four categories will be paid annually.

The issue carries an ‘AA (Stable)’ rating from Lanka Rating Agency Limited. The bank separately disclosed an issuer rating of ‘AA-(lka)’, with a stable outlook, from Fitch Ratings Lanka Limited.

The subscriptions will be restricted to qualified investors. The individual investors must make an initial investment of at least Rs.5 million, while the minimum subscription for other qualified investors is Rs.10,000. The applications above the applicable minimum must be in multiples of Rs.10,000.

The unsecured, subordinated debentures include non-viability write-down provisions, allowing the losses to be imposed on the investors’ principal at the sole discretion of the Central Bank’s Governing Board, without the debenture holders’ approval.

The mechanism can be triggered if the Governing Board determines that a write-down is necessary to prevent the bank from becoming non-viable or if the public-sector capital or equivalent support is required to prevent that outcome.
