Jaipur limb users in action

The Board of Management of the Colombo Friend-in-Need Society and the Organising Committee of Aidex 2026, a sports festival for Jaipur limb users, organised the event at the Force Station on Sir Chittampalam A. Gardiner Mawatha, Colombo, yesterday (20). Participants using artificial limbs displayed their sporting abilities at the festival. Pic by Kithsiri De Mel
