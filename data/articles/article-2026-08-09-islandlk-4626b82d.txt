SriLankan Airlines resumes flights to Kuwait

SriLankan Airlines has resumed flights between Colombo and Kuwait, with a six-weekly service commencing from 8 August 2026.

Flight UL229 from Colombo to Kuwait will depart at 17:10 hrs every Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday, arriving in Kuwait at 20:05 hrs. The return flight, UL230, will depart Kuwait at 21:10 hrs on the same days, arriving in Colombo at 05:15 hrs the following day.

For more information, please contact 1979 (within Sri Lanka), +94 11 777 1979 (international), WhatsApp +94 74 444 1979 (chat only), your travel agent, visit www.srilankan.com, or follow SriLankan Airlines on social media.
