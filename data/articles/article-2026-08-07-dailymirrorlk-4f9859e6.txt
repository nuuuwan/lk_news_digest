Speaker, MP Hector Appuhamy clash in Parliament

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne and Opposition MP Hector Appuhamy engaged in a verbal clash in Parliament during the oral question session today.

The clash broke out when the Speaker prevented the MP from raising a supplementary question.

"You are taking too much time to raise your issue," the Speaker said.

MP Appuhamy,  who responded, said he should be given time to raise the issue just like Deputy Minister Aruna Jayasekera  who took much time to answer.

"You should treat MPs of both sides equally," MP Appuhamy said.

The Speaker then mentioned that the Deputy Minister also took too much time to answer the question.
