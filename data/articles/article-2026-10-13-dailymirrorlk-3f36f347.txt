Police on the lookout for man who sexually abused 14-year-old daughter

Colombo, Oct. 13 (Daily Mirror) - An individual from Kiula in the Hambegamuwa Police area who sexually abused his 14-year-old daughter has fled from the area when the police initiated investigations to arrest him.

The victim was a schoolgirl and the eldest in the family, living with her younger brother, her younger sister and her parents. Police said her 47-year-old father is a manual worker in the area.

Investigations revealed that the suspect had sexually abused the girl at times when she was all alone in the house. Police said they initiated investigations on information from the Child Protection Authority. The girl was admitted to Embilipitiya Base Hospital for medical examination

OIC Hambegamuwa Police Thushara Sampath is conducting an investigation to arrest the absconding suspect.
