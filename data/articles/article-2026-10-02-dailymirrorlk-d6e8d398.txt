CPC says no fuel distribution or transportation issue

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) Chairman D.J. Rajakaruna said yesterday that there had been no major issues with fuel distribution or transportation, adding that a discussion had been scheduled with fuel distributors yesterday to address concerns over their commission rates.

Speaking to the Media, Rajakaruna said the University of Moratuwa was currently working on a formula to determine commissions for fuel distribution and transportation.

He expressed confidence that the matter could be resolved without difficulty.

Rajakaruna said fuel queues reported in several parts of the country were not due to distribution problems but had arisen after some private companies limited their fuel supplies.

He said 62 locations had been identified where fuel shortages had been reported, adding that these areas were affected because only privately operated fuel stations were available in those areas.

“We have requested the relevant companies to give priority to at least these 62 locations and ensure an uninterrupted fuel supply,” he said.
