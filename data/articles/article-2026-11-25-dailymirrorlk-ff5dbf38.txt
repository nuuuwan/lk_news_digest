Puerto Rico’s Jennifer Barreto crowned Miss Universe 2026 winner

NDTV - The 75th edition of Miss Universe has concluded, with Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto emerging as the winner. Reigning Miss Universe Fatima Bosch of Mexico crowned her successor.

Dominican Republic's Melissa Nunez was named the first runner-up, while Venezuela's Clara Vegas Goetz finished as the second runner-up.

The Top 5 finalists included the Philippines, New Zealand, Venezuela, the Dominican Republic, and Puerto Rico. In the final round, each finalist was asked the same question: "What does the world need most from its leaders right now?"

Jennifer Barreto delivered the winning answer. She said, "I believe the world needs leaders who are professionally empathetic. We live in a world where we often judge and question others, but a leader should stand up for their values, set an example, and raise the bar."

As Miss Universe, she supports the idea of building networks that cater to the needs of communities. The newly crowned pageant winner believes that when leaders listen to their communities and develop strategies accordingly, they can create a significant impact.
