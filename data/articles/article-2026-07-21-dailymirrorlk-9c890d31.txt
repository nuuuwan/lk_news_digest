Bi-annual inspection at Ragama Police

Officers of the Ragama Police Station today underwent their bi-annual inspection. The inspection was headed by Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) A.H. Nuwan Asanka, Divisional Commander of the Kelaniya Police Division. Officer-in-Charge of Ragama Police, Chief Inspector (CI) Saman Jayarathna, accompanied the inspection team.
