French Bar Council backs BASL’s concerns over proposed judicial retirement age increase

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - The French National Bar Council (Conseil National des Barreaux – CNB) has adopted a resolution expressing support for the Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) over concerns regarding the Government’s proposal to increase the retirement age of judges.

The resolution was adopted at the CNB’s Annual General Meeting held in Paris on July 3, 2026, following discussions between the CNB’s Committee on European and International Affairs and a delegation from the BASL.

In its resolution, the CNB noted the BASL’s concerns that extending the retirement age of serving judges could be viewed by the public as an attempt to undermine judicial independence.

The French legal body also referred to a statement issued by the Commonwealth Lawyers’ Association supporting the BASL’s position, while reaffirming that an independent and impartial judiciary is fundamental to protecting the rule of law.

The CNB expressed its full support for the BASL’s concerns and urged Sri Lankan authorities not to proceed with proposed constitutional amendments aimed at increasing the retirement age of judges.
