CCWE Fashion Week kicks off

The Ceylon Chamber of Women Entrepreneurs (CCWE) Fashion Week 2026 celebrates diversity, creativity, and empowerment under the inspiring theme, "Threads of Inclusion Woven from Every Walk of Life." The event was launched today (16) and will continue until Sunday (19), showcasing fashion as a platform for inclusion, innovation, and the remarkable talents of women entrepreneurs.
