CAASL, Vietjet Air hold high-level talks ahead of Sri Lanka launch

Ahead of Vietjet Air’s upcoming launch in Sri Lanka, a high-level meeting was held yesterday between Civil Aviation Authority (CAASL) Director General and CEO Capt. Daminda Rambukwella, Airport and Economic Regulation Director Asanga Liyanarachchi, Vietjet Air Vice President – Commercial Ta Huu Thanh, and International Sales Director Nguyen Chi Duc.

Representing Andrew The Aviation Company, Mahen Kariyawasan and Kemin Perera also participated in the discussions, which focused on preparations for Vietjet Air’s entry into the Sri Lankan market and strengthening aviation connectivity between Sri Lanka and Vietnam.
