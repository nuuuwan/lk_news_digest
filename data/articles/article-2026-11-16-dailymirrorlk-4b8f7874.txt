People’s Bank-Hayleys Mobility expand access to mobility solutions

People’s Bank Chief Executive Officer and General Manager Clive Fonseka, Hayleys Mobility Managing Director Hasith Prematillake, Director Business Development Roshini Dharmarathne, People’s Bank Deputy General Manager Retail Banking A.U.A. Anzar, Assistant General Manager Retail Banking Nalin Perera, Chief Manager Personal Loans Ravi Perera and other officials from both organisations at MoU signing ceremony

People’s Bank and Hayleys Mobility recently signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU), marking a significant step towards expanding access to innovative, world-class mobility solutions across Sri Lanka.

This strategic partnership brings together Hayleys Mobility’s automotive expertise and People’s Bank’s trusted financial strength to offer customised financing solutions, greater convenience and enhanced opportunities for customers islandwide.

The partnership aims to empower Sri Lankans with greater mobility choices while contributing to a more connected and sustainable future.
