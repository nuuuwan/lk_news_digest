PayMedia wins Sustainable FinTech Innovation Award

PayMedia (Pvt) Ltd has been recognised for its excellence in financial technology innovation, securing the Winner Award in the Sustainable FinTech Innovation category at FinTech Festival 2026, held at Shangri-La, Colombo on 3 November.

This recognition was awarded to PayMedia for its Digital Banking Solution, highlighting the company’s continued commitment to developing innovative digital financial services that support a more inclusive, accessible, and sustainable financial ecosystem.

PayMedia’s Digital Banking Solution is designed to support financial institutions in delivering modern digital banking experiences while contributing to the advancement of a more connected and technology-enabled financial ecosystem.

The award recognises the solution’s contribution to sustainable FinTech innovation and reinforces the company’s role in driving meaningful technological advancements within the industry.

Commenting on this achievement, PayMedia highlighted that the recognition represents the collective dedication, expertise, and collaborative efforts of its talented team, alongside the continued trust and support of its valued clients and business partners.
