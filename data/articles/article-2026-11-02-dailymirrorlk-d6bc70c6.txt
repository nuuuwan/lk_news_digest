Iran’s IRGC Navy says two tankers ’exploded’ after hitting mines in Hormuz

Reuters - Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps ​Navy said two ‌oil tankers "exploded" after striking mines while ​traveling through an "unauthorized ​route" in the Strait ⁠of Hormuz, ​Iranian state media ​reported on Sunday.

Iran has imposed restrictions on vessels ​seeking to ​pass through the Strait of ‌Hormuz ⁠and has attacked ships it says violate those ​restrictions, as ​it ⁠seeks to assert control ​over the strategic ​waterway ⁠and exert pressure on the ⁠US.
