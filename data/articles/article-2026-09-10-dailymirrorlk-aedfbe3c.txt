Traders express anger over removal of fish stalls at Dewata Beach

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Traders at Dewata Beach in Galle have expressed strong dissatisfaction following the removal of their fish stalls by the Coastal Conservation Department this morning.

The traders claimed they had previously been removed from their former trading locations and that officials of the Municipal Council had directed them to set up stalls at the Dewata Beach site. They questioned the decision to remove the stalls after they had been operating at the location with the knowledge of local authorities.

The traders further alleged that the Coastal Conservation Department officials arrived unexpectedly and dismantled the stalls without giving them adequate time to remove their fish and other goods.

However, officials of the Coastal Conservation Department rejected the allegations, stating that the traders had been informed in advance to remove the unauthorised structures from the beach.

Police and Special Task Force (STF) personnel were deployed to the location to provide security during the clearance operation.
