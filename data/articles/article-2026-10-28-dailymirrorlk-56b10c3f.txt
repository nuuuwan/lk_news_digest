Tiesh with Mastercard unveils Tourmaline Odyssey

From left: Tiesh Director Lasantha De Fonseka, Mastercard Sri Lanka Country Manager Mahesha Amarasuriya and Bryony De Fonseka unveil Tourmaline Odyssey collection

Association aims to celebrate Sri Lanka’s gemstone heritage, artisan craftsmanship and evolving aspirations of affluent consumers

As Sri Lanka’s affluent consumer segment continues to seek more distinctive and experience-led luxury offerings, Tiesh Sri Lanka, a leading family-owned contemporary jeweller and premier gem supplier, has collaborated with Mastercard to launch the Tourmaline Odyssey collection.

Unveiled at Shangri-La Colombo on October 16, the collection brings together Sri Lanka’s rich gemstone heritage, contemporary design and exceptional craftsmanship through one of the world’s most versatile and captivating gemstones.

Inspired by tourmaline’s remarkable spectrum of colours and individuality, the collection reflects a growing demand among consumers for products that combine authenticity, craftsmanship and personal expression. Featuring necklaces, pendants, bracelets and rings crafted in white gold, yellow gold, rose gold and silver, the collection showcases green, watermelon and other tourmaline varieties in designs ranging from timeless elegance to bold statement pieces.

The collaboration underscores a shared commitment to elevating Sri Lankan craftsmanship and expanding opportunities for local creative industries. By bringing together a homegrown luxury brand and a global technology company, the association highlights how innovation, commerce and design can create new pathways for growth while connecting Sri Lankan excellence with a broader audience.

“Tiesh has built this collection around tourmaline as its centrepiece, a gemstone chosen not for trend but for its story,” said Tiesh Director Ayesh De Fonseka.

“While tourmaline has traditionally received less attention than some other gemstones, its extraordinary beauty and versatility have made it increasingly popular among global jewellery brands. Through this collection, we hope to spotlight its creative potential while celebrating Sri Lanka’s rich gem heritage and craftsmanship.”

Mastercard’s engagement reflects its broader commitment to supporting local businesses, expanding market access and creating differentiated experiences for consumers.

As Sri Lanka’s premium and affluent segments continue to grow, Mastercard is working with partners across industries to deliver enhanced value through seamless digital payments, exclusive benefits and elevated experiences.

Mastercard Sri Lanka Country Manager Mahesha Amarasuriya stated, “Sri Lanka’s affluent consumers are increasingly seeking meaningful experiences and products that reflect quality, craftsmanship and authenticity. Our collaboration with Tiesh brings together these evolving consumer preferences with the country’s rich gem legacy, while creating additional value for the Mastercard cardholders. At the same time, it supports local enterprise by helping connect Sri Lankan creativity and craftsmanship with new audiences through seamless and secure digital commerce.”

As part of the launch and the upcoming festive season, the Mastercard cardholders will enjoy exclusive offers on the collection, adding a unique customer dimension to the partnership.

Named after the Sinhalese word ‘toramalli’, often associated with mixed or multicoloured stones, tourmaline occupies a unique place within Sri Lanka’s gem heritage.

Through the Tourmaline Odyssey collection, Tiesh and Mastercard are bringing renewed attention to this underrepresented gemstone while celebrating the artistry, innovation and entrepreneurial spirit that continue to define Sri Lanka’s luxury and creative sectors.
