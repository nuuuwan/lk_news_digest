Tea auction sees good demand as offerings reach 5.2mn kg

The auction offerings moved up marginally and totalled 5.2 million kilogrammes (M/Kgs).

Good general demand was seen, with the better teas often securing a premium.

The Ex-Estate offerings declined to 0.66 M/Kgs, from 0.70 M/Kgs on offer the previous week. The overall quality was marginally improved.

Consequently, as observed over the past couple of weeks, the better teas often edged up, following the quality and a similar trend continued this week as well.  At the lower end of the market, the BOPs continued to be a weak feature, with a price parity of Rs.200 per kilogramme in favour of the BOPF grade.

The High and Mid Grown CTC teas and particularly the better teas continued to sell consistently, whilst the secondary types were sluggish.

The Low Growns totalled approximately 2.4 M/Kgs. A fair demand was witnessed in the Leafy, Semi-Leafy and Tippy categories, whilst the Premium category met with good demand.

In the Leafy and Semi-Leafy catalogues, the Select Best and Best BOP1s were firm, whilst the Below Best together with the bolder varieties declined. The teas at the bottom maintained.  The OP1s, in general, were firm to dearer. The well-made OPs were easier, whilst the balance sold around last levels.  The Select Best OPAs were firm to dearer, whilst the Best and Below Best varieties together with the teas at the bottom declined.

The Select Best and Best PEKs were irregular, whilst the balance together with the mixed/open varieties was easier. The teas at the bottom maintained. The well-made PEK1s were firm, whilst the Below Best together with the mixed varieties were easier. The clean leaf teas at the bottom were firm.

In the Tippy catalogue, a selection of high-priced FBOPs were firm, whilst the balance together with the Best and Below Best sold around last levels. A few Select Best FF1s were firm, whilst the balance together with the Best were irregular. The Below Best and teas at the bottom were easier.

In the Premium catalogue, the Tippy teas in general appreciated.
