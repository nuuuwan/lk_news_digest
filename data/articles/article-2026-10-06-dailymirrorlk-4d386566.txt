Gnanasara Thera brought before Colombo High Court

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Bodu Bala Sena (BBS) General Secretary Ven. Galagodaaththe Gnanasara Thera, who was sentenced to a term of imprisonment, has been brought before the Colombo High Court.

Gnanasara Thera appeared before the court dressed in civilian clothing.

He was brought before the Colombo High Court in connection with a warrant issued by the court.
