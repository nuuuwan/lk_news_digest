Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Carson Cumberbatch and Hayleys with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. Crossings were witnessed in The Fortress Resorts, Sampath Bank and Hatton National Bank, accounting for 58.6% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Bank, Melstacorp and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Lanka Credit and Business Finance, Cargills Bank and Janashakthi Limited. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. Consumer Services sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to The Fortress Resorts) whilst the sector index lost 0.35%. The share price of The Fortress Resorts decreased by Rs 0.40 (1.81%) to close at Rs 21.70.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.02%. The share price of Commercial Bank lost Rs 1.00 (0.51%) to close at Rs 197.00. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs 138.50. John Keells Holdings and Melstacorp were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a loss of Rs 0.20 (1.12%) to close at Rs 17.60. The share price of Melstacorp declined by Rs 1.25 (0.70%) to close at Rs 177.75.
