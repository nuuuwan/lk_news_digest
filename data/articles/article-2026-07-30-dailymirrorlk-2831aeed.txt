Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Aitken Spence, Commercial Bank and ACL Cables with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.0 bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Hayleys, Hatton National Bank and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in Lee Hedges, Commercial Bank and Swisstek whilst retail interest was noted in Asia Siyaka Commodities, Ceylon Land & Equity and Janashakthi Limited.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hayleys and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index lost 0.55%.

The share price of Hayleys closed flat at Rs. 225.00. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs. 19.90. The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.26%.

The share price of Hatton National Bank recorded a loss of Rs. 1.00 (%) to close at Rs. 385.25. The share price of Commercial Bank declined by Rs. 1.00 (%) to close at Rs. 201.50. Lee Hedges was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Lee Hedges moved down by Rs. 0.75 (%) to close at Rs. 291.75.
