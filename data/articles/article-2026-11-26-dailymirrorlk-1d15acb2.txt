BASL Presidential candidate Rienzie meets Colombo lawyers

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - President’s Counsel Rienzie Arseculeratne, who has declared his candidacy for the post of President of the Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) for 2027/28, met lawyers practising at the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court today.

He began his career at the Attorney General’s Department, where he rose to the rank of Additional Solicitor General and headed the Criminal Division. He was appointed President’s Counsel in 1998. During his tenure at the Department, he also served as Director General of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption.

Following his retirement from the Department in 2003, he continued his active legal practice, specialising in criminal law and public law. He also served as Chairman of Hatton National Bank PLC from 2015 to 2018.
