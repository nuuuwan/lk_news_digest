Rush City – Phase 1 unveiled in Colombo

Sets new benchmark for affordable premium living in heart of Colombo

Rush Lanka Group, a trusted Sri Lankan property developer with over three decades of experience in creating quality residential developments, has unveiled Rush City – Phase 01, an exciting new residential project set to redefine affordable premium living in Colombo.

Bringing together contemporary architecture, premium lifestyle amenities and a prime location in Dematagoda, Colombo 9, just 300 metres from Baseline Road, Rush City offers the residents seamless connectivity to Colombo’s key business districts, leading schools, hospitals, transport networks and everyday conveniences.

Featuring over 400 thoughtfully designed modern apartments within a carefully planned community, the development blends smart space utilisation, contemporary design and everyday functionality to create an ideal urban lifestyle for young professionals, growing families, first-time homeowners and investors seeking long-term value in one of Colombo’s fastest-growing locations.

What truly sets Rush City apart is its promise of ‘premium living made affordable’. The development seamlessly combines the comfort, convenience and lifestyle amenities typically found in high-end residential communities, including landscaped gardens, recreational spaces, family-friendly facilities, fitness and wellness areas and vibrant community spaces, all thoughtfully designed to deliver an elevated living experience where the residents can enjoy an elevated standard of living without compromising on affordability.

Complementing its lifestyle offering are flexible payment options that make owning a home easier and more accessible for first-time buyers, young professionals, growing families and investors alike.

With its modern design, premium facilities, trusted developer reputation and accessible pricing model, Rush City provides investors with the opportunity to secure a valuable asset that has the potential to appreciate over time while attracting a strong demand from the future homeowners and tenants alike.

Rush Lanka Group Director Zaheer Ariff said, “Rush City reflects our vision of making quality urban living accessible to more Sri Lankans. This development combines affordability, convenience and modern living standards, creating lasting value for both homeowners and investors. With Rush City, we’re not just building apartments, we’re creating a vibrant community that will contribute to the future growth of Colombo while providing residents with a lifestyle they can truly enjoy.”

Established in 1992, Rush Lanka Group has spent more than three decades shaping Sri Lanka’s residential landscape, successfully delivering over 20 residential developments across Colombo. Today, the company continues to build homes across the affordable, semi-luxury and luxury segments, guided by a commitment to quality, innovation and customer satisfaction.

Its credentials, including ISO 9001:2015 certification, CIDA registration and recognition as a Great Place to Work, further reinforce its reputation as one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted property developers.

With the official launch of Phase 1, Rush City marks another significant milestone in Rush Lanka Group’s mission to reshape urban living in Sri Lanka. Offering premium living made affordable, the development presents a compelling opportunity for those looking to own a modern home in the heart of Colombo while securing a valuable long-term investment.
