Abans’ Cleantech recognised as category winner for common waste management

Recognised for excellence in environmental management, responsible resource recovery and advancing circularity through sustainable recycling practices

Cleantech (Pvt.) Ltd, Sri Lanka’s leading integrated waste management solutions provider, has been named the winner of the Common Waste Management, Processing/Recycling Plant Category at Presidential Environment Awards 2026, receiving the Silver award at the ceremony held recently.

Organised by the Central Environmental Authority, under the guidance of the Environment Ministry, the Presidential Environment Awards recognise organisations that demonstrate a strong commitment to environmental protection and responsible resource management.

For Cleantech, this achievement reflects the environmental discipline, operational excellence and continuous commitment to sustainability embedded in the everyday management of its recycling facility.

At Cleantech, responsible recycling extends beyond recovering valuable materials. It is about ensuring that every stage of the process is guided by environmental accountability, regulatory compliance and efficient resource utilisation. Systematic waste inventory management, accurate record-keeping and production traceability support transparent operations, responsible material handling and greater circularity throughout the recycling process.

The same commitment is reflected in the facility’s approach to conserving natural resources. Internal material reuse, rainwater harvesting and water reuse for operational purposes support more efficient resource consumption.

Planned machinery operations, preventive maintenance and optimised logistics further contribute to operational efficiency, energy management and reduced emissions associated with day-to-day activities. Together, these practices demonstrate how environmental responsibility can be translated into tangible action within an industrial setting.

Building on the Merit Trophy of Commendation received at the 2024 Presidential Environment Awards, this achievement marks another important milestone in Cleantech’s ongoing journey towards stronger environmental performance. It recognises the importance of maintaining high operational standards while continually improving the way waste is managed, resources are recovered and environmental impacts are addressed.
