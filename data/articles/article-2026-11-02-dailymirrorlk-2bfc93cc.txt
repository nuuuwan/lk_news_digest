Afghanistan overtake Sri Lanka in ODI rankings as Pakistan face fine

NOV.02 (Mirror Sports) - Afghanistan have climbed to sixth place in the latest ICC Men's ODI Team Rankings, achieving their highest-ever position and moving above Sri Lanka.

The rise follows a successful run of results, including a 4-0 ODI series victory over Ireland and an unbeaten campaign in the recent tri-series involving Zimbabwe and Bangladesh.

Meanwhile, Pakistan have been fined five per cent of their match fees for maintaining a slow over-rate in the ODI Tri-Series final against Sri Lanka in Lahore.

Pakistan were found to be one over short after time allowances were taken into consideration. Captain Shaheen Shah Afridi accepted the charge and the proposed sanction, avoiding a formal hearing.
