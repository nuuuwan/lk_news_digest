Certain individuals engaged in blackmailing MPs

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Certain Individuals are engaged in blackmailing Members of Parliament facing court cases in the guise of helping them to resolve such cases with the respective plaintiffs, MP Jagath Vithane alleged today.

“These individuals, who claim to be media activists, actually engage in blackmailing MPs, saying they will provide information to the media on the allegations they face if they don’t pay the amount of money demanded,” the MP told the Daily Mirror.

“One such person demanded Rs. 1 million from me and threatened to reveal to the media about a land sale which I was involved in 15 years ago. I sold a plot of land which was owned by me   and the buyers deposited money into my bank account. This so-called activist had told the buyer to say that I sold the land for an exorbitant amount and had promised to pay them a certain amount of money. The land sale was legal but this individual  has already spread a story saying I have been engaged in an illegal land deal. He demanded Rs. 1 million to stop spreading the fase story about me,” he added.
