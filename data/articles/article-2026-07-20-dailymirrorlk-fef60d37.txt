Ambeon seeks Rs.3.2bn in rights issue

Ambeon Capital PLC plans to raise up to Rs.3.2 billion through a rights issue, to strengthen its balance sheet and finance investments in high-growth sectors including technology, real estate and fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), the company said in a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE).

The board approved the capital raising on July 16, proposing to issue up to 100 million new ordinary voting shares to the existing shareholders, at Rs.32 per share, on the basis of one new share for every 10.22352863 shares held. The rights issue remains subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals.

Ambeon said the proceeds would be used to reduce the company’s gearing ratio while providing fresh equity capital for investments in future-focused businesses, particularly in technology, real estate and FMCG.

The company said the issue price of Rs.32 per share was considered “fair and reasonable” by the board, despite being above the market closing price of Rs.30.10 immediately before the board’s decision.

The directors said they had taken into account the recent market volatility, including trading patterns, following the West Asia conflict and expected the prices to normalise above the issue price.

Separately, Ambeon said it may undertake a private placement of up to 25 million ordinary voting shares, if the rights issue is fully subscribed and the eligible investors specifically apply for the placement shares. The placement will not proceed if the required subscriptions are not received.

The proposed rights issue is subject to the CSE granting approval in principle for the listing of the new shares and the shareholders approving the transaction by an ordinary resolution at a general meeting.
