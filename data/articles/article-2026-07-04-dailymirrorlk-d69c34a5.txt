People’s Bank selects 44 winners of ‘Vaasi Kotiyai 2026’

People’s Bank recently conducted the 22 weekly draws of its ‘People’s Remittance Vaasi Kotiyai 2026’ campaign, selecting 44 winners for the period from 1 January 2026 to 3 June 2026. The event was held at the Overseas Customer Services premises, Colombo 10.

Under this draw, two winners are selected each week, with each winner receiving a cash prize of Rs. 100,000. This special initiative is designed to appreciate the valuable contributions of Sri Lankan migrant workers.

The draw was attended by several senior officials of People’s Bank, including Deputy General Manager (Small and Medium Enterprises, Development and Micro Finance) S.N.B.M. Wickrama Narayana, Deputy General Manager (Risk Management) Anura Perera, Assistant General Manager (Overseas Customer Services) Buddhika Ranatungage, Assistant Chief Internal Auditor N.Y. Fernando, and Senior Manager (Overseas Customer Services) Thilini Perera. Also present was Senior Tax Officer of the Department of Inland Revenue (Western Province)  N.A.S.M. Napagoda.

The ‘People’s Remittance Vaasi Kotiyai 2026 – Send Directly and Win’ campaign aims to honour Sri Lankan expatriates who play a vital role in strengthening the national economy through their remittances while enhancing the quality of life of their families.
