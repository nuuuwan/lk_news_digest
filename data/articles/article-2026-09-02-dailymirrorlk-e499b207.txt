Polonnaruwa records Sri Lanka’s highest temperature at 37.9°C

Polonnaruwa recorded country’s highest maximum temperature of 37.9°C today (September 2), to the Meteorology Department said.

Several areas across the country also experienced temperatures above 35°C, with Monaragala recording 37.5°C, while Trincomalee and Pothuvil recorded 37.1°C each.

Mullaitivu recorded 37.0°C, while Vavuniya reached 36.6°C. Temperatures of 35.5°C in Mattala and 35.1°C in Anuradhapura were also reported.
