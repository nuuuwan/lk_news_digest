Xiteb’s xCommerz marks 3 years of int’l recognition

Three consecutive years of recognition at the Asia eCommerce Awards mark another international milestone for xCommerz, the proprietary enterprise digital commerce suite by Xiteb, reflecting sustained investment in research and development and contributions from software engineers and technology experts across Sri Lanka, Vietnam, Malaysia and India.

Developed through extensive research into international commerce practices, including those of Walmart, IKEA and Amazon, xCommerz supports single-vendor eCommerce and multi-vendor marketplaces, combining scalable enterprise architecture, flexible deployment models, advanced customisation and integration capabilities to address diverse business requirements.

The ninth Asia eCommerce Awards 2026 brought together leading technology providers, digital commerce specialists and multinational brands from Asia and Oceania, a region that includes Australia and New Zealand, with Xiteb shortlisted in three categories: Best eCommerce Innovation, Best eCommerce Solution and Best eCommerce Consultant.

Among the shortlisted competitors were Flipkart Commerce Cloud, AnyMind Group, DKSH, Intrepid Asia, Imagic Esolutions, Exabytes Network and iProspect Taiwan, with competing projects involving internationally recognised brands such as Nestlé, L’Oréal, Beiersdorf, Crocs, Unilever and Haleon.

A panel of 19 industry executives from organisations including Samsung Electronics, Philips, 3M, Beiersdorf, Electrolux, Lufthansa Group and Mondelēz International evaluated entries against criteria covering technological innovation, business effectiveness, strategic execution and commercial impact, reflecting the increasingly sophisticated expectations of the competition.

The awards are organised by Marketing-Interactive, published by Singapore-based Lighthouse Independent Media, part of Germany’s Forum Media Group, while Xiteb maintains its international corporate presence through Xiteb (Pvt) Ltd in Sri Lanka and Xiteb LLC in the United States.

Despite intense international competition, xCommerz has earned recognition for three consecutive years, including Silver awards in 2025 and bronze in 2026, reinforcing its international standing as a research-driven enterprise digital commerce suite, while Xiteb® prepares to participate in additional international awards recognising both enterprise eCommerce platforms and multi-vendor marketplace solutions.
