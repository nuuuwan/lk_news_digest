People’s Bank secures 4 C’wealth Business Excellence Awards

People’s Bank has secured four prestigious accolades at the fourth Commonwealth Business Excellence Awards 2026, earning international recognition for its excellence across banking, digital innovation, youth and student banking and SME services.

The bank was recognised with four awards: Best Banking Brand of the Year, Best Bank for Youth and Students, Best Bank in Digital Banking and Best Bank in SME Banking.

The four accolades reflect People’s Bank’s continued focus on strengthening its brand, accelerating digital transformation, supporting the financial aspirations of younger generations and empowering entrepreneurs and small and medium-sized enterprises.

People’s Bank Chairman Chandana Guniyangoda said, “People’s Bank has always remained committed to supporting Sri Lanka’s national economy and development by providing accessible, inclusive and innovative financial solutions to individuals, businesses and communities. These four international accolades are a strong recognition of that commitment and the dedicated efforts of our employees in delivering value to our customers and the nation.”

People’s Bank Chief Executive Officer and General Manager Clive Fonseka said, “These awards recognise the collective efforts of our employees to deliver innovative, accessible and customer-focused banking solutions. We will continue to invest in digital capabilities, youth and SME banking and other areas that contribute to sustainable economic growth.”

The recognition further strengthens People’s Bank’s growing portfolio of national and international accolades, reflecting its achievements across diverse areas of banking and financial services, including digital banking, financial inclusion, management excellence, sustainability, market reach and SME banking.

The four awards also highlight the bank’s contribution to the evolving banking landscape by combining its extensive physical presence with technology-driven financial services to meet the changing expectations of individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses.

With consolidated assets exceeding Rs.3.7 trillion, People’s Bank continues to play a pivotal role in supporting Sri Lanka’s economic development and advancing financial inclusion. The bank provides a comprehensive portfolio of financial products and services designed to meet the diverse requirements of individuals, businesses and institutions, supported by continued investment in digital banking technologies.
