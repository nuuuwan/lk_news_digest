Railway Controllers Union supports Sept. 17 strike

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The Railway Controllers Union has announced its support for the token strike scheduled to be launched by the Railway Joint Trade Union Alliance from midnight on September 17.

The union said the trade union action is based on two main demands: the immediate and unconditional withdrawal of calls for an apology made against the Secretary of the Railway Station Masters’ Association, and the removal of the current Railway General Manager from his post.

The union also expressed concern over what it described as the harsh conduct of the current Railway General Manager, saying it has created a negative attitude among its members.

It alleged that the General Manager had failed to take positive measures to improve the railway department and was attempting to introduce a “feudal-style” administration, causing difficulties for passengers and the Government while disregarding trade unions.

The union said it had therefore decided to support the proposed token strike. It warned that its members would remain off duty for 48 hours if an acceptable solution was not reached before midnight on September 17.

However, the union stressed that its objective was not to embarrass the Government.

It said that if the strike deviates from its stated objectives, including through political or other external influence, it would withdraw from the trade union action and return to work.
