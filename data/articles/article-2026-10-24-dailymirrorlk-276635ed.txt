Secondary market ends the week on quiet footing

The secondary market yesterday withstood yet another lackluster trading day characterized by constrained investor participation and low trading volumes. Investor sentiment appeared mixed prompting the yield curve to remain largely anchored.

Investor activity was yet again primarily clustered around mid-tenors with 15.05.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 trading at 11.35%, 11.50% and 11.55% respectively.

Further along the curve, 01.12.2031 changed hands at 11.85% and 01.11.2033 was seen trading at 12.30%. Finally, towards the belly end of the curve, 15.03.2035 traded at 12.53%.

On the external front, the LKR depreciated against the USD, standing at LKR 332.03/USD, compared to LKR 331.91/USD seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded marginally to LKR 384.90bn compared to LKR 380.98bn recorded previously.
