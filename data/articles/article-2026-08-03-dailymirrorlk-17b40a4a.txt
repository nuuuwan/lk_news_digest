Electricity tariffs to remain unchanged in third quarter

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) has decided to maintain existing electricity tariffs during the third quarter of 2026, despite an increase in electricity generation costs.

In a statement, the PUCSL said the decision was taken following a review of cost estimates submitted by the National System Operator Company.

The regulator said the increase in electricity supply costs was limited to 0.3 per cent, amounting to Rs. 417 million, and therefore did not warrant a revision of electricity tariffs. Accordingly, the tariff structure implemented during the second quarter will continue.

The PUCSL said a revenue surplus of Rs. 30,118 million recorded during the first quarter, which included additional coal-related generation costs later excluded from tariffs, has been carried forward. This leaves a remaining balance of Rs. 26,753 million.

When this balance is added to the estimated electricity supply cost of Rs. 159,274 million for the third quarter, the total cost is projected at Rs. 186,027 million, the PUCSL said.
