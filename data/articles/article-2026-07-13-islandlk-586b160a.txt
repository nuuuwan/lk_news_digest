One unforgettable night with Rajiv Sebastian…in Dubai

The city’s most awaited nostalgia night is around the corner, and it’s going to be loud, lively, and full of heart.

On Saturday, 25th July, the legendary Rajiv Sebastian teams up with the SEVEN NOTES Live Band Dubai for an electrifying ‘70’s Pop Hits & Baila Night’ at the Mercure Hotel Dubai, Barsha Heights.

In fact, Rajiv Sebastian is a name that needs no introduction. His voice, his stage presence, his connection with the crowd… it’s pure magic.

From 8:00 PM till 3:00 AM, it’s non-stop music, dancing, and memories that will take you straight to the golden era.

Yes, think classic 70’s pop hits that defined a generation; think timeless Baila favourites that make you get up from your seat before the first note even finishes; think legendary songs you grew up singing in the car, at weddings, and on radio — all performed live, with energy that doesn’t quit.

“Gather your friends, reserve your table, and relive the golden era,” says Niluk Uswaththa, of SEVEN NOTES fame.

“Bring your family and join us for an unforgettable evening! Everyone is warmly invited—young and old alike—to sit together, enjoy the music, create beautiful memories, and experience a night full of entertainment.”

By the way, the SEVEN NOTES Live Band is made up of seven passionate musicians, who work by day, doing full-time jobs in Dubai, and by night they are live, on stage, producing high quality music. They’ve shared the stage with many legendary artistes and backed some of the biggest names in the industry. For them, this isn’t just a gig. It’s love.

I’m told the entertainment scene, in Dubai, is almost back to normal, with a packed calendar of concerts, festivals and family events keeping the city alive through the summer of 2026.

However, one is only hoping that the current Middle East crsis wouldn’t suddenly escalate and change the setup in Dubai.

The SEVEN NOTES Live Band has a busy itinerary, as well. They are loaded with work during the weekends but, right now, their focus in on the Rajiv Sebastian show.
