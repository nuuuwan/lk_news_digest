Court of Appeal upholds four-year jail term for Gnanasara Thera

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal has upheld the four-year rigorous imprisonment sentence imposed on Bodu Bala Sena (BBS) General Secretary Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera for making remarks deemed insulting to Islam.

The court dismissed an appeal filed by the Thera seeking to be acquitted and released from the sentence imposed by the Colombo High Court in 2024.

The Attorney General had filed charges against him under Section 291 of the Penal Code over remarks made at a press conference held in Kirulapone on March 30, 2016, concerning the Kuragala temple.

He was accused of insulting the Islamic religion and making statements detrimental to harmony among different races and religious communities.

After finding him guilty, the Colombo High Court sentenced him to four years of rigorous imprisonment in 2024.
