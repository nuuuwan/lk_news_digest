Guniyangoda takes over as People’s Bank Chairman

Chandana Guniyangoda has assumed duties as Chairman of People’s Bank, becoming the first chairman of a state-owned licensed commercial bank to be appointed as an Independent Non-Executive Director under Sri Lanka’s strengthened banking governance framework.

Guniyangoda took office yesterday (25) at the bank’s head office, succeeding Prof. Narada Fernando.

His appointment comes as governance requirements for state-owned banks are being tightened under reforms linked to Sri Lanka’s IMF-supported programme. The framework, developed through reforms undertaken in consultation with the IMF and World Bank and reinforced by the Central Bank’s Banking Act Directions No. 05 of 2024, introduced stricter criteria for determining director independence and strengthened independent oversight at board level.

Guniyangoda has more than 28 years of professional experience, including 19 years in commercial banking, covering finance, strategic planning, business transformation, asset and liability management, risk management and regulatory reporting.

He previously served as Assistant Vice President for Finance, Strategic Planning and Business Support at NDB Bank PLC and as a Director of Development Holdings (Pvt.) Ltd., a joint venture between NDB Bank and the Export Development Board. He has also held positions at Sampath Bank PLC and Aitken Spence PLC. More recently, he was involved in restructuring institutional frameworks in the agriculture and plantation sectors under the World Bank-backed Agriculture Modernization Project.

Guniyangoda is a Fellow of CA Sri Lanka and the Institute of Certified Management Accountants of Sri Lanka. He holds an MBA in Banking and Finance from the Postgraduate Institute of Management, University of Sri Jayewardenepura, and a Master of Arts in Financial Economics from the University of Colombo.

He currently serves as Independent Chairman of People’s Insurance PLC and Group Chief Financial Officer of MG Group of Companies, while also holding several other board positions.
