MAS marks 20 years of Eco Go Beyond sustainable schools programme

MAS Holdings Co-Founder and Chairman Desamanya Mahesh Amalean presents an award to a student winner at 2014 Eco Go Beyond Awards Ceremony

MAS Holdings is commemorating 20 years of its Eco Go Beyond sustainable schools programme, celebrating two decades of sustainability education that has impacted over 377,000 students across 250 schools and engaged young people, educators, families and communities along the way.

Launched in 2006 in partnership with the Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Ministry, Eco Go Beyond was founded on the belief that education could play a powerful role in shaping a more sustainable future.

Working with the schools in the communities connected to MAS, the programme has sought to move beyond awareness alone, helping the students understand the sustainability challenges, explore their own role within them and turn learning into action.

Over the past two decades, Eco Go Beyond has evolved alongside the global sustainability agenda and the changing needs of the students, communities, country and the planet. Its approach has progressed from school-based learning to student-led projects that create a significant impact at community level, while building knowledge, confidence and agency among the next generation.

MAS Holdings CEO Suren Fernando stated, “Through the Eco Go Beyond sustainable schools programme, MAS remains committed to nurturing a generation of young people in Sri Lanka and beyond who have the knowledge, confidence and sense of responsibility to lead meaningful change.”

MAS Holdings Director Sustainable Business Amanthi Perera said, “Twenty years is a significant milestone but what stands out most is the collective commitment that has made it possible. Eco Go Beyond has endured because the decision-makers, educators, students, parents and communities have continued to believe that sustainability education matters and have rallied around that purpose.”
