Pitch Capital Properties wins triple honours at Global Laurel Excellence Awards 2026

Pitch Capital Properties (Pvt) Ltd has secured three accolades at the Global Laurel Excellence Awards 2026, strengthening its position as one of Sri Lanka’s leading real estate and land development companies.

The company received the Most Trusted Land Developer Award 2026, Outstanding Real Estate Investment Solutions Award 2026, and the Sri Lanka Property Excellence Award 2026 in recognition of its achievements in land development, innovative property investment solutions, and commitment to customer confidence.

Under the leadership of Managing Director Sugath Vithanage, Pitch Capital Properties has built a strong reputation for offering strategically located, legally secure, and value-driven property developments to local and overseas investors. The company continues to focus on transparency, professionalism, customer service, and long-term investment value.

A key area of the company’s growth has been its tourism-focused land portfolio. Pitch Capital Properties currently offers tourism land plots in prominent coastal destinations including Devundara, Hiriketiya, and Midigama, while its newest tourism land project has been introduced in Arugam Bay.

These destinations are among Sri Lanka’s recognised tourism hotspots and offer strong potential for boutique hotels, holiday villas, guesthouses, restaurants, surf-related ventures, and other hospitality investments. Through its carefully selected projects, the company aims to provide investors with access to high-potential locations that benefit from growing tourism demand and long-term land appreciation.

Commenting on the achievement, Managing Director Sugath Vithanage said the three awards reflect the trust placed in the company by customers, investors, business partners, and stakeholders. He stressed that the recognition is also a tribute to the dedication of the Pitch Capital Properties team and its continued commitment to maintaining high standards across every development.

The Global Laurel Excellence Awards recognise organisations and business leaders that demonstrate excellence in quality, innovation, customer service, and industry leadership. Winning three accolades at the 2026 edition marks an important milestone in the company’s growth journey.

Pitch Capital Properties plans to further expand its portfolio of tourism and residential developments while continuing to identify emerging investment destinations across Sri Lanka. Its latest entry into Arugam Bay forms part of this broader strategy to create secure and attractive real estate opportunities in locations with strong tourism and commercial potential.
