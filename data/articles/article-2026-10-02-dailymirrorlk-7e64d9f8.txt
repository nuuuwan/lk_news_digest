Drug-related arrests include 11 children aged 8-14

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Eleven children between the ages of eight and 14 were among 9,105 children and young people arrested in connection with drug-related offences across Sri Lanka during the past year, Sri Lanka Police said.

The arrests were made between October 30, 2025, and October 1, 2026, according to Police Media Spokesperson Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) F. U. Wootler.

He said 8,932 of those arrested were male youths, while 173 were young women.

ASP Wootler said it was particularly concerning that children as young as eight were among those taken into police custody for drug-related offences.
