Continuous flow of funds to bourse from equities and fixed income instruments

The CSE experienced a continuous flow of funds from equities to fixed income instruments amid higher interest rates, thus resulting in a notable increase in institutional investor participation yesterday.

Amid those developments, a mixed market reaction was noted in both indices. The All Share Price Index went up by 3.83 points, while the S and P SL20 rose by 0.71 points. Turnover stood at Rs 3.53 billion with seven crossings.

Top seven crossings were: O’nally Holdings, which crossed 47 million shares to the tune of Rs 2.2 billion and its shares traded at Rs 47.80, NTB 353,000 shares crossed for Rs 107 million; its shares traded at Rs 305, Dialog Axiata 1.7 million shares crossed for Rs 74 million; its shares traded at Rs 43.70, JKH 1.8 million shares crossed for Rs 35 million; its shares sold at Rs 19.50 Haycarb 200,000 shares crossed for Rs 34 million; its shares traded at Rs 171, Ambeon Capital one million shares crossed for Rs 30.5 million; its shares sold at Rs 30.50 and Sampath Bank 200,000 shares crossed for Rs 27 million; its shares sold at Rs 137.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Dialog Axiata Rs 56 million (1.3 million shares traded), Sampath Bank 56 million (175,000 shares traded), NTB Rs 53 million (175,000 shares traded), Haycarb Rs 46 million (272,000 shares traded), Hemas Rs 46 million (1.5 million shares traded), LOLC Rs 29 million (60000 shares traded) and Commercial Bank Rs 26 million (128,000 shares traded). During the day 91 million share volumes changed hands in 14490 transactions.

It is said that banking sector counters, especially NDB and Sampath Bank, performed well while the manufacturing sector, especially JKH and Hayleys, also performed well.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 336.50/75 to the US dollar in the spot market from Rs 336.35/50 the previous day, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was Rs 331.90 buying, 340.90 selling; the euro was Rs 375.9509 buying, Rs 389.8679 selling; and the pound was Rs 443.1789 buying,Rs 457.2245 selling.
