Early action needed to minimise monsoon flooding: PM

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya has highlighted the need for advance preparations to minimise flooding and its impact on Colombo and other affected districts during the upcoming rainy season.

The Prime Minister made the remarks while reviewing the progress of the Rathmalana-Kandawala Road and drainage rehabilitation project at the Western Province Council Auditorium on September 18.

She said authorities should take necessary measures well in advance, particularly in view of the rainy weather expected to affect the Western Province during the latter part of the year.

The discussion also focused on the progress of responsibilities assigned to the Western Provincial Council, Irrigation Department and Ratmalana Divisional Secretariat following decisions taken at an earlier committee meeting.

The current plans of the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation (SLLRDC), along with construction and maintenance work carried out by the Provincial Road Development Authority, were also reviewed.

The Rathmalana-Kandawala project commenced on September 10 and is scheduled for completion by June 30, 2027. The project includes Rs. 64 million for a bridge, Rs. 930 million for drainage rehabilitation and Rs. 115 million for road rehabilitation.

The Prime Minister commended the relevant institutions for working together to expedite the project and stressed that coordinated action was essential to address flooding effectively.
