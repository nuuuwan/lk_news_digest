104 new buses to join Western Province routes as police tighten bus lane enforcement

Colombo, Sep. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Metro Bus will deploy 104 newly introduced buses on seven routes in the Western Province from September 29, as authorities step up efforts to improve public transport and ease traffic congestion in Colombo.

Metro Bus Chairman S.M.U.P. Thilakaratne said all arrangements had been completed to commence operations with the new fleet, with the launch scheduled to be held under the patronage of the President.

The new buses will operate on seven routes centred around Colombo, with services aimed at helping passengers reach their destinations safely, comfortably and according to scheduled routes and times.

He said a key objective of the initiative was to reduce the number of private vehicles entering Colombo, particularly vehicles carrying only a single passenger.

Thilakaratne noted that many people currently commute to and from offices in private vehicles, often travelling alone, contributing to congestion during peak hours.

He said encouraging commuters to use public transport could reduce the number of vehicles entering Colombo and help ease traffic congestion.

Meanwhile, police will strictly enforce regulations governing bus priority lanes in Colombo and surrounding areas from September 29.

ASP Manoj Ranagala of the Police Traffic Control and Road Safety Division said the seven designated bus priority lanes in the Western Province would operate from 6 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 4 p.m. to 7 p.m.

During these hours, other vehicles will not be permitted to use or park in the designated lanes.

Ranagala said vehicles may enter the lanes only when necessary to avoid an emergency or accident, navigate severe congestion, or make a required turn.

He added that the lanes could be used by Metro buses as well as other passenger transport buses.

Police will conduct strict checks and take legal action against motorists who violate the bus priority lane regulations, he said.
