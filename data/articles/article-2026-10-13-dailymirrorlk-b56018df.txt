Expolanka Leisure Group celebrates double victory at SATA 2026

Expolanka Leisure Group announced a landmark achievement at the South Asian Travel Awards (SATA) 2026, with Classic Travel Sri Lanka winning the Gold Award for the third time and Travel Classic Bangladesh securing the Silver Award for the second consecutive year in the prestigious Leading Travel Agency (Outbound) category.

This accomplishment is particularly significant as both award-winning companies belong to the Expolanka Leisure Group, demonstrating the strength of the Group’s travel brands across multiple markets in South Asia. Securing both the Gold and Silver positions in the same category is a rare achievement and a powerful testament to the Group’s commitment to excellence, innovation, and customer-centric service delivery.

Commenting on the achievement, Sabry Bahaudeen, Chief Commercial Officer said, “The fact that both winners represent the same Group makes this recognition even more special. It reflects the dedication of our teams, the trust of our customers, and the strength of our brands across the region. We sincerely thank our customers, partners, suppliers, and stakeholders for their continued confidence and support. This achievement motivates us to continue innovating and delivering exceptional travel experiences across every market we serve.”

As Expolanka Leisure looks towards the future, the Group remains committed to pursuing excellence, strengthening its regional footprint, and continuing to set new benchmarks within the travel industry.
