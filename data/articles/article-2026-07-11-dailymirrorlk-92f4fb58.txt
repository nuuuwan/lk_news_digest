Uswatte Biscuits partners with Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage’s Olympic journey

Sri Lanka’s record-breaking javelin thrower, Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage, was honoured by Uswatte Biscuits Pvt Ltd, his official sponsor, at a special felicitation ceremony held on 22 June at Vinro Reception Hall, Kennanthudawa.

Hosted in Rumesh’s hometown, the event brought together family members, relatives, friends, neighbours, and members of the local community who have supported and encouraged him throughout his journey, creating a heartfelt celebration among the people who have stood by him since childhood.

The event celebrated a series of historic performances that have elevated Sri Lanka’s standing in international athletics and brought together sports officials, community leaders, supporters, and well-wishers to recognise Rumesh’s remarkable rise on the global stage.

Rumesh’s breakthrough season has seen him achieve unprecedented success. At the 2026 Rome Diamond League, he recorded a throw of 92.62 metres, setting a new Sri Lankan record while producing the world’s leading javelin throw of the season. The achievement also placed him among the sport’s all-time greats, ranking eighth in history at the time.

His impressive run continued with victory at the Ostrava Golden Spike athletics meet in the Czech Republic, where he secured first place with a throw of 86.57 metres against a strong international field. He further strengthened his credentials with another winning performance at the Diamond League meet in Doha, Qatar, contributing to his rise to World No. 2 in the global rankings.

These achievements have not only brought immense pride to Sri Lanka but have also demonstrated the country’s ability to produce athletes capable of competing with the very best in the world.

The felicitation ceremony also reflected the commitment of Uswatte Biscuits to recognising and supporting Sri Lankan excellence. Speaking on the occasion, a young entrepreneur and the Managing Director of Uswatte Biscuits Pvt Ltd, Shankar Perera, congratulated Rumesh on his remarkable achievements and reaffirmed the company’s commitment to supporting individuals who bring honour and recognition to Sri Lanka on the global stage.

Uswatte Biscuits, one of Sri Lanka’s renowned biscuit manufacturers, has been a trusted household name for over 70 years. Guided by a commitment to quality, innovation, and affordability, the company continues to strengthen its presence both locally and internationally while contributing meaningfully to initiatives that inspire and uplift communities. The partnership with Rumesh reflects Uswatte Biscuits’ enduring belief in recognising and supporting Sri Lankan talent capable of achieving excellence on the global stage.

In recognition of his historic accomplishments on the international stage, Uswatte Biscuits awarded a cash prize of Rs. 2.5 million to Rumesh. His coach, Tony Prasanna, was also honoured with a cash award of Rs. 500,000. The occasion also marked the beginning of a long-term partnership, with Uswatte Biscuits pledging its continued support towards Rumesh’s journey to the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles. The company reaffirmed its commitment to standing alongside the athlete as he pursues his ultimate goal of winning an Olympic medal for Sri Lanka.
