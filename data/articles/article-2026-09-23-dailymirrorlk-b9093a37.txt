CAA cracks down on baby diaper imports without importer details

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Two businesses have been fined after being found distributing imported baby diapers without mandatory information identifying the registered importer.

The action followed complaints received by the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) concerning the quality of baby diapers, including reports of diapers tearing and deteriorating during use.

As part of an island-wide investigation and raid programme, CAA officials inspected establishments in Dehiwala and Dematagoda and found imported diapers being distributed without the required name, address, telephone number or website details of the registered importer.

The CAA said the distribution of such products was in violation of Order No. 85. The stocks were seized and produced as case material before the Colombo Chief Magistrate's Court.

The two cases were heard on September 23, with both defendants pleading guilty.

Global Commodity Solution (Pvt) Ltd of Campbell Place, Dehiwala, was fined Rs.100,000, while Hallmark Industry of Ketawalamulla Place, Dematagoda, Colombo 09, was fined Rs.10,000.

The CAA reminded consumers to check importer details and the MRP on packages before purchasing baby diapers and other products that come into direct contact with babies' skin.

It also warned traders and distributors to ensure that imported products comply with mandatory labelling requirements.

Complaints regarding market irregularities can be made to the CAA on 1977 during office hours.
