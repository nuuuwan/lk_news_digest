patpat.lk wins Gold at BestWeb.LK Awards 2026

patpat.lk, Sri Lanka’s digital marketplace, has been awarded Gold for Best Mobile User Experience – Advertising and Marketing at the BestWeb.LK Awards 2026, organised by LK Domain Registry.

The recognition reflects patpat.lk’s continued focus on delivering a simple, intuitive and engaging digital experience for users across Sri Lanka.

The platform connects buyers and sellers across a wide range of categories, including vehicles, property, electronics and more, enabling users to discover products, explore options and connect with sellers through a convenient digital marketplace.

The BestWeb.LK Awards recognise excellence in Sri Lanka’s digital landscape, celebrating websites and digital experiences that demonstrate creativity, innovation, functionality and quality.

Further strengthening its performance at the awards, patpat.lk was also recognised with a Silver Award for Best Advertising and Marketing Website, highlighting the strength of its overall digital presence and marketing efforts. Commenting on the achievement, Amritha Allahakkon, Head of Products at patpat.lk, said, “We are proud to receive the Gold Award for Best Mobile User Experience. This recognition is a reflection of the team’s continued focus on making the platform simpler and more convenient for our users.”
