CID Director Shani Abeysekara promoted to Deputy Inspector General of Police

Colombo, August 7 (Daily Mirror) – Director of the Criminal Investigation Department (CID), Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) Shani Abeysekara, has been promoted to the rank of Deputy Inspector General (DIG) of Police.

The promotion has been made effective retrospectively from August 25, 2020.

The appointment was approved by the National Police Commission, which decided to elevate Abeysekara to the post of DIG in recognition of the relevant promotion process.

Abeysekara currently serves as the Director of the CID and has been involved in overseeing several high-profile criminal investigations during his police career.
