Ceylinco Life rewards sales success with global adventures

Ceylinco Life has once again turned exceptional sales performance into memorable experiences, rewarding 80 of its sales force with overseas tours to Vietnam and China as recognition for outstanding achievements in the Company’s sales competitions.

The two groups, comprising 62 sales achievers who travelled to Vietnam and 18 who journeyed to China, earned their places through strong performancesin 2025.

The Vietnam group experienced a vibrant blend of culture, history and scenic adventure, beginning in Hanoi with visits to the Ho Chi Minh Complex, One Pillar Pagoda and Tran Quoc Pagoda. A group cyclo tour around Hoan Kiem Lake added a distinctly local dimension to their exploration of the Vietnamese capital.

For the 18 achievers who travelled to China, the tour offered an immersion in the history, culture and attractions of Beijing. Their itinerary included visits to Tiananmen Square and the Forbidden City, followed by the Summer Palace and a traditional boat ride on Kunming Lake.

A highlight of the tour was the magnificent Mutianyu section of the Great Wall of China, with the convenience of a round-trip cable car ride. The group also enjoyed a spectacular acrobatic show at Chaoyang Theatre and visited the Beijing Zoo’s Panda Garden, where they had the opportunity to see the much-loved animals during their most active hours.
