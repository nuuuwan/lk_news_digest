Police officer arrested over alleged anti-government social media posts

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - A police officer attached to the Embilipitiya Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) Office has been arrested by the Central Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) for allegedly circulating social media posts intended to cause embarrassment to the President and the government.

The suspect was taken into custody today (31) during a specialised operation conducted by the Cyber Crime Investigation Division of the CCIB.

According to police, the probe was initiated following information received by the Inspector General of Police (IGP) regarding the alleged online activity. The IGP subsequently instructed the Central Crime Investigation Bureau to conduct a formal inquiry into the matter.

The arrested officer is scheduled to be produced before the Fort Magistrate's Court today.
