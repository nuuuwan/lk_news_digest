BOC opens largest debenture issue on Nov. 3

Bank of Ceylon (BOC) is set to create another milestone in the country’s capital market with the launch of Sri Lanka’s largest-ever listed debenture issue, with a maximum issue size of Rs. 30 billion.

The Bank proposes to initially raise Rs.10 billion, with an option to increase the issue to Rs.20 billion in the event of an oversubscription of the initial tranche and a further option to increase the total issue size up to Rs.30 billion, making it the largest listed debenture issue undertaken in Sri Lanka.

The proposed issue comprises Basel III compliant, Tier 2, Listed, Rated, Unsecured, Subordinated, Redeemable debentures with a non-viability write-down feature. The debentures carry an issue rating of AA (Stable) from Lanka Rating Agency, while BOC’s issuer rating stands at AA-(lka)/Stable from Fitch Ratings Lanka Ltd.

The debentures will be offered with five-year and seven-year maturities, providing with medium to long-term fixed income investment opportunities. The issue is confined to qualified investors and the minimum subscription for qualified investors is Rs.10,000, while individual investors are required to apply for at least Rs.5 million.  The debentures are offered in four different types to meet diverse requirements of qualified investors. The Fixed coupon rate of the five-year debenture will be 12.75 percent per annum while the floating coupon rate will be 1.50 percent above the applicable twelve-month Treasury Bill rate. The Fixed coupon rate of the seven-year debenture will be 13.00 percent per annum while the floating coupon rate will be 1.75 percent above the applicable twelve-month Treasury Bill rate. BOC’s largest debenture issue comes at a significant juncture for the Sri Lankan economy, at a time when the country is prioritizing sustainable, investment-led and inclusive economic growth. Against this backdrop, a stronger capital base will further enhance BOC’s capacity to mobilize and channel funds towards productive sectors of the economy, thereby supporting Sri Lanka’s next phase of economic development.

This landmark issue therefore represents more than a significant capital-raising exercise for the Bank. BOC, as the country’s leading state-owned commercial bank, remains well positioned to play a pivotal role in financing businesses, facilitating investment and supporting the broader development priorities of the country.

The Legal Department of BOC acts as the lawyers to the issue, while the Corporate Branch of the Bank acts as the bankers to the issue. The Investment Banking Division will also continue to act as the Registrar to the proposed issue, while Hatton National Bank PLC will be the trustee to the issue.

The Bank is opening the subscription list of the BASEL III Debenture Issue at 9.30 a.m. on 3rd November 2026 and will remain open for 14 market days unless the Bank decides to close it earlier based on subscription levels.

Bank of Ceylon invites eligible local and foreign investors to participate in this landmark issue and be a part of the Bank’s continuing journey of financial strength and contribution to Sri Lanka’s economic development.
