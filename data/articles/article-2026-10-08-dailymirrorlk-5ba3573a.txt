Commotion in Parliament after Minister says Opposition MP is out of his mind

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - A commotion broke out in Parliament today after Minister of Environment Dhammika Patabendi said Opposition MP Hector Appuharmy is out of his mind.

The commotion broke out when Opposition MPs rose in protest against the Minister.

Deputy Speaker Rizwy Salih who was in the chair advised the Minister not to make defamatory statements.

Leader of the House Bimal Ratnayake, who responded, requested the Deputy Speaker not to allow MPs to raise unnecessary supplementary questions during oral question sessions.
