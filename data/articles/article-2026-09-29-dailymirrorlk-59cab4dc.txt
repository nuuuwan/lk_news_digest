Further evidence in Lalith-Kugan case set for Nov. 27

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Jaffna Magistrate’s Court today fixed November 27 at 1.30 p.m. for the recording of further evidence from former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa in connection with the case relating to the disappearance of political activists Lalith Kumar Weeraraj and Kugan Muruganandhan in Jaffna in 2011.

Rajapaksa gave evidence today from around 1.45 p.m. to 3.00 p.m. before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court via video link, with the evidence being recorded by the Jaffna Magistrate’s Court.
