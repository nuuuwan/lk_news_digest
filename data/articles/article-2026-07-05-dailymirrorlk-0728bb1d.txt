A/L 2025 re-examination results released

Colombo, July 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Examinations Department has released the results of the General Certificate of Education (Advanced Level) Examination 2025 re-examination today (5).

Commissioner General of Examinations A.K.S. Indika Kumari Liyanage said candidates can check their results by visiting the official websites and entering either their examination index number or National Identity Card (NIC) number.

Candidates with inquiries regarding their results have been advised to contact the School Examinations Organisation and Results Branch of the Department through the 1911 hotline or via the following telephone and fax numbers: 011-2784208, 011-2784537, 011-2785922 and 011-2784422.
