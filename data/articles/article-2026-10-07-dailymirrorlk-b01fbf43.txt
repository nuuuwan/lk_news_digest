Two accused in Wanathavilluwa explosives case sentenced to 10 years’ rigorous imprisonment

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Two accused who pleaded guilty in a case filed by the Attorney General against six suspects, including Abu Hanifa Mohamed Musif alias Abu Dahida, over allegations of illegally possessing a firearm and explosives at the Lastery Estate in Wanathavilluwa, Puttalam, in 2018, in connection with Muslim extremists including Zahran Hashim, were sentenced to 10 years’ rigorous imprisonment by the Colombo High Court today.

The case was taken up before a three-member High Court Trial-at-Bar comprising Judges Lal Ranasinha Bandara, Rashmi Singappuli and Nayomi Wickramasekara.

The two accused, Abu Hanifa Mohamed Musif and Amir Hamza Mohamed Hamas, were sentenced to 10 years’ rigorous imprisonment after they pleaded guilty to the charges.

The Court further ordered that the period the two accused had already spent in remand custody and in prison in connection with the incident be deducted from their sentences.

The Criminal Investigation Department (CID) had, in 2018, seized a stock of nitrate believed to be an explosive substance, 12 live rounds of ammunition, a firearm and stocks of nitric acid and urea, which could be used in the manufacture of explosives.
