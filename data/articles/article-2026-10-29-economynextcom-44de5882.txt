Sri Lanka rupee 332.67/75 to US dollar spot, bond yields steady to lower

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s rupee was quoted at 332.67/75 to the US dollar spot on Thursday, while bond yields were quoted steady to lower, dealers said.

A bond maturing on 01.08.2030 was quoted flat at 11.55/60 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2030 was quoted at 11.60/62 percent, down from 11.63/65 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.02.2031 was quoted at 11.70/75 percent, down from 11.72/75 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.03.2031 was quoted flat at 11.75/80 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.12.2031 was quoted at 11.85/90 percent, down from 11.88/93 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2034 was quoted at 12.50/55 percent, up from 12.40/50 percent.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was 328.05 buying, 337.05 selling; the euro was 363.3861 buying, 377.1669 selling; pound was 436.3483 buying, 450.4567 selling.

On the Colombo Stock Exchange the All Share Price Index was up 0.60 percent, or 120.00 points, at 20,090; while the S&P SL20 was up 0.66 percent, 37.37 points, at 5,729. (Colombo/Oct29/2026)
