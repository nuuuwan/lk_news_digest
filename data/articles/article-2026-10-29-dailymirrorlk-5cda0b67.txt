Mumbai Indians part ways with bowling coach Lasith Malinga

rediff - Mumbai Indians on Thursday announced the departure of Lasith Malinga, Paras Mhambrey and Carl Hopkinson from the men’s team coaching setup. MI and Sri Lankan legend, Malinga served as bowling coach at the franchise from IPL 2024 to 2026.

'Lasith Malinga has been an integral part of the Mumbai Indians family and one of the most iconic figures in the franchise’s history. From spearheading MI’s bowling attack and playing a defining role in four IPL title triumphs and the 2011 Champions League T20 victory, to returning as a mentor and coach, Lasith’s journey with MI has been truly special. His passion, competitive spirit and commitment to the team have inspired generations of players. His legacy will forever remain an important part of Mumbai Indians,' Mumbai Indians wrote in a statement.

Mhambrey, who was assistant coach for MI Indians for four years, including during their IPL 2013 and Champions League T20 title-winning campaigns, returned to the franchise as a bowling coach for IPL 2025 and IPL 2026.

'Paras Mhambrey has shared a long and valued association with Mumbai Indians, first from 2010 to 2017 and subsequently returning in 2024. Across both spells, Paras has brought immense experience, dedication and a deep understanding of the MI culture. His contribution to developing and nurturing the team’s bowling talent, along with the relationships he has built over the years, has been greatly appreciated.'

'Carl Hopkinson joined Mumbai Indians ahead of the 2025 season, bringing valuable international experience from his time with the England men’s team. His professionalism, expertise and commitment to improving the team’s fielding standards have been valued throughout his time with the franchise,' a statement from MI read.
