Bank of Ceylon (BOC), “Bankers to the Nation”, had the honour of welcoming Mr. Zhang Hui, Vice Chairman, Executive Director and President of Bank of China, during his official visit to Sri Lanka on 16 October 2026. This significant engagement marks an important step towards strengthening the longstanding friendship between Sri Lanka and China, fostering greater financial cooperation, international trade and investment, and creating new opportunities for cross-border partnerships and shared economic prosperity.
