Kapruka scraps used-goods marketplace plan

E-commerce service Kapruka Holdings PLC said it has decided not to proceed with a proposed used-goods marketplace and will seek shareholder approval to redirect Rs. 50 million in unutilised IPO proceeds towards working capital after concluding the venture is not commercially viable under current market conditions.

In a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange dated August 31, Kapruka said its management had reassessed the proposed marketplace against prevailing market conditions, expected customer demand, competition, regulatory requirements, operating needs and expected financial returns.

The assessment concluded that launching and operating the personal cargo collection and delivery marketplace is “not commercially viable under the current market condition,” prompting management to recommend abandoning the project.

The Rs. 50 million represents unutilised funds originally allocated under Kapruka’s December 2021 IPO prospectus for the development of a used-goods marketplace.

The original plan, subsequently approved by shareholders at the company’s Annual General Meeting in September 2022, envisaged launching a marketplace platform for personal cargo collection and delivery from the Sri Lankan diaspora together with a door-to-door tracking system.

Kapruka said the unutilised IPO proceeds would instead be allocated towards the company’s working capital requirements, a move it expects would strengthen its financial position.

“The reallocation will enhance the Company’s financial stability and provide a robust foundation for its future initiatives,” the company said.

Any utilisation of the Rs. 50 million will remain subject to prior approval from Kapruka’s board of directors.

The proposed change in the use of IPO proceeds will also require shareholder approval in accordance with Colombo Stock Exchange listing rules and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka Act.

Kapruka said it intends to seek shareholder approval for the variation at its upcoming Annual General Meeting.
