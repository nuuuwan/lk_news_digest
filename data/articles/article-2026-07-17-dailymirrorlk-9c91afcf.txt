Brandix donates ECG machine to Mirigama hospital

Brandix helped strengthen the critical healthcare services for the people of Mirigama and surrounding areas with the donation of an ECG machine to the emergency care unit of the Mirigama Base Hospital last week.

The lack of an electrocardiogram (ECG) machine had slowed operations at the base hospital, with the patients and residents of the area forced to travel great distances and pay to obtain this vital test. Following the donation by Brandix, the hospital authorities said the Mirigama Base Hospital can significantly enhance its critical care services and save valuable lives.

Brandix, Sri Lanka’s premier apparel solutions provider, is a leading proponent of the country’s healthcare services for over two decades. The company is also Sri Lanka’s largest corporate blood donor for over 14 consecutive years, empowering the lives and communities islandwide.

Senior General Manager ESG and Compliance Nirmal Perera said, “over the years Brandix has cared for its associates and communities through meaningful programmes to enhance social, health and education services through its Model Village Programme. Our contribution to Mirigama Base Hospital is yet another extension of this commitment to enrich the lives and healthcare in the Gampaha district.”
