38 schools closed due to adverse weather

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 38 schools in four provinces will remain closed today (21) due to the prevailing adverse weather conditions, the Education Ministry said.

Accordingly, 35 schools in the Central Province and one school each in the Sabaragamuwa, Southern and Western Provinces will not operate today.

Provincial education authorities have been instructed to take necessary measures regarding the operation of schools in view of the prevailing weather conditions.
