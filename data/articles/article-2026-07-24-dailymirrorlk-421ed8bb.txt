Week ends in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Hatton National Bank, Ceylinco Holdings and Ceylon Grain Elevators with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.4 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 edged down. Crossings were witnessed in United Motors Lanka, Hatton National Bank and Commercial Bank accounting for 68.2% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Hayleys, LOLC Finance and Ceylon Grain Elevators whilst retail interest was noted in Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & Finance Rights, Beruwala Resorts and Browns Investments.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 1.1% and 1.0% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 2.59 Bn.

Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to United Motors Lanka) whilst the sector index gained 0.14%. The share price of United Motors Lanka decreased by Rs 0.10 (0.37%) to close at Rs 26.90.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.23%. The share price of Hatton National Bank lost Rs 3.00 (0.77%) to close at Rs 385.00. The share price of Commercial Bank moved down by Rs 0.50 (0.25%) to close at Rs 201.50.

Softlogic Life Insurance and Hayleys were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Softlogic Life Insurance recorded a loss of Rs 1.20 (1.47%) to close at Rs 80.20. The share price of Hayleys declined by Rs 0.50 (0.22%) to close at Rs 225.00.
