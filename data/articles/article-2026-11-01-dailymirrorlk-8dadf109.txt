Man crushed to death while hunting armadillo in Koslanda cave

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - A 30-year-old man who had gone hunting an armadillo that had entered a cave in the Diyahitithenna Forest in Koslanda was found dead inside the cave this morning (1), police said.

The deceased was identified as R. Ramesh, a resident of Uwamawelagama in the Diyahitithenna area of Koslanda.

Police said the victim, a married man who worked as a daily-paid labourer, had gone into the forest yesterday (31) afternoon with a 17-year-old youth living nearby to hunt the animal.

While attempting to remove rocks from inside the cave, a large rock had suddenly broken loose and crushed him, police said.

Only about two feet of the victim's body, including his feet, were visible outside the cave, while the rest remained trapped inside.

Following information received about the incident, Koslanda Police officers recovered the body from the cave. A police guard has since been deployed at the location.

Police said the victim's wife, whom he had married recently, was in the Ratnapura area at the time of the incident.

Further investigations are being conducted by the Koslanda Police under the instructions of senior police officers in the Uva Province and Badulla District.
