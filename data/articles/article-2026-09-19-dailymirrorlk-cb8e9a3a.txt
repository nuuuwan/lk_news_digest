Yields dip on back of modest buying

Modest buying dominated market sentiment yesterday, prompting a downward adjustment of the yield curve.

Market activity was primarily clustered around mid-tenures. The trading volumes appeared thin, reflecting limited overall activity.

In the 2030 segment, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded between 11.35 percent to 11.25 percent. Moving ahead, 01.02.2031 traded between 11.40 percent-11.33 percent while 01.10.2032 and 15.12.2032 both changed hands from 11.65 percent to 11.60 percent. Finally, 01.11.2033 was seen trading between 11.90 percent and 11.82 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.332.13/US dollar, compared to Rs.331.89/US dollar seen earlier.

Market liquidity in the banking system expanded marginally to Rs.324.79 billion, compared to Rs.317.84 billion recorded previously.
