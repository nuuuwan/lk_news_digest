SriLankan Airlines suspends Dammam flights until October 18

Colombo, Oct. 12 (Daily Mirror) - SriLankan Airlines has temporarily suspended all flights between Dammam in Saudi Arabia and Colombo until October 18 due to recent security-related developments and the prevailing security situation in Saudi Arabia.

In a statement issued on October 12, the airline said the safety of passengers and crew remained its priority.

Flight operations will resume once the situation is confirmed to be suitable for commercial air travel, the airline said.

Passengers seeking further information can contact the airline on 1979 within Sri Lanka or +94 11 777 1979 for international calls. They can also contact the airline via WhatsApp on +94 74 444 1979 for messages only, or seek assistance from their travel agents.
