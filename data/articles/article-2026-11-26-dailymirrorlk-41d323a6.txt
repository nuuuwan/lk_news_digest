Global Vinyl recognised at PRISL Annual Awards

Global Vinyl Ltd. has been awarded the Bronze Award for ‘Innovative Product of the Year’ for its SPC flooring at the recently concluded Plastics and Rubber Institute of Sri Lanka (PRISL) Annual Awards held at BMICH.

The recognition marks an important milestone for the company and highlights its continued commitment to innovation, product development and advancing locally manufactured flooring solutions. Global Vinyl’s SPC flooring brings together modern technology, durability and design, offering a high-performance solution for contemporary residential and commercial spaces.

With over 25 years of experience in the flooring industry, Global Vinyl has consistently evolved with changing market demands, pioneering new flooring technologies such as Luxury Vinyl Tiles (LVT) and Stone Polimer Composite (SPC) flooring and strengthening Sri Lanka’s manufacturing capabilities.

Commening on the award, the Wasantha Heenatigala (General Manager, Global Vinyl Ltd) stated “Winning the Bronze Award for Innovative Product of the Year at the PRISL Annual Awards is a proud moment for Global Vinyl. It is a recognition of our commitment to innovation, continuous improvement and our belief that world-class flooring solutions can be developed and manufactured right here in Sri Lanka.

This achievement is a testament to the dedication of our team, whose efforts in product development, manufacturing and quality have helped us continually push boundaries. We see this award not just as a milestone, but as motivation to keep innovating, investing in technology and delivering products that meet international standards and evolving customer expectations.

We remain committed to strengthening Sri Lankan manufacturing and demonstrating that locally manufactured products can compete on quality, innovation and performance.”
