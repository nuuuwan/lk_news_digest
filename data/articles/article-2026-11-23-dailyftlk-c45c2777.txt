Sri Lanka and Lebanon explore opportunities to expand bilateral trade

Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to Lebanon Anura Withanage (left) with Lebanon’s Economy and Trade Minister Amer Bisat

The Ambassador of Sri Lanka to Lebanon, Anura Withanage, met with Lebanon’s Economy and Trade Minister Amer Bisat, on 17 November 2026 to discuss ways of strengthening commercial relations between the two countries.

The discussions focused on expanding bilateral trade, diversifying traded products and addressing challenges faced by exporters and importers.

Ambassador Withanage drew the Minister’s attention to the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) concluded between the Chamber of Commerce, Industry and Agriculture of Beirut and Mount Lebanon and the Ceylon Chamber of Commerce. He noted that the agreement provided a framework for connecting businesses in both countries and identifying opportunities for trade and commercial partnerships.

The Ambassador also briefed the Minister on the Embassy’s recent promotional activities in Lebanon, including the ‘Discover Sri Lanka’ tourism promotion event, a business forum at the Beirut Chamber featuring a presentation on Sri Lanka Expo 2027 and a Ceylon tea tasting, an international tea-tasting event, and Sri Lanka’s participation in the Nations Day Festival. These initiatives introduced Lebanese businesses to a wider range of Sri Lankan products and services.

Withanage also highlighted the potential for Sri Lankan exports beyond Ceylon Tea to enter the Lebanese market.

Minister Bisat identified Lebanese olive oil and fresh fruit as products that could be introduced to buyers in Sri Lanka’s tourism and hospitality sectors. He expressed interest in participating in future trade promotion events and holding virtual meetings with representatives of the Sri Lankan business community.

The Minister also invited the Embassy to explore the possibility of organising a Sri Lankan business delegation to Lebanon. The two sides discussed the possibility of a reciprocal visit by a Lebanese business delegation to Sri Lanka, enabling companies from both countries to meet prospective partners and explore market opportunities.
