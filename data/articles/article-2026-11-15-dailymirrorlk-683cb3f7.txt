O/L, A/L students must attend school: PM

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister and Education Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya has said that students preparing for the GCE Ordinary Level (O/L) and Advanced Level (A/L) examinations must attend school regularly, urging parents to ensure their children do not miss school.

Speaking at the 80th anniversary celebrations of Akkaraipattu Muslim National School today (15), she said every child should receive 13 years of education.

She expressed concern over students who stop attending school regularly when they reach O/L and A/L classes.

“Every child must attend school. Every child should receive 13 years of education. I particularly urge parents to ensure that their children attend school,” she said.

The Prime Minister said the Government had allocated Rs. 749 billion for education under the 2027 Budget, including Rs. 562.4 billion for school education, describing it as the highest allocation in Sri Lanka’s history.

She said the new curriculum introduced for Grade 1 in 2026 would be extended to Grades 2 and 6 in 2027.

The reforms aim to give students more opportunities to learn technology, vocational education and practical life skills.

Dr. Amarasuriya said education should not be limited to passing examinations but should also help students develop leadership, teamwork and other skills needed to face a changing world.

She said that every child has the right to quality education regardless of their background, adding that the Government must ensure equal educational opportunities for all.
