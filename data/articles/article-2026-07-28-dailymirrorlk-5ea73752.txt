Multiple deaths in human-elephant conflict

Colombo, July 28 (Daily Mirror) - A Civil defence force member and a Chena cultivator of Wijeyapura in Ethimale were trampled to death by a wild elephant on Sunday night (26) which resulted in the assault on wildlife officers.

The deceased were D.M.Sugath Nayananda (42) of Kotiyagala, a Civil Security Department Constable and Liyanage Ranjith Shantha (47) of Wijeyapura. The former was father of two children.

A farmer Ranjith Shantha was on his way to his Chena cultivation when a wild elephant that roamed into the Chena trampled him to death at the scene. The Wildlife officers who rushed to the scene launched an operation to scare away the herd to a forest reserve, and the Civil Security Constable Nayanananda attached to the Wildlife Department had been hit by a marauding jumbo.

The people who ran riot accusing the wildlife officers of failing to drive away the herd roaming into the villages and cultivated land assaulted them, causing injuries to four wildlife officers. The injured were admitted to Siyambalanduwa Base Hospital.

OIC Ethimale Police Wasanthya Ananda and Wildlife Range Officer, Monaragala Harsha Ruwan Kumara are conducting investigations.

Meanwhile, the death of three individuals in the human-elephant conflict in the Anuradhapura District was reported from Anuradhapura and Kekirawa yesterday.

The victims were Usoof Ayubkhan (52), who was on his way back after taking treatment at the Jaffna hospital with his son had been trampled to death by a wild elephant frequenting near Kekirawa railway station. However, his son had run away and escaped unhurt.

The others were J.G.Shriyalatha (64) of Ketambuwawa in Nuwara Eliya on her way back after visiting her sister at Thalawa, and Karunaratne Mudiyanselage Dissanayake Bandara (48) of Srawasthipura. The latter had been hit by a jumbo when he attempted to scare away a rogue elephant that entered his home garden.
