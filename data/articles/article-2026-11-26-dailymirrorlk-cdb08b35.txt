Pelwatte Dairy shines with four winners at SLIM National Sales Awards 2026

From left: U.A. Channa Damayantha Bandara - Regional Sales Manager - (Merit Winner, Regional Sales Manager Category), I.A. Damith Niroshan Perera - Sales Executive - (Silver Winner, Sales Executive Category - FMCG - Food), Amila Prasanga Masachchi - Area Sales Manager - (Merit Winner, Territory Manager Category - FMCG - Food), Adheesha Malith Welivita - Manager - Sales Operations - (Merit Winner, Other Sales Support Staff - Manager & Above Category)

Pelwatte Dairy Industries Limited celebrates a noteworthy achievement at the SLIM National Sales Awards (NSA) 2026, with four members of its sales team receiving recognition, securing one Silver Award and three Merit Awards across four categories.

Pelwatte’s four awards acknowledge individual performance across executive sales, territory management, sales support and regional management, among more than 1,500 sales professionals representing organisations across Sri Lanka. The National Sales Awards recognise sales professionals from a variety of industries and is organised by the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM).  I.A. Damith Niroshan Perera was awarded the Silver Award in the Sales Executive Category (FMCG – Food), while Amila Prasanga Masachchi was recognised as a Merit Winner in the Territory Manager Category (FMCG – Food). Adheesha Malith Welivita received a Merit Award in the Other Sales Support Staff – Manager & Above Category, and U.A. Channa Damayantha Bandara was also recognised as a Merit Winner in the Regional Sales Manager Category.
