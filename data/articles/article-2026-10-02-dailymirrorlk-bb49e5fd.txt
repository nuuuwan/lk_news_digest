Musk reignites Meta rivalry with ‘Instagram is for girls’ remark

Hindustan Times - Elon Musk has reignited his long-running rivalry with Meta and its social media platforms after saying that “Instagram is for girls”, sparking debate over gender stereotypes online.

Musk made the comment on October 1 in response to a post by venture capitalist Geiger Capital, who said he had “officially deleted Instagram” and described the platform as an “inherently female app”.

The comment was widely shared on X, attracting thousands of reactions and a range of responses from users.

The remark has added another episode to Musk’s ongoing rivalry with Meta, which owns Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp.

The billionaire has previously exchanged criticism with Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and has frequently used X to comment on rival social media platforms.

Musk’s latest comment, however, focused on the perceived gender identity of Instagram’s user base, prompting discussion about stereotypes surrounding social media use.
