Nepal and Bangladesh to host ICC U-19 Women’s T20 World Cup 2027

Desh Sanchar - The schedule for the ICC U-19 Women’s T20 World Cup 2027 has been announced. The International Cricket Council (ICC) released the schedule on Wednesday.

The tournament, jointly hosted by Nepal and Bangladesh, will take place from January 21 to February 6. A total of 16 teams will compete in the event. The World Cup will feature 41 matches, with games held in Chattogram and Sylhet in Bangladesh, and in Kathmandu, Nepal. Nepal will play its first match against Ireland on January 22. Matches in Nepal will be conducted at the Tribhuvan International Cricket Ground and the Upper Mulpani International Cricket Ground.
