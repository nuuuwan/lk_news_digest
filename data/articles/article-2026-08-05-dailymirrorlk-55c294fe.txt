ATG achieves EcoVadis Platinum Rating

ATG Group of Companies has been awarded the prestigious EcoVadis Platinum Rating, placing the organisation among the top one percent of companies worldwide assessed for sustainability and responsible business practices.

This recognition reflects ATG’s continued commitment to environmental stewardship, ethical governance, responsible manufacturing and social responsibility across its global operations.

EcoVadis is one of the world’s most trusted providers of business sustainability ratings, evaluating over 130,000 companies globally across four key pillars: environment, labour and human rights, ethics and sustainable procurement.

The Platinum rating represents the highest level of recognition awarded by EcoVadis and is reserved for organisations demonstrating exceptional sustainability performance.

For ATG, this achievement validates decades of investment in building a responsible manufacturing ecosystem where sustainability is embedded into every stage of the business.

ATG’s vertically integrated manufacturing model enables complete control; ATG maintains complete control of its manufacturing process, from raw material selection to the production of finished gloves. This approach enables the company to consistently strengthen its environmental, social and governance performance while upholding the high standards of innovation, quality, comfort and protection expected by the customers worldwide.

ATG Group Sustainable Development Manager Sanjeewa Sandaruwan said, “Achieving EcoVadis Platinum status is a proud milestone for ATG. More importantly, it reflects the commitment of our people and the culture we have built around responsible business practices and continuous improvement.”
