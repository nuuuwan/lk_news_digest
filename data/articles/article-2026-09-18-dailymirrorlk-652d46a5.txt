Mixed secondary market sentiment, with buying edging ahead

Secondary market activity remained mixed yesterday, with both buying and selling interest observed throughout the session.

However, buying activity was marginally stronger, resulting in a slight downward movement in the belly of the yield curve. Moderate volumes were recorded during the day, although overall market activity remained relatively subdued.

Among the traded maturities, in the short-tenor segment, both the 15.10.2028 and 15.12.2028 maturities traded within the range of 10.90%-10.75%, while the 15.09.2029 maturity traded at 11.05%.

In the 2030 segment, the 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities traded between 11.45% to 11.35%. Further along the curve, the 01.02.2031 maturity traded within a range of 11.65% -11.40%.

At the longer end, the 15.12.2032 maturity traded between 11.80% and 11.70%, while the 01.11.2033 maturity traded between 12.00% and 11.95%. Finally, the 15.09.2034 maturity traded within a range of 12.20%-12.17%.

On the external front, the LKR depreciated against the USD, standing at LKR 331.89/USD, compared to LKR 330.18/USD seen earlier.

Market liquidity in the banking system contracted   317.84 bn compared to LKR 336.44bn recorded previously.
