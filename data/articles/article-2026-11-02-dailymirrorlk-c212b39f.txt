Overseas Realty launches Havelock City Residences

Overseas Realty (Ceylon) PLC announced the launch of Havelock City Residences, the fifth and final phase of Havelock City, bringing the vision of Colombo’s most prestigious integrated mixed-use development closer to completion.

The official launch of Havelock City Residences was celebrated at a glamorous launch event held on October 29, 2026, at Hilton Colombo, attended by an exclusive gathering of invited guests.

Demonstrating strong market confidence even before its official launch, 50 percent of the apartments had already been pre-sold, reflecting the continued appeal of Havelock City’s distinctive proposition – a premium residential environment combining space, greenery, connectivity and an integrated urban lifestyle.

Set across 18 acres, Havelock City is distinguished by seven acres of beautifully landscaped gardens—the largest single enclosed green space in Sri Lanka.

Designed by award-winning global design firm P&T Group, Singapore, Havelock City Residences offers spacious two, three and four-bedroom apartments, alongside expansive penthouses, thoughtfully planned to maximise natural light, ventilation, privacy and views.

The residents benefit from an extensive range of recreation facilities and lifestyle amenities including landscaped walking and jogging paths, gymnasiums, squash courts, banquet halls and dedicated spaces for socialising and relaxation. The curated assisted living concept further enhances the development, promoting accessibility, independence, safety, security and peace of mind through every stage of life.

With Havelock City Mall and Mireka Office Tower integrated within the development, the residents enjoy convenient access to shopping, dining, entertainment and everyday essentials. This seamless integration of residential, commercial and lifestyle experiences makes Havelock City a distinctive address for those seeking the convenience of city living without compromising on space and tranquility.

Overseas Realty Director and Group Chief Executive Officer Pravir Samarasinghe stated, “The launch of Havelock City Residences marks a defining milestone in our journey to realise the vision of Havelock City as Colombo’s most sought-after integrated living destinations. As the fifth and final phase, Havelock City Residences brings this vision to its culmination, offering the homeowners a rare combination of space, greenery, connectivity and contemporary luxury within an established community. We are proud to bring this final chapter to market and look forward to welcoming more elite residents to the Havelock City community.”

Developed by Overseas Realty Ceylon, the company behind Havelock City and the iconic World Trade Centre Colombo, Havelock City Residences builds on a legacy of creating landmark real estate developments in Sri Lanka. The project is professionally managed by Realty Management Services, a wholly-owned subsidiary, providing the ongoing property management and hassle-free rental and resale support services.

As the final phase of Havelock City, the development presents a golden opportunity and a last chance for the homeowners and investors to become part of an exclusive residential community, with the benefits of an integrated lifestyle.
