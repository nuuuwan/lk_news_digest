CCTV cameras to be installed at all police stations

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - Plans are underway to install CCTV cameras at every police station across the country, with procurement activities for the project already in progress, Police Media Spokesperson Assistant Superintendent of Police F. U. Wootler said.

He said steps would also be taken to monitor police stations using the CCTV footage.

The project is expected to introduce a system of surveillance across police stations islandwide.
