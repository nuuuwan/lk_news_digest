100 days of Chemmani Excavations – Human Skeletal Remains Surpass 500

Excavation work at the Chemmani mass grave site reached its 100th day today, with the number of human skeletal remains recovered from the site surpassing 500.

Accordingly, although the court had granted permission to conduct the third phase of excavations at the Chemmani mass grave for 56 days, today marked the 46th day of the current phase of excavation.

On the 100th day of excavations, eight new sets of human skeletal remains were identified, bringing the total number of human skeletal remains identified so far to 507.

Of these, 501 sets of skeletal remains have been fully excavated and recovered to date.
