Five children among 6 killed, in Mumbai chawl collapse

THE HINDU - Five children among six persons were killed in a house collapse in Mumbai’s Mankhurd area amid heavy rains on Sunday (July 5, 2026) night, officials said.

According to the BMC, when two to three tenements forming part of the three-storey structure collapsed at Janata Nagar chawl at 8.30 p.m. on Sunday (July 5, 2026).

Teams of the Mumbai Fire Brigade, police, BMC’s Ward Staff and ambulance were pressed into service. The injured were rescued and moved to Rajawadi Hospital and Shatabdi Hospital.

According to the information provided by Shatabdi Hospital, five persons brought in were declared dead.

They included a 32-year-old woman and four children aged between two and 14 years. According to the information given by Rajawadi Hospital, the condition of a 24-year-old man is stable.

Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis has expressed deep grief over the tragic incident. Stating that the government shares the grief of the bereaved families, he has announced financial assistance of ₹5 lakh to the next of kin of the deceased.
