SLINEX-26 ends with joint naval drills

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The 13th edition of the Sri Lanka–India naval exercise, SLINEX-26, concluded successfully in Visakhapatnam on September 21, 2026, strengthening maritime cooperation and operational coordination between the two navies.

The bilateral exercise was conducted under the operational supervision of the Indian Navy’s Eastern Naval Command, with the objective of enhancing interoperability, maritime cooperation and mutual understanding between the Sri Lanka Navy and Indian Navy.

The Sri Lanka Navy participated with the Offshore Patrol Vessel SLNS Sindurala, while the Indian Navy deployed the Anti-submarine Warfare Corvette INS Kavaratti and Fleet Tanker INS Jyoti.

The exercise was conducted in two phases — harbour and sea. During the harbour phase in Visakhapatnam, naval personnel participated in professional interactions, cross-deck ship visits, subject matter expert exchanges and sporting activities.

The sea phase, conducted off India’s eastern coast, featured a series of complex naval drills aimed at testing and improving tactical interoperability. The exercises included live surface firing, tactical manoeuvring, anti-piracy operations and cross-deck helicopter manoeuvres.
