Cabinet approves coal supply contracts for Lakvijaya Power Plant

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the award of contracts to two companies to supply coal to the Lakvijaya Power Plant in Norochcholai for the 2026/2027 period.

The approval follows a Cabinet decision on March 30, 2026, to procure 2.28 million metric tonnes of coal, with a ±10% variation, to meet the power plant’s operational requirements.

According to the Government Information Department, the decision to source coal from two suppliers was taken to minimise the risk of supply disruptions and ensure uninterrupted electricity generation and energy security.

Following an international competitive bidding process, nine tenders were received and evaluated by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC).

Based on the committee’s recommendations, Aditya Birla Global Trading (Singapore) Pte. Ltd. has been awarded 70% of the requirement, amounting to 1.596 million metric tonnes (±10%), while India’s Mohit Minerals Limited will supply the remaining 30%, or 684,000 metric tonnes (±10%).
