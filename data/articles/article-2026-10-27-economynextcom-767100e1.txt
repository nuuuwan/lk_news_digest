Sri Lanka rupee at 332.25/35 to US dollar spot, bond yields steady

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s rupee was quoted flat at 332.25/35 to the US dollar in the spot market on Tuesday, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

A bond maturing on 15.09.2029 was quoted at 11.07/15 percent, down from 11.08/15 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.08.2030 was quoted at 11.50/58 percent, up from 11.45/55 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2030 was quoted at 11.55/63 percent, up from 11.55/60 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.02.2031 was quoted at 11.75/79 percent, up from 11.70/75 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.12.2031 was quoted at 11.82/90 percent, up from 11.80/90 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.12.2032 was quoted at 12.00/15 percent, up from 12.00/10 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.11.2033 was quoted flat at 12.30/40 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.06.2034 was quoted at flat 12.40/50 percent.

An auction of 100,000 million rupees Treasury bills is due on October 28.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was 327.80 buying, 336.80 selling; the euro was 363.4893 buying, 377.2701 selling; pound was 436.1313 buying, 450.2397 selling.

On the Colombo Stock Exchange, the All Share Price Index was up 0.18 percent, or 36.15 points, at 19,676; while the S&P SL20 was up 0.22 percent, 12.50 points, at 5,614. (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
