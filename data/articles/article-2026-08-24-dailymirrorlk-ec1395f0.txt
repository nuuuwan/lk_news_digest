Missing Badulla Hospital specialist doctor found dead in Mahiyanganaya

Colombo, Aug. 24 (Daily Mirror) - The body of a specialist doctor attached to the Badulla Teaching Hospital, who had been reported missing, was found today (24) on a rock in the Mapakadawewa area of Mahiyanganaya.

The doctor, an anesthesiologist, had reported for duty at the hospital yesterday morning (23) before disappearing later in the day.

Police investigations, including the examination of CCTV footage, revealed that the doctor’s car had traveled towards Mahiyanganaya through Meegahakiula.

The vehicle was later found abandoned last night near the 17th-milepost along the Badulla–Mahiyanganaya canal road in the Mapakadawewa area.

Following this discovery, a joint search operation involving the Police, Crime Scene Investigation officers and the Sri Lanka Army was launched this morning. The operation led to the discovery of the doctor’s body.

Police are conducting further investigations into the circumstances surrounding the death.
