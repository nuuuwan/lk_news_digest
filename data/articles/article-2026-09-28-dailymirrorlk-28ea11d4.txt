WYLD Global named as Google Premier Partner

WYLD Global has been officially recognised as a Google Premier Partner for 2026, an upgrade from the Google Partner status the agency has held since 2025.

The Premier Partner status is reserved for the top 3 percent of the Google Partner agencies worldwide and WYLD is one of only six agencies in Sri Lanka to hold it at the time of writing.

The recognition is awarded to the agencies that consistently meet high standards in campaign management and optimisation.

WYLD met every eligibility benchmark: a 70 percent+ ad optimisation score, more than US $ 10,000 in managed ad spend over the past 90 days and a team of Google Ads-certified strategists driving the work.

“WYLD is in a phase of regional expansion, breaking into key markets and scaling the way we deliver performance. Earning the Google Premier Partner status reinforces that we’re ready to operate at a higher level, with more ambitious clients and bigger briefs,” said Chief Executive Officer Banura Sooriyapperuma.

“Social media captures interest. Google captures intent. Someone typing a search is already halfway through the door and knowing how to meet them there is what performance marketing is really about. This recognition reflects the standard we hold ourselves to,” said Performance Marketing Head Bashana Wijekoon.

For the clients, the milestone means stronger budget efficiency through continuous optimisation, greater confidence in media investment and more room to focus on strategy while WYLD manages performance and delivery. Internally, it raises the bar the team holds itself to and strengthens WYLD’s footing to take on larger briefs across markets.

Over the past year, WYLD has run campaigns across multiple industries and markets, helping brands improve acquisition efficiency, scale revenue and get more from their media investment.

The agency thanked the clients across Sri Lanka, the UAE, UK, Australia, New Zealand, the Maldives, Seychelles, Malta, India, Saudi Arabia and the United States for their continued trust and credited the achievement to Wijekoon, along with Krishan Chathuranga, Amith Jayasinghe, Mohammed Sarjun and the wider performance marketing team.
