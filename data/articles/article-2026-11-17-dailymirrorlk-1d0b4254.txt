Namal Rajapaksa among 41 accused in Hambantota protest case

Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Hambantota High Court ordered yesterday that indictments be served on 41 individuals, including Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, over alleged damage to public property during a protest held in Hambantota against the proposed sale of Mattala International Airport and the Hambantota International Port to the private sector.

High Court Judge Duminda Mudunkotuwa issued the order when the case was taken up before the Hambantota High Court.

The 35th accused, Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, who is currently in remand custody, was not produced before the Hambantota High Court as he had to appear in another case before the Colombo High Court.

Parliamentarian D.V. Chanaka, former MPs Upali Kodikara and Prasanna Ranaweera, former Provincial Councillor Ajith Prasanna and Sampath Athukorala were among those who appeared before the court. All the other accused, except three individuals, were also present.

The court officials distributed the indictments separately to the accused after they were lined up inside the courtroom.

The judge ordered that all 41 accused be released on bail, subject to two sureties of Rs. 500,000 each and a cash bail of Rs. 10,000 per person. The court also imposed a travel ban on all the accused, preventing them from travelling overseas.

The case relates to a protest held on or around October 6, 2017, during which protesters blocked the road leading to the Hambantota International Port and obstructed access to the Indian High Commission and its official residence.

The accused face charges including violating a court order, causing damage to public and private property and unlawful assembly.

The Hambantota Police had initially arrested the suspects and filed the case before the Hambantota Magistrate's Court under case number B.R. 1088. Following instructions from the Attorney General, the case was subsequently referred to the Hambantota High Court. An order to serve indictments on the accused was issued on November 2.

The case was taken up under High Court case number HAM/HC/PP/00032/2026.

Meanwhile, the lawyer appearing for the 35th accused, Chamira de Silva, instructed by President's Counsel Sampath Mendis, requested the court to issue directions on how Namal Rajapaksa should be produced at the next hearing.

The lawyer requested that Rajapaksa be produced through prison authorities if he remained in remand custody by the next hearing date, or otherwise be allowed to appear before court through the normal procedure.

The judge accordingly ordered that Rajapaksa be produced through prison authorities if he remained in remand custody on the next hearing date. Otherwise, he should appear before court through his lawyers.

The judge also directed that copies of the National Identity Cards and certificates from the respective Grama Niladharis of the 6th, 12th and 29th accused be submitted to court at a later date.
