750 balls, two days: Mackay test makes history

AUG.23 (Mirror Sports) - Australia’s victory at Mackay has gone into the record books as one of the shortest completed Tests, with the match lasting just 750 deliveries across two days.

The result lifts Australia to 80% in the World Test Championship, strengthening their position at the top of the standings. South Africa remain second on 75%.

Bangladesh, meanwhile, slipped to 55.56% after suffering their third defeat in six matches, while India sit on 53.33%.

Attention now turns to Colombo, where India and Sri Lanka are contesting the second Test of their series.
