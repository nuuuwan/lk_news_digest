Evolution Auto brings Ather’s Overtone series to Sri Lankan market

Evolution Auto Pvt Ltd has launched the Ather Overtone series in Sri Lanka, bringing the premium electric scooter to local riders. The Overtone introduces two new features, Infinite Cruise and Magic Twist, alongside three colour options.

The Ather Overtone is built for riders who want performance without compromise. Infinite Cruise lets riders hold a set speed on longer stretches, while Magic Twist adds a new layer of control to the throttle response. Together, the features mark a step up for Ather’s line-up in the Sri Lankan market.

The scooter is available in three colours: Space Grey, Lunar Grey and Still White. Each finish is designed to appeal to riders who see their scooter as an extension of their personal style, without straying from Ather’s clean, minimal design language.

The striking new designs are crafted to turn heads, while advanced features such as Infinite Cruiseand Magic Twist bring a new level of intelligence, convenience and sophistication to the riding experience, technologies traditionally associated with premium cars. More than just a new product, this series represents Ather’s commitment to premium mobility: exceptional design, thoughtful technology and an uncompromising focus on the details that elevate every ride. We want every Ather to feel not just like an electric scooter, but like a truly premium product that people aspire to own.” –Zahran Ziyawudeen - CEO - Evolution Auto Pvt Ltd.

Evolution Auto Pvt Ltd is the authorised distributor of Ather Energy electric scooters in Sri Lanka. Evolution Auto operates in the New Energy Vehicle sector, partnering with global electric vehicle manufacturers to bring their products to the Sri Lankan market, backed by sales and service support.
