Mahapola scholarship arrears to be paid in December

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - All eligible Mahapola scholarship recipients for the 2024/2025 academic year will receive their October 2026 scholarship allowance today (November 19), while arrangements are being made to settle outstanding scholarship payments in December.

The Lalith Athulathmudali Mahapola Higher Education Scholarship Trust Fund said the payments would be made to all eligible students selected under the Mahapola Scholarship Programme for the academic year.

The Trust Fund said it had taken steps to streamline the scholarship payment process to ensure students receive their financial assistance regularly and without unnecessary delays.

Meanwhile, the monthly Mahapola scholarship allowance has been increased by 100% over the past two years, from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 10,000.

The increase reflects the Government's commitment to strengthening financial assistance for university students and reducing the financial burden on them and their families.

The Trust Fund said it would continue to improve its financial and administrative arrangements to ensure the efficient, transparent and uninterrupted payment of scholarships.

It added that regular payments would help students meet their educational and essential expenses while allowing them to focus on their academic activities.
