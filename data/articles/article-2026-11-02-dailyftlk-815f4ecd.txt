PM leaves for CHOGM in Antigua and Barbuda

Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya left for Antigua and Barbuda on Saturday to attend the 2026 Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) in St. John’s. During the visit, which runs until 6 November, the Prime Minister will engage with Commonwealth leaders and take part in discussions on matters of shared interest to member states, the Prime Minister’s Media Division said.
