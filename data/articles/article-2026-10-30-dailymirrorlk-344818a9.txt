Ryanair responds to Kumar Sangakkara’s allegation

Colombo, October 30 (Daily Mirror) Ryanair has responded to Sri Lankan cricket legend Kumar Sangakkara, who accused the airline, following an incident involving the airline’s gate staff at Dublin Airport.

According to the Daily Mail, Ryanair said Sangakkara had refused to provide the required visa documentation when requested by a gate agent conducting mandatory immigration checks.

In a statement to Daily Mail Sport, the airline said the passenger had declined to provide the necessary documentation when requested.

“Following completion of these checks and verification of this passenger’s eligibility to travel, this passenger travelled on the scheduled flight from Dublin to Bristol on October 28,” the airline said.

The incident sparked widespread debate after Sangakkara accused Ryanair staff of displaying “discriminatory” and “threatening” behaviour towards him and his family at Dublin Airport.

Sangakkara, widely regarded as one of the greatest wicketkeeper-batters in cricket history, took to social media on Wednesday afternoon to express his anger over the incident.

In a post on X, he wrote: “Has anyone experienced discriminatory gate personnel @Ryanair in Dublin?”
