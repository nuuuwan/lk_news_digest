Cordelia Empress arrives in Colombo with 1,600 tourists

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - The passenger cruise liner Cordelia Empress arrived at the Port of Colombo this morning (26), carrying 1,600 tourists and 588 crew members.

During the vessel's visit, around 1,800 people from the ship are scheduled to undertake shore excursions to Colombo, the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage and Galle.

The Cordelia Empress, which primarily caters to the Indian and South Asian tourism markets, operates domestic voyages within India as well as regional international cruises.

The 210-metre-long vessel has 11 decks and 796 passenger cabins.

As part of the 2026–2027 cruise tourism season, the vessel is scheduled to make three more calls at the Port of Colombo next month, on December 17, 24 and 31.
