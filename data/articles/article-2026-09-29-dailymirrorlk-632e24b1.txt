Cabinet approves over Rs. 40 Bn fuel relief programme

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved a three-month fuel relief programme aimed at cushioning consumers and the local economy from the impact of rising global oil prices amid ongoing conflicts in the Middle East.

Under the programme, financial assistance will be provided to maintain affordable retail prices for Auto Diesel and Industrial Diesel, preventing international market price fluctuations from being passed directly on to consumers.

The Government has allocated a maximum of Rs. 15 billion for October, Rs. 13.5 billion for November and Rs. 12.15 billion for December. The total allocation for the three-month programme is therefore Rs. 40.65 billion.

The proposal was submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.
