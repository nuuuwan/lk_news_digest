Social media groups allegedly used to trade sexual content

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Groups operating on Facebook and other social media platforms are allegedly being used to trade sexual videos and private photographs of women and children, raising serious concerns over online exploitation, the Women and Child Affairs Ministry said.

The Ministry said information about the alleged activities had been received in recent days, while social media activists have also published posts and videos drawing public attention to the issue.

Following preliminary inquiries, the Ministry has referred relevant information and findings to the Sri Lanka Police Bureau for the Prevention of Abuse of Children and Women and the Computer Crime Investigation Division for further investigation.

During examinations of the relevant groups, authorities reportedly found that fake accounts were being used to attempt to sell photographs and videos of people known to the individuals operating the accounts.

In some cases, attempts had allegedly been made to distribute and sell photographs and videos of female members of their own families.

The Ministry also said many of the groups were reportedly being operated from overseas, making the investigation and prevention of such activities more challenging.

The Ministry urged parents and guardians to closely monitor children's use of social media and pay attention to the people with whom they communicate and interact online.

It warned that women and children could become victims of online exploitation even within their own homes and called on parents and guardians to take appropriate measures to protect children.

Anyone who believes a family member has become a victim of such an online operation, or is experiencing harmful or addictive behaviour associated with such activities, can seek assistance through the 1938 and 1929 toll-free helplines.

The Ministry said the helplines provide access to psychosocial counselling, legal assistance and other support services.

The Ministry said it is giving the matter its full attention and taking urgent measures to prevent women and children from becoming victims of online exploitation and to take appropriate action against those involved.
