Strict action against bribery and corruption, regardless of status: MP Asoka Ranwala

Colombo, Nov. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Former Speaker Asoka Ranwala (MP) has warned that strict action will be taken against anyone found accepting bribes or engaging in corrupt practices, regardless of their position or status, while assuring foreign investors of a secure and transparent investment environment in Sri Lanka.

He made these remarks while attending the opening ceremony of a leading hotel in Balapitiya, where he urged Japanese and other foreign investors to invest in Sri Lanka without fear or hesitation.

Ranwala said the present government had taken the necessary legal measures to prevent irregularities and protect foreign investors from corrupt practices that had reportedly occurred under previous administrations.
