Laugfs Supermarkets opens 45th outlet in Ekala

Laugfs Supermarkets has further strengthened its islandwide retail presence with the opening of its 45th outlet at No. 131, Minuwangoda Road, Ekala, Ja-Ela.

The new outlet operates 24 hours a day and offers the customers a comprehensive range of products, complemented by bakery and hot food options.

Strategically located in an area surrounded by an industrial zone and residential developments including Niwasipura and Millennium City, the outlet is well positioned to serve the growing customer base in the area and cater to the varying shopping needs throughout the day.

The opening ceremony was attended by the group chairman, group executive vice chairman and acting group managing director and group executive director of Laugfs Holdings and chief operating officer of Laugfs Supermarkets.

Sector Managing Director and CEO Retail Niroshan De Silva said, “The opening of our 45th outlet marks another important milestone in our retail expansion journey and reflects the dedication and teamwork of our people. Our continued expansion is guided by a clear focus on identifying locations with strong potential and strengthening our presence in communities where we can create greater value for the customers. The Ekala outlet further enhances our network in the Ja-Ela area and supports our commitment to making quality retail experiences more accessible and convenient to the customers.”

With the opening of the Ekala outlet, Laugfs Supermarkets continues to build on its growing retail footprint across Sri Lanka. The expansion reflects the group’s continued investment in strategically positioned outlets, while reinforcing its commitment to delivering convenience, quality and greater choice to the customers across the country.
