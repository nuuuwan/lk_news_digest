Beettu office opened in Damana to help reduce human-elephant conflict

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - A new Beettu Office has been opened under the Damana Site to strengthen efforts to reduce human-elephant conflict in the Siyambalanduwa area.

The Govidupura Beettu Office was opened on July 28 to improve the delivery of wildlife-related services to residents of Kotagoda, Weragoda, Nawgala and Pallegama, where human-elephant conflict remains a major concern.

The office is strategically located near the elephant migration corridor linking Lahugala National Park and Gal Oya National Park, as well as the Bakmitiyawa-Thimbirigolla Forest Department Reserve.

Wildlife officials said around 200 wild elephants regularly use this migration route, making the new office an important base for responding to incidents and implementing measures to minimise crop damage and protect human lives.

The establishment of the office is expected to improve rapid response capabilities and support long-term efforts to mitigate human-elephant conflict in the region.
