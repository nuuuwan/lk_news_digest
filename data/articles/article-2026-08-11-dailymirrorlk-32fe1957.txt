Nissanka Senadhipathi appears before Bribery Commission

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Nissanka Senadhipathi, former Chairman of Avant-Garde Maritime Services, appeared before the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) this morning.

Senadhipathi appeared before the Commission to provide a statement in connection with an ongoing investigation into the controversial Krrish transaction.
