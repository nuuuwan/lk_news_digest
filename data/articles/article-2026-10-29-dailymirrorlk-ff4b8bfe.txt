International Handicrafts Fair opens in Colombo

An international handicrafts fair featuring artisans from Sri Lanka, India, Nepal, Indonesia, Bangladesh and Thailand will be held from October 29 to November 1 at Havelock City Mall. Organised by the National Crafts Council under the Ministry of Industries and Entrepreneurship Development, in collaboration with the High Commission of India, the event aims to promote knowledge sharing, traditional craftsmanship, technical skills and market opportunities among local and international artisans. The opening ceremony will be held under the patronage of Deputy Minister of Industries and Entrepreneurship Development Chathuranga Abeysinghe, Ministry Secretary Thilaka Jayasundara and Indian High Commissioner Santosh Jha.
