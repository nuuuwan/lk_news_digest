CEAT Kelani accelerates partner power at ‘Autobahn 3.0’ Excellence Awards

CEAT’s Top 10 dealers in Sri Lanka with the Chairman, members of the board of directors and representatives of the senior management of CEAT Kelani Holdings

CEAT Kelani Holdings recently celebrated the strength, resilience and success of its country-wide partner network at its Partner Excellence Awards 2026.

Centred on the bold theme ‘Autobahn 3.0’, the event reflected the company’s strategic focus on advanced mobility, superior product performance and global quality standards.

Inspired by Germany’s legendary high-speed highway, the theme symbolised the precision, control and comfort that define CEAT’s German-engineered car radials, while underscoring the company’s transformation towards next-generation tyre technology across categories.

The celebration hosted 125 top dealers, 16 growth dealers, seven two- and three-wheeler distributors and their families, and two global partners of CEAT Kelani alongside the company’s sales, marketing and export teams, in what was described as a memorable showcase of collaboration and excellence.

Addressing the gathering, CEAT Kelani Chairman Chanaka de Silva paid tribute to the pivotal role played by dealers in sustaining the brand’s growth in a challenging year.

“We all know the past year brought its fair share of unpredictable weather and global chaos,” he said. “Yet, every time a customer walked into your shops looking for safety, reliability, and honest advice, you supported CEAT. You are the ones who build trust in our name, day in and day out. Our growth over this past year has been a direct result of your hard work and your dedication,” he stressed,

The awards ceremony recognised outstanding performance across multiple categories, with Abdeen Tyre Service – Akkaraipattu securing the CEAT Best Dealer Award 2025/26, followed by U & H Wheel Service (Pvt) Ltd – Colombo 2 as first runner-up and Sandeew Auto Enterprises Pvt Ltd – Colombo 14 as second runner-up.

Category excellence awards were also presented, with Sandeew Auto Enterprises Pvt Ltd. winning the Radial Category, while U & H Wheel Service (Pvt) Ltd. dominated both the Truck and Light Truck categories.
