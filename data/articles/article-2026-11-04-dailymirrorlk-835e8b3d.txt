CSE rally strengthens as ASPI climbs above 20,500

The Colombo Stock Exchange recorded a strong broad-based rally on 4 November 2026, with both benchmark indices advancing sharply as buying momentum strengthened across the market.

The ASPI surged 421.48 points, or 2.09%, to close at 20,541.12, while the S&P SL20 gained 112.52 points, or 1.97%, to 5,835.09. The ASPI remained positive through most of the session and approached 20,600 before mild late profit-taking trimmed gains.

Market turnover strengthened to LKR 3.87 billion, with 119.46 million shares traded. Singer Sri Lanka recorded the highest individual turnover at approximately LKR 428.35 million, followed by Sampath Bank and Tokyo Cement.

Crossings accounted for around 11% of total equity turnover, led by Lanka IOC with LKR 219.60 million, followed by Tokyo Cement and Dipped Products.

Market breadth was exceptionally strong, with 217 gainers against just 23 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 9.43. Foreign investors, however, remained net sellers with an outflow of approximately LKR 136.48 million.

Commercial Bank was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by Dialog Axiata, Hatton National Bank, LOLC Finance and CIC Holdings. Carson Cumberbatch recorded the largest negative contribution.

The combination of a 421-point ASPI gain, turnover above the YTD daily average and exceptionally strong breadth indicates a clear strengthening in short-term momentum. However, continued foreign selling and some profit-taking near the highs suggest the 20,500 to 20,600 region will be an important near-term test for the rally.
