Emirates tops TIME’s inaugural World’s Best Airlines 2027 rankings

Emirates has been ranked the World’s Best Airline in TIME and Statista’s inaugural World’s Best Airlines 2027 list, which recognises airlines around the world that excel in customer service and operational performance.

The airline also took first place in the Best Value and Business Travel categories, alongside high recognition in several other categories.

Emirates Airline President Sir Tim Clark said, “Undeterred by the challenges of this year, we continue to invest in product and service across almost every touchpoint. We will maintain that focus because, as this top ranking in TIME shows, customers notice when an airline gets the fundamentals right. A big credit goes to our people who bring the Emirates experience to life in the air and on the ground. Their professionalism and commitment are what our customers experience every day. In our business, there’s no finish line and we’re already working towards an even stronger 2027 and beyond, so you can expect more from Emirates.”

According to TIME, Emirates’ rankings reflect its reputation for some of the industry’s most luxurious flying, its expanding Premium Economy offering and Dubai’s enduring appeal as a destination. Partnerships with tourism organisations across the Indian Ocean, Europe, Southeast Asia and the UAE also help attract more visitors across its network.

Statista R Deputy CEO Niels Terfehr said, “Emirates’ results this year stand out for their consistency. Topping the overall World’s Best Airlines ranking, while also leading the Best Value and Business Travel categories, reflects strong performance across both passenger experience and operational reliability - the two pillars our ranking is built on. It’s a result grounded entirely in direct feedback from travellers around the world, combined with independent flight-performance data.”

The ranking is based on a quantitative study run by Statista, a leading data and business intelligence provider, which surveyed passengers on every continent. Respondents rated their satisfaction with up to five airlines they had flown with in the past three years. They scored cabin comfort, service quality, entertainment and connectivity, food and beverage, value for money and family-friendliness. Statista then combined these ratings with each airline’s operational performance data to produce an overall score.
