Graduate teacher recruitment interviews postponed in all nine provinces

Colombo, November 19 (Daily Mirror) - Interviews scheduled for tomorrow (20) for the recruitment of graduate teachers to fill vacancies in national and provincial schools across the country have been postponed in all nine provinces, the Education Ministry said.

The interviews have been rescheduled due to unavoidable circumstances, with new dates announced for each province.

However, interviews scheduled from November 21 onwards in the respective provinces will be held as originally planned.

Meanwhile, the Ministry announced that the online system for applicants to indicate their preferred schools would be available through the Ministry of Education's official website from today (November 19).

Contrary to previous information, the online system will not be deactivated today. Applicants can access it until 8.00 p.m. on the day of their respective interviews.

Applicants who have not received their interview call letters by their scheduled interview dates have been instructed to arrive early at the waiting room at the relevant interview venue.

They must bring a copy of the page containing their names from the list published on the Ministry's website on October 30, along with documents to verify their identities.

Arrangements have been made to provide the necessary authorisation to access the online system from 8.00 a.m. on the interview day.

After obtaining authorisation, applicants can access the online system and attend their interviews. They will also be allowed to return to the waiting room after completing the interview and practical test to print and submit their school preference documents before 8.00 p.m. on the same day.

Education Ministry Secretary Nalaka Kaluwewa expressed regret over any inconvenience caused to applicants by the changes.
