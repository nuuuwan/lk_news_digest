Over 100 attend Prabhakaran birthday event in Jaffna

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Residents of Valvettithurai in Jaffna marked what would have been the 72nd birthday of former LTTE leader Velupillai Prabhakaran today (26) by cutting a birthday cake at the premises identified as his ancestral home.

The event was reportedly led by Valvettithurai Urban Council Vice Chairman Nagathambi Padmanathan, who represents the Tamil National People's Front (TNPF), with the participation of Prabhakaran's relatives and more than 100 local residents.

A poster displayed on the boundary wall of the property identified it as the residence where "Tamil Eelam National Leader Velupillai Prabhakaran lived."

A similar event was held at the same location in 2024 to mark Prabhakaran's 70th birthday. On that occasion, Valvettithurai Police arrested former Northern Provincial Councillor M.K. Sivajilingam and several others over the cake-cutting event.
