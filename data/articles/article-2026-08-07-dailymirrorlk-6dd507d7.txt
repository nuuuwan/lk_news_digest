Senior Police officers transferred, subject to NPC approval

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Several senior police officers, including Senior Deputy Inspectors General of Police (SDIGs) and Assistant Superintendents of Police (ASPs), have been transferred in a major reshuffle initiated by the Police Headquarters.

Under the transfers, SDIG Sampath Kumara has been moved from the Special Bureau to the Eastern Province.

SDIG Kithsiri Jayalath, who previously served as the Senior DIG in charge of the Southern Province, has been appointed as the Senior DIG in charge of the Sabaragamuwa Province.

SDIG Waruna Jayasundara has been assigned additional duties as Director of Training and Advanced Training, while continuing his responsibilities at the Children and Women’s Bureau.

Meanwhile, SDIG K.P. Karunaratne, who was in charge of the Special Protection Division, has been transferred as the Senior DIG in charge of the Northern Province.

SDIG Buddhika Siriwardena, who previously headed the Northern Province, has been appointed as the Senior DIG in charge of the Southern Province.

Police Headquarters has also transferred six Assistant Superintendents of Police.

Among them, ASP Namal Perera, who served as Director of the Galle District Crimes Division, has been transferred to the Police Field Force.

All the transfers are subject to the approval of the National Police Commission.
