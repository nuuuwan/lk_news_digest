Underworld figure ‘Bus Lalith’ brought from Dubai

Colombo, August 25 (Daily Mirror) - Underworld figure Lalith Kannangara, also known as ‘Bus Lalith’, who was arrested in Dubai, was brought to Sri Lanka this morning (25), Sri Lanka Police said.

Police said Kannangara was brought back to Sri Lanka following his arrest in Dubai.

He is currently in the custody of Sri Lankan authorities for further investigations.
