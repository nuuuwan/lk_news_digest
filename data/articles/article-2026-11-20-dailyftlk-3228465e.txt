BOC opens new CRM at Madhu Shrine

Bank of Ceylon (BOC) has further strengthened its digital banking network by opening a new Cash Recycling Machine (CRM) at the historic Shrine of Our Lady of Madhu on 16 November, 2026.

Mannar Diocese Bishop Dr. Anthonipillai Gnanapragasam graced the ceremonial opening as Chief Guest. BOC Northern Province Assistant General Manager S. Pirapaharan presided over the event along with officials from the bank.

The new facility aims to provide greater convenience and improved access to banking services for pilgrims and devotees visiting the sacred shrine. The initiative reflects BOC’s continued commitment to expanding its digital banking network and bringing modern, accessible and convenient banking services closer to communities across Sri Lanka.
