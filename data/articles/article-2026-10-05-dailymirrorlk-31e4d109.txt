AHK Sri Lanka and Fairway join up to bring Octoberfest to Colombo

The German Industry and Commerce in Sri Lanka together with Fairway will bring Oktoberfest and experience on German culture beyond music, and celebration, organizers said.

The festival will take place from 14th to 17th October at the Colombo Helipad from 7.00 p.m. onwards.

Chief Delegate AHK Sri Lanka, Martin Klose said the partnership with Fairway will be a wonderful example of how cultural exchange can deepen the relationship between our two nations. “Through Oktoberfest, we are bringing German traditions closer to Sri Lankan audiences while creating an enjoyable platform for cultures to connect and celebrate together.” he said.

Chairman of Fairway Holdings Virath de Alwis said his organization is  honoured to partner with AHK Sri Lanka to bring an authentic German  experience to Colombo. “From the European band and authentic German cuisine to traditional games, every element has been carefully brought together to deliver a memorable celebration,” he said.

At the heart of the event are three defining pillars music, food, and games create complete and immersive Oktoberfest experience. Making its Sri Lankan debut is the Ozapft Band, travelling from Europe as part of the tour, with previous performances in Bangkok, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, and Ha bringing traditional Kompa music to Colombo.

Guests will enjoy an authentic German Oktoberfest buffet by Hilton Colombo Residencies featuring Pussalla meat products, freshly baked pretzels, crispy schnitzels, flavours and other German specialties.

With Fairway’s experience in large scale lifestyle and entertainment events together with AHK Sri Lanka’s connection to the German business and cultural community, the event will mark an exciting new chapter in bringing international and cultural experience to Sri Lanka according to organizers.
