Uninterrupted electricity supply to resume from today

Colombo, Oct. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The National System Operator (NSO) has announced that electricity demand management measures will no longer be required from today (October 22), following the restoration of Lakvijaya Power Station Unit 02 to its full capacity of 270 MW.

In a press release issued today, the NSO said the unit was restored to its full load capacity at 1.31 p.m.

The announcement follows electricity demand management measures implemented from October 19 to 21.

The NSO thanked electricity consumers for their cooperation during the period and expressed regret over the inconvenience caused.
