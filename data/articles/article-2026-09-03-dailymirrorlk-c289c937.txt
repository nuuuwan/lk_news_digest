Assidua Technologies’ RIVO and ROVER brands shine at Global Laurel Excellence Awards

From left: R. Sadish Kumar, Vickum Perera, Hewa Walgamage Roshan, Sumith Nandana and Suresh Dammika

Assidua Technologies has earned dual recognition at the Global Laurel Excellence Awards 2026, with two of its flagship brands RIVO and ROVER winning awards in separate categories, reflecting the company’s growing presence in Sri Lanka’s smart home appliance and electric mobility sectors.

The company’s RIVO brand won the Most Innovative Smart Home Appliances Brand of the Year Award 2026, while ROVER received the Most Innovative E-Bike Brand of the Year Award 2026.The dual awards recognise Assidua Technologies’ continued investment in innovation and its vision of delivering intelligent, energy-efficient technologies that improve everyday life while supporting Sri Lanka’s transition towards sustainable mobility.

Established in 2012, Assidua Technologies has evolved into one of Sri Lanka’s leading power technology and engineering solutions providers. Over the years, the company has successfully expanded its business beyond power solutions into the automobile, home appliance, technology, and agriculture sectors, providing innovative products and services to customers across industries islandwide. Today, the company serves more than 2,500 customers through a team of over 150 professionals, providing 24-hour islandwide technical support to industries including banking, healthcare, telecommunications, manufacturing, education, and government institutions.

RIVO represents the company’s consumer technology portfolio and is the latest home appliance brand launched by Assidua Technologies. The brand offers a growing range of smart home appliances, including TVs, washing machines, and air conditioners, designed to make modern living more convenient, efficient, and comfortable. With islandwide availability and a two-year warranty across its product range, RIVO combines innovative technology, stylish design, energy efficiency, and dependable performance to meet the evolving lifestyle needs of Sri Lankan households.Meanwhile, ROVER is one of Rivon Lanka’s rapidly growing mobility brands, offering modern electric bikes designed to meet the country’s increasing demand for sustainable transportation.

The brand has established a strong nationwide presence with 55 dealer points across Sri Lanka and has sold more than 2,000 electric bikes within just two years. Demonstrating its growing presence and commitment to supporting Sri Lankan sport, ROVER was also the official sponsor of the Kandy Royals franchise during the Lanka Premier League (LPL) 2026. Through its expanding dealer network, reliable after-sales support, and customer-focused approach, ROVER continues to play an important role in accelerating the adoption of environmentally friendly personal mobility solutions across the country.

Representing Assidua Technologies and Rivon Lanka at the Global Laurel Excellence Awards 2026 were R. Sadish Kumar (Director - Sales & Technical), Vickum Perera (Head of Sales, Marketing & Technical), Hewa Walgamage Roshan (Director - Finance), Sumith Nandana (Assistant General Manager), and Suresh Dammika (Head of Sales - ROVER) who accepted the awards on behalf of the company.

The Global Laurel Excellence Awards honour organisations that demonstrate outstanding innovation, leadership, and industry excellence. Winning awards for both RIVO and ROVER highlights Assidua Technologies’ ability to successfully diversify into high-growth sectors while maintaining a strong focus on quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.Commenting on the achievement, the management of Assidua Technologies said the twin awards reflect the dedication of its employees, partners, and customers while reaffirming the company’s commitment to delivering future-ready technologies that empower homes, businesses, and communities and support Sri Lanka’s journey towards a smarter and more sustainable future.
