All Island Western Band Challenge Trophy 2026 concludes in Rantambe

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The All Island Western Band Challenge Trophy – 2026, organized by the National Cadet Corps (NCC), concluded with a grand passing-out parade at the NCC Training Centre in Rantambe on October 2.

The parade featured 41 Western Band Cadet Platoons representing 30 NCC battalions from across the island.

Lieutenant Colonel HMSK Herath, Commanding Officer of the 15th Battalion, served as the Parade Commander, while Major PSK Kalikotuwa, Commanding Officer of the 04th Battalion, served as the Second-in-Command of the parade.

In the girls’ category, the Brigadier AGR Abesinghe Challenge Trophy for the All Island Western Band competition was won by Newstead Girls’ College, Negombo. Mahamaya Girls’ College, Kandy, and Dharmashoka College, Ambalangoda, secured second and third places, respectively.

In the boys’ category, the Major General TI Weerathunga Challenge Trophy was won by Nalanda College, Colombo 10. St. Thomas’ College, Matale, and Dharmaraja College, Kandy, secured second and third places, respectively.

Director of the National Cadet Corps Major General AKCS de Silva RSP ndc graced the occasion as the Chief Guest. A number of senior officers and distinguished invitees were also present.

The National Camp provided cadets with an opportunity to further develop their Western Band skills while enhancing discipline, leadership qualities and team spirit.

The passing-out parade marked the successful conclusion of the All Island Western Band Challenge Trophy – 2026.
