RBS celebrates two achievements at SLIM National Sales Awards

Two members of the RBS sales team, operating under Rhino Roofing Products Limited, have earned notable recognition at the SLIM National Sales Awards, which celebrate excellence in the country’s sales profession.

Dhanushka Ishan, National Sales Manager at RBS, won the Gold Award in the National Sales Manager category. This achievement recognizes his professional excellence and leadership among sales professionals representing industries across Sri Lanka. In addition, Liyomi Fernando received a Merit Award in the Sales Supervisors category under the Industrial, Manufacturing & Energy sector, further highlighting the talent, dedication, and capabilities of the RBS sales team.

These achievements reflect the RBS sales team’s commitment, effective leadership, strategic planning, and customer-focused approach. They also underscore the valuable contribution of talented professionals in driving business performance and achieving organizational goals.
