Home Lands breaks ground on NOVA Resort Apartments in Rajagiriya

Home Lands has officially begun construction of NOVA Resort Apartments, breaking ground at the project site in Rajagiriya on 16 October.

The ceremony was attended by Home Lands Group Chairman and Managing Director Nalin Herath, along with directors, senior management and staff.

NOVA is a premium residential development in Rajagiriya designed to bring together contemporary living, urban convenience, and resort-inspired lifestyle experiences. Featuring four contemporary towers and 224 stylish two- and three-bedroom apartments, NOVA offers a modern residential address for those seeking a lifestyle that keeps them connected to the pulse of Colombo.

“NOVA is designed to be a home full of life, where families can enjoy resort-style living every day while staying connected to the heart of the city. Breaking ground marks the beginning of that promise to our customers, and we look forward to delivering it with the trust, innovation and excellence that Home Lands is known for,” said Nalin Herath.

Located in Rajagiriya, one of Sri Lanka’s most sought-after residential hotspots, NOVA Resort Apartments offers convenient access to Battaramulla, Nugegoda, Borella and other key destinations across Colombo. With leading hospitals, schools, shopping destinations, and everyday essentials within easy reach, the development is strategically positioned for those who value connectivity without compromising on comfort and quality of life.

Designed to complement the needs of modern families and professionals, NOVA features spacious two- and three-bedroom residences with thoughtfully planned interiors, modern pantries, fully air-conditioned spaces, and generous balconies.

Each apartment is designed to maximize comfort and functionality, creating a home where residents can unwind, connect, and enjoy the everyday moments that matter. The development features 40 carefully curated lifestyle amenities spanning wellness, recreation, entertainment, social interaction, and convenience, bringing a true resort experience to everyday city living.

NOVA Resort Apartments is envisioned as a home where ambition meets every day luxury, offering residents the freedom to enjoy a vibrant urban lifestyle without having to compromise on quality. By combining contemporary architecture, practical living spaces, and an extensive range of lifestyle amenities, NOVA reflects Home Lands’ continued commitment to developing residential spaces that respond to the evolving expectations of modern homeowners.

Trusted in Sri Lanka and overseas by a growing international clientele, Home Lands has sold over 5,000 units across 24 large-scale development projects while already having delivered over 3,750 units. With a growing global presence and offices in Australia, Dubai, and London, Home Lands serves both local and international clients, delivering landmark residential projects across the country. Home Lands has earned multiple accolades, including the Best Developer – Sri Lanka award at the PropertyGuru Asia Property Awards three times, in 2021, 2024 and 2025, and in 2025 was awarded Superbrand status, becoming the only Superbrand in Real Estate in Sri Lanka.

Home Lands is the pioneer of resort-style residential developments in Sri Lanka and continues to redefine modern living, setting new benchmarks in quality, design, and lifestyle.

Guided by its core values of Trust, Innovation and Excellence, Home Lands continues to shape the nation’s real estate landscape.
