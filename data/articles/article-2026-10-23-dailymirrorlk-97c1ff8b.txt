Lankapak 2026 exhibition

The 27th edition of Lankapak, Sri Lanka’s international exhibition on packaging, processing, printing and plastics, is being held at the BMICH in Colombo from October 23 to 25. Organised by the Sri Lanka Institute of Packaging in collaboration with CDC Events, the exhibition features more than 150 stalls showcasing thousands of brands. Initiated in 1981, Lankapak provides a platform for businesses in the printing and packaging industries to showcase their products and services to a wide range of industries.
