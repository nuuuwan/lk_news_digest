Classic Travel receives double honours

Classic Travel, the outbound travel arm of the Expolanka Leisure Cluster, has further strengthened its industry standing by securing one of the industry’s most prestigious accolades at World Travel Awards 2026.

With more than three decades of experience serving the evolving travel needs of businesses across the country, Classic Travel was named Sri Lanka’s Leading Travel Company 2026, following a rigorous evaluation process and global voting by travel professionals and consumers worldwide.

Adding to the achievement, the cluster’s inbound travel arm Classic Destinations was recognised as Sri Lanka’s Leading Destination Management Company 2026, securing a second prestigious accolade for the Expolanka Leisure Cluster.

Widely regarded as the highest benchmark of excellence in the global travel, tourism and hospitality industry, the World Travel Awards are awarded only after a stringent evaluation process and extensive voting by tourism professionals and the travelling public. As such, these awards represent a direct endorsement from the industry and customers alike, reflecting the trust, confidence and reputation built by both companies.

Notably, this marks the third consecutive year that Classic Travel has been recognised as Sri Lanka’s Leading Travel Company.

Equally impressive is the achievement of Classic Destinations, which has now been recognised as Sri Lanka’s Leading Destination Management Company for the second consecutive year.

Expolanka Leisure Cluster Director Sabry Bahaudeen said, “Winning both the travel company and the destination management categories in a single year tells us something important: that the strength of the Classic brand is consistent across the entire journey, from the moment a ticket is issued in Colombo to the moment a guest arrives at a hotel in Yala or Galle.”
