Security beefed up for Easter verdict

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Police have deployed special security arrangements at the Hulftsdorp (Aluthkade) court complex today (22), ahead of the scheduled delivery of the final verdict in the case concerning the 2019 Easter Sunday attacks.

Several special police teams have been assigned to provide security at the court premises as the three-member Trial-at-Bar prepares to deliver its judgment.

The case involves 24 accused facing more than 23,270 charges, including allegations of conspiracy and aiding and abetting in connection with the Easter Sunday bombings.

The verdict is scheduled to be delivered by High Court Judges Navaratne Marasinghe, Ramanathan Kannan and Sujeewa Nissanka.
