Ceybank celebrates 35 years of stability and growth

Ceybank celebrated its 35th anniversary recently, heralding a legacy of resilience, continuity and investment expertise built throughout three and a half decades.

Having operated in diverse economic cycles and market transformations in Sri Lanka, the company’s longevity is evidence of institutional strength and the trust earned among generations of investors.

The 35-year milestone was marked with a private gathering at the Ceybank corporate office in Colombo, uniting the shareholders and leadership, to reflect on the company’s progress and path ahead.

The event was graced by Bank of Ceylon (BOC) Chairman Kavinda de Zoysa, reinforcing the deep relationship as Ceybank’s largest shareholder and affirming confidence in the company’s next phase as it moves forward with clarity and direction.

Ceybank Asset Management Ltd is jointly owned by four leading institutions, each contributing unique strengths to its governance and performance.

Sri Lanka’s largest state-owned commercial bank BOC holds 44 percent.

The country’s leading state‑owned insurer Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation Ltd has a 26 percent stake.

In addition, the Specified Undertaking of Unit Trust of India, a pioneer in Indian investment management, retains a 17.5 percent equity.

As a diversified regional conglomerate Carson Cumberbatch PLC maintains a 12.5 percent interest.

This structure positions Ceybank as a multi‑shareholder joint venture, combining the stability of Sri Lanka’s largest financial institutions with international expertise and diversified corporate backing.

Over the past 35 years, Ceybank has built a reputation for professional fund management in money market, fixed‑income, income, balanced and equity investments. Its disciplined approach to governance and risk management has guided investors through multiple market cycles, proving experience matters most when conditions turn uncertain.

Ceybank also offers extensive expertise in discretionary portfolio management, providing tailored investment solutions for corporates, institutions and high‑net‑worth individuals.

Recognised as a founding name in Sri Lanka’s unit trust industry, Ceybank manages the country’s first growth fund – the Ceybank Century Growth Fund (CCGF) and the largest balanced fund – the Ceybank Unit Trust Fund. These flagship funds highlight the depth of Ceybank’s history and the scale of investor confidence it has built, providing a strong bridge to future ambitions.
