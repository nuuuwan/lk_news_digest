Harshitha takes charge as Pavan Rathnayake makes major ODI rankings leap

NOV.04 (Mirror Sports) - Harshitha Samarawickrama will captain Sri Lanka at the upcoming ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy in the UAE, with Chamari Athapaththu absent from the 14-member squad.

The squad features the returns of Vishmi Gunarathne, Nilakshika Silva and Malki Madara, while Sathya Sandeepani earns a T20I call-up for the first time in six years.

Sri Lanka have been drawn in Group A alongside Ireland, Scotland, Papua New Guinea and hosts UAE. They begin their campaign against Ireland on November 12 in Ajman.

Sri Lanka batter Pavan Rathnayake has climbed 20 places to 16th in the latest ICC Men's ODI Batting Rankings following his outstanding performances in the Pakistan tri-series.

Rathnayake scored 427 runs in five matches, including two centuries and two half-centuries, finishing as the tournament's leading run-scorer.

Kusal Mendis remains Sri Lanka's highest-ranked batter at 12th, while Janith Liyanage has slipped from 14th to 18th. India's Shubman Gill continues to lead the rankings.
