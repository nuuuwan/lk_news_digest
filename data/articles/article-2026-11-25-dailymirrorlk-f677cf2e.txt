Sri Lanka-Nordic Business Council marks 25 years

The Sri Lanka-Nordic Business Council marked its 25th Annual General Meeting (AGM), celebrating a quarter-century of strengthening business relations between Sri Lanka and the Nordic countries.

Meridian Maritime Services Ltd Director Operations Maleena Awn was elected as President for 2026/27.

The AGM was attended by the chief guest, Royal Norwegian Embassy in New Delhi Embassy Secretary Political Affairs Tormod Nuland and featured a keynote address by Norwegian Foreign Affairs Ministry Senior Advisor Aneela Shah Khan.

In her address, Shah Khan noted that Sri Lankan exports to Norway reached nearly US $ 62 million in 2025, recording a 20 percent increase over 2024. She pointed to opportunities for greater cooperation in areas such as maritime services, renewable energy and technology, citing the collaboration between Colombo Dockyard and Norway’s Misje Eco Bulk on hybrid vessels as an example of the potential for partnerships between the two countries.

She also highlighted Norway’s support for Sri Lanka in areas including entrepreneurship, skills development and sustainable business practices, stressing the importance of policy predictability and transparency in attracting investment.

Outgoing President Shaameel Mohideen reflected on the council’s work over the past 25 years, noting the importance of its role in connecting businesses and creating opportunities for engagement with the Nordic region. He highlighted the region’s strengths in innovation, renewable energy, digitalisation and sustainable development as areas with scope for greater collaboration.

Setting out her priorities for the year ahead, Awn said the council would place greater emphasis on translating business connections into commercial partnerships. Plans include targeted trade delegations, stronger links with Nordic chambers and trade promotion organisations and greater support for SMEs seeking access to Nordic markets. She also identified opportunities for knowledge exchange and business collaboration in digitalisation, clean technology, automation, tourism, renewable energy, maritime services and logistics.

The new office bearers for 2026/27 include Senior Vice President Priyantha Subasinghe (Kalaro Managing Director), Vice President Chrishan De Mel (99X Chief Marketing and Corporate Affairs Officer) and Treasurer Dr. D.S.K. Pathirana (Sri Lanka National Technology Development Cooperative Chairman). Spillburg Holdings Director Shaameel Mohideen will serve as Immediate Past President.

The council’s committee includes representatives from Dart Global Logistics, LGL Marketing Services, Lion Brewery, Maliban Healthcare and Scan Global Logistics, with Physio Medicare and Propylon One serving by invitation. Prasath Mahalingam and Bobby Jordan Hansen continue as Honorary Members.
