India tells SL illicit funds used in Krrish project

India has informed Sri Lankan investigators that illicit funds were used in the Krrish construction project in Colombo, the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) told the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court.

The information came through a mutual legal assistance communication, a formal channel through which governments exchange evidence and information for criminal investigations and prosecutions.

CIABOC Assistant Legal Director Ama Wijesinghe said the communication was held by the Criminal Investigation Department (CID), which is conducting a joint investigation into the project with the Commission.
