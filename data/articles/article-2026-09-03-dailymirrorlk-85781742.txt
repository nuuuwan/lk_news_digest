Two suspects deported from Abu Dhabi over drugs, firearms and organised crime

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Two Sri Lankan suspects wanted for investigations into drugs, illegal possession of firearms and organised crime have been deported from Abu Dhabi and handed over to Sri Lankan authorities.

The two suspects were arrested by UAE security authorities after they were found storing videos on their mobile phones showing drones and missiles used by Iran to attack the United Arab Emirates, including footage of the weapons hitting targets and exploding.

One of the suspects has been identified as Dissanayake Mudiyanselage Bandula Dissanayake, 37, of Dunkannawa, Ashokapura. He is wanted in Sri Lanka in connection with drug-related offences and illegal possession of firearms.

The second suspect is Siyambaladuwa Pathiranalage Harsha Kumara, 36, of Borella, Colombo. He is reportedly a close associate of Parevi Sudha and is also wanted in connection with a riot at Boossa Prison.

The two suspects arrived at Katunayake Airport at around 10.15 a.m. on September 3 aboard Etihad Airways flight EY392 after being deported from Abu Dhabi.

Criminal Investigation Department (CID) officers arrested the two suspects at the airport and recorded their statements.

Bandula Dissanayake was later taken to the Colombo Terrorism Investigation Unit, while Harsha Kumara was taken to the Colombo South Division CID for further investigations under tight security.
