Court reduces sentence of couple convicted of procuring Russian woman

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal yesterday reduced the seven-year prison sentences imposed on a husband and wife convicted of trafficking and procuring a Russian national for prostitution in Sri Lanka to four years, taking into special consideration the circumstances of their children.

A Court of Appeal two-judge-bench comprising Justices P. Kumararatnam and Pradeep Hettiarachchi delivered this judgment in an appeal filed by two accused, husband and wife against their convictions by the Colombo High Court.

The accused who are husband and wife, had been convicted on charges relating to the trafficking and procuration of a Russian national between 2019 and 2020.

The first accused, the wife, had been convicted of trafficking by recruiting the woman through fraud or deception and bringing her to Sri Lanka for prostitution or other forms of sexual exploitation. She was also convicted on three counts of procuration. The second accused was convicted of aiding and abetting the relevant offences.

The High Court had sentenced the first accused to seven years’ rigorous imprisonment on each of three counts, together with fines of Rs.25,000 on each count. She was also ordered to pay Rs.500,000 as compensation.

The second accused had similarly been sentenced to seven years’ rigorous imprisonment on each of three counts, with fines of Rs.25,000 on each count and Rs.500,000 compensation. The sentences were ordered to run concurrently.

Counsel submitted that the accused had children who were affected by their incarceration. The first accused had two sons from a previous marriage, while a seven-year-old daughter from her present marriage had also been placed in a child care home. The second accused, a three-wheeler driver, was described as the sole breadwinner of the family and had responsibility for his mother and university-going sister.

The Deputy Solicitor General, appearing for the Attorney General, also agreed that the sentence was an appropriate matter for reconsideration in view of the vulnerable situation of the accused’ children.

The Court observed that sentencing involves consideration of several factors, including the circumstances in which an offence was committed. It referred to previous judicial decisions emphasising the seriousness of an offence, deterrence, protection of the community and rehabilitation among the considerations relevant to sentencing.

Accordingly, the Court reduced the sentence on each count to four years’ rigorous imprisonment, to run concurrently. The fines and compensation orders imposed by the High Court were left unchanged.
