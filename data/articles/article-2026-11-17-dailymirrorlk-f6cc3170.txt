Govt awaits LAUGFS Gas decision before taking action: Cabinet Spokesman

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The government will determine its next course of action regarding LAUGFS Gas PLC once the company announces its stance following a scheduled meeting of its Board of Directors, Cabinet Spokesman Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said today.

Addressing the weekly Cabinet press briefing, the Minister revealed that the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) yesterday summoned LAUGFS Gas officials, who agreed to communicate their final decision after consulting their director board.

"The Board of Directors is scheduled to meet this morning. The government will take necessary action once the company reveals its stance," Dr. Jayatissa said.

He added that the CAA has formally instructed LAUGFS Gas either to reduce its gas prices or to refund cylinder deposits to consumers.

Meanwhile, addressing market supply concerns, the Minister assured the public that state-run Litro Gas has already taken steps to distribute ample stocks to ease any shortages in the domestic market.
