Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Hatton National Bank, LOLC Finance and Commercial Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 750 Mn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in John Keells Holdings, John Keells Hotels and Ceylon Cold Stores.

Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Bank, Lanka IOC and Commercial Credit & Finance whilst retail interest was noted in SMB Leasing, Softlogic Finance and Citrus Leisure. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers. Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index lost 0.57%.

The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs 19.10. Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Ceylon Cold Stores) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.13%. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores moved down by Rs 0.50 (0.42%) to close at Rs 118.50. John Keells Hotels, Lanka IOC and Commercial Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of John Keells Hotels gained Rs 0.10 (0.63%) to close at Rs 16.10. The share price of Lanka IOC recorded a gain of Rs 0.50 (0.39%) to close at Rs 130.00. The share price of Commercial Bank declined by Rs 0.50 (0.25%) to close at Rs 202.50.
