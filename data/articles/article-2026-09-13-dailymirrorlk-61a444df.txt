Heavy rains over 100mm forecast in five provinces

Colombo, September 13 (Daily Mirror)- The Meteorology Department has forecast heavy rainfall of over 100mm in some areas of the North Central, Central, Sabaragamuwa, Western and North Western provinces.

The Department said a few showers are also expected in the Western Province, as well as in the Galle and Matara districts, during the morning.

The public is advised to take adequate precautions to minimize possible damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
