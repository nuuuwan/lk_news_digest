Child’s consent more important than ‘Good Touch, Bad Touch’, Child Rights Advocate says

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Child rights advocate Tushara Wickramanayaka has stressed that a child’s consent is more important than simply categorising physical contact as “good touch” or “bad touch”, calling for greater awareness of children’s rights and protection.

The advocate made the comments in a social media post contrasting two photographs involving children, while calling for greater awareness of child protection and age-appropriate sexuality education.

The post criticised the failure to fully implement the National Child Protection Policy, arguing that children remain vulnerable when adequate child protection mechanisms are not effectively enforced.

Referring to one of the photographs, the advocate thanked those who had tagged them in the image involving a politician, describing the incident as inappropriate.

However, explaining the second photograph, the advocate said it had been taken when a petition containing 40,000 signatures was recently handed over to Parliament.

According to the advocate, the event was crowded and the two children pictured, along with their parents, work with the Stop Child Cruelty Trust.
