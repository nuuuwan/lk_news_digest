Latest cadre revision reveals shortage of 7,000 medical officers: Health Minister

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - A newly completed cadre revision has identified a shortage of 7,000 medical officers across government hospitals islandwide, Health Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa informed Parliament today.

Addressing the House, the Minister stated that the Health Ministry has submitted the updated cadre revision to the Department of Management Services (DMS) under the Ministry of Finance, Planning, and Economic Development, with official approval expected by December this year.

Responding to a question raised by Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa, Dr. Jayatissa noted that no cadre revision had been carried out since 2017, adding that the latest revision was conducted on a scientific basis to reflect current healthcare demands.

Citing figures from the 2017 cadre structure, the Minister revealed that the state health sector already faces an existing shortfall of 136 specialist doctors, 1,683 medical officers, and 2,619 nursing officers.

Dr. Jayatissa assured Parliament that once the Department of Management Services grants approval, phased recruitment measures will be implemented between 2027 and 2029 to fill the 7,000 medical officer vacancies.
