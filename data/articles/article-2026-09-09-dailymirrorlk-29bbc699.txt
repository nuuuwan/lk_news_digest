A five year plan to fully digitalise education systems already in force: PM

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - A five year plan to fully digitalise the education system, including administrative matters, is already in force, Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya told Parliament today.

She said a digitalisation of Grade Six classes will be implemented next year.
