Underworld figure ‘Welle Saranga’ held in Dubai

Colombo, August 30 (Daily Mirror) - Underworld figure Saranga Pradeep, alias “Welle Saranga,” has reportedly been arrested in Dubai based on information provided by Sri Lanka’s Central Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) and State Intelligence Service (SIS).

According to reports, Dubai Police recently apprehended the suspect following information provided by the Director of the CCIB and intelligence officers who had travelled to Dubai in connection with efforts to locate and arrest Lalith Kannangara, alias “Bus Lalith.”

It was reported that cocaine and crystal methamphetamine, commonly known as “ice,” were allegedly found in the suspect’s possession at the time of his arrest.

However, Sri Lankan authorities have reportedly not yet received official notification from the relevant authorities in Dubai regarding the arrest.
