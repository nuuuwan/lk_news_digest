Kavantissa’s cause of death must be established through post-mortem: Minister

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Environment Minister Dr. Dhammika Patabendi has denied claiming that diarrhoea was the cause of death of the elephant Kavantissa, insisting that the exact cause must be established through a post-mortem examination.

He said he had never said that diarrhoea was responsible for the elephant's death.

The Minister said that the cause of death could not be determined through personal opinions or speculation by politicians or any other parties. Instead, it should be established through scientific and medical examinations and the official post-mortem report.

He said whether the elephant had died due to diarrhoea, a gunshot injury or another cause should be determined only through the findings of the relevant investigations.
