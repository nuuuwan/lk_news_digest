Two children killed in Dehiwala grenade attack

Two children aged 10 and 14 were killed following a hand grenade attack on a house along Sirisangabo Road, Kawdana, Dehiwala early this morning, police said.

The younger boy, aged 10, died shortly after being admitted to the Kalubowila Teaching Hospital, while his 14-year-old brother succumbed to injuries while receiving treatment, a hospital spokesperson said.

Their 52-year-old father and two other children were also seriously injured in the explosion and admitted to the hospital.

The circumstances surrounding the grenade attack are yet to be established, while police have launched further investigations into the incident.
