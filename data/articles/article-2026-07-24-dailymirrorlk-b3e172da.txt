Hotel Show Colombo 2026

HOTEL SHOW COLOMBO 2026, Sri Lanka’s largest hospitality trade fair, was launched today (24) at the Sirimavo Bandaranaike Memorial Exhibition Centre and will continue until July 26. The 18th edition of the annual event is organised in collaboration with the Sri Lanka Hospitality Graduates Association, CDC Events, and public and private sector stakeholders. Pix by Kithsiri de Mel
