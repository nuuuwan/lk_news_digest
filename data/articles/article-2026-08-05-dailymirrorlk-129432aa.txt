CCC’s Motor Vehicle Industry Report returns after six-year hiatus

The Ceylon Chamber of Commerce (CCC) has revived its Motor Vehicle Industry Report after a six-year hiatus, reflecting the reopening of Sri Lanka’s vehicle market, following the lifting of the import restrictions.

The Motor Vehicle Industry Report 2025/26 provides an overview of the sector, covering its economic contribution, policy developments, vehicle imports and registrations, ownership trends, global automotive developments and the growing role of artificial intelligence in the industry.

It is aimed at importers, assemblers, dealers, financial institutions, insurers, investors and policymakers.
