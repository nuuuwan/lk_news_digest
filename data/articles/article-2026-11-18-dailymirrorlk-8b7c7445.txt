IRD exceeds 2026 target with Rs. 2.44 trn collection

The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) has surpassed its annual revenue target of Rs. 2,402 billion for 2026, collecting Rs. 2,441.9 billion as of November 16, the department announced.

The collection exceeds the annual target by Rs. 39.9 billion, marking a significant boost to government revenue ahead of the end of the year.

The department said it had collected an additional Rs. 491 billion compared to the corresponding period in 2025, reflecting a 115 percent increase in revenue collection.

According to the Commissioner General of Inland Revenue, the achievement reflects improvements in tax administration, greater efficiency in revenue collection and increased public compliance with tax laws and regulations.

The department said the achievement marked an important milestone in the government’s efforts to strengthen revenue generation.
