Debate on no-confidence motion against Speaker scheduled for November 3

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Committee on Parliamentary Business has scheduled a debate on the revised No-Confidence Motion to be submitted by the Opposition against the Speaker of Parliament for November 3.

The parliamentary agenda for the first week of November was finalised at a recent meeting of the Committee on Parliamentary Business, chaired by Speaker Dr Jagath Wickramaratne.

Accordingly, the debate on the revised No-Confidence Motion is scheduled to take place on Tuesday, November 3.

On Wednesday, November 4, from 11.30 a.m. to 5.00 p.m., Parliament will debate regulations published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2508/27 under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, along with a Supplementary Estimate, for approval.

This will be followed by a debate on an adjournment-time motion moved by the Government, scheduled to take place from 5.00 p.m. to 5.30 p.m.

For Thursday, November 5, the Committee has allocated time from 11.30 a.m. to 5.30 p.m. for a debate on an adjournment motion presented by the Government.

Meanwhile, Friday, November 6, has been set aside for debates on Private Members’ Motions, from 11.30 a.m. to 5.00 p.m.
