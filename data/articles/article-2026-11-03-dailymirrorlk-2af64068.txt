Krrish case: Namal released on bail

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa and former Krrish Company Director Janaki Siriwardena, who were arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over an alleged Rs. 70 million bribe, were ordered to be released on bail by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama.

The Magistrate ordered each suspect to be released on three surety bails of Rs. 5 million each and a cash bail of Rs. 100,000.

The Magistrate also imposed a travel ban on both suspects and ordered them not to interfere with or exert any influence on prosecution witnesses.
