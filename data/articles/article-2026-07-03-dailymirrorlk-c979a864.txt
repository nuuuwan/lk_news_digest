Enfection’s AI Marketing Playbook lands in Australia

Enfection, Sri Lanka’s leading marketing technology company, has officially set up operations in Melbourne, Australia, marking a significant milestone in its regional expansion strategy and reinforcing its ambition to become a category leader in AI powered marketing transformation across the Asia Pacific region.

As part of this expansion, the company has been conducting a series of workshops in Melbourne designed to train business leaders and entrepreneurs on the latest developments in artificial intelligence and how these innovations are reshaping the marketing and business landscape. These sessions bring together practical case studies, emerging tools, and strategic frameworks tailored for businesses navigating an increasingly AI driven economy.

The most recent workshop, held at the Monash University Conference Centre, featured Lahiru Halkewella, Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer of Enfection, alongside Shezri Junaid, Chief Strategy Officer. Together, they presented on “The New Marketing Operating System,” a session focused on how the latest AI technologies are fundamentally transforming the way businesses plan, create, and execute their marketing efforts. The workshop sparked engaged discussion among attendees, many of whom were eager to explore how these tools could be applied within their own organisations.

“We see Australia as a market that is ready to move beyond the hype surrounding AI and focus on practical application and measurable business outcomes. From SMEs and entrepreneurs to agencies and larger enterprises, our role is to help organisations bridge the gap between possibility and execution by translating emerging technologies into practical marketing solutions that drive growth, improve efficiency, and create meaningful customer experiences,” said Lahiru Halkewela, Co-Founder of Enfection.

Enfection has already made notable strides in the Australian market. Recent collaborations include work with the Australian Institute of Architects and Melbourne Polytechnic, spanning a range of capabilities from immersive AI powered gaming technology to AI driven creative content generation and automation powered performance marketing solutions. These projects highlight the breadth of Enfection’s offering and its ability to adapt AI solutions across diverse industry contexts.

Drawing on extensive experience across sectors such as pharma, education, finance, and retail, Enfection aims to deliver solutions across four core pillars: intelligence, creativity, automation, and gamified platforms/experiences. Each of these pillars is infused with cutting edge AI technology, helping businesses scale efficiently while achieving stronger, more measurable returns on their marketing investments. Beyond its commercial work, Enfection remains committed to community engagement, particularly among the Sri Lankan diaspora in Australia. Initiatives such as a gamified digital Vesak platform allowed Sri Lankans living in Australia to stay connected to their culture, traditions, and heritage. These initiatives reflect Enfection’s broader philosophy that technology and culture can move forward together.

With offices now established in Colombo, Kuala Lumpur, and Melbourne, Enfection claims they it is well positioned to make AI powered marketing transformation a key export opportunity for Sri Lanka. The company aims to compete confidently with established regional players, backed by a superior understanding of local markets, deep technical expertise in Applied AI, and a track record of effective delivery.
