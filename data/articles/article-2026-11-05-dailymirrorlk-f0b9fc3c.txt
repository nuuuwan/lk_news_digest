Unimo opens Perodua showroom in Negombo

Unimo Enterprises Ltd opened its new Perodua showroom in Negombo recently.

Located at No. 393, Colombo Road, Negombo, the new showroom offers customers the opportunity to explore the Perodua vehicle range and learn about its features in one convenient location.

The opening ceremony was attended by the senior management of United Motors Lanka PLC and Unimo Enterprises Ltd, the Perodua sales team and other employees, government officials, representatives from several banks and leasing institutions, and customers.

The new showroom enables visitors to experience Perodua’s full range of Hatchback, Sedan, SUV and MPV models, including the Axia, Bezza, Myvi, Ativa, Aruz, Traz and Alza. With prices starting from Rs. 8.8 million, customers can explore the range and identify a vehicle that suits their lifestyle, family needs and budget.

Customers can also receive guidance from the Perodua sales team on vehicle features and the purchasing process. The new showroom provides a convenient point of contact for customers in Negombo and neighbouring communities to connect with the brand and explore their next vehicle.
