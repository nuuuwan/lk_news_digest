Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Aitken Spence and LOLC Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sampath Bank, Lanka IOC and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in E B Creasy & Company, Sunshine Holdings and Access Engineering whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, Browns Investments and HNB Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings and E B Creasy & Company) whilst the sector index lost 0.23%. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs 19.30. The share price of E B Creasy & Company recorded a loss of Rs 2.30 (6.20%) to close at Rs 34.80.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.06%. The share price of Sampath Bank decreased by Rs 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs 139.75.

Lanka IOC and Sunshine Holdings were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Lanka IOC lost Rs 1.00 (0.76%) to close at Rs 130.25. The share price of Sunshine Holdings declined by Rs 0.30 (1.02%) to close at Rs 29.00.
