National drowning prevention committee to be formed

Colombo, July 26 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has taken steps to establish a multi-stakeholder national mechanism for drowning prevention and lifesaving, with a progress review meeting held at the Parliament Complex to strengthen coordination among key agencies.

The meeting, which followed an earlier high-level discussion on the initiative, focused on developing a coordinated national framework to reduce water-related fatalities and improve public safety across Sri Lanka.

The session was co-chaired by Deputy Minister of Defence Major General Aruna Jayasekara (Retd.), Deputy Minister of Education and Higher Education Dr. Madhura Senevirathna, and Deputy Minister of Youth Affairs Dinindu Saman Hennayake.

Addressing the meeting, Deputy Defence Minister Jayasekara said the growing number of drowning deaths is not only a social concern but also a significant public safety issue. He stressed the need for a structured and sustainable national mechanism, noting that the absence of a coordinated approach continues to pose serious risks.

During the discussions, Deputy Minister Senevirathna proposed incorporating water safety education into the national school curriculum to improve awareness among students from an early age. He said integrating water safety into education would help promote responsible behaviour around rivers, lakes, reservoirs and coastal areas.

Meanwhile, Deputy Minister Hennayake highlighted the role young people can play in supporting lifesaving efforts, proposing structured training programmes to develop a volunteer network capable of assisting professional rescue services.

Following deliberations, participants agreed to establish a multi-stakeholder committee headed by the Sri Lanka Coast Guard, with representation from all relevant government institutions and stakeholder agencies. The committee has been tasked with formulating a practical and sustainable National Mechanism for Drowning Prevention and Lifesaving for implementation across the country.

The meeting was attended by the Chiefs of Staff of the Sri Lanka Army, Navy and Air Force, the Director General of the Sri Lanka Coast Guard, senior officials from the Ministries of Defence, Health, Education, Sports and Youth Affairs, and Tourism, as well as representatives from the Disaster Management Centre, Sri Lanka Police, the Institute of National Security Studies, the Civil Security Department, and the Sri Lanka Life Saving Association.
