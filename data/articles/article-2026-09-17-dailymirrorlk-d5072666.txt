HNB Life launches ‘In.She’  mobile app to empower women

HNB Life marked the public launch of ‘In.She’, its newly launched rewards app created to empower women through meaningful connections, rewards and lifestyle benefits.

The launch brought In.She to life through an engaging showcase of the platform, with HNB Life’s Executive Director/CEO Lasitha Wimalarathne; Executive Vice President/Chief Business Officer Sanesh Fernando, and Executive Vice President/Chief People Officer Navin Rupasinghe joining the occasion alongside guests and In.She’s network of merchant partners.

The launch also featured a showcase of In.She’s merchant ecosystem, with participating partners coming on board to engage with guests and introduce the range of benefits available through the app. Giveaways, partner activations and interactive experiences gave attendees the opportunity to experience In.She first-hand and discover what they could unlock through the platform.

At its core, In.She is built around the power of connections. Through the app, women can generate leads for HNB Life and earn In.She points for their contributions. These points can then be redeemed across a growing network of participating merchants, with 1 In.She point equivalent to Rs. 1.

The platform brings together a diverse range of lifestyle benefits, with its growing merchant network including EKHO Hotels, Vision Care, Mondy, Keells, Cool Planet, British Cosmetics, Mosh and many more. With selected offers of up to 50 percent off, users can enjoy greater value across categories including travel, dining, fashion, beauty, retail and lifestyle.

Beyond the rewards themselves, In.She creates a simple way for women to discover new brands, access exclusive benefits and make the most of the points they earn. As the merchant network continues to grow, so does the range of experiences and rewards available to its users.

The public launch of In.She was held at the WCIC RAMP UP at Cinnamon Grand Colombo on 5 August, where HNB Life took part as a Sponsor. The event provided a fitting setting for the public introduction of In.She, bringing together women, HNB Life’s leadership, merchant partners and guests for the launch of the new platform.

The In.She app can be downloaded via the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store.
