Rs. 120 million worth of laparoscopy machines donated to Health Ministry

Colombo, Oct. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Two modern laparoscopy machines worth approximately Rs. 120 million have been donated to the Ministry of Health to strengthen Sri Lanka’s free healthcare services.

The donation was made through financial contributions from Ven. Nakulugamuwe Sumana Thera, based in Singapore, and Sunil Wijewardena of Kohilawatta, Chairman of the Kadawatha Sarana Buddhist Foundation.

The equipment and relevant documents were handed over to Health and Mass Media Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa at the ‘Asidisi Madura’ premises of the Ministry of Mass Media in Narahenpita.

The two machines are to be allocated to the Kandy National Hospital and the Ragama Teaching Hospital to improve facilities for modern surgical procedures.

Laparoscopic surgery involves making small incisions rather than the larger cuts required in conventional open surgery. The technique can help reduce post-operative pain, shorten hospital stays and speed up recovery, depending on the procedure and the patient's condition.

The donated equipment is expected to expand the hospitals’ capacity to provide minimally invasive surgical treatment and improve patient care.

Members of the Maha Sangha attended the handover ceremony, which was also attended by MP Hemali Weerasekara, Dayapala Hewage, Secretary of the Kadawatha Sarana Buddhist Foundation, and others.
