Weerawansa alleges Govt. selectively pursuing investigations

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - National Freedom Front (NFF) leader Wimal Weerawansa alleged that the Government was selectively pursuing investigations against its political opponents while protecting individuals linked to the ruling administration.

Addressing the ‘People’s Battle’ rally organised by the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) at the Anuradhapura Municipal Council grounds yesterday (12), Weerawansa claimed that those who had pledged to protect the country and its people were being branded as belonging to a “black state”, while individuals facing allegations were being protected.

He questioned why investigations were allegedly not being carried out against certain individuals associated with the Government.

Referring to several political figures, Weerawansa alleged that people facing allegations of wrongdoing continued to remain outside the scope of investigations.

He also questioned the Government's position regarding former Minister Rishad Bathiudeen, claiming that despite allegations levelled against him, no action had been taken.

Weerawansa also raised concerns over a statement made by Parliamentarian Archchuna regarding funds allegedly received from the Tamil diaspora.

He alleged that the Government was required to fulfil conditions attached to such funds and claimed that money had been used during an election campaign in the North.

According to Weerawansa, the Government would therefore have to meet commitments made to the diaspora instead of fulfilling promises given to the people.
