Water leakage disrupts surgeries at Colombo National Hospital

Colombo, Nov. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Water leaking from the ceiling of operating theatres at the Colombo National Hospital has raised concerns over the safety and hygiene of areas where surgeries are performed.

The issue has been reported at the Theatre A complex, where water was seen leaking during a thyroid surgery.

According to informed sources, the problem is reportedly not limited to rainy days, as water continues to collect and stagnate in the affected areas even when there is no rain.

Meanwhile, a similar issue has also been reported at the Orthopedic Theatre Complex on the first floor of the hospital’s Accident Service Building, which has five operating tables.

Operating Table No. 1 has also been affected by water leakage, raising concerns about the conditions in which surgeries are being carried out.

The stagnant water and continuous leakage have raised fears that the affected areas could become contaminated.
