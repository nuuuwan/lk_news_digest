Dambulla National School Principal dead in road crash

Colombo, Sep. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Principal of Dambulla National School, Janaka Wijesinghe (38), died in a road accident early yesterday morning.

The accident occurred when a Colombo-bound luxury bus from Trincomalee collided head-on with the motorcar driven by the principal. The bus then struck a car parked in front of a tourist hotel before crashing into the hotel building. Two passengers on the bus sustained minor injuries.

Initial investigations revealed that the bus driver lost control of the vehicle due to excessive speed, causing it to veer onto the right side of the road.

Police said rescuers managed to extricate the principal from the wreckage with great difficulty and rushed him to the Dambulla Base Hospital, where he was pronounced dead on arrival.
