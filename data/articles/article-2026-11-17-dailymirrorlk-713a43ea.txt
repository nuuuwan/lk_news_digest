Payable CEO named ‘FinTech Leader of the Year’

Yohan Wijesiriwardane, Co-Founder and CEO of Sri Lanka’s largest payments aggregator Payable, was recognised as ‘FinTech Leader of the Year’ at Sri Lanka FinTech Festival 2026, honouring his contribution towards helping move Sri Lanka’s financial technology industry forward.

Presented at FinTech Night at Shangri-La Colombo, the special award recognises individual leadership that advances the country’s fintech sector. The peer-nominated category forms part of the Sri Lanka FinTech Awards, organised by FinTech Forum Sri Lanka in collaboration with the Ministry of Digital Economy, with the Postgraduate Institute of Management as Knowledge Partner.

Held across two days, Sri Lanka FinTech Festival 2026 brought together financial institutions, fintech businesses, policymakers, investors and technology providers to strengthen collaboration and support the development of Sri Lanka’s digital economy. Its programme included CEOs Night, the FinTech Expo and FinTech Night, combining industry discussions, technology showcases and recognition of progress across the sector.

The festival’s discussions focused on fintech innovation, sustainable business growth, financial inclusion, as well as security and fraud prevention. By bringing these conversations together with its awards programme, the event delivered a platform to share practical experiences, build partnerships and recognise the people helping Sri Lanka’s digital finance industry progress.

Speaking on the award win, Yohan Wijesiriwardane said, “Events such as the Sri Lanka FinTech Festival is exactly what the industry needs to bring together all the key players on to a single platform to discuss what we have achieved and map out a way forward to ensure further sustainable growth. At Payable, we are proud to be a part of this revolution through our payment infrastructure that currently serves more than 45,000 merchants across Sri Lanka and has processed over Rs. 170 billion in card payments to date.”

Incepted in 2014, Payable works closely with local banks, global card networks, and technology partners to deliver innovative payment solutions that simplify digital transactions for merchants and consumers alike.
