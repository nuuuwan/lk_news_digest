Over 1500 Grama Niladharis to be appointed today

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Following the publication of the new Service Minute, over 1,500 Grama Niladharis will receive letters of appointment tomorrow, the Public Administration Ministry said.

The Ministry said 1,540 individuals will be absorbed into Grade III of the Grama Niladhari Service for the first time at a ceremony to be held at Temple Trees under the patronage of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

Minister Chandana Abeyratne said the move would help resolve numerous long-standing issues associated with the Grama Niladhari Service.

The Minister said that although recruitment had been carried out under various influences in the past, the current government had actively implemented a policy of recruiting qualified individuals. He pointed out that the recruitment of these individuals would provide some relief from the severe shortage of officers at the rural level, including situations where a single Grama Niladhari has had to cover duties across two divisions in certain areas.

Furthermore, he said preliminary preparations were already underway to recruit more than 1,500 additional officers next year. This, he said, would enable the complete filling of all vacancies in the Grama Niladhari Service, excluding annual vacancies, and help deliver a more efficient and effective service to the public.

Highlighting the long history of the Grama Niladhari Service, the Minister said it serves as the primary link connecting villages with the State mechanism. He noted that the government had resolved several long-standing issues in the sector. Specifically, he said a new Service Minute had been introduced and that the uniform allowance had been increased from Rs. 5,000.
