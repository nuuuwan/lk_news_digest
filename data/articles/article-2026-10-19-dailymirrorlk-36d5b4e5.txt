NSO requests one-hour power cuts in four zones for three days

Colombo, Oct. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The National System Operator (NSO) has requested the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) and the Ministry of Energy to impose one-hour power cuts in four zones over three days, starting today (19).

According to the NSO Chief Executive Officer, the power cuts are expected to be implemented between 4.30 p.m. and 8.30 p.m. during the three-day period.
