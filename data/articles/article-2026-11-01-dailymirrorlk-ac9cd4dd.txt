Navy seizes Indian fishing trawler with seven fishermen for illegal fishing

Colombo, November 1 (Daily Mirror)- The Sri Lanka Navy seized an Indian fishing trawler and apprehended seven Indian fishermen for allegedly engaging in illegal fishing in Sri Lankan waters north of Talaimannar this morning.

The apprehension was carried out by the North Central Naval Command after naval personnel spotted a large number of Indian fishing trawlers crossing into Sri Lankan waters and engaging in illegal fishing activities.

The arrested fishermen, along with the seized trawler, are being brought to the Talaimannar jetty. They will subsequently be handed over to the Mannar office of the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources for further legal proceedings.
