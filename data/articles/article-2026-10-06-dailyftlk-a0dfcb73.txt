PR Wire Sri Lanka ranked among ‘Global Top 300’ PR Consultancies by PRovoke Media

PR Wire Sri Lanka has been named to PRovoke Media’s prestigious Global Top 300 Public Relations (PR) Agency Ranking, securing the #282 spot worldwide.

The inclusion makes PR Wire the sole Sri Lankan public relations consultancy featured on the definitive global benchmark, a list traditionally dominated by multinational holding groups headquartered in major financial capitals such as New York, London, and Singapore.

PRovoke Media, formerly known as the Holmes Report, expanded its annual industry evaluation from a Top 250 to a Top 300 to capture a broadening global market. According to PRovoke’s benchmark analysis, more than two-thirds of ranked firms recorded top-line growth over the past year, with over a third achieving double-digit expansion.

Wire Communications Consultancy Holdings Chief Advisory Dr. Nirmal De Silva said: “PR Wire’s evolution demonstrates entrepreneurial perseverance that elevates Sri Lanka’s brand equity on the world stage. For decades, Sri Lanka’s public relations ecosystem functioned primarily as a localised execution partner or regional satellite. PR Wire’s entry onto the global index redefines that benchmark, demonstrating that Sri Lankan firms can operate under the same structural, strategic, and financial standards as global consultancies.”

The milestone arrives as PR Wire marks its 15th anniversary, highlighting the growing capability of Sri Lanka’s knowledge services sector to compete on the international stage. This recognition coincides with a broader macroeconomic resurgence across Sri Lanka, where steady GDP growth and stabilisation are expanding opportunities for foreign direct investment and international trade. Key sectors including finance, trade, technology, logistics, renewable energy, and real estate continue to drive demand for integrated corporate communications, regulatory advisory, and reputation management.

What began fifteen years ago as an ambitious startup founded by Ashan Kumar has matured into a globally benchmarked consultancy.

PR Wire Founder and Managing Director Ashan Kumar said: “For an independent, Colombo-headquartered consultancy to be ranked among global giants represents a rare achievement for South Asian consultancies outside major regional economies.”

“This recognition belongs to our entire ecosystem. We extend our deepest gratitude to our visionary clients for their unwavering trust, our dedicated team, alumni and family whose passion and resilience drive our work every day, and our valued partners, global networks, and stakeholders who have shared in our 15-year journey. From day one, our foundation has been built on a simple yet enduring purpose, to foster purpose-driven conversations, turn strategy into meaningful action, and create lasting impact. It is a testament to what happens when you continue to believe, and continue changing lives,” Kumar added.

PR Wire’s place on the global ranking builds on years of sustained performance. The agency was previously named the third “Fastest Mover” across Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, and Africa by PRovoke Media and has maintained a four-year streak on PRovoke’s “Best Agencies to Work For in Asia-Pacific” list since 2022.

Wire Group Non-Executive Director Krishanthi Dhayalan said: “Modern corporate reputation is actively shaped long before an organisation becomes consciously aware of an emerging issue,” said “The expansion of our strategic intelligence and public affairs capabilities enables pre-emptive risk mitigation and protects long-term stakeholder trust. This global benchmark reflects a deliberate journey to build sustainable, high-value consultancy from Sri Lanka to the world.”

The catalyst for this rapid expansion came via a strategic private equity investment into parent company Wire Communication Consultancy Holdings (Wire Group), unifying PR Wire, Media Wire, and Digital Wire Global under a singular holding structure designed to deliver high-impact corporate counsel at scale. The holding group subsequently broadened its service portfolio through a public affairs partnership with LexAG, alongside launching Intel Wire, a strategic intelligence unit built around a proprietary Crisis Intelligence and Reputation Advisory Framework.

With active operations and network coverage expanding across Australia, New Zealand, the Maldives, and the wider AMEA region, Wire Group views the Global Top 300 ranking as an operational baseline as it continues to scale its integrated communications model globally.
