Ex-Deputy Minister Sarana Gunawardena’s wife remanded

Colombo, Sep. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Chinari Lakmanthi Hettiarachchi, the wife of former Deputy Minister Sarana Gunawardena, who was arrested over allegations of offering a bribe, was ordered to be remanded until October 6 by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama.
