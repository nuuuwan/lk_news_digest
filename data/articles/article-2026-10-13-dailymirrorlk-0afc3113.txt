Kusal Mendis to lead Sri Lanka’s squad for Pakistan Test series

Colombo, Oct. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Cricket Selection Committee has named a 15-member squad for the upcoming two-match Test series against Pakistan, which forms part of the ICC World Test Championship 2025–2027 cycle.

The series is scheduled to begin on November 9 in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, with Kusal Mendis appointed captain of the Sri Lankan side.

Sri Lanka Cricket has clarified that Pathum Nissanka will travel with the team only if he is declared medically fit.
