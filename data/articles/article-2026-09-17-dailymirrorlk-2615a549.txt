Court Assistant arrested over Rs. 500 bribe

Colombo, September 17 (Daily Mirror) - An Office Assistant attached to the Fort Magistrate’s Court has been arrested by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) for allegedly soliciting and accepting a bribe of Rs. 500.

The suspect was arrested within the premises of the Fort Magistrate’s Court.

According to the CIABOC, the arrest followed a complaint that the suspect had allegedly solicited and accepted the bribe on June 30, 2026, in exchange for issuing a copy of a court judgment relating to a concluded financial dispute case originally filed by the Colombo Fraud Investigation Bureau.

The CIABOC said its officers arrested the suspect after verifying the facts during the investigation.

The suspect is due to be produced before the Colombo Magistrate’s Court.
