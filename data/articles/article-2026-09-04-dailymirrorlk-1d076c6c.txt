Namal Rajapaksa to appear before Bribery Commission today

Colombo, September 04 (Daily Mirror) - MP Namal Rajapaksa is scheduled to appear before the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) today.

The Bribery Commission had informed Rajapaksa in writing to appear before the Commission at 9 a.m. today.

Rajapaksa was summoned in connection with an investigation into an alleged bribery incident related to the proposed establishment of a wide-body aircraft fleet for SriLankan Airlines.
