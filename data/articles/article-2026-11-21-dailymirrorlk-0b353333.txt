BOC opens new CRM machine at Madhu Shrine

Bank of Ceylon (BOC) further strengthened its digital banking network with the opening of a new cash recycling machine (CRM) at the historic Shrine of Our Lady of Madhu on November 16, 2026.

The ceremonial opening was graced by His Lordship Most Rev. Dr. Anthonipillai Gnanapragasam, Bishop of the Diocese of Mannar, as the chief guest.

S. Pirapaharan presided the event as the Assistant General Manager of Northern Province, Operations Manager and area managers and other officials also participated the event.

The new facility aims to provide greater convenience and improved access to banking services for the pilgrims and devotees to the sacred shrine.

This initiative reflects BOC’s continued commitment to expanding its digital banking network and bringing modern, accessible and convenient banking services closer to communities across Sri Lanka.
