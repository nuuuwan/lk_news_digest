Yield curve stays anchored despite modest selling

Similar to the previous day, the selling sentiment continued to dominate, albeit at a more measured pace.

The yield curve remained largely stable, supported by intermittent buying interest, while investor activity remained subdued despite the moderate trading volumes.

At the short end of the yield curve 15.09.2029 and 15.10.2029 changed hands between 10.34 percent to 10.40 percent. Investor interest was especially evident within the 2030 segment, with 01.03.2030 trading between 10.45 percent-10.40 percent and 15.05.2030 trading higher at 10.50 percent while 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 both changed hands from 10.65 percent to 10.75 percent.

Further ahead on the yield curve 01.02.2031 traded between 10.75 percent to 10.85 percent. Finally, towards the belly end of the curve 15.01.2033 changed hands from 11.20 percent to 11.30 percent while 15.10.2034 traded at 11.75 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.328.36/US dollar, compared to Rs.328.59/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.355.30 billion, from Rs.330.09 billion recorded previously.
