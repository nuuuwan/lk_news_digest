CMC hampered by severe staff shortage: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) is grappling with a severe manpower crisis, with a staggering 4,421 vacancies currently unfilled across 16 departments, Deputy Minister of Provincial Councils and Local Government Ruwan Senarath informed Parliament today.

Addressing the House, the Deputy Minister noted that while relevant authorities have approved the recruitment of 1,757 personnel for various positions, the intake remains far below what is required to operate the council effectively. It was also revealed that the CMC has been forced to recall several retired employees back into service to cope with the acute staffing deficit.

Meanwhile, responding to a query raised by Opposition MP Rohana Bandara regarding measures taken to prevent flooding in Colombo, Deputy Minister Senarath announced that immediate steps will be taken to demolish 60 unauthorized constructions within the city.

"The issue of unauthorized constructions is not confined to the CMC alone; such structures exist across all urban areas in the country, contributing significantly to disasters including flooding," he said.

Tensions flared in the chamber shortly afterward when Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne intervened to prevent MP Bandara from raising further supplementary questions. "You are taking too much time. I have reminded you several times. You seem not to understand Sinhala," the Speaker remarked.

"It is you who are wasting time. I would have finished my query by now if you had refrained from interrupting me," MP Bandara shot back.
