Spa Ceylon launches Sri Lanka’s first Nature Trail Wellness Run

More than 400 participants gathered at the scenic Diyatha Uyana Nature Trail on 18th July as Spa Ceylon hosted Sri Lanka’s first Nature Trail Wellness Run, introducing a new approach to community wellness by transforming the traditional running event into a complete wellbeing experience.

Held at sunrise, the run began promptly at 6.00 a.m., inviting participants to embrace one of nature’s most restorative moments. Set amongst lush greenery, tranquil waterways and open parklands, the carefully selected trail encouraged runners to reconnect with the outdoors while experiencing the benefits of gentle morning sunlight, fresh air and movement in nature.

Inspired by Spa Ceylon’s philosophy of bringing Ayurveda into modern lifestyles, the event extended far beyond the finish line. Participants were invited to explore a series of thoughtfully curated wellness experiences including yoga sessions, guided recovery rituals, sun care and after-sun stations, ForestVeda wellness discovery experiences, muscle recovery treatments, healthy refreshments and interactive community spaces designed to encourage meaningful conversations and human connection.
