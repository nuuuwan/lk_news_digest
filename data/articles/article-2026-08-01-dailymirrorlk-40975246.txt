Curfew imposed in Ragama Police Division

Ragama, August 01 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police have imposed a curfew within the Ragama Police Division. Police said that the restriction will remain in effect until further notice.

The measure was implemented with the primary objective of controlling and containing the unrest that erupted at the Mahara Prison. Security forces have tightened measures across the area to restore order and ensure public safety.
