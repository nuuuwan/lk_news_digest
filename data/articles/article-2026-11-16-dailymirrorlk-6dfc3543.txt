DPL Group reports Rs. 50bn revenue in 1st half 2026/27

Dipped Products PLC (DPL), a member of the Hayleys Group, recorded revenue of Rs. 50.4 billionfor the six months ended 30 September 2026, reflecting a 16 percent increase over the previous year.

Profit before tax grew by 23 percent to Rs. 4 billion, while profit after tax increased by 43 percent to Rs. 3.1billion. Profit attributable to equity holders rose by 53 percent to Rs. 2.7 billion, with earnings per shareimproving to Rs. 4.59 from Rs. 3.01.

The Hand Protection Sector delivered strong growth, recording revenue of Rs. 34.1 billion, an increase of 22 percent, while segment results improved by 44 percent to Rs. 3.3 billion. The Plantation Sector generated revenueof Rs. 16.4 billion, compared with Rs. 15.8 billion in the previous year, while segment results stood at Rs.0.89 billion. Group gross profit increased by 20 percent to Rs. 10.7 billion, with the gross profit margin improving to 21.3 percent.

Looking ahead, DPL’s strategic investments in advanced manufacturing facilities at Kottawa and Katunayake FTZ, incorporating robotic technology, expanded production capacity and sustainable infrastructure, will further strengthen its position in the global hand protection industry. These investments will supportthe expansion of high-value products, enhance export competitiveness and create a strongfoundation for sustainable growth and long-term shareholder value.
