Bus runs over Kelaniya University student

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - A university student studying at the University of Kelaniya lost his life in an accident that occurred this morning (08) in the Waskaduwa area, Kalutara.

The deceased has been identified as Sithum Sankalpa, a student of the university’s Faculty of Technology.

According to Kalutara North Police, the student was travelling with five friends on motorcycles from Colombo towards Galle on a leisure trip. His motorcycle reportedly veered and fell towards the right side of the road, causing him to fall beneath the rear wheel of a bus travelling in the opposite direction.

The bus was transporting schoolchildren at the time of the accident. The bus driver has been arrested and is due to be produced before the Kalutara Magistrate’s Court.
