Navy sailor killed in shooting in Trincomalee

Trincomalee, Nov 03 (Daily Mirror) - A Navy sailor was killed in a shooting incident targeting a three-wheeler in the Kappalthurai area of Trincomalee, police said.

The victim, a 39-year-old resident of Trincomalee, was driving the three-wheeler when the shooting occurred. He died at the scene.

Two other individuals, aged 28 and 38, from Anandankulama, sustained injuries and were admitted to Trincomalee Hospital. One of the injured victims was subsequently transferred to Anuradhapura Hospital for further treatment.

The shooting occurred last night (02), when a group of people were inside a three-wheeler parked along the roadside.

Preliminary investigations have revealed that the shooting was carried out using a T-56 assault rifle. Police suspect that a dispute linked to drug trafficking may have been the motive behind the attack.

Several police teams, including officers from the Trincomalee Division Criminal Investigation Unit, have launched investigations into the incident.
