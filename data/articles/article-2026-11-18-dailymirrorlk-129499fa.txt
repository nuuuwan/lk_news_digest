IGP holds talks with Senior UAE security officials at World Police Summit

Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Inspector General of Police (IGP) Priyantha Weerasooriya has held a series of discussions with senior United Arab Emirates (UAE) security officials on strengthening cooperation between the two countries, while attending the annual World Police Summit in the UAE from November 15 to 19.

Among those he met was Lieutenant General Dhahi Khalfan Tamim, Deputy Chief of Dubai Police and Public Security and Head of the UAE Federal Police Council.

The IGP also held discussions with Siri Bijune, a senior official of the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC), and the Director of the International Cooperation Department of the Dubai Police.

During the meetings, Weerasooriya expressed his appreciation to the UAE authorities for their diplomatic cooperation and support extended to Sri Lanka, particularly for their prompt action in apprehending, deporting and handing over suspects involved in organised criminal activities who had fled Sri Lanka and sought refuge in the UAE.

Senior Deputy Inspector General of Police Asanga Karavita also accompanied the IGP and participated in the discussions, representing the Sri Lanka Police.
