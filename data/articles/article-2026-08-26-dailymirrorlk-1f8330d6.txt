Eleven new envoys present credentials to President

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Eleven newly appointed Ambassadors and High Commissioners to Sri Lanka presented their credentials to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake at the Presidential Secretariat yesterday (25).

The new envoys represented Brazil, Bangladesh, China, Austria, the United States, the Dominican Republic, Ghana, Brunei Darussalam, Sudan, Guyana and Panama.

The newly appointed Ambassadors were Jorge Geraldo Kadri of Brazil and Wei Huaxiang of China, both based in Colombo.

Robert Zischg, Ambassador of Austria; Eric S. Meyer, Ambassador of the United States; Francisco Manuel Comprés Hernández, Ambassador of the Dominican Republic; Mohammed Abdalla Ali Eltom, Ambassador of Sudan; and Nubiela Yanarith Ayala, Ambassador of Panama, are based in New Delhi or Hanoi.

The newly appointed High Commissioners were D.M. Salahuddin Mahmud of Bangladesh, based in Colombo; Prof. Kwasi Obiri-Danso of Ghana and Siti Arnyfariza Md Jaini of Brunei Darussalam, both based in New Delhi; and Dharamkumar Seeraj of Guyana, also based in New Delhi.

Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath and Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake were also present at the occasion.
