Driver, two women arrested after police pursuit in Pannala

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Police have arrested the driver of a luxury car and two women following a police pursuit in Pannala town.

According to police, officers from the Giriulla Police fired shots after the vehicle allegedly failed to stop despite repeated signals to pull over.

Police said the vehicle continued driving, prompting officers to pursue it before stopping the car and several other vehicles in Pannala town.

The driver allegedly locked the doors and remained inside the vehicle after it was stopped. Police officers subsequently pointed a T-56 rifle at the driver and warned that they would open fire if he did not open the doors.

When the doors were not opened, police officers broke the vehicle's windows and opened the door before arresting the driver and the two women.

Police said a small quantity of suspected drugs was also recovered from the possession of the male suspect.

The three suspects are residents of the Giriulla area, police said.
