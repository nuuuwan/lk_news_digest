Catholic leaders warn of environmental destruction

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Government held discussions with Catholic religious leaders on measures that could be taken to prevent environmental damage and destruction that could adversely affect people's lives.

The issue was discussed at a meeting between President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and a delegation of Catholic religious leaders led by Archbishop of Colombo His Eminence Malcolm Cardinal Ranjith at the Presidential Secretariat today (10).

Lengthy discussions were held on measures needed to prevent environmental destruction and minimise its impact on communities and people's livelihoods.

The meeting also focused on the Government's programme to strengthen coexistence, peace and reconciliation among all communities and ensure national unity by preventing racist or religiously motivated hate activities.

The progress of investigations into the Easter Sunday attacks was also discussed.

The Catholic religious leaders commended the Government's measures to strengthen trust among communities and expressed their fullest support for these efforts.

Issues affecting Catholic communities, including infrastructure development in areas where Catholic people reside and measures to address their concerns, were also discussed.

Rev. Fr. Cyril Gamini, Rev. Fr. Julian Patrick and other priests, Deputy Minister of Religious and Cultural Affairs Muneer Mulaffer, President's Senior Additional Secretary Roshan Gamage and other officials attended the meeting.
