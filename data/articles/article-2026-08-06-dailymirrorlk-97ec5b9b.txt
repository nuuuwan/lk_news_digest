Ministers brief Cardinal Ranjith on judicial and prison reform plans

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Health and Mass Media Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa and Justice and National Integration Minister Harshana Nanayakkara met Archbishop of Colombo Malcolm Cardinal Ranjith today (06) and briefed him on ongoing judicial and prison reforms.

The discussion, held at the Archbishop’s Palace, focused on recent developments in the judicial and prison sectors, which have attracted significant public attention, as well as the government’s proposed reform programmes.

The ministers explained to Cardinal Ranjith the measures taken by the government to address challenges in these sectors, strengthen public confidence in the justice system, and ensure timely delivery of justice.

They also clarified concerns surrounding the reforms, noting that misinformation was being circulated by certain groups regarding the proposed changes.

Minister Harshana Nanayakkara briefed the Archbishop on the government’s plans to improve the efficiency of the judicial system and introduce necessary reforms in the prison sector.

The meeting was aimed at providing accurate information on the reform process and obtaining views from religious and social leaders.
