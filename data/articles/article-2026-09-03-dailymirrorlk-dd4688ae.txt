Kingdom Chapter awarded Best Chapter at BNI National Conference

The Kingdom Chapter of Business Network International (BNI) flew its flag high at the BNI Sri Lanka National Conference held on 24 July at Cinnamon Lakeside, Colombo.

The chapter was honoured with the coveted Best Chapter of the Country for 2026 award. In addition, the Kingdom Chapter secured the Green Award for the Best Chapter.

The Kingdom Chapter also celebrated outstanding individual achievements, with members receiving top national honours: Highest Number of Referrals Given (Gold) – Rohan Pandithakorralage, Highest Number of Referrals Given (Bronze) – Prasanna Perera, and the Gold Member Award – Nikitha Senaratne.

The multisession conference brought together over 350 delegates, including representatives from all 16 Sri Lankan chapters and foreign delegates from India and other participating regions. The event featured technical sessions with keynote speakers, recognition ceremonies, and networking engagements.

“We are delighted to be recognized as the best chapter in Sri Lanka. Our commitment to BNI’s core principles and our collective dedication as a team enabled us to achieve this prestigious milestone. I extend my gratitude to our member business owners and to both past and present leadership teams for their unwavering support and focus on fostering meaningful business networking,” said Prabath Perera, President of the BNI Kingdom Chapter.
