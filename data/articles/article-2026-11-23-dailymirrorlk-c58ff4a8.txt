Aruni Ranaraja to be appointed as Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to US

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Affairs Ministry Secretary Aruni Ranaraja is set to be appointed as Sri Lanka’s first female Ambassador to the United States, Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath informed Parliament today.

Addressing the House, the Minister also assured that suitably qualified individuals will shortly be appointed to fill remaining diplomatic vacancies at other Sri Lankan missions abroad, including China.
