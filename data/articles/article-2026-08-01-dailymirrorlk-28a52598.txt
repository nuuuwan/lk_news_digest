New Army Commander assumes duties

Lieutenant General Nilantha Premaratne assumed duties as the 26th Commander of the Army today (01 August 2026).

Prior to assuming duties, the newly appointed Commander paid floral tribute at the War Heroes’ Monument in Battaramulla.

Subsequently, he was accorded a Guard Turnout and a Guard of Honour parade in accordance with military traditions at the Army Headquarters. Thereafter, amidst multi-religious observances, he formally assumed command of the Army.

An alumnus of St. Servatius College, Matara, Lieutenant General Nilantha Premaratne joined the Regular Force of the Sri Lanka Army as an Officer Cadet on 14 July 1990. Upon completing his basic military training under Regular Course No. 34 at the Sri Lanka Military Academy (SLMA), Diyatalawa, he was commissioned as a Second Lieutenant on 06 June 1992 and was posted to the Regiment of Sri Lanka Artillery as a Gunner Officer.

During his distinguished military career spanning over three decades, he has held numerous command, staff and instructional appointments.
