Social media damaging children’s learning abilities, academic warns

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Social media use could be damaging children's mathematical, language and reading abilities, said a senior academic citing research involving approximately 3.5 million people in the United States.

Professor Tudor Weerasinghe of the Sri Palee Campus, University of Colombo, said research conducted over several years had indicated a significant decline in children's academic abilities associated with social media use.

Addressing the inaugural ceremony of the Institute for Media Development and Research, in Homagama he said more than 40 US states had introduced restrictions on social media use in and around schools amid growing concerns over its impact on children.

Countries including France, Germany and Australia were also taking measures to address the impact of digital platforms on children, he said.

Professor Weerasinghe warned that Sri Lanka had yet to adequately respond to the challenges arising from the rapid expansion of social media.

He said digital platforms were no longer merely tools for communication and entertainment but had become powerful forces influencing human behaviour and development.

He stressed the need to improve media literacy among children, parents and the wider public to promote the responsible use of digital platforms.
