Grade 5 Scholarship Examination 2026 results released

Colombo, Sep. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The results of the 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship Examination, held on August 9, have been released today (September 2), the Examinations Department said.

A total of 294,132 students applied for the examination, while 289,867 candidates appeared for it.

The results can be obtained through the official websites of the Department: www.doenets.lk and www.results.exams.gov.lk.

Candidates can check their results by entering their correct examination number. They can also download the examination results sheet and obtain a printed copy.

A total of 20,000 scholarships will be awarded, including 250 scholarships reserved for students with special needs.

The cut-off marks have been determined based on the total marks obtained by candidates, as well as their district and medium of instruction.

Candidates who qualify for scholarships under the special-needs category will have a special note included with their total marks indicating their eligibility for a scholarship.

Meanwhile, school principals have been provided with facilities to download and print the examination results document for their respective schools through onlineexams.gov.lk/eic, using the username and password provided when submitting applications.

Individual certificates of candidates will be sent to schools by post after the appeals process is completed.
