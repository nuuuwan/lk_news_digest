Sri Lanka condemns attacks on Saudi civilian airports

Sri Lanka has condemned the recent attacks targeting civilian airports in Saudi Arabia, which have resulted in deaths and injuries.

In a statement, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism extended its condolences to the families of those killed and wished a speedy recovery to the injured.

Sri Lanka also expressed solidarity with the Government and people of Saudi Arabia, calling on all parties to exercise maximum restraint and engage in constructive dialogue to de-escalate tensions.
