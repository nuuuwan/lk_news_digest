Free entry for for Sri Lanka-India Test series

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Cricket (SLC) has announced free public admission for the upcoming ICC World Test Championship Test series between Sri Lanka and India.

The two-match series will be played from August 15 to 19 at the Galle International Cricket Stadium, with the second Test scheduled from August 23 to 27 at the SSC Grounds in Colombo.

According to SLC, spectators can enter the Galle International Cricket Stadium free of charge through Gate No. 4.

At the SSC Grounds, public access will be available through Gates 3, 4, 5 and 7.
