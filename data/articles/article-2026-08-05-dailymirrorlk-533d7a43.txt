Mo Salah wears new shirt and mobbed by fans as Liverpool icon jets in to seal transfer

DAILY STAR - Liverpool legend Mohamed Salah has seemingly all but confirmed his new club as he was snapped sporting Trabzonspor's red and blue home kit.

The Egyptian king, 34, greeted fans and media wearing the shirt, having agreed to join the Turkish outfit following his departure from Liverpool as a free agent at the conclusion of last season.

Salah is reportedly set to sign a two-year contract after the Trabzon-based club appear to have won the race. It was heavily rumoured that Salah would make a switch to Saudi Arabia but Turkish clubs made determined efforts to secure his services as the top sides in the country look to splash the cash and add quality to their squad.

They announced: "Professional footballer Mohamed Salah, with whom we have begun transfer negotiations, will be at Istanbul Atatürk Airport General Aviation Terminal at 12:00 on Wednesday, August 5. The player is scheduled to arrive in Trabzon on the same day in the evening hours.

"The time and other details regarding the welcome program in Trabzon will be shared with the public through our club's official communication channels during the day."

Salah was met by numerous journalists outside the airport alongside supporters.
