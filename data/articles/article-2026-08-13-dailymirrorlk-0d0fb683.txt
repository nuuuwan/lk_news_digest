Ambassador Dewi showcases Indonesian culture

Indonesian Ambassador Dewi Gustina Tobing and Sri Lankan Minister Prof. Hiniduma Sunil Senevi attend the inauguration of the Indonesia Cultural Centre in Colombo, featuring a performance by the Sri Lankan-based Javanese Gamelan ensemble Gamelan Sekar Laras. Ambassador Dewi also performs during the inauguration ceremony. Pix by Nimalsiri Edirisinghe
