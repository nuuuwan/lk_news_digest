Prison officer arrested over drug supply to Magazine Prison

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - A prison officer suspected of supplying drugs to the Magazine Prison in Borella has been arrested by the Mount Lavinia Divisional Crime Investigation Bureau.

Police also recovered 100 grammes of crystal methamphetamine (Ice) and 20 grammes of heroin in connection with the arrest.

According to police, a 32-year-old resident of Medical Road, Dehiwala, was first arrested in the Mount Lavinia area with the consignment of drugs.

During questioning, the suspect reportedly revealed that an inmate serving a sentence at the Magazine Prison had provided him with a location via a WhatsApp call, following which he had collected the drug parcel.

The suspect further disclosed that the parcel was to be handed over to another individual at Wanatha Road in Borella.

Based on the information provided, officers of the Mount Lavinia Divisional Crime Investigation Bureau, under the direction of its Officer-in-Charge, planned an operation to arrest the individual who was allegedly due to receive the drug consignment.
