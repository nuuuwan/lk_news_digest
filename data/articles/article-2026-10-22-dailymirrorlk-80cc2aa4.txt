BYD hits 1,000 deliveries and 1,000 services in Kandy

BYD, the world’s leading NEV manufacturer, together with its authorised distributor in Sri Lanka, John Keells CG Auto, marks a landmark achievement as the BYD Kandy showroom crosses 1,000 deliveries and 1,000 services - the fastest such milestone reached anywhere in the Central Province.

This feat signifies BYD’s rapidly expanding footprint across the island as Sri Lanka’s fastest-growing NEV brand, driven by customer trust and John Keells CG Auto’s exceptional sales and service.
