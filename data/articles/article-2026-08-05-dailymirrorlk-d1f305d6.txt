Ceylon Chamber’s Motor Vehicle Industry Report returns after six years

The Ceylon Chamber of Commerce has released the Motor Vehicle Industry Report 2025/26, marking the return of its flagship industry publication after six years, following the suspension of motor vehicle imports in Sri Lanka.

Released as the sector enters a new phase with the resumption of imports, the report provides comprehensive insights into industry trends, policy developments, market data and the future outlook for Sri Lanka’s automotive sector.

Designed as a resource for importers, assemblers, dealers, financial institutions, insurers, policymakers, investors and other industry stakeholders, the report brings together industry data, market intelligence and policy analysis to support informed decision-making.

The report covers a broad range of topics, including the economic contribution of the motor vehicle industry, recent policy developments, global automotive trends and the growing influence of artificial intelligence on the sector.

Role of the motor vehicle industry in Sri Lanka’s economy, including key policy developments influencing the sector

Global automotive trends and emerging technologies, including the impact of artificial intelligence on the future of mobility

Comprehensive analysis of vehicle imports, registrations, fleet composition and ownership transfers

Market trends across key vehicle segments, including registration patterns by brand, fuel type, cylinder capacity and district

Industry challenges, emerging opportunities and the future outlook for Sri Lanka’s automotive sector

In addition, the publication examines the challenges and opportunities facing the industry as it adapts to a renewed import environment, changing consumer preferences, technological developments and an evolving regulatory landscape.
