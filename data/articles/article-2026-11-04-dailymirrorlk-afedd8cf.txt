‘Hasthi Sena’ Operation - Weapons and wildlife remains seized in Kala Wewa raid

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - A special raid conducted in the Kala Wewa North area under Operation ‘Hasthi Sena’ has led to the recovery of firearms, ammunition and other equipment allegedly used for illegal poaching.

The raid was carried out jointly by the Sri Lanka Army and the Department of Wildlife Conservation as part of efforts to protect wild elephants and reduce human-elephant conflict.

The items recovered included firearms, cartridges, ammunition, gunpowder, snares and hakka-patas (improvised explosive devices).

Animal meat and deer antlers were also found during the operation.

Operation ‘Hasthi Sena’ involves joint efforts by the Army and wildlife officials to prevent illegal hunting and protect wildlife, particularly elephants, from human-related threats.
