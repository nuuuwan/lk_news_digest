Firm fined over rice, expired glucose

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - A Colombo based company has been fined Rs. 200,000 after pleading guilty to charges involving the alleged alteration of original prices on basmati rice packets and the storage of expired liquid glucose stocks, the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) said.

The case was filed by CAA investigating officers against company located on Sirimavo Bandaranaike Mawatha, Colombo 14, before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court No. 5.

According to the CAA, its officers inspected the premises on June 24, 2026, and found 1 kg packets of basmati rice and a 20 kg pack of liquid glucose allegedly being prepared for sale after the original prices had been removed and replaced with higher prices.

The CAA said the practice was in violation of provisions of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act and relevant Gazette orders.

The officers also detected expired liquid glucose stocks at the premises, raising concerns over the sale or storage of goods beyond their permitted shelf life.

When the case was taken up before court, the defendant company pleaded guilty to both charges. The court subsequently imposed a total fine of Rs. 200,000 on the company.

The case highlights concerns over practices that could mislead consumers, particularly when the original price displayed on a product is removed and replaced with a higher price.

The CAA warned consumers that removing or altering the price marked on a product to impose a higher selling price is illegal, while expired goods should not be offered for sale.

Consumers have been urged to check the marked price, expiry date and other information on product labels before making purchases.

The CAA also called on consumers to report suspected violations through its hotline 1977 during office hours.
