Vaibhav Sooryavanshi scores fastest double century in List-A cricket

India’s Vaibhav Sooryavanshi scored the fastest double century in List-A cricket during India’s Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 match against UAE on Tuesday.

Sooryavanshi reached the milestone off just 52 balls, overtaking the previously held record of 103 balls by Chad Bowes for Canterbury against Otago in 2024.

The 15-year-old youngster eventually scored 204 off 53 balls before getting out. He hit 28 sixes during his knock and seven fours.

He had earlier smashed the world record of fastest List A century, reaching the milestone off just 28 balls.
