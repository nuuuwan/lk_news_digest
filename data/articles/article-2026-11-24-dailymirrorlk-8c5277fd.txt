Secondary market commences the week in lackluster manner

The secondary market opened trading for the week yesterday on the back of limited activity levels leading to low volumes, whilst mild selling interest dominated in terms of sentiment. Against this outlook, the yield curve witnessed an upward nudge across all tenors.

Among the traded maturities, the 2030 segment at the short end registered some activity, with the 01.08.2030 and15.10.2030 bonds trading from 11.25% to 11.30%. Moving along the curve, the 01.06.2033 maturity traded at 11.85%, followed by the 15.10.2034 maturity at 12.05%. Lastly, at the belly end, the 15.08.2036 bond transacted at 12.35%.

On the external front, the LKR appreciated against the USD, standing at LKR 332.58/USD compared to LKR 332.82/USD recorded previously. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to LKR 466.09bn compared to LKR 444.19bn seen previously.
