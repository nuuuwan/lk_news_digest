PUCSL seeks public feedback on electricity safety awareness programmes

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - The Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) has invited the public to provide feedback on its electricity safety awareness programmes through an online questionnaire.

The initiative aims to gather public views to help improve awareness campaigns on electrical safety across the country.

The online form is open to people from different walks of life, including teachers, Grama Niladharis, government officers, police officers, members of the armed forces, school students, electricians, technicians and private-sector employees.

Participants are required to provide their name, occupation, district and contact number before rating the awareness content on a scale of one to five stars.

The questionnaire also allows respondents to explain what they have learned from the awareness programme and make suggestions for improving future activities.

The PUCSL has said public feedback will help strengthen its electricity safety awareness efforts.
