Sri Lanka-Thailand Business Council meets Thai envoy

From left: Immediate Past President Dushyanta Basnayake, Committee Member Chandana Nanayakkara, Vice President Kamil Weerasekara, Founder and Past President Lionel Fernando, Ambassador Paitoon Mahapannaporn, Founder and President Rizan Nazeer, Vice President A.M. Musajee, Secretary Priyantha Kolonnage and Assistant Secretary Rohan Elgiriyawithana

Focuses on advancing FTA implementation and job opportunities for Sri Lankan workers

A delegation from the Sri Lanka-Thailand Business Council (SLTBC), consisting of President Rizan Nazeer and a few exco members met with Ambassador of Thailand to Sri Lanka Paitoon Mahapannaporn recently.

The strategic dialogue focused on unlocking new economic avenues, advancing implementation of the landmark Sri Lanka-Thailand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) and operationalising the newly signed labour cooperation frameworks.

The FTA was signed on February 3, 2024 but is not yet in force. The SLTBC engaged in productive discussions with Mahapannaporn to clarify on an implementation roadmap.

The SLTBC formally requested an update on the outstanding steps and approvals, potential obstacles and the definitive timeline required to bring the agreement into full force.

Discussions also centred on identifying high-potential export sectors for Sri Lankan goods into Thailand, simultaneously mapping strategic spaces for Thai investments and partnerships in Sri Lanka.

To sustain the momentum, the SLTBC requested regular updates to be shared with its members. The SLTBC offered to actively support the process, organising targeted B2B meetings and business delegations between Sri Lankan and Thai companies.

Furthermore, the SLTBC proposed jointly hosting an FTA Awareness Programme for the business community as soon as the agreement officially becomes active.

To operationalise the employment opportunities for Sri Lankans, the SLTBC warmly welcomed the recent signing of the Labour Cooperation MoU and Employment Agreement between the two nations, which is understood to open an initial 10,000 employment opportunities for Sri Lankan workers in Thailand.

During the meeting, the delegation sought clarity on the official implementation mechanisms, timelines, the sectors and occupations in greatest demand, how vacancies from the Thai employers will be communicated to Sri Lanka and the exact qualifications, experience, skills and language requirements.

The SLTBC also offered to act as a vital bridge to connect the Thai employers with Sri Lankan companies, recruitment organisations and training institutions. The collaboration aims to help identify suitable skilled candidates and to ensure they are provided the precise preparation required for the Thai market. Additionally, the SLTBC inquired if an official, verified channel or database of vacancies will be available, so it can circulate these opportunities among its members.

The visa issue was also discussed at length. The SLTBC requested Mahapannaporn to revert to getting online or at entry point to Thailand visas as previously done.

The SLTBC is committed to support the implementation of these bilateral agreements. Fostering direct institutional and commercial links, the SLTBC seeks to expand trade, attract Thai investment and open employment pathways for Sri Lankan workers.
