Quantum AI not licensed to offer securities services: SEC

Sri Lanka’s Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) yesterday issued a fresh warning to the public against investing or depositing funds with Quantum AI Limited.

The regulator asserted the company has no authorisation to conduct regulated capital market activities in the country.

The SEC said it had become aware that the company was promoting investment and trading-related services and might be soliciting funds from the public.

“Quantum AI Limited is not licensed, registered, approved or otherwise authorised by the SEC to provide securities trading, investment advisory, fund management, brokerage or any other services regulated under Sri Lanka’s securities laws,” it said.

The SEC urged investors to exercise extreme caution and refrain from opening trading accounts, depositing funds or participating in investment schemes and securities transactions offered by or through the company.

“Investors are reminded that dealing with unlicensed and unregulated entities may expose them to significant financial risks, including the potential loss of invested funds,” the regulator said.

Such dealings could also limit the regulatory protections available to investors under Sri Lankan securities laws, it added.

The SEC urged prospective investors to conduct due diligence before committing funds and verify whether the entity offering the services was duly licensed.

Details of licensed stockbrokers, investment managers, margin providers, unit trust managers and other regulated market intermediaries are available on the SEC website.
