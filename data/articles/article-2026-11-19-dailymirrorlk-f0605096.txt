Dahua partners with VS ONE as new main distributor in Sri Lanka

Dahua, a world-leading CCTV and security technology brand, has officially entered into a partnership agreement with VS ONE, a premier technology distributor in Sri Lanka, further expanding its service network across the Sri Lankan market.

The launch of this historic partnership was held under the theme “REVOLUTION - The Future of Security Starts Here” on 06th of November at the Blue Hall of Hilton Colombo. The event was attended by senior executives from both organizations, a large number of authorized distributors representing various sectors across the island affiliated with VS ONE, and a gathering of distinguished guests.

Dahua has already earned high consumer trust in the Sri Lankan market. It was highlighted at the event that this new alliance with VS ONE—a trusted distributor of several popular technology brands in Sri Lanka will bring reliable, high quality security tech solutions closer to local consumers and business enterprises, backed by a comprehensive three-year warranty.

The primary objective of this partnership is to introduce modern AI technology and intelligent camera systems to safeguard national security, commercial spaces, and residential properties. The synergy between Dahua, widely recognized as “The National Protector,” and VS ONE is set to mark a significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s security technology landscape.
