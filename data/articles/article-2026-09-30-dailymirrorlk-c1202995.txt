Groundworth launches ‘Antara’ in Thalawathugoda

Groundworth (Pvt) Ltd has launched “Antara by Groundworth”, an exclusive land development in the heart of Thalawathugoda, one of Colombo’s most established and sought-after residential suburbs.

Located within the belt of Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte and minutes from the Battaramulla administrative hub, Antara offers 12 premium residential plots in a mature, peaceful and well-connected neighbourhood.

Positioned within an established residential setting, Antara combines the ease of a fully developed address with quick access to schools, healthcare, retail, and key transportation hubs. Given the growing scarcity of land in this area, the development presents a chance to establish a presence in a proven, high-value location.

Commenting on the launch, Kasun Andrahennadi, Managing Director and Co-Founder of Groundworth Group said, “Antara is about acquiring a place in one of Colombo’s most established residential areas, where the strength of the surrounding neighbourhood is just as valuable as the land itself. We have created a limited opportunity for customers to build in a mature setting while being close to schools, healthcare, retail, transportation, and Sri Lanka’s administrative heart.”  With land sizes starting from 6.5 perches, the development offers the necessary infrastructure for those wishing to build a home in a well-established urban environment, featuring well-developed 22-ft-wide access roads, electricity and pipe-borne water connections, clear deeds, and a peaceful, green, and secure residential setting.Land prices start at Rs. 3.3 million per perch.
