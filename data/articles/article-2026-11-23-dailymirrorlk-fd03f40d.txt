ALJAZEERA - Ukraine’s president says forces sank ⁠⁠a Russian warship off the coast of Libya, hailing an “exemplary Ukrainian operation in response to Russia’s war”.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Monday that Ukrainian forces also hit a Russian dry ⁠⁠cargo ship used to transport weapons.

Zelenskyy said the Merkury, one of Russia’s newest warships, sank after Ukrainian forces launched seven naval drones on Sunday. A KA-27 combat helicopter was also destroyed in the attack, according to his statement.

He confirmed that Ukraine’s forces also attacked the cargo ship Angara. Libya’s eastern health ministry in Benghazi said all crew members had been rescued and were receiving treatment.

Authorities in eastern Libya said the ship was docked in the port of Benghazi when the explosion occurred.

“The ship was en route back to Russia and its stop was for refuelling and resupply. It is suspected that the ship was attacked by drones.

The United Nations has called for an independent investigation into Sunday’s drone attacks on the two ships.

On Monday, the United Nations Support Mission in Libya (UNSMIL) expressed “serious concern” over the attacks, calling for “an immediate, independent and transparent investigation”.

“Libya cannot afford and must not become a battleground for foreign conflicts,” UNSMIL wrote on X.

The eastern administration did not identify who was behind Sunday’s attack but said Libya was “not a party to the Russian-Ukrainian war”.

The oil-rich nation of Libya has been divided between the government in Tripoli and a rival administration in the east since the overthrow of longtime ruler Muammar Gaddafi in 2011.
