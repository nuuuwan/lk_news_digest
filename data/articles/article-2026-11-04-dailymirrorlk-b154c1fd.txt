LOLC Finance retains Most Valuable NBFI Brand position

LOLC Finance Chief Executive Officer Krishan Thilakaratne and LOLC Finance Head of Marketing Communications Prasad Perera (left) receive Brand Finance – 100 Most Valuable Brands 2026 Award from Brand Finance Lanka Chairman Ruchi Gunewardene and Echelon Media Executive Director Antoinette Ludowyk (right)

LOLC Finance has been recognised as Sri Lanka’s Most Valuable NBFI Brand for the fifth consecutive year, solidifying its market leadership in brand value within the financial services sector.

In the Brand Finance Sri Lanka 100 rankings for 2026, LOLC Finance secured the 15th place overall, recording a brand value of Rs.13,788 million alongside an AA+ brand rating, emphasising the sustained strength of the brand within Sri Lanka’s financial landscape.

Brand Finance evaluates brands based on internationally recognised standards, combining measures of brand value with a comprehensive assessment of brand strength through metrics such as marketing investment, stakeholder equity and business performance.

Brand Finance is the world’s first consultancy certified to provide ISO-compliant brand valuation and evaluation and the first to produce reports compliant with both the ISO 10668 and ISO 20671 standards.  LOLC Finance Chief Executive Officer Krishan Thilakaratne said, “This recognition reflects the relationship with our stakeholders that extends well beyond financial transactions.” LOLC Finance combines the accessibility and personal connection of face-to-face relationships, particularly for its customers, with technology designed to make financial services faster, simpler and more convenient. This people-first approach is supported by a network of more than 200 branches, complemented by digital platforms and round-the-clock customer access. LOLC Finance Head of Marketing Communications Prasad Perera said, “Retaining our position as Sri Lanka’s Most Valuable NBFI brand and ranking 15th among the country’s Top 100 most valuable brands is a strong endorsement of the strategy we have followed.’’
