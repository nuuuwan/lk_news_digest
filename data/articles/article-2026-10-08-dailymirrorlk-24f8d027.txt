MasterChef Sri Lanka takes its culinary journey to the Maldives

October 8 (Daily Mirror) - The culinary journey of MasterChef Sri Lanka Season 2 takes an exciting turn as the Top 5 contestants travel to the breathtaking Maldives for a special episode set against the stunning backdrop of the Indian Ocean.

Representing the best of the competition, Mohomad Yaseen, Sonaya Rajapaksha, Narmatha Balasubramaniyam, Mohanarajah Sathveekan and Shabnam Muzammil take their culinary skills beyond the MasterChef kitchen, facing new challenges in an entirely different setting.

Adding further significance to the special episode is renowned Sri Lankan chef Darshan Munidasa, who joins the contestants as the guest judge. His culinary expertise and perspective bring an exciting new element to this crucial stage of the competition.

The Maldives Special was made possible with the support of Visit Maldives and Legend Maldives, while the contestants were hosted at SAii Lagoon Maldives and Hard Rock Hotel Maldives, experiencing the distinctive hospitality and stunning surroundings of both resorts.

With five talented contestants, a renowned Sri Lankan chef, and the spectacular Maldivian landscape as its backdrop, the Maldives Special brings a fresh dimension to MasterChef Sri Lanka Season 2, combining culinary ambition, island hospitality, and the beauty of the Maldives in one unforgettable episode.
