Mixed sentiment keeps yield curve broadly unchanged

The secondary market continued to exhibit mixed sentiment yesterday, with trading volumes and overall market activity remaining moderate. The yield curve remained broadly stable compared to the previous week.

Within the 2028 segment, the 15.02.2028, 15.03.2028, 01.05.2028, and 15.12.2028 maturities traded within the range of 10.00% to 10.10%. Further along the curve, the 01.03.2030, 15.05.2030, 01.07.2030, 01.08.2030, and 15.10.2030 maturities traded between 10.30% to 10.48%.Further along the curve, the 01.02.2031 maturity traded at 10.50%, while the 01.12.2031 maturity changed hands at 10.75%.

The 01.10.2032 and 15.12.2032 maturities traded within the range of 11.00% to 10.90%.Further along the curve, the 15.01.2033 maturity traded in the range of 11.15% to 11.10%, while the 01.06.2033 and 01.11.2033 maturities traded at 11.25% and 11.30%, respectively. Meanwhile, the 15.10.2034 maturity changed hands at 11.48%, while the 15.08.2036 maturity traded at 11.75%.

On the external front, the LKR appreciated against the USD, standing at Rs. 327.90/USD, compared to Rs. 328.01/USD seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system marginally expanded to Rs. 348.76bn from Rs. 347.33bn recorded previously.

Furthermore, headline inflation, as measured by the Colombo Consumer Price Index (CCPI), accelerated to 8.0% YoY in August 2026 from 7.3% in July 2026. The 70bps increase was primarily due to the statistical base effect in food inflation.
