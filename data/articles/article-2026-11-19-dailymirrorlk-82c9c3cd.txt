Government executive officers to launch strike tomorrow

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Government executive officers are set to launch an island-wide one-day token strike tomorrow (20), protesting against the salary and allowance provisions in the 2027 Budget.

The Joint Committee of Government Executive Officers said 20 trade unions representing various executive service categories would take part in the trade union action.

The unions claim that the proposed allowance increases in the Budget have failed to address their concerns over salaries and benefits.
