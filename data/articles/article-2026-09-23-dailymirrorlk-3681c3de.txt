Navy Commander visits Northern Naval Command

Commander of the Navy Vice Admiral Damian Fernando commenced a two-day official visit to the Northern Naval Command on September 21, 2026, to review operational readiness, administrative functions and welfare initiatives within the Command.

Vice Admiral Fernando was received by Commander Northern Naval Area Rear Admiral Dammika Wijewardena upon his arrival. At the Command Headquarters, senior officers briefed the Navy Commander on the security situation, operational capabilities and ongoing development initiatives.

Addressing officers and sailors, Vice Admiral Fernando emphasized the importance of maintaining operational readiness through strategic awareness, capacity building, digitalization and efficient administration. He also highlighted the need to strengthen welfare support for naval personnel and their families, as well as cooperation with other government agencies in supporting fishing communities, island residents and state institutions.

During the inspection, the Navy Commander visited the Command Operations Room and inspected naval ships and craft deployed under the Northern Naval Command. He stressed the need to maintain high operational efficiency in enforcing maritime law in northern waters.

As part of the welfare initiatives, Vice Admiral Fernando declared open a newly constructed married quarters complex for officers at SLNS Uttara. He also inspected accommodation facilities for officers, senior sailors and women sailors, as well as the proposed Naval Institute, Seva Vanitha Welfare Outlet, General Mess and Command Motor Engineering Workshop.

The Navy Commander further inspected facilities at the Kankesanthurai complex and port breakwater, Point Pedro and Myliddi fisheries harbours, and the Point Pedro Naval Detachment. He instructed relevant officials to take measures to further enhance operational efficiency across the Northern Naval Command.
