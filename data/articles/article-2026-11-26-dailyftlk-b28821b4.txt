Varna wins National Silver and  Top 10 at CNCI Achiever Awards 2026

Varna Packaging Ltd., Sri Lanka’s pioneering flexible packaging manufacturer, has won a National Silver Award in the Extra-Large Category of the Manufacturing Sector and a Top 10 Award at the 25th CNCI Achiever Awards for Industrial Excellence, on 15 October 2026.

The awards come in the year Varna marks 65 years since its founding in 1961, and ahead of the completion in December of a new production building at its Ratmalana site. The building will house new gravure printing lines and add production capacity, strengthening the company’s ability to serve customers in Sri Lanka and overseas. The company scaled significantly in the last 12 months, while the factory operated through a major construction program over the past 18 months, and managed to raise Varna’s BRCGS grade from B+ to A.

“When we first entered these awards several years ago, we were awarded a Certificate of Merit,” said Managing Director Chamindra Gamage. “We chose to invest in capability and our infrastructure over the past few years. It has been a bet on the growth we see ahead in the packaging industry and Sri Lanka as a nation. Earning a national Top 10 place while the factory was being upgraded is an early sign it is paying off. In December, our new gravure facility comes online and takes us a step further.”

This year’s result follows a record 2025, in which Varna became the only flexible packaging company in Sri Lanka to win 10 national awards in a single year. Those honours came from CNCI, the Chamber of Lankan Entrepreneurs, CIPM’s Great HR Awards and FCCISL’s Sri Lankan Entrepreneur of the Year.

Over the past two years, Varna has also added several new pouch-making lines, expanded its research and development initiatives, introducing new and innovative sustainability focused products to the Sri Lankan market

Since 1961, Varna partners to create innovative packaging solutions, nurturing profound connections between brands and consumers. The range covers stand-up, flat-bottom, retort, vacuum, quad-seal and centre-seal pouches, along with printed reel stock and PE bags. Its packaging is used by brands in food, beverage, tea and coffee, dairy, bakery and snacks, fresh and frozen, pet care, household and personal care, and pharmaceuticals. These brands are in Sri Lanka and in export markets including Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and the Maldives.
