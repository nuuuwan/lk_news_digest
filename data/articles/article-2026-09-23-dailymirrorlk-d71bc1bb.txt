Sectoral Oversight Committee approves 22A, Judicature Amendment Bills

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Sectoral Oversight Committee on Governance, Justice and Civil Protection has approved the proposed 22nd Amendment to the Constitution and the Judicature (Amendment) Bills, which are scheduled for debate in Parliament on September 24 and 25.

The committee approved the Bills following consideration at a meeting held yesterday (22) under the chairmanship of MP Najith Indika, the Communications Department of Parliament said.

Attorney General, President’s Counsel Parinda Ranasinghe, officials from the Attorney General’s Department, the Ministry of Justice and National Integration, and the Legal Draftsman’s Department participated in the meeting.

The Attorney General briefed the committee on the Supreme Court’s determination on the two Bills, which was announced to Parliament by Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne on September 22. Committee members also expressed their views on the proposed legislation.

The proposed amendments seek to expand judicial capacity, improve access to appellate justice at the regional level, expedite the disposal of pending cases, retain experienced judges and strengthen the process for appointing judges.

An amendment to Article 137 of the Constitution has been proposed to increase the number of Court of Appeal judges from 19 to 24. The establishment of regional Courts of Appeal is intended to expedite the disposal of pending cases and reduce the need for litigants to travel to Colombo.

The Bills also propose measures to increase judicial capacity at the Provincial High Court level. Provincial High Courts exercise original criminal jurisdiction over serious offences, while Civil High Courts have exercised appellate jurisdiction since amendments introduced in 2006.

The resulting backlog of cases and delays in concluding trials have highlighted the need for additional courts and judges. Accordingly, provisions have been proposed to establish additional Provincial High Courts and Provincial Civil Appellate High Courts.
