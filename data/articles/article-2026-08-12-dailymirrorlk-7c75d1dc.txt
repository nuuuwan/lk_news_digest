Backhoe Saman’s mother reportedly injured in shooting

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - A shooting was reported in the Ulhitiya area of Middeniya this afternoon (12), leaving a woman injured.

The injured woman has been admitted to hospital for treatment. She is reportedly the mother of underworld figure Backhoe Saman.

Backhoe Saman is a notorious Sri Lankan organized crime figure who has previously been linked to drug trafficking and contract killings. His name has also emerged in investigations into a triple murder in Middeniya.
