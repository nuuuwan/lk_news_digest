Train services delayed on Main Line following breakdown

Colombo, Aug. 13 (Daily Mirror) - All train services operating on Sri Lanka’s Main Line have been delayed following a train breakdown between the Ambepussa and Alawwa railway stations.

Sri Lanka Railways said the disruption was caused by the breakdown of a train operating on the line.

Railway authorities are taking steps to restore normal services, while delays are expected to continue until the issue is resolved.
