Wine Evening and Fashion Show on November 26

Colombo prepares for an evening of French-inspired fashion, fine wine this November

The Sri Lanka-France Business Council of the Ceylon Chamber of Commerce, in collaboration with Modart International, will host its annual Wine Evening and Fashion Show on November 26, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. onwards, at Cinnamon Grand Colombo.

The event, which has become a feature of Colombo’s social and fashion calendar, will bring together the business community, fashion enthusiasts and guests for an evening celebrating French-inspired design, craftsmanship and fine wines.

Ambassador of France to Sri Lanka Rémi Lambert will be the chief guest, reflecting the close cultural and business connections between the two countries.

This year’s fashion showcase will feature collections inspired by the craftsmanship and design traditions of French fashion houses, with an emphasis on artistry, detail and creative expression. The runway presentation, complemented by a selection of fine wines, will be a central feature of the evening.

Beyond the fashion showcase, the event will offer an opportunity for the members of the business community and other guests to meet and connect in a relaxed setting.

Sri Lanka-France Business Council President Shaameel Mohideen said, “Events such as these provide an opportunity to celebrate the cultural and creative dimensions of the relationship between Sri Lanka and France, while creating space for people from different sectors to connect. We look forward to welcoming our members, partners and guests for an evening of fashion, craftsmanship and conversation.”

The Wine Evening and Fashion Show continues the council’s efforts to promote engagement between the two countries beyond traditional business interactions, recognising the role of culture, creativity and personal connections in strengthening bilateral relations.
