China, India, Oman eye Mattala Airport

Colombo, August 10 (Daily Mirror) - Companies from China and India are among the bidders who have submitted proposals for the operation of the Mattala International Airport, the Daily Mirror learns.

The government called for Expressions of Interest (EoI) to operate the Mattala Mahinda Rajapaksa International Airport (MRIA) in the Hambantota District, with a Cabinet-appointed committee tasked with negotiating the deal with prospective domestic and foreign investors.

The Mattala airport was developed with a nearly $200-million loan from China during the time of former President Mahinda Rajapaksa. It was launched in 2013. The deadline for the submission was initially set for June 8, but it was extended by one month.

Following the process, the authorities concerned have received 19 proposals, which are currently under evaluation.

Asked whether there were Chinese and Indian companies among those that had submitted investment proposals, Ports, Aviation and Energy Minister Anura Karunatilake confirmed this. He said there were also proposals from Oman.
