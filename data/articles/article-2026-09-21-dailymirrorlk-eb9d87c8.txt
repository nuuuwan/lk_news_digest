Cyclone Ditwah causes over Rs. 2.27 bn damage to schools, colleges

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - A recent audit report has revealed that 150 national schools and seven National Colleges of Education suffered damages exceeding Rs. 2.27 billion following Cyclone Ditwah.

According to the report, the Engineering Division of the Ministry of Education has estimated the total damage caused by the cyclone at Rs. 2,274,230,000.

However, the audit report noted that the losses had not been recognised or disclosed in the relevant financial statements.

The Engineering Division is primarily responsible for the development of infrastructure at schools, National Colleges of Education, teacher training colleges and other educational institutions across the country, the report said.

Accordingly, Rs. 8,432.29 million had been allocated for 41 construction projects in 2025.

However, an examination of the progress achieved during the year revealed that 10 of the planned activities had not been implemented, while 16 activities had recorded physical progress of less than 50%, the audit report further revealed.
