Public urged to use DMC emergency hotline 117 during heavy rains

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Disaster Management Center (DMC) has urged the public to use its emergency hotline 117 to obtain information and assistance during disaster situations triggered by the prevailing heavy rains.

In a statement, DMC Director General Major General Sampath Kotwegoda (Retd) said the hotline has been established to enable the public to quickly access information and facilitate the immediate coordination of emergency response efforts.

He said prompt communication through the 117 emergency operations number would help authorities coordinate disaster response measures, minimise the impact of potential disasters and expedite search and rescue operations for affected communities.

The DMC requested the public to make use of the 117 hotline to obtain disaster-related information and coordinate relief services during the ongoing adverse weather conditions.
