Tokyo Supermix secures triple honours

Tokyo Supermix representatives accept awards at Sri Lankan Sustainability Awards 2026

Tokyo Supermix, Tokyo Cement Group’s ready-mix concrete business unit, won three prestigious awards at Sri Lankan Sustainability Awards 2026, organised by the Ceylon Institute of Builders.

The company received top recognition in the categories: Sustainable Ready-Mix Concreate Brand, Sustainable Green Material Innovation and Sustainable Manufacturing Facility, reaffirming its leadership in advancing environmentally conscious construction practices within Sri Lanka’s ready-mix concrete industry.

These awards reflect Tokyo Supermix’s long-standing commitment to integrating sustainability into every aspect of its operations, from responsible manufacturing processes and innovative green material solutions to delivering high-quality products that support environmentally conscious construction.

This significant milestone also highlights the company’s continuous investment in innovation, operational excellence and responsible business practices that contribute to a more sustainable future for the construction industry.

Group Chief Operating Officer M. Thayananthan stated, “This outstanding accomplishment would not have been possible without the dedication, expertise and collaborative spirit of the entire Tokyo Supermix team. Their commitment to excellence and continuous improvement has played a vital role in securing this national recognition, not just in one but in three categories. As the company continues to raise industry standards, these awards serve as both a celebration of our established business practices and an inspiration to pursue even greater milestones in sustainable construction and manufacturing.”

The Sri Lankan Sustainability Awards is the premier academic and industry platform dedicated to recognise and promote sustainable practices in the construction industry. It serves as a respected industry forum that highlights the pioneering initiatives that uphold sustainability across the construction ecosystem.

Tokyo Cement Group’s unmatched commitment to sustainability is further reinforced through the wide range of concrete mix designs delivered by Tokyo Supermix.

Leveraging world-class technology and access to global expertise, Tokyo Supermix continues to advance sustainable construction practices while delivering innovative, high-quality concrete solutions.

As Sri Lanka’s largest manufacturer of cement, concrete and cement-based innovative construction products, Tokyo Cement Group has cemented its position as the leading partner in nation-building with an unwavering focus on quality, innovation and sustainability.
