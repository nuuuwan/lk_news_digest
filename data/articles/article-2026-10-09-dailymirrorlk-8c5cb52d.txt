50 corruption cases detailed in Parliament

Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake today tabled a 10-page document in Parliament detailing 50 corruption, fraud and criminal cases involving former ministers, politicians, members of the Rajapaksa family and senior state officials, rejecting allegations of political revenge.

He said the cases originated from complaints, investigations and legal proceedings dating back to 2015–2019 and were not initiated by the present Government.
