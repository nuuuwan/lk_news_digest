President congratulates Rumesh on historic gold

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has congratulated Sri Lankan javelin thrower Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage on winning the country’s historic first-ever Asian Games gold medal in the javelin event.

In a message shared on social media, the President said Pathirage’s achievement had made the entire nation proud and extended his best wishes for continued success, particularly as the athlete sets his sights on winning Olympic gold.

The President also reaffirmed the government’s commitment to supporting Pathirage and other Sri Lankan athletes pursuing international success.

“Keep reaching for the skies, Rumesh – the nation is fully behind you!” the President said.
