Manipulation by self-appointed brokers

We would like to get involved in this matter, address more issues that house hunters face.

As we agree on the  argument is that “tenant protection cannot exist without corresponding tenant responsibility” there other issues that needs to be addressed.

1. Self appointed brokers and realtors manipulate by representing the landlords only  and convincing them to charge exorbitant rents for broker commissions.

2. The Brokers do represent or negotiate on behalf of the house hunters, yet they demand for half a month’s rent as their fees, if we disagree to pay, the don’t allow us to speak to the owners or inspect the house.

3. The landlords charge exorbitant rents, almost the cost of mortgage prices from a bank.

4. Some houses are categorised as luxury and they charge between Rs. 200,000 to 300,000 because it has an extra bathroom or a third room.
