Preserving Buddhist heritage

Officials and invited dignitaries observe the digitisation of historic Buddhist palm-leaf manuscripts at Gangaramaya Temple, Colombo, during a progress review and media briefing held today (23). The project is being carried out by Gangaramaya Temple in collaboration with the India International Centre (IIC), New Delhi, under its SAMHITA initiative, with the support of the High Commission of India in Sri Lanka, to preserve and digitise valuable Buddhist manuscripts for future generations.
