Trevor Mendis joins Softlogic Holdings Board

Softlogic Holdings PLC has appointed Dr. Trevor Mendis to its Board as an Independent Non-Executive Director.

Dr. Mendis is a strategist, management consultant, banker and a pracedamic who blends his wealth of hands-on local and global exposure into the corporate world. Presently he works as a Senior Management Consultant and a Member of the Board of Study for the Postgraduate Institute of Management (PIM), University of Sri Jayewardenepura. He also serves as a Senior Independent Director of ODEL PLC and on four Board Sub-Committees.

From humble beginnings as a Junior Banker, Dr. Mendis rose through the ranks to Assistant General Manager, Corporate Planning and Business Development and Leasing in the banking industry. He has also worked at ANZ Bank, New Zealand, a Fortune 500 company, and was a Member of the Strategic Planning Committee at ANZ Bank for the North Shore Region of Auckland, New Zealand. He made a paradigm shift from banking to the consultancy and academic arena in 2008 and became the Chief Executive Officer of a private university in Sri Lanka.

Since then, Dr. Mendis’ corporate and academic career trajectory has been phenomenal and impressive. He served as an External Consultant for the United Nations (International Trade Centre of Geneva) on two occasions and as a Lead and External Consultant for the Asian Development Bank (ADB) on many occasions. He was headhunted by the UN and the ADB on all these occasions. He served as a Non-Executive Director and the Chairman of the Strategic Direction Committee of the Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation in 2019 purely on merit. He has been bestowed with two international awards in Singapore and India on merit for his achievements. He was a member of the Final Panel of Judges for the annual awards organised by the Institute of Chartered Accountants and currently serves on the Final Panel of Judges for the National Business Excellence Awards organised by the National Chamber of Commerce (NCC). He has addressed Research Conferences as the International Guest Speaker and Chief Guest both locally and internationally.

Being a Distinguished Life Alumnus of PIM and Massey University, New Zealand, with several Unique Selling Propositions and distinctive competences, he currently focuses on incomparable specialised arenas, namely Strategic Affairs, International Business, Corporate Governance, Re-engineering of Companies, Banking, Policy Formulation, Tertiary Education, AML, Data-driven Decision Making and Geopolitics, as a Consultant and an Advisor. He has evaluated more than 1,000 balance sheets across the world in his career.

Dr. Mendis is an AIB qualified banker, with a Postgraduate Diploma in Financial Management from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka. His first Master’s Degree is from the Postgraduate Institute of Management, University of Sri Jayewardenepura. His second Master’s Degree (MSc in Management) is from the prestigious Massey University in Auckland, New Zealand, and his Doctorate on Corporate Governance is from the University of Kelaniya.
