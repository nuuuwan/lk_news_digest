MR meets new Chinese Ambassador

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Former President Mahinda Rajapaksa held a cordial meeting with newly appointed Chinese Ambassador to Sri Lanka Wei Huaxiang yesterday (9) at his residence in Colombo.

Rajapaksa congratulated Wei Huaxiang on assuming duties as China's Ambassador to Sri Lanka after presenting his credentials on August 25.

Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) General Secretary and Attorney-at-Law Sagara Kariyawasam also participated in the meeting.
