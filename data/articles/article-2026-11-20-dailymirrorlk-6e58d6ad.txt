Underworld figure ‘Rotumba Amila’ brought back to Sri Lanka from Russia

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Sepala Ratnayake Amila Sampath, alias ‘Rotumba Amila’, an alleged organised crime figure and international drug trafficker who had been hiding in Russia, was brought back to Sri Lanka this morning (20) by the Criminal Investigation Department (CID).

The 44-year-old suspect, a resident of Urubokka in Matara, arrived at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake on a flight from Moscow after being apprehended in Russia by CID officers.

Investigations have revealed that the suspect maintained close links with notorious underworld figures ‘Angoda Lokka’ and ‘Makandure Madush’. He has also been linked to the assassination of businessman Surendra Wasantha Perera, popularly known as ‘Club Wasantha’, on July 8, 2024, and a murder committed in the Mulleriyawa area on April 29, 2016.

Rotumba Amila was previously arrested in the United Arab Emirates (UAE) on March 28, 2019, during a birthday party hosted by Makandure Madush. He was subsequently deported to Sri Lanka.

However, after being released on bail on May 3, 2023, he fled the country and travelled to Russia. Interpol Red Notices were subsequently obtained through several Sri Lankan courts in connection with his arrest.

CID officers took the suspect into custody upon his arrival at Katunayake Airport this morning. After recording his statement at the airport, he is due to be escorted under tight security to the CID Headquarters in Colombo for further investigations.
