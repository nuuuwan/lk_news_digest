Dialog Touch partners with HelaGo for cashless corporate mobility

Touch Card by Dialog Axiata PLC has partnered with HelaGo to enable businesses to simplify employee transportation and mobility-related payments through a seamless, secure, and cashless experience.

The partnership was formally announced on 23 September at Dialog, with the official signing ceremony attended by HelaGo Founder Danika Perera and his team, alongside Renuka Fernando, Group Chief Digital Services Officer of Dialog Axiata PLC.  Through this collaboration, corporate customers will be able to use Dialog Touch for HelaGo rides, providing organisations with a convenient and efficient way to manage transportation payments. The solution will enable greater control over travel-related expenses while supporting improved operational efficiency. Employees will also benefit from a cashless ride-hailing experience.

“As corporate mobility continues to evolve, businesses need smarter, more convenient, and secure solutions for employee travel. Our partnership with HelaGo reflects Dialog Touch’s commitment to using digital innovation to simplify corporate transportation while delivering greater convenience, visibility, and control for enterprises,” said Renuka Fernando.

“Our partnership with Dialog Touch strengthens HelaGo’s ability to provide Sri Lankan businesses with a reliable and seamless corporate travel experience. By combining convenient mobility with secure digital payments, we aim to make employee transportation simpler, smarter, and more efficient,” said Danika Perera.
