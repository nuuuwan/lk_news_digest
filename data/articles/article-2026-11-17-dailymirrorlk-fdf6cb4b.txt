SLICGL and PVI Insurance host GIPA Award Ceremony 2026 in Vietnam

Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation General Ltd. (SLICGL) hosted the General Insurance Pinnacle Award (GIPA) Ceremony 2026 in partnership with PVI Insurance Corporation in Vietnam, honouring outstanding performers from both companies for excellence in General Insurance salesmanship.

The 3rd GIPA Award Ceremony was held on 9 October at the PVI Insurance Corporation head office in Hanoi.

SLICGL’s delegation included 40 Sri Lankan GIPA winners, who joined the winners from PVI Insurance Corporation, along with their supervisors and the senior management teams of PVI Insurance Corporation and Hanoi-Re. With over 100 participants, GIPA Awards 2026 was a memorable celebration of achievement, camaraderie and international collaboration.

The third edition of GIPA continued its momentum in Hanoi with the participation of Champa Mellawa, Managing Director/CEO of J B Boda & Reinsurance Brokers Lanka and JB Boda & Co (Singapore), and with the endorsement of industry leaders from Indonesia and Singapore.

Launched in 2023, GIPA is an international recognition programme for top General Insurance sales achievers, rewarding sustainable excellence and business quality.
