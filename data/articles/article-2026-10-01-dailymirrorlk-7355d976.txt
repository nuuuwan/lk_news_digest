Price regulation of 13 costly medicines saves Govt. Rs. 5.7 bn

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daily Mirror) - Price regulation of 13 high-priced essential medicines has resulted in an estimated saving of Rs. 5.7 billion for the Government, the National Medicines Regulatory Authority (NMRA) said today.

NMRA Chairman Dr. Ananda Wijewickrema disclosed this at a media briefing held in Colombo today (01).

He said the estimated Rs. 5.7 billion saving was calculated based on the price regulation introduced for 13 major high-priced medicines.
