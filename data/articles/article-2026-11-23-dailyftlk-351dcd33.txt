Lankan elected Asia-Pacific Association of Agricultural Research Institutions President

Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy (SLCARP) Secretary/Director Prabath Wimal Kumara, has been unanimously elected President of the Asia-Pacific Association of Agricultural Research Institutions (APARi) for the 2027–2028 term.

He will also serve as a member of the APARi Executive Committee.

The election took place at the 19th General Conference of APARi, held on 20 November 2026 in Phnom Penh, Cambodia.

Kumara represented Sri Lanka at the conference under the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation.

This is the first time a Sri Lankan representative has been elected to this prestigious regional position, marking a significant milestone for Sri Lanka’s agricultural research community and its growing standing in the Asia-Pacific region.

Kumara is a graduate of the University of Kelaniya, where he also obtained a postgraduate qualification. He also holds a postgraduate qualification in Agriculture from Wayamba University of Sri Lanka and has pursued further studies in Law at the University of Colombo.

The Asia-Pacific Association of Agricultural Research Institutions (APARi) is a regional body that brings together agricultural research institutions across the Asia-Pacific to promote collaboration and the advancement of agricultural research and development.

The Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy (SLCARP) is the national body responsible for agricultural research policy in Sri Lanka, operating under the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation.
