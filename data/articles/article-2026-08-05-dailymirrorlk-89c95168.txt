Two medical institutions fined Rs. 400,000 for overcharging patients

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Jaffna Magistrate's Court has imposed fines totalling Rs. 400,000 on two medical institutions for violating the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) Act, including possessing expired medicines and overcharging patients for laboratory tests.

The cases were filed by the CAA's Jaffna District Investigation Officers following inspections carried out in July.

During a raid on Northern Central Hospitals (Pvt) Ltd in Thirunelvely on July 29, investigators discovered expired eye surgery medicines, expired Ether Liquid and Hydrogen Peroxide, as well as expired surgical equipment. The institution pleaded guilty to three charges and was fined Rs. 100,000 on each count, amounting to a total fine of Rs. 300,000.

In a separate case, New Sun Rise Medi Clinic on Navalar Road was found to have violated CAA Direction No. 67 during an inspection on July 14 by charging Rs. 550 for a Full Blood Count (FBC) test, exceeding the maximum approved fee of Rs. 400. The clinic pleaded guilty and was fined Rs. 100,000.

The court also issued a special order under Section 60(6) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act directing that details of the judgments against both institutions be published in newspapers to raise public awareness about consumer rights and regulatory compliance.
