Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Cargills, Colombo Dockyard and Ceylon Grain Elevators with the turnover crossing Rs. 8.4 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20.

Crossings were witnessed in Cargills, Hayleys and Lanka Realty Investments accounting for 72.8% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Credit & Finance, Sierra Cables and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, HNB Finance and Browns Investments. Furthermore, foreigners remained active closing as net sellers.

Food & Staples Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Cargills ) whilst the sector index gained 8.95%. The share price of Cargills increased by Rs 32.00 (5.12%) to close at Rs 657.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Hayleys, Sierra Cables and

John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index increased by 0.97%. The share price of Hayleys gained Rs 1.25 (0.56%) to close at Rs 224.00. The share price of Sierra Cables recorded a gain of Rs 2.00 (7.14%) to close at Rs 30.00. The share price of John Keells Holdings appreciated by Rs 0.10 (0.50%) to close at Rs 20.00.

Commercial Credit & Finance was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance moved down by Rs 7.25 (6.42%) to close at Rs 105.75.
