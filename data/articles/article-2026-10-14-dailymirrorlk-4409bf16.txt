Dinlanka buys 11.11% stake in Ceylon Hotels Corporation for Rs. 600mn

Dinlanka Logistics (Pvt) Ltd has acquired an 11.11 percent stake in Ceylon Hotels Corporation PLC for Rs. 600 million, according to a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange.

The company purchased 20 million shares at Rs. 30 each on October 12, 2026, NDB Securities (Pvt) Ltd said in the filing on behalf of its client.

Following the transaction, Dinlanka holds an aggregate of 20 million shares in Ceylon Hotels Corporation, which has an issued share capital of approximately 180.03 million shares.

The disclosure was submitted under Rule 36 of the Takeovers and Mergers Code, 1995, as amended in 2003, relating to transactions involving holdings exceeding 10 percent.
