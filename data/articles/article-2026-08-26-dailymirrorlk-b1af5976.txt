Legal action launched over illegal sand mining in Paranthan Proposed Reserve

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Forest conservation authorities have initiated legal action following complaints of illegal sand mining and soil transportation in the Paranthan Proposed Reserve in the Nedunkerny Divisional Secretariat Division of Vavuniya District.

The action was taken after a complaint was received through the ‘1995 Vana Surakuma’ forest protection hotline, prompting forest conservation officers to conduct a field inspection of the area.

The special inspection was carried out on August 14, 2026, focusing on the Kanagarayankulam Aru, which is located within the Paranthan Proposed Reserve and comes under the Puthur Forest Field Assistant Division of the Puliyankulam Beat in the Nedunkerny Range.

During the inspection, residents told officials that soil excavated from an agricultural well in a paddy field adjoining the reserve forest had been transported using tractors.

Forest officers had also recently arrested a person allegedly caught illegally dredging sand near a bridge along the road crossing the river.

Legal proceedings have been initiated against the suspect, with the incident recorded as a forest offence under Case No. 46/2026.

Meanwhile, illegal sand mining in the sensitive ecosystem had been discussed at a special awareness workshop held on August 11 regarding the Code of Conduct for Mineral Mining introduced by the Presidential Task Force.

Following the discussions, the Vavuniya District Secretary instructed the Nedunkerny Divisional Secretary to immediately suspend all sand mining activities in the area until measures are taken to adequately protect the ecosystem.

The ‘1995 Vana Surakuma’ hotline operates around the clock to receive information on environmental destruction, forest clearing and unauthorized mining activities.

The Ministry of Environment, together with its affiliated institutions, the Tri-Forces and the Police, continues to use information received from the public to take action against activities that threaten the country’s forests and ecosystems.
