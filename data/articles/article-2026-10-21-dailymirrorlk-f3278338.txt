NP Capital website goes offline, raising questions over business ventures

Colombo, Oct. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The website of NP Capital, a company associated with businessman Nimal Perera, appears to be inaccessible, raising questions about the status of its online presence and the various business ventures featured on the platform.

Perera has been linked to the controversial Airbus aircraft procurement deal involving Sri Lanka and has been reported as a state witness in proceedings connected to the case involving Namal Rajapaksa.

The apparent unavailability of the NP Capital website has prompted questions about whether the outage is temporary or whether the company has made changes to its online operations.

The website had featured information on various business ventures reportedly associated with the company. However, the current status of those ventures remains unclear.

There has been no verified information establishing the reason for the website’s apparent unavailability, and it is not clear whether the outage is related to routine maintenance, technical issues or a decision to take the website offline.
