Fire breaks out in unused building at Kahawatta Hospital

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - A fire broke out in an unused building at the Kahawatta Base Hospital, where waste materials had been stored, early this morning (17).

The fire erupted at around 3.45 a.m., prompting the Ratnapura Municipal Fire Brigade to rush to the scene. Firefighters battled the blaze for about an hour before bringing it under control.

The building had previously been used as an inpatient ward but was later abandoned after the ward was relocated to a newly constructed building.

Police said the fire had been completely extinguished and no injuries had been reported.

An electrical fault is suspected to have caused the fire, while the Ceylon Electricity Board disconnected the electricity supply to the area at around 4 a.m. as a precautionary measure.
