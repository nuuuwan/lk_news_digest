NPP Local Government member resigns after allegedly trespassing into private home and causing damage in Jaffna

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - Chandrakumar Ganesaruban, a member of the National People’s Power (NPP) of the Vadamarachchi South-West Pradeshiya Sabha in Jaffna, who was arrested on allegations of unlawfully entering a private residence and carrying out an assault, has resigned from his position as a Local Government Council member.

A group, including the NPP Local Government member, had allegedly attacked the house of a retired government officer in the Nelliady area by pelting stones at it and had also damaged the surveillance cameras installed within the premises. The group was subsequently arrested after the occupants of the house reported the incident to the police.

The individuals who were taken to the police station were released on police bail following questioning.

Meanwhile, the said National People’s Power member of the Pradeshiya Sabha has resigned from his position as a Local Government council member.
