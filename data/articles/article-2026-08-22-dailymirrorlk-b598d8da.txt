‘Bus Lalith’ to be brought back from Dubai

Colombo, August 22 (Daily Mirror) - Organized criminal and drug trafficker Lalith Kannangara, alias “Bus Lalith Anidda” (24), who was arrested in Dubai, is scheduled to be brought back to Sri Lanka tomorrow, police said.

A team of officers from the Central Crime Investigation Bureau left for Dubai early yesterday morning to escort the suspect back to the country.

The suspect was arrested by Dubai police in October last year following information provided by officers of the Central Crime Investigation Bureau.
