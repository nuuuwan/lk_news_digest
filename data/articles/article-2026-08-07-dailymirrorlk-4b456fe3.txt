Central Hills reservoirs near overflow levels due to heavy rains

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Water levels in several reservoirs in the western slopes of the Central Hills have risen sharply following intermittent heavy rainfall, with some reservoirs reaching or exceeding overflow levels, officials said.

An officer responsible for reservoir operations said the water levels of the Castlereagh and Maussakelle reservoirs are currently nearing overflow levels due to the heavy rains experienced in the area.

Meanwhile, steps have been taken to open the spill gates of the Canyon and Laxapana reservoirs, which are overflowing the Wimalasurendra Reservoir, the official said.

The prevailing conditions could also lead to a rise in the water level of the Kelani River, the official warned.

The official added that efforts are being made to divert water from the reservoirs to the relevant hydroelectric power stations to the maximum possible extent for power generation.
