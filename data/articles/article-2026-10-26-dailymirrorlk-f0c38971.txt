Rajinikanth’s ’Jailer 2’ becomes highest-grossing Tamil film ever

(NDTV)- Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has created box office history by becoming the highest-grossing Tamil film worldwide.

The action drama crossed the lifetime global collection of 2.0, starring Rajinikanth and Akshay Kumar, which had earned approximately Rs 691 crore during its theatrical run.

On Sunday, October 25, Jailer 2 took its worldwide gross collection to Rs 710.35 crore, according to trade tracker Sacnilk.

The film's latest milestone comes 11 days after its theatrical release on October 15. With this achievement, Jailer 2 has also emerged as the highest-grossing Tamil film of 2026.

At the domestic box office, Jailer 2 recorded a gross collection of Rs 25.70 crore on Sunday. Its total India gross now stands at Rs 466.85 crore, while its India nett collection has reached Rs 398.70 crore.

According to Sacnilk, the Tamil version contributed Rs 17.35 crore on Day 11 from 5,683 shows. The Telugu version added Rs 4.50 crore across 1,860 shows, while the Hindi version collected Rs 3.15 crore from 4,195 shows. The Kannada version contributed another Rs 0.70 crore from 216 shows.

The film's strong performance across multiple language versions has helped it maintain its box office momentum and achieve the worldwide record.

Besides overtaking 2.0 to claim the top spot among Tamil films worldwide, Jailer 2 had already registered several milestones during its opening days. The film became the fastest Tamil release to cross the Rs 300 crore and Rs 400 crore marks in worldwide collections, achieving the former within three days and the latter on Day 4.

The latest achievement further strengthens Rajinikanth's position as one of Tamil cinema's biggest box office draws.
