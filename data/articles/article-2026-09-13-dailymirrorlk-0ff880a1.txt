SJB takes fight against 22nd Amendment to Colombo streets

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa said that the Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) will stage  public protest in Colombo on September 24 against the proposed 22nd Amendment to the Constitution.

Premadasa made the announcement while addressing a meeting of SJB Colombo District local government members and electorate organisers held today (13) at the Opposition Leader’s Office.

He accused the government led by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake of seeking to establish one-party rule by undermining judicial independence and the rule of law through the proposed constitutional amendment.

Despite the government having a two-thirds majority in Parliament, the people’s democratic rights would not be allowed to be undermined, Premadasa said.

He said that the SJB was the first political party to oppose the proposed amendment and vowed to continue its campaign against what he described as the government’s attempt to weaken democratic institutions.
