President doesn't answer MPs’ phone calls: Dayasiri

Colombo, June 26 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake never answers MPs' phone calls, Opposition MP Dayasiri Jayasekera told Parliament today.

He said former Presidents such as Mahinda Rajapaksa, Maithripala Sirisena and Ranil Wickremesinghe never missed calls from MPs.
