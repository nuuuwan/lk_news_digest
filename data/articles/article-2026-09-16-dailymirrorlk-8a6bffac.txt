Railway Operating Service Union supports Sept. 17-18 action

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Railway Technological and Operating Service Union has announced that it will join the joint trade union action scheduled for September 17 and 18, while calling for the intervention of the authorities over issues affecting the railway service.

In a letter addressed to the Secretary of the Railway Joint Trade Union Alliance, the union expressed support for the proposed trade union action and said it would actively participate in the process.

The union also raised serious concerns over the performance of the current General Manager of Railways, alleging that the administration has failed to maintain the progress and standards of the railway service.

It said the long-standing issue of delayed pension processing for railway employees had also not been resolved, despite expectations that the General Manager's previous experience in using technology to streamline administrative processes would help address the problem.

The union argued that the head of the Railway Department requires expertise suited to a highly specialised engineering and operational institution, and warned that continued experimentation with the administration of the department could further affect the service.

The union said it would participate in the joint trade union action to draw the special attention of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake to the issues.
