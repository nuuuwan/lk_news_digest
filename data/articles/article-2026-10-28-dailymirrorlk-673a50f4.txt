Ashean Karthelis Fairway Holdings’ new Group MD

Recognising a contribution to organisational stability and strengthening leadership for group’s next phase of growth

Fairway Holdings (Pvt.) Ltd announced the appointment of Ashean Karthelis as Group Managing Director, marking an important milestone in the group’s continuing journey of growth, guided by its vision of ‘Creating Vibrant Communities’.

The appointment recognises Karthelis’ contribution over the past four and a half years in bringing greater structure, stability and strategic direction to Fairway’s diverse business portfolio.

Having served as Group Chief Executive Officer, he has helped strengthen organisational alignment, accountability and coordination across the group, establishing a foundation for its next phase of development.

As Group Managing Director, Karthelis will continue to provide overall group leadership across real estate, leisure, construction and interior solutions, renewable energy and other business interests, with an increased focus on driving growth, strengthening business performance and ensuring long-term sustainability.

Fairway Holdings Chairman Virath de Alwis said, “Ashean’s appointment as Group Managing Director is a recognition of the contribution he has made to Fairway over the past four and a half years. His commitment to bringing structure and stability across our businesses has helped strengthen the group’s foundations and prepare us for the opportunities ahead.” A defining influence on Karthelis’ leadership journey at Fairway was the two and a half years he spent working under the guidance and mentorship of the late Founder Chairman Hemaka de Alwis. This experience gave him a deeply rooted understanding of Fairway’s ethos, its entrepreneurial spirit and the founder’s belief in creating businesses that contribute meaningfully to people’s lives and communities.

His close working relationship with Virath de Alwis, established from the outset of his tenure, has complemented that grounding with a shared appreciation of the next generation’s ambitions for Fairway. Together, these relationships provide continuity between the group’s founding values and its future direction under Virath’s chairmanship.

Fairway Holdings Director Sundari de Alwis said, “Hemaka built Fairway with a vision that extended beyond individual businesses. He believed in creating vibrant communities and in the people who would help bring that vision to life. The time Ashean spent under his guidance gave him a valuable understanding of those beliefs and the values at the heart of Fairway.”
