SLICGL-Education Ministry commit to protect  schoolchildren

Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation General Ltd (SLICGL) and the Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Ministry share a commitment to protect Sri Lanka’s children throughout their school education.

The pledge is delivered through the Suraksha Student Insurance Scheme, which protects over four million schoolchildren.

The government of Sri Lanka, through the Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Ministry, provides the Suraksha Student Insurance Scheme free of charge to schoolchildren throughout the country.

SLICGL is proud to partner with the ministry in delivering this national initiative, which helps safeguard the students against the financial impact of unforeseen circumstances.

The eligible students include those aged five to 21 years in Grades 1-13 from government schools, government-approved private schools and Pirivena education as well as students aged four to 21 years in assisted special schools.

The scheme provides hospitalisation, outpatient and critical illness cover as well as personal accident cover for permanent and temporary disability, together with a life insurance benefit for children from low-income families on the death of a parent. The cover applies to incidents within and outside school premises.

For a child, an illness or accident can be far more than a temporary setback. It can place added pressure on a family and in some circumstances, disrupt schooling. Suraksha acts as a safety net, giving children and their families greater peace of mind and helping them stay in school despite life’s uncertainties.  The scheme is easily accessible through SLICGL’s islandwide network of 200 branches and customer service centres.
