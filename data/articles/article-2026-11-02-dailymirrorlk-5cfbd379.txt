Four spill gates opened at Deduru Oya Reservoir

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Four spill gates of the Deduru Oya Reservoir have been opened by one foot each due to heavy rainfall and rising water levels, the Irrigation Department said.

As a result, water is being released into the Deduru Oya at a rate of 2,800 cubic feet per second.

Residents living along the riverbanks downstream of the reservoir have been advised to remain vigilant as water levels may fluctuate.

The Irrigation Department has also urged residents to pay close attention to periodic announcements issued by the Department and Disaster Management authorities regarding the opening of spill gates.

The public has been advised to remain alert and take necessary precautions in response to any changes in water levels.
