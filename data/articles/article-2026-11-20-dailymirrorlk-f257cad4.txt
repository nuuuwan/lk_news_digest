2,100 cannabis plants seized in Monaragala forest reserve

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A suspect has been arrested after 2,100 illegally cultivated cannabis plants, estimated to be worth around Rs. 1 million, were discovered during a raid in the Unakanda Forest Reserve in Monaragala.

According to the Sri Lanka Army, the raid was carried out yesterday (19) by officers of the Monaragala Narcotics Unit, acting on information received from Army Intelligence.

During the operation, authorities discovered 2,100 cannabis plants, each measuring approximately three feet in height.

The suspect was arrested at the scene, while all the cannabis plants were seized by the authorities.
