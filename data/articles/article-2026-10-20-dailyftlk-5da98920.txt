President appoints Expert Committee to probe death of ‘Kavanthissa’

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has appointed an expert committee to investigate all aspects of the death of the tusker ‘Kavanthissa’, which occurred in the Siyambalangamuwa area of Galgamuwa on or around 16 October 2026, develop standard operating procedures for the welfare of wild elephants and make recommendations for a national elephant conservation program.

The committee is chaired by former Ministry Secretary A. H. Sarath Wijesinghe, with Professor T. Saminda Fernando, Professor A. G. Anil Pushpakumara, Professor U. K. Kalinga Padmalal, Dr. Jagath Gunawardena and Attorney-at-Law Charaka Jayaratne serving as members.

Additional Secretary to the President W. W. Harrison will serve as the committee’s coordinator.

The committee has been tasked with investigating the circumstances surrounding Kavanthissa’s death, including its immediate and underlying causes and determining whether any lapses or negligence occurred during its treatment. It will also develop standard operating procedures for the welfare of wild elephants and make recommendations for a national elephant conservation program.

The committee is authorised to seek assistance from both local and international experts in carrying out its work.

The committee is expected to submit a comprehensive report to the President within 30 days. The report will include its final findings on tusker Kavanthissa’s death, a proposed framework for standard operating procedures on wild elephant welfare and recommendations for a national elephant conservation program.
