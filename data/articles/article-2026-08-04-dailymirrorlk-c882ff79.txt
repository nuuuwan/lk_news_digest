Heart and Lung Transplant Programme fundraiser held

Venerable Rajawelle Subhuthi Thera, Director of Hospital Services Charity at the National Hospital of Sri Lanka; Dr. Sharmila Withana, MD, FCCP, FRCP, Consultant Respiratory Physician at Harefield Hospital Heart and Lung Transplant Centre, Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust, UK; and Dr. Asanka Senanayake, Director of Shine Hearts Charity, arrive at the inaugural Sri Lanka National Heart and Lung Transplant Programme Charity Fundraising Evening held at the Lotus Tower in Colombo.

The event was organised to raise awareness and funds to support the National Heart and Lung Transplant Programme, which provides critical care for patients with advanced heart failure, pulmonary dysfunction and pulmonary hypertension under Sri Lanka’s National Health Service. The fundraiser brought together healthcare leaders, government representatives, philanthropists and corporate partners to strengthen transplant services in Sri Lanka. Funds raised will support the Hospital Services Council Charity and Shine Hearts Charity in providing patient care, essential medicines, infrastructure development and other support services for the programme.
