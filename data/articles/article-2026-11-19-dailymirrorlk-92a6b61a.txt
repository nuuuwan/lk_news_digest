INSEE Lanka wins Gold at Presidential Environment Awards 2026

The INSEE Lanka team receiving the Gold Award from Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya

INSEE Lanka has been awarded the Gold Award of Commendation at the Presidential Environment Awards Ceremony 2026.

The award recognised the company’s excellent contribution to environmental sustainability within the Mineral Related Manufacturing Industry. The ceremony, organized by the Central Environmental Authority under the Ministry of Environment took place on 19 October, at the BMICH Main Hall.

Eng. Thusith Gunawarnasuriya, CEO, INSEE Lanka said, “This national-level recognition reaffirms that INSEE continues to demonstrate industrial growth, and environmental stewardship can coexist through responsible operations, innovation and long-term environmental commitment.”

Under the Climate and Energy pillar, INSEE continues to develop lower-carbon cement. In 2021, it became the pioneer to introduce Portland Composite Cement (PCC) to the market, reducing CO₂ emissions compared to conventional Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC). The company is focused on promoting low-carbon cement production, improving energy efficiency, and increasing thermal energy substitution through waste-derived alternative fuels, reducing dependency on fossil fuelssuch as coal.

Through INSEE Ecocycle, the company’s specialized waste management arm, a circular economy is driven utilizing cement kiln co-processing. The process transforms industrial waste into alternative fuel and raw materials in cement production, reducing reliance on coal. Additionally, the company repurposes fly ash, a waste by-product from Coal Power Plants, as a supplementary cementitious material to reduce clinker use, conserve natural resources and lower the carbon footprint of cement, as an industrial symbiosis initiative.

Parallel to these industrial innovative efforts, INSEE actively safeguards local biodiversity and ecosystems through the comprehensive rehabilitation of limestone quarries, an active animal rescue and release program at the Arruwakalu Quarry, mangrove restoration projects, rainwater harvesting, and expanded surface-water utilization.
