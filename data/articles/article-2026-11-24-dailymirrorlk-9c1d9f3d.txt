GMOA warns of tougher trade union action if Government ignores demands

Colombo, November 24 (Daily Mirror) - The Government Medical Officers' Association (GMOA) launched industrial action yesterday, accusing the Government of treating the health sector and medical professionals unfairly in the 2027 Budget.

GMOA Media Spokesman Dr. Yasitha Kannangara said the association had launched a week-long awareness campaign to highlight what it described as longstanding injustices faced by doctors.

As part of the campaign, the GMOA is using today's Full Moon Poya Day to raise awareness among the Maha Sangha about the monthly salary deductions exceeding Rs. 150,000 imposed on doctors across all grades.

He said the association would launch a public awareness campaign tomorrow in major cities across the country.

"If the Government fails to respond to our concerns by November 30, we will be compelled to launch more intensive trade union action," Dr. Kannangara said.

He warned that the GMOA was prepared to use all available trade union measures, ranging from protests to strike action, if the Government continued to ignore its demands.
