Heavy rain up to 100mm forecast for next 36 hours

Colombo, Sep. 24 (Daily Mirror) - Heavy showers of up to around 100 mm, strong winds and thunderstorms are expected in several parts of the country during the next 36 hours, the Meteorological Department warned today (24).

According to the weather forecast issued at 4.00 p.m., showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Southern, Central and North-Western provinces.

Heavy rainfall of around 100 mm is likely at some locations in these areas.

Meanwhile, fairly strong winds of 30-40 kmph are expected at times over the western slopes of the Central Hills, North-Central and North-Western provinces, as well as in Trincomalee, Hambantota and Matale districts.

The Department said the deep depression over the Bay of Bengal is weakening into a depression, and its impact on Sri Lanka's weather is expected to gradually decrease from tomorrow (25).

The public has been advised to take adequate precautions to minimise possible damage from temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
