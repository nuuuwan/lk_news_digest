USA-SL Chamber of Commerce’s New York campaign to promote Sri Lankan tourism, biz

The USA-Sri Lanka Chamber of Commerce is exploring an innovative promotional initiative to showcase Sri Lanka’s tourism, cultural heritage and business opportunities in the heart of New York City during the upcoming Christmas and holiday season.

The proposed campaign aims to bring Sri Lanka’s unique identity to one of the world’s most internationally recognised cities through a mobile digital billboard featuring high-quality photographs and videos highlighting the island’s natural beauty, rich heritage and diverse commercial offerings.

The initiative is designed to capitalise on New York City’s significant holiday-season foot traffic, reaching the American consumers, international tourists and potential business partners.

The digital showcase would feature Sri Lanka’s pristine beaches, scenic mountains, waterfalls, tea plantations, wildlife, ancient cities, Buddhist heritage, traditional arts, authentic cuisine and modern attractions, positioning the country as an attractive international travel destination.

A QR code linking to tourism information could also be incorporated, allowing the interested viewers to explore Sri Lanka’s attractions and travel opportunities.

Beyond destination promotion, the proposed initiative seeks to provide the Sri Lankan businesses with a platform to introduce their products and services to the American market. The businesses representing Ceylon Tea, spices, food products, gems and jewellery, apparel, handicrafts, wellness, hospitality and tourism, among other sectors, would have the opportunity to showcase their brands alongside the broader national promotional campaign.

The initiative could be particularly beneficial to the small and medium-sized Sri Lankan enterprises seeking greater international exposure, brand recognition and potential commercial partnerships in the United States.

By integrating tourism promotion with commercial visibility, the chamber aims to strengthen Sri Lanka’s international image while supporting opportunities for trade, investment and business development.

The proposed campaign also seeks to encourage collaboration among Sri Lankan businesses, tourism stakeholders and other organisations interested in promoting the country internationally.

Under the central message of ‘Discover Sri Lanka. Experience Sri Lanka. Visit Sri Lanka’, the campaign would present the island as a destination offering natural beauty, cultural richness, distinctive products and entrepreneurial potential.

Through this initiative, the USA-Sri Lanka Chamber of Commerce hopes to create a collective promotional platform that brings Sri Lanka closer to international audiences while opening new opportunities for Sri Lankan tourism and businesses in the United States. For more information visit www.usaslcc.org.
