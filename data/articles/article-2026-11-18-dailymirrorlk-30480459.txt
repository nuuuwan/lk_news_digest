AICPA & CIMA Sri Lanka to decode Budget 2027

Upcoming forum to bring together Treasury Secretary, business leaders and leading economists to explore implications of Sri Lanka’s Budget 2027

AICPA & CIMA Sri Lanka will host its highly anticipated Post Budget Forum 2026 on November 19, 2026, at 6:00 p.m., at the CIMA Auditorium, bringing together the policymakers, business leaders, economists, tax experts, investors and finance professionals to examine the implications of Budget 2027 and its impact on Sri Lanka’s economic future.

Held under the theme ‘Sri Lanka 2027 and Beyond: Budget Insights and Future Prospects’, the forum will provide a platform for insightful discussion and expert analysis of the policy measures shaping the country’s next phase of economic growth and development.

The keynote address will be delivered by Treasury Secretary Dr. Harshana Suriapperuma, FCMA (UK), CGMA, who will share valuable insights into the government’s economic priorities, fiscal strategy and policy direction for 2027 and beyond.

His perspectives will provide the attendees with a deeper understanding of the measures proposed in the budget and the broader vision for sustaining economic growth, strengthening fiscal resilience and driving long-term national development.

Also addressing the forum will be Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Deputy Minister Chathuranga Abeysinghe, FCMA (UK), CGMA, who will speak on the topic ‘From Stability to Strength: Making Industry and Enterprise the Engine of Sri Lanka’s 2027 Growth’.

Drawing on both the global economic shifts and domestic industrial realities, his address will examine how the local enterprises can enhance competitiveness, navigate the evolving international supply chains and leverage the Budget 2027 policies to scale innovation and export-led growth.

The forum will also feature a distinguished line-up of industry experts who will analyse the budget from multiple perspectives.

John Keells Holdings PLC Deputy Chairperson and Group Finance Director Gihan Cooray, FCMA (UK), CGMA, will discuss the business implications of Budget 2027, while Advocata Chief Executive Officer Dhananath Fernando will examine the broader macroeconomic impact on the businesses and economy.

KPMG Sri Lanka Principal Tax and Regulatory Suresh R.I. Perera, FCMA (UK), CGMA, will provide a comprehensive analysis of the budget’s tax proposals and their implications to the business sector.

The speakers will be joined by an eminent panel comprising Brandix Group Managing Director Hasitha Premaratne, FCMA (UK), CGMA, Jetwing Travels Managing Director Shiromal Cooray, FCMA (UK), CGMA, Finance and Planning Ministry Tax Policy Advisor Thanuja Perera and First Capital Holdings PLC Managing Director and CEO and Janashakthi Group Deputy CEO Dilshan Wirasekara.

Moderated by BDO Sri Lanka Managing Partner Ashane Jayasekara, FCMA (UK), CGMA, the discussion will bring diverse perspectives on the economic, fiscal, tax and business implications of Budget 2027, exploring how the organisations can navigate the opportunities and challenges ahead.

AICPA & CIMA’s annual Budget Forums are among the most anticipated post-budget knowledge platforms attracting senior finance professionals, CEOs, business leaders, policymakers, entrepreneurs, academics and students.

Renowned for the comprehensive analysis, practical insights and high-calibre speaker panels, these forums provide the participants a valuable opportunity to understand the economic, regulatory, tax and business implications of the budget directly from the policymakers and industry experts.

The forum is open to the CIMA members, students, professionals and public. The registration fee is Rs.3,500 for students and members and Rs.4,000 for non-members.
