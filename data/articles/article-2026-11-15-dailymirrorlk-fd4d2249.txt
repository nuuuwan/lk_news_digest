Saudi-led coalition says it intercepts 2 Houthi ballistic missiles targeting Riyadh

AA - The Saudi-led coalition said early Sunday that its air defenses intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by Yemen's Houthis toward Riyadh, the Saudi capital.

Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki announced the interceptions on US social media platform X.

The coalition said earlier that its air defenses intercepted and destroyed two drones above Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province.

In a subsequent statement, al-Maliki said another drone targeting the same region was intercepted and destroyed. He did not provide further details about the drone or its origin.

On Saturday, the coalition said its air defenses intercepted and destroyed two Houthi drones targeting Riyadh and southern regions.

The interceptions come amid escalating hostilities between the Houthis and Saudi Arabia, with repeated missile and drone attacks targeting the kingdom.

The Saudi-led coalition intervened in Yemen in 2015 to support the internationally recognized government after the Houthis seized the capital, Sanaa, and other parts of the country.
