SDB bank leads dedicated two- and three-wheeler leasing services at Colombo Motor Show

SDB bank is set to strengthen its commitment to accessible vehicle financing and entrepreneurial empowerment through its participation at the Colombo Motor Show 2026, scheduled to take place from 20 to 23 November at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH), Colombo. The Bank will place particular emphasis on leasing solutions for two-wheelers and three-wheelers, recognising their importance in supporting everyday mobility, self-employment and small business activities.

SDB bank aims to further strengthen its position as a key financing partner for individuals and businesses seeking practical vehicle ownership solutions.

At this year’s exhibition, the Bank will focus on its distinctive position as the banking sector’s dedicated leasing provider for two-wheelers and three-wheelers, while also offering financing facilities for private vehicles, SMEs and other customer segments.

Visitors to stalls F4 and F5 will have the opportunity to explore SDB bank’s leasing facilities, obtain personalised financial guidance and benefit from convenient on-site consultations.

Commenting on the initiative, Manoj Akmeemana, Chief Executive Officer of SDB bank said, “Access to reliable transportation can create meaningful opportunities for individuals and entrepreneurs. Through our participation at the Colombo Motor Show, we aim to make vehicle financing more accessible, convenient and responsive to our customers’ needs, while supporting their aspirations for economic progress and financial independence.”
