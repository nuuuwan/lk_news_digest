Parliament approves Rs. 17.2 bn supplementary estimate for electricity relief

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Parliament today approved Supplementary Estimate No. 05 of 2026, under the Ministry of Energy, providing Rs. 17,213 million in financial assistance to offer relief to the public on electricity bills.

The funds will be reallocated from provisions initially allocated to various line ministries for 2026 but identified as unlikely to be fully utilized by the end of the year.

The reallocation will not affect the overall expenditure ceiling approved under the Appropriation Act, No. 23 of 2025, or the Government’s maximum borrowing limit.

The Supplementary Estimate was considered and approved by the Committee on Public Finance yesterday (22).
