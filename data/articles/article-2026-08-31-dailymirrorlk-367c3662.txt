Suspected ‘ICE’ consignment discovered at Colombo Port

Colombo, August 31 (Daily Mirror) - A consignment of drugs suspected to be “ICE” has been discovered inside a container at the Colombo Port, police said.

The suspected drugs were found concealed inside towels packed in the container, according to police.

Initial investigations have revealed that the container had reportedly been brought into Sri Lanka from Pakistan.

Officers of the Central Crime Investigation Bureau are conducting further investigations into the discovery.
