Public can now ‘Ask Handun’ via WhatsApp voice messages

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The public can now send questions, requests for clarification and suggestions concerning the services of the Ministry of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development directly to the Minister through WhatsApp voice messages.

The initiative, titled “Ask Handun”, allows members of the public to send voice messages to 077 554 1646 regarding any issue related to the Ministry’s services.

According to the announcement, questions submitted by the public will be reviewed based on their importance, and answers from the Minister will be published through the Minister’s Facebook page.
