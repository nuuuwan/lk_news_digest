Secondary market remains subdued as T-Bill auction yields continue to ease

Similar to the previous trading session, the secondary yield curve experienced a subdued trading session yesterday, with moderate trading volumes and activity. Market activity was relatively low, as participation from both banks and primary dealers remained moderate.

Among the traded maturities, the 15.10.2028 and 15.12.2028 maturities traded within a range of 10.75% to 10.77%, while the 01.08.2030 maturity changed hands between 11.70% to 11.75%. Meanwhile, the 15.01.2033 maturity traded at a rate of12.25%.

Moreover, the PDMO concluded its weekly T-Bill auction, successfully raising the full initial offer of Rs. 140.0bn. The 3M and 6M bills raised Rs. 71.9bn and Rs. 50.0bn respectively. The 12M bill raised Rs. 18.1bn, lower than the initial offer of Rs. 30.0bn. The weighted average yields of the 3M and 6M bills adjusted down by 9bps and 3bps respectively to stand at 9.86% and 10.21% while that of the 12M bill remained unchanged at 10.20%.

On the external front, the LKR remained flat at 336.25/USD, compared to Monday. Overnight liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs. 165.96bn from Rs. 173.80bn recorded previously.
