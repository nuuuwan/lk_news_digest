Four gates opened at Deduru Oya Reservoir

Four radial gates of the Deduru Oya Reservoir have been opened to release water at a rate of 4,200 cubic feet per second (cusecs), the Irrigation Department said.

As of 7.00 p.m. today (9), the reservoir's water level stood at 70.72 metres above mean sea level (MSL), close to its full supply level of 70.79 metres.

Two gates have been opened by 24 inches each, while the other two have been opened by 12 inches each.

The reservoir's water capacity at the time was reported at 59,651 acre-feet, Irrigation Director (Water Management) Engineer H.M.P.S.D. Hearth said.
