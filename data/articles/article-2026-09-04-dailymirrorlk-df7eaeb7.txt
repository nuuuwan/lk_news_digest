Legal fraternity voices concern as experienced judge retires without elevation to SC

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Court of Appeal Justice Rathanapriya Gurusinghe retired on September 1, prompting renewed concerns within the legal community over the failure to elevate an experienced career judge to the Supreme Court despite the existence of four vacancies.

Justice Gurusinghe began his judicial career on March 5, 1996. Over nearly three decades he served as Magistrate and District Judge in Colombo, Nawalapitiya, Gampola, Avissawella, Chilaw, Matara, Matale and Kandy before his elevation to the Court of Appeal.

At the farewell, Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) President Rajeev Amarasuriya paid tribute to Justice Gurusinghe’s record on the Bench. He praised the retiring judge for discharging his duties with dignity, patience and fairness.

“We are aware that the former Chief Justice, Her Ladyship Justice Murdu Fernando, PC, recommended Your Lordship Justice Gurusinghe for elevation to the Supreme Court. As far as we are aware, that recommendation is still pending at the President’s Office,” Mr Amarasuriya said.

“On behalf of the Bar, I wish to place on record our sincere appreciation and gratitude for Your Lordship’s dedicated service to the Judiciary and to the people of Sri Lanka. I am confident that Your Lordship will continue to serve the profession and our system in varied and important capacities,” Amarasuriya added.
