Sri Lanka aims to become regional digital financial hub: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka aims to strengthen its digital financial ecosystem and position itself as an emerging digital financial hub by expanding digital payment infrastructure, cross-border transactions and international partnerships, Deputy Minister of Digital Economy said.

Sharing the Government's vision for the digital economy at the exhibition day of the Sri Lanka FinTech Festival 2026, the Deputy Minister said Sri Lanka needed to align its financial ecosystem with global trends while creating opportunities to access regional markets.

Strengthening public-private sector partnerships would be essential to achieving this objective, the Deputy Minister said.

The festival brought together banks, financial institutions, technology companies and international partners to exchange knowledge, experiences and innovations in the financial technology sector.

The event was organised by FinTech Forum Sri Lanka in collaboration with the Ministry of Digital Economy, with sponsorship from Visa.

The discussions also focused on strengthening Sri Lanka's digital payment infrastructure while expanding cross-border financial transactions and investment opportunities.
