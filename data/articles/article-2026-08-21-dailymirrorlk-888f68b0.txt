Over 1,600 fatal road accidents reported so far this year

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 1,654 fatal road accidents have been reported in Sri Lanka from the beginning of the year to yesterday (20), Deputy Inspector General of Police in charge of the Traffic Control and Road Safety Division W.P.J. Senadheera said.

He said 1,749 people had been killed in these fatal road accidents during the period.

Meanwhile, 4,556 people were referred for treatment following serious road accidents, while 7,295 people were treated for injuries sustained in minor accidents, he said.

A total of 3,232 cases involving property damage were also reported during the period.

Of those killed in road accidents, 576 were pedestrians, 617 were motorcyclists, 122 were pillion riders, 119 were cyclists, 139 were drivers and 173 were passengers, Senadheera said.
