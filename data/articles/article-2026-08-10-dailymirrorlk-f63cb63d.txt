SLPP General Secretary appears before CCIB

Colombo, Aug. 10(Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) General Secretary Sagara Kariyawasam appeared before the Colombo Central Crimes Investigation Bureau (CCIB) this morning in connection with an ongoing investigation.

Kariyawasam was summoned to provide a statement regarding remarks allegedly made about the Inspector General of Police (IGP).

The investigation was initiated recently on the instructions of the IGP following reports of a statement allegedly made concerning him.
