Bairaha Farms stands out at Pro-food Pro-pack & Agbiz 2026

Participants with Bairaha Farms Brand Manager Ravin Madushanka (centre) and other representatives at the event

Bairaha Farms PLC recently earned high praise for its creative participation at the Pro-Food Pro-Pack & Agbiz 2026 trade expo.

Organised by the Sri Lanka Food Processors’ Association (SLFPA), the Pro-food Pro-pack &Agbiz 2026 trade expo was held from August 21st to 23rd at the BMICH, Colombo.

Bairaha came forward with bold concepts to conduct creative ways of preparing delectable chicken dishes with the participation of culinary enthusiasts.

Bairaha contributed to the Home Cooking Challenge, which was organized by SLFPA.

A total number of 12 selected housewives competed against each other by preparing creative dishes using Bairaha products presented their talent and skill to the audience at the expo.
