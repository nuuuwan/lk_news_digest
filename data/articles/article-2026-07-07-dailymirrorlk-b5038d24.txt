Nutrition awareness session...

Save a Life Conducts Nutrition Awareness Session Under CARE Programme: Save a Life organised a nutrition awareness session under its CARE Programme for parents of preschool children from St. Emanuel, St. Michael, and Sri Ganesha preschools. The programme highlighted the importance of providing balanced and nutritious meals to support children's healthy growth, development, and overall well-being. Parents received practical guidance on child nutrition and healthy eating habits as part of the organisation's ongoing efforts to empower families and strengthen community well-being through awareness, education, and sustainable development initiatives.
