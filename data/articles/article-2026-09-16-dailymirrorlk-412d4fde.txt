Widespread rain expected across Sri Lanka over next 10 days

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Widespread rainfall is expected across the island over the next 10 days, while preparations should be made to face possible heavy rain, landslides and floods, the Meteorology Department said.

Speaking at a media briefing today (16), Department official Ajith Wijayamanne said preparations should be made to face the possible impacts of the El Niño phenomenon, particularly in view of the forecast for heavy rainfall and related hazards.

He said higher rainfall is expected during October and November.

Wijayamanne explained that October and November are generally periods of atmospheric instability in the Bay of Bengal, and that these conditions could intensify under the influence of El Niño.

He highlighted the need to closely monitor the situation and said increased rainfall is expected in the northeastern parts of the country during December.

Meanwhile, he said normal rainfall is expected in the Northern and Eastern Provinces, but warned that the Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) phenomenon could increase the risk of flooding in those areas.

He further said weather conditions characterised by higher temperatures and lower rainfall are expected during February, March and April next year.

Wijayamanne highlighted the importance of properly managing the rainfall expected to affect the island during the coming months.
