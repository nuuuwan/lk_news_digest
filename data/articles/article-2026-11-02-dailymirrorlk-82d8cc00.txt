Samsung expands easy payment facility

Samsung Sri Lanka has expanded its easy payment facility through Lesi Pay by Dialog Finance, making convenient monthly instalment options available at approximately 400 authorised Samsung outlets across the country, in addition to the Dialog showrooms.

The expanded facility aims to reach 1,000 outlets by end-2026, improving access to genuine Samsung smartphones and tablets across Sri Lanka.

Through Lesi Pay by Dialog Finance, the eligible customers can purchase genuine Samsung devices with monthly instalments starting from Rs.3,657, without requiring a credit card or guarantor, with repayment periods of up to 18 months.

The customers purchasing through the authorised channels also benefit from Samsung’s official warranty and the Samsung Break-Free offer, available for up to 24 months, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

The facility covers selected Samsung smartphones and tablets, including Galaxy A07, Galaxy F07, Galaxy F06 5G, Galaxy A17 5G, Galaxy M17 5G and Galaxy Tab A11.

Offered by Dialog Finance, Lesi Pay is a loan facility designed to help the eligible customers spread the cost of selected devices through structured monthly repayments. The facility removes the need for a credit card or guarantor, while allowing the customers to check their eligibility through the Dialog Pay app before selecting a device.

The loan approval, repayment terms and applicable interest rates are determined by Dialog Finance, subject to eligibility and the relevant terms and conditions.

To purchase a device, the customers can download the Dialog Pay app, check their eligibility, select an eligible Samsung device and collect it from a participating outlet.

The facility is available through participating Dialog showrooms and authorised Samsung retailers, making it more convenient for the customers across Sri Lanka to access the service.

Samsung Sri Lanka and Maldives Managing Director Dongkyun Lee said, “Expanding Dialog Lesi Pay to our authorised retail network is an important step towards making genuine Samsung devices more accessible to the customers across Sri Lanka. By offering convenient instalment options through more retail locations, we are giving the customers greater flexibility when purchasing their preferred devices, supported by the assurance of Samsung’s official warranty and after-sales services.”

Dialog Finance PLC CEO and Director Nazeem Mohammed said, “At Dialog Finance, our focus is on making financing simpler, more accessible and relevant to the everyday needs of Sri Lankans. Lesi Pay reflects this commitment by enabling the customers to own the devices they need through convenient and flexible monthly instalments, without the requirement for a credit card or guarantor. Expanding this facility across Samsung’s authorised retail network is an important step in bringing accessible financing closer to more customers islandwide. Through partnerships such as this, we aim to bridge the gap between financial accessibility and digital connectivity, empowering more Sri Lankans to benefit from technology that supports their daily lives.”
