Cinnamon Box Office launches Destination Event Calendar

John Keells Group Senior Vice President Global Alliances and Partnerships and Head of Corporate Affairs Dileep Mudadeniya with representatives of ‘This Is Michael – Relive the Legend’ partners: Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts Senior Vice President Colombo Hotels and Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Sri Lanka General Manager Kamal Munasinghe, Ceylon Cold Stores Ltd Assistant Brand Manager Imorich Otara Wickramarathne, Wijeya Digital and Wijeya Networks Head of Advertising Indika Jayamaha, Emerging Media (Pvt.) Unveils a new era of entertainment with world’s leading Michael Jackson tribute concert ‘This Is Michael – Relive the Legend’, presented by City of Dreams Sri Lanka

Cinnamon Box Office (CBO), the strategic ticketing engine connecting audiences, event partners and world-class productions, introduced its Destination Event Calendar at the official launch event held on August 6, 2026, at City of Dreams Sri Lanka, marking a significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s evolving entertainment landscape and reinforcing Colombo’s ambition to emerge as a vibrant regional hub for culture, creativity and entertainment.

As part of its expanding Destination Event Calendar, CBO introduced ‘This Is Michael – Relive the Legend’, the world’s premier Michael Jackson tribute show, celebrating the music and legacy of the King of Pop, presented by City of Dreams Sri Lanka.

The live show will take place on November 8 and 9, 2026, at The Podium, City of Dreams Sri Lanka, bringing this internationally acclaimed production to Sri Lankan audiences.

Recognised internationally as a leading Michael Jackson tribute production, ‘This Is Michael – Relive the Legend’ recreates the energy, artistry and spectacle of the King of Pop’s legendary performances through an electrifying concert production.

With events of this nature, CBO supports the growth of Sri Lanka’s creative economy by connecting audiences, event partners and globally recognised productions.

Speaking at the launch, John Keells Group Senior Vice President Global Alliances and Partnerships and Head of Corporate Affairs Dileep Mudadeniya said, “Cinnamon Box Office is more than a ticketing platform; it is an ecosystem that brings together entertainment, hospitality and tourism to create world-class experiences. The launch of events such as ‘This Is Michael – Relive the Legend’ reflects our commitment to bringing internationally acclaimed productions to Sri Lanka and strengthening Colombo’s position as a destination for culture, creativity and live entertainment.”

Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts Senior Vice President Colombo Hotels and Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Sri Lanka General Manager Kamal Munasinghe added, “City of Dreams Sri Lanka represents a new chapter for Colombo, where hospitality, entertainment, business and culture come together to create experiences that are meaningful for Sri Lankans and visitors from around the world. Entertainment plays an important role in this journey by giving people more reasons to visit, stay longer and experience what Sri Lanka has to offer. CBO builds on this foundation to introduce new experiences to the country.”

City of Dreams Sri Lanka presents ‘This Is Michael – Relive the Legend’, together with Imorich as the Indulgence Partner, Wijeya Newspapers as the Exclusive Print Media Partner, Daily Mirror Online, The Sun and Hi Online as the Exclusive Digital Media Partner, Emerging Media as the Outdoor Digital Media Partner, Scope Cinemas as the Cinema Partner and E FM as the Radio Partner.
