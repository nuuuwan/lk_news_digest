Cannabis worth Rs. 106m seized in air cargo

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Customs has detected more than 10.6 kilograms of suspected cannabis-based narcotics concealed inside an air cargo consignment that arrived from Bangkok, Thailand.

According to Customs, the consignment arrived as a gift shipment on SriLankan Airlines flight UL405 on July 24, 2026.

The shipment was examined at the TRICO UPB Warehouse on July 31 in the presence of an authorised representative, during which officers discovered polythene packets suspected to contain narcotic substances hidden inside metal containers.

A final inventory revealed 10.608 kilograms (including wrapping) of cannabis-related narcotics, including marijuana, hashish and hashish oil.

The seized narcotics have an estimated street value of Rs. 106.08 million.
