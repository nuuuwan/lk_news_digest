DFCC Aloka inspires future leaders

DFCC Bank, through its women-focused banking proposition DFCC Aloka, supported Avenza, an exclusive professional educational summit designed to inspire the students awaiting state university admission and help them prepare for the next stage of their academic and professional journeys.

The summit, organised by renowned ICT educator Bhanuka Ekanayake, brought together 350 students for a programme focused on learning, personal development, leadership, innovation and opportunity.

DFCC Aloka’s participation added a professional and leadership perspective to the forum, encouraging the students to think beyond the academic achievement and prepare for the wider demands of the future workplace.

DFCC Bank Vice President and Pinnacle and Aloka Proposition Head Shera Hassen was invited as a distinguished guest speaker at the summit. In her session, she shared practical insights on leadership, embracing opportunity, continuous learning and building the confidence needed to navigate academic and career transitions.

The engagement reflects DFCC Bank’s broader focus on supporting growth-oriented communities by contributing to knowledge-sharing platforms that benefit future generations.

At a time when young people are entering higher education and preparing for careers in a rapidly changing economy, exposure to industry perspectives can help them make more informed choices and develop a stronger sense of direction.

Hassen said, “Students awaiting university admission are at an important point in their lives. The choices they make, the habits they build and the opportunities they choose to pursue can shape the way they contribute to society and the economy in the years ahead. Platforms such as Avenza give young people the space to think boldly, learn from different perspectives and prepare themselves with confidence. Through DFCC Aloka, we are pleased to support initiatives that encourage leadership, ambition and lifelong learning.”

The session was well received by participants, who engaged actively with the themes discussed. The programme offered the students an opportunity to gain practical guidance from the professionals and educators while reflecting on the skills and mindset required for personal and professional success.

DFCC Bank’s support for Avenza strengthens its role as a financial institution that contributes not only through banking solutions but also through initiatives that promote knowledge, confidence and long-term development.

As Sri Lanka’s development finance pioneer, DFCC Bank continues to engage with communities and emerging talent in ways that support individual progress and national growth.
