Top A/L students receive laptops

Laptop.lk Chairman of Technocity Pvt. Ltd. Famy Ismail presents a Lenovo laptop to an outstanding G.C.E. Advanced Level student representing one of Sri Lanka’s 25 districts, at a national recognition ceremony held at The Address Colombo. The initiative by Laptop.lk aims to empower high-achieving students with digital tools to support their higher education and future careers. The event was attended by A.B.M. Ashraff, Additional Secretary to the Prime Minister’s Office, Lenovo representatives, Ministry of Education officials, principals, teachers, parents and award-winning students from across the country.
