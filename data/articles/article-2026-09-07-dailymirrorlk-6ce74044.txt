Rs. 130 Mn assets frozen in Money Laundering probe

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police have frozen properties worth more than Rs. 130 million allegedly acquired through proceeds from illegal drug trafficking, the Illegal Assets Investigation Division said.

The investigation follows a complaint lodged by the Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) for Embilipitiya and is being conducted under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act.

The assets, belonging to two individuals suspected of laundering money obtained through narcotics trafficking, include a three-storey house on one acre and two roods of land in Kavanthispura, Embilipitiya, valued at around Rs. 70 million.

A second three-storey residence located on a 23.7-perch plot in Bimbaduyaya, New Town, Embilipitiya, is valued at approximately Rs. 60 million.

Police said restraining orders were issued on September 7, 2026, freezing both properties for 14 days under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act.

Further investigations are underway into the suspects and the source of the funds used to acquire the properties.
