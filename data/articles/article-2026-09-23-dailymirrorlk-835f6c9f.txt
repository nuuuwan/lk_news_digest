Deep depression to cross coastal areas of India tonight: Met. Department

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department said the Deep Depression over the west-central and adjoining northwest Bay of Bengal is likely to move west-northwestwards and cross the coastal areas of northern Andhra Pradesh and southern Odisha, India, during tonight.

The Department said although this system will not have a direct impact on Sri Lanka, the prevailing rainy conditions in the southwestern part of the island are expected to continue due to the wind flow towards the system.

It said showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and North-western provinces and in Galle and Matara Districts while heavy falls above 100 mm are likely at some places in the above areas.

It said a few showers will occur in North-central province and in Mannar district. Strong winds of about (40-50) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of the Central hills, North-central and North-western provinces and in Trincomalee, Hambantota and Matale Districts.

The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
