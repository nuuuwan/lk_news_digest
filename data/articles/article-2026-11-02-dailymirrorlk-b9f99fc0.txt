Sampath Bank appoints Hashindra Silva as Non-Executive, Non-Independent Director

Sampath Bank PLC has appointed Hashindra Silva as a Non-Executive, Non-Independent Director, bringing to the Bank’s leadership extensive cross-sector business experience and a strong background in strategic planning, commercial management and corporate governance.

Silva is posesses experience spanning Sri Lanka’s automotive, vehicle after-sales, motor spare parts, hospitality and property development sectors. He currently serves as an Executive Director of Indra Traders (Pvt) Limited, Indra Service Park (Pvt) Limited, Indra Motor Spares (Pvt) Limited and Indra Property Development (Pvt) Limited. He also serves as a Non-Executive Director of Braybrooke Residential Properties (Pvt) Limited and Indra Hotels and Resorts (Pvt) Limited.

Educated at Trinity College, Kandy, Silva holds a Bachelor of Science (Honours) degree in Electronic and Electrical Engineering from the University of Sunderland, United Kingdom, and a Master of Business Administration from Cardiff Metropolitan University, United Kingdom.

He is a member of the Chamber of Young Lankan Entrepreneurs (COYLE), the Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID) and the Vehicle Importers Association of Sri Lanka (VIASL). He also served as a Committee Member of the Young Directors Forum (YDF) during 2024/2025, reflecting his engagement with the wider business community and corporate governance networks.
