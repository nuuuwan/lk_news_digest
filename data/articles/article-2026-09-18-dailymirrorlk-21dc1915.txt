El Niño may push up electricity demand by February: Minister

Colombo, Sep. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Ports, Civil Aviation and Energy Minister Anura Karunathilaka said electricity demand could rise further by February next year due to the impact of El Niño conditions.

Speaking at an event marking a key development in the country’s renewable energy sector, the Minister said Sri Lanka’s electricity sector was undergoing a significant transformation, with the Government focusing on expanding renewable energy, reducing generation costs and ensuring a reliable and uninterrupted power supply.

He said the country currently generates nearly 3,000 MW of solar power during the daytime, while average daytime demand is around 2,800 MW. However, demand increases to about 3,100 MW at night and is expected to rise further under El Niño conditions.

“The challenge has been that we have not had sufficient capacity to store the excess solar power generated during the day,” he said.

To address this, the Government is implementing battery storage projects with a combined capacity of 160 MW, of which 130 MW is being undertaken by WINDFORCE. The Minister said the full 160 MW capacity was expected to be connected to the national grid before the end of this year.

He said bidding had also commenced for larger battery storage projects with capacities of 400 MW, 250 MW and 150 MW, with some already at the evaluation stage. These projects are expected to be integrated into the national grid by the first half of 2027.

The Minister said expanding battery storage would allow Sri Lanka to integrate more renewable energy into the national grid while reducing reliance on costly fossil-fuel-based electricity generation at night.

He said the projects would support the Government’s objectives of increasing renewable energy use, generating cleaner electricity, reducing generation costs and providing consumers with an affordable, efficient and reliable power supply.

The 10 MW/40 MWh Battery Energy Storage System constructed in Anuradhapura was connected to the national electricity grid yesterday under the patronage of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

The energy storage system has been installed with the contribution of Storex (Private) Limited, with the aim of increasing the country’s renewable energy capacity and enhancing the stability of the electricity system.
