Former Defence Secretary Hemasiri Fernando sentenced to death over Easter Sunday attacks

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Trial-at-Bar today sentenced former Defence Secretary Hemasiri Fernando to death after finding him guilty on several charges, including criminal dereliction of duty for failing to take action to prevent the Easter Sunday terrorist attacks despite receiving prior intelligence warnings.

The judgment was delivered by a majority decision, with Judges Priyantha Liyanage and Tilakaratne Bandara finding former IGP Pujith Jayasundara guilty and sentencing him to death. However, Judge Viraj Weerasuriya dissented, acquitting the accused.

The Attorney General had separately indicted former Defence Secretary Hemasiri Fernando and former IGP Pujith Jayasundara before the Colombo Trial-at-Bar on charges including criminal negligence, dereliction of duty and other offences arising from their alleged failure to act on intelligence information warning of the imminent Easter Sunday terrorist attacks.

On February 18, 2022, the then Trial-at-Bar bench acquitted both accused without calling for their defence after the prosecution concluded leading its evidence.

The Attorney General subsequently appealed to the Supreme Court, seeking to have that acquittal set aside on the grounds that it was contrary to law.

Allowing the appeal, the Supreme Court held that the Trial-at-Bar had erred in law by acquitting the accused without calling for their defence. The Court ruled that the accused should have been called upon to present their defence before any verdict was delivered and ordered that the cases be retried.
