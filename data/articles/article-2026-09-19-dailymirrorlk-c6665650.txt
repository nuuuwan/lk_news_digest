England sweep Sri Lanka 3-0 to reclaim No. 1 T20I ranking

SEP.19 (Mirror Sports) - England crushed Sri Lanka by eight wickets in the third T20I at Old Trafford to complete a 3-0 series sweep and reclaim the top spot in the men’s T20 international rankings.

England’s bowlers restricted Sri Lanka to 124 all out in 18.5 overs before the hosts raced to 126-2 in just 9.4 overs, sealing victory with 62 balls to spare.

Jamie Overton led the bowling effort with an economical spell of 1-15 from four overs, while Sonny Baker continued his impressive progress with figures of 2-17. Jofra Archer also claimed two wickets for 23 runs, while Will Jacks finished with 2-31.

Pathum Nissanka top-scored for Sri Lanka with 36 off 24 balls, while Kamindu Mendis contributed 35 off 28 deliveries. However, England’s disciplined bowling and sharp fielding kept the visitors under pressure throughout their innings.

Jos Buttler powered England’s chase with an unbeaten 62 off 32 balls, while Harry Brook added 33 from 21 deliveries. The pair shared an 85-run second-wicket partnership after Aneurin Donald fell for 13.

Brook was run out before Buttler sealed the victory in style with a six, completing a dominant win for the hosts.

The victory marked England’s 23rd win in 26 completed T20 internationals since Brook took charge in 2024. The clean sweep also saw England move ahead of India to return to No. 1 in the world rankings.

England will now turn their attention to the three-match ODI series against Sri Lanka, which begins in Durham on Tuesday, as they continue preparations for the 2027 Cricket World Cup in southern Africa.
