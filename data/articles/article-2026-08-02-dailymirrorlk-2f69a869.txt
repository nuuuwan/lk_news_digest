Namal accuses government for Mahara prison unrest

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Claiming that the unrest at the Mahara prison is no longer an isolated incident, Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) MP Namal Rajapaksa urged the government to stop firefighting and start fixing a prison system that is showing clear signs of institutional failure.

He said in a post on X that this is the second major prison disturbance in a matter of weeks and that it is the result of poor management,  negligence and a prison system that is under immense strain.

"It is no longer an isolated incident. It is the result of poor management,  negligence and a prison system that is under immense strain," he said.

"This is no longer about one isolated event. It is about poor management, a lack of leadership and a prison system that has been allowed to deteriorate. Even today, the Department of Prisons continues without a permanently appointed Commissioner General, despite the crisis unfolding before us. Why has this critical position still not been filled properly?" he asked.

He said deploying more security may contain the situation for a day, but it will not fix the problem.

"Overcrowding, inadequate facilities, poor healthcare and the lack of meaningful rehabilitation demand urgent attention," he further said.
