Namal Rajapaksa to remain in custody till produced before Colombo Permanent Trial-at-Bar

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama today ordered that Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa be kept in custody until he is produced before the Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar on October 29 in connection with the alleged bribery incident involving the Airbus deal.

The court also directed that the suspect be produced tomorrow before the Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar, where the indictment against him has been filed.

Delivering the order, the Magistrate stated that since an indictment had already been filed before the Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar in connection with the incident, the Magistrate’s Court was no longer in a position to issue any judicial order concerning the suspect in relation to the case.

The Magistrate further stated that bail would not be granted as no exceptional circumstances had been presented to justify the release of the suspect on bail.

The Magistrate added that, as the indictment had already been filed before the Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar, the Magistrate’s Court could no longer issue an order either granting bail or remanding the suspect in connection with the case.

Accordingly, the Magistrate ordered that Rajapaksa be kept in custody until he is produced before the Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar tomorrow.
