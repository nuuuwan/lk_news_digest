Are we compelled to buy accessories with a gas cylinder?

A few months ago, I placed an order with a gas company on behalf of my son for the purchase of an empty LPG cylinder. Recently, I was informed by a representative of the company that a 12.5 kg empty cylinder was available through one of their authorised dealers, and I was provided with the dealer’s contact number.

However, when I contacted the dealer, I was informed that I could not purchase the cylinder alone. The dealer insisted that I also purchase an accessory package consisting of a regulator and hose pipe, costing approximately Rs. 4,000, as a condition for obtaining the cylinder.

I immediately brought this matter to the attention of the company’s representative and indicated that I would consider lodging a complaint with the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA), as such a conditional sale appeared unfair and potentially detrimental to consumers who already possess the required accessories.

Following my complaint, the  representative promptly arranged for another dealer who agreed to sell the cylinder without requiring the purchase of the accessory package.

I wish to bring this matter to the attention of readers so that they are aware of their rights as consumers. If a dealer insists that an LPG cylinder can only be purchased together with a regulator, hose pipe, or any other accessory that is not required by the customer, consumers should seek clarification from the supplier and, where necessary, bring the matter to the attention of the CAA.

Consumers should be free to purchase only the products they need and should not be compelled to incur additional expenses on unwanted accessories.
