President’s Office denies reports of President’s hospitalisation

Colombo, Oct 24 (Daily Mirror); The President’s Office has denied reports circulating on social media claiming that President Anura Kumara Dissanayake had been admitted to a private hospital following a sudden illness.

The President’s Media Division confirmed that the reports were false and urged the public not to be misled by unverified information circulating online.

It also requested the public to refrain from sharing such reports without first verifying their accuracy through official sources.
