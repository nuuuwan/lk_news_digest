Medihelp Hospitals wins six awards at NCQP 2026

Medihelp Hospital, Mount Lavinia achieved a milestone in healthcare excellence at the National Convention on Quality and Productivity (NCQP) 2026, securing three Gold Awards and three Silver Awards. Every quality improvement project presented by the hospital received national recognition, positioning the institution among Sri Lanka’s leading healthcare organizations dedicated to operational and clinical advancement.

Organized annually by the Sri Lanka Association for the Advancement of Quality and Productivity (SLAAQP), this year’s convention was held under the theme “People-Driven Quality and Productivity for a Resilient Sri Lanka.” The premier national platform benchmarks organizations that deliver tangible, measurable improvements through robust quality management frameworks.

Medihelp’s six award-winning initiatives, developed by multidisciplinary teams, targeted everyday healthcare challenges. Rather than relying on large-scale, top-down transformation programmes, the frontline teams focused on practical improvements to optimize resource utilization, reduce patient waiting times, strengthen clinical processes, and enhance overall patient safety.

Commenting on the achievement, Shammi Perera, General Manager of Medihelp Hospitals, attributed the success to the dedication and teamwork of the staff, noting that quality is an organizational culture rather than a destination. This national recognition underscores Medihelp’s strategic commitment to fostering grassroots innovation and clinical excellence across its expanding healthcare network.
