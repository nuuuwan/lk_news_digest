’’It is your bilateral issue, we, unlike Western countries, don’t put pressure’’ - Russian Ambassador

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - In response to a query about Sri Lanka seeking defence cooperation with the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation (NATO), Russian Ambassador Levan Dzhagaryan said that it was Sri Lanka's bilateral issue, and Russia, unlike Western countries, does not put pressure.

Asked whether Russia is concerned about Sri Lanka's engagement with NATO, the Ambassador told Daily Mirror, " No, it’s you bilateral issue. Unlike Western countries, we don’t put pressure."

Defence Ministry Secretary Air Vice Marshal (Retd) Sampath Thuyacontha held bilateral talks with NATO Military Committee Chairperson Admiral Giuseppe Cavo Dragone on the sidelines of the Chiefs of Defence Conference in Victoria, Canada recently.

Discussions focused on military modernization, cyber readiness, advanced technology, and disaster response

They also focused on upgrading hardware, acquiring new technologies, enhancing surveillance, and expanding personnel training and information sharing.

Thuyacontha also highlighted humanitarian assistance and disaster relief cooperation, including the use of drones in emergency operations, and invited NATO to strengthen long-term engagement with Sri Lanka.

NATO is a political and military alliance of countries from Europe and North America. Its members are committed to protecting each other from any threat.

NATO-Russia relations are currently in a state of deep crisis, following the full-scale war involving Ukraine.
