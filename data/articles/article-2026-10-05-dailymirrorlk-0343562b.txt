Global fuel supply disruptions driving diesel price surge: Champika

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - A series of international developments, including unrest in the Suez Canal region and Ukrainian attacks on Russian oil refineries, have contributed to a global surge in diesel prices, former Minister Patali Champika Ranawaka said today.

Ranawaka said a 40 percent reduction in Russia’s fuel supply to the global market, along with a complete halt in supplies from the United States, had also contributed to the increase in diesel prices.

Speaking at a media briefing, he stressed the need for Sri Lanka to accelerate its transition towards alternative sources of energy for power generation.

“It is time for Sri Lanka to make greater use of alternative energy sources such as wind and solar power, as many other countries have already done,” he said.
