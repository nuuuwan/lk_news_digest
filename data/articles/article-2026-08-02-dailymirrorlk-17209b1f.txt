Mahara Prison unrest damages multiple facilities

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The violent disturbance that erupted at Mahara Prison on Saturday (01) caused extensive damage to several prison facilities, including the rehabilitation division, visitors' area, prison hospital, kitchen, industrial section, and a number of prison buildings, according to the Department of Prisons.

The Department said the unrest among inmates has now been brought under control through a joint operation involving prison officials, the Sri Lanka Police, the Police Special Task Force (STF), and the Tri-Forces.
