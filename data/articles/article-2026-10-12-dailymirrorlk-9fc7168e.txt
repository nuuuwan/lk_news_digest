Ashok Abeysinghe resigns from all SJB positions

Colombo, Oct. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Former State Minister and former MP Ashok Abeysinghe has resigned from all positions he held in the Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB), with effect from October 12.

In a letter addressed to SJB Leader and Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa, Abeysinghe announced his decision to relinquish his positions as the party’s Deputy Secretary, Kurunegala Chief Organiser, President of the Samagi Sewaka Sangamaya, Trade Union Coordinating Secretary to the Opposition Leader and a member of the party’s Executive Committee.

Abeysinghe also expressed his appreciation for the opportunity to work with Premadasa, dating back to their time in the United National Party (UNP) and continuing since the formation of the SJB.

He thanked Premadasa for the support and opportunities extended to him and wished the party success in its future endeavours.
