Government will not be able to engage in politics by suppressing opponents: Namal

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The present government will not be able to engage in politics by suppressing opponents, SLPP MP Namal Rajapaksa told Parliament today.

"If the government thinks it can engage in politics successfully by suppressing opponents, then it is mistaken," the MP said.

"The government is actually covering up real issues in the country by suppressing opponents," he added.
