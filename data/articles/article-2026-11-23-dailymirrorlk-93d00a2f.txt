Trade unions launch national protest against Budget

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Several trade unions have declared today (23) a National Day of Protest against the 2027 Budget, with public sector employees from several sectors scheduled to take part in demonstrations across the country.

Development officers, management service officers, health sector employees, technical officers, Samurdhi officers, teachers, principals and doctors are among those expected to participate in the protest campaign.

General Secretary of the All Ceylon Development Officers’ Association Anuradha Seneviratne said the main demand of the trade union action was an interim allowance of Rs. 25,000 for all public sector employees.

Meanwhile, President of the Principals' Service Association Nimal Mudunkotuwa said principals would report to schools dressed in black today as a symbolic protest against the Government.

President of the All Ceylon Management Services Officers’ Union Udeni Dissanayake attributed the grievances of public sector employees to what he described as the Government's disregard for the working class.

Government Medical Officers’ Association (GMOA) Media Spokesperson Dr Lasitha Kannangara said the Budget had failed to provide solutions to the issues faced by doctors and the wider health sector.

Former MP Saman Rathnapriya said the Government Nursing Officers’ Association was also prepared to resort to strike action if the Government failed to address the concerns raised by the association.

Ceylon Teachers' Union General Secretary Joseph Stalin and University Non-Academic Staff Union President Dhammika S. Priyantha also called on the Government to take immediate steps to resolve the issues raised by trade unions.
