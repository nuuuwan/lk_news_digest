Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Commercial Bank and Richard Pieris & Company with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.6 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. Crossings were witnessed in C T Holdings, Cargills, Lanka Milk Foods accounting for 51.5% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in John Keells Holdings, Haycarb and Ceylon Cold Stores whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, SMB Leasing and LOLC Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

Food & Staples Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to C T Holdings and Cargills) whilst the sector index lost 0.27%. The share price of C T Holdings decreased by Rs 10.00 (1.92%) to close at Rs 510.00. The share price of Cargills edged down by Rs 0.25 (0.04%) to close at Rs 629.75. Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Lanka Milk Foods) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.52%. The share price of Lanka Milk Foods moved down by Rs 0.50 (0.59%) to close at Rs 84.00. John Keells Holdings and Haycarb were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a loss of Rs 0.20 (1.01%) to close at Rs 19.70. The share price of Haycarb declined by Rs 3.00 (1.70%) to close at Rs 173.00.
