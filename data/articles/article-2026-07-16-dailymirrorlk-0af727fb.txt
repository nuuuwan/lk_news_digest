Special train services to Anuradhapura for 'national pichcha mal pujawa'

Colombo, July 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Railway Department has arranged several special train services from Beliatta, Colombo Fort, Hikkaduwa and Mahawa Junction to Anuradhapura on July 25 and 26 to facilitate devotees travelling to the National “Pichcha Mal Pujawa” (offering of Jasmine flowers).

Accordingly, special trains will operate on several routes, including Beliatta–Colombo Fort–Anuradhapura, Hikkaduwa–Colombo Fort–Anuradhapura and Mahawa Junction–Anuradhapura, along with return journeys.

The Railway Department said the additional services have been introduced to provide transport facilities for large numbers of pilgrims expected to attend the offering of Jasmine flowers in Anuradhapura. Commuters have been advised to plan their journeys according to the special train schedules.
