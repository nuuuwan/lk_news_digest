Hutch partners with SLIM to honour Marketing and Business Excellence

Hutch has joined forces with the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM), aligning with four of the country’s prominent recognition and award platforms: the National Sales Awards that recognize standout sales practices that are proven with results, SLIM Digi Awards that recognize digital marketing innovation and impact, SME Development Awards that recognize the SMEs that outshine through marketing, sales, innovation and sustainable business practices, and the Effie Awards that recognize strategic thinking, execution, teamwork and performance.

As the apex body for Sri Lankan marketers, SLIM brings together a professional audience of business leaders, marketers, sales professionals, entrepreneurs, SMEs, and corporate decision-makers across its national programmes.

Hutch, through this partnership with SLIM, will be empowering and celebrating the people and businesses that are shaping Sri Lanka’s economy, recognising excellence in sales, marketing effectiveness, and entrepreneurship across platforms.

“We are pleased to partner with Hutch, a brand that continues to evolve beyond telecommunications by embracing technology and digital innovation. This partnership reflects our shared commitment to uplifting businesses, marketers and entrepreneurs through greater access to knowledge, innovation and opportunities for growth,” said Rajiv David, Vice President Events & Sustainability, Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing(SLIM).

“At Hutch, we believe true brand leadership comes from empowering the people and businesses who drive Sri Lanka’s economy forward. Our partnership with SLIM allows us to stand alongside professionals and entrepreneurs shaping this country’s business landscapeto make greater things possible,” said Hamdhy Hassen, Chief Marketing Officer, Hutch Sri Lanka.
