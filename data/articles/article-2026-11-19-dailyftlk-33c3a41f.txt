SriLankan secures Cabinet approval for $ 13 m aviation fuel contracts at four airports

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved four aviation fuel supply contracts worth $ 12.97 million for SriLankan Airlines at four international airports, covering requirements in Frankfurt, Incheon, Lahore and Ahmedabad till end-2028.

Announcing the decisions, Cabinet Spokesman and Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said the contracts have been awarded to two international suppliers following a competitive procurement process initiated by the national carrier under the Limited International Competitive Bidding procedure, which attracted eight bids.

“Based on the evaluation of bids and recommendations of the High-Level Standing Procurement Committee, the Cabinet approved the proposal to award the contracts to the lowest substantially responsive bidders,” he said at the weekly post-Cabinet meeting media briefing.

Accordingly, World Fuel Services Trading DMCC (WFS) has secured two contracts valued at $ 240,021 for Incheon International Airport and $ 293,456 for Frankfurt Airport, both covering the period from 1 November 2026 to 31 December 2028.

In addition, Aviation Service Management FZE (ASM) has been awarded the larger share of the contracts, securing a $ 5.95 million deal for aviation fuel supplies at Ahmedabad Airport from 15 November 2026 to 31 December 2028, and a $ 6.48 million contract for Lahore Airport covering 1 December 2026 to 31 December 2028.

The proposal to this effect submitted by the Ports and Civil Aviation Minister Anura Karunathilake was approved by the Cabinet of Ministers.
