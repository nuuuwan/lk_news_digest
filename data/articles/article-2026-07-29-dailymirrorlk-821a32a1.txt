Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Colombo Dockyard, John Keells Holdings and Commercial Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.0bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. Crossings were witnessed in R I L Property, Hatton National Bank nonvoting and DFCC Bank accounting for 58.8% of the turnover.

Mixed interest was observed in Sampath Bank, Hemas Holdings and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & Finance Rights, LOLC Finance and Browns Investments. Furthermore, foreigners remained active closing as net sellers.

Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to RIL Property PLC) whilst the sector index gained 1.55%. The share price of RIL Property PLC increased by Rs. 0.30 (1.26%) to close at Rs. 24.20.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank, DFCC Bank and Hatton National Bank nonvoting) whilst the sector index increased by 0.25%. The share price of Sampath Bank lost Rs. 0.75 (0.55%) to close at Rs. 136.00.

The share price of DFCC Bank moved down by Rs. 0.25 (0.20%) to close at Rs. 127.00. The share price of Hatton National Bank nonvoting recorded a gain of Rs. 1.00 (0.32%) to close at Rs. 317.00. Hemas Holdings was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Hemas Holdings appreciated by Rs. 0.10 (0.32%) to close at Rs. 31.30.
