Sathuta Builders gets Rs. 1.69 b contract for Uva-Wellassa Uni. Faculty of Medicine building

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the award of the contract for constructing the first phase of the Faculty of Medicine at Uva-Wellassa University of Sri Lanka to Sathuta Builders Ltd., worth over 1.69 billion (excluding tax).

The contract award is based on the recommendations of the Higher Level Procurement Committee, which evaluated the bids received for the project.

Approval to begin procurement activities for the planning and construction of the first phase of the Faculty of Medicine had earlier been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 9 June 2025.

Bids were subsequently invited under the National Competitive Procurement Procedure, and six bids were received, the Government said.

After the evaluation of these bids, the Higher Level Procurement Committee recommended Sathuta Builders Ltd., for the contract, and the Cabinet has now endorsed that recommendation.

The establishment of a Faculty of Medicine at Uva-Wellassa University, located in Badulla, is expected to expand access to medical education in the Uva Province and strengthen the country’s higher education sector.

The proposal to this effect was submitted by Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya in her capacity as Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Minister.
