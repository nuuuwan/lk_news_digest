Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka allots 123,055 shares to directors under ESOP

Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka PLC has allotted 123,055 ordinary voting shares to two senior executives, following the exercise of options under the company’s Employee Share Option Plan (ESOP), according to a stock exchange filing.

The company said Director and Chief Executive Officer Jiffry Zulfer Hassen was allotted 80,555 ordinary voting shares, while Director and Chief Corporate Officer Khairul Tasnim Salie received 42,500 ordinary voting shares.

The shares were allotted on July 14, 2026, at an exercise price of Rs.76.41 per share.

Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka said the allotments were approved by the board of directors through a circular resolution dated July 14, 2026.
