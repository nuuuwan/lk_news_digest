Landslide warnings issued for several areas

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The National Building Research Institute (NBRI) has issued landslide early warnings for several areas across the country amid prevailing adverse weather conditions.

The warnings will remain in effect from 4.00 p.m. today (November 3) until 4.00 p.m. tomorrow (November 4), according to the NBRI.
