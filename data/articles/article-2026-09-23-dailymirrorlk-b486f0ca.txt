Public assistance sought to trace suspect wanted for child abuse

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Police have sought public assistance to identify and apprehend a suspect wanted in connection with the abduction and sexual abuse of a 13-year-old girl at Galle Face Green within the Fort Police Division on January 23, 2026.

Police said investigations are underway following a complaint lodged with the Fort Police regarding the incident.

Police inform the public to contact Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of Fort Police Station via 071-8591560 and Fort Police Station via 011-2432635 if they have any information on the suspects appearing in the picture..
