Advisory Committee on Marine-Based Industries holds inaugural meeting to drive industry growth

The inaugural meeting of the Advisory Committee on Marine-Based Industries was held on 16 November at the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB), bringing together key public and private sector stakeholders to discuss strategic priorities, industry challenges and emerging opportunities for the development of Sri Lanka’s marine-based industries.

The meeting was attended by the Chairman of the Export Development Board, the Director General, and the Additional Director General (Development), underscoring the importance placed on the sector as a key component of Sri Lanka’s export diversification and economic growth agenda.

During the meeting, Kaushal Rajapaksha was appointed  Chairman of the Marine-Based Services Advisory Committee, while Ricky Barnett was appointed as Co-Chairman.

The EDB Chairman delivered the main presentation, outlining the current status of the sector, emerging global opportunities, and the strategic priorities required to position Sri Lanka as a competitive regional hub for marine-based services. The presentation highlighted the sector’s growth potential and the need for coordinated efforts to enhance value addition, innovation, skills development, and international market access. The Committee also discussed opportunities to strengthen marine infrastructure and tourism-related activities, including the development of marinas, sheltered anchorages, breakwaters, boatyards and other marine tourism facilities. The potential to develop existing harbors, including Galle and other coastal locations, as marine tourism and boating hubs was highlighted.

Another important area of discussion was the taxation structure and HS Code classification applicable to locally manufactured commercial boats supplied to domestic tourism operators. The Committee also emphasized the importance of fostering stronger public-private partnerships and creating an enabling environment that supports sustainable growth and increased export earnings from marine-based services.

Speaking at the meeting, Chairman Rajapaksha noted the significant opportunities available within the sector and stressed the importance of collective action to unlock its full potential.

To facilitate focused discussions and effective implementation of proposed initiatives, the establishment of specialized subcommittees covering Boat Building and Manufacturing, Shipbuilding and Ship Repair, Regulatory Affairs, Training and Skills Development, Marketing and Export Promotion, Nautical and Marine Tourism, and Offshore Services was discussed.

The Committee also highlighted the opportunities presented by Sri Lanka Expo 2027, particularly in promoting marine-related investment opportunities, showcasing industry capabilities and connecting local businesses with international buyers and investors.

The establishment of the Marine-Based Services Advisory Committee marks an important step towards strengthening Sri Lanka’s blue economy and leveraging the country’s strategic maritime location to generate sustainable economic growth and export-led development.
