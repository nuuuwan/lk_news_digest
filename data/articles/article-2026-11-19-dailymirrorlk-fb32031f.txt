Sunshine celebrates 29 wins at SLIM Awards

Diversified conglomerate Sunshine Holdings PLC has secured 29 awards at the SLIM National Sales Awards (NSA) 2026, recognising the outstanding performance of its sales professionals across the Group’s businesses.

The achievements comprise 4 Gold, 9 Silver, 9 Bronze and 7 Merit awards, reflecting Sunshine Holdings’ continued commitment to developing high-performing sales teams through innovation, sales force automation, training and professional development.

The recognition celebrates the dedication, drive and commitment to excellence demonstrated by sales professionals representing Sunshine Consumer Lanka and Sunshine Healthcare Lanka, including Sunshine Pharmaceuticals, Sunshine Medical Devices, Lina Manufacturing, Healthguard Pharmacy and Healthguard Distribution.

Commenting on the achievements, Sunshine Holdings Group CEO Shyam Sathasivam said, “Our success at the SLIM National Sales Awards reflects the exceptional commitment and capabilities of our sales professionals, who play an important role in driving our businesses forward. At Sunshine Holdings, we believe that developing our people is fundamental to building a stronger and more competitive organisation.

Through continued investments in sales force automation, innovation, training and development, we are empowering our teams to perform at their best and adapt to changing market needs. These 29 awards are a proud recognition of their hard work, and I congratulate every winner for setting an example of excellence across the Group.”

The achievements further reinforce the importance Sunshine Holdings places on building a performance-driven culture that recognises individual contributions while encouraging collaboration and professional growth.

By combining technology-led improvements with structured capability development, the Group continues to support its field staff in delivering value to customers and strengthening business performance.

Organised by the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM), the National Sales Awards recognise outstanding sales professionals across multiple industries, celebrating excellence in sales performance, leadership and professional development. Marking its 25th anniversary in 2026, the programme continues to serve as a national platform for recognising individuals who contribute to organisational success and Sri Lanka’s broader business landscape.
