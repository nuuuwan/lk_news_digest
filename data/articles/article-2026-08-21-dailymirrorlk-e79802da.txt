High Court acquits technical officer in Rs. 40,000 bribery case

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo High Court today acquitted a Technical Officer who had been charged with soliciting a bribe of Rs. 40,000 on two occasions and accepting a total of Rs. 40,000 in two instalments of Rs. 20,000 each.

The accused had faced charges relating to the alleged solicitation and acceptance of bribes in connection with his official duties.

At the conclusion of the prosecution case, Counsel appearing for the accused made an application submitting that the prosecution had failed to establish a prima facie case warranting the accused being called upon to enter his defence.

Having considered the evidence led by the prosecution, High Court Judge Mohamed Mihal observed that there were serious contradictions in the evidence of the prosecution witnesses.

The Court consequently held that the prosecution had failed to establish a sufficient prima facie case against the accused and acquitted him of all charges without calling upon him to enter his defence.

Senior Counsel Darshana Kuruppu, together with Attorneys-at-Law Anjana Adikaramge and Buddhima Kannangara, appeared for the accused.
