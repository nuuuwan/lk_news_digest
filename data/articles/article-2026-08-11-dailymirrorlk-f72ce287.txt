Minister’s brother-in-law arrested over alleged fraud

Kegalle, August 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Kegalle Fraud Investigation Bureau has arrested the 59-year-old brother-in-law of Minister K.D. Lal Kantha in connection with an alleged financial fraud involving a vehicle auction.

According to the Police, the suspect is alleged to have defrauded an individual of Rs. 1,145,000 in connection with the vehicle auction.

The suspect is scheduled to be produced before the Kegalle Magistrate’s Court tomorrow.
