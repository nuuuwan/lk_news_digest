204 Sri Lankans receive air tickets for jobs in Israel

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - Air tickets were presented to 204 Sri Lankan migrant workers selected for employment opportunities in Israel at a ceremony held at the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) auditorium yesterday (18).

The tickets were presented under the patronage of SLBFE Chairman Kosala Wickramasinghe.

The workers were selected for employment in the hotel, restaurant, industrial and agricultural sectors in Israel.

Of the 204 workers, 43 were selected for hotel-sector jobs, 144 for restaurant-sector jobs, 16 for industrial-sector jobs and one for an agricultural-sector position.

According to the SLBFE, as of August 31, 2026, a total of 800 Sri Lankans had left for Israel for industrial-sector jobs, while 20 had left for agricultural jobs, 97 for restaurant-sector jobs and 997 for housekeeping jobs.

The Bureau said the overall number of Sri Lankans who have left for employment in Israel stands at 11,607.

Meanwhile, the SLBFE warned the public about various scams involving Israeli job opportunities and urged people not to fall victim to fraudulent recruitment schemes.

The public can report information on such scams by calling the SLBFE hotline 1989 and obtain information on employment opportunities in Israel through the Bureau's official website.
