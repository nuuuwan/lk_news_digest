Week ends in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Melstacorp and Hayleys with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.3 Bn. A similar behavior was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Diesel & Motor Engineering, Singer Sri Lanka and Commercial Bank. Mixed interest was observed in Hayleys, Swisstek and Haycarb whilst retail interest was noted in HNB Finance, John Keells Hotels and Waskaduwa Beach Resort. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 1.5% and 1.2% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.53 Bn. Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hayleys) whilst the sector index lost 0.25%. The share price of Hayleys increased by Rs 2.25 (1.00%) to close at Rs 226.25. The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.12%. The share price of Commercial Bank gained Rs 0.50 (0.25%) to close at Rs 203.00. Singer Sri Lanka, Swisstek and Diesel & Motor Engineering were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Singer Sri Lanka closed flat at Rs 79.60. The share price of Swisstek recorded a gain of Rs 4.00 (5.26%) to close at Rs 80.10. The share price of Diesel & Motor Engineering edged down Rs 1.00 (0.07%) to close at Rs 1,425.00.
