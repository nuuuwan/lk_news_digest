Police curfew lifted in three Ragama GN divisions

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Measures have been taken to lift the police curfew that was imposed across three Grama Niladhari (GN) divisions in the Ragama area.

The police had enforced the curfew with immediate effect following the unrest that erupted at the Mahara Prison yesterday (01). The restriction covered the Grama Niladhari divisions of 246/Kendaliyaddapaluwa - West, 246/C Kendaliyaddapaluwa - North, and 181/G Dambuwa.

Police stated that because the overall situation has now been brought under control, steps have been taken to lift the curfew imposed on the three divisions with immediate effect.
