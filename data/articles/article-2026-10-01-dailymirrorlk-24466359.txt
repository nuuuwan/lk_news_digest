Every child must have equal opportunity to realise their potential: President

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said every child should have equal and fair opportunities to realise their potential, pursue their dreams and shape the future they wish for themselves, irrespective of their background.

In his World Children’s Day message issued for today (October 1), the President said children were the most valuable asset of a nation and that ensuring their physical and mental well-being and creating an environment conducive to their proper development were among the primary responsibilities of any government.

He said the country being built for children should not merely be one prepared to be handed over to them in the future, but one that gives space to their dreams today, listens to their voices and provides opportunities for them to realise their potential.

“For the best interests of children, we are taking the necessary steps to ensure equal access to education, guidance, social protection, opportunities for sport, leisure and recreation, proper nutrition and access to healthcare, while protecting children from all forms of violence and discrimination and safeguarding their rights,” he said.

President Dissanayake said that race, religion, economic status, geographical background or any other circumstance should not become a barrier to a child’s education or proper development.

He said the Government had already begun the process of reforming Sri Lanka’s education system and transforming it towards a skills-based system suited to the modern world, while ensuring that it did not place a burden on parents or cause distress to children.

“Our aim is to ensure that no child is left behind in the new Sri Lanka we are building, and to provide every child with opportunities for development in a safe environment,” he said.

The President said the Government’s programmes, including the expansion of educational scholarships and measures to secure the future of children growing up in child development centres and those in need of care, were aimed at creating a dignified and secure future for every child.

He also said the Government was steering the country’s economy towards a stable direction in order to provide children with a safe and happy childhood free from the effects of an unstable economy.

At the same time, he said the national operation “A Nation United” (Ratama Ekata) had been launched to bring people together to protect the younger generation from the threat of illicit drugs.

President Dissanayake also commended the Ministry of Women and Child Affairs for organising this year’s World Children’s Day celebrations under the theme “Strong Minds – Enriched Children”, declaring a National Children’s Day Week and conducting programmes aimed at strengthening children’s rights, well-being, protection, education and freedom.

He extended his heartfelt wishes to all children in Sri Lanka on World Children’s Day.
