Indices close in red

The ASPI edged down as a result of price losses in counters such as John Keells Holdings, Citizens Development Business Finance and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Seylan Bank, John Keells Holdings and Pan Asia Banking Corporation.

Mixed interest was observed in Hatton National Bank nonvoting, HNB Life and Windforce whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, Lanka Credit and Business Finance and Softlogic Holdings.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank nonvoting and Seylan Bank ) whilst the sector index gained 0.19%. The share price of Hatton National Bank nonvoting increased by Rs. 1.00 (0.30%) to close at Rs. 335.00. The share price of Seylan Bank lost Rs. 0.70 (0.71%) to close at Rs. 98.10.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.03%. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a loss of Rs. 0.10 (0.50%) to close at Rs. 19.80.

HNB Life and Windforce Limited were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of HNB Life moved up by Rs. 1.25 (0.84%) to close at Rs. 149.25. The share price of Windforce Limited declined by Rs. 0.50 (1.23%) to close at Rs. 40.10.
