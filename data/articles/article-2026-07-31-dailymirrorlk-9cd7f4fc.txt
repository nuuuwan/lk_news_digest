Lalkantha asked me to keep silent on judges’ retirement age issue: Lal Wijenayake

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - Senior lawyer Lal Wijenayake, a member of the Leadership Council, Executive Committee and National Operation Committee of the ruling National People’s Power (NPP), said yesterday that Agriculture Minister K.D. Lalkantha asked him to remain silent over the government’s move to extend the retirement age of superior court judges.

Mr. Wijenayake said he could not remain silent on the issue since he had been fighting for judicial independence for over five decades.

“One member gave me a call one day — a member of the Leadership Council, the Political Bureau and other bodies. He simply said, ‘Why don’t you keep silent on this matter?’” he said in an interview with the Daily Mirror.

He identified the person as Lalkantha and said he later wrote to him explaining his position, with copies sent to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and NPP Secretary Dr. Nihal Abeysinghe.

Mr. Wijenayake said his opposition was based on principle and not on any personal disagreement with NPP leaders.

“This is a matter of principle. To live in a civilised society, there must be the rule of law. The rule of law will prevail only if there is independence of the judiciary,” he said.

He said decisions affecting judges’ service conditions could undermine judicial independence by allowing future governments to manipulate the judiciary.

Mr.Wijenayake, who has been associated with the Lanka Sama Samaja Party for about 50 years, said he was accustomed to a system where major decisions were taken after extensive discussions.

“However, in the NPP, I do not see that kind of process. Decisions seem to be taken somewhere, but it is not clear where those decisions are actually being made. That is the problem,” he said.
