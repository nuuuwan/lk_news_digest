Easter Sunday attacks case judgment proceedings begin

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The case against 24 accused, including Naufer Moulavi, over 23,270 charges relating to conspiring, aiding and abetting suicide terrorist attacks targeting tourist hotels, churches and other locations in Colombo and several other areas on Easter Sunday in 2019 commenced at around 12.20 p.m. today for the pronouncement of the judgment.

The court proceedings were subsequently adjourned until 1.45 p.m. for the lunch break.
