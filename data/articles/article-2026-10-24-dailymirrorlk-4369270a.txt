New regulations gazetted on imprisoned Buddhist monks’ attire

Colombo, October 24 (Daily Mirror) The Acting Commissioner General of Prisons has issued an Extraordinary Gazette notification amending the Standing Orders of the Department of Prisons outlining the procedures to be followed regarding the removal of robes when imprisoning a Buddhist monk.

According to the Gazette notification, new Standing Order 356A has been inserted immediately after Standing Order 356 which says  “356A. Where a Buddhist monk is imprisoned, the Commissioner of Prisons shall consult the Department of Buddhist Affairs regarding the removal of the robes worn by such monk at the time of imprisonment and the Department of Buddhist Affairs shall inform the relevant Nikhaya or Chapter of the same, and shall notify of the actions of the Nikhaya or Chapter regarding the removal of such robes to the Commissioner of Prisons within ten days. Where no response regarding the actions to be taken is so informed, the Commissioner of Prisons may take necessary action in accordance with the relevant provisions.
