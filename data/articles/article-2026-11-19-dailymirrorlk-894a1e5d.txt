Colombo students introduced to tourism and digital opportunities

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Nearly 100 schoolchildren representing 42 schools in the Colombo District have participated in a tourism awareness programme organised by the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA).

The programme, held under an island-wide school awareness initiative launched in conjunction with World Tourism Day 2026, aimed to introduce students to Sri Lanka's tourism industry, sustainable tourism practices and emerging career opportunities.

Organised through the SLTDA's Domestic Tourism and Community Relations Division, the initiative also focuses on familiarising students with digital tools and modern technologies that can be used to promote tourism.

Students were encouraged to understand the value of the country's cultural heritage and natural attractions and explore ways of showcasing them to international audiences through digital platforms.

The programme also aims to encourage participating schools to establish dedicated School Travel and Tourism Clubs, allowing students to share the knowledge gained with their fellow students.

Teachers and students who attended the Colombo programme appreciated the opportunity, saying the sessions had provided valuable knowledge and encouraged them to explore the tourism industry further.

One participating teacher described the programme as one of the most meaningful and valuable experiences in her teaching career.

The Colombo programme was the first provincial-level activity under the island-wide initiative, which is expected to continue in other provinces.
