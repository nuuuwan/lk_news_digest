Government in discussion with importers on prices of goods

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - Amid rising prices of several goods, the government has held discussions with importers on measures to address increasing import costs linked to various factors, including the ongoing conflict in the Middle East.

Deputy Minister of Trade, Commerce and Food Security R.M. Jayawardhana said transportation and insurance costs have increased as a result of the conflict, putting upward pressure on the prices of imported goods.

He said the Ministry of Trade, led by Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe, has engaged with importers to identify measures to manage the impact of rising import-related expenses and prevent excessive price increases.

The Deputy Minister also said the government has taken steps to make essential goods available at concessionary prices through the National Cooperative Wholesale Establishment (NCWE) and Lanka Sathosa.

According to Jayawardhana, Lanka Sathosa has reduced the prices of several essential items while maintaining a competitive pricing strategy in the market.

He added that the presence of state-owned players such as Lanka Sathosa and NCWE has helped strengthen market competition and limited the ability of private traders to impose significant price increases.

The price of wheat flour wad increased by Rs. 17 per kilogram by Prima Ceylon (Pvt) Ltd and Serendib Flour Mills (Pvt) Ltd from last Saturday leading to increase in all other bakery items.
