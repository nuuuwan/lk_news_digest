Seylan Bank crosses Rs.1tn in assets

Seylan Bank PLC yesterday announced its total assets surpassed Rs.1 trillion in September, making it the fourth private sector bank in Sri Lanka to reach that level.

Pointing out its expanding scale and growth in recent years, Seylan Bank Director and Chief Executive Officer Ramesh Jayasekara said, “Surpassing Rs.1 trillion in total assets is a significant milestone for Seylan Bank. This achievement reflects the dedication of our employees and their continued focus on delivering value to our customers.”

He said the bank remained focused on building on its growth and strengthening the value it delivered to the customers.

The bank said its strategy focused on making the banking services more accessible and providing the small and medium-sized enterprises with financial and non-financial support.
