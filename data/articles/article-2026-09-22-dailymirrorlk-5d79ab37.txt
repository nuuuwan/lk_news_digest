‘Walk the talk’ on justice for Sri Lankan fishermen, Chandrasekar tells Vijay

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - In response to Tamil Nadu fishermen’s continued poaching in Sri Lankan waters, Fisheries Minister Ramalingam Chandrasekar said he had conveyed a message to Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay, asking him to “walk the talk,” as a former actor who fought for justice through his cinematic roles, and ensure it for Sri Lankan fishermen.

The Minister told Daily Mirror that he had conveyed this message to the Tamil Nadu Chief Minister through a media delegation from Tamil Nadu that met him.

“Sri Lanka’s fishermen in the north suffered a lot during the 30-year war. Now they are trying to rebuild their lives. In such a context, Tamil Nadu fishermen poach in our waters, denying a livelihood to our fishermen. It is virtually similar to slitting their throats when they struggle for their livelihood. I asked the Chief Minister, as an actor who played his cinematic role for social justice, to be fair in his role now. He can be fair in his role now by acting for justice for Sri Lankan fishermen,” the Minister said.

He said Tamil Nadu fishermen would never be able to indulge in such unlawful activities even in other Indian states such as Kerala and Andhra Pradesh.

Asked about a practical solution, he said an official-level delegation would travel to India in the future, to be followed by ministerial-level discussions later.
