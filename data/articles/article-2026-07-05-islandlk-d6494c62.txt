IIHS marks 25 years with record graduation of over 650 healthcare professionals

The International Institute of Health Sciences (IIHS) celebrated a historic milestone on June 24, 2026, graduating over 650 healthcare professionals at a ceremony in Ragama. This year’s event was particularly significant as it marked IIHS’s 25th anniversary and the first-ever graduation of its Professional Bachelor’s cohorts, with 227 students earning degrees in Nursing, Public Health, and Dental Therapy.

Chief Guest Professor Nophar Geifman of the University of Surrey urged graduates to embrace innovation and technology. Over the past quarter-century, IIHS has evolved into a “Global Airport” for healthcare careers, with its qualifications serving as a passport to international opportunities. The institution has trained over 5,000 professionals for Sri Lanka’s workforce, while more than 2,500 alumni now work abroad. CEO Dr. Kithsiri Edirisinghe emphasized that IIHS certificates are transformative tools for global education, employment, and migration.
