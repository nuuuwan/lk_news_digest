UN Human Rights Office calls on Govt to bolster accountability

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - UN Human Rights spokesperson Ravina Shamdasani said that the UN Human Rights Office calls on the Sri Lankan Government to build on the steps it has taken to bolster accountability for human rights violations and to advance reconciliation and healing.

In the past year, Sri Lanka has continued to weather a series of natural disasters and economic shocks, impacting the poorest the most, she said.

"While it is encouraging that the authorities have taken steps to tackle corruption, and there have been high-profile arrests and investigations in connection with political killings, enforced disappearances and the 2019 attacks, more needs to be done to end the long legacy of impunity.

The Government continues to apply the repressive Prevention of Terrorism Act, resulting in arbitrary arrests and prolonged detentions without charge. Torture, custodial deaths, and prison overcrowding and violence have continued. Civil society actors, activists and journalists are subjected to state surveillance, while tensions over land and religious sites continue to simmer.

We urge Sri Lanka to redouble its reconciliation and accountability efforts, and to impose a moratorium on the use of the Prevention of Terrorism Act pending its repeal, as well as the release of long-term detainees and prisoners who are held under it,” Ravina Shamdasani said.
