India firm wins $4.29 million insulin supply contract

The Government has awarded a US$4.293 million contract to India's M.J. Biopharm Private Limited for the supply of 2.7 million vials of insulin used in the treatment of diabetic patients.

The procurement, carried out under the International Competitive Bidding procedure, attracted two bids.

Following an evaluation, the Cabinet of Ministers approved a proposal submitted by Health and Mass Media Minister to award the contract to M.J. Biopharm, the only substantially responsive bidder.

The contract covers the supply of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection (Human) BP 1,000 IU, 10 ml vials, containing 30% soluble and 70% isophane insulin.

The contract is valued at US$4.293 million, inclusive of Cost and Freight (C&F) charges, and was awarded on the recommendation of the High-Level Standing Procurement Committee.
