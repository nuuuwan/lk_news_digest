President directs Met Department to assess social impact of extreme weather conditions

Colombo, Nov. 16 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has instructed officials from the Department of Meteorology to conduct a deeper analysis of current forecasts regarding prevailing hot weather and anticipated heavy rainfall, and to compile a comprehensive report on their potential social impacts.

According to the President’s Media Division (PMD), the directives were issued during a disaster management discussion held at the Presidential Secretariat this morning, where Meteorology Department officials briefed the President on the latest weather developments.

The President noted that the assessment will allow authorities to update existing contingency plans to ensure the sustainable management of drinking water, electricity, and food supplies.

He added that the report will also help identify potential threats to wildlife so that appropriate long-term protective measures can be implemented.

Furthermore, President Dissanayake emphasized that once the forecast report is finalized, field officers must be convened for follow-up discussions to coordinate action on the ground.
