Yoshitha, former Navy Commander plead not guilty to corruption charges

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Yoshitha Rajapaksa and former Sri Lanka Navy Commander Admiral of the Fleet Wasantha Karannagoda pleaded not guilty today before the Colombo High Court to corruption charges filed against them by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption.

The case was taken up before Colombo High Court Judge Ravindra Premaratne, where the charges against the two accused were read out in open court. Both defendants pleaded not guilty to the charges.

Following the pleas, the trial commenced and the court ordered that further evidence be recorded on December 10.

The case was filed against Rajapaksa and Karannagoda over allegations that in 2006, Rajapaksa was sent for a course at the Britannia Royal Naval College in the United Kingdom despite not possessing the required qualifications. Karannagoda is accused of committing the offence of corruption by facilitating the arrangement, while Rajapaksa is accused of aiding and abetting the offence.
