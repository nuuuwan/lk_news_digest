Nestomalt and Ogilvy Digital win Gold at Dragons of Asia Awards

Thusara Ranasinghe, Manager-Media, Geometry Media (far left), Rajeendra Wijayasiri, Manager Activations & Sponsorships, Nestlé Lanka (2nd left), Shevini Gunesekera, Asst. Brand Manager-Nestomalt, Nestlé Lanka (2nd right) and Shakya Subasinghe, Asst. Manager-Digital (far right), with the Gold award at the Dragons of Asia 2026

Nestlé Lanka’s malted food drink brand, Nestomalt, together with Ogilvy Digital, has won a Gold Dragon at the Dragons of Asia Awards 2026 for its Avurudu campaign, “Our Very Own New Year Filled with Power, Strength and Energy”.

Commenting on the recognition, Keashan Wijayasinghe, Senior Brand Manager-Nestomalt, Nestlé Lanka said, “Avurudu has long been a defining moment for Nestomalt to celebrate the power, strength and energy that have connected the brand with Sri Lankans for generations. Drawing on this long-standing association, the campaign reimagined these enduring brand values as a contemporary celebration of the resilience and determination of the Sri Lankan people, recognizing those who embody them through their everyday actions.”

The campaign placed fishermen, farmers, tea pluckers, tuk-tuk drivers, municipal workers, security personnel, and many other communities who keep Sri Lanka moving at the heart of Nestomalt’s Avurudu celebrations. Their unique skills and expertise inspired a fresh interpretation of familiar Avurudu traditions.
