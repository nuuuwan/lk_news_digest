Former Maho Crime OIC acquitted in sexual bribe case

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The former Officer-in-Charge of Crimes, attached to the Maho Police Station, who stood trial over allegations that he had solicited a sexual bribe from a woman, was acquitted and discharged today by the Colombo High Court.

Delivering the judgment, High Court Judge Mohamed Mihal held that the prosecution had failed to establish the charges against Sub Inspector of Police Asithgomi Alahakoon beyond reasonable doubt.

The case, instituted by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC), involved two charges alleging that the accused had solicited sexual gratification from a woman.

Focusing on the credibility of the principal witness and the consistency of the prosecution evidence, the Court found that the evidence of the complainant could not be safely relied upon.

The Court further observed that there was no sufficient evidence establishing that the accused had solicited a sexual bribe, while inconsistencies in the evidence led by the prosecution also raised doubts regarding the allegation.

With the prosecution failing to bridge these evidentiary gaps and prove the charges beyond reasonable doubt, the Court acquitted and discharged the accused.

A central contention of the defence case was that the accused officer and the complainant had been involved in a romantic relationship, and that the allegations of soliciting a sexual bribe emerged following the breakdown of that relationship.

Defence Counsel contended during the trial that It was a relationship that turned sour.

The defence had repeatedly put to the prosecution witnesses that the complaint was motivated by resentment arising from the failed relationship and that the circumstances surrounding the two individuals were inconsistent with the prosecution's version of a demand for sexual gratification.

The Court, having considered the totality of the evidence, ultimately found that the prosecution had not produced evidence sufficient to remove the reasonable doubt arising in the case.

Senior Counsel Darshana Kuruppu together with Pramuditha Senevirathne appeared for the accused.
