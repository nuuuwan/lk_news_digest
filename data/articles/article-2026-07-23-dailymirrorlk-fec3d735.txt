Hayleys Agriculture opens SL’s 1st frozen concentrated coconut water manufacturing facility

Official ribbon-cutting ceremony marking inauguration of Hayleys Nature Nest—Sri Lanka’s first frozen concentrated coconut water manufacturing facility

Inside state-of-the-art processing facility at Hayleys Nature Nest, featuring advanced pasteurisation and thermal processing equipment designed for producing high-quality frozen concentrated coconut water

Hayleys Agriculture Holdings Limited marks another milestone with the opening of Hayleys Nature Nest (Pvt.) Ltd, Sri Lanka’s first of its kind frozen concentrated coconut water manufacturing facility.

This state-of-the-art facility represents another significant step in its journey to create greater value from Sri Lanka’s rich agricultural resources.

By transforming the locally sourced coconut water into premium concentrated coconut water for export markets, Hayleys Nature Nest strengthens its commitment to innovation, sustainability and value-added agricultural exports.

Beyond expanding its global footprint, this investment supports the local coconut growers and communities while contributing to increased export earnings and the long-term growth of Sri Lanka’s agricultural sector.

“As we celebrate this milestone, we remain committed to delivering sustainable, high-quality agricultural solutions that create value for our stakeholders and showcase the best of Sri Lanka to the world,” a Hayleys Agriculture Holdings statement said.
