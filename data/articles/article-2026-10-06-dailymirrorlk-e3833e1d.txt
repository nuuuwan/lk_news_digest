Suspect arrested in Maharagama while attempting to sell protected sea shells

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - A suspect was arrested in Maharagama while allegedly attempting to sell protected sea shells valued at around Rs. 1.5 million, Wildlife officials said.

The raid was conducted by Wildlife officers following a tip-off received by the Horagolla National Park headquarters on October 4 regarding an alleged sea shell trafficking operation in the Maharagama area.

During the operation, officers arrested a Maharagama resident and seized six Cassis cornutus (Horned Helmet) shells along with shells of 18 other invertebrate species.

The shells had allegedly been offered for sale for a total of Rs. 1.5 million.

The suspect and the seized items are scheduled to be produced before the Gangodawila Magistrate's Court on October 8.

Under the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance, the possession, display, transportation and trade of protected sea shells are prohibited.

The Department of Wildlife Conservation said action is being taken to identify and apprehend individuals involved in the illegal trafficking of protected marine species.

The raid was conducted under the supervision of Horagolla National Park Warden P.A.S.U. Bandara. Wildlife Range Assistant R.M.P. Wijesinghe, Wildlife Guards P.C. Sanjeevani and I.P.C.N. Sasmitha, Field Assistant Saliya Chandrasekara and Driver R.S.S. Rathnasekara took part in the operation.
