Drug suspect Shiran Basik to be detained for another 90 days

Colombo, Nov 15 (Daily Mirror): The Colombo Magistrate’s Court today (November 15) granted permission to detain and interrogate alleged large-scale drug trafficker Shiran Basik for a further 90 days, following a request by the Central Crimes Investigation Bureau (CCIB).

Basik was arrested in Dubai in March and deported to Sri Lanka after legal proceedings there. He was taken into custody by the Criminal Investigation Department’s (CID) Airport Unit upon his arrival at the Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake, on August 14.

Following preliminary questioning, he was handed over to the CCIB for further investigations. The bureau had previously obtained a 90-day detention order on August 17.

During interrogation, Basik reportedly told investigators that Dubai Police had stopped and questioned him at a shopping mall during a period when the city was facing Iranian attacks.

He claimed that officers examined his mobile phone and found footage of the attacks, following which he was arrested and questioned daily over possible links to Iran until his deportation.

He further claimed that UAE authorities eventually instructed him to purchase his own air ticket to Sri Lanka before deporting him.

Police said a previous Interpol Red Notice issued against Basik in connection with a drug trafficking investigation had expired, and arrangements were underway to obtain a fresh notice at the time of his arrest.

Investigations have revealed that Basik had legally left Sri Lanka for Dubai on June 2, 2018, where he had reportedly maintained an extravagant lifestyle and operated two large-scale businesses.
