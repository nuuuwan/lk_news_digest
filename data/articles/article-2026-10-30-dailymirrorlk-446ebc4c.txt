Janashakthi Life among Sri Lanka’s Top 75 Most Respected Entities

Janashakthi Life has been ranked 75th among Sri Lanka’s Top Most Respected Entities in 2026, as featured in LMD’s annual ranking.

This recognition marks an important milestone for the life insurer, reflecting the reputation it has built within Sri Lanka’s corporate community and its continued focus on customer trust, innovation, sound corporate governance and long-term value creation.

Conducted by PepperCube Consultants in collaboration with LMD during March and April 2026, the study assessed the perceptions of 600 managerial-level and senior respondents across organisations listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange, the LMD 100 and Brands Annual 2025.

Janashakthi Life recorded strong rankings across several attributes, placing 26th for Leadership Profile, 27th for Financial Performance, 38th for Corporate Dynamism, 47th for Aspirational Workplace and 51st for Employee Wellbeing.

Janashakthi Life Director and Chief Executive Officer Ravi Liyanage said, “Being recognised among Sri Lanka’s Most Respected Entities is an important milestone for Janashakthi Life. Over the years, we have focused on building a strong life insurance brand offering a wide array of products and services, setting high standards for the entire industry to emulate and creating meaningful value for our policyholders and the rest of the stakeholders. This recognition encourages us to keep raising our standards and creating potential propositions to all our stakeholders.”

Janashakthi Life Chief Business Officer Namalee A. Silva said, “At Janashakthi Life, we believe that trust is built through the way we serve people, the solutions we offer and the relationships we sustain over time. Our focus is on understanding what matters to our customers and making protection relevant at every stage of life. This recognition reflects the importance of that commitment and motivates us to keep finding better ways to serve our policyholders.”
