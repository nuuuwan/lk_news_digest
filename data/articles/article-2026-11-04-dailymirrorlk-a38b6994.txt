NEO’s Hard Talk event on Friday

NEO Technologies will host its premier Hard Talk event aboard the romantic 8Degrees @ Cinnamon Lakeside, on Friday, November 6, from 6:00 p.m..

NEO will bring together top diplomats, business leaders and close allies for an evening to remember.

Renowned photographer, writer and educator Ian Lockwood will take guests on a magical journey to Serendipitys’ Sky Islands, Sri Lanka’s unique rainforest landscapes.

Following is the Sweet Spot of Management talk by NEO founder Major Tissa Aluwihare, encompassing his journey of 40 years as a visionary entrepreneur.

The actual Hard Talk Forum will encourage a candid exchange of perspectives on Sri Lanka’s commercial security industry.

For all to enjoy, there will be curated culinary delights, lovely cocktails and drinks accompanied by cool music by Sheam Dean & Band.
