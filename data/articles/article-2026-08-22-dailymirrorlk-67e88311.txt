Police clearance online system restored

Colombo, August 22 (Daily Mirror) - The digital data system used to process police clearance certificates has been restored and applications can now be submitted through the usual online procedure, the Sri Lanka Police said

The system, operated by the Police Clearance Division on the 17th floor of the Suhurupaya Building in Battaramulla, became inactive at around 12.50 p.m. on August 20, 2026.

Following notification to the Sri Lanka Information and Communication Technology Agency (ICTA), necessary measures were taken to restore the system.

The Police said applicants who had previously submitted applications through the temporary procedure using [email protected] are now required to resubmit their applications through the regular online system.

Applicants who used the temporary method are also requested to make the relevant payments as usual before resubmitting their applications.

The Sri Lanka Police requested media institutions to give publicity to the announcement and inform the public accordingly.
