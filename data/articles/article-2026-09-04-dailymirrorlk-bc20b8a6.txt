160th anniversary marked with pirith

The Malwathuhiripitiya Police Station held religious observances and a Seth Pirith sermon to invoke blessings for the country, as part of events marking the 160th anniversary of the Sri Lanka Police Service. The ceremony was held on September 2, followed by the distribution of exercise books and school bags to schoolchildren. The event was organized under the guidance of Malwathuhiripitiya Police Station OIC Inspector Piyal Gunathilaka, while Galkiriyagama Seelananda Thero conducted the Seth Pirith chanting.
