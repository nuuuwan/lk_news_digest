Sajith ready for public debate on Government report on socio-economic situation

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa, who claimed that the report compiled by the Central Bank and the Department of Census and Statistics is full of errors, challenged the Government to a public debate on it.

Premadasa stated that the report is full of errors, flaws and shortcomings.

He said it was a misleading document intended to deceive the 22 million citizens of the country. He emphasised that his criticism was based on solid facts and data and said he was ready for an open debate on the matter with any party at any time.

The Opposition Leader made these remarks during a special statement delivered today.

“Through this report, the Government is attempting to paint a falsely beautiful picture of the country’s situation. To achieve this, they have selectively included certain information while intentionally omitting other vital facts. When presenting data, there must be chronological consistency and integrity. The Central Bank and the Department of Census and Statistics have no right to present outdated data to formulate a conclusion,” the Opposition Leader said.

The Opposition Leader noted that he was making these criticisms with a thorough understanding of economics, including both macroeconomic and microeconomic principles.

“All socio-economic data in the Government report are from 2016 to 2019. For the main economic indicators, macroeconomic indicators, demographic data and life expectancy, 2024 data have been utilised. Indicators such as external trade, finance, consumer price indices, real GDP, imports and exports, prosperity indices and human development indices have also been compared with Asian and SAARC countries using recent data. However, in the section detailing socio-economic conditions, the data used are exclusively from the outdated 2016–2019 period,” he also said.
