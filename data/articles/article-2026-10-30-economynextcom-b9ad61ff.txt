Sri Lanka rupee at 332.60/70 to US dollar spot, bond yields steady

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s rupee was quoted at 332.60/70 to the US dollar spot on Thursday, weaker from 332.55/65 the previous day, while bond yields were steady, dealers said.

A bond maturing on 15.05.2030 was quoted at 11.45/47 percent, up from 11.40/45 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2030 was quoted at 11.62/65 percent, down from 11.63/67 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.12.2031 was quoted at 11.85/90 percent, down from 11.85/93 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.12.2032 was quoted at 12.10/18 percent, down from 12.15/25 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2034 was quoted at 12.60/63 percent, down from 12.60/65 percent.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was 328.10 buying, 337.10 selling; the euro was 364.7390 buying, 378.5198 selling; pound was 438.4771 buying, 452.5855 selling. (Colombo/Oct30/2026)
