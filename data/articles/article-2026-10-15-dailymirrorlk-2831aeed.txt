Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Carson Cumberbatch and Distilleries with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.9 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Dialog Axiata, Diesel & Motor Engineering and John Keells Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Sampath Bank, Lanka Milk Foods and Sierra Cables whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, Janashakthi Limited and LVL Energy Fund. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index lost 0.89%. The share price of John Keells Holdings lost Rs 0.20 (1.10%) to close at Rs 17.90.

Telecommunication Services sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Dialog Axiata) whilst the sector index decreased by 2.68%. The share price of Dialog Axiata decreased by Rs 1.30 (2.98%) to close at Rs 42.30.

Diesel & Motor Engineering, Sampath Bank and Lanka IOC were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Diesel & Motor Engineering moved down by Rs 2.50 (0.19%) to close at Rs 1,298.50. The share price of Sampath Bank recorded a loss of Rs 0.50 (0.36%) to close at Rs 138.00. The share price of Lanka IOC declined by Rs 0.50 (0.40%) to close at Rs 124.00.
