Prime Minister leaves for 2026 Commonwealth Heads of Government meeting

Colombo, Oct 31(Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya departed Sri Lanka today (31) to attend the 2026 Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM), scheduled to be held in St. John’s, Antigua and Barbuda.

The Prime Minister left the country from Bandaranaike International Airport, according to the Prime Minister’s Media Division.

During her visit from October 31 to November 6, Dr. Amarasuriya is expected to engage with Commonwealth leaders and participate in discussions on matters of mutual interest to member states.
