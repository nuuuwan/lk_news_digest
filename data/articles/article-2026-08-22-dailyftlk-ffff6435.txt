Namal grilled for six hours by FCID

Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) MP Namal Rajapaksa yesterday left the Police Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID) after recording a statement for nearly six hours in connection with an ongoing investigation.

Rajapaksa arrived at the FCID in the morning following a summons and provided a statement to investigators before leaving the premises.

Details of the investigation were not disclosed in the information provided.
