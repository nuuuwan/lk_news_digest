Police probe Wimal’s remarks over possible contempt of court

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) has launched an investigation into former Minister Wimal Weerawansa following a Colombo Magistrate’s Court order to determine whether remarks he had made at an SLPP rally in Anuradhapura amounted to contempt of court.

Police said the investigation was initiated on the instructions of the Inspector General of Police (IGP), following the court’s directive.

The matter was raised before court during proceedings relating to a complaint involving Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa in connection with the Airbus transactions.

Deputy Solicitor General Janaka Bandara, appearing for the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC), requested that an investigation be conducted into whether Weerawansa’s remarks at the September 12 rally amounted to contempt of court.

Bandara also submitted a compact disc containing a recording of the speech to court.

After considering the request, Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama directed the IGP to investigate the matter and submit a report to court.

Accordingly, the CCIB has commenced investigations into the remarks and the circumstances surrounding them.
