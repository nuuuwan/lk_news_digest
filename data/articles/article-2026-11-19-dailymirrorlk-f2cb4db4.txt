HNB FINANCE surpasses Rs.100bn in assets as 1H profit rises 50%

Profit after tax rose by 50% YoY to Rs.929mn, supported by stronger core earnings

Net interest income rose by 46% YoY to Rs.5.48bn, reflecting strong core business performance

HNB FINANCE PLC surpassed the Rs.100 billion asset milestone in the first half of the financial year 2026/27, with total assets reaching Rs.106.67 billion as at September 30, 2026.

The company also recorded a 50 percent year-on-year (YoY) increase in profit after tax to Rs.929 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026, reflecting the sustained earnings growth.

The company’s profit before tax increased by 50 percent to Rs.2.47 billion, compared to Rs.1.65 billion in the corresponding period of the previous year. The net interest income increased by 46 percent to Rs.5.48 billion, from Rs.3.76 billion, further supporting the improvement in profitability.

The company’s asset base expanded by 13 percent from Rs.94.39 billion as at March 31, 2026. Net assets increased by 37 percent to Rs.12.68 billion, from Rs.9.26 billion, contributed by the Rs.2.5 billion rights issue and growth in profitability, thereby further strengthening HNB FINANCE’s overall financial position.

The lending portfolio expanded by 7 percent to Rs. 81.95 billion, from Rs.76.33 billion during the six-month period. Leasing remained a key contributor to portfolio growth, recording a 17 percent increase to Rs.59.92 billion, from Rs.51.02 billion. The deposit base improved to Rs.71.43 billion as at September 30, 2026.

HNB FINANCE PLC Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Chaminda Prabhath stated, “The first-half results reflect the resilience of our business model and the steady progress we are making in strengthening HNB FINANCE’s financial position. With our asset base surpassing the Rs.100 billion milestone, we are encouraged by the strong financial performance during the first half of the year, reflected in the 50 percent growth in profit after tax, 46 percent increase in net interest income and continued expansion of our lending and leasing portfolios. These results demonstrate the strength of our core business operations and the effectiveness of our strategic focus on sustainable growth, operational efficiency and prudent risk management. As we move forward, we remain committed to sound governance, customer-centric innovation and delivering accessible, responsible financial solutions that create long-term value for our shareholders, customers and other stakeholders, while contributing to Sri Lanka’s economic development.”

The company remains focused on sustaining its growth trajectory through prudent financial management, strengthening its customer franchise and expanding access to financial services while maintaining a disciplined approach to business expansion.
