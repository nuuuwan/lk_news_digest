Pussalla Agri Ventures wins Sustainable Agriculture Infrastructure Award

Pussalla Agri Ventures (Pvt.) Ltd has been honoured with the Sustainable Agriculture Infrastructure Award, at the prestigious Sri Lankan Sustainability Awards 2026, recognising the company’s outstanding commitment to sustainable agriculture, environmental stewardship and responsible organic cinnamon production.

The award, presented at the grand ceremony held at The Hilton Colombo and organised by the Ceylon Institute of Builders, recognises the organisations that have demonstrated excellence in integrating sustainability into their core business operations while contributing to Sri Lanka’s sustainable development agenda.

The recognition highlights Pussalla Agri Ventures’ holistic approach to sustainable agriculture, where environmentally responsible farming practices are combined with internationally recognised quality and food safety standards.

The company has established an integrated value chain encompassing cinnamon cultivation, harvesting, processing, value addition and packaging, ensuring complete traceability and consistent product quality from farm to finished product.

Committed to protecting natural ecosystems, Pussalla Agri Ventures implements responsible agricultural practices focused on soil conservation, biodiversity protection, efficient resource utilisation and the elimination of prohibited synthetic chemicals. These initiatives support the long-term sustainability of agricultural production while preserving the environment for future generations.

The company’s commitment to international best practices is reflected in its portfolio of globally recognised certifications, including EU Organic, USDA Organic, Sri Lanka Good Agricultural Practices (SL-GAP), Food Good Manufacturing Practices (Food GMP) and Cosmetic Good Manufacturing Practices (Cosmetic GMP). These certifications validate the company’s compliance with stringent international requirements for organic production, product quality, environmental protection and consumer safety.

Pussalla Agri Ventures Chairman Philip J. Wewita said, “This recognition is a proud milestone for our organisation and reflects our commitment to sustainable agriculture and responsible business practices. Sustainability is not simply a certification or a project for us; it is embedded throughout our operations, from cultivation to the final product. We remain committed to producing premium-quality organic cinnamon while protecting the environment, empowering our farming communities and enhancing Sri Lanka’s reputation as a trusted supplier of sustainable agricultural products to global markets.”

The Sri Lankan Sustainability Awards recognise organisations that demonstrate leadership in environmental sustainability, governance, innovation, social responsibility and sustainable business excellence, encouraging businesses to align their operations with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Receiving the Sustainable Agriculture Infrastructure Award further strengthens Pussalla Agri Ventures’ position as one of Sri Lanka’s emerging leaders in sustainable agribusiness. The company continues to invest in environmentally responsible farming, internationally certified production systems and value-added product development, supporting the country’s ambition to expand exports of premium organic agricultural products while creating long-term value for customers, communities and the environment.
