Chemical release triggers water supply shutdown in Colombo and Gampaha

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The temporary suspension of water supply to several areas in the Colombo and Gampaha districts following the release of a chemical into the environment by a private factory has raised serious concerns over the safety of drinking water in the affected areas.

The National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) said the Ambatale and Biyagama water purification plants were temporarily shut down as an immediate precaution after the chemical release was detected.

NWSDB chemical engineers launched an immediate investigation and visited the affected area to assess whether pollutants had entered the Kelani River, the main water source for the two treatment plants.

NWSDB Chairman Chandana Bandara said the water supply was suspended as a precautionary measure until authorities could confirm that the water remained safe for public consumption.

Following tests and confirmation of the quality of the Kelani River water, both the Ambatale and Biyagama treatment plants resumed operations at around 4.30 a.m. today.

The NWSDB said water supply to several Colombo areas was restored by 7 a.m., while supplies to Kotte, Battaramulla and Wellawatta were restored by around 9 a.m. Water supply to Biyagama, Kelaniya and Gampaha areas was also restored from around 6 a.m.

However, questions remain over how the chemical entered the environment and whether the incident could have posed a wider threat to the Kelani River and the thousands of people who depend on it for drinking water.

Chairman Bandara said a comprehensive investigation into the incident is being jointly conducted by the Disaster Management Centre and the security forces.

He added that the NWSDB had lodged a complaint with the police and that the relevant authorities had been informed to take appropriate legal action against those responsible.

The incident has once again raised concerns over the monitoring of industrial activities near major water sources and the potential risks posed by chemical pollution to public health and the environment.
