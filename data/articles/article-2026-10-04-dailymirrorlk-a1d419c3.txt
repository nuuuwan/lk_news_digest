Sri Lanka name 16-member squad for Pakistan ODI Tri-Nation Series

The Sri Lanka Cricket Selection Committee has named a 16-member squad for the upcoming ODI Tri-Nation Series in Pakistan, featuring hosts Pakistan and England.

The squad will take part in the ODI series following the conclusion of Sri Lanka’s T20I series in Pakistan.

Kusal Mendis has been named captain of the Sri Lankan ODI team, with Pathum Nissanka, Avishka Fernando, Kamil Mishara, Pavan Rathnayake, Sahan Arachchige, Kamindu Mendis, Janith Liyanage and Dasun Shanaka among the other players selected.

Sri Lanka Cricket said Nissanka will travel with the team only if he is declared medically fit.
