SJB MP Chaminda Wijesiri granted bail

Colombo, Aug. 24 (Daily Mirror) - Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Chaminda Wijesiri, who was sentenced to 18 months in prison for threatening and assaulting two police officers, has been granted bail by the Badulla High Court pending his appeal.

The court sentenced Wijesiri on July 28 after finding him guilty of charges including wrongful restraint, assault, causing injuries, issuing death threats and causing bodily harm to two police officers in 2019.

The case was filed against Wijesiri, his driver Bandula Rathnayake and a police officer attached to the Ministerial Security Division (MSD).

The court had previously found Wijesiri and Rathnayake guilty of four charges, while MSD officer Rasika Karunathilaka was found guilty of three charges. During the proceedings, the court was informed that Rathnayake had since died.

According to the prosecution, the incident took place near the Bandarawela town roundabout on or around February 10, 2019, following an altercation after a police jeep overtook the vehicle in which Wijesiri and his group were travelling.

Wijesiri was granted bail after the Badulla High Court considered his appeal against the conviction and sentence.
