Panda Love seeks franchise partners to accelerate nationwide growth

Panda Love is seeking franchise partners as the Sri Lankan-built bubble tea brand prepares for its next phase of expansion.

The company, created by the team behind Arcadia Café & Restaurant, said it is offering entrepreneurs and business partners an opportunity to join the brand as it expands into new locations across Sri Lanka.

Panda Love has already established outlets in Bambalapitiya and Negombo and is preparing to launch its flagship store at Savoy Wellawatta on September 7.

The company has also identified Malabe as its next location.

The brand has developed a range of beverages including the Kao Kup Series, its locally inspired Ceylon Series and a Hot Series.

Its Ceylon Series includes flavours such as kiri pani, ginger and aloe vera, reflecting the company’s strategy of adapting international beverage concepts to local consumer preferences.

The flagship business model also incorporates food, snacks, merchandise and customer experiences, while Arcadia Express will operate at the same location as a food-focused sub-brand.

Panda Love said its franchise model is backed by its brand identity, product development experience and operational expertise.

The company aims to establish long-term partnerships with franchisees while building a wider network of Panda Love outlets across Sri Lanka.
