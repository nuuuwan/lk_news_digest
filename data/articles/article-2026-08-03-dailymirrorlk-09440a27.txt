Landslide evacuation warning issued for parts of Kandy

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The National Building Research Organisation (NBRO) has issued a Level 3 'Evacuation' landslide warning for the Pasbage Korale and Ganga Ihala Korale Divisional Secretariat Divisions in the Kandy District due to the prevailing adverse weather conditions.

The warning is valid for 24 hours, from 9.00 a.m. today (3) until 9.00 a.m. tomorrow (4), the NBRO said.

Meanwhile, Level 2 ('Amber') landslide warnings have been issued for several areas, including:

Nuwara Eliya District: Nuwara Eliya, Norwood, Ambagamuwa Korale, Kotmale West and Kotmale East

In addition, Level 1 ('Yellow') landslide warnings have been issued for Panwila and Medadumbara in the Kandy District; Dehiowita and Deraniyagala in the Kegalle District; and Nivitigala and Kuruwita in the Ratnapura District.

The NBRO warned that rainfall is expected to continue over the coming days and urged people living in landslide-prone areas to remain vigilant.

Authorities also advised residents in areas that have previously experienced landslides or slope failures to evacuate immediately to safer locations if they observe signs of an imminent landslide, such as cracks in the ground, leaning trees, tilting structures or unusual sounds from the ground.
