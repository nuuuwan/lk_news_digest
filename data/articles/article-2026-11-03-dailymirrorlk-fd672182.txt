Rs. 111 million contract awarded to supply 1.1 million bags of dialysis medicines

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the award of a Rs. 111.1 million contract to supply 1.1 million bags of medicines used in the treatment of kidney patients undergoing haemodialysis.

The contract has been awarded to Infuxion Lanka (Private) Limited, Sri Lanka, the lowest responsive bidder. The manufacturer of the medicines is Peritone Health Sdn Bhd, Malaysia.

The medicines are used to remove waste products and excess fluids from the bodies of patients suffering from acute or chronic kidney failure.

Two bids were received following an invitation for bids under the International Competitive Procurement Procedure.

The High-Level Procurement Committee recommended awarding the contract to Infuxion Lanka following an evaluation of the bids.

Based on the committee's recommendation, the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the contract for Rs. 111,119,990.
