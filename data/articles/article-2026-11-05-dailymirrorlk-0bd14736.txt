Tudawe opens fourth automated car wash

Tudawe Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd., the sole authorised agent for Kärcher products in Sri Lanka, officially opened its fourth automated car-wash facility at Sandagiri Tire House, No. 643, Hendala, Wattala, on November 5, 2026. The facility was inaugurated by Eshan Tudawe, Chief Executive Officer of Tudawe Trading, and Niluka Dilhani, owner of Sandagiri Tire House, along with others. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
