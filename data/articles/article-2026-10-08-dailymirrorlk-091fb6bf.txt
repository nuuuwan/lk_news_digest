SPAR introduces full cream milk powder

In a first for the Sri Lankan supermarket sector, SPAR Sri Lanka has introduced SPAR Full Cream Milk Powder, bringing New Zealand milk powder to consumers under a trusted SPAR supermarket brand at an affordable price.

The new product represents another milestone in SPAR Sri Lanka’s journey to provide the customers with quality, choice and value, while making internationally sourced products more accessible to Sri Lankan families.

“Our objective has always been to give Sri Lankan consumers better quality, better choice and better value. With the launch of SPAR Full Cream Milk Powder, we are taking another step towards that promise by bringing New Zealand milk powder under the trusted SPAR brand at a price that is accessible to more families,” said SPAR Sri Lanka CEO Kumar de Silva.

As a global supermarket brand with a strong local presence, SPAR continues to bring international standards and global sourcing opportunities closer to Sri Lankan consumers. The introduction of its own full cream milk powder further strengthens SPAR’s commitment to developing products that respond to the changing needs of today’s shoppers.

“This launch is more than introducing another product to our shelves. It is about strengthening the SPAR ecosystem, connecting global quality trusted suppliers our retail network and most importantly our customers by bringing New Zealand, milk powder under the trusted SPAR brand,” said Director Commercial Daham Gunasena.

The new SPAR Full Cream Milk Powder will be available at SPAR outlets and SPAR SaveMor outlets across Sri Lanka.
