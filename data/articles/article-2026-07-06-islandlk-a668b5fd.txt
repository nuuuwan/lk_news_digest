Declining trends among banking stocks snag early vibrant trading

CSE trading started on a positive note yesterday but the trend proved short-lived due to declining trends among especially banking sector counters.

The All Share Price Index went down by 98.08 points, while the S and P SL20 declined by 19.53 points.

Turnover stood at Rs 1.35 billion with three crossings. Those crossings were; WindForce 7.6 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 304 million; its shares traded at Rs 40, Hemas Holdings one million shares crossed for Rs 32.5 million; its shares sold at Rs 32.50 and HNB (Non-Voting) 90000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 30.2 million; its shares sold at Rs 336.

In the retail market companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Singer (SriLanka) Rs 113 million (1.5 million shares traded), WindForce Rs 69 million (1.7 million shares traded), CCS Rs 48 million (370,000 shares traded), JKH Rs 44.1 million (2.2 million shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 35 million (247,000 shares traded),Seylan Bank Rs 29 million (413,000 shares traded) and Commercial Bank Rs 25 (122,000 shares traded). During the day 41.8 million share volumes changed hands in 13397 transactions.

Negative contributors to the ASPI were HNB (down Rs 3.25 at 396.75), R I L Property (down Rs 1 at 25.90) and Dialog Axiata (down 40 cents at Rs 45.50).

Top positive contributors were Lion Brewery (up Rs 83 at 1,834), Haycarb (up Rs 3 at 160.50), and Industrial Asphalts (up 10 cents at 60 cents).

It is said that renewable energy sector counters, especially WindForce, performed well while banking sector counters, especially Commercial Bank, Sampath Bank and Seylan Bank performed well too. Manufacturing sector, especially JKH, was significantly active at the floor.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 335.00/40 to the US dollar in the spot market, from Rs 335.20/35 the previous day, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

The telegraphic transfer rate for Sri Lanka’s rupee against the US dollar was 330.75 buying, 339.75 selling; the euro was 375.4943 selling, 389.4113 buying; and the pound was 440.2150 buying, 454.2606 selling.
