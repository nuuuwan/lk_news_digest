Rough seas suspend fishing in Matara–Pottuvil sea area

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Department has issued an urgent warning to the fishing community, advising fishermen to refrain from venturing into the sea from Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota due to rough sea conditions.

The warning follows a severe weather advisory issued by the Department of Meteorology today (Aug. 6).

The department said the affected sea area is currently unsafe for coastal fishing and urged all fishermen in the region not to leave for fishing activities until further notice.

Fishing vessels departing from other parts of the country have also been warned not to enter or engage in fishing activities within the designated risk zone.

The Fisheries Department has called on the entire fishing community to remain vigilant, closely monitor weather updates and strictly follow safety instructions to prevent loss of life and property.
