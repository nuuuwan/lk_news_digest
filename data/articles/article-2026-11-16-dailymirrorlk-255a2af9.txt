Court orders CID to verify whether summons served on Indian witnesses in Namal Rajapaksa’s case

Colombo, Nov 16 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo High Court Judge Nadee Aparna Suwandurugoda has directed the prosecution to verify whether summons issued through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to witnesses residing in India in connection with the Krrish transaction case against Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa have been served on them through the Indian High Commission in Sri Lanka.

The prosecution was directed to ascertain whether the summons had been duly served on the relevant witnesses and inform court accordingly.

The court further fixed the case for hearing on January 18 and directed the Superintendent of Prisons to produce Namal Rajapaksa before court on that date if he remains in custody.
