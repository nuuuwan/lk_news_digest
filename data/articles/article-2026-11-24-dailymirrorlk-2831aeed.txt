Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Carson Cumberbatch and Hayleys with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.1bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Bairaha Farms, Melstacorp and Wealthtrust Securities Limited.

Mixed interest was observed in Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka, Sampath Bank and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, Tess Agro and UB Finance Company.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Bairaha Farms) whilst the sector index lost 0.40%. The share price of Bairaha Farms increased by Rs. 1.40 (1.67%) to close at Rs. 85.40.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.85%. The share price of John Keells Holdings declined by Rs. 0.30 (1.60%) to close at Rs. 18.40.

Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka, Sampath Bank and Wealthtrust Securities Limited were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka gained Rs. 9.00 (5.11%) to close at Rs. 185.00. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs. 148.25. The share price of Wealthtrust Securities Limited recorded a loss of Rs. 0.30 (2.78%) to close at Rs. 10.50.
