22nd Amendment does not require referendum, Supreme Court determines: Speaker

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne today announced that the Supreme Court has held that the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution does not need to be approved by the people at a referendum.

However, the Supreme Court has held that the six year term does not apply to the Chief Justice who is holding office when the Bill comes into effect.

Also, he announced the Supreme Court had determined that the Judicature Amendment Bill can be approved by a simple majority in Parliament.
