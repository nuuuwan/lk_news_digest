Indices close in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Melstacorp, Dialog Axiata and Hayleys with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.3bn.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka, Hayleys and Commercial Bank.

Mixed interest was observed in Nations Trust Bank, Dialog Axiata and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Co-Operative Insurance Company, Softlogic Finance and Cargills Bank.

Furthermore, foreigners closed as net sellers. Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hayleys and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index lost 0.30%. The share price of Hayleys lost Rs. 3.50 (1.54%) to close at Rs. 224.00. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a gain of Rs.  0.10 (0.52%) to close at Rs. 19.20.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover whilst the sector index decreased by 0.47%. Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka, Nations Trust Bank and Commercial Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors.

The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka increased by Rs. 0.25 (0.16%) to close at Rs. 154.25. The share price of Nations Trust Bank moved down by Rs. 2.50 (0.81%) to close at Rs. 304.75. The share price of Commercial Bank declined by Rs. 1.00 (0.49%) to close at Rs. 202.50.
