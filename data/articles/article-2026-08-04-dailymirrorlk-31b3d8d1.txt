Constitution amendment to extend retirement age of judges will be implemented despite opposition protests: Minister

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Constitutional amendment and the amendment to the Judicature Bill will be presented to Parliament and implemented, despite opposition protests, Minister Vijitha Herath said.

The Minister told Parliament that the sole intention of the legislations is to change the structure of the Judiciary which is aimed at ending the backlog of court cases.

He claimed that the government had obtained the blessings of tge Maha Sangha for the amendments.

"The Maha Sangha were misled by various forces, but now they have understood that situation," he said.

"The amendment is very much in line with the mandate given to the government by the people," he also said.
