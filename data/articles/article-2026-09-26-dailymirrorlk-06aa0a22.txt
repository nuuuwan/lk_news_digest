Seven injured in gas cylinder explosion at Polonnaruwa Nephrology Hospital

Polonnaruwa, September 24 (Daily Mirror)- At least seven employees were injured and admitted to the Polonnaruwa General Hospital following a gas cylinder explosion in the kitchen of the Polonnaruwa National Nephrology Hospital.

According to the Polonnaruwa Police, the exact cause of the gas leak that led to the explosion has not yet been determined. Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
