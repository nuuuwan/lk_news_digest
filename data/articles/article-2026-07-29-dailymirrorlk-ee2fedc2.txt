Wendy’s joins Sri Lanka’s fast-food race through JKH

John Keells Holdings PLC (JKH) will bring the global fast-food chain Wendy’s to Sri Lanka, marking the diversified conglomerate’s entry into the quick-service restaurant segment.

The first Wendy’s outlet is expected to open by December 2026, with the franchise agreements and other preliminary work already completed.

“JKH will diversify its consumer foods portfolio into Sri Lanka’s quick service restaurant segment through the rollout of the internationally recognised Wendy’s brand,” JKH Chairperson Krishan Balendra said in the group’s latest quarterly release.

JKH is yet to disclose the location of the first outlet, the investment involved or the number of restaurants planned under the rollout.

The group’s consumer foods segment reported earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation of Rs.1.52 billion for the quarter ended June 2026, up 21 percent, from Rs.1.26 billion a year earlier.

The segment’s performance was supported by a strong volume growth, with the beverage volumes rising 27 percent and confectionery volumes increasing 13 percent during the quarter.
