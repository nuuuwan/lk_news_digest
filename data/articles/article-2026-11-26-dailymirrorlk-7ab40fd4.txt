New fuel station on Delft Island moves closer to operation

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Fuel transportation to the newly constructed permanent filling station on Delft Island in Jaffna has been successfully completed.

Two fuel bowsers were transported by sea from Kankesanthurai Harbour to Delft Island aboard Sri Lanka Navy vessel SLNS Ranagaja, attached to the Northern Naval Command.

The filling station was established under the government’s programme to develop Delft Island as a sustainable tourist destination. It is expected to improve transportation services and facilitate fuel distribution for residents.

According to the Ministry of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources, the new facility will help streamline fuel supplies and make distribution more convenient for people living on the island.

The agreement to construct the filling station was signed in October 2025 during the launch of the Kurikadduwan Jetty development project. The foundation stone was laid in July 2026, when construction work also commenced.
