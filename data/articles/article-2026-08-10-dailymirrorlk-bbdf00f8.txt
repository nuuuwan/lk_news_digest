Mobile drug-testing bus handed over to NITM

Colombo, Aug. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Health Ministry today handed over a fully equipped mobile drug-testing bus to the National Institute of Transport Medicine (NITM) to strengthen the national programme aimed at detecting drug use among public transport personnel and improving road safety.

The clinical bus, bearing registration number PZA-0040, was officially handed over to NITM Chairman Professor Clifford Perera by Minister of Health and Mass Media Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa at the Ministry premises today (10).

The mobile drug-testing programme is being implemented under the two-year strategic plan of the National Road Safety Programme initiated by the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development.

The programme conducts random drug tests on drivers, conductors and assistants of public transport services to identify drug use while on duty. It is currently being implemented in five provinces, with plans to expand it to other provinces as the required human and physical resources are developed.

According to the Ministry, 7,150 tests have so far been conducted under the national random drug-testing programme.

The programme is expected to help reduce deaths, injuries and property damage caused by road accidents through short-, medium- and long-term measures, with cooperation from several ministries.

Jabil were among the officials present at the event.
