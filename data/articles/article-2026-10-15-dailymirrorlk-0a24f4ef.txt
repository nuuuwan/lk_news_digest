JETOUR continues expansion with showroom at Colombo City Centre

Ravindra Senarathne, COO of Euro Motors (Pvt) Ltd, and Supun Perera, CEO of Colombo City Centre, at the opening of the showroom at Colombo City Centre

JETOUR, the world’s fastest-growing SUV brands, continues to strengthen its presence in Sri Lanka with the opening of its second showroom at Colombo City Centre, bringing the brand’s growing range of SUVs and contemporary customer experience closer to consumers in the heart of Colombo.

Since entering the Sri Lankan market, JETOUR has continued to build strong momentum, establishing itself as one of the fastest-growing SUV brands in the local market. The opening of the new Colombo City Centre showroom marks another important milestone in this journey, providing existing and prospective customers with a convenient location to discover and experience the JETOUR brand.

Located within one of Colombo’s prominent lifestyle destinations, the new showroom is designed to connect JETOUR with a wider audience, including young professionals, families and automotive enthusiasts. Visitors will have the opportunity to experience JETOUR’s latest SUVs, including JETOUR T2 and JETOUR T1, and explore their design, technology, features and performance.

Ravindra Senarathne, Chief Operating Officer, Euro Motors, said, “The opening of our second JETOUR showroom is an important milestone in strengthening the brand’s presence in Sri Lanka. Colombo City Centre allows us to bring the JETOUR experience closer to a wider audience and gives customers an opportunity to experience our products in a modern and accessible environment. As JETOUR continues to grow, we are also focused on expanding our presence across the island and bringing more exciting products to the Sri Lankan market.”

The expansion extends beyond Colombo, with Euro Motors planning to establish additional JETOUR showrooms in Anuradhapura, Kurunegala, Ratnapura and Kandy soon. The growing network will enable customers across the country to access JETOUR vehicles and experience the brand closer to home.

The expansion of JETOUR’s presence in Sri Lanka will also pave the way for the introduction of new models in thefuture, further strengthening the brand’s product portfolio and giving customers more options across different SUV segments.

With its second showroom now open at Colombo City Centre, further locations planned across the island and new models on the horizon, JETOUR continues to build on its rapid growth in Sri Lanka and bring its next generation of SUVs closer to customers across the country.
