A/L 2026 practical tests to begin on Sept. 24

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - Practical tests for candidates who sat for the written examination of the 2026 G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination will be held from September 24, the Department of Examinations announced.

Accordingly, practical tests for Dancing (Indian), Oriental Music, Carnatic Music, Western Music, and Drama and Performing Arts in Sinhala, Tamil and English will be conducted from September 24 to October 3.

The practical tests for Engineering Technology will be held from September 30 to October 10, while Home Economics practical tests will be conducted from October 10 to 20.

Biosystems Technology practical tests are scheduled from October 17 to 21.

The Department said admission cards of school candidates would be sent by post to the principals of the respective schools, while admission cards of private candidates would be sent to the addresses provided in their applications.

Candidates will also be able to download their admission cards from the official Department of Examinations website, beginning seven days before the relevant practical tests.

Candidates seeking further information can contact the Department of Examinations hotline 1911 or 0112785922, 0112784537, 0112786616 and 0112784208.
