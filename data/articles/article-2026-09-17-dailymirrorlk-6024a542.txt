Floral tribute to Anagarika Dharmapala

India’s Deputy High Commissioner Maitreyi Kulkarni pays floral tribute to the statue of Anagarika Dharmapala during a commemorative ceremony marking the 162nd birth anniversary of Sir Anagarika Dharmapala and the 76th birthday of Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the Agrashravaka Maha Viharaya in Maligakanda, Colombo, yesterday. The ceremony was presided over by Sri Lanka Mahabodhi Society President Ven. Banagala Upatissa Nayaka Thero and attended by local and foreign dignitaries, including Dr. Supachal Verapunchong, founder and Secretary-General of Thailand’s BodhiGaya Vijjalaya 980 Institute and an adviser to the Dharma Alliance. Ven. Banagala Upatissa Nayaka Thero also announced that the sacred relics of Arahants Sariputta and Moggallana, enshrined at the temple, would be placed on public display for a limited period.
