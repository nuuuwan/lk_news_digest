Late IGP borrowed security officer’s weapon, claiming he wanted to examine it: Court told

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - The security officer of the late IGP C.D. Wickramaratne, testifying at the magisterial inquiry into the death of the former IGP, told court that the late IGP had borrowed his duty weapon and ammunition, claiming that he wanted to examine it.

Sergeant (697) Susantha testifying before Kaduwela Magistrate Aruna Indrajith Buddhadasa on Tuesday (28) said that it was unusual as Mr. Wickramaratne had never asked for his duty weapon before.

Western Province (South) Investigation Unit led evidence at the inquiry.

‘ I count a service of more than 16 years as the Private Security Officer of the former IGP, but he has never asked me for my duty weapon to be checked. However, on the day of the incident, he wanted my duty weapon and after obtaining my duty weapon he asked for ammunition as well. Whenever he went out for exercises, he would put on shoes but on that fateful day he was not wearing shoes. He never took bottles of water when going out for exercises. After I gave him the pistol and ammunition, he told me to bring him a bottle of water. Just as I stepped out, I heard the report of pistol shots, and I rushed to the scene to find him lying on the floor. I with the help of others in the household rushed him to the Mulleriyawa hospital. Later I learnt that he had died.” Sergeant Susantha further submitted.

Magistrate ordered police to refer the pistol and the spent cartridges to the Government Analyst for report.

The JMO Mulleriyawa Base Hospital had returned an open verdict regarding the death of the former IGP.
