Asgiri Chapter questions legal basis for removing Gnanasara Thera’s robe

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Most Ven. Medagama Dhammananda Thera, Secretary of the Asgiri Chapter of the Shyamopali Maha Nikaya, has raised serious legal concerns over the decision to remove the robe of Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera and present him before court in lay attire after his imprisonment.

The Asgiri Chapter said it respected the court verdict against the Thera but questioned whether the procedure followed by prison authorities was consistent with the relevant provisions of the Prisons Ordinance.

It said a review of the Ordinance suggested that the Thera should have been produced before court in the same robes he had previously worn.

The Chapter said the constitutional status accorded to Buddhism, together with traditional customary laws and ancient legal systems, should be taken into consideration when dealing with matters concerning Buddhist monks.

Describing the matter as one of grave concern, it said the action could establish a detrimental precedent in relation to the Buddhist Sasana.

The Chapter said it was examining the relevant legal provisions and considering its next course of action, including the possibility of re-ordaining the Thera if deemed necessary.

It also said Buddhist principles placed emphasis on forgiveness and guiding those who had erred back towards the correct path, while reaffirming its respect for the existing legal system.

The Chapter said that its immediate concern was not the Thera’s political affiliations or ideological positions, but whether the procedure adopted by prison authorities in producing him before court complied with the applicable legal provisions.
