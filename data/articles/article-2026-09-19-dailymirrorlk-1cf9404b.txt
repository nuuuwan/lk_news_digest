Sri Lanka pitches wellness, wildlife tourism to Middle Eastern market

Sri Lanka is sharpening its focus on wellness, wildlife and experiential tourism to attract more travellers from the Middle East, using its participation at the Arabian Travel Market (ATM) 2026, to promote the island beyond its traditional beach offering.

The Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau (SLTPB), together with 58 representatives from the local travel and tourism industry, showcased the country’s wellness experiences, wildlife, cultural heritage, beaches and scenic landscapes to international buyers and travel professionals at the four-day event in Dubai.

The participation formed part of Sri Lanka’s efforts to deepen engagement with the Middle Eastern travel trade and position the country as a diverse, year-round destination for regional travellers.

The SLTPB also held a dedicated press conference and engaged with international and regional media to highlight its tourism products, emerging market opportunities and initiatives aimed at strengthening the country’s appeal in the Middle East.

The campaign was supported by new memoranda of understanding with Emirates and Qatar Airways. The agreements are expected to facilitate destination promotion and strengthen Sri Lanka’s access to key international markets through the Gulf carriers’ networks.

The Sri Lanka pavilion was inaugurated by Ambassador to the United Arab Emirates Professor Arusha Cooray, Consul General in Dubai Alexi Gunasekera and SLTPB Deputy Managing Director Chamila Jayarathna.

ATM 2026, held from September 14 to 17, at the Dubai World Trade Centre, brought together tourism authorities, airlines, travel professionals, buyers and other industry stakeholders.

The event was held under the theme ‘Travel 2040: Driving New Frontiers Through Innovation and Technology’.
