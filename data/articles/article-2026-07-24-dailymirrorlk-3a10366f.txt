Janashakthi Finance marks 45th anniversary

Janashakthi Finance PLC, a subsidiary of JXG (Janashakthi Group), marks its 45th anniversary, celebrating four and a half decades of financial expertise, customer confidence, and meaningful contributions to Sri Lanka’s Non-BankFinancial Institution (NBFI) sector.

The milestone reflects the company’s evolution as a trusted financial partner, built on strong relationships, sound governance, and a commitment to delivering innovative solutions that empower individuals and businesses.

Established in 1981, Janashakthi Finance has grown into one of Sri Lanka’s long established licensed finance companies, earning recognition for its customer-focused approach, operational discipline, and ability to adapt to the evolving needs of the market.

Supported by the strength of its parent company, JXG (Janashakthi Group), the company continues to expand access to financial services while supporting individuals, entrepreneurs, micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), and communities across the country.

Over the past 45 years, Janashakthi Finance has evolved alongside Sri Lanka’s economic landscape, continuously strengthening its products, services, and customer experience to address emerging financial needs. Through its comprehensive portfolio of financial solutions, expanding reach, and relationship-driven approach, the company has enabled thousands of Sri Lankans to pursue personal aspirations, grow businesses, and create new opportunities.

A key strength of Janashakthi Finance’s journey has been its ability to build lasting relationships through trust, service excellence, and a deep understanding of customer needs. Following its rebranding from Orient Finance PLC to Janashakthi Finance PLC in 2025, the company has seen stronger customer sentiment reflected through brand studies, alongside positive customer satisfaction levels and a strong Net Promoter Score (NPS), reinforcing the confidence customers place in the institution and its ability to deliver meaningful financial experiences.

The company’s commitment to excellence has also been recognised through several industry accolades over the years. Janashakthi Finance has received recognition at the CA Sri Lanka TAGS Awards in 2024 and 2025, the IFFSA Awards in 2024, and the 10th International Islamic Finance Forum South Asia (IFFSA) Awards 2025, the SLIBFI Awards 2024, the 11th CMA Excellence in Integrated Reporting Awards, the SLIM DIGIS 2.5 Awards 2025, the Effie Awards Sri Lanka 2025, and the Best Management Practices Company Awards 2025. These recognitions highlight the institution’s progress in areas including governance, integrated reporting, marketing effectiveness, digital engagement, financial inclusion, and responsible business practices.

Rajendra Theagarajah, Chairman of Janashakthi Finance, reflected on the milestone, stating, “Over the years, we have strengthened our position by placing customers at the centre of our decisions while maintaining high standards of governance, financial discipline, and responsible growth. As Sri Lanka progresses towards greater economic opportunities, we remain committed to supporting individuals, entrepreneurs, and businesses through accessible and relevant financial solutions that contribute to national growth.”

Highlighting the company’s future direction, Sithambaram Sri Ganendran, Chief Executive Officerof Janashakthi Finance, said, “Our 45-year milestone represents the trust we have built with our customers and stakeholders over generations. Looking ahead, we will continue to strengthen our capabilities, accelerate digital transformation, embrace innovation and enhance the customer experience while delivering unique financial solutions that create greater opportunities for individuals and businesses across Sri Lanka.”

Janashakthi Finance remains committed to advancing financial inclusion by extending accessible financial solutions to individuals, entrepreneurs, MSMEs, and underserved segments.
