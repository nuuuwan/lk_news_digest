Pathum Nissanka ruled out of Tri-Nation ODI series

Colombo, Oct. 15 (Daliy Mirror) - Sri Lanka opener Pathum Nissanka will miss the upcoming ODI Tri-Nation Series featuring Pakistan and England as he has yet to receive medical clearance to return to competitive cricket.

Nissanka sustained a left hamstring injury during Sri Lanka's recently concluded tour of England. His recovery has not progressed to the point where he has been cleared to resume playing, ruling him out of the upcoming tournament.

Lahiru Udara has been named as Nissanka's replacement in Sri Lanka's ODI squad for the series.
