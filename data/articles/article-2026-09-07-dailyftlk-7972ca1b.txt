Ex-Ceylon Chamber staff celebrate 10th anniversary bash

Puvi Domingopillai (second from left) with the organising committee after the presentation

The Ex-Ceylon Chamber of Commerce Employees marked their 10th anniversary bash with a lively celebration at the ME Hotel, Colombo, recently. The wellattended event brought together former colleagues for an evening of camaraderie, games, and dancing.

A highlight of the evening was the presentation of a Plaque of Recognition to Puvi Domingopillai, former Senior Assistant Secretary General of the Chamber. Domingopillai was honored for her vision and steadfast support in initiating and sustaining the reunion bash over the past decade.

Guests enjoyed reminiscing about shared experiences, rekindling friendships, and celebrating the enduring spirit of the Chamber community.
