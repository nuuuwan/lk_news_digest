China’s Xi to visit the US from September 23-25

Reuters - Chinese President Xi Jinping ​will pay a state visit ‌to the US from September 23 to 25 at the invitation of President Donald Trump, ​China's Foreign Ministry said ​in a statement on Monday.

During the ⁠visit, Xi "will hold in-depth exchanges ​of views with President Trump on ​major issues concerning China-US relations, as well as world peace and development," a Chinese ​foreign ministry spokesperson said during ​a regular press conference shortly after the ‌announcement.

The ⁠reciprocal visits by the two heads of state within a six-month period are "of historic, milestone significance," ministry ​spokesperson Guo ​Jiakun ⁠said.

"China is willing to work with the US to ... strengthen dialogue and ​cooperation, properly manage differences, ​continuously ⁠enhance the well-being of the peoples of both countries, and promote world ⁠peace, ​stability and prosperity," ​Guo added.
