Mahanayake Theras oppose proposal to extend judges’ retirement age: BASL President

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Mahanayake Theras of the three Buddhist chapters have written to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake stating that the proposed constitutional amendment to extend the retirement age of judges is inappropriate, President of the Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) Rajeev Amarasuriya said.

He made this revelation while speaking to the media today (3) after meeting the Mahanayake Thera of the Malwathu Chapter.

Amarasuriya said the Mahanayake Thera had informed him that the chief prelates of the Malwathu, Asgiriya, Ramanna and Amarapura Chapters had sent a letter to the President expressing their opposition to the proposed constitutional amendment.

“I believe this Government has no moral right to proceed with this matter. The Mahanayake Thera informed us a short while ago that the chief prelates of the Malwathu, Asgiriya, Ramanna and Amarapura Chapters have written to the President stating that this constitutional amendment is unsuitable,” he said.
