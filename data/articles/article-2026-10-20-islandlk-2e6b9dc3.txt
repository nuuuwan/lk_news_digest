Deena thanks The Island for featuring her historic journey

The first Sri Lankan to be elected in Scotland, Deena Tissera, has extended a heartfelt thank you to The Island for a recent feature on her inspiring journey (Thursday, 15th October, 2026).

Taking to social media, Deena shared the article, titled “The Sri Lankan who made Scottish history,” and expressed her gratitude.

She wrote: “A heartfelt thank you to journalist Ivan Alvis and The Island, one of Sri Lanka’s leading newspapers, for this great piece featuring my journey,”

In her post, Deena also took the opportunity to clarify misinformation that had been circulating about her family background.

“Following the recent circulation of false information claiming I came from a powerful political dynasty, I wanted to share my own story,” she stated.

“My father was a doctor, serial entrepreneur and philanthropist — not a politician. He was my greatest inspiration and taught me the importance of compassion, hard work and service to others. I inherited his values, not a political career. I forged my own path.”

The original feature in The Island traced Deena’s remarkable path — from her early education at The British School in Colombo and her roots in Chilaw, to beauty pageants, medical science, student activism in the UK, and finally making history in Scottish politics after 15 years in British public life.

The Island feature has been widely shared and praised for highlighting the achievements of a Sri Lankan woman on the global stage.
