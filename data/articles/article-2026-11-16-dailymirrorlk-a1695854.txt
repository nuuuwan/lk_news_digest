Hemas Consumer Brands earns recognition

Hemas Consumer Brands (HCB) has been recognised as the Second Runner-Up in the Extra Large Category at National Supply Chain Excellence Awards 2026, held recently at Monarch Imperial.

This recognition reflects the company’s commitment to supply chain excellence, continuous improvement and the collective efforts of its people.

Built on teamwork, dedication and a shared commitment to doing better, this achievement reinforces HCB’s focus on strengthening its capabilities, embracing opportunities for improvement and continually raising standards across its end-to-end supply chain operation.
