Montana Governor Greg Gianforte to visit Sri Lanka

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Montana Governor Greg Gianforte, accompanied by First Lady Susan Gianforte and a state delegation, will visit Sri Lanka on October 9 to explore opportunities to expand U.S. agricultural exports and strengthen security cooperation between Montana and Sri Lanka.

The delegation is expected to meet Sri Lankan pulse importers and traders to explore new markets for Montana-grown chickpeas, lentils and dry peas, supporting greater commercial ties between businesses in the two countries.

The visit will also focus on defense cooperation through the Montana National Guard’s State Partnership Program, which has connected Montana and Sri Lanka since 2021. Montana’s Adjutant General, Brigadier General Trenton Gibson, will join the delegation.

The partnership was expanded in 2025 through a Memorandum of Understanding covering areas including disaster response, maritime domain awareness and professional military education. U.S. Ambassador to Sri Lanka Eric Meyer said the visit would create new opportunities for American farmers while deepening cooperation between the two countries
