Gas shortage expected to ease within two days

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daliy Mirror) - The prevailing gas shortage in the country is expected to be overcome within the next two days following the arrival of several LPG shipments, Litro Gas Terminal Lanka (Pvt) Ltd Chairman and CEO Channa Gunawardana said.

Speaking to the Daily Mirror, he said the company was currently distributing around 80,000 gas cylinders daily to meet local demand.

He said four LPG vessels carrying a total of 15,600 metric tonnes had arrived in the country between October 18 and November 1.

The vessel Francois arrived on October 18 carrying 3,900 metric tonnes of LPG, while Corinthian arrived on October 22 with a similar quantity. The vessel Goodwill arrived on October 24 carrying another 3,900 metric tonnes, followed by Quarantine, which arrived on November 1 with a further 3,900 metric tonnes.

Litro Gas has also said it has the capacity to release approximately three million domestic gas cylinders during November, including supplies for commercial customers.
