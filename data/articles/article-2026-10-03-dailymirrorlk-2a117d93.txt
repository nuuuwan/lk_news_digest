Is the JVP drifting away from non-alignment and supporting Palestine?

Perhaps, Dissanayake like many others was questioning what in fact the UN had delivered

The 81st session of the Unite Nations General Assembly (UNSGA) has come to an end. The theme of the session was ‘Restoring Trust, Managing Transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all’. President Dissanayake did not attend the sessions. Instead he sent his Foreign Minister to represent the country.

Perhaps, Dissanayake like many others was questioning what in fact the UN had delivered. So, it was left to his ‘able’ Foreign Minister (FM) to represent our country and elucidate Lanka’s position on matters local and international.

Speakers from many countries who spoke before our FM emphasised the need for a more representative system of governance within the UN. African nations demanded full representation at the Security Council. They called for the abandonment of the outdated system of representation set up in the aftermath of World War II which ignored African representation on UN body.

The problem of global warming and its ill-effects on newly emerging economies were raised. Many small nations (including our FM) pointed out they were being forced to bear the ill-effects of this phenomenon, though they bore the least responsibility for the creation of the same. They demanded that developed economies needed to step up to reverse the trend.

But it was Israel’s genocidal war on Palestine and the worsening humanitarian crisis in Gaza that all nations focused on. They drew attention to Israel’s ever increasing intrusions into Palestine’s West Bank and  widening attacks on West Asian countries.

They emphasised that, despite calls  to halt its attacks on Palestinians, Israel continues its ongoing genocidal attacks. Again,  despite the Gaza Peace Plan ceasefire of  October 10 2025, brokered by the US, Qatar, Egypt, and Türkiye, Gaza’s Health Ministry revealed that Israeli violations of the October 10, 2025 ceasefire agreement had caused the deaths of  1,431 Palestinians, while over another 5,026 were injured.

Since October 7 2023,  Israel has killedover 74,000 civilian Palestinians. Reports from United Nations bodies and humanitarian organisations like ‘Save the Children’ report that over 20,000 to 21,700 Palestinian children have been killed in the Gaza Strip since 2023..

Countries the world over condemned the Israeli genocide in Palestine and demandedthat Israel halt its genocidal attacks. Surprisingly,  our FM made scarce mention of Israel’s genocidal attacks on Palestine. He confined his remarks to being ‘pained by the developments in Gaza and the Occupied Palestinian territories. He added that Lanka supported the inalienable rights of the Palestinian people, including their right to self-determination!No condemnation of the numbers of civilians killed and wounded, nor the bombing of hospitals and schools or the complete destruction of homes in  Gaza.

Again,  While West-Asian countries charged they had fallen victim to the collateral damage arising from the US-Israeli aggression against Iran and called for an end to hostilities, our FM speaking on the US-Israeli war in Iran did not  condemn the aggressors.

He merely mentioned the severe impact the war had on Lanka which was dependent on the income brought in by its migrant workers. Worse, he also patted himself on the back regarding the Lankan navy saving of some of the Iranian sailors on the IRIS Denna in March 2026. Sadly, our FM did not see it fit to condemn the aggressor, or the fact that 104 unarmed cadets were killed by the US sneak attack on an unarmed Iranian ship.

So much for Lanka’s famed policy of non alignment and support for the Palestinian cause.  Many Lankans are left wondering whether the once anti-imperialist Janath Vimukthi Peramuna (JVP) has given up on non alignment and is moving toward the American sphere of influence.  This belief has garnered strength in the light of the number of American programmes our government has begun participating in --as for example Maritime Sanctions Evasion Training.

The Nuclear Smuggling Detection CapabilitiesProgramme and the SOCO Police & Crime Investigation Programme conducted in Colombo.

These programmes targeted Iran, Russia and China. The fact that Lanka did not receive an invitation to attend the last BRICS summit surely carries a message. Perhaps the world is concluding that Sri Lanka is abandoning non alignment and limiting its support of Palestine to mere words.
