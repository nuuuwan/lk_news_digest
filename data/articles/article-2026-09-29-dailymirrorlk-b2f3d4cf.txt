Sheaffer returns to  Sri Lankan market

Sheaffer, a premium writing instrument brand, has officially returned to the Sri Lankan market through Trambak Pens.

From professionals and business leaders to collectors and writing enthusiasts, generations of users have trusted Sheaffer for instruments that reflect quality, precision, and personal expression, the company says.

Customers in Sri Lanka can now explore a range of Sheaffer products, including fountain pens, rollerball pens, ballpoint pens, mechanical pencils, premium gift sets, refills, fountain pen inks, ink cartridges, and writing accessories.

Commenting on the launch, a spokesperson for Trambak Pens said, “We are delighted to bring Sheaffer back to Sri Lanka and make this iconic brand accessible to customers once again. We look forward to expanding the brand’s presence across the country while delivering the quality, authenticity, and service that customers expect from Sheaffer.”

Sheaffer products are currently available through Trambak Pens, with plans to progressively expand availability through leading stationery stores, department stores, bookstores, and selected retail partners across Colombo and other major cities in the coming months.
