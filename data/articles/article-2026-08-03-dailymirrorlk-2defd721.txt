Ceylon Chamber’s Import Section holds 91st AGM

The 91st Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Import Section of The Ceylon Chamber of Commerce was held recently at Shangri-La Colombo, bringing together the members of the import and trade community to review the section’s achievements and outline its priorities for the year ahead.

Sri Lanka Ports Authority Chairman Dr. Parakarama Dissanayake attended as the chief guest and keynote speaker, while Maersk Sri Lanka and Maldives Managing Director Biju Ravi delivered the address as the guest speaker.

Ushan de Silva of Brandix Apparel (Pvt.) Ltd was reappointed as Chairman for a second consecutive term. Sankha Gammanpila of Hemas Pharmaceuticals (Pvt.) Ltd was appointed as Vice Chairman, while Rajasooriya of Hayleys PLC was appointed as Deputy Vice Chairman.

Addressing the members, de Silva reaffirmed the Import Section’s commitment to strengthening Sri Lanka’s trade competitiveness through trade facilitation, digital transformation and responsible trade practices. While noting that the country’s economic recovery is progressing, he said businesses continue to face the rising logistics costs, supply chain disruptions and geopolitical uncertainty, highlighting the need for a more resilient and efficient trading environment.

He said the Import Section will continue to work closely with the policymakers and industry stakeholders to advance practical trade reforms, streamline border processes and support the implementation of paperless trade initiatives and the National Single Window.

He also highlighted the section’s advocacy over the past year in addressing the key trade policy issues and operational challenges faced by the importers.

de Silva further emphasised the importance of responsible trade by supporting stronger regulatory compliance and measures to combat illicit trade. Noting that efficient imports are vital to manufacturing, exports, investment and economic growth, he reaffirmed the section’s commitment to working with the public and private sector stakeholders to build a more competitive and efficient trade ecosystem.
