Sri Lanka, UAE discuss strengthening bilateral relations at UNGA

Colombo, Sep. 30 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath met with Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs of the United Arab Emirates (UAE), on the sidelines of the 81st United Nations General Assembly (UNGA81).

In a message posted on X, Minister Herath said the discussions focused on avenues to further strengthen bilateral relations between Sri Lanka and the UAE.

Key areas discussed included trade, investment, tourism and labour cooperation, as well as the welfare of the Sri Lankan community living and working in the UAE.

The meeting was held on the margins of the UN General Assembly, where leaders and senior officials from around the world are engaging in discussions on international and bilateral issues.

Herath said the two sides discussed opportunities to further expand cooperation across the identified areas, including strengthening ties between the two countries.
