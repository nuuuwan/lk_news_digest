Sri Lanka Cricket rejects reports of no ICC men’s tournament hosting until 2035

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Cricket (SLC) has rejected media reports claiming that Sri Lanka will not host any major International Cricket Council (ICC) men’s tournament until 2035, saying that hosting allocations for the 2032–2035 cycle have not yet been confirmed.

In a media release, SLC clarified that the hosting arrangements for ICC men’s white-ball tournaments covering the 2024–2031 cycle were announced on November 16, 2021.

Under those arrangements, Sri Lanka co-hosted the 2026 ICC Men’s T20 World Cup with India, while India was allocated the hosting rights for the 2029 ICC Men’s Champions Trophy. India and Bangladesh were also designated as joint hosts of the 2031 ICC Men’s Cricket World Cup.

SLC stressed that these allocations were made in 2021 and were not new decisions arising from the recent ICC meetings held in Bali.

Commenting on the ICC announcement made on November 15, 2026, SLC said the global governing body had confirmed the tournament calendar and event windows through 2035. However, the host nations for men's events scheduled during the 2032–2035 cycle have yet to be announced.

Therefore, the confirmation of tournament dates and event windows does not mean that hosting rights for the 2032–2035 cycle have been finalised or that Sri Lanka has been excluded from hosting future ICC men's tournaments, SLC said.
