Miss Teen Sri Lanka leaves for India

Colombo, August 22 (Daily Mirror) - Miss Teen Sri Lanka 2026, Divyangana Seneviratne, left the country from Bandaranaike International Airport today (22) to represent Sri Lanka at the Miss Teen International 2026 beauty pageant in Jaipur, India.

Seneviratne arrived at the Katunayake Airport today to commence her journey to India, where the international pageant is scheduled to be held from August 22 to 31.

The event will bring together Miss Teen titleholders representing around 40 countries.

Seneviratne departed from Bandaranaike International Airport at 12.30 p.m. today on IndiGo Airlines flight 6E-1280 bound for Chennai. She is scheduled to travel from Chennai to Jaipur to participate in the pageant.

Seneviratne was crowned Miss Teen Sri Lanka 2026 and is a Kandy resident who is engaged in the modelling industry on a full-time basis.

She will represent Sri Lanka at the international event, which will feature young beauty queens from across the world.
