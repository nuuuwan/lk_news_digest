Govt. has made no decision to reintroduce the death penalty

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Amid renewed calls to reintroduce the death penalty for organised crime in the wake of continuing shooting incidents, Health Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said yesterday that the Government had not taken any decision on the matter.

He said several proposals had been submitted calling for the reintroduction of the death penalty, while a proposal had also been made to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake during a recent meeting on the issue.

The Minister said underworld activities had been brought under control to some extent, noting that clashes between rival gangs, which had been reported earlier, had now declined.

He said arrests and investigations targeting organised criminal activities had also been intensified.

According to the Minister, 37 shooting incidents resulting in 17 deaths had been reported so far this year. A total of 867 firearms, including 14 T-56 rifles, had been seized between January 1 and September 10.

Responding to a question, Dr. Jayatissa said the Government was taking action under existing laws. However, he said eliminating organised crime could not be achieved within a day or two as the underworld operated through a widespread network.
