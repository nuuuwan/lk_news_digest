Week ends in green

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as Citizens Development Business Finance, Sierra Cables and Hemas Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.2 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 closed in green. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sierra Cables, Pan Asia Banking Corporation and John Keells Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Haycarb, Ceylon Tobacco Company and Commercial Credit & Finance whilst retail interest was noted in Beruwala Resorts, Browns Investments and Softlogic Capital. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 1.0% and 0.6% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.64 Bn.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 0.25%. The share price of Sierra Cables increased by Rs 2.00 (6.13%) to close at Rs 34.60. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs 19.80.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Haycarb) whilst the sector index increased by 0.45%. The share price of Haycarb recorded a gain of Rs 3.75 (2.14%) to close at Rs 178.75.

Pan Asia Banking Corporation and HNB Life were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Pan Asia Banking Corporation gained Rs 0.90 (1.63%) to close at Rs 56.00. The share price of HNB Life appreciated by Rs 0.50 (0.34%) to close at Rs 149.75.
