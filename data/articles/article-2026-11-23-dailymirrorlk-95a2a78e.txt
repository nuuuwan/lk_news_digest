Army rowers dominate Defence Services Championship

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Army emerged as the overall champions of the 14th Defence Services Rowing Championship held at the Diyawanna Rowing Centre, winning six of the seven events contested.

The Army secured both the men’s and women’s championships, with its rowers winning six gold, four silver and two bronze medals.

In the men’s category, the Army won three gold, two silver and two bronze medals, while its women’s team secured three gold and two silver medals.

The Sri Lanka Air Force finished as the overall runners-up with one gold, three silver and four bronze medals. Its men’s team won one gold, two silver and one bronze, while the women’s team secured one silver and three bronze medals.

The Sri Lanka Navy won one bronze medal in the men’s category.
