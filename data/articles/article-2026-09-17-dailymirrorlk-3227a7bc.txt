Hunas Holdings ties up with Japanese investor

Hunas Holdings PLC has entered into a strategic partnership with Japanese investor Yoshimichi Watanabe, to explore new real estate and hospitality projects, the company said in a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange.

The partnership, entered into on September 14, will focus on identifying, developing and managing potential projects by combining the expertise, networks and resources of both parties.

Watanabe is a high-net-worth investor with experience across e-commerce, hospitality and real estate in Japan and other international markets, including Bali, Indonesia.

Hunas Holdings said Watanabe has also made several investments in Sri Lanka over the years, giving him the first-hand exposure to the country’s business and investment environment.

The company disclosed it expects the partnership to support its expansion in the real estate and hospitality sectors and open opportunities to develop new projects as part of its long-term growth strategy.
