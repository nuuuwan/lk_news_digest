Fuel price revision may be considered at month-end: Minister

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A revision of domestic fuel prices may be considered at the end of September in view of the recent increase in global fuel prices, Energy Minister Anura Karunathilaka said today.

Speaking at an event in Galle, the Minister said the government was currently discussing whether the public could bear a further increase, considering that fuel prices in Sri Lanka were already at a relatively high level.

He said prevailing international fuel prices would be taken into consideration when reviewing domestic fuel prices at the end of the month.
