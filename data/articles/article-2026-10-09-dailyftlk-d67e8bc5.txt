Daraz Sri Lanka launches 10.10 Shopping Fest

Daraz Sri Lanka is set to launch its 10.10 Shopping Fest, bringing shoppers deals across electronics, fashion, beauty, home appliances and everyday essentials.

Going live at 8:00 p.m. on 9 October and continuing until 16 October, the campaign will bring together brand offers, vouchers and additional savings through participating payment partners.

Whether customers are upgrading their homes, refreshing their wardrobes or stocking up on household essentials, the Shopping Fest will offer a range of choices across different needs and budgets.

Participating brands include Celcius, Diamond, Anker, Aspor, Himalaya, Hemas, Unilever and Natures Beauty. Shoppers can also explore offers from Sharge, Tronsmart, Janet, COSRX, NutriMax Supplements, Herbal Bliss LK, Swisstek, Quantum Fitness, Moose, Amante and ProMate.

The campaign will open with Brand Rush Hour, running from 8 p.m. on 9 October to 2 a.m. on 10 October. During this six hour period, shoppers can access limited time deals and enjoy an extra 25% off with KOKO or an extra 20% off with participating bank partners on eligible purchases.

Participating bank and financial partners include Bank of Ceylon, Cargills Bank, Commercial Bank, LOLC Finance, Nations Trust Bank, Sampath Bank and Union Bank.

Following Brand Rush Hour, shoppers can continue to enjoy an extra 12% off with KOKO or bank discounts ranging from 10% to 15% during the live campaign period until 16 October. Discount rates and offer periods vary by partner.

Easy payment instalment options of three months or longer will also be available through selected banks, allowing eligible cardholders to spread the cost of their purchases. Available tenures and eligibility are subject to each bank’s terms and conditions.

Throughout the campaign, customers can explore Mega Deals, Hot Picks and Flat Discounts on selected products. Daraz Mall also brings together official brand stores and authorised sellers, giving shoppers access to branded products across a range of categories.
