Catholic Deaf Day celebrated

Ragama St. Joseph Deaf Alumni Association members, together with Catholic priests, nuns and members of the deaf community, took part in an awareness walk following the National Catholic Deaf Day Divine Liturgy held at St. Sebastian's Church, Katuwapitiya, on July 26. The procession, organised to raise public awareness and promote the inclusion of the deaf community, commenced from St. Sebastian's Church, proceeded via Baldi Junction and concluded at the church on the Chilaw–Colombo Road.
