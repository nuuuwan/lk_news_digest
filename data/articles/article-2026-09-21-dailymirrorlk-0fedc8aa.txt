Kuda Ganga reaches minor flood level due to heavy rain

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The Irrigation Department has issued a minor flood warning for the Kuda Ganga as heavy rainfall caused water levels in several major rivers to reach alert levels this morning.

The department said the water level at the Kalawellawa (Millakanda) gauging station on the Kuda Ganga had risen to 6.59 metres by 6.30 a.m. today (21), exceeding the minor flood level of 6.50 metres.

Alert levels were also recorded at several other river stations, including Hanwella on the Kelani Ganga, Ratnapura and Magura on the Kalu Ganga, Baddegama and Thawalama on the Gin Ganga, Thalgahagoda and Panadugama on the Nilwala Ganga, and Dunamale on the Attanagalu Oya.

The department said water levels were rising at Baddegama on the Gin Ganga and Thalgahagoda on the Nilwala Ganga, while levels at several other stations were gradually falling.

Heavy rainfall was recorded across several river catchment areas during the 24 hours ending at 6.00 a.m. today.

Nawalapitiya recorded the highest rainfall of 93.9 mm, followed by Kithulgala with 91.1 mm, Giriulla with 79.3 mm, Deraniyagala with 77.9 mm and Magura with 74.2 mm.

Authorities urged people living in low-lying areas, particularly those close to the Kuda Ganga, Gin Ganga and Nilwala Ganga, to remain vigilant and closely monitor further developments.
