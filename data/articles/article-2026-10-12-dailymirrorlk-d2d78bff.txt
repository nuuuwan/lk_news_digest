Union Assurance celebrates top advisers

Union Assurance recently celebrated the outstanding achievements of its agency force at the Mid-Year Rewards and Recognition ceremony, honouring the exceptional performance of its top performers for the first half of 2026.

The event brought together nearly 150 winning advisers and leaders from across the island, recognising individuals and teams who demonstrated excellence in customer service, business growth, leadership and commitment to helping Sri Lankans protect what matters most.

Held under the theme ‘Half Time – Full Force’, the event marked the midpoint of the year while setting the stage for an even stronger performance in the months ahead.

It served as a platform to acknowledge the dedication and resilience of Union Assurance’s agency force, whose efforts continue to drive the company’s growth and deliver meaningful protection solutions to the customers nationwide.

Regions, zones and clusters were honoured for their outstanding achievements. Pettah Region emerged as the Best Large Region, while Matara 02 Region secured recognition as the Best Medium Region. Northern 01 Zone was named Best Zone and North and East received the accolade for Best Cluster.

The event also recognised the country’s highest-performing new business producers under the National New Business Champion Category. Chanaka Appuhamy was crowned as the Winner, while Thinesh David Baskaran and Udan Silva were recognised as First Runner-Up and Second Runner-Up, respectively for their remarkable contributions during the period.

Union Assurance Chief Agency Officer Imtiyaz Aniff stated, “ Our agency force continues to play a vital role in driving Union Assurance’s success. Today’s recognition celebrates not only exceptional results but also the dedication, professionalism and customer focus demonstrated by our advisers and leaders across the country. The strong performance achieved in the first half of the year provides a solid foundation as we look ahead with renewed momentum and determination. I congratulate all our winners and thank every member of the agency force for their contribution to our shared success.”
