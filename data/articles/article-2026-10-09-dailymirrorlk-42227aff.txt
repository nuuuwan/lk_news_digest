Export Performance Indicators 2025 launched

The Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB) has launched its ‘Export Performance Indicators 2025’ publication, as the country prepares to pursue new export targets under its five-year development plan.

The publication was unveiled at a ceremony at The Grand Maitland in Colombo, with Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Minister Sunil Handunnetti attending as chief guest.

Addressing the gathering, Handunnetti urged exporters and public institutions to build on the sector’s recent growth and aim beyond previous achievements.

“Building on the growth achieved by all export sectors over the past two years, we must move towards new targets that surpass our own past records,” he said.

The EDB is in the midst of advancing the National Export Development Plan 2026–2030, which seeks to expand export earnings and diversify Sri Lanka’s product basket.
