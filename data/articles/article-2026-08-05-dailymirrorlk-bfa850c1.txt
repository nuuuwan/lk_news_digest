Colombo playground turned garage, 15-year project under spotlight

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Mayor Vraie Cally Balthazaar has pledged to reclaim a children’s playground that was allegedly unlawfully occupied and converted into a garage, while also promising action on several other longstanding public infrastructure issues identified during an inspection tour of the D4 Administrative District.

During the visit, the Mayor inspected a children’s playground that was found to have been illegally occupied and used as a garage.

Stressing that public spaces belong to the community and not to individuals seeking private gain, the Mayor said the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) would continue to take firm action against the misuse of public property and reclaim such spaces for public use.

The Mayor also inspected an unfinished building that has reportedly received repeated allocations of public funds for more than 15 years but remains incomplete.

The Mayor said the building would be cleared and prepared for its originally intended purpose, noting that the current CMC depot and district office would have to be relocated due to the Road Development Authority’s (RDA) road widening project.

Emphasising that public funds should never be wasted, the Mayor said the administration was committed to ensuring the long-neglected public asset is finally put to proper use.

Furthermore, the inspection also included the Jathika Pola, where discussions focused on measures that could be implemented immediately while recognising that the market’s long-term sustainability would require broader consultations and a comprehensive development plan.

The Mayor said the CMC hopes to implement a permanent and effective solution to improve conditions for residents through coordinated action.
