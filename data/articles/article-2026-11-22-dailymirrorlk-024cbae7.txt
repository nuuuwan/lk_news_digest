DMT assures uninterrupted services despite cyber attack

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) has assured the public that its services will continue without interruption despite a cyber attack targeting its official website last night.

The Department has advised the public to continue making appointments through its online portal at https://dmtappointments.dmt.gov.lk or by contacting its hotline on 0112 117 116.

The DMT had earlier announced that its official website had come under a cyber attack.
