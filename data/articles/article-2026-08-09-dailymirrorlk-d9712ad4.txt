Promoting Online Transactions

Sri Lanka has made considerable progress in digital technology, but many middle-aged and elderly citizens remain reluctant to use online banking and digital payment systems.

The Government, banks and public institutions should step up awareness and training programmes, particularly in rural areas. Online services can save citizens valuable time and money by eliminating unnecessary travel and long queues.

However, greater use of digital transactions will depend on public confidence. Strong cyber security, effective protection against online fraud and quick assistance to victims are essential.

Sri Lanka must ensure that the digital revolution benefits everyone, not only the younger generation. A safe, simple and inclusive digital system will improve efficiency, reduce costs and contribute to the country’s economic development.
