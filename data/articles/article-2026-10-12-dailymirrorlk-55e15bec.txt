Bimsaviya: Reform on Paper, Red Tape in Practice

The Bimsaviya initiative was introduced to simplify land ownership and make property transfers as easy as transferring ownership of a motor vehicle. Yet, the public continues to face bureaucratic hurdles. Some banks still demand local authority-approved plans despite Bimsaviya certificates containing plans surveyed and demarcated by the Survey Department. Some even seek further confirmation from the Survey Department regarding the documentation.

What is the purpose of issuing ownership certificates if banks and other institutions are reluctant to accept them?

The relevant authorities must urgently educate banks and local authorities on the validity of these certificates and establish uniform procedures.

A reform that fails at the point of implementation serves little purpose. The public should not suffer because government institutions have failed to coordinate.

It is time to move beyond issuing certificates and ensure that Bimsaviya delivers what it promised.
