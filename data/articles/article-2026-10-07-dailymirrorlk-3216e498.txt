Shiranthi Rajapaksa arrested

Colombo, Oct 07 (Daily Mirror) - Former First Lady Shiranthi Rajapaksa has been arrested by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC), according to reports.

She was arrested after being questioned at her residence in Kirulapona by CIABOC officers in connection with an ongoing investigation by the Bribery Commission.
