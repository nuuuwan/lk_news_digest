Improve political literacy, understand danger of 22A: Sajith

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - One has to improve his or her political literacy to understand the danger posed by the 22nd amendment to the Constitution, Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa said today.

Speaking to several party activists, Premadasa said one has to read articles 3, 4, and 83 of the Constitution clearly to understand the danger of the 22nd amendment to the Constitution.

"The hidden agenda behind the 22nd amendment is to vest all powers with a single person. One has to understand this fact," he said.
