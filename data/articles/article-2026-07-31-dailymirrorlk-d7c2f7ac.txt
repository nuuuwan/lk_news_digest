Railway catering firm fined Rs. 500,000 for overcharging water bottles

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Anuradhapura Magistrate's Court has imposed a fine of Rs. 500,000 on railway catering service provider for selling bottled drinking water above the government-approved Maximum Retail Price (MRP) on a train operating between Kankesanthurai and Colombo.

The case was filed by investigation officers of the Anuradhapura District Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) under Direction No. 96 of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, which prohibits the sale of goods above the stipulated MRP.

The violation was detected during a special inspection carried out by CAA officers, when they boarded the train in the Anuradhapura area. Investigators found that 1,000 ml bottles of locally produced drinking water, marked with an MRP of Rs. 120, were being sold to passengers for Rs. 150.

After considering the evidence presented, the Anuradhapura Magistrate ordered the company to pay a fine of Rs. 500,000.

The CAA said it has informed the Sri Lanka Railways Department about the overcharging practices in railway dining cars and noted that Jayabima Catering had previously faced legal action for similar consumer rights violations.

The Authority warned that it will continue to monitor the company's operations and take strict legal action against any future violations. It also urged traders to comply with consumer protection laws while assuring that regular inspections will continue to safeguard consumer rights.

Members of the public have been encouraged to report instances of overpricing by contacting the CAA's hotline, 1977, during office hours.
