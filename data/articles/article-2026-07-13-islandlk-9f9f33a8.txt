Stock trading inches downwards as external factors kick in

The CSE’s overall performance yesterday indicated a downward trend due to external and internal environmental factors which were not conducive to smooth trading, market analysts said.

The All Share Price Index went down by 208.33 points, while the S and P SL20 declined by 41.52 points. Turnover stood at Rs 1 billion with two crossings.

Those crossings were; Melstacorp crossed 500,000 shares to the tune of Rs 90 million; its shares traded at Rs 180 and HNB (Non-Voting) 130,000 shares crossed for Rs 41.5 million; its shares traded 319.

In the retail market companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Sampath Bank Rs 110 million (795,000 shares traded), Commercial Bank Rs million, JAT Rs 33 million (876,000 shares traded), HNB (Non-Voting) Rs 28 million (87,000 shares traded), ACL Cables Rs 26 million (273,000 shares traded), Haycarb Rs 24 million (145,000 shares traded) and CCS Rs 24 million (189,000 shares traded). During the day 79.7 million share volumes changed hands in 18972 transactions.

It is said that banking sector counters, especially Sampath Bank and HNB, performed well while construction related companies,, especially JAT Holdings, significantly performed.

Meanwhile, Alpha Fire Services announced a proposed strategic expansion to acquire the entirety of Icon Engineering Enterprises, a company specializing in Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) solutions, via a private placement share swap.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs335.70/336.00 to the US dollar in the spot market, from Rs 335.70/90 Friday, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

A bond maturing on 15.12.2029 was quoted at 11.00/15 percent, down from 11.05/15 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2030 was quoted at 11.47/53 percent, up from 11.45/50 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.06.2034 was quoted flat at 11.80/90 percent.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the US dollar was 331.3000 buying, 340.3000 selling; the euro was 374.9980 buying, 388.9150 selling; and the pound was 442.2248 buying, 456.2704 selling.
