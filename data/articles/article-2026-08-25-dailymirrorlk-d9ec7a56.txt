Castlereagh, Maussakelle reservoirs reach overflow levels

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Water levels in the Castlereagh and Maussakelle reservoirs have reached overflow levels amid intermittent rainfall in their catchment areas, reservoir engineers said.

According to the engineers, the Castlereagh Reservoir had reached an overflow level of six feet by this morning (25), while the area had received 6.3 millimetres of rainfall over the past 24 hours.

The Maussakelle Reservoir had reached an overflow level of 4.1 feet, with 9 millimetres of rainfall recorded in the area during the same period.

Meanwhile, water levels in waterfalls including Gadmore, Murray Falls and Mohini Falls have also increased due to heavy rainfall in the surrounding watershed areas.
