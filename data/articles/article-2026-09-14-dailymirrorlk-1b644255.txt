India beat Sri Lanka by 72 runs to win record Eighth Women’s Asia Cup Title

SEP.14 (Mirror Sports) - India produced a dominant performance to beat Sri Lanka by 72 runs in the Women’s Asia Cup 2026 final in Dubai, securing a record eighth title.

Batting first after being asked to set a target, India posted 183 for 5 at the Dubai International Cricket Stadium, with Shafali Verma and Smriti Mandhana leading the way.

Verma smashed 68 runs, while Mandhana added 59 as the Indian openers put their side in control early in the innings.

Harmanpreet Kaur contributed 14, Richa Ghosh scored 17, and Kamalini Gunalan remained unbeaten on 15 as India finished on 183.

Sri Lanka’s wickets were shared by Mithali Ayodhya, Chamari Athapaththu and Chamudi Praboda, who claimed one wicket each.

Chasing 184 for victory, Sri Lanka struggled against India’s bowling attack and were restricted to 111.

Chamari Athapaththu top-scored with 36, while Vishmi Gunaratne made 20 and Nilakshika Silva added 22. Imesha Dulani, Harshitha Samarawickrama, Kavisha Dilhari, Hasini Perera and Kaushini Nuthyangana failed to make significant contributions as Sri Lanka’s chase lost momentum.

Shree Charani was the star with the ball for India, taking four wickets, while Deepti Sharma claimed three. Kranti Gaud, Shafali Verma and Prema Rawat took one wicket each.

India’s 72-run victory sealed their record eighth Women’s Asia Cup title, adding another major chapter to their dominance in the tournament.
