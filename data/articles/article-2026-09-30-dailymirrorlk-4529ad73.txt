Fuel prices revised with effect from midnight

The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) announced a revision of fuel prices with effect from midnight today (30).

Accordingly, the price of Auto Diesel has been increased by Rs. 10 per litre to Rs. 392, while the price of Super Diesel has been increased by Rs. 50 to Rs. 528 per litre.

The price of Petrol 92 Octane has also been increased by Rs. 15 to Rs. 414 per litre.

However, the price of Petrol 95 Octane remains unchanged at Rs. 475 per litre, while the price of kerosene remains at Rs. 285 per litre.
