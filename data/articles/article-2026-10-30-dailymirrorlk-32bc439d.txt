Southern Cross University-NSBM Green University launch SL’s first-ever double degree

From left: NSBM Green University Board Member Dr. Sujeewa Mudalige, Southern Cross University Vice-Chancellor Prof. Tyrone Carlin, Digital Economy Deputy Minister Eranga Weeraratne, Australian High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Matthew Duckworth, NSBM Green University Deputy Vice-Chancellor Academic Prof. Thilini De Silva and Southern Cross University Associate Dean International and Engagement Prof. Darshana Sedera

A world-first for Sri Lankan higher education: a single, four-year programme awarding both a Bachelor of Business and a Bachelor of Information Technology

Southern Cross University (Australia), in partnership with NSBM Green University, launched the first and only double degree ever offered in Sri Lanka — a four-year programme in which the students graduate with both a Bachelor of Business and a Bachelor of Information Technology.

The programme will be delivered locally at NSBM Green University and opens for enrolment ahead of its 2027 commencement.

The launch — marked by the signing of a memorandum of understanding between the two universities — was graced by Digital Economy Deputy Minister Eranga Weeraratne and Australian High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Matthew Duckworth, underscoring the programme’s significance for the nation’s digital-economy ambitions and for the education partnership between Australia and Sri Lanka.

The event also formed part of NSBM Green University’s 10th anniversary celebrations.

Designed for the careers of the future, the double degree equips the graduates with the rare combination of business acumen and advanced technology skills — from data and artificial intelligence to cyber security — that the employers increasingly demand. The students study under the distinctive Southern Cross Model (fewer units at a time and no final examinations), with a paid industry internship and the opportunity to attend an Australian summer school, graduating with a globally recognised Australian qualification without leaving Sri Lanka.

“This is a proud milestone for both universities and for Sri Lankan students. We are bringing a world-class Australian degree directly to Colombo — giving the talented young people a global qualification and the business and technology skills the future will reward, here at home,” said Southern Cross University Vice-Chancellor Prof. Tyrone Carlin.

“In our 10th anniversary year, we are delighted to offer Sri Lanka’s first double degree. It reflects NSBM’s commitment to world-class, future-ready education and to opening global opportunities for our students,” said NSBM Green University Vice-Chancellor Prof. E.A. Weerasinghe.

“Sri Lanka’s future prosperity will be built on a digitally skilled workforce. A programme that equips our young people with both technology and business expertise, here at home, advances our national digital-economy agenda and I warmly welcome this partnership,” Weeraratne stated.

“Bringing Sri Lanka’s first double degree to life has been a labour of love. Our goal was simple but ambitious — to give talented Sri Lankan students a world-class Australian qualification and the combined business and technology skills the future demands, without leaving home,” Southern Cross University Associate Dean International and Engagement Prof. Darshana Sedera, who pioneered the initiative, said.
