Litro gas for Laugfs cylinders under discussion

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - Discussions are underway on the possibility of supplying Litro gas to Laugfs cylinders to prevent inconvenience to consumers in the event Laugfs Gas Company discontinues its supplies, Deputy Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development R. M. Jayawardena said.

Speaking to the media, the Deputy Minister said a special meeting would be held under the chairmanship of Trade Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe, with the participation of all relevant parties, to discuss the matter.

A recent three-day delay in the arrival of a gas shipment from the Maldives resulted in a shortage of around 240,000 Litro cylinders. However, the situation was brought under control following the arrival of a vessel carrying 3,600 metric tons of gas, after which Litro increased its daily distribution to 100,000 cylinders.

Meanwhile, Litro Chairman Channa Gunawardena said the company had increased its daily gas distribution from 1,100 to 1,600 metric tons amid the shortage faced by Laugfs consumers.

However, he clarified that the increased supply did not mean Litro gas would be directly filled into Laugfs' yellow cylinders.

Instead, Litro is supplying gas through bowsers to hotels and factories that previously obtained their supplies from Laugfs, he said.
