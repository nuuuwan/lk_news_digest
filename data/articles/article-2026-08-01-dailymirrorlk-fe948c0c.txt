Heavy rains submerge machinery brought to repair Peradeniya’s Devil’s Bridge

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - Machinery and equipment brought to carry out repair work on the historic Devil's Bridge in Peradeniya were submerged following the sharp rise in the Mahaweli River caused by heavy rainfall experienced over the past two days.

The flooding, triggered by continuous heavy rains on Thursday and Friday, inundated the work site beneath the bridge, leaving excavators, construction equipment and other materials partially underwater.

The repair work had been initiated to restore the bridge, but rising water levels forced operations to come to a standstill.

Authorities are expected to assess the extent of the damage to the machinery and equipment once floodwaters recede. No injuries have been reported.

The heavy rains have caused water levels in several rivers across the Central Province to rise, with authorities urging the public to remain vigilant and avoid flooded areas.
