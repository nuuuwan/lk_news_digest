Gnanasara Thera brought before court again

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Bodu Bala Sena General Secretary Ven. Galagodaaththe Gnanasara Thera, who is currently serving a prison sentence, was brought before court again today.

The Thera was produced before court amid heightened attention over the Prisons Department’s decision to remove his robes and have him appear in prison attire.
