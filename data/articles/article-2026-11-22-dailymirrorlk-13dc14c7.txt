Rear Admiral Dammika Wijewardana appointed Navy Deputy Chief of Staff

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Rear Admiral Dammika Wijewardana has been appointed as the new Deputy Chief of Staff of the Sri Lanka Navy with effect from today (22).

The letter of appointment was officially handed over to him by Commander of the Navy, Vice Admiral Damian Fernando, at the Navy Headquarters.

Rear Admiral Wijewardana, an alumnus of Dudley Senanayake Central College, Tholangamuwa, joined the Sri Lanka Navy's Executive Branch in 1992 as an Officer Cadet of the 10th Intake of the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU).

He was commissioned as an Acting Sub Lieutenant in 1994 and graduated from KDU in 1996 with a BSc (DS) degree in Mechanical Engineering. He later completed several professional and academic courses, including a Master's degree in Hydrography from the University of Goa, India, and a Master of Philosophy in Defence and Strategic Studies from the University of Madras.

During his naval career, he held several key appointments, including Provost Marshal, Director Naval Inspectorate, Commander of the Northwestern Naval Area, Director General Training and Commander of the Southern Naval Area.

He was promoted to the rank of Rear Admiral in 2024 and currently serves as Commander of the Northern Naval Area and Chief Hydrographer of the Sri Lanka Navy, in addition to his new appointment.

Rear Admiral Wijewardana has received several gallantry awards, including the Weera Wickrama Vibhushanaya (WWV), Rana Wickrama Padakkama (RWP) and Rana Sura Padakkama (RSP), in recognition of his bravery. He has also been awarded the Uththama Sewa Padakkama (USP) for his unblemished service, along with several other campaign and service medals.
