MEPA to claim compensation over Colombo Port oil spill

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Marine Environment Protection Authority (MEPA) is to assess the environmental damage caused by the diesel leak at the Colombo Port and seek compensation from those responsible for the pollution.

According to a MEPA spokesperson, the damage caused by the oil spill along the Muthuwella coastline would be evaluated under the Marine Pollution Prevention Act, No. 35 of 2008, before legal action is initiated against the responsible parties.

The spokesperson said legal proceedings could be instituted under the provisions of the Act in connection with the incident.

Meanwhile, a special MEPA team visited the Muthuwella coastline today (23) to conduct further investigations into the environmental impact of the spill.

MEPA said approximately 95% of the fuel flow into the sea had been brought under control, while efforts were continuing to prevent further pollution, minimise environmental damage and restore the affected coastal areas.

The incident occurred on November 21 following a diesel leak from storage tank No. 108 at the Jaya Container Terminal (JCT) of the Colombo Port.
