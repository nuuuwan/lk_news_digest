Namal produced before court over ‘Krrish’ deal

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Imprisoned Member of Parliament Namal Rajapaksa was produced before the Fort Magistrate’s Court this morning (29) in connection with an ongoing case related to the controversial ‘Krrish’ deal.

Rajapaksa has been named as the fourth suspect in a case filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court.

He was brought before court today for further judicial proceedings in connection with the bribery case.
