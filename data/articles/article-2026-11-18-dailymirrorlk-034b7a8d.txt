High Court suspends Namal’s bail in Krrish deal case

The Colombo High Court today issued an interim order suspending the operation of the bail order granted to MP Namal Rajapaksa by the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court in connection with the alleged bribery involving the Krrish deal.

High Court Judge Lal Bandara Ranasinghe also ordered that notices be issued to the respondents.
