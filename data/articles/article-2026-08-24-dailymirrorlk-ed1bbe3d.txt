No competitive pricing advantage despite liberalisation of oil trade

Colombo, Aug. 24 (Daily Mirror) - Despite somewhat liberalisation of retail oil selling, Sri Lanka is yet to secure the advantage of competitive pricing of petroleum products for its consumers.

The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) is the key regulator and stakeholder of fuel imports and distribution.

Besides, there are other foreign companies such as Lanka Indian Oil Company (LIOC), Sinopec and RM Parks involved in fuel distribution.

CPC Chairman D.J. Rajakaruna told Daily Mirror yesterday that all other players follow the CPC pricing formula though they are able to determine pricing competitively.

He said that consumers are yet to get the competitive pricing advantage as a result.

Asked about the global fuel market, he said that the surging diesel prices would impact the CPC.

It was revealed in Parliament last week that the oil import bill had almost trebled during the first half of this year compared to the corresponding period of last year.
