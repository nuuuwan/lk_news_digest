Softlogic Life Insurance acquires controlling stake in Bangladesh-based insurance company

Softlogic Life Insurance announced that it has completed the acquisition of a 60 percent controlling stake in Bangladesh-based Diamond Life Insurance Company Limited for approximately US$ 1.9 million.

The deal follows a Share Purchase Agreement signed by the Sri Lankan life insurer to acquire the majority stake in the licensed Bangladeshi life insurance entity.

‘The acquisition is in line with the company’s regional expansion strategy and represents entry into the Bangladesh life insurance market, Softlogic Life sources said.
