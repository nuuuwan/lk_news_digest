Pathirage throws 92.07m to win Zurich Diamond League

AUG.28 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka’s Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage produced another stunning performance, throwing 92.07m to win the men’s javelin at the Zurich Diamond League.

Pathirage saved his best for his sixth and final attempt, breaking the 92-metre barrier for the second time this season.

Grenada’s Anderson Peters finished second with 86.93m, while Poland’s Dawid Wegner took third with 85.16m.

Pathirage’s latest victory adds to a remarkable breakthrough season. He produced a personal-best and world-leading 92.62m in Rome in June before winning in Lausanne with an 88.14m effort.

The Zurich victory further cements the Sri Lankan’s status as one of the world’s leading javelin throwers, with Pathirage currently topping the Diamond League qualification standings.

Pathirage will now turn his attention to the Diamond League Final in Brussels on September 4-5, where he will look to cap a remarkable season on another high.
