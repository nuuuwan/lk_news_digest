China flies 19-seater electric passenger plane for first time

India Today - China has taken another major step in the space of electric transport. The country successfully flew its 19-seat CE-25A electric passenger aircraft for the first time with pilots onboard.

The aircraft could bring battery-powered aviation closer to regional passenger travel, although certification and further testing remain ahead.

China is long known for topping the charts in manufacturing electric vehicles worldwide. Now, it is taking that expertise to the skies by stepping into electric aviation and successfully flying a 19-seat battery-powered passenger aircraft for the first time.

The 10-tonne CE-25A completed its first piloted flight on November 14 in Shandong province, bringing electric aircraft a little closer to carrying passengers on regional routes. While electric planes are not new, getting one of this size into the air with a crew onboard is a significant development for the technology.
