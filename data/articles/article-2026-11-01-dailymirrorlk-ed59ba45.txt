Bay of Bengal depression set to become severe cyclonic storm by tomorrow

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - A depression over the Southeast Bay of Bengal is expected to intensify into a severe cyclonic storm tomorrow (2), the Department of Meteorology warned.

The system is currently moving west-northwestwards and is likely to develop into a deep depression over the Southwest Bay of Bengal and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal around 11.30 a.m. today.

It is further expected to intensify into a cyclonic storm around 11.30 p.m. today before developing into a severe cyclonic storm over the Westcentral Bay of Bengal by tomorrow.

The Department warned that wind speeds in the affected sea areas could occasionally reach 70-80 kmph, accompanied by heavy showers or thundershowers. Sea conditions are also expected to become very rough.

Meanwhile, showers or thundershowers are likely after 1.00 p.m. today in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Uva and Southern Provinces, as well as in the Ampara and Batticaloa districts.

Dry weather is expected to prevail in other parts of the island.
