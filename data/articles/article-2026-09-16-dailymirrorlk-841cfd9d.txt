Kotmale power generation to stop from Sept. 26 night

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Power generation at the Kotmale Hydropower Plant will be suspended from 10 p.m. on September 26 until 2 p.m. on September 27 for maintenance work, Director of the Water Management Secretariat Engineer Nilantha Dhanapala said.

Addressing the media, Dhanapala said hydropower generation would remain suspended during the maintenance period.
