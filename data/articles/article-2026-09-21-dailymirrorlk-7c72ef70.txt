CIPM sets ‘Human Edge in Age of AI’ as NHRC 2026 theme

The Chartered Institute of Personnel Management (CIPM) Sri Lanka will host National HR Conference (NHRC) 2026 this October, at Monarch Imperial, under the theme ‘The Human Edge in the Age of AI’, with Hatton National Bank PLC as Lead Event Partner.

The conference comes at a pressing moment for the employers. Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping the workplaces faster than any technology before it.

The World Economic Forum (WEF) projects that AI will create 170 million new roles this decade while displacing more than 90 million; yet, the displaced workers rarely fill the new positions. Closing that gap is a human responsibility, not a technical one and it sits at the core of NHRC 2026.

The theme rests on a clear proposition: human intelligence leads, while AI executes. AI can screen and recommend at extraordinary speed but people must steer it and carry the moral weight of decisions affecting others’ lives.

Regulators worldwide classify workforce-related AI as high risk and require human oversight, leaving HR professionals as the final authority.

For the profession, this is an elevation rather than a threat. Freed from heavy administration, HR can drive leadership, cultural change, reskilling and employee well-being. The WEF research shows the employers see skills gaps, not technology limitations, as the biggest barrier to transformation, making workforce readiness the real test of AI success.

CIPM President and NHRC 2026 Technical Committee Chairman Dr. Neil Bogahalande explained that the real contest ahead is not between people and machines.

“It is between the organisations handing over their judgment to technology and those strengthening it. Our technical agenda gives the HR leaders practical tools, from governance frameworks to reskilling. If delegates leave with the confidence to say no to a machine when it matters, we have done our job,” he added.

The programme features a practical playbook from Prof. Harin Sellahewa, alongside sessions by Shehara de Silva, Kul Mahay, Ranjan De Silva and Prof. Ajantha Dharmasiri.

Insights from Mercer will ground the discussion in data, while the HR Heads Forum and Business Leaders Forum will address Sri Lanka’s market realities, talent flows and costs.

The event also features The Great HR Debate 2026, chaired by Shanaka Fernando and held in collaboration with Toastmasters International District 82, with the British Council as Official Training and Assessment Partner. Fourteen corporate teams have been competing since August 11.

National HR Exhibition 2026, led by Davina Kern, will showcase industry innovations.

CIPM CEO U.A.C. Obeyesekere thanked the organisations supporting the conference, including Lead Event Partner Hatton National Bank and Strategic Partners Layup (e-learning), BIPO (technology) and Wijeya Newspapers (print media).

Official Partners are British Council (training and assessment), Jobdaddy.lk (hiring), Thyaga.lk (gifts and rewards), Asiri Hospital Holdings (healthcare), Brandix (global apparel solutions), PickMe (mobility) and Monarch Imperial (hospitality). Omega Line, Seylan Bank and David Peiris Motors are Co-Sponsors.

Registrations will open shortly and delegates, sponsors and partner organisations may contact CIPM Sri Lanka for details.
