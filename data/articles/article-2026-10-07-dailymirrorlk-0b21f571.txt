Govt. to seek legal advice on action to be taken against private fuel suppliers: Minister

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The government is to seek legal advice on action to be taken against private fuel suppliers, Minister Aruna Karunatilaka told Parliament today.

He said action will be taken as per the agreements signed with the suppliers.
