Train driver acquitted over wild elephant death

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - A train driver who had been charged before Kekirawa Magistrate’s Court for the death of a wild elephant that was run over by train was acquitted, and the case was terminated by Kekirawa Magistrate Udari Gunasekara.

Wildlife officers had charged the suspect driver N.A.D.P.Gunatilaka with causing the death of a wild elephant that was run over at Habarana by the Colombo-bound express train from Trincomalee driven by him. He had been attached to Trincomalee Railway Station at the time of the accident.

When the case was taken up, Attorney-at- Law Anuruddha Wijeratne, appearing for the suspect driver, told court that Wildlife officers should be liable to legal action for their failure in wild elephant management and neglect of their duty that resulted in the death of wild elephants in accidents, if the drivers of trains running through jungle areas are held responsible for such accidents.

He submitted that in terms of the railway ordinance the drivers of trains are not responsible for the death of wild elephants run over by trains and that they are exempt from that responsibility. He said that it is not possible to bring an express train to an abrupt halt as in the case of other vehicles.

However, he submitted that under Section 37 of the railway ordinance, it is a punishable offence to lead or allow any animal to frequent the railway line and that the wild elephants would not be run over by trains, provided the wild elephant population is properly managed.

Officers of the Ritigala Wildlife office made submissions to the Wildlife Department that they would withdraw the case against the railway driver. They had failed to file a charge sheet against the suspect driver under the Criminal Procedure Code in compliance with the previous court order.
