Detention fees force importers to abandon luxury cars

Colombo, July 21 (Daily Mirror) - Around 1,000 imported luxury vehicles are being abandoned at the Hambantota Port because importers cannot afford the high detention fees charged to release them, Sri Lanka Customs officials said.

He said nearly 40,000 imported vehicles are currently parked at the port. Many have remained there due to disruptions in re-export operations and the conflict in the Middle East, which has affected shipping to destinations such as Dubai. Vehicles brought in for re-export are also facing the same detention fee problem.

The Customs official said a local importer recently tried to release a luxury vehicle worth about Rs. 20 million that had been parked at the port for nearly six years. However, he was told he had to pay almost Rs. 50 million in detention charges.

This increased the total cost of the vehicle to nearly Rs. 70 million, more than three times its original value.

The official said the importer decided not to release the vehicle after learning about the charges.

Official said many importers are making similar decisions, leaving hundreds of luxury vehicles abandoned at the port as detention fees continue to rise.
