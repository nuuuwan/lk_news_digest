Public transport should be on par with free healthcare, education: President

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Public transport should be placed on the same level as Sri Lanka’s free healthcare and education systems, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has emphasised, Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake said.

Addressing the launch of the new Metro Bus service in Thalangama yesterday (29), Rathnayake said the President had stressed the importance of giving public transport the same priority as the country’s free healthcare and education systems during a recent progress review meeting.

He said the President’s encouragement had been a major source of strength in taking forward the Metro Bus initiative.

A total of 112 new Metro buses were deployed yesterday on seven routes in the Western Province, with the aim of easing traffic congestion in Colombo and providing a safer and more comfortable public transport service.

The Minister said the Government had invested approximately Rs. 7.5 billion to establish the Metro Bus service, describing the investment as a symbol of the country’s economic strength.

He said the Government had completed the initiative in less than a year and planned to introduce another 50 buses before the Sinhala and Hindu New Year for inter-city services to Jaffna, Kandy and Galle.

Rathnayake also called for a Park-and-Ride system, urging mayors to establish suitable parking facilities near Metro bus stops to encourage motorists to leave their vehicles and use public transport.

The initial Metro Bus network consists of seven main routes covering approximately 188.7 kilometres.

He said the Government also expected to expand the service to several major cities in the country and subsequently involve the private sector in the programme.
