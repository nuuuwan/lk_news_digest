Tangalle stray dog population nearly fully covered through sterilization drive

Colombo, August 12 (Daily Mirror) - A free sterilization and vaccination programme has covered almost the entire stray and community dog population in the Tangalle Municipal Council area within three months, the Association of Veterinarians for Humane Management of Animal Population said.

The programme aims to reduce unwanted litters, prevent suffering among street dogs and promote a healthier and more humane community through responsible population control.

The Association said today marks the final day of this month’s programme, with free sterilization and vaccination services available for community dogs.

Residents who care for community dogs are encouraged to bring them forward for treatment. The Association said that people do not need to own a dog to contribute to animal welfare.

The initiative seeks to reduce the number of unwanted puppies and manage the street-dog population through humane methods while promoting better coexistence between people and animals.

“Caring for a community dog is caring for our community. Together, we can prevent suffering, reduce the number of unwanted puppies and build a better future for both animals and people,” the Association said.
