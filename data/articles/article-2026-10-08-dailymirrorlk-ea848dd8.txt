Sri Lankan Olympiad winners return home

Sri Lankan students who represented the country at the International Mathematics and Science Olympiad in West Java, Indonesia, arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport in Yesterday (7), after securing three silver medals and 10 bronze medals. The competition, held from October 2 to 6, brought together participants from 23 countries. The Sri Lankan team was selected and fielded by the Science and Mathematics branches of the Education Quality Development Division of the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education.
