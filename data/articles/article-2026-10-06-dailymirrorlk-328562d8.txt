Raid uncovers hakka-patas manufacturing site in Galgamuwa

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - A special joint operation in the Galgamuwa area has uncovered a site allegedly used to manufacture hakka-patas (improvised explosive devices), while several suspects were arrested with deer skins and hakka-patas.

The raid was conducted on June 10 on the instructions of Environment Minister Dammika Patabendi, with officers from the Department of Wildlife Conservation, Police Special Task Force (STF) and Civil Security Department taking part.

The operation targeted devices and equipment used to threaten wild elephants and other wildlife, including hakka-patas, snares and illegal firearms.

During the raid, authorities identified a suspected hakka-patas manufacturing site and arrested several individuals allegedly in possession of deer skins and hakka-patas.

Further investigations are underway into the suspects and the items recovered during the operation.

The operation is being conducted in five teams by the Department of Wildlife Conservation, with security support from the STF and Civil Security Department.

A large number of Wildlife officers are participating under the direction of Assistant Director for Kurunegala and Puttalam Prashantha Wimaladasa.

The raids were launched following instructions from the Environment Minister to the Director General of Wildlife Conservation to intensify operations against hakka-patas, snares and illegal firearms used to kill or injure wild elephants.

Officials said legal action would be taken against those involved in such illegal activities, while similar raids will continue in areas frequented by wild elephants.
