325,616 raids, 324,780 arrests under ‘Ratama Ekata’

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police have arrested 324,780 suspects during 325,616 raids conducted islandwide under the ‘Ratama Ekata’ national anti-drug operation between October 30, 2025, and November 19, 2026.

According to the Police, on the instructions of the Inspector General of Police (IGP), the operation was launched with the aim of eradicating drug trafficking through measures targeting both the supply and demand for narcotics.

The multi-faceted programme focuses on disrupting drug supply networks, reducing demand, expanding law enforcement raids, rehabilitating drug addicts and strengthening community participation in combating drug-related activities.

During the period under review, detention orders were obtained against 3,896 suspects, while investigations into the illegal assets of 459 suspects were initiated. A further 2,714 individuals were referred for rehabilitation.

The operation also resulted in the seizure of 2,979 kilogrammes and 984 grammes of heroin, 3,753 kilogrammes and 508 grammes of crystal methamphetamine (Ice), and 303 kilogrammes and 886 grammes of cocaine.

Police also seized 11,274 kilogrammes and 137 grammes of cannabis (ganja), along with 17,302,791 cannabis plants. The seizures further included 1,768 kilogrammes and 521 grammes of Kush and 439 kilogrammes and 601 grammes of hashish.

In addition, 4,569,988 narcotic pills were seized, along with 1,372 kilogrammes and 192 grammes of Madanamodaka and 623 kilogrammes and 612 grammes of Mawa.

According to the report, the heroin seizures were made during 104,589 raids, while 127,642 raids resulted in the seizure of crystal methamphetamine. Cocaine was seized during 276 raids, while 69,973 raids resulted in the detection of cannabis and cannabis plants.

Meanwhile, 23,234 raids led to the seizure of narcotic pills, Madanamodaka and Mawa.
