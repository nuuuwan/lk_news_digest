Kiriwehera Chief Incumbent’s vehicle involved in road accident

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The vehicle carrying Venerable Kobawaka Dhamminda Thero, Chief Incumbent of the Kataragama Kiriwehera Rajamaha Viharaya, was involved in a road accident in the Mattala area last night (07).

The accident occurredThe accident occurred when the vehicle collided with a herd of buffaloes that had strayed onto the road, according to initial reports.

The incident took place in the Mattala area as the vehicle was travelling along the road.
