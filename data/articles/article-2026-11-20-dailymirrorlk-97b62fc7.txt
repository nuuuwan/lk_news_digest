Thai Deputy PM to visit Sri Lanka; visa-free travel deal on agenda

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Thailand’s Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs, Sihasak Phuangketkeow, is scheduled to undertake an official visit to Sri Lanka from November 20 to 23, 2026.

During the visit, Phuangketkeow will attend the Royal Katina Ceremony at the Asgiriya Maha Viharaya in Kandy on November 21 as the Royal Representative of His Majesty King Maha Vajiralongkorn of Thailand.

He is also scheduled to call on President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and Prime Minister Dr Harini Amarasuriya and hold bilateral discussions with Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath.

A significant highlight of the visit will be the signing of an agreement between Thailand and Sri Lanka on visa exemption for holders of ordinary passports.

The agreement is expected to facilitate travel between the two countries and strengthen people-to-people connectivity by easing visa requirements for ordinary passport holders.
