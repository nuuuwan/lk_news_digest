Railway catering company fined Rs. 500,000 for overcharging bottled water

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Anuradhapura Magistrate's Court has imposed a fine of Rs. 500,000 on Jayabima Catering, the railway canteen service operator, for selling bottled drinking water above the maximum retail price (MRP) on a train operating between Kankesanthurai and Anuradhapura.

The case was filed by investigating officers of the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) following a special raid conducted in the Anuradhapura area on July 9, 2026.

During the inspection, CAA officers found that a 1,000 ml bottle of drinking water, which carried a government-approved maximum retail price of Rs. 120, had been sold to consumers for Rs. 150.

Following legal proceedings, the Anuradhapura Magistrate's Court found the company guilty of violating consumer protection laws and imposed a fine of Rs. 500,000.

The CAA said it has also informed the Sri Lanka Railways Department about the overpricing of goods sold at railway canteens.

The Authority noted that legal action had previously been taken against the same institution for similar violations and warned that all business activities must strictly comply with the Consumer Affairs Authority Act and other relevant laws.

The CAA said regular inspections will continue at railway canteens, and strict legal action will be taken against any future violations.

The Authority also urged the public to report incidents of overcharging or the sale of goods above the maximum retail price on trains or elsewhere by contacting its 1977 consumer hotline.
