Victorian Premier meets Sri Lankan community leaders ahead of state election

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Victorian Premier Ben Carroll has met with members of Melbourne’s Sri Lankan community at a dinner hosted by community leader Sinantha Jayathilake, just weeks ahead of the November 28 state election.

Mr Carroll, who assumed office in July, was joined by Lily D’Ambrosio, the retiring Member for Mill Park, who served as Victoria’s Minister for Energy and Resources, Climate Action and the State Electricity Commission until July this year. She is regarded as Australia’s longest-serving energy minister.

Also present were Lauren Kathage, MP for Yan Yean, and Elizabeth Nealy, Labor’s candidate for Mill Park and a former Mayor of Banyule.

Community leaders, business owners and families used the occasion to raise issues affecting Sri Lankan Victorians, including small business development, skilled migration, education and the rising cost of living.

“The Sri Lankan community has helped build Victoria, from our small businesses to our hospitals and classrooms. Tonight was about making sure our voice is heard at the highest level, and I am grateful the Premier took the time to sit down and listen to us directly,” Mr Jayathilake said.

The dinner was held during Ms D’Ambrosio’s final weeks in Parliament, following her 24 years of representing the Mill Park electorate.
