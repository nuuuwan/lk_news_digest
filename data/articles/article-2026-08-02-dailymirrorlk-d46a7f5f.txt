Government discusses Mullaitivu land issues at civil-military meeting

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Minister of Defence Major General Aruna Jayasekara (Retd.) chaired a high-level Civil-Military Coordination Meeting at the Mullaitivu Divisional Secretariat to discuss long-standing land issues and regional development in the Northern Province.

The meeting brought together Northern Province Governor Nagalingam Vethanayahan, Deputy Minister of Co-operative Development and Chairman of the Mannar District Coordination Committee Upali Samarasinghe, Vanni District MP Dr. Sellaththamby Thilaganathan, senior government officials, and representatives of the security forces.

Discussions focused on land-related concerns in the Keppapilavu area, where lands are currently occupied by the Sri Lanka Army, and the Vadduvakkal area, where lands are being used by the Sri Lanka Navy. Addressing the meeting, Deputy Minister Jayasekara stressed that achieving lasting regional stability requires balancing national security needs with resolving outstanding land issues involving both state-owned and privately owned properties.

Following briefings by government officials and Security Forces Commanders, the Deputy Minister instructed relevant authorities to adopt a coordinated, practical, and people-centred approach to address the issues while safeguarding national security. He also highlighted the role of the armed forces in disaster response, emergency assistance, and community service, stating that their presence contributes to both security and public welfare.

Reaffirming the government's commitment to transparency, reconciliation, and sustainable development, the Deputy Minister and other government representatives directed all relevant agencies to work together to identify practical and lasting solutions to the issues raised.

The meeting also provided an opportunity for local residents to directly present their concerns to the Deputy Minister in an open forum. According to the Ministry of Defence, many residents welcomed the opportunity to discuss long-standing grievances and expressed hope that their concerns would be addressed.

Senior officials from the Ministry of Defence, Army and Navy commanders, district secretaries, representatives of relevant government institutions, and a large number of local residents also attended the meeting.
