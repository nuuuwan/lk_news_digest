eMarketingEye to host EME Level Up: AI Hospitality Connect Sri Lanka 2026

Exclusive invitation-only conference to bring together hospitality leaders to explore future of AI and digital marketing in hospitality

eMarketingEye, Asia’s leading digital marketing agency specialising in the hospitality sector, has announced EME Level Up: AI Hospitality Connect, Sri Lanka 2026, a premier invitation-only conference for the hospitality industry. The event is scheduled to take place on 14 July 2026, at Shangri-La Colombo.

Under the theme “Think. Innovate. Elevate.”, the conference will spotlight the rapidly growing role of artificial intelligence, digital marketing, data-driven strategies, and emerging technologies in transforming how hospitality brands connect with guests, strengthen their digital visibility, and accelerate business growth.

EME Level Up: AI Hospitality Connect, Sri Lanka 2026 is set to be a premier gathering for hospitality leaders, expected to bring together over 180 delegates from Sri Lanka and the Maldives for an afternoon full of insights, innovation, and meaningful networking. Featuring 7+ curated sessions and distinguished speakers, the conference will explore the latest trends, strategies, and technologies shaping the future of hospitality, digital marketing, AI visibility, and direct bookings.

Designed for hoteliers, marketing directors, hotel owners and industry decision-makers, EME Level Up: AI Hospitality Connect, Sri Lanka 2026 will offer practical insights, thought leadership, and real-world perspectives to help hospitality brands remain competitive in today’s rapidly evolving digital landscape.

Sessions will cover a wide range of future-focused topics, including AI-driven marketing, generative search and AI visibility, direct booking strategies, social media, data analytics, and the latest in hotel technology. Delegates will engage in interactive sessions, panel discussions, case studies, and product showcases, and the event will culminate in a dedicated networking session designed to foster new industry connections.

"EME Level Up: AI Hospitality Connect, Sri Lanka 2026 presents a prime opportunity for hospitality leaders to connect with industry experts, explore the evolving role of AI and digital marketing in hospitality, and gain valuable insights into the future of digital hospitality," said eMarketingEye CEO Rajitha Dahanayake.

EME Level Up: AI Hospitality Connect, Sri Lanka 2026 is proudly hosted by eMarketingEye, in collaboration with supporting partners Aven Hospitality, TrustYou, and Lighthouse, bringing together leading industry expertise and innovation to support meaningful dialogue within the hospitality sector. The event is further amplified through the support of TravelTalk Asia as the Official Trade Media Partner and DailyFT as the Official Print Media Partner.
