Lula thanks President Dissanayake, calls for stronger Sri Lanka-Brazil ties

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva has thanked Sri Lankan President Anura Kumara Dissanayake for his congratulatory message following Lula’s victory in Brazil’s election, expressing hope for stronger bilateral trade and technical cooperation between the two countries.

Responding to Dissanayake’s message, Lula recalled meeting the Sri Lankan President in India in February this year and said the Brazilian people had demonstrated that democracy could be defended against disinformation and extremism.

“The Brazilian people showed that it is possible to defend democracy against disinformation and extremism. It was a great victory!” Lula said in his reply.

He also expressed hope that Sri Lanka and Brazil could deepen technical cooperation and bilateral trade.
