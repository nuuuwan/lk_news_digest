Gangaramaya holds Sanghika Dana for late Ven. Gnanissara Thera

A Sanghika Dana ceremony was held to commemorate the second death anniversary of the late Ven. Galboda Gnanissara Thera, the former Chief Incumbent of Gangaramaya Temple, Hunupitiya. Senior Buddhist monks representing all Nikayas, along with the current Chief Incumbent of the temple, Ven. Kirinde Assaji Thera, participated in the religious observances. Pics by Kithsiri De Mel
