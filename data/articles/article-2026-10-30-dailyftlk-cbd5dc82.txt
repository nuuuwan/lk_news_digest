Bank of Ceylon celebrates World Thrift Day 2026

Encouraging Sri Lankans to cultivate the habit of saving today for a secure tomorrow

Senior Deputy General Manager/Head of Development and Branch Banking Badra Kalyanapriya Gurusinghe

Marking World Thrift Day, celebrated annually on 31 October, Bank of Ceylon (BOC) once again focuses on raising awareness among Sri Lankans about the importance of saving and developing a regular savings habit.

Established in 1939, the bank celebrates its 87th anniversary in 2026, continuing its longstanding commitment to the financial well-being and economic stability of the people of Sri Lanka while upholding the trust placed in it by generations. Emphasising that saving is essential for building a secure future for individuals as well as the nation, the bank highlights that every rupee saved today is a valuable investment in tomorrow’s financial strength.

Commenting on the occasion, Senior Deputy General Manager/Head of Development and Branch Banking Badra Kalyanapriya Gurusinghe said: “The financial decisions we make today play a vital role in building a financially secure future. Saving is not something limited to individuals with higher incomes. Anyone can build a secure financial future by regularly saving even a small amount from their income according to their ability. It is particularly important to plan and save from today for children’s futures, education, housing needs and retirement. I invite all Sri Lankans to make saving an integral part of their lifestyles. The Bank of Ceylon remains committed to helping you secure your future through reliable banking services and a range of savings solutions designed to meet your financial needs.”

The Bank of Ceylon offers a diverse range of savings accounts and financial solutions to suit every stage of life, from newborns to senior citizens. Accounts such as ‘Ran Kekulu Smart’ and ‘14+’ have been introduced to encourage children to develop the habit of saving from an early age while helping them build a stronger financial future. These accounts offer a range of attractive benefits. The bank also supports children’s educational aspirations by providing incentives such as gifts and scholarships for academic achievements, including success at the Grade Five Scholarship Examination.

Catering to the diverse financial needs of customers ranging from young adults to senior citizens, the bank offers savings solutions such as ‘BOC 18+’, Flexi Investor and ‘Smart Investor’. These products are designed to complement customers’ lifestyles and future financial goals. In addition, the bank provides special savings facilities for senior citizens, offering the financial support necessary to enjoy retirement with greater security and peace of mind.

With the rapid advancement of digital technology, the Bank of Ceylon continues to strengthen its digital service network to deliver a more convenient, efficient and secure banking experience. Through digital solutions such as BOC Flex APP, online banking facilities, customers can conveniently carry out their day-to-day banking transactions while saving time and effort. Furthermore, with a network of more than 2,500 touch points covering all 331 Divisional Secretariat Divisions across the country, the bank has succeeded in bringing banking services closer to people in both urban and rural communities.

On this World Thrift Day, the Bank of Ceylon said it encourages all Sri Lankans to take a positive step towards a financially secure tomorrow by setting aside a portion of their income according to their means.
