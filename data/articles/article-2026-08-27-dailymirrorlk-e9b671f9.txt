No Sri Lankans among tourists reported missing:  Nepal Tourism Board

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror)- The Nepal Tourism Board in its latest update said that a total of 644 people are currently reported missing including 127 Nepalese nationals and 517 foreign nationals from 32 countries.

According to the latest statement issued by the Nepal Tourism Board, no Sri Lankan nationals are among the foreigners reported missing following the devastating flash floods.

The latest update includes 65 Americans, 53 Ukrainians, 51 Malaysians and 35 Australians, while 33 British nationals and 25 Canadians are also reported missing.

According to the update, nine Singaporeans, eight Germans, eight Russians, six Latvians, six South Africans, five Japanese, five New Zealanders, four French nationals, three Belgians and three Spaniards are among those missing.
