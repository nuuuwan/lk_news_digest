Luminex suspends Oman operations

Engineering and telecommunication infrastructure company Luminex PLC announced it has temporarily suspended the operations of its Oman subsidiary, with immediate effect and would recognise a Rs.195 million provision, citing the adverse business conditions and uncertainty linked to the ongoing war.

In a stock exchange filing the company said its board had concluded that continuing the operations of Luminex International LLC was not commercially viable, under the prevailing circumstances.

The decision followed a review of the subsidiary’s operations, financial position and future commercial prospects. The board also considered economic and business conditions in Oman and uncertainties arising from the war, which the filing did not identify.

“The continuation of the subsidiary’s business operations is not commercially viable, under the prevailing circumstances,” the company said.

Luminex said the Rs.195 million provision would be recognised in the company’s financial statements in connection with the suspension. It did not provide a breakdown of the provision or specify the reporting period in which it would be recorded.

The company will continue to monitor the conditions and assess the subsidiary’s future position.
