Speaker’s moral rights to endorse Anti-Corruption Bill questioned

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The organisation called "Dinana Dakuna"  has questioned whether Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne has the moral right to endorse the Anti-Corruption (Amendment) Bill, which is scheduled to be debated and passed by Parliament today.

The Dinana Dakuna Organization has questioned the Speaker’s moral standing to endorse the proposed legislation while the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) is investigating allegations of corruption involving him.

“There is no legal impediment preventing the Speaker from endorsing the Bill. However, the issue is whether he has the moral right to do so under the circumstances,” the organisation said in a statement.

The organisation said the ongoing investigation raises questions about the appropriateness of the Speaker playing a role in endorsing legislation aimed at strengthening the country’s anti-corruption framework.
