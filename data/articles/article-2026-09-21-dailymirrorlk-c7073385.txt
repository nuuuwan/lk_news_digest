“ViYASA” National Business Facilitation Centre to open tomorrow

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The “ViYASA” National Business Facilitation Centre (NBFC), established under the Presidential Secretariat to help remove administrative and regulatory barriers affecting businesses and investors, will be officially opened tomorrow (September 22).

The centre has been established on the initiative of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake with the aim of improving the business environment and supporting faster processes in the industrial and investment sectors.

The NBFC will assist businesses in resolving issues that arise when dealing with government institutions during the establishment and operation of businesses. It will also coordinate with relevant government agencies to facilitate the required approvals and services.

The centre is located at Building C-80, Hector Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Colombo 07, and will be headed by Senior Additional Secretary to the President Seevali Arukgoda.

The opening ceremony will be held under the patronage of Minister of Labour and Deputy Minister of Finance and Planning Dr. Anil Jayantha Fernando and Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Sunil Handunnetti, with the participation of Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake.

The official website of the National Business Facilitation Centre, nbfc.presidentsoffice.gov.lk, is also scheduled to be launched tomorrow.
