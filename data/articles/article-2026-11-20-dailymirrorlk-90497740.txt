Suspect in police custody dies after falling ill

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A suspect held at the Matale Police Station died early this morning (20) after suddenly falling ill and being rushed to the Matale District General Hospital.

The 36-year-old suspect, a resident of Ampitiya, Kandy, had been detained in Cell No. 01 of the police station in connection with the possession of crystal methamphetamine (Ice).

Police said he was taken to the hospital after falling ill, but was pronounced dead upon admission.

He had been arrested yesterday (19) by the Matale Police Vice Squad in the Hithgolla area of Matale for allegedly possessing 3,010 milligrams of Ice.

According to police, the deceased was heavily addicted to narcotics and had around 10 drug-related cases pending against him at several police stations, including Kandy and Grandpass.
