Cabinet raises Aswesuma empowerment grants to Rs. 200,000, expands beneficiaries by 525

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved an increase in capital grants for eligible low-income families under the World Bank-funded Social Security Project, raising the maximum allocation from Rs. 150,000 to Rs. 200,000 while expanding the targeted beneficiaries from 10,975 to 11,500 families.

Cabinet Spokesman and Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said move was approved following the World Bank’s agreement to enhance the financial assistance and expand the program based on experience gained during its first phase.

The initiative, targeting poor and extremely poor families receiving Aswesuma welfare benefits, is being implemented with funding support from the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank (ADB). Under the ADB-funded component, capital grants have already been disbursed to 8,660 families, concluding the program implemented through that facility.

Under the World Bank-funded Social Security Project, 4,900 families across seven districts have received capital grants during the first phase. A further 6,600 families identified across five districts were initially earmarked for assistance in the second phase.

He said the revised arrangements will increase the overall number of families targeted for empowerment under the World Bank project to 11,500, while raising the maximum grant available to eligible beneficiary households to Rs. 200,000.

The Cabinet had initially approved the broader program at its meeting on 13 October 2025, with the objective of supporting vulnerable households to strengthen their economic resilience and move towards sustainable livelihoods.

The proposal to this effect was approved the proposal submitted by the Rural Development, Social Security and Community Empowerment Minister Dr. Upali Pannilage.
