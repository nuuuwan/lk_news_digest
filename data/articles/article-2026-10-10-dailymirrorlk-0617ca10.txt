Pakistan draw first blood

OCT.10 (Mirror Sports) - Pakistan defeated Sri Lanka by four wickets in the first T20I in Rawalpindi, taking a 1-0 lead in the series after chasing down a target of 183.

New captain Sahibzada Farhan smashed 89 off 53 balls, while Maaz Sadaqat struck a rapid 50 off 28 deliveries to guide Pakistan to 187-6. Their 92-run partnership put the hosts firmly in control after a flying start in the powerplay.

Earlier, Lahiru Udara top-scored with an impressive 91 off 55 balls, but Sri Lanka’s batting struggled to build momentum around him, finishing on 182-8. Naseem Shah claimed three wickets for Pakistan, while debutant Mohammad Imran impressed with 2-21.

Sri Lanka will now look to bounce back in the second T20I after their opening defeat.
