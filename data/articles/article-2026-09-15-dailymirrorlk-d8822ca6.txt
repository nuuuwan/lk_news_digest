UNDP provides equipment to Government DNA laboratory

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) has provided essential equipment to the DNA laboratory of the Government Analyst’s Department to enhance the country’s DNA testing capacity.

The equipment was formally handed over this morning (15) by UNDP Resident Representative in Sri Lanka Azusa Kubota to Government Analyst Kamini Perera at the department premises on Isuru Mawatha, Battaramulla, in the presence of Justice and National Integration Minister Harshana Nanayakkara.

Following an inspection of the equipment, the handover agreement was signed by Perera and Kubota.

A special discussion was also held under the chairmanship of Minister Nanayakkara on facilities at the Government Analyst’s Department, including progress in issuing Government Analyst reports and the need for modern testing equipment.

Acting Government Analyst Kamini Perera, UNDP Resident Representative Azusa Kubota and Canadian High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Isabella Martin also attended the discussion.
