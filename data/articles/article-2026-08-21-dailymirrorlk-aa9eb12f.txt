Port City regulations covering Marina Hotel, Home Lands and Prime Melwa head to Parliament

The government will place regulations covering three strategically important Colombo Port City ventures before Parliament, advancing the formal process governing projects undertaken by Marina Hotel Holdings, Prime Melwa Port City and Home Lands Port City.

The Cabinet approved a proposal by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, in his capacity as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to submit the regulations issued under the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act No. 11 of 2021 to Parliament.

Marina Hotel Holdings (Pvt.) Ltd. was approved by Cabinet on March 30 as a “primary entrepreneurship with strategic importance” under the Port City law, while Prime Melwa Port City (Pvt.) Ltd. and Home Lands Port City (Pvt.) Ltd. received similar Cabinet approval on May 4.

The regulations relating to Marina Hotel Holdings were subsequently published through Extraordinary Gazette No. 2483/17 dated April 10.

Separate regulations covering Home Lands Port City and Prime Melwa Port City were published through Extraordinary Gazette Nos. 2488/03 and 2488/04, respectively, both dated May 11.

The latest Cabinet decision clears the regulations for submission to Parliament, completing another procedural step following the earlier designation of the three companies as strategically important ventures under the Port City framework.

The Cabinet document did not specify the investments committed by the three companies, the concessions or exemptions attached to their strategic status, or implementation timelines for the respective projects.
