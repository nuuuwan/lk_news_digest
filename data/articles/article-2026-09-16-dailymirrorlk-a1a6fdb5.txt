KPMG webinar on tax final payment, return filing today

As the businesses approach the final stages of the income tax compliance cycle for the year of assessment 2025/26, attention is increasingly focused on the accurate preparation of tax computations, settlement of final tax liabilities and timely filing of income tax returns.

KPMG Sri Lanka Academy will host a timely webinar, providing practical guidance on finalising income tax computations, meeting final tax payment obligations and successfully filing income tax returns for the year of assessment 2025/26.

The session will also examine how the positions adopted during the current year could influence tax compliance, reporting obligations and planning considerations for the year of assessment 2026/27.

Through expert-led discussions and practical guidance, the webinar is expected to provide the participants with the knowledge and clarity required to manage the filing season successfully.

The webinar will be conducted by Principal and Head of Tax and Regulatory Suresh R.I. Perera, Principal Tax and Regulatory Rifka Ziyard and Director Tax and Regulatory Hasna Hassan.

The webinar will be held on Wednesday, September 16, 2026, from 3:00 p.m. to 4:30 p.m., via MS Teams.
