National Logistics Awards 2026 in October

The Sri Lanka Logistics and Freight Forwarders’ Association (SLFFA) will host National Logistics Awards 2026 on October 28, 2026, at Lumina Ballroom, Cinnamon Life, Colombo, bringing together leaders and professionals from across the country’s logistics and supply chain industry to recognise excellence, innovation and outstanding contribution to the sector.

Held biennially, the National Logistics Awards has evolved into a key platform for recognising organisations and professionals that demonstrate excellence and contribute towards strengthening Sri Lanka’s logistics capabilities and competitiveness.

The 2026 awards will recognise performance across the wider logistics ecosystem, with categories covering freight forwarding, airlines, ocean carriers, courier and express services, transport providers, container depots, logistics software and technology providers, project logistics, customs brokerage and warehousing and distribution, among others.

Entries will be evaluated by an independent panel comprising representatives from the public sector, corporate sector and academia, together with experienced logistics professionals.

The evaluation process will consider key areas including operational competence, service quality, innovation, track-and-trace capabilities, reliability and timeliness, together with the relevant industry performance indicators and international logistics benchmarks.

National Logistics Awards 2026 also provides an opportunity to recognise the resilience of an industry that has continued to keep Sri Lanka connected with the global markets, despite the unprecedented challenges.

From the disruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic and Sri Lanka’s economic crisis to geopolitical tensions and the Red Sea shipping disruptions, the logistics professionals have continued to facilitate the movement of essential goods, exports and imports while supporting the manufacturers, exporters, retailers and international trade.

Against this backdrop, the SLFFA believes recognising excellence is important not only to celebrate the achievement but also to encourage higher professional standards, innovation, digitalisation and continuous improvement across the industry.

The awards also seek to create greater awareness among the younger generation about the diverse career opportunities available within logistics, freight forwarding and supply chain management.

As the global supply chains become increasingly sophisticated and technology-driven, developing the next generation of skilled logistics professionals will be critical to Sri Lanka’s future competitiveness.
