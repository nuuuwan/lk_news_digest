Colombo tops goat and sheep slaughter

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo recorded the highest number of goats and sheep slaughtered at licensed slaughterhouses in Sri Lanka during the provisional 2025 period, while Batticaloa reported the highest number of cattle slaughtered, according to the latest statistics released by the Department of Census and Statistics.

The data showed that 28,388 goats and sheep were slaughtered in Colombo, followed by Batticaloa with 7,195, Kandy with 3,971 and Jaffna with 3,299 in 2025.

Meanwhile, Batticaloa recorded 30,190 cattle slaughtered during the year, the highest among all districts. It was followed by Ampara (27,078), Anuradhapura (15,707), Kurunegala (13,646) and Trincomalee (13,307).

Nationwide, 156,270 cattle were slaughtered in 2025, a decline from 164,177 in 2024.

However, the figure remained higher than the totals recorded in 2023 (146,339) and 2022 (147,562), while 151,841 cattle were slaughtered in 2021.

On the other hand, the total number of goats and sheep slaughtered across the country stood at 44,755 in 2025, compared with 47,151 in 2024, 51,463 in 2023, 43,870 in 2022 and 37,835 in 2021.

Meanwhile, Puttalam recorded the highest number of swine slaughtered in 2025, with 659 animals. The national total, however, dropped significantly to 4,129, compared with 12,765 in 2024, 12,570 in 2023, 17,862 in 2022 and 32,736 in 2021.
