Pet Expo 2026 begins

Pet Expo 2026, Sri Lanka's largest pet exhibition, is being held from July 17 to 19 at the BMICH, Colombo, from 10.00 a.m. to 6.00 p.m. Organised by the Association of Companion Animal Practitioners of Sri Lanka (ACAPSL), the event features a scientific conference, pet product exhibitions, beauty clinics and a range of activities for pet lovers, with free admission for the public.
