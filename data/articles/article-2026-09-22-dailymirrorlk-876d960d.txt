Saudi prosecution appeal leads to death sentence for  Sivarasa Anojan: Vijitha Herath

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath told Parliament today that Sri Lankan national Sivarasa Anojan has been sentenced to death in Saudi Arabia following an appeal by the Saudi prosecution.

The Minister said an appeal submitted by Sri Lanka on behalf of Anojan had been rejected.

According to Herath, the Saudi prosecutor had appealed for Anojan to be prosecuted under Shariya law rather than under the country’s cybercrime legislation. Following the appeal, the death sentence was imposed.

The Minister said the Sri Lankan Government was continuing diplomatic interventions in the case and was taking steps to obtain legal advice from both Sri Lankan and Saudi legal experts.

He also said a special delegation led by the Deputy Speaker would travel to Saudi Arabia to deliver a letter from President Anura Kumara Dissanayake to the Saudi King, seeking a pardon for Anojan.

Herath said he expected to meet the Saudi Foreign Minister next month on the sidelines of the UN Human Rights Council sessions, where he would request that the death sentence be commuted or the punishment reduced.

The Foreign Minister further said the Sri Lankan Government would bear all legal costs incurred in Saudi Arabia on behalf of Anojan. Any legal expenses already incurred by Anojan’s family would also be reimbursed by the Government.
