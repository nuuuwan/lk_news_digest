‘Justice for the Robe’ protest walk begins in Kandy

The ‘Justice for the Robe – National Commitment Pooja’ and a ‘Freedom Walk’ organised by the Maha Sangha began in front of the Sri Dalada Maligawa in Kandy this morning (10), calling for the protection of the religious rights of imprisoned Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera and other monks.

The protest walk is scheduled to proceed through several major towns before reaching Colombo on October 15.

According to the announced schedule, the walk will proceed from Kandy to Kadugannawa today (10), Kadugannawa to Kegalle on October 11, Kegalle to Warakapola on October 12, Warakapola to Nittambuwa on October 13, Nittambuwa to Kadawatha on October 14, and Kadawatha to Colombo on October 15.

The Maha Sangha launched the protest and the religious observance in response to concerns over the legal situation involving imprisoned monks and reported incidents of monks being stripped of their robes.
