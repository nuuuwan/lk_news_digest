‘Ghafoor Mama’ sentenced to 12 years in Wanathawilluwa explosives case

Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Mohamed Shariff Adam Lebbe, alias ‘Ghafoor Mama’, the third defendant in the case involving the stockpiling of explosives and prohibited materials in Wanathawilluwa, has been sentenced to 12 years of rigorous imprisonment.

The sentence was imposed today by a three-member bench of the Colombo High Court after the defendant pleaded guilty to five indictments filed against him.

The bench, comprising Justices Lal Ranasinghe Bandara, Rashmi Singappuli and Nayomi Wickramasinghe, ordered a 12-year prison term for each count, with all sentences to run concurrently.

However, the judges ordered that the seven-and-a-half years he had already spent in remand custody and under detention orders be deducted from the sentence. Accordingly, he is expected to serve approximately another four-and-a-half years in prison.

In determining the sentence, the bench considered several factors, including the defendant’s guilty plea, which avoided a prolonged trial, and his age of 63.

Meanwhile, the sixth defendant, Mohamed Rameez Mohamed Sajith, alias ‘Sajith Moulavi’, sought time to decide whether to plead guilty to the charges against him.

The court ordered that the case be taken up again at 8 a.m. tomorrow (29) to hear his decision. The Superintendent of Prisons was directed to produce him before court by 7.45 a.m.

The bench also directed authorities to provide special security for Ghafoor Mamma when producing him before the Kegalle and Batticaloa High Courts, where he faces two other pending cases.

During the proceedings, the court was informed that the Attorney General had filed 13 indictments against six defendants in connection with the case. Five of the defendants have now pleaded guilty and been sentenced.
