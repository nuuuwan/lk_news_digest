Blood donation during Nallur Festival

A total of 236 voluntary donors donated blood during a four-day blood donation camp held in Nallur alongside the annual Nallur Festival. The programme was organised by Save a Life in collaboration with the Jaffna Municipal Council, Jaffna Medical Officer of Health (MOH) Office and the Blood Bank of Teaching Hospital Jaffna, with support from Diakonia. A significant number of women and first-time donors participated in the camp, reflecting growing community awareness of the importance of voluntary blood donation. The organisers thanked all 236 donors, health authorities, volunteers, partner organisations and supporters for their contribution to the initiative, which carried the message: “Donate Blood – Save Lives.”
