Russia’s Azur Air to resume direct charter flights to Sri Lanka

Russian charter airline Azur Air is set to resume direct charter flights to Sri Lanka for the 2026/2027 winter season after a one-year break, according to Russian Travel Digest.

The airline, which is affiliated with tour operator Anex Tour, is scheduled to restart flights on November 2, 2026, with services from eight Russian cities.

The flights will operate to Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport in Hambantota using Boeing 767-300 aircraft. The expanded network includes Moscow, Samara, Tyumen, Kazan, Novosibirsk, Yekaterinburg, Mineralnye Vody and Ufa.

Moscow will have the highest frequency, with three flights every 12 days, while Yekaterinburg will have two flights during each 12-day cycle. The remaining cities will be served once every 12 days.

The initial schedules are subject to change, according to the report.

The return of Azur Air comes as other Russian airlines also expand services to Sri Lanka for the upcoming winter season. Aeroflot and S7 Airlines have announced flights to Colombo’s Bandaranaike International Airport.
