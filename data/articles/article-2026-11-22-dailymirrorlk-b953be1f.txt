Severe lightning warning issued for multiple areas

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Meteorology has issued an Amber-level weather advisory warning of severe lightning in several parts of the country.

According to the advisory, thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are expected in parts of the Ampara, Badulla, Colombo, Galle, Hambantota, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Kurunegala, Matale, Matara, Monaragala, Nuwara Eliya and Ratnapura districts.

The Department warned that the risk of severe lightning would be higher during thundershowers, particularly in the evening and night.

The public has been urged to take necessary precautions to minimise the potential damage and risks associated with lightning.
