AI safety systems to be piloted on long-distance private buses

Colombo, September 5 (Daily Mirror) - A pilot project has been launched to introduce Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology to long-distance private buses, with the aim of enhancing the safety of both passengers and drivers.

The project is being implemented on the instructions of Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake and Deputy Minister Prasanna Gunasena, with coordination by the National Council for Road Safety and under the direction of the National Transport Commission (NTC).

As the first phase of the project, the installation of technological equipment on long-distance private buses commenced today (5) at the Bastian Mawatha Bus Terminal in Colombo.

Initially, AI systems will be installed in 10 buses. Following an assessment of their effectiveness and performance, plans are in place to expand the technology to other buses operating across the country.

The initiative is aimed at using AI-based technology to improve road safety and reduce risks faced by passengers and drivers on long-distance bus services.
