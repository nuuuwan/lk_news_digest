Cattle meant for milking killed for meat: MP

Colombo, July 21 (Daily Mirror) - Cattle meant for milking are killed for meat due to lack of grasslands, Opposition MP Chamara Sampath Dassanayake alleged in Parliament today.

The MP said the Wildlife Department sues catte owners claiming that the cattle eat up grass meant for wild elephants and that the situation has created serious issues in areas such as the Maduru Oya forest reserve.

He said this has resulted in Sri Lanka spending billions for the import of milk powder.

Deputy Minister of Agriculture Namal Karunaratne, who responded, said the issue should be resolved with diligence through effective dialogue with all stakeholders.
