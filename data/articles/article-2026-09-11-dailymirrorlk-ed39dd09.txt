Live photos coming for new SIM registrations

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL) is preparing to introduce stricter regulations for the issuance and use of mobile SIM cards amid a rise in criminal activities involving illegally obtained and untraceable connections.

Speaking at a media briefing at the Department of Government Information, TRCSL Director General Air Vice Marshal Bandula Herath (Retd.) said new SIM cards are expected to require live photograph verification from later this year.

He said the move follows requests from law enforcement authorities, including the Sri Lanka Police, which have reported an increase in illegal activities carried out using SIM cards that cannot be properly traced.

Under the new system, anyone purchasing a SIM card will be required to provide a live photograph along with the necessary identification documents at the point of registration.

The TRCSL also plans to introduce a comprehensive verification system for existing subscribers, with the aim of ensuring 100% identity verification of mobile users by 2027.

Authorities say the measures are intended to strengthen the identification of mobile subscribers and prevent telecommunications services from being exploited for criminal activities.

The regulatory framework will also take into consideration the needs of the general public and foreign visitors, with the TRCSL aiming to strengthen security without creating unnecessary difficulties for legitimate users and tourists.

Meanwhile, concerns over mobile network quality were also raised during the briefing.

Herath said Sri Lanka has nearly 30 million active SIM cards, while the TRCSL currently relies largely on information provided by mobile network operators when monitoring service quality.
