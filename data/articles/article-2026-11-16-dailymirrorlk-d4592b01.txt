Chinese businessman arrested with Rs. 1.42 mn cigarettes at BIA

A Chinese businessman was arrested by Airport Customs officers at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) yesterday morning (15) for attempting to smuggle foreign-made cigarettes worth Rs. 1.42 million out of the airport through the Green Channel.

The 42-year-old businessman had arrived at the BIA from Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, at 9.10 a.m. yesterday on Malaysia Airlines flight MH-497.

Customs officers found 140 cartons containing 28,000 Chinese-made cigarettes concealed in two pieces of hand luggage.

The suspect had attempted to leave the airport through the Green Channel, where passengers are not required to make customs declarations.

He has been detained by Airport Customs officers, who are conducting further investigations.
