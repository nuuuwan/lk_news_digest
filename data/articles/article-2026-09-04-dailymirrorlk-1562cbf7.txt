Namal Rajapaksa remanded

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, who was arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over allegations of accepting a Rs. 100 million bribe in connection with a transaction involving the purchase of Airbus aircraft, has been ordered to be remanded until September 18 by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama today.
