DFCC Bank-Mels Automobiles offer leasing solutions for BMWs

DFCC Bank becomes Mels Automobiles’ 1st banking partner to enhance BMW ownership experience

Flexible leasing and exclusive after-sales benefits bring financing, authorised service and premium mobility together for BMW customers

The customers aspiring to own a BMW can now access flexible DFCC Leasing solutions together with exclusive service and warranty benefits through a new strategic partnership between DFCC Bank and Mels Automobiles (Pvt.) Ltd.

The partnership was formalised through a memorandum of understanding signed recently, with the participation of senior representatives from both organisations.

It marks Mels Automobiles’ first banking partnership since assuming the authorised BMW dealership in Sri Lanka, with DFCC Bank becoming its inaugural banking partner.

The collaboration brings together DFCC Bank’s financing expertise and Mels Automobiles’ premium automotive and after-sales capabilities, creating a more connected experience from vehicle selection and financing to servicing and longer-term ownership.

Through the partnership, the eligible customers can access DFCC Leasing facilities with repayment periods of up to seven years at competitive interest rates, subject to credit evaluation and applicable terms and conditions.

The customers obtaining financing through DFCC Bank will also receive three complimentary services and an additional two-year warranty from Mels Automobiles, extending the total warranty period, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

The financing proposition is available to the eligible customers seeking to purchase a BMW through Mels Automobiles.

To introduce the collaboration, DFCC Bank invited the DFCC Pinnacle customers to an exclusive private preview of the new BMW 7 series. The event provided the guests with an opportunity to experience the luxury sedan in an intimate setting while learning more about the financing and ownership benefits available through the partnership.

DFCC Bank Director and Chief Executive Officer Thimal Perera said, “For many customers, owning a BMW represents years of ambition, progress and personal achievement. Our role is to make the financial path towards that aspiration more considered, flexible and reassuring. By bringing together DFCC Bank’s financing expertise and the premium automotive experience offered by Mels Automobiles, we are creating an ownership journey that reflects the quality and confidence the customers expect from both brands.”

Mels Automobiles Chief Executive Officer Anushka Polonowita said, “When you choose a BMW, you are choosing the team and the institution behind it. As part of Melstacorp, Mels Automobiles brings that stability to every customer and our first banking partnership with DFCC Bank carries it into the buying journey itself. Flexible financing, genuine vehicles, authorised after-sales care and an extended warranty come together in a single promise to look after our customers well beyond the day they collect their car. This is the first of many steps in a new chapter for BMW in Sri Lanka and we intend to build it with our customers and our partners.”
