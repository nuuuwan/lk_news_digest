Protest demands justice for tusker Kawanthissa

A peaceful protest was held in front of Galle Face Green, demanding that the Government deliver justice for the iconic Sri Lankan tusker Kawanthissa, who died while receiving treatment in the Siyambalangamuwa area of Galgamuwa.

The protest was organised by a group called ‘The People Who Love the Environment’. The protesters called for an end to the killing of wild animals and urged the Government to introduce concrete solutions to address the human-elephant conflict.
