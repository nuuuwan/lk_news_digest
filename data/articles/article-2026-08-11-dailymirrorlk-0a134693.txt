Cabinet approves return of Sri Lankan refugees from India

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved measures to facilitate the voluntary return of Sri Lankans who fled to India during the war and were subsequently registered as refugees, allowing them to return through approved ports without facing legal action under the Immigration and Emigration Act.

The decision follows advice from the Attorney General to provide the necessary immigration clearance facilities for Sri Lankan refugees seeking to return to the country through the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR).

Under the approved arrangements, Sri Lankans who left the country before August 1, 2006, without using an approved port or possessing valid travel documents, will be required to obtain a clearance report from the State Intelligence Service after their identity as Sri Lankan citizens is verified through Sri Lankan diplomatic missions in India.

The clearance process will also ensure that the individuals concerned have not been involved in murder, treason or other offences for which specific legal provisions apply.

Those who meet the requirements will be issued temporary passports and permitted to enter Sri Lanka through an approved port of entry without legal action being taken under the Immigration and Emigration Act No. 20 of 1948.

The same facility will be extended to Sri Lankans who illegally migrated to India between August 1, 2006, and May 19, 2009, the end of the war, through an approved port but without a valid passport. This will apply to those whose identities have been verified and who obtain clearance from the State Intelligence Service.

However, individuals found to have committed murder, treason or other offences covered by special legal provisions will be detained at a police station and referred to the relevant authorities for further legal action.

The proposal was submitted by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs and was approved by the Cabinet to facilitate the voluntary repatriation of the affected Sri Lankans.
