Sri Lanka Customs achieves 2026 revenue target by October 13

Colombo, Oct. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Customs has achieved its revenue target for 2026 by October 13, collecting Rs. 2,208 billion against the annual target of Rs. 2,207 billion.

Accordingly, Customs has exceeded its annual revenue target by Rs. 1 billion, with more than two months remaining in the year.

The revenue collection reflects the department’s performance in meeting the Government’s revenue targets for 2026.
