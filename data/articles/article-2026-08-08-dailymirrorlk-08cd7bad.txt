Galle Gallants reach LPL 2026 Final

AUG.08 (Mirror Sports) - Galle Gallants stormed into the LPL 2026 final with a six-wicket victory over Colombo Kaps in Qualifier 2 at the R. Premadasa International Cricket Stadium in Colombo on Friday.

Chasing 177, Galle reached 177/4 in 18.4 overs, with Sam Harper leading the chase with a superb 77 off 51 balls.

Charith Asalanka added 59 off 42 deliveries, with the pair sharing a match-winning 126-run partnership for the second wicket.

Earlier, Colombo Kaps recovered from a difficult start to post 176 in 18.5 overs. Ravindu Fernando top-scored with 48 off 27 balls, while Rubin Hermann made 45 and Malsha Tharupathi scored 28.

Sachindu Colombage and Charith Asalanka claimed two wickets each for Galle Gallants.

Colombo Kaps: 176 (18.5) – Ravindu Fernando 48, Rubin Hermann 45; Sachindu Colombage 2/34, Charith Asalanka 2/34

Galle Gallants: 177/4 (18.4) – Sam Harper 77, Charith Asalanka 59; Ravindu Fernando 2/44

Galle Gallants will face Jaffna Kings in the LPL 2026 final at the R. Premadasa International Cricket Stadium.
