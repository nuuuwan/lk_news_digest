Police tighten security in Kandy ahead of fourth Randoli Perahera

Security measures have been intensified across Kandy as police prepare for the fourth Randoli Perahera of the annual Kandy Esala Maha Perahera Festival today (26).

The Sri Lanka Police have deployed a special security and traffic management plan to manage the large crowds expected to gather for the procession.

The measures include roadblocks, security checks, inspections of vehicles and individuals, tourist assistance, and designated parking arrangements aimed at maintaining public safety and smooth traffic movement.

The fourth Randoli Perahera will begin at 6.44 p.m. from the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic and travel through Dalada Veediya, Yatinuwara Veediya, Cross Street, Kotugodella Veediya, Kande Veediya, D. S. Senanayake Veediya and Raja Veediya before returning to the Temple.

The Kandy Esala Maha Perahera Festival, organised by the Sri Dalada Maligawa, began on August 18 and is scheduled to conclude on August 28.

Police have urged the public and visitors attending the Perahera to cooperate with security personnel and follow traffic instructions throughout the event.
