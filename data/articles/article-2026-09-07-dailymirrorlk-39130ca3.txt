Sri Lanka records 34 shootings this year, 17 deaths amid gang violence

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has recorded 34 shooting incidents linked to suspected underworld activity across the island, so far this year, leaving 17 people dead and 18 others injured.

The latest incidents were reported in Boralesgamuwa and Kosgoda over the weekend, with investigators examining possible links to organized criminal networks.

A 74-year-old man was shot dead at his residence on Malani Bulathsinhala Mawatha in Boralesgamuwa on Friday (September 4) night, police said.

According to preliminary investigations, two unidentified men who arrived on a motorcycle opened fire at the victim using a 9mm pistol before fleeing the scene. Police said the victim was allegedly working as a money collector for an organized crime figure known as ‘Kos Malli’.

The investigation made a breakthrough on Saturday (September 5), when Maharagama Police recovered the motorcycle believed to have been used by the assailants. The vehicle was found abandoned in a paddy field near a temple in the Mahalwarawa area of Pannipitiya.

Meanwhile, a suspected shooting attempt was reported in the Kosgoda police area early yesterday morning, with the target reportedly being a relative of the late organized criminal known as ‘Kosgoda Tharaka’.

According to preliminary investigations, an unidentified gunman attempted to shoot the individual using a 9mm firearm. However, the weapon reportedly failed to discharge on both occasions when the suspect attempted to fire. The gunman subsequently fled the area.

Police investigations into a series of recent shootings revealed that several incidents connected to disputes between rival criminal groups, including conflicts involving drug-trafficking networks.
