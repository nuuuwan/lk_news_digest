Indices close on a mixed note

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Ceylinco Holdings, Harischandra Mills and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.2bn.

Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 edged up. Crossings were witnessed in Cargills, Melstacorp and Nations Trust Bank accounting for 76.2% of the turnover.

Mixed interest was observed in Softlogic Life Insurance, Ceylon Cold Stores and Commercial Bank whilst retail interest was noted in Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & Finance, UB Finance Company and HNB Finance.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers. Food & Staples Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Cargills) whilst the sector index gained 0.15%.

The share price of Cargills edged up by Rs. 0.25 (0.04%) to close at Rs. 630.00. Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Melstacorp) whilst the sector index increased by 0.43%. The share price of Melstacorp lost Rs. 1.00 (0.55%) to close at Rs. 181.00.

Nations Trust Bank, Softlogic Life Insurance and Hatton National Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Nations Trust Bank moved down by Rs. 0.50 (0.16%) to close at Rs. 305.00. The share price of Softlogic Life Insurance recorded a loss of Rs. 2.40 (2.87%) to close at Rs. 81.10. The share price of Hatton National Bank declined by Rs. 2.00 (0.52%) to close at Rs. 384.00.
