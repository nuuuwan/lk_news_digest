17 major reservoirs and seven medium tanks spilling or releasing water

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Seventeen major reservoirs and seven medium-sized reservoirs across the country were either spilling or releasing water through spill gates as of today (5), the Irrigation Department said.

According to the department’s Water Management Branch, 11 major reservoirs were spilling, while spill gates had been opened at six others as of 12.30 p.m.

Among the major reservoirs, the Deduru Oya reservoir was releasing the highest volume of water at 4,100 cubic feet per second (cusecs), followed by Rajangana at 4,039 cusecs.

The Inginimitiya reservoir was releasing 1,500 cusecs, while Nalanda, Usgala Siyabalangamuwa and Bathalagoda reservoirs were also spilling water.

In the medium-sized reservoir category, five tanks were spilling, while spill gates had been opened at two others. Thuruwila reservoir was releasing 347 cusecs, while Wennoruwa Wewa was releasing 75 cusecs.

Meanwhile, the total effective water storage in 74 major reservoirs monitored by the department stood at 1,368,912 acre-feet, representing 46.1 per cent of their combined capacity.

The district-wise figures showed that Kurunegala reservoirs had reached 91.4 per cent of their effective storage capacity, while Nuwara Eliya recorded 94.2 per cent.

Matale district's effective storage was reported at 100 per cent, while Hambantota recorded 79.9 per cent and Matara 82.1 per cent.

However, Ampara district had recorded only 19 per cent of its effective storage capacity, the lowest among the districts listed. Vavuniya recorded 31.6 per cent, while Monaragala stood at 36.8 per cent.

The Irrigation Department warned people living in areas downstream of reservoirs that were spilling or releasing water through spill gates to remain vigilant about changes in river water levels.

It also urged residents to strictly follow safety instructions issued by the Irrigation Department and local authorities regarding reservoir spill levels and gate operations.
