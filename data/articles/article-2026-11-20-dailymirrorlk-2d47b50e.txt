Seven new airlines enter Sri Lanka over past two years

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Seven new international airlines have commenced operations to Sri Lanka over the past two years, Deputy Minister of Tourism Ruwan Ranasinghe informed Parliament today.

Addressing the House, the Deputy Minister highlighted that Sri Lanka has successfully revitalized its tourism sector—even as regional competitors such as the Maldives experience a decline—attributing this growth to the country’s prevailing political, economic, and social stability.

Deputy Minister Ranasinghe noted that seven new carriers launched flights to Sri Lanka between November 18, 2024, and November 18, 2025. In addition, ten international airlines that had previously suspended operations—including British Airways—have resumed services over the past two years, while thirteen existing carriers have expanded their flight frequencies and capacity to the island.

Looking ahead, the Deputy Minister expressed confidence that Sri Lanka will welcome 2.4 million tourists by the end of 2026, surpassing the 2,362,000 arrivals recorded in 2025.
