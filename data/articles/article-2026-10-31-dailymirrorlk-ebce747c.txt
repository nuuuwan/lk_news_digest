Union Assurance expands emotional well-being programme islandwide

Union Assurance PLC is expanding the reach of its emotional well-being initiative ‘Piripun Parapurak, Surakina Mehewarak’, beyond Colombo, as part of its commitment to supporting the students across Sri Lanka.

The latest programme was held at Pushpadana Girls’ College in Kandy, engaging over 300 students and marking another step in the initiative’s journey to make emotional well-being awareness and support more accessible to students, equipping them to navigate the challenges of modern life.

Launched in 2025, Piripun Parapurak, Surakina Mehewarak was introduced to help schoolchildren build positive mindsets and strengthen their emotional well-being.

The initiative initially engaged the students across several schools in Colombo through expert-led discussions on emotional awareness, managing academic pressures and the impact of digital screens on personal development.

Building on its long-term vision, Union Assurance has now entered the second phase of the programme, extending its reach to communities beyond Colombo and enabling more students across the country to access practical guidance and meaningful conversations on emotional well-being.

The interactive programme, delivered by qualified mental health practitioner, Counsellor and Psychotherapist Dr. Chaminda Weerasiriwardane, engaged the students in open conversations and practical exercises addressing some of the key challenges faced by the students today, including examination-related stress, academic pressures and the growing impact of digital screen dependency.

Through relatable scenarios, guided discussions and practical coping techniques, the students were encouraged to better understand their emotions, respond to challenges positively and develop healthier habits that support their overall well-being.

Union Assurance Chief Marketing Officer Mahen Gunarathna commented, “Through programmes such as Piripun Parapurak, Surakina Mehewarak, we aim to create awareness, encourage open dialogues and equip the students with the confidence and emotional skills needed to navigate these challenges positively and build resilience for the future.”
