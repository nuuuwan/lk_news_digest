Petrol cars below 1,000cc top vehicle imports in 2026

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Petrol cars with engine capacities below 1,000cc accounted for the highest customs revenue among vehicle imports in 2026, generating Rs. 137.4 billion, officials informed the Committee on Ways and Means.

Sri Lanka imported 316,000 vehicles as of June 30, 2026, generating Rs. 512.547 billion in tax revenue, according to information presented to the Committee.

Motor vehicle imports accounted for Rs. 386.726 billion of the total tax revenue collected during the period.

Cars with petrol engines below 1,000cc were the highest revenue-generating commodity category, accounting for 9.96% of total Customs revenue.

The Committee also discussed the volume of imported containers and their inspection procedures, the use of modern technology to enhance the efficiency of Sri Lanka Customs, challenges in revenue administration and future plans.

Several Committee members and officials from the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development and Sri Lanka Customs participated in the meeting.
