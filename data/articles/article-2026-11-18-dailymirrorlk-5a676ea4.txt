Lessons, courage set by my father give me strength to face challenges - Namal

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Namal Rajapaksa has sent an emotional birthday message to his father, former President Mahinda Rajapaksa, saying that his love and respect for him transcend the walls of his prison cell.

In a message shared on the occasion of his father's birthday, Namal said he was unable to be by his father's side to wish him personally as he was going through a difficult period separated from his family and loved ones.

He said the lessons, courage and example set by his father had given him strength to face challenges in life.

Recalling his father's leadership during one of the darkest periods in the country's history, Namal said Mahinda Rajapaksa had brought peace to countless families by standing firm without fear.

He said he had come to understand more deeply the patience, courage and determination his father had demonstrated while shouldering enormous responsibilities.

Namal also said his own experience of facing difficulties while fighting for the truth had helped him better understand the strength required of a father.

Although he could not be with his father on his birthday, Namal said his children would visit their grandfather and embrace him on his behalf.

Describing Mahinda Rajapaksa as a man firmly rooted in Giruwapattuwa who had endured hardships, political pressure and insults, Namal wished him good health, mental strength and happiness.

"The bond between a father and a son is something that even distance cannot break," he said in his message.
