Court rejects preliminary objections in case against Shashindra Rajapaksa

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo High Court today rejected the preliminary objections raised by the accused, seeking to have the case filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption against former State Minister Shashindra Rajapaksa and two others dismissed.

Colombo High Court Judge Ravindra Premarathne ordered that the case proceed under the existing indictment.
