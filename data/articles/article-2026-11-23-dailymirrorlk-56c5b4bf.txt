Zimbabwean Pastor Uebert Angel arrested over sexual assault

NEWS GHANA - Zimbabwe police arrested preacher Uebert Angel, founder of Spirit Embassy and the country’s ambassador-at-large, on 22 November 2026 over allegations of indecent assault and sodomy.

The Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP) confirmed the arrest in a post on social media that afternoon. “The ZRP confirms the arrest of Prophet Uebert Angel this afternoon on indecent assault and sodomy allegations. More details to follow in due course,” the statement said.

National police spokesperson Commissioner Paul Nyathi confirmed the development, saying further details would be released in due course, the state-owned Herald reported on 23 November 2026. The police have not said who made the allegations, when the alleged offences took place, or when he will appear in court.

Several Zimbabwean outlets reported that he was arrested during a Sunday church service at the Hippodrome in Harare. The Herald and NewsDay placed the arrest at the Hippodrome, the Harare headquarters of Angel’s Spirit Embassy, ZimDaily reported.

Angel, whose birth name is Uebert Mudzanire, is one of Zimbabwe’s most prominent religious figures and one of its most visible diplomats, according to EEW Magazine. He holds the title of presidential envoy and ambassador-at-large.

No public response from Angel, his lawyers or his church could be found at the time of publication. An arrest is not a charge, and the allegations have not been tested in court.

In Ghana, the preacher was in the  news this month after praising a Ghanaian cleric. On 7 November 2026, GhanaWeb reported that Angel described the founder and leader of Glorious Word and Power Ministry, Rev. Isaac Owusu-Bempah, as a genuine man of God, claiming that God revealed this to him through visions. He told his congregation that Owusu-Bempah was one of only seven prophets around the world whom God has revealed to him as genuine, although the two had never met.
