Rs. 60m for each district under road safety programme

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - The Government will provide Rs. 60 million to each district to identify and improve unsafe and dangerous locations on roads that are not part of the national road network.

The funding is part of a Rs. 1.5 billion national programme aimed at reducing road accidents by addressing physical safety hazards on roads.

Local government institutions will identify dangerous locations within their respective areas and carry out the required safety improvements under the programme.

Memorandums of Understanding were signed with relevant local government institutions on September 18 to begin implementing the initiative.

The National Council on Road Safety will provide technical expertise and monitor the progress of the projects in coordination with the relevant local authorities.

The programme was approved by the Cabinet in June following a proposal submitted by Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Ratnayake.

The Government expects the programme to enable dangerous road locations to be identified and rectified more quickly, improving the safety of motorists, pedestrians and other road users.
