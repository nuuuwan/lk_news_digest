Lankadeepa shines at Asian Miracle Awards

Lankadeepa emerged as one of the top winners at the Asian Miracle Awards securing several honours for its publications and journalists. Lankadeepa photojournalist Lahiru Harshana was awarded the title of Best Newspaper Photojournalist of the Year. The Daily Lankadeepa was named the Most Popular Newspaper of the Year, while Irida Lankadeepa received the award for Best Weekend Newspaper of the Year. Furthermore, Lankadeepa Regional Editor Niroshana Godellage was honoured with the Best Regional Editor of the Year award. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
