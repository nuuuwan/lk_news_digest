Anuradhapura launches week-long rabies prevention drive

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A week-long programme to strengthen rabies prevention and responsible animal care has commenced in Anuradhapura, with authorities targeting the sterilisation and vaccination of 525 dogs, anti-rabies vaccination of 4,000 dogs and the training of 50 dog vaccinators.

The Anuradhapura World Rabies Day Initiative, being held from September 21 to 28, brings together provincial authorities, the Anuradhapura Municipal Council, the Department of Animal Production and Health (DAPH), veterinary professionals and community organisations.

The programme is being conducted under the leadership of North Central Province Provincial Commissioner Ruwan Wijesinghe, with the support of the Anuradhapura Municipal Council and DAPH.

Organised by Vets For Future (VFF), the initiative includes a seven-day sterilisation and vaccination programme from September 21 to 27, particularly targeting areas where previous Catch-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return (CNVR) activities had been conducted.

The organisers said the programme would focus on newly matured dogs, new litters and animals that may not have been reached during previous interventions.

Meanwhile, the Anuradhapura Municipal Council, together with 11 veterinary surgeons, will conduct a large-scale anti-rabies vaccination programme targeting 4,000 dogs on September 26 and 27.

A Dog Vaccinator Training Programme will also be held from September 25 to 27, with the aim of training 50 vaccinators to strengthen local capacity for rabies prevention.

Around 15,000 awareness leaflets are expected to be distributed during the week, covering rabies prevention, vaccination, responsible pet ownership and animal welfare.

A schoolchildren's drawing competition will also be held under the theme “Dog is Man's Best Friend”, promoting kindness towards animals and responsible pet ownership.

VFF Director Dr. Chamith Nanayakkara said rabies elimination could not be achieved by a single organisation and required cooperation among government authorities, veterinary professionals, local authorities and communities.

The programme will conclude on September 28 with an Appreciation and Award Ceremony at the Kekirawa Pradeshiya Sabha, where individuals and community leaders who contributed to the initiative will be recognised and prizes awarded to the drawing competition winners.
