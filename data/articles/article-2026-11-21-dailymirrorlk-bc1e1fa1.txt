Youth skills campaign to cover 14,008 GN divisions

A national door-to-door campaign will be launched across all 14,008 Grama Niladhari divisions to identify the talents and interests of young people and direct them towards vocational training and employment opportunities.

The National Vocational Skills Promotion Programme, also known as the ‘Door to Door Skill Campaign’, is being implemented by the Vocational Education Division of the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, with the support of the Ministry of Social Empowerment.

The programme aims to reach young people who are unemployed or unable to progress through the conventional education system and help them develop vocational skills that match labour market requirements.

As part of the initiative, officials will visit villages and households to educate young people and their parents about vocational education and available training opportunities.

Deputy Minister of Vocational Education Nalin Hewage said the programme was expected to strengthen the vocational skills of young people, connect them with employment opportunities and contribute to national economic development.

He said the Government was focusing on increasing allocations for education, strengthening human resources in the education and vocational education sectors, expanding higher education opportunities through National Vocational Qualification (NVQ) pathways and supporting young people facing economic difficulties.

The Government also aims to build a skilled youth workforce to support its national targets of generating US$30 billion in export revenue and attracting four million tourists by 2030, he said.

The programme was officially launched at Isurupaya on November 21, with Prime Minister and Education Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya attending the inaugural event.
