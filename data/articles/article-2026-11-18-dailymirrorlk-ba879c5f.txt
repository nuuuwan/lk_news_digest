US, Sri Lanka discuss expanding defense cooperation, digital economy ties

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - U.S. Ambassador to Sri Lanka Eric Meyer met with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake today to discuss strengthening bilateral relations, with a focus on expanding defense cooperation and joint initiatives to combat transnational crime.

In a post on X, Ambassador Meyer noted that the two leaders exchanged views on the future of U.S.–Sri Lanka partnerships and key shared priorities.

"We discussed our growing defense cooperation, successful joint efforts to combat transnational crime, and opportunities for trusted American technology to support Sri Lanka’s digital economy," the Ambassador stated.

He added that both sides reaffirmed their commitment to building on this momentum to ensure the safety of their citizens, safeguard vital maritime trade routes supporting both economies, and foster new opportunities for Americans and Sri Lankans within a free, open, and prosperous Indo-Pacific region.
