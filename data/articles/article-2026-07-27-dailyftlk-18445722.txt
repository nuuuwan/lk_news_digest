KDK Sri Lanka strengthens regional ties through Malaysia technology visit

KDK Sri Lanka recently visited Malaysia to gain a closer look at the people, technology and engineering behind KDK’s product portfolio.

The Sri Lankan delegation was joined by KDK team members from Japan and Hong Kong, with the visit reflecting the company’s global collaboration across markets.

The program also provided an opportunity to exchange ideas, strengthen relationships and explore the latest energy-efficient technologies, including the innovations behind KDK’s newest DC fan.

KDK Sri Lanka said the visit reinforces its commitment to delivering advanced engineering, greater

energy efficiency and trusted solutions backed by global expertise and local support.
