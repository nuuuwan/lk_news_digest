DMASL to host Digital Summit Asia/Sri Lanka next month

From left: Vice Presidents of the Digital Marketing Association of Sri Lanka and the Co-Chairs of Digital Summit Asia 2026 Shalendra Mendis and Gayathri Seneviratne (individual cards), Chairman, Sri Lanka Convention Bureau and Sri Lanka Institute of Tourism and Hotel Management Dheera Hettiarachchi, Chairman, Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau Buddhika Hewawasam, President - Digital Marketing Association of Sri Lanka Arjun Jeger, Nirojan Yogeswaran - Marketing Manager - The Lotus Tower Colombo, Treasurer - Digital Marketing Association of Sri Lanka Muhammed Gazzaly

Digital Marketers Association of Sri Lanka (DMASL) is set to host Digital Summit Asia/Sri Lanka 2026, on 1st and 2nd October at Water’s Edge, Colombo.

Powered by Visa, the Summit will bring together leading voices from across Asia’s digital, marketing, creative, technology and business communities.

Marking a new chapter for the Summit, this year’s event expands its focus beyond Sri Lanka to create a regional platform for ideas, collaboration and exchange, connecting local professionals with creators, innovators, entrepreneurs and industry leaders from across Asia.

Under the theme “The Epicentre”, the 2026 Summit explores how new ideas, perspective sand possibilities emerge when different minds and disciplines come together.

The Summit’s central idea is built around a new epicentre of creativity, technology and intelligence, bringing together the people and communities shaping what comes next.

From creators and entrepreneurs to marketers, technology leaders and business professionals, Digital Summit Asia aims to create new conversations and connections that extend beyond the two-day event.

This year’s international speaker lineup features an impressive mix of regional and global industry leaders, including Dissara Udomdej (Ohm), CEO & Founder of Yell Worldwide; Naved Qureshi, Business Head, Southeast Asia at Voxxy Media; Aneek Ahmed, Founder of The Outwit Company and The Certified Strategic Marketer Platform; Farah Mehdi, Client Partner, CSAA: Consumer Business at TikTok (Byte Dance); Danny Advani, Head of Content & Business at Dot Media; Sundar Swaminathan, Marketing Science Advisor and former Head of Global Brand and International Marketing Science at Uber; Oliver Atkinson, Director of Narrative Strategy at Casual Films; Ovais Ahamed, Founder of Senator We Run Ads; Laurent Thevenet, Head of Creative Tech APAC at Publicis Groupe; and Kerem Gorkem Orhan, Client Partner, South Asia and Turkey at TikTok (ByteDance).

The programme has been designed to move beyond conventional conference formats, with two days of keynotes, interactive sessions, breakout rooms, workshops, roundtable discussions and panel conversations.

The Summit brings together perspectives from across a wide range of industries and digital disciplines, including FinTech, MarTech, travel and tourism, retail, technology, media, advertising, e-commerce, data analytics and AI.

A dedicated set of sessions will also explore the intersection of digital, travel and tourism, creatingan opportunity for professionals from the travel and tourism ecosystem to engage directly with the digital and creative communities shaping how destinations, hospitality brands and experiences connect with audiences.
