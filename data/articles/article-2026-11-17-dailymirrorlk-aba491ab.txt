Aviation experts push for safer skies, better pilot welfare

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Aviation Safety Symposium 2026, themed “A Safer Airspace, a Safer Workplace”, was held yesterday (16) at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) in Colombo under the patronage of Deputy Minister of Ports and Civil Aviation Janitha Ruwan Kodituwakku.

The conference aimed to bring together local and international aviation experts to exchange views on aviation safety, pilot welfare and future aviation operations.

The event was organised by the Airline Pilots' Guild of Sri Lanka, the Association of Airline Pilots Sri Lanka and the 5th Oneworld Cockpit Crew Coalition 3.6 Annual Conference.

The welcome address was delivered by Sri Lanka Pilots' Association President Captain Suchita Mala Wikanka Namge, while Rehan Wanni Appa delivered the keynote address.

Director General and CEO of the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka (CAASL), Captain Daminda Rambukwella, also addressed the gathering.

Representatives of the Sri Lanka Airline Pilots' Association and the Oneworld Cockpit Crew Coalition participated in the event.
