Animals, water board amendment bills passed

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Animals (Amendment) Bill and the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (Amendment) Bill were passed in Parliament today (06).

The Second Reading debate on both Bills was held from 11.45 a.m. to 5.40 p.m. before they were approved by Parliament.

The Animals (Amendment) Bill, presented by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation on July 21, seeks to expand existing legal provisions on animal transportation. Under the amendment, obtaining official permits will become mandatory for transporting pigs, sheep and goats, in addition to cattle and buffaloes.

The amendment aims to strengthen measures to control the spread of zoonotic diseases, which are diseases transmitted from animals to humans.

Meanwhile, the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (Amendment) Bill, presented by the Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply on July 21, seeks to improve the efficiency of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB), enhance water supply service management and broaden the responsibilities of the Board.
