Sarvodaya Development Finance expands regional footprint to improve access to financial services

Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC (SDF), a financial services provider in Sri Lanka, has further strengthened its regional presence with the opening of a new branch in Kantale and the relocation of its existing Dehiattakandiya branch to a new premises. These developments mark another step in the company’s ongoing efforts to enhance customer convenience, improve accessibility to financial services, and strengthen its commitment to serving communities across the country.

The new Kantale branch was officially opened on 15 October 2026 at 10.30 a.m. at No. 88/1/A, Main Street, Kantale. The opening ceremony was attended by the Chairman / Directors, Chief Executive Officer, and members of the management team of Sarvodaya Development Finance. The establishment of this new branch is aimed at providing residents of Kantale and surrounding areas with greater convenience and improved access to financial services, while strengthening the company’s presence in the region.

Meanwhile, as part of its continued focus on enhancing customer convenience and service accessibility, Sarvodaya Development Finance relocated its Dehiattakandiya branch to a new premises on 14 October 2026, commencing operations at 10.00 a.m. Previously located at No. 62/1E, New Town, Dehiattakandiya, the branch now operates from No. 29/G, New Town, Dehiattakandiya. The relocation is intended to provide existing and prospective customers in the area with more convenient access to the company’s financial services.

Through these developments, Sarvodaya Development Finance aims to strengthen its engagement with local communities, including farmers, small and medium-sized entrepreneurs, local businesses, and individual customers in Kantale, Dehiattakandiya, and surrounding areas. By improving access to formal financial services, the company seeks to support the financial needs of these communities and contribute to their economic activities.

Understanding and responding to evolving customer needs remains a key priority for Sarvodaya Development Finance. The company continues to focus on delivering accessible, reliable, and customer-centric financial services through improvements to its branch network and service delivery capabilities. Such initiatives reflect its broader commitment to building stronger relationships with customers while ensuring that financial services remain accessible to communities beyond major urban centres.

The opening of the Kantale branch and the relocation of the Dehiattakandiya branch further demonstrate Sarvodaya Development Finance’s continued commitment to customer-focused service delivery, promoting financial inclusion, and supporting the economic needs of regional communities across Sri Lanka.
