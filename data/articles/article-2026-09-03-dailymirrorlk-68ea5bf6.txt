Public urged to avoid flying kites near high-voltage lines

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The National System Operator (NSO) has urged the public to avoid flying kites underneath or near 220 kV and 132 kV high-voltage transmission lines, towers and grid substations.

The NSO said several transmission line outages have occurred after kites and kite strings became entangled with high-voltage lines.

Such incidents can cause electrical faults and automatically trip transmission lines, while the entangled kites and strings have to be manually removed under controlled and safe conditions.

The NSO warned that these incidents could seriously affect the stability and security of the national power system and may also result in generating units being tripped.

On September 2, incidents involving kites affected power system stability, requiring selected distribution feeders to be switched off as an emergency measure. This resulted in temporary electricity supply interruptions for consumers connected to those feeders.

The NSO called for public cooperation to prevent such incidents and ensure the safe, reliable and uninterrupted operation of the national electricity system.
