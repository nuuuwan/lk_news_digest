CPC inaugurates JET A-1 pipeline and fuel tank project

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) today (Oct. 2) inaugurated its JET A-1 pipeline and fuel tank project at the Muthurajawela Oil Storage Terminal.

The inauguration ceremony was held under the patronage of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

The project is aimed at strengthening the infrastructure for the storage and supply of aviation fuel in Sri Lanka.
