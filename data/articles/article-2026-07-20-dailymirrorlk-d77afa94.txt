LCB Finance opens Wellawattta premier branch

Lanka Credit and Business Finance PLC (LCB Finance), having celebrated the 10th anniversary recently, opened its 25th branch in Wellawatte, which is the third branch of the company in the Colombo district.

The branch was declared open at a strategic location facing the Galle Road, Wellawatta, by Plantations and Agricultural Industries Deputy Minister Pradeep Sundaralingam and Member of Parliament Mujbar Rahuman.

The opening ceremony was graced by distinguished invitees, consisting of reputed   entrepreneurs, potential customers, well-wishers, Chairman Dushmantha Thotawatte and the board of directors of the company.

Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director K.G. Leelananda addressing the gathering stated that the company intends to provide its financial products and services to commercial establishments, businessmen, salaried individuals and the residents in Wellawatta, contributing towards their business, social and communal upliftment.

Leelananda  stated that the company’s deposit  products, consisting of  the ‘Podiththa’ and ‘Sihina Ayojana’ investment savings schemes, fixed deposits and lending products, consisting of   business loans, gold loans, lease finance, housing loans, educational loans  and consumer loan facilities, are designed  to meet the requirements of the prospective trading and businesses enterprises, small or medium enterprises and to cater to the financial services required by the co-operative societies, with specific reference to removal of barriers to financing women’s empowerment-related projects.

He also stated that the company has planned to expand its business operations by enhancing the branch network to 30 outlets by the end of this year, which includes Jaffna as a chosen town to establish a branch.

Thotawatte addressed the gathering, explaining the remarkable expansion of the company during the last decade and invited the public to establish mutually beneficial deposit and finance business relationships with the Wellawatta branch of LCB Finance.
