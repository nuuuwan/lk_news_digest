Singer Sri Lanka joins Dialog Pay ecosystem

From left: Dialog Axiata Group Chief Marketing Officer Lasantha Theverapperuma, Dialog Enterprise Group Chief Officer Navin Pieris, Singer Sri Lanka Director Marketing Janmesh Antony and Singer Sri Lanka CEO Mahesh Wijewardene

Dialog Axiata PLC announced its partnership with Singer Sri Lanka PLC, enabling the customers to make secure and convenient digital payments using Dialog Pay across over 420 Singer showrooms islandwide.

As one of Sri Lanka’s leading retailers of consumer durables, Singer offers a wide portfolio including consumer electronics, home appliances, furniture, digital products, kitchen appliances and agricultural machinery.

With Dialog Pay integrated at Singer billing counters, the customers can simply scan the LankaQR code using the Dialog Pay app or MyDialog app to complete transactions, ensuring a fast, secure and seamless checkout experience across one of the country’s largest retail networks, supported by Singer’s trusted legacy of over 145 years, serving millions of customers annually.

Commenting on the partnership, Singer Group Group Managing Director Mahesh Wijewardene said, “At Singer, customer convenience and innovation remain at the heart of everything we do. Our partnership with Dialog Pay reflects our commitment to providing the customers with secure, seamless and rewarding payment experiences across our nationwide retail network. As digital adoption continues to grow, we are pleased to collaborate with Dialog in making everyday shopping easier while supporting Sri Lanka’s digital transformation.”

Dialog Pay, available through the Dialog Pay app and MyDialog app, brings together connectivity, digital payments and financial services within a single ecosystem, supporting Dialog’s vision of making digital payments simpler, more accessible and more rewarding for every Sri Lankan.

The customers can conveniently activate Dialog Pay using their eZ Cash account or by linking their preferred bank account, enabling them to make secure LankaQR payments, transfer funds, access their digital wallet, open savings accounts and fixed deposits and apply for loans powered by Dialog Finance.

Accepted across more than 100,000 retail outlets nationwide, Dialog Pay rewards eligible QR payments with complimentary Dialog data, encouraging greater everyday adoption while supporting Sri Lanka’s continued transition towards a more digitally connected and cash-light economy.
