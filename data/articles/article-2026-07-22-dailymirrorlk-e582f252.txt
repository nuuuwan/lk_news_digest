FCAM concludes Professional Fund Management course for Kelaniya University

Representatives of First Capital Asset Management and Department of Finance, Faculty of Commerce and Management Studies at the University of Kelaniya.

First Capital Asset Management Limited (FCAM), the wealth management arm of First Capital Holdings PLC recently concluded its Professional Fund Management short course conducted for students of the Department of Finance, Faculty of Commerce and Management Studies at the University of Kelaniya.

The programmewas designed to provide participants with practical insights into fund management and investment principles, bridging academic learning with industry expertise. The initiative concluded with a certification ceremony, recognising the successful completion of the course by the participants.
