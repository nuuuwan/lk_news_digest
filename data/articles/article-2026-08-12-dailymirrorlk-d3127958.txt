No increase in MPs’ basic salaries, Cabinet Spokesman says

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Cabinet Spokesman Minister Nalinda Jayatissa yesterday (11) denied claims that the basic salaries of Members of Parliament have been increased, saying there has been no revision to MPs’ basic pay.

Speaking at the Cabinet press briefing, Jayatissa said the increase that has been reported relates to the fuel allowance, which is calculated according to the applicable formula.

He said the increase in the fuel allowance was not limited to MPs and was applicable to government employees based on the relevant calculations.

The clarification follows claims made by Independent MP Ramanathan Archchuna, who recently shared his monthly salary sheet on Facebook, highlighting what he described as an increase in allowances received by MPs.

According to the document shared by Archchuna, his monthly fuel allowance had increased from Rs. 179,000 to Rs. 244,000.

In a Facebook post accompanying the salary sheet, Archchuna questioned the reported increase and claimed that a similar amount, multiplied across 159 MPs, was being credited to accounts in Pelawatte.

“Keep clapping and suffocate those who tell the truth,” he said, while also referring to remarks he had made in Parliament.
