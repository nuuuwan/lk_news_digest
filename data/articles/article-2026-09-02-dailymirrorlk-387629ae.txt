Janashakthi Finance records 51% increase in 1Q NPAT

Janashakthi Finance PLC has opened the financial year 2026/27 with strong growth across key performance indicators, recording a 51 percent year-on-year increase in Net Profit After Tax (NPAT) to Rs. 90.4 million for the period ended 30th June 2026 (compared to Rs. 59.9 million in 1Q FY 2025/26).

The Company recorded Profit Before Tax (PBT) of Rs. 138.2 million, reflecting a 27.1 percent year-on-year increase (compared to Rs. 108.8 million), while Net Operating Income grew 24.7 percent to Rs. 837.8 million, compared to Rs. 671.6 million recorded during the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

The strong improvement in profitability was supported by continued growth in the Company’s core lending business. Loans and Receivables Portfolio expanded to Rs. 34.5 billion, compared to Rs. 23.8 billion a year earlier, representing a 44.7 percent year-on-year increase.

Meanwhile, the Company maintained a stable deposit base, with deposits reaching Rs. 18.3 billion, compared to Rs. 16.9 billion in the corresponding period of the previous year, recording an 8.1 percent year-on-year increase.

Commenting on the performance, Rajendra Theagarajah, Chairman of Janashakthi Finance PLC, said, “Janashakthi Finance has made a strong start to the new financial year, with much improved growth in profitability and continued expansion of its core business. The 51 percent increase in net profit is a clear indication of the progress being made in strengthening the Company’s financial performance. As we move forward, our priority will be to build on this foundation through disciplined growth, sound governance and a clear focus on creating sustainable value for our customers and shareholders.”

Sithambaram Sri Ganendran, Chief Executive Officer of Janashakthi Finance PLC, said, “Our first-quarter results reflect the progress we are making across the business, with Net Operating Income growing 24.7 percent and Profit Before Tax increasing by 27 percent. The continued expansion of Loans and Receivables to Rs. 34.5 billion also demonstrates the strength of our lending franchise. We remain focused on converting this momentum into sustained growth by deepening our customer relationships, expanding our reach and continuing to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of our operations.”

The first-quarter performance builds on the growth achieved by Janashakthi Finance in recent periods as the Company continues to strengthen its position within Sri Lanka’s non-bank financial institution (NBFI) sector.

As part of JXG (Janashakthi Group), Janashakthi Finance is pursuing a focused growth strategy centred on expanding its lending and deposit franchises, strengthening its customer proposition and enhancing operational capabilities. The Company’s performance during the first quarter provides a positive foundation as it continues to execute its broader growth ambitions.
