Australia blocks international students from enrolling in graduate diploma course

(INDIA TODAY) - Australia has moved to stop new international student enrolments in the Graduate Diploma of Management (Learning), citing concerns over visa misuse, high refusal rates and course non-completion.

The Albanese Government said the decision is the first use of new powers to suspend or cancel courses that raise concerns about the integrity of Australia’s international education and student visa systems.

Providers offering the course, identified by course code BSB80120, will have their Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Student's registration automatically suspended or cancelled.
