RDB Bank participates in Fin Lit Expo 2026

RDB Bank participated in Fin Lit Expo 2026, an exhibition organised by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka on October 8, 2026, at the Centre for Banking Studies, Rajagiriya.

Through its participation, RDB Bank reaffirmed its commitment to promoting financial literacy among schoolchildren, students of technical institutions and the public.

The initiative aimed to raise awareness of the importance of financial education, encourage responsible financial habits and empower individuals to make informed financial decisions.

RDB Bank continues to support initiatives that enhance financial awareness and contribute to building a financially literate society.
