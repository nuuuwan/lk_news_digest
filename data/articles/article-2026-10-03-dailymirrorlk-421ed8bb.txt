Week ends in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Carson Cumberbatch, Commercial Bank and Singer Sri Lanka with the turnover crossing Rs. 873 Mn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. Crossings were witnessed in Standard Capital, Sampath Bank and Lanka IOC, accounting for 42.4% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in LB Finance, Commercial Bank and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Co-Operative Insurance Company, Browns Investments and HNB Finance. Furthermore, foreigners closed as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 1.1% and 1.0% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.09 Bn.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank and National Development Bank) whilst the sector index lost 0.17%. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs 140.00. The share price of National Development Bank closed flat at Rs 109.00.

Real Estate sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Standard Capital) whilst the sector index increased by 0.26%. The share price of Standard Capital increased by Rs 7.80 (21.37%) to close at Rs 44.30.

Lanka IOC and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Lanka IOC moved down by Rs 0.25 (0.19%) to close at Rs 129.00. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka declined by Rs 1.25 (0.81%) to close at Rs 152.50.
