INFRACON 2026 kicks off at BMICH

INFRACON 2026, Sri Lanka’s premier infrastructure and construction exhibition, commenced today (July 3) at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) in Colombo and will continue until July 5. The event brings together leading companies, industry professionals, and decision-makers from across South Asia, showcasing innovations and opportunities in the sector. Organized by Lanka Exhibitions and Conference Services (Private) Limited (LECS) in collaboration with SAVOR International Limited of Bangladesh, the exhibition aims to promote regional cooperation and drive trade, investment, and technological advancement in infrastructure and construction industries. Formerly known as the Construction, Power and Energy Exhibition, it has been rebranded as INFRACON 2026 to reflect its expanded scope and growing regional significance. The event is endorsed by ASHRAE and the Sri Lanka Chapter of the Solar Industry Association (SIA). Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
