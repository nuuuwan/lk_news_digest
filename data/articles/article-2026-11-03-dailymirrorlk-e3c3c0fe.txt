Constituent parties unhappy with SJB leadership, but no decision to withdraw support: Mano

Colombo, November 3 (Daily Mirror) - The constituent parties of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) are unhappy with the way its leadership is handling the Samagi Jana Sandanaya, but there has been no move to withdraw support for the party led by Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa, an ally said.

“The alliance between the SJB and its constituent parties is a virtual alliance. I deliberately refer to it as a ‘Virtual Alliance’ because the SJB leadership has yet to take any meaningful steps to strengthen and consolidate it. Despite repeated calls from several senior figures within the constituent parties, as well as from within the SJB mainstream, for concrete action to reinforce the partnership, the SJB Leader has not taken the necessary steps to move the alliance forward,” Tamil Progressive Alliance (TPA) leader Mano Ganesan told the Daily Mirror.

“Against this backdrop, it is hardly surprising that news reports about SJB allies withdrawing their support for the SJB leadership surface from time to time,” he added.

Ganesan said the TPA was a constituent party of the alliance with the SJB, established under a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed by him as TPA leader, along with its Deputy Leaders Palani Thigambaram and V. Radhakrishnan, SJB Leader Sajith Premadasa and General Secretary Ranjith Madduma Bandara.

However, he said the TPA had not taken any decision to withdraw its support.

Meanwhile, reports have emerged that All Ceylon Makkal Congress (ACMC) leader Rishad Bathiudeen had made critical remarks about the SJB leadership in Kurunegala recently.

However, when contacted by the Daily Mirror, Bathiudeen denied reports circulating in political circles that his party was planning to withdraw its support for the SJB.
