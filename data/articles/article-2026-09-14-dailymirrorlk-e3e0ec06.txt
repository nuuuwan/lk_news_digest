Sathosa Motors achieves ISO 9001:2015 Certification

Sathosa Motors PLC (SML), the authorized distributor for Isuzu vehicles in Sri Lanka, has achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification, marking an important milestone in the company’s continued commitment to quality management, customer satisfaction and operational excellence.

The certification has been awarded by Bureau Veritas Certification Holding SAS – UK Branch, following an audit of Sathosa Motors PLC’s management system. The certification confirms that the company’s management system has been audited and found to comply with the requirements of the internationally recognized ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System standard.

The scope of the certification covers the import and sales of Isuzu brand-new vehicles, sales of Isuzu Genuine Spare Parts, and after-sales services including repair and maintenance.

This achievement reflects Sathosa Motors PLC’s commitment to maintaining structured and consistent processes throughout the customer journey—from the purchase of a new Isuzu vehicle to the supply of genuine spare parts and the provision of professional repair and maintenance services.

More importantly, the certification reinforces SML’s focus on continuously strengthening its systems to deliver dependable products and services while meeting the evolving expectations of customers.

The ISO 9001:2015 accreditation is a multi-site certification, extending beyond the company’s Head Office in Colombo. It encompasses key operations within the SML network, including the Peliyagoda, Vauxhall Street and Kurunegala workshops, together with genuine spare-parts operations at branches in Rathnapura, Badulla, Dambulla, Kandy, Panchikawatta, Matara, Kurunegala and Negombo.
