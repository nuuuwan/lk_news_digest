Cabinet approves drug law reforms to expedite disposal

Colombo, July 28 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved amendments to the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance to enable the speedy disposal of confiscated narcotics and strengthen legal provisions relating to drug offences.

The proposed amendments will establish a legal framework allowing authorities to destroy confiscated narcotics without having to retain them until the conclusion of court proceedings. The new provisions will also apply to future drug seizures.

The move follows Cabinet approval granted on March 16, 2026, to amend the Ordinance based on the recommendations of a committee appointed to review the existing legal framework.

The Cabinet has also approved further amendments to Sections 54(1)(a) and 83(2), as well as the Third Schedule of the Ordinance, to strengthen provisions relating to the production and possession of dangerous drugs on the high seas. The changes take into account the provisions introduced under the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs (Amendment) Act No. 7 of 2026.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved a proposal submitted by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to publish the amendment Bill, already drafted by the Legal Draftsman, in the Government Gazette before presenting it to Parliament for approval.
