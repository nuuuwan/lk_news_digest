Sri Lanka MICE Expo 2026 begins in Colombo

The Sri Lanka MICE Expo 2026 (SL-MICE Expo 2026), organised by the Sri Lanka Convention Bureau (SLCB), officially commenced today at Shangri-La Colombo, bringing together leading international and local stakeholders in the Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions (MICE) tourism sector.

The multi-day Expo features more than 120 hosted international MICE and travel operators, along with media representatives from 26 countries, providing Sri Lanka with an important platform to showcase its potential as a premier global MICE destination.

The event brings together international buyers, travel operators, media and local industry stakeholders to promote Sri Lanka’s MICE products and services while creating opportunities for business development, networking and international collaboration.

Organised under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism, the Expo is expected to further strengthen Sri Lanka’s presence on the global MICE tourism map and promote the country as a competitive destination for international meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions.
