Pakistan, Sri Lanka think tanks sign MoU on strategic research

ISSI and SASSRI to collaborate on regional sustainability, peace and security

The Institute of Strategic Studies Islamabad (ISSI) and the South Asia Sustainability and Security Research Institute (SASSRI) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 28 September, 2026 to deepen academic research and strategic cooperation between Pakistan and Sri Lanka, the High Commission of Pakistan in Colombo said.

The virtual signing ceremony took place in the presence of Pakistan High Commissioner Major General (Retd.) Dr. Nayyar Naseer. Deputy Head of Mission Christy Ruban was present at ISSI in Islamabad.

The MoU establishes a framework for collaborative research, joint academic initiatives, exchange of scholars and shared analysis on regional sustainability, peace and security dynamics in South Asia.

Speaking on the occasion, Dr. Naseer highlighted the significance of the deep-rooted historical and diplomatic relations between Pakistan and Sri Lanka. He emphasised that institutional partnerships between leading research centres will play a pivotal role in addressing contemporary regional challenges through intellectual discourse and research-driven policy input.

The ceremony commenced with opening remarks by ISSI Executive Director Moin ul Haque and SASSRI Executive Director Prof. Maneesha S. Wanasinghe-Pasqual. Both institutes expressed their commitment to leveraging the partnership for impactful research and regular policy dialogues.

Following the formal signing, representatives from both organisations held an interactive discussion outlining prospective joint projects, public seminars and publication collaborations.

The High Commission reaffirmed its commitment to fostering stronger institutional, academic and socio-economic linkages between Pakistan and Sri Lanka.
