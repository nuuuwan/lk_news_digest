Delmege Building Products expands portfolio

From left: Kumudu Muthukumarana (DGM – Delmege Building Products), Amrith Adhihetty (Director – Delmege Forsyth & Co. Ltd), Dinusha Bhaskaran (Managing Director – Vallibel One), Prasantha Senarath (CEO – Delmege Building Products), Charith Fernando (Category Head – Delmege Air Conditioners)

Delmege Building Products officially launched its newest product, Delmege Air Conditioners recently, together with a new range of switches and sockets.

The launch marks a further step in the expansion of Delmege Building Products and reinforces its ambition to build a comprehensive portfolio of quality products and solutions, strengthening its proposition as a trusted one-stop destination for the evolving needs of its customers.

Commenting on the launch, Dinusha Bhaskaran, Managing Director of Vallibel One said, “The expansion of Delmege Building Products represents an important milestone in our journey to build a broader and more integrated offering for our customers. The introduction of air conditioners, switches and sockets is an important part of this journey.

“As we expand our portfolio, we are also focused on making our products more accessible and strengthening the ways in which we engage with and serve our customers across different segments and channels. We believe this broader approach will enable us to deliver greater convenience and value while building a stronger and more meaningful relationship with our customers.”

Commenting on the introduction, Prasantha Senarath, Chief Executive Officer of Delmege Building Products, said, “We have focused on bringing together features that deliver performance, durability, safety and value, while ensuring that our products meet the expectations of today’s customers.

“Equally important is the support that accompanies our products. From installation and servicing to our islandwide after-sales support, we remain committed to giving customers confidence in their purchase and continued peace of mind.”
