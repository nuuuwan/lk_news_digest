Sri Lanka awards Rs756.9mn road project to Asset Engineering

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Cabinet has approved a proposal to award a road improvement contract to cover an additional section of roadway in the Central Province to Asset Engineering (Pvt) Limited, minister Nalinda Jayatissa said.

The contract is valued at about 756.9 million rupees (excluding taxes).

The project will upgrade the road from kilometre 10+500 to kilometre 16+890 on the Norwood-Bogawanthalawa-Campion (B329) route.

Bids were called and 3 bids were received, Jayatissa said. (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
