OMODA & JAECOO records 144,841 global vehicle sales across June and July 2026

Hayleys Mobility, the mobility sector of Hayleys Fentons Limited and the exclusive authorised distributor for OMODA & JAECOO in Sri Lanka, celebrates the brand’s continued global success, with 144,841 vehicles sold worldwide across June and July 2026, reflecting sustained international demand for its mobility portfolio.

Driving this strong global performance was continued demand for New Energy Vehicles (NEVs), which accounted for 112,775 of the vehicles sold across the two-month period. The result reflects the growing appeal of OMODA & JAECOO’s electric, hybrid and intelligent mobility technologies, while reinforcing the brand’s expanding international footprint and alignment with the global transition towards smarter, more efficient mobility.

Commenting on the performance, Hasith Prematillake, Managing Director of Hayleys Fentons Limited, stated:“OMODA & JAECOO’s sustained global performance reflects the growing customer preference for intelligent, efficient and future-ready mobility. As the exclusive authorised distributor in Sri Lanka, Hayleys Mobility is bringing this global innovation to local motorists with the confidence of trusted after-sales support, genuine parts and long-term ownership value. This aligns strongly with Hayleys Fentons’ broader commitment to engineering-led innovation and sustainable progress.”

Roshani Dharmaratne, Executive Director of Hayleys Mobility Limited, added: “The strong performance across June and July, particularly within the NEV segment, demonstrates the pace at which the global automotive landscape is evolving.

Sri Lankan customers are increasingly seeking vehicles that bring together design, performance, safety, efficiency and intelligent technology. Through Hayleys Mobility, we are proud to offer access to this fast-growing global portfolio with the assurance of an authorised local partner that understands the needs of Sri Lankan motorists.”

With OMODA & JAECOO’s expanding global footprint and Hayleys Mobility’s trusted local presence, Sri Lankan customers have access to a new generation of premium electric, hybrid and intelligent vehicles backed by the strength, service reliability and engineering expertise of Hayleys Fentons Limited.
