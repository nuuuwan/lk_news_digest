Teachers, principals protest over module issues

Teachers and principals staged a protest in front of the Education Ministry in Colombo today (4), demanding an immediate solution to issues surrounding the teacher module system. Protesters also called for an end to salary delays, implementation of long-overdue promotions, and the resolution of administrative problems affecting the teaching service. The demonstration was organised by teachers' and principals' trade unions. Pix by Nisal Baduge
