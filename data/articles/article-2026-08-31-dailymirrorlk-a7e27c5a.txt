Ravi Karunanayake arrested after CIABOC questioning

Colombo, August 31 (Daily Mirror) - Former Minister Ravi Karunanayake was arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) following questioning over an ongoing investigation.

Karunanayake arrived at the Bribery Commission at around 8.15 a.m. after being summoned by investigators. He was questioned and his statement was recorded before officials placed him under arrest.

The investigation concerns allegations that five individuals were recruited on a contract basis to the National Lotteries Board outside approved recruitment procedures and were later attached to the former minister’s personal staff.

Investigators said Karunanayake had previously been summoned four times to appear before CIABOC but had failed to do so.

CIABOC officials also visited his residence on Rajamalwatta Road in Battaramulla two days earlier in connection with the investigation. However, Karunanayake was not at home at the time.
