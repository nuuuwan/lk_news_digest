36-year-old dies following gas cylinder explosion at Polonnaruwa Kidney Hospital

Colombo, September 26 (Daily Mirror)- A 36-year-old man has died after sustaining injuries in a gas cylinder explosion at the kitchen of the Polonnaruwa National Nephrology Hospital, police said.

The victim, a resident of the Habarana area, was among seven hospital employees who were receiving treatment at Polonnaruwa Hospital following the incident.

The explosion caused extensive damage to the hospital buildings and other property, according to police.

Police suspect that the blast may have been caused by a gas leak. Polonnaruwa Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
