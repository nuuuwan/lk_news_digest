Italian woman falls to death

Colombo, Sep. 25 (Daily Mirror) - A young italian woman who fell down the elevated region of the Rawanaella waterfall at a height of more than 100 feet yesterday afternoon died on admission to Demodara hospital.

Two young women from Italy had been on a visit to Rawanaella and one of them had fallen to death while when she stood on a boulder at the top of the waterfall. Investigations revealed that she had lost her balance and fallen. The two Italian tourists had been to Sri Lanka a couple of days ago on holiday.

The 26- year- old young woman named Valentino Hipragilto who received serious injuries died on admission to hospital..

OIC Ella Police CI I.K.M.P.Ilangasekara and SI Chandana are conducting further investigations on the instructions of ASP Ruwan Feranando and SSP Mayura Peera.
