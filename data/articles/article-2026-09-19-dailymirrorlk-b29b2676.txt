Rainfall expected to increase in southwestern areas

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - Rainfall in the southwestern parts of the country is expected to increase over the next few days from today (19), the Meteorology Department said.

Showers are expected at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-Western provinces, as well as in the Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara Eliya districts.

The Department said some areas in Sabaragamuwa Province and the Kandy and Nuwara Eliya districts could receive moderate to heavy rainfall exceeding 50 mm.

Showers or thundershowers are also likely in parts of Uva Province and the Mullaitivu, Batticaloa and Ampara districts after 2 p.m.

The public has been advised to take necessary precautions to minimise risks from temporary strong winds and lightning that may accompany thundershowers.
