Govt. has “touched the robe”- don’t go further: Duminda

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Freedom Party (SLFP) General Secretary Duminda Dissanayake urged the Government not to interfere with the robes of Buddhist monks, warning that such actions could undermine Sri Lanka’s Buddhist heritage and civilisation.

Addressing the media, Dissanayake said Sri Lanka was a country with a predominantly Sinhala-Buddhist character and that Buddhist temples and monks had played a central role in the country’s history and society.

“Every Sinhala village has temples and Buddhist monks, while some villages have more than one temple. Therefore, the Government should remember that this is a Buddhist country with a long history and a society centred around Buddhist monks and temples,” he said.

Dissanayake said the Government had now “touched the robe” and urged it not to go further.

“The Government should not get ready to set fire to the robe. You are about to undermine our history, prosperity and civilisation,” he said.

He recalled that even colonial rulers had faced resistance when attempting to challenge the influence of the Buddhist clergy and the robe.

“No one in this world can undermine the robe. Even the Mahanayake Theros have requested the Government to proceed with any court cases without touching the robe,” Dissanayake added.
