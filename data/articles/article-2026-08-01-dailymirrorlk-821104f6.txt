Sri Lanka’s Rumesh Tharanga strikes historic Commonwealth gold in Javelin

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) – Sri Lanka's 23-year-old javelin thrower, Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage, made history by winning the gold medal in the Men's Javelin Throw at the 2026 Commonwealth Games, ending the nation's 20-year wait for a Commonwealth gold medal.

The final, held at Scotstoun Stadium in Scotland and concluded early Saturday (August 1) Sri Lankan time, saw Pathirage produce a remarkable 89.75-metre throw on his second attempt to clinch the title.

The victory marks Sri Lanka's sixth-ever Commonwealth Games gold medal and the country's first gold since weightlifter Chintana Vidanage's triumph in 2006. It also ends a 76-year wait for an athletics gold, with the last coming in 1950, when the legendary Duncan White won the men's 440-yard hurdles in Auckland, New Zealand.

Pathirage overcame a strong field that included India's Olympic champion Neeraj Chopra, who claimed the silver medal with a best throw of 85.83 metres. Fellow Indian Yash Vir Singh secured the bronze medal after recording 85.41 metres.

The historic achievement also lifted Sri Lanka's medal tally at the 2026 Commonwealth Games to two, following Palitha Bandara's silver medal in the Para Discus Throw (F42-44/F61-64) event earlier this week.
