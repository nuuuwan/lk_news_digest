Failure to utilize capital development funds has slowed economic growth: Opposition MP

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The failure of government ministries to utilize funds allocated for capital development in 2026 has slowed Sri Lanka’s economic growth, SLPP MP D. V. Chanaka told Parliament yesterday.

Speaking during the second reading debate on the Budget, he said progress in capital expenditure by the ministries of Health, Transport and Highways, Sports, Justice, Trade and Buddha Sasana had been low.

He said the Ministry of Buddhasasana had spent only six per cent of the total amount allocated for capital expenditure during the first six months of 2026. The Ministry of Justice had also spent only six per cent, while the Ministry of Trade had spent just 0.2 per cent.

The Ministry of Transport had spent only 17 per cent of its capital expenditure allocation, while the Ministry of Agriculture had spent 20 per cent. The Ministry of Sports had spent six per cent and the Ministry of Health 12.7 per cent.

“One can conclude that the government has failed when one considers these facts,” the MP said.

He said economic growth had slowed because of low capital expenditure.

“Sri Lanka’s economic growth is currently 4.2 per cent compared to 5.3 per cent last year. The IMF has predicted that Sri Lanka’s economic growth will be reduced to 3.4 per cent in 2027 while Asian Development Bank has forecasted 4 per cent growth. The 7 per cent economic growth which was predicted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake seems to be unrealistic,” MP Chanaka said.

He also said Sri Lanka had failed to attract an adequate amount of foreign direct investment this year. He recalled that the country had been able to attract foreign direct investment worth Rs. 1,075 million even during the economic crisis in 2022.
