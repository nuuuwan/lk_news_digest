Mahara Prison begins transferring inmates after violent unrest

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Prisons said a number of inmates from Mahara Prison are being transferred to other prisons following the unrest that erupted at the facility yesterday (01).

According to the Department, 20 inmates from Mahara Prison have already been transferred to Boossa Prison as part of the ongoing relocation process.

Prison authorities stated that the transfers are being carried out in the wake of the disturbance, during which several prison facilities were damaged. More inmates are expected to be relocated to other prisons as required.
