Suspected drug trafficker’s Rs. 40 mn assets frozen

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Illegal Assets Investigation Division has obtained a freezing order on four properties valued at approximately Rs. 40 million, allegedly acquired using proceeds from drug trafficking by a suspected drug trafficker in Tangalle.

The assets belong to a Tangalle resident identified as Indika Prasad, alias ‘Lara’.

Investigations were initiated following a complaint received by the Inspector General of Police (IGP). Subsequent inquiries revealed that the funds used to purchase the properties could not be accounted for through the suspect’s known sources of income.

Accordingly, a freezing order was issued under Section 7(1) of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, as amended, and will remain in effect for 14 working days from today (November 17).

The properties covered by the order include a two-storey house built on an 18-perch plot of land in Danketiya, Tangalle, another plot of land with a building in the same area, a 19.20-perch plot with two buildings in Polonmaruwa, and a 37.25-perch plot of land in Walgampitiya.
