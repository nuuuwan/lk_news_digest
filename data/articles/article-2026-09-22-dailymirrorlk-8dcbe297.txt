Cabinet approves €4.93 million contract for 5 million NIC cards

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Cabinet approval has been granted to award a contract for the procurement of five million pre-printed polycarbonate cards required for the issuance of National Identity Cards (NICs).

Approval was initially granted at the Cabinet meeting held on April 7, 2025, to commence the procurement process for pre-printed polycarbonate cards for the Department for Registration of Persons.

Accordingly, bids were invited under the International Competitive Bidding procedure, with eight bids submitted for the procurement.

Following the evaluation of the bids and based on the recommendation of the High-Level Procurement Committee, the Cabinet of Ministers approved a proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Minister of Digital Economy to award the contract to M/s Muhlbauer ID Services GmbH of Germany.

The contract, valued at EUR 4.93 million, will cover the supply of five million pre-printed polycarbonate cards for the issuance of National Identity Cards.
