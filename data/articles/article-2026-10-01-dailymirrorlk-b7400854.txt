Seylan Cards offers 36-month 0% easy payment plan, special discounts

Seylan Bank PLC is offering Seylan credit cardholders greater flexibility when making high-value purchases through its 36-month zero percent easy payment plan, available from October 01 to December 31, 2026, enabling the eligible cardholders to convert purchases ranging from Rs.100,000 to Rs.3 million into a 36-month easy payment plan at zero percent interest, with no processing fee.

Adding further value to the offer, the customers can also enjoy special discounts provided by the participating merchants, allowing them to benefit from savings alongside the convenience of extended payment terms.

The initiative is designed to help the customers manage planned expenditures through convenient monthly instalments, particularly during the year-end shopping and festive season.

The offer is particularly relevant for the customers planning to upgrade household appliances, purchase electronics, enhance their lifestyle or undertake home improvements.

It also provides flexibility for other significant planned expenditures, including travel, education, healthcare, insurance, solar solutions and jewellery, subject to eligibility and applicable terms and conditions.

Seylan Bank Head of Cards Ruchith Liyanage said, “At Seylan Bank, we understand that the customers often plan significant purchases around the year-end season and our 36-month zero percent easy payment plan is designed to give them greater flexibility and convenience in managing these expenses. Whether the customers are upgrading their homes, investing in lifestyle essentials or planning other important purchases, Seylan Cards remains committed to supporting their financial convenience through solutions that put their needs first.”

With the year-end season approaching, Seylan Cards continues to support the customers by providing payment solutions that offer convenience and flexibility for their planned purchases. Through the 36-month zero percent easy payment plan, the eligible cardholders can enjoy their purchases while managing repayments through convenient monthly instalments.

The customers who wish to make use of the offer can make the eligible purchases between October 01 and December 31, 2026 and convert transactions ranging from Rs.100,000 to Rs.3 million into a 36-month easy payment plan.

To convert an eligible transaction, the customers can contact 011 2008888. The customers who do not currently hold a Seylan credit card can also apply by contacting the same hotline.
