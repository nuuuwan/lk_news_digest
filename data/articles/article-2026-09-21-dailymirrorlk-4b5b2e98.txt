Speaker receives Supreme Court determination on 22nd Amendment

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne has received the Supreme Court’s official determination on the proposed 22nd Amendment to the Constitution, according to the Speaker’s Office.

The Speaker is expected to formally communicate the Supreme Court’s determination to Parliament in due course.

The 22nd Constitutional Amendment Bill was presented to Parliament on August 18 by Minister of Justice and National Integration Harshana Nanayakkara, beginning the parliamentary process for the proposed legislation.

Following the Bill’s presentation, several petitions challenging its constitutionality were filed before the Supreme Court. A five-judge bench headed by the Chief Justice considered the petitions and subsequently informed the parties that its determination would be sent directly to the Speaker.

The Committee on Parliamentary Business has scheduled the parliamentary debate on the 22nd Constitutional Amendment Bill for September 24 and 25.
