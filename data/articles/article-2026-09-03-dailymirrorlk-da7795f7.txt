Air Vice Marshal Indika Wickramasinghe assumes duties as KDU Vice Chancellor

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Air Vice Marshal Indika Wickramasinghe assumed duties as the new Vice Chancellor of the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU).

He formally assumed office by signing his first official document amidst the chanting of Pirith by the Maha Sangha.

An accomplished Aeronautical Engineer, Air Vice Marshal Wickramasinghe has held several key engineering appointments in the Sri Lanka Air Force, including Director General General Engineering and Director General Aeronautical Engineering.

The assumption of duties was followed by a guard turnout, a ceremonial parade featuring KDU colours, and a symbolic tree planting ceremony. The event was attended by Deputy Vice Chancellors, Rectors of Campuses, Deans of Faculties, Centre Directors, senior tri-service and administrative officials, as well as officer cadets and day scholars.

Addressing the Deans of KDU faculties later in the morning, the newly appointed Vice Chancellor outlined his vision for the University during his tenure.

He identified improving the overall standards of the University through quality education, strengthening academic excellence and increasing research output as key priorities.

Air Vice Marshal Wickramasinghe called on all members of the University community to extend their fullest support, cooperation and active contribution towards achieving these objectives and further advancing KDU.

He also expressed his gratitude to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake for appointing him to the position and to the Commander of the Air Force for nominating him for the post.

Reflecting on his own academic journey, he acknowledged the Commandants, Commanding Officers, lecturers and instructors of the former Kotelawala Defence Academy (KDA) for guiding and grooming him during his studentship.

Air Vice Marshal Wickramasinghe said the experience and guidance he received during his formative years at KDA played a significant role in preparing him for the responsibility of serving as Vice Chancellor of the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University.
