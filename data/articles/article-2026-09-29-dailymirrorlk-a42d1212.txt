Yields edge up driven by modest selling pressure

Broader investor stance appeared mixed yesterday amid the limited trading volumes and investor participation as the investors await the announcement of the monetary policy decision on September 30, 2026.

However, bouts of selling, some of which stemmed from foreign investors that persisted throughout the session, prompted a modest upward adjustment of the yield curve across the board.

Among the traded maturities, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded from 11.15 percent to 11.30 percent while 01.02.2031 changed hands from 11.25 percent to 11.30 percent.

Further along the yield curve, 15.12.2032 traded at 11.75 percent. In the 2033 segment, 15.01.2033 and 01.11.2033 traded at 11.85 percent and 11.90 percent, respectively.

At the belly end of the curve, 15.06.2034, 15.09.2034 and 15.10.2034 all traded from 11.95 percent to 12.05 percent while 15.06.2035 was seen trading at 12.10 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated marginally against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.26/US dollar, compared to Rs.330.35/US dollar seen earlier.

Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.384.12 billion, compared to Rs.370.92 billion recorded previously.
