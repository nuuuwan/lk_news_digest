A journey through the past

A journey through the past: Visitors explore a unique collection of historical artefacts, antiques, collectibles and treasured pieces at Aavanagam’s “History Deep Down” exhibition, which opened at the Lionel Wendt Art Centre today. The exhibition will run until October 4 and is open to the public from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., with free admission. Pix by Kushan Pathiraja
