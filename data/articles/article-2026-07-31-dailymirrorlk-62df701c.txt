One killed, five injured in wasp attack

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - A 61-year-old tea plantation worker was killed and five others were admitted to the Lindula Regional Hospital following a wasp attack on a group of workers employed at a tea plantation in the Lindula Police Division today.

The injured who were admitted to the Lindula Regional Hospital were transferred to the Nuwara Eliya District General Hospital in critical conditions.
