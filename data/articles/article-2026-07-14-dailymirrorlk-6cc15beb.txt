Divine Marketing celebrates 7 years launching new products

Lalinda Gamage - District Secretary  Gampaha, Rienzie Nanayakkara -  Managing Director and Anthony  Marcus - Director

Divine Marketing (Pvt) Ltd, one of Sri Lanka’s trusted names in the tissue products industry, celebrated its seventh anniversary recently, introducing two innovative new products.

The company has grown into a leading supplier serving households as well as offices, hotels, restaurants, hospitals, and other commercial establishments across the country. Over the past seven years, the company has earned a strong reputation for delivering reliable, premium-quality hygiene solutions.

The new products include a Multi Fold Paper Towel Dispenser System, specifically designed for institutional and commercial use, and ‘Drywella’, a premium range of naturally dried fruits produced using advanced processing technology that preserves the fruits’ natural taste, nutritional value, and freshness. The Drywella range has been developed to meet the growing demand for healthy, high-quality food products in both local and international markets.

The anniversary celebration was graced by Gampaha District Secretary Lalinda Gamage as the Chief Guest, together with business partners, distributors, invited guests, and members of the company’s staff.

Under the leadership of Managing Director Rivenshi Nanayakkara, Director Nimali Nanayakkara, and Director Anthony Marcus, Divine Marketing continues to invest in product innovation, superior quality, and environmentally responsible solutions that respond to evolving customer needs. The company remains committed to introducing internationally compliant products while delivering exceptional value and service.

As Divine Marketing embarks on its next chapter, the company extends its sincere appreciation to its loyal customers, distributors, business partners, and dedicated employees whose trust and commitment have contributed to its success. With a strong focus on innovation and excellence, Divine Marketing looks forward to strengthening its position as a leading Sri Lankan brand while continuing to enhance the lives of consumers through quality products.
