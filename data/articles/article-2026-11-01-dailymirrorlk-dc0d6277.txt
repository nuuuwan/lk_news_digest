Lightning strike damages 11 vehicles at Hambantota Port

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Dailly Mirror) - At least 11 imported motor vehicles were damaged after a lightning strike hit a vehicle storage yard at the Hambantota Port premises.

The incident occurred amid heavy rainfall in the Hambantota area, triggering a fire in the yard where imported vehicles were parked.

The port's firefighting unit responded promptly and managed to extinguish the blaze.

Hambantota Port Police have launched an investigation into the incident.
