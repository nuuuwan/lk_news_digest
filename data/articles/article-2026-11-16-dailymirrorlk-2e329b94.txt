Market momentum cools as ASPI tests 22,000

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to negative territory on 13 November 2026, with both benchmark indices declining as selling pressure spread across several key sectors.

The ASPI fell 133.22 points, or 0.60%, to 22,024.18, while the S&P SL20 declined a steeper 71.85 points, or 1.13%, to 6,293.72. Despite the pullback, the ASPI managed to remain just above the key 22,000 psychological level.

Market turnover stood at LKR 3.23 billion, with 100.75 million shares traded. Capital Goods led activity with approximately LKR 869.21 million in turnover, followed by Materials and Banks. Dipped Products recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 561.76 million.

Crossings accounted for around 13% of total equity turnover, led by Dialog Axiata with LKR 100.00 million, followed by Dipped Products, Hemas Holdings and Sierra Cables.

Sector performance was predominantly negative, with Banks, Capital Goods, Diversified Financials and Real Estate among the weaker segments. Telecommunication Services, Transportation and Energy were among the few sectors to close higher. Foreign investors, however, returned to net buying with an inflow of approximately LKR 35.18 million.

Dipped Products stood out during the session, gaining 3.94% to LKR 73.90 while also recording the market’s highest turnover. Other notable gainers included SMB Finance [X], InsureMe and People’s Insurance.

The broad sector weakness and sharper decline in the S&P SL20 indicate that short-term momentum softened, particularly among larger-cap counters. However, positive foreign flows and strength in selected stocks provided some support, with the ASPI’s ability to hold above 22,000 remaining the key near-term indicator for market direction.
