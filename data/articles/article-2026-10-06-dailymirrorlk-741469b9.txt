Commercial High Court stays resolution to parate execute Ranfer Teas properties

The Commercial High Court of Colombo issued an enjoining order staying the board resolution to parate execute the Ranfer (Pvt.) Ltd, Ranfer Teas (Pvt.) Ltd and Kemulla Developments (Pvt.) Ltd properties until October 14, 2026.

This was when the plaint filed by Ranfer (Pvt.) Ltd was taken up before Commercial High Court Judge Viraj Ranatunge.

Farman Cassim, PC, appearing for the plaintiff, contended that the board resolution of DFCC Bank, authorising the public auction of the properties, purportedly in an attempt to recover the outstanding banking facilities, was flawed in law. It was submitted that unless an enjoining order is issued, the plaintiff would suffer irreparable loss and damage.

Cassim further submitted that each of the mortgage bonds referred to in the impugned resolution constituted a separate security, executed by different combinations of the plaintiff, second defendant and third defendant, in respect of the distinct categories of the assets.

It was contended that DFCC Bank had wrongfully amalgamated the amounts secured under the respective mortgage bonds, thereby rendering the impugned resolution legally defective.

It was further submitted that the failure to identify, in respect of each mortgage bond, the particular facility or facilities secured thereby, the amount enforceable under each bond and the applicable contractual cap, prevented the respective mortgagors from ascertaining the lawful amount required to be tendered to prevent the sale of each property.

Upon considering the submissions, the court issued an enjoining order staying the said board resolution until October 14, 2026.

Farman Cassim, PC, together with Charaka Jayaratne, Nethmi Dilanga and Vinura Kularatne, instructed by Tharushika Fernando, appeared for the plaintiff.
