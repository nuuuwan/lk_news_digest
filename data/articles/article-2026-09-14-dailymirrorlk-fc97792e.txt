Two arrested over Dehiwala hand grenade attack

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Police have arrested two suspects in connection with a hand grenade attack on a residence along Sirisangabo Mawatha in Dehiwala, in which two brothers were killed.

The attack took place on September 11, when an unidentified assailant reportedly threw a hand grenade into the residence.

The explosion claimed the lives of two brothers, aged 11 and 17, who were inside the house at the time.

Their 55-year-old father was also seriously injured in the blast and was rushed to hospital for treatment.
