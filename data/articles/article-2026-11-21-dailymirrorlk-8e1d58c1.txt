Human-elephant conflict action plan to be submitted by December 20

A preliminary draft of the National Action Plan to manage the human-elephant conflict (HEC) is expected to be submitted to the Parliamentary Oversight Committee by December 20, 2026.

The decision was taken at a special progress review and policy discussion held yesterday (20) at Parliament, chaired by Deputy Environment Minister Anton Jayakody.

The proposed action plan aims to address the human-elephant conflict by considering scientific, socio-economic and environmental factors while ensuring the protection of wild elephants and the safety and livelihoods of people affected by the conflict.

The meeting was attended by members of the Parliamentary Oversight Committee, experts serving on the Working Committee responsible for formulating the action plan and officials from relevant state institutions.

Opening the discussion, Deputy Minister Jayakody stressed the need to review the progress made by the Working Committee and establish clear timelines for completing the remaining work.

Working Committee Chairman and Environment Ministry Additional Secretary (Development) D.P.C. Udaya Kumara presented an update on the committee's work, while elephant expert Dr. Prithiviraj Fernando briefed participants on the conceptual framework of the proposed National Action Plan.

The discussion focused on developing practical measures to protect wild elephants while safeguarding public safety and the livelihoods of communities living in areas affected by the conflict.

Proposals included progressively addressing unauthorised human activities within protected areas and improving elephant habitats through environmental conservation and enrichment programmes that provide adequate food and water sources.

The Deputy Minister also presented a detailed update on the Department of Wildlife Conservation's ongoing work on the construction and maintenance of electric elephant fences, the establishment of guard posts and the operation of elephant control units across the country.

It was further decided to convene a meeting with all relevant stakeholders after the preliminary draft is submitted to finalise the National Action Plan.
