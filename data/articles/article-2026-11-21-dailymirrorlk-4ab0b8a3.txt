NASA astronaut shares stunning view of Sri Lanka from space

Colombo, Nov 21 (Daily Mirror): NASA astronaut Dr. Anil Menon has shared a striking photograph of Sri Lanka captured from aboard the International Space Station (ISS), offering a spectacular view of the island from space.

The photograph, shared on his official Instagram account, shows Sri Lanka surrounded by the deep blue waters of the Indian Ocean, with scattered cloud cover and the southern tip of India also visible.

The image, reportedly captured from approximately 400 kilometres above Earth, has attracted attention among Sri Lankan social media users.

Menon, an emergency medicine physician and US Space Force colonel of Indian origin, travelled to the International Space Station in July 2026 aboard the Soyuz MS-29 spacecraft.

He is serving as a flight engineer for Expeditions 74 and 75 during an eight-month mission involving biomedical research, space medicine and upgrades to the orbiting laboratory.

During his stay aboard the ISS, Menon has also been sharing photographs of Earth's landscapes, captured from the station's observation module.
