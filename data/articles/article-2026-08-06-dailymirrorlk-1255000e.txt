Sri Lanka seeks mutual legal assistance from five countries

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has sought mutual legal assistance (MLA) from five countries including Zambia to proceed with investigations into the US $ 2.5 million cyber theft, a Minister said yesterday.

The United States, Australia, the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and Switzerland are the other countries which Sri Lanka has sought assistance from.

Mutual legal assistance is a form of cooperation between different countries for the purpose of collecting and exchanging information. Authorities from one country may also ask for and provide evidence located in one country to assist in criminal investigations or proceedings in another.

Making his remarks in Parliament during a debate on the cyber theft that resulted in the loss of US $ 2.5 million meant be paid to Australia as party of debt servicing, Public Security Minister Ananda Wijepala said the United States intelligence authorities had shared valuable information about the transaction. However, he declined to share further details in this regard, but asserted that investigations were in progress based on that.

The opposition MPs who spoke took the Finance Ministry to task over the crime. Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Ajith P.Perera said the Committee on Public Finance (CoPF) clearly mentioned that the Finance Ministry officials should be held responsible for this mishap.
