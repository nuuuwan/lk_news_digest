OPEC Fund to provide Sri Lanka with $30 million loan for reforms

Sri Lanka is set to receive a US$30 million policy-based loan from the OPEC Fund for International Development to support its economic reforms and recovery programme.

The loan is intended to support the Government's development policy operation, titled "Sri Lanka Reforms for Growth, Resilience and Openness", which aims to restore macroeconomic stability, ensure fiscal sustainability and promote sustainable and inclusive economic growth.

The funding will complement financial assistance provided by the World Bank under its Development Policy Operation.

The US$30 million facility comprises US$24 million from the OPEC Fund's Ordinary Capital Resources and US$6 million from its Special Capital Resources.

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Finance, Planning and Economic Development Minister to obtain the loan.
