Cabinet clears Rs. 5.96 b Oliyamulla pumping station to curb Kelani River flood risk

In a bid to reduce flood risks in the Mudun Ela water catchment, the Cabinet of Ministers has approved Stage I of the Oliyamulla pumping station project at a total estimated cost of Rs. 5,962.13 million, to be implemented from 2027 to 2031, Cabinet Spokesman and Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said.

The canal system of the Mudun Ela catchment connects to the Kelani River at Oliyamulla, Peliyagoda and Pethiyagoda. When the upper regions of the river flood, water enters these urban areas through the canals, exposing low-lying lands in the catchment to flooding.

Studies recommended building a pumping station at Oliyamulla, where the catchment’s main canal meets the river, to minimise the risk.

The project is planned to be implemented with financing from the Government of Sri Lanka and the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), with pumping stations of a combined 35 m³/s capacity. Stage I will deliver a 15 m³/s station, while Stage II will add a 20 m³/s station.

The Cabinet approved proposals submitted by Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake.
