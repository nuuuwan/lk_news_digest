Training aircraft crashes during landing at Ratmalana Airport

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - A light training aircraft piloted by a trainee pilot was involved in an accident while landing at Ratmalana Airport today.

The pilot is reported to have escaped unharmed following a hard landing.

However, the aircraft sustained damage, and the runway at Ratmalana Airport has been temporarily blocked following the accident.

Clearance operations are currently underway to remove the aircraft and restore the runway to normal operations.
