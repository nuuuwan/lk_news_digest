Ma Fierté partners Isle of Rataa  to introduce ‘Duality’

Lifestyle brand Ma Fierté has partnered with Isle of Rataa to present Duality, its latest collection of handcrafted leather and hand-painted artisanal teak pieces.

The collaboration marks a natural meeting of two design-led brands that share an appreciation for quality, individuality and the enduring value of craftsmanship.

Since joining the Isle of Rataa portfolio in March, Ma Fierté has found a distinctive home for its work, with Duality now offering customers an opportunity to experience the collection within a carefully curated retail environment.

“Ma Fierté has been a wonderful brand to work with at Isle of Rataa. Since joining us in March, the partnership has been effortless, with a shared appreciation for thoughtful design, quality and craftsmanship. Their new Duality collection is a beautiful expression of that,” said Nilusha Shivaraj, Co-Founder, Isle of Rataa.

“The interplay of hand painted teak and leather creates pieces that are both distinctive and beautifully considered, with a quiet sophistication that makes them easy to live with. We’re delighted to have Duality in store and to give our customers the opportunity to experience the collection for themselves,” she added.

At the heart of Duality is an exploration of the relationship between utility and artistry. The collection brings together hand-painted teak creations alongside everyday luxury pieces including work-ready laptop sleeves, structured laptop bags and refined leather wallets.

“With Duality, we set out to honor the gap between the everyday essentials we rely on and the statement art pieces that elevate special moments. By pairing hand painted artisanal teak designs alongside everyday luxury items like work-ready laptop sleeves, structured laptop bags, and sleek leather wallets, this collection ensures you never have to choose between utility and craftsmanship,” said SafraAnver, Marketing Director at Ma Fierté.

Every piece is artisan-led and handcrafted by skilled makers within marginalised communities, making the collection as much about people and possibility as it is about design.

“Every single item in this collection is artisan led, handcrafted by talented makers within marginalised communities. Launching Duality at Isle of Rataa is not just about sharing versatile, beautifully constructed leather and teak pieces; it’s about creating sustainable opportunities for the hands that made them,” she added.

The collection reflects Ma Fierté’s wider philosophy of creating lasting, meaningful products through honest craftsmanship and natural materials.
