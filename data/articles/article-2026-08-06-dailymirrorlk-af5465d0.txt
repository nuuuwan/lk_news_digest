Govt committed to arrest wrongdoers

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Hot on the heels of the arrest of politicians implicated in past wrongdoings, Public Security Minister Ananda Wijepala said yesterday that the government is committed to bring all kinds of wrongdoers to book in conformity with the mandate it received at the last elections.

In response to a query whether the government is all out on a political witch-hunt, the Minister told Daily Mirror that the government had dealt with both politicians and others alike as far as corruption and frauds were concerned.

He said the media highlights only the cases involving high profile politicians who served in the past.

“We have not arrested anyone without a basis. We are planning to take officials of five banks to task over the unlawful transfer of money through telegraphic transfers. We do not spare anyone. But, the cases involving politicians only come to the limelight,” he said.

He said that the government is committed to take legal action in this manner.

“It is nothing but implementation of the public mandate. It is one of the main assignments entrusted upon this government. We follow a procedure in making arrests, unlike the past instances where, for example, Sarath Fonseka was arrested,” he said.

The Minister, earlier, said in Parliament that valuable information related to the Easter Sunday bombings had been extracted from former Intelligence Chief Suresh Sallay. Asked why indictments are delayed despite having extracted confessions under the Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA), he said that there are some further investigations pending with the Attorney General.
