Dengue cases surpass 94,000 as death toll reaches 72

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has recorded 94,666 dengue cases and 72 deaths so far this year, according to the latest dengue surveillance data as of midnight on August 26.

The data from the National Dengue Control Unit’s NaDSys surveillance system shows that 9,330 cases were reported during August, while July recorded the highest monthly total so far this year with 29,962 cases.

A total of 21,533 cases were reported in June, followed by 8,590 in May, 5,651 in April, 6,013 in March, 5,721 in February and 7,866 in January.

The Western Province continues to account for the largest share of dengue infections, with 50,098 cases, or 52.92% of the national total.

The Southern Province has recorded 13,811 cases (14.59%), followed by the Central Province with 8,602 cases (9.09%) and Sabaragamuwa Province with 7,770 cases (8.21%).

The North Western Province has reported 4,727 cases, while the Eastern, Uva, North Central and Northern provinces have recorded 3,938, 2,665, 1,687 and 1,368 cases respectively.

At district level, Gampaha remains the worst-affected district, recording 20,170 cases, accounting for 21.31% of the total.

Colombo has recorded 18,775 cases (19.83%), followed by Kandy with 6,892 cases (7.28%), Matara with 6,112 (6.46%), Kalutara with 6,111 (6.46%), Galle with 5,185 (5.48%) and Ratnapura with 5,029 (5.31%).

Kegalle has reported 2,741 cases, Kurunegala 2,674, Hambantota 2,514, Puttalam 2,053, Batticaloa 1,515 and Badulla 1,483 cases.
