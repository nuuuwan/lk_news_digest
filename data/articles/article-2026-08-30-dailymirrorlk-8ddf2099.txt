Sun overhead Sri Lanka’s latitudes until September 7

Colombo, August 30 (Daily Mirror) - The Sun will be directly overhead at various locations in Sri Lanka from August 28 to September 7 due to its apparent southward movement, the Meteorology Department said.

Today (30), the Sun is expected to be directly overhead at Mannar, Periyamadu, Puliyankulam, Welioya and Pulmoddai at around 12.11 p.m.

The phenomenon occurs as the Sun’s apparent position shifts southward across the latitudes of Sri Lanka during this period.
