Lanka IOC, Sinopec raise 95 Octane petrol price to Rs. 515

Colombo, Nov 5 (Daily Mirror) - Lanka IOC and Sinopec have increased the price of a litre of 95 Octane petrol by Rs. 20, bringing the new price to Rs. 515 with effect from last night (4).

Accordingly, the price of a litre of 95 Octane petrol at both fuel providers has increased from Rs. 495 to Rs. 515.

This is the second Rs. 20 increase imposed by the two companies within 10 days. On October 25, Lanka IOC and Sinopec increased the price of 95 Octane petrol from Rs. 475 to Rs. 495 per litre.

Meanwhile, the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) increased its 95 Octane petrol price by Rs. 15 to Rs. 490 per litre with effect from November 1.

Following the latest revision, motorists purchasing 95 Octane petrol from Lanka IOC and Sinopec will pay Rs. 25 more per litre than those purchasing the same fuel grade from CPC.
