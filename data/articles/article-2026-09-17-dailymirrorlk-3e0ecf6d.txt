SLPP member Renuka Perera arrested

Colombo, September 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) has arrested Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) member and former National Transport Commission (NTC) Chairman Renuka Perera over corruption allegations involving bus permits for the Southern Expressway.

According to the CIABOC, the investigation centres on accusations that Perera had illegally issued 56 Southern Expressway passenger service permits to third parties without following statutory procedures outlined in the National Transport Commission Act No. 37 of 1991.

The former chairman is also accused of bypassing recommendations set by a Cabinet decision on September 19, 2014, and failing to collect mandatory annual license fees, resulting in significant unlawful financial losses to the State while conferring improper advantages to private operators.

The suspect will be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court for further proceedings.
