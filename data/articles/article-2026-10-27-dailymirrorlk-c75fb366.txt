Former LTTE member detained for 72 hours after deportation from India

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Terrorism Investigation Division (TID) has detained a former member of the Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam (LTTE), who was deported from India, for 72 hours for questioning.

The 50-year-old suspect, a resident of Masiyapitti, Sandilipay, arrived at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake on the night of October 26 after completing a 10-year prison sentence in India.

He was arrested by Indian security forces in 2015 for allegedly attempting to reorganise the LTTE and was subsequently convicted by an Indian court.

A team of TID officers took him into custody upon his arrival in Sri Lanka. Investigations are being conducted based on intelligence information received about the suspect.
