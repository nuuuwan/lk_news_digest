Brother of underworld figure ‘Loku Patty’ shot dead in Ahungalla

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daily Mirror) - A man identified as the brother of notorious underworld figure ‘Loku Patty’ was shot dead by an unidentified gunman in the Welikanda area of Ahungalla this evening (1), police said.

The victim, Lanthuwahandhi Sugath Nuwan Kumara de Silva, was shot at around 8.10 p.m. using a 9mm pistol, according to police.

Police confirmed that the victim was the brother of the organised criminal figure known as ‘Loku Patty’.
