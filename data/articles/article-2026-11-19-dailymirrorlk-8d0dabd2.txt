CEB suffered Rs. 38,739 Mn net loss last year

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) has suffered a net loss of Rs. 38,739Mn last year, Minister of Power and Energy Anura Karunatilaka told Parliament today.

He said increasing and reducing power tariffs from time to time has largely contributed to the losses.

He refuted the claim by MP Dayasiri Jayasekera that inferior quality coal has contributed to the losses.

MP Jayasekera alleged that all decisions are made by former Minister of Power and Energy Kumara Jayakody, who is running the Ministry, although Karunatilaka is the Minister officially in charge of the subject.

However, Karunatilaka refuted the claim by the MP and said he himself makes all the decisions of the Ministry.
