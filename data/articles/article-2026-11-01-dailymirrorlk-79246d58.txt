CEYPETCO increases Petrol and Diesel prices

Colombo, Nov 01 (Daily MIrror) - The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CEYPETCO) has increased the prices of several fuel types with effect from midnight today (November 1).

Accordingly, the price of Lanka Auto Diesel has been increased by Rs. 5 per litre to Rs. 397, while Lanka Super Diesel 4 Star Euro 4 has gone up by Rs. 15 to Rs. 543.

The price of Lanka Petrol 92 Octane has been increased by Rs. 10 to Rs. 424 per litre, while Lanka Petrol 95 Octane Euro 4 has risen by Rs. 15 to Rs. 490.

However, the price of Lanka Kerosene remains unchanged at Rs. 285 per litre.
