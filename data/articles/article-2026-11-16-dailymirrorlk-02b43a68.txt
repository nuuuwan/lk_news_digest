Cargills Bank-Toyota Lanka sign landmark MoU

In a move that promises ‘A Smarter Way to Drive’, Cargills Bank PLC has announced a strategic partnership with automotive giant Toyota Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd.

The collaboration was cemented through a memorandum of understanding (MoU) officially signed recently, creating a seamless and highly rewarding pathway for the customers to own their dream Toyota vehicle.

This landmark partnership introduces a suite of exclusive financing solutions designed to give Sri Lankan motorists a smarter financial choice when investing in a world-class automobile. The joint initiative allows the prospective Toyota owners to drive home with more benefits, enjoying unprecedented flexibility and cost-saving features.

The prospective buyers can now enjoy repayment periods of up to 10 years on vehicle loans, offering greater flexibility with highly manageable monthly instalments tailored to fit their budgets.

Additionally, the partnership unlocks special interest rates with highly competitive financing options, making Toyota ownership more accessible than ever.

In a major boost for financial freedom, the customers will also benefit from a zero early settlement fee if they choose to pay off their facility after three years, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

Furthermore, the collaboration introduces a Flexi Draft facility, an innovative financial tool designed to give the customers even greater control over their funds and repayment strategies.

Toyota Lanka Chairman Masaaki Kawabata expressed his enthusiasm for the collaboration.

“It is a special day as we sign this MoU with Cargills Bank,” Kawabata stated.

“The Cargills group is a prominent and highly respected organisation and we are very happy to sign this—our first MoU with them. Moving forward, we will try our best to collaborate with the service fund to continually support and uplift our Toyota customers.”

Cargills Bank Managing Director and CEO Senarath Bandara stated, “Collaborating with Toyota Lanka allows us to bring unparalleled value to our customers. By combining their automotive excellence with our special interest rates and flexible financing, we are making the dream of owning a Toyota a reality for more Sri Lankans.”

By combining Toyota Lanka’s legacy of automotive excellence and unparalleled after-sales service with Cargills Bank’s innovative and customer-centric financial solutions, this partnership is set to redefine the vehicle financing landscape in Sri Lanka.
