People’s Bank YES Cash Carnival Draw rewards 25 customers

People’s Bank recently conducted the YES Cash Carnival Draw, providing 25 lucky customers with the opportunity to win Rs.100,000 each.

The draw ceremony was held recently at People’s Tower, Colombo 2.

The promotion was open to the customers who maintained a balance of Rs.50,000 or above in their YES savings account between the period of August 12 and September 30, 2025 and maintained the said balance until December 31, 2025.

The draw ceremony was attended by the senior officials of People’s Bank, including Deputy General Manager Recoveries Naleen Pathiranage, Deputy General Manager Retail Banking A.U.A. Anzar, Assistant General Manager Retail Banking A. Jayaasith and Senior Internal Audit Officer R.M.A.K Rajaguru, together with the other bank officials.
