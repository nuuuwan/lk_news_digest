Low-pressure system to intensify; rainy weather to continue

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The low-pressure area over the Andaman Sea region of the Bay of Bengal, northeast of Sri Lanka, is expected to intensify into a depression within the next 24 hours, the Meteorological Department said.

Although the system is not expected to directly affect Sri Lanka, rainy conditions in the southwestern parts of the country are likely to continue due to the wind flow towards the system.

Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and North Western provinces, as well as in Galle and Matara districts. Heavy rainfall of around 100 mm is possible at some locations in these areas.

Several spells of showers are also expected in the North Central Province and Mannar District, while showers or thundershowers are likely at several places in the Uva Province and Batticaloa and Ampara districts after 2 p.m.

Strong winds of 40–50 kmph are expected at times over the western slopes of the Central Hills, North Central and North Western provinces, and in Trincomalee, Hambantota and Matale districts.

The public has been advised to take adequate precautions against possible damage caused by temporary, localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
