Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Ceylinco Holdings, Sampath Bank and Nations Trust Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.4bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Amana Takaful, John Keells Holdings and Ceylon Grain Elevators.

Mixed interest was observed in Haycarb, People’s Leasing & Finance and Sri Lanka Telecom whilst retail interest was noted in LOLC Finance, Co-Operative Insurance Company and Softlogic Holdings.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers. Insurance sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Amana Takaful) whilst the sector index lost 2.48%.

The share price of Amana Takaful increased by Rs. 1.00 (4.17%) to close at Rs. 25.00. Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index increased by 0.13%. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs. 19.50. Haycarb, People’s Leasing & Finance and Sri Lanka Telecom were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Haycarb moved down by Rs. 5.50 (2.62%) to close at Rs. 204.75. The share price of People’s Leasing & Finance closed flat at Rs. 19.00. The share price of Sri Lanka Telecom appreciated by Rs. 1.30 (1.41%) to close at Rs. 93.80.
