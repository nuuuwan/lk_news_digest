Certain passengers exempt from seat-belt requirement on expressways: Police

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Certain passengers are exempt from the mandatory seat-belt requirement on expressways under the relevant Gazette notification, Police said.

According to Deputy Inspector General of Police W.P.J. Senadheera, the requirement does not apply to young children and passengers who are below four and a half feet in height.

Passengers who have been medically advised not to wear seat belts are also exempt, Senadheera said.

He added that police officers would take such circumstances into consideration when enforcing the requirement and provide the relevant exemptions.

However, Senadheera urged drivers to ensure that children and passengers who are unable to wear seat belts for medical reasons are transported safely, particularly when travelling on expressways.
