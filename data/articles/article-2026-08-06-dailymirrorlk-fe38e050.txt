Rumesh Tharanga becomes world No. 1 in men’s javelin

Colombo, August 06 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan javelin star Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage has achieved a historic milestone by climbing to the No. 1 position in the latest world rankings for the men's javelin throw, becoming the world's top-ranked athlete in the event.

Tharanga's rise to the summit follows a string of outstanding performances on the international stage, culminating in a sensational gold medal-winning display at the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Scotland. The Sri Lankan thrower secured the title with a career-best effort of 89.75 metres, further cementing his place among the world's elite.

Following his Commonwealth Games triumph, Tharanga earned a total of 1,354 ranking points, allowing him to move to the top of the latest world rankings.

Germany's Julian Weber, who previously occupied the top spot, slipped to second place with 1,348 points, while Anderson Peters of Grenada retained third position.
