BOC marks a new era in digital payments with Flex Tap & Pay

Bank of Ceylon (BOC) recently unveiled BOC Flex Tap & Pay, Sri Lanka’s first mobile banking wallet-based tap and pay solution.

Labour Minister and Finance and Planning Deputy Minister Dr. Anil Jayantha Fernando

Bank of Ceylon General Manager and Chief Executive Officer Y.A. Jayathilaka

BOC’s esteemed customer and former Sri Lankan National Cricket Captain Mahela Jayawardena performs the very first BOC Flex Tap & Pay transaction, officially unveiling Sri Lanka’s first mobile wallet with Tap & Pay.

Mastercard Country Manager Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal and Sri Lanka Sandun Hapugoda was among the distinguished participants at the launch of BOC Flex Tap & Pay
