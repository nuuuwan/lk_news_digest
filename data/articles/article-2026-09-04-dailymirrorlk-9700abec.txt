Namal ready for independent investigations, says political future rests with people

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Former Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa said he was prepared to face independent investigations at any time, while expressing concern that political statements made in Parliament were creating doubts about the independence of the police and judiciary.

Namal said some government ministers appeared to have reached conclusions about investigations, arrests and cases before the relevant law enforcement institutions had completed their work.

“Unfortunately, people have now begun questioning the independence of the police and the judicial system in this country,” he said.

He alleged that evidence had been fabricated in cases involving him and said those responsible would eventually have to explain themselves.

Namal also said he was confident that he had not committed any wrongdoing and that there was no reason for him to avoid investigations.

“We are ready to face independent investigations at any time,” he said.

Commenting on a planned event on September 12, Namal ready for independent investigations, says political future rests with peoplesaid a large crowd would attend regardless of whether he was present, claiming that the gathering would be held in support of farmers and the country.

He further said that his political future would not be determined by any political party or symbol, but by the people.

“My political journey is not decided by the compass, but by the people of this country. I will do my politics with the people,” he said.

Namal also referred to remarks made by the President regarding police officers, saying the statement was a message to the Inspector General of Police not to succumb to political influence, power or money.

He maintained that the judicial process should remain fair and expressed confidence that judges and members of the legal profession would continue to uphold justice.

The former MP also rejected suggestions that he was avoiding authorities, saying that he had appeared whenever he had been asked to do so.

“If we are afraid, we will not come. We have told everyone who asked us to come that we would come,” he said.
