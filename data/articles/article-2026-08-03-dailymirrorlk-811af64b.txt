Standard Chartered staff reflect on legacy

The completion of Standard Chartered Sri Lanka’s wealth and retail banking transfer to DFCC Bank prompted an outpouring of reflections from the employees, with many taking to LinkedIn to mark the close of a defining professional chapter.

Among the many messages shared were accounts of long careers with the international lender, relationships forged with clients and colleagues and experiences the employees said they would carry into their new roles at DFCC Bank. Thiranjan Lakmal Liyanaarachchi, who joined Standard Chartered in 2007 and spent 19 years with the bank, described it as “more than just my workplace”.

“It has been a place where I learned to lead, built lifelong relationships, embraced challenges and discovered the true meaning of teamwork and integrity,” he wrote.

Nishani Perera, the last Head of Deposits and Mortgages at Standard Chartered Sri Lanka, described the final phase as “a true roller coaster”, shaped by challenges, uncertainty and resilience. Paying tribute to her colleagues, she wished those joining DFCC success in their new chapter.

Relationship Manager Rajeewa Peiris also reflected on the professional and personal significance of the transition, thanking the clients and colleagues for their trust and support.

“As we transition to DFCC, I carry with me the same commitment to service, integrity and excellence,” he said.

These were among scores of messages shared online as the employees marked the transition.

Standard Chartered will continue its operations in the country, with a sharpened focus on corporate and investment banking.
