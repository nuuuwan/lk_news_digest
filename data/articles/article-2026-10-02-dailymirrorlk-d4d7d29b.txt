First Capital wins 3 CFA Society Sri Lanka Capital Market Awards

First Capital Holdings team with awards at CFA Society Sri Lanka Capital Market Awards 2026

First Capital Holdings PLC recently secured three awards at CFA Society Sri Lanka Capital Market Awards 2026, recognising the company’s contribution across equity research and unit trust fund management.

The awards included a Joint Silver award for Best Stockbroking Research Team for First Capital Research, which was the highest accolade presented in the category. First Capital Research also received a Bronze award for Best Sector Research Report for the report on tourism by Salaama Sanoon, while the First Capital Money Market Fund received a Bronze award for Best Unit Trust Fund.

The awards were presented at a ceremony held at Cinnamon Grand Colombo.

The CFA Society Sri Lanka Capital Market Awards recognise excellence across key areas of Sri Lanka’s capital markets, including equity research, investor relations and unit trust fund management.

First Capital Asset Management Limited CEO and Non-Independent Executive Director Kavin Karunamoorthy said, “These awards reflect the quality of the work and expertise across our research and fund management teams. They validate and reinforce our focus on delivering meaningful market insights and investment solutions that support informed decision-making.”

First Capital Holdings PLC Dimantha Mathew Chief Research and Strategy Officer added, “The recognition for our research reflects the team’s continued focus on producing relevant, independent and insightful analysis in a changing market environment. We remain committed to maintaining high professional standards and contributing to the development of Sri Lanka’s capital markets.”

The recognition further strengthens First Capital’s commitment to delivering high-quality research and investment solutions while contributing to the continued growth and development of Sri Lanka’s capital markets.
