No special treatment for Gnanasara thera in prison: Government

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The prison authorities have acted in accordance with the Prisons Ordinance in relation to Ven. Galagodaatte Gnanasara Thera, and no special treatment will be given to him, Cabinet Spokesman and Minister Nalinda Jayatissa said today.

Speaking at the weekly briefing to announce Cabinet decisions, Jayatissa said 63 Buddhist monks are currently serving prison sentences and that Gnanasara Thera is being treated in the same manner as the others.

The Minister made these remarks in response to questions about concerns raised by various parties regarding the prison attire provided to the monk.

Jayatissa reiterated that prison authorities were following the relevant regulations and that Gnanasara Thera would not receive any special privileges while in custody.
