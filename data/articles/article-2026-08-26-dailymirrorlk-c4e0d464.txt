Man declared dead in custody turns up alive at his own funeral house

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - An unusual incident has been reported from Kohobankulama in Galgamuwa after a man who was officially reported dead while in prison unexpectedly returned home.

The man, identified as D. M. Keerthisena, had reportedly been in custody at Wariyapola Prison when his relatives were informed by Nanneriya Police that he had passed away.

Believing the police notification, his family and villagers began making arrangements for the funeral. Funeral banners were put up in the village and other preparations were made to conduct the final rites.

However, the situation took a dramatic turn when Keerthisena himself arrived at the house where preparations were being made for his funeral.

His sudden appearance left his relatives and villagers shocked, with the incident creating considerable confusion and commotion in Kohobankulama.
