Lightning warning issued for several areas

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - An amber advisory for severe lightning has been issued for the Uva and Eastern provinces and the Mullaittivu, Vavuniya and Polonnaruwa districts, with the warning remaining valid until 11.30 p.m. today (20).

The Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre issued the advisory at 11.00 a.m., warning that thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are likely at several places in the affected areas.

Temporary, localised strong winds may also occur during thunderstorms, the advisory said.

The Department of Meteorology urged the public to take adequate precautions to minimise the risk of damage and injury caused by lightning.

People have been advised to seek shelter indoors and avoid standing under trees during thunderstorms. The public should also avoid open areas such as paddy fields, tea plantations and open water bodies.

The Department further advised people to avoid using wired telephones and electrical appliances connected to power sources during thunderstorms.

People using open vehicles such as bicycles, tractors and boats have also been urged to take extra precautions and avoid such exposure during thunderstorms.

The public has been warned to remain alert for fallen trees and power lines following thunderstorms.

Those requiring emergency assistance have been advised to contact local disaster management authorities.
