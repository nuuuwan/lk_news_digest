Herd of over 100 elephants terrorises Mahiyangana villages - MP

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - A herd of more than 100 wild elephants has been terrorising villages in the Mahiyangana area, destroying crops, damaging houses and property, and posing a growing threat to residents, NPP Parliamentarian Sudath Balagalla told a District Coordinating Committee meeting yesterday.

He said the situation had become so serious that additional classes at Girandurukotte Maha Vidyalaya were suspended after elephants invaded the school grounds.

“I receive telephone calls even late at night. On Tuesday night, the principal of Girandurukotte Maha Vidyalaya called me to say that wild elephants had raided the school grounds. Fearing for the safety of the children, he suspended the additional classes held after school,” Balagalla said.

He said the herd had continued to wreak havoc on cultivated land and houses, while residents remained concerned about the danger posed by the elephants.

Balagalla also noted that the human-elephant conflict threatened the lives of wild elephants, adding that the animals were moving into villages from the Maduru Oya, Randenigala, Ratugala and Wasgamuwa forest reserves due to inadequate electric fencing.

The issue was raised at the meeting chaired by Minister Samantha Vidyaratne and Uva Province Governor Kapila Jayasekara.

Vidyaratne said similar threats from wild elephants had been reported in Girandurukotte, Rideemaliyedda, Haldummulla, Koslanda and Bandarawela.

He also cited the recent deaths of seven wild elephants in the Anerve in Haldummulla and the death of a young woman following an elephant attack at Uda Diyaluma.
