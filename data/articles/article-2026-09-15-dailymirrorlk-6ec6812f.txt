Ex-Transport Authority chairman arrested over Rs. 4.1 million loss

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) arrested the former Chairman of the Western Province Road Passenger Transport Authority, Opatha Vithanage Prasanna Sanjeewa, earlier today.

According to the CIABOC, the arrest was made following an investigation into allegations of corruption during his tenure.

Investigators allege that Sanjeewa acted in violation of the Western Province Road Passenger Transport Authority Act by bypassing standard tender protocols to issue passenger transport permits.

The irregular procedure reportedly resulted in a financial loss of Rs 4,160,000 to the state while conferring an unlawful advantage to external parties.
