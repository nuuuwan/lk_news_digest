Navy builds Sri Lanka’s first waveless referee boat

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has designed and built the country’s first waveless referee boat for rowing and canoeing competitions at the Navy Water Sports Centre in Diyawanna, Battaramulla.

The Naval Boat Building Yard (NBBY) constructed the boat and delivered it to the centre in April 2026. The locally built catamaran is now in operational use during water sports events.

The project is part of the Navy’s Strategic Plan 2030, known as NAVSTRAT-2030, which focuses on self-reliance, technological development and strengthening local engineering capabilities.

Designed and approved by the Directorate of Naval Design in accordance with naval engineering standards, the vessel successfully completed performance trials before being deployed.

The high-stability catamaran has been specifically designed to minimise wake generation while operating alongside competitors. This is important in rowing events, where surface waves can affect athletes’ safety, timing and the fairness of races.

The vessel, bearing hull number NC/WL/001, is 5.53 metres long and 2.30 metres wide, with a draft of 0.16 metres and a total weight of 0.45 tonnes. It is powered by a 25-horsepower petrol outboard engine and can accommodate up to two people.
