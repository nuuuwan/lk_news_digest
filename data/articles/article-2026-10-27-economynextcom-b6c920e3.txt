Sri Lanka, FIFA discuss $25mn football development plan

ECONOMYNEXT – World football governing body FIFA plans to provide 25 million US dollars in funding to help make football Sri Lanka’s most popular sport within four years, Football Sri Lanka said in a statement on social media.

The ambitious national football development plan was discussed on October 26 at a meeting in Baku, Azerbaijan, between FIFA President Gianni Infantino, Sri Lanka’s Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports Sunil Kumara Gamage, and Football Federation of Sri Lanka (FFSL) President Jaswar Umar.

The proposed 25 million dollar funding support over the next four-year cycle will go toward national and youth teams, women’s football, and the training of coaches and referees.

“The government appreciates FIFA’s valuable support and reaffirms its full commitment to working closely with FIFA and the FFSL to advance the sport,” Sports Minister Sunil Kumara Gamage said.

Key infrastructure projects under the programme include plans to build a state-of-the-art international football stadium in the country. Development initiatives will also begin next year to establish football grounds in every district, bringing the game closer to communities nationwide.

In addition, the FFSL plans to develop approximately 40 football grounds across its member leagues by 2031.

“FIFA reiterates its firm commitment to advancing Sri Lankan football and working together to build a strong future for the sport,” FIFA President Gianni Infantino said.

Under the joint vision, FIFA will provide technical expertise and infrastructure support to the Ministry of Sports, in partnership with the FFSL, to establish a National High-Performance Centre. The facility is intended to strengthen player development, coaching standards, and clear training pathways for elite athletes across all sports.

The discussions represent an important step toward a coordinated national effort to upgrade local infrastructure, expand participation, and create greater opportunities across the football community. (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
