Mirror Missions: El Niño in Sri Lanka, Can You Protect Your Community?

What could El Niño mean for Sri Lanka and the people in your community?

Its effects can include dry weather, water shortages, extreme heat, pressure on crops and food prices, followed by periods of heavy rain, flooding and landslide risks.

Created by Daily Mirror with the support of artificial intelligence and editorial oversight, this interactive Mirror Missions challenge presents six realistic Sri Lankan scenarios that test how you would respond as conditions change.

Your result shows how decisions made before and during an El Niño period can affect water security, public health, household resilience and community readiness.

This AI-assisted Mirror Missions experience was developed to turn complex climate information into an engaging public learning challenge.

El Niño is a natural climate cycle, while climate change can intensify heat, rainfall and other weather extremes. Sri Lanka cannot prevent these risks, but better information, early preparation and coordinated community action can reduce their impact.
