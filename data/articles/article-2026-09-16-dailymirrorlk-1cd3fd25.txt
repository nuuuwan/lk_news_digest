Did Shiranthi Rajapaksa leave country after FCID summons?

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Shiranthi Rajapaksa, wife of former President Mahinda Rajapaksa left the country earlier today despite being summoned to appear before the Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID).

A member of the Rajapaksa family said Shiranthi Rajapaksa had travelled directly to the airport from a private hospital and had not previously received any summons from the FCID requesting her to appear and provide a statement.

According to the family member, the relevant letter was handed over to Shiranthi Rajapaksa by an airport official near the departure gate, shortly before her scheduled flight was due to leave at 12.50 a.m. today (16).

The family member further claimed that the notification was issued and handed over only after she had arrived at the airport, at the time she was preparing to leave the country.

The family member therefore rejected reports suggesting that Shiranthi Rajapaksa had knowingly left the country while under an earlier FCID summons, saying she had not received any such summons before arriving at the airport.
