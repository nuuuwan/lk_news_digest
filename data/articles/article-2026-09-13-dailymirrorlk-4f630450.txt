Air force officer, soldiers arrested over deer hunting at Mattala Airport

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Five individuals, including an officer and soldiers from the Mattala Air Force camp, along with two civilians, have been arrested for allegedly shooting deer roaming inside the Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport grounds and selling the meat to civilians.

The meat and skull of a shot deer have been taken into custody.

A firearm and knife used for the shooting, an Air Force jeep used to transport the venison, and the motorcycle ridden by the officer have also been taken into police custody.

Police investigations have revealed that deer on the grounds of the Mattala Airport were shot and killed, brought to a bathroom located inside the Air Force camp premises to be processed for meat, and the meat was then supplied to two civilians waiting outside the electric fence of the airport premises.
