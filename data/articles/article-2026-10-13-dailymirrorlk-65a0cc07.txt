Euro Motors expands automotive dealer network to support customers

Euro Motors leadership team with island-wide dealer and service partners

Euro Motors is strengthening its island-wide automotive ecosystem with the formal appointment and recognition of its dealership and Authorised Service Provider (ASP) partners at a special ceremony recently held at Hilton Colombo.

Held under the theme ‘Beyond the Drive,’ the event marks an important step in Euro Motors’ continued network development, bringing together partners from across Sri Lanka to make vehicle sales, servicing, repairs and after-sales support more accessible to customers in their respective Home Towns.

As part of the network expansion, Euro Motors has appointed three categories of authorised partners: Display Points, 3S Dealers offering Sales, Spare Parts and Service, and dedicated Service Partners.

Together, these partners are to provide customers with greater convenience and access to the services they need, enabling them to visit an authorised Euro Motors partner in their area for vehicle purchases, servicing, repairs, genuine spare parts and related after-sales requirements.

Ravindra Senarathna, Chief Operations Officer at Euro Motors said, “Our ambition is not simply to expand our vehicle portfolio, but to strengthen the ecosystem that supports our customers throughout their ownership journey. By appointing Display Points, 3S Dealers and Service Partners across the island, we are making it more convenient for customers to access our vehicles and the After Sales support they need, closer to where they live and operate.”

The authorised network also plays an important role in supporting customers in maintaining their vehicles in accordance with the required service schedules. Customers are required to carry out their regular services through Euro Motors’ appointed authorised dealers island wide to remain eligible for warranty claims, subject to the applicable warranty terms and conditions.

For customers, the growing network is particularly important as vehicle ownership extends beyond the initial purchase. Whether purchasing a new vehicle, maintaining a passenger vehicle, operating an EV or managing a commercial fleet, customers can now access authorised Euro Motors support through partners in their respective Home Towns.

For commercial-vehicle operators in particular, dependable after-sales support can be important in helping to minimise downtime and to keep businesses moving.
