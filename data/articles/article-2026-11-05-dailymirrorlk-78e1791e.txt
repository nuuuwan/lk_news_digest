Police turn to public to trace suspect over online harassment

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Samanthurai Police have sought public assistance to locate and arrest a 47-year-old suspect wanted in connection with several complaints of sexual harassment allegedly committed through social media platforms, including Facebook and WhatsApp.

According to the Police, the suspect, identified as Ahamed Lebbe Mohamed Aslam, is believed to have fled the area, and his current whereabouts remain unknown.

The police have released photographs of the suspect and requested the public to provide any information that could help establish his whereabouts and facilitate his arrest.

Anyone with information is requested to contact the Officer-in-Charge of the Samanthurai Police Station on 071-8591162 or the Samanthurai Police Station on 067-2260222.
