World Odyssey arrives with 524 students, faculty

The Bahamas-flagged passenger vessel World Odyssey yesterday arrived at the Port of Colombo with 524 students and faculty members and 181 crew members, the Ports and Civil Aviation Ministry said.

The vessel is scheduled to remain anchored at the Colombo Port until 23 November, 2026.

According to the Ministry, most of the students left on excursions to Kandy and popular tourist destinations in the Southern Province, while several other groups visited locations within Colombo. Visits to several Sri Lankan universities have also been arranged for student groups during their stay.

The Ministry said academic activities continue on board while the vessel is at sea, with lecturers conducting classes and students pursuing their educational programs during the voyage. All passengers entered Sri Lanka on tourist visas, generating foreign exchange earnings for the country, the Ministry added.
