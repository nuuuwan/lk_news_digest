CSE rally continues as broad buying pushes ASPI toward 21,400

The Colombo Stock Exchange extended its positive momentum for a third consecutive session on Friday, 7 August 2026, as broad-based buying interest pushed both benchmark indices higher and strengthened overall market sentiment.

The All Share Price Index gained 100.42 points, or 0.47%, to close at 21,370.09, while the S&P Sri Lanka 20 Index advanced by 16.66 points, or 0.28%, to end the session at 5,988.16.

The ASPI maintained a steady upward trajectory through most of the trading session and moved close to the 21,400 level before easing slightly towards the close. The S&P SL20 also remained firmly in positive territory, supported by buying interest across several large-cap counters.

Market activity moderated significantly from the exceptionally high turnover recorded in the previous session. Total market turnover stood at LKR 1.89 billion, with approximately 122.29 million shares changing hands.

The Banks sector dominated market turnover, generating LKR 597.45 million from 68.84 million shares. CBNK.N accounted for the majority of sector activity, recording LKR 476.45 million in turnover from approximately 67.96 million shares.

Crossing transactions remained relatively limited compared with recent sessions, contributing approximately LKR 81.87 million, or around 4% of total equity turnover. The largest crossing was recorded in CCS.N, with a turnover of LKR 38.14 million involving approximately 0.30 million shares.

Foreign investor activity remained slightly negative, with the market recording a marginal net foreign outflow of LKR 15.83 million.

Market breadth was strongly positive, with 141 securities advancing against 62 decliners. The wide advance-to-decline spread reflected improving investor confidence and buying interest across a broader range of counters.

Central Finance emerged as the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by John Keells Holdings, RIL Property, Colombo Dockyard and Richard Pieris.

Hemas Holdings recorded the largest negative contribution to the index, while Ceylon Grain Elevators, CBNK.N, Haycarb and Citizens Development Business Finance also weighed on the market.

Among notable trades, CBNK.N recorded the highest individual counter turnover of LKR 476.45 million from approximately 67.96 million shares. Despite the strong trading activity, the counter declined by 6.67% during the session.

Overall, the third consecutive market gain, together with strongly positive market breadth, pointed to improving investor sentiment and broader participation. However, the sharp moderation in turnover compared with the previous session highlighted a return to more normalized trading activity.
