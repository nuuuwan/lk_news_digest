HNB, KDU join hands to expand higher education financing opportunities

Hatton National Bank PLC (HNB) has entered into a strategic partnership with General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU) to enhance access to higher education financing for eligible students.

The collaboration, formalised through a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), will enable students enrolling at KDU to obtain tailored education loan facilities, providing greater financial flexibility to pursue their academic goals.

Under the agreement, HNB will offer education loans with a grace period incorporated into the repayment structure, allowing borrowers to pay only the applicable interest during the initial phase of their studies. The arrangement is expected to ease the financial pressure on students and their families while they focus on completing their higher education.

HNB said the initiative reflects its commitment to promoting financial inclusion, supporting educational advancement and contributing to the development of skilled human resources in Sri Lanka.

Through the partnership, the bank aims to support students in accessing quality education by providing sustainable financial solutions.

Students seeking details of the loan facilities available under the programme can obtain information through HNB branches or KDU.
