LRH celebrates World Children’s Day

Health Minister Nalinda Jayatissa, chief guest at the World Children’s Day celebration organised by the Lady Ridgeway Hospital (LRH) in Colombo, joins children and hospital staff during the event. Organised by LRH staff for the 15th consecutive year, the celebration included children undergoing in-house treatment. LRH Director Dr. Pradeep Ratnasekare and other senior health officials were also present. Pix by Waruna Wanniarachchi
