Browns Hotels & Resorts wins multiple national accolades

Calm Resort & Spa and Dickwella Resort & Spa receive recognition at Presidential Environment Awards and National Cleaner Production Awards 2026

Browns Hotels & Resorts has earned national recognition at two prominent award ceremonies in 2026 for its continued commitment to environmental responsibility and sustainable hospitality.

Two of its coastal properties, The Calm Resort & Spa in Pasikudah and Dickwella Resort & Spa, received accolades at Presidential Environment Awards and National Cleaner Production Awards 2026.

At Presidential Environment Awards 2026, The Calm Resort & Spa received a Bronze award, while Dickwella Resort & Spa was presented with a Merit award in the Industry Sector – Hotels Type A & B Category.

Further strengthening this achievement, both properties were also recognised at National Cleaner Production Awards 2026 in the Service Sector – Hotel Medium Category, acknowledging their efforts towards adopting cleaner production practices and integrating greater environmental responsibility into their operations.

Together, these recognitions highlight the practical measures undertaken at both properties to minimise their environmental footprint while maintaining responsible hospitality operations. Initiatives spanning energy efficiency, responsible water consumption, waste management, coastal conservation and community engagement form part of their ongoing efforts to operate more sustainably.

The achievements at both properties reflect the leadership and commitment of The Calm Resort & Spa General Manager Sujeewa Basnayake and Dickwella Resort & Spa General Manager Sisira Senaratne, together with their respective teams.

Browns Hotels & Resorts CEO Eksath Wijeratne said, “For Browns Hotels & Resorts, sustainability is an ongoing journey, shaped by a shared responsibility to create positive change today while safeguarding the destinations and communities that future generations will experience. While these national recognitions are a proud milestone for our group, what matters most is the progress they represent.”

These property-level initiatives are also part of a broader sustainability vision taking shape across Browns Hotels & Resorts through BIGG CARE, the group’s sustainability framework built around green governance, climate action, accountability, resource efficiency and engagement. Recently, the Group took another significant step in formalising this commitment with the launch of its Sustainability Programme and Policy, establishing a more structured approach to embedding responsible practices across its operations.

Browns Hotels & Resorts Assistant Manager Sustainability and CSR Prabuddha Siriwardhana said, “Sustainability becomes meaningful when commitments translate into everyday practices. Through BIGG CARE, we are working towards bringing greater accountability and consistency to our sustainability efforts across the group. Our focus now is to build on this momentum and continue driving measurable improvements across our operations.”
