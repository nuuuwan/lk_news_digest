Cabinet greenlights visa-free travel agreement between Sri Lanka and Thailand

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal to enter into a visa exemption agreement between Sri Lanka and Thailand for holders of ordinary passports.

The agreement aims to further strengthen friendly relations between the two countries while facilitating travel between Sri Lanka and Thailand, according to the Cabinet decision.

Under the proposed agreement, citizens of both countries holding valid ordinary passports will be permitted to enter, depart from and transit through the respective countries without obtaining a visa in advance for general tourism purposes.

The visa exemption will allow a single stay of up to 30 days, with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days within a 180-day period.

However, the exemption will not apply to employment, education, media-related activities or any other activities that require prior approval from the relevant authorities.

The Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs to sign the agreement between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Thailand.
