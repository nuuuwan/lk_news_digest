Ceylon Green Life Plantation launches Ceylon ECO Resort Project

Taking a distinctive step toward strengthening Sri Lanka’s green economy, Ceylon Green Life Plantation (CGLP) has officially launched the Ceylon ECO Resort integrated development project. Scheduled to be built on a 90-acre land area in the Panamura region of Embilipitiya, this project represents a novel development model that brings together Sri Lanka’s agriculture, tourism, education, and biodiversity conservation on a single platform. Currently maintaining over 400 acres of cultivated land, Ceylon Green Life Plantation has already played a role in popularizing greenhouse cultivation in Sri Lanka and introducing modern agricultural technology to farmers. Through greenhouse construction, technical advisory services, and the promotion of high-yield cultivation methods, the company has successfully brought about a new transformation in local agriculture.

Under this new project, plans are in place to implement numerous agricultural development programs, including an agricultural science faculty in Sri Lanka, an internationally renowned tourist resort, greenhouse cultivation zones, and cinnamon, coconut, and pineapple cultivation projects. The company’s aspiration is to create employment opportunities, attract investments, and strengthen the rural economy through these initiatives. The founder and chairman of the group, Dr. Malan Francis Peters, stated that CGLP will continue to operate as a trusted enterprise contributing to the national economy through sustainable agriculture and innovation.
