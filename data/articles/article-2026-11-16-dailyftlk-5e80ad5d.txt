Sri Lanka strengthens presence at Automechanika Dubai 2026

Automechanika Dubai 2026 successfully concluded at the Dubai World Trade Centre, bringing together leading global manufacturers, innovators and automotive industry professionals.

The event reinforced its position as a key international platform for the automotive aftermarket industry.

Held from 10–12 November, the 23rd edition featured over 1,300 exhibitors from over 50 countries, creating valuable opportunities for business networking, industry exchange and international collaboration across the automotive parts, accessories and services sectors.

Three Sri Lankan companies – Associated Motorways, Shamini Group of Companies and Thimark Technocreations – participated in the exhibition under the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB) Pavilion.

The participating companies presented their automotive products to an international audience, highlighting Sri Lanka’s potential in the automotive and engineering sectors. Their participation received positive feedback from international visitors and industry professionals, creating opportunities for business networking and future collaboration.

Sri Lanka’s participation provided an opportunity for local companies to showcase their capabilities, connect with international industry players and explore potential business partnerships and export opportunities.
