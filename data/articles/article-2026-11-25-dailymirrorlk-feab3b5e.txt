CSE rebounds as ASPI regains 21,300

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to positive territory on 25 November 2026, with both benchmark indices closing higher as buying interest improved during the first half of the session.

The ASPI gained 73.32 points, or 0.35%, to close at 21,315.84, while the S&P SL20 advanced 16.66 points, or 0.28%, to 6,062.83. The ASPI moved close to 21,400 during the morning before profit-taking emerged later in the session and trimmed part of the gains.

Market turnover remained subdued at LKR 1.45 billion, with 53.91 million shares traded. The Banks sector led activity with approximately LKR 541.05 million in turnover, while HDFC recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 281.98 million.

Crossings accounted for around 25% of total equity turnover, led by HDFC with approximately LKR 221.10 million. Commercial Bank, PickMe, Amana Bank and Softlogic Life also recorded notable block transactions.

Market breadth improved considerably, with 117 gainers against 76 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 1.54. Foreign investors, however, remained net sellers with an outflow of approximately LKR 138.43 million.

Distilleries was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by Piramal Glass, LOLC Holdings, Access Engineering and ACL Cables. Hatton National Bank recorded the largest negative contribution.

The positive close and improved breadth indicate a modest recovery in short-term sentiment. However, subdued turnover, continued foreign selling and the late-session pullback suggest buying conviction remains selective, with the 21,300 level now the immediate area to watch and a sustained move above 21,400 needed to strengthen the recovery.
