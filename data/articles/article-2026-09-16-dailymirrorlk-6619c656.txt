’Positive sign for Indian investments in Colombo Port City’

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Positive talks between Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Chinese President Xi Jinping during the BRICS summit could make a positive symbolic gesture to ease concerns surrounding the Colombo Port City and create a more favourable environment to attract Indian investment, according to CHEC Port City Colombo Deputy Managing Director, Thulci Aluwihare.

In an interview with the Daily Mirror, Aluwihare, said the meeting was “symbolic somewhat”, but added that the Chinese President’s visit to India was an indication that the two countries could now engage in more business.

He said such developments could help the Port City to address concerns among Indian investors over its association with a Chinese project company.

“There are some geopolitical headwinds, but we can address that factually, saying this is part of Colombo or the Government of Sri Lanka,” Aluwihare said.

He stressed that the Port City land remained under the ownership of the Government of Sri Lanka, while the Chinese company was the developer and investor.

The comments come as the Port City seeks to attract more Indian companies, particularly those operating global capability centres and services businesses, with the project positioning itself as a regional services hub.
