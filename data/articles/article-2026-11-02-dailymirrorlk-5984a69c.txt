Independent probes on police brutality a must!

Two cases of police brutality, one in Homagama and another in Kobeigane where two suspects taken into custody died under mysterious circumstances have raised renewed concerns regarding the conduct of the Police.

Officers at the Homagama police station or the Police media unit have failed to provide a clear response to this particular incident. With regards to the Kobeigane incident,  three officers attached to the Kobeigane police station were initially suspended and interdicted over dereliction of duty and failure to provide medical care.

On October 31, police opened fire at a fleeing three-wheeler in Mahakade junction, Kadawatha. A 16-year old boy traveling in the three-wheeler sustained injuries. The driver, reportedly a 17-year old individual fled the scene as he had no valid driving license. He had later surrendered to the police. Investigations are underway with regards to these three incidents.

If there are lapses pertaining to custodial deaths, it sets a bad precedent regarding the duties of the police. The incumbent government has a duty to acknowledge these incidents and ensure that justice is being done to perpetrators. The careless way in which the police responds to some of these incidents doesn’t look good on the apex institution that is responsible to maintain law and order in the country.
