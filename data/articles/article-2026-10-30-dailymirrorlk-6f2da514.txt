Man posing as female medical student cheats 71-year-old

An individual posing as a female medical student who cheated an elderly expatiate worker and fraudulently obtained Rs. 1.8 million and a stock of goods as presents was produced before Galle Magistrate Sameera Dodangoda and remanded till November 12.

Posing as a female medical student through AI technology, he had pretended to be in love with the 71 year old victim and obtained money and goods from him.

The suspect Ovitigala Vithanage Thikshani Pramodya (21) was an employee of a mobile phone showroom. The owner of the mobile phone showroom Emaduwa Vithanage Dimutu Sampath (24) suspected of aiding and abetting the fraud was released on bail.

The 71 year old victim from Wennappuwa, an expatriate worker in Italy had recently acquainted through the internet a young woman who claimed that she was a medical student living with her mother in a small house in Wanduramba area and requested to support her. She had sent him her photos in medical student’s dress.

The elderly expatriate worker who fell a prey to the glib talks of the suspect had sent him money and presents in the hope of living with her back in Sri Lanka.

However, when he returned to Sri Lanka on October 26 he had attempted to contact her but to no avail. When he failed to call her over the phone he made a complaint to Galle Police.

Investigations revealed that the main suspect was a young man who introduced himself to the victim by the female name of Thikshani Pramodya and spoken to him in a female voice through AI technology.

OIC Crime Investigation Branch CI B. V. Wijesiri, SI Premakumara and Sergeant 730009 Aruna had conducted investigations on the instructions of SSP S. K. S. Rupasinghe and arrested the two suspects.
