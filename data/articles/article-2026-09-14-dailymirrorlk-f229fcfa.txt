Heavy rains exceeding 100 mm forecast in several areas

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department has forecast cloudy skies with showers or thundershowers at times in the Western, Central, Sabaragamuwa, Southern and Uva provinces, as well as in the Batticaloa and Ampara districts, due to a low-level atmospheric disturbance in the vicinity of Sri Lanka.

The Department said heavy rainfall exceeding 100 mm is likely at some locations, while showers or thundershowers are expected in several other parts of the island after 1.00 p.m.

The public has been advised to take adequate precautions to minimize the risk of damage from temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
