Tepid trading amidst overall selling interest in secondary market

The secondary market concluded the trading session for yesterday on the back of moderate volumes as activity levels were limited.

The market saw mild selling sentiment kicking in, although the yield curve stood largely unchanged.

Amongst the traded maturities, within the 2030 segment, the 01.03.2030 and 01.07.2030 bonds were seen trading from 11.45 percent to 11.52 percent. Moving along the curve, the 15.10.2034 bond traded within a narrow band from 12.62 percent to 12.64 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.332.61/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.49/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.392.68 billion, compared to Rs.387.34 billion seen previously.
