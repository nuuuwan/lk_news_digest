Technical Education Department gets permanent Director General

Colombo, Oct. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the permanent appointment of Dilki Watsala Marambage, a Special Grade officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, as Director General of the Department of Technical Education and Training.

The Cabinet had earlier approved her appointment to the post on a full-time acting basis at its meeting held on January 26, 2026, when she was a Grade I officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service.

The Public Service Commission subsequently promoted her to the Special Grade of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service with effect from January 1, 2026.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya in her capacity as Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to appoint Marambage to the post on a permanent basis.
