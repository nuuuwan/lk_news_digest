Gunmen open fire at Tangalle house

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - A house belonging to a fisheries boat owner in the Kudawella area of Tangalle was targeted in a shooting early this morning (12), police said.

According to police, two individuals who arrived at the location on a motorcycle opened fire at the house before fleeing the scene.

The shooting was carried out using a 9mm firearm, police said.
