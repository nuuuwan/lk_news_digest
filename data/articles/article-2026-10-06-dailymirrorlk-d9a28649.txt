Fuel shortage returns: Auto Diesel, Petrol 92 in short supply - Union

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The country is once again facing a fuel shortage, with motorists forced to remain in long queues to obtain Lanka Auto Diesel and Lanka Petrol 92 Octane, the Sri Lanka Podujana Progressive Employees' Union-Petroleum (SLPPEU-P) claimed today.

Addressing the media, SLPPEU-P Chief Secretary Bandula Saman Kumara said motorists were being forced to obtain Auto Diesel and Petrol 92 Octane mainly from filling stations operated by the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC).

He said media reports had indicated a shortage of Auto Diesel and Petrol 92 Octane in several parts of the country, adding that the Government should take responsibility for the situation.

Kumara alleged that the Government had failed to ensure an adequate fuel supply to meet the country's requirements.

He claimed that previous governments had provided greater concessions to consumers, while the present Government had failed to provide similar relief.

He also alleged that the Government had been unable to even cover the cost of unloading fuel.

Kumara claimed that the CPC had never incurred losses and had earned a profit of Rs. 28 billion over the past eight years.

He further claimed that the CPC had recorded a profit of more than Rs. 32 billion last year.

“If the CPC has earned such a profit, people can understand how much profit the private petroleum companies could be making,” he said.

He called on the Government to take immediate steps to address the reported fuel shortage and ensure an uninterrupted supply of fuel to consumers.
