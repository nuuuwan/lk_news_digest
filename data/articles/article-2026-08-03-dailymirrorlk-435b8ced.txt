Indices close on a mixed note

The ASPI edged down as a result of price losses in counters such as Cargills, Hemas Holdings and Richard

Pieris & Company with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.4 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 edged up. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Commercial Bank, Hayleys, and Lanka Milk Foods. Mixed

interest was observed in John Keells Holdings, Dialog Axiata and Pan Asia Banking Corporation whilst retail

interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, HNB Finance and UB Finance Company. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index gained 0.18%. The share price of Commercial Bank lost Rs 0.50 (0.25%) to close at Rs 200.50.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Lanka Milk

Foods) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.08%. The share price of Lanka Milk Foods decreased by Rs 0.50 (0.59%) to close at Rs 84.50.

John Keells Holdings, Hayleys and Dialog Axiata were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs 19.90. The share price of Hayleys recorded a loss of Rs 0.50 (0.22%) to close at Rs 223.50. The share price of Dialog Axiata appreciated by Rs 0.10 (0.23%) to close at Rs 44.30.
