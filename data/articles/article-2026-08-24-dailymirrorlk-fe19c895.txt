England hit by fresh discipline storm after Carse incident

AUG.24 (Mirror Sports) - England are facing another off-field controversy after fast bowler Brydon Carse was pictured in handcuffs outside a nightclub in Derby.

Durham team-mate Matthew Potts was also pictured at the scene, while former England captain Ben Stokes appeared in the background. Carse was reportedly not arrested, with the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) and Cricket Regulator expected to establish what happened.

The incident comes less than 48 hours before Carse was due to report for England’s second Test against Pakistan at Lord’s. He had been released from the first-Test squad to play for Durham against Derbyshire.

Carse remains part of England’s squad for now, although the ECB is expected to assess the situation before deciding whether he will be available for the second Test.

The latest controversy adds to a growing list of off-field incidents involving England players over the past year, including Harry Brook, Jacob Bethell, Josh Tongue, Ben Duckett, Ben Stokes and Gus Atkinson.

New Test captain Joe Root recently urged his players to “be adults” and called on them to act as good role models. He also abolished the previous midnight curfew, giving players greater freedom away from cricket.

The Carse incident now puts that approach under fresh scrutiny.

For England, the focus will be on whether further disciplinary action is required and whether stronger measures are needed to prevent another off-field controversy.
