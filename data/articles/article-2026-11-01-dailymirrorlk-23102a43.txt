Rs. 13.5 million spent to reprint leaked Grade 3, 11 exam papers

Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Government had to spend an additional Rs. 13.5 million to reprint question papers for the 2024 Grade 3 and Grade 11 final examinations in the North Central Province after the papers were leaked to teachers conducting tuition classes, a new audit report has revealed.

According to the report released by the National Audit Office, the North Central Provincial Education Department had awarded a contract worth Rs. 54,397,908 to the Provincial Development, Construction and Machinery Agency to print the examination papers for the province's schools in 2024.

However, the department was forced to temporarily suspend the examinations after all the question papers intended for Grade 3 and Grade 11 students were reportedly found in the hands of teachers conducting private tuition classes before the examinations.

An investigation conducted by the North Central Provincial Ministry of Education revealed that the question papers had been leaked from the Provincial Development, Construction and Machinery Agency to teachers conducting tuition classes, the audit report stated.

As a result, the Provincial Education Department had to arrange for the papers to be reprinted by the Government Printing Department, incurring an additional cost of Rs. 13.5 million.

However, the audit report pointed out that no action had been taken so far to recover the financial loss caused to the Government from those responsible for the leak.

The report has instructed the authorities of the North Central Provincial Council to take immediate action in this regard.

When contacted, Secretary to the Provincial Chief Ministry K.M.H.S.K. Jayalath said a complaint had been lodged with the police regarding the incident.

He said legal action would be taken against those responsible based on the findings of the police investigation.
