Seylan Bank partners with John Keells CG Auto to drive NEV ownership in Sri Lanka

Seylan Bank PLC recently entered a strategic partnership with John Keells CG Auto (Pvt) Limited to introduce a dedicated leasing facility for customers purchasing New Energy Vehicles (NEVs) in Sri Lanka, strengthening the Bank’s commitment to sustainable transport and green mobility solutions.

The partnership will offer tailored leasing facilities for premium NEV brands of BYD and DENZA, combining competitive financial solutions with a fast-tracked approval process and personalized customer support. Through this collaboration, customers will gain easier access to NEV ownership backed by attractive lease-to-own options and island-wide service support.

Under the agreement, Seylan Bank will provide leasing facilities with repayment periods extending up to seven years. Eligible customers can also receive lease approvals within 24 hours, enabling a smoother and more convenient purchasing experience.

Commenting on the partnership, Asiri Abhayaratne, Assistant General Manager – Marketing & Sales said, “As a bank committed to supporting Sri Lanka’s future in environmental sustainability, we are pleased to collaborate with John Keells CG Auto in expanding access to electric mobility solutions. Through this partnership, we aim to provide customers with flexible and convenient financing options that make NEV ownership more accessible while contributing to environmentally responsible transportation.”

Charith Panditharatne, Chief Executive Officer of John Keells CG Auto said, “We are delighted to partner with Seylan Bank to further strengthen the NEV ecosystem in Sri Lanka. This collaboration enables customers to experience a seamless purchasing journey supported by attractive leasing solutions and enhanced customer convenience.”

As part of the Bank’s special offering, customers will benefit from competitive leasing interest rates and a 50 percent concession on document processing fees. Seylan Bank’s dedicated leasing specialists will also provide doorstep assistance and free consultancy services throughout the leasing process, including support with documentation and post-approval services.

With its extensive island-wide branch network and customer-centric approach, Seylan Bank continues to enhance financial accessibility while supporting environmentally conscious initiatives that contribute towards a greener future.
