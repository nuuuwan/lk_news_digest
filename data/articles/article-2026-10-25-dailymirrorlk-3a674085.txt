England under pressure after record-breaking collapse

OCT.25 (Mirror Sports) - Pakistan will look to secure their place in the tri-series final when they face England in Lahore, with Harry Brook’s side desperate to bounce back from their shocking defeat to Sri Lanka.

England suffered a stunning  run defeat after failing to defend 429 against Sri Lanka, exposing major weaknesses in their bowling attack. Sam Curran conceded 103 runs, while the absence of Adil Rashid raised questions about England’s team selection.

Pakistan, meanwhile, enter the contest with back-to-back victories and need just one more win from their remaining two matches to guarantee a place in the final.

England are expected to consider bringing Rashid back into the side as they fight to keep their qualification hopes alive.

With Pakistan chasing an early final berth and England under pressure to respond, the Lahore clash promises to be a crucial test for both teams.
