More than 34,000 have left forces during last five years: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - More than 34,000 have left the tri forces during the last five years, Deputy Minister Aruna Jayasekera told Parliament today.

He said 31,088 from the Army, 2,501 from the Navy and 1,394 from the Air Force have left during the last five years using the two amnesty periods granted in 2022 and 2024.
