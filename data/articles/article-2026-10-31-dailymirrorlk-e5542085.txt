Eva Pink & Strong Health Run 2026 brings Colombo together for breast cancer awareness

Eva Pink & Strong Health Run 2026, held recently on Independence Avenue in Colombo, brought together around 1,000 participants to raise awareness about breast cancer while contributing towards a meaningful cause.

Organised by Single Tree Events, with Sri Lanka’s No 1 feminine hygiene brand Eva as The Official Title Partner, the event was held during the Breast Cancer Awareness Week.

Beyond the sporting element, the initiative aimed to encourage greater awareness about breast cancer and the importance of supporting those affected by the disease, while reinforcing Eva’s commitment towards the well-being of Sri Lankan women.

ICL Brands Director Marketing Chethika Rajapaksha highlighted that Eva’s position as a market leader for over four decades came with a responsibility to make a meaningful difference in the lives of Sri Lankan women.

She explained that Eva’s involvement in the Pink & Strong Health Run was driven by its commitment to supporting women’s well-being and creating greater awareness about breast cancer among Sri Lankan society.

Emphasising that “Every life matters and every step counts”, she went on to explain how Eva would be contributing towards Apeksha Hospital, Maharagama, through the donation of sanitary napkins for the use of patients receiving treatment at the hospital.

The total number of sanitary napkins donated was determined by the combined number of steps taken by the first finishers in each of the three running categories, namely two kilometres, five kilometres and 10 kilometres.

Under the message ‘Let’s paint the city pink’, the event brought attention to breast cancer awareness while highlighting how individual participation, even through something as simple as taking a step, can contribute towards making a difference.
