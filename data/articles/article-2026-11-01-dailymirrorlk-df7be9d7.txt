Lawyers Collective pays tribute to Lal Wijenayake

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Lawyers Collective of Sri Lanka has expressed profound sorrow over the passing of Lal Wijenayake, a founding member of Lawyers for Democracy, the organisation from which the Lawyers Collective later emerged.

In a statement, the Lawyers Collective said Wijenayake had remained steadfast throughout his long and principled career in defending the rule of law, the independence of the judiciary and constitutional values that safeguard the rights of all people in Sri Lanka.

It described him as part of a long-established tradition of Sri Lankan lawyers who had sought to establish, defend and advance human rights, the rule of law and constitutionalism.

“Mr Wijenayake epitomised principled disagreement and social engagement, even at a personal or professional cost,” the statement said.

It further noted that he had dedicated himself wholeheartedly to the cause of justice, inspiring generations of lawyers to place conscience and public duty above convenience.

His contribution to Sri Lanka’s democratic and legal landscape was immense and would endure, the Lawyers Collective said.

The organisation extended its deepest condolences to his family, friends and colleagues, pledging to carry forward the values he had fought tirelessly to uphold.
