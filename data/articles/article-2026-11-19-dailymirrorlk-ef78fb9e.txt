Two arrested over pangolin killing after video found on student’s phone

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Two people, including a minor, have been taken into police custody following an investigation into the killing of a pangolin in the Medirigiriya area after footage of the incident surfaced on social media.

The investigation was launched jointly by the Medirigiriya Police and the Department of Wildlife Conservation after a teacher discovered the video on a mobile phone brought to school by a student.

Following the discovery, police and wildlife officials conducted searches at the residences of individuals suspected of involvement and several other locations.

An adult and a minor were subsequently taken into custody in connection with the incident.

However, the main suspect, who is believed to have killed the pangolin, is reportedly absconding. Police and wildlife officials are continuing investigations to arrest him.

The pangolin is an endangered species protected under the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance, and killing or illegally hunting the animal is a punishable offence.

Meanwhile, the Ministry of Environment has called on the public to provide information to the relevant authorities about the killing, mistreatment and illegal hunting of wild animals, stressing the need for strict enforcement of the law.
