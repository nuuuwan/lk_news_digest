HIV cases rise as Sri Lanka logs nearly 500 new infections in 2026

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has reported 487 new HIV cases during the first six months of 2026, marking an increase compared to the corresponding period last year,

According to the latest figures released by the National STD/AIDS Control Programme, the country recorded 249 new cases in the first quarter of 2026 and a further 238 cases in the second quarter.

This represents a 13.3% increase compared to the 430 cases reported during the first half of 2025, when 230 cases were recorded in the first quarter and 200 in the second.

The latest figures also show that males continued to account for the majority of reported infections, with the male-to-female ratio standing at 7:1 during the second quarter of 2026.

Among those aged 15 to 24, a total of 55 HIV cases were reported during the first half of this year, including 48 males, six females and one transgender person.

Also, eight AIDS-related deaths were reported during the first two quarters of 2026, with four deaths recorded in each quarter.

The cumulative number of AIDS-related deaths reported in the country has reached 820, while another 111 deaths have been classified as non-AIDS-related. The cumulative adjusted number of deaths among all reported HIV cases up to 2025 stands at 1,708.

The report further revealed that 117 HIV cases have been linked to mother-to-child transmission, with no vertically transmitted cases reported during the fourth quarter of 2025.

Meanwhile, a total of 73 HIV-positive cases have also been identified through the Immigration Health Unit, including nine cases during the second quarter of 2026.
