CCD arrests two more Chinese nationals over Port City murder

Colombo, Augusr 29 (Daily Mirror) - Two more Chinese nationals wanted in connection with the abduction and murder of a fellow Chinese national at Colombo Port City have been arrested in Sigiriya by the Colombo Crimes Division (CCD).

The arrests were made as part of an ongoing investigation into the incident, which occurred on July 23 when a clash broke out among a group of Chinese nationals at the Port City premises.

During the incident, one Chinese national was allegedly abducted by another group. His body was later found concealed in a forested area in Eheliyagoda.

Several suspects had previously been arrested and remanded in connection with the incident. Following those arrests, CCD investigators continued efforts to locate other suspects who had fled the area and gone into hiding.

The two suspects, aged 35 and 43 were arrested after investigators traced them to a location in Sigiriya.

They are currently being detained by the CCD and are being questioned over their alleged involvement in the abduction and murder, police said.
