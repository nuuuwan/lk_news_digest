The 65 food comparisons cover open market prices in Colombo District. The 2016 figures are annual averages. The 2026 figures are weekly averages from the fourth week of September. Only products with matching units and sufficiently clear descriptions were included. Minor spelling and naming differences were standardised for readability. This is a price comparison, not a formal inflation index.

The dollar comparison uses the CBSL monthly average USD/LKR spot series for September 2016 and September 2026. CBSL changed its published spot reference methodology in March 2023, so this is a historical comparison, not a banknote buying or selling quote.

This comparison shows how the prices of food, fuel and gold, together with the value of the rupee, have changed over time. Explore each category or use the AI Price Assistant to identify the largest increases and understand individual comparisons.

The figures are based on data from the Department of Census and Statistics, Ceylon Petroleum Corporation, Central Bank of Sri Lanka and published gold-market sources. These price changes are the combined result of many interconnected factors, including inflation, exchange rates, taxation, global commodity prices, supply conditions, seasonality, product quality and market location. No single factor should be viewed as the sole cause of an increase. This interactive presents a historical price comparison and is not intended to serve as a formal inflation index or a detailed analysis of the causes behind each price change.
