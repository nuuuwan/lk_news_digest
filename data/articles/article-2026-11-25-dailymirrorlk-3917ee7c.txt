Russian Prosecutor General arrives in Sri Lanka

The Prosecutor General of the Russian Federation, Alexander Gutsan, arrived at the Katunayake Bandaranaike International Airport on November 25, on a special Russian aircraft, accompanied by a 21-member delegation.

The delegation arrived from Moscow at 8.47 p.m. aboard the Russian aircraft RSD-306.

Tight security was deployed at the airport during the arrival of the Russian delegation.
