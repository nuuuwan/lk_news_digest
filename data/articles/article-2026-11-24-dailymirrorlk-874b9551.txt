Teachers’ unions to stage one-day sick leave campaign on November 30

The Alliance of Teachers and Principals, together with several other trade unions in the education sector, has decided to launch a one-day sick leave campaign on November 30 to demand the outstanding two-thirds of the teachers' and principals' salary increase.

Sri Lanka Teachers' Union General Secretary Josep Starlin said all stakeholders and trade unions in the education sector had pledged their support for the proposed trade union action.

He said teachers and principals would report sick leave on November 30 as part of a collective campaign to pressure the Government to fulfil its salary demands.

"Just as we secured victories in the past by standing together, we are determined to take strong action to win this demand as well," he said.

Starlin said the only group that had decided not to participate was the faction led by Mahinda Jayasinghe of the Lanka Teachers' Service Union.

He said the participating unions were determined to continue their struggle until the outstanding salary demands were met.
