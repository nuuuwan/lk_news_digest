Sri Lankan Parliament to debate motion calling for release of Imran Khan

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s Parliament is scheduled to hold an adjournment debate on Saturday (November 21) on a motion calling for the release of imprisoned former Pakistani Prime Minister Imran Khan.

The adjournment motion is set to be moved by Opposition MP Ajith P. Perera.

According to sources close to MP Perera, he is expected to formally hand over the motion to parliamentary authorities shortly.
