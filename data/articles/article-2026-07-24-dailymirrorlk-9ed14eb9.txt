Janashakthi Life strengthens healthcare and safety support

Janashakthi Life team members hand over directional signboards to Sri Lanka Police

Janashakthi Life, the flagship brand of JXG (Janashakthi Group), carried out a community-focused initiative in Anuradhapura during the Poson season to support healthcare services, public safety and pilgrim welfare.

As part of the programme, the company donated essential medicines to Teaching Hospital Anuradhapura and installed a new hospital name board to improve accessibility for the visitors seeking medical assistance.

Janashakthi Life also provided directional signboards to the Sri Lanka Police to support crowd movement and route guidance during the pilgrimage period. In addition, the company distributed merit books to devotees at the Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi.

Through these initiatives, Janashakthi Life reaffirmed its commitment to supporting communities and contributing to their well-being and safety.
