KPMG webinar on understanding Sri Lanka’s recent tax reforms: Navigating complexity with confidence

Principal – Head of Tax and Regulatory Suresh R. I. Perera / Director – Tax and Regulatory

Sri Lanka’s evolving tax framework has introduced a range of legislative changes that require careful interpretation by businesses and tax professionals alike. While several amendments are relatively straightforward, others contain complex provisions and unresolved ambiguities that could significantly influence tax compliance and financial decision-making. Understanding these developments is becoming increasingly important for organisations seeking to remain compliant while making informed strategic decisions.

To help professionals gain greater clarity, KPMG Sri Lanka Academy will host a webinar that examines selected complicated tax issues arising from the recent reforms. The session will provide practical guidance on determining the appropriate basis of tax computation, interpreting complex provisions within the legislation, and understanding the implications of the revised income tax computation methodology.

The webinar will be conducted by Principal – Head of Tax and Regulatory Suresh R. I. Perera, Director – Tax and Regulatory Hasna Hassan, and Principal – Tax and Regulatory Rifka Ziyard. Their combined expertise will provide participants with valuable insights into current legislative challenges and practical considerations for businesses operating in today’s changing tax environment.
