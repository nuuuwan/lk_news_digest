Gnanasara Thera appears before Colombo High Court

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Bodu Bala Sena Secretary Ven. Galagodaaththe Gnanasara Thera appeared before the Colombo High Court today as the case filed against him by the Attorney General over alleged remarks insulting Islam was taken up.

The case was called before Colombo High Court Judge Buddhika C. Ragala.

The court ordered that the case be taken up again on September 30.

Ven. Galagodaaththe Gnanasara Thera is facing charges in connection with alleged remarks concerning Islam.
