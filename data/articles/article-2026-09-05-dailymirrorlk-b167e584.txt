SDG strengthens employee leadership through int’l learning in Thailand

Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC (SDF) has reinforced its commitment to employee learning and leadership development by providing a group of top-performing employees with an international learning and development experience in Thailand, combining professional exposure, knowledge sharing and reflective leadership development.

The initiative was designed to recognise high-performing employees while strengthening the leadership capabilities required to support SDF’s long-term organisational and community-focused objectives. Through exposure to international business practices, new perspectives and different approaches to leadership, participants were encouraged to broaden their thinking and translate their learning into meaningful outcomes within the organisation.

The programme reflects SDF’s belief that sustainable organisational progress begins with the continuous development of its people. By investing in employees who demonstrate strong performance and potential, the company aims to nurture a culture that values excellence, continuous learning, innovation, empathy and responsible leadership.

During the learning journey, participants were exposed to international practices and perspectives intended to challenge conventional thinking and encourage a more forward-looking approach to business and leadership. The experience also provided employees with an opportunity to reflect on their individual leadership styles, exchange ideas and identify practical insights that can be applied within their respective roles at SDF.

Through initiatives such as the Thailand learning and development journey, SDF continues to position employee growth as an important foundation for organisational excellence, stronger customer experiences and sustainable social impact.
