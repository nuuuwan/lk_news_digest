Man arrested for filming near IGP, Chief Justice residences

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - A 40-year-old man has been arrested by the Cinnamon Gardens Police for allegedly recording videos of roads surrounding the official residences of the Inspector General of Police (IGP) and the Chief Justice in Colombo.

Police said the suspect’s mobile phone was also taken into custody following the arrest on Thursday night (6).

According to police, a Special Task Force (STF) officer stationed at the main entrance of the IGP’s official residence noticed a luxury vehicle parked nearby, with the driver allegedly recording the road area using a mobile phone.

The STF officer informed a traffic police constable on duty in the area, who then alerted the Colombo Emergency Response Division. A mobile patrol team from the Cinnamon Gardens Police was subsequently dispatched to the location and took the suspect into custody after preliminary inquiries.

Police said an examination of the suspect’s mobile phone revealed video footage showing the official residences and surrounding roads of both the IGP and the Chief Justice.

During questioning, the suspect claimed that he recorded the videos as a hobby. However, police said further inquiries revealed inconsistencies in his statements regarding his residence and other details.
