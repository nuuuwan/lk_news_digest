17-year-old arrested over Kadawatha three-wheeler incident

Colombo, November 1 (Daily Mirror)- A 17-year-old boy has been arrested after surrendering to the Kadawatha Police Station over an incident in which he allegedly drove a three-wheeler despite police orders to stop at Mahakade Junction in Kadawatha yesterday afternoon.

The three-wheeler was later found concealed inside a house in Gonahena and taken into police custody.

A 16-year-old boy was injured yesterday afternoon when police opened fire on a three-wheeler at Mahakade Junction in Kadawatha.
