FLH launches ‘FLH udmsh lemlre’ programme

Future Life Holdings Chief Executive Officer Danushka Udugama and Future Life Holdings Chief Administrative and Marketing Officer Kasun Perera

The FL Foundation of the FLH group has launched the ‘FLH udmsh lemlre’ programme to support the education of children from low-income families and help them build a better future.

Established in 2018, the FL Foundation carries out a range of social development initiatives aimed at empowering children and women from low-income families.

Through its ‘FLH Sathkaara’ programme, the foundation has so far implemented 235 projects across the country, providing assistance worth over Rs.110 million and directly benefiting around 45,000 Sri Lankans.

The ‘FLH udmsh lemlre’ programme is the latest initiative under this continuing social commitment, with the primary objective of helping children from low-income families continue their education without interruption. The programme is driven by the belief that education has the power to change a child’s future. Donors, together with a leading bank, have joined the initiative to support the children in need.

Under the first phase, the monthly educational expenses of 100 children will be covered for a period of one year. Beyond education, the programme will also provide support for needs such as healthcare and sports, based on the age and individual needs of each child. The FLH group also plans to launch a range of programmes through its establishments across the country in the coming weeks and months, aimed at supporting the education, knowledge and overall well-being of thousands more children.

Children in need of assistance under the programme can be identified and referred through local Grama Niladharis, religious leaders or school principals. Individuals who wish to support the education of children in need can also join the programme as sponsors.

Further information about the ‘FLH udmsh lemlre’ programme can be obtained by visiting www.sathkaara.lk or calling 070 541 0547.

Information about the ‘FLH Sathkaara’ programmes and the assistance provided through them is also available on the Sathkaara.com website, Sathkaara app and its Facebook, Instagram and YouTube platforms.
