ALT X certified Great Place to Work again

ALT X (Altered Experience) has once again been certified as a Great Place to Work, marking its second consecutive year of receiving the globally recognised certification.

This year’s recognition is made even more significant by ALT X achieving one of the highest employee trust scores in Sri Lanka, reinforcing its commitment to building an exceptional workplace where people thrive alongside business performance.

Awarded by Great Place to Work, the certification is based entirely on confidential employee feedback, measuring the levels of trust, pride, collaboration and workplace experience within an organisation.

Receiving this recognition for the second year in succession reflects the consistency of ALT X’s people-first philosophy and the culture it has intentionally built as the company continues its rapid global expansion.

ALT X Managing Director Kabeer Rafaideen stated, “Being recognised as a Great Place to Work for the second consecutive year, while achieving one of the highest employee trust scores in Sri Lanka, is an incredible milestone for us. This recognition belongs to every member of the ALT X team who continues to shape our culture every single day.”

ALT X Head of Human Resources Nimali Seneviratne added, “Receiving this certification for the second consecutive year validates the culture we continue to nurture and motivates us to keep raising the benchmark.”
