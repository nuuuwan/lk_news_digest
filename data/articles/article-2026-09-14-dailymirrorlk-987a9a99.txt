Arrivals dip marginally in first week of Sept. as Australia climbs to third

Sri Lanka’s tourist arrivals slipped marginally in the first six days of September, with 31,885 visitors recorded against 32,246 in the comparable period last year, while Australia climbed to third place among source markets following Jetstar’s launch of its first direct low-fare service from Melbourne last month, as per Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) data.

Daily arrivals averaged 5,314 over the six days, marginally below the 5,374 daily average recorded over the same dates in 2025, ranging from a low of 4,524 on September 2 to a high of 5,933 on September 5.

Australia contributed 2,036 arrivals for a 6 percent share, ahead of Germany with 1,762 and China with 1,645, having ranked fifth in September last year with a 5.7 percent share. Australian arrivals have reached 78,074 so far this year, already ahead of the 77,380 recorded over the whole of January to September last year.

SLTDA’s revised growth scenarios project September arrivals of 160,420 under the lower scenario, 178,466 under the conservative scenario and 202,070 under the optimistic scenario. At the current run rate the month would close at around 159,420, marginally short of even the lower scenario, while reaching the conservative figure would require about 6,108 arrivals a day over the remaining 24 days, roughly 15 percent above the present average.
