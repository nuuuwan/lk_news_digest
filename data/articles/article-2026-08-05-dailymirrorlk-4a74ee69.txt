Why was Akila Kariyawasam arrested?

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Former Minister Akila Viraj Kariyawasam was arrested today by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over allegations that he misused state-funded employees for personal purposes while serving as the Senior Adviser to the President on Administrative Affairs.

Kariyawasam was appointed to the post on July 29, 2022, by then-President Ranil Wickremesinghe.

According to CIABOC, Kariyawasam allegedly recruited four individuals as members of his official personal staff attached to the Presidential Secretariat. Investigators claim that three of them were assigned to work at a private fuel station owned by him, while the fourth employee received salaries and allowances despite not being assigned any official duties.

The Commission alleged that the salaries and allowances paid to the four employees amounted to Rs. 2,768,836.14, causing a financial loss to the state and constituting an act of corruption.

Kariyawasam was arrested at around 1:45 p.m. and is expected to be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate's Court.
