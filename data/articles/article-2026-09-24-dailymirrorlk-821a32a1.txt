Indices close in green

Indices closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Commercial Bank, Dialog Axiata and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.3 Bn. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sampath Bank, Dipped Products and E B Creasy & Company. Mixed interest was observed in SMB Leasing, R I L Property and Sierra Cables whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, Asia Siyaka Commodities and York Arcade Holdings. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to E B Creasy & Company and Aitken Spence) whilst the sector index gained 0.46%. The share price of E B Creasy & Company moved up by Rs 0.60 (1.76%) to close at Rs 34.70. The share price of Aitken Spence appreciated by Rs 1.50 (1.08%) to close at Rs 139.75.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index increased by 0.35%. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs 140.25.

Dipped Products and SMB Leasing were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dipped Products gained Rs 2.90 (5.00%) to close at Rs 60.90. The share price of SMB Leasing recorded a gain of Rs 0.10 (10.00%) to close at Rs 1.10.
