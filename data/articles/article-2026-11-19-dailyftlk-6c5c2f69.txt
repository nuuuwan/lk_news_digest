Rajanganaya Saddharathana Thera remanded over Ruwanweliseya clash

Rajanganaya Saddharathana Thera and another individual were arrested yesterday and remanded till 24 November after appearing at the Anuradhapura Police Station to provide statements.

The arrests came a day after the Anuradhapura Chief Magistrate ordered the Anuradhapura Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) to appear in person before Court on 26 November and explain why Saddharathana Thera and other suspects allegedly involved in instigating a clash near the Ruwanweliseya had not been arrested and produced before Court. The order, issued on 17 November, followed allegations by lawyers representing four Buddhist monks, two male civilians and a woman currently in remand custody over the incident.

Lawyers for the remanded suspects told Court that more than 25 individuals, including Saddharathana Thera, had allegedly instigated the clash at the Satyagraha site in the Dansal Zone near the Ruwanweliseya. They alleged that while their clients had been arrested and remanded, those accused of provoking the confrontation had been allowed to remain at large. The lawyers also questioned the conduct of police officers deployed at the scene, particularly the Anuradhapura Headquarters Chief Inspector, arguing that the confrontation could have been prevented had police acted appropriately.
