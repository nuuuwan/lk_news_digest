Sri Lanka expands Japan job opportunities for skilled professionals

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Foreign Employment Agency (SLFEA) has taken steps to expand employment opportunities in Japan for Sri Lankan professionals through a new partnership with Japan-based METATECHNO INC.

The SLFEA, an affiliate of the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with METATECHNO INC. to strengthen the recruitment and placement of qualified Sri Lankan professionals in Japan.

The agreement aims to promote legal, ethical and transparent recruitment processes while ensuring compliance with labour laws, regulations and standards of both Sri Lanka and Japan.

METATECHNO INC., a company specialising in IT engineering services and job placement, will work with the SLFEA to identify, recruit and place skilled Sri Lankan workers to meet Japan’s growing demand for professional talent.

The collaboration is expected to create new employment opportunities, particularly in Information Technology, Engineering and other specialised fields.

The MoU was signed by METATECHNO INC. Chairman Nakayama Hijiri and SLFEA Chairman Anandalal Hettiarachchi.

Officials including LNBTI Director Ravindra Perera, SLBFE Additional General Manager (Corporate Affairs) L.A.P.K. Liyanawala, and the General Manager of SLFEA attended the signing ceremony.

The initiative is expected to further strengthen Sri Lanka-Japan cooperation while expanding safe, legal and quality foreign employment opportunities for Sri Lankan professionals.
