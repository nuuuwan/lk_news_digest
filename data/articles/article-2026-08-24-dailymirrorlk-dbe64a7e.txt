Lankan labour migration out to prevent foreign investment: Ravi K

Colombo, Aug. 24 (Daily Mirror) - Whilst highlighting that around 300,000 Sri Lankan workers have migrated during the last few years, opposition MP Ravi Karunanayake said over the weekend that this could create new issues.

"It was the Board of Investment (BOI) Chairman Dhammika Fernando and its senior officials who have revealed that issue. They have said high labour migration will prevent much needed foreign investments coming into Sri Lanka. Sri Lankan government should focus on this matter as the Sri Lankan economy would suffer without foreign investments," MP Karunanayake said.

Also, he said the government should do away with the Rs 2.5 billion subsidy given to private sector fuel suppliers. "Four foreign players were allowed to get into the local fuel market for them to invest and earn a profit. In such a situation, the Sri Lankan government should not pay subsidies to these foreign players.

Also, he said the remark made by former President Ranil Wickremesinghe that Sri Lanka is heading for another economic downturn should be taken seriously. "It was Ranil Wickremesinghe who helped the Sri Lankan economy to rebound after the bankruptcy in 2022. Therefore, a remark made by such a person should not be taken lightly," he said.
