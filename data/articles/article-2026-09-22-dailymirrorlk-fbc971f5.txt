Short and mid tenure yields ease amid selective buying

The buying interest continued to dominate the market sentiment. However, buying activity was concentrated mainly in the short to medium-term tenure, resulting in a downward adjustment in yields across the shorter maturities, extending up to the 2033 maturity.

Conversely, the belly end of the curve, particularly the 2034 and 2035 maturities, witnessed modest selling interest. Nevertheless, the yields in this segment remained largely stable. Both investor participation and market volumes appeared moderate yesterday.

At the short end of the curve, 15.12.2028 traded at 10.70 percent, while 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 were both seen changing hands from 11.25 percent to 11.15 percent. Further along the curve, 01.02.2031 traded between 11.35 percent to 11.25 percent and 01.10.2032 and 15.12.2032 both traded within 11.55 percent-11.45 percent. At the belly of the curve, 15.01.2033 and 01.11.2033 traded between 11.80 percent and 11.65 percent. Finally, at the belly end of the curve, 15.06.2034 traded at 12.00 percent and 15.06.2035 traded higher at 12.10 percent. On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.35/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.13/ US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system marginally expanded to Rs.325.72 billion, compared to Rs.324.79 billion recorded previously.
