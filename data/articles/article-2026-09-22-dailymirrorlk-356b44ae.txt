Resus Energy commissions 2 MW Ampara solar plant

Resus Energy PLC kicked off the commercial operations at a new two megawatt (MW) solar power plant in Ampara, expanding the renewable energy producer’s generation portfolio.

The facility, located in Damana, was commissioned on September 18 and is expected to generate about 3.5 gigawatt-hours of electricity annually for supply to the national grid, the company said in a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange.

The projected output implies an average capacity factor of about 20 percent.

The plant was developed through Resus Eastern Solar (Pvt.) Ltd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Resus Energy.
