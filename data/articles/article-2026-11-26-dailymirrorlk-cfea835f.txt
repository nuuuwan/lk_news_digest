Prakash Schaffter honoured with SLII Hall of Fame Award 2026

Prakash Schaffter, Executive Deputy Chairman of Janashakthi Life and Deputy Chairman of JXG (Janashakthi Group), was honoured with the Hall of Fame Award 2026 by the Sri Lanka Insurance Institute (SLII), in recognition of his longstanding contribution to Sri Lanka’s insurance industry.

The award was presented on 18 November at the opening ceremony of the 23rd International Insurance Congress 2026, held at Cinnamon Life, Colombo.

The SLII Hall of Fame Award recognises professional achievement, insurance qualifications and longstanding service to the industry and the Institute. The 23rd International Insurance Congress 2026 was held on 18 and 19 November under the theme “Navigating New Horizons and Shaping the Future of Insurance”.

An alumnus of the University of Cambridge and the University of London, Prakash was among the youngest professionals to qualify as a Fellow of the Chartered Insurance Institute (CII). He has also served as President of the Insurance Association of Sri Lanka and as a member of the Council of the Sri Lanka Institute of Directors, contributing to the advancement of professional and institutional standards across the sector.
