CSE extends decline as selling pressure broadens

The Colombo Stock Exchange closed lower for a second consecutive session on 9 September 2026, with both benchmark indices retreating amid broad-based selling pressure.

The ASPI fell 76.23 points, or 0.35%, to 21,466.00, while the S&P SL20 declined 20.66 points, or 0.34%, to 6,033.87. The ASPI slipped below the 21,500 level during the session as recovery attempts failed to hold.

Market turnover remained subdued at LKR 972.23 million, with 43.11 million shares traded. The Capital Goods sector led activity with LKR 283.19 million in turnover, while Access Engineering recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 128.26 million.

Market breadth weakened sharply, with only 60 gainers against 147 decliners, while foreign investors recorded a net outflow of LKR 71.29 million.

Ceylinco Insurance was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Hayleys, Lion Brewery, Carson Cumberbatch and John Keells Holdings. Melstacorp provided the strongest positive contribution.

The combination of turnover falling below LKR 1 billion, weak market breadth and renewed foreign selling suggests softer near-term sentiment, with the ASPI’s ability to hold around the 21,450 to 21,500 range likely to be important for the next few sessions.
