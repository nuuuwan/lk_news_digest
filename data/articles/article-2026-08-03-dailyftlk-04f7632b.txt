Intex-InMac Sri Lanka 2026 set to showcase future of textile sourcing and manufacturing

South Asia’s leading textile sourcing platform, Intex, together with InMac, the dedicated textile and garment technology exhibition, will return to the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) from 5 to 7 August, bringing together the global textile and apparel industry under one roof.

The landmark exhibition reinforces Sri Lanka’s position as a leading destination for textile sourcing, apparel manufacturing, and industrial innovation.

Intex-InMac Sri Lanka 2026 is endorsed by several leading industry institutions and trade bodies, including the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB), Industrial Development Board (IDB), the Embassy of Indonesia in Sri Lanka, the Joint Apparel Association Forum (JAAF) and its member associations, and the Sri Lanka – China Business Cooperation Council (SLCBCC), among others.

This year’s exhibition will feature more than 250 exhibitors representing over 15 countries and regions, showcasing the latest innovations across the textile and apparel value chain. Intex will present fashion fabrics, fibres, yarns, denim, trims, accessories, sustainable and functional textiles, chemicals, dyes, and textile processing solutions. Meanwhile, InMac will introduce next-generation garment machinery, Artificial Intelligence (AI)-enabled sewing systems, automated cutting and spreading technologies, digital printing solutions, factory automation, and smart manufacturing technologies designed to improve productivity while reducing manufacturing costs.

The event is expected to attract buyer delegations and trade visitors from more than 12 countries, creating valuable opportunities for international sourcing, technology partnerships, investment, and business expansion.

EDB Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Mangala Wijesinghe said: “Industry platforms such as Intex Sri Lanka 2026 foster trade, innovation, and sustainable growth, enabling exporters to build stronger relationships and expand into new markets.”

A key highlight of the exhibition will be the three-day Interactive Business Forum (IBF) Seminar Series, organised with the support of GTEX-ITC and the Moratuwa University Textile Association (MUTA). The forum will bring together internationally recognised industry experts and thought leaders to discuss emerging trends, technological advancements, and the future direction of Sri Lanka’s textile and apparel industry.

Speaking about the technology showcase, Worldex India Executive Director Arti Bhagat said: “The future of Sri Lanka’s apparel industry will be shaped by smarter factories, advanced technologies, and continuous innovation. Through InMac, we are bringing world-class manufacturing solutions closer to Sri Lankan manufacturers, helping them enhance productivity, optimise costs, and build globally competitive, future-ready factories.”

For more than a decade, Intex has successfully connected global suppliers with Sri Lanka’s apparel industry, strengthening supply chains, promoting international collaboration, and supporting the country’s ambition of achieving $ 8 billion in apparel and textile exports.
