Shiranthi Rajapaksa admitted to Mount Elizabeth Hospital in Singapore

Colombo, September 17 (Daily Mirror)- Shiranthi Rajapaksa, wife of former President Mahinda Rajapaksa, who left the country yesterday morning, is presently in Singapore undergoing a medical check-up at the elite Mount Elizabeth Hospital, sources told the Daily Mirror last evening.

Shiranthi left the island early yesterday, sparking controversy as she faces a summons to appear before the Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID) on September 24 over alleged financial irregularities linked to the Siriliya Saviya Foundation.

While in Colombo last week, the former First Lady had reportedly been admitted to a private hospital and travelled to the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) yesterday directly after being discharged.

Upon learning that she was attempting to leave the country, a group of FCID officers reportedly rushed to the airport to remind her of the summons. It is learnt that the notice had initially been sent to her residence at Carlton House in Tangalle.

FCID officers reportedly served a notice at the airport informing her to appear before the division on September 24 to record a statement. The notice was accepted by a relative on her behalf.

A member of the Rajapaksa family, who wished to remain unnamed, said the relevant letter was handed over to Shiranthi Rajapaksa by an airport official near the departure gate shortly before her scheduled flight.

However, the family member said Shiranthi Rajapaksa is expected to return to the country on September 19 and face the summons on September 24.
