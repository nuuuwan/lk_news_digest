Dheera Hettiarachchi appointed acting chairman of SLTPB

Sri Lanka Institute of Tourism and Hotel Management (SLITHM) and Sri Lanka Convention Bureau (SLCB) Chairman Dheera Hettiarachchi was appointed Acting Chairman of the Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau (SLTPB) last week, Mirror Business learns.

The appointment fills the vacancy left by Buddhika Hewawasam, who stepped down upon completing his two-year term amid conflicting accounts of his exit. His last day in office is understood to have been October 30. Mirror Business reported earlier this month that the government was expected to name an acting head within the week, as the bureau faced a narrow window to finalise its budget for next year.

Hettiarachchi, a former Cluster Head for Travel and Tourism at SLITHM, has led both SLITHM and the SLCB since October 2024.

He takes over a bureau that has struggled to deploy its funds, having utilised only Rs.2.5 billion of the Rs.8 billion allocated for promotions in 2025, or roughly 31 percent. Its leadership has also been unsettled, with 13 chairmen since 2007 and an average tenure of under 18 months.

The appointment also comes as Budget 2027 proposes abolishing the Tourism Development Levy, a key source of funding for the bureau’s promotional work, from April 1, 2027.
