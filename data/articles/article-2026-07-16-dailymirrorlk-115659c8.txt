Sri Lankan Rupee depreciates further against US Dollar

Colombo, July 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee has further weakened against the US Dollar, the latest exchange rate data released by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka revealed.

The Central Bank’s daily exchange rate data showed that the selling price of one US Dollar was recorded at Rs. 341.08 today (16), while the buying price stood at Rs. 331.67.

The selling price of one US Dollar was recorded at Rs. 340.92 yesterday (15), indicating a slight depreciation of the rupee against the US currency
