Lakarcade expands luxury footprint with 8th showroom at Shangri-La Colombo

Lakarcade, Sri Lanka’s premier luxury heritage retail brand, has inaugurated its 8th showroom at Shangri-La Colombo, marking a significant milestone in its expansion. This new outlet represents the brand’s third collaboration with the Shangri-La Group, joining existing locations at One Galle Face Mall and Shangri-La Hambantota. The partnership underscores a mutual commitment to delivering world-class retail experiences while celebrating Sri Lanka’s rich artistic traditions.

Central to Lakarcade’s mission is creating sustainable market linkages for rural artisans, connecting their authentic craftsmanship directly with international travelers and global clientele. By situating traditional masterworks within premium hospitality environments, the brand bolsters the tourism-allied economy, enhances artisan livelihoods, and generates vital foreign exchange for the country.

Managing Director and CEO Anil Koswatte expressed gratitude to Shangri-La’s management for their shared vision, emphasizing that this expansion will amplify the global reach of local craftsmen while uplifting grassroots artisan communities. The official opening ceremony took place on August 7, 2026, with Deputy Chairman Darup Peiris, Manjaree Peiris, and Shangri-La Colombo General Manager Andreas Streiber in attendance. Streiber welcomed Lakarcade, highlighting the alignment with Shangri-La’s commitment to authentic cultural experiences and support for local artisans and tourism.
