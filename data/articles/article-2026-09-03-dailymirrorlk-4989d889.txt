Secondary market makes mixed moves amidst moderate volumes

The secondary market activity continued to showcase a mixed tone, with the early hours of the session exhibiting selling pressure, which reversed course towards the latter part of trading as some buying sentiment kicked in.

The overall market sentiment continued to be shaped by the ongoing geopolitical tensions and oil price fluctuations.

Trading activity levels were moderate and amongst the trades executed yesterday, starting at the short end, the maturities bearing the dates 01.05.2028, 15.10.2028 and 15.12.2028 traded from 10.05 percent to 10.10 percent.

Moving along, the 15.12.2029 maturity traded from 10.37 percent to 10.30 percent, whereas the 15.05.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 bonds traded from 10.65 percent to 10.50 percent. At the belly of the curve, the 01.02.2031 and 01.03.2031 maturities traded from 10.83 percent to 10.65 percent, followed by the 15.12.2032 maturity, which changed hands from 11.05 percent to 11.00 percent.

Within the 2033 segment, the 01.06.2033 and 01.11.2033 bonds traded from 11.55 percent to 11.35 percent. The 15.10.2034 maturity transacted from 11.80 percent to 11.65 percent and lastly at the long end, the 15.08.2036 maturity traded from 11.87 percent to 11.80 percent.

The PDMO conducted its weekly T-bill auction yesterday, where a total of Rs.80.0 billion was raised, in line with the initial offer. The three-month bills raised Rs.35.0 billion, while the six-month and 12-month bills raised Rs.25.0 billion and Rs.20.0 billion, respectively, on par with the amounts initially offered.

The weighted average yields declined across the board, with the three-month yield declining 10bps to 8.96 percent, the six-month yield declining 17bps to 9.27 percent and the 12-month yield declining 8bps to 9.81 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.328.05/US dollar, compared to Rs.327.90/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system marginally contracted to Rs.337.05 billion, from Rs.348.76 billion recorded previously.
