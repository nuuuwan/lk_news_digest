National programme planned to ensure children’s online safety

The Government has begun preliminary discussions to establish an inter-ministerial national programme to ensure children’s online safety and digital well-being.

The initial discussion was held at Temple Trees on November 2 under the leadership of Deputy Minister of Digital Economy Eranga Weeraratne.

The meeting focused on identifying the responsibilities and future roles of key ministries in developing a coordinated national framework to protect children from online abuse, cyberbullying and other digital threats.

Addressing the meeting, Secretary to the Prime Minister Pradeep Saputantri said children faced various challenges amid the rapid development of digital technology.

He stressed the need to move beyond isolated interventions and establish a national programme bringing together State institutions, experts and international organisations.

He said the proposed programme would focus on empowerment, safety, accountability-based design, protection, response and governance.

Deputy Minister Eranga Weeraratne said the Government was taking steps to provide internet access to all secondary schools by the end of this year and extend the facility to primary schools next year.

However, he highlighted that providing children with access to technology must go hand in hand with ensuring a safe digital environment.

He also said Sri Lanka hoped to provide policy leadership on children's online safety at regional and international levels by engaging with other South Asian countries.

Representatives from the World Health Organization (WHO) and UNICEF made technical presentations at the meeting, while Community Health Specialist Dr. Asiri Hewamalage shared information based on international research and evidence.

Ananda Galappatti also presented a proposed digital framework covering the entire State sector.

Representatives from seven ministries, including the Ministries of Digital Economy, Health and Mass Media, Education, Women and Child Affairs, Defence, Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs, and Youth Affairs and Sports, outlined the current situation, challenges and proposed measures relating to child protection.

Legal matters and observations from the Attorney General's Department were also discussed. Additional Secretary to the Prime Minister A.B.M. Ashraf outlined the proposed future action plan and timeline.

The meeting was attended by ministry secretaries, government officials and representatives of local and international non-governmental organisations.
