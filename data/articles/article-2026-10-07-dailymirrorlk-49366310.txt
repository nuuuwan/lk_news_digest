CBC Finance inaugurates new Head Office in Colombo

CBC Finance PLC inaugurated its new corporate Head Office this week in Colombo 08.

The move from Kandy to Colombo represents a key milestone in this corporate trajectory, aligning executive leadership with Sri Lanka’s principal economic hub while preserving its legacy of regional stability.

The opening ceremony was graced by Sharhan Muhseen, Chairman of CBC Finance PLC and Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC; Sanath Manatunge, MD/CEO of Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC and Hasrath Munasinghe, COO of Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC; who attended as the Chief Guests.
