Unrest reported at Mahara Prison; Security stepped up

Colombo, August 1 (DailyMirror): An unrest has been reported inside the Mahara Prison, prompting authorities to deploy additional security forces to the area.

Police said several special police teams have been dispatched to assess and manage the situation inside the prison.

Security has also been heightened around the prison, with Police and the Special Task Force (STF) deployed to maintain order and prevent any escalation.
