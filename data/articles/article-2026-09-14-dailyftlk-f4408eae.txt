Prime Group celebrates major milestone as The Colombo Border tops off all three towers

Prime Lands Residencies PLC, has announced the completion of the superstructures of all three towers at The Colombo Border, marking a key construction milestone.

The breakthrough follows the prior topping out of Tower Cosmos and confirms that the entire project has moved through its most complex construction phase, ahead of the original schedule.

The Colombo Border by Prime Lands Residencies PLC is a landmark residential development that redefines urban living at the gateway to Colombo. Situated in the rapidly emerging residential hub of Peliyagoda, the project has been designed to offer a complete lifestyle experience that combines convenience, connectivity, recreation, sustainability, and community living.

Spread across approximately 6.5 acres of Prime real estate, The Colombo Border comprises three residential towers, Brielle, Cosmos, and Amaa, offering thoughtfully designed two- and three-bedroom residences and few four-bedroom penthouses. Approximately 5 acres of the development have been dedicated to landscaped gardens, leisure spaces, recreational amenities, and open green areas, creating a vibrant and enriching environment where residents may enjoy a balanced lifestyle surrounded by comfort, convenience, and nature.

The completion of the superstructure of all three towers marks a major milestone for The Colombo Border, bringing one of Colombo’s largest integrated residential developments significantly closer to completion with handover set to commence from 2027 mid onwards.

The pace at which the project has advanced reflects the strength of the construction planning behind it, as well as the coordinated execution of two specialised engineering teams working in parallel across the site. Prime Construction Ltd., the Group’s in-house construction arm, leads the construction of two towers, while Sanken Lanka Ltd., leads the construction of the other tower.

The successful completion of the superstructure of all three towers, achieved through the coordinated efforts of two dedicated construction teams, reflects the meticulous planning, seamless execution, and engineering excellence of Prime Lands Residencies PLC.

Prime Lands Residencies PLC Chairperson Sandamini Perera said: “The completion of the superstructures across all three towers represents another important achievement for the Group and for the wider development of the project.” She noted that maintaining this pace of construction, without compromising on quality, reflected the Group’s consistent commitment to delivering its projects on schedule, adding, “The trust placed in the Prime brand by buyers is valued at every stage of the project. It represents an investment in which the company will continue to honor with quality engineering and construction excellence as The Colombo Border moves into its next phase.”

Redefining modern condominium living in Sri Lanka, The Colombo Border offers residents an exceptional lifestyle through its strategic location, expansive green spaces, premium amenities, sustainable design, and a strong sense of community. The development has earned the confidence of homeowners and investors alike. With Tower Cosmos now completely sold out with limited two and three BR apartments are available starting from Rs. 48 million upwards, the project reflects The Colombo Border’s outstanding market appeal and long-term investment potential. The Colombo Border presents an ideal opportunity for existing homeowners looking to expand their investment portfolio, as well as new buyers seeking a future-ready home in one of Colombo’s fastest-growing residential destinations.
