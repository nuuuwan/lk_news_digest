Number affected by El Niño climbs to 84,546 as vegetable prices set to rise

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The number of people affected by the worsening impact of El Niño conditions has increased to 84,546, raising concerns over a possible surge in vegetable prices in the coming days.

According to the latest figures released by the Disaster Management Centre (DMC), 24,144 families have been affected by the weather conditions as of yesterday.

The number of affected people has risen from 81,868 recorded on Tuesday (25), indicating a further increase in the impact of the prevailing conditions.

However, the number of affected families has declined from 25,628 reported on Tuesday.

The worsening weather conditions are also expected to affect vegetable supplies, with prices likely to increase sharply in the next few days.

The Northern, Eastern, North Central, Southern and North Western provinces are affected by the dry spell prevailing at the moment.

Former President of the Manning Market Traders Association S. M. Upasena told the Daily Mirror that vegetable prices could increase in the coming days. He said prices are somewhat stable now, but could soar during the coming days. "The dry spell had resulted in reduction of water available for cultivation of vegetables. This could result in soaring of prices," he said.

The price of beans stood at Rs. 300 per kilo, cabbage at Rs. 150, carrots at Rs. 200, leeks at Rs. 150, and capsicum at Rs. 400. Upasena said these prices could increase during the coming days.
