Parliament approves second reading of Budget 2027

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Parliament approved the second reading of the 2027 budget by a majority of 98 votes with 158  members voting in favour and 60 MPs against. Four members were absent during the time of voting.

MPs Dilith Jayaweera, Nayana Wasalatilaka, Kadar Masthan and Jeewan Thondaman were among those who were absent.
