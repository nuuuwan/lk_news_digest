No exam papers opened or leaked: Exams chief

Colombo, Aug. 13 (Daily Mirror) — Commissioner General of Examinations A.K. Indika Kumari Liyanage has categorically denied rumors of an examination question paper leak, assuring candidates that the examinations scheduled for tomorrow (14) will proceed as planned.

In a special statement, Liyanage said no packets containing question papers had been opened and that no examination materials had been issued or taken outside the designated examination coordinating centres.

She urged candidates not to panic or be misled by false information circulating on social media and other platforms.

The Commissioner General called on all candidates t report to their respective examination centres on time, according to their assigned timetables.
