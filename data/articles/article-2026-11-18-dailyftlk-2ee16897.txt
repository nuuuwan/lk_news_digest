Cabinet green lights revised intellectual property fees

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved submitting to Parliament for concurrence the Intellectual Property Regulations No. 01 of 2026, which revise the fees charged by the National Intellectual Property Office for its services.

The regulations were prepared under the Intellectual Property Act No. 36 of 2003 and published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2509/17 dated 6 October 2026. They update the fee structure to reflect contemporary circumstances.

The office's fees were last revised by regulations published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2146/37 on 27 October 2019.

The proposal to this effect was submitted by Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe.
