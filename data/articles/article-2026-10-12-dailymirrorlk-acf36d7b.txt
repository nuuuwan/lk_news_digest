World Travel Awards 2026  City of Dreams Sri Lanka earns dual recognition

City of Dreams Sri Lanka celebrates two prestigious titles at World Travel Awards 2026: Asia’s Leading New Resort 2026 and Sri Lanka’s Leading Luxury Resort 2026

Named Asia’s Leading New Resort and Sri Lanka’s Leading Luxury Resort

City of Dreams Sri Lanka has marked a significant milestone in its first year of operation, earning two prestigious titles at World Travel Awards 2026: Asia’s Leading New Resort 2026 and Sri Lanka’s Leading Luxury Resort 2026.

The recognition places City of Dreams Sri Lanka firmly on both the regional and national hospitality map, reinforcing its emergence as a new destination within Asia’s luxury tourism landscape and its contribution to the evolution of Sri Lanka’s premium hospitality offering.

The Asia’s Leading New Resort title is particularly significant as the property establishes its identity within a competitive regional market, while the Sri Lanka’s Leading Luxury Resort accolade reflects the growing depth and sophistication of the country’s luxury tourism proposition.

As South Asia’s first fully integrated resort, City of Dreams Sri Lanka brings together luxury accommodation, dining, entertainment, wellness, retail, business and events within a single destination in the heart of Colombo. Anchored by Nüwa, its ultra-luxury hotel and Cinnamon Life, the destination has been developed to offer guests a seamless spectrum of experiences, combining international standards of hospitality with the distinctive character and warmth of Sri Lanka.

City of Dreams Sri Lanka Vice President and Property General Manager Michael Habashi said, “We are deeply honoured to receive these two World Travel Awards in our first year. They represent an important milestone for City of Dreams Sri Lanka but more importantly, they are a reflection of the passion, commitment and attention to detail demonstrated by our colleagues every day. We set out to create a destination that would bring a new dimension to luxury hospitality and integrated resort experiences in Sri Lanka and this recognition gives us confidence as we continue that journey. Our ambition is not simply to build on what we have achieved but to keep raising the bar, creating experiences that resonate with our guests and contributing meaningfully to Sri Lanka’s position as a leading destination in the region.”
