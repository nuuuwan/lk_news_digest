From SL to NY Times Square: Infinite Grace Foundation launches in the US

From left: Director Siyana Rushdhun, Co-Founders Rushdhun Ramzy and Stephanie Siriwardhana

The Infinite Grace Foundation, founded by Stephanie Siriwardhana, has launched its United States chapter, marking a new phase in the organisation’s development from a Sri Lankan humanitarian initiative into an international organisation.

The launch took place in New York’s iconic Times Square, marking a significant milestone in the Foundation’s international expansion.

The US chapter, co-founded by Stephanie Siriwardhana and Rushdhun Ramzy, alongside directors Siyana Rushdhun and Dushyanth Weeraman, aims to extend this mission beyond borders, serving American communities of all backgrounds while strengthening ties with Sri Lanka.

Planned initiatives include an “I SEE YOU” Mental Health Carnival in the United States, mental wellbeing programs for children and young people, partnerships to explore prison rehabilitation and second-chance opportunities, and a US fundraising gala to support the Foundation’s growing mission.

Founder and Chairperson Stephanie Siriwardhana said: “What began with a simple act of kindness in Sri Lanka has grown into something I could never have imagined. Seeing Infinite Grace in Times Square is incredibly emotional, but this isn’t about a billboard. It’s about taking the love, dignity and hope that started back home and sharing it with the world. We want to build bridges between countries, create meaningful opportunities and remind people everywhere that they matter.”

Infinite Grace Foundation USA Co-Founder Rushdhun Ramzy said: “Our vision is to build an organisation that connects communities, expertise and resources across borders. We want to create a meaningful impact here in the United States while opening new opportunities to strengthen the life-changing work that began in Sri Lanka.”

The Infinite Grace Foundation has spent over a decade championing prison rehabilitation, mental wellbeing, education and humanitarian assistance. Its national mental health movement, “I SEE YOU”, has reached communities through schools, public conversations and free wellbeing experiences, culminating in its inaugural fundraising gala at Cinnamon Life in September 2026.

With its guiding values of Love, Dignity and Purpose, Infinite Grace Foundation’s US expansion represents more than geographical growth. It is the beginning of a new international chapter dedicated to creating lasting change through compassion, collaboration and measurable impact.
