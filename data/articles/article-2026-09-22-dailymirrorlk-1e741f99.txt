15 convicted over Easter attacks get sentences ranging from 200 to 260 years

The High Court Trial-at-Bar today sentenced 15 accused convicted over the 2019 Easter Sunday terror attacks to prison terms ranging from 200 to 260 years.

The first accused, Mohamed Ibrahim Mohamed Naufer alias Naufer Moulavi, was sentenced to 220 years of rigorous imprisonment. The second, third, fourth and fifth accused were each sentenced to 240 years, while the sixth and seventh accused were each sentenced to 200 years.

The eighth accused was sentenced to 260 years, while the ninth and tenth accused were each sentenced to 220 years. The eleventh and fifteenth accused were each sentenced to 200 years, while the sixteenth and eighteenth accused were each sentenced to 220 years. The nineteenth accused was sentenced to 200 years.

The High Court Trial-at-Bar, comprising Justices Navaratne Marasinghe, Ramanathan Kannan and Sujeewa Nishshanka, delivered the verdict.

Furthermore, the properties of all 15 convicted accused were ordered to be confiscated.

The verdict was delivered as the final judgment in the trial relating to the Easter Sunday terror attacks, which claimed the lives of 269 people and injured more than 500 others.
