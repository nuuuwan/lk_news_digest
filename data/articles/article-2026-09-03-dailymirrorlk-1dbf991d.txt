Tea sale average eases to Rs.1,174 in August

The national tea sale average edged lower in August from the previous month and remained below the year-ago levels, while the dollar-denominated average recorded a sharper decline, the latest industry data showed.

The national sale average fell to Rs.1,174.46 per kilogramme in August, down Rs.1.64 from Rs.1,176.10 in July. In US dollar terms, however, the average increased marginally to US $ 3.53, from US $ 3.50 a month earlier.

Compared with August 2025, the rupee-denominated average was Rs.7.81 lower than Rs.1,182.27, while its dollar value dropped by a sharper US $ 0.39, from US $ 3.92.

The divergence was also evident in the cumulative performance, with the national average for the first eight months of 2026 rising in rupee terms while weakening when measured in dollars.

The cumulative national sale average stood at Rs.1,165.66, up Rs.9.29 from Rs.1,156.37 in the corresponding period of 2025. In dollar terms, however, the average fell to US $ 3.62, from US $ 3.87, a decline of US $ 0.25.

Among the elevations, the Low Grown teas remained the strongest performer in rupee terms, averaging Rs.1,276.83 in August. Although this was Rs.16.23 below July, it was Rs.25.74 higher than the Rs.1,251.09 recorded a year earlier.

The High Grown teas averaged Rs.1,057.06, down Rs.4.89 month-on-month and Rs.49.14 from August 2025, while the Medium Growns averaged Rs.954.82, marginally higher than July but Rs.74.28 below a year earlier.

On a cumulative basis, the High Grown and Low Grown teas recorded gains in rupee terms against 2025, while the Medium Growns declined. However, all three elevations recorded lower cumulative averages in US dollar terms, highlighting the weaker foreign-currency value of the tea prices this year.
