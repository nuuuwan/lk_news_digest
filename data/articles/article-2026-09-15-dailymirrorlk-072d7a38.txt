El Niño: Disaster drills ordered in landslide-prone districts

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka is stepping up disaster preparedness in several landslide-prone districts as authorities anticipate a shift towards wetter weather under the influence of El Niño over the coming months.

The decision was taken at the fifth meeting of the Special Cabinet Subcommittee and Officials’ Committee appointed to take necessary measures to address the impact of the El Niño climate phenomenon, held at the Presidential Secretariat yesterday (14).

The meeting was chaired by Minister of Environment Dr. Dhammika Patabendi.

As the impact of the El Niño climate phenomenon is expected to transition from the prevailing dry conditions to wetter weather over the next two months, the committees discussed measures to prepare for the anticipated changes in weather conditions.

Particular attention was given to the possibility of increased rainfall triggering disasters such as floods and landslides. The committees decided to raise public awareness and conduct disaster preparedness drills to familiarise communities with the appropriate measures to be taken during emergencies.

The meeting also decided to launch a public awareness programme through electronic and print media.

Discussions were held on the importance of establishing operational centres at the Divisional Secretariat level to ensure communities are adequately prepared to respond to potential disasters.

Meanwhile, the committees reviewed the progress of relief measures being implemented for people affected by the prevailing El Niño conditions, as well as planned measures to address the anticipated changes in weather.
