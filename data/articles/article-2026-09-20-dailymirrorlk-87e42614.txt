Landslide warnings issued for six districts

Colombo, September 20 (Daily Mirror)- The National Building Research Organisation (NBRO) has issued landslide early warnings for six districts following adverse weather conditions prevailing in some parts of the country.

The warnings will be in effect until 07.00 a.m. tomorrow (21), the NBRO said.

Accordingly, the warnings have been issued for Galle, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Nuwara Eliya and Ratnapura districts.
