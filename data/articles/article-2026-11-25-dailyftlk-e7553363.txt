Sri Lanka must use foreign policy to protect Palestinian rights: Sajith

Calls for an end to what he describes as the genocide in Gaza and the West Bank

Backs two-State solution with Israel and Palestine living side by side in peace

Says protecting human rights must be one of the 12 pillars of foreign policy

Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa on Monday urged the Government to use Sri Lanka’s foreign policy to protect the rights of Palestinians and to support a two-State solution under which Israel and Palestine live side by side in peace.

Speaking in Parliament, Premadasa said Sri Lanka must champion the cause of human rights.

“We must ensure that the rights of the Palestinians who are suffering in the West Bank and Gaza are protected,” he said.

He said Sri Lanka should support the existing international agreement on two States living side by side in a peaceful and harmonious environment. He referred to United Nations Resolutions 242 and 338 and said they had been reinforced by later international accords, including the Abraham Accords and the Camp David Accords, as well as other unanimous conclusions reached by the international community.

“We must ensure that through our foreign policy the genocide in Gaza and the West Bank is stopped. We must protect the rights of the Palestinians and also ensure that the two States, Israel and Palestine, live in harmony and conduct their State activities through peaceful approaches,” Premadasa said.

He listed the protection of human rights and democratic values, together with respect for international law, as two of the 12 pillars on which he said Sri Lanka’s foreign policy should be judged.

“We must ensure that we respect international laws, international organisations, international conventions, customs and practices,” he said.
