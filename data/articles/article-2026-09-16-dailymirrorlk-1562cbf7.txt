Namal Rajapaksa remanded

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, who was named as a suspect and produced before court by the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) in connection with alleged financial irregularities and money laundering linked to the purchase of Airbus aircraft for SriLankan Airlines, was ordered to be remanded until September 23 by Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena today.

After considering all submissions, the Magistrate said the order on the bail application would be made on September 23 and ordered that the suspect be remanded in custody until then.

During the proceedings, it was submitted that steps had been taken on September 11 to name Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa as the fourth suspect in the case pending before the Fort Magistrate’s Court against former SriLankan Airlines Chief Executive Officer Kapila Chandrasena and his wife over an alleged bribery incident relating to the purchase of aircraft for SriLankan Airlines.

It was further submitted that when the case was called today, the suspect had already been remanded by the Colombo Chief Magistrate in connection with investigations conducted by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) into the same Airbus transaction.

Accordingly, Namal Rajapaksa was produced before the Fort Magistrate’s Court today through prison officials.
