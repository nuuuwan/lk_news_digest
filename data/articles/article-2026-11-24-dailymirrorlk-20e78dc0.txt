Marikkar questions appointments and alleged interference at Foreign Employment Bureau

Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP S. M. Marikkar has questioned the appointment of several individuals to the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE), alleging political favouritism and interference in the institution's administration.

Speaking in Parliament yesterday, Marikkar claimed that some directors appointed to the Bureau from the foreign employment sector had failed to meet the conditions attached to their appointments.

He specifically questioned the appointment of an individual named Kamal Weerathunga, alleging that he had failed to meet the requirement of facilitating overseas employment for at least 30 workers annually.

He further questioned the authority under which the Deputy Minister's private secretary maintains an office within the SLBFE and allegedly intervenes in its administrative affairs.

"How can this individual issue instructions to the Board of Directors? Are these actions not unlawful?" he asked.

Marikkar argued that the staff of a Minister or Deputy Minister should operate from the respective Ministry rather than from institutions functioning under it.

Referring to previous cases in which former Ministers faced legal action over the alleged misuse of resources belonging to institutions under their ministries, he questioned whether similar conduct was taking place at the Foreign Employment Bureau.

He called on the Deputy Minister to respond to the allegations and explain the appointments and alleged interference in the institution's administration.
