Mahanayake Theras had received wrong information: Justice Minister

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror)- Claiming that the Mahanayake Theras had received wrong information regarding the extension of the retirement age of judges, Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara said yesterday that they briefed the Mahanayake Theras about the true purpose of the move.

The Minister told reporters after meeting the Mahanayake Theras in Kandy yesterday that they visited the Mahanayake Theras and briefed them about various concerns raised in society about the government's policy decision to extend the retirement age of judges.

He said they explained the government's true purpose of the proposal and what led to such a move, adding that the Mahanayake Theras understood the situation.

Responding to a question, the Minister refuted claims that the proposal is targeting the current Chief Justice, stating that the retirement age is extended for all judges in Magistrate Courts to the Supreme Court as per the proposal.

He said the extension of the retirement age of judges is only one measure in easing the backlog of cases and added that several other measures, including increasing the number of courts, recruiting new judges and filling vacancies in the Government Analytical Department, are being taken.

A government delegation led by Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara and Health Minister Nalinda Jayatissa visited the Mahanayake Theras yesterday morning to brief the Mahanayake Theras regarding the extension of the retirement age of judges.
