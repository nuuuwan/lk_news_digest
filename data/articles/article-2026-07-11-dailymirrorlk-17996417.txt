Government committed to equal access to education for all children: PM

Colombo, July 11 (Daily Mirror) - The government is committed to ensuring equal access to education for all children, while transforming the President's Fund into a truly people-oriented institution, Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya said.

Addressing the "Sarasavi Dirio Abhiman 2026" programme at Temple Trees today (11), the Prime Minister said no child, including those with special needs such as neurodivergent conditions, autism and dyslexia, should be excluded from the education system.

She said universities have a responsibility to provide the necessary accessibility facilities for students with disabilities and that the government is working to improve inclusive education through assistive technologies, digitalisation, disability-friendly public transport and accessible infrastructure.

The Prime Minister said that students with disabilities are a valuable human resource capable of contributing to the country's sustainable development and said the government's ultimate goal is to build a more inclusive and civilised society that recognises the value of every individual.
