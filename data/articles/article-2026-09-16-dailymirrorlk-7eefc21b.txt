RDA urges headlights on expressways

The Road Development Authority (RDA) has urged motorists to switch on their headlights while travelling on expressways due to adverse weather conditions and reduced visibility.

The RDA also advised drivers to maintain a safe distance from other vehicles and strictly follow instructions displayed on electronic signboards along the expressways.

Motorists have been urged to exercise extra caution and adjust their driving according to the prevailing weather conditions to ensure road safety.
