SLID celebrates 25 years of advancing corporate governance in Sri Lanka

The Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID) will mark a 25-year journey of advancing corporate governance and board leadership in Sri Lanka at its Annual Members’ Meeting 2026 and 25th Anniversary Celebration today, at the Cinnamon Grand Colombo.

The evening will bring together directors, business leaders, members and partners to celebrate SLID’s contribution to strengthening the quality of governance and leadership in Sri Lanka, while looking ahead to the evolving demands placed on boards and organisations.

Adding distinction to the occasion will be Chief Guest G. Pradeep Saputhanthri, Secretary to the Prime Minister of Sri Lanka, and Guest of Honour Dewi Gustina Tobing, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the Republic of Indonesia to Sri Lanka and Dean of the Diplomatic Corps in Sri Lanka.

The evening will also feature a keynote address by Mahinda Samarasinghe, Former Ambassador of Sri Lanka to the United States of America, on the theme: “Tariffs: Defining the New World Order” How trade, geopolitics and economic power are being reshaped.

The 25th anniversary celebration will pay tribute to the individuals and organisations that have been part of SLID’s journey, with special recognition of its past CEOs, Chairpersons and valued partners whose contributions have helped shape the Institute over the years.

Looking towards its next chapter, SLID will also unveil the relaunch of its website and the launch of the SLID App, strengthening its digital presence and creating a more connected and accessible experience for its members. The enhanced digital platforms will bring SLID’s programmes, knowledge resources, insights and member engagement closer to its community, wherever they are.
