17 empty shipping containers fall overboard off Colombo

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - Seventeen empty shipping containers fell into the sea from the Maldives-flagged cargo vessel MV MSS GRAPHENE within the outer harbour limits of Colombo early this morning, 1 August 2026.

According to a report received by the Colombo Information Fusion Centre (IFC), rough sea conditions are believed to have caused the containers to tumble overboard. The vessel had reportedly departed Malé, Maldives, bound for the Port of Colombo on 28 July.

Following the incident, the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources issued an emergency alert regarding the drifting hazards.

The department said that the containers fell overboard approximately 17 nautical miles west of the Port of Colombo,.

Authorities said that prevailing ocean currents present a high possibility that the containers could drift toward the coastline, particularly along the coastal stretch between Beruwala and Negombo.

The Sri Lanka Navy has deployed craft to the area to monitor the situation and maintain continuous observation of the affected sea region.

Meanwhile, the Maritime Rescue Coordination Centre (MRCC) Colombo has issued a navigational warning to alert all vessels operating in the vicinity. Local fishermen and mariners who spot any of the drifting containers have been instructed to immediately notify the Radio Communication Centre of the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources or report to officials at the nearest fisheries harbour.
