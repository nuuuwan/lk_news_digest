United Motors sees 11.31% stake change hands in Rs.3.08bn deal

United Motors Lanka PLC (UML) yesterday disclosed that 11.31 percent of its issued shares changed hands through three transactions, worth a combined Rs.3.08 billion.

UML said R.R. Takahashi sold a total of 114 million shares in the company to M.A. Yaseen, across three transactions carried out on July 24, July 31 and August 06.
