Parliamentary seat falls vacant

A vacancy has arisen in Parliament following the resignation of Sri Lanka Muslim Congress (SLMC) National List MP M.S. Abdul Wahid, the Election Commission has been informed.

Acting Secretary General of Parliament Hansa Abeyratne informed the Chairman of the Election Commission in writing that Abdul Wahid had submitted his resignation from the post of Member of Parliament.

According to the resignation letter, the resignation will take effect from September 14, 2026.

The Election Commission was informed of the vacancy in accordance with Section 64(5) of the Parliamentary Elections Act No. 1 of 1981.
