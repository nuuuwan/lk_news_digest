People’s Bank rated above private banks after two-notch Fitch upgrade

Fitch Ratings upgraded People’s Bank’s National Long-Term Rating by two notches to ‘AA+(lka)’ from ‘AA-(lka)’ with a Stable Outlook, placing the state-owned lender ahead of all private sector commercial banks in Sri Lanka on Fitch’s national rating scale, the bank claimed.

With the upgrade, People’s Bank now jointly holds the highest National Long-Term Rating among domestic commercial banks, which the bank described as an important endorsement of its underlying strengths, extensive customer franchise and dominant position within Sri Lanka’s banking industry.

In announcing the rating action, Fitch recognised the significantly stronger domestic franchise of People’s Bank relative to large private sector banks, highlighting its market-leading customer reach, extensive branch network, strong deposit franchise and established market position, which provide significant advantages in funding strength and stability.

The upgrade follows Fitch’s recalibration of Sri Lanka’s national rating scale after the recent improvement in the country’s sovereign credit rating. The bank credits this revision with enabling greater recognition of its distinctive competitive strengths and relative creditworthiness within the domestic banking sector.

People’s Bank Chairman Chandana Guniyangoda called the upgrade “a strong recognition of the confidence and trust the institution has earned over six and a half decades.”

“Being rated above all private sector banks is particularly encouraging and reinforces the importance of combining our national development mandate with sound commercial banking principles, strong governance and financial discipline,” he added.

The upgrade comes as the bank pursues a strategic transformation of its business model, with greater emphasis on commercial competitiveness, operational efficiency, customer-centricity and digital innovation.

Chief Executive Officer/General Manager Clive Fonseka termed the upgrade “an important endorsement of the fundamental strengths of People’s Bank and the direction in which we are moving.”

“Our objective is not merely to preserve our position as one of Sri Lanka’s largest banks, but to establish ourselves among the best-performing and most resilient financial institutions in the country,” he stated.
