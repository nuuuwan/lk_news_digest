Sri Lanka targets full digital ID implementation by August 2027

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror)- ​Sri Lanka is set to issue its first National Digital Identity (Digital ID) this year, with nationwide implementation targeted for completion by August 2027, unlocking a strategic digitalization initiative that has remained stalled for over a decade.

​Speaking at a media briefing to announce the SLT Mobitel financial results for the first half of 2026 held in Colombo this week , Deputy Minister of Digital Economy Eranga Weeraratne confirmed that the long-delayed initiative has entered its active rollout phase.

​“We have established Digital ID, the project is currently in the implementation phase, and we are hopeful to have the first Digital ID issued within the course of the year and also to have the first full implementation of Digital ID by August next year,” Weliange stated.

​He added that the project had been put on hold and was in discussions since 2012, but the Government has now been able to unlock it and progress with its execution.

​According to the Deputy Minister, the Digital ID framework will function in tandem with the National Data Exchange, which is being established to integrate scattered state sector databases and eliminate institutional silos. The Government is also developing the 'Supiri App' to provide a unified single-window portal for citizens to access public services seamlessly.

​Weliange noted that establishing a secure digital identity system is a vital component in transitioning Sri Lanka from a cash-heavy society toward a cashless digital payments ecosystem, while accelerating sectoral digitalization in smart education, healthcare, agriculture, transport, and tourism.

​To support nationwide digital adoption, the Government is working to expand geographic network coverage from 94 percent to 98 percent over a two-to-three-year fast-track development period. This expansion will require the installation of 600 to 800 new telecommunication towers, alongside ongoing school fiberization initiatives, to ensure a minimum speed of 2 Mbps across all Provinces.
