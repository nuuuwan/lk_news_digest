Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Melstacorp and Ceylon Tobacco Company with the turnover crossing Rs. 16.8 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in C T Holdings, Cargills and John Keells Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Sunshine Holdings, Sierra Cables and Haycarb whilst retail interest was noted in LOLC Finance, Browns Investments and Cable Solutions. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Food & Staples Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to C T Holdings and Cargills ) whilst the sector index gained 1.91%. The share price of C T Holdings increased by Rs 25.75 (4.91%) to close at Rs 550.50. The share price of Cargills lost Rs 5.50 (0.80%) to close at Rs 680.75.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sunshine Holdings) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.85%. The share price of Sunshine Holdings moved down by Rs 0.30 (1.02%) to close at Rs 29.20.

John Keells Holdings and Sierra Cables were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a gain of Rs 0.10 (0.51%) to close at Rs 19.90. The share price of Sierra Cables closed flat at Rs 34.60.
