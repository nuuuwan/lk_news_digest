Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Carson Cumberbatch, Dialog Axiata and SMB Leasing with the turnover crossing Rs. 595 Mn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in John Keells Holdings and Citizens Development Business Finance. Mixed interest was observed in Haycarb, Commercial Bank and Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, SMB Leasing nonvoting and Waskaduwa Beach Resort. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

Diversified Financials sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index lost 0.77%. The share price of Commercial Bank closed flat at Rs 204.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Haycarb and Tokyo Cement Company) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.55%. The share price of Haycarb closed flat at Rs 210.00. The share price of Tokyo Cement Company Non-Voting declined by Rs 1.40 (2.28%) to close at Rs 60.10.

John Keells Holdings and Citizens Development Business Finance were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of John Keells Holdings decreased by Rs 0.10 (0.53%) to close at Rs 18.90. The share price of Citizens Development Business Finance lost Rs 1.00 (2.86%) to close at Rs 34.00.
