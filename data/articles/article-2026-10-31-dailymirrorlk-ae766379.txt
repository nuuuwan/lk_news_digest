Sri Lanka needs men and women of spirituality

It is sad to see Sri Lanka, although blessed with four major religions, beset with social disharmony. The main reason for this sad situation is lack of spirituality in the lives of many Sri Lankans including  politicians and religious leaders. Sri Lankans need to transcend their practice of religiosity and learn to practice spirituality which embodies virtuous living. Call to transcend religiosity  does not mean denial of genuine religiosity. Sri Lanka needs men and women of compassionate neighbourly love to promote social harmony and a better quality of life for its people.
