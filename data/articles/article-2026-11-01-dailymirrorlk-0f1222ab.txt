SLPP to launch islandwide village-level meetings tomorrow

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) will launch a series of islandwide village-level meetings tomorrow (November 2), beginning in the Colombo District, under the guidance of its National Organiser, MP Namal Rajapaksa, party Operations Head Johnston Fernando said.

Speaking at a meeting with SLPP constituency organisers in the Colombo District, Fernando said the meetings would be conducted across the country five days a week.

He said the campaign aimed to educate the public about what the party described as unfulfilled government promises and alleged financial irregularities under the current administration.

Fernando instructed party organisers to highlight several issues, including the release of 323 containers, the alleged coal tender irregularities, the Central Bank hacking incident and funds allegedly transferred to incorrect accounts.

He also asked organisers to draw public attention to election promises on tax exemptions, including the proposed removal of taxes on essential goods, healthcare and school supplies, which he claimed had not been fulfilled by the Government.
