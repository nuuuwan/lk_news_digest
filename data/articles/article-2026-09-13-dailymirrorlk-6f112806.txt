Gnanasara Thera reportedly missing after SC ruling

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Bodu Bala Sena (BBS) General Secretary Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera is reportedly missing after the Supreme Court annulled the presidential pardon granted to him in 2019.

According to the Sunday Island, prison officials had visited the Thera’s temple in Nawala, Rajagiriya, to take steps to return him to prison to serve the remainder of his six-year sentence.

However, monks at the temple had reportedly told prison officials that they were unaware of his current whereabouts.

The Supreme Court recently ruled that the 2019 presidential pardon granted to Gnanasara Thera was null and void, holding that the decision was arbitrary and violated public trust and principles of natural justice.

Gnanasara Thera had been sentenced to six years in prison for contempt of court and had served around nine months before then-President Maithripala Sirisena granted him a presidential pardon in May 2019.

Following the Supreme Court ruling, he is required to serve the remaining portion of his sentence, subject to applicable legal and prison procedures.

Meanwhile, a senior BBS official told the Daily Mirror that ethnic tensions had declined after Zahran Hashim was identified as the leader of the National Thawheed Jama'ath, which the official said was responsible for the coordinated Easter Sunday bomb attacks.

The official also claimed that Gnanasara Thera had never attempted to create ethnic conflict in the country.

However, representatives of the All Ceylon Jamiyyathul Ulama (ACJU), during a meeting with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, expressed appreciation to the Government for maintaining ethnic harmony over the past two years.

The ACJU representatives also stressed the need for action against hate speech being disseminated through social media.
