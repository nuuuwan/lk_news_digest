Naval, fishing communities warned against venturing into deep sea as cyclone Murasu intensifies

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Meteorology has issued a red warning for strong winds and rough seas, advising naval personnel and fishing communities to refrain from venturing into deep-sea areas bounded by 10°N–15°N and 80°E–90°E until further notice, following the intensification of a deep depression in the Southeast Bay of Bengal into Cyclone Murasu.

According to the Department, Cyclone Murasu was located near latitude 12.1°N and longitude 85.3°E at around 11.30 a.m. on November 1, 2026.

The cyclone is expected to move west-northwestwards and further intensify into a Severe Cyclonic Storm over the West-Central Bay of Bengal by the morning of November 2. It is then likely to move northwestwards before crossing the Andhra Pradesh coast in India as a Severe Cyclonic Storm between midnight on November 2 and the early hours of November 3, 2026.

The Department warned that wind speeds over the affected sea areas could reach 70–80 kmph at times, accompanied by heavy thundershowers. Sea conditions along the coastal belt stretching from Mannar to Trincomalee, via Kankesanthurai, are also expected to become very rough.

In view of the deteriorating weather conditions, naval and fishing communities have been strongly advised not to venture into the identified deep-sea areas until further notice.

Those already operating in the affected waters have been urged to move to safer locations without delay.
