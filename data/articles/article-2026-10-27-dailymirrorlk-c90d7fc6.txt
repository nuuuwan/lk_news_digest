SLT-Mobitel unveils Apple iPhone 18 Pro series

Deputy General Manager Enterprise Business Asanka Jayawardena hands over the latest Apple iPhone 18 Pro to a customer, accompanied by Senior Manager Enterprise Business Randy Janze (left) and Account Manager Enterprise Business Rukshani Wickrama Seneviratne

SLT-Mobitel Mobile has ushered in the next era of mobile technology, unveiling the highly anticipated Apple iPhone 18 Pro series in Sri Lanka, through Sri Lanka’s most reliable 5G network.

Poised to redefine premium smartphone experiences in the island, the revolutionary next-gen line-up blends Apple’s peak innovation with SLT-Mobitel’s ultra-fast connectivity.

At an event, held recently, SLT-Mobitel handed over the first iPhone 18 Pro series devices to the customers, providing the opportunity to experience 5G on Apple’s latest smartphones.

The launch showcases SLT-Mobitel’s drive to equip Sri Lankans with premium global technology and maximising its potential through advanced network capabilities.

iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max are now available through SLT-Mobitel, together with the iPhone Duo, widening the range of Apple devices on offer. With Mobitel’s reliable connectivity and trusted service, SLT-Mobitel continues to strengthen its position as the trusted choice for the customers seeking the best and latest Apple devices.

Mobitel’s 5G coverage supports everyday digital needs such as high-quality streaming, faster downloads, video calls and improved connectivity on compatible devices within the 5G coverage areas.

To mark the launch, the customers who move to iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone 18 Pro Max 5G with SLT-Mobitel stand a chance to win 2TB of Mobitel data, an Apple Watch Series 12 or a MacBook Neo. Selected customers will also receive Apple AirPods 5 and an original 20W power adapter.

SLT-Mobitel is dedicated to connecting Sri Lankans with the latest technology through its premium smartphone portfolio and mobile network services.

The availability of iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo highlight the company’s focus on offering the customers greater choice, convenience and a superior 5G experience. The customers can visit the SLT-Mobitel outlets for more information. Terms and conditions apply.
