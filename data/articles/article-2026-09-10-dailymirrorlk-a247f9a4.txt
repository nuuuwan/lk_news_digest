Why Sri Lanka is one of Asia’s most underrated countries, says world traveller Cameron

Sri Lanka is one of the most underrated countries in Asia, according to world traveller Cameron, who praised the island’s natural beauty, wildlife, food, historic sites and hospitality in an interview published on Instagram.

Cameron, who said he had visited all 54 countries in Asia, was asked which country he felt was the most underrated.

“There's many, but I think that one stands out against the rest, and that is Sri Lanka,” he said.

Describing Sri Lanka as a small country with an incredible variety of attractions, Cameron highlighted Sigiriya as one of the places that stood out to him.

He described the famous rock fortress as a giant rock rising from the middle of the country and said the views from the top were so spectacular that they almost looked AI-generated.

He also praised Sri Lanka’s wildlife and safaris, particularly the opportunity to see elephants in their natural environment.

Cameron said Colombo was another attraction, describing it as a vibrant city that was busy without being overwhelmingly crowded.

He also had high praise for Sri Lankan food, calling it “so good” and saying it was still underrated.

In the south, Cameron highlighted Galle and its Dutch colonial heritage. He said the historic Galle Fort, beaches, greenery and street cafés gave the area a relaxed coastal atmosphere and, in some places, reminded him of the Caribbean.

“Beautiful people, insane wildlife, beaches, hospitality is great,” Cameron said, summing up what he liked about Sri Lanka.

Cameron became the youngest person to visit every country in the world at the age of 25. He completed his journey across all 195 countries with North Korea as his final destination.

He has said that growing up with debilitating obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) played a major role in his decision to undertake the seven-year journey, which he viewed as a form of real-world exposure therapy.

Today, Cameron shares his experiences to encourage people to build resilience, manage challenges and rethink what they believe is possible.

He is also the founder of Humanity Effect, a non-profit organisation that has supported more than 1,000 children in Nigeria through education and safe housing.

His story and humanitarian work have been featured by international media including Forbes, CNN, CBS, FOX, NBC and Netflix.
