EY to help financial institutions unlock next phase of sustainability reporting

As the financial institutions enter the second year of SLFRS S1 & S2 reporting, the focus is increasingly shifting beyond compliance towards delivering sustainability disclosures that support informed decision-making and long-term value creation.

To support organisations navigating this next phase, EY will host its advanced workshop ‘Beyond Climate First: Building Decision-Useful Sustainability Disclosures’, tailored for the banks, finance companies and insurance companies that have already completed their first year of sustainability reporting.

The workshop will be facilitated by Ernst & Young Sri Lanka Partner Climate Change and Sustainability Services Sampath Jayawardena.

With over two decades of experience spanning sustainability, climate change and assurance, Jayawardena is a recognised leader in the field and a passionate advocate for knowledge sharing.

Through practical insights, real-world experiences and lessons learned from early adopters, he will guide the participants through the evolving sustainability reporting landscape and the opportunities that lie ahead.

The participants will gain valuable insights into the transition from Climate-Related Risks and Opportunities (CRROs) to Sustainability-Related Risks and Opportunities (SRROs), reassessment of existing disclosures, comparative information requirements, recent amendments to SLFRS S2 and practical approaches to climate scenario analysis.

The session will also provide a clear roadmap to help the organisations strengthen their reporting practices and prepare for the next phase of sustainability disclosures leading into 2027.

The session is designed for CFOs, CROs, corporate strategy professionals, sustainability teams, internal auditors and other stakeholders involved in sustainability reporting and governance.
