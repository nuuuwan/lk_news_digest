13-member committee appointed to oversee anti-corruption action plan

Colombo, Oct. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A 13-member committee has been appointed to oversee the implementation of measures assigned to Parliament under the National Anti-Corruption Action Plan 2025–2029, Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne told Parliament today.

He said the committee, chaired by the Deputy Chairperson of Committees, was appointed following a decision by the Committee on Parliamentary Business at its meeting on September 25, 2026.

The committee comprises MPs Harshana Nanayakkara, Sunil Watagala, Kaushalya Ariyarathna, J. C. Alawathuwala, Anuradha Jayaratne, Shanakiyan Rasamanickam, Oshani Umanga, Prageeth Madhuranga, Chandima Hettiaratchi, Darmapriya Wijesinghe, Dewananda Suraweera and M. Nizam Kariapper.
