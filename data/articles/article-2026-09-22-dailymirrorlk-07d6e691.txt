New Sri Lanka Cricket Bill gets Cabinet nod

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the publication of a new Sri Lanka Cricket Bill aimed at reorganizing and strengthening the administration of cricket in the country.

The decision was taken at the Cabinet meeting held on June 8, 2026. Under the proposed legislation, Sri Lanka Cricket will be authorized to govern, control, regulate, promote, develop and administer the sport of cricket in Sri Lanka.

The Bill has been prepared by the Legal Draftsman to establish the legal framework governing the structure, powers and functions of Sri Lanka Cricket. It seeks to promote the sport based on principles of good governance, integrity, transparency and accountability.

The proposed legislation also emphasizes inclusion and equal opportunities for those involved in cricket, while seeking to protect the welfare of participants. It further includes provisions aimed at safeguarding the sport from corruption and other improper activities.

The Bill has received clearance from the Attorney General, according to the Cabinet decision. Following the approval, the Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports has been authorized to publish the proposed legislation in the Government Gazette.

The Bill will subsequently be presented to Parliament for consideration and approval. If enacted, the legislation is expected to provide a formal legal framework for the future administration and development of cricket in Sri Lanka.
