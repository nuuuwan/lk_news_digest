Newburgh Ella recognised as SL’s 1st certified paperless operation hotel

Newburgh Ella – The Tea Factory Resort by Browns Hotels & Resorts has been recognised by the National Cleaner Production Centre (NCPC), Sri Lanka, as the first hotel in Sri Lanka to achieve a certified paperless operation, marking a significant milestone in the country’s journey towards more resource-efficient and digitally enabled hospitality.

The certification was formally presented by NCPC Director General and CEO Samantha Kumarasena to Browns Hotels & Resorts CEO Eksath Wijeratne, recognising Newburgh Ella’s achievement in establishing a paperless hotel operation.

The recognition is particularly significant for Newburgh Ella, a 41-room resort housed within a former tea factory dating back to 1903. A building that once relied on handwritten ledgers, registers and paper records to document the daily movement of tea, now operates through digital systems across guest services and hotel operations.

Newburgh Ella’s paperless approach extends far beyond replacing the printed menus or introducing digital check-in. The hotel has digitised the processes across departments, creating a connected system that allows information, approvals, monitoring and documentation to move digitally throughout the operation.

The guests can complete their registration digitally, access dining menus electronically and receive the bills and folios without the printed documents. In finance and administration, the approvals, records and documentation are managed through digital systems, reducing the need for physical files and paper-based circulation. The digital approach continues across the operational departments. The engineering teams use digital systems to monitor areas such as electricity consumption, allowing the usage to be recorded and reviewed efficiently. In the kitchen, the chefs record temperature checks digitally, creating an accessible record for food safety monitoring and audits.

The housekeeping teams use digital devices to manage and record their daily activities, while the stores and inventory processes are also systemised digitally, allowing the stock movements and related documentation to be maintained electronically rather than through multiple physical registers.

The same systems make routine compliance and audit documentation more efficient. Since operational information is recorded within structured digital systems, the teams can access records when required without searching through physical files, while the managers can monitor processes and maintain documentation in a consistent and traceable manner.

“Newburgh Ella was created to give a historic property a new chapter without losing what makes it special. The paperless operation is part of that thinking. We have looked at the way a hotel works, from the guest journey to the back office and challenged ourselves to remove the unnecessary paper wherever technology allows us to do so. Having this work recognised by the NCPC is an important milestone for Browns Hotels & Resorts,” said Wijeratne.
