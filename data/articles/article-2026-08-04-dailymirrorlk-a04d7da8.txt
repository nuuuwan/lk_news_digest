Two reservoirs spill as 73 major reservoirs hold 39% capacity

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - Only two reservoirs under the Irrigation Department were reported to be spilling as of 12.30 p.m. today, while the average storage capacity of the country's 73 major reservoirs remained at 39.3%, the latest Water Management Division report said.

The department said the Ambewela Reservoir in Badulla District, a major reservoir on the Mahaweli Ganga, is spilling through one overflow section. The Bomuruella Reservoir, also located in Badulla District on the Mahaweli Ganga, was reported to be spilling through three overflow sections.

No reservoirs had spill gates opened at the time of reporting.

The Irrigation Department said the country's 73 major reservoirs currently hold 1,310,756 acre-feet of water, with an effective storage of 1,168,139 acre-feet, representing 39.3% of total capacity.

Among the irrigation ranges, Kurunegala recorded the highest effective storage level at 77.1%, followed by Puttalam at 72.2%, Kandy at 70.8%, and Monaragala at 58.8%.

Lower storage levels were reported in Ampara (23.0%), Batticaloa (24.9%), and Trincomalee (29.3%).

The department advised residents living downstream of the spilling reservoirs to remain vigilant as river water levels may fluctuate. People in downstream areas have also been urged to follow instructions issued by the Irrigation Department and local authorities regarding reservoir spilling and any changes in river conditions.
