Malwatta Mahanayake raises concerns over Animal Welfare Bill’s impact on peraheras

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Mahanayake of Malwatta Chapter Ven.Thibbatuwawe Sri Sumangala Siddhartha Thera warned that the proposed Animal Welfare Bill would create obstructions to the Kandy Esala Perahera and other Perahera festivals and that the elephants marching in processions without adequate security methods is a risky undertaking.

During a meeting with Central Province Governor Dr. Suranga Fernando the Ven. Mahanayake Thera pointed out that the possibility of obstructions to the use of elephants in the Kandy Esala Perehera, if the proposed legislation was passed, has been gathering momentum.

The governor yesterday met the Mahanayake Theras of Malwatta and Asgiriya Chapters after attending religious observance at the Sri Dalada Maligawa.

Ven. Thera stressed that it is the duty of the relevant ministers to create public awareness and dispel the misgivings if the proposed legislation was not liable to create such obstacles. He pointed out that it has been brought to his notice that elephants conducted in the procession should not be in chains and that the government’s responsibility is to clarify regarding the issue without being silent.

Mayor of Kandy Chandrasiri Wijenayake told the Mahanayake Thera that individuals in the opposition have resorted to false propaganda to mislead the masses.

The Mahanayake Thera commended the government for appointing an individual with a thorough understanding of the needs of the Central Province as governor.

Later, the governor met the Mahanayake of the Asgiriya Chapter Ven. Warakagoda Sri Gnanarathana Thera as well and put forward his views regarding the ongoing development activities in the Central Province.
