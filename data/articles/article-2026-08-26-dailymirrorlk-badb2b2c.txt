Sri Lanka, India join forces to build bridge

The Sri Lanka Army Engineers and the Indian Army Engineer Task Force (ETF) have jointly completed the construction of a 100-foot Bailey Bridge at the Kaballawa Bridge site in Kurunegala.

A team from the 1st Sri Lanka Engineers (1 SLE) worked alongside personnel from the Indian Army ETF on the project, which began on August 22 and was completed on August 24.

The newly constructed bridge is approximately 100 feet long and 17 feet wide. It is expected to improve connectivity in the area and facilitate the movement of the public.
