Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Hemas Holdings, LOLC Holdings and Cargills with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.2 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. Crossings were witnessed in Ceylon Hotels Corporation, Commercial Bank, CIC Holdings, accounting for 56.8% of the turnover.

Mixed interest was observed in Hemas Holdings, R I L Property and Hatton National Bank whilst retail interest was noted in SMB Leasing, LOLC Finance and HNB Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

Consumer Services sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Ceylon Hotels Corporation) whilst the sector index gained 0.86%. The share price of Ceylon Hotels Corporation increased by Rs 0.30 (1.09%) to close at Rs 27.80.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Hemas Holdings) whilst the sector index increased by 0.30%. The share price of Hemas Holdings declined by Rs 0.40 (1.31%) to close at Rs 30.20. Commercial Bank, Haycarb and CIC Holdings were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Commercial Bank lost Rs 0.25 (0.13%) to close at Rs 199.75. The share price of Haycarb moved down by Rs 2.50 (1.26%) to close at Rs 196.00. The share price of CIC Holdings closed flat at Rs 26.50.
