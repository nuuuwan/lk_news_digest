Showers forecast in several areas today

Colombo, September 26 (Daily Mirror)- The Meteorology Department has forecast showers at times in the Western and Sabaragamuwa provinces, as well as in the Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya districts today.

Meanwhile, showers or thundershowers are expected at a few locations in Uva Province and in the Batticaloa and Ampara districts after 2.00 p.m.

The public has been advised to take necessary precautions to minimize potential damage from temporarily strong localized winds and lightning that may occur during thundershowers.
