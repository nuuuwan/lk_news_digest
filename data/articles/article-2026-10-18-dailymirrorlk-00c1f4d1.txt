Seven illegally imported vehicles worth Rs. 20 million seized by Jaffna Police

Oct. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Jaffna Police have seized seven vehicles worth Rs. 20 million that were illegally imported into Sri Lanka.

The operation was carried out based on information received by the Jaffna Police. Of the seized vehicles, four are reportedly high-end motorcycles, none of which has a registration book.

Of the remaining three vehicles, one was seized due to fraudulent registration, while the other two were confiscated because they are banned in Sri Lanka.

Police stated that one suspect has been arrested in connection with the incident and that further investigations are underway.
