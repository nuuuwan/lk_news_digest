David Pieris Automobiles opens new BAIC showroom in Kandy

Rohana Dissanayake - Group Chairman & Managing Director, David Pieris Group together with Ethen, Country Manager, BAIC, Mahesh Gunathilake - Director, DPA and Dhanika de Silva -Director, DPA cutting the ribbon to open new showroom

David Pieris Automobiles (Private) Limited (DPA), the four-wheeler automotive arm of the David Pieris Group, has expanded its BAIC showroom network with the opening of its newest showroom in Kandy.

Located Katugastota Road, Kandy, the new showroom brings BAIC’s range of modern vehicles closer to customers in Kandy and the surrounding areas, further strengthening the brand’s presence in Sri Lanka.

The opening marks another significant step in DPA’s efforts to expand access to BAIC vehicles across the country, supported by the growing demand for modern, feature-rich and technologically advanced mobility solutions.

The new Kandy showroom joins BAIC’s existing flagship showroom on Pannipitiya Road, Battaramulla, together with its showrooms in Hyde Park Corner Colombo 02, Kurunegala, Matara, Ratnapura, Jaffna and Ampara. The expanding network enables customers in key regions to conveniently explore BAIC’s vehicle range and receive professional sales assistance.

David Pieris Automobiles currently markets three BAIC models in Sri Lanka, catering to a range of customer requirements and preferences.

The portfolio includes the BAIC X55, a contemporary SUV offering a combination of design, performance, technology and practicality; the BAIC BJ30, a distinctive SUV designed for customers seeking versatility and a bold driving experience; and the BAIC X3e, a fully electric vehicle that reflects BAIC’s commitment to the future of sustainable mobility.
