Protect animals during floods: AWC

Colombo, August 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Animal Welfare Coalition (AWC) has urged the public to take immediate measures to protect pets and domestic animals amid heavy rainfall and flash flood risks reported in several parts of the country.

The AWC warned that thousands of animals could die during sudden floods if they are left tied up, chained, or locked in cages, as they may be unable to escape rising waters.

It requested owners to release cattle, goats, and other domestic animals from ropes and chains before evacuating to safer locations, allowing them a chance to move to higher ground.

The public was also urged not to leave dogs and other pets confined to cages during emergencies, and to open cage doors and release chained animals before leaving their homes.

The AWC further called on people to assist vulnerable street animals by guiding them to safer locations or providing temporary shelter whenever possible.

“Animal lives are as valuable as human lives,” the forum said, urging the public to share the message and help protect animals during disaster situations.
