HNB FINANCE relocates Nelliady branch

Newly relocated HNB FINANCE branch premises at No. 216/1, Jaffna Road, Nelliady

HNB FINANCE Managing Director and CEO Chaminda Prabhath at ceremonial opening of relocated Nelliady branch

HNB FINANCE PLC has relocated its Nelliady branch to No. 216/1, Jaffna Road, Nelliady, strengthening its presence in the Northern province and expanding customer access to formal financial services.

The relocation responds to the growing demand for financial solutions in the region and forms part of HNB FINANCE’s wider strategy to support households, entrepreneurs and businesses through improved access to savings, credit, leasing and other financial services.

The Nelliady branch offers HNB FINANCE’s comprehensive portfolio of financial solutions including gold loans, leasing, fixed deposits, savings and microfinance facilities. The customers can also access specialised gold-backed lending products such as Gold Plan and VIP Gold Loan, which provide convenient access to liquidity with flexible repayment options.

Located on modern and accessible premises in the Nelliady town, the relocated branch was ceremonially opened by HNB FINANCE Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Chaminda Prabhath, in the presence of the senior management representatives, distinguished guests and customers.

Prabath said HNB FINANCE remained focused on strengthening its position as a preferred financial services provider while expanding access to solutions that address the evolving needs of the customers.

“It is a privilege to be part of the opening of our new Nelliady branch and I extend my appreciation to everyone who joined us on this occasion. HNB FINANCE commenced its journey 26 years ago and since then we have grown into one of Sri Lanka’s leading financial services companies. Today, we are able to provide the customers with a comprehensive range of solutions covering their key financial requirements.

Through the relocated Nelliady branch, we aim to further improve the customer experience while widening access to financial solutions. Our focus goes beyond delivering quality financial services and we remain committed to building long-term relationships by understanding the needs of our customers and supporting them through different stages of their economic journey,” Prabhath said.
