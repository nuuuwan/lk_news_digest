Cabinet clears 11 contracts for Kahathuduwa-Ingiriya expressway section

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the award of 11 contract packages for the construction of the first phase of the Ruwanpura Expressway, covering the 22.18-kilometre section from Kahathuduwa to Ingiriya.

The first phase, extending from Kahathuduwa (km 1+820) to Ingiriya (km 24+000), including the system interchange, was approved by the Cabinet at its meeting held on July 27, 2026.

Bids were invited under the National Competitive Procurement System to select contractors for the implementation of the project through 11 separate contract packages.

Following the evaluation of the bids received, the Cabinet approved the proposals submitted by the Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development to award the contracts to the selected bidders.

The awards were made based on the recommendations of the High Level Procurement Committee.
