Probe reveals grenade attack hit wrong house in Dehiwala

Colombo, September 11 (Daily Mirror) - A police probe has revealed that a hand grenade attack in Dehiwala early this morning was aimed at a different residence, but the attackers mistakenly targeted the wrong house, killing two children and seriously injuring their father.

The attack took place at around 3:20 a.m. today (11) at a residence along Sirisangabo Mawatha, Dehiwala.

Two children, aged 11 and 17, were killed in the attack. Their 55-year-old father was also injured and was admitted to Kalubowila Teaching Hospital for treatment.

According to preliminary police investigations, the attackers had allegedly intended to target a neighbouring residence. The house is reportedly linked to a drug distributor associated with organized-crime figure “Badowita Asanka.”

However, the grenade was allegedly thrown at the adjacent residence by mistake, resulting in the deaths of the two children who were inside the house.

Police believe the attack was carried out as part of an ongoing dispute between rival organized-crime groups associated with “Badowita Asanka” and “Kos Malli.” The incident is being investigated as an act of retaliation linked to the continuing conflict between the two factions.

Police have launched investigations to identify the individuals who carried out the attack.
