EDOTCO commits US $ 10mn to expand Sri Lanka telecom infrastructure

EDOTCO Services Lanka, a telecommunications infrastructure provider, will invest US $ 10 million to expand its network in Sri Lanka, adding 130 macro towers, 300 smart lamp poles and 650 energy sites over the next 30 months.

The company has signed an investment agreement with the Board of Investment (BOI) for the expansion, which will increase the shared infrastructure available to the mobile network operators and support wider network coverage and capacity.

As EDOTCO completes a decade of operations in Sri Lanka, the company said it had invested about US $ 30 million in the country since commencing operations in July 2016, taking its total investment and new commitment to around US $ 40 million.

EDOTCO has so far deployed more than 950 smart lamp poles and nearly 800 energy sites across Sri Lanka, working with the mobile network operators, municipal councils and government agencies.

The company is also expanding its operations beyond smart lamp poles and energy solutions after becoming Sri Lanka’s first licensed infrastructure provider.

The licence allows EDOTCO to develop and operate macro towers, antenna structures, fibre and submarine cable infrastructure, in-building solutions, data centres and active network solutions.

The expansion comes as Sri Lanka seeks to improve the telecommunications infrastructure needed to support its Digital Economy Blueprint, higher data usage and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence.

EDOTCO Sri Lanka Country Managing Director Gayan Koralage said the company would focus its next phase of investment on infrastructure capable of supporting new technologies and wider digital access.

“Our next decade is about building digital infrastructure platforms that will enable future technologies, stronger economic growth and greater digital inclusion,” Koralage said.

BOI Chairman Duminda Hulangamuwa said the investment reflected confidence in Sri Lanka’s digital economy and would contribute to strengthening national connectivity.

“This US $ 10 million investment by EDOTCO Sri Lanka is an indication of the confidence in our nation’s digital economy,” Hulangamuwa said.

Malaysia-headquartered EDOTCO Group operates telecommunications infrastructure across several Asian markets, providing shared tower and related network infrastructure to mobile operators.
