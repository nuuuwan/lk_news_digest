SLIC General appoints Asiri Wickramarachchi as Acting CEO

Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation General Limited (SLIC General) has appointed Asiri Wickramarachchi as Acting Chief Executive Officer, effective 21 September 2026.

Wickramarachchi brings over 19 years of insurance experience across leading local and multinational insurers, with expertise spanning underwriting, claims, reinsurance and technical operations. Prior to his appointment, he served as Chief Officer- General Insurance at SLIC General, overseeing the company’s key technical functions.

Commenting on the appointment, Nusith Kumaratunga, Chairman of Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation said, “We are pleased to appoint Wickramarachchi as Acting Chief Executive Officer of SLIC General. His technical expertise, industry experience and strong understanding of our business position him well to lead the company at this important stage. Having served as Chief Officer – General Insurance, Asiri brings valuable continuity to our leadership as we take SLIC General forward.”
