Political and public service cooperation can speed up development: PM

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya said development projects could be completed more quickly and deliver better results to the public when political authorities and public servants work together towards a common, people-oriented goal.

She made these remarks during a review meeting on September 18 on the progress of the rehabilitation of Rathmalana-Kandawala Road and the drainage systems on both sides of the road within the Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia Municipal Council limits.

The project, which began on September 10, 2026, is scheduled to be completed by June 30, 2027.

Implemented by the Provincial Road Development Authority (PRDA), the project includes the construction of a bridge at a cost of Rs. 64 million, rehabilitation of the drainage system at Rs. 930 million and rehabilitation of the road at Rs. 115 million.

The Prime Minister reviewed the progress of work assigned to the Western Provincial Council, Irrigation Department and Ratmalana Divisional Secretariat, as well as plans being implemented by the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation (SLLRDC).

She commended the progress of the project and praised officials for coordinating closely with each other and the political authorities.

The meeting was attended by Colombo District Coordinating Committee Chairman and MP Lakshman Nipuna Arachchi, Ratmalana Divisional Coordinating Committee Chairperson and MP Samanmalee Gunasinghe, Colombo District Coordinating Committee Roads Sub-Committee Chairman Dewananda Suraweera, Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia Mayor Parakum Shantha, Western Province Chief Secretary K.G. Pradeep Pushpakumara and government officials.
