Livestock, industrial gains lift CIC Holdings’ 1Q earnings

CIC Holdings PLC reported a 19.7 percent year-on-year (YoY) increase in group net profit to Rs.1.81 billion for the first quarter ended June 30, 2026, as the stronger livestock and industrial operations offset a decline in its core agriculture business.

The profit attributable to the equity holders rose 23.2 percent to Rs.1.51 billion, lifting the earnings per share to Rs.0.80, from Rs.0.65 a year earlier.

Revenue, however, grew at a slower 2.7 percent to Rs.22.03 billion, indicating that the improvement in earnings was driven largely by the stronger margins and a shift towards the better-performing business segments.

Gross profit increased 10.3 percent to Rs.6.01 billion, with the gross profit margin improving to about 27.3 percent. Operating profit rose 12.7 percent to Rs.3.20 billion, while profit before tax advanced 17.5 percent to Rs.2.76 billion.

The improvement came despite the net finance costs rising 16.4 percent to Rs.676.67 million, reflecting the higher working capital requirements during the quarter.

Livestock solutions recorded the strongest profit growth, more than doubling to Rs.660.25 million from a year earlier. The segment revenue increased to Rs.4.90 billion, from Rs.4.45 billion, supported by the higher feed volumes, poultry demand and veterinary care.

Industrial solutions delivered the fastest revenue expansion, with the turnover climbing 40.6 percent to Rs.2.63 billion. The segment profit rose 62.2 percent to Rs.533.90 million.

Agri solutions remained CIC’s largest business, accounting for about 45 percent of the segmental revenue. However, revenue declined YoY to Rs.9.97 billion, due to the seasonal factors. The segment remained the group’s largest profit contributor, generating Rs.1.08 billion.

The health and personal care revenue edged up to Rs.4.80 billion, from Rs.4.73 billion, while the segment profit stood at Rs.662.51 million.

The overall net segmental results rose 11.8 percent to Rs.2.94 billion.

CIC’s total assets crossed Rs.100 billion during the quarter, reaching Rs.100.14 billion, while the total equity stood at Rs.55.69 billion.
