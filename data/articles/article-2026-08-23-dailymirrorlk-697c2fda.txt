Man killed during dispute at Party in Kekirawa

Colombo, Aug. 23 (Daily Mirror) - A 56-year-old man was killed following a dispute that broke out during a party at his residence in Kottalbadha, Kekirawa early this morning.

Police said the victim, a resident of the same area, was allegedly attacked with a sharp weapon by a man from a neighbouring house following an argument that escalated during the gathering.

The victim sustained serious injuries in the attack and died as a result.

Kekirawa Police launched an investigation after receiving information about the incident.

The suspect has reportedly fled the area following the attack, and police are conducting further investigations to locate and arrest him.
