Union Assurance honours top performers at agency distribution awards 2025

Union Assurance, Sri Lanka’s longest-standing private life insurer, has recognised the achievements of its leading advisers and agency leaders at the Agency Distribution Annual Awards 2025 held at Shangri-La Colombo.

Held under the theme “Legacy to Infinity”, the awards ceremony celebrated the contributions of generations of advisers and leaders who have helped strengthen the company’s mission of protecting Sri Lankan families while driving future growth.

The event was attended by John Keells Holdings PLC Chairperson Krishan Balendra, along with senior officials of Union Assurance, including Chief Executive Officer Senath Jayatilake and Chief Agency Officer Imtiyaz Aniff.

More than 300 advisers and leaders from across the country were recognised for their achievements in new business generation, customer service, talent development, leadership and regional performance.

The awards covered Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond and Platinum categories, as well as Best Region, Zone and Leader awards. The highest honour, the Ultimate Champion Award, was presented to Upul Priyantha, Senior Personal Portfolio Director of the Kirulapone Region, who was also named New Business Champion for his outstanding performance.

Top performers received rewards valued at over Rs. 60 million, including a company-maintained Mercedes-Benz, cash incentives and local and overseas travel opportunities.

Jayatilake said the achievements reflected the strength of Union Assurance’s agency network and its contribution towards expanding customer reach and building trust.

Aniff commended the advisers for their dedication, professionalism and commitment to developing meaningful relationships with customers.
