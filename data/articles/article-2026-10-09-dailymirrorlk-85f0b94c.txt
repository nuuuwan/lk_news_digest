Sri Lanka Sustainability Summit 2026 to bring global sustainability voices

Sri Lanka Sustainability Summit 2026, themed ‘Reimagining Sri Lanka’s Responsible Future’, organised by the Sri Lanka Brand Forum, under its Sustainable Brand Initiative and convened with Commercial Bank of Ceylon as Title Partner, is to bring together local commitment and global expertise around one important conversation: how responsible business and sustainability can contribute to a more resilient, accountable and sustainable future in Sri Lanka.

A community dedicated to strengthening Sri Lanka’s brand and creating meaningful conversations about the country’s future is brought together by the Sri Lanka Brand Forum. The forum is expanding this conversation into the area of sustainability and responsible business through the Sustainable Brand Initiative, recognising that long-term progress of the country is intimately linked to how organisations lead, use resources, treat people and contribute to communities.

Sri Lanka Brand Forum Co-Founder Rohan Somawansa said, “Sri Lanka has shown remarkable resilience in the face of adversity. The next step is to turn that resilience into responsible action across the way we lead businesses, care for our resources and create opportunities for our people.  In order to ask the right questions and contribute to creating a future we can be proud to pass on to the next generation, we want to bring together local commitment and global expertise through this summit.”

The summit’s chief guest address, to be delivered by a leading figure from the public and private sector, will speak to exactly this — framing responsible business and sustainability as central to Sri Lanka’s economic and social future.

The address will reinforce the importance of how organisations treat people, resources and communities and how these responsibilities connect to sustainable growth.

Sri Lanka Sustainability Summit 2026 will feature four global keynote speakers, each bringing a distinct perspective on responsible business, sustainable growth and meaningful impact.

Shankar Venkateswaran — Co-Founder and Managing Partner, ECube Investment Advisors, former Head of Sustainability, Tata Group. He opens the day exploring what it actually takes to build a responsible corporation — not just policies but leadership and culture that hold up under pressure.

Dr. Ravi A. Fernando — Chairman and CEO, Global Strategic Corporate Sustainability, Executive in Residence, INSEAD Sustainable Business Initiative. He brings a human, marketing-rooted perspective to why sustainability matters to how brands are built and trusted.

Dr. Shraman Jha — Chief Executive Officer, Hindustan Unilever Foundation. He makes the case that sustainability isn’t a cost to growth — it’s what makes growth last.

David Galipeau — Founding Partner, SDGx.io, formerly with the United Nations. He closes the keynotes by looking honestly at where capital, technology and impact need to meet — creating meaningful outcomes for people and communities, not just balance sheets.
