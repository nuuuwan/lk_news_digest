Central Crime Bureau named PTA detention site

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - A new Extraordinary Gazette notification has designated the Central Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) as a place of detention under Sri Lanka’s Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act, No. 48 of 1979.

The notification, published as Extraordinary Gazette No. 2509/11 dated October 6, 2026, was issued under Section 9 of the Prevention of Terrorism Act by President and Minister of Defence Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

According to the Gazette, the Central Crime Investigation Bureau has been formally declared a detention location for the purposes of the provisions of the Act.

The location specified in the schedule is the Central Crime Investigation Bureau, Department of Labour Secretariat Building, 7th Floor, Colombo 05.
