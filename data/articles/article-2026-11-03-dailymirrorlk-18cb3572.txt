‘Senti - Talk to Your Fleet’ takes Sri Lankan AI and IoT innovation to int’l stage

Softlogic Information Technologies (Pvt) Ltd has secured a Bronze Award at the National ICT Awards – NBQSA 2026 for ‘Senti – Talk to Your Fleet’ in the Transport in Industrial category.

The company has also received the ASOCIO Digital & AI Award 2026 in the AI in Manufacturing / Supply Chain category, recognizing its work in applying artificial intelligence to business operations.

Together, these achievements show how Sri Lankan technology is increasingly being used to solve practical business challenges and demonstrate how the Internet of Things (IoT) and artificial intelligence (AI) can assist organizations in converting operational data into insights that can be put to use.

Knowing the whereabouts of vehicles is just one aspect of the difficulties faced by businesses managing supply chains, transportation, and distribution. It is equally crucial to understand how they are driven, which vehicles need maintenance, and how fleet data might influence operational decisions. By combining AI and IoT, Senti - Talk to Your Fleet meets this demand by offering real-time fleet operations visibility and supporting more informed decision-making.

Senti helps organizations better understand fleet activity and make more informed operational decisions by connecting vehicle data with intelligent insights. Its applications are relevant to industries where vehicle utilization, delivery reliability, and operational visibility are critical to daily operations, such as manufacturing, retail, healthcare, agriculture, and public transport.

This achievement reflects our strategic commitment to leveraging technology, data, and intelligent insights to solve real-world challenges and deliver measurable business value. Senti is recognized in the Transport in Industrial category by the NBQSA Bronze Award. In addition, Softlogic Information Technologies is recognized by the ASOCIO Digital & AI Award in the AI in Manufacturing/Supply Chain category, which reflects the company’s wider efforts to apply AI to supply chain and industrial challenges.

Understanding what operational data can reveal and how those insights might help everyday decisions is crucial for organizations considering fleet technology. Softlogic Information Technologies seeks to make AI and IoT more useful for businesses looking for better operational visibility with solutions like Senti.
