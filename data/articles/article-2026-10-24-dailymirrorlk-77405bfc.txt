Man arrested with two pistols, ammunition in Mawanella

Colombo, October 24 (Daily Mirror)- A 34-year-old man was arrested with two pistols, five magazines, 156 lead pellets and 20 rounds of ammunition for a 12-bore firearm during a raid in the Kirigala Junction area within the Mawanella Police Division.

The raid was conducted by officers from the Kegalle Divisional Crime Investigation Unit following information received by the unit.

A 72-hour detention order has been obtained for the suspect, while further investigations are being carried out by the Mawanella Police.
