People’s Leasing shines at CSE Masterminds Quiz 2026

People’s Leasing & Finance PLC (PLC) emerged as the winner in the Leasing & Finance Category at the 8th edition of the CSE Masterminds Quiz 2026, organized by the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE).

Held on 9 October at Shangri-La, Colombo, the prestigious competition brought together 100 teams representing financial institutions, non-bank financial institutions, private sector organizations and state sector institutions from across Sri Lanka. The event provided a platform for corporate teams to demonstrate their knowledge, analytical skills and intellectual agility across a diverse range of subjects.

Demonstrating exceptional subject knowledge, teamwork and composure, the People’s Leasing team secured the winning position in the Leasing & Finance Category, distinguishing itself in a highly competitive corporate environment.

The winning team comprised Shiranga Arseculeratne, Sithumini De Silva, Osanda Abeywardana, Shashika Peiris and Ishini Amararathne. Their dedication, preparation and collaborative spirit played a pivotal role in securing this achievement and bringing recognition to People’s Leasing.
