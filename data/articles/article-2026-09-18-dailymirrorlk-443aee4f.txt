Siyapatha Finance recognised among SL’s Top 100 Most Valuable Brands

Siyapatha Finance PLC has been recognized among the Top 100 Most Valuable Brands in Sri Lanka in the Brand Finance Sri Lanka 100 Most Valuable Brands 2026 ranking.

Further strengthening this achievement, Siyapatha Finance has also been ranked among the Top 10 Most Valuable Brands in the Financial Services category, within the non-banking finance sector.

Commenting on the recognition, Siyapatha Finance PLC Chief Executive Officer, Mathisha Hewavitharana said, “This recognition is a reflection of the trust we have built with our customers, partners and stakeholders. We are deeply grateful for this recognition and remain committed to building an even stronger and more trusted brand that contributes positively to Sri Lanka’s growth and prosperity.”

According to the Brand Finance Sri Lanka 100 Most Valuable Brands 2026 ranking, Siyapatha Finance has made its maiden entry into Sri Lanka’s Top 100 Most Valuable Brands, recording a brand value of Rs. 1.4 billion and achieving a BBB brand strength rating.
