PUNISHMENT IS ONE THING. HATRED IS ANOTHER!

Few dispute the punishment imposed on Ven. Gnanasara Thero. Like any other prisoner, he must abide by prison regulations. No one is above the law.

But those who relentlessly condemn and humiliate him should reflect on how the Buddha treated Angulimala, who killed 999 people, yet attained Arahantship through the Buddha’s guidance.

If the Buddha could show compassion to Angulimala, why should we deny compassion to another human being?

Metta, Karuna, Mudita and Upekkha are not mere words to preach, but principles to practise.

Uphold the law, but do not abandon compassion. That is the true test of Buddhist values.
