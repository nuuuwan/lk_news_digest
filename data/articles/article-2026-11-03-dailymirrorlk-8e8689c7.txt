ComBank and Toyota Lanka renew partnership to offer leasing solutions nationwide

Sanath Manatunge, Managing Director/CEO of Commercial Bank and Masaaki Kawabata, Chairman of Toyota Lanka exchange the agreement in the presence of senior representatives of the two companies

Commercial Bank of Ceylon and Toyota Lanka (Private) Limited have renewed their strategic partnership to make owning a new Toyota vehicle more accessible and affordable through attractive leasing packages.

The renewed collaboration will promote leasing facilities for the full range of Toyota passenger and commercial vehicles through Commercial Bank’s island-wide branch network, offering both existing and new customers an opportunity to finance their vehicle purchases on favourable terms.

With this arrangement, Commercial Bank will offer the lowest interest ratesand flexible repayment plans that can be tailored to customers’ income patterns. Customers obtaining lease facilities for Toyota vehicles will also qualify for a waiver of credit card joining and annual fees for the first two years on cards issued by the Bank.

Toyota customers will benefit from the flexible residual value repayment options that significantly lower monthly rentals by deferring up to 50 percent of the cost of the vehicle for one year, scaling down to 30 percent for a five-year tenure, payable at the end of the lease period.

Additionally, a special benefit will be offered on brand-new Toyota vehicles leased under this arrangementthrough a partnership between Commercial Bank and Commercial Insurance Brokers (Pvt) Ltd. (CIBL). To further enhance the financial flexibility, the premium payments for insurance policies placed via CIBL can be converted into 6- or 12-month 0 percent interest Easy Payment Plans (EPP), with handling fees completely waived, the Bank said.

Complementing these financing benefits, Toyota Lanka will provide free vehicle registration, the first three standard services at no charge, and a manufacturer’s warranty covering three years or 100,000 kilometres, whichever occurs first.

By combining flexible financing with savings on vehicle-related costs and after-sales benefits, the initiative is designed to help customers manage the financial commitment of purchasing a new vehicle with greater convenience and confidence, the Bank said.
