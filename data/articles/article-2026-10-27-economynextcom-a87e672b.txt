Sri Lanka’s Standard Capital triggers mandatory offer after majority stake acquisition

ECONOMYNEXT – A mandatory offer has been triggered for the remaining voting shares of Sri Lanka listed firm Standard Capital PLC after an investor acquired a controlling stake in the listed firm, according to a market filing.

Abishek Sithampalam acquired 3.91 million ordinary voting shares, representing 70.54 percent of Standard Capital’s issued voting shares, in transactions completed on October 2, giving him control of the company.

The stake was acquired from Gulf East Finance Ltd, Standard Industries Pvt Ltd, Amina Investments Ltd and Eastern Ocean Investments Ltd for about 156.3 million rupees.

Under Sri Lanka’s takeover regulations, the acquisition of a controlling stake above the mandatory threshold requires the acquirer to make an offer to purchase the remaining shares held by minority shareholders.

The transaction marks a significant shift in the ownership structure of the investment banking and brokerage firm, positioning Sithampalam as its dominant shareholder.
