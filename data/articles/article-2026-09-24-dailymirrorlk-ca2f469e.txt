CCC urges targeted poverty relief after Fitch upgrade

Sri Lanka must ensure its economic recovery reaches the poorer households while preserving the fiscal discipline behind its latest sovereign rating upgrade, the Ceylon Chamber of Commerce (CCC) said.

Reacting to Fitch Ratings’ decision to raise Sri Lanka’s long-term foreign-currency rating from ‘CCC+’ to ‘B-’, with a Stable Outlook, the CCC called for targeted poverty reduction alongside measures to strengthen public finances.

“A targeted approach to poverty reduction should also remain a priority to ensure that the benefits of economic recovery and growth are broadly shared across society,” it said in a statement.

The CCC said Sri Lanka must maintain the primary budget surpluses and sustain revenue mobilisation, while gradually reducing debt and debt-service ratios to create more fiscal space.

It also called for stronger foreign exchange reserves to protect the gains made since the economic crisis.

The upgrade recognised progress on fiscal consolidation, debt restructuring and structural reforms, the CCC said.

It urged the government to build on that stability through trade facilitation, digital and labour reforms aimed at attracting investment and supporting growth.
