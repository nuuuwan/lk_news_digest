1,665 schools operate with fewer than 50 students each

Colombo, September 17 (Daily Mirror)- In the wake of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake’s remarks on the plan for restructuring the school system, statistics reveal that 1,665 schools, including one national school, operate with fewer than 50 students each.

According to statistics compiled for last year, 49,189 students study in these schools, with 10,324 teachers assigned to them. Another 1,648 schools have student populations between 51 and 100, accommodating 122,891 students with 16,923 teachers.

The number of schools with 101 to 200 students stands at 2,149, accommodating 314,851 students with 34,450 teachers. Those with student numbers between 201 and 500 amount to 2,546, with 812,019 students and 56,925 teachers.

In the next category, covering schools with up to 1,000 students, there are 1,208 schools with 831,999 pupils and 4,071 teachers.

The 1,001–1,500 category comprises 372 schools, with a student population of 448,994 and 23,804 teachers. Meanwhile, 186 schools have more than 1,501 but fewer than 2,000 students, accounting for 318,966 students and 16,041 teachers.

The Ministry has identified 192 schools with 2,001–3,000 students each. Their total student population is 459,537, with 21,744 teachers.

A further 68 schools have student populations ranging from 3,001 to 4,000. Altogether, 230,518 students study in these schools under 10,214 teachers, according to last year’s statistics.

At the other end of the scale, only 33 schools have more than 4,000 students each. They account for 158,580 students and 6,813 teachers.

The Ministry has identified 65 schools with fewer than 10 teachers each, 4,242 with 10–25, 1,744 with 26–50, 721 with 51–100, and 275 with more than 100.

President Dissanayake recently spoke about the government’s plan for sweeping reforms in the education sector. He questioned the viability of running some schools with only a few students each.

According to the concept paper prepared by the Ministry, the government is determined to provide a primary school for every child within a radius of three kilometres of their residence. Every education zone will have a primary school or a secondary school with a strong primary section and all the necessary facilities.

In selecting a school for development under this category, eight factors, including the number of Grade 1 admissions over the past three years, admission requests for the current year under review, the ability to absorb students from nearby schools, the number of children under five years of age and available infrastructure facilities, will be considered, according to a Ministry document.
