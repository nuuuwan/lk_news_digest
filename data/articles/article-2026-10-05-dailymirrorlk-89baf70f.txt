Case against former Minister Kumara Jayakody fixed for further evidence

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo High Court Judge Rashantha Godawela today ordered that the bribery case filed against former Cabinet Minister Kumara Jayakody be taken up for further evidence on November 3.

The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) filed the case over alleged corruption charges.

The court directed that further evidence in the case be presented when the matter is called on November 3.
