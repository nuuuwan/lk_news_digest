Harini Amarasuriya arrives in Antigua and Barbuda for CHOGM 2026

Colombo, Nov 02 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya arrived in Antigua and Barbuda on November 2 to participate in the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) 2026.

The Prime Minister arrived at V. C. Bird International Airport in Antigua, where she was received by Michael Joseph, Minister of Health, Wellness, Environment and Civil Service Affairs of Antigua and Barbuda.

During her visit, Prime Minister Amarasuriya is also scheduled to participate in the Commonwealth Women’s Forum.
