Probe ordered into burning of Kumbuk trees on Wilpattu border

Colombo, September 18 (Daily Mirror) - An immediate and impartial investigation has been ordered into the burning and destruction of Kumbuk trees in the Nelum Wewa area of Parana Eluwankulama in Vanathavilluwa, located within the boundary zone of the Wilpattu National Park.

Deputy Environment Minister Anton Jayakody has instructed the Secretary to the Ministry of Environment to conduct the investigation and submit an urgent report on the incident.

The Ministry said the destruction of natural vegetation protecting water resources in the ecologically sensitive area could pose a serious threat to the wider ecosystem.

It said that if the investigation establishes that the burning was a deliberate act of sabotage or ecocide, the maximum penalties available under the existing legal framework would be sought against those responsible.

The Ministry said action would be taken under the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance, Forest Conservation Ordinance and National Environmental Act against anyone found responsible for damaging sensitive ecological zones and catchment areas, regardless of their status or position.

A joint on-site inspection is scheduled to be carried out today (18) by officials from the Department of Irrigation, Forest Conservation Department, Department of Wildlife Conservation, local administrative authorities and security forces.
