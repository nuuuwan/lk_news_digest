e-Passports within less than a year: Minister

Colombo, August 8 (Daily Mirror) - The government is planning to commence issuance of e-Passports within less than a year under the standards of the International Civil Aviation Organisation (ICAO), Public Security Minister Ananda Wijepala said yesterday.

He told Parliament that a new procurement process has been initiated to procure the Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) security system and that tenders will be called for that soon.

Responding to a question raised by SJB MP Mujibur Rahman, the Minister said the procurement process for the KPI systems had to be cancelled due to some shortcomings.

He said four e-gates have already been installed at the Bandaranaike International Airport and that plans are in place to install twelve more e-gates within the next year in line with the introduction of e-Passports.

Responding to a question regarding the reduction of the number of pages in the new P series passport, the Minister said the number of pages has been reduced from 64 pages to 48 pages in the new passport.

He said no issue has arisen due to the reduction of pages, adding that the Sri Lankan Passport is on par with International standards.
