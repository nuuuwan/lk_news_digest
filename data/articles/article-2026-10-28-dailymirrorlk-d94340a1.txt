Minister calls for boosting coconut industry productivity

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Plantations and Community Infrastructure Minister Samantha Vidyarathna has called for research-based solutions to increase productivity in Sri Lanka’s coconut industry and address challenges faced by coconut growers.

The Minister made these remarks during an inspection tour of the Coconut Research Institute in Lunuwila on October 27, where discussions were held on the institute’s research activities and future development programmes for the coconut industry.

During the visit, the Minister reviewed ongoing research, new technologies introduced for coconut cultivation and measures to make these innovations more accessible to growers.

He emphasized the need to direct research towards current challenges in the field and instructed officials to identify factors affecting coconut cultivation and introduce practical solutions without delay.

Discussions also focused on challenges faced by coconut growers, obstacles to research activities and possible measures to overcome them. The importance of connecting scientific research and technical expertise with the practical needs of the industry was highlighted.

The meeting further explored measures to increase national coconut production, improve cultivation productivity and strengthen the industry’s contribution to the national economy.

Deputy Minister of Environment Anton Jayakody, regional political representatives, Ministry Secretary Gunadasa Samarasinghe and other ministry officials also attended the event.
