Heavy rainfall, thunderstorms expected in six provinces

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - A low-level atmospheric disturbance persisting in the vicinity of Sri Lanka is expected to bring showers or thundershowers and heavy rainfall to several provinces, the Meteorological Department said today.

Accordingly, cloudy skies with showers or thundershowers are expected at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Uva, Eastern and Southern provinces.

Heavy rainfall exceeding 100 mm is likely at some locations, according to the forecast issued at 4 p.m. today (15).

The public has been urged to take adequate precautions to minimise damage caused by temporarily strong localised winds and lightning during thundershowers.

For further information, the public can visit Disaster Management Centre⁠ or contact the DMC hotline on 117.
