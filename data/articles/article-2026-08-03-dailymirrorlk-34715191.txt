AAT Sri Lanka felicitates 2,179 passed finalists

The Association of Accounting Technicians of Sri Lanka (AAT Sri Lanka), the pioneer in accounting education and professional skills development in the country, recently held its Passed Finalists’ Diploma Awarding Ceremony, at the BMICH, Colombo, honouring the students who successfully completed the final level of the January 2026 AAT examinations.

A total of 2,179 passed finalists, representing every district of the island, were invited to receive their Diploma in Accounting and Business, each accompanied by a guest to witness this significant milestone in their academic journey.

Owing to the large number of recipients, the ceremony was conducted across three sessions, welcoming an audience of approximately 4,000 throughout the day, making it one of the largest Passed Finalists’ Diploma Awarding Ceremonies organised by AAT Sri Lanka.

The morning session was graced by Fintrex Finance General Manager and CEO Jayathilake Bandara as the chief guest, with CA Sri Lanka President Tishan Subhasinghe as the guest of honour.

The afternoon session featured Colombo Re Insurance Brokers (Pvt.) Ltd CEO Roshini Silva as the chief guest, joined by CA Sri Lanka Council Member Saman Siri Lal as the guest of honour, while the evening session was graced by Capital Maharaja Group Finance Director Lalith Fernando as the chief guest, with CA Sri Lanka Council Member Thivanka Jayasinghe as the guest of honour.

The occasion was further distinguished by the presence of AAT Sri Lanka President Dr. Chamara Bandara, who led the proceedings alongside the vice president, secretary, members of the Governing Council and CEO of AAT Sri Lanka.

Having successfully completed the final level of the AAT qualification and secured the NVQ Level 5 equivalent Diploma in Accounting and Business, the passed finalists are now equipped with the knowledge, practical skills and competencies required to embark on careers in accounting, finance and business.

Upon completion of the prescribed practical training and the People and Leadership Skills Workshop, they become eligible for the Member of the Association of Accounting Technicians designation, marking the next milestone in their professional journey.
