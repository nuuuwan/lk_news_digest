Unrest erupts at Kuruwita Prison

Ratnapura, August 07(Daily Mirror): A tense situation has erupted among inmates at the Kuruwita Prison today (07), leading to disruptions within the facility.

According to a spokesperson for the Prison Department, the unrest initially broke out last night (06). Authorities took immediate steps at the time to contain the situation and restore order.

However, tensions flared up again among the inmates this morning.
