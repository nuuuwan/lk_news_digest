SLPP hints at rally to rival Anuradhapura event

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) MP D.V. Chanaka said discussions were underway to organise a major rally to rival the recently held Anuradhapura rally, with the aim of pressuring the Government to fulfil its promises to the people.

Chanaka said a large number of people in the country continued to hold SLPP leaders Namal Rajapaksa and former President Mahinda Rajapaksa in high regard.

He claimed that despite attempts to imprison Namal Rajapaksa, the public was rallying around him.

“Today, we also discussed holding a rally—one that rivals the Anuradhapura rally—to urge the Government to fulfil the promises it made to the people,” Chanaka said.

“There are people in this country who hold Namal Rajapaksa and Mahinda Rajapaksa in high regard. While attempts are being made to imprison Namal Rajapaksa—who is poised to defeat Anura Kumara Dissanayake in 2029—the people of the country are uniting today to protect him. That is the very initiative currently being set in motion,” he said.

Chanaka's remarks come amid efforts by the SLPP to mobilise its supporters and build political pressure on the Government.
