250 Ceylon King Coconut Villages to be established

Colombo, Sep. 2 (Daily Mirror) - A national programme to establish 250 “Ceylon King Coconut Villages” across Sri Lanka over the next three years was launched today (2) at the Coconut Cultivation Board’s Mahayaya Estate in Kurunegala.

The programme, implemented by the Ministry of Plantation and Community Infrastructure, aims to increase king coconut production and productivity while expanding international markets for king coconut and related products.

Under the programme, 250 king coconut villages are expected to be established by 2028, with 75 villages in 2026, 80 in 2027 and 95 in 2028.

The programme will involve planting 212,500 new king coconut plants, rehabilitating around 221,500 existing trees and establishing one micro-processing facility in each village.

Officials expect the initiative to benefit around 21,250 king coconut growers and create a formal data and marketing system connecting growers, collectors and exporters.

The programme targets an increase of 25 million nuts in Sri Lanka’s annual king coconut harvest by 2030, while increasing export earnings from king coconut and related products by more than US$12 million.

The initiative also aims to create new income and employment opportunities in king coconut collection, transportation, processing and export, thereby strengthening the incomes of rural families.

For 2026, 75 king coconut villages are planned, with around 500–1,000 trees to be cultivated in each village. Around Rs. 405 million has been allocated for the programme this year.

As part of the 2026 programme, financial assistance of Rs. 700 per existing tree will be provided for fertiliser and mulching for 20,300 trees. A further 37,500 new king coconut plants are to be provided to growers at a subsidised price of Rs. 150 each.

At the launch, subsidy cheques, king coconut and coconut plants, equipment for controlling whitefly and agricultural machinery were distributed among growers. Certificates were also presented to king coconut growers and exporters.

More than 600 growers from Kurunegala and Gampaha districts attended the event.

The programme is being implemented with the support of the Ministry of Plantation and Community Infrastructure, Coconut Cultivation Board, Coconut Development Authority and other institutions and stakeholders involved in the coconut sector. Local institutions and regional stakeholders will also be involved in establishing the king coconut villages.

Plantation and Community Infrastructure Minister Samantha Vidyaratne, Deputy Minister of Agriculture and Livestock Namal Karunaratne, Minister of Rural Development, Social Security and Community Empowerment Upali Pannilage, North Western Province Governor Tissa Kumarasiri, Deputy Minister of Plantation and Community Infrastructure Sundaralingam Pradeep and officials from relevant institutions attended the launch.
