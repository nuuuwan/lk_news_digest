Iconic tusker Kavantissa dies in Galgamuwa

Colombo, Oct 16 (Daily Mirror) - The iconic tusker Kavantissa, regarded as one of the largest and tallest elephants in Sri Lanka, has died after suffering from health complications for some time.

The ailing elephant was found collapsed in the Siyambalangamuwa area this morning (16), wildlife officials said.

Medical treatment had been provided since September 24 for an injury to its front right leg, reportedly caused by a gunshot.

Although the wound had shown signs of healing, the tusker had developed other serious symptoms in recent days, including refusing to eat, according to reports.

Kavantissa, which roamed the Galgamuwa region, was widely admired for its imposing stature and was considered one of the country's most prominent tuskers.
