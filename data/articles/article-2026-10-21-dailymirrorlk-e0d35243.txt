Swadeshi Khomba expands Herbal range

Three new variants: Lily and Gotukola, Lemongrass and Aloe and Cucumber bring new herbal experiences to a brand trusted by generations

Swadeshi Khomba, Sri Lanka’s No. 1 herbal brand from The Swadeshi Industrial Works PLC, has introduced three new variants of its iconic Khomba Herbal – Lily and Gotukola, Lemongrass and Aloe and Cucumber – marking an exciting new chapter in the brand’s more than eight-decade journey.

For the first time, Swadeshi Khomba is extending its herbal offering with three distinctive variants developed to cater to different consumer needs, while remaining rooted in the natural herbal goodness for which Khomba has been trusted by generations.

The three new additions are Swadeshi Khomba Lily and Gotukola for youthful skin, Swadeshi Khomba Lemongrass for long-lasting freshness and Swadeshi Khomba Aloe and Cucumber for cool skin.

At the heart of all three variants is margosa, locally known as kohomba, the trusted botanical that has defined the Khomba brand for generations and is known for helping maintain clean, healthy-looking skin.

The new range is conveniently packed in attractive cartons and offered at an affordable price, making it an ideal choice for everyday family use.

Swadeshi Khomba Lily and Gotukola for youthful skin combines the goodness of Kohomba with Lily and Gotukola. Lily is known for its gentle soothing qualities, helping to leave the skin feeling soft, refreshed and beautifully cared for, while gotukola, traditionally valued in herbal care, helps nourish and revitalise the skin to support a naturally healthy appearance.

Swadeshi Khomba Lemongrass for long-lasting freshness combines kohomba with the refreshing goodness of lemongrass. Known for its fresh herbal character, lemongrass helps leave the skin feeling fresh and revitalised, creating an uplifting bathing experience ideal for everyday use.

Swadeshi Khomba Aloe and Cucumber for cool skin brings together kohomba, aloe vera and cucumber for a refreshing skincare experience particularly suited to Sri Lanka’s tropical climate.

Swadeshi Deputy Chairperson and Managing Director Chulodhara Samarasinghe said, “The new range reflects Swadeshi’s commitment to bringing the benefits of carefully selected herbal ingredients to modern consumers while continuing to evolve with changing personal care expectations, whilst maintaining our quality and trust with new plant-based formulations.”
