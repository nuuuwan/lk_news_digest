Siyapatha Finance receives independent GHG verification

Siyapatha Finance PLC has strengthened its commitment to climate action with the receipt of a Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Verification Opinion from the Sri Lanka Climate Fund (Pvt.) Ltd, following the independent verification of its 2025 organisational-level GHG statement.

The recognition marks an important milestone in Siyapatha Finance’s sustainability journey, reinforcing its commitment to credible emissions measurement, transparent environmental reporting and responsible business practices.

The Verification Opinion was presented at Certificate Awarding Ceremony 2026, organised by Sri Lanka Climate Fund and held at the Main Auditorium of the Environment Ministry recently. The ceremony brought together organisations that have undertaken GHG quantification and verification initiatives as part of their efforts to strengthen environmental performance and climate-related practices.

Siyapatha Finance’s organisational-level GHG statement was independently verified against the requirements of ISO 14064-1:2018 and the GHG Protocol, with the verification process conducted in accordance with ISO 14064-3:2019.

The verification covers the company’s operationally controlled business activities, providing an independently assessed and credible representation of its reported greenhouse gas emissions.

The verification provides Siyapatha Finance with a robust foundation to systematically measure its environmental footprint, identify opportunities for emissions reduction and strengthen environmental performance across its operations. It also enhances the credibility and transparency of the company’s emissions reporting, supporting a more structured and accountable approach to managing its environmental impact.

As part of its broader sustainability agenda, Siyapatha Finance has progressively integrated environmental considerations into its operations and decision-making processes. Its initiatives include energy conservation, greenhouse gas emissions management, sustainable finance, environmental awareness and responsible resource consumption.

Siyapatha Finance stated that the measurement and independent verification of GHG emissions represent an important foundation for understanding its environmental impact, identifying opportunities for improvement and establishing meaningful climate-related targets.

The company remains committed to advancing responsible business practices across its operations while supporting customers and communities through sustainable financial solutions.  The independently verified GHG statement represents another significant step in Siyapatha Finance’s ongoing sustainability journey, reinforcing its focus on accountability, measurable environmental performance and meaningful progress towards climate action.

Through continued efforts to embed sustainability into its operations and business practices, Siyapatha Finance aims to contribute to a more resilient and sustainable future while creating long-term value for its stakeholders.
