Storex Renewables partners world’s no. 1 Sigenergy to answer Sri Lanka’s solar policy shift

As Net Metering and Net Accounting close to new applicants, Storex Renewables introduces a future-proof integrated storage solution built for the Energy Independent era

Storex Renewables Ltd., has launched SigenStor Neo, an all-in-one solar and battery storage system from Sigenergy, in Sri Lanka. The only scalable and future-proof solution comes as the Ministry of Energy moves all new rooftop solar connections to the Net Plus scheme, closing Net Metering and Net Accounting to new applicants.

With the closure of practically available solar schemes, consumers are now required to have their own storage and be independent in their solar power generation, making on-site storage more valuable for maximising self-consumption and controlling costs. Storex Renewables, which brings prior experience implementing large-scale battery systems, positions SigenStor Neo as its answer to that shift.

Sigenergy Neo Systems integrate the energy management system, inverter, battery and backup module into a single unit. It connects to existing solar installations, so homeowners and businesses already on Net Metering or Net Accounting can add storage without replacing their current setup. The battery is rated for 10,000 charge cycles, the highest count in the market. It carries an IP66 rating for protection against dust and weather exposure in Sri Lankan conditions. The Sigenergy inverter is perhaps the first available in stock in Sri Lanka that can grow with your demand. Whilst traditional battery-based solar systems do not offer future upgradability of batteries of different sizes during different stages, Storex offers a premium future-proof solution. The Storex Sigenergy system switches to backup power with zero disruption to connected loads, and allows power to be drawn efficiently from panels installed at different angles or orientations.

The product is available for both residential and commercial installations with all leading installers such as SOLAR THERM, AIRO Engineering and selected partners/resellers.

Sigenergy Regional Manager Marvin Xiao said, “We were looking for the best partner to distribute our products in Sri Lanka. We found that partner in Storex.”

Storex Renewables Director Lasith Wimalasena said, “As part of the Windforce PLC Family, we have over two decades of experience in renewable energy and have delivered the largest battery and solar projects in the market. SigenStor Neo will not be just another product on the shelf. It is a premium product integrated and genuinely easy to use, and that is what we are bringing to Sri Lankan homes and businesses.”

Further elaborating on the subject, Storex Renewables Manager Praboda Welagedera explained that “This is the only solution of its kind fully integrated with inverter, battery and solar, where customers have peace of mind in terms of installation or compatibility issues. It simply works with any existing solar installation as an internal capacity enhancement with battery storage that can also grow.”
