No documents missing from 323-container probe, Minister insists

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara dismissed claims that the committee report on the controversial 323 containers had gone missing, insisting that all relevant documents and records remain securely in Parliament.

Speaking on the matter, Nanayakkara said the committee was appointed by the Speaker of Parliament and that all official records, including statements made by around 10 witnesses, were safely maintained.

He said he was currently completing the final stages of preparing the committee's report as its chairman.

Explaining the delay, the Minister said the committee had to examine a substantial volume of evidence, comprising approximately 15 files of transcripts and related documents.

Accordingly, Parliament had approved a three-month extension of the committee's term, with the final deadline set for December 21.

Nanayakkara said the drafting of the report had been completed and that it was currently undergoing final typing. He expressed confidence that the report would be completed by the end of next week.

Rejecting allegations that documents had been lost or misplaced, he reiterated that all committee records remained intact and securely held within Parliament.
