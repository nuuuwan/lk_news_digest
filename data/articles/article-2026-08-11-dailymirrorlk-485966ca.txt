Biometric system proposed for future electronic passports

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror)The provision is aimed at facilitating the implementation of a biometric data entry system for electronic passports to be issued in the future, under a new Immigration Bill approved by the Cabinet.

The Cabinet has approved the publication of the Bill in the Government Gazette and its subsequent submission to Parliament for approval.

The Bill is intended to replace the Immigration and Emigration Act No. 20 of 1948.

A previous Bill prepared for this purpose had been presented to Parliament, but further proceedings could not be completed following the dissolution of the Ninth Parliament on September 24, 2024. The said Bill was also subsequently subject to a stay order issued by the Supreme Court.

In view of these developments, the Cabinet on August 18, 2025, approved the drafting of a new Bill.

Later, at its meeting on December 10, 2025, the Cabinet approved the inclusion of appropriate provisions in the new Bill to designate the Secretary to the Ministry in charge of Immigration and Emigration as the Country Signing Certification Authority responsible for implementing the biometric data entry system for future electronic passports.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs to gazette the Immigration Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman and thereafter present it to Parliament for approval.
