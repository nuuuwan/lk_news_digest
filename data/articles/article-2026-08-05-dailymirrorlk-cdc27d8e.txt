Textile industry’s future depends on innovation and technology: Minister Handunnetti

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The future growth and competitiveness of Sri Lanka’s textile and apparel industry will depend on innovation, efficiency and the adoption of new technologies, Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Minister Sunil Handunnetti said.

Addressing the inauguration of ‘Intex – InMac Sri Lanka 2026’, South Asia’s leading international textile and apparel technology exhibition, held today (05) at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH), the Minister said the apparel sector remains Sri Lanka’s largest export-earning industry and a key pillar of the economy.

He said the exhibition would serve as a catalyst for investment, technology transfer and the introduction of modern solutions to strengthen the industry’s competitiveness in global markets.

Minister Handunnetti said the government plans to make significant investments to develop the Eravur Textile Park and enhance the education and training sector through the Sri Lanka Institute of Textile and Apparel (SLITA).

He also said discussions were held with the Indian Minister during the recent ‘Bharat Tex’ exhibition in India regarding the Free Trade Agreement (FTA), garment quotas and GSP+ concessions.

Inviting industry stakeholders to participate in ‘Expo 2027’, scheduled to be held at the BMICH from January 14 to 17, 2027, the Minister highlighted the importance of continued collaboration to develop Sri Lanka’s export industries.

The opening ceremony was attended by Industrial Development Board Chairman Yasas Hewage, Export Development Board Chairman Mangala Wijesinghe, Joint Apparel Association Forum Chairman Felix Fernando, government officials, ambassadors and foreign representatives.

The exhibition, which continues until August 7, features more than 250 exhibitors representing over 15 countries, showcasing the latest technologies and innovations in the textile and apparel sector.
