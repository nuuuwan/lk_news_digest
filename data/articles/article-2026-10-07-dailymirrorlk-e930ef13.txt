Contempt proceedings against Dilith, Gammanpila, others fixed for October 15

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The contempt of court proceedings against six individuals, including former Ministers Wimal Weerawansa and Udaya Gammanpila and Parliamentarian Dilith Jayaweera, over allegations that they made false and contemptuous statements concerning ongoing judicial proceedings related to the Easter Sunday attacks, were today fixed for October 15 by the Court of Appeal.

The case was taken up before a three-judge Bench of the Court of Appeal comprising President of the Court of Appeal Justice Rohantha Abeysuriya, Justice Chamath Morais and Justice Adithya Patabendige.

The proceedings are related to a complaint filed by Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena, who alleged that the statements were made in connection with court proceedings involving retired Major General Suresh Sallay.
