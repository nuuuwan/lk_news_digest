Seylan Bank partners Colombo Motor Show 2026 as Official Banking & Leasing Partner

Seylan Bank PLC has reaffirmed its commitment to supporting Sri Lanka’s automotive industry by partnering with the 20th edition of the Colombo Motor Show 2026 as the Official Banking & Leasing Partner for the 10th consecutive year.

This makes Seylan Bank the longest-standing partner of the Colombo Motor Show, further reflecting its longstanding association with one of Sri Lanka’s prominent automotive exhibitions.

The four-day exhibition is scheduled to take place from 20th to 23rd November 2026 at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH), marking the first time the Colombo Motor Show will be held as a four-day event.

As one of Sri Lanka’s leading international motor and motor accessories exhibitions, the Colombo Motor Show will bring together vehicle enthusiasts, prospective buyers, industry professionals and automotive service providers.

The 2026 edition is expected to feature over 200 trade stalls covering motor vehicles, accessories, spare parts, lubricants, tyres, batteries, leasing facilities and other motor-related services.

Through its continued partnership, Seylan Bank will showcase a comprehensive range of financial solutions designed to support customers in meeting their personal and business needs, with a particular focus on Seylan Leasing facilities. Visitors to the exhibition will have the opportunity to receive on-the-spot leasing evaluations and expert guidance from Seylan Leasing Specialists, helping them explore vehicle financing options conveniently during the event.

In addition to its leasing solutions, Seylan Bank will present its Credit Card offerings, Current and Savings Accounts, and Digital Banking services, giving visitors access to a broader portfolio of financial products and services under one roof.

Commenting on the partnership, Eugene Seneviratne, Deputy General Manager, Retail Banking, Seylan Bank PLC said, “Our 10th consecutive year as the Official Banking & Leasing Partner of the Colombo Motor Show reflects our continued commitment to supporting the aspirations of Sri Lankan motorists and the wider automotive industry.”

Organised by Asia Exhibition and Conventions (Pvt) Ltd, the Colombo Motor Show 2026 is expected to offer visitors a comprehensive experience featuring automotive products, services and live motor-related activities. The exhibition will provide a platform for vehicle lovers and potential customers to explore their interests and connect with businesses across the automotive sector.
