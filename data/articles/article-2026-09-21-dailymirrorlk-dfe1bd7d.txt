Southern Expressway bus crash injures over 10

More than 10 people have reportedly been hospitalised following a collision involving a bus and several vehicles on the Southern Expressway this morning.

The accident occurred between the Gelanigama and Dodangoda interchanges when a bus travelling from Matara to Colombo collided with several other vehicles.

More than 10 people injured in the accident have reportedly been admitted to hospital for treatment.
