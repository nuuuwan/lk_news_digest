Vijitha Herath to address UN General Assembly today

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath is scheduled to deliver Sri Lanka’s National Statement at the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York today (28).

Herath, who is also the Minister of Foreign Employment and Tourism, arrived in New York on Sunday (27) to attend the high-level UNGA sessions.

In a post on X, formerly known as Twitter, the Foreign Minister said he was looking forward to engaging with United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres, Foreign Ministers and senior UN officials during his visit.

According to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism, Herath is also scheduled to hold bilateral meetings with Guterres, senior UN officials and Foreign Ministers from several countries on the sidelines of the General Assembly.
