‘Not political revenge’: Bimal tables 50 corruption, fraud and criminal cases in Parliament

Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake today tabled in Parliament a 10-page document containing details of 50 corruption, fraud and criminal cases involving former ministers, politicians, members of the Rajapaksa family and senior state officials, rejecting allegations that the Government was pursuing political revenge against its opponents.

Rathnayake said the cases listed were not newly initiated by the present administration but stemmed from complaints, investigations and legal proceedings dating back to the 2015–2019 period.

He accused previous administrations of suppressing investigations through political influence and said the present Government had allowed investigators to carry out their duties without political interference.

The Minister said the Government was not selectively targeting opposition politicians but was pursuing cases that had remained unresolved for years.

The document includes cases involving former ministers Johnston Fernando, Wimal Weerawansa, Mahindananda Aluthgamage, Basil Rajapaksa, Rajitha Senaratne, Ravi Karunanayake, Prasanna Ranatunga, A.H.M. Fowzie, Bandula Gunawardana and Rohitha Bogollagama.

It also lists investigations and legal proceedings involving members of the Rajapaksa family, including Namal, Yoshitha, Gotabaya, Shiranthi, Rohitha, Chamal and Shashindra Rajapaksa.

Among the major cases referred to are the Central Bank bond transactions, the Krrish deal, MiG aircraft procurement, the Divi Neguma funds case, the sil cloth distribution case and allegations involving the Carlton Sports Network.

The document also includes investigations into several high-profile criminal cases, including the Easter Sunday attacks, the killing of rugby player Wasim Thajudeen, the murder of journalist Lasantha Wickrematunge, the disappearance of journalist Prageeth Eknaligoda and attacks on journalists Keith Noyahr and Upali Tennakoon.

Cases involving former Central Bank Governor Arjuna Mahendran, businessman Arjun Aloysius, former Defence Secretary Hemasiri Fernando, former Police Chief Pujith Jayasundara and former presidential secretary Lalith Weeratunga are also included.

Alleged misuse of 153 Sathosa employees for political activities, reportedly causing a loss of Rs. 40 million. The document lists five High Court case references dating back to 2016.
