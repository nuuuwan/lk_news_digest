Hero-Abans Auto celebrates excellence at national convention

Hero-Abans Auto recently hosted its prestigious Partners’ Convention at ITC Ratnadipa, Colombo, bringing together over two hundred sales dealers from across the island along with senior representatives from Abans and Hero MotoCorp India. Held on July 4th under the inspiring theme “Accelerating Towards the Future,” the event celebrated the brand’s remarkable growth and strengthened commitment to transforming Sri Lanka’s two-wheeler industry.

The convention highlighted the rapid progress Hero has achieved since its relaunch in Sri Lanka in April 2025 through its partnership with Abans Auto. As the world’s No. 1 manufacturer of motorcycles and scooters, Hero MotoCorp has quickly established a strong presence in the local market, driven by its focus on quality, innovation, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Addressing the gathering, company representatives expressed confidence in the brand’s future, emphasizing that Hero is more than just a two-wheeler manufacturer. It represents freedom, dependability, and outstanding performance, empowering riders to overcome barriers and embrace new opportunities with confidence. Through its expanding product portfolio and customer-centric approach, Hero aims to redefine the riding experience for Sri Lankan consumers.
