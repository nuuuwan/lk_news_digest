SL rallies agencies behind US $ 36bn export push

Sri Lanka is shifting its ambitious US $ 36 billion export drive towards execution, with the government calling for high-level intervention, stronger inter-agency coordination and timely implementation of measures under the National Export Development Plan (NEDP) 2026-2030.

The push places the ports and logistics sector firmly within the implementation framework of the NEDP, as the policymakers seek to translate the country’s export strategy into measurable outcomes and strengthen Sri Lanka’s position as a competitive regional logistics and knowledge-intensive export hub.

The implementation priorities were discussed at a high-level meeting at the Ports and Civil Aviation Ministry, chaired by Ports and Civil Aviation Minister Anura Karunathilaka and attended by the senior ministry officials, Export Development Board (EDB) Chairman Mangala Wijesinghe, Director General Erandika Dissanayake and other senior officials.

The discussions centred on the actions to be undertaken by the Ports and Civil Aviation Ministry and its relevant institutions as lead implementing agencies under the NEDP, signalling an increased focus on converting the export blueprint into sector-level action.

Among the priority areas identified were improvements to the port and logistics infrastructure and services, trade and logistics facilitation, stronger connectivity and more efficient movement of export cargo. Developing Sri Lanka’s capabilities as a regional maritime and logistics hub was also highlighted.

The discussions placed particular emphasis on the high-level intervention, coordination between agencies and timely execution of the identified actions, with collaboration between the EDB, Ports Ministry, port-related institutions and other stakeholders seen as critical to delivering measurable results.

The NEDP, formulated by the EDB, under the guidance of the Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Ministry, with technical assistance from the Asian Development Bank, targets increasing Sri Lanka’s exports to US $ 36 billion by 2030. The plan was developed following consultations with the public and private sector stakeholders.
