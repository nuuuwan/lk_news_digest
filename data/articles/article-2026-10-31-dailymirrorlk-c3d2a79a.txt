Leema Creations wins Industrial Excellence Award

Leema Creations, recognized by the industry in Sri Lanka and overseas during the past decade has proven once again that they are at the pinnacle of excellence in the interior industry marking another major milestone in the company’s corporate journey.

‘The Industrial Excellence Award (Large Category) 2026’ recognizes its sustained leadership and excellence in Sri Lanka’s interior design and manufacturing industry.

Leema, with a good track record on service delivery in interior architecture, proved that they are among the best in the interior industry by winning the Industrial Excellence Award 2026 (Manufacturing Sector Large Category/Silver), at the awards ceremony held on 15 October at the Cinnamon Life, City of Dreams, organized by the Ceylon National Chamber of Industries.

By winning this Award, for the fifth consecutive time, Leema’s exposure and credibility has significantly increased in the market place. This significant accolade for utmost level of creativity, innovation, originality and inspiring practices in the field of architecture, interiors and design signifies our status and reputation as among the best in the industry.
