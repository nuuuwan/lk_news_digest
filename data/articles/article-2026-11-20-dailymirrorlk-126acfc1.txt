Restored Golden Canopy of Sri Dalada Maligawa unveiled

The newly restored Golden Canopy (Ran Viyana) of the historic Sri Dalada Maligawa (Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic) in Kandy was unveiled today (20).

Restoration work on the iconic canopy—which shelters the two-storey inner shrine built in 1592 AD by King Vimaladharmasuriya I housing the Left Sacred Tooth Relic of Gautama Buddha—commenced in 2025. Over time, the gold leaf plating had tarnished and worn away, diminishing its original luster and grandeur.

Originally constructed and offered to the Temple in 1987 by then-Prime Minister late Ranasinghe Premadasa to enhance the beauty and security of the shrine's interior and frontage, the canopy was initially designed by the State Engineering Corporation under the leadership of Engineer Gemunu Silva, alongside local and foreign engineering teams. Prior to the current project, the canopy underwent restorations twice, in 2002 and 2018.

Today's unveiling ceremony was attended by the Most Venerable Mahanayake Thero of the Asgiriya Chapter, venerable members of the Maha Sangha of both the Malwathu and Asgiriya Chapters, a Thai delegation led by the revered meditation master Most Venerable Luang Por Anan Akiñcano of Wat Marp Jan in Thailand, Diyawadana Nilame Pradeep Nilanga Dela, and other distinguished guests.

Carried out under the advice of the Mahanayake Theros of the Malwathu and Asgiriya Chapters, the guidance of the Diyawadana Nilame, and the full sponsorship of the Vishwa Parami Foundation, the 2,900-square-foot surface of the Golden Canopy was restored using durable 24-karat gold mosaic technology. The project incorporated technical consultations, designs, and recommendations from a renowned Italian company, alongside advisory support from leading international engineering firms and contributions from several local companies.

Additionally, the metal framework of the canopy underwent a thorough engineering inspection and necessary repairs, while the golden lotus motifs and all other embellishments were fully restored. The twin pinnacles (Koth Karali) atop the canopy were crafted in pure gold by the world-renowned Singaporean jewelry firm Royal Insignia, and the structure has been illuminated with a specialized "High-Beam Eye Light" system manufactured by Japan's Iwasaki Electric Co.
