Catholic Church  expects satisfactory judgements on other cases pertaining to Easter Sunday attacks

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Catholic Church  expects satisfactory judgements on other cases pertaining to the Easter Sunday bomb attacks, National Mass Communications Director Fr. Jude Krishantha said.

"The investigations on the Easter Sunday bomb attacks are far from over. There are more cases pending and we hope others who are responsible will also be subjected to a fair trial," Fr. Krishantha told the media.
