Janashakthi Life concludes Shilpa Shakthi 2026

Janashakthi Life has successfully concluded the 2026 edition of Shilpa Shakthi, its flagship educational initiative dedicated to supporting the grade five scholarship examination candidates.

Marking the programme’s 12th consecutive year, Shilpa Shakthi once again reinforces Janashakthi Life’s long-standing commitment to empowering young learners through education and creating opportunities for academic success.

Conducted in collaboration with Derana FM, the 2026 programme reached students across key regions of Sri Lanka through a series of 12 educational seminars, successfully benefiting 6,568 grade five scholarship examination candidates.

The seminars provided the students with expert academic guidance, examination preparation techniques and motivation, helping them build the confidence and knowledge needed to excel in one of the most significant milestones of their educational journey.

Designed to bridge the educational disparities and improve access to quality learning opportunities, Shilpa Shakthi continues to support students regardless of their geographical location. The programme also recognised the important role parents play in a child’s educational success by incorporating dedicated guidance and motivational sessions that equipped them with practical insights on supporting their children throughout the scholarship examination journey.

Janashakthi Life Director and CEO Ravi Liyanage said, “For the past 12 years, Shilpa Shakthi has remained a meaningful expression of our commitment to empowering young minds through education. We are proud to have supported thousands of students once again this year by providing them with the knowledge, guidance and confidence they need to pursue their aspirations. At Janashakthi Life, we believe that investing in education is an investment in the future of our communities and our nation and we remain committed to creating opportunities that help young learners realise their full potential.”
