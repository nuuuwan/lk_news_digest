Innovation, technology key to future of apparel industry: Handunnetti

Sri Lanka’s textile and apparel sector must accelerate investment in innovation and advanced technology to remain globally competitive, Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Minister Sunil Handunnetti said on Tuesday, as the country seeks to strengthen its largest export industry.

Speaking at the inauguration of the Intex-InMac Sri Lanka 2026 international textile and apparel technology exhibition, at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall, Handunnetti described the apparel industry as the country’s highest foreign exchange earning export sector and a cornerstone of the national economy.

He said the future growth of the industry would depend on innovation, efficiency and technological advancement, adding that the exhibition would serve as a platform to attract investment, facilitate technology transfer and connect the local manufacturers with the global suppliers. Handunnetti also announced that the government plans to make significant investments to develop the Eravur Textile Park, while strengthening the industry skills through the Sri Lanka Institute of Textile and Apparel, aimed at improving the sector’s long-term competitiveness. Highlighting the efforts to expand market access, Handunnetti said he recently met India’s Minister of Textiles during the Bharat Tex exhibition, where the discussions focused on the proposed free trade agreement, garment export quotas and GSP+ trade concessions.
