Minister orders probe into mass fish death at Kilinochchi Tank

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Minister of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources Ramalingam Chandrasekar has requested a comprehensive investigation into the mass death of fish and other aquatic organisms at the Kilinochchi Tank.

The Minister contacted Northern Province Governor Nagalingam Vethanayagam and requested him to obtain a detailed report from the Department of Irrigation on the causes of the incident.

A large number of aquatic organisms, including large fish, freshwater prawns and fingerlings, have been found dead and floating in the lower section of the tank.

The Minister stressed the need for a formal investigation, particularly in view of concerns over the tank's water-release procedures and possible issues arising from irrigation activities.

The incident has severely affected families dependent on freshwater fishing for their livelihoods. Fishermen have suffered losses not only of fish stocks cultivated through prolonged efforts but also of fishing equipment, including nets.

The Minister requested the Governor to obtain comprehensive information on the damage suffered by fishermen, the impact on their livelihoods and the possible causes of the incident, and submit a detailed report.

He also stressed the need to assess the impact of the fish deaths on the tank's water quality and the surrounding environment, identify the root causes and take appropriate measures to provide relief to the affected fishing community.
