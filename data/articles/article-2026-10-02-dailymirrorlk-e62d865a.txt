Probe ordered into licence, number plate delays

Colombo, Oct. 3 (Daily Mirror) - A parliamentary oversight committee has ordered a comprehensive investigation into delays in issuing vehicle number plates and driving licences, directing officials to submit a full report within 45 days.

The directive was issued by the Sectoral Oversight Committee on Infrastructure and Strategic Development during a meeting at Parliament chaired by Member of Parliament S. M. Marikkar. Officials were asked to identify the causes of the delays, including administrative shortcomings, technical problems and issues related to procurement and tender procedures.

Department of Motor Traffic officials informed the committee that number plates for vehicles registered after March 10, 2026, are currently being issued within 14 days.

However, a significant backlog remains in the printing of driving licences, with approximately 678,000 licences awaiting production.

Officials said two additional printing machines are expected to be installed by October 15, increasing the department’s daily printing capacity from around 4,600 to 8,200 licences. The committee stressed the need to improve technical infrastructure and fill vacant administrative positions to prevent further delays in public services.

The committee also discussed the expansion of rooftop solar power and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) with officials from the Ministry of Energy and relevant agencies. Particular attention was given to grid stability, frequency control and reverse power flow caused by fluctuating solar generation during periods of low daytime demand.

Officials said plans are underway to install around 400,000 smart meters, initially prioritising consumers who use more than 180 units of electricity. The committee also considered battery storage solutions at distribution-transformer level in urban areas such as Colombo and Kotte.
