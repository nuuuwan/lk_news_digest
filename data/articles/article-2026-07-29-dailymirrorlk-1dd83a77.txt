Janashakthi Life among SL’s 50 Best Workplaces for 2026

Janashakthi Life has been recognised among Sri Lanka’s 50 Best Workplaces for 2026 by Great Place To Work Sri Lanka.

The 50 recognised workplaces comprise 17 Large, 13 Medium, 10 Small, and 10 Micro workplaces, with Janashakthi Life recognised in the Medium category, comprising organisations with 251–500 employees.

The recognition is based on the Great Place To Work® Trust Index Employee Experience Survey, which evaluates employees’ experiences of trust, pride, and workplace relationships, alongside a Culture Audit that assesses the organisation’s people practices. The Trust Index contributes 75 percent of the overall score, while the Culture Audit accounts for the remaining 25 percent. Janashakthi Life’s inclusion follows its Great Place To Work Certified status and an 88 percent Employee Trust Index™ score achieved in 2025, reinforcing the consistency of its employee experience and workplace practices.

“Being recognised among Sri Lanka’s 50 Best Workplaces, together with the strong sense of inclusivity and camaraderie experienced by our employees, reflects the strength of our culture and the leadership philosophy that shapes our workplace. The endorsement of our employees is a great encouragement to further strengthen our workplace and reinforce the values we all live by. Our values of respect, collaboration, and integrity are strongly reflected in the Great Place To Work workplace health indicators, while being performance-driven and embracing innovation remain firmly embedded in our culture. It is particularly encouraging to note that our team members feel they are given equal opportunities, empowered to contribute, and supported with the guidance needed to reach their fullest potential,” said Ravi Liyanage, Director/CEO of Janashakthi Insurance PLC.

“This achievement reflects the collective commitment of our people and leaders to building a people-centred and values-driven culture where wellbeing, growth, and high performance go hand in hand. We remain committed to further strengthening this culture and the employee experience,” said Danushka Seneth D. Samarasinghe, Head of Human Resources at Janashakthi Insurance PLC.
