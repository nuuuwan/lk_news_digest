Cabinet moves to amend Act to address difficulties in functioning of Language Institute’s Board

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal to amend the National Language Education and Training Institute Act, No. 26 of 2007, in order to address practical difficulties in the functioning of the institute’s Management Board.

Under the Act, the Secretaries to the Ministries in charge of Education, Public Administration, Finance, and Constitutional Affairs and National Integration serve as ex officio members of the Management Board of the National Language Education and Training Institute. However, it has been observed that the busy schedules of these senior officials make it difficult for them to take part in the Board’s monthly meetings.

To resolve the issue, the Cabinet has approved a proposal made by the Justice and National Integration Minister Harshana Nanayakkara, instructing the Legal Draftsman to prepare a draft bill amending the relevant sections of the Act.

The amendments are intended to allow arrangements to be made for the appointment of ex officio members to the Management Board in a manner that minimises these problems.
