Government expenditure increase in 2027

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Total government expenditure and expenditure for main sectors are projected to increase in 2027, as per the Appropriation Bill which was presented to Parliament yesterday.

Accordingly the total state expenditure will increase to approximately Rs 4992 billion in 2027 compared to Rs 4545 billion in 2026.

Allocations for the President, Prime Minister, defence, health, public security and education have also been increased in 2027.

The total allocation for the President has been increased to Rs 11.2 billion compared to 2027 to Rs 8.2 billion in 2026. Allocation for the Prime Minister has been marginally increased to Rs 1.02 billion compared to Rs 898 million in 2026.

The defence budget for 2027 has been increased slightly to Rs 458 billion compared to Rs 455 billion in 2026. Amount allocated for health has also increased to Rs 590 billion compared to Rs 514 billion in 2026. Allocation for education has been increased to Rs 328 billion in 2027 whereas the amount allocated in 2026 was Rs 301 billion. The allocation for public security has been increased to Rs 230 billion compared to Rs 183 billion in 2026.

The capital expenditure allocated for education, defence, health is lower next year than the recurrent expenditure as it has been the case during the past year while a greater amount of funds have been allocated for the ministry of transport, highways and urban development, agriculture, energy and housing as capital expenditure as per the Appropriation Bill.
