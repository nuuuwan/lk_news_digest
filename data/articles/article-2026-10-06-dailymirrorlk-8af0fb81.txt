Quiet start to week in secondary market

While the secondary market continued the subdued sentiment from the closure of the previous week, the trading volumes and activities remained limited.

Over the 2030 segment, both the 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities traded between 11.15 percent and 11.25 percent. At the belly-end, the 15.10.2034 bond traded at 12.00 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee marginally appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.60/US dollar, compared to Rs.330.62/US dollar seen earlier.

Moreover, the Census and Statistics Department published that Sri Lanka’s unemployment rate increased to 4 percent in 2Q 2026, up by 0.2 percent year-on-year and 0.3 percent quarter-on-quarter.

The number of unemployed people in Sri Lanka during 2Q 2026 was estimated at 340,240, while the unemployment rate among women and men stood at 6.1 percent and 2.8 percent, respectively.
