Five elephant control units receive Rs. 90 million vehicle boost

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Five Elephant Control Units in the Puttalam Wildlife Zone have received new single-cab vehicles worth approximately Rs. 90 million to strengthen operations against human-elephant conflict.

The vehicles were purchased by the Ministry of Environment to improve the facilities available to wildlife officers engaged in field operations.

The beneficiaries are the Elephant Control Units in Karuwalagaswewa, Nawagattegama, Mahakumbukkadawala, Puttalam and Wanathavilluwa.

The vehicles were handed over on November 24 at the Puttalam Assistant Wildlife Zonal Office at an event attended by Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government Prof. Chandana Abayaratna and Deputy Environment Minister Anton Jayakody.

The Department of Wildlife Conservation has been facing a shortage of vehicles, making it difficult for officers to carry out their duties effectively.

In particular, the lack of transport has created obstacles in responding to reports of elephants entering villages, conducting elephant-driving operations and monitoring dangerous situations.

The new vehicles are expected to improve the operational capacity of the five units and help officers respond more effectively to emergencies.
