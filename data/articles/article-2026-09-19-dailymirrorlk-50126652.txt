Govt., public should respect railway workers: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Minister of Transport and Highways Dr. Prasanna Gunasena expressed appreciation for railway officers and employees who continued working to maintain train services despite the ongoing trade union action.

Speaking at a media briefing, Gunasena said the contribution of employees who remained on duty and worked under difficult conditions to maintain railway services should be specially recognised.

He said 187 train journeys were operated yesterday, while services including the express train to Jaffna, including the Yal Devi, were maintained.

The Deputy Minister also thanked employees who reported for duty despite not participating in the trade union action.

He said some railway officers had worked for nearly 24 hours continuously to maintain services while giving priority to passenger safety and preventing accidents.

According to Gunasena, certain railway operations that normally require the involvement of a large number of employees were carried out with only a limited workforce during the trade union action.

“This cannot be considered a normal duty. The officers and employees who took responsibility for maintaining a public service during difficult times should be clearly appreciated. They should be respected by the entire government and by the people who travel by train,” he said.

The Deputy Minister said the employees who continued to protect the railway service for the public amid the disruption deserved the gratitude and respect of both the Government and the people.
