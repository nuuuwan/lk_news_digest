India opens safe harbour to 25 Sri Lankan fishing boats

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Around 25 Sri Lankan multi-day fishing vessels stranded near Indian waters have been granted permission to enter Indian territorial waters and seek shelter from Cyclonic Storm Arnab, following diplomatic intervention by the Ministry of Fisheries, Aquatic and Marine Resources.

The Ministry made a formal request to the Indian Government through the High Commission of India in Sri Lanka, securing permission for the vessels to move into safer waters amid deteriorating weather conditions in the Bay of Bengal.

According to a weather advisory issued by the Department of Meteorology on October 27, 2026, Cyclonic Storm Arnab is expected to make landfall along the Andhra Pradesh coast of India by October 28.

The advisory warned that wind speeds in sea areas between latitudes 10°N and 20°N and longitudes 80°E and 95°E could reach between 80 and 140 km/h, while wave heights could rise to between 3 and 4 metres.

The Department of Meteorology has advised fishing communities to avoid venturing into the affected deep-sea areas due to the hazardous conditions.

Following the diplomatic intervention, the crews aboard the Sri Lankan vessels have been instructed to leave the high-risk areas immediately and navigate towards designated safe locations.

The Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources has also informed vessel owners of the safety procedures and precautions to be followed during the emergency.

The Ministry has urged the fishing community to remain vigilant and closely monitor weather updates issued by the Department of Meteorology as the cyclone approaches the region.
