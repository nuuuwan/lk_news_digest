Medical reports indicate Shiranthi Rajapaksa needs bypass surgery, court told

Colombo, Oct. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Cheif Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama postponed the case until 2.45 p.m. to allow both the prosecution and the defence to examine the medical report submitted by the National Hospital, Colombo regarding the health condition of Shiranthi Rajapaksa.

The Magistrate stated that the medical reports indicate that the suspect requires bypass surgery.
