Month ends in green

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as Browns Investments, Carson Cumberbatch and Windforce with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.0 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Access Engineering, Sierra Cables and Citizens Development Business Finance. Mixed interest was observed in Browns Investments, Ceylon Cold Stores and Overseas Realty whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, HNB Finance and ACME Printing & Packaging. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

During the month, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 gained 1.0% and 1.2% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 3.64 Bn.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables and Access Engineering) whilst the sector index gained 0.33%. The share price of Sierra Cables decreased by Rs 0.40 (1.09%) to close at Rs 36.20. The share price of Access Engineering lost Rs 0.30 (0.40%) to close at Rs 74.40.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Browns Investments and Ceylon Cold Stores) whilst the sector index increased by 0.74%. The share price of Browns Investments moved up by Rs 0.40 (8.16%) to close at Rs 5.30. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores recorded a loss of Rs 1.25 (1.03%) to close at Rs 120.25.

Citizens Development Business Finance was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Citizens Development Business Finance appreciated by Rs 1.00 (2.86%) to close at Rs 36.00.
