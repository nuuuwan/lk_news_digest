Sri Lanka stocks close up, ASPI gains 0.27-pct

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Colombo Stock Exchange closed up on Tuesday, CSE data showed, with the benchmark All Share Price Index moving up 0.27 percent.

The ASPI was up 52.11 points at 19,693 (19,692.67), while the more liquid S&P SL20 was up 0.19 percent, or 10.44 points, at 5,612 (5,612.31).

Positive contributors to the ASPI were Dialog Axiata (up 0.48 percent at 42.20 rupees), Carson Cumberbatch (up 1.02 percent at 670 rupees), Ceylon Cold Stores (up 1.25 percent at 121.50 rupees), Laugfs Gas (up 5.07 percent at 37.30 rupees), and Lion Brewery (Ceylon) (up 1.86 percent at 1,849.75 rupees).

Colombo Dockyard (down 3.74 percent at 95.30 rupees), Vallibel One (down 1.19 percent at 83 rupees), Hatton National Bank (down 0.27 percent at 367.50 rupees), LOLC Finance (down 2.22 percent at 4.40 rupees), and LOLC Holdings (down 0.64 percent at 390 rupees) were top negative contributors.

Market turnover was 849.37 million rupees. Materials led turnover with 229.66 million rupees.

Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka announced a first interim dividend of 2.75 rupees per share for the financial year 2026/27, subject to withholding tax. The ex-dividend (XD) date is November 5, 2026, and the dividend payment is scheduled to be dispatched on November 25, 2026. (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
