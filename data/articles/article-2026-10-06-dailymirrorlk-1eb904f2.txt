Trincomalee Prison Assistant Superintendent arrested over alleged bribe

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Assistant Superintendent of the Trincomalee Remand Prison, was arrested by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) today (06) over an alleged Rs. 1.235 million bribe.

He was arrested at around 3.29 p.m. while inside his office, according to CIABOC.

The suspect is alleged to have solicited and accepted the bribe through a prison jailor from a supplier providing dry rations to inmates at the Trincomalee Prison.

The arrested suspect is due to be produced before the Trincomalee Magistrate's Court.
