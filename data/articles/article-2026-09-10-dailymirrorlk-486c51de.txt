Chamathka Jewellers opens 23rd branch in Negombo

Chamathka Jewellers has further strengthened its islandwide presence with the opening of its 23rd branch at Thilakma Square, Negombo.

With over two decades of experience in the jewellery industry, Chamathka Jewellers has built a distinctive identity around beautifully crafted S925 sterling silver jewellery embellished with natural precious and semi-precious gemstones.

Its collections feature an extensive variety of gemstones, including Blue, Pink and Yellow Sapphires, Rubies, Emeralds, Aquamarines, Amethysts, Topaz, Tourmalines, Garnets, Peridots and vibrant multi semi-precious stone creations. Over the years, the brand has continued to evolve with changing tastes while remaining true to its passion for colourful, wearable fine jewellery. More recently, Chamathka Jewellers has captured the attention of a growing Gen Z audience, with stylish, contemporary and eye-catching designs that bring natural gemstone jewellery into everyday fashion.

From delicate pieces for daily wear to bold statement jewellery and timeless gifts, Chamathka’s collections combine distinctive design with attractive and accessible price points, allowing a wider generation of jewellery lovers to experience the beauty of natural gemstones.

The opening at ThilakmaSquare, Negombo marks another important milestone in the brand’s continued expansion across Sri Lanka. The new Chamathka Jewellers outlet gives shoppers in Negombo and surrounding areas convenient access to the brand’s extensive jewellery collections in a vibrant new retail setting.

With its 23rd branch now open, Chamathka Jewellers continues its journey of making colourful, contemporary gemstone jewellery more accessible across the island — bringing together natural gemstones, modern design and attainable luxury for a new generation.
