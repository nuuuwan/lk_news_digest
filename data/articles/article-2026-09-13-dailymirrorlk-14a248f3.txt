India vs Sri Lanka: Asia Cup final clash set for Dubai

SEP.13 (Mirror Sports) - India and Sri Lanka will battle for the Women’s T20 Asia Cup title in Dubai on Sunday, with both sides heading into the final unbeaten.

Record seven-time champions India are aiming for an eighth title, while defending champions Sri Lanka are looking to retain the trophy they won in 2024 after defeating India in the final.

India reached the final after beating Bangladesh by 40 runs, while Sri Lanka edged Pakistan by four wickets in a low-scoring semifinal.

India’s batting has been led by Shafali Verma and Smriti Mandhana, who have scored 168 and 154 runs respectively. Deepti Sharma has also impressed with the ball, claiming 11 wickets.

For Sri Lanka, captain Chamari Athapaththu has been a key performer with both bat and ball, while Harshitha Samarawickrama and Sugandika Kumari have also made important contributions.

India have won nine of their last 10 T20Is against Sri Lanka, but the defending champions will take confidence from their eight-wicket victory over India in the 2024 Asia Cup final.
