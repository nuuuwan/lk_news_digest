Was ‘Lankeswara’ shot dead? Elephant killed, tusk reportedly removed in Kahatagasdigiliya

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daliy Mirror) - Another elephant has reportedly been shot dead in a forest reserve in the Kahatagasdigiliya area of Anuradhapura, raising fresh concerns over the killing of elephants in the country.

The incident is believed to have taken place around two days ago, with reports suggesting that one of the elephant’s tusks had been removed after it was killed.

Residents in the area suspect that the elephant could be the tusker known as ‘Lankeswara’, although its identity has not yet been officially confirmed.

The elephant is reportedly not among the 13 elephants previously identified by the Government for special protection.

The suspected killing has raised questions about the safety of Sri Lanka’s remaining tuskers and the effectiveness of measures introduced to protect elephants.

Authorities are yet to officially confirm the identity of the dead elephant or the circumstances surrounding its death.
