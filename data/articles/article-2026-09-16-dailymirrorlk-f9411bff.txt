Supermarkets in Bandaragama, Matugama fined for overpriced eggs, altered prices

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Two supermarket branches in Bandaragama and Matugama have been fined a total of Rs. 400,000 in two separate cases for selling eggs above the marked price, altering original prices on food products and offering expired beverages for sale.

Officers of the Kalutara District Office of the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) filed the two cases before the Panadura Magistrate’s Court against the supermarket branches.

During an inspection conducted on August 31 at a supermarket branch on Panadura Road, Bandaragama, officials found a pack of 10 eggs marked with a Maximum Retail Price (MRP) of Rs. 590 being sold for Rs. 615. Expired soft drinks were also found stocked for sale.

When the case was taken up before the Panadura Magistrate on September 3, the defendant company pleaded guilty to the charges and was fined Rs. 200,000.

Meanwhile, an inspection conducted at a supermarket branch in Matugama on August 21 found five 100g packs of sweet potato chips being offered for sale after their original marked prices had been removed and altered.

The case, filed for violating Direction No. 85 and Section 10(4) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, was taken up before the Panadura Magistrate on September 14. The defendant company pleaded guilty and was fined a further Rs. 200,000.

Accordingly, the two separate cases concluded on September 3 and September 14 resulted in combined fines of Rs. 400,000.

The CAA urged consumers to check the marked price of products and compare it with the price charged on the bill. Consumers were also advised to be vigilant about products with erased, altered or replaced prices and to check expiry dates when purchasing food and beverages.

Consumers can report market irregularities to the CAA through its 1977 hotline during office hours.
