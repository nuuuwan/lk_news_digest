‘Sili-sili’ bags cannot be free

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Traders cannot provide “sili-sili” polythene bags free of charge to consumers when selling goods, the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) said.

The CAA said the requirement is contained in Direction No. 95, published under Extraordinary Gazette No. 2456/41 on October 1, 2025, which came into effect on November 1, 2025.

The Direction applies to bags made of Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE) or Linear Low-Density Polyethylene (LLDPE) with handles, commonly known as “sili-sili” bags.

If a trader provides such a bag, a price must be charged, and the price must be prominently displayed at the business premises. The amount charged must also be separately stated on the consumer’s bill.

The CAA advised traders to maintain purchase invoices, bills and relevant supplier records relating to the bags.

Consumers have also been urged to check whether the bag price is displayed and whether the charge appears on their bills.

The CAA warned that legal action would be taken against traders who violate Direction No. 95 and encouraged consumers to use reusable shopping bags whenever possible.
