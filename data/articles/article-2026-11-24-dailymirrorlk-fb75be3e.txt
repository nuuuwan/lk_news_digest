Supermoon to light up Sri Lankan skies tonight

Skywatchers in Sri Lanka will have a spectacular celestial treat tonight as the Moon is set to appear bigger and brighter than usual in an astronomical spectacle known as a 'Supermoon', astronomer Gihan Weerasekara said.

He explained that a Supermoon occurs when the Moon reaches a point in its elliptical orbit where it comes particularly close to Earth, making it appear noticeably larger and brighter.

The Moon's average distance from Earth is around 384,000 kilometres. However, tonight it will come within approximately 360,000 kilometres of Earth, making it about 25,000 kilometres closer than its average distance.

As a result, the Supermoon will appear around 6.5% larger in diameter and approximately 14% brighter than an ordinary full Moon.

Weerasekara said the public would have an opportunity to witness the spectacular natural phenomenon, weather permitting.
