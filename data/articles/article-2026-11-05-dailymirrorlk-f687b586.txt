Sri Lankan High Commissioner presents credentials to Botswana President

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s High Commissioner to Botswana, Air Chief Marshal Udeni Rajapaksa (Retd), has presented his Letters of Credence to Botswana President Advocate Duma Gideon Boko at a ceremony held at the President’s Office in Gaborone on November 3.

Rajapaksa, who is based in Pretoria, South Africa, conveyed the greetings and best wishes of Sri Lankan President Anura Kumara Dissanayake to President Boko.

He reaffirmed Sri Lanka’s commitment to strengthening bilateral relations and cooperation with Botswana.

During the meeting, Rajapaksa highlighted opportunities to expand cooperation in trade, investment, education, tourism, capacity-building and people-to-people exchanges.

He also briefed the Botswana President on Sri Lanka’s recent economic developments, recovery efforts and sustainable development initiatives.

The discussions also focused on cooperation between the two countries at international forums, including the United Nations, the Commonwealth and the Group of 77.

President Boko welcomed the new High Commissioner and expressed Botswana’s willingness to strengthen relations with Sri Lanka.

Both sides also discussed the importance of South-South cooperation in addressing common challenges and promoting peace, prosperity and sustainable development.

Rajapaksa served in the Sri Lanka Air Force for 37 years and was its 19th Commander.
