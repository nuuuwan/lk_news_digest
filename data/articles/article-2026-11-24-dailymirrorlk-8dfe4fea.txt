Elephant caught on camera sneaking into kitchen for food

A video showing a young elephant poking its head into the kitchen of a house in the Wilpattu area in search of food is circulating on social media.

Wildlife officials said wild elephants have been frequently visiting houses within the Wilpattu Wildlife Zone, attracted by the ongoing mango harvest.

In one such incident, the elephant approached a house and stretched its trunk into the kitchen, apparently searching for food.

The occupant managed to capture the unusual encounter on camera, showing the young elephant cautiously reaching into the kitchen with its trunk.
