Technomedics shines among Best Workplaces in Asia 2026

Technomedics International (Pvt) Ltd has achieved a significant milestone in its workplace journey, earning recognition for the first time among the 100 Best Workplaces in Asia in the Small & Medium category, while also securing a place among the 50 Best Workplaces in Sri Lanka in the 2026 Great Place To Work Best Workplaces rankings.

At the regional level, Technomedics was ranked 64th among the Best Workplaces in Asia in the Small & Medium category. It is also the only Sri Lankan organisation from the Health Care sector to feature among the 200 organisations recognised across the Large and Small & Medium categories of the 2026 Best Workplaces in Asia list.

Across the Small & Medium category, ten Sri Lankan organisations were recognised on the 2026 Best Workplaces in Asia list, with Technomedics representing the Health Care sector. For the company, the recognition marks a new stage in its workplace journey, taking its recognition beyond Sri Lanka for the first time.

In Sri Lanka, Technomedics was ranked 4th among Medium Workplaces across all industries, while being the only organisation in the Health Care sector recognised within the category. The company’s first-ever regional recognition extends its established presence among Sri Lanka’s Best Workplaces to an Asia-wide platform.

Linus Jeganathan, Managing Director, Technomedics said, “This honour is particularly notable within the Healthcare sector. Six organisations from the sector were included in the 100 Best Workplaces in Asia Small & Medium list, with Technomedics placed alongside organisations from markets including Vietnam, Greater China/India, Singapore and Japan. Technomedics’ inclusion is therefore a significant achievement for a Sri Lankan healthcare organisation placing it among some of Asia’s key healthcare markets.”
