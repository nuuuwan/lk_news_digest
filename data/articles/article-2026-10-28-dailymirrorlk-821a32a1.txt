Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Carson Cumberbatch and Ceylon Cold Stores with the turnover crossing Rs. 849mn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Softlogic Life Insurance, CIC Holdings and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in Panasian Power, Haycarb and Prime Lands Residencies whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, LOLC Finance and Ceylon Land & Equity.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers. Materials sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to CIC Holdings and Haycarb) whilst the sector index gained 0.25%. The share price of CIC Holdings decreased by Rs. 0.10 (0.40%) to close at Rs. 24.90. The share price of Haycarb appreciated by Rs. 1.25 (0.63%) to close at Rs. 199.75.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index increased by 0.69%. The share price of John Keells Holdings moved up by Rs. 0.20 (1.13%) to close at Rs. 17.90.

Panasian Power and Softlogic Life Insurance were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Panasian Power gained Rs. 0.40 (4.00%) to close at Rs. 10.40. The share price of Softlogic Life Insurance recorded a gain of Rs. 1.00 (1.39%) to close at Rs. 72.80.
