Chamara Sampath, Deputy Minister clash in Parliament over Ella tree-felling allegations

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - A heated verbal clash erupted in Parliament today between Opposition MP Chamara Sampath Dassanayake and Deputy Minister Ruwan Ranasinghe over allegations surrounding the felling of trees near the Ella playground.

The exchange began when Deputy Minister Ranasinghe responded to an earlier claim by MP Dassanayake that trees bordering the Ella playground were cut down because they obstructed the view of a hotel owned by the Deputy Chairman of the Ella Pradeshiya Sabha.

Refuting the allegation, Deputy Minister Ranasinghe maintained that the trees were old and posed an imminent safety hazard during storms, before directing sharp criticism at the Opposition MP.

In response, MP Dassanayake insisted he had raised the issue respectfully without personally insulting the Deputy Minister, before leveling a fresh personal remark. "I will now reveal that Deputy Minister Ranasinghe, who was previously a resident of Ella, has relocated to Colombo and enrolled his children in a leading school," the MP stated.

Visibly agitated by the comment, Deputy Minister Ranasinghe demanded that the remarks be expunged from the Hansard. "MPs cannot make personal allegations in this manner, and I propose that all of his remarks be expunged from the Hansard," he said.
