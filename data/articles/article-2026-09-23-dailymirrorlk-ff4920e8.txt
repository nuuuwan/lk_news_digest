Aberdeen Holdings achieves carbon neutral certification

From left: Expo Commodities Global Assistant Manager Marketing and Corporate Communications Aaqil Munavver, Aberdeen Holdings Manager Corporate Secretarial and Compliance Najiha Carrim, Group CFO Anushka De Silva, Group Director Abdullah Kassim, NCPC Director General and CEO Samantha Kumarasena, RECP Expert Mihirisi Weerakkody, Certifications Manager and Senior RECP Expert Hiruni Kumarathunga and Co-Energi (Pvt.) Ltd Sustainability Consultant Divantha Ekanayake

Aberdeen Holdings (Pvt.) Ltd has achieved the carbon neutral certification from the National Cleaner Production Centre (NCPC), marking a defining milestone in the group’s sustainability journey and reinforcing its ambition to build businesses that create value responsibly. For a group that has spent more than three decades building and expanding businesses across industries and markets, the certification represents more than an environmental achievement. It reflects a deliberate step towards ensuring that future growth is supported by greater environmental accountability, stronger resource efficiency and responsible business practices.

The carbon-neutral certification process involved a comprehensive assessment of Aberdeen Holdings’ organisational greenhouse gas emissions and environmental practices, covering areas including energy and water consumption, transportation, waste management and other operational activities. This assessment enabled the group to take a closer, data-driven view of its environmental footprint and undertake the necessary measures towards achieving carbon neutrality.

“Receiving this certification is an important milestone for Aberdeen Holdings,” said Aberdeen Holdings Group Director Abdullah Kassim.
