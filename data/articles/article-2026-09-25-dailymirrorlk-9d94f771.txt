Yield curve edges higher amid modest trading activity

The secondary market yield curve edged slightly higher during the session yesterday, with trading activity remaining modest amid moderate market volumes.

Among the traded maturities, the 15.12.2029 was quoted in the 10.65% to 10.75% range. In the 2030 segment, the 01.08.2030 traded between 11.20% to 11.10%, while the 15.10.2030 changed hands within the 11.10% to 11.30% range.

The 01.02.2031 traded between 11.15% to11.30%, while the 15.12.2032maturity saw a notable increase in yields, trading within the 11.65% to 11.75% band. In the 2033 segment, the 01.06.2033 traded between 11.70% to 11.86%.Meanwhile, the 15.10.2034 was traded between 11.90% to 11.97%.

On the external front, the LKR appreciated against the USD, standing at LKR 329.02/USD, compared to LKR 329.89/USD seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to LKR 354.06bn compared to LKR 363.87bn recorded previously.
