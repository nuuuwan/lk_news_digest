Presidential Secretariat to obtain 16 vehicles on lease: MP reveals

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - MP Chamara Sampath Dassanayake revealed that the Presidential Secretariat is set to acquire 16 vehicles on lease.

He told Parliament that a gazette notification has already been issued regarding the leasing of the vehicles.

“Please look into this matter if you are not already aware of it,” the MP told members of the government benches.
