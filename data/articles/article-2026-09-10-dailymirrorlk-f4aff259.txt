Aircraft purchase commissions must be accepted, deal becomes void if rejected: Prof. Sonal

Katunayake, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The commission paid to the buyer is included in the price of an aircraft, and an aircraft purchase transaction could become null and void if the buyer refuses to accept the commission, former Airport and Aviation Services Company Limited (AASL) Board Director Professor Sonal Fernando said.

Speaking at a press conference held at the Katunayake Airport Garden Hotel yesterday (9), Prof. Fernando said the commission offered by the company selling an aircraft must therefore be accepted by the buyer.

He said aircraft could be purchased directly from the manufacturer, through its authorised representative or through a broker, adding that purchasing through a broker or representative company could be more profitable.

Describing the global aircraft market as being controlled by a “mafia”, Prof. Fernando said SriLankan Airlines' decision to purchase the latest Airbus A350 aircraft for its fleet was a foolish move.

He claimed that an Airbus deal that had been prepared for purchase during his tenure on the AASL Board was subsequently cancelled by the previous government.

Prof. Fernando also said there was no immediate need to modernise or expand the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) at Katunayake, claiming that its existing facilities would be sufficient for another 25 years.
