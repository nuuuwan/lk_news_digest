El Niño may bring above-average rainfall, flood risk in October-November

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka could experience above-average rainfall during October and November due to prevailing El Niño conditions, raising the possibility of flooding in some areas, the Department of Meteorology has warned.

Director General of the Department of Meteorology Ajith Wijemanna said heavy rainfall exceeding seasonal averages could be expected during the period.

He said the forecast was based on an analysis of weather data covering the next six months.

Speaking to the media on the prevailing weather conditions, Wijemanna said around 30% of Sri Lanka’s annual rainfall is normally received during October and November.

He warned that if rainfall exceeds normal levels, reservoirs could fill rapidly, increasing the risk of flooding in certain areas.

“Rainfall spreads across the entire country during October and November, and heavy rainfall is possible. This does not mean that flooding will definitely occur, but we need to be prepared for the possibility,” he said.

Meanwhile, Wijemanna said the effects of the El Niño phenomenon are expected to intensify during December, January and February.
