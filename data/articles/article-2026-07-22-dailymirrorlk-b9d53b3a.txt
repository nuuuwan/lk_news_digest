Divolca Nova range earns three SLSI quality certificates

Divolca Electric (Pvt) Ltd received SLSI 1000, SLSI 734 and SLSI 734-2 certificates for the Divolca Nova series of switches and switch sockets recently. The certificates were presented by SLSI Director General Dr. Siddhika Senaratne to Divolca Electric Sales and Marketing Director Senal Samarawickrama. Quality Control Officer (Divolca) Wasana Rathnayakee Manager (Divolca) Sajith Madhusankha Head of Sales (Divolca) Chandana Gunasena and SLSI Senior Director A.P. Kandage were also present at the occasion.
