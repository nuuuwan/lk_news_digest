BOI fast-tracks Northern Industrial zones

Colombo, Oct. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The Board of Investment (BOI) of Sri Lanka plans to open land reservations for investors at the proposed Paranthan Export Processing Zone by the end of November 2026, while infrastructure development at both Paranthan and Kankesanthurai (KKS) is scheduled to begin in early January 2027.

The plans were discussed during a visit to the Northern Province by BOI Chairman Duminda Hulangamuwa and Director General Dr Sulakshana Jayawardena to assess investment opportunities and accelerate the development of the two industrial zones.

The visit brought together government officials, regional administrators and investors to discuss infrastructure development, investment facilitation and opportunities to expand export-oriented industries in the region.

At the proposed Paranthan Export Processing Zone, the BOI delegation met Kilinochchi District Secretary, Paranthan Divisional Secretary, the Coordinating Secretary to Minister Bimal Rathnayaka and officials from the Road Development Authority, Provincial Road Development Authority, Railway Department and Electricity Distribution Ltd.

Following the discussions, the BOI committed to opening land reservations for investors by the end of November 2026 and commencing internal infrastructure development at the site by early January 2027.

A similar timeline was agreed for the Kankesanthurai industrial zone at a meeting held at the Tellippalai Divisional Secretariat. The meeting was attended by the Jaffna District Secretary, Tellippalai Divisional Secretary, Chairman of the Cement Corporation, representatives of Minister Bimal Rathnayaka, relevant infrastructure officials and the Officer-in-Charge of the Sri Lanka Police in KKS.

The BOI will also organise an investment promotion and familiarisation visit in November 2026 for leading investors and investor associations to showcase opportunities in the Northern Province, including the proposed industrial zones.

During the visit, the BOI delegation met existing and prospective investors to identify obstacles affecting investment and business operations.

Investors highlighted the need for clearer and more consistent procedures for land valuation and allocation, faster approvals and clearances from relevant government agencies, and improved coordination among state institutions.

In response, Hulangamuwa proposed establishing a single-point coordination mechanism to follow investor-related issues through to resolution. The mechanism is expected to work with relevant government institutions and, where necessary, political and administrative leadership to expedite pending approvals and address implementation bottlenecks.

The BOI Chairman and Director General also met Northern Province Governor Nagalingam Vethanayahan and briefed him on the proposed industrial zones, their implementation schedules and target completion timelines.

During the meeting, Hulangamuwa sought the Governor’s support to establish a Single-Point Investor Facilitation Centre in the province to provide investors with a central point of contact for approvals and services from regional and national government institutions.

The Chairman also proposed developing a regional manpower information database to document the availability, qualifications and skills of the Northern Province’s workforce. The initiative is intended to help investors identify suitable employees and highlight the region’s human resources alongside its industrial infrastructure.

The delegation also visited sea cucumber and seaweed cultivation projects in Ariyalai, Jaffna, where it met industry stakeholders to discuss the challenges facing the sector and opportunities for expansion.

The BOI said the discussions highlighted the potential to develop these activities into export-oriented businesses capable of generating employment and income across the Northern Province.

The proposed industrial zones, investor facilitation initiatives and efforts to develop export-oriented industries form part of the BOI’s broader efforts to attract investment to the region.

The BOI said it remained committed to working with investors and local stakeholders to translate the province’s investment potential into employment opportunities, higher incomes and sustainable economic development.
