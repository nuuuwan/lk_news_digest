Namal well aware of Parliamentary Standing Orders: Madugoda

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) National Organiser and MP Namal Rajapaksa deliberately avoided referring to his recent arrest during his speech in Parliament as discussing a matter pending before court would be sub judice, SLPP MP Chanaka Madugoda said.

Speaking in Parliament, Madugoda said Rajapaksa was well aware of the Standing Orders of Parliament, unlike certain government members who, had a history of attempting to bomb Parliament and set fire to the premises.

The MP was responding to Deputy Minister of Justice Sunil Watagala, who had said he expected Rajapaksa to speak about his arrest and the conduct of the judiciary.

Madugoda said Rajapaksa had acted in accordance with parliamentary procedure by refraining from commenting on the matter while it was before the courts.
