Sri Lanka to test Nigerian crude

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) is making fresh attempts to explore the possibility of importing Nigerian crude oil amidst surging fuel prices in the world market, according to an official.

The CPC, earlier, brought a sample of crude oil from Nigeria only to find it contaminated with water.

After that, CPC Chairman D.J. Rajakaruna told Daily Mirror yesterday that his office sought a fresh sample for a specification test.

He said that increasing fuel prices remain challenging for Sri Lanka.

"We keep exploring alternate markets for fuel purchases. We have to test whether Nigerian crude can be refined locally," he said.

Sri Lanka has an oil refinery in Sapugaskanda. It accounts for 25- 30 percent of supplies to the local market.
