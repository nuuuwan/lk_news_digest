Aitken Spence Hotels cleared to raise up to Rs. 5bn via debentures

Aitken Spence Hotel Holdings PLC has received in-principle approval from the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) for a listed debenture issue to raise up to Rs. 5 billion, with subscriptions set to open on September 15.

The hospitality group will initially offer 30 million listed, rated, unsecured, senior and redeemable debentures at Rs. 100 each to raise Rs. 3 billion. It has the option to issue a further 20 million debentures in the event of an oversubscription, increasing the maximum issue size to Rs. 5 billion.

The issue comprises five-year and seven-year debentures with four fixed-rate options.

The five-year Type A debentures offer an interest rate of 13 percent payable annually, while Type B carries a 12.60 percent rate payable semi-annually, equivalent to an annual effective rate of 13 percent. The seven-year Type C debentures carry a 13.15 percent rate payable annually, while Type D offers 12.74 percent payable semi-annually, translating to an annual effective rate of 13.15 percent.

Capital Alliance Partners Limited is the manager to the issue, while Central Depository Systems (Private) Limited has been appointed registrar.
