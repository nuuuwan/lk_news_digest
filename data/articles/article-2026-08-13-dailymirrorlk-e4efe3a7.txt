Sri Lanka names squad for Women’s Asia Cup 2026

Colombo, August 13, (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Cricket has announced a 15-member squad for the Women’s T20 Asia Cup 2026, which will be held in the United Arab Emirates from August 28 to September 13.

Experienced all-rounder Chamari Athapaththu will lead the Sri Lankan side, with Harshitha Samarawickrama named vice-captain.

Chamari Athapaththu (Captain), Imesha Dulani, Sanjana Kavindi, Vishmi Gunarathne, Harshitha Samarawickrama (Vice Captain), Kaveesha Dilhari, Nilakshika Silva, Hasini Perera, Kaushini Nuthyangana (Wicketkeeper), Dewmi Vihanga, Sugandika Dassanayaka, Chamudi Praboda, Chethana Vimukthi, Kawya Kavindi and Mithali Ayodya.
