Railway unions warn of Sept. 17 strike over unresolved issues

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Railway trade unions have warned that their planned strike on September 17 will go ahead unless the Government provides satisfactory solutions to the administrative and passenger-related issues confronting the Railway Department.

Sri Lanka Railway Station Masters' Union (SLRSMU) President Sumeda Somaratne said the Government should take immediate steps to appoint a new General Manager of Railways to address the administrative problems within the department.

He said the failure to appoint a new General Manager and resolve the outstanding issues could result in the trade unions proceeding with the strike as planned.

Somaratne said railway passenger associations had also, for the first time, extended their support to the proposed trade union action, citing the failure to properly address long-standing passenger concerns, particularly train delays.

Meanwhile, railway trade unions are scheduled to meet Deputy Minister of Transport tomorrow (14) to discuss the issues affecting the Railway Department.

The unions said they would decide on the next course of action following the discussion, but warned that the September 17 strike would proceed if satisfactory solutions were not provided.

The proposed trade union action could affect railway services across the country, with unions urging the Government to address the issues before the strike date.
