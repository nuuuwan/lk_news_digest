New Rothschild Tea Factory achieves all-time record price of Rs.2,950 per kg

New Rothschild Tea Factory, situated in the Pussellawa planting region and managed by Browns Plantations, achieved an all-time record price of Rs.2,950 per kilogramme for the PEK1 grade in the Western Medium elevation, at the weekly tea auction held on September 8 and 9, 2026.

The tea was auctioned by Ceylon Tea Brokers PLC and purchased by M/S. Vintage Teas Ceylon (Pvt.) Ltd.

New Rothschild Tea Factory is eminent for manufacturing high-quality, award-winning teas and has the distinction of achieving top prices more often at the weekly tea auctions.
