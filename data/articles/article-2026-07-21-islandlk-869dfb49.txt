First Capital Asset Management successfully concludes Professional Fund Programme for University of Kelaniya

First Capital Asset Management Limited (FCAM), the wealth management arm of First Capital Holdings PLC, a subsidiary of JXG (Janashakthi Group), recently concluded its Professional Fund Management short course conducted for students of the Department of Finance, Faculty of Commerce and Management Studies at the University of Kelaniya.

The programme was designed to provide participants with practical insights into fund management and investment principles, bridging academic learning with industry expertise. The initiative concluded with a certification ceremony, recognising the successful completion of the course by the participants.
