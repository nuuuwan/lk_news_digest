Dickwella returns to T20I Squad after five years

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Niroshan Dickwella earned a call-up to Sri Lanka’s T20I squad for the first time in half a decade as the country’s cricket board announced the squad for the Asian Games men’s cricket competition on Tuesday.

Ashen Bandara also returned to the squad after three years, with the two bringing their experience to the Aichi-Nagoya Games this time around.

Sohan de Livera, Sineth Jayawardena, Wanuja Sahan and Garuka Sanketh received their maiden T20I call-ups, with Sahan Arachchige named captain of the side.

Sri Lanka earned a direct qualification to the Asian Games quarterfinals, and will open its campaign in Nissin on September 29.

Sahan Arachchige (c), Sohan de Livera, Wanuja Sahan, Niroshan Dickwella (wk), Ashen Bandara, Dushan Hemantha, Lasith Croospulle, Chamika Karunaratne, Nuwan Thushara, Sineth Jayawardena, Tharindu Ratnayake, Binura Fernando, Nuwanidu Fernando, Vijayakanth Viyaskanth, Garuka Sanketh
