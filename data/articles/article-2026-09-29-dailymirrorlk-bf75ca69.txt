Exhibition highlights surgeon’s good work

The recently held exhibition by reputed surgeon Dr. Gamini Goonetilleke was  like no other. Titled  “Beyond the Scalpel: A Unique Visual Chronicle of Surgery, Humanity and Four Decades of Service, “ it was a journey through War & Peace depicting man’s inhumanity to man. Land mine disasters was especially sad to see.

Care Concern Compassion toward his patients be it in Colombo,  Jaffna,  or rural Polonnaruwa was an eye opener to all. A visual chronicle of four decades of surgery humanitarian service and national duty was a wonder to behold.

The good doctor’s contribution to uphold the highest ethics of  medicine must never be overlooked. His contribution to uplift man through surgery need to be recognised by the Government of Sri Lanka.

I suggest he be awarded any of the following awards. Sri Lankabhimanya Deshamanya, Deshabandu, Sri Lanka Shikamani, Sri Lanka Thilaka, Vidya Jothy or Vidya Nidhi.

The Nation must show gratitude & say Thank You to Dr. Gamini Goonetilleke, one of its distinguished citizens.
