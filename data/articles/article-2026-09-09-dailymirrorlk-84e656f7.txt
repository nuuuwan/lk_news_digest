Rajnath Singh meets Sajith Premadasa

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Visiting Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh met Sri Lanka’s Leader of the Opposition Sajith Premadasa in Colombo during his three-day official visit to the island.

The meeting provided an opportunity for the two leaders to exchange views on matters of mutual interest and the broader relationship between India and Sri Lanka. Singh and Premadasa also exchanged gifts as a gesture of goodwill, reflecting the longstanding ties between the two neighbouring countries.

Singh’s visit, from September 8 to 10, is aimed at further strengthening bilateral defence and strategic cooperation between India and Sri Lanka. His programme includes high-level engagements with Sri Lankan political and defence leaders.

Earlier, Singh held talks with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, with both sides reaffirming their commitment to strengthening cooperation between the two countries. Singh described his meeting with President Dissanayake as “warm and extremely productive.”

Singh said India and Sri Lanka, as “civilisational twins, close neighbours and maritime partners,” would continue to work together for the development and welfare of their peoples, as well as for regional safety, security, peace and prosperity.
