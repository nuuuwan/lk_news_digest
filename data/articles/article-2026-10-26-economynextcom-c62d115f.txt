Sri Lanka’s Nations Trust Bank to raise Rs13.8bn in rights issue

ECONOMYNEXT — Sri Lanka’s Nations Trust Bank PLC (NTB) plans to raise 13.87 billion rupees through a rights issue to strengthen its Tier 1 capital base and support balance sheet expansion.

The bank will issue 45,467,286 Ordinary Non-Voting Convertible Shares at 305 rupees each. Existing holders of both voting and non-voting shares will be offered 3 new shares for every 22 shares held as of November 18.

“The Rights Issue is intended to strengthen the Company’s Common Equity Tier 1 capital base and support the continued growth of its Balance Sheet and risk-weighted assets while maintaining prudent regulatory capital buffers,” the lender said in a market filing.

Following the transaction, the bank’s Tier 1 Capital Ratio is projected to increase from 12.63 percent to 14.98 percent, while the total capital ratio will rise from 16.28 percent to 18.63 percent.

Stated capital will increase from 13.99 billion rupees to 27.86 billion rupees if fully subscribed.

“The enhanced capital base will further improve the Company’s ability to withstand potential economic and business risks and support sustainable long-term growth.”

The proceeds will help expand the bank’s loan book, focused mainly on term and working capital facilities, following recent growth including the acquisition of HSBC’s retail banking portfolio.

NTB disbursed an average of 45 billion rupees per month in new loan facilities in the second quarter of 2026 and expects to deploy the proceeds within three months.

Shareholders will vote on the proposal at an Extraordinary General Meeting on November 16, 2026. The shares will trade ex-rights on November 17, and the issue is scheduled to close on December 14, 2026. (Colombo/Oct26/2026)
