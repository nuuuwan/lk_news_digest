Fair weather elsewhere, showers after 1 p.m.

Colombo, October 29 (Daily Mirror) - Mainly fair weather conditions are expected in other parts of the island, but showers would occur at a few places in the Uva Province and in the Batticaloa, Ampara and Hambantota districts after 1.00 p.m.

Several spells of showers are also expected in the Western and Sabaragamuwa provinces and in the Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Galle and Matara districts.

The public has been advised to take adequate precautions to minimise possible damage caused by temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
