Sri Lanka wins bronze medal in Men’s T20 Cricket at Asian Games

Sri Lanka registered a massive 63-run victory over Bangladesh in the Men’s T20 Cricket third-place playoff at the Asian Games to secure the bronze medal.

Sri Lanka was in a difficult position after losing four wickets for 29 runs in the powerplay. However, they made an excellent comeback to post a respectable total of 165/9. Tharindu Ratnayake played an explosive innings of 65 runs off just 32 balls, hitting seven fours and five sixes.

Sahan Arachchige supported him with 33 runs, while Ashen Bandara scored 18. Ratnayake and Arachchige put together a 50-run partnership off 25 balls, with Ratnayake completing his half-century in just 26 balls.

Bangladesh also got off to a poor start in their chase of 166. After reaching 50 runs in 8.1 overs, Bangladesh had already lost six wickets for 66 runs by the end of the 9.4th over. Nasum Ahmed top-scored for Bangladesh with 26 runs, while Yasir Ali Chowdhury scored 21 and Parvez Hossain Emon made 20.

Sri Lanka clinched the bronze medal by bowling Bangladesh out for 102 runs in 17.3 overs.

India and Pakistan are set to face each other today in the gold-medal match.
