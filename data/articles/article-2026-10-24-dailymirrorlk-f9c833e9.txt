Suspected narcotics found in sacks and travel bags on Naotunna beach

Colombo, October 24 (Daily Mirror)- At least 10 sacks and 11 travel bags containing suspected narcotics were seized on Naotunna beach in Thalalla, Matara, this morning, the Sri Lanka Navy said.

The recovery was made during a joint search operation conducted by the Sri Lanka Navy and the Coast Guard.

Further details regarding the seized consignment have not yet been released.
