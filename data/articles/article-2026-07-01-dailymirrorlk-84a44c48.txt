Australia one win away from history! INFOGRAPHIC

July.01 (Mirror Sports) - Australia have booked their place in the ICC Women's T20 World Cup final after extending their unbeaten run to six matches, moving within one victory of a record seventh title.

The six-time champions have been the tournament's standout side, cruising through the group stage before sealing an emphatic eight-wicket victory over the West Indies in the semi-finals. Time and again, different players have stepped up to deliver match-winning performances, underlining Australia's exceptional depth and consistency.

Now set to contest their eighth Women's T20 World Cup final, Australia will look to add another chapter to their remarkable legacy when they take the field on Sunday. With a flawless campaign so far, the reigning powerhouse remains the team to beat as it closes in on another historic triumph.
