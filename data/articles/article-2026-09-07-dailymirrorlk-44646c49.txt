Southern Expressway service area land leased for 99 years

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The property used for a service area on the Southern Expressway was leased for 99 years at an annual rent of just Rs. 10,000 during the previous Rajapaksa administration, Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development Bimal Rathnayake revealed.

Rathnayake made the disclosure while responding to concerns raised over the poor maintenance, cleanliness and high food prices at the service area.

He said the government was looking into the management of the facility and had raised concerns with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Labour and the Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation.

According to the Minister, the existing service area at Welipenna is operated by a company affiliated with the Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation and is not directly managed by the Road Development Authority (RDA) or the Ministry of Transport.

He said only one service area is currently operating across the 312-kilometre expressway network, while another facility is being developed in the Udukawa area.

Rathnayake suggested that irregularities may have occurred in the original leasing transaction, given that the property had reportedly been leased for 99 years at an annual payment of Rs. 10,000.

He said the government intends to establish additional service areas along the Central Expressway and other expressways to provide improved facilities for motorists and passengers.
