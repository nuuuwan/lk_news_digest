Euro Motors opens new Anuradhapura showroom and service complex

Euro Motors has officially opened its new showroom and automotive facility in Ghanikkulama, Nelumkulama, Anuradhapura, marking a significant expansion of its regional presence and strengthening access to passenger and commercial vehicle solutions in the North Central province.

Positioned as the largest fully-fledged passenger and commercial vehicle facility in the region, the new complex brings together an extensive portfolio of automotive brands under one roof.

Its passenger vehicle portfolio features Jetour, Dongfeng, JAC and Joylong, while its commercial vehicle range includes JAC trucks, Dongfeng trucks, Bonluck buses and Ankai buses.

Complementing this diverse brand offering, the facility also provides dedicated vehicle display areas, after-sales service and genuine spare parts, creating a comprehensive automotive destination for individual motorists, businesses, fleet owners and transport operators.

Euro Motors Chief Operating Officer Ravindra Senarathna said, “A showroom should be more than a place where the customers come to choose a vehicle; it should be a destination they can rely on throughout their ownership journey. That is the thinking behind our investment in Anuradhapura. By investing in dedicated infrastructure and bringing vehicle display, servicing and genuine spare parts together, we are strengthening our ability to serve individual motorists and businesses in the region. Our commitment to the customers extends well beyond the point of purchase and this facility reflects the importance we place on providing continued support throughout vehicle ownership.”

The establishment of the new facility represents a substantial long-term investment by Euro Motors in its regional network. The complex features purpose-built infrastructure and dedicated service facilities designed to support its growing customer base, strengthen after-sales capabilities and enhance the availability of its vehicle brands and services beyond major urban centres.

Bringing together passenger and commercial vehicle offerings across the Euro Motors portfolio, the facility caters to diverse customer requirements, from individual transportation to business and commercial fleet operations. The customers can explore vehicle options, access servicing facilities and source genuine spare parts at a single location, offering greater convenience and continuity throughout their ownership experience.
