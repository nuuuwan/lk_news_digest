Suspended Colombo Fort Magistrate Kosala Senadheera reinstated

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The Judicial Service Commission (JSC) has taken steps to reinstate Kosala Senadheera, who was suspended while serving as the Colombo Fort Magistrate, with immediate effect.

Following a formal inquiry, the JSC has cleared Senadheera of all charges and attached him to the Colombo Magistrate’s Court. He will also receive his back pay for the period of his suspension.

Senadheera had been suspended with effect from July 10, 2024, following allegations that electricity and water had been illegally obtained for his official residence.

The inquiry conducted by the JSC, which is headed by the Chief Justice, lasted 27 months, with evidence recorded from more than 20 witnesses.

Senadheera is also the most senior magistrate in the special grade of the judicial service.

The reinstatement takes effect immediately, with his service restored along with the salary and other entitlements due for the suspension period.
