Liquor prices go up over bottle production costs

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Liquor prices have been increased from November 1 following a rise in the cost of manufacturing empty bottles, liquor companies said.

The cost of a 750ml empty bottle has increased to Rs. 160, while 375ml and 180ml bottles now cost Rs. 140 and Rs. 109 respectively.

Accordingly, refundable bottle deposits have also been increased. The deposit for a 750ml bottle has gone up from Rs. 60 to Rs. 100, a half bottle from Rs. 40 to Rs. 70 and a 180ml bottle from Rs. 20 to Rs. 30.

Companies that do not accept returned bottles have also passed the additional packaging costs on to consumers.

A senior Excise Department official said companies had informed the Department of the increase in bottle deposits, citing higher production costs.
