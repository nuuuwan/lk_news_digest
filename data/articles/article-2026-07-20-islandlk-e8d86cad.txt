AA3 Holdings unveils Lanka’s largest ex-stock heavy equipment showroom in Kiribathgoda

AA3 Holdings has opened Sri Lanka’s largest ex-stock heavy equipment showroom and fully equipped workshop in Kiribathgoda, responding to rising industry demand. The state-of-the-art facility houses the country’s biggest inventory of ready-to-deliver machinery, enabling faster customer access and strengthening the company’s market position.

Chairman Supun Perera called the expansion a strategic milestone to enhance service capabilities and deliver an unparalleled ownership experience. AA3 Holdings, a subsidiary of the Udeshi Group with roots dating to 1986, is a leading provider of construction and mining equipment in Sri Lanka and the Maldives.

The new showroom lets clients explore top-tier earthmoving, excavation, and road construction machinery firsthand. Its advanced workshop features cutting-edge diagnostic tools and specialized repair equipment for rapid, precision servicing. After-sales support includes factory-trained technicians focused on maximizing uptime and operational efficiency.

CASE Vice President Emre Karazli noted Sri Lanka’s growing infrastructure and road network opportunities, emphasizing the brand’s commitment to environmentally responsible, high-performance equipment. He also mentioned CASE’s ongoing factory development in India.

Shalahh Chaathurvedi, CASE Managing Director, announced that Colombo will host the company’s annual dealer convention over two days, with over 150 international delegates – positioning it as one of Sri Lanka’s largest MICE events this year.
