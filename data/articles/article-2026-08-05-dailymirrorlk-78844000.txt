July tea sale average climbs to Rs.1,176 per kg

The national tea sale average rose in July, supported by firmer Low Grown prices, although the industry’s performance in US dollar terms continued to trail last year, amid the currency movements.

The national tea sale average increased to Rs.1,176.10 (US $ 3.50) per kilogramme in July, up Rs.22.61 and US $ 0.05 from June’s average of Rs.1,153.49 (US $ 3.45).

Compared with July 2025, the average was Rs.48.50 higher in local currency but US $ 0.24 lower in US dollar terms. The year-to-date national sale average stood at Rs.1,164.50 (US $ 3.63), up Rs.11.43 from the corresponding period last year, although it remained US $ 0.24 lower in dollar terms.

The Low Grown teas continued to underpin the market, with the average price rising Rs.33.65 month-on-month and Rs.85.98 year-on-year to Rs.1,293.06 per kilogramme. The High Grown teas also strengthened, gaining Rs.12.70 from June and Rs.26.58 from a year earlier to average at Rs.1,061.95.

In contrast, the Medium Grown teas remained under pressure, with the average easing Rs.2.62 from June and Rs.27.96 from July last year to Rs.953.94 per kilogramme.

The Sri Lanka Tea Board said the cumulative averages for the High Grown and Low Grown teas recorded positive variances in rupee terms, while all elevations posted negative variances in the US dollar terms compared with the corresponding period of 2025.

The Medium Growns remained the weakest-performing category, recording declines in both rupee and dollar terms.
