Sri Lanka bourse targets 150,000 new investors with branch rollout

ECONOMYNEXT — The Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) is aiming to expand its active retail investor base to between 100,000 and 150,000 by setting up branches across all districts and launching an agent network, the bourse’s chairman Dimuthu Abeyesekera said.

Speaking in Colombo at a forum titled ‘From Resilience to Sustainable Growth’, organized jointly by the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka (SEC), the chairman said the number of active market participants currently stands around 60,000, down from a peak of 78,000 reached last year.

He noted that during the 2023–2024 period, active investors stood at just 22,000 to 23,000, representing a negligible fraction of Sri Lanka’s investable population.

“Right now, it has come down to 60,000 because of the market situation. But what I’m saying is… only point 0.2% of the public who can invest are in the stock market; that is not enough,” Abeyesekera said.

To drive domestic participation, the exchange opened three regional branches this year, with plans to establish more branches over the next two years until every district has a physical presence.

“Our target is to have a branch in every district. That should be our target. Then only we can definitely go and show this Colombo Stock Exchange,” he said.

The chairman pointed out that each newly established branch already houses operations for seven to eight stockbroking firms, reflecting strong industry interest in regional expansion.

The CSE is also awaiting regulatory approval from the SEC to introduce an agent scheme, which will allow licensed agents to canvas retail investors outside Colombo.

He explained that while digital account opening is fully operational, physical branches remain critical to building public visibility and trust, drawing parallels to retail commercial banking networks. (Colombo/Oct28/2026)
