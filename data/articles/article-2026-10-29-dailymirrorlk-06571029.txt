Homagama Police HQI suspended over death of suspect in custody

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Headquarters Inspector of the Homagama Police Station, M.R.S. Prajan Senanayake, has been suspended with immediate effect by the Inspector General of Police (IGP) over alleged dereliction of duty and failure to properly supervise a suspect who died in police custody.

The incident followed the arrest of P.D. Ishara Harsha Kumara on October 25 by a team of Homagama Police officers for allegedly possessing 490 milligrams of ICE (crystal methamphetamine). The suspect subsequently died while in police custody.

Investigations reportedly revealed several lapses in the Headquarters Inspector’s duties, including failure to properly monitor the suspect while he was detained in a cell, issue appropriate instructions to investigating officers, check on the suspect’s condition and verify the accuracy of arrest records.

The suspension was also linked to allegations that two individuals had been illegally detained at the Homagama Police Station.

Accordingly, Senanayake’s suspension took effect on October 28 due to the alleged negligence and shortcomings in his supervisory responsibilities.

Meanwhile, three other police officers, including the Officer-in-Charge of the Crimes Division at the Homagama Police Station, had already been suspended with effect from October 26 in connection with the incident.

The suspended Crimes Division OIC was arrested on the evening of October 28 and was scheduled to be produced before court on October 29.
