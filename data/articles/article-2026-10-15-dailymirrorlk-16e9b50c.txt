Airline representatives discuss rising costs, airport upgrades

Colombo, Oct. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Board of Airline Representatives (BAR) Sri Lanka has discussed measures to address rising operational costs, improve ground handling procedures and upgrade airport infrastructure at its fourth Executive Board meeting held in Colombo today (15).

The meeting, held at the SriLankan Airlines Boardroom, was chaired by BAR Chairman Dimuthu Tennakoon, with Vice Chairman Sudesh Rupasinghe and country heads of major airlines operating in Sri Lanka also participating.

The discussions focused on key operational challenges and developments affecting international airlines operating in the country.

Participants explored ways to streamline ground handling procedures, manage increasing operational expenses and improve infrastructure at Sri Lanka’s key aviation gateways.

The executive committee also considered strategies to improve operational efficiency and advocate for aviation policies that support the industry.

BAR Sri Lanka reaffirmed its commitment to promoting sustainable growth in the aviation sector, enhancing passenger experiences and supporting the country’s tourism and trade industries.
