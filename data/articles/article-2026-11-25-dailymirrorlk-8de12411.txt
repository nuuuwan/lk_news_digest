Bank of Ceylon debentures to list on CSE today

Bank of Ceylon’s five and seven-year Basel III-compliant debentures will list on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) today. The five-year debentures carry a fixed annual coupon of 12.75 percent and mature on November 6, 2031. The seven-year debentures pay 13 percent a year and mature on November 6, 2033. The interest on both series is payable annually. The longer-dated securities offer a coupon 25 basis points higher than the five-year series.

The annual effective rates are of 12.75 percent and 13 percent, respectively. Both series include a non-viability write-down provision, under which the investors could lose the principal, if the bank is deemed non-viable.
