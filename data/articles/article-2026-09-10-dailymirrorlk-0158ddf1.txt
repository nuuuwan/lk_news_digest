Rs. 400,000 fine for selling non-SLS extension cords

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - A company operating a shopping centre in Bandarawela was fined Rs. 400,000 after pleading guilty to four charges relating to the sale of electrical extension cords without the mandatory SLS certification, the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) said.

The Badulla District Investigation Officers of the CAA had filed the case before the Bandarawela Magistrate’s Court against the shopping center located on Badulla Road, Bandarawela.

The charges included selling extension cords without the mandatory SLS certification mark of the Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI), failing to provide the minimum warranty period and selling the products without valid invoices from suppliers.

During an inspection conducted on August 28, 2026, officers found extension cords worth Rs. 2,790 being offered for sale without the required SLS mark, in violation of Directive No. 63 published under Extraordinary Gazette No. 2048/39 dated December 7, 2017.

Following the company’s guilty plea, the court imposed a total fine of Rs. 400,000.

The CAA said electrical products operating at voltages not exceeding 250 volts, including plugs, socket outlets, extension cords, adapters, fused connection units and conversion plugs, are among the products subject to mandatory SLS certification. Cable reels are also required to comply with SLS 1532.

Consumers have been advised to check for the SLS certification mark and warranty information when purchasing electrical equipment and to obtain a valid bill to help prevent electrical accidents and fires.

Complaints regarding such irregularities can be made to the CAA hotline 1977 during office hours.
