Israel’s Arab parties signal readiness to join opposition to oust Netanyahu: Report

Times of India - Israel’s Arab parties have signalled their willingness to cooperate with opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot’s bloc to form an alternative government and end Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s rule.

The Arab parties are projected to win 19 seats, while the opposition bloc has 48, leaving them with a combined 67 seats, above the 61 needed for a majority.

The Joint List said, “Without us, it's impossible. But together with us, it is certainly possible,” as Yair Golan urged bold leadership to push for a government of change.
