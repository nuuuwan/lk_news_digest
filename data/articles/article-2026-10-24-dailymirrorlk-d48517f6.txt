Colombo’s biggest Halloween experience ‘Creepfest 26’ set to return

‘Creepfest 26’ is set to return to Colombo this Halloween, bringing a new edition of the youth-led celebration that has brought together music enthusiasts and Halloween fans.

Building on its previous edition, this year’s event will feature an expanded programme of entertainment and themed activities, with a focus on engaging young audiences while supporting a charitable cause.

Jointly organised by the Rotaract Clubs of American National College, Cinnamon Gardens and Royal Institute of Colombo, in collaboration with the Interact Clubs of Colombo North and Mahanama College, Creepfest 26’ reflects the collective efforts of young people seeking to create engaging experiences while contributing positively to society.

As part of this commitment, the organisers have placed fundraising for Apeksha Cancer Hospital, Maharagama, at the centre of this year’s event. Funds raised through Creepfest 26’ will be directed towards the hospital following its conclusion, demonstrating how youth-led entertainment initiatives can also serve a wider social purpose.

The event will feature performances by four DJs, Vehan, Vortex, Uveen and Shenal, forming a key part of the evening’s entertainment programme.

Creepfest 26’ will take place on Friday, 30 October 2026, from 6.00 p.m. onwards at Arcade Independence Square, Colombo.
