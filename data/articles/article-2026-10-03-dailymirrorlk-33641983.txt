People’s Bank partners with Waters Edge for Oktoberfest

People’s Bank has partnered with Waters Edge as the Official Banking Partner for the annual Oktoberfest at the Edge 2026, scheduled to be held on 16th, 17th  and 18th October at Waters Edge.

The partnership agreement between People’s Bank and Waters Edge was formalized recently in the presence of I. K. Indika, Deputy General Manager – Payments & Cards, People’s Bank, and  Iyantha Abeysinghe, General Manager – Waters Edge, together with senior officials from both organizations.

The partnership marks another step in strengthening the relationship between People’s Bank and Waters Edge, while providing an opportunity for People’s Credit Cardholders to enjoy exclusivebenefits during the event.As part of the partnership, People’s Credit Cardholders can enjoy a 25 percent discount on ticket prices.

Oktoberfest at the Edge 2026 is expected to attract approximately 3,000 diners from Colombo and its suburbs, creating a valuable opportunity for People’s Bank to engage with them and promote its Credit Cards.
