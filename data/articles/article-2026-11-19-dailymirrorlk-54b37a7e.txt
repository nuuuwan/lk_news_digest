Solar-powered street lights planned for elephant-prone roads in Tantirimale

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Solar-powered street lights are to be installed along roads in elephant-prone areas of Tantirimale as part of measures to reduce the threat posed by wild elephants to residents.

The necessary funding for the project will be provided through the Wildlife Fund, Deputy Environment Minister Anton Jayakody said.

The proposal was discussed at a special meeting held in Tantirimale on November 14 to find sustainable solutions to the human-elephant conflict affecting the area.

The meeting also discussed establishing a new Elephant Control Unit in Tantirimale and setting up security posts near Tantirimale Vidyalaya, Tantirimale Hospital and Ehetugala to improve protection against elephant intrusions.

Authorities are also considering measures to drive elephants roaming around the Tantirimale temple premises, forest reserves and nearby villages back into Wilpattu National Park.

Local Pradeshiya Sabha members, Wildlife Conservation Department officials and representatives of farmers' organisations and funeral aid societies attended the meeting.

Deputy Minister Jayakody said the Government was committed to reducing human deaths and crop damage through scientific and practical measures, with the cooperation of local communities.
