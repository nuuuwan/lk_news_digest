Secondary market ends week on a muted note

The secondary market recorded a subdued trading session, characterised by limited activity and low trading volumes.

Despite the muted market participation, the secondary market yield curve edged slightly higher from the belly to the long end of the curve.

Among the actively traded maturities, the 15.10.2030 maturity traded at a yield of 11.60 percent, while the 01.11.2033 and 01.07.2037 maturities were traded at the rates of 12.10 percent and 12.60 percent, respectively.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated marginally against the US dollar, standing at Rs.336.30/US dollar, compared to Rs.336.40/US dollar seen earlier.

Furthermore, the Sri Lankan rupee also appreciated against the other major currencies such as GBP, EUR, JPY, CNY and AUD.

Overnight liquidity in the banking system slightly expanded to Rs.167.97 billion, from Rs.163.41 billion recorded previously.
