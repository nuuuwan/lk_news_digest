NSO withdraws request for electricity tariff hike

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The National System Operator (NSO) has withdrawn its request for an electricity tariff increase for the October–December quarter of 2026, citing improved reservoir storage levels and favourable rainfall forecasts.

The decision marks a reversal of its earlier position, under which the NSO had proposed a 2.88% increase in electricity tariffs for the final three months of the year.

The initial proposal was made citing an expected revenue shortfall of Rs. 4.4 billion during the quarter.

Following a preliminary review, the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) had reduced the proposed increase to 1.46% and conducted public consultations on Thursday (5).

However, in a statement issued after the stakeholder consultation, NSO (Pvt) Ltd announced that it would no longer seek a tariff increase for the final quarter of the year.
