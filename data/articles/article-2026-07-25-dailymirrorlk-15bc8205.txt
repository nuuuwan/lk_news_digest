At least 35 killed in Syria bus collision, health ministry says

(The Times of Israel) - Thirty-five people were killed and 30 injured in a collision between two passenger buses on Syria’s Deir al-Zor-Damascus road, a health ministry official tells state news agency SANA.

The collision involved a bus carrying members of the Internal Security Forces and a civilian passenger bus, state news agency SANA reports, citing the interior ministry.
