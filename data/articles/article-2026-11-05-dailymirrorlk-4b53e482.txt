Navy seizes trawler carrying narcotics worth over Rs. 4.2bn

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has seized a multi-day fishing trawler suspected of smuggling a consignment of narcotics, valued at over Rs. 4.2 billion, during an operation conducted in international waters off the southern coast.

The Navy intercepted the trawler during an operation on the high seas and recovered approximately 189.246 kilogrammes of suspected narcotics, comprising 132.026 kilogrammes of crystal methamphetamine (Ice) and 57.220 kilogrammes of heroin.

The trawler was brought to the Dikowita Fisheries Harbour by the Navy this morning (05), where the narcotics found in 10 sacks aboard the vessel were inspected.

The seized drugs have an estimated street value exceeding Rs. 4.2 billion.
