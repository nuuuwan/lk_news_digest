‘30BestBarsIndia’ to launch ‘Best Bars Sri Lanka’ in early 2027

Sri Lanka’s bar scene has been gaining recognition on the international stage, with a growing number of establishments earning global accolades and making their presence felt across Asia’s cocktail landscape.

Building on this momentum, 30BestBarsIndia; the annual ranking and awards platform founded by Vikram Achanta and Radhakrishnan Nair in 2019to recognise India’s leading bars and the people behind them; is expanding its footprint to the island with the launch of Best Bars Sri Lanka.

Dedicated to recognising the country’s leading bars and the people shaping its beverage industry, the inaugural ranking of Sri Lanka’s Top bars will be unveiled at an awards evening in Colombo in early 2027, marking the first edition of the initiative. Over the past few years, Sri Lanka has developed a distinctive bar culture, bringing together its culinary heritage, indigenous ingredients, homegrown spirits and a new generation of hospitality talent. Bars such as Smoke & Bitters, Raa, Ropewalk and Uncle’s have helped bring the country’s cocktail scene to wider attention, with their approach to local flavours and contemporary drinks finding an audience beyond the island. Best Bars Sri Lanka aims to build on this growing recognition by creating a platform that celebrates the breadth of the country’s bar landscape, from independent neighbourhood establishments to hotel and restaurant bars, while recognising the teams, bartenders and ideas that contribute to its evolution.

Vikram Achanta, Co-Founder, 30BestBarsIndia said, “Over the past few years, through our visits to Sri Lanka and our interactions with its bar community, we’ve seen how much the country’s bar scene has grown. There is a real sense of energy, with bars developing their own identities, drawing on local ingredients and traditions, and earning recognition beyond the island. We felt it was the right time to bring that community together and celebrate what it has built through a platform of its own.

Best Bars Sri Lanka will give the country’s bars and the people behind them an opportunity to be recognised on a wider stage.”

Radhakrishnan Nair, Co-Founder, 30BestBarsIndia, adds, “Just as 30BestBarsIndia has championed local bars across India, our hope is that Best Bars Sri Lanka will play a similar role in honouring the work that goes into building a strong bar industry in the country. Recognition gives bars and their teams a benchmark to aim for. It encourages them to keep raising their standards and helps the wider community learn from one another. We hope this initiative brings greater visibility to the talent and creativity in Sri Lanka’s bars, and gives the industry a platform to connect with the region and the world.
