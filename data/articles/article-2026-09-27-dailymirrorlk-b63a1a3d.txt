Thileepan statue installed in Nallur removed by police

Sep. 27 (Daily Mirror) - A memorial statue of Thileepan, who died after undertaking a hunger strike over demands related to the aspirations of the Tamil people, was removed and taken away by police a short while ago from Nallur, Jaffna.

A statue was erected in Nallur in his memory following the commemoration last week of the 39th anniversary of Thileepan’s death, after he undertook a hunger strike.

Following the conclusion of the final day of the commemoration yesterday, the statue was removed by police this morning, September 27, and taken away in a vehicle.
