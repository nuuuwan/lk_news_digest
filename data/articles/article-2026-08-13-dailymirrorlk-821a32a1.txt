Indices close in green

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, National Development Bank and Dipped Products with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.1 Bn.

Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 closed in green. Crossings were witnessed in Melstacorp, John Keells Holdings and Sampath Bank accounting for 44.6% of the turnover.

Mixed interest was observed in Sierra Cables, Seylan Bank nonvoting and Dipped Products whilst retail interest was noted in Softlogic Finance, HNB Finance and Renuka Agri Foods. Furthermore, foreigners remained active closing as net buyers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Melstacorp) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.08%. The share price of Melstacorp increased by Rs 0.50 (0.27%) to close at Rs 186.50.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index increased by 0.25%. The share price of Sampath Bank gained Rs 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs 137.00.

Sierra Cables, John Keells Holdings and Ceylon Guardian Investment Trust were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Sierra Cables moved up by Rs 0.70 (2.22%) to close at Rs 32.20. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a gain of Rs 0.10 (0.50%) to close at Rs 20.00. The share price of Ceylon Guardian Investment Trust appreciated by Rs 2.75 (1.24%) to close at Rs 225.00.
