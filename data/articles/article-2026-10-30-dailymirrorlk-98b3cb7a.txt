Three injured in factory explosion in Padukka

Colombo, Oct 30 (Daily Mirror) - Three employees were injured in an explosion at a factory in the Meepe area of Padukka today (30), police said.

The injured, including two male workers and a female employee, were admitted to the Homagama Base Hospital for treatment.

The explosion also caused extensive damage to a nearby house, with a roof tile reportedly falling on the female worker's head during the blast.

According to police, the explosion occurred when workers attempted to cut and modify a fuel storage tank using a gas torch without first removing the fuel remaining inside it.

The tank had reportedly been purchased at an auction for use at the factory to store lubricants.
