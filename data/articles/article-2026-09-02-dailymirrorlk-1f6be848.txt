Sri Lanka, Brazil agree on new cooperation framework for sugarcane project

Sri Lanka and Brazil have agreed to sign a Supplementary Adjustment Agreement to facilitate the implementation of the Sri Lanka Sugarcane Project, aimed at strengthening technical cooperation in the sugarcane sector.

Sri Lanka and Brazil entered into a Technical Cooperation Agreement in September 2008 to promote bilateral cooperation in areas of mutual benefit.

Under this framework, a Cooperation Agreement for the implementation of the Sri Lanka Sugarcane Project was approved for signing at a Cabinet meeting held on May 25, 2026. The agreement involves the Brazilian Cooperation Agency, the Federal University of São Carlos, Sri Lanka’s Ministry of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development and the Sugarcane Research Institute.

The Technical Cooperation Agreement requires individual programmes and projects to be implemented through complementary agreements to ensure coordination between the two countries.

The agreement is expected to provide a framework for continued technical cooperation and knowledge-sharing between the two countries in the sugarcane sector.
