Pan Asia Bank-Toyota Lanka bring reliable mobility solutions to customers

Pan Asia Bank Director and CEO Naleen Edirisinghe exchanges signed agreement with Toyota Lanka Chairman Masaki Kawabata in the presence of officials from both organisations

No guarantors, approval within a day, islandwide doorstep service and many more benefits

Pan Asia Bank has partnered with Toyota Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd to make vehicle ownership more accessible, convenient and reliable for the customers through customised leasing solutions, one-day financing approvals and islandwide doorstep service.

The partnership brings together Toyota Lanka’s trusted mobility solutions with Pan Asia Bank’s flexible financing capabilities, enabling the customers to move from vehicle selection to financing with greater speed, ease and confidence.

Under the partnership, the Toyota Lanka customers will have access to Pan Asia Bank’s leasing facilities for periods of up to seven years, with no guarantors required and lease approvals within one working day. The bank will also provide islandwide doorstep service, enabling the customers to access financing support with greater convenience.

The partnership extends beyond vehicle financing, with the customers also eligible for pre-approved solar loan facilities and pre-approved credit card facilities, including zero percent interest instalment plans for insurance premium payments. These additional facilities are intended to provide the customers with greater flexibility in managing both their vehicle-related and broader financial requirements.

Pan Asia Bank Director and CEO Naleen Edirisinghe said, “This partnership brings financing and vehicle purchasing closer together, while giving the customers greater choice and convenience. We are pleased to offer flexible leasing solutions alongside additional facilities that support their wider financial needs and look forward to creating lasting value through this partnership.”

The memorandum of understanding will deliver a more seamless customer experience by combining vehicle solutions with accessible financial services by Pan Asia Bank. Through close collaboration over the one-year partnership, the partners will work towards maximising business opportunities while responding to the evolving customer requirements.

Toyota Lanka Chairman Masaki Kawabata said, “This partnership gives our customers greater convenience by bringing together accessible and flexible mobility financing options. We look forward to working with Pan Asia Bank to enhance the overall purchasing experience and provide the customers with solutions that support their mobility and financial needs.”
