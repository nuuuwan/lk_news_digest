Fake SLS helmets: Company fined Rs. 80,000

Colombo, Aug. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Kurunegala Magistrate’s Court has fined a company Rs. 80,000 for manufacturing and selling safety helmets bearing a falsely printed SLS standard logo without obtaining the required certification from the Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI).

The case was filed by the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) against RICO TRADING CO (PVT) LTD, located at the Heraliyawala Industrial Estate in Kurunegala, for manufacturing and selling helmets under the “TEN STAR” brand with the fake SLS logo.

During a raid conducted by CAA officers in the Kurunegala District on July 27, 57 safety helmets bearing the fake logo were seized from the premises.

When the case was taken up before the Kurunegala Magistrate’s Court on July 31, the company pleaded guilty and was fined Rs. 80,000.

The court also ordered the destruction of the helmets produced as case items.

The CAA urged the public to report establishments manufacturing or selling substandard products or products bearing fake SLS logos by calling its 1977 hotline during office hours.
