Sri Lanka, Bangladesh move to strengthen bilateral ties

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka and Bangladesh have agreed to strengthen bilateral cooperation in trade, commerce, education, tourism and other key sectors following talks between Foreign Affairs Minister Vijitha Herath and Bangladesh State Minister for Foreign Affairs Humaiun Kobir.

The two officials met at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism this morning to discuss ways of further strengthening relations between the two countries.

During the meeting, Kobir thanked Minister Herath for inviting him to Sri Lanka and noted that his visit marked the arrival of a high-level Bangladeshi official since 2024.

He said both countries had faced economic challenges and stressed the importance of closer cooperation to achieve mutual prosperity.

Kobir also appreciated the Government's Clean Sri Lanka and Prajashakthi programmes, noting that poverty alleviation was a key priority for both countries.

Responding, Minister Herath said Sri Lanka's policy was to work closely with regional partners for mutual benefit. He sought Bangladesh's support in expanding cooperation in trade, commerce, education and tourism.

He also requested Bangladesh to facilitate the participation of a business delegation at the upcoming Expo 2027, with Kobir agreeing to extend his support.

Kobir, meanwhile, requested Sri Lanka to consider a Government-to-Government agreement to procure medicines from Bangladeshi manufacturers.

Minister Herath also invited Bangladesh to consider reviving the Preferential Trade Agreement between the two countries to boost bilateral trade.

Recalling that diplomatic relations between the two countries were established in 1972, both sides reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening their longstanding friendship and cooperation.

Following the discussions, the two countries signed a Memorandum of Understanding on establishing the Bangladesh-Sri Lanka Joint Consultative Commission for Comprehensive Cooperation.
