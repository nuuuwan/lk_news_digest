Expose the Names of cheating rogue shops

Daily, we find news reports of  cheating shops  and  fines imposed  by  courts.   But names of shops are never given in these reports.

Why are the names of shops raided by the Central Environmental Authority and fined by the courts hidden from media reports?

Shops are raided by the Central Environmental Authority for offences such as polluting the environment, trading without a licence, and deceiving the public by selling goods. Later, fines are imposed on them by the courts. Yet, in newspapers and television news, the names of those shops or their owners are not mentioned.

This is a major problem. After a wrong doing has been committed and proven in court with a fine imposed, the public must be informed about it. It is not just a punishment for one person. It is a lesson for the whole business  community.

When names are hidden in reports, that same shop commits the same offence again. Other shops also commit the same offence without any fear. In the end, it is the innocent consumer and the environment that suffer the loss. Recent report of shops selling poisonous boondi  is alarming as it may lead to serious health issues in litttle kids.

Once punished by law, publishing names is not defamation. It is publishing the truth. Therefore, we request that the names of shops proven guilty by the court, along with the amount of the fine, be reported publicly. Only then will the law have real value. Only then the public and environment would be protected.
