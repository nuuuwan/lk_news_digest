Landslide blocks railway line near Kadigamuwa for third time

Colombo, Oct. 16 (Daily Mirror) - A landslide near Kadigamuwa Railway Station has blocked the railway line for the third time since train services resumed on the upcountry railway line following Cyclone Ditwah.

A section of earth from a nearby hillside has been washed onto the railway track amid the prevailing heavy rains in the central hills.

Railway workers are currently at the scene, engaged in removing the earth and clearing the blocked railway line.

The incident marks the third occasion on which earth from the same hillside has fallen onto the railway line near Kadigamuwa Railway Station since the resumption of train services following Cyclone Ditwah.
