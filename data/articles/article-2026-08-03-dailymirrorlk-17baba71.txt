Bumrah ruled out, Aaqib Nabi earns India test call-up

AUG.03 (Mirror Sports) - India have called up Jammu & Kashmir pacer Aaqib Nabi to replace injured Jasprit Bumrah for the two-Test series against Sri Lanka. Bumrah has been ruled out after failing to recover fully from a left knee niggle, while Nabi receives his maiden Test squad call-up after impressive domestic performances.
