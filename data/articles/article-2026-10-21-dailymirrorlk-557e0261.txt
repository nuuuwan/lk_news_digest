Landslide at Kadigamuwa disrupts train services to Gampola, Peradeniya

Colombo, Oct. 21 (Daily Mirror) - Train services on the railway line beyond Rambukkana have been disrupted following a landslide at Kadigamuwa Railway Station this afternoon.

As a result, trains that departed from Colombo Fort at 3.35 p.m. bound for Gampola and Peradeniya have been restricted to Rambukkana, railway authorities said.

The disruption follows continuing landslide incidents amid prevailing adverse weather conditions.
