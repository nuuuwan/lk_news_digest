Alpha Tours marks 50-year partnership with Korean Air

From left: Alpha Tours Managing Director Priyantha Mendis, Western Province Governor and Presidential Special Envoy for Foreign Investment Hanif Yosoof, Ambassador of the Republic of Korea to Sri Lanka Miyon Lee, Hanjin Logistics President and CEO Sam Sug Noh, Finco Holdings Vice Chairman Priyanjith Weerasooria and Hanjin Logistics President and CMO Emily Lee Cho

Alpha Tours, a member of Finco Holdings, marked a significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s aviation and travel industry by celebrating 50 years as the general sales agent (GSA) for Korean Air, making it the airline’s longest serving GSA across its global network.

The golden jubilee celebration was held at The Hilton Colombo, bringing together leaders from the aviation, diplomatic and business communities to commemorate five decades of partnership.

The event reflected the long-standing relationship between Alpha Tours and Korean Air, highlighting a partnership that has contributed to strengthening passenger and cargo connectivity between Sri Lanka and international markets over the past five decades.

A special highlight of the evening was the presence of Hanjin Logistics Corporation President and Chief Marketing Officer Emily Lee Cho, the granddaughter of the late Cho Choong-hoon, the founder of Hanjin Group and Korean Air.

Her participation underscored the historic significance of the occasion and the enduring relationship between the two organisations.

The distinguished gathering of guests, included Finco Holdings Chairman S.C. Weerasooria, Western Province Governor and Presidential Special Envoy for Foreign Investment Hanif Yusoof, Ambassador of the Republic of Korea to Sri Lanka Miyon Lee, Civil Aviation Director General and Chief Executive Officer Capt. Daminda Rambukwella, Hanjin Logistics President and CEO Sam Sug Noh, Korean Air in Singapore Regional Manager Jin Ho Cho, Korean Air in Singapore General Manager Cargo Justin Chung and Finco Holdings Vice Chairman Priyanjith Weerasooria. Representatives from the aviation sector, cargo, passenger and travel industries also attended the event.  The anniversary marks more than a business partnership. It reflected of long-standing collaboration in supporting air travel, trade and connectivity between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Korea, recognising Alpha Tours’ position as one of Korean Air’s longest-standing international partners.

As both organisations look ahead, the milestone also reaffirmed a shared commitment to strengthening aviation links and building on a partnership that has spanned half a century.
