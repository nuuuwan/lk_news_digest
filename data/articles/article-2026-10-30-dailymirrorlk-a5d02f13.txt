Halambage smashes unbeaten 80 as Sri Lanka defeat Bangladesh in Hong Kong Sixes

Colombo, Oct 30 (Daily Mirror) Sri Lanka made a winning start to their title defence at the Hong Kong International Sixes, defeating Bangladesh in a thrilling Group B encounter at the Tin Kwong Road Recreation Ground.

The highlight of the match was a sensational batting display by Vishen Halambage, who smashed an unbeaten 80 runs off just 17 deliveries to guide Sri Lanka to victory.

Chasing a challenging target of 128 runs, Sri Lanka reached 128 for one in just 5.2 overs, sealing the victory with four balls to spare.

Batting first after being sent in, Bangladesh posted an imposing 127 for four in their allotted six overs.
