IIA Sri Lanka’s 11th National Conference to shape future of internal audit in AI era

More than 400 internal audit, governance, risk and technology professionals representing over 100 public and private sector organizations are expected to gather in Colombo this week  (13 August) for the 11th National Conference of The Institute of Internal Auditors Sri Lanka (IIA Sri Lanka).

The conference, to be held at Cinnamon Life, Colombo, will focus on the theme “Transforming Internal Audit – Leading with Intelligence, Integrity, and Impact in the AI-Accelerated Era.”

Against a backdrop of rapidly advancing artificial intelligence, data analytics, emerging technologies and increasingly complex organizational risks, the conference will explore how internal audit must evolve to remain relevant, trusted and capable of creating meaningful organizational value.

Muzawwir Manzeer, President of IIA Sri Lanka, said technological transformation is fundamentally changing both organizational risks and expectations of internal audit. “AI is not simply changing the tools internal auditors use; it is changing the risks organizations face, the speed at which decisions are made and the expectations placed on assurance functions. Internal auditors must develop the intelligence to understand emerging risks, maintain the integrity and professional judgement required to challenge effectively, and demonstrate tangible impact within their organizations,” he said.

Tishan Subasinghe, President of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (CA Sri Lanka), will attend as Chief Guest, while entrepreneur and Industrial Development Board Chairman Yasas Hewage will deliver the keynote address. The conference program is structured around three interconnected pillars — Intelligence, Integrity and Impact.

The Intelligence session will examine how analytics, AI and emerging technologies can enable smarter, more continuous and forward-looking assurance. Speakers include Natalie Warawita of MAS Holdings, Tharaka Mahabage of Kaya and Obada Hattab of ZeeDimension. The Integrity session will focus on professional judgement, ethical leadership, critical thinking and the evolving skills required as AI becomes increasingly embedded in business and assurance processes. Dr. Prasenna Balachandran, Vice President – Risk and Governance at Inqube Global, will share perspectives on maintaining professional excellence in a technology-enabled environment.

The Impact theme will explore how internal audit can move beyond traditional assurance and contribute more meaningfully to organizational resilience and strategic decision-making. Speakers include Tahsinur Rahim, Head of Audit, Risk and Compliance, and Dhayalaruban Thangaraja, Director – Internal Audit at BDO Malta.

A Cross-Theme Panel Discussion will bring Intelligence, Integrity and Impact together, featuring Yasith Fernando of Dialog Finance, Suren Rajakarier of KPMG and Chaaminda Kumarasiri Director and Management consultant, moderated by Numair Cassim, Chief Internal Auditor at Amana Bank.

Established in 2005, IIA Sri Lanka is the national professional body dedicated to advancing internal audit and strengthening governance, risk management and assurance practices. As an affiliate of The Institute of Internal Auditors, the global professional body for internal auditing, IIA Sri Lanka connects local professionals with internationally recognized standards, certifications, and research and professional-development opportunities.
