Attractive Offers, But Beware of What You Get!

Consumers should exercise caution when responding to the tempting special offers and discounts advertised by fast-food outlets. Offers such as “Buy One, Get One Free” and heavily discounted meal deals may appear too good to resist. However, consumers should be equally mindful of the quality and actual contents of what they are paying for.

I recently had an unpleasant experience with an international fast-food chain. Enticed by an attractive discount, I ordered 20 chicken drumsticks. To my dismay, the order arrived with only 10 drumsticks and 10 winglets!

When I contacted the outlet to question this discrepancy, I was informed that it was company policy and that the order was part of a special promotion. However, when I asked why an offer advertised as 20 drumsticks had been fulfilled with 10 winglets instead, there was no convincing explanation.

To their credit, the staff were extremely courteous and offered to replace the 10 winglets. Unfortunately, by then it was rather late at night, and the offer came too late to be of any practical use.

Do not allow attractive discounts to cloud your judgment. A special offer should not mean a compromise on what was advertised or what the customer is entitled to receive. After all, a discount is no license to mislead the consumer.
