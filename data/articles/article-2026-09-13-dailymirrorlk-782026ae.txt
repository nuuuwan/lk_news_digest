Dehiwala ‘Made in China’ footwear racket exposed

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has raided a clandestine footwear manufacturing facility in Kawdana, Dehiwala, where footwear allegedly assembled locally was being labelled as “Made in China” and supplied to reputed retail chains.

The raid was carried out on September 11 following a lengthy investigation into invoices and the supply chain of footwear purchased from a well-known clothing and fashion retail chain.

According to the CAA, investigations revealed that soles and other components of the footwear had been imported separately as raw materials, allegedly to avoid import duties applicable to finished footwear.

The imported components were subsequently assembled and glued together at the facility in Kawdana to produce complete pairs of shoes.

Investigators further found that the footwear assembled locally was packaged and labelled as “Made in China” before being distributed to well-known retail outlets. The products allegedly did not carry the name and address of the actual manufacturer.

The CAA said such practices could mislead consumers about the actual country of manufacture and make it difficult to identify the responsible manufacturer in the event of defects or other issues with the footwear.

The Authority also warned that such practices could create unfair competition for local manufacturers and traders who comply with tax and other legal requirements.

During the raid, CAA officers seized nearly 1,000 pairs of footwear along with raw materials used for their assembly.

Further investigations are underway, and legal action will be taken against those responsible upon completion of the investigations, the CAA said.

The Authority said it would continue conducting follow-up raids based on invoices and supply-chain information to curb illegal trade practices, protect consumers and ensure fair competition in the market.

The public can provide information on suspected fraudulent or misleading trade practices to the CAA through its 1977 hotline during office hours.
