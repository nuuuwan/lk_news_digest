Deepfake images used to target women political leaders: Prime Minister

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya today revealed that deepfake images are being used to target women political leaders in Sri Lanka, which poses a threat to democracy and the functioning of democracy.

The Prime Minister told Parliament that this is a global issue and that the platforms that facilitate this technological violence should take up this issue more seriously.

She said women in public positions across the world are facing this issue despite their political backgrounds, political and ideological affiliations.

“I received a letter detailing how deepfake images are being used to target women political leaders in this country. As women political leaders, when we come into these positions, we are aware of the kind of backlash to expect,” she said.

The Prime Minister said this is not a problem that can be tackled individually or even by an individual country and added that it is something to be tackled globally.
