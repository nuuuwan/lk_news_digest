Parliamentary committee to summon local authorities over Ditwah response

Colombo, Sep. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Select Committee of Parliament to conduct a comprehensive study on the lack of preparedness to face Cyclone Ditwah and report to Parliament and submit its proposals and recommendations in that regard has decided to summon representatives of Pradeshiya Sabhas and Municipal Councils to examine how local authorities coordinated their response during the disaster.

The Committee will particularly examine the role played by local authorities in official communication and coordination during the disaster situation.

At a recent meeting held in Parliament under the chairmanship of Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa, the Committee sought information from members of the National Council for Disaster Management and the District Secretaries of Kandy and Matale on disaster management efforts in the two districts.

The Committee focused on early warning systems, communication between government institutions and emergency response procedures during the heavy rainfall, landslides and floods experienced in Kandy and Matale from November 23 to 27, 2025.

It also discussed the need to establish formal written procedures for emergency disaster situations, conduct regular disaster preparedness drills and further improve the efficiency of early warning systems.
