Lighter school bags in future?

Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya recently said that children will be able to take lighter school bags in future. With a smaller number of textbooks, the government is planning to bring about some essential reforms to the education system.

This is a welcome move given the evolving nature of the education system in South Asia and the world over. Children should be provided with an opportunity to have interactive methods of learning rather than relying on textbooks and what is taught in the classroom. They should be given the space to solve problems using analytical skills outside of their classrooms. Back in the day, children were encouraged to become scouts and girl guides because there were various activities that allowed them to think critically and solve problems.

They had to work in teams and sometimes take the lead to guide their team to win various games, encouraging the spirit of brotherhood. But today we see that children are mostly left to themselves and they have limited opportunities to socialise. Parents nowadays are more inclined to see the report cards and the grades but are not willing to appreciate various skills of their children. Their children maybe good singers, artists and sportsmen but parents still have an archaic pattern of thinking which may hinder their child’s talents. Education is just one aspect of a child’s future and a holistic approach is essential to inspire future generations.
