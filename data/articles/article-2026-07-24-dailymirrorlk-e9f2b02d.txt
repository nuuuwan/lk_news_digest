Eco Spindles-Gunadamin Elephant House lead Ruwanwelisaya cleanup

Eco Spindles (Pvt.) Ltd and Gunadamin Elephant House marked the period following Poson Poya Day with meaningful environmental action, bringing together employees, volunteers and community partners to protect the sacred Ruwanwelisaya premises while demonstrating how discarded plastic can be transformed into valuable resources.

Conducted in Anuradhapura on June 30, 2026, the day following Poson Poya Day, the corporate social responsibility initiative brought together 60 volunteers, including 20 employees from Eco Spindles and Elephant House, alongside representatives from the Thilaka Eco Centre and local communities.

The project focused on responsible waste collection, segregation and recycling while raising awareness among pilgrims and visitors about the importance of safeguarding Sri Lanka’s cultural and religious heritage sites.

Approximately 350 kilogrammes of discarded plastic bottles were collected during the cleanup. The recovered bottles will be processed by Eco Spindles and transformed into monofilaments and yarn, reinforcing the company’s commitment to advancing a circular economy by converting plastic waste into useful materials.

Eco Spindles CEO Recycling Prashantha Malimbadage said, “Protecting the environment must go hand in hand with preserving the places that hold deep cultural and spiritual value for our nation. This initiative demonstrates that responsible waste management and recycling can create lasting environmental value while encouraging communities to become active participants in the circular economy.”

Elephant House Head of Marketing Dilshani Edirisinghe said, “Our vision is to ensure that, as a responsible organisation, our activities create a positive impact on society for future generations. By working with Eco Spindles, volunteers and local partners, we were able to promote responsible behaviour among visitors while contributing to the protection of an important national heritage site.”
