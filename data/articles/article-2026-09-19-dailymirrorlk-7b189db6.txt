Heavy rainfall recorded, river levels normal

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) -Several areas across the country recorded significant rainfall during the past 21 hours, with the highest rainfall of 42 mm recorded at Magura in the Kalu Ganga basin, the latest water level and rainfall situation report issued by the Irrigation Department said.

The report, issued at 6.30 a.m. today (19), showed that water levels at the monitored major rivers remained within normal ranges, with no river recording levels above the alert, minor flood or major flood thresholds.

At Kithulgala, the water level of the Kelani Ganga stood at 1.83 metres, against an alert level of 3.00 metres and a minor flood level of 4.00 metres.

At Ratnapura, the Kalu Ganga was at 1.54 metres, well below its 5.20-metre alert level, while at Magura, the level was 3.90 metres, against a 4.00-metre alert level.

The Kelani Ganga at Glencourse stood at 10.13 metres, compared with an alert level of 15 metres.

The Irrigation Department said that the monitored river stations were in normal condition, although rainfall was recorded across several river basins.

The report covered the Kelani Ganga, Kalu Ganga, Gin Ganga, Nilwala Ganga, Walawe Ganga, Kirindi Oya, Menik Ganga, Kumbukkan Oya, Heda Oya, Maduru Oya, Mahaweli Ganga, Yan Oya, Maa Oya, Malwathu Oya, Mee Oya, Deduru Oya, Maha Oya and Attanagalu Oya river basins.
