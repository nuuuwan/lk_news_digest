133 construction workers to leave for Israel tomorrow

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 133 Sri Lankan construction job seekers are scheduled to leave for Israel tomorrow (23) for employment in the country’s construction sector, the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) said.

An awareness programme for job seekers selected for employment in the Israeli construction and infrastructure sectors, along with the distribution of air tickets, was held at the SLBFE auditorium on September 14 under the patronage of SLBFE Chairman Kosala Wickramasinghe.

Air tickets were distributed to 133 job seekers bound for employment in the construction sector and 17 job seekers selected for jobs in the infrastructure sector.

The programme was aimed at providing the prospective migrant workers with necessary information and guidance ahead of their departure for employment in Israel.
