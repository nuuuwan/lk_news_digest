Nuwara Eliya records lowest minimum temperature at 13.5°C

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Nuwara Eliya recorded the lowest minimum temperature in Sri Lanka at 13.5°C, according to temperature observations issued by the Meteorology Department today.

The temperature map for September 8 showed Bandarawela recording 16.6°C, while Badulla recorded 18.0°C and Monaragala 23.0°C.

Among other areas, Katugastota recorded 21.4°C, Ratnapura 24.4°C, Matale 25.9°C, and Kandy-area observations showed relatively cooler conditions compared to most lowland areas.

Meanwhile, temperatures remained comparatively high in several parts of the country, with Jaffna and Mannar recording 28.1°C, Colombo 28.0°C and Puttalam 27.9°C.

The Department of Meteorology's latest observations indicate a marked temperature contrast between the central hills and the warmer northern and coastal regions.
