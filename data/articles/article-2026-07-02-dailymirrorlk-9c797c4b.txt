India to tour Sri Lanka for two-test series in August

Colombo, July 02 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Cricket said that the India men's cricket team will tour Sri Lanka in August 2026 for a two-match Test series.

The series will form part of the ICC World Test Championship (WTC) 2025–2027 cycle, with both teams competing for valuable championship points.

According to the official schedule, the first Test will be played at the Galle International Cricket Stadium from August 15 to 19.

The second and final Test will take place at the Singhalese Sports Club (SSC) Ground in Colombo from August 23 to 27.
