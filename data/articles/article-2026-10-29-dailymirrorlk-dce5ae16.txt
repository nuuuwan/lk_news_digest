Ailing elephant calf ‘Thal-etaya’ to be transferred to Udawalawe for treatment

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Steps are being taken to transport an ailing elephant calf known as ‘Thal-etaya’ to the Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home for further medical treatment.

The operation is being carried out under the guidance of the Assistant Director of Wildlife for Polonnaruwa, with the involvement of officers and staff from the Manampitiya Wildlife Office.

Wildlife veterinarians from Giritale and other areas are also participating in the operation to provide urgent medical care and ensure the calf’s safe transportation to Udawalawe.

Wildlife officials and veterinary teams are working together to facilitate the calf’s transfer and provide the necessary treatment.
