Chinese nationals fined Rs. 76.4 mn over Sinharaja wildlife theft

A group of Chinese nationals arrested for collecting endemic insects and reptiles in the Sinharaja Forest Reserve has been fined a record Rs. 76.4 million by the Kalawana Magistrate's Court.

The suspects, comprising several men and a woman, were arrested by wildlife officers on November 9 while allegedly collecting insects and reptiles endemic to Sri Lanka in the buffer zone of the Sinharaja Forest Reserve.

Wildlife officers had recovered more than 250 individual animals belonging to nearly 40 species from their possession.

The suspects were subsequently produced before the Kalawana Magistrate's Court and remanded until November 23.

Delivering the order, Kalawana Magistrate Yasas Three Priyangika Dissanayake imposed a total fine of Rs. 76.4 million on the Chinese nationals. The court also ordered the confiscation of all items seized in connection with the case.

Wildlife authorities described the fine as the highest ever imposed in Sri Lanka in a wildlife-related offence.

The case was handled by Wildlife Range Officer K. D. Nadeesha Muthukalum, with the assistance of Assistant Wildlife Range Officer S. T. Ruwan Kumara and Wildlife Warden S. H. T. Maduranga.
