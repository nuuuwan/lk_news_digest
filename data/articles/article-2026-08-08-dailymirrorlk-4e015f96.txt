SriLankan Airlines resumes Colombo–Kuwait flights

Colombo, August 8 (Daily Mirror) - SriLankan Airlines has resumed flights between Colombo and Kuwait, offering six services per week.

According to the airline, flight UL229 will depart Colombo at 5:10 p.m. every Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, arriving in Kuwait at 8:05 p.m.

The return flight, UL230, will leave Kuwait at 9:10 p.m. on the same days and arrive in Colombo at 5:15 a.m. the following day.

The airline will operate regular passenger services between Colombo and Kuwait on all days of the week except Wednesday.
