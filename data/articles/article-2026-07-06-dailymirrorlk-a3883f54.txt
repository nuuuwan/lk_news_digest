Commander of the Navy meets with Commander of the Air Force

The newly appointed Commander of the Navy, Vice Admiral Damian Fernando met with the Commander of the Air Force, Air Marshal Bandu Edirisinghe, at Air Force Headquarters today (06 July 2026).

Upon his arrival the Commander of the Navy was accorded a ceremonial Guard of Honour, in keeping with long-standing military traditions. During the cordial meeting that followed, the two service chiefs exchanged views on matters of mutual interest, with particular focus on the collective role of the Sri Lanka Navy and Air Force in strengthening national security and further enhancing inter-service cooperation.

The visit concluded with the exchange of mementoes as a gesture of mutual respect, reflecting the enduring camaraderie and close professional ties between the two Armed Forces.
