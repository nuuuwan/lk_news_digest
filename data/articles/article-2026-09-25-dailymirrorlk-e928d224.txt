HNB Bank’s Central Collections Unit receives ISO 9001:2015 Certification

HNB, Senior Manager – Central Collections, Arjuna Damunupola; HNB, Vice President – Recoveries, Sampath Vithana; HNB, MD/CEO, Damith Pallewatte; and Bureau Veritas, Country General Manager, Shan Nanayakkara, sharing the MoU alongside representatives from both organisations

HNB PLC has secured ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certification for its Central Collections Unit (CCU), covering debt collection and recovery activities within personal financial services. The certification was issued by Bureau Veritas Lanka following an assessment of the unit’s processes, controls and service standards.

The certification confirms that the CCU’s recovery processes meet an internationally recognized quality standard, covering how the unit engages customers, documents cases and manages recovery timelines. It applies specifically to collections and recovery activities within personal financial services, an area where HNB has aligned its recovery practices with regulatory expectations and customer protection standards.

HNB Senior Manager - Central Collections, Arjuna Damunupola said, “This certification confirms that our processes meet an internationally recognized standard and gives customers a clear, consistent experience when their accounts are being managed for Collections. It also gives us a structured basis to keep improving how we handle cases, from documentation through to resolution.”

Under the ISO framework, the CCU applies a risk-based approach to segment accounts by delinquency stage and route them to the appropriate recovery path, from early reminders to structured repayment plans, before any escalation. This supports HNB PLC’s efforts to resolve under-performing and non-performing accounts through documented repayment agreements while managing impairment levels.
