Indices close in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Distilleries and Hayleys with the turnover crossing Rs. 666 Mn. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Access Engineering, Commercial Bank and John Keells Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Sampath Bank, Panasian Power and Hayleys whilst retail interest was noted in Waskaduwa Beach Resort, Muller & Phipps and SMB Leasing. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.09%. The share price of Commercial Bank decreased by Rs 0.75 (0.38%) to close at Rs 195.25. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs 138.50.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Access Engineering and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.11%. The share price of Access Engineering lost Rs 0.20 (0.27%) to close at Rs 74.50. The share price of John Keells Holdings moved up by Rs 0.10 (0.57%) to close at Rs 17.70.

Panasian Power was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Panasian Power declined by Rs 0.10 (0.99%) to close at Rs 10.00.
