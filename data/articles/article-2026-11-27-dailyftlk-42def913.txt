Defence Secretary calls for stronger regional collaboration on shared security challenges

Attends NSA level meeting of Colombo Security Conclave in Malé

Defence Secretary Air Vice Marshal (Retd.) Sampath Thuyacontha attended the 8th National Security Advisers’ (NSA) Meeting of the Colombo Security Conclave (CSC), in Malé, Maldives on 25 November.

Defence Secretary is among the top defence heads of regional countries attending the Conclave.

Addressing the gathering the Defence Secretary expressed his appreciation to the Government of Maldives for hosting the meeting and for its hospitality. He observed that the Colombo Security Conclave has steadily evolved over the years into an important regional platform for addressing shared security challenges through dialogue, cooperation and collective action. He noted that the growing engagement among member countries reflects a common recognition that regional security and stability are fundamental to sustainable development and prosperity.

Highlighting the importance of the Conclave’s five pillars of cooperation, Maritime Safety and Security; Countering Terrorism and Radicalisation; Combating Trafficking and Transnational Organised Crime; Cyber Security and Protection of Critical Infrastructure and Technology; and Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief, the Defence Secretary stressed the need for strengthened regional collaboration in an increasingly interconnected and complex security environment.

Further, the Defence Secretary highlighted the importance of strengthened regional cooperation in  addressing common security challenges, including illicit drug trafficking, and outlined Sri Lanka’s  “Ratama Ekata” National Mission, which aims to combat the drug menace through enhanced law  enforcement, disruption of supply networks, preventive measures, public awareness and rehabilitation  initiatives. He also emphasised the need for greater regional collaboration to combat illegal, unreported  and unregulated (IUU) fishing through information sharing, coordinated surveillance and effective  enforcement, while stressing the importance of protecting submarine communication cables as critical infrastructure through enhanced maritime domain awareness and coordinated regional responses.

Drawing on the recent experience of Cyclone Ditwah, he further emphasised the importance of strengthening regional disaster preparedness, early-warning mechanisms, emergency response coordination, humanitarian assistance and the sharing of knowledge and best practices on climate change adaptation and resilience.

He emphasised Sri Lanka’s commitment to the objectives of the Colombo Security Conclave and expressed the country’s readiness to work closely with all participating nations to strengthen practical cooperation, enhance institutional engagement and advance shared regional security priorities. He also pointed out the collective responsibility of the participating countries to work towards a peaceful, secure and prosperous Indian Ocean Region.

The 8th NSA Meeting brings together representatives of CSC member countries to discuss regional security priorities and further strengthen cooperation in addressing common and emerging challenges in the Indian Ocean Region.

The Chief of National Intelligence, Additional Secretary for South Asia and SAARC of the Ministry of  Foreign Affairs Varuni Muthukumarana, and Senior Assistant Secretary of the Ministry of Defence Chinthaka Abeykoon, were also part of the Sri Lankan delegation.
