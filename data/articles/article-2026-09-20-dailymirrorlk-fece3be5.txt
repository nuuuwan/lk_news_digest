104 violations detected in islandwide raids on lunch sheets, ‘Sili-Sili’ bags

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has detected 104 violations during a special islandwide raid and market inspection programme targeting lunch sheets and LDPE/LLDPE “Sili-Sili” bags.

The raids were conducted on September 15, with preliminary reports from district offices indicating violations in 22 districts.

Of the violations detected, around 43 involved lunch sheets, while approximately 58 were related to “Sili-Sili” bags and three involved other polythene products.

The CAA said lunch sheets and food wrappers must comply with the labelling requirements stipulated under Direction No. 70, published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2151/58 dated November 29, 2019.

Stocks of lunch sheets that failed to meet the prescribed requirements were detected in several districts and taken into custody as case productions for legal proceedings.

Meanwhile, under Direction No. 95, published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2456/41 dated October 1, 2025, LDPE/LLDPE “Sili-Sili” bags cannot be provided to consumers free of charge.

Where such bags are provided, their prices must be clearly displayed at the business premises, while the amount charged must also be included in the consumer’s bill.

The CAA said it would conduct follow-up investigations along the supply chain, including suppliers, distributors and manufacturers, and take legal action against relevant parties pbased on the findings.

The authority said goods taken into custody would be produced before the relevant courts, while raids and market inspections targeting lunch sheets and “Sili-Sili” bags would continue islandwide.
