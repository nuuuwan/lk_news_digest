CM Vijay inaugurates 148 houses for Sri Lankan Tamils in Krishnagiri rehabilitation camp

THE HINDU - Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay on Thursday (November 26, 2026) inaugurated, through video conferencing from the Secretariat in Chennai, 148 newly constructed houses at a cost of ₹8.52 crore at the Sri Lankan Tamil Rehabilitation Camp at Pambar Dam in Krishnagiri district.

According to an official release, the State government has provided basic amenities in these houses at a cost of ₹1.37 crore. Mr. Vijay also launched the disbursal of enhanced monthly cash assistance to 56,638 Sri Lankan Tamils belonging to 19,710 families living in camps.

The monthly financial assistance for heads of families has been increased from ₹1,500 to ₹1,800, while that for family members aged 12 and above has been increased from ₹1,000 to ₹1,200. The financial assistance for children below 12 years has been increased from ₹500 to ₹600. This would involve an additional expenditure of ₹14.95 crore annually for the government.

Minister for Welfare of Non-Resident Tamils K. Thennarasu, Chief Secretary M. Sai Kumar, Secretary, Public and Rehabilitation Department, E. Sundaravalli, were among those present.

In a separate development, High Commissioner of Australia to India Philip Green, Australian Consul General in Chennai Silai Zaki, and Deputy Consul General at the Australian Consulate General in Chennai Cameron Noble, among others, called on the Chief Minister.

Deborah Thiagarajan, founder and director of Dakshina Chitra; Maya Thiagarajan, managing trustee; and Sharath Nambiar, director of the museum, also met Mr. Vijay.
