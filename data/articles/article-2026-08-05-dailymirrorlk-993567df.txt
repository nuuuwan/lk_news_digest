Luxury bus destroyed in suspected arson attack at Kaduwela Bus Stand

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - A luxury bus was destroyed in a suspected arson attack at the Kaduwela main bus stand early this morning, police said.

According to the Kaduwela Police, two unidentified individuals arrived on a motorcycle and set fire to the bus, which was parked near the weekly fairground, before fleeing the scene. The targeted vehicle was operating on the Kaduwela–Matara expressway route.

The blaze completely destroyed the luxury bus, while two standard-service buses operating on the Kaduwela–Kollupitiya route, which were parked nearby, sustained minor damage.

Police said the incident occurred between 2:30 a.m. and 2:45 a.m. The suspects were reportedly spotted by the driver and conductor of another bus parked nearby. After the two men shouted, the suspects fled the area on their motorcycle.

The driver and conductor of the targeted bus were asleep inside the vehicle at the time of the attack. They managed to escape unharmed after being alerted by their colleagues.

Police believe the arson may have been linked to a personal dispute. Investigations are underway to identify and arrest the suspects.
