Cinnamon Box Office launches

Cinnamon Box Office (CBO), a strategic ticketing platform connecting audiences, event partners and world-class productions, was officially launched on August 6 at City of Dreams Sri Lanka, introducing a destination event calendar aimed at transforming Colombo into a regional hub for entertainment experiences.

The platform brings together entertainment, hospitality and tourism, creating new opportunities for audiences, event partners and Sri Lanka’s creative economy through year-round cultural, lifestyle and entertainment experiences.

Backed by more than a decade of experience in delivering entertainment and lifestyle events through Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts, Cinnamon Life and City of Dreams Sri Lanka, CBO combines expertise in ticketing, event access, hospitality and audience engagement.

The platform has previously supported a range of premium experiences, including Broadway productions such as Mamma Mia the Musical and The Sound of Music, culinary events featuring international personalities, knowledge forums, lifestyle events and celebrity-led experiences.

As part of its new destination event calendar, CBO will present “This Is Michael – Relive the Legend”, an international tribute production celebrating the music and legacy of Michael Jackson. The show will be held on November 8 and 9, 2026, at The Podium, City of Dreams Sri Lanka.

Speaking at the launch, Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts Senior Vice President – Head of Corporate Communications and GAP, Dileep Mudadeniya, said CBO was more than a ticketing platform, describing it as an ecosystem connecting entertainment, hospitality and tourism while creating opportunities for Sri Lanka to host world-class productions.

Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Sri Lanka General Manager and Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts Senior Vice President – Colombo Hotels Kamal Munasinghe said the initiative represented a new chapter for Colombo, where hospitality, entertainment, business and culture come together to create meaningful experiences for locals and international visitors.

CBO said it has already generated over Rs. 400 million in revenue and facilitated more than 10,000 ticket sales, reflecting the growing demand for premium entertainment experiences in Sri Lanka.
