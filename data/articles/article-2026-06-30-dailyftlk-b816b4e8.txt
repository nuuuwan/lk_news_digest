Sri Lanka joins EUROBIKE Frankfurt 2026

The world’s leading trade fair for bicycles and e-mobility, EUROBIKE 2026, concluded successfully at Messe Frankfurt from 24–27 June 2026, bringing together industry leaders, manufacturers, buyers, and cycling enthusiasts from around the world.

Frankfurt hosted the 34th edition of EUROBIKE, welcoming the international B2B cycling community. Around 800 exhibitors from over 40 countries presented their latest products, technologies, and solutions across the cycling and mobility sectors.

Sri Lanka was represented by two exhibitors, Asiabika Industrial Ltd., and Samson Rubber Industries, showcasing their expertise in rubber products and manufacturing solutions for the international market.

Sri Lankan visitors and exhibitors actively engaged with international industry stakeholders at EUROBIKE 2026, creating opportunities to exchange knowledge, explore emerging trends, and strengthen global business connections.

EUROBIKE Frankfurt 2026 provided an important platform for networking, collaboration, and exploring future opportunities, bringing together companies and professionals from across the world to contribute to the growth and development of the global cycling and mobility industry.
