SLBFE portal opens today for 16,000 Israel job applicants

Colombo, Aug. 10(Daily Mirror) -The Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) will open its online registration portal today (Aug. 10) for Sri Lankans seeking employment in the restaurant and renovation sectors in Israel.

The portal will open at 12 noon for registration of 4,000 applicants for restaurant jobs. Male applicants aged 25 to 40 with at least one year of restaurant kitchen experience can apply.

Applicants must have completed at least eight years of schooling, possess good English communication skills, and be physically and mentally fit to perform tasks such as lifting heavy loads, standing for long periods and washing kitchen utensils. They must also have no food or cleaning-product allergies.

The portal will open at 2 p.m. for the renovation sector, with registrations for 12,000 applicants across four categories: putty and gypsum work, sunk box and block stone construction, plastering, and ceramic tiling.

Applicants for renovation jobs must be aged 25 to 45, have the required professional qualifications, completed at least 10 years of schooling and possess basic English knowledge.

In both sectors, applicants must not have previously worked in Israel, while their parents, spouse and children should also not be working there.

Those who previously applied for renovation-sector jobs but failed the final test will not be eligible to apply again. However, applicants who previously applied for jobs in other Israeli sectors and meet the qualifications for any of the four renovation categories may apply.

The SLBFE said applicants' professional skills will be assessed by qualified Israeli representatives and urged job seekers to apply only for positions for which they have the required experience and skills.
