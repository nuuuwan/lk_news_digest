50 low-floor buses added to Metro fleet

Colombo, Auguat 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved a proposal to procure an additional 50 comfortable low-floor buses to expand modern public transport services in urban areas, following the successful procurement of 100 such buses under the Public Transport System Modernisation Programme.

The relevant supplier has already imported 100 low-floor buses into the country under the programme, which aims to improve passenger transport facilities and introduce more efficient urban mobility solutions.

The government has identified the need to expand the Metro Bus Project beyond the Colombo Metropolitan Region, with the first phase planned for Kandy, Galle and Jaffna, considering the high passenger demand, strategic importance of these cities, and their role as major regional transport and tourism hubs.

Accordingly, the Transport, Highways and Urban Development Ministry proposed increasing the Metro Bus fleet by procuring an additional 50 buses as a 50 per cent re-order under government procurement guidelines, based on the contract awarded on March 6, 2026 for the purchase of the initial 100 buses.

The Cabinet approved the proposal to proceed with the additional procurement, enabling the expansion of comfortable and accessible public transport services in key urban centres.
