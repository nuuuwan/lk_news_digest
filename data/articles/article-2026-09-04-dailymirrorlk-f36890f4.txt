Suspected remains of missing Fast Attack Craft officer recovered

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has recovered remains suspected to be those of the officer who went missing following the maritime incident involving Fast Attack Craft P 4447 on August 15.

The Sri Lanka Navy Fast Attack Craft ran into distress and sank about three nautical miles off the Angulana coast, prompting a swift rescue operation that saved 11 of the 12 sailors on board

Following the incident, the Navy conducted an extensive search for the missing officer across surrounding sea areas and along the coastline from Panadura through Colombo to Chilaw. The Navy also coordinated with the Sri Lanka Air Force, Angulana Police, local fishing communities and other relevant agencies.

Naval divers recovered the remains from the vessel this morning (September 4), after underwater conditions permitted specialized diving operations to be carried out safely.

The Navy said the recovery of the remains marks the conclusion of the search and recovery phase. Postmortem, identification and other required procedures will now be carried out.

An investigation is continuing into the circumstances surrounding the incident.

The Navy said reports from the rescued personnel about a possible collision with a submerged object in the area are also being examined. However, no conclusion has been reached on the cause of the incident, pending completion of the investigation.
