Gotabaya’s writ petition fixed for order

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal today fixed September 22 to deliver its order on the writ petition filed by former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa, seeking an order preventing his arrest in connection with ongoing investigations into the 2019 Easter Sunday terror attacks.

Accordingly, the court will rule on September 22 whether to proceed with the petition or dismiss it at the threshold.

Counsel for both the petitioner and the respondents concluded their oral submissions when the petition was taken up before a two-judge bench comprising Court of Appeal President Justice Rohantha Abeysuriya and Justice Sarath Dissanayake.

In the petition, Rajapaksa has cited Inspector General of Police Priyantha Weerasooriya, Criminal Investigation Department (CID) Director Shani Abeysekera, the Officer-in-Charge of the CID’s Special Investigations Unit and the Attorney General as respondents.
