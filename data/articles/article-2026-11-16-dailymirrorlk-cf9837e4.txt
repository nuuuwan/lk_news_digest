Balangoda Kassapa Thero remanded until tomorrow

Trincomalee, Nov 16 (Daily Mirror) - Balangoda Kassapa Thero was remanded until tomorrow (17) after being produced before the Trincomalee Magistrate’s Court today (16) over his failure to appear at a previous hearing in a case involving the alleged unauthorised installation of a Buddha statue on the Trincomalee beachfront and several other charges.

The court had issued an arrest warrant for the Thero after he failed to appear when the case was taken up on November 11.

When he was produced before Trincomalee Chief Magistrate M.S.M. Samsudeen, no lawyer appeared on his behalf. The Thero personally addressed the court and explained the reasons for his absence.

He told the court that protecting Buddhism and safeguarding the continuity of the Buddhasasana were responsibilities shared by the Government and Buddhist monks.

He further argued that his absence was justified under universal law, claiming that he had embarked on a life-threatening hunger strike in support of that cause.

However, the Magistrate ordered that he be remanded until tomorrow and directed that he be produced before the court again.
