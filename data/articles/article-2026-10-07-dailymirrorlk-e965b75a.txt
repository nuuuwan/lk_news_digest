Kananathan condoles with Nigeria over Air Force tragedy

Former Ambassador to Nigeria, Amb. Kana Kananathan, has expressed his profound sorrow over the tragic Nigerian Air Force aircraft crash in Ondo State, which reportedly claimed the lives of 25 passengers and seven crew members.

Kananathan spoke yesterday with senior officials of the Nigerian Air Force and conveyed a message of condolence to the Government and people of Nigeria, the Armed Forces and the bereaved families.

“This is a heartbreaking tragedy and a painful moment for Nigeria. I extend my deepest condolences to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, the Nigerian Air Force and, above all, the families who have lost their loved ones,” Kananathan said.

Having served as Sri Lanka’s Ambassador accredited to Nigeria, Kananathan said the country and its people remained close to his heart.

“I stand in solidarity with my Nigerian brothers and sisters during this period of national mourning. May the departed souls rest in eternal peace, and may their families find strength and comfort at this difficult time,” he added.
