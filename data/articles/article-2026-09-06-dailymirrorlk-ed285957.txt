Nalinda Jayatissa heads to Timor-Leste for 79th WHO Regional Committee Session

Colombo, Sep. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Health Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa will leave for Timor-Leste today to attend the 79th session of the WHO Regional Committee for South-East Asia in Dili, the Health Ministry said

The Sri Lankan delegation includes Ministry Secretary Dr. Anil Jasinghe, Deputy Director-General of Health Services (Planning) Dr. S. Sridharan, Deputy Director-General of Health Services (Public Health Services) Dr. S. M. Arnold, Senior Professor of Genetics and Biomedical Informatics Vajira H. W. Dissanayake, and Director of Planning at the Health Ministry Dr. Janaki Vidanapathirana.

The World Health Organization (WHO) will convene the 79th session of its Regional Committee for South-East Asia in Dili, Timor-Leste, from September 7 to 10, 2026 with the participation of WHO Director-General Dr. Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus.

Health ministers, senior health officials, WHO representatives and other delegates from Member States will participate in the four-day meeting.

Dr. Jayatissa, who was elected Chairperson of the Regional Committee at its 78th session held in Colombo in 2025, will continue to serve in the position until a successor is elected.
