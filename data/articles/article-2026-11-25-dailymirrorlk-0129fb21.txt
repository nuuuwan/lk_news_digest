Seven Sri Lankans register for IPL 2027 auction

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Seven Sri Lankan cricketers have registered for the IPL 2027 player auction, with fast bowler Matheesha Pathirana and all-rounder Wanindu Hasaranga featuring in the highest base price bracket of INR 2 crore.

Pathirana and Hasaranga are among 30 players registered in the top bracket, which includes two Indians and 28 overseas players.

Five other Sri Lankans – Charith Asalanka, Dushmantha Chameera, Kusal Mendis, Maheesh Theekshana and Sonal Dinusha – have registered in the INR 1 crore base price category.

The initial auction register, shared with franchises on Tuesday evening, features a total of 1,220 players, including 939 Indians and 281 overseas cricketers. The list will be further trimmed before the final auction.

Among the prominent international names registered is England all-rounder Ben Stokes, who has entered the December 16 mini-auction in Goa with a base price of INR 2 crore.

Stokes, who retired from international cricket earlier this year, has represented Rising Pune Supergiant, Rajasthan Royals and Chennai Super Kings in the IPL. He has played 45 matches across five seasons, scoring 935 runs and taking 28 wickets.

Meanwhile, 12 Bangladesh players have also registered for the auction, although prominent names such as Mustafizur Rahman and Shakib Al Hasan are absent from the initial list.

The Bangladesh players registered are Hasan Mahmud, Parvez Hossain Emon, Ripon Mondol, Rishad Hossain, Saif Hassan, Shak Mahedi Hasan, Shamim Hossain, Shoriful Islam, Tanzim Hasan Sakib, Towhid Hridoy, Md Rubel and Taskin Ahmed.

The presence of Bangladesh players comes amid last year's controversy involving Mustafizur Rahman, who was released by Kolkata Knight Riders following an instruction from the BCCI amid geopolitical tensions between India and Bangladesh.

Of the 1,220 players in the initial register, 184 are capped Indian players, 170 are capped overseas players and 42 represent Associate nations. The list also includes 127 uncapped Indian players who featured in the previous IPL season, 628 other uncapped Indian players and 69 uncapped overseas players.

The BCCI has asked franchises to submit requests for additional players not included in the initial register by 5.00 pm IST on November 27. Franchises must also submit the names of players they wish to include in the auction by December 1.
