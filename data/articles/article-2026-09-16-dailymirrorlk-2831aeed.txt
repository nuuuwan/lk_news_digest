Indices close in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Melstacorp and Commercial Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.2bn.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Lanka Milk Foods, Ceylon Cold Stores and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Bank, Sierra Cables and Access Engineering whilst retail interest was noted in Cable Solutions, Cargills Bank and R I L Property.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Lanka Milk Foods and Ceylon Cold Stores) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.10%. The share price of Lanka Milk Foods decreased by Rs. 0.80 (0.93%) to close at Rs. 85.20. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores moved up by Rs. 0.75 (0.63%) to close at Rs. 119.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings and Sierra Cables) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.05%. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs. 19.30. The share price of Sierra Cables declined by Rs. 0.40 (1.13%) to close at Rs. 35.00. Commercial Bank was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Commercial Bank recorded a loss of Rs. 1.00 (0.49%) to close at Rs. 204.00.
