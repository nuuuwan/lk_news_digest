Dull Friday at CSE, hopes shift to next week

Trading on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) ended on a dull note Friday, with investors adopting a cautious stance amid mixed sentiment. Market analysts attributed the lacklustre session to investors pinning their hopes on the coming week, when positive quarterly results are expected from the banking sector and select counters.

Amid those developments both indices indicated mixed reactions. All Share Price Index down by 100.42 points while S and P SL20 up by 27.01 points.

Turnover stood at Rs 1.89 billion with three crossings. Those crossings were reported in CCS 300,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 38 million and its share price traded at Rs 127, CT Holdings 43000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 22 million and its share price traded at Rs 508 and Commercial Credit Credit and Finance 200,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 21.70 million and its share price traded at Rs 108.50.

In the retail market top seven companies that have mainly contributed to the turnover were Cargills Bank Rs 476 million (69 million shares traded), Commercial Credit and Finance Rs 155 million (1.4 million shares traded), Sierra Cables Rs 116 million (3.7 million shares traded), Singer (SriLanka) Rs 77 million (one million shares traded), JKH Rs 50 million (2.5 million shares traded) and Melstcope Rs 48 million (250,000 shares traded). During the day 122 million shares volumes changed hands in 15076 transactions.

It is said that banking and financial sector performed well especially Commercial Credit and Finance. During the day banking sector especially Cargills Bank also performed significantly well. Manufacturing sector especially JKH also moderately traded at the floor.

BPPL Holdings resolved to declare a First Interim Dividend of 0.25 rupees per share for the financial year ending March 31, 2027, with the ex-dividend date set for August 18, 2026.

Meanwhile, Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka &amp; Finance reported the subscription details for its Rights Issue of Preference Shares, receiving total subscriptions for 80,410,925 shares valued at Rs 804.1 million out of the offered 99,912,312 shares.

Yesterday the Central Bank announced the US Dollar rate as against rupee. The rupee was quoted at Rs  335.48/55 to the US dollar in the spot market from Rs 335.55/80 the previous day, while bond yields dipped on the shorter tenors, dealers said.
