Sri Lanka, UAE discuss Trincomalee regional energy hub

Colombo, Sep. 24 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka and the United Arab Emirates have discussed the ongoing UAE-India-Sri Lanka tripartite initiative to develop Trincomalee as a regional energy hub, during a meeting between UAE Ambassador to Sri Lanka Khaled Nasser Al Ameri and Deputy Speaker of Parliament Dr. Rizvie Salih.

The Ambassador paid a courtesy call on the Deputy Speaker at Parliament on September 23, with the discussions focusing on further strengthening bilateral relations and cooperation between the two countries.

The Deputy Speaker highlighted the longstanding and cordial relations between Sri Lanka and the UAE and expressed appreciation for the UAE’s support to Sri Lanka, particularly the economic contribution of the large Sri Lankan workforce employed in the UAE.

He also welcomed the increasing number of tourists travelling from the UAE to Sri Lanka and stressed the importance of further strengthening cooperation in the tourism sector.

The Deputy Speaker said the Sri Lanka-UAE Parliamentary Friendship Association is expected to be revived in the Tenth Parliament, with the aim of strengthening parliamentary relations and expanding cooperation across various sectors.

Ambassador Al Ameri praised the contribution of Sri Lankan workers to the economies of both countries, noting that Sri Lankan workers in the UAE are highly valued for being skilled, peaceful and respectful.

He also highlighted the strong trade relations between Sri Lanka and the UAE and stressed the importance of maintaining strong air connectivity between the two countries.

The discussions further focused on enhancing cooperation in education and higher education, as well as opportunities to strengthen cooperation in security.

The Ambassador also commended Sri Lanka’s natural beauty, cultural richness and the hospitality of its people, and reaffirmed the UAE’s continued support for Sri Lanka as a close and trusted friend.

Secretary General of Parliament Kushani Rohanadeera was also present at the meeting.
