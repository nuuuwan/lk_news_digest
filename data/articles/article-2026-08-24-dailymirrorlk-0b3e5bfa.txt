Top Oman diplomat due in Tehran for talks on Hormuz, Iran says

TIMES OF ISRAEL - Oman’s Foreign Minister Sayyid Badr Albusaidi will visit Tehran on Tuesday to continue talks between Iran and Oman on the Strait of Hormuz, Iran’s Foreign Ministry Spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei says.

On Sunday, Baghaei said the army chief of Pakistan, which has mediated between the United States and Iran, would visit Tehran Monday.
