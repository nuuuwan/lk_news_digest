Tourist arrivals contract in August despite crossing 1.5mn arrival milestone during month

Sri Lanka welcomed 191,704 tourists in August 2026, marking a 3.3 percent decrease compared to the same month last year.

The August figure also reflects a 2.6 percent month-on-month drop from the 196,845 arrivals recorded in July, a month that similarly experienced a 1.7 percent year-on-year (YoY) contraction.

Despite the recent monthly moderation in growth, the cumulative tourist arrivals for the first eight months of 2026 successfully crossed the 1.5 million milestone, reaching 1,535,122.

India continued to dominate as the top source market in August, accounting for a 25.0 percent share, with 47,253 arrivals. The United Kingdom held steady in the second place, bringing in 19,588 tourists for a 10.0 percent share.

China climbed to the third place, supplying 12,321 visitors and capturing 6.0 percent of the market, displacing Germany, which dropped to the fourth, with 11,405 arrivals, also representing a 6.0 percent share.

France rounded out the top five, with 10,885 visitors and a 6.0 percent share. Further down the rankings, the Netherlands, which secured the ninth spot, with 6,082 tourists in August 2025, completely fell out of the top 10 source markets in August 2026.

An analysis of the YoY market performance reveals a distinct softening across several key European source destinations. Italy experienced the steepest drop, plummeting 27.5 percent to 8,883 arrivals, while Spain saw a significant contraction of 16.0 percent to record 7,431 tourists.

Germany reported an 8.8 percent decline and the United Kingdom posted a marginal 0.9 percent dip. Consequently, the daily average arrivals for August stood at 6,184, reflecting a minor contraction in the late-summer travel season, compared to the 6,395 daily average documented in August 2025.

Despite these fluctuations, driven largely by external factors, the authorities remain focused on value-added tourism, guided by the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority leadership’s shift toward a scientific model based on Tourism Satellite Accounting surveys, to prioritise the definitive industry objectives of achieving five million arrivals and generating US $ 10 billion in tourism income.
