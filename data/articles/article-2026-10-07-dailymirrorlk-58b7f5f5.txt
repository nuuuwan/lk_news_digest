SLFFA elects new leadership at 45th AGM

The Sri Lanka Logistics and Freight Forwarders’ Association (SLFFA) held its 45th Annual General Meeting (AGM) at Cinnamon Grand Hotel in Colombo, electing new office bearers for the 2026/2027 term.

The event was graced by Ports and Civil Aviation Minister Anura Karunathilaka as the chief guest, alongside Ports and Civil Aviation Ministry Secretary W.W.S. Mangala as the guest of honour.

At the meeting, MAC Supply Chain Solutions (Pvt.) Ltd Managing Director Andre Fernando was elected as Chairman of the SLFFA for 2026/2027.

Joining the executive leadership team are Foreway Logistics Managing Director Shavindra Dias as Vice Chairman and Scanwell Logistics Colombo Director Priyan Seilman as Treasurer.

The newly elected Executive Committee features representatives from leading logistics enterprises: Aitken Spence Cargo (Pvt.) Ltd, David Pieris Logistics (Pvt.) Ltd, Expolanka Freight (Pvt.) Ltd, Famous Pacific Shipping Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd, Freight Links International (Pte) Ltd, GAC Logistics (Pvt.) Ltd, Hellmann Worldwide Logistics (Pvt.) Ltd, Salota International (Pvt.) Ltd, Shermans Logistics (Pvt.) Ltd and Speedmark Transportation Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd.

The committee reaffirmed its commitment to advancing industry collaboration and strengthening Sri Lanka’s logistics and freight forwarding sector.

Addressing the gathering, the association outlined a strategic roadmap aimed at ‘fast-tracking Colombo to be the next cargo hub’ by expanding beyond traditional transshipment into a fully integrated regional logistics hub. The proposed initiatives include developing sea-air multimodal bonded corridors with 24-to-48-hour connections to compete effectively with the established international hubs.

To support the growing trade volume, the SLFFA highlighted key infrastructure developments at Bandaranaike International Airport as well as the East and West Container Terminals at the Port of Colombo.

Additionally, the SLFFA Cargo Services has submitted a proposal to invest in the LCL cargo and multi-country consolidation facilities under a private-sector investment initiative led by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority.

Over the past year, the SLFFA achieved notable milestones in trade digitalisation and financial governance:

Digital infrastructure: Launched ASYHUB in collaboration with Sri Lanka Customs to accelerate processing and info-sharing.

Paperless trade and training: Implemented digital delivery order validations via the NAVIS system, trained over 650 industry professionals and submitted a digital transformation report to the government policymakers advocating for electronic bills of lading.

Member protection: Successfully defended member interests regarding the Cargo Account Settlement System (CASS) and launched a centralised SLFFA Debtor Database to promote disciplined credit management across its membership of over 180 companies.

The association reiterated the importance of continuous workforce development through its dedicated training division, the Academy of International Trade and Transport (AITT).

Beyond the business initiatives, the SLFFA highlighted its ongoing social responsibility contributions, which include supporting paediatric operating facilities at Lady Ridgeway Hospital, assisting the Arthur Perera Elders’ Home and donating Rs.3.2 million to the Rebuild Sri Lanka initiative.

The newly elected leadership and executive committee will continue to work towards strengthening the freight forwarding and logistics industry in Sri Lanka, promoting industry collaboration and advancing the interests of the SLFFA members.
