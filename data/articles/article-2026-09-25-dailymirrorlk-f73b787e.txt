LB CIM-Thyaga bring digital gifting services

As the flagship digital platform of LB Finance PLC, LB CIM continues to evolve beyond traditional digital banking by offering the customers access to a comprehensive ecosystem of financial and lifestyle services through a single mobile application.

It entered into a strategic partnership with Thyaga, Sri Lanka’s premier digital e-gifting voucher platform, to offer digital gifting services directly through the LB CIM app.

The partnership was officially launched at a signing ceremony held at LB Finance corporate office, marking another milestone in LB CIM’s journey to evolve beyond traditional financial services into a comprehensive lifestyle ecosystem.

Through this collaboration, the LB CIM users can seamlessly send personalised digital gift vouchers through the app by entering any preferred gift amount for their friends and loved ones. The recipients can then redeem the gifted value across Thyaga’s network of over 200 partner merchants islandwide, choosing from leading brands spanning fashion, dining, supermarkets, lifestyle and everyday essentials.

The integration delivers a convenient and flexible digital gifting experience while empowering the recipients with the freedom to choose how and where they use their gift. Through this partnership, both organisations reinforce their shared commitment to leveraging technology to enhance everyday life for people across Sri Lanka.

LB Finance Executive Director Ravi Tissera stated, “At LB CIM, we are continuously reimagining how digital finance can integrate with daily lifestyle needs. Partnering with Thyaga enables us to extend our ecosystem beyond financial services and deliver real value through improved access to digital gifting.”

LB Finance Digital Business Head Janaka Ekanayake added, “This collaboration reinforces our focus on innovation driven by customer convenience. By embedding digital gifting into LB CIM, we are making essential lifestyle services more accessible through a single, trusted digital platform.”

Thyaga co-founder and CEO Sachin Wickramasinghe commented, “We are pleased to partner with LB CIM to broaden access to digital gifting solutions. Together, we are simplifying how people connect and share gifts across Sri Lanka, while offering a smooth and secure user experience.”

The digital gifting feature is now live on the LB CIM app. The initiative strengthens LB CIM’s positioning as a next-generation digital platform that integrates finance, lifestyle and essential services into one seamless experience.
