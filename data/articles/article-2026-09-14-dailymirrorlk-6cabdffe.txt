Two university lifters make history with six medals from World Championship

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Two Sri Lankan university students who made history by becoming the first from the country to participate in an international university weightlifting competition returned to the country early today after securing a total of six medals at the World University Weightlifting Championship held in Doha, Qatar.

The duo, who won three gold medals and three bronze medals at the championship, arrived at the Bandaranaike International Airport at 2.05 a.m. today (Sept. 14) on Qatar Airways flight QR 662 from Doha.

The championship was held in Qatar with the participation of university weightlifters from 50 countries.

L.G.A.S. Liyanagedara, a fourth-year student of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, won three gold medals in the 60kg weight category. He secured gold medals in the snatch, clean and jerk, and overall total weightlifting events.

Liyanagedara, who is a resident of Polonnaruwa, made history through his performance at the international university-level competition.

Meanwhile, T.M.N.S. Dissanayake, a first-year student of Sabaragamuwa University, secured three bronze medals in the 65kg weight category.

Dissanayake won bronze medals in the snatch, clean and jerk, and overall total weightlifting events. He is a resident of Galgamuwa.

The achievements mark a significant milestone for Sri Lankan university weightlifting, with the two athletes bringing home six medals from a competition featuring athletes from 50 countries.
