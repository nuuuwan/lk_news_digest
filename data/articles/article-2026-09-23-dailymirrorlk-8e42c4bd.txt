Correction

In the story titled “Prime lending rate edges up despite cuts by most banks”, published on Monday, several bank-wise prime lending rates were incorrectly stated. The correct figures are as follows:

Pan Asia Banking Corporation recorded the steepest reduction of 79 basis points to 11.76 percent, followed by Union Bank, which cut 66 basis points to 13.23 percent, and People’s Bank, which reduced its rate by 59 basis points to 10.83 percent. NDB Bank and Cargills Bank lowered their rates to 11.79 percent and 12.76 percent, while Hatton National Bank, Nations Trust Bank and DFCC Bank reduced theirs to 10.78 percent, 10.93 percent and 11.53 percent respectively. Sampath Bank, Seylan Bank and Commercial Bank raised their rates to 11.74 percent, 11.40 percent and 11.19 percent. Union Bank and Amana Bank recorded the highest prime rate in the market at 13.23 percent, and Hatton National Bank the lowest domestic rate.

Among foreign banks, Public Bank lowered its rate by 76 basis points to 11.63 percent and State Bank of India by 67 basis points to 10.46 percent, while HSBC raised its rate to 10.49 percent and Habib Bank reduced its rate to 10.88 percent. Indian Bank recorded the highest foreign bank rate at 13.06 percent, and Standard Chartered the lowest among lenders at 9.65 percent.
