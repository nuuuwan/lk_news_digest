WISTA Sri Lanka reappoints Chamila Bandara as President

The Women in Shipping and Trading Association (WISTA) Sri Lanka successfully held its 11th Annual General Meeting recently at The Kingsbury Hotel.

Sri Lanka Ports Authority Chairman Dr. Parakrama Dissanayake graced the event as the chief guest.

The highlight of the evening was the presentation of the prestigious WISTA Sri Lanka Personality of the Year Award to Worldlink Shipping Colombo (Pvt.) Ltd Director Marketing Swabha Wickremesinghe, in recognition of her outstanding contributions made to the maritime and logistics sectors.

Chamila Bandara was unanimously reappointed as President of WISTA Sri Lanka for another term, reflecting on the confidence and trust placed in her leadership by the membership.

She serves as a Member of the Group Management Committee of Hayleys Advantis Limited and Director of Advantis Express licensee of FedEx and IML Delivery Systems.

Meridian Maritime Services Director Operations Maleena Awn was re-elected as Vice President, Centrum Marine Consultancy Director Patali Karunarathne was re-elected as Secretary, Hayleys Advantis General Manager Assurance and Insurance Shiyamalee Karunanayake was re-elected as Treasurer, GAC Marine Services Manager Operations Felicita Alwis was elected as Assistant Secretary and McLarens Lubricants Director Finance Hannelore Baldsing was re-elected as Assistant Treasurer.

The newly elected Executive Committee consists of Shermans Logistics Director/CEO Diruni Chanmugam, SALOTA International President/Director Nilu Mediwake Wickremasinghe, Regency Teas General Manager Operations Amali Mudunkotuwa Mendis, Sri Lanka Ports Authority Chief Law Officer Aparna Thilakarathne, South Asia Gateway Terminals Manager Projects Kalani Dassanayake, Prudential Shipping Lines General Manager NVOCC Surangi Gunasekara, South Asia Gateway Terminals General Manager  Commercial and Marketing Udara Cumaratunga, Hayleys Energy Services Lanka Deputy General Manager Aruni Wijepala, Mercmarine Group of Companies Assistant Manager Crewing and Compliance Niroshini D.A.D.Y., Spectra Logistics Senior Manager Business Development Niroshini De Silva, Hayleys Advantis Group Head of Human Resources Imali Jayathilake, Biselko International Deputy General Manager  Business Development Rasika Premasiri, Pership Synergy Manager Freight Operations Ruchika Rajaratne, Deep Ocean Managing Director Deepika Wijesuriya, Ceymed Healthcare Services Assistant Manager Dilki Bartholomeusz and Hellmann Worldwide Logistics Executive Administration and Administrative Support Maria Douglas.
