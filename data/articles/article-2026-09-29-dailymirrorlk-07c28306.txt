Massive Cigarette haul destroyed

Sri Lanka Customs today (Sept. 29) destroyed a massive consignment of cigarettes worth approximately Rs. 269.82 million, which had been falsely declared as raw cashew nuts in cargo from Dubai and was allegedly intended for re-export to Australia. Following a tip-off, Customs officers inspected the consignment and discovered 1,798,800 cigarettes concealed in 8,994 cartons. After the completion of legal proceedings and investigations, the confiscated cigarettes were destroyed in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Ordinance at the Kerawalapitiya waste management facility in Wattala, in the presence of Customs Spokesman Chandana Punchihewa and officers of the Customs Revenue Task Force.
