Hilton partners with SampathCards and Visa for Oktoberfest in Sri Lanka

ECONOMYNEXT – The Hilton Colombo is hosting the 33rd edition of its annual Oktoberfest in Sri Lanka from October 29 to November 7, in partnership with SampathCards and Visa.

The 10-day cultural festival features authentic Bavarian food, traditional entertainment from Munich’s Oompah Bavarian Band, and exclusive ticket discounts of up to 15 percent for Sampath Cardholders.

The celebration of Bavarian culture this year brings the festive energy of the original Oompah Bavarian Band from Munich, traditional servers in authentic German attire, and an array of cultural highlights.

The 2026 edition will continue that tradition through Bavarian-themed buffet, live entertainment, games, activations and the vibrant atmosphere that has made Oktoberfest a much-anticipated annual occasion.

“At Sampath Bank, we believe the value of a card extends beyond the transaction. It is also about the experiences, moments and opportunities it brings to our customers. Our partnership with Hilton Colombo and Visa allows us to bring that philosophy to life through Oktoberfest, giving Sampath Cardholders added value as they enjoy one of Colombo’s most anticipated celebrations,” Darshin Pathinayake, Chief Business Intelligence Officer, Sampath Bank PLC, said.

“Oktoberfest at Hilton Colombo is a celebration that has become part of the fabric of Colombo’s social calendar, and we are delighted to welcome it back for its 33 rd year. Our continued partnership with SampathCards, together with Visa, enables us to create an experience that combines the authenticity of Oktoberfest with the level of Hilton hospitality and value our guests have come to expect. We look forward to welcoming our guests to another memorable celebration this year,” Kapila Mohotti, Director – Food & Beverage at Hilton Colombo, said.

“Year after year, Oktoberfest has become a much-loved tradition in Colombo, and we are proud to have been part of this journey. At Visa, we believe in turning everyday moments into extraordinary experiences and partnerships like this strengthen that promise,” Avanthi Colombage, Country Manager, Sri Lanka and Maldives, Visa, said.
