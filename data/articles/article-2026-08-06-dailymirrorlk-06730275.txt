TRCSL orders blocking of 146 illegal online gambling websites

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL) has instructed all telecommunications service providers to block access to 146 unlicensed online gambling websites in Sri Lanka in a move aimed at curbing illegal online gambling activities.

TRCSL Director General Bandula Herath said all telecommunications service providers have been directed to restrict access to the identified websites from within Sri Lanka.

The directive was issued following a notification from the Gaming Regulatory Authority, which operates under the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, in accordance with the provisions of the Gaming Regulatory Authority Act, No. 17 of 2025.

According to the Gaming Regulatory Authority, operating or promoting online gambling services in Sri Lanka without a valid licence is an offence under Sections 15, 16 and 18 of the Act and is punishable under Section 44.

The authorities said the latest measure is intended to strengthen enforcement against illegal online gambling operations and ensure compliance with the country's regulatory framework.
