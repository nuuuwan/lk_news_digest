Secondary market activity stalls ahead of T-bond auction

The secondary market opened trading for the week on a dull note as the investors broadly remained on the sidelines in anticipation of today’s T-bond auction.

On that note, trades were minimal, amidst thin volumes. Among the trades that were executed, the 15.10.2030 bond saw some activity and was recorded trading at a yield of 11.38 percent, followed by the 01.12.2031 maturity, which transacted at 11.60 percent.

Moving along towards the belly, the 15.10.2034 bond traded from 12.15 percent to 12.20 percent.

The PDMO is scheduled to hold a T-bond auction today, October 13, 2026, at which a sum of Rs.100.0 billion is expected to be raised via the 15.09.2029, 01.12.2031 and 15.08.2036 maturities.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee marginally appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.85/US dollar, compared to Rs.330.96/US dollar seen earlier.  Market liquidity in the banking system slightly contracted to Rs.398.95 billion, compared to Rs.399.14 billion recorded previously.
