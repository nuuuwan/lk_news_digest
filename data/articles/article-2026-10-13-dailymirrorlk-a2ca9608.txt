Zahira ’98 holds first badminton tournament

The Zahira College Batch of ’98 held its inaugural badminton tournament for old boys on October 3 at the St. Thomas’ College Sports Complex, with 24 teams participating. Colombo Badminton Club won both the Masters and Open categories. Shazly Oowise featured in both winning teams, partnering Rizwan Thahir in the Masters and Reezan Seedin in the Open category. The champions received Rs. 15,000 each, along with trophies, medals and certificates, while the runners-up received Rs. 10,000 each and awards. Pix by Waruna Wanniarachchi
