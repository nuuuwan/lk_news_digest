‘Hasthi Sena’ launched to protect elephants, tackle human-elephant conflict

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Government today (2) launched the ‘Hasthi Sena’ (Elephant Force) operation in Galkiriyagama, under the North-Western Wildlife Zone, to protect elephants and minimise human-elephant conflict.

The islandwide operation is being jointly implemented by the Ministry of Environment, the Armed Forces and the Department of Wildlife Conservation.

Speaking at the launch, the Environment Minister said the initial phase would focus on protecting 17 elephants currently facing threats.

Plans are also underway to cover around 80 per cent of areas inhabited by elephants through the operation, while the remaining 20 per cent will be addressed through a separate initiative, the Minister said.

The operation will also involve continuous raids and legal action against the use of illegal firearms, hakka-patas (improvised explosive devices), unauthorised electric fences and snares, which pose serious threats to elephants.

A total of 60 military personnel have been deployed for the operation, with 10 operational teams organised into six groups.

The operation will be conducted islandwide under the direct supervision of Army Commander Lieutenant General Nilantha Premaratne, with the participation of relevant government institutions and security forces.

Several senior military officers and wildlife officials attended the launch, including North-Western Wildlife Director Pushpantha Wimaladasa, Ministry of Environment adviser Ravindra Kariyawasam, Major General Kaminda Silva, Brigadier Basnayake and Lieutenant Colonel Abeywickrama.

The authorities said the initiative would focus on scientific and planned measures to safeguard elephant populations while reducing threats arising from human-elephant conflict.
