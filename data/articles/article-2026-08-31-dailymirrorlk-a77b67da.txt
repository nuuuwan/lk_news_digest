Maithripala Sirisena honoured as ‘President of the Happiest Era’

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - Former President Maithripala Sirisena has been honoured with the title ‘President of the Happiest Era’ at a special international awards ceremony held at Temple Trees in Colombo.

Sirisena attended the ceremony as the chief guest, while Indian actor Garuda Ram, known for his role in the KGF film series, was among the international guests.

The ceremony, held on August 29 as part of the Global Brand Awards 2026 and SHE FOR THE NATION – Season 2, brought together business leaders, entrepreneurs, professionals, women leaders and representatives of international brands from Sri Lanka and overseas.
