CBSL declares TM App among latest prohibited pyramid schemes

The Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) has expanded its crackdown on illegal pyramid schemes, determining that TM App has engaged in, conducted and promoted a prohibited scheme under the Banking Act.

The monetary watchdog yesterday published an updated list of 25 companies and applications found to be operating such schemes.

The Financial Consumer Relations Department said the determination was made under Section 83(C) of the Banking Act, No. 30 of 1988, as amended.

The latest notice, issued yesterday, identified TM App as a prohibited scheme and reiterated that a further 24 companies, mobile applications and affiliated entities had also been ascertained and determined to be operating prohibited pyramid schemes.

The entities include Tiens Lanka Health Care (Pvt.) Ltd, Best Life International (Pvt.) Ltd, Mark-Wo International (Pvt.) Ltd, VML International (Pvt.) Ltd, Global Lifestyle Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd, Fast3Cycle International (Pvt.) Ltd, Sport Chain App and Sport Chain ZS Society Sri Lanka, OnmaxDT, MTFE App and its affiliated groups, Fastwin (Pvt.) Ltd, Fruugo Oline App and Fruugo Oline (Pvt.) Ltd, Ride to Three Freedom (Pvt.) Ltd, Qnet/Questnet, Era Miracle (Pvt.) Ltd and Genesis Business School, Ledger Block, Isimaga International (Pvt.) Ltd, Beecoin App and Sunbird Foundation, Windex Trading, The Enrich Life (Pvt.) Ltd, Smart Win Entrepreneur (Pvt.) Ltd, Net Fore International (Pvt.) Ltd/Netrrix, Pro Care (Pvt.) Ltd and Shade of Procare (Pvt.) Ltd, SGO/sgomine.com, I.C.A.N. Advertising (Pvt.) Ltd and its affiliated websites and Infinity Rover (Pvt.) Ltd.

The CBSL has repeatedly warned the public against investing in or promoting pyramid schemes, noting that such operations are prohibited under the Banking Act. It has urged the public to exercise caution and verify the legitimacy of the investment opportunities before committing funds.
