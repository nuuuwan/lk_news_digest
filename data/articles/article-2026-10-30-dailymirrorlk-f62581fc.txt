Ruling party, Opposition in for battle today to seal fate of NCM against Speaker

Colombo, October 30 (Daily Mirror)-  A stormy party leaders meeting is expected today as the ruling party and the Opposition are in for a battle to seal the fate of the no confidence motion (NCM) against Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne.

Chief Opposition Whip Gayantha Karunathilaka told Daily Mirror that the Opposition will inform the ruling party that they are not prepared to dilute the motion as requested by the Opposition. “We will inform our stand at today’s party leaders meeting. We will tell them that we will stick with the original meeting and will not be amending the motion,” he said.

The ruling party is of the opinion that the no faith motion should be amended as certain matters in it will have a subjudice effect. It is of the opinion that a matter concerned with a court case cannot be debated in the House. The Opposition is of the opinion that the hearing of the case that has been filed against the Speaker is yet to begin and there is no subjudice effect as claimed by the ruling party.

As many as 50 allegations have been made against the Speaker which includes renting an apartment without using the official residence which is located close to the parliamentary complex violating the establishment code. A sum of Rs 760,000 is paid as rent for the apartment leased and feeding his personal secretary without making the due payments and partiality when handling House matters.

The motion is however included in the order paper for November 3, 2026 but says it will be a revised motion.

Meanwhile, it was reported that an inventory of the Speaker’s official residence which went missing a month ago still remains missing.
