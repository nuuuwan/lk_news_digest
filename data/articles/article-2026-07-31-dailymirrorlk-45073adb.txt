Auto Vision Motor Show 2026

Sri Lanka’s largest motor show, Auto Vision Motor Show 2026, was launched at the BMICH in Colombo on July 31. The three-day exhibition, which runs until August 2 from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. daily, features the latest supercars, SUVs, motorcycles, body kits and a wide range of vehicles imported to Sri Lanka, while offering visitors exclusive show discounts on vehicle purchases.
