Kusal Mendis ruled out of remaining LPL 2026 matches

Colombo, July 22 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Kaps captain Kusal Mendis has been ruled out of the remainder of the Lanka Premier League (LPL) 2026 after sustaining a hamstring injury.

Mendis suffered a right hamstring injury while attempting a run during the Colombo Kaps' match against the Kandy Royals at the SSC Ground in Colombo on July 19.

Following medical assessments, he has been withdrawn from the rest of the tournament and will return to the National High Performance Centre to begin his rehabilitation program.
