Pathum Nissanka ruled out of Pakistan Test series

Colombo, Oct 26 (Daily Mirror) - Pathum Nissanka will miss Sri Lanka’s upcoming two-match Test series against Pakistan as he continues to recover from a left hamstring injury.

The Cricket Selection Committee has named Lahiru Udara as Nissanka’s replacement for the series.

Udara is already in Pakistan as a member of Sri Lanka’s ODI squad and will join the Test squad as his replacement.
