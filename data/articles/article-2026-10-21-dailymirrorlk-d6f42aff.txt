’Mahason Pideniya’ ritual to ward off evil

The ‘Mahason Samayama’ ritualistic performance was held today at the site of the satyagraha demanding the release of Ven. Gnanasara Thera at Viharamahadevi Park Colombo last night. The event included a ‘Mahason Pideniya’ offering and symbolic rituals conducted by traditional exorcists from Matara under the guidance of Pitiduwe Siridhamma Thera, also known as Siri Samanthabhadra Thera.
