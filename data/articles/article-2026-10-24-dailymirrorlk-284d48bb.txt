Diplomatic corps marks International Diplomat Day in Colombo

Colombo, Oct 24 (Daily Mirror) - The Diplomatic Corps accredited to Sri Lanka commemorated International Diplomat Day 2026 at the Embassy of Indonesia in Colombo on Saturday (October 24), bringing together more than 150 participants representing over 21 diplomatic missions, the United Nations and Sri Lanka’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism.

Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath attended the event as the Chief Guest, which was also attended by diplomats, their family members and other distinguished guests.

Opening the event, Dean of the Diplomatic Corps and Indonesian Ambassador to Sri Lanka Dewi Gustina Tobing paid tribute to diplomats serving away from their home countries and highlighted the importance of international cooperation in addressing global challenges, including climate change, armed conflicts, inequality and rapid technological advancements.

She also announced a charitable initiative by the Diplomatic Corps to provide essential supplies to local communities in Sri Lanka, reflecting the diplomatic community’s commitment to social responsibility and goodwill.

United Nations Resident Coordinator in Sri Lanka Marc-André Franche, addressing the gathering, stressed the importance of the UN Charter, international law and a rules-based international order in maintaining global peace, security and cooperation. He said respect for international rules and national sovereignty was essential to preventing conflicts and building trust among nations.

In his address, Minister Vijitha Herath highlighted friendship, peace, mutual understanding and cooperation as key principles guiding relations among nations. He said Sri Lanka’s longstanding international relationships were built on shared values, cultural ties and a common commitment to democracy and the peaceful resolution of conflicts.

The Minister also commended ambassadors and their families for their contributions to strengthening bilateral relations and fostering closer ties between countries.

The celebration featured sporting and recreational activities, including mini soccer, badminton, volleyball, tennis, table tennis, chess and dominoes. Traditional Sri Lankan games, karaoke and activities for children were also organised to promote friendship and camaraderie among diplomats and their families.

The event concluded with the handover of donations, including food supplies, stationery and other essential items, through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for distribution to local communities.
