Maliban recognised at National Supply Chain Excellence Awards 2026

Maliban Biscuit Manufactories (Pvt.) Ltd received a Commendation Award under the category of Best Supply Chain Practicing Organisation 2026 – Extra Large Scale, Manufacturing, at National Supply Chain Excellence Awards 2026, held recently at Monarch Imperial.

The awards ceremony was organised by the Institute of Supply and Materials Management, Sri Lanka.

For Maliban, the Commendation Award reflects the importance placed on maintaining an efficient and integrated supply chain as part of its broader manufacturing and business operations.

As a long-established Sri Lankan manufacturer, the company’s ability to maintain consistency and efficiency across its operations is supported by supply chain practices focused on continuous improvement and operational effectiveness. As one of Sri Lanka’s leading biscuit manufacturers, with a legacy spanning more than seven decades, Maliban places strong emphasis on developing an integrated and resilient supply chain that supports business growth while ensuring product availability, service excellence and customer satisfaction.

The achievement is a testament to the collective dedication, expertise and commitment of the company’s supply chain professionals and all supporting teams, whose contributions play a vital role in maintaining operational efficiency and delivering value across the organisation.

Maliban remains committed to further enhancing its supply chain practices, embracing innovation and adopting industry best practices to strengthen its position in the manufacturing sector.

This recognition marks another significant milestone in Maliban’s ongoing journey towards supply chain excellence.
