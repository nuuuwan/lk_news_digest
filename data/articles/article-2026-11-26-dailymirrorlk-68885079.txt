No income? You could still face Rs. 400,000 fine for missing tax deadline

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Taxpayers who fail to file their income tax returns by November 30 could face penalties ranging from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 400,000, even if they have no income to declare, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) has warned.

According to IRD Media Spokesperson Nandana Kumara, November 30 is the deadline for submitting income tax returns for the 2025/2026 year of assessment, cautioning that those who miss the deadline could face penalties under existing tax laws.

He stressed that the obligation to file a return remains in place for individuals legally required to do so, regardless of whether they earned any income during the relevant year.

Failure to submit the required return could result in a minimum fine of Rs. 50,000, with penalties reaching as much as Rs. 400,000, he warned.

Meanwhile, Kumara said the IRD had issued yellow and red notices to registered taxpayers with outstanding income tax payments for the 2024/2025 year of assessment, as well as those liable to pay taxes for 2025/2026.

He urged taxpayers to fulfil their filing and payment obligations within the stipulated deadlines to avoid penalties and additional charges.

Addressing concerns over increased tax liabilities arising from cash-based transactions, Kumara explained that expenses relating to cash transactions covered by regulations introduced in August 2023 cannot be claimed as deductions when calculating taxable income.

However, he clarified that the regulations do not apply to individuals who purchased land before August 2023.
