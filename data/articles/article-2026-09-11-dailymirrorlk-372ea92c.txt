KDU to be converted into an international higher education institute

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Kotelawala Defence University is to be developed as an international higher education institute, Deputy Minister Aruna Jayasekera told Parliament today.

Responding to a question raised by MP Ajith P. Perera, the Deputy Minister said new courses such as national defense, and marine studies will be introduced soon while more focus will be on carrying research and other activities.
