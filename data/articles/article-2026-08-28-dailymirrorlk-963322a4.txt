Former Speaker denies vehicle, fuel misuse allegations

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Former Speaker Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena has strongly denied allegations concerning his use of vehicles and fuel during his tenure, claiming that findings of a special audit report are being distorted by certain groups to mislead the public.

In a statement, Abeywardena said the special audit conducted into the 8th and 9th Parliaments had identified several irregularities relating to fuel allocations, vehicle privileges and expenditure involving senior parliamentary positions, including the Speaker, Deputy Speaker, Chairman of Committees and Secretary-General of Parliament.

He said the audit report had confirmed that he used only vehicles officially provided by Parliament, despite earlier allegations that he had used nine vehicles.

According to Abeywardena, the vehicles had been assigned to him for official duties and security purposes in accordance with circulars issued by the Presidential Secretariat and applicable government provisions.

He also said he had no objection to legal action being taken if there was evidence of any wrongdoing or irregularities.

The special audit had examined vehicle use, fuel consumption and related expenditure during the 8th and 9th Parliaments.

The report revealed that during the final two years of the 9th Parliament, the Speaker’s Office recorded an average monthly fuel consumption of 3,994 litres in 2023 and 6,122 litres in 2024.
