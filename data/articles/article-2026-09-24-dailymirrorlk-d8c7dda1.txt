Police uncover real story behind viral ‘extortion’ video

Colombo, Sep 24 (Daily Mirror) - Pettah Police have launched an investigation into a video circulating on social media that was widely shared as an alleged extortion incident in the Maradana-Panchikawatta area.

Preliminary investigations have found that the incident arose from a dispute between a customer and a mobile phone repair shop over an Apple iPhone 13 Pro Max, police said.

According to police, the customer handed over the phone for a display repair at a shop in the Pollack Market shopping complex on Main Street, Pettah, on August 30. The shop charged Rs. 16,000 for the repair and returned the phone to the customer.

The customer returned the following day, claiming that the repair had not been properly carried out. During an argument, he began recording the shop premises on his mobile phone. The shop owner allegedly took the phone from him and kept it at the shop.

On September 20, the customer returned to the shop carrying a sword and allegedly damaged property while demanding the return of his phone and a refund of the repair fee. He then fled the scene in a car.

Pettah Police subsequently reported the facts to the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court and launched an investigation. Police traced the registered owner of the vehicle used to flee the scene and arrested the main suspect on September 23.

The suspect has been identified as a 44-year-old resident of Koswatta, Battaramulla. Police have taken the vehicle into custody and are conducting further investigations to recover the sword allegedly used in the incident.

Police said preliminary investigations revealed that the incident was linked to a dispute over the mobile phone repair and was not an organised extortion incident.
