Sri Lanka, Qatar seek more jobs for skilled workers

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka and Qatar have discussed measures to further strengthen bilateral cooperation in labour and foreign employment during a meeting between Sri Lanka’s Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Employment Arun Hemachandra and Qatar’s Minister of Labour, Dr. Ali bin Saeed bin Smeikh Al Marri.

The meeting was held in Doha during Deputy Minister Hemachandra’s official visit to Qatar from September 20 to 23, coinciding with the 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations between the two countries.

Discussions focused on expanding employment opportunities for skilled and professionally qualified Sri Lankan workers, strengthening worker welfare and protection, promoting transparent recruitment practices and improving the mutual recognition of vocational and professional qualifications.

Deputy Minister Hemachandra noted that labour and foreign employment remain key areas of Sri Lanka-Qatar bilateral relations, with nearly 140,000 Sri Lankans currently employed in Qatar.

He also highlighted the importance of reconvening the Sri Lanka-Qatar Joint Committee meeting after several years, saying the mechanism could help translate bilateral discussions into practical benefits for workers and employers.

The two sides also discussed strengthening skills development programmes and increasing access for Sri Lankan workers to skill-based and higher-value employment opportunities in Qatar.

The Sri Lankan delegation included Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to Qatar, Sitara Khan, along with other officials.
