57 officers of tri-forces receive ’’Vishishta Seva Vibhushana’’ medals

Colombo, Sep 30 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 57 serving and retired senior officers of the Sri Lanka Army, Navy and Air Force were presented with the “Vishishta Seva Vibhushana” medals by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake today.

The awards were presented at the Presidential Secretariat in recognition of the distinguished and dedicated service rendered by officers of the regular forces.

Addressing the ceremony, President Dissanayake said the recipients had made an exceptional contribution to the country and its people while remaining committed to their duties and responsibilities under all circumstances.

He said they had continued to serve regardless of whether it was day or night, whether weather conditions were favourable or adverse, or whether their lives were at risk.

The President said the country needed a public service characterised by such dedication and accountability.

The “Vishishta Seva Vibhushana” is a distinguished military honour established by Presidential proclamation. It is awarded to officers of the three Armed Forces who have completed at least 25 years of continuous service and maintained a commendable service record.

The conferment, forfeiture and restoration of the medal are governed by regulations issued by the President and published in the Gazette.
