APEX Global Talent to expand study, employment opportunities in Japan and Korea

APEX Global Talent (Pvt) Ltd will launch a tripartite initiative today to expand study and employment opportunities for Sri Lankans in Japan and South Korea, in collaboration with Korea’s KIICA and Japan’s KOKUSAI HOLDING.

The inauguration ceremony was held at the Waters Edge Hotel in Battaramulla, bringing together representatives from the three countries to discuss future cooperation in education and employment.

APEX Global Talent Chief Executive Officer Manisha Basnayake said the event would feature the signing of agreements on study opportunities between the company and its Japanese and Korean partners. A roadmap outlining future collaborative programmes among Sri Lanka, Japan and South Korea will also be unveiled.

The ceremony will include the traditional lighting of the oil lamp, followed by a ribbon-cutting ceremony incorporating Japanese and Korean cultural elements.

APEX Global Talent Chief Operating Officer Charitha Basnayake and Miya Matsu from Japan are scheduled to introduce the programme, while representatives from Japan and South Korea will share their views on the initiative.

An agreement between APEX Global Talent and Nihon Lanka International will also be signed in connection with the programme. Lasitha Udaya Kumara, a director of Nihon Lanka International and a Justice of the Peace, is expected to share his views on the initiative.

The programme aims to strengthen international partnerships and create broader opportunities for Sri Lankans seeking overseas education and employment.
