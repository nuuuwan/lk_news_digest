Carsons Group acquires 10.05% stake in C T Holdings

Carson Cumberbatch PLC and five affiliated companies have acquired a combined 10.05 percent of the voting rights in C T Holdings PLC, according to a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE).

The six companies purchased a total of 20.25 million shares in C T Holdings on August 24 and 25, marking a sizeable investment by Carson Cumberbatch Group in the diversified conglomerate.

Lion Brewery (Ceylon) PLC made the largest individual purchase, acquiring 5.07 million shares, representing 2.52 percent of C T Holdings.

Rubber Investment Trust Ltd purchased 3.44 million shares or 1.71 percent, while Ceylon Guardian Investment Trust PLC and Ceylon Investment PLC each acquired about 3.33 million shares, equivalent to 1.66 percent apiece.

Ceylon Beverage Holdings PLC bought 3.17 million shares, representing 1.57 percent, while Carson Cumberbatch PLC directly acquired 1.90 million shares or 0.94 percent.

The announcement did not disclose the acquisition price, the identity of the sellers or whether the group intends to increase its holding further.

Carsons said the purchases were reported to the CSE, Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka and C T Holdings on August 25 and 28, in line with the regulatory requirements.

C T Holdings is the controlling shareholder of Cargills (Ceylon) PLC, whose operations span retail, food manufacturing, restaurants, banking and property development.
