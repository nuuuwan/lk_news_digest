Bring reusable bags to Colombo Book Fair, CEA urges public

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Environmental Authority (CEA) has urged consumers visiting the Colombo International Book Fair to bring an environmentally friendly, reusable bag to carry the books they purchase and contribute to protecting the environment.

CEA Director General R.S.P. Kapila Rajapaksa said it had been observed in recent years that plastic bags, particularly ‘silly sili’ bags, are extensively used to carry books at the Colombo International Book Fair, where a large number of consumers purchase books.

The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has announced through Gazette No. 2456/41 dated October 1, 2025, that ‘silly sili’ bags should not be provided free of charge to any consumer. If such bags are sold, the amount charged for them must be mentioned in the bill.

The law has been introduced to prevent environmental damage caused by the use of single-use polythene and plastic products.

Therefore, the CEA said the public could contribute to fulfilling their responsibility to protect the environment by bringing an eco-friendly reusable bag when purchasing books and minimising the use of polythene bags.

Furthermore, the sale, offering for sale, giving away free of charge, displaying or using single-use plastic drinking straws; single-use plastic plates, cups, spoons, forks and knives; as well as food wrappers or ‘lunch sheets’ manufactured using polythene as a raw material in the country are prohibited by law.

The CEA said an agreement had been reached following discussions with the relevant parties to ensure that these prohibited items are not used in any manner at food outlets within the premises where the book fair is held.

The CEA, in collaboration with the Book Publishers Association, has also taken steps to conduct a special awareness programme to inform traders in charge of book stalls and food outlets, as well as the public, about these matters on September 25, 2026, when the book fair begins.

Accordingly, the programme will be conducted under the theme “Read Smart, Carry Smart”, with the aim of holding the book fair in an environmentally friendly manner while preventing environmental damage caused by polythene and plastic.
