New kitchen opened at Welikada Prison’s women’s unit

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - A new kitchen aimed at improving food preparation and nutrition for inmates and young children at the Welikada Prison Women’s Unit was opened today (17), along with a Samadhi Buddha statue to promote the mental and spiritual well-being of female inmates and officers.

The opening ceremony was held under the patronage of Deputy Minister of Mass Media Dr. Kaushalya Ariyaratne, with the participation of Acting Commissioner General of Prisons Prasad Hemantha Kumara.

This is the first facility of its kind established in a prison in Sri Lanka to ensure proper food and nutrition for female inmates and their young children.

Previously, meals for the women’s unit were supplied by the main prison, leading to difficulties in meeting the nutritional needs of young children living with their mothers. At present, 36 children are staying in the unit with their mothers.

The new kitchen is expected to help address these concerns by ensuring that their nutritional requirements are properly met.

Following the opening, the Deputy Minister and prison officials inspected the new facility and the Buddha shrine.

The kitchen and the Samadhi Buddha statue were established through a financial donation by Manoji Wadugodapitiya, a member of the Prison Inspection Committee and Chairperson of PAM Hydro Power Company.

Meanwhile, 12 modernised toilets and bathrooms have recently been installed for female inmates, along with new fans to improve their living conditions.

Speaking at the event, Deputy Minister Ariyaratne said the Government had taken steps to speed up the conclusion of cases involving remand prisoners by tripling the issuance of forensic reports and expediting court procedures.

She also said more opportunities had been provided for drug addicts to voluntarily undergo rehabilitation.
