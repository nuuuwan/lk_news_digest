Four killed in four road accidents

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Four people were killed in separate road accidents reported in Kotawila, Matara, Koslanda and Keppetipola on September 8, police said.

In the first incident, a three-wheeler travelling along the Laabima–Sultanagoda road in the Kotawila Police Division overturned after its driver lost control. The 68-year-old driver from Sultanagoda was critically injured and later died while receiving treatment at Matara Hospital.

Later that evening, a fatal collision was reported near the Godagama Highway entrance along the Matara–Akuressa road. A motorcycle attempting to overtake a bus collided with an oncoming motorcycle. One rider was thrown under the bus and died at the scene. The deceased was identified as a 19-year-old from Pitabaddara. The other motorcyclist was injured and admitted to Matara Hospital.

In Koslanda, a motorcycle travelling towards Wellawaya collided head-on with an oncoming tipper truck in the Udumulla area along the Koslanda–Wellawaya road. The 58-year-old motorcyclist, a Police Sergeant attached to the Koslanda Police Station, died after being admitted to Koslanda Hospital. The tipper truck driver has been arrested, and Koslanda Police are conducting further investigations.

Meanwhile, a 19-year-old passenger was killed in another accident in the Alakolagala area along the Nuwara Eliya–Badulla road in the Keppetipola Police Division. The three-wheeler in which he was travelling crashed into the rear of a parked lorry. He succumbed to his injuries while receiving treatment at Welimada Hospital.
