Treasury intensifies focus on public sector financial discipline

The Treasury is sharpening its focus on strengthening internal audit mechanisms across the public sector, with a fresh programme targeting tighter financial controls, greater spending discipline and stronger oversight of e-procurement.

The Department of Management Audit has launched a series of awareness programmes aimed at strengthening the capacity of Internal Audit Units and sharpening their role in public financial management.

The latest programme, held yesterday (8), brought together Chief Internal Auditors, Internal Auditors and Provincial Internal Audit Directors overseeing Special Spending Units, Provincial Councils, District Secretariats and government projects.

The programme placed particular emphasis on strengthening financial control and discipline within public institutions, while enhancing the role played by internal auditors in managing public finances.

E-procurement and the audit controls required around the procurement process were also highlighted as key areas of focus.

Greater attention is placed on the management and oversight of public expenditure, increasing the importance of effective internal controls across government institutions.

The Department said the programme series is also expected to improve knowledge-sharing and coordination among internal auditors and build a stronger internal audit function across the public sector.

The latest session was held under the chairmanship of Deputy Secretary to the Treasury M.A.N. Hapugala. Treasury Secretary Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma delivered a message, while Department of Management Audit Director General M.R.G.A.B. Muthukude also participated.

The first programme under the initiative, targeting Chief Internal Auditors and Internal Auditors covering ministries and government departments, was held on 5 August.
