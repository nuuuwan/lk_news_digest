Namal calls for immediate action against online abuse networks

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - SLPP National Organiser and Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa has called on the Government to take immediate action over reports of WhatsApp, Facebook and Telegram networks allegedly being used to distribute intimate images without consent and exploit women, children and other vulnerable individuals.

In a post on X, Namal said the reported misuse of digital platforms required urgent government intervention, while also highlighting what he described as the growing use of politically motivated fake accounts and coordinated online networks to harass, manipulate and target individuals.

“Digital platforms cannot become a safe haven for criminals, abusers or those who weaponise them for political purposes,” he said.

Namal said the issue should not be addressed through warnings, excuses or victim-blaming, stressing that criminal networks must be identified, investigated and dismantled.

He called for victims to be protected, illegal content to be removed, digital evidence to be preserved and perpetrators to be prosecuted under the law.

“The Government already has the institutions and mechanisms to act. What is needed now is leadership, coordination and urgency,” he said.

Namal urged the Government to act decisively and transparently, arguing that its responsibility was not to tell victims to be more careful, but to ensure criminals could not exploit them with impunity.

“Protecting the dignity, safety and rights of every woman and child must come before politics. Justice must find the perpetrators, not blame the victims,” he added.
