Two High Court Judges sworn in as Court of Appeal Judges

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - High Court Judges Navaratne Marasinghe and H.M.R.M. Thilakaratne were sworn in as new Judges of the Court of Appeal this morning (6) at the Presidential Secretariat.

The two judges took their oaths before President Anura Kumara Dissanayake at the Presidential Secretariat.
