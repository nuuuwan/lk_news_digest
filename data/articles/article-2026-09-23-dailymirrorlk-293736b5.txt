FiNAC HR Optimizer more relevant today: Datamation Chief

With work models of both private companies and government institutions shifting dramatically toward flexible, tech-driven and human-centred forms, ‘governance’ has become a key requirement for business success.

According to a prominent Sri Lankan software development company, workforce management software (WFM) has emerged as an indispensable tool for streamlining processes and improving productivity and by deploying the right WFM software containing strong controls, organisations can reduce operational costs, boost employee engagement and position themselves for long-term success in a competitive market. “Most banks and large businesses today run hybrid or remote teams; that’s not a passing phase; it’s simply how work happens now. The problem is the way most companies price work, staff projects and prove compliance was built for a world where everyone sat in one building. Nobody notices the gap until a project quietly slips or a regulator asks for evidence that doesn’t exist,” said Datamation Systems (Pvt.) Ltd Chairman and Managing Director Fazal Issadeen.

‘FiNAC HR Optimizer’, the workforce management software developed by Datamation, is more relevant today, because the working model has changed globally and most companies’ governance model hasn’t caught up or adapted to that change.

“That’s exactly why it should be seen as an investment, not a cost. The real cost is the one you can’t see right now: idle time, a project quietly running over budget, an office bigger than the business actually needs. HR Optimizer doesn’t add that cost; it makes it visible, so leadership can act on it,” added Issadeen, the Chief Architect of HR Optimizer.

He further said that there’s a second kind of cost hiding in the same blind spot: a fraud case or a data leak with no evidence trail to answer it.

“Ten years ago, that was rare. Today it’s common and expensive when it happens. Paying a little every month to stay covered against a rare, expensive hit isn’t a cost; it’s an investment. It’s no different from vehicle insurance: accidents are expensive, so you pay a monthly or annual premium to keep you covered if and when one happens.”

“This is a global problem. HR Optimizer was built to be global-ready from day one. Banks already have to prove to their regulator exactly what controls they have in place, with real evidence, not just a policy on paper and it meets that expectation already.

“In markets like Dubai, Singapore and London, regulatory expectations are even stricter. Companies there can’t just tick a compliance box; they have to practice proper governance and show proof of it. HR Optimizer is global-ready because it was built to answer a global problem,” he emphasised.

“As the working world moved to hybrid and remote, the risks that come with not seeing that world clearly - cost, fraud, compliance - are real, not hypothetical. And the standard that governs it needs to hold up anywhere in the world. That’s the case for HR Optimizer: not a tool that watches people but the governance layer that lets a business run with confidence in the way work actually happens now.”

Datamation Systems Ltd has been successfully providing business solutions since 1986. It is a software development firm with a mission to provide innovative services and solutions and a vision of serving clients locally and across the globe.
