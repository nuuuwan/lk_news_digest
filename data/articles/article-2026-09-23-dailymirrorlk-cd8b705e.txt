Couple arrested as assets worth Rs. 47.5 Mn frozen in money laundering probe

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - A 47-year-old man and his 45-year-old wife have been arrested in Padavi Parakramapura in connection with a money laundering investigation, while assets worth more than Rs. 47.5 million have been placed under a 14-day freezing order.

The arrests were made by the Illegal Assets Investigation Division following a complaint received by the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) alleging that the man had accumulated significant assets through illegal drug trafficking.

Acting on the instructions of the Inspector General of Police, the matter was referred to the Illegal Assets Investigation Division for further investigation. Investigators subsequently identified several properties and vehicles allegedly acquired using proceeds from illicit activities.

The frozen assets, with a combined estimated value of around Rs. 47.5 million, include a 15-perch plot of land with a two-storey building in Padaviya New Town. The property, registered in the name of the suspect's wife, has been valued at approximately Rs. 27.5 million.

Authorities also identified a 15-perch property with a house in Vijayapura, Anuradhapura, valued at around Rs. 12 million. The assets under the freezing order further include a van valued at Rs. 6.5 million and a Dimo Batta lorry valued at approximately Rs. 1.5 million.

The couple were arrested under provisions of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act. The freezing orders covering the properties and vehicles came into effect on September 22, 2026, and will remain in force for 14 days as investigations continue.

The Illegal Assets Investigation Division is conducting further inquiries into the source of the assets and the alleged financial activities linked to the couple.
