Hunnasgiriya eatery closed over unsanitary conditions

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - An eatery located near a long-distance bus stop in Hunnasgiriya has been ordered to temporarily close after Public Health Inspectors found it operating under unsanitary conditions.

The action followed a special inspection conducted by Public Health Inspectors at several eateries located near long-distance bus stops within the Hunnasgiriya Public Health Inspector division, under the Udadumbara Medical Officer of Health (MOH) area.

The eateries are located along the Kandy-Mahiyangana road, where large numbers of passengers use the bus stops daily for meals and other sanitary needs.

Legal action was taken against two eateries found to have violated sanitary requirements. The owners and employees were produced before the Teldeniya Magistrate’s Court on October 6.

The court imposed fines totalling Rs. 75,000. The respective hotel owners were fined Rs. 36,000 and Rs. 33,000, while employees were fined Rs. 6,000.

The court also ordered the temporary closure of one of the eateries until the identified deficiencies are rectified.

A further hearing has been scheduled for the submission of a report confirming that the shortcomings at the premises have been addressed.

Public Health Inspectors Santhusha Bandara of Hunnasgiriya, Tharanga Bandara of Madugalla and Thisara Weerasekara of Udadumbara participated in the inspections.

Health authorities said regular inspections of eateries and hotels located near long-distance bus terminals would continue to ensure compliance with required sanitary standards.
