Publicis Groupe Sri Lanka relocates to ‘The Optimist’

Sapna Seneviratne lighting the traditional oil lamp to mark the opening of the company’s new office

Publicis Groupe Sri Lanka has relocated operations to the 20th floor of The Optimist, Colombo, celebrating a significant milestone in its growth in the country.

The move reflects the Groupe’s ‘Power of One’ philosophy, creating a shared environment where creative, media, digital and content teams can collaborate more closely and deliver integrated solutions for clients.

Sapna Seneviratne, CEO, Publicis Groupe Sri Lanka said, “With ‘The Power of One,’ we bring our disciplines together in one setting to strengthen collaboration and creativity. The Optimist supports that ambition as a place which amplifies the energy and authenticity of who we are, as we begin the next chapter of our growth in Sri Lanka and a stronger expression of the unified identity we have already built.”

The new office at The Optimist provides state-of-the-art facilities, equipped with panoramic views and modern amenities to enhance employee wellbeing and productivity.

Ganatharan Jeyakumar, CEO, Elegance Workspaces added, “Publicis Groupe Sri Lanka has been a valued partner to Elegance and The Optimist for the past six years, and we are genuinely delighted to welcome them to our newest location.
