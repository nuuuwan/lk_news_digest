Sextortion: Don’t pay, don’t panic

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Computer Emergency Readiness Team (SLCERT) has urged the public to remain vigilant against sextortion, an online crime in which perpetrators threaten to share intimate images or videos unless victims meet their demands.

Sextortion can target anyone and may begin through social media, messaging apps, dating platforms or online games. Perpetrators often build trust before obtaining intimate material and then demand money, more images or other favours.

With the rise of generative AI, criminals can also create explicit deepfake images and use them to threaten victims.

SLCERT advised victims to stop engaging with perpetrators, preserve evidence such as messages and threats, and seek help before blocking and reporting the account.

The public is also urged to be cautious about sharing personal or intimate material online, avoid trusting strangers easily and learn how to block and report suspicious accounts.

If threatened, remember: You are the victim, not the culprit.
