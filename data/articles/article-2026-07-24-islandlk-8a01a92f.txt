Investors going for ‘fundamentally strong stocks’ amid market tensions

CSE investors are focusing on fundamentally strong stocks, especially banking stocks, Softlogic Stockbrokers Assistant Vice President Eardly Kern said.

‘The market is anyway a bit volatile because investors are worried about the escalation of Middle East tensions, Kern told the ‘The Island Financial Review’.

However yesterday the market was positive while both indices moved upwards. The All Share Price Index went up by 49.81 points, while the S and P SL20 rose by 25.22 points. Turnover stood at Rs 1 billion with five crossings.

Those crossings were reported in JKH which crossed 2.7 million shares to the tune of Rs 342 million; its shares traded at Rs 19.60, Pickme 500,000 shares crossed for Rs 82 million; its shares traded at Rs 167, HNB 104,000 shares crossed for Rs 40 million; its shares sold at Rs 385, NDB 200,000 shares crossed for Rs 21.6 million; its shares sold at Rs 108 and Sampath Bank 150,000 shares crossed for Rs 20.5 million; its shares traded at Rs 137.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; NDB Rs 49.9 million (461,000 shares traded), JKH Rs 47.9 million (2.4 million shares traded), Melstacorp Rs 46.1 million (256,000 shares traded), HNB Rs 40 million (104,000 shares traded), HNB (Non-Voting) Rs 36 million (113,539 shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 26.1 million (190825 shares traded) and Commercial Bank Rs 20.1 million (129,245 shares traded). During the day 37.7 million share volumes changed hands in 13073 transactions.

It is said that banking sector counters, especially HNB and NDB, played a dominant role throughout the day while the manufacturing sector, especially JKH, was also very much active at the floor.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 336.30/40 to the US dollar in the spot market , from Rs 336.35/40 the previous day, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was 331.60 buying, 340.60 selling; the euro was Rs 376.0793 buying, Rs 389.9963 selling; and the pound was Rs 442.8111 buying,Rs 456.8567 selling.

A bond maturing on 15.09.2029 was quoted at 11.20/25 percent.
