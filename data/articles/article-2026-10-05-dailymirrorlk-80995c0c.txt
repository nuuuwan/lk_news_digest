Signature campaign against CBSL investment company ban

A signature campaign was held today (5) at Independence Square to protest the decision by the Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka to ban commercial investment companies operating in the country.

Organised by Civil Intelligence Front Chairman Sanjeewa Mahawaththa, the campaign was attended by a large number of depositors who had invested their funds in these companies.

A heated exchange also took place between police officers and Mahawaththa over the holding of the campaign within the Independence Square premises.
