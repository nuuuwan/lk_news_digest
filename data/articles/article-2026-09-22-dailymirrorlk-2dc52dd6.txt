Cargills Bank recognizes 12th anniversary Spend Campaign winners

Cargills Bank held a ceremony recently to recognize the winners of its inaugural 12th Anniversary Spend Campaign, presenting them with symbolic dummy cheques to mark their achievement.

The special event celebrated 60 credit cardholders who were selected through a raffle draw based on their total credit card spend during the months of July and August.

The campaign rewards cardholders across three key daily expense categories: supermarket, fuel, and utility bills. Under the campaign terms, the recognized winners will spend up to their allocated reward values using their Cargills Bank credit card between September 15 and October 15, 2026, with the bank directly crediting the full reimbursement amounts back to their credit card accounts at the end of October 2026.

The gathering brought together campaign winners and bank leadership to highlight the success of the anniversary initiative. Representing Cargills Bank at the recognition event were Chief Strategy Officer Talaal Maruzook, Head of Card Services Kaushi Fernando, and Chief Manager Card Services Shihan Pathiraja, alongside bank officials.

“Reaching our 12th anniversary is a major milestone, and launching our first-ever Spend Campaign was our way of honoring our cardholders through an engaging event while offering direct, practical value,” said Kaushi Fernando, Head of Card Services. “Instead of traditional vouchers, reimbursing actual spending on everyday essentials delivers immediate financial relief straight back to their credit card accounts.”
