Groundworth launches Malabe Urban One land development

Groundworth Group has launched Urban One by Groundworth, a premium land development in Pittugala, Malabe, strategically located within the area’s thriving IT and education hub.

Offering only 17 residential plots, Urban One provides access to two Outer Circular Expressway interchanges, educational institutions, healthcare, retail and everyday conveniences, positioning it as an opportunity for both residential development and long-term investment.

Urban One, which is situated in a rapidly developing residential neighbourhood, offers both accessibility and close proximity to some of Malabe’s major commercial and educational destinations. The Kaduwela Interchange, which connects New Kandy Road and the Low-Level Road to Colombo, is around 4 kilometres away from the development, while the Athurugiriya Interchange on the Outer Circular Expressway (E02), offering direct access to the Southern Expressway, is only 2 kilometres away.

Groundworth Group Managing Director and Co-Founder Kasun Andrahennadi stated, “Urban One brings together the factors that make a location practical for both living and investment - accessibility, proximity to education and everyday amenities and a neighbourhood that continues to develop. We have created a limited opportunity for customers to build a home or invest in an area supported by Malabe’s growing IT and education ecosystem.”

While SLIIT is around 1.5 kilometre away, Urban One is about one kilometre from Pittugala Junction and Malabe Town, offering easy access to one of the area’s major universities and supporting the need for accommodation for professionals and students.

The development is about 3.5 kilometres from Gateway College, which will shortly open in Malabe. Medihelp Athurugiriya and Dr. Neville Fernando Teaching Hospital are around 2.5-3 kilometres away, while SPAR, Keells and GLOMARK are all about 1.2 kilometres away. Leading schools, banks, restaurants and other everyday conveniences are all close to the development.

With land sizes starting from six perches, Urban One offers plot sizes suited for residential development, supported by well-developed 30-ft road access, electricity and pipe-borne water connections and clear deeds. The development provides access to the rising demand surrounding Malabe’s IT and education corridor and is set within a peaceful and rapidly developing residential neighbourhood. The starting price per perch for land is Rs.1.85 million.

Urban One by Groundworth is now open for site visits, with only a limited number of plots available.
