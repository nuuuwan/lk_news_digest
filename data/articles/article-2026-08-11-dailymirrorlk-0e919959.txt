Immigration system outage disrupts one-day passport service

Colombo, August 11 (Daily Mirror) - The one-day service system of Sri Lanka’s Department of Immigration and Emigration has experienced a technical disruption, hindering the issuance of passports and other travel documents.

The system outage has affected the processing of applications under the one-day service, causing inconvenience to members of the public who require their travel documents urgently.

The Department’s Travel Division issues passports under both one-day and normal services.

A similar computer system failure in February temporarily disrupted departmental services, including passport and one-day passport services, before the system was restored.

Further information on the latest disruption and the expected restoration of the one-day service system is awaited from the Department of Immigration and Emigration.
