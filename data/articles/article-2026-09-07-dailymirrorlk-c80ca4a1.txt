Green number plates for electric vehicles confirmed as legal

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Motor Traffic has clarified that green-background number plates issued for electric vehicles are legally valid, amid concerns over the use of such plates on vehicles.

Commissioner General of Motor Transport, Retired Air Vice Marshal Thushara Fernando, said green number plates are legally issued for electric vehicles and that there is no issue with vehicle owners using them.

He said the Department is taking steps to ensure that all electric vehicles are issued with green-background number plates.

According to the Commissioner General, white and yellow number plates will continue to be issued for other categories of vehicles, in accordance with the existing system.

The clarification follows concerns raised by some electric vehicle owners after they were questioned by traffic police over the use of green number plates.

Meanwhile, the Department of Motor Traffic has also prepared number plates for 88,740 registered vehicles.

Of this total, 56,233 number plates have been prepared for vehicles registered between June 10 and August 31, 2026, while another 32,507 number plates have been prepared for vehicles registered during 2023 and 2024.

The Department has requested the relevant vehicle owners to visit its office in Narahenpita and collect their prepared number plates.

The Department said the move to provide green number plates for electric vehicles is intended to clearly identify them from other vehicles while maintaining the legal validity of the registration plate system.
