Control Union Sri Lanka convenes industry leaders to strengthen food safety systems

Control Union Sri Lanka Managing Director Roshan Ranawake delivers welcome address

Control Union Academy Sri Lanka, in collaboration with Foundation FSSC, hosted Food Safety Forward, an FSSC Awareness Session for Stronger Food Safety Systems, Version 7, at the BMICH, Colombo.

The session brought together the food industry professionals, food safety practitioners, business leaders and other stakeholders for an afternoon focused on the changing food safety landscape and the future readiness of Sri Lankan businesses.

Control Union Sri Lanka Managing Director Roshan Ranawake delivered the welcome address.

This was followed by the keynote address titled ‘Safe Food, Strong Businesses, Global Trust, Shaping the Future of Sri Lanka’s Food Industry’, delivered by Janak Badugama of the Export Development Board of Sri Lanka.

FSSC India and Sri Lanka Representative Smita Murty introduced the participants to FSSC, offering perspectives on the evolving global food safety expectations and how the local businesses can prepare for what lies ahead.

The panel discussion ‘Preparing Sri Lankan Food Businesses for Emerging Global Food Safety Requirements’ brought together expertise from international food safety systems, export development, food science and industry leadership, featuring Murty, Badugama, Prof. K.K.D.S. Ranaweera of the Food Science and Technology Department at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and agribusiness entrepreneur and export specialist Annes Junaid.

The discussion was moderated by Control Union Sri Lanka Commercial Manager Sunera Wickramaarachchi and explored the evolving global food safety expectations, emerging scientific risks, market access, management commitment and food safety culture, along with the practical steps the Sri Lankan businesses can take to strengthen both performance and competitiveness.

The formal proceedings concluded with a vote of thanks delivered by Control Union Sri Lanka General Manager Kaushalya Rajapaksha.

The session was hosted throughout by Control Union Sri Lanka Quality Manager Uthuthara Rajapaksha.
