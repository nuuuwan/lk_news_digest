Tomorrow’s rally is the start of the “people’s struggle” — SLPP’s Cassilingam

The SLPP will launch what it describes as a fresh “public struggle” at a major rally in Anuradhapura tomorrow (12), accusing the Government of pursuing political opponents while failing to provide relief to people struggling with the rising cost of living.

SLPP Jaffna District Organiser Geethanath Cassilingam said the rally would highlight what he described as the failure of the NPP Government to fulfil promises made when President Anura Kumara Dissanayake was elected two years ago.

“Two years on, the Government seems to cover up its failures by carrying out political revenge on the opposition. The people are struggling to survive, while the Government has failed to provide the relief it promised,” Cassilingam said.

He alleged that the Government had failed to attract major foreign investments or create sufficient employment opportunities, forcing talented Sri Lankan youth to continue seeking opportunities overseas.

“Foreign investors come to the country and sign MoUs, but nothing moves beyond that. Files remain untouched in ministries,” he claimed, adding that the proposed Sinopec investment also remained in limbo.

Cassilingam said farmers outside Colombo continued to face rising agricultural costs and delays in payments, while the Government was considering imports of essential commodities including rice, sugar and fish.

He also criticised the Government’s tax policies, claiming that increased taxation was placing further pressure on businesses and consumers.

“Although incomes remain low, high taxes have forced small businesses to close while many others continue to struggle,” he said.

Cassilingam questioned the Government’s priorities amid concerns over shootings and grenade attacks, criticising President Dissanayake’s recent remarks about consulting a fashion designing company on a new police uniform.

“While our Treasury is being robbed by unknown hackers, the President is more keen to talk about a new police uniform,” he said.

He said the Government should instead focus on strengthening law enforcement and addressing the country’s security concerns.

Cassilingam said tomorrow’s rally would give the SLPP an opportunity to raise the concerns of people facing higher living costs, declining incomes, taxation and unemployment.

“We will prove to the people that the NPP took office by hoodwinking them with false promises. Two years on, it is time to reveal the true face that they hold. Tomorrow is the start of the public’s struggle,” he said.
