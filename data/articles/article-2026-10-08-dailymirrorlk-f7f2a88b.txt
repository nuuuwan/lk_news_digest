New motorcycle registration to begin at five district offices

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The registration of new motorcycles will commence at the Jaffna, Hambantota, Anuradhapura, Kurunegala and Gampaha District Offices from October 19, Commissioner General of Motor Traffic Retired Air Vice Marshal Thushara Fernando said.

He said the service would be introduced at the five district offices after providing the necessary orientation to the relevant officials.
