Missing girl found with baby in cave: Government launches intervention

Colombo, Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has initiated interventions to ensure the safety and future well-being of a 15-year-old girl and her two-month-old baby, who were found living in a remote cave in the Knuckles mountain range after she had been missing for nearly five months.

The girl and her infant daughter were discovered in a forest area near Meemure following an investigation by Ududumbara Police. They were subsequently taken into police protection and referred to the National Hospital in Kandy for medical examinations and treatment.

Police have arrested a 23-year-old man, believed to be the infant's father, and a relative who allegedly assisted him. The two suspects have been remanded until November 20 after being produced before the Teldeniya Magistrate's Court.

Minister of Women and Child Affairs Saroja Savithri Paulraj said all relevant authorities had been instructed to take immediate and continuous action to ensure the well-being of the girl and her infant.

In a statement, the Minister said the incident was deeply shocking and highlighted the growing vulnerabilities faced by children amid economic, social and political challenges.

She stressed the importance of strengthening trust and communication between parents and children, noting that families should be safe environments where children can seek protection and support.

The Minister also called on State institutions and all parties involved in protecting vulnerable individuals to become more accessible and trustworthy to the public.

As a long-term measure to address the vulnerabilities of children, the Ministry plans to introduce a new 'Alternative Care Policy in 2027', with steps, including securing the necessary budgetary allocations, already underway.

Emphasising that child protection is a collective responsibility, Minister Paulraj urged the public to pay greater attention to the safety, rights and well-being of children.
