National media guidelines proposed to protect children’s privacy

Colombo, Sep. 24 (Daily Mirror) - National Media Guidelines have been prepared to protect the dignity, privacy and confidentiality of children when reporting news involving them, the Ministerial Consultative Committee on Women and Child Affairs was informed.

The matter was discussed at a Committee meeting held in Parliament on September 22 under the chairmanship of Minister of Women and Child Affairs Saroja Savitri Paulraj.

The guidelines aim to ensure that the best interests of children are protected while maintaining accuracy and fairness in reporting.

They also seek to prevent the unnecessary exposure of children and their families through print, electronic and social media.

The Committee further said the need to protect the dignity and rights of children with disabilities in media reporting.

Deputy Minister Dr. Namal Sudarshana, other Deputy Ministers, Members of Parliament and officials of the Ministry of Women and Child Affairs participated in the meeting.
