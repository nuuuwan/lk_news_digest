Central Bank releases Sri Lanka Socio-Economic Data – 2026 publication

Sri Lanka Socio-Economic Data – 2026, the annually published data folder of the Central Bank, is now available for public information.

This easy-to-carry booklet contains information categorised under 14 topics, namely, Country Profile, Key Economic Indicators, Country Comparisons, Socio-Economic Conditions, Human Resources, National Accounts, Agriculture, Industry, Economic and Social Infrastructure, Prices and Wages, External Trade and Tourism, External Finance, Government Finance and Money, Banking and Finance.

As the data folder presents a wide range of socioeconomic data of the current interest in a concise and summary form, it will serve as a useful publication for the policymakers, researchers, academics, professionals, students and public.

The electronic version of the publication can be accessed through the Central Bank website: https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/en/publications/other-publications/statistical-publications/sri-lanka-ocioeconomic-data-folder.

The printed copies of the publication will be available for sale at Rs.100, at the Sales and Distribution Counter of the Central Bank, located at the Central Point Building, Chatham Street, Colombo 1. Tel: 011-2444502.
