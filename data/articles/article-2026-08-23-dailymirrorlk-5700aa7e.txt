People’s Bank sustains strong growth momentum with Rs. 32.6bn record pre-tax profit in 1H 2026

People’s Bank continued its growth during the six months ended June 30, 2026, delivering profitability supported by core banking performance, improved margins, and disciplined financial management.

The bank recorded a standalone Profit Before Tax (PBT) of Rs. 32.6 billion for the period, alongside a record Profit After Tax of Rs 20.5 billion.

Total operating income increased by 17.7 percent to Rs 95.2 billion, compared to Rs 80.9 billion in the same period last year, while net interest income grew by 18.9 percent to Rs 82.4 billion from Rs 69.3 billion. The Net Interest Margin (NIM) increased to 4.4 percent from 4.1 percent. Total assets stabilized at Rs 3.8 trillion, and gross loans and advances surpassed the Rs 2.0 trillion milestone for the first time, reaching Rs 2.1 trillion.

The Total Capital Adequacy Ratio improved to 18.2 percent by June 30, 2026, compared to 17.9% at the end of the first quarter and 16.5 percent at the end of 2025. The Tier I Capital Adequacy Ratio stood at 12.7 percent. Liquidity remained stable, with the All Currency Liquidity Coverage Ratio at 212.3 percent and the Rupee Liquidity Coverage Ratio at 255.9 percent.

At the Group level, Profit Before Tax was Rs 37.4 billion, representing a 20.6 percent growth compared to Rs 31.0 billion in the previous year. Group total assets stabilised at Rs 4.1 trillion, while gross loans and advances increased to Rs 2.4 trillion. Group net interest income grew by 22.9 percent to Rs. 97.0 billion from Rs 79.0 billion, and Group Net Interest Margin improved to 4.8 percent from 4.4 percent.

Chairman Narada Fernando stated, “People’s Bank has continued to demonstrate strength, resilience and financial stability despite a complex global environment shaped by geopolitical developments and persistent economic uncertainties.”

He noted that the bank’s performance is “underpinned by strong financial fundamentals, sound governance practices and a prudent risk-management framework, enabling it to navigate prevailing challenges while maintaining positive momentum across key financial indicators.”

The Chairman reaffirmed the bank’s commitment to supporting the country’s economic priorities, emphasising, “The Bank’s continued role in facilitating economic activity and strengthening financial inclusion remains central to its purpose of fostering sustainable and inclusive growth, while creating long-term value for stakeholders and contributing to the country’s development.”

Chief Executive Officer and General Manager Clive Fonseka highlighted “the Bank’s continued resilience and strong underlying performance amid a dynamic global and domestic environment.”

He noted, “The Bank’s robust core income generation, improved margins, strengthened asset quality and stable balance sheet position reflect the effectiveness of its strategic focus and disciplined financial management, enabling People’s Bank to maintain positive momentum while responding to evolving challenges.”

Fonseka emphasised, “The Bank’s strong capital and liquidity position, supported by prudent risk-management practices, provides a solid foundation for sustainable growth and continued financial resilience.”

Commenting on the bank’s ISO 22301:2019 certification, he added the milestone reflects an “unwavering commitment to operational resilience, service continuity and customer trust,” and “underscores People’s Bank’s commitment to maintaining critical banking services and ensuring business continuity in the face of unforeseen disruptions.”
