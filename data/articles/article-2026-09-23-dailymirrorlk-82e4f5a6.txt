CSE extends rebound as ASPI holds above 21,000

The Colombo Stock Exchange extended its recovery on 23 September 2026, with both benchmark indices closing higher for a second consecutive session despite some late profit-taking.

The ASPI gained 31.14 points, or 0.15%, to close at 21,085.15, while the S&P SL20 advanced 18.64 points, or 0.31%, to 5,947.50. The ASPI briefly moved above 21,200 during early trading before easing through the session as investors booked profits.

Market turnover stood at LKR 1.32 billion, with 83.44 million shares traded. The Capital Goods sector led activity with LKR 326.80 million in turnover, while Sampath Bank recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 233.80 million.

Crossings accounted for around 37% of total turnover, led by a LKR 210.00 million block in Sampath Bank. Dipped Products and E B Creasy also recorded sizeable crossings of approximately LKR 102.25 million and LKR 99.82 million, respectively.

Market breadth remained positive, with 110 gainers against 89 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 1.24. Foreign activity was broadly neutral, with a marginal net outflow of LKR 2.62 million.

Commercial Bank was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by Dialog Axiata, Overseas Realty, Hatton National Bank and Hemas Holdings. Carson Cumberbatch was the largest negative contributor, followed by Lion Brewery, Ceylinco Insurance, LAMB.N and Hayleys.

The market maintained its post-upgrade recovery for a second session, but the retreat from early highs suggests buying momentum moderated as the day progressed. Positive breadth remains supportive, although turnover is still relatively subdued, indicating that investors continue to be selective.
