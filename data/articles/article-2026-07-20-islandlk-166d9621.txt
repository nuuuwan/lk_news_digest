Ather showcases innovation at Colombo EV Motor Show 2026

Ather Energy, through its Sri Lankan distributor Evolution Auto, showcased strong momentum at the Colombo EV Motor Show 2026 with its premium electric scooter, the Ather 450X. The three-day event drew significant customer engagement, reinforcing the brand’s reputation as the “super scooter” in the local market.

Highlighting its commitment to real-world riding needs, Ather introduced two new features to Sri Lanka: Infinite Cruise, which maintains consistent speed to reduce rider fatigue during long commutes, and Magic Twist, an intuitive regenerative braking system that allows riders to slow down by twisting the throttle backwards—even bringing the scooter to a complete halt without using brake levers.

Visitors also explored the scooter’s connected technology and engineering, including a display of its battery pack and aluminium chassis. The positive feedback and strong interest underscore growing demand for premium EV solutions in Sri Lanka. Evolution Auto reaffirmed its dedication to expanding Ather’s accessibility, inviting potential customers to experience the new features at local Experience Centres. As the country embraces sustainable mobility, Ather aims to play a pivotal role in shaping the future of electric commuting through innovation and customer-focused experiences.
