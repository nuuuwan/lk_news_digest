Gampola garbage dumped illegally near Moragahakanda Reservoir; two arrested

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Two suspects have been arrested for illegally dumping garbage collected under a contract awarded by the Gampola Municipal Council near the Moragahakanda Reservoir, inside the Giritale-Minneriya Nature Reserve.

The contractor, who had been awarded the garbage disposal contract by the Gampola Municipal Council, was required to transport the waste to the Digampathaha Waste Recycling Centre in Dambulla using trucks.

However, the trucks allegedly diverted from their route near Naula and proceeded to dump the garbage illegally on the slopes of the Moragahakanda Reservoir, near the Moragahakanda Viewpoint along the Elahera Road.

Forest officers stationed at the Elahera area arrested two suspects along with two trucks on the night of November 24 while they were allegedly dumping the garbage.

The suspects were produced before the Naula Magistrate's Court on the afternoon of November 25.
