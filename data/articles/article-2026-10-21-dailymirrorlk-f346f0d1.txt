Early selling gives way to measured buying

Monday’s selling momentum persisted through the early hours of trading yesterday. However, buying sentiment appeared towards the latter part of the day, keeping the overall investor sentiment mixed and prompting the yield curve to remain largely anchored. Trading volumes remained moderate unlike Monday, which saw elevated volumes driven by a notable rise in investor participation.

At the belly of the yield curve, 15.06.2034 and 15.09.2034 traded at 12.55% while 15.01.2033 was seen changing hands between 12.30% to 12.20%. Within the 2031 segment 01.12.2031 traded between 11.95% to 11.85% and 01.02.2031 traded from 11.80% to 11.70%. Finally, 01.07.2030 and 01.03.2030 traded within 11.65%-11.55% and 11.50%-11.40% respectively.

On the external front, the LKR depreciated against the USD, standing at LKR 331.54/USD, compared to LKR 331.28/USD seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded marginally to LKR 389.29bn compared to LKR 386.40bn recorded previously.
