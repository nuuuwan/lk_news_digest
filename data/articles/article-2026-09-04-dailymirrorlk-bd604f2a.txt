’’Never asked mahout to produce elephant before police’’ - DIG

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Police never instructed a mahout to bring his elephant after a dagger-like weapon was found in his possession during a road search conducted amid this year’s Kandy Esala Perahera, Central Province Deputy Inspector General of Police Sudath Masinghe said.

Speaking at the Kandy District Coordinating Committee meeting yesterday (3), DIG Masinghe said police officers had stopped and searched the individual as part of security checks conducted during the Perahera.

He said the police had only asked the man to produce the necessary documents to establish that he was a mahout.

The DIG’s clarification came following social media posts that portrayed the incident as though police officers had asked the mahout to bring the elephant to prove his occupation.

He said the incident had subsequently been turned into a subject of humour on social media, with various entertaining posts being circulated.

According to DIG Masinghe, the humorous handling of the incident had even helped police officers relieve some of the fatigue they experienced while performing their duties during the Perahera season.
