Gnanasara Thera’s robes forcibly removed under pressure from NPP leadership: Gammanpila

Colombo, Oct. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Pivithuru Hela Urumaya Leader Udaya Gammanpila today alleged that the robes of Ven. Galaboda Aththe Gnanasara Thera were forcibly removed at Welikada Prison under pressure from the top leadership of the National People's Power (NPP), despite objections from the monk and prison officers.

Addressing a press conference in Colombo while dressed in black, Gammanpila said he had visited the Thera at Welikada Prison on October 8 at the monk’s request and had been informed about the circumstances surrounding the removal of his robes and the attempt to make him wear a prison uniform.

Gammanpila said the Thera had told him that prison officials had attempted to dress him in a prison jumpsuit, but that he had strongly objected.

He claimed that a similar incident had occurred in 2018, when officials abandoned an attempt to make the monk wear prison clothing following his protest. However, on this occasion, he alleged, a large group of prison officers had joined the effort to dress the Thera in the prison uniform despite his objections.

Gammanpila further alleged that, during the incident, a prison officer had activated a mobile phone camera to enable a person at another location to observe the proceedings.

He claimed that the incident had taken place due to pressure from the NPP’s top leadership, although the monk and the officers involved had been reluctant to proceed.

Describing the incident involving the monk’s remaining traditional clothing, Gammanpila said the Thera had been deeply distressed when his anda-kada, the traditional waist-cloth worn by Buddhist monks, was removed.

According to Gammanpila, when officers reached out to take the garment, the monk, overcome by emotional distress, threw it upwards instead of handing it over. The garment subsequently became caught in a ceiling fan that was spinning at the time, he claimed.

Gammanpila reiterated that, according to the account given to him by the Thera, the monk had been made to wear the prison uniform despite objections from both the monk and the officers involved.

The allegations were made at a press conference in Colombo on October 12. The claims concerning the alleged involvement of senior NPP figures and the circumstances surrounding the removal of the monk’s robes have not been independently verified.
