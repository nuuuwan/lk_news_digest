Six DIGs transferred

Colombo, Nov 22 (Daily Mirror): Six Deputy Inspectors General of Police (DIGs) have been transferred with immediate effect by Inspector General of Police (IGP) Priyantha Weerasooriya, subject to the approval of the National Police Commission.

Accordingly, Woman DIG W. J. Padmini, who was in charge of the Kalutara District, has been transferred to the Kegalle District.

DIG O. Hewavitharana, who was in charge of the Kegalle District, has been transferred to the Jaffna District, while DIG G. H. Marapana, who served in Jaffna, has been transferred to the Kalutara District.

Meanwhile, DIG K. A. J. Kodithuwakku, who was in charge of the Central Criminal Investigation Division (CCID), has been transferred to the Police Welfare Division.

DIG W. V. Ginige, who was serving in the Police Welfare Division, has been transferred to the CCID.

In addition, DIG H. K. R. Fernando has been transferred from the State Intelligence Service (SIS) to serve as DIG in charge of the Special Bureau Range.
