Port City sharpens focus on Gulf for next wave of investments

Officials say investments secured by the development have surpassed US$ 2bn, with the figure targeted to exceed US$ 4bn in the coming years

Opportunities pitched to UAE businesses spanned real estate development, financial services, regional headquarters and business continuity operations

UAE Minister of Foreign Trade says from immediate reconstruction needs to the immense potential of its logistics, technology, and tourism sectors, Sri Lanka stands out as one of the most appealing destinations for UAE capital

Port City Colombo is sharpening its focus on the Gulf as a key source of investment for its next phase of growth, as the Special Economic Zone (SEZ) looks to build on more than US$ 2 billion in investments secured to date and deepen its links with businesses seeking a foothold in South Asia.

The Gulf-focused investment drive was pushed to the forefront at the ‘Sri Lanka Beyond Your Dreams’ engagement in Dubai, where Port City was presented to more than 400 UAE-based business leaders, investors and institutional stakeholders as a platform connecting Gulf capital and businesses with opportunities in South Asia.

CHEC Port City Colombo Deputy Managing Director Thulci Aluwihare said investments secured by the development have surpassed US$2 billion, with the figure targeted to exceed US$ 4 billion in the coming years.

The opportunities pitched to UAE businesses spanned real estate development, financial services, regional headquarters and business continuity operations, with the focus extending to companies seeking to establish or expand their South Asian presence.

Sri Lanka has been looking to deepen its broader economic relationship with the UAE, including through a proposed Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA).

Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath stressed the importance of finalising the CEPA, saying it would provide a framework to expand trade, investment and economic cooperation between the two countries. He also positioned Port City as a practical vehicle for translating closer bilateral ties into investment.

“As a SEZ with a dedicated legal and regulatory framework and competitive incentives, Port City Colombo is where Sri Lanka’s economic mission becomes a physical reality,” Herath said.

“For UAE businesses and investors, PCC offers a modern and internationally designed platform and a natural gateway to South Asia’s growth, while keeping you connected to the opportunities and strengths of the Gulf.”

UAE Minister of Foreign Trade Dr. Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi, meanwhile, signalled interest among the UAE investment community in Sri Lankan opportunities, pointing to logistics, technology and tourism as sectors offering significant potential.

“Sri Lanka offers considerable opportunities for our investment community, which continues to seek long-term, high-impact projects in growing economies,” Al Zeyoudi said.

“From immediate reconstruction needs to the immense potential of its logistics, technology, and tourism sectors, Sri Lanka stands out as one of the most appealing destinations for UAE capital,” he added.

The Gulf strategy also seeks to position Port City as more than a destination for capital, with the SEZ pitching itself as an operating base for GCC businesses expanding into South Asia.

Colombo Port City Economic Commission Chairman Harsha Amarasekera PC said Gulf investors were increasingly looking for diversified, resilient and globally connected growth platforms.

“At a time when Gulf investors are increasingly seeking diversified, resilient, and globally connected growth platforms, Port City Colombo offers a compelling proposition: a modern Special Economic Zone with an internationally benchmarked regulatory framework, a trusted and neutral business environment, and direct access to one of the world’s fastest-growing regions,” Amarasekera said.

Colombo Port City has entered into its first formal partnership with a UAE free-zone authority, creating a structured pathway for businesses operating in the two special economic zones to access opportunities in Sri Lanka and the UAE.

The Colombo Port City Economic Commission and CHEC Port City Colombo (Pvt) Ltd signed an investment promotion agreement with the Umm Al Quwain Free Trade Zone Authority during the ‘Sri Lanka Beyond Your Dreams’ engagement in Dubai.

The agreement is expected to provide UAE-based businesses with a direct, institutionally supported route into Port City Colombo, while giving companies registered within the Colombo SEZ structured access to the UAE’s established free-zone ecosystem.

According to Port City Colombo, the agreement marks its first formal partnership of this nature with a UAE free-zone authority, establishing a new institutional link between the two jurisdictions.

The agreement was signed by Umm Al Quwain Free Trade Zone Authority General Manager Johnson Modayil George, Colombo Port City Economic Commission Director General Revan Wickramasuriya and CHEC Port City Colombo Deputy Managing Director Thulci Aluwihare.
