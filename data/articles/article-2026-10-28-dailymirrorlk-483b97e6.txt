Girl, 15, dies after falling from three-storey house

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A 15-year-old girl has died after falling from the upper floor of a three-storey house in Grandpass, Colombo.

The incident took place on Tuesday evening (27) at a house on Layards Broadway, according to Police.

The girl, who was a resident of Grandpass, suffered serious injuries in the fall and was admitted to the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children. She later died while receiving treatment.
