Sri Lanka promotes tourism, Ceylon Tea and cuisine in Washington

WASHINGTON D.C. - Sri Lanka promoted its tourism attractions, Ceylon Tea and traditional cuisine to an American audience at a special reception hosted by the Embassy of Sri Lanka in Washington D.C. in collaboration with the International Club of DC.

The event was held on September 18, 2026, at the Embassy premises, with a large number of members of the International Club of DC attending the reception.

Welcoming the guests, Charge d’Affaires Madhuka Wickramachchi invited them to visit Sri Lanka and experience its rich culture, tropical beauty and hospitality.

Wickramachchi highlighted the exceptional diversity Sri Lanka offers tourists, particularly in terms of culture, natural beauty, history and adventure.

He also recalled the famous words of American author Mark Twain during his journey to Sri Lanka about a century ago, using the author's observations to highlight the island's enduring appeal to visitors.

The reception combined tourism promotion with a showcase of Sri Lankan culture, food and tea.

Several Sri Lankan dance troupes from the Washington D.C., Maryland and Virginia (DMV) area performed traditional dances during the evening.

The performances gave the audience an opportunity to experience Sri Lanka's rich dance traditions through distinctive rhythms, colourful costumes and traditional movements.

Guests were also served traditional Sri Lankan cuisine, giving them an opportunity to experience the country's culinary traditions, distinctive flavours and hospitality.

Freshly brewed hot and iced Ceylon Tea was served to guests as part of efforts to promote Sri Lanka's globally recognised tea industry and introduce the product to a wider American audience.

The Embassy said the combination of tea, food and cultural performances created an engaging experience for the guests and helped generate greater interest in Sri Lanka as a tourism destination.

The event was also aimed at strengthening people-to-people ties between Sri Lanka and the United States.

The Embassy sought to highlight Sri Lanka as an attractive destination for affluent and niche American travellers interested in culture, adventure, natural beauty and warm hospitality.

The International Club of DC, which has members from a range of professional sectors, brought together a large and diverse group of guests for the event.

The club promotes international cultural experiences through art, music, dance and theatre, while also providing opportunities for its members to experience cuisines from different parts of the world.

The Embassy said its collaboration with the International Club of DC provided an opportunity to introduce Sri Lanka's cultural heritage, cuisine and Ceylon Tea to an audience interested in international experiences.

The reception therefore served as a platform to promote Sri Lanka beyond its traditional tourism image, presenting its culture, cuisine, tea, natural beauty and hospitality as part of a wider experience for international visitors.

The event was organised by the Embassy of Sri Lanka in Washington D.C. as part of efforts to deepen cultural links and people-to-people connections between Sri Lanka and the United States.
