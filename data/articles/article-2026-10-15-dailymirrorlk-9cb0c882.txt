Monks gather at Presidential Secretariat

Buddhist monks and other participants of the ‘Freedom March’, organised to demand the release of Ven. Galagodaatte Gnanasara Thera, chant Pirith in front of the Presidential Secretariat in Colombo today (15). A tense atmosphere prevailed as the march reached the area amid tight security measures. The sixth day of the march began in Kadawatha and proceeded to Colombo, with the Maha Sangha leading the participants in their appeal for Gnanasara Thera’s release. Pix by Nisal Baduge
