Two arrested with Rs. 86.6 million worth of ’Kush’ at BIA

Two Sri Lankan passengers were arrested by officers of the Airport Police Narcotics Bureau at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) early this morning (16) while attempting to smuggle in 'Kush' cannabis worth Rs. 86.64 million.

The suspects, aged 35 and 46, are welders from the Nedunkerni area and a businessman from Kandy.

Police said the two had purchased the consignment in Thailand before travelling to Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, from where they arrived at the BIA at around midnight on November 16 on Malaysian Airlines flight MH-179.

They had concealed 8.664 kilograms of 'Kush' in four packets inside four boxes of Jack Daniel's whiskey bottles, which were packed in two suitcases.

The suspects were due to be produced before the Negombo Magistrate's Court today (16). Detention orders have been obtained for further investigations.
