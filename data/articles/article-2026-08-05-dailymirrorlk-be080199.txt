Regen Renewables and Suntech Power to drive SL’s renewable energy growth

Regen Renewables reinforcing its partnership with global photovoltaic (PV) technology leader Suntech Power

Regen Renewables (Pvt) Ltd is reaffirming its long-standing partnership with Suntech Power, demonstrating a shared commitment to accelerating the country’s transition towards clean energy.

The milestone was celebrated during Suntech Power’s 25th anniversary celebrations, held alongside InterSolar Europe 2026 in Germany, where the Directors of Regen Renewables were invited as guests in recognition of their longstanding partnership and contribution to expanding Suntech’s presence in Sri Lanka.

The celebration also marked a significant milestone in Suntech’s growth journey with its strategic partnership with HYSOLAR, strengthening its position as a global industry leader.  Suntech has also earned consistent recognition as one of Bloomberg NEF’s Top 10 Most Bankable PV Brands, reflecting the high level of confidence that financial institutions, developers, and investors place in the company’s products, performance, and long-term reliability.

With over 25 years of photovoltaic expertise, more than 55 GW of modules supplied across over 100 countries, and a portfolio of landmark projects - including Germany’s largest floating solar plant, Japan’s Kanzaki Utility PV Plant, South Africa’s 250 MW Kimberley Droogfontein Solar Plant, San Francisco International Airport Terminal 3, IKEA MLFU Sydney and the Beijing International Stadium BIPV Project, Suntech continues to strengthen its global leadership through innovations such as its Ultra T 3.0 high-efficiency quarter-cut modules, delivering reliable and sustainable clean energy solutions worldwide.

For more than fourteen years, Regen Renewables has served as the authorised agent for Suntech Power in Sri Lanka, delivering world-class solar technology to residential, commercial, and industrial customers. Together, the two companies have consistently introduced reliable, high-performance solar solutions that enable businesses and households to embrace renewable energy with confidence.

Commenting on the partnership, Suntech Chairman Yang Jianliang said, “On behalf of Suntech, I would like to express our sincere gratitude for the valuable partnership we have built with Regen over the years. As we proudly celebrate Suntech’s 25th anniversary, we reaffirm our commitment to delivering high-quality, reliable clean energy solutions to the Sri Lankan market. We look forward to deepening our collaboration with Regen and exploring new opportunities for mutual growth in the years ahead.”

Sharing his thoughts, Lakmal Fernando, Managing Director of Regen Renewables (Pvt) Ltd said, “Over fourteen years we have shared many successes, overcome many challenges, navigated market changes and adopted evolving technologies. What has remained constant is the professionalism, integrity and friendship of the Suntech team. We are proud to have walked alongside Suntech for fourteen of its twenty-five years, and we look forward to continuing that journey together in the years ahead.”
