Muted trading continues to appear in secondary market

Amidst the weekly T-bill auction held yesterday, the secondary market activity and trading volumes remained low, while the overall yield curve stood broadly stable across all maturities.

At the short-end, 01.05.2028 and 15.09.2029 traded at 10.70 percent and 10.99 percent, respectively.

Moving ahead, 01.08.2030 traded at 11.30 percent, while 15.10.2030 changed hands at 11.35 percent.

Moreover, the 15.12.2032 and 15.06.2034 bonds were dealt at 11.72 percent and 12.15 percent, respectively.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.332.55/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.58/US dollar recorded previously.

Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.440.31 billion, compared to Rs.466.09 billion seen previously.

The PDMO is yet to publish the T-bill auction results for the auction held on November 25, 2026.
