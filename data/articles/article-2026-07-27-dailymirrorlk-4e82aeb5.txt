Tepid trading persists ahead of T-bond auction

The secondary market opened the week on a quiet note, especially owing to the T-bond auction set to take place on July 30, 2026.

The trading volumes were moderate while the yield curve remained largely anchored.

At the short end of the curve 15.09.2029 was seen trading at 11.23 percent. Investor interest was visibly centred across the 2030 segment, with 01.07.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 all trading between 11.60 percent and 11.68 percent. Finally, 15.01.2033 traded at 12.27 percent while 01.11.2033 changed hands at a lower rate of 12.19 percent.

The PDMO announced the issue of T-bonds worth Rs.250.0 billion, scheduled for July 30. The auction offers four maturities: 2031 (Rs.90.0 billion), 2034 (Rs.80.0 billion), 2036 (Rs.50.0 billion) and 2037 (Rs.30.0 billion) maturities bearing coupons of 11.60 percent, 11.70 percent, 10.85 percent and 10.75 percent, respectively.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee marginally appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.336.25/US dollar, compared to Rs.336.26/US dollar seen earlier. Overnight liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.173.80 billion, from Rs.163.07 billion recorded previously.
