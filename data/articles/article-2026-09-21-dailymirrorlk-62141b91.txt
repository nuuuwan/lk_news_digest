No fuel price hike as government plans concession: Minister

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - Energy Minister Anura Karunathilaka today assured that fuel prices will not be increased in the upcoming price revision at the end of this month.

Speaking to the media, the Minister said the Government was preparing to provide a concession on fuel prices, as announced by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

He said a decision on the amount of the concession would be taken shortly.

The Minister noted that the Government had previously granted fuel price concessions in April, May, June and July to ease the financial burden on the public.
