Janashakthi Finance wins Silver at BestWeb.LK 2026 for Digital Excellence

Janashakthi Finance PLC has been recognised with the Silver Award in the Best Finance & Insurance Website category at BestWeb.LK 2026, Sri Lanka’s premier web competition.

The accolade recognises the company’s continued focus on digital excellence, innovation, and creating more accessible, inclusive, and convenient digital experiences for its customers.

The recognition reflects Janashakthi Finance’s ongoing investment in strengthening its digital capabilities and its commitment to elevating the way customers engage with the Company online. At the centre of this digital journey is its corporate website, which serves as a key digital touchpoint for customers, investors, and other stakeholders.

Developed as part of Janashakthi Finance’s broader digital transformation journey, the website was designed to reflect the company’s refreshed brand identity while creating a modern, intuitive, and customer-centric experience. Built to accommodate modern customer expectations, the platform enables users to navigate information with ease, explore financial solutions, and connect with the Company through a seamless digital journey.

The website brings together Janashakthi Finance’s portfolio of financial solutions, including Fixed Deposits, Leasing, Gold Loans, Savings, and Islamic Finance, alongside corporate information, career opportunities, branch details, and customer contact channels. With content available in Sinhala, Tamil, and English, and a responsive design across desktop and mobile devices, the platform is structured to make financial information and services more accessible to customers across Sri Lanka.

Commenting on the recognition, Sithambaram Sri Ganendran, Chief Executive Officer of Janashakthi Finance PLC said, “We are proud to receive the Silver Award for Best Finance & Insurance Website at BestWeb.LK 2026. This recognition reflects our continued investment in strengthening our digital capabilities and, more importantly, our focus on building digital experiences to meet the evolving needs of our customers. Our website is an integral part of how we make information and financial services more accessible, convenient, and engaging.

The achievement also recognises the collective contribution of the teams behind the platform and their continued efforts to enhance it. We see this award as encouragement to keep raising the bar in digital innovation and customer experience.”

The website combines functionality, accessibility, and creative execution with the technology and infrastructure required to deliver a reliable and consistent digital experience. Its development has placed emphasis on user-friendly navigation, responsiveness, performance, accessibility and a secure and stable platform, while ensuring a consistent experience across devices.

Dharana Korale Arachchi, Head of Marketing, Janashakthi Finance PLC added, “Our approach to the website centred on creating a strong digital brand experience that makes it easier for customers to discover our offerings, access relevant information, and engage with the brand. We focused on creating a clear, intuitive, and consistent customer journey, while ensuring the website reflects Janashakthi Finance’s recognition of evolving customer needs. As customer expectations and digital behaviours continue to evolve, we remain committed to consistently enhancing our digital presence, creating more meaningful customer interactions, and strengthening how we connect with our audiences.”

The BestWeb.LK competition provides a platform for .lk websites to strengthen online visibility, enhance brand presence, and showcase creativity and excellence in Sri Lankan web development. The Silver Award in the Best Finance & Insurance Website category provides independent recognition of Janashakthi Finance’s digital execution and the collective effort behind the platform.
