5 MW floating solar power plant in Dambulla connected to national grid

Colombo, Sep. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The 5 MW “Diyajanani” floating solar power plant constructed on the Ibbankatuwa Tank in Dambulla was connected to the National Grid this afternoon (18) under the patronage of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

The project was implemented under the Government’s national programme to generate electricity through sustainable energy sources.

Constructed across 10.5 acres, the solar power plant is expected to generate enough electricity annually to meet the needs of approximately 7,500 households.

It is also projected to reduce annual carbon dioxide emissions by around 6,440 metric tonnes and save approximately 2.5 million litres of diesel used for power generation each year.

According to project details, the resulting savings in diesel imports are expected to save the country around USD 3 million in foreign exchange annually.

The project, constructed by Diyajanani (Pvt) Ltd, was developed with the expertise and support of local engineers and technicians. Around 150 people from the surrounding communities were provided employment during the construction period.

Following the unveiling of a commemorative plaque marking the connection of the plant to the National Grid, President Dissanayake inspected the control room and observed its operations.

Several community welfare initiatives were also implemented as part of the project.

A generator and Rs. 800,000 were provided to the Sri Vivekarama Temple for the construction of its Dhamma Hall, while Rs. 2 million was allocated to the fishing community to construct mud ponds and purchase fishing equipment.

A further Rs. 1 million was donated to establish a green park under the Clean Sri Lanka programme.

Meanwhile, Rs. 800,000 was provided to Anakatava Primary School to supply all students with school equipment and other essential materials for the year.

Another Rs. 400,000 was donated to Weera Mohan Jayamaha School and for the implementation of a water project.

Several other community welfare initiatives were also carried out in connection with the project.
