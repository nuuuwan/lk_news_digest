Hayleys Mobility celebrates 1,000 new journeys across Sri Lanka

Hayleys Mobility Limited, the mobility arm of Hayleys Fentons Limited, celebrated the delivery of its 1,000th vehicle within its first nine months of operation. The milestone was marked at a special event held at the OMODA & JAECOO Flagship Store at Altair, Colombo, reflecting the growing confidence Sri Lankan customers have placed in the company’s future-ready mobility solutions.
