Namal, Yoshitha brought before courts

Colombo, Oct. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, who is in remand custody in connection with investigations into the KRRISH deal, was brought before the Colombo Magistrate’s Court today (13).

The case filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) is scheduled to be taken up before Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama.

Namal Rajapaksa was brought to court by prison officers to be produced in the case.

Meanwhile, Yoshitha Rajapaksa, son of former President Mahinda Rajapaksa, was also brought before the Fort Magistrate’s Court today in connection with a separate court proceeding.
