Galle Face child abuse case: Police seek information to identify suspect

Colombo, Sep 24 (Daily Mirror): Sri Lanka Police have launched an investigation to identify and apprehend a suspect allegedly involved in the abduction and sexual abuse of a 13-year-old girl at Galle Face Green in Colombo.

The incident reportedly occurred on January 23, 2026, within the jurisdiction of the Fort Police Division. Following a complaint lodged at the Fort Police Station, police have commenced inquiries to trace the suspect and bring the individual before the law.

As part of the investigation, police have released a composite sketch of the suspect and urged the public for any information that could assist in identifying or locating the individual.
