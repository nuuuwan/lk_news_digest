Govt. targets teacher shortages in North, East

Colombo, Aug. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Government is to give priority to schools facing teacher shortages, particularly in the Northern and Eastern provinces, while expediting the recruitment of teachers for key subjects such as Science and Mathematics.

The matter was discussed at the Ministerial Consultative Committee on Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, chaired by Prime Minister and Education Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya at Parliament.

The Committee highlighted the need to identify schools and areas with acute teacher shortages and take immediate steps to fill vacancies.

Special attention was drawn to schools in difficult and underserved areas, where teacher shortages continue to affect the quality of education.

The Committee also discussed streamlining the teacher transfer system and ensuring that transfers are carried out transparently and in accordance with approved policies.

Officials were also asked to review the criteria used to classify schools according to their level of difficulty and the system of providing hardship allowances to teachers serving in such schools.

The meeting also focused on shortages of academic staff in universities, strengthening the National Institute of Education and improving quality assurance mechanisms in the education sector.

Measures to resolve issues relating to school land and infrastructure, as well as improving facilities for children with special educational needs, were also discussed.

Deputy Minister of Education and Higher Education Dr. Madhura Seneviratne, Deputy Minister of Vocational Education Nalin Hewage, other Ministers, Deputy Ministers, MPs and ministry officials participated in the meeting.
