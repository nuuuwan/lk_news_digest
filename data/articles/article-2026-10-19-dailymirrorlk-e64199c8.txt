LOLC Life Assurance-Pan Asia Bank celebrate 11 years of synergy

Marking over a decade of synergistic growth, LOLC Life Assurance hosted the Sparkle of Excellence felicitation and recognition night to honour the stellar bancassurance performance of 2025.

The event celebrated 11 years of bancassurance partnership with Pan Asia Banking Corporation PLC (PABC), highlighting shared achievements and future operational excellence.

What began in 2015 has evolved into a strong and established partnership, supported by growing business volumes, an expanding branch network and a shared commitment to creating greater value for customers.

Today, the partnership extends across 47 PABC branches, enabling LOLC Life Assurance to serve the customers across Sri Lanka.

The recognition evening brought together senior management, sales leadership, branch representatives and top-performing teams from both organisations. It provided an opportunity to recognise the commitment and achievements of those who have contributed to the continued growth and success of the partnership.

PABC Director and Chief Executive Officer Naleen Edirisinghe said, “We are pleased to celebrate 11 years of a strong and valued partnership with LOLC Life Assurance, an organisation backed by the reputation and strength of the LOLC group, which has made our association a rewarding experience. The progress achieved throughout this journey reflects the strong synergies between our two organisations and our shared commitment to delivering greater value to our customers through bancassurance. As we look ahead, we remain committed to further strengthening this partnership, exploring new opportunities for mutual growth and success, and achieving many more milestones together.”

LOLC Life Assurance Director and LOLC General Insurance Chairman and Principal Officer Kithsiri Gunawardena said, “Our relationship over the past decade with PABC reflects the trust and mutual respect built between our two organisations over the years. Beyond the business relationship, it is the openness, cooperation and strong support extended by both sides that have made this journey particularly meaningful.  We sincerely appreciate the confidence PABC has placed in LOLC Life Assurance and value the strong relationship we have built together. Looking ahead, we remain committed to deepening this partnership, leveraging our collective strengths and further enhancing the value we create for the PABC customers, while delivering greater convenience and a seamless experience.”
