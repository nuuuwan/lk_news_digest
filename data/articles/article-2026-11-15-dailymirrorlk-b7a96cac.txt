Veterinary officers to launch islandwide sick leave campaign on Nov 17

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Government Veterinary Officers' Association has decided to launch an islandwide sick leave campaign on November 17, protesting against staff shortages, inadequate facilities and the failure to provide additional allowances in the 2027 Budget.

Association Chairman Upul Ranjith Kumara said the decision was taken due to the government's failure to address several longstanding issues affecting veterinary officers in the public service.

He said the approved cadre of the public veterinary service was 931 officers, but only around 600 were currently serving.

Despite having to perform duties assigned to the full cadre, the serving officers had not received any additional financial benefits or reasonable recognition for their increased workload, he alleged.

The association had submitted a proposal seeking an additional duty allowance before the presentation of the 2027 Budget. However, the government had failed to provide a satisfactory response to the request, he said.

Kumara warned that if the government continued to ignore their demands following the November 17 trade union action, the association would take further measures.

He said its executive council had unanimously decided to withdraw from all development programmes scheduled for 2027.

The association has also decided to suspend the submission of reports to higher authorities from November and refrain from attending meetings, including regional coordination committee meetings, where political interference is involved.
