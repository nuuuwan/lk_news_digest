Three-Wheeler Fares in  Nuwara Eliya

Three-wheeler drivers in Nuwara Eliya appear to be having a field day, with minimum fares reportedly around Rs. 400. Foreign tourists may be charged even more.

It is not unusual to see tourists bargaining with drivers and, at times, arguments taking place over fares. This can create a very negative impression of Sri Lanka at a time when tourism income is important to our economy and the country is making every effort to attract more visitors.

One of the main issues is the absence of metered three-wheelers in Nuwara Eliya. Passengers are therefore left to negotiate the fare before every journey.

The relevant authorities should intervene without delay and regulate the industry by making fare meters compulsory and ensuring that reasonable charges are displayed and enforced.

Nuwara Eliya is one of Sri Lanka’s leading tourist destinations. We should not allow unreasonable transport fares and unpleasant arguments to damage the image of our country.

The time has come for the authorities to act before serious damage is done to Sri Lanka’s tourism industry.
