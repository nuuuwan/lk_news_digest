Fort Court to hear case against Ranil today

Colombo, Sep. 30 (Daily Mirror) - A case filed under the Offences Against Public Property Act against former President Ranil Wickremesinghe and former Presidential Secretary Saman Ekanayake is scheduled to be taken up today (30) before the Colombo Fort Magistrate’s Court.

The case relates to allegations concerning the alleged misuse or misappropriation of state funds.

The Criminal Investigation Department (CID) had previously informed the court that investigations into the matter had been completed and that relevant investigation extracts had been forwarded to the Attorney General for legal advice.

Following the CID’s submission, Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena ordered that the case be called today to receive the Attorney General’s position and further instructions.
