‘Caution’ level heat alert issued for 13 districts

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Meteorology has issued a ‘Caution’ level heat alert for 13 districts covering the Northern, North-Central and Eastern Provinces, as well as Moneragala, Hambantota and Ratnapura, for today (2).

The Met Department said the heat index, the temperature felt by the human body, is expected to reach ''Caution'' levels in some areas of the affected Provinces and districts during the daytime.

The public has been advised to remain cautious of the effects of increased daytime temperatures.
