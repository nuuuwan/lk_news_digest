Man held for attempting to bribe police to free drug suspect

Colombo, August 04 (Daily Mirror) - A 29-year-old man from Thennekumbura, Kandy, has been arrested for allegedly attempting to bribe the Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of the Theldeniya Police Station with Rs. 500,000 to secure the release of a suspected drug trafficker without producing him before court.

Police said the incident stems from an operation carried out on July 31, during which officers from the Theldeniya Police Station arrested a suspect in possession of 2 grams and 385 milligrams of ICE (crystal methamphetamine). Theldeniya Magistrate's Court subsequently granted a detention order to facilitate further investigations.

Based on information obtained during the investigation, police later arrested a suspected drug trafficker in the Thennekumbura area and seized 10 grams and 795 milligrams of ICE from his possession.

Following the arrest, the 29-year-old suspect allegedly approached the OIC of the Theldeniya Police Station and offered a bribe of Rs. 500,000 in exchange for the trafficker's unlawful release.

Acting on the information, the OIC and a team of officers arrested the suspect at the police station while he was allegedly attempting to hand over the cash. Police also recovered 100 currency notes of Rs. 5,000 each from his possession.

Police said steps have been taken to inform the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption so that further legal action can be instituted against the suspect under the Bribery Act.
