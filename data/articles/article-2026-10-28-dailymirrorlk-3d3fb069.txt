Lubricant traders warned to check Government agreement numbers

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has warned traders selling engine oil, gear oil, lubricants and grease to ensure that all products carry the required Government agreement number, in accordance with the relevant regulations.

Under Direction No. 88, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2327/36 dated April 12, 2023, under Section 10(1) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 9 of 2003, lubricants and grease cannot be imported or manufactured except under a valid agreement entered into with the Government of Sri Lanka in accordance with the applicable legal provisions.

The direction also requires every unit of lubricant or grease stored, stocked, sold, packed, repacked for sale or displayed for sale to carry the number assigned to the relevant Government agreement.

The agreement number must be printed on the container, pack or wrapper, or displayed on a label firmly attached to the product.

The CAA has advised traders to verify the Government agreement number before purchasing stocks, obtain details of suppliers, importers and manufacturers, retain purchase bills and invoices, and check the labels of all lubricant and grease products offered for sale.

The Authority has emphasised that the labelling requirements apply to retailers as well as importers and manufacturers.

The CAA conducts market inspections and special raids to check compliance with the regulations governing lubricants and grease.

Legal action will be taken against responsible parties where violations are identified following the necessary investigations, the Authority has warned.
