Glasgow Commonwealth Games Kalinga Kumarage qualifies for 400m  Semi-finals

Sri Lankan sprinter Kalinga Kumarage booked his place in the men’s 400m semi-finals at the Glasgow Commonwealth Games after an impressive performance in the opening round.

The athletics competition is being held from July 27 to August 2, with Sri Lanka represented by 19 athletes.

Competing in Heat 5 of the first round, Kumarage finished second in a time of 46.28 seconds to secure automatic qualification for the semi-finals. Based on the overall first-round results, he ranked 11th among the qualifiers.

The men’s 400m semi-final is scheduled to take place at 5.40 p.m. (Sri Lanka time) today (30).

Sri Lanka’s other entrant in the event, Kalhara Indupa, was unable to compete after sustaining an injury before the team departed for Glasgow.

In the men’s 200m, Aruna Darshana finished fourth in Heat 7 with a time of 20.96 seconds, while Malith Thamel placed second in Heat 10 in 20.99 seconds. However, neither athlete progressed to the semi-finals.

Meanwhile, Birmingham 2022 Commonwealth Games silver medallist and Paris 2024 Paralympic bronze medallist Palitha Bandara is scheduled to compete in the Men’s Discus Throw F42–44/F61–64 final on the morning of July 30.

Bandara enters the event as one of Sri Lanka’s strongest medal hopes. His personal best in the discus throw stands at 44.20 metres.
