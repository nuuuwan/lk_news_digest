Sinopec promises accurate fuel dispensing

Sinopec Energy Lanka has said that its fuel dispensing equipment is designed to ensure accuracy and reliability at every fuel station.

In a social media post, the company said its precision-engineered fuel pumps are built to measure and dispense fuel accurately, ensuring that customers receive the exact quantity of fuel purchased.

The company noted that its fuel dispensing systems are designed to maintain high standards of precision and operational efficiency, reinforcing its commitment to providing reliable service to consumers.
