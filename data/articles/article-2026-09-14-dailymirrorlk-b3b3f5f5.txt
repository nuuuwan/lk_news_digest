Education must empower future generation to lead social transformation: PM

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister and Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Dr. Harini Amarasuriya said the Government’s objective is to create a learning environment that nurtures future leaders capable of driving positive social transformation through greater investment in education.

The Prime Minister made these remarks while attending an event held at Prince of Wales’ College, Moratuwa, today (14) to mark the 150th anniversary of Prince and Princess of Wales’ Colleges.

She said education should produce a future generation capable of understanding society and providing leadership to steer it towards a better and more progressive path.

“At this transformative moment, as new educational reforms are being introduced to achieve this important objective, schools such as Prince of Wales’ College have a special role to play,” she said.

The Prime Minister said the Government has prioritised human resource development, with particular emphasis on education.

“We are systematically increasing the allocation for education from one budget to the next. However, this process must be carried out in a manner that encompasses all the principles embodied in the vision that gave rise to free education,” she said.

She also called for the support of all stakeholders in achieving these objectives.
