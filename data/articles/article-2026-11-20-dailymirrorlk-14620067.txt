CEA warns of deteriorating air quality in several areas

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Environmental Authority (CEA) has warned of deteriorating air quality in several parts of the country, urging vulnerable groups to take necessary precautions as slightly unhealthy conditions are expected to continue over the next few days.

In a statement issued today (20), the CEA said elevated levels of airborne particulate matter, including PM2.5 and PM10, had been recorded at several air quality monitoring stations across the island.

The affected areas include Jaffna, Kilinochchi, Mannar and Mullaitivu in the Northern Province, as well as Anuradhapura, Puttalam, Trincomalee, Colombo, Kandy and Chilaw.

The Authority attributed the deterioration to wind currents carrying increased concentrations of particulate matter into Sri Lanka from outside the country.

According to the CEA, the current air quality levels fall under the slightly unhealthy category of Sri Lanka’s Air Quality Index, potentially posing health risks to sensitive groups.

Children, older adults and individuals with existing respiratory conditions are among those advised to take precautions during this period.

The CEA further cautioned that similar air quality conditions are likely to prevail across several parts of the country over the next few days.
