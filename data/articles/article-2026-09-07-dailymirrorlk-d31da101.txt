Manhunt contestants join Colombo peace parade

Contestants from around the world participating in the Manhunt International 2026 pageant take part in a special Peace Parade themed “One World – One Humanity – World Peace” along the streets of Colombo yesterday (06). Around 35 contestants, dressed in colourful national costumes representing their respective countries, joined the parade to promote a message of global unity, harmony and peace.

The parade was held as a precursor event to the grand finale of the 24th edition of Manhunt International, which is scheduled to be held on Wednesday, September 9, at Cinnamon Life – City of Dreams, Colombo. Pix by Waruna Wanniarachchi
