Seven new envoys present credentials to President

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Seven newly appointed Ambassadors and High Commissioners presented their Letters of Credence to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake at a ceremony held at the Presidential Secretariat today (17).

The newly appointed envoys represent the Republic of Korea, Switzerland, the European Union, Egypt, Laos, Namibia and Singapore.

The envoys, in order of precedence, are Kim Youn-Jung of the Republic of Korea, Daniel Hunn of Switzerland, Sylvia Hartleif of the European Union and Ahmed El-Sayed Abdel Halim Amer Helal of Egypt.

The other three are Vithaya Xayavong of Laos, Wing Commander Alex L. Tukuhupwele (Retd.) of Namibia and Patrick Daniel of Singapore.

While the envoys from South Korea, Switzerland, the European Union and Egypt are based in Colombo, the representatives of Laos and Namibia are based in New Delhi. Singapore’s High Commissioner-designate is based in Singapore.

The respective governments and the European Union appointed the envoys to represent them in Sri Lanka with the agreement of the Government of Sri Lanka.

Following the formal presentation of their credentials, President Dissanayake held cordial discussions with the newly appointed diplomats.

Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath and Secretary to the President Dr Nandika Sanath Kumanayake were also present at the ceremony.
