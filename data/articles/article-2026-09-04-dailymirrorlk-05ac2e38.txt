Man arrested for circulating ex-wife’s nude photographs on social media

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - A 36-year-old man has been arrested in connection with the alleged circulation of nude photographs of a woman on social media, police said.

The suspect was arrested by officers attached to the Kandy Sub-Unit of the Computer Crime Investigation Division of the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) during an operation in the Ginigathena area yesterday (September 3).

The arrest was made following a complaint lodged with the police regarding the alleged distribution of the photographs on social media.

Police said preliminary investigations had revealed that the suspect and the woman had previously been married and were legally separated at the time of the alleged offence.
