Gun discovered after clash between Chinese nationals at Port City

Colombo, July 23 (Daily Mirror) - A pistol and a communication device have been found in a vehicle linked to a clash between two groups of Chinese nationals at the Colombo Port City, police said.

According to police, two Chinese nationals were injured in the clash and admitted to a private hospital for treatment.

Police further said that another Chinese national, who is believed to have been injured during the altercation, was allegedly taken away from the scene by members of the opposing group involved in the dispute.

A search of a vehicle found at the scene led to the discovery of the pistol and the communication device.

The Colombo Harbour Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
