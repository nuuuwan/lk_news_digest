Station Masters express condolences over Spain train accident

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Railway Station Masters’ Association has expressed its deepest condolences to Spain following a tragic train accident that reportedly claimed several lives and left many others injured.

In a letter addressed to the Ambassador of Spain, the association said it was deeply saddened by the loss of lives and extended its sympathies to the bereaved families, injured passengers, affected railway employees and the people of Spain.

The association said railway professionals in Sri Lanka understood the devastating consequences of such tragedies, adding that the loss of lives in a railway accident was not merely a national tragedy but a moment of grief shared by the international railway community.

It also requested the Spanish Ambassador to convey its condolences and solidarity to the relevant Spanish authorities, the Spanish railway community and families who had lost their loved ones.

The association wished the injured passengers a speedy and complete recovery and expressed solidarity with the people of Spain during the period of mourning.
