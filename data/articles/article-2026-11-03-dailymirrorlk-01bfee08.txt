Alleged misuse of NLB funds: CIABOC fails to appear in Ravi Karunanayake case

The case against former Finance Minister and Member of Parliament Ravi Karunanayake, who is accused of causing a loss to the Government by paying the salaries of three employees, recruited purportedly for a special project of the Ministry of Finance, using funds of the National Lotteries Board, was postponed until February 23 by Colombo Additional Magistrate Liyanwarsha Vithana.

The Magistrate also ordered that notice be issued on the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC), as no representative of the Commission appeared before court when the case was taken up today.

The allegations relate to the period from January 12, 2015, to May 22, 2017, during which Karunanayake served as the Minister of Finance. It is alleged that he caused a loss to the Government and conferred an unlawful benefit on three individuals by recruiting Kaluaarchchige Thilan Niyomal Perera and D.M. Savini Peiris as Coordinating Officers and Sumith Priyantha Sigera as a Management Assistant to the National Lotteries Board and paying their salaries and allowances using the Board's funds.

The suspect was arrested by the CIABOC in connection with the allegations and produced before court. He was subsequently released on bail on August 31.

Former National Lotteries Board Chairperson Shyamila Perera had been named as the first suspect in the complaint. She was also previously produced before court and released on bail.

Senior Counsel Asoka Weerasooriya, together with counsel Sanjay Marabe and Pasan Karunarathna, appeared for the second suspect, Ravi Karunanayake.
