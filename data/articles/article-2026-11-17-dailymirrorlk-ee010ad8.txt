Court orders report on market value of ‘Kavantissa’s pair of tusks

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Maho Magistrate’s Court has ordered the wildlife authorities to submit a report on the market value of the tusks of iconic tusker ‘Kavantissa’ through the Director of the Government Valuation Department on November 25.

Magistrate Anuruddhika Seneviratne issued the directive to Galgamuwa Wildlife Range Officer Attorney-at-Law Chathura Gunaratne. Also, the Magistrate ordered Professor P.P.M.A. Wijesundara of the University of Peradeniya to submit the final post-mortem report on the tusker’s death to the court the same day.

The court orders were made following submissions by Senior Attorney-at-Law Wijitha Wickramanayake, who appeared on behalf of the Sri Lanka branch of the Protect Elephant World Global Association.

Presenting his case, Senior Counsel Wickramanayake cited a legal precedent from the Kekirawa Magistrate’s Court concerning the electrocution of the tusker ‘Deegadanthu 1’. He said that the Government Valuation Department had valued ‘Deegadanthu 1’ at Rs. 90 million, and, citing newspaper reports, noted that the Attorney General had subsequently instructed the Director General of Wildlife to prosecute the suspect in that case, an insurance company manager, under the Offences Against Public Property Act.

Mr. Wickramanayake urged the court to take note of widespread public speculation suggesting that Kavantissa died due to drug toxicity while under the care and supervision of veterinary surgeons.

Currently, two suspects, including a Skills Development Officer, remain in remand custody in connection with the shooting of the iconic tusker.
