Sri Lanka Women storm into Asian Games gold medal match

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s women’s cricket team booked their place in the gold medal match of the 2026 Asian Games with a commanding eight-wicket victory over arch-rivals Pakistan in the first semi-final on September 20.

Batting first after Sri Lanka won the toss and elected to bowl, Pakistan posted a strong 177 for four in their allotted 20 overs. Ayesha Zafar top-scored with a blistering 71 off 40 balls, while Shawal Zulfiqar made 54 off 50 deliveries. The pair put together a crucial 115-run partnership to lay the foundation for Pakistan’s competitive total.

Sri Lanka, however, produced a dominant response, chasing down the target in just 17.5 overs to finish on 178 for two. The victory was secured with 13 balls to spare.

Captain Chamari Athapaththu led the successful chase with a superb 83 off 50 balls, striking seven fours and six sixes. Hasini Perera provided valuable support with an unbeaten 42 off 26 balls as Sri Lanka maintained control throughout the chase.

The victory sends Sri Lanka into the women’s cricket gold medal match at the Asian Games, while Pakistan will now compete in the bronze medal playoff.
