LB Finance completes incorporation of Philippine subsidiary

LB Finance PLC yesterday announced it completed the incorporation of its wholly owned subsidiary in the Philippines, marking the company’s entry into the Southeast Asian financial services market.

The subsidiary, LB Finance Philippines Inc., received its Certificate of Incorporation and authority to operate as a finance company dated September 1, 2026, LB Finance said in a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange yesterday.

The documents were received by the Sri Lankan non-bank financial institution on September 17.

The move follows approval granted by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka in December 2025 for LB Finance to establish the subsidiary with an initial investment of US$ 650,000.

LB Finance had earlier said the Philippine operation would engage in the provision of financial services.
