Golden canopy of Dalada Maligawa to be unveiled tomorrow

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The restored golden canopy above the chamber housing the Sacred Tooth Relic at the Sri Dalada Maligawa in Kandy is scheduled to be unveiled tomorrow (20).

Restoration work on the canopy began in 2025 after the gold plates covering the structure became discoloured and deteriorated over the years, causing it to lose its original lustre and beauty.

The latest restoration has been carried out using modern engineering technology and mosaic gold leaf, with the expertise of several professionals specialising in gold-related work.

The project was undertaken under the guidance and advice of the Most Venerable Mahanayaka Theros of the Malwathu and Asgiri Chapters, under the leadership of the Diyawadana Nilame of the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. The Wat Marp Jan Monastery in Thailand also supported the restoration.

The golden canopy was originally commissioned and gifted to the temple in 1987 by the late Ranasinghe Premadasa, who was then Prime Minister of Sri Lanka.

The gift was intended to enhance the beauty of the temple's interior and frontal areas while also strengthening security around the sacred chamber.

The State Engineering Corporation undertook the original design and construction, with local and foreign engineering teams working together to complete the challenging task.

The canopy has since become one of the most prominent features of the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, attracting the attention of local and foreign devotees and tourists.

It has undergone restoration on two previous occasions, in 2002 and 2018, making the latest project its third major restoration.
