Man shot dead, wife injured in Kalutara shooting

Colombo, October 9 (Daily Mirror) - A man was shot dead and his wife injured in a shooting near a hotel on Nagoda Road in Kalutara, police said.

The shooting was carried out using a T-56 assault rifle, with the suspect reportedly arriving at the scene on a motorcycle before opening fire.

Kalutara South Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
