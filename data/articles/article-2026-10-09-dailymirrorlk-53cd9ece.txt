Damaging Metro buses could lead to public property charges: Minister

Colombo, Oct. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Private bus owners or drivers who attack Metro buses could face legal action for damaging public property, Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake said.

Speaking to the media, the Minister said around 100 Metro buses were currently in operation, but the majority of private bus operators had not caused any obstructions. He said only a few private bus owners or drivers were involved in such incidents.

He urged private bus operators to work cooperatively with the Metro Bus Service, noting that the service would be opened to private sector participation within a year.
