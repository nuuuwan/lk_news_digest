JVP led government is more right wing than RW: Sajith

Colombo, July 13 (Daily Mirror) - The present government led by the Janatha Vimukthi Peramuna (JVP) is more right wing than former President Ranil Wickremesinghe, Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa said today.

"The JVP led government pledged to amend the agreement which the Sri Lankan government got into with the IMF in its manifesto. However, it seems that the government is becoming more right wing than former President Wickremesinghe as no changes were made to the IMF agreement," Premadasa told a public gathering in Pannala.
