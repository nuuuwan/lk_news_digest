Heavy rainfall of around 100 mm expected in five provinces

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Heavy rainfall of around 100 mm is likely at some places in the North Central, Central, Sabaragamuwa, Western and North Western provinces today, the Meteorological Department said.

According to the weather advisory issued at 6.00 a.m. today (13), the public has been urged to take adequate precautions to minimise possible damage caused by temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

The authorities also advised the public to remain vigilant during periods of thunderstorms and take necessary safety measures.
