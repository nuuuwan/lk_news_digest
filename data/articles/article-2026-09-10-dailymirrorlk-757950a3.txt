Former Air Chief and astrologer Sumanadasa at Bribery Commission

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Former Commander of the Sri Lanka Air Force, Air Chief Marshal Roshan Goonetileke, arrived at the Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID) this morning (10).

His appearance follows a notice instructing him to present himself before the division at 9:30 AM today.

According to reports, the former Air Force Commander was summoned to record a statement in connection with the ongoing investigations into the controversial MiG aircraft transaction.

Meanwhile, former royal astrologer Sumanadasa Abeygunawardena also arrived at the Bribery Commission this morning to record a statement in connection with an ongoing investigation concerning the 'Siriliya Saviya' organization.
