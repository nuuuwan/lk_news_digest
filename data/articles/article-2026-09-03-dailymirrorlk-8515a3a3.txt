New Court of Appeal Judge Sajeewani Dilka Lakmali sworn in

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Sajeewani Dilka Lakmali Karunannayake was sworn in as a Judge of the Court of Appeal before President Anura Kumara Dissanayake at the Presidential Secretariat this afternoon (3).

She previously served as an Additional Solicitor General at the Attorney General’s Department.

Her appointment has been made to fill a vacancy on the Court of Appeal.

Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake was also present at the swearing-in ceremony.
