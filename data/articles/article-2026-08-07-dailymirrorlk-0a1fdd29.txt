The transport system needs an upgrade !

Sri Lanka is a country that needs to be more serious about road discipline. One has to pray that they get back home in one piece due to reckless driving practices that put many people at risk. The spate of road accidents on Accident 1st, a Facebook page dedicated to make people aware of road accidents speak volumes about  drivers’ lack of patience. People sometimes wonder whether they have obtained licenses in a proper manner when looking at their reckless driving.

When looking at reckless driving practices,  it becomes more clear that issuance of driving licenses should be regulated. It goes beyond passing an exam or checking whether the new learner is abiding by road signs while the examiner is seated on the other side. It has to go beyond passing safety cones for three wheelers because most three wheeler drivers drive carelessly. They sometimes don’t seem to care about the lives of their passengers.

Recently two motorcyclists succumbed to injuries after they came at high speed and collided with a trailer truck. This shows why there has to be a system to introduce speed limits in every town, street and junction. It may be annoying for some drivers,  but there has to be a system to minimise road accidents. Many European countries follow the speed limit system,  and drivers are fined the moment they cross these limits. Perhaps the time is right to fully automate the transport system to safeguard lives of people.
