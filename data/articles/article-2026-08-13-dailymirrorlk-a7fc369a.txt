Seylan Cards rewards programme offers chance to win return air tickets to Malaysia

Seylan Bank has launched its latest Seylan Cards Redeem & Win promotion, giving Seylan Cardholders another exciting reason to make the most of their accumulated reward points.

Running from 1 July to 31 August 2026, the campaign offers Seylan Cards Rewards Programme members the opportunity to win two return air tickets to Malaysia, courtesy of Classic Travels.

Designed to encourage greater participation in the Seylan Cards Rewards Programme, the promotion allows every Seylan Visa Infinite, Visa Signature, and Visa Platinum Cardholder along with Seylan World Mastercard Cardholders, who redeem any amount of reward points during the campaign period to be automatically entered into the grand prize draw. Customers can enjoy a wide range of redemption options available through the rewards platform which can be accessed at www.seylancardsrewards.com

Commenting on the campaign, Ruchith Liyanage, Head of Cards at Seylan Bank PLC said, “At Seylan Bank, we continuously strive to reward our Cardholders with meaningful experiences that extend beyond everyday banking. Through this campaign, we hope to encourage more customers to explore the value of the Seylan Cards Rewards Programme while giving one lucky winner the opportunity to enjoy an unforgettable trip to Malaysia.”

The Seylan Cards Rewards Programme enables Cardholders to redeem their accumulated reward points across a diverse range of lifestyle, dining, shopping, travel, and partner offers, ensuring that every transaction brings customers closer to exciting rewards.

The Redeem & Win campaign further underscores Seylan Bank’s commitment to rewarding customer loyalty by enhancing its Cardholder value proposition with exclusive rewards, special privileges, and memorable lifestyle experiences that go beyond everyday banking.
