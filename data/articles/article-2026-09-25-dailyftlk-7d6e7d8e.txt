Amana Bank strengthens footprint with 49th Self Banking Centre in Eravur Pattu

Opening of the Eravur Pattu Self Banking Centre by Amana Bank’s Assistant Vice President Arshad Adhnan (right) along with Eravur Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha Chairman

M. Muralitharan, Eravur Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha Member A.S.M. Rasmi, and Eravur Branch Manager B.M. Mahir

Amana Bank recently opened its 49th Self Banking Centre in Eravur Pattu, further strengthening the bank’s presence in the region and bringing greater banking convenience to customers in the area. Strategically located at Meerakerny Road, Michnagar, Eravur, the new Self Banking Centre provides convenient 24x7 access for cash withdrawals, cash deposits and cheque deposits for individuals, businesses and the wider community in and around Eravur Pattu.

With this opening, Amana Bank’s presence in the Eastern Province has expanded to 14 Self Banking Centres, complementing its nine fully fledged branches across the province.

The opening ceremony was attended by distinguished guests including Eravur Pattu Pradeshiyasaba Chairman M. Muralitharan, together with community leaders, business representatives, customers, and well-wishers from the area. Representing Amana Bank at the occasion were Assistant Vice President - SME and Regional Branches Arshad Adhnan, Head of Retail Sales and Acquisition Channels Mohamed Asmil, Manager – Offsite SBC Operations Imran Mohamed, Branch Manager Eravur Mahir Badurdeen,  Branch Manager Kaduruwela Jiffry Razik, and other officials of the Bank.

Speaking at the event, Assistant Vice President – SME and Regional Branches Arshad Adhnan stated, “The opening of our 49th Self Banking Centre in Eravur Pattu marks another step in bringing convenient and accessible banking services closer to the communities we serve. With this facility, we aim to support the evolving banking needs of individuals and businesses in the region and strengthen our relationship with the people of Eravur Pattu.”

Amana Bank PLC is a stand-alone institution licensed by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka and listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange, with the Jeddah-based IsDB Group being the principal shareholder of the Bank. The IsDB Group is a ‘AAA’ rated multilateral development financial institution with a membership of 57 countries. Testifying to its position as a leading practitioner of the non-interest-based banking model, Amana Bank was recognised amongst the Top 50 Strongest Islamic Banks in the World by The Asian Banker.

Amana Bank does not have any subsidiaries, associates, or affiliated institutions apart from its engagement with OrphanCare as its Founding Sponsor.
