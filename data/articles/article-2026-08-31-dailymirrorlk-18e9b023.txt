No special trains for today’s return rush: Station Masters

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - Long-weekend commuters warned to make alternative travel arrangementsThe Railway Station Master’s Union has raised concerns over the railway’s inability to operate special train services today (Monday (31)) to accommodate the large number of passengers expected to return to Colombo and other urban centres after the long weekend.

Association President Sumedha Somaratne, speaking at a press conference, urged passengers who had travelled to their hometowns for the holidays to make alternative transport arrangements, warning that additional train services would not be available tomorrow.

A large number of people left Colombo and other cities for their villages last Wednesday, taking advantage of the two holidays falling during the week.

With the long weekend ending today, thousands of passengers are expected to begin their return journey today.

However, the Station Masters’ Union said the railway currently faces difficulties in providing special services to handle the anticipated surge.

The warning could leave holidaymakers facing overcrowded regular trains and limited transport options as they attempt to return to work and other commitments after the extended break.

Somaratne therefore called on passengers not to rely on special railway services and to make necessary arrangements in advance.
