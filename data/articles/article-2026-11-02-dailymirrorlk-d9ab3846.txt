Three-wheeler drivers seek Rs. 10 fare hike

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The National Joint Three-Wheeler Drivers and Industrial Workers' Association has proposed increasing three-wheeler fares following the latest fuel price hike, its General Secretary L. Rohana Perera said.

Accordingly, the association has proposed increasing the fare for the first kilometre from Rs. 110 to Rs. 120, while the fare for each subsequent kilometre would be increased from Rs. 90 to Rs. 100.

Perera said the association had previously requested a fare revision following an earlier fuel price increase but had received no response.

"Fuel prices have increased by Rs. 25, while the prices of other essential goods have also gone up. Even finding gas has become difficult. We are being pushed into an increasingly difficult situation, and consumers need to understand this," he said.

He said the association would submit its proposal to the National Transport Commission (NTC) and await a response over the next few days.

If the authorities fail to respond favourably, the association would discuss the matter with the relevant institutions responsible for adjusting fare meters and take steps to revise the fares, he added.
