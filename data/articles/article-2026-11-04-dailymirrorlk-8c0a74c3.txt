Chinese vegetable prices surge as greenhouse cultivation declines

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The wholesale prices of several Chinese vegetables have increased sharply due to a decline in greenhouse cultivation in the Welimada, Haputale and Nuwara Eliya areas, traders said.

According to wholesalers at the Nuwara Eliya Specialised Economic Centre, the price of a kilo of broccoli had increased to Rs. 3,600 as of yesterday (3).

The wholesale price of red bell pepper had also risen to between Rs. 2,500 and Rs. 2,600 a kilo, while yellow bell pepper was selling at between Rs. 2,100 and Rs. 2,600.

Meanwhile, basil was priced at Rs. 600 to Rs. 700 a kilo, red cabbage at Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,100, and mint at Rs. 900 to Rs. 1,000.

Traders said these vegetables are mainly cultivated in the Welimada, Haputale and Nuwara Eliya areas, adding that the reduction in greenhouse cultivation had led to a drop in supply and pushed up wholesale prices.
