CaFFE holds digital voter education programme in Jaffna

The Campaign for Free and Fair Elections (CaFFE) on Wednesday conducted a digital voter education programme in the Jaffna District using the “Learn, Play, Vote” gaming application developed with the support of LIRNEasia.

The programme, aimed at educating young voters on the electoral process through an interactive digital platform, was held under the leadership of CaFFE Executive Director Surangi Ariyawansha.

Former Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation News Director and digital media trainer Junaid M. Harish, Head of the Department of Political Science at the University of Jaffna and President of the University’s Teachers’ Association Dr. Vigneswaran, and former Head of the Department of Economics and Senior Lecturer at the University of Jaffna C. Udayakumar served as resource persons.

Senior Research Manager at LIRNEasia Isuru Samarathunga attended the event as the chief guest.

Participants were introduced to key aspects of the electoral process through the innovative digital platform, which combines learning with interactive gameplay to promote greater awareness of democratic participation among young people.
