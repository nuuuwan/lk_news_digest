SLT 3Q shareholder profit jumps 69% to Rs.3.7bn

Sri Lanka Telecom PLC’s (SLT) profit attributable to the shareholders rose 69.4 percent to Rs.3.70 billion in the September quarter, helped by the stronger operating earnings and a lower tax charge, while the mobile subsidiary Mobitel’s net profit nearly tripled.

The group revenue rose 10.9 percent to Rs.32.69 billion in the three months ended September 30, 2026, according to the interim financial statements filed with the Colombo Stock Exchange.

The earnings per share climbed to Rs.2.05, from Rs.1.21 a year earlier.

The direct costs rose 8.8 percent, slower than revenue, lifting the gross profit by 13.3 percent to Rs.15.71 billion.

The operating profit increased 25.8 percent to Rs.5.33 billion, widening the operating margin to 16.3 percent, from 14.4 percent.

The higher interest income of Rs.458 million, up from Rs.29 million a year earlier, helped offset a foreign exchange loss of Rs.195 million, compared with a gain of Rs.159 million.

The group pre-tax profit rose 17.1 percent to Rs.4.47 billion. The income tax charge fell to Rs.766 million, from Rs.1.63 billion, accounting for more than half the increase in the group net profit, which rose 69.3 percent to Rs.3.71 billion.

Mobitel’s quarterly revenue climbed 20.6 percent to Rs.14.54 billion, while the operating profit rose 92.8 percent to Rs.2.38 billion, SLT said in its earnings release.

The mobile operator’s net profit rose 195.5 percent to Rs.1.85 billion.

At the parent company, revenue rose 11.7 percent to Rs.20.55 billion and the operating profit increased 29 percent to Rs.3.20 billion.

The standalone pre-tax profit fell 8.9 percent to Rs.2.34 billion as other income declined and the company recorded a foreign exchange loss.

A lower tax charge lifted the standalone net profit by 26.5 percent to Rs.1.80 billion.

For the first nine months of 2026, the group revenue rose 11.1 percent to Rs.94.00 billion and the profit attributable to the shareholders increased 59.4 percent to Rs.10.30 billion.

The earnings per share rose to Rs.5.71, from Rs.3.58. The net cash from the operations increased 21.9 percent to Rs.31.61 billion, while the cash spent on property, plant and equipment nearly doubled to Rs.18.40 billion.
