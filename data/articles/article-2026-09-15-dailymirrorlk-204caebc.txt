Two rivers on flood alert

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Irrigation Department has issued alerts for the Maguru Ganga and Attanagalu Oya as several parts of the country recorded heavy rainfall over the past 24 hours.

According to the Department’s river and rainfall monitoring report issued today (15), most major river basins remained at normal levels, while water levels in the Maguru Ganga and Attanagalu Oya areas were recorded at alert status.

The highest 24-hour rainfall of 131.7 mm was recorded at Glencourse, according to measurements taken at various Irrigation Department and Ceylon Electricity Board stations.

The Department continues to monitor river levels and rainfall across major basins, with the information being used to assess potential flood risks and support disaster management operations.

Authorities have urged the public in low-lying areas to remain alert to changes in river levels, particularly in areas where alerts have been issued.
