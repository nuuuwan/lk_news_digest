Sri Lanka Treasury bill yields rise on shorter term, less bills sold

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Treasury bill yields rose on the shorter tenor at Wednesday’s auction, with 86.48 billion rupees of bills sold of an offered 100 billion, data from the Public Debt Management Office showed.

The 3-month bill was up 2 basis points at 9.34 percent, with 50 billion rupees offered and 68.09 billion rupees sold.

The 6-month bill was unchanged at 9.44 percent, with 25 billion rupees offered and 9.34 billion rupees sold.

The 12-month bill was unchanged at 9.95 percent, with 25 billion rupees offered and 9.03 billion rupees sold.

The 6- and 12-month bills are available on tap. (Colombo/Oct28/2026)
