No impact on Kandy Esala Perahera from Animal Welfare Act: Governor

There is no need for public concern that the proposed Animal Welfare Act will affect the Kandy Esala Perahera or other traditional cultural events, Central Province Governor Dr. Harsha Suranga Fernando said.

Assuming office, the Governor said Cabinet approval had been granted for the Animal Welfare Act and stressed that its provisions do not seek to interfere with cultural events.

He said the Act contains specific provisions relating to elephants and other animals, while making clear that there would be no restrictions imposed on cultural events such as the Kandy Esala Perahera.

“There is no need to have unnecessary fears or create issues over the Act based on views circulated by certain groups with negative attitudes,” he said.

The Governor described the Kandy Esala Perahera as a world-renowned cultural festival and assured that none of its traditional events would be reduced or affected.

He added that the Act would instead contribute to the protection and further development of cultural events while ensuring animal welfare.
