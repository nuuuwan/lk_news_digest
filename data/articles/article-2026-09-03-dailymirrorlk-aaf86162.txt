Zellers pioneers country’s first white chocolate centre-filled range

Zellers has pioneered Sri Lanka’s first White Chocolate Centre-Filled range, creating a new chocolate experience that combines creamy white chocolate, indulgent centre fillings and contrasting textures in every bite.

The introduction marks another step in Zellers’ journey as a chocolate innovator, transforming conventional white chocolate into something more surprising and indulgent. Inspired by globally trending centre-filled chocolates and dessert-led flavours, Zellers has brought this experience to Sri Lankan consumers with a distinctly local appeal.

At the heart of the range is a richer, milkier and smoother white chocolate shell, complemented by exciting centre fillings and added textures. The result is a melt-in-the-mouth experience with an unexpected burst of flavour at the centre.

The range debuts in two dessert-inspired variants, Red Velvet Cookie Cream Filled White Chocolate and Blueberry Cheesecake Cream Filled White Chocolate.

The Red Velvet variant combines smooth white chocolate with a luscious red velvet cookie cream centre and crunchy red velvet cookie crumbles dipped. Beyond taste, its striking appearance makes it an eye-catching chocolate experience, bringing together colour, creaminess and crunch in a format inspired by the popular red velvet dessert.

The Blueberry Cheesecake variant pairs creamy white chocolate with a rich blueberry cheesecake cream filling, balancing fruity, tangy sweetness with smooth cheesecake indulgence. Its distinctive purple-toned appearance adds another visual dimension, making the chocolate as appealing to look at as it is to experience.

Together, the two variants are designed to create a genuine “wow” moment from the first bite. The combination of smooth white chocolate, hidden fillings, vibrant appearances and contrasting textures brings an element of discovery that moves beyond the conventional chocolate experience.

The new range also reflects changing preferences among younger consumers and trend seekers who increasingly look for new textures, flavours and visually engaging products. By combining globally popular dessert-inspired concepts with an accessible chocolate format, Zellers continues to bring international chocolate trends closer to Sri Lankan consumers.

As one of the first local brands to consistently challenge conventional chocolate experiences, Zellers continues to position itself as a pioneer in flavour and format innovation. The introduction of Sri Lanka’s first White Chocolate Centre-Filled range gives a new twist to familiar chocolate indulgence and opens up fresh possibilities within the local category.

With this latest innovation, Zellers once again demonstrates its ambition to challenge the market, create unexpected chocolate experiences and continue shaping what comes next for Sri Lankan chocolate consumers.
