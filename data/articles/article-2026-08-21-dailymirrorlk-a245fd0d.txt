Sri Lankan shooters return home with 10 medals from International Championships

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s national shooting team returned to the island after an impressive international campaign, winning a total of 10 medals — three gold, four silver and three bronze — at two major shooting championships held in Indonesia and the Philippines.

Representing the National Rifle Association of Sri Lanka (NRA-SL), the Sri Lankan contingent competed against athletes from nearly 40 countries and produced a series of outstanding performances, bringing further recognition to the country on the international stage.

At the Bali Open IPSC Handgun & PCC Championship 2026 in Indonesia, the Sri Lankan shooters secured six medals, including two gold, one silver and three bronze.

One of the highlights of the competition was Sri Lanka’s performance in the PCC Optics – Junior category, where the young shooters achieved a remarkable clean sweep of the podium. Sri Lankan athletes claimed first, second and third places, underlining the emerging talent and potential within the country’s shooting community.

The Sri Lankan team continued its medal-winning run at the Kadayawan Festival Shooting Championship 2026 (Senator Bato Cup) in the Philippines.

The contingent won a further four medals — one gold and three silver — taking Sri Lanka’s overall tally from the two championships to 10 medals.

The combined achievement of three gold, four silver and three bronze medals represents a significant success for Sri Lankan shooting at the international level.

The victorious shooters received a warm welcome upon their return to Sri Lanka at Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake.

A large gathering, including National Rifle Association of Sri Lanka President Air Vice Marshal Vajira Senadheera, association officials and parents of the athletes, was present at the airport to welcome the team.
