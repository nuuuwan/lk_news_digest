Home Lands breaks ground on ‘Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo’

From left: Amaya Herath, Executive Director of Home Lands Group; Mahela Jayawardene, Home Lands Brand Ambassador; Harsha Amarasekera, Chairman of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission; Dr. Susil Ranasinghe, Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply; Nalin Herath, Chairman and Managing Director of Home Lands Group; Duminda Hulangamuwa, Senior Economic Adviser to the President; Eng. Kumudu Lal Bogahawatta, Secretary to the Ministry of Housing, Construction and Water Supply; Xiong Hongfeng, Managing Director of CHEC Port City Colombo (Pvt) Ltd; and Harshani Herath, Director of Home Lands Group

Home Lands Group officially commenced construction of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo with a ceremonial groundbreaking held at its construction site in Central Park district, Port City Colombo.

The day’s proceedings began in the morning with the receiving of blessings from religious leaders followed by the laying of the foundation stone and the ceremonial commencement of construction activities in accordance with auspicious times. The evening celebrations brought togetherdistinguished dignitaries including government officials, investors, business leaders, stakeholders, and members of the media to commemorate this significant milestone and celebrate yet another remarkable chapter in the Home Lands journey.

The ceremonial groundbreaking was officially carried out by distinguished invitees together with the Chairman, and the Management of Home Lands Group, symbolically marking the commencement of construction of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo. Beyond breaking ground, the ceremony ushered in a new era for Sri Lanka’s real estate sector and the beginning of what is set to become The Indian Ocean’s Next Wonder.

Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo is an ultra-luxury twin-tower development, the most iconic landmark within Port City Colombo. Inspired by an internationally renowned ultra-luxury lifestyle brand, Central Park Boulevard introduces a new benchmark for luxury living, with Home Lands leading the way as the pioneer of this unique concept in Sri Lanka. Located within the Central Park District of Port City Colombo, the development overlooks and merges with 40-acres of vast modern lush green Central Parklandwith landscaped gardens, scenic waterways and tranquil pathways, offering residents an unparalleled blend of nature, exclusivity, and high-rise sophistication.

The landmark development will feature an exceptional collection of luxury residences, including deluxe apartments, sky villas, penthouses, andEmerald Mansions, complemented by an extensive array of world-class amenities designed to deliver an elevated lifestyle unlike anything previously experienced in Sri Lanka.

The groundbreaking follows an extraordinary period of momentum for Home Lands. Earlier this month, the company successfully hosted the Grand Launch Weekend of Central Park Boulevard, widely recognised as Sri Lanka’s largest real estate launch event. The three-day series of events attracted investors, prospective homeowners, business leaders, and industry stakeholders from across Sri Lanka and overseas. This was followed by the opening of the Central Park Boulevard Sales Gallery at Port City Colombo, where invitees were given an immersive preview of the development, reinforcing the overwhelming market response and setting the stage for the next chapter of this landmark development.

Together, these milestones reflect not only the strong market confidence in Central Park Boulevard but also Home Lands’ financial strength and capability to independently undertake and deliver a development of this scale and value, further reinforcing its position as Sri Lanka’s market leader.

Construction of Central Park Boulevard is scheduled for completion within 40 months. This ambitious timeline reflects Home Lands’ strength as a fully integrated developer with an exceptional track record of delivering high-quality developments on schedule.

Having successfully delivered more than 3,750 homes across Sri Lanka, the company has consistently demonstrated its capability to execute landmark projects efficiently while maintaining the highest standards of quality, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction.

With construction now officially underway, Central Park Boulevard represents far more than another development. It stands as a symbol of Home Lands’ commitment to delivering world-class developments and its vision in further elevating Sri Lanka as the world’s best real estate investment destination.
