Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Overseas Realty and Hayleys with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Overseas Realty, Ambeon Capital and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka, Nations Trust Bank and Dialog Axiata whilst retail interest was noted in Waskaduwa Beach Resort, LOLC Finance and Singer Finance.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. Real Estate sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Overseas Realty) whilst the sector index gained 1.95%. The share price of Overseas Realty gained Rs 0.80 (1.67%) to close at Rs. 48.60.

Transportation sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka) whilst the sector index increased by 1.42%. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka increased by Rs. 2.00 (1.29%) to close at Rs. 156.75.

Nations Trust Bank, John Keells Holdings and Ambeon Capital were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Nations Trust Bank moved down by Rs. 0.75 (0.25%) to close at Rs. 295.00. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a gain of Rs. 0.10 (0.56%) to close at Rs. 17.80. The share price of Ambeon Capital appreciated by Rs. 0.40 (1.40%) to close at Rs. 28.90.
