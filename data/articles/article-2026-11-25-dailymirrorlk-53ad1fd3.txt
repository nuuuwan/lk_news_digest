Sri Lanka Women crown Emerging Nations Trophy after beating Ireland

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Women clinched the ICC Women’s Emerging Nations Trophy 2026 after defeating Ireland Women by six wickets in the final played at the Karwan Sports Club in Ajman, United Arab Emirates, today (November 25).

Having won the toss and elected to field, Sri Lanka produced an impressive bowling performance to dismiss Ireland for just 96 runs in 19.3 overs.

Sri Lanka's bowlers struck early to put Ireland under pressure, with the Irish batters struggling to build partnerships against a disciplined bowling attack.

In reply, Sri Lanka reached the target of 97, scoring 99 for four in 12.4 overs to seal a comfortable victory with 44 balls remaining.

Openers Vishmi Gunaratne and Sanjana Kavindi provided Sri Lanka with a positive start in the run chase, laying the foundation for the successful pursuit.

The victory capped an unbeaten campaign for Sri Lanka, who won all eight matches in the tournament, including the group stage, Super Six round and final.
