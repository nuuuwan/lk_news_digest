Ex-Western Province Health Minister arrested

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Former Western Province Provincial Health Minister Angage Jagath Thilakasiri Perera has been arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over alleged bribery charges.

He is due to be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate, according to the Bribery Commission.
