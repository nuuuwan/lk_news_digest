Kelani Valley commuters stranded after sudden train cancellation

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Commuters travelling on the Kelani Valley railway line have been severely inconvenienced following the repeated cancellation of the Colombo Fort–Avissawella semi-express train without prior notice.

The Railway Department cancelled the 4.00 p.m. semi-express train from Colombo Fort to Avissawella today, marking the second cancellation this week. The same train was also cancelled on two occasions last week, with no reason provided by railway authorities.

Due to the cancellation, a large number of passengers are expected to rely on the 4.25 p.m. Avissawella-bound train, resulting in overcrowding at Colombo Fort and Maradana railway stations.

Commuters boarding from stations including Baseline and Narahenpita will also face difficulties as they may have to wait for the following train due to limited space.

Passengers have expressed frustration over the repeated cancellations, while railway authorities have yet to provide an explanation or a solution to the issue.
