Ambewela launches new Mango Flavoured Yoghurt

Ambewela has once again raised the bar in product innovation with the launch of its new Mango Flavoured Yoghurt, bringing together the rich, authentic taste of mango and the creamy goodness of Ambewela yoghurt.

Made from pure, fresh milk sourced from Ambewela’s own farms, the new Mango Flavoured Yoghurt reflects the brand’s unwavering commitment to quality, freshness, and food safety. Infused with the rich taste of mango, a favourite among Sri Lankans, this delicious yoghurt offers a refreshing and wholesome treat that perfectly balances indulgence and nutrition.

Available in a convenient 80g cup, it is ideal as an on-the-go snack, a light dessert, or a delicious everyday indulgence. Its smooth, creamy texture and rich mango flavour make it an irresistible choice for consumers of all ages.

Since introducing its iconic Set Yoghurt in 2009, Ambewela has earned the trust of Sri Lankan consumers through its commitment to premium quality. Building on that legacy, the brand has continued to expand its flavoured yoghurt range to meet evolving consumer preferences. The introduction of Mango Flavoured Yoghurt reflects Ambewela’s ability to blend Sri Lanka’s favourite local flavours with modern dairy innovation, further strengthening its position in the premium yoghurt category.

With this latest addition to its portfolio, Ambewela continues to redefine the yoghurt experience by delivering products that combine exceptional taste, superior quality, and trusted nutrition. More than just a yoghurt, Ambewela Mango Flavoured Yoghurt celebrates the flavours Sri Lankans love, crafted with the care and excellence that have made Ambewela the country’s most trusted dairy brand.
