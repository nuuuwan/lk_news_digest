Court of Appeal upholds Gnanasara Thera’s sentence

The Court of Appeal yesterday upheld the four-year rigorous imprisonment sentence imposed on Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera over charges of insulting Islam and making statements that could disrupt religious harmony.

The Court dismissed the monk’s appeal seeking to overturn the conviction and sentence imposed by the Colombo High Court, and to be acquitted of the charges.

The case relates to statements made by Gnanasara Thera concerning the Kuragala Temple at a press conference held in Kirulapone, Colombo, on 30 March, 2016. The Attorney General had filed charges under Section 291 of the Penal Code, alleging that his statements insulted Islam and threatened harmony between ethnic and religious communities.

In 2024, the Colombo High Court found Gnanasara Thera guilty and sentenced him to four years of rigorous imprisonment, along with a fine of Rs. 100,000. Gnanasara Thera has also faced separate legal proceedings, including a contempt of court conviction and another case involving statements deemed insulting to Islam.
