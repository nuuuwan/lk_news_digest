GMOA plans islandwide hospital protests

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Government Medical Officers’ Association (GMOA) has announced plans to launch protests at major hospitals across the country over the coming days, demanding solutions to long-standing salary and allowance issues, staff shortages and concerns over repressive laws.

In a statement, the GMOA said the protests were intended to highlight several unresolved issues affecting medical professionals and the country's free healthcare system.

The Association said doctors and other healthcare workers had made considerable sacrifices during Sri Lanka's economic crisis, including foregoing one-third of their additional duty and holiday allowances in support of economic measures introduced by successive governments.

However, despite government statements on the country's economic recovery, adequate steps had yet to be taken to address the grievances of healthcare professionals, the GMOA claimed.

It further said it had repeatedly sought discussions with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya and Health Minister regarding the issues affecting the healthcare sector.
