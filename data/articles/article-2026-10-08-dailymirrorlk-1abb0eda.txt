DFCC Bank achieves ISO 22301:2019 certification

DFCC Bank has achieved ISO 22301:2019 certification for its Business Continuity Management System (BCMS), reinforcing its commitment to maintaining critical banking services and protecting customer interests during unexpected disruptions.

The internationally recognised certification validates the bank’s business continuity framework, confirming that its governance, risk management, contingency planning and response mechanisms meet global best practices for operational resilience.

The achievement marks a significant milestone in DFCC Bank’s ongoing efforts to ensure uninterrupted access to essential banking services while strengthening its ability to anticipate, respond to and recover from a wide range of operational challenges.

ISO 22301:2019 is the globally accepted standard for Business Continuity Management Systems, providing organisations with a structured approach to identifying potential threats, preparing for disruptions and ensuring the continuity of critical business operations.

For DFCC Bank, the certification reflects years of investment in resilience, risk management and preparedness across the organisation. The framework enables teams to effectively manage operational risks, coordinate response efforts and minimise the impact of unforeseen events on customers, employees and other stakeholders.

DFCC Bank Senior Vice President Integrated Risk Management and Chief Risk Officer Kushan Jayasuriya stated, “Banking services play an essential role in the daily lives of individuals, families and businesses. Our responsibility is to ensure that critical services remain available, even during challenging circumstances. Achieving ISO 22301:2019 certification is a recognition of the strong business continuity framework we have built and the collective commitment shown by teams across the bank. More importantly, it provides a strong platform for us to continuously enhance our resilience as risks and operating conditions evolve.”

The certification was achieved through a coordinated, bank-wide effort to embed business continuity principles into governance, strategic planning, operational processes and day-to-day decision-making. It also reflects DFCC Bank’s culture of preparedness and its commitment to continuous improvement.

As the operating environment continues to evolve, DFCC Bank will continue to strengthen, test and enhance its business continuity capabilities in line with the international standards, ensuring reliable service delivery and building greater confidence among the customers, regulators and stakeholders.
