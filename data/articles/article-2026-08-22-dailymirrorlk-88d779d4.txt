Sri Lanka’s Rumesh Tharanga wins Lausanne Diamond League

Colombo, August 22 (Daily Mirror) - Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage produced a stunning performance at the Lausanne Diamond League in Switzerland, securing victory in the men’s javelin throw with an impressive effort of 88.14 metres.

The Sri Lankan athlete edged India’s Olympic champion Neeraj Chopra by just nine centimetres in a thrilling contest. Chopra finished second with a throw of 88.05 metres, his best performance of the 2026 season.

Grenada’s Anderson Peters claimed third place with a throw of 86.69 metres.

Pathirage’s victory adds another significant achievement to his remarkable 2026 season. He has already recorded Diamond League victories in Rome and Doha, while also producing a world-leading personal best of 92.62 metres earlier this year.
