Allianz Lanka opens new auto center

Allianz Lanka has strengthened its motor claims support network with the introduction of a new Auto Center facility in Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, enhancing convenience and accessibility for Allianz Lanka customers seeking vehicle repair and claims assistance.

Strategically located in one of Colombo’s key suburbs, the facility is expected to deliver a faster, smoother, and more seamless experience throughout the motor claims and repair journey.

Through access to state of the art, advanced vehicle repair technologies, and experienced automotive professionals, Allianz Lanka customers can benefit from a more efficient repair process and enhanced support following a motor accident. The facility has been integrated into Allianz Lanka’s customer service ecosystem to help simplify the journey from claim notification to vehicle restoration.

Allianz Lanka Customers can also take advantage of Allianz Lanka’s 1 Touch Claims service, a hassle-free claims solution designed to streamline the motor claims experience. Backed by a network of trusted cashless partner garages, the service enables eligible customers to manage claims and repairs with greater convenience and reduced administrative burden.

This initiative underscores Allianz Lanka’s commitment to delivering simple, seamless, and customer-focused insurance experiences. By combining innovative claims solutions, trusted repair partners, and accessible service touchpoints, Allianz Lanka continues to create greater value for customers.
