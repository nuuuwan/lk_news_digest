Sri Lankan rupee weakens against USD

The Sri Lankan Rupee recorded a slight depreciation against the US Dollar today (19), with both the buying and selling rates edging upwards compared to the previous day.

According to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, the US Dollar’s buying rate increased to Rs. 328.37 from Rs. 328.33, while the selling rate rose to Rs. 337.27 from Rs. 337.23.
