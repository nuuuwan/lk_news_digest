Seat belts help reduce expressway accident casualties: RDA

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Road Development Authority (RDA) Director General Anuradha Hettiarachchi said yesterday that casualties from road accidents on expressways have declined following the introduction of mandatory seat-belt regulations for all passengers.

He told the media that the number of casualties in yesterday’s multiple-vehicle accident on the Southern Expressway was limited because passengers were wearing seat belts.

Hettiarachchi said accidents on expressways have increased in recent days due to rainy weather and urged motorists to exercise greater caution when driving under wet conditions.

He identified speeding, poor lane discipline and failure to maintain a safe distance between vehicles as some of the main causes of accidents on expressways.

The RDA Director General also said vehicles that are not roadworthy have been observed entering expressways, posing an additional risk to motorists.

He urged drivers to maintain proper lane discipline, keep a safe distance from other vehicles and drive at appropriate speeds, particularly during rainy weather.
