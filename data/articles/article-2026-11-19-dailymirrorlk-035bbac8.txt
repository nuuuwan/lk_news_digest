Milan Rathnayake resists as Pakistan close in on victory

Nov.19 (Mirror Sports) - Milan Rathnayake struck an unbeaten half-century to keep Sri Lanka fighting, but Pakistan remained firmly in control of the second Test after reducing the hosts to 309/9 at stumps on Day 3.

Sri Lanka, who were bowled out for just 98 in their first innings, now hold an overall lead of only 82 runs after Pakistan posted 325 in reply.

Rathnayake remained unbeaten on 60, receiving valuable support from Kusal Mendis (53) and Sonal Dinusha (54), who also scored half-centuries. However, Pakistan's disciplined bowling attack continued to make regular breakthroughs despite missing injured pace spearhead Saqib Khan.

Aamer Jamal led the attack with three wickets for 67 runs, while Razaullah claimed two wickets and Mohammad Abbas also picked up two.

Rathnayake's late resistance offered Sri Lanka some hope, but with just one wicket remaining, Pakistan will start Day 4 as strong favourites to wrap up the match and secure victory.
