Extreme weather alerts:Are we ready?

The Ditwah experience showed how many people in high risk areas didn’t have access to proper communication. As a result Sri Lanka lost many lives. According to the UN,  over 2000 landslides occurred during Ditwah,  but many people hardly had any time to escape or rescue their loved ones. The country is experiencing inclement weather since Sunday and many parts of the country have been flooded.

It is unclear as to whether these communities were informed at least two days prior to such inclement weather. People still don’t trust responsible authorities such as the Met Department. Social media is now flooded with overflowing rivers, flood and landslide red alerts but are these enough. How many of these messages are communicated in Sinhala and Tamil? What are the backup plans if people have to be evacuated like during Ditwah?

By  November,  one year would have passed since Ditwah occurred; but to what extent have we recovered ? There are some people still in temporary shelters and whether inclement weather would add more burden to these shelters remains a question.
