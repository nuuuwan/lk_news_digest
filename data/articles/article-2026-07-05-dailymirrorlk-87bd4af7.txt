SEVEN HEAVEN! Australia Crowned Women’s T20 World Cup Champions

July.05 (Mirror Sports) - Australia produced a dominant display to defeat England by seven wickets in the ICC Women's T20 World Cup final at Lord's, securing a record seventh title.

After electing to field, Australia restricted England to 150/4 from 20 overs before chasing down the target with ease. Beth Mooney starred with a match-winning 64, while Phoebe Litchfield added 48 in a crucial century partnership after the early loss of Georgia Voll.

Australia completed the tournament unbeaten, winning all seven matches to extend their dominance in women's cricket. The triumph marks their seventh Women's T20 World Cup title from the tournament's 10 editions.
