Six hundred thousand lose Aswesuma

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - As many as 600,000 have lost the Aswesuma facility which they received earlier. The payment has been cut, an Opposition MP told Parliament yesterday.

“We have seen the report that a little boy from Welikanda has written to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake notifying him of the Aswesuma payment of his parents who are visually challenged have been cut. This is not an isolated incident as the payment has been cut for 600,000 recipients,” MP J. C. Alawathuwela alleged.

He said Sri Lanka’s GDP growth this year has declined to 4.4 percent compared to 5.5 percent which was the growth rate at the time the present Government came to power. This he said is low compared to the growth rate of few other nations in the region. “The growth rate of India currently stands at 7 percent, Vietnam 8.4 percent and Malaysia 5.2 percent.” he said.

He said the lifestyle of 60 percent of the population has declined.

Ruling party MP Sunil Ratnasiri who referred to the allegation made by MP Alawathuwela on the slashing of Athwesuma of the visually challenged couple in Welikanda, said the couple has been transferred to the category of recipients who receive a monthly payment of Rs 10,000. The list of recipients eligible to receive Rs 10,000 monthly will be published soon,” he said.
