July tourism earnings fall 10% YoY

The tourism earnings fell 10.4 percent year-on-year (YoY) to US $ 285.5 million in July 2026, from US $ 318.5 million a year earlier, as the visitor arrivals also edged lower during the month.

The tourist arrivals declined 1.7 percent YoY to 196,845 in July, compared with 200,244 visitors in the corresponding month of 2025.

The weaker July performance extended the decline in tourism receipts so far this year. The earnings during the first seven months fell 11.5 percent to US $ 1.80 billion, from US $ 2.03 billion in the corresponding period of 2025.

The visitor arrivals, however, recorded a much smaller decline. Sri Lanka welcomed 1.34 million tourists during January-July, down 1.8 percent from 1.37 million a year earlier.

The sharper contraction in earnings relative to the arrivals indicates that the industry generated less dollar revenue per visitor during the period compared with a year earlier.

On a month-on-month basis, however, the tourism earnings recovered strongly in July, rising nearly 89 percent from US $ 151.1 million in June, according to the latest Weekly Economic Indicators published by the Central Bank.

The Central Bank noted that the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority revised its methodology for estimating the monthly tourism earnings in May, to improve the accuracy and representativeness of the data.

The revised methodology incorporates country-specific tourist arrivals, average daily expenditure and average duration of stay.
