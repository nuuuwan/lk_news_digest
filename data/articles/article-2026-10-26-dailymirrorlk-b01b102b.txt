SLPP establishes 60% of electorate-level branch committees

Colombo, October 26 (Daily Mirror) - More than 60% of the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna’s (SLPP) electorate-level branch committees have been established nationwide, with the party expecting to complete the remaining committees by early next year, National Convener D.V. Chanaka MP said.

Speaking at a progress review meeting of the party’s affiliated organisations, Chanaka said around 50% of the party’s auxiliary organisations were also operational.

He said the remaining 40% of electorate-level branch committees would be established by early next year.

The party also plans to conduct 4,000 electoral division meetings by the middle of next year as part of its organisational activities.

The programme is being carried out under the guidance of SLPP National Organiser and MP Namal Rajapaksa.
