Nawaloka Urology and Nephrology Centre offers early detection, advanced services

Nawaloka Advanced Urology and Nephrology Centre at Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo, is committed to advancing urinary healthcare by providing specialist urological and nephrological expertise, internationally accepted modern diagnostic facilities and comprehensive treatment solutions.

It also encourages individuals to recognise warning signs and seek professional medical attention without delay.

At Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo, Advanced Urology and Nephrology Centre provides access to urological assessment and treatment by senior, experienced specialists, supported by advanced technology and a patient-focused approach. Under this specialist expertise, patients can receive individualised attention for a wide range of kidney, urinary tract and male reproductive health conditions, in line with international standards and guidelines.

Beyond infection management, the centre’s services extend to comprehensive modern kidney, ureter and bladder stone treatment (e.g., keyhole and endoscopic laser treatment); curative prostatic surgery (e.g. TURP and Laser) for benign disease and prostate cancer (e.g. Radical prostatectomy) and minimally invasive endoscopic and laparoscopic procedures for a wide range of kidney and urological surgical procedures, including renal transplantation. Specialist services also encompass complex bladder and urethral reconstructive surgery, genitourinary cancer surgery for conditions affecting the kidneys, bladder and prostate and male genital health including the management of erectile dysfunction (impotence). It also provides comprehensive services for male and female incontinence (involuntary uncontrolled urine leakage).

Advanced diagnostic imaging, including ultrasound, advanced AI-guided CT and MRI, supports accurate assessment and treatment planning when clinically indicated. With modern facilities, specialised surgical capabilities and access to emergency urological services around the clock, Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo provides a broad spectrum of urological services in line with international standards.

The centre encourages early assessment and timely medical attention for urinary and kidney health concerns as symptoms arise.
