EDB plans promotional push for giftware and handicrafts

Sri Lanka’s Export Development Board (EDB) plans to commission a promotional video showcasing the country’s giftware, lifestyle products and handicrafts, highlighting the strengths and capabilities of these segments of the creative industry.

The board has invited bids from the eligible production firms to develop a professional audiovisual presentation showcasing the sector’s potential, according to a procurement notice.

The EDB, which operates under the Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Ministry, will select a service provider through national competitive bidding. The notice did not disclose the estimated contract value, intended audience or distribution plans for the video.

Bids close at 2:00 p.m. on November 19, 2026, with technical proposals scheduled to be opened at 2:15 p.m. the same day.

The applicants must submit technical and financial proposals in separate sealed envelopes, enclosed within a sealed outer envelope. The bids must remain valid for 77 days from the submission deadline and be accompanied by a bank guarantee of Rs.50,000.

The submissions can be placed in the tender box at the EDB’s Procurement Unit or sent by registered post to its office at No. 42, Nawam Mawatha, Colombo 2, to arrive before the deadline. The late bids will be rejected.
