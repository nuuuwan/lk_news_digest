Vijitha Herath expresses condolences over death of Sheikh Ahmed bin Rashid Al Maktoum

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath has expressed his condolences to the Royal Family of Dubai and the people of Dubai following the death of Sheikh Ahmed bin Rashid Al Maktoum.

In a message posted on X, Herath said he was “deeply saddened” by the passing of Sheikh Ahmed and extended his “deepest condolences and heartfelt sympathies” to the Royal Family and the people of Dubai.

Sheikh Ahmed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, the younger brother of UAE Vice President, Prime Minister and Dubai Ruler Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, died on September 21 at the age of 76.
