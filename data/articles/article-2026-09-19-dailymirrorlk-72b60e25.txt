Ranil unveils programme for Sri Lanka’s future

Colombo, September 19 (Daily Mirror) - Former President Ranil Wickremesinghe today unveiled a new programme which he described as a plan for the future of Sri Lanka.

The programme proposes a new Constitution and a new electoral system under which 125 Members of Parliament would be elected from electorates.

Wickremesinghe said discussions were being held with all political parties, excluding the National People’s Power (NPP), to work towards a common national agenda.

He said the responsibilities under the proposed programme would ultimately be handed over to the younger generation.

“Responsibilities under this programme will be handed over to the younger generation,” he said.
