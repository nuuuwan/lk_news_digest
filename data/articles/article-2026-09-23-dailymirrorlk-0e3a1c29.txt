Suspect arrested over Rs. 24.85 Billion foreign currency transfer

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - A 35-year-old man has been arrested in Wellampitiya in connection with an investigation into the alleged illegal transfer of foreign currency reserves worth Rs. 24.85 billion out of Sri Lanka, police said.

The arrest was made on September 22, 2026, by the Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID), which is conducting the investigation on the instructions of the Inspector General of Police following a complaint lodged at Police Headquarters.

According to investigators, 89 companies registered in Sri Lanka are suspected of transferring foreign currency out of the country through telegraphic transfers under the guise of importing goods. However, authorities allege that the goods declared in the transactions were never actually imported into Sri Lanka.

Investigators said the suspect is alleged to have aided and abetted the transfer of Rs. 24.85 billion in foreign currency through five of the 89 companies identified in the investigation.

The arrested suspect, a resident of Dam Street, Colombo 12, is being investigated in connection with the alleged financial offences.

The FCID has launched further investigations under the provisions of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act.
