Financial turnaround, growth earn Nawaloka Hospitals’ CFOs top national accolades

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC is celebrating a dual triumph in financial leadership, with its Group Chief Financial Officer Theja Vimuktha De Silva and Chief Financial Officer Sampath Thennakoon securing top national honours at prestigious industry awards in 2026.

De Silva was named as Best CFO of the Year 2026 at the CA Sri Lanka Best CFO Awards, organised by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka.

De Silva said, “This recognition is a testament to the collective resilience, discipline and vision of our entire team at Nawaloka Hospitals. Moving from a challenging financial period to sustainable profitability and double-digit growth demonstrates that strategic financial leadership goes beyond numbers— it is about empowering teams, strengthening governance and ensuring that our financial stability directly translates into superior patient care and long-term shareholder value. I am deeply grateful to our Chairman Dr. H.K. Jayantha Dharmadasa, Deputy Chairperson Anisha Dharmadasa, the board and our dedicated finance team for their support.”

Meanwhile, Thennakoon achieved a remarkable Triple Gold victory at CPM Sri Lanka Management Leadership Excellence Awards 2026, receiving Gold for Executive Decision Maker Leadership (Private Western Medicine Hospitals), Demonstrating Visionary Leadership Competency and the Overall Gold Award in Executive Decision Maker Leadership.

Thennakoon noted, “Receiving these CPM Sri Lanka Gold awards is an honour that reflects our shared commitment to strategic discipline and forward-thinking leadership across the group. In today’s complex healthcare landscape, financial decision-making must seamlessly align sound risk oversight with aggressive pursuit of growth and innovation. This recognition reinforces our vision to drive strategic investment in cutting-edge medical technology while maintaining the highest standards of accountability and operational excellence.”

These accolades recognise the transformative financial leadership behind Nawaloka Hospitals’ robust recovery, corporate governance and growth trajectory. Following a loss of Rs.305 million in FY23/24, the Nawaloka group returned to profitability with Rs.56 million in FY24/25.

Building on this momentum, the group revenue expanded by 20.3 percent to Rs.13.24 billion in FY2025/26, with reported profit after tax reaching Rs.1.05 billion.

Reflecting the heightened market confidence, Nawaloka Hospitals’ market capitalisation doubled from Rs.7.47 billion to Rs.14.94 billion during FY25/26. The share price surged from Rs.5.30 to Rs.10.60, hitting a peak of Rs.15.20 during the financial year. This value creation was underpinned by strategic debt restructuring, improved liquidity and rigorous capital allocation.

Complementing the financial resilience, the group reinvested approximately Rs.1.54 billion into advanced medical equipment, including cutting-edge AI-powered radiology, reinforcing its commitment to clinical excellence and patient care.
