Bombuwala Consultants leads management excellence programme for   Wayamba Labs

Bombuwala Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd successfully conducted a two-day management training programme for Wayamba Diagnostic Medical Laboratory (Pvt.) Ltd, supporting the organisation’s efforts to strengthen the leadership capabilities, enhance managerial effectiveness and build a more collaborative and accountable workplace culture.

Designed and delivered by Bombuwala Consultants, the programme brought together practical management insights and interactive learning to help the Wayamba Labs’ managers respond effectively to organisational priorities and evolving workplace requirements.

The training was conducted by two experienced professionals from Bombuwala Consultants: Sujith Bombuwala, MBA (PIM – University of Sri Jayewardenepura), PGDBM (University of Colombo), PGD.Mkt. (SL), Practising Marketer (SL), Certified Consultant (SL), MSLIM and MCIPM and Thilina Bandara, Programme Lead/Lead Consultant, B.Sc.Eng. (Hons) – University of Moratuwa, MBA (PIM – University of Sri Jayewardenepura) and Certified Trainer.

The programme focused on strengthening the managers’ ability to achieve organisational objectives while supporting employee well-being, encouraging cross-functional collaboration and promoting responsible leadership.

The key areas included collaborative leadership, scientific decision-making, effective communication, time management, prioritisation, accountability and adaptability. Particular attention was also given to fairness, integrity, inclusiveness and respect in the workplace.

The programme also covered the resilience framework, practical case studies, AI readiness, marketing and sales growth strategies, interactive discussions and public speaking, equipping the managers with valuable insights for professional and organisational development.

The programme further highlighted the importance of learning from past experiences while remaining open to new technologies, changing operational demands and improved working practices. By combining professional guidance with practical activities, Bombuwala Consultants aimed to equip the Wayamba Labs’ managers with approaches that can be applied directly in their day-to-day responsibilities.

Through this management development initiative delivered by Bombuwala Consultants, Wayamba Labs aims to nurture responsible, capable and forward-thinking managers who can lead their teams effectively, strengthen organisational performance and contribute to the organisation’s long-term development.
