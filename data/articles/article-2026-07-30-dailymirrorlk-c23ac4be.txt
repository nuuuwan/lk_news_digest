Palitha Bandara wins Sri Lanka’s first medal at 2026 Commonwealth Games

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s Palitha Bandara secured the country’s first medal at the 2026 Commonwealth Games after claiming silver in the men’s discus throw F42-44/F61-64 para athletics final.

The silver medal marks Bandara’s second consecutive Commonwealth Games podium finish in the event, having also claimed silver at the Birmingham 2022 Commonwealth Games.
