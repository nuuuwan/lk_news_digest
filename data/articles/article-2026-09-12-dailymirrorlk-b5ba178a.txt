Emergency help at your fingertips: Police introduce new SOS app

Colombo, Sep. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police has introduced a new mobile application aimed at helping members of the public quickly notify police during emergencies. Police Media Spokesperson Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) F.U. Wootler said the “Sri Lanka Police” app is currently available on the Google Play Store and can be used by Sri Lankan citizens who possess a National Identity Card (NIC).

The application includes an SOS emergency feature that allows users to request immediate police assistance. According to ASP Wootler, when the SOS button is continuously pressed for 10 seconds during an emergency, the user’s location information will be transmitted to the nearest police station, enabling officers to respond more quickly.

The app provides an additional way of contacting the police alongside the existing 119 emergency hotline. Members of the public can use the application to alert police to emergencies, including situations where a child may be facing danger or distress.

Police have emphasized that the SOS function must not be used for testing, pranks or false emergency reports. Location sharing is mandatory when the SOS function is activated, and users are advised to enable the necessary permissions, particularly location access, in advance so the feature can operate effectively.

The new SOS service is currently being introduced as a pilot project in the Colombo District. Police have also warned the public against misusing emergency services, noting that legal action was taken against more than 91 individuals in 2025 for misusing the 119 emergency hotline.

To use the service, users must download and register through the “Sri Lanka Police” app on the Google Play Store. Once opened, the home screen displays a red SOS button at the bottom centre, along with services including Police News, the Emergency Hotline, Station Finder and E-Services.

Police have further advised users to prioritise their personal safety when activating an SOS request. If possible, people facing an emergency should move away from danger and seek a safe location while waiting for police assistance.

Download and install the “Sri Lanka Police” App from the Google Play Store.

Open the app and go to the Home Screen. You will find the red SOS button at the bottom centre, alongside services such as Police News, Emergency Hotline, Station Finder, and E-Services.

In an emergency, select the red SOS button when you or someone nearby requires immediate police assistance.

After selecting SOS, carefully read the instructions displayed by the app and complete the required steps. If the app requests permissions, such as access to your location, it is advisable to enable them in advance so that the service can function effectively. Location information may be used in accordance with the app’s data-safety information provided on Google Play.

While making an SOS request, move away from danger and seek a safe location if possible. In a life-threatening situation, always prioritise your personal safety.
