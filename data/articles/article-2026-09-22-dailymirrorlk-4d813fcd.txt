15 found guilty, 9 acquitted in Easter Sunday terror case

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Seven years after the 2019 Easter Sunday attacks, 15 of the 24 accused standing trial on 23,270 charges, including conspiring to carry out suicide terrorist attacks targeting tourist hotels, churches and other locations in Colombo and several other areas, and providing assistance and support for the attacks, were found guilty.

The verdict was delivered by the three-member High Court Trial-at-Bar comprising Justices Navaratne Marasinghe, Ramanathan Kannan and Sujeewa Nishshanka.

The Trial-at-Bar concluded the lengthy trial, which lasted nearly five years, on August 24.

The sentences of the convicted accused are scheduled to be pronounced shortly.
