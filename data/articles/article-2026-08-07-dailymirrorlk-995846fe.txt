Unrest at New Magazine Prison leaves 11 inmates injured

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Eleven inmates were injured after unrest broke out at New Magazine Prison close to midnight yesterday, prompting prison authorities to bring the situation under control with the assistance of the Sri Lanka Police and the Police Special Task Force (STF).

Prisons Spokesperson A.C. Gajanayake said a group of inmates became disorderly during the incident, which was contained by prison officials with support from the police and STF.

Following the disturbance, security at the prison was strengthened with the deployment of the Sri Lanka Police, the Police Special Task Force, and the armed forces.

The injured inmates were admitted to the prison hospital for treatment, while nine inmates were transferred to another prison facility.
