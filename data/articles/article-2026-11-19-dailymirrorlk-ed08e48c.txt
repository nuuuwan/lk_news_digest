Jaffna-Chennai flights cancelled over poor air quality

Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Flights scheduled to operate between Jaffna and Chennai today (19) have been cancelled due to deteriorating air quality and poor visibility, which have affected aircraft operations.

The decision was taken as the prevailing conditions have created difficulties for aircraft taking off and landing at the airport.

The Central Environmental Authority (CEA) said air quality in Jaffna had reached unhealthy levels, with the Air Quality Index (AQI) exceeding 200.

An AQI reading above 200 is considered very unhealthy and can pose health risks to the public.
