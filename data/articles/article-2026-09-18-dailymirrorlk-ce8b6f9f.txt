Firms risk penalties over ownership disclosure failures

Sri Lankan companies have until September 30 to submit details of their beneficial owners to the Registrar of Companies or risk penalties and legal action, the Department of Registrar of Companies said.

The requirement applies to every company incorporated or registered under the Companies Act No. 7 of 2007, or any previous legislation governing companies, that had beneficial owners when the Companies (Amendment) Act No. 12 of 2025 came into operation.

Under Section 130H(1) of the amended Act, companies must disclose the required information in the prescribed form and manner within six months of the legislation taking effect.

The Registrar General of Companies urged directors and company secretaries who have yet to comply to submit the information by the deadline.

Failure to comply may result in administrative penalties. The Department warned that non-compliance could also constitute an offence carrying, upon conviction, a fine of up to Rs. 1 million, imprisonment for up to 10 years, or both.
