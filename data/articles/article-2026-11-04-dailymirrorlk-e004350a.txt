Cybersecurity campaign launched

Representative of The Asia Foundation in Sri Lanka Johann Rebert hands over a National PR Kit to Senior Deputy Inspector General of Police for the Western Province, Sajeewa Medawatta, in the presence of Deputy Inspector General Dharshika Kumari and Deputy Inspector General Manjula Mahagirilla, during the launch of a national cybersecurity awareness campaign in Colombo. The campaign, jointly launched by The Asia Foundation and the Sri Lanka Police, aims to raise public awareness of cybersecurity and help prevent cybercrime among businesses and the wider community.
