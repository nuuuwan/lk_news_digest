Union Bank appoints Anil Keshary Shah to Board

Union Bank announced the appointment of Anil Keshary Shah as a Non-Independent Non-Executive Director to the Board of Directors.

Anil Keshary Shah is a banking professional and leadership consultant with over three decades of experience in banking, finance, strategic leadership and business management across Nepal and South Asia.

He has held several leadership positions in the banking sector, including serving as Chief Executive Officer of Nabil Bank Limited, and Chief Executive Officer of Mega Bank Nepal Limited. Prior to these appointments, he served as Head of Consumer Banking and Chief Operating Officer of Standard Chartered Bank Nepal Limited.

Shah currently serves as Chairperson of Lead Nepal Inc., a leadership development organization focused on nurturing future leaders and promoting inclusive growth. He also serves as a Non-Executive Director of Nabil Bank Limited and is actively engaged as an advisor and mentor to several educational, social and sustainability-focused organizations, including Teach for Nepal and WWF Nepal.

Shah holds a Bachelor of Business Administration in International Business Finance from the George Washington University, USA, and a Master of Business Administration from the Faculty of Management Studies, University of Delhi, India. He has also completed Executive Education in Leadership for the 21st Century at the John F. Kennedy School of Government, Harvard University.
