Bribery Commission urged to probe Chief Justice

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - The National Trade Union and Civil Front has called on the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption to launch a comprehensive investigation into allegations made in Parliament concerning Chief Justice Priyathbandhu Surasena.

Trade Union Expert and social activist Dr. Chamal Sanjeewa has urged the Bribery Commission to investigate allegations raised in Parliament by Opposition MPs Rohini Kaviratne, Dayasiri Jayasekara and Ajith P. Perera concerning the Chief Justice.

The group has specifically called for an impartial inquiry into allegations relating to the accumulation of assets by the Chief Justice and his family members, as well as claims concerning delays in recovering compensation for the environmental damage caused by the X-Press Pearl disaster.

The organisation also called for investigations into allegations surrounding alleged container fraud, coal-related fraud and Treasury-related fraud, while urging authorities to examine allegations previously raised against the Speaker and the current Presidential Secretary.

It further requested that the Commission disclose the legal progress of such investigations to the courts without delay.

Speaking at the event, social activist Achala Divakara said authorities must take concrete measures to recover the losses caused by the X-Press Pearl disaster.

He said allegations concerning possible delays involving the recovery process, including claims directed at senior state officials, should not simply be dismissed without investigation.

“Whether these allegations are true or false must be established through an impartial investigation,” he said, stressing that the government should take immediate action to uncover the facts and recover any funds owed to the Sri Lankan State.

The calls come amid growing demands for greater scrutiny of allegations involving senior public officials, with activists insisting that no individual should be above independent investigation.
