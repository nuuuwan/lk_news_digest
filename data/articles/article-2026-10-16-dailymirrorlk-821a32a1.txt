Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Carson Cumberbatch, Distilleries and Commercial Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5bbn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Ceylon Tobacco Company, Diesel & Motor Engineering and Sampath Bank.

Mixed interest was observed in Dialog Axiata, Singer Sri Lanka and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, Citrus Leisure and Ceylon Land & Equity.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Ceylon Tobacco Company) whilst the sector index gained 1.03%. The share price of Ceylon Tobacco Company edged down by Rs. 1.00 (0.06%) to close at Rs. 1,778.00.

Diversified Financials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Credit & Finance) whilst the sector index increased by 2.29%. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance appreciated by Rs. 1.40 (1.56%) to close at Rs. 91.40.

Dialog Axiata, Singer Sri Lanka and Sampath Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata gained Rs. 0.20 (0.47%) to close at Rs. 42.50. The share price of Singer Sri Lanka moved down by Rs. 0.80 (1.11%) to close at Rs. 71.00. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs. 138.00.
