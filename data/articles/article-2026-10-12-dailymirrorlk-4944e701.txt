Prime recognised in Australia for bringing FDIs to SL

Prime has been recognised for its contribution towards attracting significant foreign direct investment (FDI) to Sri Lanka through real estate, at Honor Roll & Business Awards 2026, presented by Australia Sri Lanka Business Council, marking another milestone in its three-decade journey of taking Sri Lankan real estate to the global stage.

The ceremony was held recently at Pullman Melbourne on the Park, Melbourne, in the presence of The Right Honourable Lord Mayor Nicholas Reece, High Commissioner for Sri Lanka in Australia Yasoja Gunasekara and Australia Sri Lanka Business Council President Dilkie Perera.

It acknowledges Prime Group’s efforts to promote Sri Lanka as a destination for real estate investments and connect overseas Sri Lankans and international investors with opportunities in the local real estate sector.

Prime Group Chairman Premalal Brahmanage said, “For the past three to four years, Prime has been on a clear mission to take Sri Lankan real estate to the world. We have consistently said that Sri Lanka can export real estate and today, we are turning that vision into reality. This recognition is a reflection of the growing impact of that effort, bringing valuable foreign currency into Sri Lanka through the sale of our properties to international investors.

This is not limited to Australia; our reach extends across the UK, Canada, the USA, GCC and markets around the world. More recently, Prime Marina 1 and Prime Marina 2 have marked a significant milestone in this journey, demonstrating the potential of Sri Lankan real estate to compete on a global stage. We are proud to be creating properties in Sri Lanka that attract international investment and in doing so, contribute to the country’s economic growth.”

“The tremendous success of Prime’s recent Australia and New Zealand tour with our Brand Ambassadors, Bathiya & Santhush (BNS) was yet another strong testament. Its engagement with the Sri Lankan diaspora, prospective international investors across major cities such as Melbourne, Sydney, Adelaide, Brisbane, Perth, Christchurch, Aukland, etc. demonstrated the vision of taking the Sri Lankan music industry to the globe. For us, this is more than entertainment; it is about Prime transforming global connections into global investment opportunities and value for Sri Lanka,” stated Prime Group Co-Chairperson Sandamini Perera.
