GCE O/L rescrutiny results released

Colombo, September 26 (Daily Mirror)- The Department of Examinations has released the rescrutiny results of the 2025 (2026) General Certificate of Education Ordinary Level (GCE O/L) Examination.

The results are now available through the official websites of the Ministry of Education, the Department of Examinations, and the examination results portal at www.doenets.lk and www.results.exams.gov.lk.

Candidates seeking further information or clarification regarding the rescrutiny results can contact the Postal Examinations Organization and Results Branch of the Department of Examinations.

Inquiries can be made through the hotline: 1911, direct lines: 011 278 4208, 011 278 4537, 011 278 5922 or fax: 011 278 4422.
