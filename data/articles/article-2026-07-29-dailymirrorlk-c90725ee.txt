Cabinet approves SLTB scrap metal disposal contracts worth nearly Rs.196mn

The Cabinet has approved the awarding of contracts for the disposal of the scrap metal accumulated at the Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB), with a total procurement value of Rs.195.89 million.

The approval follows an earlier Cabinet decision on October 21, 2024, to dispose of the scrap metal stockpiled at the SLTB.

In line with the National Competitive Procurement Procedure, bids were invited and nine bids were received.

Following the evaluation process, the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Transport, Highways and Urban Development Acting Minister to award the contracts to the bidders who had submitted the highest substantially responsive bids, based on the recommendation of the High-Level Procurement Committee.

The approved bidders are M/s. Udaya Industries (Rs.16,536,695), M/s. Ashok Steel Industries (Pvt.) Ltd (Rs.89,424,153.88), M/s. Kalana Tech Engineering (Rs.1,691,210), M/s. S.R. Steel (Pvt.) Ltd (Rs.47,578,448), M/s. Ceylon Steel Casting (Pvt.) Ltd (Rs.1,225,500) and M/s. IWW Steel Industries (Pvt.) Ltd (Rs.39,434,186.40).

The total value of the approved procurement amounts to Rs.195,890,193.28.6.
