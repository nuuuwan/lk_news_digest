Union Bank CEO Dilshan Rodrigo appointed as SLID Senior Vice Chairman

Union Bank Director and Chief Executive Officer Dilshan Rodrigo has been re-elected as Senior Vice Chairman of the Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID) for the second consecutive year. The appointment was confirmed at the institute’s Annual General Meeting held recently.  A dedicated and active member of the SLID who previously served as Vice Chairman, Rodrigo’s continued leadership underscores his profound impact and ongoing contributions to corporate governance, excellence and the wider corporate sector in Sri Lanka.
