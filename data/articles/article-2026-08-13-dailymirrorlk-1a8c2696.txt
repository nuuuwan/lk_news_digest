General Secretary of Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Ranjith Madduma Bandara in an interview with Daily Mirror stated that his party will be forming an alliance with UNP for upcoming elections.He stated that the government has failed to deliver on the promises given to the public and is driving the country towards a dictatorship. He further stated that the government’s inability to take actions against members and associates of their party who are alleged of corruption and crimes is questionable.

Q While the opposition alleges that the popularity of the government is lost, there is an equally weak opposition. Being the main opposition party, what is your response?

This government has broken all the promises given to the public. The economic reliefs they promised were not fulfilled. There is no recruitment in the government sector. Many factories in the private sector are shut down. We challenge the government to hold provincial council elections. The government keeps on postponing matters. We have observed the government for almost two years. We can see the path they have taken. The government pledged the people that they will eradicate corruption, but instead what we see is they (Govt.) are also robbing. The coal procurement scam is one of the major scandals that happened. It is the opposition who revealed this to the public. As a result, the government was compelled to remove the Secretary and the Minister. These issues show how corrupt the government is and how weak they are. As many as 323 containers were released without a probe. This has never happened in our history. It is alleged that these containers belong to the supporters of the government. I remember MP Archuna stating that these containers contained weapons. Also a 2.5 Million dollar scandal occurred and the Minister of Finance, who is the President, should be responsible. They point fingers at us about the Central Bank scandal, but what has happened under their government is greater in terms of numbers.

Q When SJB broke away from UNP and Ranil Wickremesinghe, the latter was used by Rajapakse’s and made the President.  Do you think separating from RW is a mistake?

During 2019/2020 period party members requested to make Sajith Premadasa the Party Leader. By following that request we attempted to make Sajith Premadasa the leader of the party for the parliamentary elections in 2019. We made the request, but since there was no such opportunity we had to form Samagi Janabalawegaya under Sajith Premadasa’s leadership.

We are content with our decision as SJB won 60 seats at the parliamentary elections while the UNP was able to secure only one seat. Also, we became the main opposition party. This means the decision we took was correct. Today, irrespective of the voters’ strength of the UNP, the people have requested both the parties to join. We will be forming an Alliance.

QRecently we saw Ranil Wickremesinghe returning to the Opposition Head office. What was the discussion about? Was is about the alliance? Where would Ranil stand in the UNP, SJB or in the Opposition?

That discussion was on the 22nd Amendment. The power of the Executive lies with the JVP, 2/3rd of the legislature lies with them. Why are they extending the retirement age only in the judiciary in a context where they have failed to recruit the required officials for the vacant positions. It was also discussed in Parliament that the promotions and transfers of the judges were done not on a requirement basis, but for political needs. We had that particular discussion with Ranil along with other parties against the politicisation of the judiciary.

We are merely forming an alliance with the UNP for the upcoming elections. Legally, a member of the SJB cannot become a member of the UNP and the same applies for the members of the UNP.

Q The recent judgment against former IGP and Defence Secretary relating to the Easter Sunday attacks have proved there had been major miscommunications, mishaps and negligence with regard to National Security under your government. Where did it go wrong?

Yes, we were in power when the Easter attacks occurred. Before the constitutional coup which took place, I was the Minister of Law and Order. But at the time of the Easter Attacks took place the Minister of Law and Order was then President Maithripala Sirisena. As per my knowledge and understanding SIS reports are sent to the President and to the Minister of Public Security, the IGP and the Secretary of the Ministry of Law and Order. Other officials of the government were not informed about this.

I will refrain from commenting about the judicial decision. We request the government to find the perpetrators of this crime and bring them to court.

The investigations allegedly revealed that a business person, attached to the JVP, has funded the Easter bombings. Why is the government hiding these facts? Two sons of a JVP candidate were involved in this group who committed suicide. We urge the government to act on these parties too.

Q Do you think that you can now guarantee such an incident would not occur under a future government you intend to set up?

When I was the Minister of Law and Order there were reports about a clash between devotees of certain mosques. We also received information about Saharan. Acting on that information we obtained a blue warrant to arrest him.

In my opinion, the position of Minister of Law and Order should be assigned to a single person who is committed full time to the cause of national security. When the responsible authorities possessed information and failed to take action, that definitely amounts to a serious issue.

Today the government has failed to provide security to even the prisoners. There are many shootings happening in different parts of the country. The government should take steps to ensure that there is more national security. I ensure that under a future regime of ours we would ensure the security of the people.

Q SJB suspended the membership of its organizer over allegations of bribery. That person is now out on bail. In this context what is your opinion about the governments’ efforts on eradicating corruption?

We did not oppose the government’s act of arresting our former Organizer. The government can’t take action against people who have committed an offence. But he is not yet proven guilty regarding the issue. As a Party we have cancelled his party membership.

The investigations allegedly revealed that a business person, attached to the JVP, has funded the Easter bombings. Why is the government hiding these facts? Two sons of a JVP candidate were involved in this group who committed suicide. We urge the government to act on these parties too

A court case has been filed against the Minister of Energy over a corruption that occurred when he was in the Fertilizer Corporation. What are the actions taken by the Government? A Deputy Cabinet Minister and a Mayor were convicted over a matter related to a building. I would like to question the government on what did they do about it as well.

Q While your party complained against the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution, those supporting the government argue that the culture of interfering with the judiciary and the chief justice started from the time of J. R. Jayewardene, the leader of your mother party UNP and the procedure was followed by Chandrika’s government and then byMahinda Rajapaksa. What is your say?

A constitution will be amended from time to time as per the need of the time. In NPPs manifesto, it is clearly mentioned that they will bring about a new constitution. It is in the practice for a newly appointed government to get approved during the first two years after securing power. It is evident that this government will amend the constitution to fulfill their political needs and protect their own who are accused of corruption. Also, we see that they are trying to oppress the opposition to take this country towards a dictatorship.

The power of the Executive lies with the JVP, 2/3rd of the legislature lies with them. Why are they extending the retirement age only in the judiciary in a context where they have failed to recruit the required officials for the vacant positions

One Minister of the government stated during a recently held press conference that there will be no more Presidential Elections held by the Government. This means either they have to amend the Constitution or drag the country towards a dictatorship. People need to act to protect their democratic rights. We challenge the government that if they are popular and that people are fond of them, to do an investigation and conduct Provincial Council Elections.
