Power & Energy Expo Sri Lanka 2026 GoodWe concludes successful participation

GoodWe, a global leader in inverter and energy storage solutions, successfully concluded its participation at Power & Energy Expo Sri Lanka 2026, in partnership with its trusted distributor Brantel Lanka.

Held amid Sri Lanka’s accelerating transition to renewable energy and grid modernisation, the expo brought together key industry stakeholders from across the region.

GoodWe showcased its latest residential hybrid solar, energy storage and smart energy management solutions, reinforcing its commitment to advancing clean energy adoption.

For the residential segment, the company showcased its ET LV hybrid inverter, with the Lynx A G4 battery system and ES Uniq hybrid inverter, with the Lynx U G3 battery system. Designed for high efficiency, reliable backup and smart energy management, the solutions attracted strong interest for their compact design, easy installation and intelligent monitoring capabilities.

Throughout the event, GoodWe’s booth attracted strong interest from industry professionals, project developers and prospective partners eager to explore solutions for Sri Lanka’s evolving energy landscape.

The company engaged in productive discussions on partnership opportunities, product deployment strategies and the broader policy direction shaping the nation’s clean energy transition.

GoodWe extend its gratitude to the organisers, partners and visitors who made the event a success and remains committed to supporting Sri Lanka’s journey toward a resilient, low-carbon energy future.

GoodWe is a world-leading PV inverter and energy storage systems manufacturer listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (Stock Code: 688390). The company has more than 6000 employees located in 15 different countries and a team of over 1000 engineers working at its R&D centres to continuously optimise and advance energy storage technology.
