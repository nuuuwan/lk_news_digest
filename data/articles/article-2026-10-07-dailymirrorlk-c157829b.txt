Former NSB Chairman Pradeep Kariyawasam arrested

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Pradeep Kariyawasam, the husband of former Chief Justice Shirani Bandaranayake and former Chairman of the National Savings Bank (NSB), has been arrested by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC).

According to the Bribery Commission, Kariyawasam was arrested in connection with investigations into allegations involving former First Lady Shiranthi Rajapaksa.
