Crop damage compensation payments begin today

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The payment of compensation to farmers for crops damaged due to adverse weather conditions in several districts will commence today (04), the Agriculture and Farmers' Insurance Board said.

Accordingly, compensation for affected farmers in the Polonnaruwa District is scheduled to be credited to their respective bank accounts today.

The Board said arrangements have been made to pay compensation to farmers in all other affected districts by September 15.

Paddy, maize and other crops have been damaged due to dry weather conditions in Hambantota, Ampara, Puttalam, Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa and Monaragala districts.

Following reports of crop damage by farmers, officials visited the affected cultivation areas and assessed the extent of the damage.

Based on these assessments, arrangements have been made to credit the relevant compensation to the farmers' bank accounts.
