Magnitude 6.5 earthquake recorded in Indian Ocean; no tsunami threat to Sri Lanka

Colombo, November 24 (Daily Mirror) - A magnitude 6.5 earthquake was recorded in the Indian Ocean in the Mauritius-Reunion region at 4.36 p.m. today (24), according to preliminary seismic data.

The earthquake occurred at a depth of 10 kilometres, with its epicentre located at latitude 17.78° South and longitude 65.82° East.

However, there is no tsunami threat to Sri Lanka as a result of the earthquake, and all coastal areas of the country have been declared safe, authorities said.

The announcement was made in consultation with relevant national and international agencies.

The public has been informed that there is no need for concern regarding a tsunami threat to Sri Lanka at present.
