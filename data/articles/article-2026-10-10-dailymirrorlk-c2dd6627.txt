RW explains circumstances behind Gnanasara Thera’s presidential pardon

Former President Ranil Wickremesinghe has explained the circumstances surrounding the presidential pardon granted to Venerable Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera, saying the decision was taken after discussions with Buddhist monks, ministers and then President Maithripala Sirisena.

Speaking at a meeting of United National Party lawyers at the party’s political office in Colombo, Wickremesinghe said concerns had been raised over the possibility of Gnanasara Thera being disrobed while serving his prison sentence.

He said the matter arose after Gnanasara Thera was sentenced to imprisonment by the Court of Appeal over an incident involving the obstruction of proceedings at the Homagama Magistrate’s Court.

Wickremesinghe said he had a letter sent by then Justice Minister Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe to Venerable Rahula Kotapitiye Thera concerning the matter, in which assurances had reportedly been given that Gnanasara Thera would not be disrobed.

He said several Buddhist monks later approached him and explained the customary disciplinary procedures followed by the Sangha in such cases. They had argued that disrobing a monk over an offence that did not constitute a parajika offence could create serious religious and constitutional concerns.

According to Wickremesinghe, the monks also pointed out that insulting or obstructing a court was not conduct befitting a member of the Sangha and that disciplinary action could be taken through the relevant religious authorities.

He said the matter was subsequently discussed with then President Maithripala Sirisena, who had the constitutional authority to grant a presidential pardon.

Wickremesinghe said the decision was taken after considering that Gnanasara Thera had already spent a year in prison and that the question of whether he should continue serving his sentence or be pardoned had arisen.

He added that the matter had also been discussed within the Cabinet and with several ministers before the pardon was granted.

Wickremesinghe further said that reasons were not routinely provided in writing for every presidential pardon, noting that prisoners were often granted sentence reductions or released on occasions such as Independence Day and Vesak.

He said the pardon granted to Gnanasara Thera was handled in a similar manner, adding that he had not personally issued the pardon, which was a decision made by then President Sirisena.
