Vijitha Herath to make special statement in Parliament on Sivarasa Anojan

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath is scheduled to make a special statement in Parliament tomorrow regarding the case of Sri Lankan national Sivarasa Anojan, who has been sentenced to death in Saudi Arabia.

Minister Herath confirmed to the Daily Mirror that he would address Parliament on the matter tomorrow.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism earlier said it was closely monitoring Anojan’s case, which is currently undergoing judicial proceedings in Saudi Arabia.

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and Minister Herath met the parents of Sivarasa Anojan this morning and assured them that the Government was making every possible effort to secure a pardon for Anojan through both legal and diplomatic channels.
