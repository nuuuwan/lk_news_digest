Sri Lanka, Thailand discuss FTA and visa-free travel

Thai Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Sihasak Phuangketkeow met Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya at Temple Trees on November 23 to discuss strengthening trade, tourism and economic cooperation between the two countries.

Discussions focused on advancing the Sri Lanka–Thailand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) to boost bilateral trade and improve market access for businesses in both countries.

The two sides also discussed the Memorandum of Understanding signed on November 22, 2026, granting 30-day visa-free entry to Sri Lankan ordinary passport holders visiting Thailand. The move was recognised as an important step towards promoting people-to-people exchanges and strengthening bilateral relations.

Enhancing tourism cooperation, increasing tourist arrivals and developing tourism-related partnerships were also discussed.

The two sides exchanged views on maritime cooperation, recognising Sri Lanka's strategic location and its importance as a maritime and port hub in the Indian Ocean.

Opportunities to expand cooperation in agriculture, particularly in food processing, technology transfer and the exchange of expertise, were also explored.

Prime Minister Amarasuriya invited Thai investors and business representatives to participate in Expo Sri Lanka, scheduled for January 2027, and explore investment opportunities in the country.

Regional cooperation through the Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation (BIMSTEC) was another key area discussed.

The Prime Minister also expressed appreciation to the Thai Government for its support and assistance to Sri Lanka following Cyclone Ditwah. Labour cooperation between the two countries was also discussed.

The meeting was attended by Thai Ambassador to Sri Lanka Paitoon Mahapannaporn, Secretary to the Prime Minister Pradeep Saputhanthri, Additional Secretary to the Prime Minister Sagarika Bogahawatta and officials from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism, along with the visiting Thai delegation.
