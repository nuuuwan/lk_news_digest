LOLC Finance expands islandwide reach

LOLC Finance Chief Operating Officer Montini Warnakula and Head of Channels Prasanna Karandagolla at ribbon-cutting ceremony marking LOLC Finance Mirigama branch opening

LOLC Finance Head of Gold Loan Nishantha Jayasekara and Head of SME and Personal Finance Charith Jagoda at ribbon-cutting ceremony marking LOLC Finance Galagedara branch opening

Reinforcing its commitment to bringing trusted financial services closer to communities across Sri Lanka, LOLC Finance PLC, the country’s largest non-bank financial institution (NBFI), has further expanded its islandwide footprint through the opening of two new branches in Mirigama and Galagedara.

The Mirigama branch, located at No. 14/1, Amarathunga Mawatha, Mirigama, was ceremonially declared open on June 18, 2026, while the Galagedara branch, situated at No. 179/B, Rambukkana Road, Galagedara, commenced operations on July 02, 2026.

The openings mark another significant milestone in LOLC Finance’s ongoing strategy of enhancing accessibility and delivering convenient financial solutions to the customers across the country.

As Sri Lanka’s largest NBFI, LOLC Finance continues to strengthen its presence in key regional markets, ensuring that individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses have greater access to a comprehensive portfolio of financial products and services.

The new branches have been established to meet the growing demand for reliable financial solutions while supporting the economic aspirations of the communities they serve.

The Mirigama and Galagedara branches will offer the full spectrum of LOLC Finance’s services, including savings and fixed deposits, leasing facilities, business and personal loans, gold loans, SME financing, digital financial solutions and a range of value-added financial services designed to cater to the diverse needs of customers.

The ceremonial opening of the Mirigama branch was graced by LOLC Finance Chief Operating Officer Montini Warnakula and LOLC Finance Head of Channels Prasanna Karandagolla, together with the members of the management, staff, customers and well-wishers.

The Galagedara branch was ceremonially declared open in the presence of LOLC Finance Head of Gold Loan Nishantha Jayasekara and LOLC Finance Head of SME and Personal Finance Charith Jagoda, along with the distinguished guests, customers and the members of the local community.

With one of the largest branch networks among Sri Lanka’s NBFIs, LOLC Finance continues to invest in expanding its physical presence alongside its growing suite of digital financial solutions, enabling the customers to enjoy seamless and convenient banking experiences through multiple channels.

Backed by decades of industry leadership, financial strength and customer trust, LOLC Finance remains committed to delivering innovative, accessible and responsible financial services while contributing to the country’s sustainable economic growth.
