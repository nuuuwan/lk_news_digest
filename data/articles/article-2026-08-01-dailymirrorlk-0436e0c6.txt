Minor flood warning issued for low-lying areas of Kelani River valley

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Irrigation Department has issued a warning for minor flood situation within the next 48 hours in the low-lying areas of the Kelani River valley.

The Department said a minor flood situation could develop in the low-lying areas of the Kelani River along the Dehiowita, Ruwanwella and Seethawaka Divisional Secretariat Divisions.

It said a significant and high-intensity rainfall has currently been recorded in the upper and middle catchment areas

Residents of these areas, river users and motorists travelling through the region have been strongly urged to act with extreme caution because current forecasts indicate that future high-intensity rainfall could occur, potentially causing a sudden rise in river water levels.
