Illegal firearms, trap guns, and explosives seized in Tirappane during ‘Hasthi Sena’ operation

Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - A cache of illegal firearms, trap guns, hakkapatas (explosive devices), snares, and gunpowder used for poaching wild animals has been seized during a special joint operation conducted in the Tirappane area—a region frequently roamed by the iconic tusker ‘Agbo’, the Environment Ministry said.

The search was carried out under the ‘Hasthi Sena’ national operation, an initiative aimed at protecting wild elephants and mitigating human-elephant conflict while safeguarding human lives and property.

The special operation covered several areas, including Galkulama, Getamalakanda, Muriyakadawala, Periyamiyankulama, Kirindegama, Nirawiya, Thalagulu Viharaya, Nabadawawewa, Wannankulama, Orukkankulama, and Uttimaduwa.

Conducted under the guidance of the Ministry of Environment, the operation was spearheaded by troops of the 7th Sri Lanka Armoured Corps (SLAC), operating under the 212 Infantry Brigade.

The mission was organized under the supervision of Major General Kaminda Silva, General Officer Commanding (GOC) of the 21 Infantry Division, and Brigadier Suranga Basnayake, Commander of the 212 Infantry Brigade. It was led on the ground by Lieutenant Colonel Prabath Abeywickrama, Commanding Officer of the 7th Sri Lanka Armoured Corps, with the participation of Squadron Commander Major Dimuthu Mullewidana.

A 75-member Sri Lanka Army contingent, comprising seven officers and 68 other ranks, took part in the raid. Representing the Department of Wildlife Conservation, Assistant Director Channa Suraweera joined the operation alongside a team from the Tirappane Beat Wildlife Office, which included three Range Security Officers, six Range Assistants, eight Wildlife Guards, and two Senior Assistant Officers. Ministry of Environment Advisor Ravindra Kariyawasam was also present during the operation.
