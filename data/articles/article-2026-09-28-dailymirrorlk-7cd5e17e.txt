Drivers to face demerit points under new system from October

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A six-month pilot project to introduce a demerit points system for drivers who commit traffic offences will begin in October, Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake said.

Speaking at a press conference held at the Ministry of Transport this morning (28), Rathnayake said the pilot programme is expected to continue until March 31 next year.

The Ministry has decided to introduce the “Demerit System” with the aim of reducing road accidents in Sri Lanka.

Under the proposed system, drivers will be allocated a fixed number of points for a period of two years, with points being deducted based on traffic law violations committed on the roads.

The Ministry also plans to implement the system digitally by further expanding the existing online system introduced for the payment of fines related to traffic offences.

The digital demerit system is expected to enable authorities to record traffic violations and deduct points from drivers accordingly.
