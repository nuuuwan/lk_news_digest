Colombo High Court upholds nine-month jail term for Gnanasara Thera over insulting Islam remarks

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo High Court has upheld the nine-month prison sentence imposed by the Colombo Magistrate’s Court on Bodu Bala Sena Secretary Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera after finding him guilty of making statements insulting Islam during a media briefing, Colombo Additional Magistrate Lahiru Silva observed in court today.

On January 1, 2025, the Colombo Magistrate’s Court sentenced Ven. Gnanasara Thera to nine months’ imprisonment and imposed a fine of Rs. 1,500.

Ven. Gnanasara Thera had filed an appeal before the Colombo High Court challenging the sentence.

While reading out the High Court’s decision in open court today, the Additional Magistrate said the High Court had dismissed the appeal filed by the accused and upheld the sentence, paving the way for the relevant sentencing order to be enforced.

Thereafter, President’s Counsel Anura Meddegoda, who appeared for Ven. Gnanasara Thera, informed court that an appeal had been filed before the Supreme Court against the High Court’s order.

The President’s Counsel requested that a date be fixed to call the case until the Supreme Court considers the appeal.

After considering the submissions, the Additional Magistrate fixed the case for February 25 and ordered prison officials to produce the accused monk before court on that date.

A warrant had previously been issued against Ven. Gnanasara Thera in connection with this case for failing to appear before court.
