CAHM at SLIIT expands global training opportunities

The Colombo Academy of Hospitality Management (CAHM) at SLIIT is Sri Lanka’s premier private institute dedicated to education in Hospitality, Commercial Cookery, Patisserie, Tourism and Events. Through its partnership with William Angliss Institute (RTO 3045), Australia, CAHM has established a benchmark for world-class hospitality education by combining international expertise with practical training relevant to both local and global industries.

The academy goes beyond conventional classroom learning by providing students with hands-on experience in state-of-the-art kitchens, training restaurants and simulated hospitality environments. This immersive approach ensures that graduates are career-ready and equipped with the professional skills demanded by the international hospitality industry.

CÉ LA VI Singapore, located at the iconic Marina Bay Sands, is a renowned rooftop dining and lifestyle destination featuring a rooftop restaurant, sky bar and club lounge. Inspired by the vibrant diversity of Southeast Asia, CÉ LA VI combines modern Asian flavors with dynamic spaces to create memorable dining and nightlife experiences.

Known for its iconic rooftop locations, energetic atmosphere and elevated guest experiences, CÉ LA VI has established an international presence across major destinations, including Singapore, Taipei, Tokyo, Dubai and London, with further global expansion underway.

Strengthening CAHM’s commitment to international industry exposure, its partnership with CÉ LA VI Singapore provides selected students with the opportunity to undertake a three-month international internship. The programme offers students valuable hands-on industry experience, exposure to international hospitality standards, cultural exchange and the opportunity to work in a multicultural professional environment.
