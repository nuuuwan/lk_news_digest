Water supply temporarily suspended in Colombo and Gampaha areas

Colombo, August 25 (Daily Mirror) - The National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) has temporarily suspended the water supply to several areas in the Colombo and Gampaha districts due to an emergency situation that may affect the quality of water at the intake point of the Kelani River.

While issuing a notice, the NWSDB said the decision was taken following a notification from the Disaster Management Centre.

“Due to an emergency situation that may affect the quality of water at the intake point where water is taken from the Kelani River, the water supply to the Ambatale and Kelani South Bank Water Treatment Plants has been temporarily suspended,” the NWSDB said.

The two treatment plants supply water to several areas in the Colombo and Gampaha districts.

The NWSDB said consumers would be informed in due course regarding the restoration of the water supply.

It also requested the cooperation of consumers during the temporary suspension.
