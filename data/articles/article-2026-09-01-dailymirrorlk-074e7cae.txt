KIST continues to expand sustainable Aloe Vera cultivation in Kilinochchi

KIST, a brand with a heritage of over 70 years marketed by Cargills Food and Beverage Limited, has marked significant milestone in its Aloe Vera cultivation initiative with participating farmers delivering their first harvest to the KIST fruit processing facility in Kilinochchi.

The Aloe Vera cultivation programme was introduced through a contract farming model to promote sustainable cultivation and create long-term livelihood opportunities for farming communities in Kilinochchi. Implemented with the support of Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH, the initiative focuses on empowering 100 women farmers by providing access to essential cultivation equipment, technical guidance and agricultural extension services. Farmers are also supported through financial assistance facilitated by Cargills Bank, with corporate guarantees from Cargills Food and Beverage Limited enabling access to financing without traditional collateral requirements.

As part of this initiative, KIST purchases harvested Aloe Vera at a pre-agreed price, ensuring a clear commercial pathway for farmers. The delivery of the first harvest reflects the successful implementation of this farming model. With continuous supervision and technical support from the company, the average yield per Aloe Vera leaf has increased from approximately 350 grams to 500 grams, demonstrating improved cultivation efficiency and farming practices.

Aloe Vera is widely recognized for its cosmetic and medicinal properties, with increasing consumer demand driven by its natural health benefits. The initiative supports the development of a consistent local supply of Aloe Vera in Sri Lanka while strengthening local agricultural production.

Commenting on the initiative, Arjuna Kumarasinghe, Managing Director of Cargills Food and Beverage Limited said, “We will continue to encourage farmers to adopt high-tech agricultural practices to enhance productivity and increase output volumes, in order to better support the requirements of commercial fruit processing. This will strengthen the agricultural value chain and contribute to a more efficient and sustainable farming ecosystem. Through initiatives such as these, KIST remains committed to delivering its promise of Trusted Taste & Goodness while creating lasting value for farmers, consumers and the nation.”

The harvested Aloe Vera is processed at the KIST fruit processing facility in Kilinochchi, the company’s primary processing facility. The facility plays an important role in the local economy and is operated almost entirely by women, reflecting the company’s commitment to empowering women and promoting inclusive development. The processed Aloe Vera is used in the production of KIST Aloe Vera Drink, ensuring value addition takes place locally while supporting rural farming communities. In addition to Aloe Vera, the facility processes locally grown fruits including mango, passion fruit and woodapple, which are used in KIST’s fruit nectar and jam range.
