Final decision on NCM against Speaker tomorrow: MP

Colombo, September 04 (Daily Mirror)- The Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) parliamentary group is scheduled to meet tomorrow at the Opposition Leader’s Office to make a final decision on the proposed no-confidence motion against Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne, Opposition MP Ajith P. Perera said today.

Perera said the parliamentary group would also decide on the date on which the motion would be handed over to Parliament.

According to the MP, more than 40 allegations have been levelled against the Speaker, including claims that he exerted undue influence on certain parliamentary employees in connection with the alleged disappearance of items from the inventory of his official residence.

Perera alleged that several employees had been summoned before the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) in connection with corruption allegations involving the Speaker. He further alleged that the Speaker had exerted undue influence on those employees.
