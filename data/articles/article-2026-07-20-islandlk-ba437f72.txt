AHC Group expands into higher education with launch of Institute of International Diplomacy

Diversified AHC Group has expanded its business portfolio into the education sector with the official launch of the Institute of International Diplomacy (IID), marking a strategic move to strengthen professional and higher education opportunities in Sri Lanka.

The institute was officially inaugurated during the Human Rights Leadership Summit held at the Grand Oriental Hotel, Colombo. AHC Group Chairman and Peace Ambassador Dr. Nalaka Silva serves as the Founding Chairman of the newly established institution.

The Institute of International Diplomacy aims to provide internationally focused academic and professional education across a broad range of disciplines, including international diplomacy, human sciences, human rights and international law, cybercrime law, general law, agriculture, arts and culture. The institute has been established with the objective of equipping students and professionals with globally relevant knowledge and qualifications to meet the demands of an increasingly interconnected world.

Addressing the gathering, Dr.Nalaka Silva said the establishment of the institute reflects AHC Group’s long-term commitment to investing in knowledge and human capital development. He noted that, at a time when the higher education landscape is becoming increasingly competitive, the institute seeks to create new opportunities for school leavers, university aspirants and working professionals by offering internationally recognized academic pathways and industry-relevant learning.

The Institute of International Diplomacy will offer a comprehensive portfolio of qualifications, including professional certificate programmers, diplomas, higher diplomas, bachelor’s degrees, postgraduate diplomas (PGDs), master’s degrees and doctoral (PhD) programmes. These programmes will be delivered in collaboration with internationally recognised universities and academic institutions, enabling students to pursue globally accepted qualifications while studying in Sri Lanka.
