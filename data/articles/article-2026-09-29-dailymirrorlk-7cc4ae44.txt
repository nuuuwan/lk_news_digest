Vijitha Herath meets Iranian Foreign Minister

Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath held discussions with Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi on the sidelines of the 81st United Nations General Assembly in New York.

In a post on X, Herath said the two ministers exchanged views on current regional and international developments.

He said he reiterated Sri Lanka’s call for the de-escalation of tensions and the peaceful settlement of disputes through dialogue and diplomacy.

Herath also encouraged the continuation of negotiations aimed at restoring regional stability at the earliest.

The meeting took place during the High-Level Week of the 81st UN General Assembly, where Herath is leading the Sri Lankan delegation. The Foreign Minister delivered Sri Lanka’s national statement at the General Debate on September 28.
