Yoshitha Rajapaksa, Daisy Forrest money laundering case fixed for pre-trial conference

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo High Court today ordered that the case filed against Yoshitha Rajapaksa and Daisy Forrest, over allegations that they committed an offence under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act by depositing nearly Rs. 57 million, allegedly earned through unlawful means, into three bank accounts, be called on November 3 for a pre-trial conference.

When the case was taken up today, accused Yoshitha Rajapaksa appeared before court. The second accused, Daisy Forrest, was not present in court.

President’s Counsel Anil Silva, who appeared on her behalf, submitted a medical report to court.
