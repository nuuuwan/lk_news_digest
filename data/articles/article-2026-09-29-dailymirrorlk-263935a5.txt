Cabinet approves continuation of education reforms for Grades 2 and 6 in 2027

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the continuation of Sri Lanka’s education reform programme, with reforms for Grades 2 and 6 scheduled to be implemented in 2027, while the reforms introduced for Grade 1 in 2026 will continue.

The decision was taken based on the concept paper prepared by the National Education Commission, the National Curriculum Framework approved by the National Steering Committee for New Education Reforms, and the School-Based Evaluation and Assessment Guidelines.

The National Curriculum Framework and its implementation methodology, initiated by the National Institute of Education (NIE) in 2019, had been approved by Cabinet in March 2023.

However, following the change of government in 2024, it was decided to introduce the new framework initially to Grades 1 and 6 in 2026, with appropriate adjustments to align it with the new Government's education policies. The introduction of vocational subjects from Grade 10 onwards was also proposed.

A concept paper titled “Transformational Change in General Education in Sri Lanka” was subsequently submitted to the Parliamentary Sub-Committee on Education Reforms in October 2025.

Following a Cabinet decision in January 2026, the implementation of reforms for Grade 6 was postponed to 2027, while reforms for Grade 1 proceeded in 2026.

As part of the latest preparations, the National Education Commission reviewed and redrafted the concept paper, incorporating policy recommendations.

The revised document is to be reviewed by the National Steering Committee for New Education Reforms and its sub-committees covering five key areas: curriculum reform, human resource development, education administration and infrastructure development, evaluation and assessment, and public awareness and promotion.

The NIE's 2023 National Curriculum Framework was subsequently revised based on these recommendations and received approval from the NIE Academic Affairs Board and Institutional Council before being approved by the National Steering Committee on August 13, 2026.

Meanwhile, activity books and teacher guides covering all three terms are being prepared for Grades 1 and 2 for 2027, while modules and teacher guides for all three terms are being prepared for Grade 6.

The NIE has also trained master trainers for primary and secondary-level subjects, with provincial-level plans for teacher training currently being prepared.

In addition, the Evaluation and Assessment Sub-Committee has prepared a practical Policy Guide on School-Based Evaluation and Assessment, taking into account the resources available within the school system. The guide has been approved by the National Steering Committee.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by Prime Minister and Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Harini Amarasuriya to continue the reforms introduced for Grade 1 in 2026 and implement the reforms for Grades 2 and 6 in 2027.
