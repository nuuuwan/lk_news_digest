AES expands digital reach through strategic partnership with Kapruka

Abans Environmental Services (AES), Sri Lanka’s market leader in janitorial and facility management services, is expanding its service offering on Kapruka.com, the country’s largest e-commerce platform, with several services already live and more confirmed for launch. Under this partnership, Abans Environmental Services is proud to introduce several services under its portfolio. A longer list of future services that are ready to be offered to the public is currently under discussion between both parties.

The partnership was inked on 16 July 2026 with Kapruka Assistant General Manager Damitha Gunawardhana and Project Manager Himali Silva, while Director Zev Pestonjee, Assistant General Manager Chaminda Bandara, Senior General Manager Sarath Weragoda and Brand Manager Waruna Mendis signed on behalf of Abans Environmental Services Ltd.

As part of the collaboration, these services offered by Abans Environmental Services are now available for booking through the Kapruka platform. Customers can access sofa cleaning services, carpet shampooing services, apartment cleaning services, house cleaning services, and pest control services, providing households with convenient access to trusted, professional-grade cleaning and maintenance solutions through a familiar online marketplace.

The partnership will continue to expand, with floor cleaning services and glass cleaning services confirmed for introduction on the platform in the coming phase.

Established as part of the Abans Group, Abans Environmental Services has been serving Sri Lanka since 1984 and is widely recognised for its expertise in janitorial, housekeeping, and integrated facility management services. The company is ISO 9001 and SEDEX certified, and has maintained ISO 9001 certification since 1999, making it the only company in Sri Lanka’s janitorial and housekeeping sector to hold this certification continuously. Headquartered at 141 Kirula Road, Colombo 05, the company delivers its services through professionally trained personnel, specialised equipment, and certified cleaning chemicals while also providing tea services, pest control services, landscaping services.

Founded in 2002 by entrepreneur Dulith V. Herath, Kapruka Holdings PLC has grown into Sri Lanka’s leading e-commerce platform, offering online shopping, gifting, logistics, global shopping, and digital marketplace services to customers locally and internationally. Through continuous innovation and strategic partnerships, Kapruka has expanded its marketplace beyond retail products to include a growing portfolio of professional services.

The collaboration between Abans Environmental Services and Kapruka reflects the increasing demand for trusted, professionally managed home and commercial services delivered through convenient digital platforms. By combining ABANS’ decades of operational expertise with Kapruka’s nationwide digital reach, the partnership aims to simplify access to reliable environmental and facility management solutions while supporting the continued growth of Sri Lanka’s digital economy.
