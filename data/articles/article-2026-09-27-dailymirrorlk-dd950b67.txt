Navy seizes heroin and ICE in Jaffna raid

Jaffna, Sep 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has seized a cache of suspected heroin and “ICE” (crystal methamphetamine) during a special operation at the Kusumanthurai beach area in Madagal, Jaffna early this morning.

The pre-dawn operation was conducted by naval fleet units and personnel from SLNS Agbo under the Northern Naval Command.

During the operation, naval personnel also apprehended one suspect and seized a boat believed to have been involved in the alleged smuggling attempt.
