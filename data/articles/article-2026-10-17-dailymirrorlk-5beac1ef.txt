Wathupitiwala Export Processing Zone honoured at National Productivity Awards

Gampaha District Secretary Lalinda Gamage presents award certificate to Wathupitiwala Export Processing Zone Senior Deputy Director Buddhi Prasad Silva in the presence of Wathupitiwala Export Processing Zone Assistant Director Devika Senarath

The Wathupitiwala Export Processing Zone (BOI) received an Evaluation Award under the Public Sector State Enterprises Category at the 2025/26 National Productivity Awards, organised by the National Productivity Secretariat.

The awards ceremony covering the Western and North Western provinces was held recently at the Agra Auditorium of the Gampaha District Secretariat.

This award was presented in recognition of the implementation of the 5S concept to enhance productivity, the efficient service facilities established for the investors and employees and the overall operational excellence achieved recently within the Wathupitiwala Investment Zone.
