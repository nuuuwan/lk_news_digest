Five businesses fined over defective diapers

Colombo, September 17 (Daily Mirror) - Five businesses have been fined a total of Rs.140,000 after being found selling and distributing baby and adult diapers without the required importer details, following complaints from consumers that some diapers deteriorated and tore during use.

The action was taken following complaints received by the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA), which subsequently conducted investigations and raids at the relevant establishments.

Legal action was filed against the five businesses for allegedly displaying for sale and distributing diapers without the importer’s name, address, telephone number or website details, in violation of Direction No. 85.

The cases were taken up before Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court No. 05 on September 16, where all five defendants pleaded guilty.

The court also ordered the destruction of the entire stock of diapers produced as case productions. The stock included 81 packs of baby diapers and Baby Pants in various sizes and six packs of adult protective diapers.

The CAA urged consumers to check importer details and other required labelling information when purchasing baby or adult diapers.

Consumers who encounter products that deteriorate, tear or show other quality-related problems are advised to retain the packaging and purchase receipt, where possible, when making a complaint.

Complaints regarding such market irregularities can be made to the CAA through its 1977 hotline.
