Cabinet approves digital infrastructure upgrade for e-Court project

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal to provide official digital communication facilities to 350 courts across the country as part of the Government’s e-Court digitisation programme.

The proposal was submitted by the Minister of Justice and National Integration, with the move aimed at strengthening digital infrastructure and accelerating the implementation of the e-Court system.

The decision builds on the framework approved by the Cabinet on December 15, 2025, to establish a comprehensive digital system for the country’s courts.

Under the programme, digital case file management systems are being developed for Magistrates’ Courts, District Courts and High Courts.

The provision of digital communication facilities to courts is expected to support the wider transition to electronic case management and improve the efficiency of judicial administration.
