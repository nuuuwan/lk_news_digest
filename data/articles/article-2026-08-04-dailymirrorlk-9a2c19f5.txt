Cabinet approves constitutional and legal amendments to strengthen judicial independence

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet Ministers has approved the publication of draft amendments to the Constitution and the Judiciary Organization Act aimed at further strengthening the independence of the judiciary and expediting the disposal of pending cases.

The decision was taken at the Cabinet meeting held on July 27, 2026, where approval was granted to prepare amendments to the relevant laws.

Following the Cabinet approval, the Attorney General provided constitutional clearance for the Twenty-Second Amendment to the Constitution Bill and the Judiciary Organization (Amendment) Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved a proposal submitted by Minister of Justice and National Integration to publish the two amendment Bills in the Government Gazette and subsequently present them to Parliament for approval.

The proposed amendments are expected to introduce measures aimed at enhancing the efficiency of the judicial system while addressing the existing backlog of cases.
