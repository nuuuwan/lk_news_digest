Seylan Bank opens 295th ‘Pahasara’ library

Seylan Bank PLC marked another milestone in its flagship environmental, social and governance initiative by opening its 295th ‘Pahasara’ library at Giri/Gemunu Central College, Ingaradaula, Pannala.

The ceremony was held recently under the patronage of Zonal Assistant Director Education North Western II Prasad Kinigama, Principal K.M. Wijerathne, Deputy General Manager Corporate Banking Nilanthie De Meral, Seylan Bank Assistant General Manager Marketing and Sales Asiri Abhayaratne, Chief Manager Marketing Nalin Karunaratne, Senior Manager Sustainability Sandrine Gomez,  Regional Manager North Western Region Upendra Adhikari and Branch Manager Pannala Branch N.G.R. Sanjeewa, along with the Seylan Bank staff.

The newly refurbished library was officially handed over following the completion of Pahasara Week, a series of student-focused engagement activities conducted jointly by Seylan Bank and the school. As part of the initiative, the bank donated books along with a laptop and smart board, to enhance teaching and learning with digital tools.

Abhayaratne said, “The opening of the 295th library represents a significant step as the bank moves steadily toward its commitment of establishing 300 Pahasara libraries across the country. Seylan Bank remains committed to funding library refurbishments and providing essential educational resources to cultivate students’ knowledge and skills.”

De Meral added, “As we near the completion of 300 Pahasara libraries across the country, we at Seylan Bank, are reminded of the immense potential of education in bridging Sri Lanka’s youth with the tools for success. Furthermore, we’re recognise each Pahasara chapter as a vital platform in nurturing the next generation of socially conscious and responsible citizens through the expansion of access to effective learning material.”
