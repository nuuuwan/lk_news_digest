Sri Lankan Ambassador visits Anojan Sivarasa at Saudi prison

Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to Saudi Arabia, O. L. Ameer Ajwad, visited Anojan Sivarasa at Dammam Central Prison on October 8 to inquire about his well-being and current situation.

During the visit, the Ambassador held an extensive discussion with Sivarasa, listened to his needs and concerns, and assured him that the Embassy would provide all possible consular and legal assistance under the guidance of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism.

The Ambassador also shared his contact details with Sivarasa to facilitate direct communication.

Following the meeting, Ambassador Ameer Ajwad said Sivarasa was in good health and that Saudi prison authorities were attending to his well-being.

The Ambassador also met the Director of Dammam Central Prison and the prison’s medical doctor, thanking them for facilitating the visit and for the care provided to Sivarasa.

The Sri Lankan Embassy is also assisting Sivarasa with the appeal process before the Saudi Supreme Court.
