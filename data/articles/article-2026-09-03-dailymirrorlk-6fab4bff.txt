Dudley Sirisena spends Rs. 3.5 mn to renovate Polonnaruwa Railway Station

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan businessman Dudley Sirisena said he had spent Rs. 3.5 million to renovate the Polonnaruwa Railway Station and pledged to maintain the station at his own expense for one year.

“I promised you, and I have given the people of Polonnaruwa a beautiful railway station. From now on, it is your duty to keep it safe and secure for future generations. Therefore, I have taken responsibility for maintaining it for you for one year at my own expense,” Sirisena said.

The renovated Polonnaruwa Railway Station was handed over to the public today under the Sri Lanka Railways Department’s “Dream Destination” People’s Programme to modernise and upgrade 100 railway stations.

The programme is being implemented under the leadership of the Clean Sri Lanka Secretariat and the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development, with support from private businesses through financial contributions and services.

The renovation of the Polonnaruwa Railway Station was carried out by the Araliya Group of Companies with a contribution of Rs. 3.5 million.

The project included the renovation of the existing station building and the construction and improvement of facilities including restrooms, a railway goods depot, clergy facilities and separate male and female restrooms.

The renovated station was officially opened to the public today.
