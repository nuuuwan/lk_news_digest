Rumesh Tharanga takes on world’s best in Budapest tonight

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan javelin thrower Rumesh Tharanga is set to compete in the men’s javelin throw at the World Athletics Ultimate Championship in Budapest, Hungary, today (13), with the event scheduled to begin at 9.40 p.m. Sri Lanka time.

Tharanga enters the competition as the world’s leading javelin thrower this season, having recorded a best throw of 92.62 metres. He also recently secured the Diamond League javelin title.

Germany’s Julian Weber is expected to pose one of the strongest challenges to Tharanga. Weber recently set a championship record of 90.40 metres at the European Athletics Championships.

Olympic champion Arshad Nadeem of Pakistan, Grenada’s Anderson Peters and Trinidad and Tobago’s Keshorn Walcott are among the other leading javelin throwers competing in the event.

The prestigious championship, which began on September 11, brings together athletes who have achieved major success at the Olympics, World Championships and leading international circuits, including the Diamond League. The competition concludes today.

The winner of each event will receive a prize of US$150,000, while cash prizes will be awarded through eighth place, with the eighth-placed athlete receiving US$10,000.
