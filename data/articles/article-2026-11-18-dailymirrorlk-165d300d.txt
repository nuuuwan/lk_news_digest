Cargills Deputy Chairman V. R. Page sells 7 mn shares for Rs. 4.46bn

Cargills (Ceylon) PLC Deputy Chairman and Group Chief Executive V. R. Page sold 7 million ordinary shares in the company for about Rs. 4.46 billion on 16 November, according to a filing with the Colombo Stock Exchange.

The largest of the 10 transactions involved 6,999,701 shares at Rs. 637 each, generating Rs. 4.459 billion and accounting for more than 99.99 percent of the shares sold. The nine smaller transactions covered 299 shares at prices ranging from Rs. 640 to Rs. 680 apiece, raising a combined Rs. 196,503.25.

Among the smaller trades, Page sold 102 shares at Rs. 670 each and 100 shares at Rs. 640 each. The highest price disclosed was Rs. 680 apiece for 13 shares.

Across all 10 transactions, the sale amounted to Rs. 4,459,006,040.25.

Page, an executive director of Cargills, has notified the board on the same day under Section 200(2) of the Companies Act.
