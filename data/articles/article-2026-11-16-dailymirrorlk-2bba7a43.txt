Senior citizens inconvenienced following Maharagama Post Office relocation

The decision to shift the Maharagama Post Office 2 km away from the town is a thoughtless move that has caused great hardship, especially to senior citizens.

Earlier, the Post Office was located in the heart of the town, right in front of the public market. It was easily accessible to pensioners who come to collect their pension, to pay bills, and to attend to postal work. They could finish their market work and postal work in one visit, without extra travel.

It was also convenient for business people. They could walk in, without spending on petrol or wasting time.

Now, pensioners, many of whom are old, sick and living on a small pension, are forced to travel 2 km away. A three-wheeler costs Rs. 150 to Rs. 200 one way, Rs. 300 to Rs. 400 for a return trip. That is a heavy burden for a pensioner. Those who cannot afford it are forced to walk in the sun and rain, cross busy roads, and risk falls and accidents.

A Post Office is an essential public service. It should be in the town, for the people, not far away. I earnestly request the authorities to bring back the Maharagama Post Office to its original location in the town, in front of the market, where it belongs.
