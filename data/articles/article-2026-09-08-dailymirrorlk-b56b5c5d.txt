Foreign Minister to represent Sri Lanka at UN General Assembly

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake will not attend this year’s United Nations General Assembly (UNGA), with Sri Lanka to be represented at ministerial level, Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath said today (08).

Speaking at the Cabinet decisions media briefing, Herath said he would represent Sri Lanka at the UN General Assembly and deliver the country’s address on September 28.

He said he is scheduled to leave for New York on September 26 to attend the annual UN gathering.

The decision means President Dissanayake will not personally attend the General Assembly session this year, with Sri Lanka’s delegation to be led at ministerial level.
