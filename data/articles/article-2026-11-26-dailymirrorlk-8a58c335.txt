23rd Best Corporate Citizen Sustainability Awards  CBL Investments named as Sri Lanka’s Best Corporate Citizen

From left: Rathika de Silva, Sanath Manatunga – CEO Commercial Bank (Second Runner-Up), Phillip Ward – Director and Country Representative UNWFP, Shea Wickramasingha – Chairperson CBL Investments (Winner), Krishan Balendra – Chairperson Ceylon Chamber, Roshan Rajadurai – MD Talawakelle Tea Estates (First Runner-Up)

CBL Investments has been named as Sri Lanka’s Best Corporate Citizen for 2026, taking the top honour at the 23rd Best Corporate Citizen Sustainability (BCCS) Awards, organised by The Ceylon Chamber of Commerce.

The awards ceremony, held at Shangri-La Colombo yesterday, recognised organisations setting high standards in corporate citizenship and sustainability across the country.

Talawakelle Tea Estates and Commercial Bank of Ceylon were named as the First and Second Runners-Up, respectively.

Recognised as Sri Lanka’s leading corporate sustainability awards, the BCCS Awards celebrate businesses that place responsible practices at the heart of their operations.

They recognise organisations that balance economic performance with social and environmental responsibility and demonstrate how business can contribute to wider national progress.

Talawakelle Tea Estates also secured the Category B Best Corporate Citizen Sustainability Award 2026, presented to companies with an annual turnover of less than Rs.15 billion.

Elpitiya Planations and Kelani Valley Plantations were named respective First and Second Runners-Up in this category.

The Top Ten Corporate Citizens award recognises organisations demonstrating outstanding sustainability performance.

This year’s 10 recipients are CBL Investments, Citizens Development Business Finance, Commercial Bank of Ceylon, Elpitiya Plantations, Hatton National Bank, Haycarb, Hemas Holdings, Kelani Valley Plantations, Sampath Bank and Talawakelle Tea Estates.

The Best Projects Sustainability Awards recognise targeted initiatives through which businesses address specific social and environmental priorities.

The winning projects for 2026 are Citizens Development Business Finance – CDB Elevate: Ditwah Recovery Centre – Restoration of the Information Technology Centre (ITC), University of Peradeniya; Coca-Cola Beverages Sri Lanka, with the Coca Cola Foundation as a key partner and funder – Strengthening Women and Youth Engagement in Informal Waste Management in Sri Lanka (SWYFT); Dipped Products – Firstlight Kirigovi Samithiya – Sri Lanka’s First FSC-Certified Rural Smallholder Rubber Farmer Society; Hayleys Fentons – Roots of Resilience – Mangrove Ecosystem Restoration Project and Mirigama Dendro Power – Closing the Water Loop: A Circular Water Reuse Project for Renewable Energy.

The Triple Bottom-Line Awards recognise excellence across the three pillars of sustainability.

Talawakelle Tea Estates received the Environmental Sustainability Award, MAS Holdings – the Social Sustainability Award and Bank of Ceylon – the Economic Sustainability Award.

The Category Awards recognise organisations for their performance across specific areas of corporate responsibility.

Talawakelle Tea Estates received awards for Strategic Environmental Leadership and Governance – Climate Action, Energy Security and Decarbonisation – Climate Resilience, Adaptation and Disaster Preparedness – Resource Efficiency, Circular Economy and Pollution Prevention – Biodiversity, Water Security and Nature-Positive Development and was joint winner of the Supplier Relations Category; MAS Holdings for Sustainable Communities, Value Chains and National Impact and Customer Relations; Nestlé Lanka for Workforce and Workplace Relations; Dipped Products won joint category awards for Supplier Relations and Governance; Sampath Bank for Community Relations; Commercial Bank of Ceylon a joint award for Governance; DFCC Bank a joint award for Governance; Singer (Sri Lanka) for Financial Performance and Bank of Ceylon for Economic Contribution.

The awards also recognised organisations leading sustainability practices within their respective sectors.

The 2026 sector champions are Talawakelle Tea Estates – Agriculture; Hayleys Fentons – Construction; Hemas Holdings – Diversified Holdings; Commercial Bank of Ceylon – Finance; Oxford College of Business – Knowledge Services; CBL Investments – Manufacturing; Singer (Sri Lanka) – Retail and South Asia Gateway Terminals under the sector – Other.

Further recognition went to CBL Investments, which received the Award for Resilient Practices, Haycarb, which received the Sustainability Innovation Award and Talawakelle Tea Estates, which won the Award for the Best Sustainability Report/Disclosure.
