As many as 840 million women across the globe subjected to gender based violence: PM

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Highlighting the need to end gender based violence, Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya revealed in Parliament today that 840  million women in the globe have been subjected to gender based violence.

She stressed the need for action with regard to ending gender based violence.

Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa also stressed the need for Sri Lanka to ratify various international covenants Sri Lanka has entered into.

These sentiments were expressed in the House to commemorate the week of the campaign against gender based violence.
