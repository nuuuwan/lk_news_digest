New programme launched to qualify young people as “Construction Assistants”

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - A new training programme has been launched to qualify young people as “Construction Assistants”, with the aim of providing them with dignified, secure and stable employment in the construction sector.

The initiative seeks to address the growing shortage of workers in the construction industry despite the availability of a large number of job vacancies.

Officials have identified the use of titles such as “labourers”, “helpers” and “assistants”, the lack of social recognition attached to such jobs, and the informal nature of employment without formal terms of service or social security benefits such as EPF and ETF as key factors discouraging young people from entering the sector.

The programme aims to overcome these barriers by promoting construction jobs as dignified and secure careers, offering respectable job titles, stable incomes and employment security.

It is being jointly implemented by the Ministry of Housing, Construction and Water Supply, the Sinha Regiment of the Sri Lanka Army, the National Construction Association and the Sri Lanka Mahaweli Authority.

The first training programme, comprising 40 youth identified by the Mahaweli Authority from Mahaweli System C, commenced today (14) at the Sinha Regiment Corps Headquarters in Ambepussa.

The launch was held under the patronage of Housing, Construction and Water Supply Minister Dr. Susil Ranasinghe, Ministry Secretary Eng. Kumudulal Bogahawatta, Army Deputy Chief of Staff Major General Janaka Pallekumbura, Mahaweli Director General Jayantha Herath and President of the National Construction Association, among others.

The five-day residential programme at the Ambepussa Army Camp will provide participants with basic knowledge of the construction sector, workplace discipline and professional conduct, as well as teamwork and other essential skills.

Upon completion of the training, successful participants will be designated as “Construction Assistants” and referred directly to construction companies offering fixed salaries, EPF and ETF benefits and other allowances.

The programme is expected to help address the shortage of labour in the construction sector while creating a new career pathway for unemployed youth that offers professional dignity, financial stability and social security.
