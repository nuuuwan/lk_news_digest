Sri Lanka name 15-member T20I squad for Pakistan series

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Cricket Selection Committee has named a 15-member squad for the upcoming three-match T20I series against Pakistan.

The Sri Lanka Men’s National T20I squad will depart for Pakistan on October 4, 2026, where the team is scheduled to take part in a three-match T20I series, a two-match Test series and a One-Day International Triangular Series involving England.

The T20I series against Pakistan will be played in Rawalpindi on October 9, 11 and 13.

Sri Lanka will then participate in the ODI Triangular Series from October 18 to 31, while the two-match Test series is scheduled to begin on November 9.

Kusal Mendis has been named captain of the Sri Lankan T20I squad.
