Amana Bank opens 49th Self Banking Centre in Eravur Pattu

Amana Bank Assistant Vice President Arshad Adhnan along with Eravur Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha Chairman M. Muralitharan, Eravur Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha Member A.S.M. Rasmi and Eravur Branch Manager B.M. Mahir opens Eravur Pattu Self Banking Centre

Amana Bank recently opened its 49th Self Banking Centre in Eravur Pattu, further strengthening the bank’s presence in the region and bringing greater banking convenience to the customers in the area.

Strategically located on Meerakerny Road, Michnagar, Eravur, the new Self Banking Centre provides convenient 24x7 access for cash withdrawals, cash deposits and cheque deposits for individuals, businesses and the wider community in and around Eravur Pattu.

With this opening, Amana Bank’s presence in the Eastern province has expanded to 14 Self Banking Centres, complementing its nine fully-fledged branches across the province.

The opening ceremony was attended by distinguished guests including Eravur Pattu Pradeshiya Sabha Chairman M. Muralitharan, together with the community leaders, business representatives, customers and well-wishers from the area.

Representing Amana Bank at the occasion were Assistant Vice President SME and Regional Branches Arshad Adhnan, Head of Retail Sales and Acquisition Channels Mohamed Asmil, Manager Offsite SBC Operations Imran Mohamed, Eravur Branch Manager Mahir Badurdeen, Kaduruwela Branch Manager Jiffry Razik and other officials of the bank.

Speaking at the event, Adhnan stated, “The opening of our 49th Self Banking Centre in Eravur Pattu marks another step in bringing convenient and accessible banking services closer to the communities we serve. With this facility, we aim to support the evolving banking needs of individuals and businesses in the region and strengthen our relationship with the people of Eravur Pattu.”
