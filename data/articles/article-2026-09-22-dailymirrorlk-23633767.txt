HNB Life celebrates success at NAFLIA 2026 with 10 awards

HNB Life PLC walked away with 10 Life Insurance Industry Sales Awards held during NAFLIA 2026, celebrating the outstanding contributions and achievements of its Advisor and Bancassurance and Salesforce.

The achievement was further elevated by a coveted National Level Best of the Best Award, reflecting the skill and dedication of HNB Life’s salesforce across the island.

The award-winning team included M. A Thanuka, Desika Lakmini, U. S. Palitha, H. G. C. S. Kumara, Anjula Prasath Thilakarathne, G K Erangi Chathurika, G. L. Deepa Shamalie, Salasteen Alanbaker, K. G. C. D. Dharmakeerthi, and H. M. I. Madhuwanthi, who secured the National Level Best of the Best Bancassurance Sales Officer Award.

Commenting on the achievement, Lasitha Wimalaratne, Executive Director / Chief Executive Officer of HNB Life, stated “This is a truly proud moment for all of us at HNB Life. Winning 10 awards at a national platform such as Life Insurance Industry Sales Awards at NAFLIA is a reflection of thecommitment and hard work of our incredible salesforce. I would like to congratulate each and every winner on this welldeserved recognition. Their success inspires all of us and highlights the culture of excellence we continue to build at HNB Life.

Congratulating the winners, Harindra Ramasinghe, Executive Vice President / Chief Business Officer - Advisor Distribution, stated, “What makes this achievement special is seeing our Advisors recognised for their dedication and professionalism. Every award represents the effort, resilience, and customer focus demonstrated by our Advisors throughout the year. I am incredibly proud of our winners and grateful for the contribution they make in helping more Sri Lankans secure their futures through life insurance. Congratulations to all our award recipients on an outstanding achievement.”
