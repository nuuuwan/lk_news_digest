President telling new lies to mark second anniversary: Namal

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - SLPP MP Namal Rajapaksa accused President Anura Kumara Dissanayake of travelling across the country and telling “new lies,” claiming that the Government has little to show for its achievements over the past two years.

Speaking in Parliament, Rajapaksa said the President appeared to have moved away from his earlier promises and was now presenting a fresh set of pledges at public rallies across the country.

He called on the Government to stop making false promises and instead focus on delivering tangible results for the people.

Rajapaksa also claimed that inflation, which he said was negative when the Government assumed office, had increased to 8.1%, while the economic growth rate had fallen from 5.3% to 4.1%.

“The President is promising to establish 1,000 4G towers. He seems to be unaware that 5G is already available. He has also promised to construct 450 tanks. We urge the Government to at least clean two tanks in drought-affected areas,” he said.
