Fake banker, big food order, then theft: Police hunt suspect

Colombo, August 21 (Daily MIrror) - The Police are seeking public assistance to identify and apprehend a suspect allegedly involved in a series of fraudulent activities and thefts targeting hotels and commercial establishments.

According to police, the suspect visited a hotel within the Slave Island Police area on August 3, 2026, and allegedly introduced himself as an officer of a private bank. He reportedly placed a large food order, claiming that a group of people would arrive later for a dinner party.

Police said that during a busy period at the hotel, the suspect allegedly stole several items before fleeing the premises. Hotel management subsequently lodged a complaint at the Slave Island Police Station.

Further investigations have reportedly revealed that the same suspect may have carried out similar thefts in other police areas, including Cinnamon Gardens, Peliyagoda and Nugegoda.

Police have released photographs and CCTV footage of the suspect and are appealing to members of the public who may have information regarding his identity or whereabouts to come forward.

Anyone with relevant information is requested to contact the Officer-in-Charge of the Slave Island Police Station on 071-8591561 or the station's main telephone line at 011-2433829.

Police have also advised hotel operators, business owners and members of the public to remain vigilant and exercise caution when dealing with unfamiliar individuals who visit commercial premises.
