HNB Life’s Ramasinghe and 7 sales leaders recognised at GAMA Global Awards

Award winners and team members of HNB Life at GAMA International Management Awards

HNB Life PLC continued to demonstrate the strength of its leadership and progress of advisor development culture on the global stage, with eight of its leaders receiving recognition at the GAMA Global Awards 2026.

Leading the achievements was Harindra Ramasinghe, EVP / Chief Business Officer – Advisor Distribution, who was honoured with the coveted Master Firm Award, one of GAMA Global’s highest recognitions, awarded to leaders who demonstrate excellence in developing people, building high-performing teams, driving sustainable business growth, and fostering a culture of excellence within their organizations.

The recognition was further complemented by seven additional awards received by HNB Life leaders across the International Management Award and Frontline Leader Award categories, reflecting the depth of talent, leadership capability, and professional excellence within the company’s advisor network.

The award recipients were, Master Firm Award - Harindra Ramasinghe, Samantha Hemakumara – Diamond International Management Award, L. M. Ishan Promos Buddhika – Platinum Frontline Leader Award, Kapila Jayasinghe – Gold International Management Award, Gayan Lokukodikara – Silver International Management Award, G. G. Thakshila Ruwanmali Galdolage – Silver Frontline Leader Award, Velauthapillai Senthurupan – Bronze International Management Award, Gangasiri Gunathilake – Bronze International Management Award.

Congratulating the award recipients, Executive Director / Chief Executive Officer of HNB Life, Lasitha Wimalaratne said, “These awards reflect the strength of our advisor channel and the incredible people behind it. Harindra’s Master Firm Award is important and speaks to the leadership and commitment he has brought to building a high-performing team and developing future leaders.”

Commenting on the recognition, EVP / Chief Business Officer – Advisor Distribution Channel, Harindra Ramasinghe, stated, “While I am honoured to receive this recognition, I see it as a reflection of the collective efforts of our advisors, leaders, and teams across the country.”
