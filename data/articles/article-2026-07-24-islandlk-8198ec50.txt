First Capital enhances service experience with upgraded Negombo branch

First Capital Holdings PLC, a subsidiary of JXG (Janashakthi Group) and a pioneering force in Sri Lanka’s capital markets landscape has unveiled the newly refurbished Negombo branch, reflecting its continued commitment to delivering a premium customer experience through enhanced service standards, modern interiors and a more comfortable environment for customers. As one of the company’s top-performing locations, the upgraded branch represents First Capital’s ongoing investment in strengthening its customer touchpoints and providing all clients with a seamless, elevated service experience.

The refurbished branch also features an expanded team to provide more personalised guidance and support, empowering customers to make informed financial decisions and strengthen their journey towards greater financial wellbeing and stewardship.
