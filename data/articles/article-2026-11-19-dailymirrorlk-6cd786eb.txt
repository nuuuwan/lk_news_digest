Teachers, principals to stage Black Day protest on Monday

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Lanka Independent Teachers' Service Association has called on teachers and principals across the country to take part in a Black Day protest on Monday (23), demanding a Rs. 25,000 living allowance and payment of the remaining two-thirds of the promised salary increase.

The association said rising inflation had placed a heavy burden on teachers and principals, affecting their living standards and increasing the need for immediate salary adjustments.

It urged teachers and principals to join the protest to press the authorities to address their demands, including the payment of the outstanding salary increase.

The association has called on its members to unite and take action under the campaign slogan, urging them to come forward to win their demands.
