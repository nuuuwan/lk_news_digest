SUV driver who assaulted pedestrian near Borella Kanatta released on bail

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The 37-year-old man arrested for allegedly assaulting a pedestrian following a road altercation near the Kanatta Roundabout in Borella has been released on bail by the Hulftsdorp Magistrate’s Court yesterday (22).

The suspect, a resident of Borella, was released on two sureties of Rs. 50,000 each after being produced before Magistrate’s Court No. 08 in Hulftsdorp.

According to the Police., the incident occurred around 3.30 p.m. on November 20 at a pedestrian crossing near the Kanatta Roundabout within the Borella Police Division.

Police said two pedestrians were crossing the road while the traffic signal was red when an argument broke out with the driver of a SUV travelling from Rajagiriya towards the Kanatta Roundabout.

During the altercation, the driver had allegedly got out of the vehicle and assaulted one of the pedestrians.

Following a complaint, Borella Police launched an investigation and arrested the suspected driver on the afternoon of November 21.

After his arrest, the suspect was produced before the Colombo Chief Judicial Medical Officer (JMO) for a medical examination, which reportedly found that he was addicted to cannabis.

He was subsequently produced before the court and granted bail.
