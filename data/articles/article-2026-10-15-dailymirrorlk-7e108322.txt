Arugam Bay named Asia’s best beach

Colombo, Oct. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Four Sri Lankan beaches have been ranked among Asia’s 20 best beaches for 2026 in the Condé Nast Traveller Readers’ Choice Awards, with Arugam Bay claiming the top spot.

Arugam Bay topped the regional rankings with a score of 98.84, placing Sri Lanka ahead of several popular beach destinations across Asia. Located on the island’s eastern coast, Arugam Bay is particularly renowned for its surfing opportunities.

Bentota was the next highest-ranked Sri Lankan destination, securing ninth place with 88.33 points. Tangalle ranked 14th with 83.33 points, while Negombo placed 19th with 78.92 points.

Thailand’s Railay Beach secured second place with 94.44 points, followed by Coral Beach on Malaysia’s Pangkor Island with 93.33 points.
