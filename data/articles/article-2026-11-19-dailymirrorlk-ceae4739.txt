Seylan Bank extends financing facilities to Electricity Generation Lanka

Seylan Bank PLC recently extended financing facilities to Electricity Generation Lanka Private Limited (EGL), a key successor entity established following the restructuring of the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB), up to Rs.6.6 billion, further strengthening its commitment to Sri Lanka’s power and energy sector.

The transaction represents another significant milestone in the enduring collaboration between Seylan Bank and Sri Lanka’s power sector and reflects the bank’s continued commitment to supporting nationally significant infrastructure and strategically important sectors of the economy.

The financing marks an extension of Seylan Bank’s long-standing partnership with the CEB into the restructured electricity sector. With the bank continuing to support EGL’s funding, trade finance and operational requirements, Seylan Bank’s financing facilities are expected to support the entity’s ongoing commercial and operational requirements associated with maintaining critical generation infrastructure and ensuring the continuity of electricity generation across the island.

Seylan Bank also surpassed Rs.1 trillion in total assets in September 2026, becoming the fourth private sector bank in Sri Lanka to achieve the landmark and marking a significant milestone in its growth journey among Sri Lanka’s leading private sector banks.

EGL plays a critical role within Sri Lanka’s restructured electricity sector, having been entrusted with strategically important power generation assets essential to meeting the country’s rising demand for electricity. As a principal generation entity in Sri Lanka, EGL is responsible for maintaining the continued operation and availability of major generation infrastructure, making its operations integral to the reliability and stability of the national electricity supply.

Through its partnership with EGL, Seylan Bank continues to contribute towards the reliability, resilience and long-term sustainability of Sri Lanka’s electricity generation sector, while further strengthening its position as a trusted banking partner to the country’s critical infrastructure institutions.
