Belihuloya Rest House reopens as EKHO Belihuloya

The Belihuloya Rest House has reopened as EKHO Belihuloya, marking a new chapter for one of Sri Lanka’s familiar roadside landmarks while preserving the character and nostalgia that have defined it for generations.

Set amidst the cool, green landscapes of Belihuloya and beside the flowing waterway that has long been part of its identity, the Rest House has, for decades, been more than simply a place to stay.

For generations of travellers, it was a welcome pause along the journey, a place to stop for tea, enjoy lunch, take in the surroundings and listen to the soothing sound of water flowing beside the property.That sense of familiarity remains at the heart of EKHO Belihuloya. The refurbishment was approached with a clear intention: to preserve the spirit of the original Rest House while creating an experience suited to the expectations of the modern traveller. Rather than completely reinventing the property, its much-loved public areas retain the relaxed character and understated charm associated with the traditional Sri Lankan rest house experience.

Guests returning after many years will still find a familiar sense of place, the connection to nature, the pleasure of a leisurely meal and the unmistakable atmosphere created by the waterway running alongside the property.The guestrooms, however, have undergone a complete transformation, introducing contemporary interiors, enhanced comfort and a refreshed design inspired by Belihuloya’s natural surroundings. The result is a property where modern hospitality and heritage exist comfortably side by side.The reopening also reflects the evolution of Belihuloya itself.

The reopening also comes during a milestone year for Ceylon Hotels Corporation PLC, which celebrates its 60th anniversary in 2026.

Under the EKHO identity, the story of Belihuloya is therefore not being replaced, but carried forward.There will still be tea by the water, leisurely lunches and spontaneous stops along the journey. There will still be memories for those returning after many years and now, a new generation will have the opportunity to create memories of its own.
