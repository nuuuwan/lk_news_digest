Teejay Group posts strong recovery in 2Q

The Teejay Group yesterday said it has achieved a decisive improvement in its 2Q FY2026/27 performance, reflecting tangible gains from its ongoing restructuring and transformation initiatives.

Notably, the group recorded a net profit of Rs. 0.4 billion for the quarter ended 30 September 2026, representing a significant 168% increase over the corresponding period of the previous year. This strong quarterly result enabled the group to recover substantially from the Rs. 0.5 billion loss recorded in 1Q, lowering the cumulative loss for the first half to a marginal Rs. 0.1 billion.

On an encouraging note, the group delivered revenue of Rs. 18.3 billion for the quarter ended 30 September 2026, recording an 18% year-on-year (YoY)

growth, while first-half revenue increased by 9% compared with the corresponding period last year. This performance underscores the group’s ability to sustain business momentum and respond effectively to evolving customer expectations and market dynamics, despite persistent geopolitical and economic uncertainties across key export markets. Its marketing strategy remains centred around strengthening strategic customer relationships, capitalising on emerging market opportunities, optimising the product mix and diversifying the customer and market portfolio to support sustainable long-term growth.

Meanwhile, gross profit for the quarter reached Rs. 1.6 billion, representing a 30% YoY increase, supported by 18% revenue growth during the period. This improvement was driven by higher business volumes and stronger revenue generation. The group continued to demonstrate disciplined cost management, with distribution expenses declining by 9% year on year to Rs. 0.13 billion, while administrative expenses decreased by 5% to Rs. 0.84 billion compared with the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year.

Maintaining a strong financial position, the group continued to demonstrate balance sheet stability and healthy liquidity, supported by a robust cash balance of Rs. 11.08 billion. As of 30 September 2026, the group’s net asset base remained strong at Rs. 33.8 billion, equivalent to Rs. 46.88 per share.

Chairman Ajit Gunewardene said: “While the operating environment continued to present challenges, the group made encouraging progress in strengthening its performance during the second quarter in FY2026/27. Our strategic priorities and disciplined execution of the transformation agenda strengthened overall performance and reinforced the group’s ability to navigate challenging times. We remain committed to building on this momentum, responding proactively to evolving market conditions and creating sustainable long-term value for our shareholders.”

CEO Pubudu De Silva added: “Teejay Lanka is pleased to report improved second quarter earnings for FY2026/27 despite external headwinds. The sustained financial strength and healthy liquidity position in the quarter under review reinforces the group’s resilience and its capacity to meet ongoing business requirements while implementing strategic growth initiatives. We believe our winning strategy will help drive greater profitability during the upcoming quarters of the financial year.”

As the group continues to adapt its operating model to the complexities of the evolving global business environment, the transformation process will involve certain adjustments that may exert short-term pressure on financial performance in the coming quarters. These measures are necessary to strengthen operational efficiencies, build a more sustainable business model and establish a stronger foundation for long-term profitability. While this transition may present some near-term challenges, the group remains focused on delivering lasting improvements in business performance and shareholder value.

Against this backdrop, the group will continue to advance its transformation agenda, balancing immediate operational priorities with the structural changes required to ensure long-term business sustainability and competitiveness.
