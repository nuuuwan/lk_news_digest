Sri Lanka AI Week 2026 opens

Sri Lanka AI Week 2026 commenced on September 25, bringing together technology leaders, innovators and industry stakeholders to explore the growing role of artificial intelligence (AI) in Sri Lanka’s digital future.

The flagship AI Conference and AI Exhibition are being held on September 29 and 30, with the two-day conference featuring more than 60 speakers from 16 countries.

The AI Exhibition, being held at Monarch Imperial, Colombo, features more than 35 exhibitors showcasing AI-powered technologies and solutions from global and local brands, including Huawei, ZTE, Sampath Bank, Snowflake, EDOTCO, Connex and NCINGA.

Minister of Digital Economy Eng. Eranga Weeraratne graced the opening ceremony, which was attended by Prime Minister’s Secretary Pradeep Saputhanthri, Secretary to the Ministry of Digital Economy Waruna Sri Dhanapala, Director General of Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka Rtd. Air Vice Marshal Bandula Herath, SLT Group Chairman Dr. Mothilal De Silva and SLT-MOBITEL CEO Riyaaz Rasheed, among other distinguished guests.

The exhibition will remain open until September 30 from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m., providing the public with an opportunity to experience AI technologies and explore their applications across various industries and everyday activities.

Organisers said the event, held under the theme ‘AI Amplified,’ aims to showcase how AI innovations are being translated into practical technologies and solutions while contributing to discussions on Sri Lanka’s digital transformation and AI-powered future. Pix by Pradeep Dilrukshana
