FCID questions Rohitha Rajapaksa over SupremeSAT deal

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Rohitha Rajapaksa, the youngest son of former President Mahinda Rajapaksa, appeared before the Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID) today (26) to provide a statement in connection with the ongoing investigation into the SupremeSAT satellite deal.

He arrived at the FCID this morning to record his statement as part of the investigation.
