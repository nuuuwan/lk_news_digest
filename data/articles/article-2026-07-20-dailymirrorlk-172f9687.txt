Sampath Bank Hosts Fifth SME Business Circle in Jaffna

Building on the success of four previous SME Business Gatherings held in Colombo, Kiribathgoda, Kurunegala, and Anuradhapura, Sampath Bank proudly hosted its fifth SME Business Gathering in Jaffna on 09 July 2026, further strengthening its commitment to empowering entrepreneurs across Sri Lanka.

The event brought together entrepreneurs, business leaders, industry experts, and strategic partners for an evening of meaningful networking and knowledge sharing. Engaging discussions centred on economic trends, sustainability, innovation, and business resilience, equipping participants with valuable insights to navigate an evolving business landscape.

More than a series of events, the Sampath SME Business Gatherings have become a growing platform for collaboration, learning, and inspiration—reflecting the Bank’s vision of being a trusted growth partner for Sri Lanka’s SME community through knowledge-driven engagement and innovative banking solutions.
