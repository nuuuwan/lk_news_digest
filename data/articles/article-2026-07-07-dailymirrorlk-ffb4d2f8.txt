Rohana Randombage appointed Director General of Sustainable Energy Authority

Colombo, July 7 (Daily Mirror) - Chartered Engineer Rohana Randombage has been appointed as the new Director General of the Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority.

He received his appointment letter from Minister of Energy Anura Karunathilaka today (07).

Randombage holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Peradeniya and a Master’s degree in Engineering Project Management from Auckland University of Technology. He also holds a Master’s degree in Business Management.

With over 24 years of experience in local and international industries and businesses, Randombage is expected to lead the Sustainable Energy Authority in achieving its objectives as the country’s key institution responsible for renewable energy development.
