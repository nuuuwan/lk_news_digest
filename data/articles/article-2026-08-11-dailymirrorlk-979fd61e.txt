Six State bungalows up for 30-year commercial leases

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has invited investment proposals from the private sector to commercially develop and operate several State-owned bungalows in Colombo under 30-year lease agreements.

The Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development has called for proposals for the adaptive reuse, refurbishment, development, operation and maintenance of the properties, with the aim of converting them into tourism-related commercial ventures.

The properties listed for development are B13, B14 and B20 on Stanmore Crescent, C61 and C62 on Paget Road, and E01 and E02 on Skelton Road, all in Colombo 5.

The proposed developments include tourism-related retail outlets, boutique suites, cafés and restaurants.
