Sri Lanka to host Sustainability Summit 2026 next month

Sri Lanka Brand Forum has announced that it will host the Sri Lanka Sustainability Summit 2026 on 7 December at Cinnamon Grand, Colombo.

Organised under the Forum’s Sustainable Brand Initiative, the summit will convene under the theme “Reimagining Sri Lanka’s Responsible Future.”

The forum will convene business leaders, sustainability practitioners, policymakers, investors and academics around a pivotal strategic shift: why Responsible Business Conduct (RBC) has evolved from a voluntary compliance exercise into an indispensable prerequisite for companies seeking to stay competitive, investable and trusted.

Responsible Business Conduct reflects the fundamental expectation that businesses identify, prevent and address adverse impacts across their operations and value chains on people, planet and society. It covers fair labour standards, human rights, environmental stewardship, robust governance and transparent stakeholder engagement, these expectations are anchored in global benchmarks such as OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights. Increasingly, global buyers, institutional financiers and regulators use these frameworks  as a baseline criteria for market engagement.

The summit will feature four distinguished international keynotes, delivering operational, academic, and board-level perspectives.

Shankar Venkateswaran, Managing Partner & Co-Founder, ECube Investment Advisors will open the summit by exploring what is required to build a genuinely responsible enterprise, bringing an investor’s and director’s lens to leadership, corporate culture, and governance frameworks that withstand commercial pressure. Dr. Ravi A. Fernando, Chairman & CEO, Global Strategic Corporate Sustainability Pvt. Ltd. will provide a strategic, market-led perspective on the indispensable role sustainability plays in building trusted, future-proof global brands. Dr. Shraman Jha, Chief Executive Officer, Hindustan Unilever Foundation will unpack why sustainable practices must not be treated as a cost centre or tax on business growth, but rather as the foundational baseline for resilient, sustained profitability.  Anthony M. Watanabe, Chief Sustainability Officer, Indorama Ventures PCL will share practical insights from both industry and academia on translating responsible conduct into measurable operational decarbonisation and circularity within global supply chains.

Commenting on the initiative,  Rohan Somawansa, Co-Founder of Sri Lanka Brand Forum said, “Responsible Business Conduct is no longer optional for Sri Lankan companies. It is the licence to operate in global markets and the foundation of brands that last. With the Sri Lanka Sustainability Summit, we want to move the conversation from intent to action, and help our business community lead the region in building a responsible future.”

The Sri Lanka Sustainability Summit 2026 is supported by Strategic Partners the Asia Marketing Federation (AMF), and the World Marketing Council (WMC), Marketing Education Partner: the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM), Knowledge Partner: CIM Sri Lanka, Outdoor Partner: Emerging Media.
