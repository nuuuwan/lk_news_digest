HNB Life among Top 20 Most Outstanding Women-Friendly Workplaces again

HNB Life PLC has once again been recognised among Sri Lanka’s Top 20 Most Outstanding Women-Friendly Workplaces, at Satyn Women-Friendly Workplace Awards 2026, highlighting HNB Life’s ongoing efforts to build a culture that supports, empowers and enables women to achieve their full potential.

The prestigious recognition highlights organisations that have demonstrated exceptional efforts in creating an environment where women can thrive, grow professionally and realise their full potential.

Adding to this achievement, HNB Life Manager People and Culture Chamika Jayawardena was recognised as She Wins 2026 – Most Outstanding Female in the Sri Lankan Workplace, a distinguished individual honour that celebrates women who have made a significant impact within their organisations and the broader workplace ecosystem.

HNB Life Executive Director and Chief Executive Officer Lasitha Wimalaratne said, “Being recognised among Sri Lanka’s Top 20 Most Outstanding Women-Friendly Workplaces again is a reflection of the culture we have carefully nurtured at HNB Life over the years. We firmly believe that empowering women is not merely a workplace initiative but a business imperative that drives performance and long-term sustainability. On behalf of HNB Life, I’d like to extend our sincere appreciation to Satyn Magazine for this recognition, as it inspires us and many more organisations to foster inclusive workplace cultures to make meaningful contributions to society and the economy.”

Executive Vice President and Chief People Officer Navin Rupasinghe stated, “At HNB Life, we are committed to building a workplace where diversity is embraced, inclusion is practiced and every employee feels valued and supported. This recognition is a testament to the collective efforts of our teams in creating a culture that enables women to thrive both personally and professionally.”
