Rs. 1.6 bn approved for printing 2027 school textbooks

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the award of contracts worth Rs. 1,614.69 million, excluding VAT, to 21 printing houses for the printing of school textbooks and modules required for 2027.

The Department of Educational Publications invited bids under the National Competitive Procurement Procedure for the printing of 421 types of school textbooks and modules, including materials for Grades 1, 2 and 6, which will be distributed free of charge to schoolchildren in 2027.

Following the evaluation of the bids and recommendations of the High-Level Standing Procurement Committee, the relevant 421 types of textbooks and modules were selected for printing.

The Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by Prime Minister, in her capacity as Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, to award the contracts to the 21 selected printing houses.
