Showing Compassion to Ven. Gnanasara?

This is in reference to the comments made by Mr. Upali Weerasinghe  regarding the punishment imposed on Ven.Gnanasara Thero and published in the Daily Mirror on  October 20.

He states that Buddha treated Angulimala  with compassion even though Angulimala killed 999 people, and yet attained Arahantship with the guidance of Buddha.

Upali Weerasinghe argues that if Buddha showed compassion to Angulimala, we should also show compassion to Ven. Gnanasara Thero.

There is a difference between the two cases. Angulimala attained spirituality after committing crimes (killings) and never indulged in questionable activities after becoming an arahant. But Ven. Gnanasara after becoming a Buddhist monk shouted without any mercy at Mrs. Ekneligoda during the proceedings of court hearing of her husband’s assassination case.

There was another instance where  Gnanasara Thero donning the yellow robes had spoken ill of Islam.

Thus Gnanasara Thero by his behaviour had tarnished the ideals of Buddhism,  and not the Commissioner of Prison or the Government.

Therefore will it be reasonable to show compassion on him and release him from sentence of imprisonment, when he himself has not shown any sympathy towards Mrs. Ekneligoda?
