74-year-old man injured in Ahungalla shooting

Galle, Oct 04 (Daily MIrror) - A 74-year-old man was injured in a shooting at a house in the Galwehera area within the Ahungalla Police Division early this morning (04), police said.

The injured man, a resident of Galwehera, Ahungalla, was admitted to the Balapitiya Hospital for treatment.

Police suspect that a pistol was used in the shooting. Preliminary investigations have revealed that the gunman had fired at the victim through a window of the house.

Ahungalla Police are conducting further investigations to identify and arrest the suspects.
