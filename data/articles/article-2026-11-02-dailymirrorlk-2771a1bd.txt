Crowds flock to IRD as TIN requirement takes effect

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - A large number of people have visited the Inland Revenue Department’s (IRD) head office to obtain Taxpayer Identification Numbers (TIN), resulting in severe congestion.

Speaking at a media briefing in Colombo, IRD Spokesperson Nandana Kumara urged the public to use the online system to obtain their TIN numbers instead of visiting the department’s offices.

He also requested the public to refrain from visiting regional offices unless absolutely necessary, in order to ease congestion.

The requirement to submit a TIN number for eight specified economic transactions, including banking and financial activities, came into effect yesterday (Nov. 1).

Accordingly, all resident individuals who had reached the age of 18 years on or before December 31, 2023, as well as those who turn 18 on or after January 1, 2024, are required to obtain a TIN.

The TIN is mandatory when opening an account at a bank or financial institution, obtaining approval for a building plan, registering a motor vehicle and renewing a driving licence.

It is also required when registering land or ownership of land, registering a business, transferring shares in a company incorporated in Sri Lanka, and obtaining a credit card.

Meanwhile, the Inland Revenue Department announced that its website would be temporarily unavailable today (02) from 2.00 p.m. to 4.00 p.m. Sri Lanka time due to scheduled system maintenance and updates.
