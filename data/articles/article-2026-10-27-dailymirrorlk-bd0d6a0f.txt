Laugfs Gas explores partial or full exit from terminal subsidiary

Laugfs Gas yesterday said it is weighing a partial or full sale of its terminal subsidiary, with the preliminary talks also covering investment and commercial collaboration options.

In a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange the company said it was holding informal discussions with the interested parties on strategic options for its wholly-owned subsidiary Laugfs Terminals Limited.

The subsidiary owns and operates the 30,000-metric-tonne liquefied petroleum gas terminal at the Hambantota International Port. A full disposal would see Laugfs Gas exit its shareholding in the terminal business. However, a sale remains one of several options under consideration, with no transaction agreed.

The company said no agreement had been reached on the structure or terms of a transaction and no definitive transaction agreement had been signed.

“There is no certainty that these discussions will result in a transaction,” Laugfs Gas said, adding that the financial impact could not be determined at this stage.

The disclosure did not name the interested parties or provide a potential valuation or timeline. It also did not specify the investment or commercial collaboration arrangements being considered.
