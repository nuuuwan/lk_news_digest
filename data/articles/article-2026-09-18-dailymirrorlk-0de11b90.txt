'Pakistan Lifestyle Exhibition 2026'

The Pakistan Lifestyle Exhibition 2026 was inaugurated today at the Galle Face Helipad in Colombo. The exhibition will be open from 2 p.m. to midnight until September 20 It will showcase Pakistan’s rich culture, heritage and craftsmanship, featuring fashion, handmade textiles, traditional jewellery, home décor, cuisine and more.
