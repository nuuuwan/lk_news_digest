Sri Lanka storm into tri-series final

OCT.27 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka stormed into the Pakistan ODI Tri-Series final after crushing Pakistan by eight wickets in the fifth match in Lahore.

Dilshan Madushanka starred with a superb five-wicket haul as Pakistan were bowled out for just 110 in 25.3 overs.

Kamindu Mendis then smashed an unbeaten 62 off 60 balls to lead Sri Lanka to 114/2 in just 18.1 overs, sealing a dominant victory with 191 balls to spare.

Sri Lanka will now face Pakistan in the final of the tri-series, which also features England.
