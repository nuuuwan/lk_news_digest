Tourism Development Levy to be removed under Budget 2027: Vijitha Herath

The government would remove the Tourism Development Levy imposed on the tourism industry through Budget 2027, to reduce the tax burden and create new growth opportunities, Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath said.

He made these remarks while attending the 61st anniversary celebration of The Hotels Association of Sri Lanka, as the chief guest.

Herath said the tourism industry had previously been subjected to double taxation, placing an additional burden on the businesses operating in the sector.

Acknowledging the internal and external challenges faced by the industry, he said the government was committed to making tourism a leading contributor to the economy through a long-term strategic plan.

He stressed that the private sector played a key role in sustaining the industry, while the government’s responsibility was to establish the necessary policies, laws and regulatory framework to facilitate its growth.

Removing the double taxation burden would provide relief to the small and medium-scale hotel operators, newly established restaurants and other tourism-related businesses, he said.

Herath reaffirmed the government’s commitment to supporting the industry and called on all stakeholders to actively contribute to its continued development. Tourism Deputy Minister Prof. Ruwan Ranasinghe, ambassadors and representatives of government and private sector institutions associated with the hotel industry were also present.
