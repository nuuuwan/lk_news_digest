Dehiwala zoo’s hippo calf named ‘Momo’

Colombo, October 2 (Daily Mirror) - After capturing the hearts of children across the country, the Nile hippopotamus calf recently born at the Dehiwala National Zoological Gardens has officially been named ‘Momo’.

The name was selected from nearly 1,000 suggestions submitted by children as part of a special naming competition.

Young Avandha Tamari, who first proposed the winning name, was recognised at a special ceremony held at the zoo on October 1 to mark World Children’s Day.

The awards were presented by Deputy Minister of Environment Anton Jayakody.

The ceremony was attended by officials of the Ministry of Environment and the Department of National Zoological Gardens, including Director General R.C. Rajapaksa, Director of Operations Anoma Priyadarshani, Director of Administration Janaka Thushara Balasuriya, Director of Animal Health and Nutrition Dhammika Dassanayake, Veterinary Surgeon in Charge of the Dehiwala Zoo Dinuka Sonnadara, Deputy Director W.G.K.H. Samarasekara and Assistant Director Piyushiya Samanmali.

Zoo officials also praised the animal management officers, keepers, veterinary surgeons and animal hospital staff for their continued efforts to ensure the health and wellbeing of the young hippo.

Momo has already become a favourite among children and zoo visitors, drawing considerable attention since its birth.

The Department of National Zoological Gardens has invited families and visitors to visit the Dehiwala Zoo and meet the newest member of its animal family.
