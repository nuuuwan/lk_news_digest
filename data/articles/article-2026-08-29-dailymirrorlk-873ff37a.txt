Bribery Commission officers visit Ravi Karunanayake’s residence

Colombo, August 29 (Daily Mirror) - Officers from the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) visited the residence of MP Ravi Karunanayake today, but left after finding that the MP was not in Colombo.

According to a close associate of Karunanayake, the MP had already left Colombo by the time the CIABOC officers arrived at his residence.
