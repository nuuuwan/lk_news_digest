Parliament to debate Chief of Defence Staff (Repeal) Bill on September 9

Colombo, Sep. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Sectoral Oversight Committee on Governance, Justice and Civil Protection has approved the Chief of Defence Staff (Repeal) Bill, which is scheduled to be debated in Parliament on September 9 for its Second Reading.

The Bill was considered when the Committee, chaired by MP Dr. Najith Indika, met recently in Parliament. A group of officials, including Defence Secretary Air Vice Marshal Sampath Thuyacontha, was summoned before the Committee.

The proposed legislation seeks to repeal the Chief of Defence Staff Act, No. 35 of 2009, which established the post to coordinate activities between the three Armed Forces and the Ministry of Defence.

The Committee was informed that, given the country’s present circumstances, there is no longer a need for a separate office or position to coordinate the three services. Such coordination is now being carried out through the Ministry of Defence as part of the ongoing restructuring of the Armed Forces.

It was also noted that transferring the functions of the Chief of Defence Staff’s office to the Ministry could help reduce the Government’s financial burden amid the country’s current economic situation.
