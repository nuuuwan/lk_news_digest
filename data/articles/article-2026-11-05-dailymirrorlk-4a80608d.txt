Rupee strengthens slightly against US Dollar

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee strengthened slightly against the US Dollar on Thursday (05), with both buying and selling rates declining compared to the previous day.

According to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, the US Dollar buying rate fell from Rs. 328.18 on Wednesday to Rs. 328.08, while the selling rate dropped from Rs. 337.13 to Rs. 337.02.
