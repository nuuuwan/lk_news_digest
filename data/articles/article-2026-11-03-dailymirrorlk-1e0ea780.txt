Namal brought to court over Krrish deal

MP Namal Rajapaksa, who is currently in remand custody, was brought to the Colombo Magistrate’s Court by prison officials this morning in connection with the Krrish deal case.

The complaint filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over the controversial Krrish deal is scheduled to be taken up before the Colombo Chief Magistrate today.

Rajapaksa was produced before court in connection with the case.
