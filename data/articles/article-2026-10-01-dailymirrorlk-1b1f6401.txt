Jaffna recycling hub launched

Save a Life officials and partners launch the Jaffna Recycling Hub, a Material Recovery Facility established to strengthen the collection, sorting, recovery and recycling of plastic waste in Jaffna. The facility is part of Save a Life’s Plastic Zero Initiative and was established with the support of Ceylon Cold Stores and Viridis Recyclers to promote sustainable waste management and reduce plastic waste entering streets, land and waterways.
