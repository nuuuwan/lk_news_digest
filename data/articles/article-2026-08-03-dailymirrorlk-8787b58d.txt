University of Peradeniya suspends academic activities due to adverse weather

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The University of Peradeniya has temporarily suspended all academic activities and examinations until further notice due to the prevailing adverse weather conditions.

In a notice issued to students, the university administration said the decision was taken to ensure the safety of students and staff. However, it confirmed that all essential services on the campus will continue without interruption.

Students who are able to travel safely to their hometowns have been permitted to leave the university. Those who are unable to return home because of the severe weather may remain in university hostels, except for the Sarasavi Medura Hostel, after informing the relevant hostel authorities. The administration stressed that no student would be compelled to vacate their hostel accommodation.

The university has also made arrangements to accommodate students staying in private boarding houses located in high-risk areas if they require temporary shelter on campus. Hostel authorities have been instructed to provide accommodation for such students and maintain the necessary records.
