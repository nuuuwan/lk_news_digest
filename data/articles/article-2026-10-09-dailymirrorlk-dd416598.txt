Sterling Aftercare opens Ratmalana facility

Executive Director Ravi Perera, General Manager Deepa Munasinghe and Chief Financial Officer Nalin Warnakula

Sterling Automobiles Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd recently opened a dedicated Sterling Aftercare collision repair and body shop facility in Ratmalana, bringing professional collision repair closer to the customers across the Western province.

The new centre directly delivers on Sterling’s core commitment to make expert repairs accessible by pairing trained technicians and modern repair technology with complete body repair, painting and refinishing services under one roof.

Operating under the established service benchmarks of the Sterling Aftercare network, the centre ensures that proximity does not come at the expense of technical precision.

The purpose-built facility was specifically designed to handle full structural accident repairs, cosmetic body fixes, quick-turnaround repair solutions and comprehensive vehicle painting and refinishing for all makes and models, including Japanese and European vehicles.

Sterling Automobiles Lanka CFO Nalin Warnakula said, “The main goal of opening this dedicated facility is to provide our customers with professional, high-quality collision repair. It’s stressful enough to have an accident without having to look for dependable service across town. We restore vehicles with meticulous attention to structural safety, durability and factory-grade finish by placing skilled technicians, cutting-edge repair technology and full painting and finishing capabilities directly in Ratmalana.”

Following an accident, motorists often find unclear turnaround times, fragmented repair channels and questionable safety standards. The entire recovery process is streamlined into a smooth, documented workflow at the Ratmalana site. This includes structured vehicle reception, systematic damage assessment, precision body repair, specialised painting, strict multi-point quality checks and verified handover.

The expansion strengthens Sterling Automobiles’ position as a comprehensive aftercare provider, helping the motorists at every stage of vehicle ownership, from advanced diagnostics and collision restoration to routine maintenance and mechanical services. The company, which was established in 2007, backs the new location with a network of more than five service centres, more than 400+ workforce, an active customer base of over 100,000.

Individual car owners, corporate fleet managers, insurance assessors and automotive trade partners who need consistent repair standards and documented turnaround times are all directly served by the Ratmalana operation.
