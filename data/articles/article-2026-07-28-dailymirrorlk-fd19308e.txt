Sri Lankan gets 89 days in Australian prison for train assault

July 28 (Daily Mirror) - A 22-year-old Sri Lankan man has been sentenced to 89 days in prison by an Australian court for assaulting a fellow passenger with a glass bottle aboard a train in Brisbane.

The man, whose identity has not been disclosed, was convicted after striking an unidentified person on the head with a glass bottle during the incident on a Brisbane commuter train.

The Australian court imposed an immediate custodial sentence, describing the assault as a serious offence. He was ordered to serve 89 days in prison, just under three months.

Australian authorities treat violent offences on public transport seriously due to the risks posed to passengers in confined spaces. Queensland's public transport network is monitored by police and transit officers, with CCTV footage often used in investigations.

No further details have been released regarding the victim's condition or the circumstances that led to the assault.
