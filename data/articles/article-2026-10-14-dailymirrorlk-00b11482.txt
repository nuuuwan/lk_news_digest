IRD urges taxpayers to submit returns by November 30

Colombo, Oct. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) has reminded taxpayers to submit their income tax returns for the 2025/2026 year of assessment by November 30, 2026.

The department has urged taxpayers to meet the deadline to fulfil their tax obligations.

For further information, taxpayers can call the IRD hotline on 1944 or visit the department’s official website at www.ird.gov.lk.
