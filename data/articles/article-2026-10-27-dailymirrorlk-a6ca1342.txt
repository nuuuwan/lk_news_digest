SLID and ACCA announce second National Director Awards

Following the success of the inaugural National Director Awards, the Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID), in collaboration with the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) announced the return of the SLID–ACCA National Director Awards for its second year.

Applications opened on 23 October and will close on 30 November, culminating in the National Director Awards ceremony on 10 February 2027.

Established as a national platform to recognise outstanding directorship, the Awards celebrate individuals whose leadership, governance and stewardship make a measurable contribution to their organisations, stakeholders and the wider economy.

In a significant expansion of its scope, the 2026/27 SLID–ACCA National Director Awards will introduce three new award categories, marking an important milestone in recognising excellence in governance across Sri Lanka’s public and private sectors.

The Best Governed Organisation Award will recognise organisations demonstrating exceptional corporate governance, board effectiveness and accountability, while the Excellence in Public Sector Governance Award will honour public institutions and state-owned enterprises setting benchmarks in transparency, responsible stewardship and public value creation. The Public Sector Leadership Award will recognise outstanding individual leadership and contributions to strengthening governance within the public sector.

For the first time, the Awards will recognise both individual directors and organisations, reinforcing their position as a national platform for celebrating and advancing excellence in governance, leadership and boardroom practices.

Dinesh Weerakkody, Chairman of SLID said, “Strong boards are the foundation of strong institutions, resilient businesses and a competitive economy. Through the SLID–ACCA National Director Awards, we seek to elevate the importance of effective board leadership and recognise directors who are making a meaningful contribution to advancing corporate governance standards across Sri Lanka”

Anitra Perera, Chief Executive Officer of SLID emphasized, “More than recognition, the Awards are about setting higher standards for the future of Sri Lanka’s boardrooms. We strongly encourage directors and organisations across Sri Lanka to participate, put forward those making a real difference in the boardroom, and help us recognise the leadership that is shaping the future of governance in our country.”

Nilusha Ranasinghe, Head of ACCA South Asia added, “ACCA is committed to championing responsible leadership and sustainable business practices, recognising that long-term prosperity is built on transparency, accountability, ethical decision-making and strong governance. Our collaboration with SLID reflects our shared commitment to equipping and inspiring directors to lead with both financial insight and purpose—strengthening organisations and creating sustainable value for business and society.”
