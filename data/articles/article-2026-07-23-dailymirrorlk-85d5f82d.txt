One injured in shooting near Kuppiyawatta Cemetery in Dematagoda

Colombo, July 23 (Daily Mirror) - A person has sustained injuries following a shooting incident that occurred near the Kuppiyawatta cemetery in Dematagoda.

According to reports, the incident took place when two individuals travelling on a motorcycle were targeted and fired upon by two unidentified suspects who had arrived on a separate motorcycle.

The victim who was injured in the shooting was rushed to the Colombo National Hospital.
