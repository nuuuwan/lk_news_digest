Vallibel Finance among Sri Lanka’s top 100 most valuable brands

Vallibel Finance, Sri Lanka’s leading financial institution among the top five finance companies in Sri Lanka, has been recognised among the top 100 most valuable brands in the Brand Finance Sri Lanka Top 100 Most Valuable Brands 2026 ranking.

Furthermore, it is noteworthy that the company has secured a position among the top five most valuable brands within the financial services sector.

Brand Finance publishes the annual ranking of the world’s most valuable brands across various countries.

Headquartered in London, Brand Finance is one of the world’s leading independent brand valuation and strategy consultancy firms. Through its globally recognised brand valuation methodology, Brand Finance evaluates the financial values and strength of brands.

According to the Brand Finance brand valuation rankings, Vallibel Finance PLC has recorded a significant growth in its brand value this year.

According to the Brand Finance brand rankings, Vallibel Finance PLC has advanced five positions, moving up in 2026 compared to 2025.

Furthermore, the company’s brand value has increased from Rs.1.9 billion to Rs.2.5 billion, while also achieving an A- brand strength rating.

This reflects an important milestone for the company, demonstrating its strong brand image, customer trust and outstanding performance in the financial services sector.
