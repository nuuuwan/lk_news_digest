Graduate teacher recruitment interviews: Details published online

The Ministry of Education has published interview details for graduates shortlisted for recruitment to Grade 3-I (a) of the Sri Lanka Teachers Service to fill vacancies for Sinhala, Tamil and English-medium teachers in national and provincial schools.

The details, including the interview date, time, venue and interview board number, have been published on the Ministry’s official website with effect from October 30, 2026.

The information covers candidates from all provinces and all three language mediums, including full-time disabled candidates.

The Ministry has also published instruction sheets G3 and G4, outlining the requirements for attending the interviews, along with forms F2, F3, F4 and F5, which candidates must submit with the relevant documents.

Candidates have been advised to carefully follow the instructions and attend their interviews on the specified date and time with all required documents.

The Ministry said interview call letters would also be sent by registered post to the addresses provided by applicants in their application forms, commencing November 2, 2026.
