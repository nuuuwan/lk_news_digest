Leonid meteor shower to illuminate Sri Lankan skies tonight

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Leonid meteor shower, described as the most significant meteor shower of the year, will be visible over Sri Lanka tonight (17), astronomer Gihan Weerasekara said.

The meteor shower can be observed in the eastern sky, with Mars and Jupiter also visible in its vicinity, he said.

The best opportunity to observe the meteor shower will be during the early hours of tomorrow (18), Weerasekara added.
