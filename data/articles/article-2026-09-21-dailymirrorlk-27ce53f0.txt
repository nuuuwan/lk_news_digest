Landslide red warnings issued for six districts

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The National Building Research Organisation (NBRO) has issued landslide early warnings for ten districts amid adverse weather conditions affecting several parts of the country.

The warnings will remain in effect until 4.00 p.m. tomorrow (22), the NBRO said.

The affected districts are Colombo, Galle, Gampaha, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Kurunegala, Matara, Nuwara Eliya and Ratnapura.

Level 3 (red) warnings have been issued for Neluwa in Galle District, Walallawita in Kalutara, Pasbage Korale in Kandy, Aranayake, Deraniyagala, Mawanella, Yatiyanthota and Dehiowita in Kegalle, Ambagamuwa, Kotmale East, Kotmale West and Norwood in Nuwara Eliya, and Ratnapura in Ratnapura District.
