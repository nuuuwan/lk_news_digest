Selective early buying gives way to late selling, keeping sentiment mixed

Activity was largely concentrated in the belly of the yield curve, where early buying interest drove a modest decline in yields before fading as the session progressed.

At the short end of the yield curve, 15.05.2028 and 01.10.2028 traded between 10.50 percent to 10.60 percent. Moving ahead, 01.08.2030 traded between 11.30 percent to 11.40 percent while 15.10.2030 was seen trading within the 11.35 percent to 11.45 percent range.

Further along the yield curve, 01.02.2031 traded between 11.60 percent to 11.43 percent while 15.10.2034 changed hands between 12.10 percent to 12.25 percent. At the long end of the curve, 15.08.2036 and 01.07.2037 traded between 12.65 percent - 12.80 percent and 12.70 percent - 12.82 percent, respectively.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.335.78/US dollar, compared to Rs.336.05/US dollar seen earlier.

**Overnight liquidity data for the day was unavailable on the Central Bank website at the time of writing.
