Wild elephant damages Welikanda station, train window

A wild elephant damaged a glass window at the Welikanda Railway Station last night while attempting to enter the station in search of food.

According to railway officials, the elephant was seen approaching the station and trying to enter through a window. It inserted its trunk through the grille of the window while searching for food, damaging the glass and the window grille.

The elephant also damaged a window of a train compartment that was at the station at the time.

The elephant later moved away from the station and returned to a nearby forest area.
