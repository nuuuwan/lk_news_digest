Court orders police not to arrest suspect pending AG’s instructions

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Police have requested media institutions to refrain from further publishing or broadcasting a previous notice seeking public assistance to apprehend a 47-year-old man wanted in connection with several complaints of alleged sexual harassment through social media platforms.

In a clarification issued on November 5, the Police Media Division said the Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) in charge of the Ampara Division had informed the authorities that the suspect had filed a writ petition before the Court of Appeal seeking an order preventing his arrest.

When the matter was taken up before the Court of Appeal, the court had directed that the suspect should not be arrested until instructions were received from the Attorney General, according to the police clarification.

The police had earlier released the suspect's photographs and asked the public for help locating him, saying he had allegedly fled the area and his whereabouts were unknown.
