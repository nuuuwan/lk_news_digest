Four missing after drowning at Pamunugama beach

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - A woman and three children have gone missing after drowning while bathing in the sea at Epamulla beach in Pamunugama today (31).

The missing persons have been identified as a 35-year-old woman, two girls aged four and 12, and a 13-year-old boy.

According to reports, the four were among a group of people who had been bathing in the sea when they were swept away.

Search and rescue operations are currently underway to locate the missing woman and children.
