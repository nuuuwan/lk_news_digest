SJB Calls for Stronger Protest Against 22A

Colombo, Sep.19 (Daily Mirror) - United Trade Unions Convener and Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) Executive Board Member Ananda Palitha has called for a stronger and coordinated public protest against the proposed 22nd Constitutional Amendment on September 24 and 25.

In a letter addressed to the SJB leadership, Ananda Palitha said the party's Working Committee, trade unions, civil society groups and party organisations had not been convened to discuss how opposition to the proposed amendment could be strengthened and expanded.

He proposed that the Opposition Leader and SJB members immediately initiate discussions with other opposition parties, party organisations, lawyers, trade unions and civil society groups to organise a strong public protest on both days.

He said protests should include media discussions, demonstrations, posters and leaflet campaigns, while urging opposition groups to maintain pressure on the Government both when the amendment is presented in Parliament and when it is taken up for a vote.

Palitha also proposed displaying banners across the country calling for the amendment not to be passed and organising urgent joint meetings to coordinate the campaign.

He said opposition political parties, trade unions, civil society organisations and local-level organisations should work together to demonstrate public opposition across electoral districts, Pradeshiya Sabhas and Urban Councils.

He further urged the SJB to take immediate steps to prevent a situation where the Government could pass the proposed amendment without facing significant public opposition.
