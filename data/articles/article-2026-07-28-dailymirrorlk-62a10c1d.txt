LPL 2026 Playoff Race Heats Up

July.28 (Mirror Sports) - The Lanka Premier League 2026 has entered its decisive phase, with six league matches remaining before the playoffs. Galle Gallants lead the points table after 14 matches, while Colombo Kaps and Jaffna Kings remain firmly in contention. Dambulla Sixers and Kandy Royals are also in the hunt, making every remaining fixture crucial in the battle for the top four.

Kandy Royals' Lahiru Udara leads the Orange Cap race with 222 runs, while teammate Nuwan Thushara tops the Purple Cap standings with 11 wickets. The top four teams will qualify for the playoffs, which begin on August 5 at the R. Premadasa International Cricket Stadium in Colombo. The LPL 2026 final is scheduled for August 8.
