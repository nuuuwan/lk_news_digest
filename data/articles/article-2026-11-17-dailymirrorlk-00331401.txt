Man arrested over sword-wielding incident near Kurunegala bus stand

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - A 48-year-old man who allegedly caused a public disturbance while wielding a sword near the Kurunegala bus stand has been arrested by police.

The incident occurred on November 15, with video footage showing the suspect behaving aggressively while carrying a sword in the area subsequently going viral on social media.

Following investigations into the footage, officers from the Kurunegala Police arrested the suspect in the area yesterday (November 16).

The suspect is a resident of Galabawewa in Thalawa. Police also recovered the sword allegedly used during the incident.

He is scheduled to be produced before the Kurunegala Magistrate's Court today (November 17).
