Ravi, Shani could face stronger charges over Easter Sunday attacks, says Gammanpila

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - In the wake of former IGP Pujith Jayasundara and former Defence Secretary Hemasiri Fernando being sentenced to death for failing to prevent the Easter Sunday bomb attacks, Pivithuru Hela Urumaya (PHU) leader Udaya Gammanpila claimed that similar or even stronger charges could be brought against Public Security Ministry Secretary Ravi Seneviratne and CID Director Shani Abeysekara.

Addressing a media conference, Gammanpila said that while the two convicted officials did not have the earliest opportunity to learn of the planned attacks by Zahran Hashim, Seneviratne, then the DIG in charge of the CID, and Abeysekara, then the CID Director, had prior knowledge because they were already investigating into several crimes allegedly linked to Zahran.

He said the two officers were in charge of investigations into the defacing of Buddha statues in Mawanella, the Wanathawilluwa explosives case, the bomb experiment in Batticaloa and the killing of two police officers in Vavunathivu.

According to Gammanpila, information relating to Zahran's planned attacks emerged during the interrogation of suspects in those investigations.

He therefore alleged that the two officers should be charged with criminal negligence, claiming they had knowledge of the impending attacks but had failed to act on the information.
