England change ODI squad before Sri Lanka decider

SEP.27 (Mirror Sports) - England have called up Dan Lawrence to replace Jos Buttler for the third and final ODI against Sri Lanka at The Kia Oval today, September 27, with the three-match series level at 1-1.

Buttler has been ruled out after suffering a left hamstring injury during England’s chase in the second ODI at Headingley.

The England wicketkeeper-batter was playing a key role in the chase after scoring 66 when he felt discomfort while completing a second run in the 38th over. He briefly left the field before returning later in the innings and adding three more runs to finish with 69. England eventually fell 16 runs short of Sri Lanka’s 321 for nine.

His absence leaves England without one of their key white-ball players for a match that will decide the ODI series.

Lawrence has now been brought into the England squad as Buttler’s replacement. The Surrey batter is yet to make his England debut in limited-overs cricket and was withdrawn from Surrey’s County Championship match against Somerset to join the national squad.

England also have wicketkeeping options in Jordan Cox and Tom Banton. Cox is expected to be the leading option to take over the gloves in Buttler’s absence.

Cox has played three ODIs for England and could now be handed the opportunity in a high-pressure series decider at The Kia Oval.

Sri Lanka levelled the series with a 16-run victory at Headingley, setting up a winner-takes-all clash at The Kia Oval.

The third ODI is scheduled to begin at 10.30am local time on September 27.
