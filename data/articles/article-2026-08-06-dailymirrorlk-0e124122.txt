Landslide risk rises in areas affected by Ditwah, NBRO warns

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The risk of landslides and rockfalls has increased in areas affected by Cyclone Ditwah due to recent heavy rainfall, the National Building Research Organisation (NBRO) warned.

According to NBRO Senior Scientist Dr. Wasantha Senadeera, the ground has become saturated with water following the prevailing rainy conditions, increasing the possibility of slope failures.

He urged the public to remain vigilant as landslides and rockfalls could occur even with light rainfall under the current conditions.

Dr. Senadheera said weather forecasts indicate an increase in rainfall on August 7 and 8, adding that high-intensity showers could trigger earth slips, soil erosion and rockfalls.

He advised motorists to exercise caution when travelling through vulnerable areas and urged people living near canals and waterways to remain alert. He also warned residents to move away immediately if they notice signs such as the emergence of new water springs or leaning electricity poles.

Meanwhile, 21 incidents related to the adverse weather conditions over the past few days have been reported, including 15 incidents in the Kegalle District and four in the Nuwara Eliya District, he said. Five deaths have also been reported due to landslides.

The NBRO currently operates nearly 330 automated rain gauges and continuously monitors data from its systems, along with those of the Department of Meteorology and the Irrigation Department. The organisation will issue warnings if heavy rainfall conditions are detected, Dr. Senadheera added.
