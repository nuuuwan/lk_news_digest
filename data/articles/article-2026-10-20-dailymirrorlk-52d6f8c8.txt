Sri Lanka to host 69th IPA World Congress 2026 in Colombo

Colombo, Oct. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka is set to host the 69th World Congress of the International Police Association (IPA) from October 20 to 31, 2026, at the Cinnamon Lakeside Hotel in Colombo.

According to the Police Media Division, the event will mark a historic milestone as the first-ever IPA World Congress to be held in the Asian region.

Approximately 450 delegates representing 69 countries are expected to participate in the international gathering, including presidents, secretaries-general and other distinguished members of IPA national sections.

The congress aims to strengthen international cooperation, enhance professional collaboration among police officers and foster lasting friendships between law enforcement personnel worldwide.

The programme will include official ceremonies, congress meetings and cultural excursions, providing delegates with opportunities to experience Sri Lanka’s rich heritage, cultural diversity and hospitality.

Inspector General of Police (IGP) Priyantha Weerasooriya is scheduled to address the 69th IPA World Congress on behalf of the host nation.

Established in 1950 in Great Britain, the International Police Association is the world’s largest police organisation, comprising 69 national sections and a membership of more than 600,000.
