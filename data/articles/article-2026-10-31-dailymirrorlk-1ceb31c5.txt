16-year-old injured as police open fire on three-wheeler in Kadawatha

Gampaha, Oct 31 (Daily Mirror) - A 16-year-old boy was injured after police opened fire on a three-wheeler at Mahakade Junction in Kadawatha this afternoon (31).

The incident occurred while police officers were conducting traffic duties in the area. According to reports, officers had signalled the three-wheeler to stop, but the driver had allegedly ignored the order and continued driving.

Police subsequently opened fire at the vehicle, and two bullets struck the back of the 16-year-old boy who was travelling inside the three-wheeler.

The injured boy, who sustained serious injuries, was rushed to Ragama Hospital for treatment.

The Kadawatha Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
