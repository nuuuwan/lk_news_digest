Kelaniya Perahera opens 400-seat tourist enclosure

The Kelaniya Raja Maha Viharaya has launched a 400-seat Tourist Enclosure for the 100th Kelaniya Duruthu Maha Perahera, scheduled for January 21, 2027, at 7.30 p.m. The enclosure offers reserved seating, live commentary, complimentary refreshments, dedicated parking and priority entry for visitors. The centenary procession will feature caparisoned elephants, traditional dancers, drummers and fire performers. Tickets can be booked through [www.kelaniyaperahara.com] Pix by Kushan Pathiraja
