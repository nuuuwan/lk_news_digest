Sri Lanka Softlogic Finance Sept quarter swings to Rs101mn loss

ECONOMYNEXT — Sri Lanka’s Softlogic Finance PLC recorded a net loss of Rs. 100.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, swinging from a net profit of Rs. 84.3 million in the same quarter last year, amid the non-recurrence of one-off derecognition gains, higher credit impairment, and rising personnel expenses, interim accounts showed.

The group posted an annualised basic loss per share of Rs. 0.42 for the second quarter (Q2), down from annualised basic earnings of Rs. 0.35 per share a year earlier.

Total income fell 18.7 percent to Rs. 319.7 million from Rs. 393.1 million in the comparative period, primarily due to the absence of a Rs. 140.1 million one-off gain from the derecognition of financial assets recorded last year.

Fee and commission income dropped 53.9 percent to Rs. 14.7 million from Rs. 31.9 million, while other operating income rose 87.8 percent to Rs. 19.2 million from Rs. 10.2 million.

However, core lending performance strengthened. Interest income expanded 35.5 percent to Rs. 285.8 million from Rs. 210.9 million, supported by growth in personal and gold loan advances. Interest expenses rose 23.4 percent to Rs. 150.7 million from Rs. 122.1 million due to higher customer deposit mobilization, resulting in a 52.1 percent rise in net interest income to Rs. 135.1 million compared to Rs. 88.8 million.

Profitability was sharply eroded by loan-loss provisions, with impairment charges swinging to a net cost of Rs. 8.9 million from a sizable provision reversal of Rs. 83.7 million credited in the previous year. This dragged net operating income down 54.9 percent to Rs. 160.1 million from Rs. 354.7 million.

Personnel costs increased 18.3 percent to Rs. 131.3 million from Rs. 111.0 million, lifting total operating expenses 9.8 percent to Rs. 252.3 million. Consequently, operating profit before taxes swung to a loss of Rs. 92.1 million from a profit of Rs. 124.7 million. VAT on financial services and SSCL contracted 78.9 percent to Rs. 8.5 million from Rs. 40.4 million.

Total assets stood at Rs. 7.84 billion as of September 30, 2026, up from Rs. 7.34 billion as of March 31, 2026, supported by an increase in securities purchased under repurchase agreements. On the liabilities side, customer deposits expanded 20.9 percent to Rs. 4.52 billion from Rs. 3.74 billion, while total shareholders’ funds stood at Rs. 2.90 billion

Softlogic Finance maintained a Tier 1 and Total Capital Adequacy Ratio of 46.20 percent, well above regulatory minimums. The board recommended a 5-for-12 rights issue at Rs. 2.50 per share to raise Rs. 1.00 billion to strengthen core capital and expand vehicle leasing, subject to shareholder approval on November 13, 2026.

The company’s shares closed at 3.60 rupees at the end of the September quarter. (Colombo/Oct30/2026)
