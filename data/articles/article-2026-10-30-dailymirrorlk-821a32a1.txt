Indices close in green

Indices closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Distilleries, Hatton National Bank and Dialog Axiata with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.2bn.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Hatton National Bank, Commercial Bank and Softlogic Life Insurance.

Mixed interest was observed in People’s Leasing & Finance, Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting and Panasian Power whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, ACME Printing & Packaging and Waskaduwa Beach Resort.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index gained 1.50%. The share price of Hatton National Bank increased by Rs. 8.00 (2.13%) to close at Rs. 382.75.

The share price of Commercial Bank moved up by Rs. 0.50 (0.25%) to close at Rs. 199.50.

Diversified Financials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to People’s Leasing & Finance) whilst the sector index increased by 1.94%. The share price of People’s Leasing & Finance closed flat at Rs. 17.00. Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting and Softlogic Life Insurance were also included amongst the top turnover contributors.

The share price of Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting closed flat at Rs. 58.00. The share price of Softlogic Life Insurance appreciated by Rs. 0.30 (0.41%) to close at Rs. 74.30.
