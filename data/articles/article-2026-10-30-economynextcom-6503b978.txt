Sri Lanka stocks open up; banks lead turnover

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Colombo Stock Exchange opened higher on Friday, CSE data showed, with the benchmark All Share Price Index moving up 0.29 percent.

The ASPI was up 59.43 points at 20,326.58, while the more liquid S&P SL20 was up 0.52 percent, or 30.01 points, at 5,803.93.

Positive contributors to the ASPI were LOLC Finance (up 4.44 percent at 4.70 rupees), Distilleries Company of Sri Lanka (up 0.92 percent at 55.00 rupees), John Keells Holdings (up 0.55 percent at 18.20 rupees), and Hatton National Bank (up 0.33 percent at 384.00 rupees).

Asiri Hospital Holdings (down 2.72 percent at 25.00 rupees), Richard Pieris and Company (down 2.66 percent at 25.60 rupees), ACL Cables (down 1.66 percent at 89.00 rupees), Royal Ceramics Lanka (down 1.38 percent at 43.00 rupees), and Sunshine Holdings (down 1.37 percent at 28.70 rupees) were top negative contributors.

Market turnover was 222.28 million rupees. Banks led turnover with 98.90 million rupees.

Bairaha Farms PLC reported a 49.7 percent fall in net profit to 213.7 million rupees for the September 2026 quarter, hit by higher taxes, finance costs, and losses in joint venture operations.

The poultry firm attributed joint venture losses to raw material cost pressures, noting an increase in the special commodity levy on corn/maize from 25 rupees to 50 rupees per kilogram, along with an import cap of 20 percent on approved corn that forced the use of pricier local rice alternatives.

Meanwhile, Watawala Plantations PLC declared a first interim dividend of 80 cents (Rs. 0.80) per share for the financial year ending March 31, 2027, with the ex-dividend date set for November 9. (Colombo/Oct30/2026)
