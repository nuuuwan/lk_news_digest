90-day extension obtained to detain Suresh Sallay

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Criminal Investigation Department (CID) has secured approval to detain retired Major General Suresh Sallay, former head of the State Intelligence Service, for a further 90 days as investigations into the 2019 Easter Sunday terrorist attacks continue.

Sallay was arrested by the CID on February 25 in Peliyagoda and has since remained in custody in connection with the investigations into the Easter Sunday attacks.
