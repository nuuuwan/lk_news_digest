Extending judges’ retirement age affecting judicial independence a false argument: Nalinda

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - Claiming that various factions can have different opinions when constitutional amendments or amendment to certain Acts are brought, Health Minister Nalinda Jayatissa reiterated yesterday that the government has a mandate to provide relief to people by reducing backlog of cases in courts.

He told reporters that there are large numbers of pending cases in all courts across magistrate’s Courts to the Supreme Court.

The Minister said the government is planning to take several steps to ease the backlog of cases in courts which include increasing number of courts, filling vacancies in the courts and extending the retirement age of judges by two years.

"No one should panic about it. I wonder how the independence of the judiciary is affected when extending the retirement age of judges, increasing number of courts and filling vacancies in courts. I hope judges and lawyers also want to see the reduction of pending cases to ensure trust in the judicial system," he said.

He said it is a false argument that extending the retirement age of judges is affecting the independence of the judiciary adding that a judge who had worked independently till 60 years will not be a threat to judicial independence after 60 years.

The Minister appealed to everyone to support the efforts to ease the backlog of cases and provide relief to the people.
