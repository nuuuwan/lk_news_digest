All schools in Hatton Education Zone closed tomorrow due to bad weather

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - All schools under the Hatton Education Zone will remain closed tomorrow (August 5) due to prevailing adverse weather conditions.

The decision was taken following advice from the Central Provincial Director of Education as heavy rainfall and unfavourable weather conditions continue to affect the area.

Education authorities said the closure has been implemented as a precautionary measure to ensure the safety of students and teachers. Further updates will be issued depending on weather conditions.
