International Dhamma Programme to mark Esala Full Moon Poya on July 29

Colombo, July 28 (Daily Mirror) - An International Dhamma Programme will be held on Wednesday, July 29, to mark Esala Full Moon Poya Day, bringing together participants for an online Dhamma talk via Zoom.

The programme will be conducted from 6.00 p.m. to 7.30 p.m. (Sri Lanka time/IST) and is open to participants worldwide.

Those wishing to attend are required to register in advance through the official Zoom registration link -https://us06web.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_3s5sp5UCQF6wtgZbdgGbTQ.

Upon registration, participants will receive a confirmation email containing details on how to join the webinar.
