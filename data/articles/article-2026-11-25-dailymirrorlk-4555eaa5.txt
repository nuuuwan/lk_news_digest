Postal Department to take up courier service: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Postal Department is focusing on strengthening its courier services and thereby become a state courier service provider, Deputy Minister of Mass Media Kaushalya Ariyararatne told Parliament today.

The Deputy Minister said the department will be converted into a strategic service providing entity and is looking towards capturing 40 percent of the market share in courier services in the future.
