'Only Mahanayake Theras can remove monk’s robe’: Defence objects to Gnanasara Thera’s prison attire

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Another case filed against Bodu Bala Sena organisation Secretary Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera over allegedly making a statement insulting Islam has been fixed for October 23 for further hearing by Colombo High Court Judge Buddhika C. Ragala.

At the previous hearing, the court issued a warrant against Ven. Gnanasara Thera as he had failed to appear before the open court in connection with this case.

Accordingly, when the case was taken up today, the accused, Ven. Gnanasara Thera, who is currently in prison, was produced before the open court wearing prison attire.

The Attorney General has filed this case against Ven. Gnanasara Thera under the provisions of the Penal Code, stating that his remarks concerning the Holy Quran of Muslims had caused an insult to the Islamic religion.

At today’s hearing, Counsel Sanjaya Marambe, appearing for the accused, addressed the court, objecting to the removal of Ven. Gnanasara Thera’s robe by prison officials.

“My client suffered severe mental distress following the denial of the presidential pardon. That is why he had gone to the monastery. Furthermore, under Article 9 of the Constitution, this is a country where Buddhism is given the foremost place. Only the Mahanayake Theras have the authority to remove a Buddhist monk's robe, not the prison authorities. We object to the removal of his robe by prison officials and request that an order be issued in this regard.”

The Judge, addressing the court, stated that the defence has the opportunity to approach a higher court regarding the objection.

Counsel Sanjaya Marambe and Pasan Karunaratne appeared for the accused, while the Attorney General was represented by Senior State Counsel Supun Ranasinghe.
