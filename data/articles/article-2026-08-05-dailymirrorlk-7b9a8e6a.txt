Experts to share insights on Sri Lanka’s latest tax reforms at KPMG webinar

The latest tax reforms introduced in Sri Lanka have reshaped the country’s tax landscape, bringing both simplified amendments and complex provisions that require careful consideration.

For businesses and tax practitioners, understanding these changes is critical to ensuring compliance while making well-informed decisions in an increasingly dynamic regulatory environment.

To support professionals in navigating these developments, KPMG Sri Lanka Academy will conduct a webinar exploring selected complicated tax issues arising from the recent legislative reforms. The programme will cover the methodology for determining the appropriate basis of tax computation, examine unresolved ambiguities within the legislation, and provide a detailed review of the revised income tax computation methodology. Participants will benefit from practical guidance that can be applied in real-world business situations.

The webinar will be presented by Suresh R. I. Perera, Principal – Head of Tax & Regulatory, Hasna Hassan, Director – Tax & Regulatory, and Rifka Ziyard, Principal – Tax & Regulatory at KPMG in Sri Lanka, offering attendees valuable technical knowledge and practical perspectives.
