KDK advises consumers to purchase their products only through authorised channels

From left: Christy Leung of the Sales and Marketing Department (Southeast Asia, KDK Hong Kong), Danial Cheng, Manager of the Sales and Marketing Department (Southeast Asia, KDK Hong Kong), Hemant Kundanmal, Chief Marketing Officer of Elite Radio & Engineering and Tetsuji Koyano, Manager of the KDK Business Development Department (Overseas IAQ Marketing Center, PES Division, KDK Company Japan.

It has come to the attention of KDK Company that certain parties are involved in promoting, distributing and selling unauthorised products under the KDK brand in Sri Lanka.

The members of the public are urged to exercise caution and to purchase the KDK products only through authorised channels.

Purchasing from unauthorised sources may expose consumers to counterfeit products, misleading representations, invalid warranties, absence of authorised after-sales support and products that may not comply with the applicable certification and labelling requirements for the Sri Lankan market.

Consumers are strongly advised to purchase KDK products only through Elite Radio & Engineering Co. (Pvt) Ltd. and its authorised dealer network.

KDK actively protects its trademarks, intellectual property rights and the integrity of its brand. The company will continue to take appropriate legal action against the unauthorised use of its trademarks and against any person or entity involved in the importation, promotion, distribution or sale of unauthorised KDK branded products.

KDK has already initiated legal proceedings in Sri Lanka against parties dealing in unauthorised KDK branded products.
