Monaragala and Batticaloa SMEs educated on alternative capital raising

Panellists at the Monaragala Issuer Forum (from left): Himashi Wickramasinghe, Manager – Commercial, CSE.; Lakmal Jayarathne, CEO of M Power Capital Securities Limited; Saman Sri Lal - Council member, CA Sri Lanka; Semini Kuruppu - Manager, Corporate Affairs - Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka; Punyamali Saparamadu, Senior Vice President – Commercial, CSE.

The Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE), together with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka (SEC) and theInstitute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (CA Sri Lanka), successfully concluded a series of issuer forums in Monaragala and Batticaloa last week as part of an ongoing island-wide initiative to foster awareness of capital market opportunities in alternate capital raising that are available for small and medium enterprises in Sri Lanka.

The forums were held in Monaragala on 16 July at the Silanrich Hotel and in Batticaloa on 18 July at the FG Golden River Hotel.

The Issuer Forums provided SME participants with valuable insights into strengthening their businesses and accessing growth opportunities through the capital market. Session further covered the SME Creditworthiness Rating Framework, the SME Magazine, the Entrepreneur Awards programme, and the importance of maintaining sound financial management practices to support long-term business success.

Participants also gained a comprehensive understanding of Initial Public Offerings (IPOs), regulatory requirements, succession planning in family businesses, and the opportunities available through the CSE’s Empower Board. The forums concluded with engaging panel discussions that enabled participants to interact directly with industry experts and obtain practical guidance on scaling their businesses and preparing for capital market participation.

The panel of speakers included Manuri Weerasinghe, Director – Corporate Affairs, SEC; Semini Kuruppu, Manager – Corporate Affairs, SEC; Saman Sri Lal, Alternate Chairman of the CA SME Connect Committee, Council Member of CA Sri Lanka and Managing Partner of RTA Sri Lanka; Lakmal Jayarathne, CEO of M Power Capital Securities Limited; Krishnaveny Karmegam, Associate Director of Deloitte Sri Lanka; Atchuthan Srirangan, Vice President – Corporate Finance & Advisory, First Capital Holdings PLC; Punyamali Saparamadu, Senior Vice President – Commercial, CSE; and Himashi Wickramasinghe, Manager – Commercial, CSE.
