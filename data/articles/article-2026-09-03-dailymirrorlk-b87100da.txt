Online applications planned for 2027 Grade 6 school admissions

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Parents of students who sat the 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship Examination will have to await further instructions on the online application process for Grade 6 school admissions in 2027.

The Education Ministry said applications for school placements will be called through an online system based on the results of the 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship Examination.

School principals will be required to submit the relevant applications through the system for students seeking admission to Grade 6 next year.

The Ministry said the dates for submitting applications and detailed instructions on the process will be announced in due course.
