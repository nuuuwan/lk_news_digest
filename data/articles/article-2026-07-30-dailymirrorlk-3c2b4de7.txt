Fleet of 104 Metro buses arrives

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Another fleet of 104 new buses for Metro Transit, a subsidiary of the Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB), has arrived in the country.

According to officials, the new fleet is expected to enhance the efficiency and quality of public transport services across Sri Lanka.

They had announced earlier that the buses will be equipped with card-based electronic fare payment systems and are expected to be deployed for passenger services by September 20.
