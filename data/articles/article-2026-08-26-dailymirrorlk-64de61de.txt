Jerome Fernando’s Glorious Church becomes first Sri Lankan Church to register with UK Charity Commission

Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Under the leadership of Jerome Fernando, his ‘The Glorious Church’ continues to expand and formalize its operations across its four branches in the United Kingdom, a statement from Jerome Fernando’s office said.

To mark this progress, a new office was opened on Buckingham Palace Road, London, recently.

With the official registration of the Glorious Church with the Charity Commission for England and Wales a new office was also opened, becoming the first Sri Lankan-founded church to achieve registration with the UK's Charity Commission a historic milestone for a ministry that began in Sri Lanka and has since spread across the globe.

The Church now plans to further expand its administrative and legal operations for the European region from its London base, marking a milestone in its journey toward realizing its global vision of establishing "a branch in every nation,” the statement said.
