360 Skin Clinic brings doctor-led care to Negombo

Dr. Sarah Fazy, Dr. Evanjana Silva with the 360 Skin Clinic Negombo staff

A new chapter in aesthetic and cosmetic care has begun in Negombo with the opening of 360 Skin Clinic - Negombo, bringing its doctor-led approach to skin, hair and body treatments to the city.

Founded and led by Dr. Sarah Fazy, Founder of 360 Skin Clinic and 360 Aesthetics, popularly known as Dr Cherry, 360 Skin Clinic is an ISO 9001:2015-certified cosmetic, medical, and aesthetic centre specialising in all skincare treatments. The Negombo opening marks the clinic’s third branch as it celebrates five years in the industry, with plans for further expansion across Sri Lanka.

Located at 42 Chilaw Road, Negombo, the new branch occupies more than 4,000 square feet and has been designed as a modern, spacious destination that combines medical care, aesthetics, and wellness. The facility features more than 10 treatment rooms, dedicated therapy rooms and a Moroccan steam room. For Dr Sarah, the expansion is about more than establishing another location. It is about bringing professional, doctor-led aesthetic care closer to clients outside Colombo. “It is a very proud and special moment for me as we officially open our third 360 Skin Clinic branch. Having a location in Negombo has been a long-standing dream, and I am incredibly proud to see it become a reality. More importantly, this allows us to bring the standard of care we have established in Colombo closer to clients in Negombo and the surrounding areas,” said Dr Sarah.

The decision to expand to Negombo was also influenced by the growing number of clients travelling to Colombo for treatments. Dr Sarah also highlighted the importance of seeking qualified medical guidance when undergoing aesthetic treatments. “Aesthetic treatments are not simply about beauty. They involve the skin, anatomy, and the patient’s overall safety. Having qualified medical professionals involved ensures that treatments are appropriately selected and responsibly performed. Patient safety and quality of care remain at the heart of everything we do at 360,” she added.

The Negombo branch brings together medical dermatology, advanced aesthetics and modern technology, offering personalised care for concerns including acne, pigmentation and signs of ageing. Its services include advanced facials and skincare, hair treatments, microneedling, laser treatments, as well as minimally invasive and non-invasive aesthetic procedures.

The clinic also offers a range of beauty and self-care services, including manicures, pedicures, nail art and lip blush, creating a comprehensive destination for skin health, aesthetics and personal care.

Under the guidance of Dr. Sarah Fazy, the Negombo operation is led by Dr. Evanjana Silva, Medical Officer at 360 Skin Clinic – Negombo, and a team of trained professionals. Treatments are carried out according to established protocols, with modern equipment and technology supporting the clinic’s focus on quality and patient safety.

Its ISO 9001:2015 certification further reflects the clinic’s commitment to quality management and established standards across its operations. Conveniently located on Chilaw Road, the new branch is accessible to clients from Negombo and surrounding areas, while its proximity to Bandaranaike International Airport further enhances its accessibility.

The opening of 360 Skin Clinic – Negombo marks another milestone in the clinic’s five-year journey, while strengthening its vision of making professional, safe and doctor-led aesthetic care more accessible across Sri Lanka.
