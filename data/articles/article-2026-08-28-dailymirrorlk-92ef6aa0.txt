Expert highlights hidden holiday risks for children

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - School holidays provide children with more time to relax and play, but they can also increase their exposure to preventable accidents and illnesses, Consultant Paediatrician Dr. Maheshaka Wijayawardena warned.

He urged parents to remain vigilant, particularly when children are outdoors, travelling or engaging in recreational activities.

Dr. Wijayawardena stressed the importance of water safety, noting that heavy rains can make rivers, lakes and other waterways dangerous.

Children should not be allowed to swim or bathe in unfamiliar waters and must be closely supervised around water, as drowning can occur silently within a very short time.

He also urged parents to ensure children use seat belts or appropriate child restraints in cars and properly fitted helmets on motorcycles. Children walking near roads should use pedestrian crossings and younger children should be accompanied by an adult.

Food and drinking-water safety should also be maintained, with families advised to consume hygienically prepared food and use boiled and cooled water where the safety of the water supply is uncertain.

At home, medicines, cleaning chemicals, pesticides and other potentially dangerous substances should be kept out of children’s reach, while balconies, windows and electrical appliances should be secured.

Dr. Wijayawardena also highlighted online safety, urging parents to monitor age-appropriate screen time and teach children not to share personal information, passwords, photographs or their location with strangers online.

He further advised families to take precautions against dengue by using mosquito repellent, wearing clothing that reduces mosquito exposure and eliminating stagnant water around homes.

“School holidays should be a time of happiness, exploration and family memories—not preventable tragedy,” he said.
