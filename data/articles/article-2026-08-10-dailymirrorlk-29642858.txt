Great Place To Work Sri Lanka announces 2026 Best Workplace in technology sector

Winners of the 10 Best Workplaces in the technology industry for 2026

Great Place To Work Sri Lanka has officially announced the Best Workplaces in the Technology Industry for 2026.

The 2026 study by Great Place To Work Sri Lanka surveyed over 4,500 employees across the technology sector, including IT, IT Solutions, ITES, and Telecommunications, achieving an impressive average response rate of 85 percent. From 26 organizations shortlisted, the 10 Best Workplaces were selected using the globally recognized Great Place To Work For All model, which combines the Trust Index survey (75 percent) with the Culture Audit (25 percent).

The Trust Index Survey measures employee perceptions related to trust, pride, and camaraderie. The Culture Audit evaluates the organizations’ people practices and cultural frameworks. To ensure absolute statistical validity, the study maintained a 95 percent confidence level and a 5 percent margin of error. Employee honesty was protected through strict confidentiality guidelines, pre-survey awareness, and an anonymous hotline, followed by rigorous post-survey audits. Eligible organizations required over 20 local employees, a formal Great Place To Work Certification, and a minimum threshold of 70 percent positive employee perception.

The study revealed critical benchmarks. The Best 10 organizations achieved a remarkable average Trust Index score of 90 percent.

Furthermore, these leading workplaces outperformed the Rest in the study by 13 percent when it comes to positive employee sentiment regarding discretionary effort, one of the most important indicators of healthy workplace culture.

While compensation continues to be a growth area sector-wide, the Best significantly outpaced their peers in compensation satisfaction.

Employees also highlighted executives’ business acumen, leadership’s role modelling behaviour, and an inclusive, welcoming culture as defining strengths.

To celebrate these achievements, a learning forum was hosted  recently titled “The AI Workplace Shift: How Emerging Leaders Stay Relevant in a Changing World,” where they honored the 10 Best Workplaces in the Technology Industry for 2026. Presented in order of ranking, the organizations are: Altrium (Private) Limited, Dijital Team, SYNERGEN Health (Pvt) Ltd, Gapstars (Pvt) Limited, Activ8 Asia PVT LTD, Adapt Information Technologies (Pvt) Ltd, SYNERGEN Technology Labs (Pvt) Ltd., Stelacom Pvt Ltd, BoardPAC (Pvt) Ltd, and Perfect Business Solution Services (Pvt) Ltd.

The forum brought together industry experts to explore the intersection of AI, workplace culture, and human capability. Pranath Fernando, Manager – Digital Acquisition, DFCC Bank PLC, and Vice President – Digital Services Chapter, FITIS, delivered the keynote session on ‘Leading Through the AI Shift: Culture, Capability & Change,’ sharing insights on how leaders can navigate AI-driven transformation by fostering adaptable cultures, building future-ready capabilities, and leading change with purpose while the  fireside  chatwith  Fayaz Fazul, Head of Product, Koko and Manori Unambuwe, Vice President – Global Strategy, IronOne Technologies, explored how leading technology organizations are leveraging AI to drive innovation and build future-ready workplaces.

Additionally, Great Place To Work Sri Lanka, presented key findings from the insights study on the Best Workplaces™ in the Technology Industry 2026 in Sri Lanka.

The forum concluded with a keynote by Dilupa Pathirana, Founder and CEO, Barista Coffee Lanka, on “The Human Edge: What AI Can’t Replace,” emphasizing the enduring importance of empathy, judgment, creativity, and critical thinking in an AI-driven world.
