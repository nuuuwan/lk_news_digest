Maldives’ One Realty expands into Sri Lanka with Kadawatha office

Maldivian design and construction company One Realty expanded its regional footprint with the opening of One Realty Lanka (Private) Limited, officially inaugurating its first Sri Lankan office in Kadawatha on 30 July, as part of its long-term growth strategy in South Asia.

The office was officially inaugurated by Chief Executive Officer Yaaseen, together with Chief Operating Officer Nipuna Neranja and Chief Financial Officer Dipun Krishad, in the presence of clients, business partners, invited guests, and members of the company’s Sri Lankan and Maldivian teams.

Having built a reputation over more than two decades in the Maldives, One Realty enters the Sri Lankan market with expertise spanning architecture, engineering, construction, project management and interior solutions. The company has delivered a wide range of residential, commercial and hospitality developments, positioning itself as an integrated design-and-build specialist.

The Sri Lankan operation has been established to offer end-to-end construction solutions for homeowners, businesses and property investors, combining design, planning, construction and project management under one roof.

Speaking on the occasion, the company’s leadership said Sri Lanka presents significant opportunities for high-quality design and construction services as demand continues to grow for professionally managed building projects.

The company also intends to strengthen its presence among Sri Lankans living overseas who are seeking reliable partners to undertake construction projects locally, offering structured project coordination and regular progress reporting throughout the construction process.

The launch marks another milestone in One Realty’s regional expansion, reflecting its vision of bringing internationally developed expertise from the Maldives to neighbouring markets while maintaining a strong focus on quality, transparency and long-term client relationships.

With operations now underway, One Realty Lanka expects to work closely with homeowners, businesses, developers and industry partners, contributing to Sri Lanka’s evolving construction sector through innovative design and professionally managed project delivery.
