BMI Group launches Ahasa TV with focus on education and quality content

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Business Media International (BMI) Group has launched Ahasa TV, a new television channel dedicated to education, quality programming and family-oriented content, with the aim of offering Sri Lankan viewers an alternative to the increasingly fragmented media environment.

Announcing the launch at a gathering, BMI Group Chairman Nilanga Karunarathne said the new channel was conceived following extensive research and consultations conducted across the country. According to Karunarathne, the BMI team engaged with more than 10,000 viewers to understand their expectations and identified a strong demand for a dedicated educational television platform that could appeal to viewers of different generations.

He said Ahasa TV has been designed around the concept of bringing families together rather than encouraging individuals to consume content in isolation. The channel intends to provide educational and entertaining programmes that children, parents and other family members can watch and discuss together, creating what its founders describe as a shared learning environment.

Karanunarathne said the channel would distinguish itself by placing emphasis on the quality and value of its programming rather than following conventional broadcasting formulas.

Addressing concerns regarding the network's use of frequencies previously allocated to the CSN channel, Group Chairman Karunarathne clarified that utilizing available airtime and technical infrastructure is a standard industry practice. He said that acquiring these frequencies—which have been used by multiple channels over the years—does not imply any political affiliation or hidden agenda tied to former operators.

He assured the public that Ahasa TV's news operations will be 100% independent, focusing strictly on national interests and discussing issues pertinent to the citizens, rather than serving political entities.

The launch of Ahasa TV is backed by a team of experienced professionals from Sri Lanka's media, broadcasting, engineering and corporate sectors. At the centre of the channel's content strategy is veteran media personality and academic Professor Senesh Dissanayake Bandara, who has been appointed Chief Content Officer and Channel Head.

The channel's leadership team includes Chairperson Sabeetha Hettiarachchi, while Ajith Ranasinghe serves as Chief Executive Officer. Ranasinghe brings more than 35 years of senior-level experience in finance and business administration and is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants.

Engineer Nilupa Ranaweera has been appointed Chief Operating Officer, bringing more than 28 years of experience in broadcasting and transmission engineering. He is recognised for his work in pioneering tapeless high-definition broadcasting workflows in Sri Lanka and has held technical leadership positions across several major media organisations.

Kisho Vithanage, who has 25 years of experience in broadcast marketing and media revenue generation, serves as Chief Revenue Officer, completing the core executive team behind the new channel.

Meanwhile, veteran journalist Upendra Herath has taken charge as Head of News and Current Affairs. Having been involved in several media start-up projects throughout his career, Herath said he viewed the news division as one of the most influential components of any television station.

The channel also plans to introduce discussion programmes and new formats that move beyond conventional television news presentation.

According to the Ahasa TV team, the objective is not simply to add another channel to Sri Lanka's broadcasting landscape, but to develop a media platform capable of responding to changing audience expectations while maintaining a strong emphasis on education, information and responsible entertainment.
