Rs. 677 mn cigarettes destroyed

Cigarettes worth over Rs. 677 million, seized by Sri Lanka Customs officers at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) between April 22 and July 27, 2026, being destroyed at the Western Power Plant in Kerawalapitiya. The consignment comprised 2,802,960 cigarette sticks, with an estimated commercial value of Rs. 677,069,105. Pix by Nimalsiri Edirisinghe
