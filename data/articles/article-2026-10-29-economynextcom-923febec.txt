Sri Lanka regulator lists Max App Media as prohibited pyramid schemes

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s central bank has listed Max App Media Ltd and its affiliates as prohibited pyramid schemes and warned the public against dealing with them.

“The Central Bank of Sri Lanka has conducted an investigation and ascertained and determined that “Max App Media Ltd. together with its affiliated Max LK (Pvt) Ltd., Max Media Lanka (Pvt) Ltd.” had engaged, conducted and promoted a prohibited scheme,” the regulator said.

The following companies/apps have also been ascertained and determined as prohibited schemes under Section 83 (C) of the Banking Act, No. 30 of 1988, as amended.

MTFE App, MTFE SL Group, MTFE Success Lanka, MTFE DSCC Group

Max App Media Ltd. and its affiliated Max LK (Pvt) Ltd., Max Media Lanka (Pvt) Ltd.
