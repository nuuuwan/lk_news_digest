Kotmale reservoir spill gates opened; flood warning issued for downstream areas

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Mahaweli Authority has begun opening the spill gates and mud sluice of the Gamini Dissanayake Reservoir in Kotmale from 3.00 p.m. today (Aug. 3) due to the continuing heavy rainfall in the upper catchment area.

In a notice issued at 1.00 p.m., the Mahaweli Authority said the spill gates are being opened gradually on the instructions of the Director General of the Mahaweli Authority and the Director of the Water Management Secretariat to safeguard the reservoir and reduce the risk of flooding downstream.

Approximately 250 cubic metres of water per second is to be released into the Kotmale Oya.

The Authority has urged residents and local authorities to remain on high alert as there is a risk of flooding in low-lying areas along the Kotmale Oya, including Nugawela, Riverside, Mawathura, and along the Mahaweli River from Ulapane to Gampola, Weligalla, Gelioya, Peradeniya, Katugastota and Polgolla.

People living in these areas have been advised to take necessary precautions to protect lives and property, while authorities noted that the volume of water released may change depending on inflows to the reservoir.
