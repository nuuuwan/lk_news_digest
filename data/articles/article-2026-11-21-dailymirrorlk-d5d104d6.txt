LOLC Life Assurance top sales performers tour Switzerland and Vietnam

LOLC Life Assurance recently recognised 75 of its top-performing sales professionals with exclusive overseas incentive tours to Switzerland and Vietnam, in recognition of their outstanding performance and contribution to the company’s continued growth.

The initiative comprised two separate sales competitions for the company’s life agency and bancassurance sales channels.

Twenty-five top-performing sales professionals across the two channels were rewarded with an exclusive tour to Switzerland, while 50 high-performing sales professionals earned a fully sponsored tour to Vietnam.

The tours provided the winners with an opportunity to celebrate their achievements while experiencing new destinations and cultures alongside fellow high performers.

LOLC Life Assurance Chief Operating Officer and Principal Officer Jayantha Kalinga said, “For us, these tours represent more than a reward. They recognise what our people can achieve when ambition is matched by commitment and consistent effort. Each of our winners has demonstrated the drive to go beyond expectations while creating value for our customers. We are proud to celebrate their achievements and hope these experiences inspire them to continue reaching greater milestones.”

He further added, “At LOLC Life Assurance, we believe a career in sales offers opportunities beyond financial rewards. It provides the opportunity to build meaningful relationships, develop professionally and grow through new experiences. Alongside structured training, continuous development, attractive earning opportunities and international exposure, we recognise and celebrate the achievements of those who consistently raise the bar. Our aim is to create an environment where ambitious individuals have the support and opportunities to build successful and rewarding careers.”
