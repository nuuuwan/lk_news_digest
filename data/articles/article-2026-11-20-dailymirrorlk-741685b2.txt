Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by 8 wickets to win Test series 1-0

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Pakistan defeated Sri Lanka by eight wickets on the fourth morning of the second Test at the Rawalpindi International Cricket Stadium today, securing the two-match series 1-0 after the opening match ended in a draw.

Chasing a target of 90 runs for victory, Pakistan reached 90 for two wickets to complete a comfortable win.

Sri Lanka's first-innings batting collapse proved costly after Pakistan elected to bowl first under seam-friendly conditions. The visitors were bowled out for just 98 in 35.3 overs, with captain Kusal Mendis top-scoring with 32 runs, while Sonal Dinusha contributed 21.

Pakistan's pace attack dominated the innings, with Mohammad Abbas, Razaullah and Aamer Jamal claiming three wickets each for 14, 21 and 24 runs respectively.

In reply, Pakistan posted 325 runs, establishing a substantial first-innings lead of 227 runs. Ghazi Ghori scored 54, Azan Awais made 47, Tayyab Arif contributed 46, while Razaullah added 42. Sri Lankan fast bowler Lahiru Kumara was the pick of the bowlers, claiming five wickets for 95 runs.

Sri Lanka showed greater resistance in their second innings after being forced to follow on. Half-centuries from Kusal Mendis (53) and Sonal Dinusha (54), together with an unbeaten 66 from Milan Rathnayake, helped the visitors reach 316 before being bowled out early on the fourth day.

However, their second-innings recovery left Pakistan with a target of just 90 runs to win. The hosts completed the chase for the loss of two wickets, sealing the series victory.
