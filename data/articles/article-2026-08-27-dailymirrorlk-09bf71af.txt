Couple arrested with Rs. 70 mn worth of heroin, ‘Ice’ in Matara

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror) - A married couple allegedly involved in drug trafficking was arrested in Matara today (27) after police uncovered a large consignment of heroin and ‘Ice’ during a raid on a beauty salon.

The Matara Divisional Crime Investigation Bureau said the raid was conducted based on information received by the Matara Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) and under the supervision of the Matara Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP).

Police said the 30-year-old male suspect, a resident of Welegoda, Matara, was found in possession of 2.45 kilograms of heroin and 50 grams of ‘Ice’. His 26-year-old wife was allegedly found carrying six grams of heroin.

Police also seized Rs. 625,000 in cash suspected to be proceeds from drug trafficking, along with two electronic weighing scales and four mobile phones.

Preliminary investigations have revealed that the couple had allegedly been involved in drug trafficking for a considerable period, police said.
