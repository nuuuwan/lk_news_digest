Siyapatha Finance opens branch in Wennappuwa

Siyapatha Finance PLC unveiled its 67th branch in Wennappuwa, further strengthening the company’s presence in the North Western Province.

The branch was officially declared open by Siyapatha Finance PLC Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Mathisha Hewavitharana.

Speaking at the ceremony, Hewavitharana remarked: “We are deeply honoured to be of service to the people of Wennappuwa. Reaching communities like this one is what our branch network is for, and we look forward to supporting the ambitions of families and businesses here as we have across the island.”

With a comprehensive product portfolio catering to the ever-evolving financial needs of entrepreneurs, small and medium-scale enterprises (SMEs) and ambitious individuals, the Wennappuwa branch offers a range of services including leasing, fixed deposits,  gold financing, business loans, personal loans, fast draft, factoring, and Smart Pay, the company’s  bill payment facility.
