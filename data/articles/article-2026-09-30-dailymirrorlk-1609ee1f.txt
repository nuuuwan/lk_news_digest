Government working on several relief packages: Nalinda

Colombo, Sep. 30 (Daily Mirror) - Health Minister Nalinda Jayatissa said today that the Government is discussing several relief packages to be provided to the people while maintaining economic stability.

Speaking to reporters, Jayatissa said the Government would provide relief without compromising economic stability, adding that President Anura Kumara Dissanayake is expected to announce the measures in his Budget speech on October 12.

He said the Government was managing the economy amid external shocks arising from tensions in the Middle East.

The Minister also said the Government had already taken steps to provide relief ahead of the Budget, including the fuel subsidy.
