CRIB holds 36th AGM, highlights growth and strategic transformation

The Credit Information Bureau of Sri Lanka (CRIB) convened its 36th Annual General Meeting (AGM) on 9 September.

The event brought together CRIB’s Board of Directors and representatives of member institutions to review the performance and strategic direction for the financial year ended 31st December 2025.

Chairman K.G.P. Sirikumara said, “As the economy transitions from crisis management to growth reactivation, credit intelligence serves as a critical enabler of recovery. In post-crisis recovery phases, CRIB becomes an instrument of trust reconstruction - enabling financial institutions to move toward data-driven, risk-calibrated credit underwriting frameworks that empower MSMEs and support sustainable real-sector growth.”

Driven by heightened economic activity and increasing reliance on data-led lending, CRIB reported strong operational and financial outcomes for 2025. Total credit and analytical reports issued expanded by 41 percent to 13.46 million. The adoption of benchmarked CRIB Score reports grew by 89 percent to 7.7 million, while citizen self-inquiries via MyReport surged 79 percent to 77,000, reflecting greater public financial awareness. Financial results were equally resilient, with total revenue rising 45 percent to Rs. 2.41 Billion, generating a Profit After Tax of Rs. 1.48 Billion (up 42 percent) and an Earnings Per Share improvement of 44 percent to Rs. 5,924.

Director/ General Manager Pushpike Jayasundera highlighted the Bureau’s strategic shift in focus: “2025 marked the acceleration of our strategic shift from a traditional credit information provider to a predictive credit intelligence institution. “By combining advanced data and behavioral analytics with our extensive data assets, we are empowering institutions to move toward scientific, risk-based decision-making while expanding financial inclusion for thin-file individuals and MSME segments.”

The Bureau also highlighted its robust digital architecture, connecting nearly 70 licensed financial institutions and laying the groundwork for integrating Artificial Intelligence across its core data lifecycle, including acquisition, validation, and dissemination. Additionally, CRIB affirmed active adoption and implementation of the Personal Data Protection framework, which includes appointing dedicated Data Protection Officers and developing a sector-wide data usage code of conduct to ensure maximum transparency and security.
