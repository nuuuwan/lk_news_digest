New-look export processing zones and new markets to enhance export earnings

Developing new-look export processing zones and accessing new markets are among key strategies Sri Lanka is to adopt to enhance its export earnings, Finance Deputy Minister Dr. Anil Jayantha said yesterday.

He told Parliament that developing plug and play investment zones, with readymade locations, is one of the key strategies Sri Lanka has to adopt to increase the export earnings.

“Around 20 percent of Sri Lankan exports go to the USA but we are looking to enter into African markets,” he said responding to a question raised by MP Ravi Karunannayke, in the House.

He said diversifying products, going in for knowledge-based export sectors that bring high value, rather than relying on few single products or limited markets, free trade agreements, increasing value addition, innovation, technology adaptation, reducing the cost and time of exporting, strengthening exporters’ market access through better intelligence are some of the strategies Sri Lanka would adopt soon.

Dr. Jayantha said the Katunayake export processing zone brought US $ 80,795 million, while Biyagama US $ 30,413 million, Koggala US $ 2,306 million, Mirjjwila US $ 85.1 million, Kandy US $ 193.1 million, US $2,414 million, Seethawaka US $ 20,483 million, Wathupitiwela US $ 5,747 million, Mirigama US $ ,819 million, Malawatta US $ 1,319 million, Polgahawala US $ 3,945 million, Mawathagama US $ 2,582 million, US $ 1,136 million and Bingiriya EPX earned US $ 65 million from 2020 to 2025.

Furthermore, it was revealed that 74,576 women, 64,812 men and 433 expatriates work in the 16 export processing zones in Sri Lanka.
