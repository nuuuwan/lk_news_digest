Three-wheeler plunges into Mohini Ella ravine, two killed

Maskeliya, July 22 (Daily Mirror) - Two people were killed and two children were critically injured after a three-wheeler traveling from Nallathanniya towards Maskeliya plunged down a steep ravine near Mohini Ella, police said.

According to initial reports, the three-wheeler was carrying four passengers, including two elderly adults and two children, when the accident occurred.

The two elderly passengers died at the scene, while the two children sustained critical injuries and were admitted to hospital for emergency treatment.
