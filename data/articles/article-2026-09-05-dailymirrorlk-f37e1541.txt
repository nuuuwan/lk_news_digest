Fabindia Sri Lanka celebrates first year of operations

Fabindia Sri Lanka/Swadeshi team – the Chairperson of Swadeshi Amari Wijewardene, Deputy Chairperson/Managing Director of Swadeshi Chulodhara Samarasinghe, Chief Executive Officer of Swadeshi Amila Udawatte, Invitees Danu Innasithamby, Anudi Gunasekara and officials of Fabindia Sri Lanaka/Swadeshi

Fabindia Sri Lanka celebrated its first anniversary, marking a remarkable year of bringing India’s rich artisanal heritage, sustainable craftsmanship, and timeless lifestyle experience to Sri Lankan customers.

Since opening its flagship store at Havelock City Mall in partnership with The Swadeshi Industrial Works PLC, Fabindia has been warmly embraced by customers seeking authentic, handcrafted products that celebrate culture, quality, and conscious living.

The launch introduced one of India’s most respected lifestyle brands to Sri Lanka, offering beautifully crafted apparel, home furnishings, accessories and gifts inspired by centuries of traditional craftsmanship.

Over the past year, Fabindia Sri Lanka has become more than a retail destination. It has evolved into a place where heritage meets contemporary living, attracting customers who appreciate natural fabrics, sustainable practices, and products with a meaningful story behind them.

Chulodhara Samarasinghe, Managing Director of The Swadeshi Industrial Works PLC said: “Our first year has been an inspiring journey of building connections through craftsmanship, culture and conscious living. We are deeply grateful to our customers, partners and dedicated team for embracing the Fabindia philosophy. As we celebrate this milestone, we remain committed to offering products that are authentic, responsibly made and timeless in their appeal.”

Fabindia’s philosophy of empowering artisan communities, preserving traditional skills, and promoting sustainable lifestyles continues to resonate strongly with consumers seeking products with purpose. Through every handcrafted garment, home accessory and lifestyle collection, the brand reflects a commitment to preserving heritage while embracing modern elegance.

The first anniversary is also an opportunity to thank its loyal customers, whose continued support has been instrumental in the success of Fabindia Sri Lanka’s partnership and its journey to deliver exceptional products that blend tradition with contemporary style.

Established in 1960, Fabindia is one of India’s leading lifestyle brands, connecting over 55,000 artisans and farmers with modern markets. The brand is renowned for its commitment to traditional craftsmanship, sustainable sourcing, natural materials, and products that celebrate the richness of Indian heritage.

To commemorate the occasion, the store will celebrate with exclusive anniversary offers, curated collections and special in-store experiences, inviting customers to rediscover the beauty of handcrafted living.

As Fabindia Sri Lanka enters its second year, the brand looks forward to strengthening its relationship with Sri Lankan consumers by continuing to inspire mindful living, celebrate artisanship, and deliver exceptional products that blend tradition with contemporary style.
