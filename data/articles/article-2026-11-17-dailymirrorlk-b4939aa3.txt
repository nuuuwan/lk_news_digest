Home Lands awards construction of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo to CHEC

Wang Gang (left) and Nalin Herath after signing the agreement

Home Lands Group has awarded the construction of its landmark 40-storey twin-tower development project at Port City Colombo, Central Park Boulevard to the world-renowned China Harbour Engineering Company (CHEC) in a major construction, conceptual and structural design collaboration.

The strategic construction collaboration between Home Lands and CHEC began earlier this year with Home Lands awarding the construction of its iconic mega project Pentara Residencies Thummulla Handiya to CHEC.

The construction of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo being undertaken by CHEC marks the second time Home Lands has placed its trust on CHEC to deliver a world class product based on CHEC’s expertise and impressive track record. The agreement was signed by Nalin Herath, Chairman of Home Lands Group, and Wang Gang, Managing Director of China Harbour Engineering Company Ltd South Asia and South East Asia.

As one of the world’s leading engineering and construction companies, CHEC brings decades of expertise across Asia, Africa, Europe and the Middle East. Its work across ports, highways, bridges and large-scale reclamation projects has earned the company a global reputation for quality, scale and timely delivery.

The architecture of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo has been designed by SOG Architects (Singapore) and PWA Architects (Sri Lanka). SOG Architects, an internationally acclaimed design studio with experience across Asia, Europe and the Middle East, brings global design excellence, blending timeless architecture, refined spatial planning and contemporary luxury. PWA Architects, one of the country’s respected architectural practices, brings deep local insight, ensuring the design responds to Sri Lanka’s climate, culture and way of life.

The interiors have been curated by Blaine Robert Design, the acclaimed Malaysian studio renowned for sophisticated residential, hospitality, retail and commercial environments across the region. With over three decades of design and build expertise, the studio brings timeless elegance, bespoke detailing and meticulous craftsmanship to every residence and shared space.

The Principal Structural Engineer of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo is East China Architectural Design & Research Institute (ECADI), one of China’s foremost design institutes, with work spanning supertall towers such as Tianjin 117 and Wuhan Center, major transport hubs including Shanghai Pudong International Airport, and projects across 23 countries. The structural design is independently peer reviewed by Meinhardt Singapore, part of the global Meinhardt Group with over 4,500 specialists across Asia, Australia, the Middle East and Europe, ensuring every structural element meets the highest international standards.

Speaking on the project, Nalin Herath said, “Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo sets a new benchmark for luxury living in Sri Lanka. For every element of this development, we have engaged the finest expertise in the world, and Home Lands brings these elements together under one vision and one standard. Our customers deserve a true masterpiece, and that is what we are delivering. With construction progressing ahead of schedule, we are on track to complete the project in 36 months.”

The piling works of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo were completed in just 88 days, against the six months originally planned, by three leading contractors: Access Engineering PLC, Sanken Construction’s San Piling (Pvt) Ltd., and D P Jayasinghe Piling Company (Pvt) Ltd. Completing piling in less than half the scheduled time marks a major early milestone.

Overlooking and merging with the 40-acre Central Park, Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo rises as high-end, ultra-luxury 40-storey twin towers, designed to become a defining landmark on the Colombo skyline. Every resident enjoys a choice of sweeping views of the parkland, the Indian Ocean and the city, along with world-class amenities.

Its global lifestyle is inspired by one of the world’s most renowned luxury lifestyle brands. Backed by world-class partners at every stage, Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo is a true reflection of the Home Lands promise: Trust, Innovation and Excellence.
