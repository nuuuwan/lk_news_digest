Week ends in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Hayleys and R I L Property with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.7 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Vallibel One, Sunshine Holdings and ACL Cables. Mixed interest was observed in Dialog Axiata, Haycarb and Swisstek whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, Janashakthi Limited and Agstar. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 gained 1.4% and 0.9% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.99 Bn.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sunshine Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 1.89%. The share price of Sunshine Holdings increased by Rs 0.70 (2.43%) to close at Rs 29.50.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to ACL Cables) whilst the sector index increased by 1.66%. The share price of ACL Cables moved up by Rs 1.60 (1.70%) to close at Rs 95.90. Dialog Axiata, Haycarb and Swisstek were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata gained Rs 2.00 (4.30%) to close at Rs 48.50.

The share price of Haycarb recorded a gain of Rs 3.00 (1.39%) to close at Rs 218.50. The share price of Swisstek appreciated by Rs 3.50 (4.54%) to close at Rs 80.60.
