Bimal lost ports portfolio over opposition to KKS Port deal, alleges Rauff Hakeem

Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Muslim Congress (SLMC) leader MP Rauff Hakeem, speaking in a lighter vein, stirred controversy in Parliament during the budget debate when he remarked that Minister Bimal Rathnayake was stripped of his ports portfolio because he had objected to developing the Kankesanthurai Port with Indian assistance.

His jovial remarks evoked an angry reaction from Mr. Rathnayake, who insisted that he was not someone to be laughed at, and urged Mr. Hakeem to be straightforward instead of joking.

Minister Anura Karunathilaka, who now holds the ports portfolio, intervened to say that Mr. Rathnayake had done a great deal of work for the development of the Kankesanthurai Port, making it easy for him to press ahead from where his predecessor stopped.

Mr. Hakeem noted that both ministers—who had swapped portfolios—had become angry over remarks he had made jokingly.

He also challenged the government to hold a midterm election if possible, drawing a comparison to the political setbacks faced by U.S. President Donald Trump.
