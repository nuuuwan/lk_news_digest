Are Our Awards About Music or Marketing?

Every award night tells the same story. A trending cover, someone else’s song with a new beat wins “Best Performance.” A viral hit wins “Most Popular.”

Meanwhile, living legends who can still represent Sri Lanka on any international stage are not even nominated. They are alive. They can still perform. But they are unseen.

This is not an attack on newcomers. We celebrate them. It is inspiring to see new artists fill halls and build massive fan bases so fast. They deserve their spotlight.  But why is it only they are highlighted?

Why do award committees keep highlighting the same few faces who just popped into the industry, while ignoring senior artists who can actually sing live for hours without auto-tune?

How are nominees chosen? Is it vocal excellence and originality or is it views, sponsorship and who is closest to the organisers?

An award should honour greatness not just what trended for two weeks. Popularity is not greatness.

If we keep pushing covers over original mastery,  we tell the world Sri Lanka has no world-class voices. That is false. We do. We are just refusing to highlight them.  Give our living legends the main stage while they are still here to sing.
