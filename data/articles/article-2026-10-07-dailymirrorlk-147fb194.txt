CSE selling deepens as ASPI falls towards 20,500

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to firmly negative territory on 7 October 2026, with both benchmark indices closing lower as selling pressure strengthened through the session.

The ASPI declined 132.68 points, or 0.64%, to close at 20,517.55, while the S&P SL20 fell 27.88 points, or 0.48%, to 5,816.40. After briefly approaching 20,700 during early trading, the ASPI trended steadily lower and closed near the day’s low.

Market turnover improved to LKR 1.97 billion, with 60.40 million shares traded. The Energy sector dominated activity with LKR 570.24 million in turnover, almost entirely driven by Lanka IOC, which recorded approximately LKR 568.36 million.

Crossings accounted for a significant 44% of total turnover, led by Lanka IOC with a LKR 469.90 million block. Ceylon Cold Stores recorded combined crossings of approximately LKR 315.06 million, while John Keells Holdings accounted for LKR 91.50 million.

Market breadth remained sharply negative, with only 52 gainers against 158 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 0.33. Foreign investors also remained net sellers, recording an outflow of approximately LKR 23.14 million.

Commercial Bank was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Dialog Axiata, Carson Cumberbatch, Hayleys and Tokyo Cement. Ceylinco Insurance provided the strongest positive contribution.

Despite the improvement in headline turnover, the high concentration of block transactions, weak market breadth and the ASPI closing near its intraday low indicate that underlying sentiment remains firmly negative. With the index now approaching the 20,500 level, investors are likely to focus on whether buying support emerges around current levels in the next session.
