Marking World Pharmacists Day

Association of Practicing Pharmacists President Gayani Nagahage presents a commemorative token to Health and Mass Media Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa during APPCON 2026, held yesterday (29) at Waters Edge, Battaramulla, to mark World Pharmacists Day. Association Secretary Buddhika Godage was also present. The conference focused on the evolving role of pharmacists in Sri Lanka, professional development, medication safety and their contribution to public health. Pix By Jayamal Chandrasiri
