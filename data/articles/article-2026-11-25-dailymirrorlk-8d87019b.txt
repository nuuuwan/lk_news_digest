EFL Global receives Green Business Leadership Award

EFL Global has won Green Business Leadership of the Year Award 2026 in the Transport and Logistics Sector, presented by the Green Building Council of Sri Lanka, at the 15th Annual Green Building Awards and Green Economy Forum 2026.

Presented following a formal assessment, the award recognises the company’s efforts to embed environmental sustainability into its logistics operations through emissions monitoring, resource efficiency, operational optimisation and stakeholder engagement.

Guided by its vision of achieving net-zero emissions by 2050, EFL Global has made decarbonisation a strategic priority and is working towards science-based targets through the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi). Its roadmap includes collaboration with third-party service providers to decarbonise its supply chain, introduction of renewable energy at facilities and exploration of electric vehicles and other lower-carbon logistics solutions.

To support this work, the company collects and validates data on Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions, alongside relevant Scope 3 emissions across its value chain, in line with the GHG Protocol. It uses the GLEC Framework and Clean Cargo methodologies to calculate shipment-level emissions, supporting a more detailed understanding of emissions associated with freight movements.

An initiative to integrate its CargoWise enterprise resource planning system with an emissions data management platform is intended to further improve data accuracy. This forms part of the company’s ongoing work to strengthen emissions monitoring and reporting.

At the same event, EFL Global Manager Sustainability Semini Hasanthi received Outstanding Green Professional of the Year Award 2026. The recognition acknowledged her contribution to translating the sustainability ambitions into practical programmes, strengthening emissions management and reporting and advancing its decarbonisation agenda.
