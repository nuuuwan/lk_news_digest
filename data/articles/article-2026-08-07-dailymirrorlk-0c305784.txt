President calls for emergency meeting over unrest at prisons: Minister

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has called for a meeting of all stakeholders involved in prisons today, Minister of Public Security Ananda Wijepala informed Parliament.

"It is observed that there is a conspiracy to disrupt prisons, but details can only be revealed after a complete probe," the Minister said.
