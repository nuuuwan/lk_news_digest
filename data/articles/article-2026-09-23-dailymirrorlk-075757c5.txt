Omega Traders marks 45 years

Opens new lentil and orid dhal processing facility in Wattala

Celebrating 45 years in Sri Lanka’s food commodity industry, Omega Traders is entering a new chapter with the opening of its modern lentil and orid dhal processing factory at 258, Canal Road, Wattala.

The Australian High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Matthew Duckworth attended the inauguration as the chief guest, while Sri Lanka Standards Institution Director General and CEO Dr. Siddhika G. Senaratne attended as the guest of honour.

For more than two decades, the company has supplied mysoor dhal to the consumers across Sri Lanka through an extensive network of wholesalers, retailers, distributors and other customers. The new facility represents a significant step forward in this journey, enabling Omega Traders to strengthen both the quantity and quality of its processing operations.

The new red lentil processing facility has an approximate capacity of 150 metric tonnes per eight-hour production day, significantly enhancing the company’s ability to serve its existing customer base while preparing for future growth.

Equipped with new-generation, high-end cleaning, processing, sorting and quality-control machinery, the facility provides Omega Traders with greater control over the entire processing operation.

Advanced colour-sorting technology enables the identification and removal of undesirable grains and foreign material, while quality is monitored at multiple stages, from incoming raw materials through processing to the finished product.
