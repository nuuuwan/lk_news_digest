Businessman arrested at BIA with Rs. 6.68 mn worth of ‘Kush’

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - A 44-year-old businessman from Weligama, Matara, was arrested yesterday by Customs officers at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) after he was allegedly found attempting to smuggle 668 grams of ‘Kush’ valued at around Rs. 6.68 million into the country.

The suspect had arrived at the BIA from Bangkok, Thailand, on SriLankan Airlines flight UL-405 at around 4.30 p.m. on September 12, Customs officials said.

According to Customs, the suspect had attempted to pass through the Red Channel while carrying the consignment concealed in the two side pockets of the jacket he was wearing.

The 668-gram stock of ‘Kush’ had been packed into three pellets and tightly wrapped with sellotape, officials said.

The suspect and the seized drugs were subsequently handed over to officers of the Katunayake Airport Police Narcotics Bureau for further investigations.
