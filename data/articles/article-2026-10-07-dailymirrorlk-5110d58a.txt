Shiranthi Rajapaksa remanded, former NSB Chairman granted bail

Former President Mahinda Rajapaksa’s wife, Shiranthi Rajapaksa, who was arrested over alleged corruption, was ordered to be remanded till October 13 by Colombo Additional Magistrate Darshima Premaratne.

Meanwhile, former National Savings Bank (NSB) Chairman Pradeep Kariyawasam, who is the husband of former Chief Justice Shirani Bandaranayake, was ordered to be released on bail.

He was released on two sureties of Rs. 1 million each and a foreign travel ban was also imposed on him.

Shiranthi Rajapaksa was produced before court over allegations that she had obtained Rs. 10 million from the National Savings Bank on the pretext of providing a CT scanner to the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children through the ‘Siriya Saviya’ organisation, but had allegedly failed to provide the scanner and had dishonestly misappropriated the government funds for her personal use.

Pradeep Kariyawasam was produced before court over allegations that, in his capacity as an official of a state bank, he had facilitated the transfer of the relevant funds to the ‘Siriya Saviya’ account, in violation of the proper procedure.

The prosecution alleged that the first suspect, Kariyawasam, had committed an offence under Section 70 of the Bribery Act by aiding and abetting the second suspect, Shiranthi Rajapaksa, in connection with the alleged offence.

The second suspect was also charged under Section 70 of the Bribery Act for allegedly aiding and abetting the first suspect in connection with the incident.

She was further charged under the Public Property Act No. 12 of 1982 for allegedly misappropriating, for her own benefit, funds belonging to the State bank that had been credited to the relevant account.
