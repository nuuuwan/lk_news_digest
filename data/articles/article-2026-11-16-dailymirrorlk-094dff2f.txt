Namal questions timing of trade union leader’s arrest

Colombo, Nov. 16 (Daily Mirror) - SLPP National Organiser and MP Namal Rajapaksa has questioned whether the arrest of an opposition-affiliated trade union leader is an attempt by the Government to suppress growing protests against the 2027 Budget.

In a post on X, Rajapaksa said that while he had no objection to a thorough, impartial and transparent investigation into any alleged wrongdoing, the timing of the arrest was questionable.

He pointed out that the arrest came as trade unions were increasingly expressing dissatisfaction with the Budget and preparing to mobilise against what they considered injustices.

"Is this merely a coincidence, or is the Government attempting to discourage dissent and suppress the growing trade union movement?" he questioned.

Rajapaksa also criticised what he described as a contradiction in the Government's approach to trade union action, saying that the political movement now in power had spent years leading protests, strikes and trade union campaigns against previous administrations.

"It is easy to champion the right to protest when in opposition. The real test of democracy is whether you respect that same right when in power," he said.

He said that accountability should not be used as a pretext to intimidate or silence those who express dissent.
