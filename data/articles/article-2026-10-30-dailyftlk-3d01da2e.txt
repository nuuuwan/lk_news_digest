Hayleys Fentons secures National Gold and Top 10 recognition at CNCI Achiever Awards

Hayleys Fentons Managing Director Hasith Prematillake (left) alongside the Hayleys Fentons team, celebrating the National Gold Award in the Extra-Large Service Sector and Top 10

Energynet, Hayleys Electronics and Fentons Smart Facilities secure National Silver and Bronze Awards

Hayleys Fentons Ltd., secured the prestigious National Gold Award in the Extra-Large Service Sector and a coveted Top 10 Award at the CNCI Achiever Awards 2026, organised by the Ceylon National Chamber of Industries (CNCI) and held at Cinnamon Life, City of Dreams, Colombo.

The recognition extended across the company’s diversified business portfolio, bringing its collective achievements to five national awards.

Energynet Ltd., a subsidiary of Hayleys Fentons with 35 years of leadership in the UPS market, secured the National Silver Award in the Extra-Large Service Sector. With over 18,000 UPS systems installed islandwide, Energynet provides mission-critical power solutions backed by a comprehensive service and support network.

Hayleys Electronics Limited, which specialises in delivering high-quality electronics and engineering solutions across Sri Lanka, received the National Bronze Award in the Extra-Large Service Sector. The company provides electrical, instrumentation and material-handling equipment from leading global manufacturers, supported by comprehensive technical services.

Fentons Smart Facilities, a prominent player in Sri Lanka’s facilities management industry, was also recognised with the National Bronze Award in the Medium Service Sector. The company delivers comprehensive property and facilities management solutions, including hard and soft facilities management services, backed by a strong commitment to service excellence.

These accolades highlight the collective strength of the Hayleys Fentons business ecosystem, demonstrating consistent performance across multiple sectors and reinforcing the company’s ability to deliver innovative, high-quality solutions while maintaining strong operational standards.

Hayleys Fentons Managing Director Hasith Prematillake said: “These recognitions reflect the dedication of our people and our collective strength. Built on over a century of engineering excellence, we remain committed to driving innovation, strengthening operational performance and delivering sustainable value to our customers, stakeholders and the nation.”

The CNCI Achiever Awards recognise organisations that demonstrate excellence across a comprehensive range of business and operational criteria, including business performance, productivity, value addition, innovation, research and development, quality standards, employee practices, occupational health and safety, environmental responsibility and social contribution. The awards’ structured, multi-stage evaluation process further underscores the significance of these national recognitions.
