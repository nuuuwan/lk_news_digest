‘India Calling’ forum set to unlock bilateral trade and investment barriers

In a major push to redefine economic ties between Sri Lanka and India, the Lanka India Business Association (LIBA) has announced the highly anticipated corporate forum, ‘India Calling’. Scheduled for August 6th at the Taj Samudra, Colombo, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, the full-day event is designed to bridge commercial gaps, unpack regulatory friction, and establish a framework for shared cross-border prosperity.

LIBA is hosting the forum to actively catalyze deeper private sector integration, moving bilateral talks from policy theory into actionable investment corridors. The event arrives at a critical juncture as Sri Lanka actively recalibrates its macroeconomic framework and eyes stronger integration with the Indian market.

The forum boasts an extraordinary lineup of keynote speakers flying in from India to address critical facets of the economic relationship:

Montek Singh Ahluwalia: The former Deputy Chairman of India’s Planning Commission and a key architect of India’s 1991 economic reforms, Ahluwalia will discuss India’s massive economic transformation, and the specific structural lessons Sri Lanka can adopt to drive its own recovery.

Professor C. Raja Mohan: One of India’s leading foreign policy strategists and academics, Professor Mohan will present on the geopolitical necessity of “shared prosperity” as a sustainable, long-term strategy for regional stability.

Devdutt Pattanaik: The renowned mythologist, author, and corporate consultant will offer an unconventional perspective, breaking down the “Monsoon Market Myth” to explain how deep-rooted cultural narratives shape consumer behavior and market dynamics in South Asia.
