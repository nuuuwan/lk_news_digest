Can an ill-prepared plan ever deliver results?

This road runs alongside the Serpentine Canal, beginning just before the railway crossing when travelling through Dematagoda. Large quantities of plastic, polythene and other waste have accumulated along the roadside.

Such indiscriminate dumping not only pollutes the environment but also poses a serious health risk due to foul odours and the breeding of mosquitoes.

The ‘Clean Sri Lanka’ programme and the relevant local authorities should take immediate steps to clear the accumulated waste from the site. At the same time, a proper mechanism should be introduced to prevent such indiscriminate dumping from recurring.
