World scene for Sri Lankan Reggae

Sri Lanka’s reggae heartbeat is back on the road, and it has been a busy run since last September, 2025.

Yes, JAYASRI are spending their summer 2026 commitments in Europe with the full band, carrying the sound of home to stages across the globe.

Rohitha and Rohan of JAYASRI spent time in Sri Lanka, between international tours, touching down in Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, Japan, Malaysia, South Korea, Dubai and Oman.

“The Sri Lanka shows were packed, loud, and full of that special energy you only get performing for your own people,” says Rohitha.

“It’s always great and full of fun to perform for our people in packed venues, and gratitude goes out to every fan, organiser, backing band and crew member who made those nights happen.”

A standout moment came in Colombo, where JAYASRI received the Legendary Icons of Sri Lankan Reggae and World Music Award at the Platinum Excellence Awards 2026.

The band members say having won international honours in Europe before, bringing a major award home, to the motherland, made it even more meaningful.

Now Europe calls again and JAYASRI returns for the festival season, with a headline stop at Austria’s ONE LOVE REGGAE FESTIVAL 2026, on the 7th and 8th August, in Wiesen.

It’s one of the biggest reggae gatherings on the continent, and JAYASRI will be sharing the bill with legends like Alpha Blondy and Solar System, The Wailers, Alborosie and Shengen Clan, Culture, Dawn Penn, The Congos, Christopher Ellis and more.

This beautiful reggae festival is set in nature, with camping, food stalls, DJs, a DUB club, drumming, art, yoga, kids’ zones and workshops; it’s reggae the way it was meant to be felt, says Rohitha.

Last Saturday (4th), JAYASRI did their thing with WAYO at the YAMAYA CONCERT, held at the prestigious Indigo, at The O2, in London.

They will also join WAYO’s European run on 11th July in Brescia, and 18th July in Naples, Italy.

Across the Atlantic, the group DADDY and JAYASRI have already been live in Canada.

Rohitha and Rohan with the Legendary Icons of Sri Lankan Reggaeand World Music Award

They played Edmonton’s Glamour Night on 20th June and Toronto’s Lankan Fest Open Air on 27th June.

“From the top hemisphere to the bottom, it’s a season of travel, music, and connecting with audiences everywhere. Feeling blessed,” says the band.

There’s more for the fans, too. A brand-new JAYASRI T-shirt collection is finished and will be available soon through JAYASRI Merchandising. All proceeds, they say, will support underprivileged children and their education in Sri Lanka.

After the summer, the plan is to return to Sri Lanka around the end of September 2026.

“And a final note of respect: Thanks and much respect to Ivan Alvis of The Island newspaper, for all you are doing towards the music scene for decades. We love you.”
