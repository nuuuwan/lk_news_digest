Water supply to be restored within next few hours

Colombo, August 25 (Daily Mirror) - The National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) said the temporarily suspended water supply will be restored within the next few hours following the resolution of an emergency situation that affected water quality at the Kelani River water intake point.

The emergency situation was resolved with the support of the tri-forces, according to the NWSDB.

Following recommendations from the Disaster Management Centre, laboratory tests were conducted to assess the quality of the water before resuming distribution. The NWSDB said water distribution would resume after confirming that the water meets the prescribed quality standards.

Water supply to areas located close to the water treatment plants is expected to be restored within the next few hours.

However, the NWSDB warned that the full restoration of water supply to areas including Battaramulla, Kotte, Nawala and Nugegoda may take longer.

The NWSDB requested the public’s cooperation with the measures taken to ensure the health and safety of consumers.

The public can contact the NWSDB’s 24-hour toll-free hotline on 1939 for further information.
