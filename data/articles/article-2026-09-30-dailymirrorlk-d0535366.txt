Channa Wijewardena named Diabetasol’s Brand Ambassador

Head of Nutrition at Kalbe, Kishan Canagasabey presenting a souvenir to Channa Wijewardena

Diabetasol has announced Channa Wijewardena, Founder and Creative Director of Channa-Upuli Dance Troupe, as the face of its new campaign, “Keep Life in Rhythm.”

The idea behind the campaign is simple: everyone has a rhythm to their life — the passions, people and routines that make them who they are. A diagnosis of diabetes can feel like it puts all of that on hold. “Keep Life in Rhythm” sets out to challenge that, moving away from the idea that diabetes has to define or limit a person, and towards a more grounded message: that with the right balance, discipline and nutrition, life can go on much as it always has.

“Having diabetes for me is about finding the right balance, being disciplined about my choices and taking care of myself,” Wijewardena said. “I still want to enjoy my work, my passions and my everyday life. Diabetes is something I manage, but it doesn’t define who I am.”

Nutrition sits quietly at the centre of that balance. Head of Nutrition at Kalbe, Kishan Canagasabey notes that the choice of nutrition matters as much as the decision to seek it out. “When someone with diabetes considers a nutritional drink, the real question isn’t just whether it’s nutritious — it’s whether it’s the right choice for their condition,” he said. “Research has moved well past the idea that one formula suits everyone. For someone managing diabetes, that distinction matters.”

Diabetasol has been formulated with that distinction in mind — a low glycemic index that allows its carbohydrates to be absorbed more slowly, along with high protein content, fibre, no added sucrose and essential vitamins and minerals, all designed around the everyday needs of people living with diabetes.

With over 30 years of expertise globally and more than a decade in Sri Lanka, Diabetasol continues to sit at the intersection of nutrition and everyday life. Locally, the brand is brought to market through Hemas Pharmaceuticals as a partner of Kalbe, which marks 60 years in operation this year.
