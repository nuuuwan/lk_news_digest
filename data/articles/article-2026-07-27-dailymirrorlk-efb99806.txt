Union Assurance holds Leader Conference 2026

Launches largest sales leadership rewards and recognition scheme in Sri Lankan life insurance industry

Union Assurance brought together its agency leaders from across the country at Union Assurance Leader Conference 2026, held at Grandeeza Hotel, Negombo, under the theme ‘Transform to Triumph’.

The conference served as a platform to celebrate leadership excellence, share the company’s strategic direction and inspire leaders to elevate performance while continuing to build one of Sri Lanka’s strongest agency distribution networks.

A key highlight of the conference was the launch of Union Assurance’s latest Leader Reward Scheme, the largest rewards and recognition programme introduced for agency leaders in Sri Lanka’s life insurance industry.

Designed to inspire sustained excellence and accelerate leadership development, the scheme reflects the company’s commitment to providing industry-leading support, recognition and growth opportunities for its agency force, empowering the leaders to reach their full potential.

The conference also celebrated the achievements of agency leaders who delivered exceptional results for the first half of the year. By recognising and rewarding excellence, Union Assurance continues to foster a culture that empowers its leaders to succeed while reinforcing its position as a partner committed to their long-term growth and success.

Union Assurance Chief Agency Officer Imtiyaz Aniff said, “First and foremost, I congratulate every leader who has achieved remarkable success in the first half of the year and has been recognised as a champion. Your dedication, resilience and ability to inspire your teams are what make our agency distribution one of the strongest in the industry. More importantly, you are the driving force behind our purpose of protecting what matters most for thousands of Sri Lankan families, helping them secure their futures with confidence.”
