JXG enters LMD’s Most Respected Entities in Sri Lanka 2026 for first time

Financial conglomerate JXG (Janashakthi Group) has been ranked 44th in LMD’s Most Respected Entities in Sri Lanka 2026, marking the Group’s first appearance in the prestigious listing. The recognition places JXG among Sri Lanka’s most respected companies and marks an important milestone in the Group’s master-brand journey.

As the holding company, JXG was also recognised across key attributes including Aspirational Workplace, Business Expansion, Business Innovation, Corporate Culture, Corporate Dynamism, Crisis Management, Employee Wellbeing, Honesty and Integrity, and Leadership Profile. The recognition reflects the growing strength of the JXG brand and the collective contribution of its businesses and people in building trust and confidence among its stakeholders.

JXG’s subsidiaries, including Janashakthi Life and First Capital, have also been recognised in the main table of LMD’s Most Respected Entities 2026 rankings, while Janashakthi Finance has been recognised under the Business Expansion attribute, reflecting the strength of the Group’s individual businesses alongside its growing master brand.

Conceptualised and commissioned by LMD and conducted by PepperCube Research Consultants, the Most Respected Entities special edition recognises companies based on an independent survey of Sri Lanka’s business community.

Group Chief Marketing Officer Gamika de Silva said: “This is a proud moment for all of us at JXG. We set out to build a Group brand that is not just seen but lived by our people and reflected in everything we do. Ultimately, the true testament to any brand lies in its people, and the credit for this recognition goes to everyone across JXG who brings our values to life every day. This recognition is an encouraging milestone for us, while also reminding us to keep raising the bar and earning the respect of our stakeholders through what we do.”

JXG’s approach to building trust is anchored in its core values of Integrity, Collaboration, Respect, Performance Driven and Innovation. These values guide how its businesses operate, how its people engage with one another and how the Group serves its customers and wider stakeholders.

As JXG continues to expand its businesses, the Group remains focused on strengthening its market presence, creating long-term value and contributing to the growth of Sri Lanka’s financial services sector, guided by its purpose, Breaking Barriers, Forging Futures.
