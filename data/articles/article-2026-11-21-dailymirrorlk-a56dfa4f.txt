Rs. 1.66 billion spent on 72 stalled education projects

Around Rs. 1,664 million has been spent on 72 education development projects launched between 2018 and 2021, of which 20 remain stalled due to contractual issues, the Committee on Public Accounts (COPA) was informed.

The projects were discussed when COPA met in Parliament on November 19 under the chairmanship of MP Kabir Hashim to examine the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education's Tri-Annual Reports for 2021–2023, the Auditor General's Report for 2024 and its current performance.

Of the 72 projects, 35 have been completed, while work on 13 is ongoing. Four projects have been suspended, and another 20 remain stalled due to contractual issues.

Officials informed the Committee that steps were being taken to resolve the contractual issues and restart the stalled projects.

Meanwhile, nearly Rs. 1,600 million allocated in 2019 for the development of selected schools and the purchase of equipment under a programme to establish three National Schools in each Divisional Secretariat Division was also discussed.

Officials said the programme had been discontinued following a policy decision. The Committee instructed them to submit a detailed report on the funds allocated to each school and the development work carried out.

The Committee was also informed that a stock of computers ordered by the Ministry had been rejected as they failed to meet the required technical specifications. No payments had been made for the computers, while the supplier had filed two court cases over the matter.

The progress of the National Education Management Information System (NEMIS) was also reviewed. Officials said priority had been given to entering teacher information and student assessment data into the system.

Teacher transfers in several provinces are already being handled through NEMIS, while plans are underway to extend the system to all provinces and National Schools.

Officials further informed the Committee that delays in preparing estimates, a shortage of technical officers and tender-related issues had affected the utilisation of capital allocations.

COPA also raised concerns over delays in responding to audit queries and instructed officials to take prompt action to resolve outstanding matters.

Deputy Ministers Nalin Hewage and Major General (Retd.) Aruna Jayasekara, along with several MPs, attended the meeting.
