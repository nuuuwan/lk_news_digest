SriLankan Airlines extends suspension of Riyadh, Dammam flights until Oct. 24

Colombo, Oct. 16 (Daily Mirror) - SriLankan Airlines has extended the suspension of all flights between Colombo and the Saudi Arabian cities of Riyadh and Dammam until October 24, 2026, citing the prevailing security situation in the region.

In a statement issued today (16), the national carrier said flight operations would resume once conditions were assessed as suitable for commercial aviation.

The airline said the safety and security of passengers and crew remained its highest priority.

Passengers seeking further information can contact the airline on 1979 within Sri Lanka, +94 11 777 1979 from overseas, or via WhatsApp on +94 74 444 1979 (chat only). They can also contact their travel agents or visit www.srilankan.com.

SriLankan Airlines apologised for the inconvenience and thanked passengers for their understanding and cooperation.
