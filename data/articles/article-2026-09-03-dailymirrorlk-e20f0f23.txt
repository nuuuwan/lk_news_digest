Foreign tourist gets sex invitations under hotel door

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Three Sri lankan men were arrested by the Unawatuna Tourist Police after allegedly slipping written invitations under the room door of a 63-year-old Australian woman’ inviting her to engage in sexual activity with them.

Police said the woman, who had arrived in Sri Lanka, had been staying at a leading hotel in Unawatuna for more than a week.

According to police, the three suspects, who are from the Dehiwala and Panadura areas, were staying in a room adjacent to that of the Australian tourist.

The suspects allegedly placed slips of paper containing the invitations, written in English, under the woman’s room door.

Following a complaint, officers from the Unawatuna Tourist Police arrested the three suspects and handed them over to the Habaraduwa Police for further investigations.

The suspects were due to be produced before the Galle Magistrate’s Court yesterday.

A police team under OIC Habaraduwa police IP Prasanna Elgiriyage and SI Karunadasa is conducting further investigations on the instructions of SP Jagath Samarasinghe
