SLPP says Mahinda Kahandagama was never a party member

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) has said that Mahinda Kahandagama was never a member of the party and that no disciplinary action could therefore be taken against him.

Responding to a question on whether the party planned to take action against Kahandagama, an SLPP representative said there had been confusion over his political affiliation.

He said Kahandagama had been nominated by Vasudeva Nanayakkara when the parties were part of an alliance, and had contested under the common ‘Pohottuwa’ symbol.

“Kahandagama was never a member of our party. He was someone nominated by Vasudeva, and we accepted that nomination,” he said.

He said the same arrangement applied to other individuals nominated by different parties within the alliance, who contested elections under the common symbol.

The representative also clarified that Vasudeva Nanayakkara himself was a member of the Nava Sama Samaja Party (NSSP) and not the SLPP.

“Vasudeva belongs to the Nava Sama Samaja Party. When we formed the alliance, the respective parties nominated individuals for us to field as candidates. Consequently, they contested under the common ‘Pohottuwa’ symbol,” he said.

He added that the fact that Kahandagama contested under the SLPP’s common symbol did not mean that he was a member of the party.
