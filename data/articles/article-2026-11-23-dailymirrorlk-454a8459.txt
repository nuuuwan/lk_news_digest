Democrats win largest House majority since 1992 after flipping North Carolina seat

BBC - The Democratic Party has won the largest majority in the US House of Representatives since 1992, after Democrat Chuck Hubbard unseated Republican Representative Virginia Foxx in North Carolina.

The race was called on Sunday, with Hubbard beating Foxx by 330 votes with 99% counted. It was one of the closest contests of the recent midterm elections, in which Democrats also secured the Senate.

The party has now won at least 258 House seats in the midterms compared to Republicans, who hold at least 173 seats, with four races still yet to be called.

Neither party had won as many House races since 1992, when Bill Clinton won the presidency and Democrats won a total of 258 seats.

Sunday's result came after Foxx, who has represented North Carolina's 5th Congressional District since 2005, requested a recount. This is allowed under state law when the margin of victory is under 1%.

"I think the American people are just sick of what's going on and they wanted a change. They voted for change," Hubbard told the BBC News Channel after his victory. He pledged to work alongside his fellow lawmakers to hold President Donald Trump accountable.

Earlier, he wrote on X that he had spoken to Foxx, "who graciously conceded following the results of the recount process".

Hubbard added: "I deeply appreciate her commitment to a smooth transition of the office and constituent casework. We may not always agree, but we both love North Carolina and the people of the Fifth District."

Foxx, meanwhile, said in a statement: "After running a campaign focused on my track record of commonsense solutions and conservative values, I'm humbled and grateful that so many North Carolina voters responded by supporting my candidacy, even if we came up just a few votes short."

Hubbard's win, which comes after he lost to Foxx in the 2024 midterms by nine percentage points, followed a number of Democratic victories in deeply Republican districts including South Carolina, Texas, Alaska and Iowa.

Hubbard, like many Democrats running in the midterms, focused on the rising cost of living in the US during the war with Iran - a top issue for voters.

Trump himself - as well as Vice President JD Vance - have suggested inflation caused by the war in Iran was probably to blame for the Republican losses.

Hubbard has also said he will also prioritise working families and small businesses, supporting public education and expanding access to affordable healthcare, according to his website.

The remaining House races yet to be called are in Tennessee and Minnesota, while two elections in Louisiana will go to a runoff in December. In Tennessee's fourth district, Democrat Victoria Broderick leads by fewer than 300 votes, and in Minnesota's first district, Democrat Jake Johnson is winning by fewer than 500 votes.

Democrats may use their upcoming majority in the House and control of the Senate to investigate conflicts of interest and other actions by the president and those close to him, while the Senate also has the power to confirm or block the president's cabinet and judicial nominations. They will also be able to pass legislation through Congress easily, though Trump still has to give final sign off for it to become law.

However, even with control of both chambers of Congress, they will still have limits on their power as Trump can veto legislation.
