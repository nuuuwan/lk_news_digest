Pay&Go partners with Sejaya Micro Credit to simplify loan repayments

MegaPay (Pvt) Ltd, operator of Pay&Go, has partnered with Sejaya Micro Credit Ltd., a licensed microfinance company serving clients via 55+ branches across the country and a fully owned subsidiary of Gojo & Company Inc., Japan.

The partnership gives Sejaya’s clients a faster way to repay loan installments and supports both companies’ commitment to financial inclusion.

Sejaya clients can now repay loan instalments through Pay&Go’s mobile app, its network of over 1,000 self-service kiosks island-wide, or Pay&Go POS machines available at stores across the country. Repayments can be made 24/7, without a branch visit. Clients receive instant digital receipts and payment confirmation.

“This partnership is a step closer to our mission of making payments accessible to every Sri Lankan. Together with SEJAYA, we are putting that convenience directly in the hands of those who need it most,” said Vardan Aslibekyan, General Director, MegaPay (Pvt) Ltd.

Many of Sejaya’s borrowers are self-employed entrepreneurs running shops, tailoring businesses, and other small enterprises. Both companies are exploring ways to extend Pay&Go POS solutions to these clients through a “Repay & Grow” initiative, which aims to let Sejaya borrowers accept card and bill payments in their own shops. Further details will be shared as the initiative progresses.

“Our clients deserve financial services that work around their lives, not the other way around. Pay&Go makes that possible,” said Rizanth Francis, Chief Executive Officer, Sejaya Micro Credit Ltd.

The two organisations also discussed plans to expand digital payment acceptance across the country, with a shared focus on increasing cashless transactions, promoting financial literacy, and supporting economic empowerment through payment technology.
