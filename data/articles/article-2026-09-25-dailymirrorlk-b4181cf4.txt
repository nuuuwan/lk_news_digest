Sri Lanka beat England to level ODI series

Colombo, Sep 25 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka fought back brilliantly to defeat England by 16 runs in the second ODI at Headingley on September 24, levelling the three-match series 1-1. The victory came after Sri Lanka had lost the opening match and now sets up a decisive final ODI at The Oval on Sunday.

Sri Lanka's innings was led by captain Kusal Mendis, who produced an outstanding 121 from 100 balls. His century, supported by useful contributions from the middle and lower order, helped Sri Lanka reach 321 for 6 from their 50 overs.

England made a flying start to their chase, with Tom Banton and Ben Duckett putting together 143 runs for the opening wicket. Banton scored 73 while Duckett made 67, leaving England in a strong position to chase down the target.

Sri Lanka turned the match around through left-arm spinner Dunith Wellalage. He dismissed both England openers and captain Harry Brook, finishing with 3 for 49. His spell triggered a collapse that changed the momentum of the match.

Jos Buttler then appeared to be taking England towards victory, scoring 69 from 49 balls. However, he suffered a hamstring problem and had to retire hurt with England still needing 68 runs from 75 balls. Buttler later returned to the crease despite the injury, but England struggled to maintain the required scoring rate.

Will Jacks kept England's hopes alive with a fighting 52, but Sri Lanka's bowlers held their nerve during the closing overs. England finished on 305 for 9, falling 16 runs short of the target.

The result means the ODI series is level at 1-1. The final match will be played at The Oval in London on Sunday, September 27.1
