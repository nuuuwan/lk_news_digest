Allianz Lanka certified as a Great Place to Work again

Allianz Lanka has once again been recognised as a Great Place to Work in Sri Lanka, earning the prestigious certification for the third consecutive year.

The achievement reflects the company’s continued commitment to building a workplace culture centred on trust, collaboration, innovation and employee well-being.

The globally recognised Great Place to Work certification is awarded based on employee feedback and evaluates workplace excellence across key areas including credibility, respect, fairness, pride and camaraderie.

Achieving this distinction for three consecutive years reinforces Allianz Lanka’s position as one of the leading employers in Sri Lanka’s insurance industry.
