HNB Finance marks Children’s Day with special programme

HNB Finance marked World Children’s Day 2026 with a special programme attended by nearly 4,000 students at Debarawewa President’s College in Tissamaharama.

The initiative formed part of the company’s corporate social responsibility (CSR) agenda, with a focus on children’s wellbeing, community engagement and the promotion of positive financial habits from an early age.

Organised in keeping with this year’s World Children’s Day theme ‘Strong Minds-Enriched Children,’ the programme brought together students, teachers and HNB Finance  representatives for a series of educational, creative and recreational activities aimed at encouraging self-confidence, creativity and emotional wellbeing.

The celebrations were attended by Debarawewa President’s College Principal R. A. S. Keerthirathne and HNB Finance Head of Microfinance Savings and Talent Development Anura Udawatta as chief guests. HNB Finance Tissamaharama Branch Manager, Kithsiri Malaviarachchige, representatives from the company’s Head Office and Tissamaharama branch, and members of the school’s teaching staff were also present.

Supported by ‘Yalu’, HNB Finance’s children’s savings account, the programme was held from 7.30 a.m. to 1.30 p.m. and featured interactive competitions, creative activities and educational entertainment. Special performances by teachers added to the celebrations, while participating students were treated to ice cream. Prizes sponsored by the Yalu Children’s Savings Account were also presented to students who participated in and won various competitions.
