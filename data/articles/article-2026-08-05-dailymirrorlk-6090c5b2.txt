CID probes Ethiopia link in Canada job scam

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Criminal Investigation Department's (CID) Human Trafficking, Smuggling and Maritime Crime Investigation Division is conducting further investigations to uncover links between a suspect arrested in Kilinochchi and the organised criminal gang operating in Ethiopia that abducted three Sri Lankans after luring them with promises of jobs in Canada.

The CID has arrested a suspect from Kilinochchi in connection with a human trafficking and job racket that led to the abduction of three Sri Lankans in Ethiopia.

According to the Police, the three victims were promised employment in Canada, trafficked to Ethiopia via Dubai, and subsequently held captive by an organised crime syndicate. Following coordinated international efforts, Ethiopian authorities rescued the abducted Sri Lankans.

The investigation was initiated by the CID's Human Trafficking, Smuggling and Maritime Crime Investigation Division following information forwarded by the Ministry of Defence to the Inspector General of Police on May 27, 2026. To support the operation, necessary assistance was secured through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Sri Lankan Embassy in Ethiopia, and the Ethiopian Police Department.

Investigations revealed that the suspect from Kilinochchi arranged travel for three individuals aged 25, 26, and 27, who reside in the Jaffna, Vavuniya, and Kilinochchi areas. The plan involved sending the victims to Ethiopia via Dubai before supposedly departing for Canada. The suspect had collected Rs. 6.4 million upfront to cover air tickets and visas, under an agreement where each individual would pay an additional Rs. 10 million upon reaching Canada.

After arriving in Ethiopia via Dubai, the three Sri Lankans were abducted by an organised criminal group operating within the country. The gang demanded a ransom of USD 30,000 per person for their release, which was communicated to the victims' relatives through the Kilinochchi facilitator. In response, the relatives handed over nearly Rs. 20 million to the local suspect.

Ethiopian authorities intervened to rescue the three abducted Sri Lankans and arrested four Pakistani nationals and one Ethiopian national connected to the trafficking syndicate. Officials from the Sri Lankan Embassy in Ethiopia subsequently took measures to repatriate the three victims following an official inquiry.

Police said the CID is conducting further investigations to uncover the links between the arrested Kilinochchi suspect and the criminal gang operating in Ethiopia.
