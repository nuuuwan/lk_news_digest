Ex-Police Chief, Defence Secretary taken to prison after death sentence

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - Former Inspector General of Police (IGP) Pujith Jayasundara and former Defence Secretary Hemasiri Fernando were taken to prison on Friday after the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar sentenced them to death over their failure to prevent the 2019 Easter Sunday terrorist attacks despite receiving prior intelligence warnings.

The majority of the three-judge bench found both former officials guilty of dereliction of duty and aiding and abetting murder, ruling that they failed to take necessary action despite intelligence indicating that coordinated suicide bombings were imminent. Prosecutors argued that the pair neglected their official responsibilities by failing to implement preventive security measures, ultimately allowing the attacks to take place.

The case relates to the Easter Sunday bombings of April 21, 2019, when coordinated suicide attacks targeted churches and luxury hotels across Sri Lanka, killing 260 people and injuring hundreds more, including foreign nationals.
