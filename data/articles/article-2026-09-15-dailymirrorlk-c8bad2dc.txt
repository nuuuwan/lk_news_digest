Online Police Clearance Certificate service suspended

Colombo, Sep. 15 Daily Mirror) - The online issuance of Police Clearance Certificates has been temporarily suspended due to system maintenance, the Sri Lanka Police said.

The Police said members of the public can obtain their Police Clearance Certificates from the respective police stations during this period.

The public has been advised to contact their relevant police station for further information regarding the issuance of the certificates.
