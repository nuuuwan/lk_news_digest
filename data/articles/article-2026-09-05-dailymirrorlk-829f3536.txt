Two youths drown in Nanu Oya canal

Colombo, September 5 (Daily Mirror) - Two youths drowned while bathing in the Nanu Oya canal in the Radella Glasgow Estate area, Nanu Oya, police said.

The deceased have been identified as 23-year-old Perumal Thivakaran of Edinburgh Estate, Nanu Oya, and 24-year-old Amalagan Dickson, 24, of Haputale.

According to Nanu Oya Police, the two youths had been bathing in the canal with several relatives when Amalagan reportedly drowned and disappeared underwater.

Perumal had then entered the canal in an attempt to rescue him but also drowned.

Police officers, assisted by local residents, recovered the bodies of both youths from a deep section of the canal.

The bodies were taken to the Nuwara Eliya District Hospital for post-mortem examinations.

Nanu Oya Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
