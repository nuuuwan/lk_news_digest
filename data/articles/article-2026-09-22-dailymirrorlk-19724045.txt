China company wins US$40.84M Muthurajawela aviation fuel contract

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - China Petroleum Pipeline Engineering Company Limited has been awarded a major contract worth US$40.84 million and Rs. 8,548.75 million to develop a new aviation fuel storage and distribution facility at Muthurajawela.

The Cabinet of Ministers approved the contract following an international competitive procurement process in which three bids were submitted. The decision was based on the recommendations of the High Level Procurement Committee.

The project includes the construction of a 92,000-cubic-metre aviation fuel storage tank complex and related facilities at Muthurajawela. It will also include a dedicated fuel pipeline connecting the storage facility with the fuel filling terminal at Bandaranaike International Airport.

In addition, the existing fuel terminal at Muthurajawela will undergo necessary modifications as part of the project.

The Cabinet had initially approved the overall project at its meeting on July 29, 2024. The contract award was subsequently approved following the evaluation of the bids.
