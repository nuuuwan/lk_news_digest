Sri Lankan troops join major anti-gang operation in Haiti

Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Haitian Prime Minister Alix Didier Fils-Aimé has praised Sri Lankan troops serving under the international Gang Suppression Force (GSF) for their contribution to a successful joint anti-gang operation conducted with the Haitian National Police (PNH).

The operation was carried out on November 15, 2026, against the armed Kokorat San Ras gang in the Artibonite region of Haiti.

The pre-dawn operation targeted gang strongholds in Tibwadòm and Carnifice in the Gros-Morne area, where security forces cleared and secured a church and a school reportedly occupied by gang members.

The joint forces also recovered firearms, ammunition and motorcycles while dismantling barricades and trenches that had obstructed National Road 5. The roadways have reportedly been cleared after remaining inaccessible for several years.

Prime Minister Fils-Aimé commended the successful operation in a post on X, recognising the efforts of the Haitian National Police, the GSF and Sri Lankan personnel.

The operation is considered an important step towards dismantling armed gang networks, restoring access to vital roadways and improving security for civilians in affected communities.

Sri Lankan troops serving with the GSF are deployed at operational hubs, including Port-au-Prince, Gonaïves and Estrella. Their duties include conducting armoured vehicle patrols, protecting key supply routes and supporting local authorities in operations against armed gangs.
