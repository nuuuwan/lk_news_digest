Sri Lanka goods exports rise 21.66-pct in Sept 2026, driven by electronics, spices

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s merchandise exports rose 21.66 percent to 1.38 billion dollars in September 2026 from a year ago, driven by electronic components, spices, data from the export promotion office showed.

Exports of electrical and electronic components recorded a growth in September 2026, increasing by 88.35 percent year-on-year to reach 68.54 billion dollars.

“This performance was supported by significant growth in exports of Insulated Wires and Cables (82.59%), Switches, Boards and Panels (31.49%), and Electrical Transformers (33.46%). Exports of Telephone Sets and Audio/Video Equipment and Parts increased by 234.82%, while Boilers, Piston Engines, Pumps and Vacuum Pumps recorded growth of 508.71%,” the Export Development Board said.

Export earnings from apparel and textiles rose by 3.46 percent year-on-year to 439.78 million dollars in September 2026, the EDB said.

Tea export earnings declined by 1.38 percent year-on-year to 135.11 million dollars in September 2026.

Coconut-based products recorded an 8.88 percent year-on-year decline in export earnings to 111.60 million dollars in September 2026.

Other export crops recorded a 76.82 percent year-on-year decline, reaching 2.71 million dollars in September 2026.

Total merchandise export earnings for the period January to September 2026 amounted to 10.76 billion dollars, reflecting a 5.35 percent increase compared to the same period in 2025.

Exports to the US saw an 18.06 percent increase to 281.84 million dollars in September 2026.

India continued to be the second-largest export destination, with exports increasing 29.65 percent to 113.22 million dollars in September 2026.

Exports to the UK declined 3.17 percent to 71.71 million dollars, despite Sri Lanka enjoying preferential access to the UK market under the UK’s Developing Countries Trading Scheme (DCTS), which grants duty‑free entry on about 92 percent of product lines. (Colombo/Oct26/2026)
