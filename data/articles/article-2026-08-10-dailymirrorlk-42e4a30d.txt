Sagara Kariyawasam arrested

Colombo, Aug. 10(Daily Mirror) -Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) General Secretary Sagara Kariyawasam was arrested by the Colombo Central Crimes Investigation Bureau (CCIB) a short while ago in connection with an ongoing investigation.

Kariyawasam was summoned to the CCIB this morning to provide a statement regarding remarks allegedly made about the Inspector General of Police (IGP).
