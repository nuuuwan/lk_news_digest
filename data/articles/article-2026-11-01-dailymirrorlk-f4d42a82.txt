Litro Gas prices remain unchanged for November

Litro Gas Lanka has announced that domestic LP gas prices will remain unchanged throughout November.

The company said the decision was taken to provide relief to consumers despite rising international gas prices and challenging exchange rate conditions.

Accordingly, the price of a 12.5kg domestic gas cylinder will remain at Rs. 4,365, while a 5kg cylinder will continue to be sold at Rs. 1,750.

The price of a 2.3kg cylinder will also remain unchanged at Rs. 815.
