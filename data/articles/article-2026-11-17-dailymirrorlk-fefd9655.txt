35 schoolchildren hospitalised after suspected food poisoning in Watawala

Thirty-five schoolchildren were admitted to the Watawala Regional Hospital on November 17 after falling ill following a breakfast provided at their school in Templestowe Estate, police said.

Chief Inspector Chandana Gamage, Officer-in-Charge of the Watawala Police, said the children had developed symptoms suspected to be caused by food poisoning.

However, a doctor at the Watawala Regional Hospital said 34 children were admitted after experiencing vomiting, fainting and chest discomfort following the meal served on the morning of November 17.

According to the students, the breakfast consisted of rice, potatoes, beetroot curry and a leafy vegetable dish.

The affected children were from Grades 3 to 5, the District Medical Officer said.

He said their conditions were not serious and that they were treated before being handed over to their parents and teachers.

Nuwara Eliya District MP Manjula Suraweeraarachchi also visited the hospital to check on the condition of the affected children.

The Watawala Police and Public Health Inspectors are conducting separate investigations into the incident.
