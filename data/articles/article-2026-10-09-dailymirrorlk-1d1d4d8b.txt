Police seek CCTV footage after porcupine killed in Palawatta

Police and wildlife officials are seeking CCTV footage to identify individuals who allegedly killed and took away a porcupine from Perera Mawatha in Palawatta in the early hours of October 3.

The incident was reportedly captured on a security camera at a nearby house, but the three-wheeler's registration number was not clearly visible, making it difficult to identify those involved.

A resident, who lodged a complaint with the Thalangama Police, has been attempting to obtain additional CCTV footage from houses along the road to help the investigation.

According to those involved in the effort, Rusiran Perera, who witnessed the incident, sought assistance from neighbouring residents but received no response from some households.

Following an intervention by Dr. Ven. Omalpe Sobhitha Thera, Chairman of JAN, an officer from the Thalangama Police visited the area to check security camera footage. An officer from the Department of Wildlife also visited the area to assist in the inquiry.

However, the officers were reportedly unable to access footage from some houses because residents did not open their gates or provide contact details.

A police officer is expected to revisit the area to check the cameras. If residents continue to withhold access, the police may have to seek a court order to obtain the relevant footage.

Those seeking justice for the animal have warned that delays could result in crucial footage being overwritten and other evidence being lost.

Residents of Perera Mawatha who have CCTV footage showing vehicles travelling along the road between 2 a.m. and 2.15 a.m. on October 3 have been urged to assist the investigation.

Motorists who travelled along the road during that period and have dashboard camera footage have also been asked to come forward with any information that could help identify the vehicle and those responsible.
