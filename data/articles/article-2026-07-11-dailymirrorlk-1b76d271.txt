Amber warm weather advisory issued for six districts

Colombo, July 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre has issued an Amber Warm Weather Advisory for Sunday (July 12), warning that the heat index — the temperature felt by the human body — is likely to reach the 'Caution' level in several parts of the country during the daytime.

The advisory, issued at 5.00 p.m. today (11), covers the districts of Mullaittivu, Vavuniya, Batticaloa, Ampara, Trincomalee and Monaragala.

Authorities advised the public to be prepared, as elevated heat index levels could pose health risks, particularly to those engaged in outdoor activities, children, the elderly and individuals with chronic illnesses.

The public is urged to stay hydrated, avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight during peak daytime hours and take necessary precautions to minimise the effects of the hot weather.
