British Airways returns to Sri Lanka after 11 years with special fares

ECONOMYNEXT – British Airways which resumed direct flights between Sri Lanka and the United Kingdom after 11 years, has introduced special airfares to mark the occasion.

Passengers traveling from Colombo to London Gatwick can book World Traveller economy class return tickets starting from 255,000 rupees, with discounts of up to 10 percent also offered, the Ministry of Ports and Civil Aviation said.

The inaugural British Airways flight arrived in Colombo on October 24.

The carrier will operate three flights a week using Boeing 777 aircraft.

Featuring three cabin classes — Club World, World Traveller Plus, and World Traveller — the service will is expected to boost travel between Sri Lanka and the UK, strengthening tourism, trade, and business relations.

Resuming flights reconnects Sri Lanka with the airline’s global flight network, Neil Chernoff, Chief Planning and Strategy Officer at British Airways, was quoted as saying.

Ruwan Waidyaratne, Managing Director of Hayleys Advantis, said the resumption of service develops aviation connectivity between Sri Lanka and the United Kingdom.

State-owned national carrier SriLankan Airlines has historically maintained a highly profitable sector to the United Kingdom, with the Colombo (CMB) to London Heathrow (LHR) route being one of its most resilient and lucrative premium long-haul sectors. (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
