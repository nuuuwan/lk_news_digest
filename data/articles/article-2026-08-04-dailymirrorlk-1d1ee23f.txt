Court rejects bid to obtain Chamika Karunaratne’s blood sample for DNA test

Colombo, August 04 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo District Court today refused a request seeking an order directing Sri Lankan cricketer Chamika Karunaratne to provide a blood sample for a DNA test in connection with an ongoing paternity dispute.

The application was made during proceedings relating to a plaint filed by airline pilot Rasika Sewwandi, who is seeking a court order directing the Registrar General to issue a birth certificate naming Karunaratne as the father of her child.

Delivering its order, Colombo District Court Judge Lalith Kannangara held that the plaintiff had failed to establish sufficient grounds to justify compelling the cricketer to provide a blood sample for DNA analysis. Accordingly, the request was rejected.

Despite dismissing the application for a DNA test at this stage, the court ordered that the case be called again on October 9 for further proceedings.

Jagath Wickramanayake PC with Niranjala Gunathilaka Instructed by Ramzi Bacha Associates appeared for Chamika Karunaratne, while Wijeyadasa Rajapakshe PC appeared for Rasika Sewwandi.
