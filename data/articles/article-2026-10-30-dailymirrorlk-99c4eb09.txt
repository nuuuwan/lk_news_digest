DCSL brings beer operations under parent company

Distilleries Company of Sri Lanka PLC (DCSL) has brought its subsidiary’s brewing, packaging and distribution activities under the parent company, consolidating beer and hard liquor operations within the listed entity.

The transfer from wholly owned DCSL Breweries Lanka Ltd took effect on October 28, 2026, following approval from the Department of Excise for the transfer of the relevant licences, the company said in a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange.

DCSL will now undertake the brewing, packaging and distribution of beer alongside its existing business of distilling, bottling and distributing hard liquor.

The company said the change would offer greater convenience for both DCSL and retail shops, but would not significantly affect Group profits.

Following the transfer, DCSL will produce and distribute licensed beer brands Heineken, Tiger and Anchor in the Sri Lankan market, together with the Group’s own brands Niya, Bison and DCSL Beer.

The move shifts responsibility for the beer operations from the wholly owned subsidiary to the parent, bringing the Group’s beer and hard liquor activities under the same operating company.
