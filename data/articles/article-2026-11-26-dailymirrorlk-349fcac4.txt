Man who crawled under train to save soldier commended

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Army Commander Lieutenant General Nilantha Premaratne has commended a civilian who risked his life to rescue a female soldier trapped beneath a train following an accident in Godagama, Homagama, on November 18.

Chinthaka Gamage met the Army Commander at Army Headquarters yesterday (25), where he received special recognition for his courageous intervention in saving the injured soldier.

During the incident, Gamage crawled underneath the train and pulled the soldier to safety. He also remained with her until she was admitted to hospital to ensure she received prompt medical attention.

Commending his actions, Lieutenant General Premaratne praised Gamage's courage, compassion and selflessness, saying that coming forward without hesitation to protect others during emergencies was a noble human quality that everyone in society should possess.

He further noted that Gamage's decision to risk his own safety to save another person's life demonstrated how an individual's bravery could make a profound difference, serving as an example of courage, kindness and humanity.
