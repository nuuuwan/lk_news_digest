Water supply to be suspended in several Anuradhapura areas tomorrow

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) has announced that the water supply to several areas in Anuradhapura will be temporarily suspended for eight hours tomorrow (23).

The suspension is necessary to carry out essential maintenance work at the Kalawewa Water Treatment Plant, the NWSDB said.

Accordingly, the water supply will be suspended from 8.30 a.m. to 4.30 p.m. tomorrow in Ihala Gama, Kalawewa, Maradankadawala, Thirappane and Kekirawa.

The NWSDB has urged residents in the affected areas to store sufficient water in advance to avoid inconvenience during the interruption.

The public has also been requested to use water sparingly until the supply is fully restored.
