Court directs counsel to advise Gnanasara Thera to appear in another contempt case

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal today directed the counsel appearing for Ven. Galagodaaththe Gnanasara Thera to advise him to be present in court in connection with a contempt of court application after the Thera failed to appear before court.

The matter was taken up before a two-judge Bench comprising Justices Mayadunne Corea and Lakmali Karunanayake.

President’s Counsel Anura Meddegoda, appearing for Ven. Gnanasara Thera, informed court that his client was scheduled to undergo surgery. However, he failed to produce any medical certificate before court to substantiate the reason for his absence.

State Counsel appearing for the Attorney General submitted that the presence of the respondent was essential in a contempt of court proceeding. The State Counsel accordingly moved that a warrant be issued against the respondent or that he be directed to appear before court today, if possible.

Having considered the submissions, the Court of Appeal directed President’s Counsel Anura Meddegoda to advise his client to be present in court.

The matter was subsequently kept down to enable the respondent to appear before court.
