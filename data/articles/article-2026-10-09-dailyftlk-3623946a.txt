Make your big purchases more affordable with Seylan Cards’ 36-month 0% easy payment plan and special discounts

Seylan Bank PLC is offering Seylan Credit Cardholders greater flexibility when making high-value purchases through its 36-Month 0% Easy Payment Plan, available from 1 October to 31 December 2026, enabling eligible cardholders to convert purchases ranging from Rs. 100,000 to Rs. 3 million into a 36-month easy payment plan at 0% interest, with no processing fee.

Adding further value to the offer, customers can also enjoy special discounts provided by participating merchants, allowing them to benefit from savings alongside the convenience of extended payment terms. The initiative is designed to help customers manage planned expenditures through convenient monthly instalments, particularly during the year-end shopping and festive season.

The offer is particularly relevant for customers planning to upgrade household appliances, purchase electronics, enhance their lifestyle or undertake home improvements. It also provides flexibility for other significant planned expenditures, including travel, education, healthcare, insurance, solar solutions and jewellery, subject to eligibility and applicable terms and conditions.

Commenting on the initiative, Seylan Bank Head of Cards Ruchith Liyanage said, “At Seylan Bank, we understand that customers often plan significant purchases around the year-end season, and our 36-month 0% easy payment plan is designed to give them greater flexibility and convenience in managing these expenses. Whether customers are upgrading their homes, investing in lifestyle essentials or planning other important purchases, Seylan Cards remains committed to supporting their financial convenience through solutions that put their needs first.”

With the year-end season approaching, Seylan Cards continues to support customers by providing payment solutions that offer convenience and flexibility for their planned purchases. Through the 36-month 0% easy payment plan, eligible cardholders can enjoy their purchases while managing repayments through convenient monthly instalments.

Customers who wish to make use of the offer can make eligible purchases between 1 October and 31 December 2026 and convert transactions ranging from Rs. 100,000 to Rs. 3 million into a 36-month easy payment plan. To convert an eligible transaction, customers can contact 011 2008888. Customers who do not currently hold a Seylan Credit Card can also apply by contacting the same hotline.
