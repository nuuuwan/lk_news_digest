“You have come nicely dressed today”: Speaker to Sajith

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne complimented Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa on his choice of attire in Parliament today (25), after Premadasa attended the session dressed in orange to mark the launch of the global ‘16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence’ campaign.

During his remarks in Parliament, Premadasa said that although the campaign was observed over 16 days, efforts to protect women and girls from violence should continue throughout the year, 24 hours a day.

He also commended Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya for her remarks on violence against women, particularly her observation that abuse often occurs within close personal relationships.

Premadasa said he had worn orange to express solidarity with women and children.

As the Speaker urged him to conclude his remarks due to time constraints, Premadasa thanked the Chair for allowing him to speak despite interruptions.

Responding in a lighter vein, the Speaker remarked, “You have come nicely dressed today,” before inviting the Prime Minister to respond.
