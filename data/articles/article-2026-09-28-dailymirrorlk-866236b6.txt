Warrant issued on Chamika Karunaratne for failure to appear before court

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Additional Magistrate Kasun Kanchana Dasanayake today ordered that cricketer Chamika Karunaratne be arrested and produced before court after he failed to appear before court.

The warrant was issued when a complaint filed by a female pilot seeking maintenance payments for her child was taken up for inquiry and Karunaratne failed to appear before court.
