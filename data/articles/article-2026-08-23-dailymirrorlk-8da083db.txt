SLAF Culinary festival concludes in Sigiriya

The third edition of the Sri Lanka Air Force (SLAF) Culinary Arts Competition 2026 concluded successfully on August 22 in Sigiriya, under the patronage of Air Vice Marshal Lasitha Sumanaweera, Chief of Staff of the Sri Lanka Air Force.

Jointly organized by the SLAF and the Chefs’ Association of Sri Lanka in accordance with World Association of Chefs’ Societies (WACS) standards, the competition aimed to enhance the professional skills, creativity and knowledge of culinary personnel while providing a platform to showcase culinary excellence.

The Overall Team Championship was won by the team from SLAF Base Katunayake, with SLAF Base Ratmalana securing the runner-up position. Corporal Dhananjaya of SLAF Headquarters, Sri Jayewardenepura, received the Most Prominent Mixologist Award, while Civil Servant M. B. S. P. Kumara was named the Most Outstanding Culinary Master.

The competition featured 325 competitors across 422 events, bringing together culinary professionals from the Tri-Services, leading hotels in the Cultural Triangle and school students from the Central and North Central Provinces.

Beyond the competitive element, the event provided opportunities for knowledge-sharing and professional development, allowing emerging chefs and young participants to engage with experienced culinary professionals and gain exposure to international culinary standards.

Held in the culturally significant destination of Sigiriya, the festival also attracted local and foreign tourists, adding to its cultural and tourism appeal while reaffirming the Sri Lanka Air Force’s commitment to promoting culinary excellence, innovation and emerging talent.
