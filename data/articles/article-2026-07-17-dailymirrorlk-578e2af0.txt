Sheraton Colombo relocates Ceylon Bake Shop to hotel lobby

Sheraton Colombo Hotel announced the relocation of its Ceylon Bake Shop to a vibrant new space in the hotel lobby, marking the beginning of an exciting new chapter for the café.

Designed to offer greater accessibility and a refreshed guest experience, the new Ceylon Bake Shop combines contemporary comfort with an expanded menu that caters to every occasion—from a quick morning coffee to a leisurely lunch or an afternoon catch-up.

Coffee lovers can now indulge in an enhanced beverage selection featuring expertly crafted specialty coffees, premium teas, signature handcrafted drinks, and an exciting range of matcha creations that are set to become new guest favourites. Guests can also enjoy an enticing selection of freshly baked breads, artisanal pastries, cakes, and sweet treats, making Ceylon Bake Shop the perfect destination for a quick bite or an indulgent escape.

Complementing the beverage experience is a significantly expanded all-day dining menu. Guests can enjoy an extensive selection of fresh salads, gourmet sandwiches, burgers, handcrafted pizzas, pastas, café classics, indulgent desserts, and signature breakfast offerings, transforming Ceylon Bake Shop into a destination for both casual dining and indulgent treats.

Whether stopping by for a handcrafted latte, discovering a new favourite matcha, or enjoying a wholesome meal, guests are invited to experience the refreshed Ceylon Bake Shop in its welcoming new home at the heart of Sheraton Colombo Hotel.
