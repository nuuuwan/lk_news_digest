Police hunt three Chinese nationals in Port City murder case

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror) — The Sri Lanka Police have appealed to the public for information to help locate three Chinese nationals wanted in connection with an alleged abduction and murder at Colombo Port City

According to the Police, the incident occurred on July 23, 2026, when a group of three Chinese nationals was reportedly attacked. One of the individuals was allegedly abducted and subsequently killed.

The Colombo Crimes Division has arrested two suspects in connection with the incident, and they have been remanded. Police are continuing investigations to locate three other suspects believed to be involved.

Police said the three wanted individuals had entered Sri Lanka on tourist visas, and their visa validity periods have since expired. Police have expressed concern that the suspects could attempt to leave the country illegally by air or sea.

Police have released photographs and details of the wanted individuals and are urging anyone who may have information about their whereabouts to contact the authorities immediately.

Anyone with information is requested to contact the Officer-in-Charge of the Colombo Crimes Division at 071-8591735.
