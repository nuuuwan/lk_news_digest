Sri Lanka rupee at 332.52/60 to US dollar spot, bond yields flat

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s rupee was quoted at 332.52/60 to the US dollar in the spot market on Wednesday, weaker from 332.40/50 the previous day, while bond yields were steady, dealers said.

An auction of 100,000 million rupees Treasury bills was ongoing.

A bond maturing on 15.09.2029 was quoted at 11.05/15 percent, down from 11.08/15 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.08.2030 was quoted at 11.55/63 percent, up from 11.55/60 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2030 was quoted at 11.62/68 percent, up from 11.63/65 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.02.2031 was quoted flat at 11.75/80 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.12.2031 was quoted at 11.87/93 percent, down from 11.85/95 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2034 was quoted flat at 12.50/60 percent.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was 327.90 buying, 336.90 selling; the euro was 362.1052 buying, 375.8860 selling; pound was 435.0348 buying, 449.1432 selling.

On the Colombo Stock Exchange, the All Share Price Index was up 0.67 percent, or 131.19 points, at 19,823; while the S&P SL20 was up 0.74 percent, 41.68 points, at 5,653. (Colombo/Oct28/2026)
