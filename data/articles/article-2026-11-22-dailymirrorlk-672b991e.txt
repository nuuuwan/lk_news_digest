FBI, US Justice Department conclude week-long visit to Sri Lanka over cyber fraud investigations

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - A team of officials from the US Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and the US Department of Justice has concluded a week-long visit to Sri Lanka aimed at combating transnational cyber financial fraud networks and bringing those responsible to justice.

The visit, which took place from November 16 to 22, involved close cooperation with the Attorney General's Department and several key investigative units of the Sri Lanka Police.

These included the Computer Crime Investigation Unit (CCIU), the Central Criminal Investigation Bureau (CCIB) and the Technical Assistance Unit (TAU).

A key focus of the visit was advancing investigations into scam centres operating in Sri Lanka and targeting both American and Sri Lankan citizens.

During the programme, officials from the two countries reviewed investigative strategies relating to two major ongoing scam centre investigations and retrieved and examined digital evidence from more than 80 electronic devices seized during raids conducted by the Sri Lanka Police.

The US officials also conducted specialised training on digital evidence analysis and strengthening coordination in investigating cross-border criminal activities.

The programme helped establish the technical and legal groundwork needed to build stronger cases against cybercriminals before the courts.

Officials highlighted the importance of international law enforcement cooperation, noting that transnational criminal networks were defrauding victims worldwide of billions of US dollars.

Although the FBI delegation's visit has concluded, US and Sri Lankan authorities will continue working together to pursue further investigative leads, disrupt criminal networks and protect people in both countries.
