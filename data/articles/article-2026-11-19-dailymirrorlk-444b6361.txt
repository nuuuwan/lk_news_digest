Namal remanded till December 2 over Airbus case

Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, who is an accused in the Airbus transaction case being heard before the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar, was ordered to be further remanded till December 2 by the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar today.

The case was taken up before a three-judge bench comprising High Court Judge Priyantha Liyanage (President), High Court Judge Thilakaratne Bandara and High Court Judge Nuwan Dhammika Bandara.

During the proceedings, President's Counsel Romesh de Silva, appearing for the accused, made submissions seeking bail for Namal Rajapaksa.

However, following submissions by Deputy Solicitor General Janaka Bandara, appearing for the prosecution, that no exceptional circumstances had been presented before court to justify granting bail, the bench ordered that the accused be further remanded till December 2.
