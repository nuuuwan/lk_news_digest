Hotel employee opens fire with air rifle during Kegalle party

Kegalle, Sep 19 (Daily Mirror)- A person was injured after a hotel employee allegedly opened fire with an air rifle during a party at a hotel in Kegalle town last night (18), police said.

According to the Police Media Division, an argument that broke out during the party had escalated, resulting in a tense situation at the hotel.

Preliminary investigations have revealed that the hotel employee had fired the air rifle while attempting to control the situation arising from the dispute, police said.

The injured person was admitted to the Kegalle Teaching Hospital for treatment.

The Kegalle Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
