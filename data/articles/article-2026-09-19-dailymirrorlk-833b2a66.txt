Two arrested within an hour over armed robbery at Trincomalee liquor store

Trincomalee, Sep 19 (Daily Mirror)- Two suspects, including a police sergeant, have been arrested within an hour of an armed robbery at a liquor store near Anuradhapura Junction in Trincomalee, police said.

The suspects had allegedly entered the liquor store last night (18) and threatened employees with a T-56 firearm before fleeing with cash.

Acting on information received about the robbery, Trincomalee Police officers arrested the two suspects while they were staying at a temporary lodging facility within the Uppuveli Police Division, police said.

One of the suspects has been identified as a Police Sergeant attached to the Uppuveli Police Station and serving at an armoury in the Kilipunchimale area.

The suspects, aged 54 and 61, are residents of Hakmana, according to police.

Police also recovered Rs. 11,500 in cash suspected to have been stolen from the liquor store, along with the motorcycle allegedly used in the robbery, a T-56 firearm and a magazine containing 29 rounds of ammunition.
