Severe lightning warning issued for several areas

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - An Amber Advisory for severe lightning has been issued for several provinces and districts, valid until 11.30 p.m. today (13).

The Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre said thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are likely at several places in the Eastern, Uva, North Central, Central and Sabaragamuwa provinces, as well as Vavuniya, Mannar, Mullaitivu and Kurunegala districts.

Temporary localized strong winds may also occur during thundershowers, the advisory said.

The public has been urged to take adequate precautions, including seeking shelter indoors and avoiding trees, open areas, water bodies and open vehicles during thunderstorms.

People have also been advised to avoid using wired telephones and connected electrical appliances during thunderstorms and to beware of fallen trees and power lines.

For emergency assistance, the public has been advised to contact local disaster management authorities.
