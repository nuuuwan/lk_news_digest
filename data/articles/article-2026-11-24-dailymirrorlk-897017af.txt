Government must be reminded of teachers’ salary demands, says Starlin

Colombo, November 24 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Teachers' Union General Secretary Josep Starlin said trade unions had a responsibility to remind the Government of the salary demands made by teachers and principals, which he claimed had been forgotten after those who once championed the cause came to power.

He said teachers and principals had played an important role in the struggles for the education sector, including the campaign to secure the outstanding two-thirds of their salary increase.

Starlin said more than 10,000 teachers had gathered in Colombo on June 26 to demand that the then President immediately grant the remaining salary adjustment.

Several teachers were injured during the protest, including one who remains blind to this day, he said.

"Having engaged in such intense struggles, is there really anything left that still requires a reminder?" he questioned.

He said the Government had a responsibility to fulfil the demands that had been raised before it assumed power.

"If a Government that came to power partly as a result of the struggles led by teachers chooses to sideline the demands of teachers and principals, we consider it our responsibility to remind them and secure what is rightfully ours," he said.
