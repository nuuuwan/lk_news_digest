Dr. Roshanie Dissanayake takes over as Science Ministry Secretary

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has appointed Dr. Roshanie Dissanayake as the new Secretary to the Ministry of Science and Technology.

The letter of appointment was formally handed over to Dr. Dissanayake by Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake at the Presidential Secretariat this morning (11).

Dr. Roshanie Dissanayake has previously served as an Additional Secretary (Sri Lanka Administrative Service – Special Grade) at the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government.
