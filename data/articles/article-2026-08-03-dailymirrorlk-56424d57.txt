Major flood warning issued for Kelani River Valley

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Irrigation Department has issued a major flood warning for low-lying areas of the Kelani River Valley, stating that a major flood situation could develop within the next 24 hours due to heavy rainfall in the upper and middle catchment areas of the river basin.

Accordingly, residents in low-lying areas within the Dehiowita, Ruwanwella and Yatiyanthota Divisional Secretariat Divisions have been urged to remain highly vigilant and take necessary precautions.

The department said significant, high-intensity rainfall has been recorded in the upper and middle catchment areas of the Kelani River Basin, while further heavy rainfall is expected according to current weather forecasts. This could result in a rapid and sudden rise in river water levels.

People living in the affected areas, those using the river, and motorists travelling through these regions have been advised to exercise extreme caution. Disaster management authorities have also been requested to take necessary precautionary measures.

Meanwhile, the Irrigation Department has warned of a possible minor flood situation in low-lying floodplain areas of the Kalu River within the next few hours.

The warning applies to the Pelmadulla, Nivithigala, Ratnapura, Kuruwita, Ayagama and Elapatha Divisional Secretariat Divisions in the Ratnapura District.

As such, residents, motorists and passengers travelling through these areas have been urged to remain alert and take all necessary precautions, while disaster management authorities have been instructed to take appropriate action.
