Forensic report submitted to court in Ven. Hemaratana Thera case

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Government Analyst has reported to the Anuradhapura Magistrate’s Court that the rug, which is a case production in the case against Ven. Pallegama Hemarathana Thera, contained semen of the suspect. However, his report indicated that the strands of hair subject to analysis were not those of the girl who is alleged to have been raped by the suspect and those of another woman. Govt. Analyst further reported that any stains of blood were not visible in the rug.

The victim had stated to police that the Ven. Thera who raped her wiped out semen with a rug at the foot of Ven. Thera’s bed. The Govt. Analyst’s report was filed of record in the case No. B22424/2026. A copy of the report has been submitted to the Attorney General as well.

The rug had been seized by the police during investigations and referred to the Govt. Analyst’s report since the victim had stated to Nittambuwa Police that the Ven Thera, who raped her, wiped out semen that dropped on the floor with a rug at the foot of the bed. She had identified the Ven. Thera’s room, its contents including his bed and the rug.

Investigations at the scene had been conducted by a team of police officers, including OIC Gampaha Division Women and Children’s Bureau IP Vinicia Premaratne, IP Wasundara Gunaratne, with the help of Anuradhapura Police,

The case has been postponed by Anuradhapura Magistrate Pasindu Wickremaratne for January 08, 2027 pending Attorney General’s advice.
