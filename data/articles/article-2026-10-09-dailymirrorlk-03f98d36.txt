Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to France presents credentials to Macron

Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to France, Sugeeshwara Gunaratna, presented his Letters of Credence to French President Emmanuel Macron at the Élysée Palace on October 5, marking the formal beginning of his tenure as Sri Lanka’s representative in France.

Following the ceremony, Ambassador Gunaratna held a brief meeting with President Macron, during which he conveyed the greetings and best wishes of Sri Lankan President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

He highlighted the longstanding bilateral relations between Sri Lanka and France and expressed Sri Lanka’s interest in further strengthening cooperation in political, economic, trade, maritime and cultural sectors, as well as in multilateral forums.

President Macron conveyed his greetings to the President, Government and people of Sri Lanka and reaffirmed France’s commitment to strengthening bilateral ties and expanding cooperation across various sectors.

Ambassador Gunaratna is a career diplomat with more than 25 years of service. His previous positions include Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to Ethiopia and Permanent Representative to the African Union, Deputy Permanent Representative to the United Nations in New York, and Deputy High Commissioner to the United Kingdom.
