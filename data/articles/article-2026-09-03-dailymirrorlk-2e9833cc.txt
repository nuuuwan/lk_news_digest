Three Colombo rail routes to get electric trains

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Construction of Sri Lanka’s long-awaited electric railway network is expected to commence by May or June next year, Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayaka said.

Speaking at an event, Minister Rathnayaka outlined the government’s plans to modernise the country’s public transportation system, with the first phase of the railway electrification project targeted for completion by 2030.

The initial phase will focus on three major commuter corridors with high passenger demand. These routes include the Main Line extending from Colombo Fort to Ragama, the Coastal Line from Colombo Fort to Panadura and the Kelani Valley Line from Maradana to Makumbura.

The project will also involve a significant modernisation of railway infrastructure, including the construction and upgrading of passenger stations.

According to the Minister, the new stations will go beyond their traditional role of issuing tickets and will be equipped with modern amenities and additional passenger facilities.

“The stations that will be built will not just be ordinary stations that merely issue tickets. They will be equipped with modern conveniences and additional facilities for passengers,” Minister Rathnayaka said.
