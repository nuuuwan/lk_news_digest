Nine deaths reported due to adverse weather conditions

Colombo, August 22 (Colombo) - Nine deaths have been reported in several districts due to adverse weather conditions across the country, according to the Disaster Management Centre (DMC).

The DMC’s detailed situation report, issued at 6.00 a.m. today (22), said 102 incidents had been reported across 15 districts, affecting 4,232 families and 16,614 people.

The affected districts are Ampara, Anuradhapura, Galle, Hambantota, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Kurunegala, Matale, Nuwara Eliya, Kandy District, Puttalam, Ratnapura, Badulla and Trincomalee.

The report recorded nine deaths, nine injuries and two missing persons. A total of 733 houses had been partially damaged.

The DMC said 25 families had been accommodated at safe centres, comprising 102 people, while eight families with 01 person were staying at relatives’ homes.

Among the reported deaths, two occurred in Nawalapitiya, Kandy, following a collapse on August 3. Three deaths were reported in Nuwara Eliya following flooding in Mangogamuwa on the same day, while one person drowned in the sea in Puttalam on August 2.

The DMC continues to monitor the situation and coordinate relief and response measures in the affected areas.
