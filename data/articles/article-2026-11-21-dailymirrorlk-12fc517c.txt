NBSL recognises employee excellence and achievements

The ‘All Staff’ celebration of National Building Society Limited (NBSL), organised to recognise employee achievements during the first two quarters of the 2026/27 financial year, was held recently in grand style at CLIO Grand Hotel in Ganemulla.

Held under the theme ‘Rise Together – Never Give Up’, the event celebrated the dedication, capabilities and achievements of the employees who contributed to achieving the organisation’s corporate objectives.

Selected employees, including the recipients of the NBSL Eagles Award for outstanding individual, branch and exceptional performance, were presented with certificates, awards and overseas travel opportunities.

The event was led by NBSL’s senior management and featured renowned businessman and motivational speaker Kishu Gomes as the chief guest. His insightful speech, which drew on his business experience and personal journey, inspired the employees and gave them renewed motivation for the road ahead. The celebration was further enriched by a group performance by the NBSL staff.

Operating under National Housing Act No. 37 of 1954, NBSL provides a wide range of services, including support for the development of housing and other structures in Sri Lanka, improvement of living standards and land and property management. The organisation also caters to the housing and financial needs of the public through investment management and credit facilities.

More than 250 NBSL employees, operating through 26 branches across the island, continue to serve the customers under the organisation’s tagline ‘Close to your heart’.

As NBSL continues to introduce new concepts and financial services to meet the housing and related needs of the public, it remains committed to recognising the dedication and achievements of its employees while further expanding its services in the quarters ahead.
