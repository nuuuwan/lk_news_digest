Suresh Sallay’s wife urges President not to extend detention order

Colombo, August 21 (Daily Mirror) - The wife of former State Intelligence Service (SIS) Director Maj. Gen. (Retd.) Suresh Sallay has appealed to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake not to extend the detention order against her husband, citing his serious heart condition.

In a letter dated August 20, S.B.M.S.B. Sallay requested that her husband be transferred to remand custody under judicial supervision while continuing to receive specialised medical treatment.

She said Sallay’s health had deteriorated during his detention, particularly after he began a hunger strike. He was admitted to the National Hospital of Sri Lanka on June 7 and transferred to the hospital’s Cardiology Unit on June 27.

According to the letter, Sallay later underwent a cardiac procedure during which an Implantable Cardioverter-Defibrillator (ICD) was implanted due to his continuing heart problems.

His wife said he requires close and specialised medical monitoring following the procedure and warned that transferring him from the National Hospital back to Criminal Investigation Department (CID) custody could place his life at serious risk.

She therefore urged President Dissanayake, who is also the Minister of Defence, to allow the existing detention order to expire and transfer Sallay to remand custody so that his detention and the ongoing investigation could be conducted under judicial supervision.

She also assured the authorities that neither Sallay nor his family intended to obstruct or interfere with the investigation. She said they were willing to cooperate with the relevant authorities fully.
