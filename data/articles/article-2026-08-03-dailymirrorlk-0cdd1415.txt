Jay Shah’s Tanzania milestone, US ban, Sri Lanka gold & FIFA leadership crisis

AUG.03 (Mirror Sports) - Global sports headlines featured a new cricket milestone in Tanzania, a major anti-corruption ban, Sri Lanka’s Commonwealth Games gold and fresh FIFA leadership controversy.

International Cricket Council (ICC) Chairman Jay Shah officially inaugurated the Tanzania Cricket Association's (TCA) new arena on Sunday, 2 August 2026, marking a major milestone for cricket in the East African nation. The ceremony comes just days before Tanzania hosts the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup Challenge League B 2026 from 8 to 18 August, with the new venue set to serve as the tournament's principal ground.

The state-of-the-art facility is expected to play a key role in the growth of cricket in Tanzania, providing a permanent base for international matches, player development, coaching, umpire education and grassroots programmes. Construction of the arena began in May 2025 and was completed in July 2026, following the project's launch in late 2024.

Speaking at the inauguration, Shah described the arena as "a significant milestone for cricket in Tanzania" and praised the Tanzania Cricket Association for its vision and commitment to expanding the sport. He said the ICC would continue supporting its Members in developing infrastructure and strengthening cricket across the region.

Tanzania Cricket Association Chairman Dr B.S. Sreekumar called the opening of the TCA Arena a defining moment for cricket in the country, saying the facility would create new opportunities to host international events, develop future generations of players and inspire more young Tanzanians to take up the game.

US cricketer Bodugum Akhilesh Reddy has been banned from all forms of cricket for eight years after being found guilty of breaching three provisions of the ICC Anti-Corruption Code. The sanction relates to the 2025 Abu Dhabi T10 tournament in the United Arab Emirates and is backdated to 21 November 2025, when he was provisionally suspended.

An ICC Anti-Corruption Tribunal found the 26-year-old guilty of attempting to influence matches, encouraging another participant to engage in corrupt conduct, and obstructing the ICC's investigation by deleting data and messages from his mobile device.

Reddy represented the USA in four T20 Internationals, taking one wicket on his debut against the Cayman Islands in the North America T20 Cup.

The Football Association of Wales (FAW) has become the first FIFA member association to withdraw its support for Gianni Infantino’s re-election as FIFA President, citing concerns over governance, leadership and decision-making.

The move follows controversy surrounding plans to create a private company to manage FIFA’s major competitions, including the World Cup. Although the proposal was later dropped, it has increased pressure on Infantino ahead of the 2027 FIFA presidential election.

The FAW said Infantino had lost its confidence to lead world football, while other British football associations are yet to follow its decision after supporting a UEFA-led boycott of FIFA competitions over the issue.

Sri Lanka have secured their first gold medal at the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games, moving onto the medal table with one gold and one silver. The achievement places Sri Lanka joint 17th on the table alongside Jersey and Samoa, with a total of two medals.
