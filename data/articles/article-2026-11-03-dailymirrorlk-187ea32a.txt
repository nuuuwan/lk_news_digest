Namal granted bail in Krrish case but remains in prison pending Airbus case hearing

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Despite being granted bail in the Krrish case, Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa was taken back into prison custody after completing the bail formalities, pending his production before the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar in connection with the alleged Airbus bribery case.

On October 28, the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar ordered that Rajapaksa be produced before the court through prison authorities on November 19.

Rajapaksa is facing charges in connection with the alleged Airbus bribery incident, and his production has been scheduled for the next hearing of the case on November 19.
