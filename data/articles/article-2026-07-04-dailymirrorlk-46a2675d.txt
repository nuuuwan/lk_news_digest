Adhitya Ayurveda takes traditional Sri Lankan Ayurveda expertise to South Korea

Adhitya Ayurveda, one of Sri Lanka’s leading holistic healing organisations, has taken its latest step in its international journey through a wellness knowledge-sharing program conducted in South Korea in collaboration with Shriveda Ayurveda Korea.

The informative session was led by Dr. Kasun Gunawardhana, who shared the principles of authentic Sri Lankan Ayurveda and its relevance in addressing the health challenges of modern lifestyles.

The presentation focused on several key areas including Ayurvedic detoxification, preventive wellness, digestive health, Panchakarma therapies, pulse diagnosis, and practical daily routines that support long-term physical and mental wellbeing.

Drawing upon centuries of traditional wisdom, Dr. Gunawardhana highlighted how Ayurveda views health not merely as the absence of disease, but as a harmonious balance between body, mind, and soul. Participants were introduced to the concept of “Ama” (toxin accumulation), the importance of digestive strength, and simple yet effective lifestyle practices that can help restore balance in today’s demanding world.

The event also showcased the unique value of Sri Lankan Ayurvedic traditions, including the use of medicinal herbs, natural ingredients, and personalized healing approaches that have been preserved and practiced for generations. Through engaging discussions and knowledge exchange, participants gained a deeper understanding of how traditional healing systems can complement modern wellness goals.

This collaboration between Adhitya Ayurveda and Shriveda Ayurveda Korea represents a shared vision of making authentic Ayurvedic knowledge accessible to a wider international audience while building meaningful bridges between cultures through health and wellness.

Sharing his thoughts on Ayurveda, Dr. Kasun said, “Ayurveda views health not merely as the absence of disease, but as a harmonious balance between the body, mind, and your soul. Its timeless principles remain highly relevant in today’s fast-paced world, encouraging individuals to achieve optimal wellbeing through mindfulness, proper nutrition, and a deeper connection with nature. As Adhitya Ayurveda continues to expand its global presence, initiatives such as these reinforce our commitment to preserving Sri Lanka’s rich Ayurvedic heritage and inspiring healthier communities around the world.”

He added, “We extend our sincere appreciation to Shriveda Ayurveda Korea, all participants, and our wellness partners for their support and enthusiasm. Together, we are creating new opportunities to share the timeless wisdom of Ayurveda beyond borders and contribute to a healthier future for global communities.”

The Adhitya Ayurveda family includes one of Sri Lanka’s leading holistic healing and luxury resorts nestled in the heart of Anuradhapura. It is an ISO-certified, award-winning retreat where ancient Ayurvedic wisdom and Sri Lankan traditional medicine are combined. Adhitya Ayurveda has also opened Adhitya Ayurveda Colombo, situated in the vibrant locale of Colombo 3. Additionally, they also operate Mangata, Sri Lanka’s premier Ayurveda retreat on the Galle Coast.
