SampathCards offers up to 60% discounts this festive season

A Season Better Spent’ campaign brings exclusive offers and privileges for Sampath credit and debit cardholders until December 31, 2026

SampathCards is making the Christmas and New Year season even more rewarding with an exciting range of exclusive discounts of up to 60 percent for all Sampath credit and debit cardholders.

Running from November to December 31, 2026, the annual seasonal campaign brings together an extensive portfolio of offers across a diverse network of merchant partners, giving the customers more opportunities to enjoy the festivities while making their seasonal spending go further.

Themed ‘A Season Better Spent’, the campaign features exclusive discounts and privileges across multiple categories, including retail and fashion, giftware, wellness and watches, footwear, online shopping, eyewear and hearing, auto care, baby and kids, home care, electronics and furniture, travel and leisure, sports, supermarkets, jewellery and dining.

The extensive selection caters to a variety of festive needs, from finding the perfect gifts for loved ones and preparing homes for the celebrations to enjoying dining, shopping and leisure experiences throughout the holiday season. The cardholders can also benefit from extended settlement plan options, adding greater flexibility to their purchases.

Sampath Bank Head of Card Centre Chirath Samarasekara said, “The festive season is a time of giving, sharing and creating cherished memories with family and friends. We recognise that it is also a period when the customers look forward to making the most of their spending and this year’s SampathCards seasonal campaign is designed to make those experiences even more rewarding. Through our extensive network of merchant partnerships, we are bringing the cardholders an exciting range of exclusive offers and privileges across multiple lifestyle and shopping categories. Our aim is to deliver meaningful value to our customers while making SampathCards a preferred payment choice throughout the festive season.”

Open to all Sampath credit and debit cardholders, the campaign offers the customers the opportunity to enjoy savings across a wide selection of participating merchants, with discounts and privileges varying by merchant, category and applicable card type.
