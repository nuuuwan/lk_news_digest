Polonnaruwa records highest temperature of 33.1°C

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Polonnaruwa recorded the highest temperature in Sri Lanka at 33.1 degrees Celsius on October 27,the Department of Meteorology said.

The temperature was recorded by the Polonnaruwa Meteorological Station during the afternoon.

The department’s map shows the maximum temperatures reported by meteorological stations across the island during the afternoon of October 27.
