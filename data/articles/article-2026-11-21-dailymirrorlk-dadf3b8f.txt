Tudawe Trading Company opens 4th automated car-wash facility in Wattala

Tudawe Trading Company (Pvt.) Ltd, the sole authorised agent for Kärcher products in Sri Lanka, officially opened its fourth automated car-wash facility at Sandagiri Tire House, No. 643, Hendala, Wattala, recently.

The facility was inaugurated by Tudawe Trading Chief Executive Officer Eshan Tudawe, Sandagiri Tire House owner Niluka Dilhani and others.

Tudawe Trading Company marks another milestone in modernising the country’s vehicle-care industry with the opening of its fourth Karcher automated car-wash facility at Sandagiri Tire House.

Powered by Karcher’s advanced German cleaning technology, the CW3 automated car-wash system delivers a consistent, paint-safe vehicle wash in under eight minutes, redefining convenience, efficiency and quality.

Paint-safe technology: Specially designed washing components help minimise scratches and protect vehicle paintwork.

German engineering: Precision automation and controlled detergent application ensure reliable cleaning results.

Resource efficiency: Optimised water and detergent consumption helps minimise wastage.

Smarter business model: Reduced reliance on manual labour, improved operational efficiency and the potential to serve more vehicles per day.

With the rising labour costs, increasing demand for convenience and growing expectations for professional vehicle care and automated car washing presents an attractive opportunity for entrepreneurs, fuel stations, automotive service centres and commercial investors.

Backed by Karcher’s globally established cleaning technology and Tudawe Trading’s over 40 years of local industry experience, the CW3 represents a shift towards a more efficient, scalable and professional car-wash industry in Sri Lanka.

Tudawe said, “We are not simply introducing another car wash. We are introducing a smarter way of doing business. Our vision is to transform Sri Lanka’s traditional car-wash industry through German technology, greater productivity and consistent quality – creating value for motorists, operators and investors alike.”
