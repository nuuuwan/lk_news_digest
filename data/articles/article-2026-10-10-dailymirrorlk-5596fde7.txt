Eight reservoirs spilling as gates remain open at three major tanks

Eight reservoirs under the Irrigation Department were spilling as of 1 p.m. today (10), while spill gates remained open at three major reservoirs, according to the department’s latest reservoir status report.

The report listed Ambewela Tank and Kande Ela Tank in Badulla, Yan Oya and Rajangana in Anuradhapura, Deduru Oya in Kurunegala and Wan Ela in Trincomalee among the major reservoirs spilling or releasing water. Two medium-sized reservoirs, Bomuruella in Badulla and Denagama Wewa in Galle, were also spilling.

At Rajangana Reservoir, two gates were open by five feet, two by four feet and another two by two feet, releasing 5,365 cubic feet of water per second. Deduru Oya Reservoir was releasing 2,800 cubic feet per second through two gates opened by 24 inches.

Yan Oya Reservoir was releasing 68 cubic feet per second through three gates opened by half a foot, while Wan Ela was listed as spilling at one foot, with a discharge of 10 cubic feet per second.

The department urged people living in downstream areas of reservoirs that are spilling or releasing water to remain vigilant about changes in river water levels and strictly follow instructions issued by the Irrigation Department and local authorities.

Meanwhile, the effective water storage of the department’s 74 major reservoirs stood at 35.3 per cent of their total capacity, according to the report.

Kurunegala recorded an effective storage level of 82 per cent, while Monaragala recorded levels of 85 per cent and 28.5 per cent in two separate entries in the report. Nuwara Eliya recorded 99.3 per cent, while Hambantota stood at 54.6 per cent.

Irrigation Director (Water Management) Engineer H.M.P.S.D. Herath issued the report.
