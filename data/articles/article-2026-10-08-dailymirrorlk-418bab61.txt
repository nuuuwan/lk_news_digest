NBSL opens new Colombo City Office

With the objective of catering to the needs of its members from all walks of life, NBSL recently expanded its operations with a new City Office at No. 50, Thurstan Road, Colombo 3.

The new City Office will add value to the services offered to a wide audience with quality financial products such as Annual Subscription Block, ‘Wardhana’ and Wardhana Plus, floating mortgage, key loan, ‘Nivahana’ and ‘Sithpiyasa’ as key offerings.

At NBSL, ‘Close to your heart’ isn’t just a slogan; it’s its mission.

NBSL is dedicated to guiding its members towards achieving their financial aspirations and securing their dream homes and business places.

With a suite of tailored construction solutions and exceptional customer service, NBSL supports their journey every step of the way.

NBSL continues to embrace innovation, convenience, personalised service and customer-focused solutions.

A corporate entity established decades ago in Sri Lanka as a diversified group affiliated to Future Life Holdings, NBSL was founded in 1955 and has been operational since 1998 under National Housing Act No. 37 of 1954, with a clear and meaningful purpose—to help individuals and families achieve the dream of owning a home, while contributing to stronger communities and national development.

From its strong foundation in housing finance, NBSL has grown into a comprehensive financial services organisation, offering a range of solutions designed to support its members at different stages of their financial journey.

Its products and services include savings and investment solutions and housing finance.
