Alumex quarterly profit jumps sevenfold as margins improve

Alumex PLC reported a sevenfold increase in net profit for the three months ended June 2026, as lower cost of sales, stronger operating profit and higher finance income offset a marginal decline in revenue.

The aluminium manufacturer posted a net profit of Rs.149.9 million for the quarter, compared with Rs.21.4 million in the corresponding period last year. Earnings per share rose to 25 cents from four cents.

Revenue declined 3.8 percent year-on-year to Rs.3.58 billion from Rs.3.72 billion. However, cost of sales fell at a faster rate of 8.4 percent to Rs.2.84 billion, lifting gross profit by 19 percent to Rs.733.9 million.

As a result, the gross profit margin improved to 20.5 percent from 16.6 percent a year earlier.

The gains at gross profit level were partly offset by higher operating expenses. Distribution expenses rose 11 percent to Rs.162.2 million, while administrative expenses increased 20 percent to Rs.311.1 million.

Despite the higher expenses, profit from operating activities climbed 23 percent to Rs.270.2 million. The operating profit margin improved to 7.6 percent from 5.9 percent in the year-earlier quarter.

Finance income rose sharply to Rs.185.9 million from Rs.65.5 million. Finance costs also increased by 28 percent to Rs.324.4 million, but net finance costs narrowed to Rs.138.5 million from Rs.188.3 million.

This helped lift profit before tax to Rs.131.8 million from Rs.30.5 million.

The bottom line was further supported by a tax reversal of Rs.18.2 million, compared with a tax expense of Rs.9.1 million in the corresponding quarter.

Alumex’s balance sheet expanded during the quarter, with total assets rising to Rs.18.84 billion as at end-June from Rs.17.53 billion at end-March. Inventories increased to Rs.6.14 billion from Rs.4.43 billion over the same period.

Short-term interest-bearing borrowings rose to Rs.7.74 billion from Rs.6.47 billion at end-March, while total equity increased to Rs.5.38 billion from Rs.5.23 billion.

The company recorded a net operating cash outflow of Rs.1.35 billion during the quarter, widening from Rs.589.3 million a year earlier. The filing showed that the increase in inventories accounted for a cash outflow of Rs.1.72 billion, compared with Rs.595.1 million in the corresponding period.

Alumex, a subsidiary of Hayleys PLC, had a market capitalisation of Rs.10.72 billion at the end of June.
