Minor flood warning issued for Mahaweli River Basin

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - A minor flood warning has been issued for low-lying areas along the Mahaweli River Basin due to the possibility of further rainfall in the upper catchment areas and the recent opening of spillway gates of the Kotmale Reservoir.

The Irrigation Department said there is a risk of a minor flood situation developing within the next 24 hours.

The warning applies to low-lying areas in the Udunuwara, Udapalatha, Yatinuwara, Doluwa, Ganga Ihala Korale, Gangawata Korale and Harispattuwa Divisional Secretariat Divisions.

Residents living near the Mahaweli River in these areas have been urged to remain vigilant and take necessary precautions to protect themselves from possible flooding.

Disaster management authorities have also been requested to take proactive measures, while the public has been advised to closely monitor the situation as further rainfall is expected.
