Sri Lanka reaffirms commitment to UN engagement ahead of UNGA

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath met with United Nations Resident Coordinator in Sri Lanka Marc-André Franche at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism this morning.

The discussion focused on key matters relating to the upcoming 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA), including Sri Lanka’s priorities for the session.

The two sides also discussed Sri Lanka’s more than 70-year partnership with the United Nations, the UN80 process, the UN Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF), and Sri Lanka’s Third Voluntary National Review on the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
