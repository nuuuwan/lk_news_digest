Kananathan Hails President Alassane Ouattara Côte d’Ivoire’s Economic Transformation on Independence Day

Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Former High Commissioner  Kananathan Congratulates Côte d’Ivoire President Alassane Ouattara on Independence Day, Commends Nation’s Economic Rise

Kananathan has conveyed his warmest congratulations and best wishes to President Alassane Ouattara, the Government, and the people of the Republic of Côte d’Ivoire (Ivory Coast) on the occasion of the country’s Independence Day, as they are celebrating today.

In his congratulatory message to the President Ouattara  Ambassador Kananathan praised the remarkable progress achieved by Côte d’Ivoire in advancing economic development, and promoting peace and stability in West Africa.

He noted that Côte d’Ivoire has emerged as one of Africa’s fastest-growing economies through sound economic policies, infrastructure development, and a commitment to regional integration, making it an attractive destination for international investment.

Côte d’Ivoire, commonly known as Ivory Coast, is one of West Africa’s leading economic powers and serves as the world’s largest producer and exporter of cocoa beans, supplying approximately 40 percent of global cocoa production. The country is also a major exporter of coffee, cashew nuts, rubber, palm oil, cotton, and tropical fruits.

Over the past decade, Côte d’Ivoire has maintained strong economic growth driven by investments in infrastructure, manufacturing, agriculture, energy, telecommunications, and financial services. The Port of Abidjan is among the busiest and most important commercial ports in West Africa, serving as a key gateway for regional trade.
