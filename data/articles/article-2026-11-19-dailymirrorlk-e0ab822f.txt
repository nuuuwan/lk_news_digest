71 kg of heroin bound for Sri Lanka seized in Chennai, among 2026’s biggest hauls

INDIA TODAY - The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) has seized 71.24 kg of heroin in Chennai and arrested two people for allegedly attempting to smuggle the consignment to Sri Lanka through the coastal route. The seizure is among the largest single heroin hauls reported in the country so far in 2026.

The operation was conducted on November 17 and 18, following specific intelligence about a large quantity of heroin being transported from Chennai for onward smuggling to the neighbouring island nation.

Acting on the information, DRI officers kept the suspected vehicle under surveillance before intercepting it along with an escort two-wheeler. A search of the vehicle led to the recovery of 69 packets containing an off-white powdery substance, weighing 71.24 kg in gross weight.

The contraband was seized under the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985. The two vehicles allegedly used in the operation were also confiscated.

Preliminary investigation revealed that the consignment was intended to be smuggled into Sri Lanka through the coastal route via Tiruchirappalli, with Chennai serving as the transit point, the DRI said.

Two people allegedly involved in the trafficking operation were arrested under the NDPS Act and remanded to judicial custody.

The agency described the seizure as one of the largest single heroin hauls reported in the country so far this year.

The latest operation comes weeks after the DRI seized 137 kg of methamphetamine from a coastal village near Nagapattinam in October. That consignment was also suspected to have been intended for smuggling to Sri Lanka.

The two seizures point to continued attempts to use India's coastline as a route for trafficking narcotics to the neighbouring country, prompting intelligence-led operations by enforcement agencies.
