Hashan Tillakaratne appointed Sri Lanka batting coach

Sri Lanka Cricket (SLC) has appointed former Sri Lanka cricketer Hashan Tillakaratne as the Batting Coach of the Senior Men’s Program.

Tillakaratne will be based at SLC’s National High Performance Center and will work with national players as part of the Senior Men’s Program.

The former World Cup winner has previously served as the batting coach of the Sri Lanka men’s national team and as head coach of the Sri Lanka women’s national team. He has also served as head coach of the Bangladesh Women’s National Team.

Tillakaratne represented Sri Lanka from 1989 to 2003, featuring in 83 Test matches and 200 One-Day Internationals.

He accumulated more than 8,200 international runs across the two formats during his playing career.
