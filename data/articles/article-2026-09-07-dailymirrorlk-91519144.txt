Luxury car crashes into safety fence on Southern Expressway

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - A luxury car travelling along the Southern Expressway crashed into the central safety fence after veering off the roadway in Dodangoda this morning (07).

The accident occurred near the 32.4-kilometre post, when the vehicle reportedly collided with barricades placed on the expressway in connection with ongoing road maintenance work.

Police said the car was travelling from the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake towards Matara when the accident occurred.

Following the collision with the maintenance barricades, the vehicle veered across the roadway and crashed into the central safety fence.

A woman travelling in the vehicle reportedly sustained minor injuries in the accident.

The impact caused significant damage to the expressway’s central safety fence and the road-maintenance barricades, while the luxury vehicle also sustained extensive damage.
