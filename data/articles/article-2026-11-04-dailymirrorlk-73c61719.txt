Fire erupts at auto spare parts shop in Katugastota

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - A fire broke out at an auto spare parts shop in the Kudugala area of Katugastota at around 5:30 a.m. today (November 4).

According to the police, traffic along the Kandy–Jaffna A9 main road near Kudugala was temporarily disrupted for nearly an hour due to the incident.

Firefighting teams from the Kandy and Matale Fire Units were deployed to the scene and successfully brought the blaze under control.

The cause of the fire has not yet been determined, while Katugastota Police have launched further investigations into the incident.
