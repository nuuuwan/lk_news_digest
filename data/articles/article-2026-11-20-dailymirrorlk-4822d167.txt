Police seek public help to arrest Ahungalla shooting suspect

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police have sought public assistance to locate and arrest a suspect wanted in connection with a fatal shooting in Welikanda, Ahungalla.

The Ahungalla Police are conducting further investigations into the murder, which was committed using a pistol-type firearm on October 1, 2026.

Police said the suspect had evaded arrest and fled the area. His photograph and personal details have been released to the public to assist in locating him.

The suspect has been identified as Liyanage Dinesh Suranga, 38, of No. 567/C/1, Welikanda, Ahungalla.

Members of the public who have information regarding his whereabouts are requested to contact the Officer-in-Charge of the Ahungalla Police Station on 071-8591485 or the police station on 091-2264026.
