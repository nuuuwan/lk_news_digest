Jay Jay Textile Lanka knitting a brighter, greener future for Batticaloa

Marking a major milestone in Sri Lanka’s regional industrialization journey, Jay Jay Textile Lanka (Pvt) Ltd has officially commenced operations at the Board of Investment (BOI) Textile Manufacturing Zone in Punnakudah, Eravur Pattu.

Representing the largest recent investment in the Eastern Province’s textile sector, the state-of-the-art facility is engineered to deliver high-performance knitting, dyeing, and finishing operations built on eco-friendly technology.

Committed to fostering a balanced regional development, the project has already generated over 350 direct employment opportunities for individuals in the Batticaloa District, offering stable and dignified career opportunities closer to home.

Beyond direct employment, the facility has revitalized the regional micro-economy. By integrating local service providers for daily operational logistics including employee transport, catering and accommodation, the facility indirectly impacts a substantial number of community members, breathing fresh economic vigour into Eravur Pattu.

Furthermore, by establishing advanced local fabric production, the venture contributes directly to national macroeconomic stability by reducing foreign exchange outflow on imported fabrics.

Jay Jay Textile Lanka places employee well-being at the core of its operational strategy. Recognizing that workforce health directly drives productivity, the company provides 100 percent free daily meals and comprehensive transport facilities for all staff members. This initiative eliminates logistical and financial burdens for workers, ensuring a safe, dignified, and supportive environment.

Operating in line with the highest global environmental standards, the Punnakudah plant demonstrates that large-scale industrial growth can coexist with resource conservation, by implementing the following measures.

Water recirculation: The facility recycles and reuses 70 percent of operational water, dramatically lowering freshwater extraction.

Low-impact processing: Advanced machinery minimizes chemical and energy usage across dyeing and finishing operations.

Long before commercial operations commenced, Jay Jay Textile Lanka established active community engagement programmes across the Eastern Province. They include:

Coastal conservation: Regular beach cleanup initiatives along the Eastern coastline.

Social cohesion: Hosting community Vesak Dansal programs to foster cultural unity.

Disaster relief: Providing direct aid, emergency meals, and logistical support during extreme weather events.

Community upliftment: Conducting capacity building programmes for local school students.

Jay Jay Textile Lanka is supported by its parent company, Jay Jay Mills Lanka (established in Sri Lanka in 2004), and its ultimate parent company, Jay Jay Mills India, which brings over four decades of world-class textile manufacturing expertise. With an established operational network across Avissawella, Matara, Monaragala, Trincomalee, and Mannar, this new Eastern expansion reinforces the group’s long-term commitment to position Sri Lanka as a sustainable apparel manufacturing hub.
