21 foreign nationals arrested over online financial fraud in Colombo

Colombo, Nov 03 (Daily Mirror) - A group of foreign nationals allegedly involved in online financial fraud and other illegal internet-based activities operating from Sri Lanka has been arrested in the Maradana Technical Junction area of Colombo.

The arrests were made during a joint raid conducted by officers from the Police Central Crime Investigation Bureau, the Criminal Investigation Department (CID), and the Department of Immigration and Emigration.

The illegal operation was reportedly being conducted inside a three-storey building in the Maradana Technical Junction area, where 21 foreign nationals suspected of being connected to the racket were taken into custody.

Among those arrested are 12 Chinese nationals, six Bangladeshi nationals, and three Indian nationals.
