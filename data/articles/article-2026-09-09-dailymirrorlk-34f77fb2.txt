President approves extension of Rs. 25,000 rent allowance for Ditwah-affected families

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has approved a proposal to extend the monthly Rs. 25,000 rent allowance provided to families affected by Cyclone Ditwah until December 31 this year, Leader of the House Bimal Ratnayake told Parliament today.

The announcement was made after Opposition MPs raised concerns that some affected families would be left without assistance as the rent allowance scheme was due to end this month.
