‘Suwa Husma’ Every Breath Matters: People’s Leasing leads a national call for healthier lungs

As a responsible corporate citizen, People’s Leasing & Finance PLC remains steadfast in its commitment to creating meaningful and lasting change in the communities it serves, going beyond its role as a leading financial services provider to contribute towards building a healthier, stronger and more sustainable nation.

Reaffirming this commitment, the company launched the special initiative ‘Suwa Husma’ on November 24, to raise awareness about lung diseases, promote respiratory health and encourage people to embrace healthier lifestyles, under the message of ‘Every Breath Matters.- Healthy Lungs, Healthy Life’.

As an important step in taking this message beyond conventional awareness campaigns and into the wider community, the Sri Lanka College of Pulmonological (SLCP) will serve as the principal medical and technical partner of the Every Breath Matters initiative, bringing essential medical expertise, professional credibility and national healthcare experience to the campaign.

As an initiative awareness idea, People’s Leasing has dedicated its 2026 annual calendar to this important theme, using it as a creative communication platform to bring the message of lung health and disease prevention into homes, workplaces and communities across the country. This initiative marks the beginning of a series of community-focused programmes and awareness campaigns the company has lined up, with the broader objective of educating the public, encouraging preventive healthcare and supporting national efforts to safeguard the well-being of the present and future generations.

“People’s Leasing believes that as a responsible corporate citizen, our responsibilities extend far beyond providing financial services and achieving business performance. We have a broader responsibility to contribute towards the well-being of society and the future of our nation. This belief was one of the key motivations behind our decision to launch the ‘Suwa Husma’ national programme.

At People’s Leasing, we believe that our responsibility as a corporate citizen extends far beyond financial services and business performance. We have a greater role to play in contributing to the well-being of society and the future of our nation. We selected lung health as the focus of our 2026 annual calendar because it is an issue that touches every family and deserves greater public attention.

Through this initiative, we hope to take an important health message to every corner of the country, encourage greater awareness and support the collective effort towards building a healthier nation. We believe that a healthy nation is the foundation of a prosperous nation,” said People’s Leasing Chairman Prof. Ajantha Samarakoon, expressing his views on why the company chose this cause.

The company intends to deliver the message – communication, public engagement, partnerships and practical action.

“Alongside the launch of the ‘Suwa Husma’ national programme, we have dedicated our 2026 annual calendar to lung health, utilising this widely accessible communication medium to keep an important health message alive and visible among the public throughout the year. Our intention is to transform awareness into meaningful action.

By dedicating our 2026 annual calendar to lung health, we are using a familiar and widely accessible communication platform to keep an important health message in the public eye throughout the year. We want people to understand the importance of protecting their lungs, recognising potential warning signs and seeking timely medical advice.

This calendar is just the beginning of a broader programme of activities that People’s Leasing has planned in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders. Through these initiatives, we aim to reach diverse communities, encourage preventive healthcare practices and make a practical contribution to improving the quality of life of our people,” said People’s Leasing General Manager and CEO Sanjeewa Bandaranayake.

The SLCP joining the project as the principal medical and technical partner brings the essential medical expertise, professional credibility and national healthcare experience to the campaign.

“Lung health is fundamental to the overall well-being and quality of life of every individual; yet, respiratory diseases continue to pose a significant health challenge. One of the most effective ways to address this challenge is through public awareness, early recognition of symptoms, timely medical intervention and preventive healthcare practices. We greatly appreciate the initiative taken by People’s Leasing and its partners to bring the importance of lung health into the national conversation. What makes this programme particularly valuable is its intention to reach people across the country, taking essential health messages beyond hospitals and healthcare institutions and into everyday communities.

We hope this initiative will encourage people to pay greater attention to their respiratory health, protect their lungs and recognise that keeping our lungs healthy is ashared responsibility. A healthier nation begins with healthier lungs,” stated SLCP President Dr. D.M. Sumana Handagala.

The initiative goes beyond a single awareness campaign, with a series of programmes lined up to educate the public on the importance of maintaining healthy lungs, understanding the risks of lung diseases and adopting practices that promote better respiratory health. With an islandwide outreach strategy, these programmes aim to reach every corner of Sri Lanka, engaging and educating people across all communities, irrespective of age, background or geographical location. As an important first step in taking this vital message into homes and workplaces across the country, People’s Leasing has dedicated its 2026 annual calendar to the theme of lung health, transforming an everyday communication platform into a year-long awareness initiative.

Through these collective efforts with its partners, the company aims to inspire greater public awareness and contribute towards building a healthier nation where every breath truly matters.
