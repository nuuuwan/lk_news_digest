Australian High Commissioner highlights bilateral ties in Colombo

Australian High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Matthew Duckworth briefed senior editorial staff from leading Sri Lankan media institutions recently on Australia’s diplomatic mission and strategic bilateral initiatives in the country.

The media briefing, held in Colombo, was organized by the OzLanka Journalists’ Consortium in collaboration with Australia’s Market Development Facility (MDF).

During the session, High Commissioner Duckworth held a productive discussion covering Australian investments, development partnerships, and Australia’s broader contributions to Sri Lanka’s economic growth.

Addressing the gathering, the High Commissioner emphasized the strength of the relationship between the two nations ahead of a major diplomatic milestone next year.

“Australia deeply values our longstanding partnership with Sri Lanka, and it is significant to note that next year marks 80 years of our bilateral relations,” Duckworth said. “We take this very seriously and look forward to telling this story more broadly next year.

As neighbours in this region, we recognize each other as long-term partners and friends, and we want to continue supporting and building upon these ties.”

Highlighting the development aspect of the bilateral partnership, Market Development Facility (MDF) Sri Lanka’s, Country Director Maryam Piracha outlined the Australian government-funded initiative’s core mission in the country.

Operating in Sri Lanka since 2015, MDF focuses on promoting sustainable, inclusive economic growth by co-investing with private sector businesses in high-value, export-oriented sectors such as tourism, specialty coffee, and agriculture.

“MDF is committed to building partnerships that enhance competitiveness, drive economic diversification, and create inclusive opportunities particularly for women and smallholder farmers across Sri Lanka’s key value chains, in both the agriculture and tourism sectors,” Piracha noted during the session.
