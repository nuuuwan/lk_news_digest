Groundworth launches ‘The Highway’ land development near Kottawa Expressway Interchange

Groundworth (Pvt.) Ltd has launched ‘The Highway by Groundworth’, an exclusive land development located just 1 km from the Kottawa Expressway Interchange, offering premium residential plots with convenient access to the Southern Expressway (E01) and Outer Circular Expressway (E02).

Positioned inside an established and rapidly developing residential corridor, The Highway offers residents easy access to Colombo, Galle, Matara, Kadawatha and Katunayake, while keeping essential amenities, schools, healthcare and public transport close by. The development’s proximity to High Level Road (A4) also provides an additional connection between Pannipitiya and Colombo.

Groundworth Group Managing Director and Co-Founder Kasun Andrahennadi stated, “We wanted to create an opportunity where the location does much of the work in defining the value of the property. The Highway is built around one of the strongest practical advantages a property can offer: connectivity. Being just 1 km from the Kottawa Expressway Interchange means our customers can connect quickly to key destinations across the island while still having schools, healthcare, transport, and everyday conveniences close to home.”

The Highway comprises only 12 plots, with land sizes beginning from six perches. Clear deeds, 22-ft road frontage, pipe-borne water and electricity connections, and a planned residential environment are all aspects of the development, which is intended for those looking to build high-end homes in a well-connected location. Land prices start from Rs.2.1 million per perch.

The Highway is now open for site visits, with only a limited number of plots available.
