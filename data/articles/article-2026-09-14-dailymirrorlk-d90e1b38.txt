Staff Conference 2026 HNB Life celebrates 25-year journey, exceptional success

HNB Life brought together the employees from across the island for its 2026 Staff Conference, a memorable gala evening held as part of the company’s 25th anniversary celebrations.

The event marked not only a milestone year in the organisation’s journey but also celebrated one of the most successful years in the company’s history, recognising the people and teams who continue to drive HNB Life forward.

The evening was a celebration of the extraordinary progress achieved during the past year, which saw HNB Life reach new heights across business performance, customer engagement and industry recognition.

While the achievements were remarkable, the focus of the event remained firmly on the people behind the success, the employees whose dedication and belief in the company’s purpose have helped shape its journey.

Executive Director and CEO Lasitha Wimalaratne said, “As we celebrate 25 years of our journey, it is important to remember that our greatest achievement has always been our people.”

EVP and Chief People Officer Navin Rupasinghe said, “This evening is about celebrating the people who make HNB Life what it is.”
