Sri Lanka approves performance-based framework for SOE collective agreements

ECONOMYNEXT — Sri Lanka’s Cabinet of Ministers has approved a new performance- and sustainability-based framework for collective agreements in State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs), which will take effect from January 1, 2027, minister Nalinda Jayatissa said.

The decision follows an earlier cabinet decision when the government decided to extend existing collective agreements in state enterprises under their current terms and conditions until December 31, 2026, while preparing a revised structure for 2027.

To facilitate the transition, the Secretary to the Treasury appointed an expert committee tasked with reviewing the current collective agreement mechanism and formulating the new framework.

Following consultations with relevant stakeholders, the committee submitted its report.

According to Jayatissa, the expert committee recommended aligning workplace pacts with key statutory regulations:

“It is recommended that collective agreements be implemented within state-owned enterprises, subject to the Industrial Disputes Act, the Trade Unions Ordinance, the Shop and Office Employees Act, and other relevant labour laws.”

The new structure also addresses employee incentives and financial returns.

“Recommendations have also been put forward regarding a framework for profit-sharing and bonuses.” (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
