PE Plus CEO Gehan Amarasinghe wins 3 Golds

A moment of pride for PE Plus (Pvt.) Ltd and The Capital Maharaja Group as CEO Gehan Amarasinghe was honoured with three Gold awards at Management Leadership Excellence Awards 2026, organised by the Institute of Chartered Professional Managers of Sri Lanka (CPM Sri Lanka).

Amarasinghe received Gold awards in Quality and Productivity, Sustainability Goals-Led Initiatives and Manufacturing – Industrial and Household Products.

The recognitions celebrate his leadership in advancing operational excellence, productivity, sustainability, innovation and business transformation at PE Plus.

Held on September 15, 2026, at Monarch Imperial, the awards brought together leading professionals and business leaders to recognise the outstanding achievements in management and leadership.

This remarkable triple Gold recognition is a proud milestone for Amarasinghe, PE Plus and The Capital Maharaja Group and a reflection of the collective commitment of the PE Plus team to excellence, innovation, sustainability and responsible growth.
