LCB Finance opens 26th branch in Anuradhapura

The 26th branch of Lanka Credit and Business Finance PLC (LCB Finance) was declared opened recently, at No 514/A/2A, Maithripala Senanayake Mawatha, New Town, Anuradhapura.

It was opened by North Central Province Chief Secretary Ranjan Jayasinghe, amidst a gathering of distinguish invitees, consisting of Assistant District Secretary Prasanna Yasarathna, Anuradhapura SSP Prasad Vithanage, Ceylon Electricity Board North Central Province DGM Pramith Jayawardena, along with reputed businessmen, entrepreneurs, office bearers of co-operative societies, prospective customers, the chairman and board of directors of the company.

The company’s CEO and Executive Director K.G. Leelananda addressing the gathering disclosed the remarkable progress made by the company during the last 10 years, through its existing network of 25 branches, commencing from the establishment of its first branch in Galle.

Leelananda disclosed that through the Anuradhapura  branch,  the company intends to participate in providing financial products to meet the needs of  the core economic drivers in the Anuradhapura district,  consisting of those in the agriculture sector, entrepreneurs in the tourism sector, those involved in  providing hospitality services to the local pilgrims visiting the sacred Buddhist and archaeological sites and the wholesale and retail traders involved in the sale and distribution of agricultural produces islandwide.

He stated the company’s financial products, namely LCB Business, LCB Ran Senasuma, LCB Leasing, LCB Regini, Sihina Ayojana, LCB Podiththa, LCB Prarthana and Co-operative loans.

Board of Directors Chairman Dushmantha Thotawatte addressing the distinguished invitees described the involvement of the board of directors in directing the finance business operations of the company to the satisfaction of the existing customer base of the company, adhering to the regulatory and statutory requirements. He invited those present to establish mutually beneficial finance business relationships with the Anuradhapura branch.
