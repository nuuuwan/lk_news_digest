Victor Ratnayake’s younger brother found dead at Athurugiriya home

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The body of the younger brother of veteran musician Victor Ratnayake was discovered inside his residence in the Boralugoda area of Athurugiriya.

The deceased was identified as Ratnayake Arachchilage Don Freeman, 65, according to Athurugiriya Police.

Police investigations have revealed that he was a cameraman by profession and had been living alone at the house.

The body was discovered after residents in the area alerted police on the afternoon of November 18, following a foul smell emanating from the house.

Police officers who inspected the premises found the body inside and suspect that he had died approximately two days before it was discovered.

A magisterial inquiry was conducted at the scene by Thalangama Inquirer into Sudden Deaths and Justice of the Peace Premalatha Abeywardena.

The cause of death has not yet been established, and Athurugiriya Police are conducting further investigations.
