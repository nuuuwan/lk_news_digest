Ex-Maldives President Nasheed offers help in Sivarasa Anojan case

Former Maldivian President Mohamed Nasheed has offered assistance in the case of Sri Lankan national Sivarasa Anojan, who faces a death sentence in Saudi Arabia.

Nasheed expressed his willingness when President’s Counsel Faiszer Musthapha and President’s Counsel Upul Kumarapperuma met him to discuss possible avenues for humanitarian intervention in Anojan’s case.

During the meeting, Nasheed told the two lawyers that while he considered Anojan’s conduct “deplorable”, he was willing to assist the Sri Lankan national on humanitarian grounds.

The meeting comes as Sri Lanka steps up legal and diplomatic efforts to seek relief for Anojan, following the enhancement of his sentence to death by a Saudi appellate court.

Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath told Parliament that Musthapha and Kumarapperuma had agreed to provide legal assistance in the case and travel to Saudi Arabia at their own expense. They are expected to coordinate with Anojan’s legal representatives and relevant Saudi authorities to explore available legal remedies.

The Government has also said it will bear the legal costs associated with the case, as Anojan is a registered worker with the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment.
