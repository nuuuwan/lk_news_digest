LAUGFS Gas expects three LPG shipments in November

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - LAUGFS Gas PLC has arranged three LPG shipments expected to arrive in Sri Lanka during November as the company seeks regulatory approval to resume domestic supplies through its Hambantota terminal.

According to the company, the first shipment from OQT is expected to arrive at Hambantota Port between November 12 and 14, while a Geogas shipment is scheduled to arrive at Colombo Port between November 15 and 17. A third shipment from OQT is expected at Hambantota between November 24 and 28. However, the arrival dates remain subject to shipping arrangements, port operations and regulatory approvals.

The company said distribution was expected to resume gradually following the arrival and clearance of the cargoes, subject to the necessary authorisations for domestic releases from the Hambantota terminal.

In a statement, LAUGFS Gas apologised for the difficulties caused to households and businesses by the ongoing supply disruptions and said it remained committed to restoring LPG availability.

The company attributed the supply challenges partly to the conflict in the Middle East and disruptions to maritime transport through the Strait of Hormuz, which have affected international LPG supply chains and created procurement and shipping difficulties for Sri Lanka and other importing countries.

Meanwhile, LAUGFS Terminals Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of LAUGFS Gas, has sought three months of temporary permission to use its Hambantota terminal for domestic LPG supplies. The US$65 million facility, which commenced operations in 2019, has a storage capacity of 30,000 metric tonnes and is Sri Lanka's largest LPG receiving, storage and entrepôt terminal.

However, regulatory conditions linked to the terminal's status under the Board of Investment's Commercial Hub framework have restricted the release of LPG for domestic consumption. Under the relevant regulations, domestic releases are linked to 40% of the annual value of re-export turnover.

The company said the COVID-19 pandemic, Sri Lanka's economic and political crisis, foreign exchange shortages and sovereign credit rating downgrades had affected its ability to maintain the required re-export levels, resulting in restrictions on domestic supplies through the terminal.

LAUGFS said it was working with the BOI, Ministry of Finance, Sri Lanka Customs and the Department of Imports and Exports Control to complete its transition out of Hub status and establish the necessary operating arrangements, with applicable duties and taxes payable.

The company further said the BOI Chairman had informed it yesterday that, following instructions from President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, he had written to the Secretary to the Treasury requesting that the necessary approvals be facilitated within one week. However, the approvals remain pending.

As an additional measure to strengthen supply security, LAUGFS Gas has also called a fresh tender for direct LPG deliveries to Colombo, scheduled to commence in January 2027.

The company also clarified that neither LAUGFS Gas PLC nor LAUGFS Terminals Ltd. is itself designated by the United States Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).

LAUGFS Gas said it appreciated the patience of its customers and business partners and would provide further updates as shipment arrivals and regulatory approvals were confirmed.
