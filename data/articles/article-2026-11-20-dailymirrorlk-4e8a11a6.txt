NURA set to launch in early 2027

NURA, the international network of AI-powered preventive health screening centres operated by FUJIFILM DKH LLC, is set to expand to Sri Lanka through a strategic partnership with Innovative Health Solutions (Pvt) Ltd., a subsidiary of the Hayleys Group.

A health screening at NURA typically takes around 120 minutes, without the need for fasting or a doctor’s prescription. Guests move through a carefully sequenced series of scans and tests covering major lifestyle-related diseases and common cancers, using advanced screening methods designed with comfort and safety in mind.

The screening experience includes a one-on-one doctor consultation, with the health report provided on-site, allowing guests to complete the entire process in a single visit. The streamlined process is designed with guest comfort in mind, making comprehensive health screening convenient and easy to fit into a busy routine.

NURA’s screening features Fujifilm’s globally trusted Japanese imaging technology, including ultra-low-dose CT systems that use up to 97 percent less radiation than conventional CT scans. Drawing on decades of Fujifilm’s imaging research, AI-assisted technologies enhance image quality and help doctors identify potential areas of concern, supporting clinical assessment and decision-making.

Commenting on NURA’s expansion to Sri Lanka, Masaharu Morita, Founder and Director of NURA said, “In Japan, regular health screening is part of everyday life. People make it a habit long before they have any reason to worry. NURA brings that culture to the world. We already have centres in India, Mongolia, Vietnam, Thailand and Dubai, and we are proud to be coming to Sri Lanka next.

NURA is a dedicated health screening centre where AI supports our doctors and ultra-low-radiation CT makes annual screening practical. Our goal is to make preventive care something everyone in Sri Lanka can approach with confidence and ease.”
