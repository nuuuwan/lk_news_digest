Rajangane Saddharathana remanded until November 26

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Rajangane Saddharathana, who was arrested in connection with a clash during a Satyagraha protest near the Ruwanweli Maha Seya in Anuradhapura, has been remanded until November 26 by the Anuradhapura Magistrate’s Court.

Saddharathana and three others were arrested today (18) by the Anuradhapura Headquarters Police over an altercation that took place on November 12 near the sacred site.

According to police, Saddharathana had arrived with a group at the dansala premises near the Ruwanweli Maha Seya, where Ven. Balangoda Kassapa Thera and several others were staging a fast-unto-death protest, and allegedly instigated a confrontation.

The arrests came following a directive issued yesterday (17) by the Anuradhapura Chief Magistrate, ordering the Anuradhapura Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) to appear before the court and explain why the suspects linked to the clash had not been arrested and produced before court.

Following the directive, Saddharathana and the other suspects were summoned to the Anuradhapura Police Station earlier today to record statements before being arrested and produced before court.
