Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Dialog Axiata and Carson Cumberbatch with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.0 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20.

Crossings were witnessed in Lanka IOC, Ceylon Cold Stores and John Keells Holdings accounting for 44.5% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Credit & Finance, Asiri Surgical Hospital and Commercial Bank whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, Softlogic Capital and HNB Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Energy sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Lanka IOC) whilst the sector index lost 1.72%. The share price of Lanka IOC decreased by Rs 1.75 (1.36%) to close at Rs 127.00.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Ceylon Cold Stores) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.29%. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores lost Rs 1.00 (0.84%) to close at Rs 118.00.

Commercial Credit & Finance, John Keells Holdings and Asiri Surgical Hospital were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance edged down by Rs 0.10 (0.11%) to close at Rs 94.90. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a loss of Rs 0.10 (0.54%) to close at Rs 18.40. The share price of Asiri Surgical Hospital appreciated by Rs 0.10 (1.00%) to close at Rs 10.10.
