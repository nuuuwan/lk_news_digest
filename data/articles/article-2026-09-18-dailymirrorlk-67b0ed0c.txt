Govt. aims to increase Samurdhi loans to Rs.70 bn next year

Colombo, Septemeber 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Government plans to increase the value of loans provided through the Samurdhi banking system from around Rs.60 billion last year to Rs.70 billion next year, Minister of Rural Development, Social Security and Community Empowerment Dr. Upali Pannila said.

Addressing an awareness programme for Samurdhi officials in Matara and Hambantota, Minister Pannila said the Samurdhi banking system should be used as an important mechanism to economically empower low-income communities.

He said approximately Rs.60 billion in loans had been disbursed to the public through the Samurdhi banking system last year, while the Government expects to increase the amount to Rs.70 billion next year.

The Minister said the Government's objective was to eradicate poverty within the current generation by transforming people from dependency into economically and socially empowered citizens.

He said the Samurdhi Development Department must be comprehensively restructured to respond to current social, political and economic conditions and public aspirations.

The Government has entrusted the department with empowering 1.2 million ‘Aswesuma’ beneficiary families by 2029 through a range of programmes.

These include the ‘Savibala’ loan scheme, micro-credit facilities for small businesses, vocational training loans for youth, self-employment training and equipment, and livelihood development programmes.

The Minister was addressing the ‘Praja Saviyata New Balayak’ programme, which is being conducted islandwide to strengthen and streamline Samurdhi empowerment initiatives.

The Matara and Hambantota district programme was held on September 16 at the Rabindranath Tagore Memorial Auditorium of the University of Ruhuna.
