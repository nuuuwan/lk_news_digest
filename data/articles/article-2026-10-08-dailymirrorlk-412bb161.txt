16 former ministers convicted in bribery cases since NPP came to power: Watagala

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Minister of Public Security Sunil Watagala told Parliament today that 16 former ministers and three former state ministers have been convicted in cases filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) since the National People's Power (NPP) came to power.

He said a former Chief Minister, four wives of former ministers, seven children of former ministers and six former local government chairpersons were among those arrested in connection with CIABOC investigations.

According to Watagala, the arrests were made between October 2024 and October 2026.

The Deputy Minister said investigations into allegations of bribery and corruption had been expedited following measures taken by the present government to strengthen the independence of state institutions.

He said effective action against corruption had gained momentum only after the NPP government assumed office.

Watagala further said that a total of 226 people had been arrested in connection with bribery and corruption allegations, of whom 55 were politicians.
