Limited activity weighs on the secondary market

The secondary market was characterized by a dull trading session with limited activity yesterday.

Among the trades that wereexecuted, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities traded between 11.42% - 11.50%. Moving ahead, 01.02.2031 and 15.10.2034 traded at 11.60% and 12.30% respectively. Finally, over the longer end, 01.07.2037 changed hands at 12.80%.

According to the data published by CBSL, the external sector continued to reflect the repercussions of the geopolitical landscape. The current account registered a deficit of USD 149Mn in June 2026, marking the third consecutive monthly deficit.

On the external front, the LKR appreciated against the USD, standing at Rs. 335.72/USD, compared to Rs. 335.78/USD seen earlier. Overnight liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs. 204.74bn from Rs. 179.31bn recorded previously.
