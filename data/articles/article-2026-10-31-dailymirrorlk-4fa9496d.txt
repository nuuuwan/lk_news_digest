Microchips to help monitor stray dog health and location

The Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha has launched a programme to implant microchips in stray dogs to collect and maintain information about the animals, enabling authorities to monitor their health and whereabouts.

The system will allow officials to access important details, including vaccination records and the location of individual dogs, in the event of dog-bite incidents or other situations requiring such information.

Stray dogs remain a growing concern in several parts of the country, with even the popular tourist destination of Galle facing problems caused by roaming dogs.

However, collecting accurate information and maintaining proper records of stray dog populations have remained challenging for authorities.

Under the new initiative, microchips will be implanted in stray dogs within the Homagama Pradeshiya Sabha area, allowing officials to identify individual animals and retrieve their relevant records when necessary.

The programme is also expected to help authorities locate the animals and obtain information about their vaccination status and health condition.

Meanwhile, a separate programme aimed at eradicating rabies through dog vaccination and registration was recently held at the Hatton-Dickoya Urban Council Hall.
