Jaffna man arrested after returning from India released by court

Jaffna, Oct 30 (Daily Mirror) - A 50-year-old man from Jaffna, who was arrested at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) after returning to Sri Lanka from India, has been released by the Mallakam Magistrate’s Court.

The man, identified as Kanthasamy Krishnakumar of Amman Kovilady, Masiapitty, Jaffna, was arrested by officers of the Terrorism Investigation Division (TID) at the airport on the 26th, following his arrival from Tamil Nadu, India.

He was brought to Jaffna on Thursday and produced before the Mallakam Magistrate’s Court, which ordered his release.

Krishnakumar was arrested in the Rameswaram area of Tamil Nadu in 2015 and subsequently served a five-year prison sentence. Following his imprisonment, he was transferred to the Trichy Special Camp, where he remained in detention.

He was recently released from the camp before returning to Sri Lanka.

Meanwhile, the Mallakam Magistrate’s Court ordered that the case be called again on November 12.
