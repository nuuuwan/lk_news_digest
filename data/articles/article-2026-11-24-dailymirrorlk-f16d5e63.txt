Sri Lanka Set Up Emerging Nations Trophy Final Against Ireland

Nov.24 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka will face Ireland in the final of the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy 2026 after both teams completed unbeaten Super Six campaigns, winning all three of their matches in Ajman.

Sri Lanka secured a thrilling four-run victory over Pakistan, while Ireland defeated the Netherlands by 44 runs to book their places in the title decider. The final is scheduled to take place at the Seven Districts Sports Club on Wednesday, November 25.

Imesha Dulani and Sathya Sandeepani played key roles in Sri Lanka's narrow victory over Pakistan. Dulani top-scored with 56 off 38 deliveries, helping Sri Lanka post 140 for six after being asked to bat first.

Pakistan struggled early in their chase, losing three wickets for just 16 runs. Omaima Sohail (36) and Tuba Hassan (24) attempted to revive the innings, while Tasmia Rubab provided late resistance with an unbeaten 23 off just 11 balls.

However, Sandeepani claimed three wickets for 29 runs as Pakistan were restricted to 136 for nine, handing Sri Lanka a four-run win. Umm-e-Hani also impressed with the ball, taking three wickets for 17 runs.

Ireland secured their place in the final with a convincing 44-run victory over the Netherlands. Lara Maritz led the batting effort with an unbeaten 57 off 45 balls, while Cara Murray delivered an outstanding bowling performance, claiming five wickets for just 17 runs.

Ireland posted 162 for three before bowling out the Netherlands for 118 in 19.3 overs to complete their unbeaten Super Six campaign.

Meanwhile, Scotland registered their first Super Six victory, defeating Zimbabwe by 74 runs. Captain Kathryn Bryce delivered an impressive all-round performance, scoring 69 off 48 balls before taking two wickets for 16 runs. Kirstie Gordon also played a major role with the ball, claiming four wickets for 13 runs as Zimbabwe were dismissed for 72 in 16.2 overs while chasing 147.

Sri Lanka A continued their impressive run at the ACC Rising Stars tournament in Doha, defeating Bangladesh A by seven wickets to secure their place in the semi-finals with an unbeaten record.

After restricting Bangladesh A to 145 in 42.5 overs, Sri Lanka A comfortably chased down the target, reaching 146 for three with almost 30 overs to spare.

Ravindu Fernando led the bowling effort with an impressive four-wicket haul, claiming 4/30, while left-arm spinner Wanuja Sahan took three wickets for 20 runs to keep Bangladesh A under pressure.

In the chase, Nishan Madushka remained unbeaten on 51, while Pavan Rathnayake contributed 45 as the pair shared a crucial 81-run partnership to guide Sri Lanka A to victory.

The result ensured Sri Lanka A finished the group stage unbeaten and advanced to the semi-finals in convincing fashion.

UK to Host 2035 Women's World Cup as Euro 2028 Qualifying Draw Approaches

The United Kingdom has been confirmed as the host of the 2035 FIFA Women's World Cup, with England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales set to jointly stage the tournament.

The 2035 edition will feature 48 teams competing in 104 matches over 39 days, marking the first time a football World Cup has been held in the UK since the men's tournament in 1966. The joint bid proposes 19 venues across the four nations, with the possibility of additional stadiums being included.

Meanwhile, attention is turning to the UEFA Euro 2028 qualifying draw, which is scheduled to take place at the International Convention Centre in Belfast at 17:00 GMT on December 6.

A total of 54 national teams will be drawn into 12 qualifying groups, with six groups containing four teams and the remaining six featuring five. England, Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland, despite being among the tournament's hosts, will also compete in the qualifying campaign.

Twenty teams will secure direct qualification through the group stage, while the remaining qualification places will be determined under UEFA's arrangements for the host nations and play-off process.

The Euro 2028 qualifying campaign is scheduled to begin on March 26, 2027, with the play-offs set for March 23–28, 2028. - BBC
