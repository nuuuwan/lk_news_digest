Wildlife Department hunts suspect over alleged Yala poaching

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Wildlife Conservation has launched a probe to apprehend a man allegedly involved in poaching inside Block V of Yala National Park, after a video capturing the incident was handed over to wildlife officials.

The incident reportedly took place on the afternoon of August 10 in the Veheragala area of the park. A safari jeep driver who recorded the incident submitted the video to the Block V park warden on the same day.

Following the complaint, wildlife officials searched the area on August 11 and 12 but failed to locate the suspect. Officials later learned that he had left his usual place of residence.

The department said investigations are continuing to locate and arrest the suspect, who has a previous record of offences.

According to wildlife officials, the suspect had previously been arrested and produced before the Wellawaya Magistrate’s Court, which imposed a fine of Rs. 150,000. He was also sentenced to two years’ imprisonment, suspended for five years.

As he was unable to pay the fine, the suspect had spent three months in remand custody.

The latest alleged poaching incident has also been brought to the attention of the Wellawaya Magistrate’s Court, while further investigations are underway.
