Digital motor insurance in Sri Lanka: How to check and show proof of cover to police

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police has issued a special announcement to raise public awareness regarding the transition to digital motor insurance cards across Sri Lanka.

Following a joint decision by the Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL), the Insurance Association of Sri Lanka, and industry stakeholders, insurance companies began issuing Digital Motor Insurance Cards in place of conventional physical certificates on May 1, 2026. Additionally, the Agricultural and Agrarian Insurance Board—established under the Agricultural and Agrarian Insurance Act No. 20 of 1999 under the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation—introduced digital insurance cards for motor vehicles under its purview starting July 20, 2026. The Inspector General of Police has given special attention to this initiative and issued relevant guidelines and directives to all police stations island-wide.

Under this system, insurance coverage from general industry providers can be provided as a Digital Motor Insurance Card, via a mobile application, or as a printed certificate issued upon the policyholder's specific request. For covers provided by the Agricultural and Agrarian Insurance Board, policyholders can access digital cards via a QR code and URL sent to their mobile phones, request printed certificates, or receive a digital card. Drivers remain legally responsible for presenting their insurance certificate under Section 104(1) of the Motor Traffic Act. Under Section 3, Chapter II of the Electronic Transactions Act No. 19 of 2006, the legal recognition, effect, and validity of these digital insurance cards cannot be denied.

To facilitate instant verification, authorities have launched the National Insurance Verification System alongside the Agricultural Insurance Policy Verification System. Law enforcement officers and policyholders can verify policy validity using toll-free USSD codes by dialing *1338# for general insurers or *1918# for the Agricultural and Agrarian Insurance Board and entering the vehicle or policy number. Alternatively, users can send an SMS with their details to 1338 or 1918, or call the respective hotlines at 1338 or 1918 to speak with customer service officers. Additional verification options for Agricultural Board policies include direct QR code scanning or URL access via SMS/email to view live database records.

When using the 1338 USSD and SMS services, vehicle numbers must be formatted with initial letters or numbers, a hyphen, and a 4-digit number without spaces (e.g., 311-1234, CC-1234, CCC-1234), replacing the character "ශ්‍රී" with "SRI" (e.g., 1-SRI-1234). For 1918 services, vehicle numbers should omit provincial letters and spaces, replacing "ශ්‍රී" with a hyphen (e.g., CC-1234, CCC-1234, 123-4567, 1-1234). All calls and SMS messages sent to short codes 1338 and 1918 are completely free of charge.

Transitional provisions ensure that existing physical insurance certificates issued prior to May 1, 2026, and July 20, 2026, will remain valid until their set expiration dates. Furthermore, insurance providers will temporarily continue issuing temporary cover notes until they are officially phased out.

In the event of an accident investigation, printed copies can be requested from the insurance company, printed directly from the app interface, or downloaded by scanning the policy’s QR code. In cases where a digital card cannot be displayed on-site, police officers can verify the vehicle's coverage using the provided USSD or SMS lookup services.
