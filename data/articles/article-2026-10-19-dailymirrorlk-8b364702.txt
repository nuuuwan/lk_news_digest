G. L. Peiris summoned before CIABOC

Colombo, Oct. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Joint Opposition Convenor Professor G. L. Peiris has been summoned to appear before the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) tomorrow.

Prof. Peiris told the media that the Commission had requested him to give a statement regarding a discussion he held after assuming duties as Minister of Foreign Affairs in 2009.
