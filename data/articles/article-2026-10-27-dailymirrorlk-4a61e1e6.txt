Getting a passport? Five regional offices introduce online booking

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Immigration and Emigration will introduce an online appointment booking system for passport applications at five regional offices from October 29.

The system will be mandatory for applicants seeking passports under both the One-Day Service and Normal Service at the regional offices in Matara, Kandy, Kurunegala, Vavuniya and Jaffna, the Department said.

Applicants must visit the Department’s official website and select the “Book an Appointment to Obtain a Passport at a Regional Office” option to reserve a date and time before visiting the selected office.

To register, applicants must provide their National Identity Card (NIC) number or passport number and a valid mobile phone number. They must also obtain a photo studio receipt from an approved photo studio before beginning the registration process.

Once the registration is completed successfully, applicants will receive an SMS notification confirming the appointment date, time and selected regional office.

The online system also allows family appointments for up to five immediate family members, comprising a maximum of two adults and three children. Such appointments are restricted to the applicant, spouse and children.

For children under 16, the NIC number of either the mother or father must be provided during registration.

Applicants must bring their original NICs and all other required original documents when submitting their applications. Those applying under the One-Day Service must report to their selected regional office before noon on the scheduled appointment date.

The Department said passport applications would not be accepted at the five regional offices without a valid online appointment. Applicants who fail to attend on the scheduled date will have their appointments cancelled and must make a fresh booking.

However, applicants requiring urgent passport services may visit the Department’s head office in Battaramulla without an online appointment if the available appointment dates do not meet their emergency requirements.

The online appointment system does not apply to passport applications submitted at the Battaramulla head office.

Further information and online bookings are available through the Department’s official website: https://www.immigration.gov.lk/.
