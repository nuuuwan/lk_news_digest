Flood warnings extended for Gin and Nilwala river basins

Colombo, September 26 (Daily Mirror)- The Irrigation Department has extended the flood warnings issued for the Gin Ganga and Nilwala River Basins for another 24 hours.

Residents living in areas at risk, as well as motorists travelling along roads in the affected regions, particularly minor roads, have been urged to exercise extreme caution due to the prevailing conditions.

The flood warnings were initially issued at 2.00 a.m. on September 24, 2026.
