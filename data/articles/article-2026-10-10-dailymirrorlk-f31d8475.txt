Apeksha Hospital to strengthen cancer care, address treatment delays

Steps will be taken to ensure uninterrupted cancer treatment services at Maharagama Apeksha Hospital, with urgent attention given to medical equipment, medicine supplies, staff shortages and the expansion of treatment facilities, Deputy Health Minister Dr. Hansaka Wijemuni said.

He made these remarks at a special hospital management review meeting held at the hospital auditorium to discuss its current operations, improvements to patient care and future development plans.

The meeting focused on ensuring continuous cancer treatment through a planned programme while addressing issues affecting the hospital’s services.

Dr. Wijemuni highlighted the need to prepare a new master plan for the hospital with the assistance of the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB), making maximum use of the available land.

Discussions also focused on expediting the construction of clinical buildings, including a new paediatric ward complex planned for Rolle.

The meeting also reviewed measures to complete the CT Simulator facilities to expand radiotherapy services and reduce waiting lists. The construction of bunkers for new Linear Accelerator (LINAC) machines to be provided under an Asian Development Bank (ADB) project was also discussed.

Officials discussed strengthening coordination with the Medical Supplies Division (MSD) to ensure the uninterrupted supply of essential medicines and surgical equipment required by clinics and wards. Emergency procurement and other corrective measures were also considered to address medicine shortages and related issues.

The Deputy Minister said staff vacancies involving medical officers, nursing officers, radiologists, pharmacists and health assistants would be filled gradually under a planned programme implemented by the Ministry of Health and Mass Media. Some newly appointed health assistants are expected to be assigned to the hospital following appointments scheduled for this weekend.

Other matters discussed included air-conditioning systems, medical equipment, digitalisation, improvements to internet services and staff career-related issues.

Dr. Wijemuni pointed out the need to make patient care services at Apeksha Hospital more efficient and systematic to provide better healthcare services to the public.
