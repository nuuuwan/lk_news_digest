HNB Life celebrates multiple wins at National Sales Awards 2026

HNB Life PLC marked another significant achievement at the National Sales Awards 2026, where seven members of its team were honoured for excellence in sales and sales support.

The recognition highlights the outstanding contributions made by the company’s professionals, whose efforts continue to make a meaningful impact on customers and communities across Sri Lanka.

Among the winners, Chamara Gunarathna secured a Bronze Award in the Sales Supervisor Category, while Iresha Maduwanthi received a Bronze Award in the Front Liner Category and Niranji Wijesinghe was recognized with a Bronze Award in the Other Sales Support Staff (Assistant Manager) Category.

Merit Awards were earned by Chathuranga Sandaruwan in the Front Liner Category, Sandali Lekamge and Sachin Subasinghe in the Sales Supervisor Category, and Senthurupan Velauthapillai in the Territory Manager Category.

These accolades earned by HNB Life’s professionals demonstrate the pivotal role they play in helping customers secure their financial futures while contributing to the growth of the life insurance industry in Sri Lanka.

Commenting on the achievement, HNB Life’s Executive Director / CEO Lasitha Wimalaratne said, “We are proud of our award winners and delighted to see their hard work and dedication being recognized on a national platform.

These achievements are a testament not only to individual excellence but also to the culture of learning, collaboration and customer-centricity that defines HNB Life. On behalf of the entire organization, I extend my heartfelt congratulations to each winner for this remarkable accomplishment. Their success inspires us all and reflects the high standards we continue to uphold as a company.”
