CAA clarifies medical test prices circulating on social media

Colombo, September 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has clarified that an advertisement circulating on social media listing prices for various medical tests is not an official advertisement issued by the Authority.

The CAA said the public should not assume that the prices listed for tests such as FBS, RBS, HbA1C, Lipid Profile, LFT, RFT, UFR, CRP, CT, MRI, Ultrasound, Mammogram and DEXA are maximum charges prescribed by the Authority.

According to the CAA, the maximum charge prescribed for a Full Blood Count (FBC) test, including the report, is Rs. 400, while the maximum charge for a Dengue NS1 Antigen test, including the report, is Rs. 1,200.

These charges were prescribed under Section 20(5) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act through Order No. 67 published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2186/17 dated July 29, 2020.

The CAA also said the maximum fee for a COVID-19 PCR test is Rs. 6,500, while the maximum fee for a COVID-19 Rapid Antigen Test (RAT) is Rs. 2,000.

These fees were prescribed under Order No. 75 published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2240/30 dated August 12, 2021.

The Authority stressed that the prices circulating on social media for FBS, RBS, HbA1C, Lipid Profile, LFT, RFT, UFR, CRP, CT scans, MRI scans, Ultrasound scans, Mammograms and DEXA scans are not maximum rates prescribed under the relevant CAA orders.

The CAA urged the public to exercise caution when sharing or relying on information circulated on social media and to obtain accurate information from official sources.

Further information can be obtained by calling the CAA hotline 1977 during office hours.
