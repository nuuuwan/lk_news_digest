CA Sri Lanka, BASL partner to strengthen professional collaboration via strategic MoU

The MoU was signed by CA Sri Lanka President Tishan Subasinghe (right) and BASL President Rajeev Amarasuriya, marking an important step in enhancing cooperation between two of Sri Lanka’s leading professional bodies

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (CA Sri Lanka) and the Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 17 July to strengthen engagement between the accounting and legal professions, promoting knowledge exchange, professional development, and other initiatives of mutual interest.

The partnership seeks to provide a platform for both professions to engage on matters of common interest, recognising the increasingly interconnected nature of legal, financial, and regulatory matters in today’s business environment.

Under the MoU, CA Sri Lanka and the BASL will explore opportunities for joint initiatives, including continuing professional development programs, knowledge-sharing forums, technical discussions, and other capacity-building activities aimed at enhancing the competencies of members of both professions.

CA Sri Lanka President Tishan Subasinghe highlighted the importance of closer collaboration between the accounting and legal professions.

“As the business environment continues to evolve, there is significant value in our professions working together to support organisations, strengthen governance, and contribute to the country’s development,” he said.

He further noted that the MoU reflects CA Sri Lanka’s commitment to fostering professional engagement, facilitating the exchange of knowledge, and creating learning opportunities that benefit members and the wider professional community.

BASL President Rajeev Amarasuriya described the MoU as a significant milestone in the relationship between the two professions.

“This is a historic agreement and the first formal collaboration of its kind between the BASL and CA Sri Lanka. As the legal and accounting professions increasingly operate within overlapping regulatory and commercial environments, it is important that we work together to share knowledge, strengthen professional competencies, and address matters of mutual interest,” he said.th
