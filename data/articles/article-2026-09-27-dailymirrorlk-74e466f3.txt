Sri Lanka among top 10 fastest-improving tourism destinations

Colombo, Sep. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has been ranked among the top 10 countries in the world for the fastest improvement in the Travel and Tourism Development Index (TTDI) between 2024 and 2026, according to the 2026 Travel and Tourism Development Index released by the World Economic Forum (WEF).

Sri Lanka ranked 10th on the list, recording a 4.33% increase in its TTDI score in 2026 compared to 2024.

Albania topped the list with a 7% increase in its index score, while Vietnam and Laos ranked second and third, recording increases of 6.3% and 6.1%, respectively.
