Reigning Miss International 2025 Catalina Duque visits Lakarcade flagship showroom

Reigning Miss International 2025 Catalina Duque, along with the Miss International Beauty Pageant Executive Director Stephen Diaz, paid an exclusive visit to Lakarcade’s flagship showroom located on Staples Street, Colombo.

Duque and Diaz are currently visiting Sri Lanka to attend the Grand Finale of Miss International Sri Lanka 2026, organized by RT International Group Ltd.

During her visit to Lakarcade, Sri Lanka’s leading novelty gift and souvenir store, Duque experienced the rich heritage, authentic artistry, and vibrant cultural craftsmanship of Sri Lanka.

The distinguished guests were warmly welcomed by the management and team at Lakarcade. Speaking on the occasion, representatives of Lakarcade expressed their delight in hosting international pageant royalty and highlighting Sri Lanka’s traditional craftsmanship and cultural heritage on a global platform.

Duque explored Lakarcade’s collection of Sri Lankan handicrafts, traditional masks, and authentic souvenirs, leaving with memorable keepsakes of her stay in the island nation.
