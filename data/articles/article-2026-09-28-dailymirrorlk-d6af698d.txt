Expressway could take 171 years to repay loans

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s expressway network could take more than 171 years to repay the principal amount of loans obtained for its construction, based on current average annual net earnings, according to a recent audit observation.

The audit found that loans amounting to Rs. 611.37 billion had been obtained from domestic and foreign sources for the construction of the expressway network. With average annual net profit reported at around Rs. 3.56 billion, the revenue generated by the expressways remains insufficient to repay the principal, without taking interest costs into account.

The audit noted that the current income from expressway operations is inadequate to meet both loan repayments and interest obligations. As a result, the required funds to service these commitments are being provided through allocations from the General Treasury.

In response to the audit findings, the management said expressway toll charges had been maintained at relatively low levels as a public welfare measure. This policy, however, has limited the revenue available to meet the financial obligations associated with the expressway projects.

The audit recommended the use of modern technology to reduce operational costs while introducing targeted measures to increase revenue from the expressway network.

It also called for stronger internal financial controls, including proper procedures, formal written instructions and improved financial oversight, following a review conducted under Section 38(1) of the National Audit Act No. 19 of 2018.

Meanwhile, the audit also revealed that the Road Development Authority (RDA) has lost more than Rs. 4.614 billion in revenue due to irregularities involving expressway tickets over a period of four years and nine months from November 2019 to July 2024.

The audit found that more than 133.8 million entry tickets were issued during the period, but exit tickets had not been issued for more than 18.2 million vehicles.

The discrepancy has raised concerns over revenue collection and ticket management on the country's expressways.

According to the audit findings, the RDA was losing close to Rs. 1 million in expressway revenue each day as a result of the reported ticket irregularities.
