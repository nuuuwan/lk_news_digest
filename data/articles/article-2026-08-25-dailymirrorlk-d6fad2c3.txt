New service category for medical, dental specialists

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the establishment of a new service category, “Specialist Medical and Specialist Dental Services,” within the Sri Lankan health service.

At its meeting on January 12, 2026, the Cabinet approved the appointment of an Officers’ Committee to make recommendations on establishing a separate service category for medical and dental specialists.

The committee has since completed a comprehensive review and submitted its recommendations.

Based on the committee’s report, the Cabinet approved a proposal by Health and Mass Media Minister to establish the new service category and appoint another Officers’ Committee, chaired by the Secretary to the Ministry of Health and Mass Media, to draft the service constitution governing the new category.

The proposed service constitution is expected to set out the structure and applicable conditions for the specialist medical and dental services.
