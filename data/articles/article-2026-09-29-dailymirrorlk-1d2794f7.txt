Four injured as pavement caves in near old Kandy Prison

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Four people were injured and admitted to Kandy National Hospital this morning (29) after a section of a pavement suddenly collapsed near the old prison in Kandy town.

The victims, three men and one woman, were walking along the pavement when a section of the walkway in front of a nearby shop gave way beneath them.

Local residents suspect that severe soil erosion caused by water flowing through a canal behind the commercial shops may have weakened the ground beneath the pavement.
