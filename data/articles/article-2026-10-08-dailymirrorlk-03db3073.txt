Economic census to be conducted in 2026 and 2027

Sri Lanka would conduct an economic census in 2026 and 2027, to gather comprehensive information on agricultural and non-agricultural economic activities, Census and Statistics Department Director General K.M.D.S.D. Karunaratne said.

Accordingly, the information on agricultural activities would be collected in 2026, while the information on non-agricultural economic activities would be collected from formal establishments with more than 50 employees in the industry, trade and services sectors, during the same year, she said.

In 2027, the census will focus on non-agricultural economic activities involving fewer than 50 workers, covering both formal and informal establishments.

Karunaratne said the agricultural component would be conducted in accordance with the international recommendations prepared for the 2020 census round, including the FAO World Programme for the Census of Agriculture (WCA-2020) recommendations.

She said the economic census would provide updated information on the structure and scale of economic activities in the country and assist in future planning, policymaking and economic decision-making.
