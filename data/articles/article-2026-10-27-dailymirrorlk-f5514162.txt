Ceybank marks first year of partnership with CAMS in Sri Lanka

Ceybank Asset Management Ltd. (Ceybank) is building on a partnership with Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS), India’s largest Mutual Fund Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA).

The partnership has supported Ceybank Asset Management’s efforts to strengthen its operating framework, advance its digital transformation and enhance investor experience for Sri Lanka’s evolving unit trust market.

Through the partnership, CAMS has worked closely with Ceybank to support Fund operations, enhance investor experience, strengthen distributor capabilities and advance technology-led efficiencies, helping build a more scalable foundation for the company’s next phase of growth.

Speaking on the occasion, Ian Ferdinands, CEO & Executive Director, Ceybank said, “Our partnership with CAMS has helped strengthen our operations framework and enhance the experience we deliver to investors and distributors. CAMS brings extensive experience from India’s mutual fund ecosystem, and their expertise in technology, process excellence and investor experience has supported our efforts to build a stronger foundation for growth. We look forward to continuing this partnership as we further enhance our capabilities and deliver greater value to our stakeholders.”

Anuj Kumar, Managing Director, CAMS added, “We are grateful to Ceybank Asset Management for the trust placed in CAMS as their technology and service partner. We see this as the beginning of a long-term relationship built on shared commitment, service excellence, governance and investor-centricity.”

Building on the progress achieved through the partnership, Ceybank and CAMS will continue to focus on strengthening digital access, enhancing investor experience and building distributor capabilities to drive the long-term development of Sri Lanka’s unit trust market.
