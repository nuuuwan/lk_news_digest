ASUS & ROG launch new 2026 lineup in Sri Lanka

ASUS has announced its latest premium laptop lineup, introducing a new generation of AI-powered Zenbook, Vivobook devices to consumers across the country along with its latest gaming & creator laptops, led by the flagship ROG Zephyrus Duo, TUF Gaming 16 along with creator focused ProArt PX13.

Bringing together cutting-edge AI capabilities, Ceraluminum Design, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 50 Series graphics, ultra-portable designs & next-generation computing experiences for gamers, student creators and professionals alike.

Eric Ou, Regional Head, ASUS South Asia said, “At ASUS, we are committed to delivering innovation that empowers every user. With our 2026 lineup—from dual-screen Zenbook & ROG Zephyrus to ProArt for creators, Vivobook & TUF Gaming laptops for students—we’re bringing cutting-edge technology to meet the evolving needs of consumers in Sri Lanka.”

The ASUS Zenbook DUO(UX8407AA) comes with dual3K OLED touch screens, enabling seamless multitasking &flexible workflows.It is designed for professionals and creators seeking a more dynamic and efficient multitasking setupas it’s backed by Intel Core Ultra 9 Series 3 processor and delivers up to 32 hours of battery life.

ASUS is further expanding its portfolio with ProArt PX13 (HN7306EA), a 2-1 convertible device for creators on the go, which is 1.38kg lightand is powered by AMD Ryzen Halo platform, combined with a 3K OLED Touchscreen.

For the ROGlineup, the Zephyrus DUO (GX651AX) anchors the portfolio as an advanced gaming & creator laptop, featuring dual 16-inch 3K OLED Nebula Touch Displays, flexible multi-mode usability and up to NVIDIA RTX 5090 GPU. Combined with Intel Core Ultra processors, advanced AI acceleration, and ROG Intelligent Cooling technology, the Zephyrus Duo is designed for users who demand exceptional gaming, creative and productivity capabilities from a single device.

Further strengthening the lineup, the TUF Gaming 16 combines military-grade durability, powered by up to the latest Intel Core i7 processor &NVIDIA RTX 5060 GPU, it is designed for users who need a versatile device for gaming, learning, content creation, and everyday productivity.In addition to new models, ASUS ROG also introduced the refreshed ASUS Vivobook S14 (M3407GA), Vivobook 15 (M1502NAQ) along with Strix Scar 18 (G835).
