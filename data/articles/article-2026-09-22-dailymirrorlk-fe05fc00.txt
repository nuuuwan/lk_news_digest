15 convicted in Easter Sunday case, each handed 220-year sentence

The High Court Trial-at-Bar today sentenced each of the 15 accused found guilty in the Easter Sunday terror case to 220 years of rigorous imprisonment.

The accused were found guilty of 23,270 charges, including conspiring to carry out suicide terrorist attacks targeting hotels, churches and other locations in Colombo and several other areas on Easter Sunday in 2019, as well as providing assistance and support for the attacks.

Accordingly, each of the 15 accused was sentenced to 220 years of rigorous imprisonment.

The court also ordered the confiscation of the properties belonging to all 15 accused.
