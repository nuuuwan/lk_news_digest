Rs. 1.2 bn approved for expressway maintenance

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the purchase of new vehicles and machinery worth an estimated Rs. 1.2 billion for the operation and maintenance of Sri Lanka’s expressways.

The country currently has around 312 kilometres of expressways, which have been in operation for about 10 years.

Three more expressway sections, covering around 92 kilometres, are expected to be opened between 2027 and 2028. These include the Port Access Expressway, Central Expressway Section I from Kadawatha to Mirigama, and Section III from Pothuhara to Galagedara.

The Road Development Authority (RDA) has identified the need for additional vehicles and machinery to maintain the new expressways. Some vehicles and machinery currently in use on existing expressways also need to be replaced due to their age.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved a proposal submitted by the Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister to purchase 21 vehicles and machinery items at an estimated cost of Rs. 1.2 billion.

The cost will be covered using revenue generated from the expressways.
