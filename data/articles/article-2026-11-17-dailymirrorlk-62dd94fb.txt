Road through Maduru Oya National Park to close at night from Nov. 20

The road section passing through Maduru Oya National Park, between Panijja Oya and Kandegama, will be closed to traffic daily from 6 p.m. to 6 a.m. with effect from November 20.

The closure applies to the Mah Oya–Aralaganwila road and has been introduced to prevent accidents involving wildlife crossing the road at night.

Authorities have observed that vehicles travelling through the national park during nighttime hours frequently cause accidents, resulting in injuries and deaths among wild animals.

The public has been requested to use alternative routes during the closure period.
