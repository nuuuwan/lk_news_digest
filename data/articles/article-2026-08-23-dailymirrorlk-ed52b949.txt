Final farewell to Nanda Malini

The funeral of veteran Sri Lankan singer Dr. Nanda Malini was held at the Borella General Cemetery yesterday (22).

Her remains were placed at the Nanda Malini Sangeetha Ashram (Music Academy) at No. 30, Vijayaba Mawatha, Nawala Road, Nugegoda, where members of the public gathered to pay their final respects.

Following the funeral rites and Pansukula ceremony, the funeral procession carrying her remains left the Sangeetha Ashram at around 3.30 p.m. and proceeded to the Borella General Cemetery via Parliament Road and Rajagiriya.

The funeral was attended by family members, fellow artistes, dignitaries and a large number of members of the public who gathered to bid farewell to one of Sri Lanka’s most celebrated voices.
