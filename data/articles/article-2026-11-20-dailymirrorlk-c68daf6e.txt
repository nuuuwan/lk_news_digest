NTB unveils exclusive cardholder privileges

Nations Trust Bank has launched its festive season campaign, ‘A Season Wrapped in Joy with Nations Trust Bank Cards’, bringing cardholders a wide range of seasonal offers and privileges

Commenting on the campaign, Nations Trust Bank’s Head of Cards, Niluka Gunatilake said, “Finding the ideal gift, treating yourself to something special, spending time with loved ones around a table, or taking a well-deserved break are all moments that make the festive season memorable. We want our cardholders to enjoy more of these moments with ‘A Season Wrapped in Joy,’ which offers seasonal deals and privileges that make these celebrations even more rewarding.”

Nations Trust Bank American Express & Mastercard cards offer the opportunity to take advantage of savings in a variety of lifestyle categories, from festive shopping and dining with loved ones to planning a holiday, enjoying entertainment, or finding the perfect gift. The campaign adds value to the experiences and moments that make the season special with offers that include dining, shopping, travel, entertainment, and gifting, giving cardholders more opportunities to enjoy the season.

By visiting www.nationstrust.com, cardholders can learn more about the seasonal offers and privileges offered by Nations Trust Bank Cards. Availability may differ, and all offers are subject to merchant-specific terms and conditions.
