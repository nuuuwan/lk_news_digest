What led to Shiranthi Rajapaksa’s arrest?

Colombo, Oct 07 (Daily Mirror) - Former First Lady Shiranthi Wickramasinghe Rajapaksa was arrested today by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over allegations relating to the failure to provide a CT scanner intended for the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children.

According to the allegations, a sum of Rs. 10 million was obtained from the National Savings Bank through the Siriliya Saviya organization for the purpose of providing a Computed Tomography (CT) scanner to the children's hospital.

Investigators allege that the CT scanner was not provided to the hospital using the funds obtained and that the money was instead misappropriated. The allegations include offences relating to corruption and the unlawful misappropriation of government funds under the Public Property Act.

Rajapaksa was arrested at approximately 9:56 a.m. on Wednesday at her residence on Poorwarama Road in Kirulapone, Colombo, according to CIABOC.

She was later taken to the Bribery Commission, where she is still being questioned.

She is expected to be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate's Court.
