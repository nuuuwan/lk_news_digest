Air quality in several areas turns ‘unhealthy for sensitive groups’

Colombo, October 30 (Daily Mirror)- Air quality in several parts of Sri Lanka deteriorated to unhealthy levels this morning, according to real-time Air Quality Index (AQI) data published by IQAir.

As of 11.30 a.m., Jaffna recorded an AQI reading of 142, followed by Puttalam at 119 and Anuradhapura at 114. These readings placed the three areas in the “unhealthy for sensitive groups” category.

Colombo recorded an AQI of 90, falling within the “moderate” category.

According to AQI guidelines, readings between 101 and 150 indicate unhealthy air quality for sensitive groups. People with heart or lung conditions, older adults and children may experience health effects, while the general public is less likely to be affected.
