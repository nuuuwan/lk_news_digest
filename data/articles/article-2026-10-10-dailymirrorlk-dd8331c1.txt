Lanka IOC marks 24th anniversary with community-focused CSR initiative

Manil Jayesinghe, S. Renganathan and K. Raghu together with Lanka IOC CSR team participated in CSR activity organised to commemorate company’s 24th anniversary

Lanka IOC PLC marked its 24th anniversary recently with a series of celebrations and community-focused initiatives, reaffirming its commitment to being a responsible corporate citizen in Sri Lanka.

The anniversary celebrations were held at the company’s head office in Colombo, with the participation of Senior Independent Non-Executive Director Manil Jayesinghe, Independent Non-Executive Director S. Renganathan, Managing Director K. Raghu, senior vice presidents, vice presidents and employees of Lanka IOC.

As part of the anniversary celebrations, Lanka IOC extended its support to visually impaired members of the Sri Lanka Welfare Society of the Blind Women by donating 100 dry-ration packs containing essential household food items. The donation was received by society President W.M. Renuka and subsequently distributed among its members.

The society expressed its appreciation to Lanka IOC for its continued support over the years.

The company has regularly extended assistance to the organisation through donations of dry rations, white canes and other essential items, particularly in connection with Lanka IOC’s anniversary celebrations.

Raghu said that it was a pleasure to participate in initiatives that give back to the community while celebrating the company’s anniversary. He emphasised the importance of sharing the company’s achievements and happiness with communities in need.

This CSR initiative reflects Lanka IOC PLC’s continued commitment to supporting vulnerable communities and contributing to their well-being. Through such initiatives, the company continues to demonstrate that its success goes beyond business performance, with a strong focus on creating a meaningful and positive impact on society.
