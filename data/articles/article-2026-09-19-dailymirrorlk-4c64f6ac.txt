Trump announces ‘permanent’ US security control over Greenland

WASHINGTON -  US President Donald Trump has announced that the United States has reached an agreement with Denmark and Greenland that he says gives Washington permanent control over security and other needs in Greenland.

Trump made the announcement in a post on his official X account, saying the agreement would give the US the “complete ability” to take necessary measures to secure and defend Greenland and the United States.

> “The United States of America has entered into an Agreement with The Kingdom of Denmark, and Greenland, that gives the United States permanent control over security, and all other needs, in Greenland, completely addressing ALL of our many U.S. concerns,” Trump said.

He said there would be “NO COST” to the United States under the agreement and claimed that no US adversary would be able to establish a base, maintain a military presence or make sensitive investments in Greenland without Washington’s written approval.

Trump also said the US would immediately begin developing a larger military presence at suitable locations in Greenland.

Describing the arrangement as an “Infinite Life” Agreement, Trump said it would have no end and called the development “historic” and “special.”

He said the US would work with the people of Greenland on the development and construction of the military presence.

The announcement comes amid longstanding US interest in Greenland’s strategic importance in the Arctic and Trump’s previous calls for greater US control over the territory.
