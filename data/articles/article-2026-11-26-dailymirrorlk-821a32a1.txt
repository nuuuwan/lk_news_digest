Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Distilleries, Piramal Glass and LOLC Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Housing Development Finance Corporation, LB Finance and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka. Mixed interest was observed in Dialog Axiata, Dipped Products and Hatton National Bank whilst retail interest was noted in SMB Leasing nonvoting, Industrial Asphalts and Muller & Phipps. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Housing Development Finance Corporation and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.03%. The share price of Housing Development Finance Corporation decreased by Rs 1.30 (1.90%) to close at Rs 67.10. The share price of Commercial Bank declined by Rs 0.50 (0.23%) to close at Rs 213.00.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover whilst the sector index increased by 1.23%.

Dialog Axiata, Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka and LB Finance were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata closed flat at Rs 47.50. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka moved up by Rs 2.00 (1.08%) to close at Rs 187.00. The share price of LB Finance recorded a gain of Rs 1.50 (0.98%) to close at Rs 154.50.
