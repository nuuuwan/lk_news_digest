Education key to promoting equality and mutual respect: PM

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Education is the most powerful tool to create public understanding of mutual respect and equality, Prime Minister and Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Dr. Harini Amarasuriya said.

She made these remarks while participating in the 2026 Milad-un-Nabi celebrations organised by the Muslim School Development Branch of the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education.

The Prime Minister said Sri Lanka is a diverse country with more than 21.7 million people representing different ethnicities, languages, cultures and religions, and that such diversity should be considered one of the country's greatest strengths.

“Our vision is to build a Sri Lanka where every citizen enjoys equal respect, equal opportunities and equal access to public services,” she said.

Dr. Amarasuriya said the Government believes education is a key tool for achieving social mobility and equality.

She said that Sri Lanka's free education system has enabled children to pursue higher education regardless of their family background.

The Prime Minister also said schools should provide opportunities for students to interact with one another and learn about their own culture as well as the cultures of other communities.

She said the Government's new education reforms aim not only to introduce new curricula but also to bring changes to teaching and learning methods.

“Through this, our aim is to instill in students the self-confidence necessary to have a positive impact on society in practice,” she said.

She added that creating future citizens who can contribute to a social system where everyone can live together in harmony and dignity is a fundamental objective of the education reform process.

The Prime Minister said the teachings and example of Prophet Muhammad remain particularly relevant in a world facing serious political, economic and humanitarian challenges.

The celebrations were attended by Deputy Minister of Education and Higher Education Dr. Madura Seneviratne, Ministry Secretary Nalaka Kaluwewa, Commissioner General of Examinations Indika Kumari Liyanage, Senior Lecturer in Arabic and Islamic Civilization at the University of Peradeniya Dr. S. M. Tamim, Major N. T. Nasumutdeen, ministry officials, principals, teachers and students.
