Gnanasara Thera seeks fresh pardon from President

Bodu Bala Sena General Secretary Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera has called on senior Buddhist monks to seek a fresh presidential pardon from President Anura Kumara Dissanayake following the Supreme Court’s decision to invalidate his previous pardon.

Speaking at the launch of his book Navayay Paha Vikurthiya at the Sambuddhatva Jayanthi Mandiraya in Colombo yesterday (20), Gnanasara Thera said he was prepared to abide by the law but questioned why he should face punishment over procedural errors allegedly committed by officials.

“If officials made a mistake in the procedure, why am I being punished? The current President can correct any mistake made in the process followed by former President Maithripala Sirisena,” he said.

He urged senior monks to intervene and request President Dissanayake to grant him a fresh pardon, saying he should be allowed to continue his religious activities.

The Thero also spoke about his 87-year-old mother and expressed concern that he might not get another opportunity to see her if he is imprisoned.

“I don’t know how my fate has been written. This is not a time when I am afraid. Everyone has a responsibility to respect the laws of the country. I will bow to the law,” he said.

Rejecting allegations that he was acting on behalf of politicians, Gnanasara Thera said he was not a follower of any political figure.

“We are not anyone’s followers. We are followers of the Buddha,” he said.

He also maintained that the legal proceedings against him had originated during the former Yahapalana administration and thanked those who had supported him over the years.

The Supreme Court on September 10 ruled that the presidential pardon granted to Gnanasara Thero by former President Maithripala Sirisena in 2019 was invalid. The ruling has left questions over the enforcement of his remaining prison sentence for contempt of court.
