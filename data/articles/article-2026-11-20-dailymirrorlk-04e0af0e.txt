Mahela Jayawardene appointed Delhi Capitals Director of Cricket

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Former Sri Lankan captain Mahela Jayawardene has been appointed Director of Cricket of the Delhi Capitals franchise, with responsibility for overseeing its cricket operations across multiple international leagues.

GMR Sports, the co-owners of Delhi Capitals, announced the appointment on Friday, confirming that Jayawardene will oversee the franchise's teams in the Indian Premier League (IPL), Women's Premier League (WPL), International League T20 (ILT20), Major League Cricket (MLC) and The Hundred.

He will also take charge as Director of Cricket of the franchise's women's team competing in the WPL.

Delhi Capitals co-owner and GMR Sports Chairman Kiran Kumar Grandhi said the appointment would bring additional experience and strategic leadership to the franchise, particularly its women's cricket setup.

He said the franchise aimed to build on the foundations already established in the Women's Premier League and develop a team capable of consistently challenging for the title.

Grandhi added that Jayawardene's understanding of modern cricket and experience in building competitive teams would help take Delhi Capitals Women to the next level as the franchise seeks to establish itself among the leading teams in women's franchise cricket.

Jayawardene was previously associated with the Mumbai Indians as their head coach until the 2026 IPL season, after which the two parties parted ways.

Interestingly, Delhi Capitals was the last IPL franchise Jayawardene represented as a player, having turned out for the team during the 2012 and 2013 seasons.
