Heavy rainfall above 100mm expected in several areas

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Heavy rainfall exceeding 100mm is expected in several parts of Sri Lanka as the depression currently located over the northwest Bay of Bengal, northeast of the island, is forecast to intensify into a deep depression within the next 12 hours.

Although the system is not expected to directly affect Sri Lanka, rainy conditions in the southwestern parts of the country are expected to continue due to the prevailing wind flow towards the system.

According to forecast, showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and North Western provinces, as well as in the Galle and Matara districts. Heavy falls exceeding 100mm are likely at some places in these areas.

Several spells of showers may also occur in the North Central Province and Mannar District.

Strong winds of around 40–50 kmph are expected at times over the western slopes of the Central Hills, North Central and North Western provinces, and in the Trincomalee, Hambantota and Matale districts.

The public is advised to take adequate precautions to minimise damage caused by temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
