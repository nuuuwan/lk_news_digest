Gamani Corea Foundation Memorial Lecture on Sri Lanka’s foreign policy on 4 Nov.

The Gamani Corea Foundation will host the annual Dr. Gamani Corea Memorial Lecture 2026 on 4 November 2026 at the Bandaranaike Centre for International Studies (BCIS), Colombo 07.

The lecture, titled “The Paradoxes of Sri Lanka’s Foreign Policy in the 21st Century,” will be delivered by Ambassador (Retd.) Ravinatha Aryasinha, former Foreign Secretary of Sri Lanka and Executive Director of the Regional Centre for Strategic Studies. He also serves as a Board Member of the Gamani Corea Foundation, BCIS and MARGA.

The lecture will examine the complexities and contradictions shaping Sri Lanka’s foreign policy in the 21st century, providing an opportunity to reflect on the country’s evolving role in international affairs.

The program will commence at 4:30 p.m. Those interested in attending are encouraged to register on or before 29 October 2026. Registration: https://forms.gle/riTuHqKuDtojZ9TJ6
