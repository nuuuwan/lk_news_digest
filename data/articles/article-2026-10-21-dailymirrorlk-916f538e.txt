KIRIN Flooring enters int’l market with ‘Flooring Show UK 2026’ presence

KIRIN Floors, the innovative Sri Lankan flooring brand introduced by St. Anthony’s Interior Solutions Limited, has taken an important step towards international market entry by showcasing its locally manufactured flooring solutions at ‘The Flooring Show UK 2026’ in Harrogate, United Kingdom, in September.

As the only Sri Lankan flooring brand represented at the exhibition, KIRIN’s participation marks a significant milestone for the brand and Sri Lankan manufacturing.

KIRIN is manufactured at St. Anthony’s Interior Solutions’ state-of-the-art facility in Dankotuwa, providing the foundation for consistent supply, product development, technical specifications and competitive pricing. The brand builds on the manufacturing heritage of St. Anthony’s Industries Group, which has been a pioneer in Sri Lankan manufacturing and plastic processing since the 1960s.

Indoor air quality and product safety are important factors, even though they may not be visible. ‘FloorScore’ and ‘ASSURE’ certifications ensure indoor air quality and safety especially in applications such as bedrooms and living areas. Choosing certified flooring can provide additional confidence that the product meets recognized requirements for chemical emissions and other specified safety criteria.

‘FloorScore’ certification tests flooring products for the release of certain chemicals into the indoor air assuring better indoor air quality whilst the ‘ASSURE’ certification provide additional confidence that the flooring has been tested against specific requirements for product safety from any internationally-banned hazardous chemicals.

A key technological feature of KIRIN flooring is the globally recognized patented UNILIN locking technology. Its precision click-lock system enables efficient, adhesive-free installation while contributing to dimensional stability and a seamless finished appearance.

Commenting on KIRIN’s participation at ‘The Flooring Show UK 2026’, Lahiru Jayasinghe, Executive Director and Chief Executive Officer of St. Anthony’s Interior Solutions Limited, said:

“We are exceedingly proud to represent Sri Lanka at‘The Flooring Show UK 2026’ and to showcase a flooring product that is proudly manufactured in our own country. This is an important milestone for KIRIN and for all of us involved in Sri Lankan manufacturing.

We want to demonstrate that a product manufactured in Sri Lanka can be developed with the quality, consistency and standards required to approach international markets. At the same time, we want to assure our Sri Lankan customers that the quality we are taking to international markets is the same quality we are committed to delivering in our motherland, without compromise.”

Designed for residential and commercial applications, KIRIN offers contemporary designs alongside functional benefits including durability, termite resistance, water resistance, anti-slip properties, scratch and stain resistance, acoustic comfort and fire-retardant properties. The flooring is manufactured without formaldehyde and carries a 10-year warranty. Compared to alternatives such as tiles and cement based flooring, KIRIN offers both natural comfort in every step whilst its anti-slip properties ensure safety.It can also replace polyester carpets, which shed inhalable microplasticfibres and require frequent replacement, thereby reducing potential health risks.

KIRIN’s presence at ‘The Flooring Show UK’ represents more than an exhibition appearance. It marks the brand’s first steps towards international flooring markets while showcasing the capabilities of Sri Lankan manufacturing to a global industry audience.
