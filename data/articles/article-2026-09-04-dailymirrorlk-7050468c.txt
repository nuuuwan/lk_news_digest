Two drowned after boat capsizes in Kattankudy

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Two people have reportedly drowned while another person was rescued after a boat capsized in a tank in Kattankudy, Batticaloa.

Police said the body of one of the victims has been recovered, while another person remains missing.

The Sri Lanka Navy has launched a search and rescue operation to locate the missing individual.
