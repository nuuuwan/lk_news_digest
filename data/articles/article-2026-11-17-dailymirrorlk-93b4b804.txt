Cabinet approves aviation fuel supply contracts at four foreign airports

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the award of contracts to supply aviation fuel to SriLankan Airlines at four international airports in Frankfurt, Incheon, Lahore and Ahmedabad.

Eight bids were received following a limited international bidding process to select suppliers for the four airports.

After evaluating the bids, the Cabinet approved a proposal submitted by the Minister of Ports and Civil Aviation to award the contracts to the lowest responsive bidders.

The awards were made based on the recommendations of the High Level Standing Procurement Committee.
