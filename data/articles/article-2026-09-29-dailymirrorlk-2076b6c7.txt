Baby hippo’s winning name to be announced on Oct. 1

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The winning name for the adorable Nile hippopotamus calf born at the Dehiwala Zoo will be announced on October 1, in conjunction with World Children’s Day.

The name was selected from numerous suggestions submitted by children following an invitation to name the young hippo.

Zoo authorities said the winning name will be officially unveiled at the Dehiwala Zoo on October 1, creating a special occasion for children and visitors.

A special prize will also be presented to the young winner who proposed the selected name.

Visitors are invited to the Dehiwala Zoo on World Children’s Day to witness the announcement and discover the new name chosen for the young hippo calf.

The initiative is expected to provide children with an opportunity to be part of a memorable occasion involving one of the zoo’s newest and most popular residents.
