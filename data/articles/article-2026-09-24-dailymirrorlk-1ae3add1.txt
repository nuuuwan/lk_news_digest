Bill on dog registration and rabies elimination presented in Parliament

Colombo, September 24 (Daily Mirror)- A Bill titled the “Elimination of Rabies and Registration of Dogs” was presented to Parliament today by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government.

The proposed legislation aims to eliminate rabies in Sri Lanka through the vaccination of dogs, measures to control the dog population, and the regulation of dog breeding for commercial purposes. It also provides for the registration of dogs across the country.

The Bill further proposes the establishment of a National Rabies Elimination Advisory Committee and Regional Rabies Elimination Advisory Committees to support and oversee efforts to eradicate rabies.

If enacted, the legislation will repeal the Rabies Ordinance (Chapter 476) and the Dog Registration Ordinance (Chapter 477), replacing the existing legal framework with new provisions for rabies control and dog registration.
