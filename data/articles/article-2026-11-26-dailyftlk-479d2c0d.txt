Islandwide Scientific strengthens leadership team to expand scientific and research solutions in Sri Lanka

New management appointments reinforce company’s focus on service excellence, technical expertise and future growth

Islandwide Scientific Ltd., a leading importer, distributor and indenting agent for scientific, analytical and laboratory instruments in Sri Lanka, is continuing to expand its product and service offerings for the country’s science, research and industrial sectors under its newly appointed leadership team.

Established in 1991, the company has built a strong presence in Sri Lanka’s scientific instrumentation industry, serving universities, research institutions, Government laboratories, industrial quality control facilities and environmental monitoring agencies. Its strengthened leadership team will focus on building on this foundation, expanding access to advanced scientific technologies and enhancing the technical support and services available to customers.

Mangala Nanayakkara has been appointed Managing Director of Islandwide Scientific, bringing extensive financial and operational management experience to the role. A Fellow Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka, she previously served for 20 years as Director of Finance and Operations at the company. She also brings strategic leadership experience gained as a former Executive Director of one of Sri Lanka’s leading business groups.

Commenting on her appointment, Nanayakkara said, “Islandwide Scientific has built a strong reputation through its technical expertise, international partnerships and commitment to customer service. Our priority is to continue strengthening these capabilities while responding to the changing needs of Sri Lanka’s scientific and research community. We remain committed to providing our customers with reliable solutions and specialised technical support to advance their work.”

Supporting the company’s future direction, Thesath Nanayakkara and Yasas Nanayakkara have been appointed Advisory Board Members, while Dhammika Silva who brings 30 years of experience in strategic operations management, has been appointed General Manager.

Thesath Nanayakkara, who oversees Technology and Data Innovation on the Advisory Board, holds a PhD in Computational and Applied Mathematics from the University of Pittsburgh, USA, and is also a Mathematics and Industrial Statistics graduate of the University of Colombo, Sri Lanka. His professional experience includes serving as a Quantitative Strategist and currently as a Machine Learning Scientist at leading US multi-national organisations, bringing expertise in machine learning, quantitative research and data analytics.

Yasas Nanayakkara brings 15 years of experience in Sales and Marketing. He is a Chartered Marketer (CIM, UK) and holds a Master of Business Administration from the University of Southern Queensland, Australia.

The team brings expertise spanning technology and data science, sales and marketing, operations and scientific instrumentation.

Under its new leadership, Islandwide Scientific will continue to develop its portfolio of internationally sourced scientific and analytical equipment while expanding its service capabilities. These include professional installation and training, rapid turnaround repairs, precision field calibration, and preventive maintenance services, supported by an experienced technical team and a dedicated engineering workshop at its Colombo head office. The company’s extensive network of international manufacturers also enables it to bring specialised instrumentation and technical expertise to Sri Lankan laboratories and industrial facilities.
