CAL to acquire 51% of Hong Kong fund manager AFC

Capital Alliance Holdings PLC (CAL) has signed an agreement to acquire a controlling 51 percent stake in Hong Kong-based Asia Frontier Capital Ltd (AFC), extending its fund management business into additional frontier markets.

The proposed acquisition, through wholly- owned Singapore subsidiary CAL Global Pte. Ltd, remains subject to regulatory approvals, including clearance from Hong Kong’s Securities and Futures Commission. The announcement did not disclose the purchase price or an expected completion date.

AFC manages more than US $ 125 million for investors across Europe, Asia and North America. Its funds include the multi-country AFC Asia Frontier Fund and dedicated strategies investing in Vietnam, Iraq and Uzbekistan.

The transaction would connect CAL’s operations in Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and Singapore, with AFC’s Hong Kong investment platform and teams in Vietnam, Uzbekistan and Iraq. It would also broaden CAL’s access to international investors and expand its investment offering beyond its existing markets.

“Frontier markets offer some of the most compelling growth stories in the world; yet, they remain hard for international investors to reach,” CAL Group CEO Kanishke Mannakkara said.

“It also takes us into three more frontier markets, giving us a platform to build a deeper presence there over time and into Hong Kong, one of the world’s most important financial hubs.”

Founded in 2013 by Thomas Hugger, AFC has also been a long-standing investor in Sri Lankan and Bangladeshi equities.

AFC founder and CEO Hugger said the partnership would preserve the firm’s investment approach and decision-making independence while strengthening its research, technology, compliance and operating capabilities.

“Our investors will continue to benefit from the same approach to managing their money, with the same team, investment process and independence in our decisions,” he said.

Hugger said CAL would bring the research and data capabilities, frontier fixed-income expertise and relationships with the investors across South Asia.

Following completion, the companies intend to explore expanding AFC’s product range to include frontier fixed-income investments and additional country-specific strategies, subject to applicable regulatory requirements.

CAL, established in 2000 and listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange, provides investment banking, asset and wealth management, fixed-income, stockbroking and research services. It manages assets exceeding US $ 1.2 billion across Sri Lanka and Bangladesh.
