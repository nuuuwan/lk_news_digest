Govt briefs Chief Prelates on judicial reform programme

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Responding to reports of a letter sent to the President opposing the proposed constitutional amendment to extend the retirement age of judges, Cabinet Spokesman Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said that the Government has briefed the Chief Prelates of three Nikayas on its proposed judicial reform programme.

Speaking at the Cabinet media briefing, Dr. Jayatissa said he and the Minister of Justice had met the Chief Prelates of the Asgiriya, Malwathu and Ramanna Nikayas collectively, followed by a separate discussion with the Chief Prelate of the Amarapura Nikaya.

He said the discussions were not limited to the proposed change to the retirement age of judges, but covered the Government’s broader judicial reform programme.

According to Dr. Jayatissa, the reforms are aimed at addressing the backlog of cases, streamlining judicial processes and improving the overall efficiency of the justice system.

The clarification comes amid reports that the Chief Prelates had expressed opposition to the proposed constitutional amendment concerning the retirement age of judges.
