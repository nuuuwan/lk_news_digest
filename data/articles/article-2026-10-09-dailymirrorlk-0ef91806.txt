Sri Lankan man surprises Russian tourists with fluent Russian

Colombo, Oct. 9 (Daily Mirror) - A Sri Lankan man has attracted attention online after surprising a group of Russian tourists with his fluent Russian language skills during a chance encounter in Sri Lanka.

In a video shared by Russia Today (RT), the man is seen becoming visibly excited after learning that the visitors were from Russia before engaging them in an enthusiastic conversation in their native language.

He told the tourists that he had studied Russian about 10 years ago and demonstrated his knowledge by naming several Russian cities, including Moscow and Murmansk.

The tourists appeared impressed by his language skills, while the video of the encounter has since circulated online, drawing attention to the man’s ability to speak Russian.
