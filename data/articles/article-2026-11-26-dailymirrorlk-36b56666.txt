Third phase of public sector salary hike to take effect in January 2027 – PM

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister and Education Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya has assured teacher and principal trade unions that the third phase of the public sector salary increase will be implemented in January 2027.

She gave the assurance during a meeting with representatives of the Teacher-Principal Trade Union Alliance at the Ministry of Education in Isurupaya on November 25.

Discussions focused on salary anomalies in the teacher and principal services, allowances and measures to improve the professional standards of teachers and principals.

The Prime Minister said the Government had taken steps to establish an Education Professional Council to address salary discrepancies and improve the quality and professionalism of the education service.

She said the draft legislation required to establish the council had already been sent to the Legal Draftsman's Department for preparation.

Dr. Amarasuriya noted that although the 1994 Teachers' Service Constitution introduced salary improvements, essential reforms to strengthen the professionalism and quality of the teaching service had not been implemented over the past three decades.

She also said five conditions relating to the salary structure proposed in the Subodhini Report had yet to be fulfilled.

Meanwhile, a Salary and Pension Commission had been appointed to address salary and pension anomalies across the public service, including those affecting teachers and principals. Trade unions have also been given an opportunity to submit their proposals to the commission, she said.

The Prime Minister further said the Government had allocated Rs. 749 billion for education in this year's Budget, describing it as the highest allocation made for the sector so far.

She also said the Government was considering the possibility of introducing a telephone allowance for principals.

Deputy Education Minister Dr. Madura Seneviratne, Deputy Labour Minister Mahinda Jayasinghe, ministry officials and trade union representatives, including Ven. Yalwela Pannasekara Thera and Joseph Stalin, attended the meeting.
