Over Rs. 408 million earned from expressway traffic in one week

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Road Development Authority (RDA) has generated more than Rs. 408 million in revenue from vehicles using Sri Lanka’s expressway network during the past week.

According to the RDA, a total of 1,095,895 vehicles travelled on the Southern Expressway, Colombo Outer Circular Highway, Colombo–Katunayake Expressway and Central Expressway between August 24 and 30.
