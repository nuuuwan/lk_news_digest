Homagama Crimes OIC arrested over suspect’s death

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The former Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of the Crimes Division at the Homagama Police Station was arrested this evening by officers of the Western Province South Divisional Crimes Division in connection with the death of a suspect while in police custody.

The arrested officer, aged 32, is a resident of Bellapitiya, Horana. He is scheduled to be produced before the Homagama Magistrate’s Court tomorrow.

Investigations into the incident are being conducted by the Western Province South Divisional Crimes Division.

The Superintendent of Police in charge of the Homagama District suspended three police officers earlier in connection with the incident.

The suspended officers include two Sub-Inspectors who served as the OIC of the Crimes Division and the OIC of the Anti-Corruption/Raids Unit at the Homagama Police Station, respectively, and a Police Constable who was on duty at the time of the incident.
