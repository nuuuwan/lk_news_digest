Chinese Ambassador and Pathfinder Founder discuss broadening relations

Chinese Ambassador Wei Huaxiang (third from left) with Pathfinder Foundation Founder Milinda Moragoda. At the meeting, the Chinese Embassy was also represented by Counsellor Mei Jing and Second Secretary Jin Enze. The Pathfinder Foundation was represented by its Chairman, Ambassador (Retd.) Bernard Goonetilleke; Director, Ambassador (Retd.) Ahmed Jawad, and Consultant, Strategic Communications G.S. Godakanda

Chinese Ambassador Wei Huaxiang (right) with Pathfinder Foundation Founder Milinda Moragoda

The Ambassador of the People’s Republic of China to Sri Lanka, Wei Huaxiang, met the Pathfinder Foundation Founder Milinda Moragoda recently, to exchange views on how to further strengthen cooperation between China and the Pathfinder Foundation.

The Pathfinder Foundation’s engagement with China dates back many years. A key milestone in this relationship was the establishment of the China-Sri Lanka Cooperation Studies Centre (CSLCSC) in October 2015.

Over the years, this partnership has evolved through numerous initiatives, including international conferences, academic and scholarly exchanges, joint publications and collaborative research projects. At present the Pathfinder Foundation maintains 10 active Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with leading Chinese think tanks, universities, research institutions and other organisations.

During the meeting, Moragoda briefed Ambassador Wei on the work the Pathfinder Foundation had undertaken in collaboration with numerous Chinese institutions, think tanks, educational and other institutions. Ambassador Wei expressed interest in upgrading Sri Lankan agricultural practices with Chinese know-how, concluding a China-Sri Lanka free trade agreement to facilitate the introduction of new technology and facilitate trade, providing know-how on artificial intelligence, and addressing human-elephant conflict using experience gained from Yunnan Province, among other areas.

Moragoda said the Pathfinder Foundation was ready to cooperate with the Chinese Embassy to achieve its goals and agreed to continue discussions with the Embassy to determine how the Foundation could work jointly with it. He invited Ambassador Wei to grace the Pathfinder Ambassador’s Roundtable, a regular feature that provides a forum for foreign heads of mission to share their experiences and strategies to further improve bilateral relations.

Moragoda presented the Chinese Ambassador with a copy of the report on ‘Colombo Maritime Dialogue’ concluded in May 2026 and a selection of Pathfinder publications, including several translations.

The year 2026 also saw several exchanges between the Pathfinder Foundation and leading institutions in China, further broadening the scope of engagement.

Looking ahead, 2027 is expected to see further expansion of this relationship, including trilateral cooperation initiatives that bring together the Pathfinder Foundation, Chinese institutions and partners from third countries.
