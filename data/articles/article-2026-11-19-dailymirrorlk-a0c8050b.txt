Trump, Putin and Xi to hold historic meeting in China

The Telegraph - Xi Jinping will soon hold talks with Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in an unprecedented trilateral meeting on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (Apec) summit in China.

The three leaders are scheduled to meet at 2.30pm local time (6.30am GMT), with discussions likely to focus on the prospects for an nuclear arms-control pact. Although, a full deal to limit weapons appears unlikely.

China, Russia and the US possess the largest arsenals of nuclear weapons in the world.

Mr Trump has already described the trilateral meeting as a “win”, pointing to the lavish welcome and ceremonial fanfare he received upon arriving in China.

“This is a great president, one of the great presidents of China, and he’s never gone to the airport to meet somebody,” Mr Trump said in an interview with Fox News on Thursday, when asked what he hoped to achieve from the meeting.

He added: “They’re going hot and heavy on building nuclear weapons. I’d love ​them not to do it.”

The meeting is also expected to address global conflicts, including the war in Ukraine, which has intensified in recent weeks amid attacks on energy infrastructure and a key Crimean bridge, which was struck again on Wednesday night.

During a speech on Thursday, Putin said Russia was an integral part of the Asia-Pacific region’s food ⁠and energy security.

Meanwhile, Mr Trump told the audience the US has been a Pacific power for hundreds of years and touted the strength of its economy.

The US president is currently holding bilateral talks with Mr Xi. Earlier, Mr Trump held a closed door meeting with Putin, which was not on the official agenda.
