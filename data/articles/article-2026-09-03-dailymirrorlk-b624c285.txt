Vidullanka completes Rs.1.8bn Ranna solar project

Vidullanka PLC has completed the construction of its 10-megawatt (MW) Ranna Solar Power Project, following an investment of Rs.1.8 billion, with the facility now awaiting grid energisation before commencing commercial operations.

The listed renewable energy producer said the plant is fully ready for commercial operation, according to a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange, dated September 1.

The project is owned and developed by DPV Solar Energy (Pvt.) Ltd, a joint venture between Vidullanka and David Peiris Group. Vidullanka holds a 50 percent equity interest in the venture.

Commercial generation, however, is dependent on the energisation of a newly constructed grid interconnection line by Electricity Distribution (Pvt.) Ltd (EDL), the surviving distribution entity under Sri Lanka’s unbundled electricity utility framework.

Vidullanka said the final testing and commissioning would be carried out once EDL energises the interconnection line, after which the plant would begin supplying electricity to the national grid.

Once commissioned, the Ranna project will add 10 MW of renewable energy capacity to the national grid while expanding Vidullanka’s domestic solar generation portfolio.
