Renovated Bambalapitiya Railway Station opens for public

Public infrastructure project undertaken under the Clean Sri Lanka programme in coordination with the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development, with sponsorship from the Dawoodi Bohras of Sri Lanka.

The renovated and upgraded Bambalapitiya Railway Station was formally opened for public use on 10 September, following the completion of wide-ranging improvements aimed at providing commuters with a cleaner, safer and more comfortable station environment.

The opening took place at the Bambalapitiya Railway Station in the presence of Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, Minister of Transport and Highways Bimal Ratnayake, senior officials of Sri Lanka Railways and other government institutions, together with representatives of the Dawoodi Bohra community.

The project was undertaken under the government’s Clean Sri Lanka programme, in coordination with the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development and Sri Lanka Railways, with the renovation sponsored by the Dawoodi Bohras of Sri Lanka.

Prime Minister Amarasuriya said the initiative reflected the importance of shared responsibility in improving and maintaining public spaces. “A clean Sri Lanka is built through shared responsibility, where communities contribute, the Government facilitates and public institutions maintain with pride. This project is a commendable example of civic partnership and responsible citizenship.”

The initiative also complements the government’s Clean Sri Lanka and Dream Destination programmes, which place emphasis on improving public spaces, strengthening civic responsibility and creating cleaner, safer and more welcoming environments for residents, commuters and visitors. A representative of the Dawoodi Bohra community said the project reflected the community’s longstanding commitment to Sri Lanka and to public service.

“Sri Lanka has been home to the Dawoodi Bohra community for nearly two centuries, and contributing to the wellbeing of the country we call home is central to our values. We are grateful to the Government, Sri Lanka Railways and all authorities whose support made this project possible.”

The Dawoodi Bohra community extended its appreciation to the Prime Minister, the Minister of Transport and Highways, Sri Lanka Railways, the Colombo Municipal Council, law enforcement authorities and other government and civic institutions whose guidance, coordination and support contributed to the successful completion of the project. The restoration forms part of the community’s continuing commitment to supporting civic and social initiatives that contribute positively to Sri Lanka and the wellbeing of the wider public.
