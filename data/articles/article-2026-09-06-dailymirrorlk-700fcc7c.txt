President pledges swift approval of 22nd Amendment

Colombo, Sep. 6 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake today said the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution would be approved by Parliament soon after the Supreme Court communicates its determination to the Speaker.

Addressing a public rally in Bulathsinghala, the President said the proposed constitutional amendment was essential to strengthen measures against corruption.

He said the Government had taken several steps to expedite the judicial process, including recruiting additional personnel to the judicial service, speeding up the issuance of reports by the Government Analyst’s Department, and establishing more High Courts to clear the backlog of pending cases.

The President noted that some cases had remained unresolved for 15 to 20 years, resulting in daily arrests as authorities work to address the long-standing backlog.

“Some claim that we are carrying out political revenge. If there had been no backlog of cases, there would not be this many arrests,” he said.
