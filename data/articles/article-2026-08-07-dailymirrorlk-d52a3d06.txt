MP Archchuna shares salary sheet, claims increase in MPs’ fuel allowance

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Independent Parliamentarian Ramanathan Archchuna has shared his monthly salary sheet on his official Facebook page, highlighting what he claimed was an increase in the allowances received by Members of Parliament.

According to the document shared by Archchuna, his fuel allowance has increased from Rs. 179,000 to Rs. 244,000.

In a Facebook post accompanying the salary sheet, Archchuna questioned the public over the alleged increase and claimed that a similar amount multiplied by 159 MPs was being paid into accounts in Pelawatte.

“Keep clapping and suffocate those who tell the truth,” he said, while also referring to his remarks in Parliament.

Archchuna had told Parliament yesterday that the monthly salaries of MPs had been increased by Rs. 60,000.

He claimed that the new monthly salary of an MP was Rs. 484,000.
