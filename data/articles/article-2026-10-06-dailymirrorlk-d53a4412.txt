Financial literacy rises to 58%: Prime Minister

Sri Lanka’s financial literacy rate has increased from 35 percent to 58 percent, due to the positive measures taken by the government, Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya said yesterday.

She made these remarks while attending the 40th anniversary celebrations of the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE), at the World Trade Centre in Colombo, as the chief guest.

Dr. Amarasuriya said the government aimed to ensure that knowledge of money and financial system was not limited to a select group but was accessible to all citizens.

“Our objective is to empower every citizen with the awareness needed to manage their earnings effectively, save properly, invest with an understanding of risks and avoid falling victim to fraudulent financial schemes,” she said.

She said the government was particularly focusing on schoolchildren and was taking steps to introduce Entrepreneurship and Financial Literacy as a new subject area for Grades 6 to 9, under the revised school curriculum.

She also highlighted initiatives such as the Stock Market Game and school-based capital market clubs would help children and young people gain practical knowledge of entrepreneurship and investment from an early age.

Dr. Amarasuriya said the government was committed to strengthening the country’s capital market and creating new investment opportunities for the younger generation, moving beyond traditional savings methods. She urged the young people to think differently and create opportunities rather than wait for them.

“Let us foster a culture of sound financial literacy within society, not merely to secure marks in an examination but to benefit throughout one’s entire life,” she said.

The event also featured the launch of the Stock Market Game programme, while a commemorative cover and stamp marking the CSE’s 40th anniversary were unveiled. Dr. Amarasuriya also rang the bell to commence market trading.

CSE Chief Executive Officer Rajeeva Bandaranaike and the officials led by Securities and Exchange Commission Chairman Senior Professor D.B.P.H. Dissabandara were among those present.
