PickMe Explore activates Momo naming initiative

PickMe CEO Jiffry Zulfer presents mountain bike to winner Avandha Tamari

More than 500 suggestions submitted digitally as Dehiwala Zoo names baby Nile hippopotamus

PickMe Explore brought families closer to one of Colombo’s newest attractions by supporting the public naming initiative for the Dehiwala National Zoological Gardens’ baby Nile hippopotamus, now officially named Momo, another step towards unifying the experiences through Colombo Connect.

Evolved from PickMe Events, PickMe Explore is the company’s broader platform for discovering attractions, activities and experiences. Colombo Connect operates within Explore as its city-focused initiative, linking people with places and experiences across the capital

The hippo naming programme demonstrated how that connection can extend beyond discovering or visiting an attraction to actively participating in its story.

Following an invitation by the National Zoological Gardens Department for the public to propose names, PickMe created a dedicated activity within Explore. The users could submit a suggested name and their contact details through the app, enabling the entries to be collected and organised digitally before being handed over to the department for consideration.

More than 500 suggestions were submitted through PickMe Explore. Together with the entries received directly by the zoo, nearly 1,000 suggestions from children were considered in the overall competition, with the department selecting the winning name.

Young Avandha Tamari, who first proposed Momo, was recognised at a ceremony held at the Zoo on October 1 to mark the World Children’s Day. Environment Deputy Minister Anton Jayakody presented the awards, while PickMe sponsored a mountain bicycle for the winner.

PickMe CEO Jiffry Zulfer said, “We used the reach and capability of PickMe Explore to support a public initiative and help the zoo engage with its visitors in a new way.”
