2028, 2034, 2036 and 2037 maturities experience buying interest

The secondary market yield curve recorded buying interest, particularly in the 2028, 2034, 2036, and 2037 maturities, while trading volumes and overall market activity remained moderate throughout the session.

Amongst the traded maturities, in the 2028 segment, 15.02.2028, 15.03.2028, 01.05.2028, 01.09.2028, 15.10.2028 and 15.12.2028, witnessed active trading, with maturities trading from 10.05 percent to 10.30 percent.

Further along the curve, the 15.10.2029 and 15.12.2029 maturities traded at 10.55 percent to 10.56 percent, while the 15.05.2030 and 01.08.2030 maturities changed hands in the range of 10.70 percent to 10.75 percent.

The 01.02.2031 maturity traded at 10.95 percent. Meanwhile, the 15.01.2033 maturity traded in the range of 11.30 percent to 11.36 percent, while the 15.10.2034 maturity traded between the range of 11.70 percent to11.80 percent. At the longer end of the curve, both 15.08.2036 and 01.07.2037 maturities traded at 12.00 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.17/US dollar, compared to Rs.331.23/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.278.51 billion, from Rs.300.25 billion recorded previously.
