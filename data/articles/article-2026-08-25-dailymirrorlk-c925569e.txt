Mangrove restoration begins in Araly

Mangrove restoration activities have commenced in Araly in Jaffna under Save a Life’s Mangrove Guardians Project, bringing together community members and volunteers to restore and protect valuable Avicennia mangrove ecosystems.

The initiative is being implemented with the support of ChildFund Sri Lanka and in collaboration with the Department of Forest Conservation. The programme focuses on restoring degraded coastal habitats, protecting existing mangrove cover and encouraging greater community involvement in conservation efforts.

Through the initiative, local communities and volunteers are taking part in mangrove restoration activities aimed at strengthening coastal ecosystems and improving their ability to withstand environmental threats.

The project also seeks to promote long-term community stewardship of mangrove habitats while contributing to the development of more environmentally resilient coastal communities.
