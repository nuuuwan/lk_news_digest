Construction of apartment complexes for plantation community considered: PM

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Construction of apartment complexes for the plantation community is being considered, Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya told Parliament today.

Responding to a question raised by Mano Ganesan, the Prime Minister said this is being considered as no constructions can be undertaken on some lands on the hillsides as they are exposed to landslides.
