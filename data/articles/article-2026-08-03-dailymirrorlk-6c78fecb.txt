Another prison riot

Chaos at the Mahara prison on Saturday evening raises new questions regarding the issue of prison overcrowding. While there are no reports regarding the reasons as to why the unrest occurred, family members of inmates claim that they weren’t allowed to visit their inmates that evening. They were seen blaming authorities for forcibly keeping their inmates. But this is similar to what happened at the Negombo prison about a month ago. During the previous incident, Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara said that he would accept the responsibility. But accepting responsibility alone doesn’t solve this issue. Just after several inmates were transferred to other prisons, one of them reportedly committed suicide. This raises questions regarding several unresolved issues that have been impacting the country’s prison system for several years. In this backdrop, it is imperative to revisit remanding suspects on charges of possessing drugs and other minor crimes. At least 21 inmates and 8 prison officers  succumbed to injuries during the Negombo prison riot. According to criminology experts there are more remand prisoners than convicted prisoners in our prisons. On the other hand many of our prisons were built during the British period. Therefore,  the government should immediately look at resolving this crisis before it blows out of proportion.
