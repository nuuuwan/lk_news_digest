Liquor outlets closed today

Colombo, Oct. 3 (Daily Mirror) - All wine stores and liquor sales outlets across Sri Lanka will remain closed today (October 3) in observance of World Temperance Day, the Department of Excise announced.

The closure is being implemented as part of the annual observance of World Temperance Day, which is held on October 3 to raise awareness of the temperance movement and promote moderation or abstinence from alcoholic beverages.

Accordingly, liquor sales will not be permitted at licensed outlets across the country today.
