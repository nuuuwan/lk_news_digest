Police curfew imposed around Mahara Prison

Ragama, August 01 (Daily Mirror) - .A police curfew has been imposed with immediate effect until further notice across three Grama Niladhari divisions surrounding the Mahara Prison within the Ragama Police division to maintain peace and order. The affected areas include 246/Kendaliyaddapaluwa – West, 246/C Kendaliyaddapaluwa – North, and 181/G Dambuwa.

The decision follows a clash that broke out among inmates inside the Mahara Prison premises this afternoon. Seven injured prisoners were subsequently rushed to the Ragama Hospital, where one inmate succumbed to his injuries. The remaining six individuals are currently undergoing treatment at the hospital.

Following the outbreak of unrest inside the facility, relatives of the inmates and local residents began gathering in the surrounding area. Police said that the police curfew was enforced in the three adjacent divisions to prevent any further escalation and bring the surrounding situation under control.
