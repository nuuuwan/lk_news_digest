Week ends in green

Indices closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Hatton National Bank and Commercial Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.1 Bn. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Lanka Milk Foods, Muller & Phipps and John Keells Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in HNB Finance, Commercial Credit & Finance and ACL Cables whilst retail interest was noted in R I L Property, Greentech Energy and Janashakthi Limited. Furthermore, foreigners closed as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 2.4% and 1.6% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 2.00 Bn.

Diversified Financials sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 0.45%. The share price of John Keells Holdings moved up by Rs 0.10 (0.55%) to close at Rs 18.40.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Credit & Finance) whilst the sector index increased by 0.53%. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance recorded a gain of Rs 0.20 (0.21%) to close at Rs 93.50.

Lanka Milk Foods, HNB Finance and Muller & Phipps were also included amongst the top turnover contributors.The share price of Lanka Milk Foods decreased by Rs 0.80 (0.96%) to close at Rs 82.20. The share price of HNB Finance lost Rs 0.30 (4.05%) to close at Rs 7.10. The share price of Muller & Phipps appreciated by Rs 0.10 (4.76%) to close at Rs 2.20.
