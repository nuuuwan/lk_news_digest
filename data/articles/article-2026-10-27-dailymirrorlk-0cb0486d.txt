Champika to make serious  disclosures on coal purchases on November 12

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Former Minister Patali Champika Ranawaka said today that he will make some disclosures on the fraudulent deals which President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and two Ministers, namely Kumara Jayakody and Anura Karunatilaka, have engaged in during the coal purchases and on a campaign he is carrying out with several journalists and YouTubers.

"I will reveal the connections these persons in the government have with some foreign sources," Ranawaka told during a media briefing.
