Sri Lanka rupee closes at 332.40/50 to US dollar spot, bond yields edge up

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s rupee closed at 332.40/50 to the US dollar in the spot market on Tuesday, weaker from 332.25/35 the previous day, while bond yields closed slightly higher, dealers said.

A bond maturing on 15.09.2029 closed flat at 11.08/15 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.08.2030 closed at 11.55/60 percent, up from 11.45/55 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.02.2031 closed at 11.75/80 percent, up from 11.70/75 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.12.2031 closed at 11.85/95 percent, up from 11.80/90 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.01.2033 closed at 12.15/30 percent, up from 12.15/25 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2034 closed at 12.5/60 percent, up from 12.45/55 percent. (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
