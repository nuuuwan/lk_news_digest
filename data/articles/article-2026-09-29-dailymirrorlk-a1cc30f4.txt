Namal Rajapaksa further remanded over Air Bus deal

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, who was arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption in connection with an investigation into the Airbus deal, was ordered to be further remanded till October 13 by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama today.

The Court was informed that investigations into the incident had been completed and that the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) and the CIABOC would take steps to file indictments against the suspect.
