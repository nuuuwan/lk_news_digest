Oktoberfest at The Edge returns to Waters Edge

To feature Gypsies, Misty, D Major and an unlimited Bavarian feast

Three nights from October 16 to 18, powered by People’s Bank, after last year’s edition sold out well in advance

Waters Edge will bring Oktoberfest at The Edge back from October 16 to 18, reviving the celebration that sold out all three nights well in advance last year and returning with a bigger edition built around an unlimited Bavarian buffet and three of the country’s most in-demand live bands.

Powered by People’s Bank, Oktoberfest at The Edge 2026 will unfold at the Pavilion at The Edge, on the banks of the Diyawanna in Battaramulla, from 6:00 p.m. onwards on each of the three evenings, running from Friday through Sunday.

Oktoberfest traces its roots to Munich in 1810, when the city turned out to celebrate the wedding of Bavaria’s Crown Prince Ludwig and it still opens each year with the mayor tapping the first keg to the cry of “O’zapft is!”, which translates to “It is tapped!”.

The festival now draws around six million visitors a year, yet its appeal continues to rest on what the Bavarians call Gemütlichkeit, the easy warmth of strangers sharing long wooden benches, raising their steins to a chorus of “Prost!” and swaying arm in arm to a brass band well into the night.

The Bavarian table is built for sharing and for lingering, anchored by Hendl, the spit-roasted chicken with its crisp, golden skin and Haxn, the slow-roasted pork knuckle prized for its crackling. Alongside them come Weisswurst, the mild white sausage traditionally eaten with sweet mustard and Brezn, the oversized soft pretzel, with Sauerkraut to cut through the richness and a warm Apfelstrudel to finish.

It is this tradition that the unlimited Bavarian buffet at Waters Edge draws on and it remains the feature that distinguishes the event from other Oktoberfest celebrations in the city. With the feast built into the pass, guests are free to go back for a second helping or a third, for as long as the music plays.

In Munich’s tents, it is the brass bands that keep the crowd on its feet and at Waters Edge that task falls to three of the country’s best-loved acts. Misty will open the celebrations on Friday, October 16, before D Major takes the stage on Saturday, October 17. Gypsies, a household name in Sri Lankan music for more than five decades, will bring the weekend to a high-energy finale on Sunday, October 18, while DJ Neeon, a familiar face from last year’s edition, will be behind the console on all three nights.

Waters Edge has been staging Oktoberfest celebrations for close to a decade, including a four-day edition in 2017, for which a Bavarian band was flown in and has had Bavarian cuisine in its repertoire since the Bavarian Garden opened on its Boardwalk in 2016.

Last year’s three-night run played to full houses throughout, securing the event’s place among the most anticipated dates in Colombo’s social calendar.

Presented under the supervision of Waters Edge Chairman Chathura Krishantha Perera and General Manager Iyantha Abeysinghe, the 2026 celebration is aimed at once again setting the benchmark for Oktoberfest celebrations in Colombo.

“Oktoberfest is at its best when people pull up a bench, share a table with those they have just met and stay longer than they planned. That is the feeling our team has worked to recreate this year, from the kitchen to the stage,” Abeysinghe said.
