Second Metro Bus Depot begins operations

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s second metro bus depot, constructed in Kadawatha, commenced operations today as part of efforts to modernise and expand the country’s public transport system.

The facility is being operated under the Lanka Metro Transit (Private) Company, which was established to introduce a more modern and efficient approach to public transport services.

According to the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development, the Kadawatha depot forms part of a broader plan to expand metro bus services across the Western Province.

Under the expansion programme, additional running yards are planned in Ratmalana, while central workstations are expected to be established in Talangama and Homagama.

The initiative is aimed at strengthening the infrastructure required to support an expanded metro bus network and improve public transport services in the Western Province.
