LOLC Finance-Continental Cars promote Volkswagen vehicles across Sri Lanka

LOLC Finance Chief Operating Officer Montini Warnakula and Continental Cars Deputy Chairman Charu Senavirathna with their teams during MoU handover ceremony

LOLC Finance PLC has entered into a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Continental Cars (Pvt.) Ltd to jointly promote the Volkswagen vehicles across Sri Lanka, creating enhanced opportunities for the customers seeking attractive and flexible vehicle financing solutions.

This islandwide initiative brings together the financial strength and extensive customer reach of LOLC Finance, with the globally recognised Volkswagen brand represented in Sri Lanka by Continental Cars.

Under the partnership, LOLC Finance will offer a range of value-added financial benefits to the customers purchasing the Volkswagen vehicles. These include discounted insurance premiums through approved insurance partners, flexible and customised repayment plans and operating lease facilities designed for corporate entities to cater to diverse customer requirements.

The eligible customers will receive a complimentary credit card facility, subject to applicable eligibility criteria.

As part of the promotional initiative, Continental Cars will offer exclusive discounts of Rs.100,000 on selected Volkswagen models. The customers purchasing either the Volkswagen Taigun or Virtus models will be eligible to receive a Rs.100,000 discount, further enhancing the value offered under the partnership.

LOLC Finance Chief Operating Officer Montini Warnakula said, “This partnership with Continental Cars reflects our commitment to providing the customers with greater choice, convenience and value. By combining our flexible financing solutions with the strength of the Volkswagen brand, we are creating an attractive proposition for the customers while expanding opportunities within Sri Lanka’s automotive and financial services sectors.”

To enhance customer engagement and accessibility, Continental Cars will arrange displays of Volkswagen vehicles on selected LOLC Finance branch premises, providing the prospective customers with convenient opportunities to experience the vehicles, learn more about the available models and explore financing solutions under the partnership.

Continental Cars Deputy Chairman Charu Senavirathna added, “Volkswagen has long been recognised for its distinctive blend of German engineering, sophisticated design, performance and premium quality. Our partnership with LOLC Finance further strengthens the value proposition for the customers by making the Volkswagen experience more accessible through attractive financing solutions and exclusive benefits. We believe this collaboration will enable more customers across Sri Lanka to experience the prestige and confidence associated with owning a Volkswagen, while enjoying greater convenience and value throughout their purchase journey.”

The collaboration represents a strategic step towards delivering greater value and convenience to the customers while strengthening ties between Sri Lanka’s financial services and automotive sectors.
