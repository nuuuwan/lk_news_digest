Severe lightning warning issued for several provinces

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department has issued a warning that thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are likely to occur at several places in the North Central, North Western, Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Uva and Southern provinces, as well as in the Ampara, Batticaloa and Mannar districts.

The department said temporary localized strong winds may also occur during thundershowers and urged the public to take adequate precautions to minimise damage and ensure safety.

People have been advised to seek shelter indoors and avoid standing under trees during thunderstorms. The public is also requested to avoid open areas such as paddy fields, tea plantations and water bodies.

The department further warned against using wired telephones and electrical appliances connected to power sources during thunderstorms.

People are also advised to avoid travelling in open vehicles such as bicycles, tractors and boats, and to remain alert for fallen trees and downed power lines.

In case of emergencies, the public is urged to contact local disaster management authorities.
