Wild Elephants push down garden wall to eat jak

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Three wild elephants which had entered Sigiriya town in the early hours of last morning had pushed down a parapet wall of a home garden to eat jak fruit from a tree.

The owner said a herd of three wild elephants had entered the garden at about 3.30am after pushing down the 200ft long garden wall and eaten jak fruits.

They said they informed the wildlife officers on the incident but to no avail. They pointed out that their house is in close proximity to the Wildlife Department office, but they came out with the excused that they were not in a position to visit the scene for want of a vehicle.

Residents of Sigriya area said they have been facing the threat of wild elephants since long and that they are living in constant fear. They pointed out that groups of tourists visiting Sigiriya area and frequenting the roads during day and night are at the mercy of the marauding jumbos.
