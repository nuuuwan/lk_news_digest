Aendra joins Renuka City Hotel Board

Renuka City Hotel PLC has appointed Aendra Bandaranaike to its Board as an Independent Non-Executive Director.

Bandaranaike is a senior corporate executive with over 25 years of experience in finance, management, marketing, and business development.

He began his career at Sherman Sons Ltd. and subsequently joined Delmege Forsyth and Company Ltd. as Marketing Manager, where he was instrumental in introducing several specialist interior products to the Sri Lankan market. He currently serves as Deputy CEO of the Life Style Cluster at Delmege Forsyth and serves as a Non-Executive Director of Ceylinco General Insurance Limited.

Bandaranaike is an Associate of the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants, UK.
