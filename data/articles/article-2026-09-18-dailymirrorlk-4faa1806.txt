Sri Lanka’s shoppers experience elevated through AI

Elevating a shopper’s experience, during the process of buying clothes, Mirraa has introduced an AI-inspired virtual fit-on platform, digitalizing the process.

This all happens when a shopper walks up to a large screen on the shop floor, takes one photo, and within ten seconds sees themselves wearing any garment in the store's catalogue, at full height. No fitting room, no queue, no app to download.

They can also do the same on their own phone by scanning a QR code on the rack, making the whole experience only under a minute.

This AI virtual fit-on is also possible on ethnic wear — sarees, lehengas, kurtis, sherwanis and more — garments that virtually no other try-on tool in the world can render accurately. Most platforms are built only for western clothing.

Manoj Murali, a Director from Mirraa, said this is now being tested in over ten clothing stores in Colombo.

“Chennai-based the Chennai Silks became the first store to have this. Only this week, Fashion Bug and Nolimit also joined the list”, he said.

Started to roll out in Mumbai, Colombo has become only the second stop over for the trial of this theme.

Very soon, other clothing stores namely Zuzi, Kamsins would be incorporating into their stores.

“This becomes handy because certain high-quality fabrics cannot be fitted on and handled. In such instances, AI fit-on is the go-to. We believe this is a strong local technology story worth covering,”.

“Virtual try-on has changed retail elsewhere in the world. Sri Lanka is getting it now, not in five years,” he added.
