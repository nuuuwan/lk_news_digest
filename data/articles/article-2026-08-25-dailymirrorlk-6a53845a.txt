BOC Launches CFlex Corporate to Expand Digital Cash Management Services

Bank of Ceylon recently launched BOC CFlex Corporate, its comprehensive digital cash management solution, at a prestigious event held at Cinnamon Life, Colombo.

The launch was graced by Mr. Kavinda de Zoysa, Chairman of Bank of Ceylon together with Mr. Y A Jayathilaka, General Manager/Chief Executive Officer of Bank of Ceylon, members of the Board of Directors, Corporate and Executive Management, together with distinguished corporate customers and other invitees.

BOC CFlex Corporate is designed to empower large corporate, small and medium-sized enterprise customers to efficiently manage payments, collections, liquidity and other banking activities through a single, secure digital platform.

The introduction of CFlex Corporate marks another important milestone in Bank of Ceylon’s ongoing digital transformation journey, strengthening the Bank’s ability to deliver convenient, secure and technology-driven banking solutions tailored to the evolving needs of Sri Lanka’s business community.
