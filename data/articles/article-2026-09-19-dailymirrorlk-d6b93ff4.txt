Trump bans CNN, MSNBC and Politico from White House over ‘fake news’

Washington- U.S. President Donald Trump said he has banned CNN, MSNOW and Politico from the White House, accusing the media outlets of repeatedly publishing what he called “fake news.”

In a post on his official X account, Trump said the ban was effective immediately and accused the three outlets of reporting “fiction and lies” about his administration and the United States.

Trump referred to CNN as “Fake News CNN” and claimed that MSNOW had recently changed its name from MSNBC due to a lack of “viewership and credibility.”

He also accused Politico of receiving an $8 million subscription from the U.S. government under former President Joe Biden, describing the payment as “illegal and ridiculous” and alleging that it amounted to corruption.

“Media Outlets shouldn’t be able to constantly write or report FICTION and LIES when they’re covering the President of the United States, the Trump Administration, or the United States of America,” Trump said.

He added that other media outlets could also face similar action.

“Other Fake News Media Outlets to follow,” Trump said in the post, which he signed off as “President DONALD J. TRUMP.”
