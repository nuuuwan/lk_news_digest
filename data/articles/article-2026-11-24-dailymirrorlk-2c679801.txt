Devotees mark Il Poya Day

Buddhist devotees observe Il Full Moon Poya Day at the Hunupitiya Gangaramaya Temple in Colombo today (24). The day marks the conclusion of the annual Kathina robe-offering season, during which devotees offer robes to Buddhist monks following the Vas (rains retreat). It also commemorates several significant events in Buddhist history. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
