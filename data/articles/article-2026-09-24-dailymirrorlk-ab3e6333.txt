Revenue license system goes offline in eight provinces for four days

Colombo, Sep 24 (Daily Mirror) - The electronic Revenue License (eRL 2.0) system will be temporarily suspended in eight provinces from Friday, September 25, to Monday, September 28, 2026, due to a scheduled system upgrade.

The temporary suspension will affect the issuance of electronic revenue licenses in the Central, North Central, North Western, Southern, Eastern, Northern, Sabaragamuwa and Uva provinces.

Authorities said the upgrade is being carried out to improve the efficiency and enhance the quality of the electronic revenue licensing service provided to the public.

Following the completion of the system upgrade, the eRL 2.0 system is scheduled to resume operations on Tuesday, September 29, 2026, with normal services expected to continue thereafter.
