Free-roaming ponies raise accident concerns in Nuwara Eliya

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Residents of Nuwara Eliya have raised concerns over the risk of traffic accidents caused by ponies roaming freely on public roads while being used for tourism activities.

Dashboard-camera footage recorded from a vehicle travelling along a main road in Nuwara Eliya on September 13 showed an accident after a pony suddenly entered the roadway.

Residents said several similar incidents had been reported previously, with some resulting in serious damage to vehicles and injuries to people.

Although pony rides are a popular tourist activity in Nuwara Eliya, residents stressed the need for a proper regulatory mechanism to ensure such activities do not endanger motorists, pedestrians or other road users.

They said pony owners and handlers should be held responsible for keeping the animals under control, preventing them from straying onto public roads and ensuring that relevant traffic and safety regulations are followed.

Residents also called on the Nuwara Eliya Municipal Council and other relevant authorities to take urgent action to address the issue.

They proposed introducing a permanent system to regulate free-roaming ponies, allocating designated areas for tourism-related pony activities and bringing pony owners and operators under proper legal supervision.

They warned that immediate action was necessary to prevent a potentially serious accident involving a pony and road users.
