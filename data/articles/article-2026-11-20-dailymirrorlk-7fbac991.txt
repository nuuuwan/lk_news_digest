Sri Lanka welcomes two millionth tourist of 2026

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka welcomed its two-millionth tourist for 2026 today (20), marking the third consecutive year the country has surpassed the two-million tourist arrival milestone.

The milestone visitor, a Russian national accompanied by two family members, arrived at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake this morning.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism described the achievement as a significant milestone for the country's tourism industry, particularly amid numerous global challenges.

Following 2018, Sri Lanka crossed the two-million tourist arrival mark on December 26, 2024, November 17, 2025, and November 20, 2026.

The Ministry said the tourism industry's ability to maintain its growth despite multiple global crises reflects continued international confidence in Sri Lanka as a travel destination.

It also expressed confidence that tourist arrivals in 2026 would surpass the figures recorded in 2025, further strengthening the country's tourism sector.
