DMC prepares emergency response plan ahead of scholarship and A/L exams

Colombo, August 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Disaster Management Centre (DMC) has activated emergency preparedness measures to minimise disruptions to the upcoming Grade 5 Scholarship Examination and G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination due to possible adverse weather conditions.

The DMC said a special coordination desk will be set up at its operations room to monitor weather-related impacts on examination activities during August 6, 7 and 8.

The decision was taken following a special discussion held today (05) between officials of the Department of Meteorology, the DMC and the Examinations Department.

The Centre said disaster response teams and boats have been placed on standby to assist students and examination officials in flood-affected areas if required.

The DMC had previously deployed boats during last year’s G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination in November to transport students who were unable to reach examination centres due to floods in parts of the Galle and Matara districts.

The Grade 5 Scholarship Examination will be held on August 9, while the 2026 G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination will commence on August 10.
