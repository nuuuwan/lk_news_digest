CSE slips below 21,000 as turnover falls to second-lowest level YTD

The Colombo Stock Exchange opened the week on a weaker note on 21 September 2026, with both benchmark indices closing lower amid subdued activity and broad-based selling pressure.

The ASPI declined 77.08 points, or 0.37%, to 20,979.18, while the S&P SL20 fell 19.44 points, or 0.33%, to 5,909.45. The ASPI weakened steadily through the session and closed near the day’s low, slipping below the 21,000 level.

A key feature of the session was the exceptionally low trading activity. Equity turnover fell to just LKR 750.35 million, the second-lowest daily turnover recorded YTD, with 48.73 million shares traded. The Capital Goods sector led activity with LKR 154.10 million in turnover, while John Keells Holdings recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 106.44 million.

Crossings accounted for around 20% of total turnover, led by JKH.N with approximately LKR 77.01 million, followed by KHL.N and CCS.N.

Market breadth remained firmly negative, with only 71 gainers against 135 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 0.53. Foreign investors, however, remained marginal net buyers with an inflow of LKR 19.11 million, although overall foreign participation remained limited.

Lion Brewery was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Melstacorp, C T Holdings, Ceylinco Insurance and Hatton National Bank. Haycarb provided the strongest positive contribution.

The combination of the ASPI falling below 21,000, turnover dropping to one of the lowest levels of the year and weak market breadth points to a clear deterioration in near-term participation and sentiment. The next session will be important in determining whether buying support emerges around the 21,000 area or whether the market extends its recent weakness.
