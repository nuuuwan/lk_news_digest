Easter Sunday attacks: People need to  know the truth

There is much hype about the Easter Sunday attacks verdict. By Wednesday ( September 23) Sri Lanka will know what the verdict is. But sections of the community have mixed reactions towards this verdict for many reasons. The verdict comes seven years later, and by now 269 innocent lives have been sacrificed as a result of an extremist agenda.

There have been promises about various masterminds, individuals whose names have been rarely mentioned, whereabouts of individuals such as Sarah Jesmin and so on. In fact there are many loopholes that need to be addressed. But the ultimate expectation for people is to hear a  judgment that would serve justice for victims and hold perpetrators accountable.

This country has been misled by far too many political promises and this is one promise that people really looked up to and believed in. It has been two years since President Dissanayake assumed office and it is high time that some of his main election promises were fulfilled. That is one way of maintaining popularity and accountability as a politician, which has now become an extremely rare trait.
