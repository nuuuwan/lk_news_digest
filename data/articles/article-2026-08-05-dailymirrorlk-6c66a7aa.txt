BOC celebrates 87 years of trust, service and transformation

Bank of Ceylon (BOC) celebrated its 87th anniversary at a ceremony held at the bank’s head office. The ceremony began with special religious sermons, invoking blessings on the staff and on all customers of the bank.

The event was further enriched by the presence and participation of its valued corporate customers and entrepreneurs, making it a truly meaningful celebration.

The ceremony was attended by Chairman Kavinda de Zoysa, board of directors, General Manager and Chief Executive Officer Y.A. Jayathilaka, corporate and executive management and the bank’s staff.
