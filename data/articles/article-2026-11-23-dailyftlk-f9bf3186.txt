Sri Lankan construction products and services at Build Bangladesh 2026 in Dhaka

Sri Lankan construction material manufacturers and service providers participated in the 31st Build Bangladesh 2026 International Expo, held from 12 to 14 November 2026 at the International Convention City Bashundhara (ICCB) in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

The participating companies showcased their products and services at the Sri Lanka Construction Sector Pavilion, organised by the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB) in collaboration with the High Commission of Sri Lanka in Dhaka.

The Sri Lankan companies participating in the exhibition included S-Lon Lanka Ltd., Rhino Roofing Products Ltd., Iceman Technologies Ltd., Samson Rajarata Tiles Ltd., QJ Design Studios Ltd., RBS Company, Sanmal International Ltd., Eco Paints Ltd., St. Anthony’s Coatings Ltd., and Puritas.

Recognised as one of Bangladesh’s leading international exhibitions for the building and construction industry, the event featured a wide range of construction materials, equipment, technologies, architectural products, interior solutions, sanitary ware and smart and green building technologies. The exhibition was organised by CEMS-Global USA.

Bangladesh offers considerable opportunities for international manufacturers and service providers in the building and construction sector, driven by economic growth, strategic development initiatives and increasing demand for residential, commercial and industrial infrastructure.

The exhibition brought together property developers, architects, civil engineers, procurement professionals and major importers, providing a platform for Sri Lankan companies to establish business contacts, explore partnerships, identify potential buyers and expand their presence in the Bangladeshi market.
