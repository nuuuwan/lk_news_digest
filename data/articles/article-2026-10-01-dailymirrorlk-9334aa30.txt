Court of Appeal dismisses Gotabaya Rajapaksa’s writ petition

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal today dismissed a writ petition filed by former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa seeking to prevent his arrest over ongoing investigations into the 2019 Easter Sunday terror attacks.

The verdict was delivered by a Court of Appeal two-judge-bench comprising Justice Rohantha Abeysuriya and Justice Sarath Dissanayake.
