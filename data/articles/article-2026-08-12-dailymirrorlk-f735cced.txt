SMS Holdings celebrates 25 years

From left: SMS Holdings Director Ashan Senevirathne, former Petroleum and Petroleum Gas Cabinet Minister Chandima Weerakkody, Premium International (Pvt.) Ltd Managing Director and CEO Prasantha Kularatna, SMS Holdings Chairman and Managing Director Shehan Senevirathne and SMS Holdings Director Nesha Senevirathne

Celebrating its 25th anniversary, SMS Holdings marks a remarkable entrepreneurial journey that has transformed a single paving stone manufacturing business into a diversified enterprise spanning construction, engineering, landscaping, plantations, hospitality, manufacturing and exports, while remaining steadfast in its commitment to quality, innovation and sustainable national development.

Over the past quarter century, the group has consistently evolved by embracing innovation, investing in people and expanding into new industries while remaining true to its founding values of quality, integrity and customer trust.

Founded by entrepreneur Shehan Senevirathne, SMS Holdings was built on a straightforward yet ambitious vision: to create locally manufactured products that could rival the imported alternatives in both quality and durability.

Inspired by the lack of quality paving solutions available while undertaking a project at his own home, Senevirathne recognised an opportunity to develop world-class paving products that met international standards while supporting Sri Lankan manufacturing.

SMS Holdings Founder, Managing Director and CEO Shehan Senevirathne said, “Twenty-five years ago, we started with a simple belief that Sri Lankan businesses could produce products equal to the very best in the world. Every milestone we have achieved since then has been possible because of the dedication of our employees, the trust of our customers, the support of our partners and the confidence placed in us by every stakeholder who has been part of this journey.

This anniversary is not only a celebration of our past but also a commitment to continue building businesses that create sustainable value for Sri Lanka while showcasing authentic Sri Lankan products in international markets.”
