Sri Lanka Customs to receive UK technical assistance under new MoU

Sri Lanka Customs is set to receive technical assistance and capacity-building support from the United Kingdom under a proposed Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) aimed at improving customs operations and trade facilitation.

The proposed agreement between His Majesty's Revenue and Customs of the United Kingdom and Sri Lanka Customs will establish a framework for cooperation in implementing the World Trade Organization's Trade Facilitation Agreement.

The initiative is expected to support customs and trade reforms through technical assistance, training programmes, workshops, knowledge sharing and educational visits.

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Finance, Planning and Economic Development Minister to enter into the proposed MoU.

The agreement is expected to strengthen the capacity of Sri Lanka Customs and improve the implementation of trade facilitation measures.
