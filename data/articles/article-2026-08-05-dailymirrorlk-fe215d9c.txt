Harsha preassurised not to include word ’fraud’ in COPF report: MP

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Ruling party members of the Committee on Public Finance (COPF) preassurised Commitee Chairman Harsha de Silva not to include the word 'fraud' in the report he submitted to Parliament on the USD 2.5 million theft, Opposition MP Chithral Fernando alleged today.

The MP told Parliament that the word 'fraud' was included after Opposition members of the COPF fought for it.

"A dictionary was brought by those in the ruling party and got some words in the report," he added.
