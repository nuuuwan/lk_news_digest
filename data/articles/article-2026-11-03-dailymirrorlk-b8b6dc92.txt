EPF raises ACL Cables stake to 10%

The Employees’ Provident Fund (EPF) increased its stake in ACL Cables PLC to 10 percent by end-September, adding just over three million shares during the quarter, according to a stock exchange disclosure.

The EPF held 71.87 million shares as at September 30, 2026, up from 68.86 million shares or 9.6 percent at end-June.

The increase amounted to 3,013,582 shares, lifting its ownership by 0.4 percentage points over the three months.

In an October 30 filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange, company secretary Corporate Affairs (Private) Limited said the EPF’s holding had reached 10 percent of ACL Cables’ issued share capital through a series of transactions.

The disclosure was made pursuant to Appendix 8A of the exchange’s Listing Rules. It did not disclose the purchase prices or total value of the transactions.
