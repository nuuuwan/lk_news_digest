International Dhamma programme on ’Kamma’ to be held on Poson Poya Day

Colombo, June 28 (Daily Mirror) - An international Dhamma programme titled "Kamma" will be held online on Poson Full Moon Poya Day, June 29, with the sermon to be delivered by the Venerable Henepola Gunaratana Mahathera.

The programme will take place via Zoom from 6.00 p.m. (Colombo time) and is open to participants from around the world.

Those wishing to attend are required to register in advance through the designated Zoom registration link - https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/r70kBuxqRGy4NwREO66OnQ

Following registration, participants will receive a confirmation email containing details on how to join the online session.
