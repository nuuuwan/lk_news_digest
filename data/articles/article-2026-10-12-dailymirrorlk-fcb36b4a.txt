Daily Mirror wins DR Wijewardene Challenge Trophy 2026

Daily Mirror emerged champions of the inter-department cricket tournament for the DR Wijewardene Challenge Trophy 2026, defeating Hokandara B in the final by 27 runs. The annual tournament was held at the Narahenpita Shalika Cricket Ground over the weekend, with 23 teams representing various departments of Wijeya Newspapers competing for the title. The tournament was organised by the company’s Welfare Society.

Daily Mirror reached the final after defeating Finance, Hokandara, Transport A and Transport B. Hokandara B secured the runners-up position after overcoming Web Development, Transport, Dispatch and Advertising.

Daily Mirror captain Darshana Sanjeewa Balasuriya received the championship trophy from Ranjith Wijeyewardene, Chairman of Wijeya Newspapers (Pvt) Ltd. Hokandara B captain Ishan Chathura received the runners-up trophy from Udesh Gunathunga, Executive Director of Wijeya Newspapers (Pvt) Ltd.

Thilina Weerawansa of Daily Mirror was named Man of the Series and received the award from Chandreeni Scheffer, Head of Finance at Wijeya Newspapers (Pvt) Ltd. Darshana Sanjeewa Balasuriya won the Best Batsman award, while E. Sampath of Hokandara B received the Best Bowler award.

Deputy Chairman of Wijeya Newspapers Ltd Sujan Wijeyewardene, Welfare Society President Duminda Sanjeewa Balasuriya, Welfare Society Secretary A.B. Kumarathunga and Daily Mirror Editor-in-Chief Jamila Hussain were among those present at the awards ceremony.

Also present were Welfare Society Treasurer Roshan Thanthirige, Wijeya Newspapers General Manager (Print and Production) Dhammika Wimalarathne, Assistant General Manager (Stores) Tharanga Aththanayake, Assistant General Manager (Electrical) Mr. Pathirana and Transport Manager Buddhika.
