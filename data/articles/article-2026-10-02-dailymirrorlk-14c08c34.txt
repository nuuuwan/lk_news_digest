Teacher trainer arrested over sexual harassment

Colombo, Oct. 3 (Daily Mirror) - A lecturer of Addalaichenai Teacher Training College in Ampara was arrested by Ampara Division's Women and Children’s Bureau on suspicion of causing sexual harassment by showing pornographic video films to female teacher trainees.

Police said the suspect had been carrying on that immoral activity for a long time.

Investigations revealed that the teacher trainees refrained from complaining against the suspect, fearing victimisation in terms of reduced marks for subjects taught or supervised by him.

However, the police initiated investigations on information received by Ampara Division Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) Priyantha Bandara Kasturiarachchi and arrested the suspect after recording statements of the lady teachers.

The suspect was to be produced before Ampara Magistrate yesterday.

IP K.T.P.Tharanga is conducting further investigations on the instructions of OIC Ampara Division Women and Children’s Bureau CI Dayani Gamage, ASP Sampath Wikramaratne and SSP Priyantha Bandara.
