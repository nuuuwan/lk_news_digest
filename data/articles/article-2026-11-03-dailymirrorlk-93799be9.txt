Raid Amazones 2026 participants arrive in Sri Lanka

SriLankan Airlines welcomes first group of Raid Amazones 2026 participants: A total of 146 participants, crew members and media personnel arrive at the Bandaranaike International Airport on November 2 aboard flight UL564 from Paris. The flight was operated entirely by female pilots and cabin crew, highlighting women’s empowerment. SriLankan Airlines is the naming rights partner of Raid Amazones 2026, which brings around 300 people to Sri Lanka for an adventure expedition featuring exploration, cultural discovery and outdoor activities.
