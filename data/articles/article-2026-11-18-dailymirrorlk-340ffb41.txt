Akvan intensifies into severe cyclonic storm, fishermen warned

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Cyclone Akvan over the Bay of Bengal has intensified into a severe cyclonic storm, prompting authorities to warn naval and fishing communities against venturing into deep-sea areas northeast of Sri Lanka.

The Department of Meteorology said the storm was located around 650 kilometres northeast of Trincomalee at 5.30 a.m. yesterday (18), near latitude 12.7°N and longitude 85.5°E.

Moving north-northwestwards, the storm is expected to cross the Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Visakhapatnam in India around 11.30 a.m. on November 20.

Naval and fishing communities have been advised not to venture into deep-sea areas bounded by 10°N–20°N and 80°E–95°E until further notice. Those already operating in these areas have been urged to move to safer locations.

The Department also requested fishing and naval communities to remain alert to future weather forecasts and warnings.

Meanwhile, mainly fair weather is expected in the sea areas around Sri Lanka during the next 24 hours.

Winds are expected to blow from the northwest or north at speeds of 30–40 kmph. However, wind speeds could increase to 50–55 kmph in sea areas extending from Kankesanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa.

These sea areas are expected to be rough at times, while other sea areas around the island will remain moderate.

Temporarily strong gusty winds of 60–70 kmph and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers, the Department said.
