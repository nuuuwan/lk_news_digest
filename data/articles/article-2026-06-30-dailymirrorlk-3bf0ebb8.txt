BOI gets new chairman and members

Colombo, June 30 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has appointed Presidential Senior Economic Adviser Duminda Hulangamuwa as the new Chairman and a member of the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka, the President’s Media Division (PMD) said.

The other members appointed to the Board are Vish Govindasamy, Harshani Randiligama, Mangala Karunaratne and K. A. Vimalenthirarajah.

The letters of appointment were presented to them this afternoon (30) at the Presidential Secretariat by Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake.
