BCCI vice-president praises Sonal Dinusha for consistent form in Colombo Test series

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) vice-president Rajeev Shukla has commended Sri Lankan batter Sonal Dinusha for his consistent performances against India in the ongoing Test series. Shukla described Dinusha as a potential great in Test cricket and remarked that he “looks like Tendulkar”.

Dinusha, 25, has already scored two centuries in the home series against India and continued his excellent run with a half-century on the final day of the second Test in Colombo. He forged a century partnership with teammate Keshara Nuwantha, helping Sri Lanka avoid a series whitewash and improve their position in the match.

Amidst an otherwise inconsistent Sri Lankan batting line-up, Dinusha’s temperament, shot selection, and ability to play extended innings have distinguished him as the best batter of the series. At the time of writing, he has accumulated 544 runs in nine innings across five Tests, averaging 77.71, with two centuries and three half-centuries.

Shukla expressed his admiration on his X account, stating: “This Sonal of Srilanka is an amazing player. Looking like Tendulkar. Can be a wonderful test player in international cricket. @OfficialSLC”
