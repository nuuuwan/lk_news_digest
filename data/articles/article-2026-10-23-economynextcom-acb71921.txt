Sri Lanka rupee closes at 332.10/20 to US dollar spot, bond yields lower

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s rupee closed at 332.10/20 to the US dollar in the spot market on Friday, stronger from 332.20/35 the previous day, while bond yields closed lower, dealers said.

A bond maturing on 01.02.2031 closed at 11.70/75 percent, down from 11.73/78 percent.
