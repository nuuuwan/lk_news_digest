Universal Gateway Holdings accelerates F&B growth with twin Colombo launch

Universal Gateway Holdings (Pvt) Ltd has bolstered its footprint in Sri Lanka’s hospitality sector with the launch of a novel dual-dining concept, Thai Fusion and Global Kitchen, operating under one roof at 60, Davidson Road, Colombo 4, adjacent to Duplication Road.

Speaking to Mirror Business following the launch , Universal Gateway Holdings Chairman and Managing Director Mohamed Afthab outlined an ambitious regional growth strategy that aims to expand the group’s restaurant portfolio from its current 11 branches to between 30 and 40 outlets across Sri Lanka and the region over the next three to four years.

“The food and beverage sector under Universal Gateway Holdings is growing rapidly,” Afthab said. “Our vision is not simply to increase the number of restaurants, but to introduce distinctive concepts and cuisines to the Sri Lankan market while maintaining high standards across our brands”.

The newly unveiled five-storey establishment in Bambalapitiya operates daily from 11:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., catering to corporate diners, families, and international visitors. “Our objective is to develop strong restaurant concepts that offer premium quality food, consistency and memorable dining experiences,” he added.

Occupying the ground and mezzanine levels, Thai Fusion delivers an authentic fine-dining experience led by a specialized team of gourmet chefs brought in from Thailand. With a seating capacity of 70, the venue offers an artfully crafted menu featuring traditional Thai salads, soups, fragrant curries, fresh seafood specialties, and signature desserts.

“With Thai Fusion, we are particularly focused on maintaining authenticity, and following its establishment, we intend to expand the brand to other key locations around Sri Lanka,” Afthab explained.

On the upper floor, Global Kitchen operates as an 80-seat multi-cuisine restaurant designed for diverse palates, placing special emphasis on imported premium steaks and gourmet burgers alongside a comprehensive selection of Pakistani, Chinese, Indian, and other international dishes.

The opening of the twin-concept destination took place under the patronage of the Deputy Speaker of Parliament, Dr. Rizvie Salih, and Sarah Salih, alongside the participation of heads of diplomatic missions from Indonesia, Iran, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Nepal, Pakistan, Palestine, Türkiye, and Vietnam, together with business leaders and dignitaries.

Universal Gateway Holdings currently oversees a network of 11 branches across Sri Lanka and the Maldives. The portfolio is anchored by four Indian Summer restaurants situated in Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, and Galle, as well as four Global Kitchen outlets and the flagship Thai Fusion venue. In the Maldives, the group operates both Indian Summer and Global Kitchen within a single integrated property.

Looking ahead, the company is preparing to introduce a renowned Indonesian restaurant franchise to Colombo within the next six months to further diversify its expanding culinary portfolio.
