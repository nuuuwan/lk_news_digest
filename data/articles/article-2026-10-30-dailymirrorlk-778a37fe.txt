Bread and bakery product prices increased by Rs. 5

Colombo, October 30 (Daily Mirror) - The price of a 450g loaf of bread and other bakery products, including short eats, will be increased by Rs. 5 with effect from midnight today (October 30), the All Ceylon Bakery Owners’ Association (ACBOA) announced.

Association President N. K. Jayawardena made the announcement at a press conference held at the association’s headquarters in Raddolugama this morning, following a decision taken by its executive committee.

He said companies engaged in the sale of wheat flour in Sri Lanka had increased the price of flour by Rs. 13 per kilogram with effect from midnight on October 20.

Jayawardena said that when the association raised the matter with Acting Director General of the Consumer Affairs Authority Samantha Karunaratne, she had pointed out that increasing wheat flour prices without notifying the Authority was improper.

He added that the Acting Director General had also requested bakery owners to refrain from increasing the prices of bakery products until the return of the Minister of Trade, who is currently overseas.

However, Jayawardena said bakery owners across the country had exerted considerable pressure on the association to increase product prices in line with the rise in wheat flour prices.

He said the association had complied with the Government’s request and refrained from increasing prices for 10 days, during which the Minister had also participated in discussions on the matter.

Jayawardena further noted that the ongoing conflict in the Middle East and the unfavourable global political situation had made it difficult to expect a reduction in wheat flour prices.

He said the association had therefore decided, with regret, to increase the price of a 450g loaf of bread and other short eats by Rs. 5, effective from midnight today.
