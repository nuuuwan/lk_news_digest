Technical committee to decide on mothers with children under five leaving for foreign jobs

Colombo, Sep. 24 (Daily Mirror) - A final decision on the issue of mothers with children below five years leaving the country for foreign employment will be taken by a technical committee, the Ministerial Consultative Committee on Women and Child Affairs was informed.

The issue was discussed during the Committee meeting held in Parliament on September 22 under the chairmanship of Minister Saroja Savitri Paulraj.

The Committee also discussed implementing a programme to reimburse educational expenses incurred for children of women engaged in foreign employment.
