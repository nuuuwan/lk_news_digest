‘Rotumba Amila’ to be brought back to Sri Lanka today

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Amila Sampath, alias ‘Rotumba Amila’, an alleged organised crime figure and drug trafficker arrested by Russian security forces, is scheduled to be brought back to Sri Lanka today (20).

He is wanted by Sri Lankan authorities over several alleged murders, extortion activities and large-scale drug trafficking operations believed to have been carried out in the Southern Province.

According to reports, Sampath had fled the country and gone into hiding before being arrested by Russian security authorities in March 2025.
