Court orders SSP to explain failure to arrest Rajanganaya Saddharathana over Anuradhapura clash

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Anuradhapura Chief Magistrate Siyapath Sasindu Wickramaratne has ordered the Anuradhapura Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) to personally appear before Court on November 26 and explain why Rajanganaya Saddharathana and other suspects allegedly involved in instigating a clash near the Ruwanweliseya had not been arrested and produced before Court.

The order was issued yesterday (17) after lawyers appearing for four Buddhist monks, two male civilians and a woman, who are currently in remand custody over the incident, accused the police of failing to take action against those they claimed had provoked the confrontation.

Senior lawyers Indika Imbulpitiya and Nuwan Bellanthudawa, appearing for the suspects, told Court that more than 25 people, including Rajanganaya Saddharathana, had allegedly instigated the clash at the Satyagraha site in the Dansal Zone near the Ruwanweliseya.

They alleged that while their clients had been arrested and remanded, those who had allegedly provoked the incident were being allowed to remain at large without being arrested.

The lawyers also questioned the conduct of the police officers deployed at the scene, particularly the Anuradhapura Headquarters Chief Inspector, claiming that the confrontation could have been prevented if the police had acted appropriately.

Referring to video footage of the incident, they said there were grounds to question whether police officers had facilitated the groups allegedly responsible for triggering the violence.

The lawyers sought bail for Kahathagasdigiliya Vijitha, Balangoda Anuruddha, Pilimathalawa Revatha and Deniyaya Soratha Theras, along with two male civilians and a woman, who were remanded until November 26 over allegations of assault and obstructing police officers in the discharge of their duties.

They argued that the Magistrate's Court had the authority to grant bail under the Bail Act and that there was no justification for keeping the suspects in further remand custody.

However, the Chief Magistrate rejected the bail application and directed SSP Prasanna Brahmanage to appear personally before open Court on November 26 to explain why Rajanganaya Saddharathana and the other alleged suspects had not been arrested and produced before Court.

Balangoda Kassapa Thera, the first suspect in the case, was not included in the latest bail application. Explaining the omission, lawyer Indika Imbulpitiya told the media that no bail application had been made on his behalf as he was also facing an allegation of contempt of Court.

Balangoda Kassapa Thera and the other suspects, including four monks, two male civilians and a woman, were remanded until November 26 by the Anuradhapura Chief Magistrate on November 11.
