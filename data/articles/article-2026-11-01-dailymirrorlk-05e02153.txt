10-year-old boy drowns in Keselgamu Oya

Colomo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) – A 10-year-old schoolboy died after falling into Keselgamu Oya while accompanying his grandfather to cut grass in Linpodwatte, within the Bhagawantalawa Police Division, today, police said.

The deceased, Penny Thiban, was a grade five student at a school on the estate.

According to police, the boy had accompanied his grandfather to the river in the afternoon. When the grandfather later searched for the boy after failing to find him nearby, the child’s body was found in the river.

The body was taken to the Bhagawantalawa Regional Hospital, where it was kept pending further examination.

The hospital’s District Medical Officer, A.S.K. Jayasuriya, said the body would be transferred to the Dikoya Base Hospital for a post-mortem examination.
