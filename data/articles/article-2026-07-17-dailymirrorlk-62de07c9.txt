Thai Sumeda Enterprises wins Business of the Year 2026 Award

Thai Sumeda Enterprises (Pvt.) Ltd, one of Sri Lanka’s leading manufacturers and exporters of incense sticks and fragrance-related products, has been honoured with the prestigious Business of the Year 2026 Award in the Large Category for Manufacturing Excellence.

The award was presented at Business World International Awards Ceremony 2026, held recently at Mount Lavinia Hotel, recognising the company’s outstanding contribution to Sri Lanka’s manufacturing sector and its growing international presence.

Founded in 1989, under the leadership of Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Sumeda Elpitiya, Thai Sumeda Enterprises has evolved from a small entrepreneurial venture into one of Sri Lanka’s most recognised incense manufacturing brands. Over the past three decades, the company has earned a strong reputation for producing high-quality incense sticks, incense powder, fragrance oils, air fresheners and other aromatic products that are distributed both locally and internationally.

The company’s commitment to quality, innovation and customer satisfaction has enabled it to establish a significant footprint in export markets while maintaining its position as a trusted household brand in Sri Lanka.

By combining traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing processes, Thai Sumeda has consistently delivered products that meet international standards and changing consumer preferences. Beyond its commercial success, Thai Sumeda Enterprises has played an important role in supporting community development and rural employment. Through its production network, the company provides income-generating opportunities for numerous families across the country, contributing towards economic empowerment and sustainable livelihoods.

Accepting the award on behalf of the company, Sumeda Elpitiya expressed gratitude to the employees, distributors, business partners and customers for their continued support and dedication. He noted that the recognition reflects the collective efforts of the entire organisation and serves as motivation to continue pursuing excellence in manufacturing and business operations.

The latest achievement further strengthens Thai Sumeda Enterprises’ reputation as a leader in Sri Lanka’s manufacturing industry and highlights the company’s ongoing commitment to quality, innovation and sustainable growth while promoting Sri Lankan products in global markets.
