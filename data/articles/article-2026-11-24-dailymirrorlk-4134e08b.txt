China firm wins $828,000 drug supply contract

The Government has awarded a US$828,000 (approximately Rs. 260 million) contract to a Chinese pharmaceutical company for the supply of 920,000 pre-filled syringes of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, a blood-thinning drug used in the treatment of cardiac patients.

The procurement, which was carried out under the International Competitive Bidding procedure, attracted seven bids.

Following an evaluation of the bids, the Cabinet of Ministers approved a proposal submitted by Health and Mass Media Minister to award the contract to Shenzhen Techdow Pharmaceutical Co. Ltd of China, which was identified as the substantially responsive lowest bidder.

The contract, valued at US$828,000, includes Cost and Freight (C&F) charges.

The award was made on the recommendation of the High-Level Standing Procurement Committee.
