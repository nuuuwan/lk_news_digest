Hayleys Solar-PMF Finance expand access to solar energy

In a move aimed at accelerating solar adoption across Sri Lanka, Hayleys Solar has joined forces with PMF Finance PLC to provide accessible financing solutions that help the residential and commercial customers invest in clean energy with greater ease and confidence.

The partnership brings together Hayleys Solar’s extensive expertise in renewable energy and PMF Finance’s over 43 years of experience in Sri Lanka’s financial services sector, creating a seamless pathway for the customers seeking to invest in sustainable energy solutions.

The customers will gain access to customised financing solutions designed to reduce the upfront cost of solar installations, enabling greater long-term savings on electricity bills while supporting Sri Lanka’s transition towards a more sustainable and resilient energy future.

Hayleys Fentons Deputy Managing Director Energy Roshane Perera stated, “This partnership with PMF Finance marks an important step in our mission to make solar energy more accessible to a wider segment of society. By integrating flexible financing solutions with our renewable energy offerings, we are enabling more customers to confidently invest in clean energy while enjoying long-term financial and environmental benefits.”

PMF Finance Director and Chief Executive Officer Prof. (Dr.) Dewasiri N. Jayantha stated, “We are pleased to collaborate with Hayleys Solar in supporting Sri Lanka’s transition towards sustainable energy. Through our financing solutions, we aim to make solar adoption more achievable for the customers by easing financial constraints and providing convenient repayment options.”
