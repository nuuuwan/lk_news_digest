HNB FINANCE opens new Dehiwala branch

HNB FINANCE Managing Director and CEO Chaminda Prabhath ceremonially opens new Dehiwala branch

HNB FINANCE PLC has further expanded its islandwide presence with the opening of its 77th branch in Dehiwala, strengthening access to a comprehensive range of financial services for the customers in the area.

The new HNB FINANCE Dehiwala branch is located at No. 176, Galle Road, Dehiwala.

The branch was ceremonially opened under the patronage of HNB FINANCE PLC Managing Director and CEO Chaminda Prabhath and Deputy CEO Maduranga Heenkenda, with the participation of the members of the company’s senior management and invited guests.

The new branch will provide the customers in Dehiwala and surrounding areas access to HNB FINANCE’s range of financial solutions including gold loans, leasing, fixed deposits and saving facilities.

The customers will also have access to HNB FINANCE’s specialised gold loan solutions, including Gold Plan and VIP Gold Loan facilities, which have been designed to cater to varying customer requirements. These facilities provide the customers with convenient access to finance together with flexible repayment options.

Prabhath said, “Our strategic objective is to further strengthen HNB FINANCE’s position as a preferred non-bank financial services provider offering a comprehensive range of financial solutions. Through our new Dehiwala branch, we aim to provide the customers in Dehiwala and surrounding areas with greater access to our latest financial solutions together with an enhanced service experience.”

“Our ability to tailor financial solutions to different customer requirements has enabled HNB FINANCE to remain responsive even in a challenging economic environment. We remain committed to delivering service excellence and we believe the customers will experience that same commitment through our new Dehiwala branch,” he added.

The opening of the Dehiwala branch forms part of HNB FINANCE’s continued efforts to expand its customer reach and strengthen accessibility to financial services across key commercial and residential centres in the country.
