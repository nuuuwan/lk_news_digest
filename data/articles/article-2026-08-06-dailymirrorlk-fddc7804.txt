Legal support for Malabe woman harassed over feeding street dogs

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - A woman who has spent her own money caring for street dogs has received support from the Animal Welfare Coalition (AWC) after an incident involving her while feeding animals in the Malabe area went viral on social media.

The woman, who has dedicated her time and personal resources to helping stray dogs, provides food, arranges rabies vaccinations and supports sterilisation programmes aimed at controlling the street dog population, according to the AWC.

The organisation said it had previously assisted her during a dispute related to dogs on her private property and has decided to extend legal support once again following the latest incident.

The AWC said it has taken legal action in similar cases involving alleged harassment of animal welfare activists, including complaints against Kaduwela Mayor Ranjan Jayalal, submissions to the Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka and requests for investigations into the conduct of police officers.

The organisation said that there is currently no law in Sri Lanka preventing people from feeding animals in public places, adding that the proposed Animal Welfare Act would recognise caring for animals, including providing food, as a “duty of care”.

Calling on the public to continue helping animals responsibly, the AWC advised people to use plates or bowls when feeding street animals, avoid littering and clean the area after feeding.

The organisation said it would stand with the woman and take necessary legal action if she faces any unlawful pressure from authorities.
