Severe lightning advisory issued for Uva, Eastern Provinces and two districts

Colombo, September 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre has issued an advisory warning of severe lightning activity in several areas, valid until 11.30 p.m. today (18).

According to the advisory issued at 11.00 a.m., thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are likely at several places in the Uva and Eastern provinces and in the Mullaittivu and Polonnaruwa districts.

Temporary localised strong winds may also occur during thundershowers, the Department of Meteorology warned.

The public has been urged to take adequate precautions to minimise possible damage caused by lightning activity.

The Department advised the public to seek shelter, preferably indoors, and avoid taking shelter under trees during thunderstorms.

People were also advised to avoid open areas such as paddy fields, tea plantations and open water bodies while thunderstorms are occurring.

The public has been cautioned against using wired telephones and electrical appliances connected to power outlets during thunderstorms.

People should also avoid travelling in open vehicles such as bicycles, tractors and boats during thunderstorms.

The Department further urged the public to be cautious of fallen trees and power lines following thunderstorms.

Those requiring emergency assistance have been advised to contact their local disaster management authorities.
