President pays final respects to Lal Wijenayake

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has paid his final respects to veteran left-wing political activist and senior lawyer Lal Wijenayake, who passed away on October 31.

The President visited a private funeral parlour in Kandy this afternoon (01), where the remains of the late lawyer are lying, to pay his last respects.

During the visit, President Dissanayake also conveyed his heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family.

Wijenayake who was a senior member of the National People’s Power (NPP) and served on its Executive Committee, was a prominent figure in Sri Lanka’s left-wing political movement.
