Chinese culture meets Lyceum students

Students from Lyceum International Schools in Nugegoda, Kurunegala and Anuradhapura experienced Chinese culture firsthand at a special cultural exchange programme, “Chinese Culture Meets Sri Lankan Students,” held at Lyceum International School, Nugegoda, on September 21. Supported by the Chinese Embassy and the Lumbini Confucius Classroom, the event featured workshops on traditional dance, martial arts, calligraphy, painting, and music, along with a display of Chinese cultural artefacts. Educational supplies were also donated to the participating schools.
