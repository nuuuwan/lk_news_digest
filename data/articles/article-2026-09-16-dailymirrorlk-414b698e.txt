SEC hurdle stalls Industrial Asphalts takeover

The takeover of Industrial Asphalts (Ceylon) PLC (IAC) by Arcasia Investment & Trading (Pvt) Ltd and ATX Partners (Pvt) Ltd has hit a regulatory hurdle, despite the offerors securing acceptances representing 50.16 percent of the company.

The offer, which commenced on 21 August and closed on 12 September, received valid acceptances for 1.88 billion shares, equivalent to 50.16 percent of IAC’s issued shares. The acceptances came from two shareholders, Ramanan Govindasamy and Srikumar Balasubramaniyam, with whom the offerors had entered into binding agreements to purchase their holdings.

Crossing the 50 percent threshold is a key condition of the offer, which was initially voluntary and subsequently converted into a mandatory offer.

However, completion has been complicated by IAC’s transfer to the Colombo Stock Exchange Watch List on 7 September over its failure to submit the Annual Report for the financial year ended 31 March 2026.

Govindasamy, an executive director of IAC, is consequently restricted under CSE Listing Rules from transacting directly or indirectly in the company’s shares while it remains on the Watch List, unless prior approval is obtained from the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

The SEC, in a letter dated 11 September, informed the offerors that approval for the transfer of shares held by persons subject to the restriction would not be granted until IAC remedies the non-compliance that resulted in its placement on the Watch List.

Corporate Services (Pvt) Ltd, acting as registrar to the offer, has requested the CSE to make an announcement on the matter in accordance with Rule 24 of the Company Take-overs and Mergers Code.
