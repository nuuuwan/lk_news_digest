Former diplomat Kananathan congratulates President Ibrahim Traoré of Burkina Faso

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Former Diplomat Kana Kananathan has conveyed his warm congratulations to His Excellency Captain Ibrahim Traoré, President of Burkina Faso, and the people of Burkina Faso on the occasion of their Independence Day.

In his message, Ambassador Kananathan commended President Traoré as a young and dynamic leader who has inspired many across Africa through his commitment to national stability and national development. He also extended his best wishes for the President’s continued success in guiding Burkina Faso toward lasting peace and  economic progress.

Ambassador Kananathan has maintained close links with President Ibrahim Traoré since well before he assumed the presidency in 2022. Their longstanding association predates President Traoré’s rise to national leadership and reflects years of mutual engagement and cordial relations.

Burkina Faso is a landlocked West African nation whose economy is driven primarily by gold mining and agriculture.

President Captain Ibrahim Traoré came to power in 2022 following a military transition. His administration has overseen the withdrawal of French military forces from Burkina Faso and has pursued policy measures aimed at increasing national control over strategic resources,  the gold sector, alongside initiatives to strengthen agriculture and promote economic development.

Kananathan noted that Sri Lankan entrepreneurs have significant opportunities to explore partnerships in Burkina Faso’s largely untapped gold refining and agro-processing sectors, supported by the country’s ongoing efforts to promote value-added industries. He said these sectors offer promising prospects.
