Arcasia, ATX move to take control of Industrial Asphalts

Arcasia Investment and Trading and ATX Partners have launched an offer to acquire all shares of Industrial Asphalts (Ceylon) PLC (IAC), at Rs.0.40 per share, valuing the company at about Rs.1.5 billion.

The offerors have already secured binding commitments for 50.16 percent of IAC, paving the way for a change in control.

IAC Director Ramanan Govindasamy has agreed to sell his entire 48.03 percent stake, while Srikumar Balasubramaniyam will sell a further 2.14 percent, both at Rs.0.40 per share. Together, the agreements cover 1.88 billion shares.

Once the shares are tendered, the voluntary offer will automatically convert into a mandatory offer under the Company Takeovers and Mergers Code, requiring the offerors to accept the shares tendered by the remaining shareholders at the offer price.

Arcasia and ATX, which currently hold no shares in IAC, will split the shares acquired through the offer equally.

A detailed offer document will be sent to the IAC shareholders within 28 days of the July 24 announcement.
