Met. Dept. warns of haze, poorer air quality as cyclonic storm Akvan shifts winds

The Department of Meteorology has warned that air quality across Sri Lanka could worsen and hazy conditions could persist over the next few days. It said changes in wind patterns caused by Cyclonic Storm Akvan are behind the conditions.

A Department official said the unusual haze over Colombo comes from winds that the cyclonic system is pushing towards Sri Lanka from the direction of India. The official said current atmospheric conditions could lower air quality in several parts of the country in the coming days.

The haze is expected to be most noticeable at night and in the early morning, though it could also develop at times in the afternoon. The Department said mist could form alongside the haze and reduce visibility, and it urged the public to stay vigilant while the conditions last.
