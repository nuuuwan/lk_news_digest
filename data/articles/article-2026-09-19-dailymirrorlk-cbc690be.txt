Beverlee Holdings redefines property development in Thalahena

Beverlee Holdings (Pvt.) Ltd is redefining property development in Thalahena through its multi-unit residential project, highlighting the company’s growing contribution to Sri Lanka’s property development and construction sector.

The project reflects Beverlee Holdings’ end-to-end design-and-build service, bringing land assessment, architectural design, structural engineering, interior design, project coordination and final handover under one roof.

For the customers, this means a more convenient process with fewer parties to coordinate and clearer accountability from start to finish, showcasing the company’s signature ‘design-and-build’ approach. Beverlee Holdings has completed over 70 such projects across Sri Lanka.

Operating continuously for close to two decades, Beverlee Holdings has developed expertise across residential developments, commercial spaces, retrofits and hospitality-focused projects. Its Thalahena initiative reflects the company’s broader approach to adding value with carefully finished living environments.

“Clients want a smoother way to manage property development without having to coordinate several separate contractors. Our model brings the key stages together, from design and engineering to construction and finishing, so the process is clearer and more convenient. The same approach guides our boutique villa projects along the Hiriketiya-Dickwella coastal belt, where we are combining modern luxury with Sri Lanka’s tropical setting,” said Beverlee Holdings Managing Director Bemal Gamage.

The company’s services include sustainable design and build, property development, architectural design, interior design, refurbishment and real estate investment. The integrated model gives their clients a single point of responsibility throughout the development process. Beverlee Holdings continues to focus on craftsmanship, practical pricing and disciplined project management.
