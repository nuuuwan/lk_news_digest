Jaffna Int’l Trade Fair 2027 to kick off on January 29

The 17th Jaffna International Trade Fair (JITF), Northern Sri Lanka’s largest and most established multi-sector trade exhibition, will be held from January 29 to 31, 2027, at the Muttraweli Ground, Jaffna.

Organised by Lanka Exhibition & Conference Services in association with the Chamber of Commerce and Industries of Yarlpanam, the JITF continues to serve as a key platform for promoting trade, investment, innovation and business development in the Northern province.

The exhibition will feature 400+ stalls presenting an extensive range of products, services and advanced technologies across multiple industries.

Building on the success of previous editions, the 2027 exhibition is expected to feature more than 400 stalls, showcasing a diverse range of products, services and technologies across industries including construction, automobiles, agriculture, food and beverages, apparel, ICT, education, healthcare, financial services and hotel supplies.

The previous edition attracted an impressive 96,375 visitors and over 4,500 invited business delegates including corporate leaders, entrepreneurs, investors and industry professionals

The exhibition provides valuable opportunities for businesses to expand their market presence, introduce new products, establish partnerships and connect with potential customers and investors.

The JITF also plays an important role in supporting the economic development of Northern Sri Lanka by connecting the local entrepreneurs and SMEs with national businesses, investors and industry stakeholders.

It serves as an effective platform for business networking, knowledge exchange and exploring emerging commercial opportunities in the region.

Reaffirming its commitment to environmental sustainability, the 2027 exhibition will continue its Plastic Neutral Initiative for the second consecutive year. Dedicated plastic collection and recycling stations will be established throughout the exhibition premises, encouraging the exhibitors and visitors to participate in responsible waste management.

The collected plastic will be channelled to approved recycling partners for responsible recycling and upcycling.

With its extensive industry participation, strong business engagement and continued focus on sustainability, the 17th Jaffna International Trade Fair 2027 aims to further strengthen its position as a leading platform for trade, innovation and sustainable economic growth in Northern Sri Lanka.
