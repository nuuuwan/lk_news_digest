Daya Nellampitiya passes away

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Veteran Sri Lankan songstress Daya Nellampitiya has passed away at the age of 91, her family members said.

Nellampitiya was renowned for performing Virindu, a traditional form of Sinhala folk music, and had also performed several duets with her husband, the late Sena Fonseka, who was also a prominent Virindu artist.

She also served as a dance instructor at several leading schools and was widely recognised as a dedicated teacher who played a significant role in preserving and passing on the Kandyan dance tradition to generations of children.
