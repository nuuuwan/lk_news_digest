People’s Bank named Sri Lanka’s Best Digital Bank at Euromoney Awards

People’s Bank has once again reinforced its position as a leader in Sri Lanka’s financial services sector by being named Sri Lanka’s Best Digital Bank at the prestigious Euromoney Awards for Excellence 2026, recognizing its continued commitment to digital transformation, technological innovation and inclusive, customer-centric banking.

Commenting on the achievement, Chairman of People’s Bank, Chandana Guniyangoda stated, “We are honoured to receive this prestigious international recognition, which reaffirms our commitment to digital innovation and transformation. We will continue to come up with novel customer centric digital banking features that conform to the highest standards in security, and governance. People’s Bank is proud to contribute to Sri Lanka’s digital transformation and the nation’s vision of achieving its digital economy goals by 2030.”

Chief Executive Officer/General Manager of People’s Bank, Clive Fonseka, added,“Our digital platforms are designed around convenience, security and accessibility. This recognition reflects the trust of millions of customers and the dedication of our employees in making banking simpler, smarter and more inclusive.”

With a total asset base exceeding Rs. 3.9 trillion and a customer base of over 15.2 million, People’s Bank serves individuals, small and medium-sized enterprises, large corporate and public sector institutions. Its extensive network of 746 branches and service centers ensures a strong presence across the country, connecting customers in both urban and rural communities with a comprehensive range of financial services.

Since embarking on its digital transformation journey in 2015, People’s Bank has made significant strides in redefining the banking experience through technology. Today, more than 80 percent of customer transactions are conducted digitally, while its digital banking platforms serve over four million users. These milestones demonstrate the growing adoption of digital banking and the Bank’s commitment to delivering secure, convenient and seamless financial services.
