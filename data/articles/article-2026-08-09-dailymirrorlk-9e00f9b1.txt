Pizza Hut joins Dialog Pay merchant ecosystem

From left: Dialog Finance Senior Manager Merchant Payment Acquiring Rienzo Rodrigoe and Pizza Hut Head of Marketing, Strategy and Planning Malith Soysa

Dialog Axiata PLC announced its partnership with Pizza Hut Sri Lanka, enabling the customers to make secure LankaQR payments using Dialog Pay across Pizza Hut restaurants islandwide.

The partnership extends digital payment convenience to Sri Lanka’s #1 quick-service restaurant brand, with a network of over 126 restaurants across the country, while rewarding the customers with complimentary Dialog data on eligible LankaQR transactions.

The customers can now use Dialog Pay when dining in, ordering takeaway or ordering via the hotline (0112729729) for deliveries from Pizza Hut, enjoying a secure and convenient way to pay for their favourite pizzas, pasta, sides, desserts and beverages. The partnership offers the customers greater flexibility across every Pizza Hut experience, whether dining at a restaurant or enjoying meals at home.

Pizza Hut Sri Lanka Marketing Strategy and Planning Head Malith Soysa said, “Enhancing the customer experience goes beyond the quality of our food. It also means making every interaction with Pizza Hut as convenient and seamless as possible. Through our partnership with Dialog Pay, we’re introducing another secure payment option for customers whether they dine in, order takeaway or enjoy our delivery service, while continuing to make every Pizza Hut experience even more convenient.”

Dialog Pay, available through the Dialog Pay app and MyDialog app, brings together connectivity, digital payments and financial services within a single ecosystem, supporting Dialog’s vision of making digital payments simpler, more accessible and more rewarding for every Sri Lankan.

The customers can conveniently activate Dialog Pay using their eZ Cash account or by linking their preferred bank account, enabling them to make secure LankaQR payments, transfer funds, access their digital wallet, open savings accounts and fixed deposits and apply for loans powered by Dialog Finance.
