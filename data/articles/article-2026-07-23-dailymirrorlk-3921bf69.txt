Speaker, MP clash verbally over raising of questions

Colombo, July 23 (Daily Mirror) - Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne engaged in a verbal clash with MP Chanaka Madugoda during which the former accused the MP of taking too much time on questions.

He also clashed with MP Ramanathan Archuna as well over raising questions.

MP Madugoda then proposed that answers for oral questions be submitted by the relevant Minister before the sessions, so that there will be enough time for supplimentary questions.
