Sri Lankans spend Rs. 560Mn daily on alcohol

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Alcohol-dependent individuals in Sri Lanka collectively spend around Rs. 560 million a day on alcohol, with the majority of consumers belonging to low-income groups, the Alcohol and Drug Information Centre (ADIC) has revealed.

The Centre’s Programme Director, Amarathna Thennakoon, said around 20,000 people die annually in Sri Lanka due to alcohol consumption, averaging approximately 55 deaths a day.

He made these remarks while addressing a programme held in Anuradhapura on November 17 to educate journalists in the district on the harmful effects of alcohol and drug use.

Thennakoon also revealed that between 20 and 22 per cent of the country’s total population regularly consume alcohol.

According to him, 43 per cent of the male population and 12 per cent of the female population in Sri Lanka consume alcohol.

Globally, the latest analysis of data from the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation (IHME) has found that the age-standardised alcohol-attributable mortality rate declined by approximately 26 per cent between 2010 and 2024.

The analysis, published by the International Alliance for Responsible Drinking in August 2026, also found a 23 per cent decline in the rate of alcohol-related disability-adjusted life years over the same period.

Meanwhile, the World Health Organization’s latest comprehensive global report, published in 2024 using 2019 data, estimated that alcohol consumption was responsible for 2.6 million deaths worldwide
