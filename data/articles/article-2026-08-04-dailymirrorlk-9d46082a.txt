Schools in Kandy, Nuwara Eliya to reopen tomorrow

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - All government schools in the Kandy and Nuwara Eliya districts will reopen tomorrow (05), the Central Province Director of Education announced.

The schools were closed yesterday (04) owing to adverse weather conditions and continuous heavy rainfall that affected the two districts.

The decision to temporarily close the schools had been taken on the instructions of the Secretary to the Central Province Ministry of Education to ensure the safety of students and teachers.
