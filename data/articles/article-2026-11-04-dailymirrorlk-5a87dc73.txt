Yield curve dips on the back of modest buying

The yield curve yesterday inched down by around 5bps, driven by modest buying. Investor activity appeared limited while trading volumes were moderate.

Within the 2030 segment, 01.03.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded at 11.45% and 11.60% respectively, while further ahead on the curve, 01.02.2031 was seen trading at 11.70%.

Moreover, 01.06.2033 traded at 12.35% and towards the belly-end of the curve, 15.10.2034 changed hands from 12.57% to 12.49%. Finally, 15.08.2036 traded between 12.87% to 12.80%.

On the external front, the LKR stood flat at LKR 332.6/USD. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to LKR 389.68bn compared to LKR 400.92bn seen previously.
