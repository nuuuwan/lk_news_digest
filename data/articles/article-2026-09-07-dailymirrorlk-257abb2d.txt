Sri Lankan youth unite to shape National Climate priorities

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Young people representing all nine provinces have come together to discuss Sri Lanka’s pressing environmental and climate challenges at the Local Conference of Youth (LCOY) Sri Lanka 2026 National Consultation.

The consultation, organised by Save a Life, followed a series of provincial-level consultations conducted with nine partner organisations across the country.

The initiative provided a platform for youth to highlight environmental and climate concerns identified in their respective communities and contribute to a national youth perspective.

The programme included expert sessions on biodiversity conservation and ecosystem restoration, the circular economy, and changing weather patterns and climate change in Sri Lanka.

Participants also engaged in thematic discussions covering climate justice, ecosystem restoration and the circular economy.

The recommendations and views gathered through the provincial and national consultations are expected to form part of the National Youth Climate Statement, which will consolidate youth perspectives on Sri Lanka’s environmental and climate priorities.
