Sri Lanka begins talks on strengthening digital workforce and institutions

Colombo, July 15 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has launched discussions with international development partners to strengthen the country's digital transformation by focusing on human resources and institutional capacity alongside technology.

The inaugural meeting of the newly established Development Partner Working Group on Digital Transformation was held on July 13 with the aim of preparing the country for a more inclusive and effective digital future.

The discussion was co-chaired by Deputy Minister of Digital Economy Eranga Weeraratne and UN Resident Coordinator Marc-André Franche. Secretary to the Ministry of Digital Economy Waruna Sri Dhanapala, representatives of the United Nations and international development partners also attended the meeting.

Participants agreed that technology alone would not ensure the success of Sri Lanka's digital transformation, stressing the need to build a skilled workforce and strengthen institutions responsible for delivering digital public services.

The meeting also focused on improving coordination between the government and development partners to accelerate the delivery of accessible digital services. Discussions highlighted the importance of enhancing the capacity of public institutions and implementing structured training programmes for government officials involved in digital service delivery.

The initiative aims to support the government's efforts to build an equitable and inclusive digital economy that provides equal opportunities for all Sri Lankans.

Officials agreed to continue working together to advance Sri Lanka's digital transformation and improve public service delivery through stronger institutional collaboration and capacity building.
