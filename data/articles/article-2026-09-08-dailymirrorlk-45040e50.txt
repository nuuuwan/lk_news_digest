Galle school principal arrested over Rs. 250,000 bribe

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The Principal of Buonavista College in Galle was arrested by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) while allegedly accepting a bribe of Rs. 250,000.

According to CIABOC, the principal was arrested yesterday inside a shop located along the Galle-Habaraduwa road during an operation conducted by its investigative officers.

The arrest followed a complaint made by a resident of the Galle area over the admission of her two children to the school. CIABOC said the principal had initially demanded Rs. 600,000, at a rate of Rs. 300,000 for each child.

The amount was subsequently negotiated down to Rs. 300,000, of which Rs. 150,000 was allegedly demanded and paid as an initial payment. Following the payment, one child was admitted to Grade 1 for the 2026 academic year.

The principal allegedly later demanded a further Rs. 50,000 from the remaining amount to facilitate the admission of the second child to Grade 8. After the second child was admitted, he allegedly demanded another Rs. 250,000 to complete the remaining admission procedures.

According to CIABOC, the principal had allegedly threatened to remove both children from the school if the final payment was not made.

Investigative officers subsequently arrested the suspect while he was allegedly soliciting and accepting the final payment of Rs. 250,000.

CIABOC said the suspect is scheduled to be produced before the Galle Magistrate’s Court for further legal proceedings.
