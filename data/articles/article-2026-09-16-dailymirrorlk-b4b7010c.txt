A helmet, a lesson and a safer ride to school

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - For some children in Nuwara Eliya, the start of the new school term came with an unexpected but important gift a helmet to help keep them safe on their daily journey to school.

A special programme was held at the Nuwara Eliya Police Station on Tuesday (16), where free helmets were distributed to children from low-income families who had been travelling to preschool and school on motorcycles without proper head protection.

The initiative was organised by officers of the Nuwara Eliya Division Traffic Branch, led by OIC Chief Inspector Roshan Cooray.

Traffic officers had identified motorcycle riders who were observed transporting children without helmets in recent days. Instead of merely issuing warnings, officers compiled a list of the children involved and arranged helmets for them through sponsorship provided by Allianz.

The programme was aimed at turning road safety warnings into a practical measure, ensuring that children are better protected while travelling to school.

Police said the initiative also served as a reminder to parents and motorcycle riders that a short journey without a helmet could still put a child's life at risk.
