Sri Lankan Ambassador visits Anojan Sivarasa in Saudi Prison

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan Ambassador to Saudi Arabia O.L. Ameer Ajwad has visited Anojan Sivarasa at Dammam Central Prison to inquire about his well-being following a decision by Saudi Arabia’s Supreme Court to set aside his death sentence and refer the case for a fresh hearing.

During the visit, the Ambassador and Sivarasa discussed the upcoming legal proceedings and the steps required for the retrial, including the need for appropriate legal support and the accurate presentation of relevant facts before the court.

Ambassador Ajwad reaffirmed the Sri Lankan Embassy’s commitment to providing continued consular and legal assistance to Sivarasa throughout the judicial process.

He also stressed the importance of close coordination between Sivarasa, his legal counsel and the Embassy, while respecting the independence of the Saudi judiciary and established legal procedures.

The Ambassador also met prison authorities and thanked them for their cooperation in looking after Sivarasa’s welfare and assisting the Sri Lankan diplomatic mission.
