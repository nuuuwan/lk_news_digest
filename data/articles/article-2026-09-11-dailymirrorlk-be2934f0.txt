PM calls for universities free from harassment and violence

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya has highlighted the need to create a free and peaceful university environment free from harassment and violence, while urging universities not to ignore sensitive cases involving student harassment.

The Prime Minister made the remarks at a meeting of the Subcommittee on the Higher Education Sector held at Parliament on September 10.

She said everyone has a moral responsibility to ensure a safe and peaceful educational environment, adding that sensitive situations related to harassment should not be overlooked.

The Prime Minister also stressed the need for universities to provide proper support and effective mechanisms to address complaints and problems related to harassment, while ensuring the trust and safety of students.

Deputy Minister of Education and Higher Education Dr. Madura Seneviratne, Secretary to the Ministry Nalaka Kaluwewa, UGC Chairman Senior Professor Kapila Seneviratne and several university vice-chancellors attended the meeting.
