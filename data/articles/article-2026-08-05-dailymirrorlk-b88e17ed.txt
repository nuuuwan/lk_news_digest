Over 400 drugstore raids conducted to ensure consumer safety

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has conducted 471 raids and investigations at drugstores across the island this year as part of measures to ensure consumer safety and strengthen regulations in the pharmaceutical sector.

A special islandwide raid programme conducted on August 3 and 4 resulted in inspections of 109 drugstores, with 86 violations detected, the CAA said.

The detected offences included selling expired medicines, keeping expired pharmaceutical products for sale, selling medicines without mandatory consumer information, and failing to comply with prescribed labelling requirements.

The Authority said violations were also identified involving medical devices, baby products and other consumer goods sold at drugstores.

During the raids, expired medicines, pills, vaccines, syringes, creams, baby products, pregnancy test kits, medical equipment and other consumer items were seized. The seized goods will be produced before the relevant Magistrate’s Courts.

Meanwhile, the CAA said raids conducted at 362 drugstores between January 1 and June 30, 2026, resulted in courts imposing fines totalling Rs. 2,269,500 on vendors.

Consumers have been advised to check expiry dates, labels and mandatory product information when purchasing medicines and health-related products. Complaints regarding violations can be reported to the CAA via the hotline 1977 during office hours.
