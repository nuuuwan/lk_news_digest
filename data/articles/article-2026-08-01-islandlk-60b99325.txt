Angelo Mathews leads Kandy Royals at launch of new cancer drug facility project

Kandy Royals cricket team captain Angelo Mathews and members of the Lanka Premier League (LPL) franchise participated in a series of events organised by their primary sponsors, Kelun Lifescience and Celogen, including the foundation stone-laying ceremony for a new manufacturing complex for cancer treatment drugs.

The Kandy Royals team, one of the prominent franchises in the LPL, visited the pharmaceutical manufacturing facility located at the Pallekele Export Processing Zone on July 30, where they were welcomed by employees led by Kelun Lifescience and Celogen Chairman V. Nadarajah, Vice President Gyan Prakash Ujailan, Director I. Sathya Murthi and Managing Director Rishi Kumar Nadaraja.

The new manufacturing complex is expected to strengthen Sri Lanka’s pharmaceutical production capacity, particularly in the field of cancer treatment medicines.

Speaking at the event, Mathews said support from institutions such as Kelun Lifescience and Celogen provided a major boost to the development of cricket in Sri Lanka.

He stressed the importance of identifying talented players from the grassroots level, particularly through schools, and nurturing them to reach national and international standards.

Mathews expressed appreciation for the contribution made by private sector organisations in supporting cricket and helping create opportunities for emerging players.

The Kandy Royals are backed by Kelun Lifescience and Celogen as their primary sponsors for the ongoing LPL tournament.
