Hayleys backs Sri Lanka Expo 2027 as Gold sponsor, opening a path to global markets for Sri Lankan SMEs

Hayleys PLC receives partner recognition for Sri Lanka Expo 2027 at the Partnership Appreciation

Sri Lanka’s largest listed conglomerate supports the nation’s flagship trade exhibition as it returns for the first time since 2012

Hayleys PLC has joined Sri Lanka Expo 2027 as a Gold Partner. The Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB) will host the international trade exhibition from 14 to 17 January 2027 at the BMICH, Colombo, which returns after 15 years.

The Expo is expected to bring together about 750 Sri Lankan exporters and over 1,500 international buyers, investors and trade delegates. It covers sectors ranging from apparel, rubber and engineering to tea, spices, coconut products, ICT and logistics.

In September 2026, the country’s merchandise exports reached $ 1.38 billion, the highest monthly value on record. Total exports for the first nine months of the year rose 5% to $ 13.6 billion.

Hayleys is Sri Lanka’s number one ranked listed company (LMD 100 – 2025/26) and is one of the country’s largest export earners. The Group accounts for around 5% of the country’s export income, close to $ 800 million in foreign exchange. It operates in more than 20 countries and its products reach over 80 markets.

Its export strength is built on value addition across the Group. Hayleys exports gloves, activated carbon, coconut fibre-based eco solutions products, textiles, aluminium extrusions, agricultural products and Ceylon tea. Its transportation and logistics businesses help keep the country’s trade moving as a facilitator for country’s exports. Each of these businesses competes on technology, research and quality in demanding international markets.

“Growing exports is a key pillar of Sri Lanka’s economic future, and it has been at the heart of Hayleys’ strategy,” said Hayleys PLC Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Mohan Pandithage. “Our growth and the nation’s growth rest on Sri Lankan products competing, and winning, in global markets. At the Expo, we are proud to feature Hayleys’ own sustainable innovations, home-grown solutions that have already won over customers in over 80 markets.”

The Group’s purpose is “to inspire an inclusive world and a thriving planet”, and that purpose shapes how it builds export value. Hayleys businesses bring smallholder farmers, out-growers and rural communities directly into global value chains, so they share in the value created. This work is driven by Hayleys Lifecode, the Group’s ESG and sustainability framework. Hayleys companies have together been named Sri Lanka’s Best Corporate Citizen six times by the Ceylon Chamber of Commerce.

Through its sponsorship, Hayleys aims to help Sri Lanka’s small and medium enterprises compete on the international stage the Expo provides.

“Sri Lanka’s SMEs are the next link in that chain. As Gold Sponsor of Expo 2027, we want to help local entrepreneurs do what Hayleys has done for generations: adopt sustainable practices, showcase their innovative products, and grow their portfolio to reach global markets. When they grow, Sri Lanka’s export base grows with them,” said Hayleys PLC Group Executive Director Sarath Ganegoda.
