Lanka Metro expands Colombo bus network with seven new routes

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - Lanka Metro Transit has introduced seven new bus routes covering Colombo and its suburbs, as part of efforts to provide a more efficient, convenient and regular public transport service.

The newly introduced route network aims to improve connectivity between key residential areas, transport hubs and the city centre while offering passengers more reliable travel options.

Lanka Metro Transit said the expansion of the route network is aimed at strengthening urban mobility and improving public transport accessibility for commuters travelling within Colombo and surrounding areas.
