Strong winds of up to 50 kmph forecast for several provinces

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Strong winds of around 40–50 kmph are expected at times over the North Central, North Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and Southern provinces due to the active south-west monsoonal conditions, the Meteorology Department warned.

The Department have urged residents in the affected areas to remain vigilant and be prepared for possible weather-related hazards.

The strong winds could damage roof tiles and sheets, power and communication lines, while tree branches and large avenue trees may also break or be uprooted.

Damage to paddy crops, banana and papaya trees, orchards and harbour yachts is also possible. Sea water inundation could occur in low-lying areas along the coast.

The public has been advised to be cautious of damaged roof tiles and sheets, fallen trees and power lines, and to remain alert to further developments in the weather.

People have also been urged to contact local disaster management authorities for emergency assistance and follow future weather advisories issued by the Department of Meteorology.
