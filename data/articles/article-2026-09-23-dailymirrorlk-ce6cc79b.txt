Vidullanka-led  consortium leads  Rs. 5bn transmission tender

Vidullanka PLC, in partnership with India’s Skipper Limited, has emerged as the lowest-cost bidder for a Rs. 5 billion power transmission project funded by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), paving the way for the renewable energy company’s entry into Sri Lanka’s high-voltage grid infrastructure sector.

The consortium has bid to construct 132-kilovolt transmission lines under Package 3, Phase II of the National Transmission and Distribution Network Development and Efficiency Improvement Project, Vidullanka said in a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange yesterday.

The scope covers the construction, installation, testing and commissioning of the transmission lines and associated infrastructure for the Ceylon Electricity Board’s National Transmission Network Service Provider.

The project is expected to be implemented over 18 months from the commencement of the contract.

Vidullanka cautioned that emerging as the lowest-cost bidder did not constitute a formal contract award. The bid remains subject to the completion of technical evaluations and the receipt of a Letter of Award from the employer.

Subject to the contract being secured, Vidullanka will handle local execution and project management in collaboration with Skipper Limited. The project would mark Vidullanka’s formal entry into transmission-line construction and high-voltage grid infrastructure, broadening its operations beyond renewable power generation, engineering, procurement and construction services, and utility-scale battery energy storage systems.

Vidullanka currently has interests in hydropower, solar and biomass-based electricity generation.

The company said the project, once formally awarded and supported by definitive agreements, was expected to contribute positively to future revenue and earnings. It would also provide a platform to compete for further transmission infrastructure projects in Sri Lanka.
