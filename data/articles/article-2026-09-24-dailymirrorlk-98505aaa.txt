Central Bank freezes assets of 6 firms and their directors over deposit-taking concerns

The Central Bank has issued orders freezing the assets of six companies and their directors as it investigates suspected unauthorised finance business and deposit-taking.

The orders prevent the companies and directors from disposing of, transferring or otherwise dealing with their property and other assets. The Colombo High Court has subsequently confirmed and extended the orders, the Central Bank said in a statement dated September 22.

The Central Bank said it had already determined that four of the firms had carried on finance business and/or accepted deposits in breach of the Finance Business Act. They are Singhe Capital Investment Ltd, Pro Shop Advertising Holdings (Pvt.) Ltd, Athens International Education Centre (Pvt.) Ltd and Eyon Lanka Investment & Film Production International Company (Pvt.) Ltd.

Investigations are continuing into the other two firms, Kasagala Green Plantation (Pvt.) Ltd and Ceylon Green Life Plantation (Pvt.) Ltd.

The Central Bank did not disclose the value of the frozen assets or the amount of money that may have been collected from the public.

The regulator said it was also investigating several other institutions and individuals, following complaints about possible unauthorised finance business or deposit-taking.

Only institutions licensed under the Banking Act or Finance Business Act or specifically exempted from those laws, may accept public deposits, the Central Bank said.

It urged the people to check an institution’s authorisation before depositing money and to report suspected violations.
