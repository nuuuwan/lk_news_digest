Justice Minister should make a statement on unrest in prisons immediately: Sajith

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa urged the Minister of Justice, who is also in charge of prisons, to immediately make a statement on the unrest in prisons.

"One inmate has already died in the Kuruwita prison and one in the Magazine. We now hear of an unrest in Pallansena. The Justice Minister should inform the House immediately on the situation," Premadasa said.
