Indian national arrested at BIA with cocaine and ecstasy worth Rs. 454 million

An Indian national has been taken into custody by Sri Lanka Customs at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), Katunayake, after being found in possession of cocaine and ecstasy valued at an estimated Rs. 454 million.

The 46-year-old suspect, an engineer by profession, arrived in Sri Lanka from Paris via Doha on Qatar Airways flight QR 654 on October 28, 2026.

Officers of the Narcotics Control Unit of Sri Lanka Customs detained the passenger for further examination after suspicious substances were detected in his luggage.

A subsequent inspection confirmed that the consignment contained 5,121 grammes of cocaine valued at approximately Rs. 256 million and 13,173 grammes of ecstasy, equivalent to nearly 23,000 pills, valued at around Rs. 198 million.

The total estimated market value of the seized narcotics is Rs. 454 million.

Sri Lanka Customs has initiated preliminary investigations into the incident. The suspect, the seized narcotics and other case productions are to be handed over to the Police Narcotics Bureau for further investigations and legal action.
