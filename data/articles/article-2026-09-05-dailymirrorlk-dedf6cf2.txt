Litro gas prices slashed

Colombo, September 5 (Daily Mirror) - The prices of Litro domestic LP gas cylinders have been reduced with effect from today (05), Litro Gas Lanka announced.

According to Chairman Channa Gunawardana, the price of a 12.5 kg cylinder has been reduced by Rs. 100 to Rs. 4,365.

The price of a 5 kg cylinder has been reduced by Rs. 42 to Rs. 1,750, while the price of a 2.3 kg cylinder has been reduced by Rs. 20 to Rs. 815.
