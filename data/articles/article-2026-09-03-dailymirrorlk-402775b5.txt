Botham recovers after first-of-its-kind cancer surgery

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Dehiwala Zoo’s beloved sea lion “Botham” is recovering well following a special cancer surgery described as the first of its kind in Sri Lanka, zoo officials said.

Botham, who has been a popular attraction at the zoo for many years, was diagnosed with squamous cell carcinoma, a type of cancer, after becoming seriously ill.

Following the diagnosis, zoo staff and veterinary officers explored treatment options. A special surgical procedure was recommended under the guidance of foreign veterinary specialist Paolo, an expert in sea lions and other marine animals.

The surgery was performed at the Dehiwala Zoo on June 30, 2026, under the supervision of the Ministry of Environment.

The operation was carried out by a team including foreign expert veterinarian Paolo and veterinary officers of the Department of National Zoological Gardens. The foreign veterinary team volunteered their services for the procedure.

Zoo officials said the veterinary and animal-care teams made a major contribution to the successful operation.

Botham is now recovering under close veterinary supervision and is reported to be in very good health following the surgery.

Zoo officials expressed hope that Botham will make a full recovery and return to his usual activities soon.
