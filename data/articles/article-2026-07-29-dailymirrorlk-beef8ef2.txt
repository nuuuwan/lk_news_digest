Siddhalepa honoured with GlobalHealth Sri Lanka Awards 2026

Siddhalepa Ayurveda product range and Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort have been honoured with two accolades at the inaugural GlobalHealth Asia-Pacific Sri Lanka Conference & Awards 2026.

Siddhalepa Ayurveda products received the “Excellence in Herbal Wellness & Functional Nutrition in Sri Lanka” award, while Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort was recognised as the ‘Holistic Health & Wellness Service Provider of the Year in Sri Lanka.’

The awards were presented during the GlobalHealth Sri Lanka Awards ceremony held at ITC Ratnadipa, Colombo, on 21 July.

GlobalHealth Asia-Pacific is a premier regional platform dedicated to connecting healthcare providers, aesthetic centres, wellness destinations, and policymakers across more than 14 countries. The GlobalHealth Sri Lanka Awards celebrate organisations that uphold the highest standards of professional practice, ethical governance, service excellence, and patient-centred innovation.

From its origins in Sri Lanka over 90 years ago, Siddhalepa Ayurveda products have grown into a globally recognised representation of Sri Lankan Ayurveda. Trusted by generations and embraced internationally, the product range continues to carry Sri Lanka’s healing heritage beyond its shores, offering natural healthcare solutions that have become part of everyday lifestyles around the world.

Complementing this achievement, Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort was honoured with the “Holistic Health & Wellness Service Provider of the Year in Sri Lanka” award.

Commenting on the achievement, Asoka Hettigoda, Chairman and Managing Director of Siddhalepa Group said, “These recognitions reaffirm our commitment to preserving the authenticity of Sri Lankan Ayurveda while ensuring its benefits continue to reach people in meaningful ways.

Through our herbal healthcare products, we have taken Sri Lanka’s Ayurvedic heritage to consumers across the world, while Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort continues to offer authentic healing experiences that showcase the true value of Ayurveda. We are honoured to receive these awards on behalf of our dedicated team and the generations of customers who have placed their trust in Siddhalepa.”
