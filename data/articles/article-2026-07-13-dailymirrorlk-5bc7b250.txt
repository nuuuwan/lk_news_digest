FINAL FOUR CONFIRMED! Only four World Cup Finals are Still Possible

July.13 (Mirror Sports) - The FIFA World Cup 2026 semi-final line-up is now complete, guaranteeing that one of football's traditional powerhouses will lift the trophy. Defending champions Argentina are chasing a fourth world title, France are bidding for a third crown, while Spain and England are both aiming to add a second star to their shirts.

Each possible final offers a compelling storyline. A France vs England showdown would produce the first-ever FIFA World Cup final between the European rivals. France vs Argentina would revive the unforgettable 2022 World Cup final, while Spain vs England would be a rematch of the UEFA EURO 2024 final. A Spain vs Argentina decider would pit the reigning European champions against the defending world champions in a historic first.

With Lionel Messi, Kylian Mbappé, Lamine Yamal, Harry Kane and Jude Bellingham all still in the hunt for football's biggest prize, the race to crown the FIFA World Cup 2026 champions is set for a thrilling finish.
