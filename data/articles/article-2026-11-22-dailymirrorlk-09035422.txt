Divulapitiya e-waste facility’s licence suspended after fire

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The environmental licence of an electronic waste processing facility in Wewagedara, Divulapitiya, has been temporarily suspended following a fire that broke out at the premises on November 16.

Minister of Environment Dr. Dhammika Patabendi said the decision was taken as a precautionary measure following the incident, which raised concerns over possible environmental pollution caused by burning electronic waste.

He said the Central Environmental Authority (CEA) had also issued a series of recommendations to prevent air pollution and contamination of the surrounding environment.

The recommendations aim to minimise the environmental impact of smoke and other pollutants released during the fire, as well as prevent possible contamination from electronic waste materials.

The CEA is also expected to take necessary steps to ensure that environmental risks arising from the incident are properly addressed.

The facility's environmental licence will remain suspended temporarily, pending further action by the relevant authorities.
