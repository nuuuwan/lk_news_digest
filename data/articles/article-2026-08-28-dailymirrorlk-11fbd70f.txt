Expressways generate Rs. 124.57m in two days

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Country’s expressways generated a total revenue of Rs. 124.57 million from 325,316 vehicles during August 26 and 27, according to the latest expressway revenue figures.

On August 26, a total of 163,763 vehicles used the E01, E02, E03 and E04 expressways, generating Rs. 60.41 million in revenue.

The E01 and E02 sections recorded the highest traffic, with 97,149 vehicles generating Rs. 35.61 million, while E03 recorded 45,942 vehicles and Rs. 18.90 million. E04 recorded 20,672 vehicles and Rs. 5.90 million.

On August 27, 161,553 vehicles travelled on the expressways, generating Rs. 64.16 million—an increase of about Rs. 3.75 million compared with the previous day.

The E01 and E02 sections recorded 102,027 vehicles and Rs. 41.14 million in revenue, while E03 recorded 35,004 vehicles and Rs. 15.99 million. E04 recorded 24,522 vehicles and Rs. 7.03 million.

Overall, the two-day total stood at 325,316 vehicles and Rs. 124,574,925 in revenue.
