Only one out of four crude coconut oil importers pay taxes: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Only one out of four crude coconut oil importers pay taxes while the remaining three companies have defaulted, Deputy Minister Nishantha Jayaweera told Parliament today.

Responding to a question raised by MP S.M. Marikkar, the Deputy Minister said the collection of default taxes is being delayed due to appeals.
