Colombo air quality reaches unhealthy level, IQAir reports

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Air pollution in Colombo reached an unhealthy level yesterday morning, with the Air Quality Index (AQI) rising to 178, international air quality monitoring platform IQAir said.

The reading was recorded at around 8.30 a.m. on November 17, with fine particulate matter (PM2.5) identified as the main pollutant.

The PM2.5 concentration was recorded at 94.5 micrograms per cubic metre, while the temperature stood at 28°C.

Under the US AQI classification, a reading between 151 and 200 is considered unhealthy, meaning exposure may pose health risks to the general public, with greater concerns for sensitive groups.

IQAir's hourly forecast indicated a gradual improvement in air quality, with the index expected to decline to around 100 by the evening.

Residents, particularly children, older people and those with respiratory conditions, are advised to limit prolonged or strenuous outdoor activities while pollution levels remain high.

IQAir's live monitoring page listed four monitoring stations in Colombo, supplied by three contributors.

The air quality readings can change throughout the day depending on weather conditions and pollution levels.
