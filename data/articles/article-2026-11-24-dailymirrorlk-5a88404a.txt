Man posing as British national arrested over gold jewellery thefts

Colombo, November 24 (Daily Mirror)- The Fort Police have arrested a suspect accused of stealing gold jewellery worth nearly Rs. 2 million from several jewelry shopping complexes in Colombo by posing as a customer interested in purchasing gold.

During the arrest, police also recovered 2,800 milligrams of heroin from the suspect's possession.

Among the recovered stolen items were a half-sovereign rose gold ring adorned with 68 small diamonds, a gold ring set with a pink sapphire and diamonds, and several other pieces of gold jewellery.

According to police, the suspect allegedly committed the thefts on multiple occasions during September and this month. The suspect reportedly visited jewellery shopping complexes within the Colombo Fort, Harbour, and Kollupitiya police divisions, areas frequently visited by foreign nationals purchasing gold jewellery.

Posing as a British national, the suspect allegedly pretended to be interested in buying jewellery, inspected the items presented by shop employees, and stole them while posing as a potential customer.
