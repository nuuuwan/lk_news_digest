Court rejects Gota’s plea to lift overseas travel ban

SC fixes 5 Mar. 2027 to consider his appeal seeking to prevent arrest under PTA

The Colombo Fort Magistrate’s Court yesterday rejected a request by former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa to temporarily lift the overseas travel ban imposed on him in connection with investigations into the 2019 Easter Sunday terrorist attacks.

Rajapaksa had sought permission to travel to Singapore for follow-up medical examinations and treatment following heart surgery at Mount Elizabeth Hospital. He appeared before Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena yesterday, when the court delivered its decision on the application.

The travel ban was imposed on 3 June, 2026. During previous court proceedings, Additional Solicitor General Dileepa Peiris informed the court that investigations concerning the former President had reached an advanced stage, although he had not been named a suspect at the time.

In a separate proceeding, the Supreme Court yesterday fixed 5 March, 2027 to consider an appeal filed by Rajapaksa seeking to prevent his arrest under the Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA) in connection with the Easter Sunday attacks investigation.
