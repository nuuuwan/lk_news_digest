Sri Lanka trio rise in ICC T20I rankings

Nov.24 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka's impressive performances in the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy 2026 have brought rewards in the latest ICC Women's T20I Player Rankings, with Vishmi Gunaratne, Kavisha Dilhari and Sugandika Kumari making notable gains.

Vishmi has climbed to 51st in the Batting Rankings after moving up 37 places from her pre-tournament position of 88th. She has been the tournament's leading run-scorer with 314 runs at an outstanding average of 104.66.

Meanwhile, Imesha Dulani has advanced three places to 23rd following her quickfire 56 off 38 deliveries against Pakistan.

Sri Lanka have also made significant progress in the Bowling Rankings. Kavisha Dilhari has climbed to 12th after taking 13 wickets in the tournament, while Sugandika Kumari has moved up eight places to 13th with eight wickets.

The rankings boost comes as Sri Lanka continue their impressive campaign after booking their place in the tournament final.
