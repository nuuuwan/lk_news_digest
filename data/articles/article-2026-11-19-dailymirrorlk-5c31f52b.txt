Namal produced before High Court over Airbus deal

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - SLPP MP Namal Rajapaksa was produced before the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar yesterday in connection with the controversial SriLankan Airlines Airbus deal.

Rajapaksa, who is currently in custody, was brought before the court by prison officials.

The case, filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC), was taken up before a three-judge bench.

The charges relate to alleged bribery in connection with the purchase of Airbus aircraft by SriLankan Airlines. The Bribery Commission has filed charges against Rajapaksa and several others over the deal.
