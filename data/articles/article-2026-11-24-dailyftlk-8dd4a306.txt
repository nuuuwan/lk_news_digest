Justice for All reiterates call for withdrawal of anti-terrorism Bill

Justice for All yesterday called for the withdrawal of the Protection of the State from Terrorism Bill (PSTB), telling Parliament’s Sectoral Oversight Committee on Governance, Justice and Civil Protection that the Bill is unworkable at present and must be withdrawn.

The organisation said it was invited by the Committee on 20 November to a meeting to consider the PSTB, which was held yesterday. At the meeting it raised its concerns over the Bill, the process followed and what it called multiple draconian clauses that make the PSTB an oppressive law, much worse than existing laws in Sri Lanka.

“At the outset we note that the PSTB has been introduced to repeal the Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA), a law that has been used and abused for over four decades,” Justice for All said in a statement.

“Justice for All has conducted several activities across the country demanding the repeal of the PTA and most recently challenged the PSTB in the Supreme Court,” it added.

The organisation said that, after President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said he would consult on the PSTB and another law, it wrote to him on 9 November asking for a meeting to discuss the matter. It sent a second letter dated 19 November repeating the request, after receiving no response from the Government to the first.

“We continue to await a response from the President to his own promise to hold consultations,” Justice for All said.
