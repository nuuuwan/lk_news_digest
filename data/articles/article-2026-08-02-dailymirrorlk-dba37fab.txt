World breastfeeding week begins

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - World Breastfeeding Week began yesterday (1) and will continue until August 7, with health authorities launching a nationwide campaign to raise awareness about the importance of breastfeeding.

Speaking at a media briefing at the Health Promotion Bureau, Community Medical Specialist at the Family Health Bureau's Child Nutrition Unit, Dr. Hiranya Jayawickrama, said the campaign aims to educate the public on the benefits of breastfeeding for both babies and mothers.

Health officials advised that babies should be exclusively breastfed for the first six months of life, as breast milk provides complete nutrition, strengthens immunity and supports healthy growth and development.

They also noted that breastfeeding helps protect children from infections and certain diseases while benefiting the mother's health and strengthening the bond between mother and child.

World Breastfeeding Week is observed annually during the first week of August and is jointly led by the World Alliance for Breastfeeding Action (WABA), the World Health Organization (WHO) and UNICEF.
