Maryland memorial service for Nanda Malini, Siddhartha Thera

A memorial service was held at the Maryland International Buddhist Temple in the United States yesterday (23) to pay tribute to veteran lyricist Ven. Rambukan Siddhartha Thera and legendary Sri Lankan singer Dr. Nanda Malini.

The ceremony was attended by Sri Lankan Acting Ambassador to the United States Maduka Wickramarachchi and members of the Sri Lankan community in Maryland. Pic by Sisira Wijesinghe, Washington
