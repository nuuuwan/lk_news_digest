Prison overcrowding to ease in one month: Minister

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror)- Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara said yesterday that overcrowding in prisons will be reduced in a month or one and half months as the government has taken steps to expedite issuance of reports by the government Analyst Department.

The Minister told media after visiting Anuradhapura prison that some 23,000 prisoners connected to drug related charges are in remand prison due to delay in reports of the Government Analyst.

He said the government has recruited 70 officers to the Government Analyst Department and procured three analysis machines to expedite the issuance of reports.

The Minister said about 400 to 500 reports are being issued daily by the Government Analyst Department by now.

He said the government has taken steps to establish a joint mechanism in all prisons in order to ensure safety and rights of prisoners and to protect property and the lives of prisoners.
