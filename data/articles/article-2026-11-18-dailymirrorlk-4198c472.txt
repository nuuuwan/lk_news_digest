SDF records Rs. 964.92mn PBT end of 1H 2026/27

Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC (SDF) recorded a profit before tax of Rs. 964.92 million for the six months ended 30 September 2026, an increase of 24.5 percent year on year, while strengthening regional access to financial services and supporting community development.

Profit after tax rose by 22.3 percent to Rs. 416.47 million. Gross income increased by 39.0 percent to Rs. 3.99 billion, while net interest income grew by 29.1 percent to Rs. 2.10 billion. Total assets reached Rs. 43.96 billion, up 17.6 percent from 31 March 2026, and shareholders’ funds increased by 6.0 percent to Rs. 4.69 billion.

Nilantha Jayanetti, Chief Executive Officer of Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC said, “Our first-half performance reflects continued progress in building a financially resilient institution rooted in inclusive development. Our investments in regional access and customer service bring us closer to the individuals, entrepreneurs and households we serve. We remain focused on profitable growth, supported by responsible lending and disciplined cost management.”

SDF strengthened its regional presence by opening a new branch in Kuliyapitiya, Badulla, Dehiaththakandiya and Kanthale recently to improve customer convenience and access to financial services.

SDF secured first place among Category 3 listed finance companies in the K Seeds Investments ranking for the first quarter of 2026/27 assessed across ten equally weighted performance indicators.

Lanka Rating Agency Limited also reaffirmed SDF’s ‘BBB-’ entity rating with a Stable Outlook, recognizing its improving financial profile, strong capital position, expanding lending portfolio, and enhanced profitability.
