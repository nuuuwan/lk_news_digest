Colombo Uni. Faculty of Management and Finance International Research Conference tomorrow

The 21st International Research Conference on Management and Finance (IRCMF 2026) organised by the Faculty of Management and Finance, University of Colombo will be held tomorrow from 8.00 a.m. to 5.00 p.m. onwards at the faculty premises.

Over the past 21 years, IRCMF has evolved into a significant international academic platform for advancing research, facilitating knowledge exchange, and fostering collaboration among academics, researchers, and industry practitioners. The conference brings together local and international scholars and business professionals to discuss contemporary issues, emerging trends, and developments in management, finance, and related disciplines.

This year’s conference will feature two distinguished keynote speakers. Professor Carol T. Kulik from the University of Adelaide, will speak on relevance and rigor of management research. Her speech focuses on how to make management research more relevant to the broader society and how to link our research with journalism. The second Keynote speaker Professor Sasanka Perera will speak about alternative research methods in social sciences which I believe is a timely necessity for the advancement of management research.

The conference also features several important initiatives that extend beyond conventional research paper presentations.

A dedicated Doctoral Colloquium provides an opportunity for doctoral researchers to present their ongoing research, receive constructive feedback from experienced academics, and engage in scholarly discussions to strengthen their research contributions. This initiative aims to support emerging researchers and foster the next generation of research scholars.

The Industry Dialogue which adds a practitioner’s perspective to the conference, is a panel discussion on compelling practical management problems. This year the theme of this segment is leading disruptive transformation and the panelists will be Arcasia Holdings Chairman Aravinda de Sliva, Dialog Axiata PLC Group CEO Supun Weerasinghe  and MAS Silueta CEO Ruwan Keragala.

Furthermore, the conference will recognise research excellence through its Best Paper Awards, acknowledging outstanding research contributions presented at the conference. These awards reflect the faculty’s commitment to encouraging high-quality research and recognising scholarly contributions across the disciplines represented at IRCMF.
