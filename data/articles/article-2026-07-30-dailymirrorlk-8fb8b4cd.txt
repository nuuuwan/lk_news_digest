Kamindu guides Kaps to crucial LPL win

July.30 (Mirror Sports) - Colombo Kaps boosted their Lanka Premier League 2026 playoff hopes after holding their nerve to defeat Dambulla Sixers by four wickets in a high-scoring contest at the Pallekele International Cricket Stadium on Wednesday

Set a target of 175, the Kaps attacked from the outset through Rubin Hermann, whose aggressive 48 from 26 balls put the chase firmly on track. Captain Kamindu Mendis then produced a calm 39 to steady the innings before Ben McDermott (33) and Sadeera Samarawickrama (26) combined to carry Colombo over the line with six balls remaining.

The Sixers had earlier built a competitive total of 174 for 5 after another classy display from Reeza Hendricks. The South African opener struck 80 from 54 deliveries, registering his third fifty of the tournament, while Gulbadin Naib's unbeaten 33 from just 13 balls gave Dambulla a late surge. Shahnawaz Dahani finished as Colombo's most successful bowler with two wickets.

Dambulla fought back through Dushmantha Chameera and Akila Dananjaya, who claimed two wickets each, but the Kaps held their composure to seal an important victory and keep their momentum alive in the race for the knockout stage.

Reeza Hendricks 80 (54), Gulbadin Naib 33* (13), Sikandar Raza 23; Shahnawaz Dahani 2/41.

Rubin Hermann 48 (26), Kamindu Mendis 39, Ben McDermott 33, Sadeera Samarawickrama 26; Dushmantha Chameera 2/32, Akila Dananjaya 2/32.
