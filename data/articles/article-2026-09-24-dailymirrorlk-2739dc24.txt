BOI pitches Eastern textile zone to foreign missions as fabric supply hub

The Board of Investment (BOI) has showcased its 255-acre Punnaikuda Eravurpattu Textile Manufacturing Zone to a visiting diplomatic delegation, seeking investment in local fabric production to reduce Sri Lanka’s apparel industry’s reliance on imported materials.

The visit was part of a three-day Eastern province tour organised with the Foreign Affairs Ministry for 25 representatives of Colombo-based foreign missions.

The BOI said the tour was intended to promote the region’s investment, economic and tourism opportunities.

Sri Lanka’s apparel industry imports much of the fabric it uses. The BOI is positioning the Eravurpattu zone to attract mills that can supply the manufacturers locally and retain more value within the country.

It also expects the project to create skilled jobs in the Eastern province, though it provided no figures for the investment secured or jobs created to date.

The delegation toured a Jay Jay Textile facility in the zone. Chief Executive Deepak Kumar said the company, which has operated in Sri Lanka for more than 20 years, was targeting an eventual production capacity of 100 tonnes a day at Eravurpattu. The press release did not give a timetable for reaching that capacity or state the plant’s current output.

Kumar said the BOI duty and tax concessions supported the investment in automated machinery. The facility also has water treatment plants and high-efficiency boilers, according to the BOI.

Diplomats from Switzerland, Pakistan and Vietnam welcomed the opportunity to inspect the zone.

Vietnamese Ambassador Trinh Thai Tam pointed to its dyeing and printing facilities and suggested that the Vietnamese investors examine the opportunities available there.
