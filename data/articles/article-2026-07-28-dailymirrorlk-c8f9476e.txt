India recall Jadeja for Sri Lanka Tests

July.28 (Mirror Sports) - Shubman Gill will lead India in a two-Test series against Sri Lanka, with the opening match set to begin in Galle on August 15 as part of the ICC World Test Championship 2025-27.

The biggest talking point is the return of all-rounder Ravindra Jadeja, who is back in India's Test squad for the first time since January after recovering from a tennis elbow injury that kept him sidelined since May.

Sai Sudharsan and Jasprit Bumrah have also been named in the squad, although both remain subject to fitness.

KL Rahul has been appointed vice-captain, while Rishabh Pant, Yashasvi Jaiswal and Dhruv Jurel bolster the batting line-up. Kuldeep Yadav will partner Jadeja in the spin department, while Mohammed Siraj, Prasidh Krishna and Gurnoor Brar provide the pace options.

India head into the series looking to bounce back after recent Test series defeats to New Zealand, South Africa and Australia. However, they have won their last two Test series in Sri Lanka, claiming victories in 2015 and 2017.

The first Test will be played in Galle from August 15-19, with the second scheduled for Colombo from August 23-27.

India's squad for the Tests against Sri Lanka: Shubman Gill (C), KL Rahul (VC), Yashasvi Jaiswal, Rishabh Pant (WK), Sai Sudharsan*, Dhruv Jurel (WK), Ravindra Jadeja, Kuldeep Yadav, Manav Suthar, Jasprit Bumrah*, Mohammed Siraj, Prasidh Krishna, Gurnoor Brar, Devdutt Padikkal, Saransh Jain. ( * Subject to fitness).
