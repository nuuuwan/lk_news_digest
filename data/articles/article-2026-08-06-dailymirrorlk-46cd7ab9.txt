CMC extends deadline for business trade licences until August 31

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) has extended the deadline for business premises operating within its limits to obtain trade licences until August 31.

The original deadline for obtaining the required trade licences was set to expire on June 30. However, the CMC has granted an additional period for business owners to complete the process.

The Municipal Commissioner said legal action will be taken against business establishments that fail to obtain the required trade licence before August 31, in accordance with the provisions of the Municipal Council Ordinance.
