Principals to decide on school openings tomorrow

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - School principals in the Nuwara Eliya and Hatton education zones have been given the authority to decide whether to open their schools tomorrow (21) in view of the prevailing severe weather conditions.

Nuwara Eliya Zonal Education Director D.M.S.B. Dissanayake said principals had been instructed to assess the weather conditions in their respective areas and decide whether to open the schools.

Speaking to the media this afternoon (20), he said principals had also been instructed to inform the respective Zonal Education Office of their decisions.
