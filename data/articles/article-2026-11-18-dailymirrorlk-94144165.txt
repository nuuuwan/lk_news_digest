Rajanganaya Saddharathana arrested

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Rajanganaya Saddharathana and another individual have been arrested after appearing at the Anuradhapura Police Station to provide statements, police said.

The arrests come a day after Anuradhapura Chief Magistrate Siyapath Sasindu Wickramaratne ordered the Anuradhapura Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) to personally appear before Court on November 26 and explain why Saddharathana Thera and other suspects allegedly involved in instigating a clash near the Ruwanweliseya had not been arrested and produced before Court.

The order was issued yesterday (Nov. 17) following allegations by lawyers representing four Buddhist monks, two male civilians and a woman currently in remand custody over the incident.

Senior lawyers Indika Imbulpitiya and Nuwan Bellanthudawa, appearing for the remanded suspects, told Court that more than 25 individuals, including Rajanganaya Saddharathana Thera, had allegedly instigated the clash at the Satyagraha site in the Dansal Zone near the Ruwanweliseya.

They alleged that while their clients had been arrested and remanded, those accused of provoking the confrontation had been allowed to remain at large without being taken into custody.

The lawyers also questioned the conduct of police officers deployed at the scene, particularly the Anuradhapura Headquarters Chief Inspector, arguing that the confrontation could have been prevented had the police acted appropriately.
