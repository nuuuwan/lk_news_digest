One dead, two injured in Moratuwa Soysapura Housing Complex fire

Colombo, Sep. 23 (Daily Mirror) - One person was killed and two others sustained injuries following a sudden fire that broke out in Block 'C' of the Soysapura Housing Complex in Moratuwa tonight (23).

According to the police, the injured individuals were caught in the blaze and have been rushed for medical treatment. The Moratuwa Fire Unit deployed two fire trucks to the site, and firefighters are actively working to bring the situation under control.

While the exact cause of the fire has not yet been officially confirmed, preliminary investigations suspect that a gas leak may have triggered the blaze.
