‘AI Amplified’ media launch held

A media launch was held to announce the “AI Amplified” initiative, jointly organized by the Ministry of Digital Economy and SLT-Mobitel. The initiative will feature four major events, including the AI Summit, AI Exhibition, and National AI Awards, showcasing Sri Lanka’s potential in artificial intelligence. The event was attended by Deputy Minister of Digital Economy Eng. Eranga Weeraratne, Secretary to the Ministry of Digital Economy Waruna Sri Dhanapala, and Principal Advisor to the President on Sri Lanka’s Digital Economy. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
