Siyapatha Finance opens newest branch in Narammala

Siyapatha Finance PLC unveiled its 69th branch in Narammala recently, further strengthening the company’s presence in the North Western Province.

The branch was declared open by Siyapatha Finance PLC Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Mathisha Hewavitharana, in the presence of Chief Operating Officer (COO) Rajeev De Silva, Siyapatha Shilpaloka recipient Dumashi Navindya, Senior Management and staff members.

Speaking at the ceremony, Siyapatha Finance CEO  Hewavitharana remarked, “We are delighted to extend our network to Narammala. This is a town with a genuinely diversified base — trade and services, small-scale manufacturing in textiles, timber and furniture, and a strong agricultural sector built on paddy and coconut — and its road links to Kurunegala and Colombo make it a natural fit for our expansion strategy.”

With a comprehensive product portfolio catering to the ever-evolving financial needs of entrepreneurs, small and medium-scale enterprises (SMEs) and ambitious individuals, the Narammala branch offers a range of services including leasing, fixed deposits, savings, gold financing, business loans, personal loans, fast draft, factoring, and Smart Pay, the Company’s  bill payment facility.
