Fire catches around 25 houses in Grandpass

Colombo, Oct. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Around 25 houses have caught fire in the Mahawatta area of Grandpass, Colombo, the Colombo Fire Service Department said.

According to the department, six fire engines have been deployed to bring the blaze under control and prevent it from spreading to nearby properties.

The cause of the fire and details of any casualties or injuries have not yet been confirmed.
