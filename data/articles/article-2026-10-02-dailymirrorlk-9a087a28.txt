Macroeconomic stability achieved within two years: Labour Minister

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has achieved macroeconomic stability within the past two years, although some experts had earlier said it could take at least five years, Labour Minister and Deputy Minister of Finance Dr. Anil Jayantha Fernando said.

He said this achievement was possible because of the contribution, cooperation and commitment of all stakeholders involved in the country’s economic recovery.

Dr. Fernando made these remarks on September 30 at a ceremony held at the Ministry of Labour to sign the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) for the Decent Work Country Programme (DWCP) for 2026–2032 with the International Labour Organization (ILO).

He said the main challenge now was to build on the economic stability achieved and move the country towards economic transformation.

“The main challenge before us now is to develop the right strategies to move forward from economic stability and take the country towards economic transformation,” he said.

He said this transformation should support the Government’s wider goal of creating “A Thriving Nation – A Beautiful Life”.

Dr. Fernando said economic growth and development were important to achieve this goal. He added that the Government had given priority to inclusive and sustainable economic growth in its Budget.

He said the Government was now preparing the 2027 Budget, which is expected to be presented later this year. The Budget is being prepared by considering the country’s past and present economic situation and current needs.

The Minister also stressed the importance of decent work in the country’s economic transformation. He said decent work was closely linked to workers’ rights, better working conditions, dignity, freedom and democratic values.

He said the Decent Work Country Programme had been prepared through cooperation between the Government, employers’ organisations and trade unions. The programme focuses on common priorities in the world of work.

Deputy Minister of Labour Mahinda Jayasinghe, ILO Country Director for Sri Lanka Jonny Simpson, New Zealand High Commissioner to Sri Lanka David Pine, Secretary to the Ministry of Labour S. M. Piyatissa, Commissioner General of Labour Nadeeka Wataliyadda, and representatives of employers’ organisations, trade unions and other local and international organisations attended the event.
