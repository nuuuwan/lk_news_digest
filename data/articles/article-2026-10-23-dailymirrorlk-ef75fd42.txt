Palmyrah Seeds Planted Along Valukkai Aru

Volunteers from Save a Life, together with the Palmyrah Conservation and Sustainable Livelihood Development Working Group (PCSD-WG), plant palmyrah seeds along Valukkai Aru, starting from Araly Bridge, under the RECAP Programme. The initiative aims to protect the local environment, preserve the cultural significance of palmyrah trees and support sustainable community livelihoods. Save a Life plans to continue the planting programme, encouraging more volunteers and community members to participate in environmental conservation efforts.
