Ruling party MPs pose for photo in Parliament canteen

Colombo, Sep. 24 (Daily Mirror) - A group of ruling party MPs posed for a photograph with Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) MP Namal Rajapaksa at the members' canteen of Parliament today.

Opposition MP Kins Nelson revealed this during the debate on the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution.

He alleged that Minister Sunil Handunetti had criticised the Opposition despite MPs of his camp posing for a photograph with MP Rajapaksa.

"The Minister said the Opposition staged a protest today against the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution. He should not be cruel as we saw a group of National People's Power (NPP) MPs posing for a photograph with MP Rajapaksa. We also like to pose for a photograph with the Prime Minister," the MP said.
