Special Saturday service for urgent document attestation

Colombo, Oct. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism will offer a special Saturday service for individuals requiring urgent document attestation on October 17, 24 and 31.

The service will be available from 8.30 a.m. to 4.30 p.m. exclusively at the Consular Affairs Division Head Office in Battaramulla.

The Ministry said individuals seeking document attestation under the special arrangement must obtain an appointment in advance through the e-Channeling system.

The Ministry clarified that the special service will be available only at the Battaramulla head office and will not be offered at other regional offices.
