Sri Lanka’s Bank of Ceylon to raise upto Rs30bn in debenture sale

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s state-owned commercial bank, Bank of Ceylon plans to raise 30 billion rupees through the issuance of Basel III-compliant debentures, making it the largest listed debenture issue undertaken in the country’s capital market.

Up to 300 million Basel III compliant, Tier 2, listed, rated, unsecured, subordinated, redeemable 5-year (2026/31) and 7-year (2026/33) debentures will be offered, providing medium to long-term fixed income investment opportunities.

The proposed instruments will carry a non-viability write – down feature, the company said.

BoC proposes to initially raise 10 billion rupees, with an option to increase the issue to 20 billion rupees in the event of an oversubscription of the initial tranche and a further option to increase the total issue size up to 30 billion rupees.

The issue is confined to qualified investors and the minimum subscription for qualified investors is 10,000 rupees, while individual investors are required to apply for at least 5 million rupees.

The debentures are offered in four different types to meet diverse requirements of qualified investors.

The fixed coupon rate of the five-year debenture will be 12.75 percent per annum while the floating coupon rate will be 1.50 percent above the applicable 12-month Treasury Bill rate.

“BOC’s largest debenture issue comes at a significant juncture for the Sri Lankan economy, at a time when the country is prioritizing sustainable, investment-led and inclusive economic growth,” the state-owned lender said.

“Against this backdrop, a stronger capital base will further enhance BOC’s capacity to mobilize and channel funds towards productive sectors of the economy, thereby supporting Sri Lanka’s next phase of economic development. This landmark issue therefore represents more than a significant capital-raising exercise for the Bank. BOC, as the country’s leading state-owned commercial bank, remains well positioned to play a pivotal role in financing businesses, facilitating investment and supporting the broader development priorities of the country.”

BOC is a Corporate Finance Advisor licensed by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka.

This debenture issuance was structured and managed by the Investment Banking team with expertise in structuring innovative capital market instruments.

The issue follows BOC’s pioneering Bond issuance, another transaction structured and managed by the Bank’s Investment Banking Division, reflecting BOC’s continued leadership in developing innovative capital-market solutions while advancing sustainable finance in Sri Lanka.

The Legal Department of BOC acts as the lawyers to the issue, while the Corporate Branch of the Bank acts as the bankers to the issue.

The Investment Banking Division will also continue to act as the Registrar to the proposed issue, while Hatton National Bank PLC will be the trustee to the issue.

The subscription list opens at 9.30 am on November 3 and will remain open for 14 market days unless the bank decides to close it earlier based on subscription levels.

Applications for the debentures will be accepted from qualified investors in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Prospectus.

The Prospectus and application forms can be downloaded from the web sites of the CSE; www.cse.lk and BOC; www.boc.lk.

Duly completed application forms should be enclosed in an envelope marked “BOC Debenture Issue” together with the remittance for the full amount payable on application and delivered to the Investment Banking Division at 7 th Floor of BOC Head Office or collection points mentioned in the prospectus.

The debenture issue is expected to further enhance the bank’s capacity to support corporate, SME, retail and institutional customers while maintaining prudent regulatory capital buffers.

“Bank of Ceylon invites eligible local and foreign investors to participate in this landmark issue and be a part of the Bank’s continuing journey of financial strength and contribution to Sri Lanka’s economic development.”

The debentures carry an issue rating of AA (Stable) from Lanka Rating Agency, while BOC’s issuer rating stands at AA-(lka)/Stable from Fitch Ratings Lanka Ltd.
