New Irrigation DG appointed

Colombo, Sep. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the appointment of Engineer P.M. Jayadeera as the new Director General of Irrigation, effective from October 6, 2026.

The post will fall vacant following the retirement of the incumbent Director General of Irrigation, Dr. (Eng.) K. Weligepolage, who is due to retire from public service on October 6 upon reaching the compulsory retirement age.

Accordingly, Cabinet approved a proposal submitted by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation to appoint P.M. Jayadeera, who currently serves as Additional Secretary (Water Resources Development) at the Ministry.

Jayadeera is an Engineer in the Special Grade of the Sri Lanka Engineering Service.
