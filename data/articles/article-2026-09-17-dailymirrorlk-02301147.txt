Dr. Hans, where is the “People’s Digital Forum?”

If we are building a digital Sri Lanka, shouldn’t the people themselves have a digital seat at the table?

We have appointed one of the country’s most experienced technology leaders to drive digital transformation. We hear about digital government, digital public services, digital identity, AI, etc. But where is the digital equivalent of a public forum where citizens can say what they need, identify problems, challenge proposals and contribute ideas?

Perhaps Sri Lanka needs a national “People’s Digital Forum” — a forum where people could organise discussions around subjects that mattered to them. It could be structured by province, district, local authority OR subject: infrastructure, education, health, agriculture, environment and countless other areas.

Citizens could raise issues, propose solutions, discuss and vote/like on the most prominent ones. These could then be formally referred to the authoritative secretaries or even placed before District Coordinating Committees for consideration.

This would not merely digitise government but would digitize public participation.

Dr. Hans Wijayasuriya, while we are building the digital infrastructure for tomorrow, could we also build a digital platform where the people themselves have a voice in deciding what tomorrow should look like for them?
