Government to seek legal advice on action against private fuel companies

Colombo, October 07 (Daily Mirror)- The government will seek legal advice on possible action against private fuel companies if they continue to restrict fuel supplies and inconvenience the public, Energy Minister Anura Karunathilake told Parliament today.

The Minister said the government does not currently have the authority to order private fuel companies to supply fuel to their fuel stations. However, the Ministry has instructed the companies to maintain minimum fuel stocks in the country in accordance with the agreements entered into with them.

He said a discussion with private fuel operators is scheduled to take place today at the Presidential Secretariat. If the companies continue to limit fuel supplies, the government will seek legal advice on possible action under the terms of the agreements and the powers vested in the Ministry under the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) Act.

Responding to a question raised by SJB MP S.M. Marikkar, Minister Karunathilake said private fuel companies are permitted under their agreements to increase fuel prices to cover their costs under the fuel pricing formula.

However, he noted that the companies have not exercised this option and have instead kept their prices close to those announced by the CPC.
