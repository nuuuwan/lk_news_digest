Namal Rajapaksa and ex-Krrish director Janaki Siriwardena further remanded

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Former director of Krrish Company Janaki Siriwardena and Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa who were arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption over an alleged Rs. 70 million bribe, were ordered to be further remanded till October 16 by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama.
