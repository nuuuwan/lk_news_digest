Unilever Sri Lanka appoints 1st female head of sales

Unilever Sri Lanka has appointed Nilushi Jayatileke as its Head of Sales, becoming the first woman to hold the role in the company’s history.

Nilushi has a strong track record of business development with the Unilever group, across multiple countries.

The appointment also reflects Unilever Sri Lanka’s commitment to diverse leadership, with women now holding 50 percent of departmental leadership positions.

Gayan Rupasinghe, current Head of Sales, has been appointed by Unilever South Africa as Head of Customer Strategy for its ‘Home Care’ business.
