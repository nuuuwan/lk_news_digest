Chinese Dragon Café opens 15th outlet in Kandy

Chinese Dragon Café, one of Sri Lanka’s long-standing names in Chinese cuisine, has further strengthened its presence in the country with the opening of its newest restaurant in Kandy, marking another important milestone in the brand’s expansion journey.

The new outlet brings the brand’s much-loved Chinese cuisine to one of Sri Lanka’s most celebrated cities, extending a legacy that began more than eight decades ago.

Located at No. 454 Piachaud Garden, Kandy, approximately 230 meters from Peradeniya Road towards Piachaud Garden, near Lighthouse Church, the restaurant becomes Chinese Dragon Café’s 14th outlet in Sri Lanka and 15th outlet worldwide.

Renowned for its rich cultural heritage, historical significance and scenic surroundings, Kandy is also one of the country’s leading tourism and commercial destinations, making it a strategic location for the brand to reach a wider customer base comprising local residents, business communities and visitors from across Sri Lanka and overseas.

Chinese Dragon Café Chief Operating Officer Lahiruwan Lakmal said, “Kandy is a city with a distinctive identity, bringing together a rich cultural heritage, a thriving tourism sector, business activity and a diverse community. We recognise the potential of this market and see this expansion as an important opportunity for Chinese Dragon Café to reach new customers. We are delighted to bring our brand and much-loved Chinese cuisine to Kandy. We look forward to welcoming the community and establishing long-term relationships with our customers in the city.”
