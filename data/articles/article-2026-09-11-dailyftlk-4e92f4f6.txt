Treinetic marks 12th anniversary with expansion beyond tech into hospitality and lifestyle

Software product engineering company opens The Legends Lounge in Malabe

Treinetic Ltd., marked its 12th anniversary with the opening of The Legends Lounge, a new hospitality and lifestyle venture, in Malabe on 9 September 2026. The opening marked the Sri Lankan software product engineering company’s expansion beyond the technology sector. The new venue brings together food, coffee, wellness, work and leisure within a single lifestyle-focused space.

The Legends Lounge was developed with an emphasis on quality ingredients, carefully crafted food and beverages, health-conscious choices and signature drinks. It also offers a comfortable setting for customers to work, study, socialise and relax.

Treinetic Founder and CEO Imal Hasaranga Perera said, “The idea grew from a personal need for a place where health-conscious customers could confidently enjoy food made with quality ingredients. Our aim is to serve food and beverages that people can trust while creating an experience that makes them want to return.”

The launch reflected Treinetic’s broader culture of innovation and experimentation as the company celebrated 12 years in the software product engineering industry and embarked on a new chapter through The Legends Lounge.
