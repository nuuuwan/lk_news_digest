Havelock City Mall marks third anniversary

Havelock City Mall (HCM) is celebrating its third anniversary with “Three Is On Us” - a dedicated celebration honouring the shoppers, retail partners, and team members who have driven its journey over the past three years.

Running from 16 to 25 October, the festivities bring together exclusive shopper rewards, entertainment, and partner recognition, reflecting HCM’s appreciation for the community behind its success.

As part of the anniversary celebrations, HCM will recognize the crucial role played by its retail partners and staff in shaping this milestone. A special awards ceremony will honour tenant partners with 34 awards across various categories, while dedicated employees will be recognized for their contributions to the mall’s growth.

Shoppers can look forward to exclusive discounts of up to 30 percent across participating brands, along with live music and entertainment throughout the anniversary period. Adding to the excitement, visitors who spend Rs. 10,000 or more can participate in a special scratch-card promotion offering guaranteed rewards. Designed to create a festive atmosphere, these activities offer family and friends numerous opportunities to celebrate together from 16 to 25 October.
