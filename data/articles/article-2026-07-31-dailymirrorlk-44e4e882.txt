Govt. to permit private sector to purchase dengue vaccine initially: Minister

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Government is planning to permit the private sector to purchase the dengue vaccine once the approval was granted to the vaccine in Sri Lanka, Health Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said yesterday.

He told reporters that the process of approving the dengue vaccine in Sri Lanka is in the final stage and that private sector will be allowed to purchase the vaccine in the first stage.

The Minister said steps are also being taken to adopt the Wolbachia mosquito control method for one and half yeas starting from September this year.

Dr. Jayatissa said he was expecting that these measures will contribute to control the spread of dengue in the country.

Meanwhile, Sri Lanka has recorded 84,091 dengue cases so far this year, with the dengue-related death toll reaching 61, according to the National Dengue Control Unit (NDCU).

According to the NDCU, the highest number of dengue cases has been recorded from the Gampaha, Colombo, Kandy, Matara, Kalutara, and Galle districts.
