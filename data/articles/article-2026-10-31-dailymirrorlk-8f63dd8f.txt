Junior Open Squash Championship concludes with 176 players from nine countries

Colombo, OCt 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Junior Open Squash Championship 2026, an official Asian Junior Super Series (AJSS) event, concluded at the Squash Complex, Sri Lanka Air Force Base, Ratmalana, with the participation of 176 junior players from nine countries.

The five-day tournament, held from October 24 to 28, featured 54 international competitors representing Bangladesh, China, Hong Kong-China, Macao-China, Malaysia, Nepal, Pakistan, Singapore and Sri Lanka.

The competition included 10 age categories across the boys' and girls' Under-11, Under-13, Under-15, Under-17 and Under-19 divisions.

In the boys' Under-19 category, Malaysia's Kesawan Gunasekaran won the title, while Sri Lanka's Matheesha Wijesekara finished as runner-up. In the girls' Under-19 category, Rashnima Shre Ragu claimed the championship, with Thvyashini Muthukumar finishing second.

The tournament was organised under the auspices of the Asian Squash Federation in collaboration with Sri Lanka Squash, providing young players with an opportunity to gain international exposure, competitive experience and ranking points towards national and Asian Squash Federation rankings.

The event also enabled Sri Lankan junior players to compete against international opponents while supporting efforts to promote the country as a destination for international sports events.

Star Gold, a brand under RG Brothers, was the main sponsor, while Winged Falcon served as the official racket partner.
