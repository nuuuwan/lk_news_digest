Diana Gamage pleads guilty to visa offence

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Former MP Diana Gamage today (26) pleaded guilty before the Colombo Magistrate’s Court to a charge of remaining in Sri Lanka without a valid visa.

When the case was taken up before Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama, Attorney-at-Law Mohan Seneviratne, appearing on behalf of Gamage, informed the court that his client was pleading guilty to the charge.
