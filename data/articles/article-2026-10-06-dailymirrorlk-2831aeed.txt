Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Commercial Bank and Ambeon Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.2 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. Crossings were witnessed in Access Engineering, Lanka IOC and Sunshine Holdings accounting for 40.6% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Bank, John Keells Holdings and Richard Pieris & Company whilst retail interest was noted in HNB Finance, Renuka Agri Foods and LVL Energy Fund. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Access Engineering and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index lost 0.55%. The share price of Access Engineering decreased by Rs 0.20 (0.25%) to close at Rs 79.40. The share price of John Keells Holdings moved down by Rs 0.20 (1.08%) to close at Rs 18.40. Energy sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Lanka IOC) whilst the sector index increased by 0.15%. The share price of Lanka IOC lost Rs 0.50 (0.39%) to close at Rs 128.50.

Sunshine Holdings and Commercial Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Sunshine Holdings recorded a loss of Rs 0.60 (2.07%) to close at Rs 28.40. The share price of Commercial Bank declined by Rs 1.00 (0.50%) to close at Rs 201.00.
