Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Cargills , Dialog Axiata and Citizens Development Business Finance with the turnover crossing Rs. 797mn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in C T Holdings, Ceylinco Holdings nonvoting and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in Haycarb, Sierra Cables and Sampath Bank whilst retail interest was noted in HNB Finance, Janashakthi Limited and Hunas Falls Hotels. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.01%. The share price of Sierra Cables closed flat at Rs. 34.60. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a loss of Rs. 0.10 (0.50%) to close at Rs. 19.80.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Haycarb) whilst the sector index increased by 0.45%. The share price of Haycarb increased by Rs. 5.00 (2.69%) to close at Rs. 190.75. C T Holdings and Sampath Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of C T Holdings lost Rs. 5.75 (1.04%) to close at Rs. 544.75. The share price of Sampath Bank appreciated by Rs. 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs. 140.00.
