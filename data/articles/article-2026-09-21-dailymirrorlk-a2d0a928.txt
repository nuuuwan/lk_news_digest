Government to appeal to Saudi King for Anojan’s pardon

Colombo, Sep. 21 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake met the parents of Sivarasa Anojan, a Sri Lankan national sentenced to death in Saudi Arabia, at the Presidential Secretariat this morning.

The President assured them that the Government was making every possible effort to secure a pardon for Anojan through both legal and diplomatic channels.

He said the Government would make a formal written diplomatic appeal to the King of Saudi Arabia seeking a pardon for Anojan.

The President also briefed Anojan’s parents on measures already taken by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, including the submission of appeals to the relevant authorities.

He further assured them that the Government was closely monitoring the progress of these efforts and would continue to pursue all possible avenues to secure a pardon.
