Cabinet green lights new building complex for Puttalam District Secretariat

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the construction of a new building complex for the Puttalam District Secretariat, including a four-storey office building, a 1,000-seat auditorium and a parking area.

The new complex will be built within the premises where the existing Secretariat is located.

A building complex consisting of three sections was first proposed for the Puttalam District Secretariat in 2013. To date, however, only its southern four-storey building has been constructed.

Due to insufficient space and facilities in that building, divisions of the office have had to be maintained at various locations. This has hindered the coordination of administrative activities and the direct supervision of operations by the District Secretary.

Constructing a building complex equipped with modern facilities has therefore been identified as a priority. The objectives are to establish office divisions formally under one roof, strengthen administrative coordination, facilitate public services, and provide adequate facilities for district-level meetings and conferences.

The proposal to this effect was submitted by Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government Minister Prof. A.H.M.H. Abayarathna.
