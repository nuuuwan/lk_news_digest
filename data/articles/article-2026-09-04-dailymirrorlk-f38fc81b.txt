ICBS’ LEVEL UP 2.0 on Sept. 9 in Kandy

Brings professional platform back to Kandy with a sharper focus on action

Imperial College of Business Studies (ICBS) returns to Kandy with LEVEL UP 2.0 – The Next Million Mindset on September 9, 2026, at Kandy Myst by Cinnamon.

Following 2024’s Inspiring Future Leaders edition, the platform now shifts focus from inspiration to practical, actionable investment in career and wealth.

Kandy is home to a growing base of professionals, entrepreneurs and business leaders. Opportunities to engage directly with established industry figures remain limited. LEVEL UP was created to close that gap and the 2026 edition builds on the momentum of last year’s debut.

Built around the theme ‘Building Wealth. Careers. Futures.’, the evening features three keynote conversations. Dialog Axiata PLC Head of Business Fixed Broadband and International Services Tharindra Gooneratne will speak on investing in wealth. LAUGFS Gas Cluster CEO Dr. Niroshan J. Pieris will address career investment. Infinite Grace Works (Pvt.) Ltd Director Stephanie Siriwardhana will close the segment on investing in yourself.

The three conversations will converge in a panel discussion moderated by AICPA & CIMA Sri Lanka Country Head Tharindu Wijewardhana, followed by audience Q&A, dinner and networking.

Doors will open from 6:00 p.m. onwards, with registrations open at 94 76 618 9860.

Through LEVEL UP 2.0, ICBS continues to build a platform for Kandy’s professional community, one built on direct access, real conversations and long-term connection.

ICBS was established in 1985. The college offers business education in partnership with international institutions, with a focus on critical thinking, practical expertise and ethical leadership. ICBS prepares students for careers in business through industry-relevant programmes.
