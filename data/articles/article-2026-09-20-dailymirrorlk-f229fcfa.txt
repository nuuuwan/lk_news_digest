Heavy rains exceeding 100 mm forecast in several areas

Colombo, September 20 (Daily Mirror)- The Meteorology Department has issued a heavy rainfall advisory for the Western and Sabaragamuwa provinces, as well as the Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Galle, and Matara districts, due to the prevailing active Southwest Monsoon.

The Department warned that some areas in these regions could receive heavy rainfall exceeding 100 mm.

Kegalle District recorded the highest rainfall during the 24-hour period ending at 6.00 a.m. today, with 103.5 mm of rain reported.
