Navy intercepts trawler suspected of smuggling narcotics in southern waters

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has intercepted a multi-day fishing trawler suspected of transporting a consignment of narcotics during an operation conducted in international waters off the southern coast.

The trawler, along with its crew, is being escorted to shore for further investigation.

The Navy said additional details regarding the operation would be released in due course.
