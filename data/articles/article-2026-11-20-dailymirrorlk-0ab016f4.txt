Poland says Russian missile hit its territory during attack on Ukraine

FRANCE 24 - Warsaw on Friday said that at least one Russian missile hit southeast Poland during Moscow's overnight attack on Ukraine, adding that authorities were investigating three impact sites, with a drone and a possible second missile also suspected.

"There are three impact sites. In Nowy Lipowiec, we can already confirm it was a Kh-101 missile, in the second place there is also talk of a missile, in the third it was most likely a jet-powered drone," Poland's Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said, according to Polish media.

"Nobody was hurt, we did not note any infrastructure destruction," he added.

The Russian embassy in Warsaw did ‌not immediately respond to an emailed request for comment.

Drones have repeatedly strayed ⁠into the airspace of countries on NATO's eastern flank since Russia invaded Ukraine in February 2022, fuelling tension with the NATO military alliance and stoking fears ​that the war could spread.

Footage ‌shared on social media from what appeared to be a security camera at a property in the nearby village ​of Chmielek showed an explosion in the distance.

Polish Infrastructure Minister Dariusz Klimczak said on X that a traffic controller had heard a loud bang near railway premises in Rudnik ​nad Sanem, about 50 kilometres west of Nowy Lipowiec.

He said no visible ​damage to the railway had been found but that an ​inspection vehicle had been dispatched.

Klimczak also said air traffic controllers had been evacuated at the airport in Rzeszow, the largest ​city in southeastern Poland.

Prime Minister Donald Tusk said he had received reports about security in the area and that there was currently no threat from the air.

European countries are worried that Russia's war in Ukraine could spill ⁠over Ukraine's border, and are seeking a place at talks on ending the conflict.
