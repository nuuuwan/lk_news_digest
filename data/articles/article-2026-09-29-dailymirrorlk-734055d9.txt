Lanka Aluminium Industries opens 10th retail outlet in Kalutara

Lanka Aluminium Industries PLC opened its 10th retail outlet in Nagoda, Kalutara recently.

“Our vision is to enable our customers to be able to access our solutions and services right where they need it. To support this vision, we will continue to expand our retail outlets across the island providing easy access to technical advice, shop drawings, wind load calculations, and other technical services,” said Dinal Peiris – Chairman, Lanka Aluminium Industries PLC.

In addition to high-quality aluminium extrusions, the retail outlets also carry general accessories required for the making of doors and windows, all under one roof.

“With the expanding presence of our fully owned sales outlets across the country, the company’s responsibility and accountability is extended and brought closer to our end-customers through these sales and service centers, which creates empathy with the trade while adhering to our strict corporateethical standards,” added Susantha Paranagama, Head of Retail Development.
