Indeewari receives Global Star of WiLAT Award

Indeewari Chandrasekara MILT, Executive Committee Member of WiLAT Sri Lanka, has been awarded the prestigious Global Star of WiLAT (SoW) Award 2026, one of WiLAT Global’s recognitions for emerging women leaders in logistics, transport and supply chain.

The award was presented at CILT International Convention 2026, at Hopewell Hotel, Hong Kong.

The SoW Award is a global initiative of Women in Logistics and Transport (WiLAT), established to recognise and encourage young, progressive and talented women professionals making a meaningful impact in the industry.

Beyond celebrating achievement, the programme serves as a leadership development and succession initiative, identifying the emerging female leaders committed to advancing the aspirations and future growth of WiLAT.

WiLAT functions as a global network within the Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (CILT), advancing professional development, mentoring, networking, knowledge sharing and leadership opportunities for women. WiLAT Sri Lanka is an integral part of this global network, working in alignment with WiLAT Global to advance women’s professional development and leadership in Sri Lanka.

Chandrasekara’s recognition reflects a journey of dedication, leadership and service within the WiLAT fraternity. Having contributed to WiLAT Sri Lanka since 2018, she began as an IGNITE Mentoring Programme mentee before returning as Project Chair of IGNITE 11 in 2025/26, where she led the programme and championed the development of future industry professionals.

Reflecting on her journey, Chandrasekara shared, “WiLAT has been much more than a professional network in my journey. It has given me the confidence to grow, courage to lead and opportunity to give back. From joining as an IGNITE mentee to serving as a mentor and project leader, WiLAT shaped both my career and my leadership philosophy. This recognition is not just a celebration of my journey but a reminder of the importance of creating opportunities for the next generation of women to lead, innovate and thrive in our industry.”

Under her leadership, WiLAT Sri Lanka strengthened strategic industry partnerships that created internship opportunities for IGNITE mentees, bridging academia and industry while providing practical exposure and career development opportunities.

Her contributions have also extended beyond Sri Lanka. She served as Editor of Wings of Change, the Global WiLAT newsletter and within CILT Sri Lanka as Editor of Link Magazine and Project Chair of the Ideathon, an international virtual initiative conducted during the COVID-19 pandemic.

A Gold Medallist and graduate of the University of Moratuwa and a CIMA Passed Finalist, Chandrasekara currently serves as Unit Supply Chain Manager at Unilever Sri Lanka, contributing to supply chain transformation, operational excellence, digital innovation and capability development.

WiLAT Sri Lanka warmly congratulates Chandrasekara MILT on this prestigious international recognition and celebrates her continued commitment to inspiring, mentoring and empowering the next generation of logistics, transport and supply chain professionals.
