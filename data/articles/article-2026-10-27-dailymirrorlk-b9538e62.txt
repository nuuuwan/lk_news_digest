Sri Lanka’s sexual health and HIV experts forum to begin on October 29

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The 31st Annual Session of the Sri Lanka Sexual Health and HIV Experts Forum will be held in Colombo from October 29 to 31, bringing together local and international experts to discuss developments in sexual health and HIV.

The conference will take place in Colombo, with the inaugural ceremony scheduled for October 29 at 6.30 pm under the patronage of Deputy Minister of Health Dr. Hansaka Wijemuni.

Representatives of the British HIV Association, which is sponsoring the conference, are also expected to participate alongside local and foreign experts.

The conference aims to promote knowledge sharing and support the development of policies relating to sexual health and HIV in Sri Lanka and internationally.

The Health and Mass Media Ministry said experts attending the conference would also be available to engage with media institutions to raise public awareness of reproductive health and HIV-related issues.

The ministry has invited newspapers, radio and television stations, and online media platforms to engage with the experts and disseminate information to the public.
