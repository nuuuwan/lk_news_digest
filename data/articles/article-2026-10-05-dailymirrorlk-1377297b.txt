Cancer patients face shortage of essential medicines, colostomy bags

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Cancer patients receiving treatment at Apeksha Hospital in Maharagama are facing a shortage of several essential medicines and surgical consumables, including colostomy bags, according to an appeal issued by the hospital.

The hospital has sought donations of several medicines and surgical items required for the treatment and care of patients.

Among the medicines requested are Tigecycline 50mg injections, Methotrexate 500mg injections, Fluorouracil 1g injections, Methotrexate 2.5mg tablets, Voriconazole 200mg tablets and Chlorambucil 2mg capsules.

The hospital has also requested adult colostomy bags and plates, which are required as surgical consumables for patients.

Specialist Dr. Chamal Sanjeewa, Chairman of the Medical and Civil Rights Doctors' Trade Union Alliance, said the shortage of medicines and surgical equipment made it important for the public to assist the hospital by donating the items required by patients.

He urged donors to prioritise essential medicines and surgical equipment rather than food items when providing assistance to Apeksha Hospital.

According to the hospital's notice, donated medicines must be accompanied by the original invoices, while only medicines approved by the National Medicines Regulatory Authority (NMRA) will be accepted.

Donations will be accepted on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. and on Saturdays from 8 a.m. to 12 noon.
