Surgery planned for sick zebra at Dehiwala Zoo

A female zebra at the Dehiwala Zoo is receiving treatment for a digestive tract obstruction, with surgery recommended by veterinary specialists after its condition failed to improve with medical treatment.

The zebra, one of two female zebras at the zoo, has been showing signs of illness since September 29, 2026.

Tests and scans revealed a blockage in its digestive tract, and the zoo's veterinary team has been treating the animal.

However, as its condition did not return to normal, the zoo authorities sought the expertise of Dr. Dhammika Perera, a senior lecturer specialising in equine species at the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine, University of Peradeniya, on October 8.

Following further examinations, the veterinary team, on the advice of the specialist, recommended surgery to remove the obstruction, as it could not be resolved through medical treatment alone.

The surgery was scheduled for the evening of October 9, with the participation of the specialist veterinarian and the Dehiwala Zoo's veterinary officers.

The National Zoological Gardens Department said it was taking the necessary steps to provide appropriate medical care and improve the animal's condition based on specialist advice.

The department also said further updates on the zebra's health would be provided as necessary.
