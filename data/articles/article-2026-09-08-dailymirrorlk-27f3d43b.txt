Profitable Lanka Sathosa outlets face closure as property owners reclaim buildings

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Several profitable Lanka Sathosa outlets have been forced to close, while others are facing possible closure after property owners sought the return of buildings leased to the state-run retailer, Deputy Minister of Trade, Commerce and Food Security R. M. Jayawardhana told Parliament.

Responding to a question raised by SLPP MP Chanaka Madugoda, the Deputy Minister said the Lanka Sathosa outlets in Karapitiya and Hikkaduwa had been closed due to difficulties related to the availability of suitable space.

He also revealed that the Thanamalvila outlet is facing closure after its building owner, former Minister Jagath Pushpakumara, requested the return of the premises.

Jayawardhana said finding suitable locations for new Lanka Sathosa outlets has become challenging, as the Ministry requires buildings with essential facilities, including adequate parking and a minimum floor area of 3,000 square feet.

He further noted that a number of loss-making outlets had been closed because they were established in locations that were not commercially viable or market-oriented.
