Vijitha Herath telephones Nepal Foreign Minister; offers support

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath said he held a productive call with Nepal’s Foreign Minister, Shisir Khanal, to discuss the current situation following the devastating floods.

He said in a post on X that to ensure Sri Lanka provides the right support, the Disaster Management Centres will coordinate closely on any specific assistance required.

"Sri Lanka remains steadfast in its solidarity and commitment to our close friend and neighbour, Nepal", the Minister said.
