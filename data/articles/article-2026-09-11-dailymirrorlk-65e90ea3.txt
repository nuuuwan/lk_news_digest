SLPP to hold mass rally in Anuradhapura tomorrow

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) will hold a mass rally in Anuradhapura tomorrow (12), National Convener D.V. Chanaka said.

He said the rally would be held to protest what the party described as the Government’s failure to fulfil promises made to farmers and the public.

Chanaka said the rally would also seek to pressure the Government to fulfil the commitments it had made to the people.
