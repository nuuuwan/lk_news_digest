Drug trafficker’s property seized

Land and houses in the Dambulla area belonging to a suspected drug trafficker Shiran Bazik, who had been arrested by Dubai security forces and handed over to the Sri Lanka Police, were seized yesterday by the Central Crime Investigation Bureau, and entry was prohibited pending further investigations.

Police said, after investigating illegal assets belonging to the suspect, they obtained a court order to seize those pending further investigations.

The investigators prohibited entry into three blocks of land and two houses at Thittawlgolla in Dambulla belonging to the suspect.

The notice indicated that the owners could claim their rights to the land and property through action in the High Court
