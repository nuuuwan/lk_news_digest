Yaden Laboratories becomes first SL pharmaceutical manufacturer to exhibit at CPHI Europe 2026

Yaden Laboratories (Pvt) Ltd, a Sri Lankan manufacturer of sterile injectable pharmaceutical products, has achieved a milestone by becoming the first Sri Lankan pharmaceutical manufacturing company to exhibit at CPHI Europe 2026, held in Milan, Italy, from 6 to 8 this month.

The achievement represents a significant step forward for Sri Lanka’s pharmaceutical manufacturing industry, highlighting the country’s growing capabilities and ambitions to establish itself within the global pharmaceutical supply chain. CPHI Milan 2026, one of the world’s largest and most influential pharmaceutical industry exhibitions, brought together an international community for which published event figures indicated over 62,000 pharmaceutical industry professionals, more than 2,900 exhibiting companies and representation from over 166 countries.

The three-day exhibition provided a global platform for pharmaceutical manufacturers, innovators, technology providers, distributors and industry leaders to explore strategic partnerships, emerging technologies and international business opportunities.

Against this backdrop, Yaden Laboratories’ participation stands as a landmark achievement, placing Sri Lanka’s pharmaceutical manufacturing industry alongside some of the world’s most established pharmaceutical companies.

Under the leadership of Chairman and Managing Director Shashimal Dissanayake and Chief Executive Officer Prakash Babu, Yaden Laboratories has continued to invest in advanced pharmaceutical manufacturing technologies, infrastructure and internationally aligned quality standards.

The exhibition also offered opportunities to engage with international pharmaceutical manufacturers, distributors and potential strategic partners, laying the foundation for future collaborations, technology transfers and expansion into new export markets.

Commenting on the historic milestone, Chairman and Managing Director Shashimal Dissanayake said, “I first attended CPHI Europe as a visitor in 2013, and since then, it has always been my ambition to see a Sri Lankan pharmaceutical manufacturing company represented at this international exhibition. Thirteen years later, witnessing Yaden Laboratories exhibit alongside more than 2,900 global pharmaceutical companies is an immensely proud moment, not only for me personally, but for our entire team and for Sri Lanka.”
