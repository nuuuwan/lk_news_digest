Thabbowa Reservoir spill gates to be opened, residents warned

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Two spill gates of the Thabbowa Reservoir are expected to be opened by 10 p.m. today to control the rising water levels amid prevailing rainy weather conditions, the Irrigation Department said.

Each gate is expected to be opened by six inches, releasing an estimated 220 cubic feet of water per second into the Mee Oya.

The Irrigation Department has urged residents living along both banks of the Mee Oya downstream of the Thabbowa Reservoir to remain vigilant and closely monitor changes in water levels.

The department said the extent of spill gate openings could be adjusted depending on the volume of water flowing into the reservoir and rainfall in its catchment area.

Residents have been advised to follow announcements and safety instructions issued by the Irrigation Department and Disaster Management authorities.
