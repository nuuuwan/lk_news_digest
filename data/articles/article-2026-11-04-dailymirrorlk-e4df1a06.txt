57th Infantry Division joins ‘Hasthi Sena’ operation

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The 57th Infantry Division has joined ‘Operation Hasthi Sena’ to identify unauthorised electric fences installed by villagers to protect their properties from wild elephants in several villages near Nelliya and Kumbukgete.

The Sri Lanka Army, together with officials from the Department of Wildlife Conservation, conducted a special inspection on November 4 covering Valgala, Uthuruwella, Ahugoda, Polwatthayaya and Puhulyaya.

The operation was carried out within the area of responsibility of the 571st Infantry Brigade under the 57th Infantry Division, with the aim of protecting wild elephants and reducing human-elephant conflict.

During the inspection, officials found that around 50 to 60 houses in the area were surrounded by locally constructed electric fences that did not meet the required standards.

Although the fences were not connected to the main electricity supply at the time of the inspection, officials observed that they could be connected whenever necessary.

Villagers informed officials that wild elephants had not entered the area during the past one-and-a-half months but usually visited during the harvest season.

Wildlife officials advised residents to obtain approval from the Department of Wildlife Conservation before installing electric fences and to ensure that such fences comply with the required standards and legal provisions.

The Department also plans to conduct awareness programmes for villagers with the support of Divisional Secretaries and Grama Niladharis.
