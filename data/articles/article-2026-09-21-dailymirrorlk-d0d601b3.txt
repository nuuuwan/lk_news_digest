One dead, seven injured following wasp attack in Lindula

One plantation worker was killed and seven others injured in a wasp attack while they were working at the Waltrim Estate today (21).

The eight workers were reportedly attacked after a wasp nest attached to a tree in the tea estate was suddenly disturbed amid prevailing adverse weather conditions.

The deceased is a 77-year-old father of three and a resident of the Len Thomas section of the Waltrim Estate.

The seven injured workers were treated at the Lindula Regional Hospital and subsequently discharged.

The deceased’s body has been taken to the Nuwara Eliya District General Hospital for a post-mortem examination.
