Odel resumes Colombo 7 mall construction

Odel PLC has resumed construction of the first phase of its long-delayed mall project in Colombo 7, with completion expected within the next one-and-a-half to two years.

In a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange, the Softlogic Holdings subsidiary said the mixed-use development had originally comprised two zones, but the project plan had since been revised to prioritise the completion of Zone 1.

“Construction work of Zone 1 has recommenced and is progressing as at date,” the company said.

Odel did not disclose the current level of construction completed, the revised investment value or an expected opening date for the development.

Zone 2, comprising a 100-perch block of land, will remain available for future development. The company provided no timeline or further details for that phase.

Odel said the disclosure was made to clarify the current status of the project and address speculative or inaccurate representations surrounding the development.
