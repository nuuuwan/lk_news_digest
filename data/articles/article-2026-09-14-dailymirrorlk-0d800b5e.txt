Hilton Colombo celebrates 39 years at heart of  Sri Lankan hospitality

Sri Lanka’s flagship Hilton property reflects on nearly four decades of iconic occasions, culinary experiences and warm hospitality in heart of Colombo

Hilton Colombo marks its 39th anniversary this September, celebrating nearly four decades as one of Sri Lanka’s pioneering international hotels and the flagship Hilton property in the country.

Since opening in 1987, the hotel has grown alongside Colombo itself, becoming a familiar part of the city’s story and a place where international travellers, local guests, world leaders, families and generations of team members have come together.

Over the years, Hilton Colombo has been the setting for moments that have shaped both the hotel and the city around it. The hotel has hosted Commonwealth Heads of State, former US presidents and visiting dignitaries, while its event spaces have hosted occasions ranging from CHOGM 2013 and Colombo Fashion Week to official state banquets and national celebrations.

More than 20,000 weddings have been celebrated at the hotel, making Hilton Colombo part of countless family stories spanning across generations.

The hotel’s role in Colombo has extended well beyond accommodation. With 368 guestrooms and suites, six restaurants and bars and 2,891 square metres of meetings and events space, Hilton Colombo has long served as a meeting point for business, celebration and leisure.

Its pillar-less Grand Ballroom has welcomed some of the city’s most significant corporate and social gatherings, while venues such as The Blue and Ruby have provided distinctive settings for occasions both large and intimate.

Hilton Colombo has also played a defining role in the city’s dining and social landscape. Graze Kitchen presents international and local cuisine through its signature show kitchens, whilst Emperor’s Wok, the hotel’s specialty Chinese restaurant, showcases the fine-dining heritage of the Chinese cuisine.

L.A.B. has become renowned for leisurely afternoons by the Lotus Pond and lively jazz evenings, Café Kai for handcrafted cakes and pastries, SunsetBlu for poolside dining and after-dark energy and Stella Karaoke Lounge & Bar for nights centred on music and good company.

Oktoberfest at Hilton Colombo, now a tradition spanning more than three decades, remains one of the hotel’s most recognisable annual celebrations and will be taking place this year from October 29 to November 07.

Hilton Sri Lanka Area General Manager and Hilton Colombo General Manager Manesh Fernando said, “Hilton Colombo has had the privilege of growing alongside this city and being part of many of its defining moments.”
