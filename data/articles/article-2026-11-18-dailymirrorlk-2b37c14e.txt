Lahiru Kumara five-for fails to halt Pakistan

Nov.18 (Mirror Sports) - Pakistan strengthened their grip on the second Test against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi, taking a commanding 227-run first-innings lead before reducing the visitors to 74 for 3 at stumps on day two.

Sri Lanka, who were dismissed for 98 in their first innings, still trail by 153 runs after Pakistan posted 325, with Lahiru Kumara claiming five wickets despite valuable lower-order contributions from the hosts.

Pakistan resumed the day at 121 for 3, holding a 23-run lead, but Kumara struck repeatedly to put the home side under pressure. The Sri Lankan paceman claimed three wickets during the morning session before completing his five-wicket haul with figures of 5 for 95.

Azan Awais, who was unbeaten on 47 overnight, fell without adding to his score, while Saud Shakeel made 24 and nightwatcher Saqib Khan contributed 37. Salman Agha was dismissed for 21 as Pakistan faced further resistance from the Sri Lankan attack.

However, Pakistan's lower order turned the innings around with important contributions after lunch. Ghazi Ghori registered his maiden Test half-century, scoring 54 off 90 deliveries, while Razaullah struck an aggressive 42 off 42 balls, including three sixes.

Their efforts helped Pakistan reach 325, establishing a substantial 227-run advantage.

Sri Lanka's second innings began poorly as Lahiru Udara departed for seven, caught at cover off Saqib Khan. Razaullah then removed Dinesh Chandimal for a duck, leaving the visitors in early trouble at 10 for 2.

Kamindu Mendis briefly counterattacked with 16 off 25 balls but edged Jamal to slip, leaving Sri Lanka at 39 for 3.

Dhananjaya de Silva provided some resistance with an unbeaten 24, while Pasindu Sooriyabandara remained not out on 18 as bad light brought an early end to proceedings at 4.38pm local time.

Sri Lanka will resume day three at 74 for 3, still 153 runs behind Pakistan, with the hosts firmly positioned to push for victory.
