AIA sets new national record with 416 MDRT qualifiers

AIA Insurance Lanka announced its continued leadership in the life insurance industry, having once again secured the position of Sri Lanka’s #1 MDRT (Million Dollar Round Table) Company for the second consecutive year, with the highest number of MDRT-qualified insurance professionals in the country.

With an outstanding 416 MDRT qualifiers in 2026 across its Agency Distribution and Partnership Distribution channels, AIA continues to raise the bar for professionalism and customer-centric service in the insurance industry. With this achievement, AIA sets a new national benchmark as the highest number of MDRT-qualified professionals ever recorded by a single company in Sri Lanka.

MDRT membership is internationally recognised as the pinnacle of achievement in the life insurance and financial services profession. Earning MDRT status requires advisers to meet rigorous production benchmarks, demonstrate exceptional professional expertise, uphold the highest ethical standards, and consistently deliver outstanding value to customers.

Commenting on this milestone, Chathuri Munaweera, Director/Chief Executive Officer of AIA Insurance Lanka, said, “Achieving the position of Sri Lanka’s #1 MDRT company for the second consecutive year, while setting a new national record with 416 MDRT qualifiers, is a remarkable accomplishment for AIA. This milestone reflects the professionalism and customer commitment of our distribution force, who consistently go above and beyond to help Sri Lankans live Healthier, Longer, Better Lives.”

AIA Sri Lanka’s achievement is part of a broader success story across the region. In 2026, AIA Group marks 12 consecutive years as the world’s #1 MDRT Multinational Company, further strengthening its position as the global benchmark for professional excellence in life insurance. Across multiple markets, AIA has consistently nurtured one of the world’s largest communities of MDRT-qualified insurance professionals, reflecting the strength of its people, culture, and commitment to customer outcomes.
