Week ends in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Hatton National Bank, LOLC Holdings and Ambeon Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 958 Mn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Commercial Bank, Lanka Realty Investments and Ambeon Capital. Mixed interest was observed in LOLC Holdings, Softlogic Capital and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Singer Finance Rights, ACME Printing & Packaging and HNB Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 gained 2.3% and 2.4% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.4 Bn. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index lost 0.51%.

The share price of Commercial Bank decreased by Rs 0.25 (0.13%) to close at Rs 199.25. Diversified Financials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Lolc Holdings and Softlogic Capital) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.33%.

The share price of Lolc Holdings lost Rs 14.25 (3.66%) to close at Rs 375.25. The share price of Softlogic Capital closed flat at Rs 9.20. Lanka Realty Investments and Ambeon Capital were also included amongst the top turnover contributors.

The share price of Lanka Realty Investments recorded a gain of Rs 0.20 (0.43%) to close at Rs 46.20. The share price of Ambeon Capital declined by Rs 0.90 (3.11%) to close at Rs 28.00.
