Govt. prepares for El Niño food supply risks

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development Ministry has begun preparations to ensure an uninterrupted supply of essential food items and stable prices amid the possible global impact of the El Niño weather phenomenon.

A special discussion on the issue was held at the Ministry today (25) under the patronage of Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe.

Although the direct impact of El Niño on Sri Lanka is expected to be minimal, officials noted that disruptions in other countries could indirectly affect the availability and prices of imported essential food items.

As a precautionary measure, the stocks held by importers of essential food commodities were reviewed, while relevant data from Sri Lanka Customs was also examined.

The Minister urged importers to act responsibly and refrain from making statements that could create unnecessary fear among the public, stressing that the issue should be handled as a national concern.

Discussions were also held on ensuring adequate supplies of essential commodities, including dhal, potatoes, salt, dried fish and sugar, while maintaining stable market prices.

It was agreed to continuously monitor market conditions and weather developments and take necessary measures to prevent possible shortages or price increases.

The meeting was attended by Ministry Secretary K.A. Wimalenthirarajah, Consumer Affairs Authority Chairman Hemantha Samarakoon, Cooperative Wholesale Establishment Chairman Kosala Wilbhava and Lanka Sathosa Chairman Ravindra Fernando, along with other officials and representatives of essential food importers.
