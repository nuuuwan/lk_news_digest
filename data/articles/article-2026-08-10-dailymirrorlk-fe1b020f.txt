Three underworld suspects arrested in Malaysia to be brought back to Sri Lanka

Colombo, Aug. 10(Daily Mirror) -Three individuals suspected of involvement in organized crime, including “Ratmalana Saima,” a key associate of alleged organized crime figure Kanjipani Imran, who were arrested in Malaysia, are scheduled to be brought back to Sri Lanka today under a special security operation.

The operation was carried out through cooperation between Interpol, Malaysian Police and a Sri Lankan police team comprising Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) Rohan Olugala, Director of the Walana Anti-Corruption Force, and ASP Wasantha Pitiyakumbura of the Legal Division. Following their arrival in Sri Lanka, the suspects are expected to be handed over to the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) for further investigations.

According to investigators, “Ratmalana Saima” had allegedly served as a key operator in a drug network and other criminal activities linked to Kanjipani Imran. Investigations have also reportedly revealed that he accumulated significant wealth through the alleged narcotics trade and owned several businesses, including a five-storey hotel in Thailand.

Authorities believe Saima’s arrest has dealt a significant blow to the alleged organized crime and drug operations connected to Imran in Malaysia. The arrests were the result of a special operation launched several weeks ago after ASP Rohan Olugala and ASP Wasantha Pitiyakumbura travelled to Malaysia to assist in locating and apprehending the suspects.

Working through the Sri Lankan Embassy in Malaysia and with the assistance of Malaysian law enforcement authorities and Interpol, investigators reportedly tracked down and arrested Saima, who was described as one of Imran’s closest associates.
