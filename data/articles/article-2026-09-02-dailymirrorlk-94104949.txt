Sri Lanka, China to ease immigration procedures for business and investment

Sri Lanka and China are set to strengthen cooperation on immigration matters, with the Cabinet approving the signing of a bilateral Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) aimed at facilitating business, investment, tourism and professional exchanges between the two countries.

The agreement between Sri Lanka’s Department of Immigration and Emigration and China’s National Immigration Administration is expected to improve coordination on visa, immigration and emigration matters affecting citizens involved in business, investment, technology and scientific activities.

The MoU will provide a framework for the two countries to exchange official information and intelligence and maintain regular discussions and visits between senior immigration officials.

From a business perspective, the agreement could support smoother cross-border movement of investors, entrepreneurs, professionals and other visitors, helping facilitate stronger economic and commercial links between Sri Lanka and China.

The proposed cooperation will also include measures to combat organised crime and human trafficking, while workshops will be conducted to enhance the knowledge, skills and capacity of immigration officials.

The draft MoU has received clearance from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism and the Attorney General.

Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs to proceed with the signing of the agreement.
