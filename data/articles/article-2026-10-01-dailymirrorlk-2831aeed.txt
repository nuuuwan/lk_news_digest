Indices close in red

The ASPI edged down as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Commercial Bank and Carson Cumberbatch with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.6 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Access Engineering, Softlogic Life Insurance and Lanka Realty Investments. Mixed interest was observed in John Keells Holdings, Softlogic Capital and Sampath Bank whilst retail interest was noted in LOLC Finance, Industrial Asphalts and Agstar. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Access Engineering and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.10%. The share price of Access Engineering increased by Rs 1.10 (1.39%) to close at Rs 80.00. The share price of John Keells Holdings lost Rs 0.20 (1.06%) to close at Rs 18.70.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index closed flat 0.00%. The share price of Sampath Bank recorded a gain of Rs 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs 140.00.

Softlogic Life Insurance and Softlogic Capital were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Softlogic Life Insurance moved up by Rs 2.40 (3.24%) to close at Rs 76.50. The share price of Softlogic Capital appreciated by Rs 0.60 (6.59%) to close at Rs 9.70.
