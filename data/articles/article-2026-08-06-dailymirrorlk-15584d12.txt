Non-SLS helmets seized; two traders fined

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Mahara Magistrate's Court has fined a retailer and a distributor for selling and distributing protective helmets without the mandatory Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLS) certification mark and ordered the destruction of the seized stock.

The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) said legal action was instituted by its Gampaha District Investigation Unit against New Indigahamula Oil Mart in Weliweriya and Rider Helmet in Makola North for violating Directive No. 59 issued under the Consumer Affairs Authority Act.

According to the CAA, a raid carried out on July 28 led to the seizure of 42 protective helmets that did not bear the required SLS 517 certification mark.

Investigations revealed that the Weliweriya outlet had offered the non-compliant helmets for sale, while the Makola-based business had distributed the helmets to the retailer.

When the case was taken up before the Mahara Magistrate's Court, both defendants pleaded guilty to the charges.

The court imposed a fine of Rs. 5,000 each on the retailer and the distributor, amounting to a total of Rs. 10,000, and ordered the immediate destruction of all 42 seized helmets.

The CAA urged the public to report the sale or distribution of helmets without the SLS certification mark by calling its 1977 hotline during office hours.
