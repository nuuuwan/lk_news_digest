My last days in Parliament: MP Archchuna

Colombo, July 21 (Daily Mirror) - Referring to his political future, independent MP Ramanathan Archchuna today said he is currently spending his last days in Parliament.

"I will not be in Parliament for long. I will be in Parliament for two more weeks only," he told the House.

He said the case filed by Oshala Herath asking the Judiciary to remove me from Parliament will create a wrong impression that Sinhalese people do not want independent MPs.
