Will 1,000cc-plus motorcycles be allowed in Sri Lanka?

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The government is considering whether to permit high-powered motorcycles with engine capacities exceeding 1,000cc for special purposes, including sports activities, Deputy Minister of Transport Prasanna Gunasena informed Parliament today.

The Deputy Minister said a committee has been appointed to examine the matter and that a final decision will be made based on its recommendations.

He made these remarks while responding to a question raised by Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Suranga Rathnayaka, who inquired whether the Ministry of Transport was considering removing existing legal restrictions on motorcycles exceeding 1,000cc.

The MP also asked whether such motorcycles could be permitted under strict regulations, particularly for use on designated racing tracks or other suitable locations.

Referring to several high-powered motorcycles currently in police custody, Rathnayaka further questioned whether arrangements could be made to release them.

In response, Deputy Minister Gunasena said the government would decide on the necessary measures after receiving the committee's recommendations.
