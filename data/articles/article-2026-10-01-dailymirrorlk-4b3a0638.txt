Lightning advisory issued for several areas

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daliy Mirror) - The Department of Meteorology has issued an Amber-level severe lightning advisory for several parts of the country, warning of thundershowers and severe lightning until 11.30 p.m. today (Oct. 1).

The advisory, issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre at 10.30 a.m., is in effect for the Central, Sabaragamuwa, Uva, Eastern and North Central provinces and the Mannar District.

The Met. Department said thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are likely at several locations in the affected areas, with temporary localized strong winds also possible during thunderstorms.

The public has been urged to take adequate precautions to minimize the risk of lightning-related incidents and damage.

People have been advised to remain indoors during thunderstorms and avoid seeking shelter under trees. They have also been warned to stay away from open areas such as paddy fields, tea plantations and open water bodies.

The Department further advised the public to avoid using wired telephones and electrical appliances connected to the power supply during thunderstorms.
