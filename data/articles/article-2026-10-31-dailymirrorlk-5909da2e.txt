Perera & Sons opens 9th RO water plant at Ellagala Junior School

Perera & Sons (P&S), in collaboration with the Sri Lanka Navy, marked another milestone in its community welfare efforts with the opening of its ninth reverse osmosis (RO) water purification plant at Ellagala Junior School in Tissamaharama recently.

The facility was inaugurated by P&S Director Corporate Affairs Ruvini Kariyawasam, reaffirming the company’s commitment to supporting communities through meaningful and sustainable initiatives.

Established in 1984, Ellagala Junior School serves a community of 180 students and 22 teachers. The new RO water plant is a valuable addition to both the school and surrounding community of over 1,000 villagers, providing purified drinking water not only to the students and teachers but also to the residents of the area. This initiative aims to support the well-being of the wider community by making safe drinking water more accessible and contributing to a healthier environment for all.

Through this collaborative initiative, P&S and the Sri Lanka Navy have joined hands to address an essential community need, supporting a better environment for learning, growth and development.

The opening of the ninth RO plant marks another important milestone in P&s’ ‘Manu Mehewara’ corporate social responsibility initiative, which seeks to create a positive impact beyond the company’s business operations. Through initiatives that address essential community needs, P&S continues to demonstrate the importance of collaboration in delivering meaningful and lasting benefits to the communities it serves.
