Deep Depression to intensify into Cyclonic Storm today

Colombo, Nov. 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Deep Depression over the Bay of Bengal is expected to intensify into a Cyclonic Storm by this evening (16), the Department of Meteorology said.

The system, located around 620 kilometres north-east of Trincomalee, is likely to further develop into a Severe Cyclonic Storm by tomorrow (17), while moving away from Sri Lanka.

Meanwhile, several spells of showers are expected tomorrow in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-Western provinces, as well as in the Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Galle and Matara districts.
