Private sector to join Metro Bus Service within a year: Bimal

Colombo, Oct. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The private sector will be given an opportunity to participate in the Metro Bus Service within a year, Transport Minister Bimal Rathnayake said.

Speaking to journalists following an event in Colombo, the Minister said more than 100 Metro buses were currently in operation and stressed that the issue of obstructions should not be generalised to all private bus operators.

He said the vast majority of private bus operators had not caused any obstructions, with incidents reported involving only a few buses.

The Minister urged private bus operators to cooperate with the Metro Bus Service and align their operations with the programme.

He also warned that those who damage Metro buses could face serious legal consequences under laws relating to the damage of public property.
