Deputy Minister Arun Hemachandra to lead Sri Lankan delegation to Qatar

Colombo, Sep. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Employment Arun Hemachandra will lead a Sri Lankan delegation to Qatar from September 20 to 23 for the Sri Lanka–Qatar Joint Committee Meeting on labour and foreign employment cooperation.

The Joint Committee is being reconvened after more than five years and is expected to provide a platform for both countries to review existing cooperation and identify areas for further collaboration.

Discussions will focus on expanding employment opportunities for Sri Lankan workers, promoting safe, transparent and responsible recruitment practices, strengthening worker welfare and protection, developing skills, and improving institutional coordination between the two countries.

During his visit, Deputy Minister Hemachandra is scheduled to meet Qatar’s Minister of Labour, Dr. Ali bin Saeed bin Samikh Al Marri, and the Undersecretary of the Ministry of Labour, Sheikha Najwa bint Abdulrahman bin Nasser Al-Thani. The discussions will focus on further strengthening bilateral cooperation in labour and foreign employment.

The Deputy Minister is also expected to meet members and representatives of the Sri Lankan community in Qatar to hear their concerns and discuss issues affecting Sri Lankans living and working in the country, particularly matters related to employment and worker welfare.

Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism said Sri Lanka and Qatar maintain longstanding bilateral relations, with labour and manpower cooperation forming an important part of the partnership. The visit is expected to further strengthen cooperation while supporting Sri Lanka’s efforts to expand safe and dignified overseas employment opportunities and enhance the welfare and protection of Sri Lankan migrant workers.
