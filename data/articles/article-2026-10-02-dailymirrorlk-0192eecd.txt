Exams Dept. key to future-ready education: PM

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Examinations Department plays a vital role as a trusted partner in the Government’s efforts to build a future-ready education system based on social justice, Prime Minister and Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Dr. Harini Amarasuriya said

She made these remarks while attending the ‘Vajra Prabha’ ceremony held at the Department of Examinations premises on October 1 to mark the institution’s 75th anniversary.

Established in 1951, the Examinations Department is Sri Lanka’s principal national assessment institution, responsible for conducting major examinations including the Grade 5 Scholarship Examination, G.C.E. Ordinary Level and Advanced Level examinations and various competitive examinations, as well as issuing examination results and certificates.

The department evaluates hundreds of thousands of candidates each year and plays a key role in maintaining credibility in the country’s education and professional sectors.

A commemorative stamp and anniversary magazine marking the department’s 75th anniversary were launched during the ceremony. Commemorative gifts were also presented to retired Commissioners General of Examinations.

Addressing the gathering, Prime Minister Amarasuriya said the public has placed considerable trust in the Examinations Department as an institution that plays an important role in determining the future of children.

She said the department contributes to ensuring fairness and justice in society by providing opportunities for individuals to progress based on their abilities and dedication.

The Prime Minister commended the commitment of department officials in conducting the G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination without disruption, particularly during severe disasters in recent years, and praised their willingness to go beyond their regular duties when necessary.

She said the department’s culture of working together towards a common objective during critical situations was commendable.

The Prime Minister also appreciated the support provided by the department towards successfully conducting examination-related activities for the recruitment of teachers scheduled to take place this year.

She highlighted the need to make appropriate use of new technology and digitalisation to preserve the public trust built in the institution over the past 75 years.

At the same time, she highlighted the department’s responsibility to protect highly sensitive examination information in an increasingly digital environment.

The Prime Minister said the Government would take steps to expand necessary training opportunities to strengthen the capacity of Department of Examinations staff to meet emerging technological challenges.

Deputy Minister of Vocational Education Nalin Hewage, Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Ministry Secretary Nalaka Kaluwewa, Commissioner General of Examinations Indika Liyanage and Additional Commissioners General of Examinations and other officials attended the event.
