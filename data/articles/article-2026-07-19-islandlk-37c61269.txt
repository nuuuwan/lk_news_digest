SLT-MOBITEL powers next-gen mobility solutions

SLT‑MOBITEL Mobile has partnered with HelaGo, the country’s emerging ride‑hailing platform, to introduce exclusive mobile packages designed to enhance the connectivity needs of HelaGo drivers nationwide. The collaboration was formalised at an event held recently at the Mobitel Headquarters in the presence of HelaGo and SLT-MOBITEL leadership teams.

Through this partnership, SLT-MOBITEL aims to empower Sri Lanka’s ride-hailing community while accelerating the nation’s digital transformation. By leveraging its state-of-the-art 5G network with the widest nationwide coverage, SLT-MOBITEL enables drivers to stay seamlessly connected, more productive, and highly responsive on the move, ensuring a superior network experience.

The new lineup features three tailored plans to fit different driver schedules and budgets. The premier Rs. 596 monthly package is valid for 30 days and includes 750 minutes, 750 SMS, 5GB of anytime data, alongside a generous 15GB of dedicated app data for the HelaGo app, WhatsApp, and Google Maps. For shorter-term needs, a highly affordable Rs. 196 weekly package offers 150 minutes, 150 SMS, 1GB of anytime data, and 3.5GB of dedicated app data. Additionally, for drivers seeking a budget-friendly, short-term option, the Rs. 46 daily package provides 1-day validity and includes 20 minutes, 20 SMS, 125MB of anytime data, and 512MB of app data.

Supporting drivers through reliable connectivity, SLT-MOBITEL continues to play a key role in advancing Sri Lanka’s digital transformation journey.
