Over 890 illegal firearms seized in first nine months

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Police have seized 891 illegal firearms across the country between January 1 and September 17 this year, Cabinet Spokesman and Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said.

Speaking at the Cabinet media briefing today, Minister Jayatissa said the firearms seized during police operations included 14 T-56 assault rifles, 33 pistols and 25 revolvers.

He said police had seized a significantly higher number of firearms last year, with 2,404 firearms taken into custody during operations conducted in 2025. Among them were 74 T-56 assault rifles.

Minister Jayatissa also disclosed that 40 shooting incidents had been reported across the country since the beginning of this year.

Of these, 35 incidents were identified as organised shootings, he said.

A total of 22 people have been killed in shooting incidents reported so far this year, according to the Minister.
