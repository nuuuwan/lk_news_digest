Who will guard the guards themselves?

Colombo, Oct. 3 (Daily Mirror) - A stock of case productions including a repeater gun has been stolen from the Bibila Magistrate’s Court.

Courts Registrar Dayaratne Baddevithana has on Thursday (01) complained to police that 50 case productions including a repeater gun, jewellery, cash, parts of a weapon, and SIM cards, relating to cases pending in court, are missing from the case production room.
