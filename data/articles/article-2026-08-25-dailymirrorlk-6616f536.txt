Over 16,600 people affected by adverse weather

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 16,647 people from 4,241 families have been affected by adverse weather conditions reported across several districts of Sri Lanka from 6.00 p.m. on August 3 to 6.00 a.m. today (25), to the latest situation report issued by the Disaster Management Centre (DMC) said.

The affected population has been reported across 15 districts, with Colombo recording the highest number of affected persons at 7,736, followed by Kandy with 4,925 and Ratnapura with 2,487.

According to the DMC report, nine deaths have been reported during the period, while 60 people have been injured and two people are reported missing.

A total of 741 houses have been partially damaged, while eight houses have been completely damaged.

The report also indicates that 25 families are currently staying in the homes of relatives, while arrangements have been made to provide assistance to families affected by the adverse weather.

The situation has affected communities in Ampara, Anuradhapura, Galle, Hambantota, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Kurunegala, Matale, Matara, Colombo, Puttalam, Ratnapura, Badulla and Trincomalee districts.
