One dead, 18 injured during stampede in Sialkot at TikTok star’s wedding event

ARAB NEWS - One person was killed while 18 were injured this week in a stampede during a wedding event of a TikTok star in Pakistan’s eastern city of Sialkot, police said on Sunday, booking organizers for violating safety standard operating procedures.

The incident took place on Saturday evening as a large crowd assembled for a wedding event of Ansar Mehmood, who uses the alias of “Mr. Pattlo” on TikTok, at the Faraz Cricket Ground on Daska Road.

Police said in a statement on Saturday that as per preliminary investigations, event organizers invited a large number of people by advertising prize draws for motorcycles, cars and other valuable items at the event through social media. During the event, police said an announcement made over the loudspeaker asked participants to enter the ground simultaneously, triggering a stampede.

One person was killed while 18 others were injured in the stampede, the police statement said.

“Six people have been arrested in connection with the incident, while TikToker Ansar Mehmood alias Pattlo, who allegedly invited people to the wedding celebration through social media, remains at large,” Shahid Mehmood, a local police officer, told Arab News.

The injured individuals were provided medical assistance and have been discharged from hospitals, police said. The stampede disrupted traffic on the main Daska-Sialkot road, while incidents of disorder and damage to public property were also reported, it added.

Cases have been registered against nine individuals, including Mehmood, the ground owner, other organizers and 40 to 45 unidentified security guards, police said. Suspects have been charged under sections 322, 431, 440, 188, 109, 148 and 149 of the Pakistan Penal Code, as well as Section 16 of the Maintenance of Public Order.

“The charges include violations of safety standard operating procedures (SOPs) and permit conditions,” the statement said.
