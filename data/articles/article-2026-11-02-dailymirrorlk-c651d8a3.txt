Kandy trader fined for hiding 28 Litro gas cylinders

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - A trader in Kandy has been fined by the Kandy Magistrate’s Court for claiming that gas cylinders were unavailable while keeping 28 Litro LPG cylinders in stock.

The Court also ordered the confiscation of all 28 cylinders, each weighing 12.5kg, which were seized during a raid conducted by the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA).

Legal action was taken against D.D. Gurusinghe Trading, an authorised Litro Gas sales outlet at No. 124, Kotugodella Street, Kandy.

The special raid was carried out by investigation officers of the CAA’s Kandy District Office on October 29, following complaints from consumers.

During the operation, a CAA officer posing as a customer visited the outlet and requested a 12.5kg Litro gas cylinder. However, the trader claimed that no cylinders were available for sale.

Following the refusal, CAA officers inspected the premises and found 28 Litro gas cylinders despite the trader’s claim.

The cylinders were seized and produced before the Kandy Magistrate’s Court as case productions.

Following the proceedings, the Court imposed a fine on the trader and ordered that all 28 cylinders be confiscated and forfeited to the State.

Under Section 16(a) of the Consumer Affairs Authority Act No. 9 of 2003, traders are prohibited from falsely claiming that goods are unavailable when they have such goods in their possession for sale.

The CAA said it would continue raids and investigations to prevent traders from concealing goods and misleading consumers.

Consumers who come across similar practices can inform the CAA by calling its 1977 hotline during office hours.
