Sysco LABS MD at SLASSCOM CEO Breakfast Panel to discuss cybersecurity

Reinforcing its commitment to advancing industry dialogue, Sysco LABS recently participated in the latest SLASSCOM CEO Breakfast Forum, where Managing Director Thushera Kawdawatta joined a distinguished panel of business and technology leaders to discuss the evolving role of cybersecurity in building organizational resilience.

Held under the theme “Transforming Cyber Security into Business Resilience,” the forum brought together respected voices from across the corporate, technology, and cybersecurity sectors, including Aditi Sawhney - Senior Vice President – Security Solutions Product Management, Mastercard Asia Pacific; Jayantha Fernando - Partner, Heritage Partners; Madu Ratnayake - President, SCYBERS; and Thushera Kawdawatta - Managing Director, Sysco LABS Sri Lanka. The discussion was moderated by cybersecurity strategist and policy advisor Asela Waidyalankara.
