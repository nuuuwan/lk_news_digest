Grade 5 scholarship exam to be held at 2,723 centres tomorrow

Colombo, August 8 (Daily Mirror) - The 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship Examination will be held tomorrow (09) at 2,723 examination centres across the island, with 294,089 candidates expected to sit for the examination, the Examinations Department said.

Examinations Commissioner General Indika Kumari Liyanage made the announcement at a special media briefing held this morning (08).

Of the total candidates, 220,366 have registered for the Sinhala medium, while 73,723 have registered for the Tamil medium.

The second question paper will be conducted first from 9.30 a.m. to 10.45 a.m. Following a short break, the first question paper will be held from 11.15 a.m. to 12.15 p.m.

The Department has advised candidates to arrive at their examination centres at least one hour before the examination begins.

The Commissioner General said tuition classes related to the examination ended at midnight on August 5 and that advertising related to the scholarship examination through print and electronic media is prohibited until the examination concludes.

Candidates have also been advised to carefully read the instructions on the question papers, correctly indicate their examination numbers and provide answers in clear handwriting.

Students have been strictly advised not to bring mobile phones, smart watches, notes or other electronic devices into the examination hall.
