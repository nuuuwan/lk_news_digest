Former Deputy Excise Commissioner denied bail over Rs.21.17 Mn bribery allegations linked to W.M. Mendis

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama, today refused bail to former Deputy Commissioner of Excise Channa Jayanath Alwis Weerakkody, who is being held over allegations of accepting bribes through cheques issued from the accounts of W.M. Mendis & Company.

Appearing for the suspect, Senior Counsel Asoka Weerasooriya submitted that his client had no ability to influence any of the six matters outlined at the end of the first B report.

Counsel further informed court that the suspect’s daughter had been receiving psychiatric treatment for some time and that her condition had worsened following his arrest.

He also assured court that his client was willing to extend his fullest cooperation to the investigations in any manner required and sought bail on his behalf.

The investigation conducted by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption relates to allegations that the suspect received 12 cheques amounting to Rs. 21.17 million from current account No. 805, maintained by W.M. Mendis & Company at the Bank of Ceylon’s Corporate Branch, during 2015.

Investigations have revealed that the suspect had allegedly accepted cheques issued unlawfully from accounts belonging to W.M. Mendis & Company, a duly licensed entity that operated under his supervision while he was serving as the Excise Superintendent of Kalutara and Acting Excise Superintendent (Distilleries) in Aluthgama.

The investigation has also examined a series of cheques allegedly issued from the company’s bank account.

Investigators also informed court that statements had to be recorded from the directors of W.M. Mendis & Company who had signed the relevant cheques.

Officers also informed court that statements needed to be recorded from the suspect’s family members as part of the ongoing investigations.

Further investigations were also underway to determine whether the suspect had used the alleged bribe money amounting to Rs. 21.17 million to unlawfully acquire assets and properties or make investments, and whether such funds had been used for money laundering.

Following the submissions made by both parties, the Magistrate ordered that the suspect be further remanded till December 1 and directed investigators to expedite the ongoing investigations and conclude them without delay.

Senior Counsel Asoka Weerasooriya, Sanjaya Marambe and Pasan Karunarathna appeared for the suspect.
