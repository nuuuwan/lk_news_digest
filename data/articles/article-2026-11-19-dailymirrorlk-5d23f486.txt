CSE rebounds as ASPI regains 21,500

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to positive territory on 19 November 2026, ending a four-session losing streak as buying interest strengthened across the market.

The ASPI gained 140.36 points, or 0.66%, to close at 21,543.12, while the S&P SL20 advanced 45.47 points, or 0.75%, to 6,142.43. The ASPI recovered above 21,500 during the morning and consolidated around that level through most of the afternoon.

Market turnover remained subdued at LKR 1.73 billion, with 53.81 million shares traded. Activity was heavily concentrated in Hatton National Bank, which generated approximately LKR 754.11 million in turnover.

Crossings accounted for around 37.5% of total equity turnover, with all crossing activity concentrated in HNB.N through 15 transactions totaling approximately LKR 648.84 million.

Market breadth improved significantly, with 140 gainers against 67 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 2.09. Foreign investors, however, remained net sellers with an outflow of approximately LKR 95.85 million.

Commercial Bank was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by Hatton National Bank, Sri Lanka Telecom, Distilleries and LOLC Holdings. Hayleys recorded the largest negative contribution.

The return to positive territory and stronger market breadth indicate an improvement in short-term sentiment following the recent decline. However, subdued turnover and continued foreign selling suggest buying conviction remains moderate, with 21,500 now an important support area and 21,600 the immediate level to watch for further upside.
