Fly A World Consultants recognised for excellence in travel consultancy

Fly A World Consultants and Tourism (Pvt) Ltd has been honoured with the Excellence in Travel Consultancy Services Award in the Small Scale Services Sector at the Honor of Business Talent 2026, recognising the company’s outstanding contribution to Sri Lanka’s travel and tourism industry.

The award celebrates organisations that demonstrate excellence in customer service, business performance, and professional standards. It highlights Fly A World’s commitment to providing reliable, personalised, and high-quality travel solutions for both leisure and business travellers.

Founded by M. C. M. Shahood, Founder and Director, Fly A World Consultants and Tourism has established itself as a trusted name in the travel industry.

The company is a Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka licensed travel agency, a registered member of the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA), and a member of the Travel Agents Association of Sri Lanka (TAASL).

Fly A World offers a comprehensive range of travel services, including international air ticketing, visa consultation, inbound and outbound tour operations, hotel reservations, travel insurance, and customised holiday packages.

The company also specialises in creating tailor-made travel experiences for individuals, families, corporate clients, and student travellers, providing complete support from travel planning and documentation to destination management.Over the years, the company has built a strong reputation through its customer-focused approach, professional expertise, and dedication to delivering seamless travel experiences. By continuously enhancing its service capabilities and strengthening industry partnerships, Fly A World has become a trusted travel partner for clients seeking efficient, reliable, and hassle-free travel solutions.

Commenting on the achievement, Founder and Director M. C. M. Shahood said that the award reflects the dedication, professionalism, and hard work of the entire Fly A World team, as well as the continued trust and confidence placed in the company by its valued clients and business partners.

He added that this recognition will further inspire the company to enhance its service standards, expand its range of travel solutions, and strengthen its position as one of Sri Lanka’s leading travel consultancy providers.
