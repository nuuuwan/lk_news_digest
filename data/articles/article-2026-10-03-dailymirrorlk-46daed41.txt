IndiGo resumes key India-Sri Lanka flights

India’s leading low-cost carrier IndiGo has resumed daily flights on key routes between India and Sri Lanka, strengthening air connectivity between the two neighbouring countries and supporting tourism, trade and business travel.

The airline resumed services on three important routes from October 1, including New Delhi-Colombo, Bengaluru-Colombo and Tiruchirappalli (Trichy)-Jaffna.

The resumption of daily flights between New Delhi and Colombo as well as Bengaluru and Colombo, is expected to cater to demand from tourists, business travellers and diplomatic passengers travelling between India and Sri Lanka.

Meanwhile, IndiGo’s direct service between Tiruchirappalli Airport in Tamil Nadu and Jaffna International Airport resumed on October 1 after a three-month suspension.

The Trichy-Jaffna service is scheduled to operate three days a week, on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays.

On these days, the flight will depart Trichy at 1.05 p.m. and arrive in Jaffna at 1.50 p.m. It will leave Jaffna for Trichy at 2.30 p.m.

A total of 88 passengers travelled on the flight when the service resumed yesterday.

The restoration of the direct air link has been welcomed by people in the Northern province, as it provides a faster and more convenient travel option for those travelling between northern Sri Lanka and Tamil Nadu.

The Trichy-Jaffna route is also expected to facilitate long-standing cultural, family and commercial ties between Tamil Nadu and the Northern province.

The renewed air links between the two countries are expected to contribute to the further development of tourism, trade and economic and social relations between India and Sri Lanka.
